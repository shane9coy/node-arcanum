# Ghayat al-Hakim (غاية الحكيم)

**Author:** Maslama al-Majriti (attributed, d. ~1008 CE)
**Original language:** Arabic
**Original period:** ~10th-11th century
**Status:** Public domain (medieval work)
**Public translation status:** Coming soon

## Description

*Ghayat al-Hakim* ("The Aim of the Wise") is the Arabic source text for the Latin *Picatrix*. It is the foundational encyclopedic grimoire of the Arabic astral-magical tradition, covering planetary magic, lunar mansion workings, talismanic construction, and the broader esoteric sciences of its period.

The Latin translation (made at the court of Alfonso X of Castile in 1256) circulated widely in medieval Europe and shaped the Western magical tradition for centuries.
