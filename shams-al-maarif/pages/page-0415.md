# Page 415 (English translation)
Beginning of Kaukab:
Indeed I believe in your Lord, so listen to me (Q 26:17), clear error (Q 26:18), k 40, h 70, You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help (Q 1:5).
Respond by the right of the noble worshipped one, and by the right of the sovereign who prevails over you whose command is Sa'fṣ, and by the right of Lahtahṭīl. Moses said: What you have brought is sorcery (Q 10:81), k. By the right of God the Mighty I adjure you, O Saydagh and Shamtamāyāʾīl and Barqān, by the right of the worshipped sovereign. Glorious is He who relieves every debtor. Glorious is He who dispels every sorrow. Glorious is He who made water flow in seas and springs. Glorious is He who placed His treasures between the kāf and nūn. Glorious is He who when He wills a thing says to it "Be" and it is, k (1).

O God, subdue for me the servant of this sura, as You subdued the sea for Moses, and the fire for Abraham, and the mountains and iron for David, and the jinn and humans and the wind and the devils for Solomon, prayers and peace be upon them, and the sun and moon and all things for our master Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, that You subdue for me the servant of this sura to fulfill my need, by the right of Your great and greatest name. O God, O swift, O near, O responder, O extender, O loving, O possessor of the glorious throne, O originator, O restorer, O doer of what He wills. I ask You by the light of Your face which filled the pillars of Your throne, and by Your power with which You have power over all of Your creation, and by Your mercy which encompassed all things. There is no god but You, O helper, help me and fulfill my need in this hour by Your mercy, O most merciful of the merciful. O God, cast love for me into the heart of the servant of this sura, they love them as the love of God, and those who believe are strongest in love for God, He loves them and they love Him (Q 2:165). Do not feed him, and prostrate and draw near (Q 96:19).

Embers of Kaukab:
And they say: When is this promise (Q 34:29), clear exaltation. O God, I ask You, O powerful, O omnipotent, O gentle, O victorious, O guide. Respond, O Isrāfīl, and you, O Shamkhursh, hearing and obedient, by the right of these names. Guide us to the straight path (Q 1:6), by the right of Lahṭal, and indeed it is a mighty book, falsehood does not approach it from before it nor from behind it, a revelation from the wise, the praised (Q 41:42). Respond, O servant of this sura, by the right of Qirshat, and fulfill my need. Glorious is He who relieves every debtor. Glorious is He who purifies every imprisoned one. Glorious is He who dispels every sorrow. Glorious is He who made water flow in seas and springs. Glorious is He who placed His treasures between the kāf and nūn. Glorious is He who when He wills a thing says to it "Be" and it is, k (2).

O God, subdue for me the servant of this sura, as You subdued the sea for Moses, and the fire for Abraham, and the mountains and iron for David, and the jinn and humans and the devils for Solomon, prayers and peace be upon them, and the sun [and moon] [watermark text at bottom obscures some parts but continues the prayer as above]
