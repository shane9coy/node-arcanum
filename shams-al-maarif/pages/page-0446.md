Security and Safety: I ask You, O Allah, by Your beautiful attributes and all Your supplications, that You make easy for me human and essential acts of obedience, the degrees both near and far, and that You make me one who thanks You perpetually without pause. Protect me by Your perfect light and universal grace that I should not rely upon Your blessings which distance me from You. Grant me an ever-advancing position in striving to attain Your pleasure, for You are the One capable of all things and the One who repels all harm. O Allah, protect me by Your perfect light, O Possessor of majesty and generosity.

The Work of Goodness and Prosperity:
The Ism al-Shukur contains one letter of the Greatest Name. This name holds many mysteries for its practitioner. Whoever desires goodness and prosperity should perform its litany. Its client-officer's name is Muntayal (Muntiyayil), and he rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 526 ranks, and in each rank there are 526 assigned clients. The supplication of the Ism al-Shukur is:

"O Shakur (the Most Thankful), You are the One who has spread gratitude in the hearts of the saints, and You are the One who has prepared the hearts of Your servants and Your loved ones to praise You through dignity and nearness. You are the One who magnifies Your glory through Your deeds for whoever holds fast to Your name. Al-Wahhab (the Ever-Giving), I ask You by the secret of the reality spread in gratitude and by Your hidden gratitude encompassed within praise, that You make me one who thanks Your blessings, one who remembers Your favors in secret and openly, one who bears Your sublime bounties. Grant me from the light of praise and goodness in the worlds of Your unveiling a preparation and a matter. Enter me into the circle of those whom Your encompassing light surrounds and the sources of Your sanctity, along with blessings from You, for by You both secretly and openly You are the One who praises Yourself for character traits and the One praised by every tongue at every time and moment."

Fulfilling Needs and Achieving Lofty Degrees:
The Ism al-Ala contains one letter of the Greatest Name. This is most excellent for the seeker of lofty degrees and is also extremely swift in effect for needs. Its client's name is Haytayil (Haytaiyil), and under him are 13 officers, each of whom oversees 110 ranks, and in each rank there are 110 clients who carry out daily the creative actions of ease. The supplication of the Ism al-Ala is:

"O Ala (the Most High), You are the Most High who established greatness and magnificence for Your own essence, and You made known Your own greatness—therefore there is no glory except Your glory. You are the One purified from any notion that the Great is made great by the magnifying of magnificence. O Aziz, O Jalil, You have glorified Your essence and magnified Your attributes. I ask You by the secret of the exaltation of Your greatness in the station of establishment, and by the subtleties of Your magnificence and the place of certainty, and by the radiance of Your countenance's light and Your permanence and Your splendor in the sites of both realms of existence, that You make me one who draws near from the darkness of the separation of existence to the illumination of the light of the Presence and the fountains, and that You grant me from the vastness of Your generosity an essence that encompasses the people of the heavens and the earths. You clothe me from the light of Your manifestation with awe by which they are humbled before me on the Day of Assembly. Shelter me beneath Your perfect shade in the place of unveiling at the transformation of the earth—not the earth—when it is transformed on the Day when none shall benefit except the kingdom. Make me complete in essence through the perpetuity of sensory existence by witnessing the traces of Your creation and the vision of what is witnessed—for You
