# Page 635 (English translation)
[635]

[Boxed Header]
Chapter: On the Virtues of the Name "Malik" (King)
[/Boxed Header]

[Heading]
The True Owner of All Things is Only Allah, the Most High:
[/Heading]

From its meaning is meant that Essence which is the true Owner of all things, and the ultimate end of all things is toward Him alone. This is the attribute of the Lord, the Most High, because His sovereignty encompasses the World of Dominion (Malak) and the World of Malakut and the World of Jabriyut. And this is because this Name contains three letters: Mīm, Lām, and Kāf. The Mīm is from the letters of isolation (kisar al-ahad) and is from the circles of letters, and these letters are for manifestation (ẓāhir) because when Allah, the Most High, desired to manifest, this letter became latent in its outer form, while in the interior it has its circles because it has no reality to which letters could be joined. For this reason He created the Mīm and made its form latent, so that its head would be before it, while its interior would be connected to the falling from the worship of the interior of Divine Unity. The Mīm is the manifest ruler, and Allah, the Most High, created its severed head, and then for its sake He created the Chair (Throne) so that it would encompass the forms of determined existents, and then from its light He created the Lawh (Tablet) and the Pen (Qalam) which flies—this He distinguished, and with it He encompassed the circle of the Word so that the complete ethics of Lordship would be upon it. And He created the heavens with the head of Lordship and the head of encompassing the head of the celestial sphere, and He distinguished its luminaries because it is related to one of the pillars of the Footstool (Arsh), which are dedicated to the lofty sciences through the Name of Dominion (Malik) and the letter Mīm.

[Heading]
The Repetition of Mīm in the Sacred Name of the Prophet ﷺ:
[/Heading]

Likewise, this letter was repeated three times with three endpoints in the blessed name of our Master (the Prophet). If you place it opposite Malik, all governance will be connected to you. And if you place it opposite governance, the luminaries of governance will become manifest in your intellect. And this is the final letter.

And the Lām is the letter through which Allah, the Most High, aided the Jabriyut. When its burden became mingled with the luminaries of Malakut, no letter was found that would be connected to it. Then Allah, the Most High, manifested the World of Dominion (Malak) from the interior of the Lām, from which the meaning is "toward." Then Allah, the Most High, created from it the World of Dominion along with the secrets of Jabriyut and the secrets of Malakut.

Point: Know that when Allah, the Most High, created this world which possesses intellect, He granted to each individual within it an intellect appropriate to their rank. Thus He created the Point of Truth (Ayn al-Haqq) and placed within it various signs for accepting luminosity and the unveiling of the secrets of Malakut. Then He created humanity. Then He created the mountains and placed minerals within them, and the Mīm is its cloud. Because this is the encompassing of the circle of intellects, and its number is forty. For this reason Allah, the Most High, settled it in His most wondrous creation and spoke to it, and in the worlds of luminaries He created it with infinity and eternity and with the Spirit of spirits. Thus wisdom of prophecy became within it, and within it is specification. The Spirit is from the World of Jabriyut, and the World of Malakut is the First Intellect, and the Second Intellect is ranked with the worlds—its function is to grant them powers and to bring them aid, and this is the means of receiving perfections and acquiring secrets. Thus for this reason its name remained "stages."

Allah, the Most High, appointed many angels upon the Spirit who cast the secrets of the unseen with these realities of Malakut. Thus this world is designated the World of Dominion (Malak), and this world encompasses three worlds: the World of Plants, the World of Animals, and the World of Minerals. Among all animals, the most beautiful and excellent is humanity, and the interior of Dominion in the human encompasses many essences and selves and hearts. And because the world of Power wanders through the gardens and rose-gardens of the World of Plants from the leaven of potency, such a place was made in which there are many dangers of countless jinn. I say that this is based on the qualities of the divine ones, and within its right there are seven holy eyes whose speech is the ultimate form. And it conveyed to the ends of the Spirit the thought of the two parts of humanity, and the rational soul also conveys blessing upon it. Now, the good one should return from here.
