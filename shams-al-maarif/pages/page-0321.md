# Page 321 (English translation)
Abraham, peace be upon him, was seated in the catapult. He then recited "Bismillah" (In the name of Allah), and by His blessing the fire became cool and safe for him.

Narrative: Imam Awza'i, may Allah's mercy be upon him, states: One night I was sitting, when suddenly a thought arose in my heart and great fear began to overwhelm me. I immediately recited "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim" (In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful). At that very moment, that thought departed. A voice came saying: You have sought refuge with something very great. Every letter of "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim" is a key to the divine names. The ba' is the name "Al-Basir" (the All-Seeing), the sin is the name "Al-Sami'" (the All-Hearing), the mim is the name "Malik" (the King), the alif is the name "Allah," the lam is the name "Al-Latif" (the Subtle), the ha' is the name "Al-Hadi" (the Guide), the ra' is the name "Al-Razzaq" (the Provider), the ha' is the name "Hann" (the Compassionate), and the mim is the key to "Al-Mani'" (the Withholder) and "Al-Mu'ti" (the Giver). These are the names with which supplication is made at the beginning of every matter. Any supplication made with these names will be accepted. If we were to write all their properties completely, time would be consumed and pens would tire. Some of their properties have already been mentioned earlier. They have one great accord (awfaq) with which the "thaman kalima" (the eight words) [is connected]. Every person should gather its letteral and numerical temperament in one place and witness the wonders of Allah's grace, whose explanation is impossible. In it is the Supreme Name (Ism A'zam), and whoever makes a supplication with it will be accepted. This is the form of the talisman:

His Eminence Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with him, states that the Commander of the Faithful, Ali, may Allah honor his face, took my hand and we came at the time of evening toward the place of Baqi'. He said to me: O Ibn Abbas, recite. I recited "Bismillah." He began telling me the mysteries of each letter until dawn broke. Write this down.

The strange and wondrous benefits of Surah Fatiha:
Allah states: He expands and narrows [ provision]. The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Fatihat al-Kitab" (the Opening of the Book) was given to me from beneath the Throne. And he said: Whoever enters his house and recites Surah Fatiha and Surah Ikhlas, poverty will depart from him and abundance of good will occur. The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said: Fatihat al-Kitab is the supplication for every illness. Know that whoever understands the secret of "Al-Hamd" (the Praise) has understood the secret of Paradise, and every book that is connected to Paradise. His Eminence Ali, may Allah honor his face, said: If I wished to write the explanation of Surah Fatiha and the sea, I would load seventy camels. Some great scholars say this surah contains one thousand apparent and one thousand hidden properties. Muslimah Kalam ibn Ibrahim states: "Umm al-Kitab" (the Mother of the Book), meaning Surah Fatiha, is the peak and pillar of the Quran. In it are those five names with which Allah the Exalted honored this surah above all other surahs. Among these names is the Supreme Name, and whatever supplication is made with it is accepted.
