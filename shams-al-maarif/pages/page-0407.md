# Page 407 (English translation)
[Boxed Heading]
For the Summoning of the King of Jinn

"And the Trumpet is blown, and suddenly they will emerge from their graves hastening to their Lord" — the Messengers have spoken truly. "It is but one cry, and behold, they will all be presented before Us."

"This is for summoning the king of jinn. This verse should also be recited along with it:

"And the jinn race knew indeed that they would be seized. This is Hell which you were promised; enter it today in accordance with what you used to disbelieve."

Writing this verse on the forehead of one possessed by jinn or suffering from epilepsy will be beneficial. He speaks in the language of jinn.

The verse "Today We will set a seal upon their mouths, and their hands will speak to Us, and their feet will bear witness to what they used to earn" is for summoning a fleeing person.

Beginning from "Ya Sin" with the letter of the People of the Book, read up to "then he had no power to proceed" (i.e., Surah Ya-Sin 36:1–78), then read "Or darknesses in a deep sea covered by waves upon waves and clouds, so that he would have no light" (i.e., Surah An-Nur 24:40) to its end — read this five times, and say "Indeed, He is able to bring it back" three times.

"On the Day when secrets are tested, he will have no strength and no helper" — three times. "A bird in a cage, humbled and confined, and whoever he plays with shall be rescued from creation only by the presence of blessings..." to the end. Then say:

"O Allah, I ask You by the honor of Your prophet and beloved Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that You fulfill my need and grant me my request."

"O Allah, I ask You by the station of Your beloved Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that You fulfill my need and grant me my request." Then make a supplication — it will be accepted immediately.

"And We did not teach him poetry, nor is it befitting for him. This is only a reminder and a clear Quran, to warn whoever is alive and establish the Word against the disbelievers."

[Boxed Heading]
The Operation of Bringing a Person or Animal Under Possession

This is for a person or animal who does not come under control.

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Glory be to Him Who has subjected this to us, and we could never have accomplished it. If We willed, We could send down upon them from the sky a sign, to which their necks would remain humble. And indeed, for you in the cattle is a lesson — We give you drink from what is in their bellies, from between digested matter and blood, pure milk, pleasant to those who drink — and from the trees, He brings forth a growing thing emerging from Mount Sinai, giving oil and seasoning for food. And for you in the cattle is a lesson. We feed you from what is in their bellies, and from them you derive many benefits, and from them you eat. Upon them and on ships you are carried." — to this point.

"O Lord, I ask You by Your name with which You opened the realm of command and creation, by the manifestation of the true and apparent, for the sake of descent and elevation, a matter and an existence, a manifestation and a revelation for the one to whom guidance was given, known for the one who was made to witness, unknown for the one to whom it was revealed — by what its brightness has illuminated from Him, much — do not strike in the uniqueness of what was perfected from the Perfected One: O Knowing, O Wise, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord. And I ask You, O Allah, by Your name, by this increasing emanation from the throne of obligation and possibility, united for the manifestation of the Greatest Name by sacred light, the Powerful, the Omnipotent."
