Properties of the Precious Stone and its Secrets and Mysteries and Symbols

Know that the ancient philosophers have discussed extensively regarding the precious stone, for it possesses actual efficacy. That is, its effect is manifested from eternity prior to any created thing, and many philosophers have alluded to this. This stone is triangular, and within it are three veins, which signify the rational soul, the expansive spirit, and the corporeal form bound by regulation, and it is a single distinguished stone. The elaboration of all these is as follows: for we have mentioned the colors and extracted various colors from them. And the person who has erred is he who says that these, namely the colors, are the same as those which the people have assigned general names, whereas the predecessors have criticized this, for according to them the stone and its dust represent analysis and composition, dissolution and coagulation, taking and repelling, death and life. Although each of these operations is a word that is the opposite of another. When you gather all the words together, then the operation will be complete; otherwise each word indicates half of the operation. For example, analysis and composition, or like refinement and densification, purification and whitening and sublimation—each of these words applies truthfully to half the operation, not to the entire operation. Thus when one says "analysis," what is meant thereby is the separation of the refined from it, so that distinction occurs in each one, and the dense becomes dry such that no absolute densification remains in it, while the refined remains completely, having no absolute densification within it. And by composition is meant the gathering together, the gathering of the distinguished refined substance and the gathering of the homogeneous substance in the same form, both refined and dense. This continues until both exist in a single action and are perfect in the natural color, such that one does not separate from the other at all.

Fire removes the spirit from the body:

Know that when a body has undergone the operation of separation, calcination, or projection, its spirit becomes separated from the body. For if the spirit were not separated, then the body also would not be calcined or projected, nor would its moisture be lost. For this moisture is such a thing that it opposes fire, so that the form of this body may not be corrupted. And among minerals, see that there is no body of silver or gold that can withstand fire to such a degree, and apart from these two, when other bodies are placed upon fire, both the refined and dense parts separate from them. And when a body has been purified and then moisture is added to it in place of that which has been expelled from it, that constitutes purification. And the necessity of adding this moisture arose because the nature that had originally placed moisture in this body was not in accordance with equilibrium; for if it had been in equilibrium, the body would have been a perfect elixir. But the fire overcame it.

Fire unites scattered bodies:

Therefore, it became necessary to add such moisture to it, and without fire this composition was impossible, for fire is such a thing that it gathers together the parts of the body and extracts the fruit from the furnace, and it also effects separation in mixed bodies. For this very reason the great philosophers have said that whoever has not discovered the secret of fire cannot know anything of this art, because the harm of fire exceeds its benefit. The student will know from fire kindled and burned what degree of fire this body can burn at and from what degree this body will not melt. This experiment is extremely necessary and most difficult. And know that when the moisture of a body has departed by fire and the difference between its refined and dense parts has occurred, it is called half of coagulation and half of the operation and the beginning.
