# Page 648 (English translation)
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Jabbar"

Know that they call "Jabbar" [the Compeller] one who rules over everyone with supremacy and whom nothing hinders in his sovereignty. And this is the essence of the Exalted Lord, and He alone is Jabbar al-Mutlaq [the Absolute Compeller], who compels everyone. And to look into its details is part of disclosure, because the Most High is witnessed in this magnificent [name] through the world of the kingdom of dominion, which is also called the world of testimony [shahādat], because it is the nearest to those who engage in verification. But this is their own action, so the supremacy and force are for the sake of Allah. When He brings down water from the heaven with His mercy, He takes it according to what is known, and this is a true pillar; and if His aspects were different, it would descend upon the earth. Allah the Exalted says:

"And you see the mountains, you think them solid, but they pass by like clouds—the work of Allah, who has perfected everything."1

[Heading]
From some things comes sustenance, from others comes destruction:

Some destructions are such that through them human existence continues, and some are such in which there is human destruction. Consider that if water comes abundantly upon some small destructions, it becomes harmful for it—whether it be the water of alchemy or the water of mercy. And in similar form there is such destruction upon which the abundance of water does not bring harm. Thus from this name it is known that for every world there is a limit, just as within a tree there are roots and branches, and within the branches are leaves, and within them are flowers and fruits, and for each of these there is a limit and number appropriate to its worthiness.

[Heading]
The system of the four elements is established through compulsion and force:

Thus in air, fire, and water there is the secret of compulsion and force, because if this secret were not present, the order of the universe would be corrupted. And these four elements are the very magnificent things from which the order of the universe is established.

[Heading]
The fruit of human refinement:

When a human refines his self, the caliphate and the distinction [khilāfat wa jibbat] are achieved within him, and his spirit becomes pure and his character refined. Thus take from these the temperaments, and if the service and the establishment of the temperaments and their relation to the head of compulsion and force did not exist, and from them the head were not established, the body would be destroyed and corrupted. Jabbar has established it with the head of compulsion, and from this is the body and the order of the universe and the arrangement of existence and corruption. And for this reason the order of the universe has appeared with the head of relation and severance, because all causes are divine names, and these are divine relations until no need of the creatures arises in any direction.

[Heading]
The order of the body is established with innate heat and the four temperaments, which are connected to the compulsive power:

The order of the body is with innate heat, and the remaining four temperaments, and the secret of these four temperaments is from the compulsive power. Thus when it transfers to the last of the temperaments, the secret of compulsion and supremacy rises from the four temperaments. In summary, in this manner there are his secrets in the world of the kingdom—that is, the world of testimony. The name of Jabbar, whose secret is from Jabbar, is that He created one world for the sake of its creation.
