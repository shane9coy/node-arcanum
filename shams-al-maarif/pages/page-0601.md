# Page 601 (English translation)
O Allah, I ask You to send me the Letter Nun, O Tabni in the higher realms, until I obtain from it the 'creed' of Madir. Answer me, Yatalali, with the light of the curtains and the light of the Creator. Appear, O Nun, by He than whose light no light is greater. Answer the caller, honoring Nun and the Pen and what they inscribe, and by the fire and the light and the shade and the heat, and by the heaven and the deception and the resting place of spirits. By Hawliya Guliyan Bi-Nuriyan Bi-Nuriyani 2 'Aliyyun 2 Tal'un, Fahralun Siman Shan Diyan on the Day of Judgment, by a thousand "La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-'aliyy al-'azim."

The 'Azima for it is this:

Answer O King Sapharya for the night, by the right of the chain Shalshash Shahi' Sardih Mardimih Mahyish Nu'jam, O He Yamuh 2 Pearl of Lights, answer and trust. The hastening, the warning, the Hour. God bless you and upon you.

And its name is this, and light a lime kiln to establish the places of treasure.

The Letter Nun:

This letter is hot and dry. When this letter is written for one with headache, half-headache, joint pain, and convulsions, binding it to the head brings relief. When written on green atlas fabric wrapped in silk and kept close, along with the Surah Nun, it proves effective.

For love and making the tongue speak:

Love and acceptance are attained, and enemies' tongues will be silenced.

Useful for facilitating childbirth:

If this is written on a porcelain dish and given to a pregnant woman, there will be ease in delivery.

For wounds and abscesses to heal:

If this letter is written in some vessel and washed with water, then that water is used to wash wounds or abscesses or mixed into salve, relief comes very quickly. This is the rule for private communion: recite its supplication ninety times; a light like the sun will appear and will fulfill the sign. It is necessary to write this letter and keep it close during communion. The servant's name for it is Qishyakal; it will present itself and complete whatever task you command. Its form is this:

[Diagram: A simple hand-drawn rectangular frame. Inside the frame are two vertical columns of the Arabic letter nun (ن), with four letters in each column, aligned along the left and right interior edges.]

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Peace be a statement from a Merciful Lord.
