# The Discovery of the Sage Plato

The sage Plato has discovered it, and by the blessing of this name, he is connected to the beloved and the possessor and the name of the believer. For this reason, the reality of the desire is obtained, and for the aspirant, this name is very great.

### The Method of Observing the Reality of Faith:
Whoever wishes to observe the reality of faith. It is binding upon him that after every prayer, he continues to make its remembrance according to his practice, and whoever reads it in seclusion one hundred times after every prayer, the desired rank will be attained, and carnal desires and melancholy thoughts will depart. Even an atom's worth of doubt about the forbidden will not enter the heart. The spiritual discipline for it is forty days. From this discipline, matters will be revealed that are beyond description.

### The Recitation of This Name Eliminates Doubt and Obsession:
Whoever suffers from doubt or obsessive disorder, let him write this name and drink it for twenty-one days. Allah the Most High will heal him, but let him engrave its talisman on gold or silver and always keep it with himself. Allah the Most High will give him health from the disease of obsession. The recitation of it is forty-three days, after every prayer, according to its number—that is, multiply one hundred thirty-six and recite. So that its "ayn qalba" talisman will descend and perform spiritual illumination. Under it are eight officers, and each officer is the ruler of many worlds. The form of its talisman is this.

And its remembrance (dhikr) is this:

Bis'millāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm. Ṭ. Rabbi āminnī bi-riqā'ika min riqā'ika bi-ismika tashraḥ bihā ṣadrī wa amiddanī bi-nāriqatin min faiḍika al-aqdas al-nafīs al-anfas. Fa-innaka sāmi'u al-aṣwāti wa mujību al-da'awāti. As'aluka bi-sirri sirriyāni rūḥika al-qadīm an tahdiyanī ilā ṣirāṭika al-mustaqīm. Ṭ. Wa taḥyin rūḥī bi-l-īmāni al-qawiyyi. Fa-innaka rabbi wa bi-yadika sam'ī wa baṣarī. Allāhumma malliknī nāṣiyata khādimi 'awālimi ismika al-mu'mini wa-ush'raḥ ṣadrī bi-mulāqāti 'abīdika wa qul bi-bayānī li-tumaddaniya bi-'awālimihi wa yaqḍī ḥājatī yā rabba al-'ālamīna. Ṭ.

Whoever makes this name his regular practice, the Lord Most High will grant him the awe and the sweetness of faith.
