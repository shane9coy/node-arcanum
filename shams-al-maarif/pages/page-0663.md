# Page 663 (English translation)
Whoever reads this dhikr, Allah the Exalted will raise his rank and grant him unveiling (kashf).

[Heading in a double-line box]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wahhab"

Know that Wahhab is the One who bestows gifts without self-interest, and He bestows gifts (hibah) in great abundance. He is called Wahhab. And these gifts are from the Divine side, befitting Him. Because He bestows without compensation, and it is He who gave you the eye, the nose, the ear, the tongue, and the form, and granted you extraordinary favors, and completed your creation so that you may accept the call of the caller.

[Heading]
The Trust and the Amanah: All refused to bear it, but humanity took it up:

"Truly, the Trust was offered to the heavens, the earth, and the mountains, but they refused to bear it. Yet humanity took it up, for it is unjust and ignorant." And it is an allusion and an attribute. The cause preceded in your oneness with them.

[Heading]
The best of creations in the world is humanity:

He made the love for you and your heart a seat of the heart, and your intellect a seat of the sciences, and your soul a seat of the special ones, and your heart a seat of the pure letters. And with the unveiling of lights, He granted you the lower world. And in the human world, He made your sustenance free through the movement of sensory lights, so that whatever meanings of speech reach you, you may acquire them. Then He gave you ears through which various sounds are heard. And He gave you in the human world the movement of speech, so that whatever intellectual meanings reach you, you may fully acquire them. Then He granted you the science of wisdom, which you obtain in various ways, so that He may grant you a hidden secret. And that secret is the one with which the Trustworthy Messenger came. And He gave food to the Divine Pole. He granted you the life of the Abode of Resolution and the World of Barzakh, so that you may witness the spirits there. Then He gave you the path of favor toward the Abode of Eminence, and He caused your growth along with your character. Then He granted you blessings in Paradise for the purpose of beholding Him, within which are His gaze and gradual causes whose number only Allah the Exalted knows.

[Heading]
With outward honors, He also bestows inward excellences:

Thus, these are His countless outward blessings, and opposite them are also inward blessings.

[Heading]
Moses desired to belong to the community of the Prophet:

It is related in a hadith that Moses, peace be upon him, said: "O Lord, I have seen written in the Torah that there is a nation whose angels will be within their breasts." "O Lord, who are they?" He said: "They are the community of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace." And Allah the Exalted praised them so much that Moses was amazed. And Allah the Exalted said: "O community of Muhammad, I have given you gifts before you asked, and I have forgiven you before you sought forgiveness. So look now at what blessings Allah has bestowed upon you from eternity."

Thus, whoever wishes to attain proximity through this name, it is binding upon him to be characterized by bestowing gifts and generosity without compensation, and to keep something to give from his own self. This dhikr is extremely beneficial for the liberation of the people of Allah. And the spiritual exercise for this name is forty days. In number, multiply number by number, and recite with austerity. Its complete practice is this, and its glorification is this:

In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. Glory be to al-Wahhab al-Quddus; there is no deity but He, the Actor of what He wills.
