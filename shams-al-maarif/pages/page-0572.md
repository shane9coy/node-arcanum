**Method of Making Crystal:**

One excellent recipe for crystal is: however much quicklime you have, soak it for one month. Then strain it and add four waqīyahs of common salt per ratl and let it stand. In three days it will solidify like crystal.
And it is better to soak it for two months; then strain it and add gold and dissolve to purify it. In three days it will solidify like crystal.

**Sayings of the Sages:**

The Properties of the Sages say: the best of all is the sweet-smelling stone; from it the sages make the elixir, and they point to it, and it is what they have concealed. And these are ten things: (1) Cinnabar (2) Sulfur (3) Orpiment (4) Mercury (5) Tar (6) Vitriol (7) Sal ammoniac (8) Lead (9) Antimony (10) Iron sulfide. Cinnabar is the most excellent of all transforms. The trick is: grind the sand mem or māghar with blood.

**Solidifying Mercury:**

However much mercury you wish to purify, dissolve it well with radish for three hours until it becomes black. Then boil it in vinegar and salt until it becomes yellow. Then make a hole in a brick and put it inside, and apply quicklime on top of the mercury so that the impurity becomes clean. Then cover it with a still alembic on top, and melt lead and tin and pour it on top until a finger's width rises upon it, and afterwards do the same. The mercury will solidify like stone.

**Coloring Iron and Making Silver from It:**

The talisman of Mars: formula for iron filings—however much you wish, soak in sal ammoniac water for several weeks. Then dissolve well by hand, put in a kiln, and keep on fire until two periods of the day. Then take it off while hot and put in a tub, sprinkle tar water over it and churn until it becomes like white. And after making it white in sulfur, take one ūqīyah and dissolve it in fine olive oil, filter through cloth, and mix with mercury in a strong clay vessel, seal with clay, and place in a half-baked kiln for one night. Then repeat the operation three times in the same manner. Then it will become completely like white. One dirham of this calcined substance, add eight dirhams of purified tin—it will become silver. Then add two dirhams of silver to it, seal it, and test by touch.
