# Page 421 (English translation)
419

**Acquiring Safety in Travel and Residence:**

The names As-Salām (the Peace) and Al-Mu'min (the Believer) are suitable for the person over whom another's awe and fear prevails. Especially for travelers, by the blessing of the remembrance of these names, Allah the Exalted keeps him safe from every frightening situation in travel and residence.

**Protection of Trade Goods:**

Whoever writes the letters of **As-Salām Al-Mu'min** in an 8×8 square and keeps it with wealth and trade, will be protected from theft, drowning, and fire. If this square is placed in grain, there will be immeasurable blessing and wealth will be protected from loss.

**Practice for Obtaining Honor and Rank:**

The name **Azīz** (the Mighty) is also a great and glorious name. It is very beneficial for one whom enemies have greatly humiliated. If a great person's honor and wealth have been diminished for any reason, let him remember this name—the Exalted Allah will restore his lost honor and wealth, and no person will be able to harm or damage him. Whoever maintains consistency in the remembrance of this name, his noble self will become refined and his value will be elevated; enemies will not be able to harm him.

**Victory over External and Internal Enemies:**

Know that enemies are of two kinds: one who is constantly on the lookout for enmity and, upon finding opportunity, causes the severest harm—such as predators and harmful animals; and the second enemy is one who outwardly expresses much love but harbors enmity hidden in the heart. These are very envious human beings. There is also a spiritual enemy, namely sin. Whoever maintains consistency in the remembrance of this name, Allah the Exalted protects that person from the evil of enemies. If the numbers and letters of the name Azīz are engraved on a crystal tablet in a four-by-four square and hung around the neck of a human or animal, Allah the Exalted will prolong his life and grant him blessing.

**The Destruction of the Tyrant and Victory over Enemies:**

The names of Allah the Exalted **Jabbār** (the Compeller) and **Shakūr** (the Most Appreciative) are very great names. The one who remembers them, Allah the Exalted keeps him safe from every great rebellious person, and great defiant and rebellious individuals become humbled before him. If both are written on a crystal tablet when Mars is free from malefic aspect and is conjoined with the Moon, and kept with oneself, then every tyrant and rebellious person who sees him will love him and begin to serve him. If after half the night passes, one performs one hundred supererogatory rakʿahs and remembers these names to the point that a state of spiritual elation occurs, then makes a malediction for any tyrant, that tyrant will immediately be destroyed—provided he is indeed a tyrant. However, forgiveness and overlooking is better, for which there is great reward with Allah the Exalted.

**Practice Beneficial for Every Need:**

The divine names **Al-Bāri' Al-Musawwir** (the Creator, the Fashioner) are beneficial for every task and need. If the name **Quddūs** (the Holy) is combined with the name **Khāliq** (the Creator), then [its power is] elevated—
