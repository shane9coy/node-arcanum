# Page 169 (English translation)
He knows all and executes His decrees among His subjects. It indicates the existence of the sovereign and the Wise of Absolutes dominion, just as the Noble Prophet (peace be upon him) said that Allah wrote the Book and placed it upon His Throne, and that His Mercy preceded His Wrath. Similarly, the Noble Prophet's (peace be upon him) statement regarding Sa'd ibn Mu'adh al-Ansari is that the Throne shook at his death. This is a matter of the highest rank. In summary, all these matters indicate the decrees of Allah the Most High that appear upon the Throne, so that it may be known that upon the Throne appear the traces of the Complete Power of Allah the Most High. Therefore, Shin came at the end of the letters of the Throne. This is the tawhid of the multi-faceted world, since the natural order of everything is its throne. Therefore, Shin is the throne of all letters. This is only due to this lofty rank and excellent arrangement. Among all letters, the only one whose throne is complete is the letter Alif, because it is the root of the letters, the beginning of all letters, and their apex. Therefore, the maḥṣūl (content) of Alif is appropriate for it, because Shin resembles the form of Alif. Therefore, their correspondence is shared in terms of form.

Three letters emanate from Alif—Alif itself—and similarly three letters emanate from Shin—Shin, Ya, Nun—so there is only one proportion between the two. A letter that is compound of three letters cannot become the throne of Shin without Shin, because it would not indicate goodness. Shin indicates the Kursi (Chair), and it is not far-fetched that the Kursi carries the Throne, just as the body is the throne of the sun. In reality, every subtle thing is established with the dense thing. Therefore, Alif is the most subtle among all letters. It is difficult among the letters, and no other letter can precede it at the end of a word. It indicates both pre-eternity and post-eternity. It is appropriate that Shin, which is the knower of the Kursi, is attributed to the realm of the Throne—the realm of the world—alongside the tongue, and Shin is the final letter of the Throne. Therefore, when examined with detailed vision, its final letter is Nun, and Nun is the fish that carries the world of the cosmos. Thus Nun seeks aid from Shin, and the world of the cosmos seeks aid from Nun, and similarly all worlds seek aid. Therefore, the realm of the rank seeks aid from Nun.

Allah the Most High said: "Nun. By the Pen and what they inscribe." So the Pen takes aid from that Nun which is the manifest form of that matter whose interior is Alif, which indicates the hidden secret. The secret of Shin cannot be understood on the day when its action becomes appropriate—in its first hour, the letter Shin should be written one thousand times. The day was specified for it because there is a difference between them: some are for seeking good and some for seeking evil. In the bodily world, the secrets of Shin are so numerous they cannot be counted. The first hour of the week is for good, and the first hour of Tuesday is for evil, as mentioned previously. The secrets of Shin in the world of bodies are countless, but placing it in words, no vessel can bear it, because its properties make that pain even stronger. Among its special properties is that if a pregnant woman lifts a name from it, she will have great ease in giving birth. Within the depths of Shin's secrets are also very many talismans that cannot be counted. This matter occurs in its "Fierce" Name. To the person who has been given knowledge of Shin's rank, its actions, its detailed composition—that is, the natural correspondence of Shin, Ya, Nun—its secrets of numerical correspondence, its occupations and its disposals, a very great treasure of perfections has been attained.

Shin is derived from "elevation" (ʿuluww) for which there is no elevation above it. And it is derived from "mercy" (riḥmah) for which there is no mercy above it, nor is anyone deprived of its lights. Shin is derived from "fierceness" (shiddah) for which there is no fierceness above it, nor is there any purpose besides it. So look at how you find intensity as purpose and witness, and mercy as deprived and returning. You have found elevation and loftiness and eminence etc. for the seeing of the aforementioned without condition, provided that obedience to Allah the Most High and His Messenger and the honor of Muslims is considered obligatory. The honor of Allah remains everlasting. The honor of the prophets is the existence of messengership, and the honor of the believers is the existence of faith. This is the mention of Shin's three ranks.
