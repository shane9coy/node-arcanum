There are strange and wonderful secrets in the letter correspondences for those who have a taste for the wisdom of calculation. Such people are very few out of a hundred. In truth, Allah the Most High is the one who grants mastery of all secrets.

Chapter 9: Explanation of the Name 'Azīz (Glorious) with properties for victory over enemies and honor before rulers

The square of this name is 94, but its extraction is only possible through the head of interference, because it contains a surplus letter. Whoever [writes it] during the honor of Mars, on a silver plate, and keeps it with him, will obtain victory over enemies. Whoever frequently mentions it and fears being humiliated before any ruler, will have honor in the sight of that ruler and everyone, will find honor in religion and the world, and will remain safe from fear. Its mention is appropriate for one whose name is 'Abd al-'Azīz. Whoever has understood the secrets of this name, Allah the Most High will adorn his inner being with the secrets of honor. Do you not know that this name 'Azīz points towards the name Jamīl with the calling Yā? Its number is 94. This is an even-odd, future-deficient [number]. Its parts point towards the letter Nūn, which is the circle, center, and sustenance of the outer world for everything in the inner world, because everything is humiliated before it for its manifest sustenance. It is two degrees after 'Azīz, so the first is inner authority and the second is outer. Its letter-names are 178, which point towards two great names: Mālik and 'Alīm. Its form is this:

Chapter 10: Explanation of the Name Jabbār (Compeller) with inscription and properties for protection from the evil eye and awe among Allah's creation

Whoever frequently mentions it, awe of him will spread to all, and no one will be able to look at him with an evil eye. Its square is 4x4, placed during the honor of Mars with the head of interference. Whoever writes it and keeps it with him will be awe-inspiring before people; whoever sees him will be humbled before him. He will attain his desire. Its mention is appropriate for one whose name is 'Abd al-Jabbār, and also for one whose name is Mūsā. Its literal number is 288 and its written number is 206. The first is even-odd, deficient, which comes from the multiplication of the name number, which is from the surplus numbers. Its 225 numbers point towards the name of Allah the Most High, the Maker, because precedence is necessary in it.
