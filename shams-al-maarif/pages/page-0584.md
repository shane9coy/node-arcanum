# Page 584 (English translation)
The spirituality of the letter Alif requires no incense:
Know that in the spirituality of the letter Alif, you have no need of incense, but in the remaining letters you should light incense. Write the letters and the cold [substances] and hang them in the air, and for the purpose of love, throw them into fire. The seeker may manipulate [them] as he wishes, but after [doing so] he should say: ajib ya alif wa if'al li kaza wa kaza

The Letter Ba
This letter is cold and dry, and it is among the letters of Ba'iyyah, and this letter is the secret of the heart's interior and the existence of things. Its manipulation extends until the Day of Resurrection. Through its help, people obtain knowledge of the realities of existence and the demonstration of divine oneness. In the letter Ba, there is an indication toward both the higher and lower worlds, and God Most High has granted this letter the honor that Basmalah begins with it, and Adam's scroll also began with it.

The beginning of the Quran's descent was with Alif and Ba:
Know that when God Most High sent down the noble Quran upon the Prophet Muhammad, may peace be upon him, Gabriel, peace be upon him, came to the Messenger, may peace be upon him, and said: iqra' bi-ism muhammad bi-ism rabbik. Therefore the letter Ba was designated for the essence, and the attributes of the essence are established as its manifestation along with the prominent head, and you have already recognized its counterpart, and the commentators of attributes are established with the head of abrogation. When God Most High created the letter Ba, He created with it 24 Mucals (appointed angels), and under each Mucal He appointed so many angels that only God knows their number, and all these angels glorify God. For this reason, the letter Ba is the key to the treasures of books, and the secret of every line is within it, and it is among the forms of Alif.

Know that when you write the letter Ba with its complete and hand [form], and also write those names that begin with the letter Ba, then if an impoverished person keeps it with him, God Most High will expand his sustenance, and if written in the same manner and given to a patient suffering from constipation to drink, he will find relief. If you write the letter Ba 16 times and Basmalah 16 times and also write this verse, it is sufficient for every purpose: badi'u al-samawat wa al-ard. And if kept with oneself, all difficulties will become easy. When there is intention of love and acceptance, then sit on a moonlit night facing the moon, and write the letter Ba 16 times and Alif Lam Mim 16 times, and you should say: ajib ya khuddam harf al-ba' bi-haqqi Bismi Allah al-Rahman al-Rahim. Then greet the moon and pass your hand over your face, and lick what you have written with your tongue. Continue doing so until the fourteenth night; difficulties will become easy and every need will be fulfilled.

For becoming popular among the masses:
And if you write the letter Ba and the lunar names on your palm, and face the moon while reading the دعوت (incantation) and اخلاص (sincerity), and say:aji'bu ya ruwaniyyatu al-huruf wa al-qzu hajati wa amzuju ruwaniyyati bayna al-khawater, the purpose will be achieved. And if you write the letter Ba and incense and henna and this verse badi'u al-samawat wa al-ard and those names that begin with the letter Ba in a glass vessel, then pour jasmine oil into that vessel and apply the oil to your face, you will become accepted. On Friday, whoever writes the letter Ba with Basmalah and those names that begin with the letter Ba and ties it to his arm, God Most High will open his chest for him and remove lethargy and laziness from him. And if any person desires to obtain love, then making the name the foundation and joining it with those names that begin with the letter Ba with the pronoun, and breathing upon it after reading this verse, the purpose will be achieved. And if this letter is written 16 times on three papers and fed to a grain of wheat, by God's grace love will be obtained. Every work will be blessed with goodness and prosperity. And when you have a need at any time, make a silver ring or set a gemstone of power in it, and write the letter Ba with the name al-Muslah, and wear it.
