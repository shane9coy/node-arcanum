[Urdu interlinear translation:]
"O Allah, I ask You, O Pure One and Lord of the devoted, O He who answers the prayers of the distressed. I ask You by the right of Allah Allah Allah, the Living, the Sustainer, that You deliver So-and-so son of So-and-so from distress and occupy him with So-and-so son of So-and-so, that he be seized by every pain and well-being, just as the lengthening of his night is by the love of So-and-so son of So-and-so, until everything in the heavens and the earth is His alone. Thus let the heavens and earth become constricted for So-and-so son of So-and-so, that he see nothing except by his thought; his remembrance remains upon his tongue with intensity of love. Who is there that intercedes before him except by his knowledge? Thus this verse intercedes for So-and-so son of So-and-so. This is not like the intercession of creation; the Speech of Truth knows what is before them and behind them. Similarly, So-and-so son of So-and-so himself knows that So-and-so son of So-and-so is a follower and obedient before him, a follower of his command, an acceptor of his call, a fulfiller of his need. Love is gathered in his heart, and they cannot encompass anything of his knowledge except by what He wills. Thus So-and-so son of So-and-so encompasses with love, loyalty, and purity. His Kursi has encompassed the heavens and the earth. Similarly, I have made your heart expansive and inclined for So-and-so son of So-and-so, until there remains no strength of patience from him, and he fulfills all the interests that you seek, without enmity. And the guarding of both does not incapacitate Him, and He is the Most High and Magnificent. O Allah, I ask You, O So-and-so son of So-and-so's heart, place the love of So-and-so therein, until he obeys him and does not oppose any matter. By the right of this noble verse, let the servants of So-and-so son of So-and-so take up this matter upon themselves. Soften his heart and soften his limbs with the love of So-and-so son of So-and-so, by the right of these generous verses."

A saint narrates that when it becomes dark, he rises and performs prayer and recites this supplication:

"My God, You are the Provider, and hope is cut off except from You, and hopes have failed except in You, and roads are blocked except to You. O Trust of whoever has no trust other than You. O Allah, I ask You by Your Great Name, the Greatest: Allah, there is no deity except He, the Living, the Sustainer." [The verse]
