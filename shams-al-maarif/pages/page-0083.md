An old woman was sleeping near him, and she had brought herself by uttering the Word "Kul la ilaha illallah." He called her and made her join the meal. He recited these words:

**بِسْمِ اللهِ ثِقَةً بِاللّٰهِ وَ تَوَكُّلاً عَلَيْهِ اذْهَبْ مَرَّهَا وَ صَفْهَا** (In the name of Allah, trusting in Allah and relying upon Him, go, rubbing it and cleansing it) And he ate with her. By the blessing of this noble Name, a person suffering from eye ailment is healed. When he places his hand upon his eye and reads it, and when someone intends to travel and places his foot in the stirrup, he should also recite it—then no evil and no affliction shall reach him in the journey. And when a believing servant says "Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim," Satan becomes small like a written line. O Muhammad ﷺ, you would recite for every person who was departing on a journey: You ﷺ would say, "When you depart on a journey, recite **بِسْمِ اللهِ وَ عَلٰی مِلَّةِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ** (In the name of Allah and following the religion of the Messenger of Allah)." You ﷺ said, "Begin the journey by reciting Bismillah, and also say:

**اَعُوْذُ بِاللهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِیْمِ وَ مِنْ وَعْثَاءِ السَّفَرِ**
(I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan and from the hardships of travel)

The Noble Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him and his family) said to Hudhafa bin Abdallah, may Allah be pleased with him, while his hand was in great pain: "If you had recited Bismillah, the angels would have lifted you up and the people would have seen you." Behold, what blessing is in this Name whose reciter is lifted up by angels. Satans flee from it. The effect of poison is cut off by the remembrance of Bismillah—and all of this our Noble Prophet ﷺ has told us, so that you wonder at the grace of the Lord of Majesty and His secrets, for without His command neither can anyone move nor can anyone remain still. There should be no doubt in this. This is among the secrets of Bismillah. Jesus, peace be upon him, would dispel all pains and sorrows with Bismillah.

**Chest Expansion:**

Whoever fasts on Thursday and on Friday, the first day of the moon, binds it upon his right arm, Allah the Most High will expand his chest for him, and laziness and negligence will depart from him. There will be blessing in every endeavor. The veils of unveiling will be lifted before him, and his appearance will be between the higher and lower realms, and a beautiful fragrant form standing in perfect shape will appear before him and speak with him politely. His light will not be extinguished. Whoever mentions the letter Ba with sincerity, light will manifest upon him, for it is among the Three Names, and the letter Ba is prefixed to those Names, each of which is cool, merciful, and sufficient for difficult tasks, such as Bari, Baqi, and Ba'ith. All these are properties of Bismillah. The reason is that Alif stands upon the head of splendor, and this is the rank of Huwa (He), and Ba is also present in Allah's Names Basir (All-Seeing), Sami' (All-Hearing), and Batin (All-Knowing). Each Name has specific meanings.

**The Virtues of Divine Names Beginning with the Letter "Ba"**

[/Decorative border]

These are Bari, Baqi, Ba'ith, Basir, Badi', and Batin. The Name Bari is for the purpose of reform. It assists in good deeds and in honoring one's parents. Whoever recites it 223 times for any purpose, combining it with the name of the person from whom he desires the matter, that matter will be accomplished—example: just as his name is, so too is the help. Then, in the same manner, write the letters of the first Name al-Bari and form a line; then write one letter of this Name and one opposite it, thus: M A L I F B A R I D D. Then recite it repeatedly until the first line reaches the end; then reverse the end to the beginning and drop the first letter—this is the station and destination. For whatever purpose you make the last letter the final one, drop the last letter. Four lines will remain; the letters marked for annihilation remain—write them with "Bism" and keep them in your pocket. Then recite those words upon it and blow:

**يَا رَبِّ م ر ب ال و ر ع ا ل ا ر ب ال ش ر ي ف ال ک ل ي ب ل ط ي ف ز ب و ي ن ا ال س ب ع ل ن س ر ع ال ط ف ک ع م**
