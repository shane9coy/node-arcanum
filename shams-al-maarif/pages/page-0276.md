Chapter 22: The Name of Bāsiṭ,1 for obtaining happiness, sustenance, and other properties

Whoever is fearful and mentions it shall obtain security, and whoever is sorrowful and mentions it shall obtain joy. If one inscribes it upon a ring during the first hour of Friday and wears it, their speech becomes pleasant, and whoever sees them shall love them. If a wealthy person mentions it, their sustenance shall be expanded, and their heart shall live with felicity. This name is the mention of Isrāʾīl,2 upon him be peace. From it "Sarāḥiyyān"3 appears outwardly, just as "Sirāniyyah"4 appears from the hidden. For one whose work is blocked, the mention of it is most suitable. Whoever mentions it abundantly, ease shall be obtained for their spirit and sustenance shall be expanded. Whoever persists in its mention, their realms shall obey them. Do you not see that this points toward the name of Allāh, the Most High, which is "the Near"?5

Its number is 72. The two thus points toward the two Sabahāt,6 and 70 points toward the ʿAyn al-Shayʾ.7 Its power is appropriate for this specification, which the letter sīn demands. For this reason, the alif is from it and it seizes it,8 calling out to it.9 Its letter-numbers are 240, pointing toward the name of Allāh, the Most High, "the Manifest."10 This is constructed in the triangular number,11 whose word yields profit,12 and this is its form:

Chapter 23: The Secrets and Properties of the Name of Khāfiḍ,13 for the destruction of enemies and the like

This name is very useful for cursing the tyrant. Multiply its numbers into the name of the tyrant and recite it at the appropriate midnight; then he shall be destroyed swiftly. Its number is 1481. These are the abjad numerals. Its letter-numbers are 500, pointing toward the two names of Allāh, the Most High: al-Mughīth14 and al-Mājid.15 Its inscription is this:
