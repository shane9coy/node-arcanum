# Page 349 (English translation)
One who loves for the sake of God—if you were to spend all that is on the earth, He is the All-Wise—and I have cast upon you love from Myself, and that you might be fashioned under My eye.

O God, I ask You, O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, O Eternal, O Loving: that You place love and affection in the heart of so-and-so, and seize his heart with the form of love, until he becomes my obedient one and does not oppose me in anything. Regarding the secrets of "Al-Wadud" and this verse, O servants of "Ahya Ahiya," take my stamp upon yourselves, O luminous one. Then attract the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so. Set in motion between him and me the spirituality of love and affection, "They love them as God loves" and other verses—blessed.

Furthermore, write on paper with musk and saffron and rose water these three verses which are mentioned above, along with "In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful," and also write this:
