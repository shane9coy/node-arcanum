# Page 683 (English translation)
**Its figure and recitation bring expansion in sustenance:**

There is also a special property in this name for fulfilling needs and bringing expansion in sustenance. If a person is grieving or in need, let him recite this name and his need will be fulfilled. And if someone inscribes its figure on gold or silver at an auspicious time and keeps it with him, God Almighty will bring expansion upon him. And if he calls the assigned spirit, it will come immediately. Its form is this:

**And its verbal recitation is this:**

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّى اَسْأَلُكَ يَا لَطِيْفُ بِیَاهٍ 3 يَا حَنَّانُ يَا مَنَّانُ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا ذَاالْجَلَالِ وَالْاِكْرَامِ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا رَبَّاهُ 2 سُبْحٰنَكَ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ وَلَا اِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ وَلَا مَعْبُوْدَ سِوَاكَ يَا لَطِيْفُ اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ الْحَقُّ الْحَقِيْقُ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا مَنْ لَمْ يَتَّخِذْ صَاحِبَةً وَلَا وَلَدًا وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَّهُ كُفُوًا اَحَدٌ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا بَدِيْعَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضِ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا مُجِيْبُ يَا هٍ 13 اَجِبْ تَبَارَكَ اللّٰهِ فِىْكَ وَافْعَلْ كَذَا وَكَذَا مِمَّا اُرِيدُ وَاَجِهِزْ لِى مَطْلُوبِى يَا شَمْخٍ اَشْمَاخٍ الْعَالِىْ عَلٰى كُلِّ بَرَّاخٍ يَا لَطِيْفُ اللَّطَفِ 2 اَنْتَ الْحَاجِزُ لَمْ تَغِبْ يَا رَبَّاهُ 2 اَنْتَ الْحَاكِمُ لَا يُحْكَمُ عَلَيْكَ حَاكِمٌ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا 2 اَنْتَ السُّلْطَانُ الْقَوِىُّ لَمْ يَقْهْ غَلَبَتَكَ قَوِىٌّ يَا لَطِيْفُ يَا مَنْ هُوَ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ فِىْ شَأْنٍ سَوِّخِرْ لِى خَادِمَ هٰذَا الْاِسْمِ يَفْعَلْ لِى كَذَا وَكَذَا بِاَلْفِ لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ اِلَّا بِاللهِ الْعَلِىِّ الْعَظِيمِ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوسُ كَهْتَهَصٍ حَمَقْتَصٍ اَسْأَلُكَ بِاسْمِكَ الْعَظِيمِ الْاَعْظَمِ الَّذِىْ اِخْتَصَّ بِهِ الْاِحْصَاءُ مِنْ خَلْقِكَ اَنْ تَقْضِىَ حَاجَتِى يَا رَبَّ الْعٰلَمِينَ ۝

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, I ask You, O Gentle, by Your name Biya[h] (3). O Benevolent, O Bountiful, O Gentle, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor, O Gentle, O Lord (2). Glory be to You; there is no god except You, and there is no god besides You, and there is no worshipped besides You, O Gentle. O God, You are the True, the Most True, O Gentle, O One who did not take a spouse nor offspring, and there is none equal to Him, O Gentle, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Gentle, O Responsive, O He[h] (13). Answer! Blessed is God in you, and do such and such for me from what I desire, and prepare for me my request, O Lofty One, Most Lofty, the Most High above every one who rises, O Gentle of gentleness (2). You are the Preventer; do not be absent, O Lord (2). You are the Judge; no judge passes judgment upon You, O Gentle (2). You are the Mighty Sovereign; no mighty one has overcome Your might, O Gentle, O One who every day is in a matter. Dispatch for me a servant of this name who will do for me such and such. By the thousand: "There is no power nor strength except through God, the Most High, the Most Great. There is no god except You, the Sovereign, the Holy" (gibberish). I ask You by Your great, most great name, with which You have distinguished the enumeration among Your creation, that You fulfill my need, O Lord of the worlds.

**And this name has the property of transmuting paper into gold or silver and making water into ghee. Its assigned spirit is Rayyān. Recite the name 7,000 times according to the conditions of spiritual discipline, and recite the invocation 21 times. On Friday night, after the evening prayer, perform two prostrations, reciting Surah Al-Kahf in the first and Surah Ya-Sin in the second, then after that recite the name. The assigned spirit will come and give you a black stone that will be your purpose. It will not give you its furnace. Light the incense of 'ūd and frankincense. When you wish to dismiss it, say the supplication: "Withdraw!" and it will depart.

**Calling the assigned spirit by giving incense to the stone will cause the spirit to appear:**

When you wish to call the assigned spirit, place this black stone in fire and light the incense; the assigned spirit will appear. And its verbal recitation is this:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ اللَّطِيفُ الْخَفِىُّ عَنْ نَظَرِ الْعُيُوْنِ الْمُنَزِّهُ عَنْ اِدْرَاكِ الْعُقُوْلِ وَالْاَفْكَارِ الْعَالِمُ بِالْإِحَاطَةِ
