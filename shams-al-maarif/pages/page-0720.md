# Page 720 (English translation)
The saying of al-Siddiq al-Akbar — he did not grant anyone comprehension of his knowledge: 'All are helpless [before it].'

For this reason, al-Siddiq al-Akbar, may Allah be pleased with him, said: Allah the Most High has established only this one path for His creation to attain knowledge of Himself — that they become incapable of comprehending His knowledge.

**Sayings of the Sufis:**

Some of the great Sufis say that no one knows God except God Himself. The reciter of this name should always engage his fears within the principles and rules. He should reflect both outwardly and inwardly, in this world and the next, and contemplate this verse:
أَفَلَا يَتَفَكَّرُونَ...*

If you have made your tiny extent so vast that you have reached the lowest depths (asfal al-sāfilīn), even then Allah the Most High will gather guidance and lights for you. As He has said describing the people of faith:
ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ الْأَوَّلِينَ وَثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ الْآخِرِينَ*

There is no particular dhikr prescribed for these two names; rather, they are names of action and gradual practice. The aspirant should begin by reciting both names, so that he begins to see his self within tawḥīd. Thus, if you see yourself within tawḥīd, understand that you are a monotheist in your self, not in the reality of tawḥīd itself.

And so long as you adopt the spiritual path, it is necessary that you purify your actions for Allah's sake alone and work without selfish motive, because working with selfish motive is part of the self's portion, not the portion of dhikr of Allah. You must maintain sincerity in every state.

**If there is objection, doubt, or suspicion in your self, you cannot achieve anything:**

And when there is objection within your self, you cannot influence any scholar. Until you cast it out from your outward and inward, keep the recitation of Surah al-Ikhlāṣ and these names:
هُوَ الْأَوَّلُ وَالْآخِرُ وَالظَّاهِرُ وَالْبَاطِنُ...*

Abandon all idle speech and perform ghusl every Friday and on every day. Recite these names because these are the principles, and from them the spiritual Wayfarer obtains spiritual openings. Recite them in every prayer's retreat in accordance with their wirds. At that time, unveiling will occur, and you will see how the True and Exalted One manifests in His actions — though He in His essence is One and not multiple.

**Upon the completion of tawḥīd, light will manifest**

Thus, when you have this vision, a light will manifest upon you, and you will know the reality from your inward.

**Allah is closer than the jugular vein**

The True and Exalted One will be closer to you than your jugular vein. When you have come to know this, it is now binding upon you to continue upon it constantly, until this unveiling is granted to you. And when you do so, the system of the Name will [unclear], and the system of the Last Name and the reality will be present [damaged source/watermark overlay].

In summary, this light will appear upon the tablet of the forehead and the unveiling of the unseen will be granted. The numerical square of this name is useful for repelling enemies and obtaining the lights of the world of the supernal. Write it upon a silver tablet and hang it around the neck of a child who does not cry; by its blessing, the child will begin to speak. And if one writes it on a copper plate and keeps it with oneself, the knowledge of the unseen will be granted, and [one will know] of absent people [damaged source/watermark overlay]
