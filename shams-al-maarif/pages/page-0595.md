# Page 595 (English translation)
595

**The Harm Will Be Removed**
When you write this and give it to the afflicted one to rinse with, the harm will be removed from him. Its afshār (incantation) is this:
Ajib ayyuhā al-malek hūṭiyāl bi-ḥaqq shemḥaṭ satʿaṭūṭ shalakh ajib wa tawakkal bi-dhā wa dhā al-waḥā al-waḥā walā ḥawla walā quwwata illā billāh al-ʿaliy al-ʿaẓīm ṭā

**The Letter Yāʾ (ی)**
This letter is among the solar letters and lunar letters, and among the equivalents of this letter is "yā." Its assistance will be from the Greater World. This letter is from the reality of felicity, since after it are ten, and ten with "sī" added to it belongs to the Master of the Beginnings of the Path.

**Deliverance from Lust and Repentance from Drunkenness**
The fire of passion will be removed by it. And when you write this one hundred times and wash it and give it to drink to a person who is deeply immersed in sin and transgression and who drinks wine, God will grant him the good fortune of repentance. And if you write this letter one hundred times on a clay tablet and dig a well with it, sweet water will emerge and there will be blessing in it. Know that the letter "yā" is among the Divine Names, and the name in which the letter yā is found will not be accepted quickly if the form of the letter yā is not [present]. Its talismanic form is this:

[Note: A 6×6 grid magic square. Each of the 36 cells contains the duplicated Arabic character "ی ی" (yā-yā).]

Whoever practices its khalwa (spiritual retreat) obtains power over spiritual spirits for whom fate is decreed. He should sit in retreat for as many days until the one who brings the white pigeon—his deputy—appears. When that one appears, whatever you desire he will fulfill. Its name is "every qāʾil," and its invocation is this:

Bi-smi llāhi al-raḥmāni al-raḥīm. Yā muḥyī yā mumīt yā ḥayy yā qayyūm lā ilāha illā anta aḥyī qalbī bi-dhikrika wa ilayka ashkū ḍuʿfa quwwatī wa qillata ḥīlati. Ḥanīn allāhumma habhu min ʿindika tuʿīnī ʿalā maṣāliḥ aradtuhā li-ṭāʿatika yā ḥawīd yā allāh yā munʿimu bil-naʿīmi qablahu istiḥqāqahā yā muḥsin yā mujammil yā mutafaḍḍal yā arḥama al-rāḥimīn sakhkhhar lī ḥarfa al-yāʾi ḥattā yaqḍī ḥājatī min maʿāshī yā mawlāya fīka al-mustaʿān wa ʿalayka al-taklān fa-innī uqīmu ʿalayka bika tafʿalu lī mā urīd wa aḥsin fī laylī wa nahārī wa ghudwī wa āṣālī. Saṭāy wa samaṭāy wa adʿāy wa sharshāyā ahyā sharshāyā adūnāy aṣbāwat al-shidāy kalīm. Subḥāna man bi-dhikrihi taṭma'innu al-qulūb. Salīṭū bā yā labakhāy sabaṭāy wa qad qulnā mā qulnā wa aqsamnā mā aqsamnā ʿalayka bi-nafsika wa kayfa yakūnu aw yaʿazzu man qaḍā allāhu ajib wa tawakkal bi-dhā wa dhā bi-ḥaqq [damaged source]
