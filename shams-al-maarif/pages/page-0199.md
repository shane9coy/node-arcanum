# Page 199 (English translation)
Your mercy is of good and sustenance and blessing and grace, by Your grace and generosity and beneficence, and that You make me independent of all besides You by Your grace, O God (3 times) O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, Creator of the heavens and the earth, O Sovereign of dominion, O Possessor of majesty and honor. I ask You, O God, by the light of Your noble face which has filled the pillars of Your great Throne.

And by Your power with which You were able to create Your creatures, and by Your mercy which encompasses all things. There is no god but You. Glory be to You; I was indeed among the wrongdoers. And You are the Most Merciful of those who show mercy. I ask You and call upon You to sustain upon me the blessing and good and abundant sustenance, and to give me from Your vast treasuries what will make me independent of all besides You, O He who when He wills a thing says to it "Be" and it is. Indeed, You are over all things capable.

O God (3 times) O Most Merciful (3 times) There is no god but You, the Giver, the Treasurer of the treasures of blessing, the Beneficent, the Most Bountiful, the Noble, the Bestower. Grant me, O God, much wealth and soaring good fortune and sustenance by Your vast grace, O Most Bountiful, O Most Bountiful. Pour upon me blessing and good and make me independent of all besides You by Your grace, and make me truly wealthy such that no poverty comes after it ever. Indeed, You are God beside which there is no god, the Giver, the Bestower, the Noble, the Provider, the Responsive, the Most Bountiful. O God, You are the Sustainer of all things, the Ancient, the Guardian, the Most High, the Great.

Grasp me with Your great greatness, O Great One, O Greater than every great thing. I ask You, O God, by the right of Your great and greatest name, the most glorified, by which when invoked You answer and by which when asked You give, and by the right of all Your most beautiful names, both those I know and those I do not know, and by the right of the Torah and what is in it, and by the right of the Gospel and what is in it, and by the right of the Psalms and what is in it, and by the right of the mighty Quran and what is in it, and by the right of the name by which You established the seven heavens and what is in them, and by the right of all Your allies and chosen ones, and by the right of Your angels brought near, and by the right of Your prophet Muhammad, may God bless him and his family and them all, I ask You and call upon You to grant me abundant good dominion and soaring sustenance and ample blessing by Your grace, O Most Bountiful, and by Your generosity, O Most Generous, and by Your beneficence, O Beneficent, and by Your nobility, O Honoring, and by Your giving, O Giver, by the abundant blessings, O God—5 times. I ask You, O Sustainer of all the worlds, by Your manifestation, O Sustainer of the heavens and the earth. Every vessel is obedient to Your subsistency, submissive with humility, hopeful with anticipation. I ask You, O God, You are the One who contracts and expands, and You are the truest of speakers, when You said in Your mighty Book: "Call upon Me; I will answer you." I have believed in You, O God, and I call upon You to grant me abundant wealth and blessing and generous sustenance, O God—5 times. O Knowing, O Great of the worlds.

O God, by the right of kaf-hya-ayn-sad, ha-mim-ayn-sin-qaf, that You grant me sustenance that is lawful, vast, blessed, from where I do not know nor perceive. Indeed, God is over all things capable. O God, O Most Merciful, here I am seeking response, relying upon the manifest of sincerity from Your subsistency, O Powerful—grant me victory over whoever transgresses and wrongs, with triumph for me and victory until they are overcome.
