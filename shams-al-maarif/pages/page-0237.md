# Page 237 (English translation)
Praise him, because he is a great dignitary and one of noble standing. He will inquire from you what you desire. Through Allah's righteous servant, request from him your needs—whatever you wish, he will fulfill it. Ask him for a servant who will be obedient to you, who will manage whatever you desire. Request an omen from him when you require something for any need. Recite the Surah and utter his name, and he will appear. Whatever you desire, exert your control over it. Quickly fumigate with frankincense and bark of Jawi, and maintain taqwa of Allah the Most High. Keep your intention sincere, and you will achieve much. The supplication is this:

O Allah, I indeed ask You by the Qaf of power and encompassing, and by the Lam of the Preserved Tablet and gentleness, and by the Ha of noble aspiration and guidance, and by the Waw of divine authority, that You make for me power and encompassing over the subtleties of the tablet-created entities, rejoicing in the splendor of noble aspiration, guided and guiding whomever You wish to guide. You are the Guide; whoever You guide is guided. O You whose light has illuminated all the directions, transformations, formations, events, changes, divisions, counting, separation, and the singular. Say: "He is Allah, One," unique in the permanence of His ancient sovereignty, without transformation or corporealization. O Allah, I indeed ask You by the Waw of uniqueness and by the Isolated Alif, which is the origin of the originary creation, and by the Ha of eternal life and by the Dal of eternal permanence, without presence of time or number, without consort or offspring. You are Allah, the Unique, the One, the Eternal. O Allah, make me one of the unique ones and a singular among the singulars, and endow me with a creation from the spiritual creation of the Isolated Alif, until after that I plunge into the seas of the intimates among the singulars, and revive my soul with a wisdo[m]ly breath from Your breaths and extended spirituality by the knowledge of eternity, until I become one returning with felicity and guidance and its directions among Your servants on the Day of Resurrection.

O Allah, I indeed ask You by the Sad of truth and patience, and by the Mim of sovereignty and glory, and by the Ha of wakefulness and certainty, that You make me truthful and sincere, a possessor of glory and majesty, assisted by wakefulness, firm in certainty, extended by the greatness of Your generosity, and by a truthful one from Your angels I seek assistance for the rectification of my worldly and otherworldly affairs. And make for me a helper without dependency, through a miracle lasting forever, who did not beget nor was begotten, nor has any equal. O Allah, bind me with the dogs of Your sufficiency until I do not turn to any of Your creatures, and illuminate me with the light of that luminosity until I achieve the splendor of success and salvation among Your intimate servants. Indeed, You are over all things powerful, and worthy of answering, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

O Allah, I ask You with the Qaf of power and encompassing... that You make for me power and encompassing over the subtleties of the Tablet of Creation, which is delightful with the splendor of noble aspiration, for whom You wish guidance, for that one there is guidance, and You are the Guide for whomever You wish to give guidance, O You whose veil encompasses all directions of transformations, confirmations, events, changes, number and division, and number. He is Allah, One, in the permanence of His ancient sovereignty without change or corporealization. O Allah, I ask You with the Waw of uniqueness and the Isolated Alif, which is the origin of the primordial creation, and with the Ha of divine eternal life, and with the Dal of eternal permanence, whose limits are without time or number, who has no wife or offspring. You are Allah, the One and the Eternal.
