# Page 356 (English translation)
254

What is before them and what is after them. Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us. Indeed, You are the Knowing, the Wise. He knows what is upon the land and in the sea; not a leaf falls but He knows it, nor any grain in the darkness of the earth, nor anything wet or dry, but it is in a clear book. And they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except what He wills. He has encompassed all things in knowledge. And Allah is behind them, encompassing them. Rather, it is a noble Quran in a Preserved Tablet. And His power has encompassed the kingdom of the heavens, and all shall return to Him. Our Lord, You have encompassed all things with mercy and knowledge. The spirits have departed and the faces have vanished, and they wander in the depths of their similitudes, scattering in the products of His creation, taking form in the molds of spiritualities for the witnessing of the variation of forms in the molds of composition in the circuit of the mornings, with the emergence of judgment upon indication and the emergence of knowledge—its apparent is apparent like power, and its hidden is the hidden of the command, and it is the secret of confirmation for the acceptance of the flows of judgment and the disposition thereof. His chair has encompassed the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great. Expand for us from Your abundance in knowledge and understanding whereby we dispose among created things. There is no power and no strength except through You. I have raised my destitution and humility to You before You; do not disappoint my hope in You, for You are the Vast, the Great Lord. I ask You by the diversity of the lives of spiritual spirits and by the kinds of secrets of the Great Sovereign, the Most Great, by whose manifestation the parched livers of the people of evident love have been nourished; they have wandered in the valleys of the purity of their inner selves and the lights of their essences, and they called: O He whose chair has encompassed the heavens and the earth, and whose preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great! O Generous, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Forbearing! O He who is Allah, there is no deity except He, the Living, the Eternal. No drowsiness overtakes Him nor sleep. To Him belongs what is in the heavens and what is on earth. Who is it that intercedes with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is after them, and they encompass not a thing of His knowledge except what He wills. His chair has encompassed the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great.

I ask You, O Allah, by the right of these great verses and these noble names, that You illuminate our hearts, expand our sustenance, and refine our characters. O One who befriends hearts and conceals faults! O One who removes afflictions! O One who pardons sins! O One who knows the unseen! You know what was of my supplication and my apology in my privacy, and my actions from my lapse, and my exoneration from my sin. And You, O Allah, know my resolve and the One aware of my intention, and the Knower of my inner thought. To You belongs the sovereignty, my Lord—I take hold of my forelock, and You are my goal. O He to whom my request and my hope go in my distress, my companion in my solitude, my reprover and my lesson, my deliverer from my stumbling and my trial. I called upon You, my God, and indeed I have fallen short of what You commanded me, and I committed what You forbade me. By Your prestige You protected me, and by Your secret You preserved me. O Most noble of the noble, O Helper of those who seek help! O Master of the Day of Judgment! You know what I conceal in conscience, and You are the Director of affairs both small and great, and I am Your poor servant, submissive in humility—here I am.
