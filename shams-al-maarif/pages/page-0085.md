# Page 85 (English translation)
The Secrets of Creation, the Subjugation of Spirits, and the Revelation of Secrets

Whoever mentions the Ism-i Sami' (the Great Name of God, the All-Hearing) for a number of days equal to its numerical count, and asks Allah the Almighty for anything, the Exalted will grant it to him. Likewise, whoever has a need should write it upon his palm, then multiply the Names by the days and mention them according to their numerical values, and Allah the Almighty will accept his supplication. After multiplying by the days, the result is four thousand two hundred and seventy. By the blessing of this Name, one can meet spirits and also ask them questions and receive answers. There are many hidden secrets and great works in this Name. If you make an effort and practice it, you will succeed. The special quality of the Ism-i Sami' (the Name of the All-Hearing) of Allah the Almighty is that if one adds to it the Ism-i Basir (the Name of the All-Seeing), or writes the light of Sami' or Basir at a propitious time and keeps it with oneself, but places one's hand upon an unconscious person, he will regain consciousness immediately. It is the property of the Masters of Secrets.

One of the Masters of Secrets saw Ibrahim ibn Harun unconscious. At that very moment, he wrote the page with the Name and placed it upon him, and read this Name seven times, blowing upon him, and he regained consciousness. Whoever writes this Name before sleeping and keeps it with himself will hear the speech of jinn. He will command the spirits as he wishes. If anyone persists in the remembrance of this Name, the secrets of creation will be revealed to him. The secrets of hearts will become known, and he will witness the divine lights, and the divine luminaries will become manifest upon him.

The Secrets of the Name Salam

The Name Salam of Allah the Almighty is for peace and safety. On the Day of Judgment, only the mention of the Holy Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) will be made. When his Ummah passes over the Bridge (Sirat), he will say: "O Salam! Grant safety! O Salam! Preserve safety!"

The Secrets of the Letter Mim and the Blessed Name of the Master of the Worlds

The letter Mim is one of the lights of the letters, and when the first and last of a letter are the same, such as "Mim" and "Nun," it points to its inner stability and tranquility. This is due to the power that is within it. This letter is from the letters of the Preserved Tablet. When Allah the Almighty created this letter, He created a radiant light. It is also from the letters of resolution. Because of its encompassing nature, the sun runs in the fourth heaven by means of it. By its blessing and secret, Allah the Almighty established the kingdom of the visible and invisible realms. The entire universe also depends upon this Mim. By the blessing of the secret of this Mim, help is given in actions. This is the final station of Basmalah. In this Mim is the secret of maturity, as Allah the Almighty has said: "When he reaches his maturity and reaches the age of forty years." The numerical value of Mim is also forty. Allah the Almighty has appointed ninety angels to guard it from the spirits of the angels. This is the secret that Allah the Almighty placed at the beginning of His Prophet Muhammad's blessed name, Muhammad (peace be upon him). This is the secret of the invisible realm and the secret of the kingdom within its encompassing. Thus, both the kingdom and the invisible realm are gathered within it. Whoever writes the form of Mim forty times every day and recites "O Allah, Lord of Sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will without measure," Allah the Almighty will make the means of goodness and blessing easy for him, and wherever he goes, sustenance will come to him.
