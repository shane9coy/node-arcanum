O Allah, You are the Eternal, Allah, O Judge of the Day of Judgment, I ask You by the right of Your names which are the most ancient and most known of names, I ask You, O Knowing, O Manifest, have mercy upon me and be gentle with me in calamities and their descent, and bring me near with a nearness that attains the desired, O Compassionate, O Merciful. By the staff, picture me, O Keeper of the Signet, until I behold You with clear sight, and grant my need in what benefits my affair. O Allāh, by the writing of the pen in the Preserved Tablet, bring forth and show me, by the right of ḍāṣī ḍawḍī ḍayḍar, by what is pleasing to the Possessor who is One and Eternal. Indeed, your Lord is surely watching from the ambush, O Allah, O One, O Eternal, answer by the right of Solomon son of David, by a thousand "there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the High, the Mighty."

The operation of this amulet is as follows:

Whoever is honored by any affairs, from it answer, O Angel, by the right of salṭaʿ 2 ʿāṭiḥ bāh ʿālamalḥīmī, bāh ʿālamalḥīmī, sūḥa ṣamāṣa, yaradnaḥi, ṣīdiyāhi, bimūsarahuya, sharāhiya, adūnāy, aṣbaʾūf alī, shaday, answer and trust in such-and-such.

The Letter ʿAyn (ع):

This letter is cold in nature, and from this letter light gleams. When the moon is in its house, write this letter upon paper along with the names of ʿAynah and keep it with yourself; all creatures shall be obedient to it.

Kund dhhunī dūr kartā hai:

If one writes it and keeps it with oneself, Allah the Most High and Glorious will grant him victory over it.

The sick shall have healing:

This letter is also written for shortness of breath along with this verse: "ʿĀlim al-ghayb wa al-shahādah." Place it in a vessel with some honey, wash it, and administer it; healing shall occur. And if on Friday it is written on white silk along with the names, and this letter is placed beneath a ring and worn, there shall be love and acceptance among all people. And when the incense and the breaking of the fast are according to the instructions, write on bluish silk cloth and apply Bengal quince, burn incense, and after the breaking of the fast read over it and blow upon it.

The place shall become desolate:

Wherever you wish to make desolate, bury it there. That place shall become desolate. The method of its retreat is this: write the letter and place it beneath your head while occupied with spiritual exercises. Burn incense of agarwood, ambergris, and musk; the need shall be fulfilled.
