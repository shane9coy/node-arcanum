And its dhikr is this:
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Heir of all things, of the harmonies and dominions and seas and heavens and orbits, and to You all affairs return, O Living One, You are the Living, the Everlasting. I ask You by the sanctification of Your names and Your attributes and Your oneness and the qualities of Your essence, that You make me among the heirs by the realities of Your secrets that are diffused in the hidden mysteries and the stations, by Your lights. And cause that to persist upon me, that You dwell within Your sanctuary together with Your messengers and Your beloved ones. Truly, You are Allah, the Everlasting, the Heir.

Whoever persists upon this dhikr, Allah the Most High will make him an heir of the intimate ones and the people of spiritual perfections.

[Heading enclosed in a decorative rectangular border with slanted hash marks]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Rashid"

Know that al-Rashid is the one who is the point of reference for all affairs and the one who aids the seekers without any advisor or counselor, and this is none other than the essence of God the Most High, who has shown all creation the path of His guidance. This name should be read in seclusion, corresponding to its numerical value. From reading it, there will be the effect that whatever he looks upon, he will guide it. Its trustee (angel) is Sartayil; he comes to the reciter and teaches him the instruction of guidance.

The transgressor should keep its talismanic design with him; the habitually sinful one should abandon wine:
[Note: The above line is underlined]

The transgressor should keep this design with him, and he will abstain from sins, and the habitually drunk one should be given it for forty days, and he will repent from wine. Its design is this:

And its dhikr is this:
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are al-Rashid, who has inspired the people of Your obedience with guidance through the illuminations, and the reciters with success through Your favor and dependence upon You. I ask You, O You who have given every created thing knowledge as a gift, and have fashioned it for what is suitable for it from endowments, that I ask You, that the gaze of Your eternity be upon me with guidance and right direction, O Allah, O al-Rashid.
