# Page 709 (English translation)
O Helper, give sufficiency
For opening the doors of goodness:
Whoever recites this supplication, Allah the Most High will open for him the doors of good.

[Boxed Header]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "al-Ḥayy" (The Living)
[/Boxed Header]

This name has occurred in the Holy Quran in this verse.
And in the world of humanity, the meaning of ḥayāt (life) is natq (speech), that is, that which is called "to speak," or with the prefix/alif which begins with these same meanings.

From this the wisdom of life becomes apparent, with all its attributes:
And it is the movement, apparent and hidden, and from it power and wisdom become apparent, along with the subtlety of heat and solar heat and the heat of the soul and minerals, with the secrets of the degrees of antiquity, and the mystery of knowledge with the method of governance. Rather, life belongs to inanimate objects too, that is, their existence by which they are found. With this same mystery, for the sake of establishing tawḥīd, and the ranks of Allah are for the Living One alone, wherever He wills and in whatever manner He wills. The meaning of "Ḥayy" is "the perceiving agent."

The dhikr should be continued by the reciter on the breath, and he should eat little, so that the light of gnosis may enter within him:
The reciter of this name should continue his breath with the dhikr, and eat little, because the light of wisdom does not find room in every belly that is filled with food. Indeed, the Messenger ﷺ said:
"Wisdom does not enter a stomach filled with food."
He should keep his body alive with purity and cleanliness, and should also incorporate the name al-Qayyūm with it; the angel Jibrā'īl will be present and will clothe him with that robe. His dhikr is this:
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Eternal Living One who gave life to [that which is] contrary to death and to the perishing, the Eternal upon whom nothing emerges, and from the living and poverty and transition, You are the Ancient, the Compeller, the Everlasting of existence by the essence of eternity. Your attributes are that You may guide. And by the attributes I ask You, by the antiquity of Your life and the everlastingness of Your essence and the eternity of Your attributes, that You may guide me in the paths of the special ones among servants and the truthful among the saints, that You may place me with the noble chosen ones. And give life to my heart, O Living One before every living thing, O Sustainer of the world through the governance of existents from the worlds of creation, from every world. I ask You to grant me what You have apportioned for me in Your knowledge, without hardship and without movement of sin, there being no doubt about the tranquil ones. And You have made every thing alive from among minerals and plants, from every silent and speaking [thing]. I ask You by the secret of life within existents and by the power of sustenance among creatures.
