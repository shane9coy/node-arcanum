**4- Dahan:**
The fourth mansion is Dahan. Its letter is dāl (د). When the moon descends into it, a most vile and corrupt spirituality manifests from it; therefore, actions of harm and corruption appropriate to it occur.

**5- Baṭn:**
The fifth mansion is Baṭn. Its letter is hāʾ (ہ). When the moon enters it, a spirituality of intermediate degree is produced from it; for this same reason, actions of both good and evil occur in it [damaged source: obscured by a grey handwritten stamp or mark].

**6- Hind:**
The sixth mansion is Hind. Its letter is wāw (و). This mansion is most noble. It is good for love and for gathering people who have become estranged. Because the spirituality that emerges from it greatly assists in the treatment of diseases, goodness, and works of righteousness.

**7- Dharraʿ:**
The seventh mansion is Dharraʿ. Its letter is zāʾ (ز). When the moon enters it, spirituality descends from it and assists in the treatment of diseases. Whoever at this time is occupied in the remembrance of God, a revelation of the malakūt world occurs upon him. It is extremely useful for the poor and seekers of truth, and it is the best for all works.

**8- Nashara:**
The eighth mansion is Nashara. Its letter is ḥāʾ (ح). When the moon enters it, a spirituality is produced in it that assists in the work of theft.

**9- Farrakh:**
The ninth mansion is Farrakh. Its letter is ṭāʾ (ط). When the moon enters it, a most vile spirituality is produced from it. Good actions are not preferable in it.
