In the heart of Al-Lawh, by the secret of "Inni anā Allāhu lā ilāha illā anā" (Verily, I am Allah, there is no god but I), on the Day of the Cry, I ask You by the secret of Your All-Collecting Name and Your Illuminating Light, by every heard and hearing, that You grant me insight into the ranks of Your Presence in existence, through the secrets You have placed in the Praised Station. Expand my heart in the land of the greatest sainthood, and spread my secret for attaining the realities of the traces of the Most Beautiful Names, and make me generous in spending and disposing of the treasures of sustenance, O He in whose hand is absolute judgment and with whom is all creation, O Extender.

Strange and Wonderful Benefits and Secrets:

Al-Khafid is a Name in which there are very great secrets for the reciter. Its attendant is named Mita'il. It rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 1,481 rows, and in each row there are 1,481 attendants. They are appointed for honor and majesty, all under the command of the honored Israfel. The prayer of the Name Al-Khafid is this:

"O Khafid, You are He who has lowered the ranks of the people of denial in the depths, and You are He who crushes them with Your overpowering might and Your overturning attributes, and You are He who magnifies Himself over them for what You have created them for at the distribution of good deeds and bad deeds. I ask You by the secret of secrets in the hearts of the righteous and the good, and by the light of the luminous lights spread throughout the regions, that You make me one who lowers himself and my secret in the station of servitude, humbling myself to You at the manifestation of the descents by the secret of Lordship and human realities. Grant me an abundant share of divine knowledge, truly You are the Hearer of prayer, the Powerful over what You will, O Lord of the worlds."

[Decorative box containing: Healing from illness and the good of this world and the hereafter]

In Al-Rafi' there are three letters from among the letters of Al-Halim. In it there are such subtle wisdoms that Allah the Exalted opens the inner sight. By its prayer every illness departs and the patient becomes healthy. This Name is from the wisdom of Allah the Exalted. Its attendant is named Marqabil, and under him are four officers, each of whom rules over 351 rows, and in each row are 351 assigned attendants who are tasked with repelling ailments. The attendant comes to the one who recites this Name and presents matters of this world and the hereafter. If he chooses this world, he obtains only this world but is deprived of the hereafter. If he chooses the hereafter, there is good in both places. The prayer of Al-Rafi' is this:

"O Allah, You are Al-Rafi' who raised the prophets and saints by Your most luminous light, and You are He who perfected for the souls of the people of love and affection the glorifications of Your Face and Your supreme Presence, and You are He who manifests lowliness and nobility in the hearts of Your saints for encompassing the worlds of things, and You are He who raised the degrees of recognition and the status of the people of recognition by certainty at the widening of great trials and the neglect of seeking help. I ask You by the secret of the letters Kaf and Nun, and by the secret of the secrets of knowledge, and by the secret of the meanings of the secret, by the words of the letters of the fortress in the directed elevation, the divestment of the best of the precious one, and by the secret of the treasures of the cycles of benefit at the unveiling of the guarded judgment, that You raise my witnessing above the known things
