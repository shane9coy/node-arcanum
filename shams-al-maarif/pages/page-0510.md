# Page 510 (English translation)
**The human faculties are also of three kinds:**

The second state is the state of dwelling in the world, and the determination of the days of life, and the transformation of human faculties, and obtaining benefit from pleasure and desire, and providing its means through the stimulation of the instruments and limbs. The third state is of the afterlife and its related matters, such as the intermediate realm (barzakh) and resurrection and the reckoning and paradise and hellfire, etc. The names related to it are the All-Merciful, the Beneficent, the Gentle, the Forgiving—whose demand is forgiveness and leniency and pardon from sins. **"And indeed, Allah is to the people kind and merciful"** (Quran 2:143). The attribute of mercy includes two things, so their explanation has already been given above. Under these three names, one name each is recorded from these names, which indicate the same matter and are specific to this state, that He alone is it.

**"The Forgiving, the Merciful, the Holy, the Accepting of Repentance, the Source of Peace, the Granter of Security, the Guardian, the Oft-Forgiving, the Bestower, the Extender, the Honorer, the Subtle, the Thankworthy, the Majestic, the Noble, the Responsive, the All-Encompassing, the Loving, the Sufficient, the Beneficial, the Light, the Guide, the Helper, the Knowing, the Guardian, the Wise, the Rightly Guided, the Patient, the Possessor of Glory and Generosity"**

Thus, the objective is indicated from these mentioned names, and reliance has been placed upon them alone, because they indicate this state—this is an admonition from **"And indeed, your Lord possesses forgiveness toward the people despite their wrongdoing"** (Quran 13:6). The Beautiful Names of God and the Most Exalted Attributes—the three names and "I believe in Allah"—are completed here **"And Allah is behind them [i.e., all of them] encompassing"** (Quran 11:20), because in it the mysterious letters are individual and paired, and the Greatest Name is omitted in all four elements, and the remaining letters are in the circle. So understand them as we have explained. The basis is the interpretation of letters, and the indication of the extended mission is because it is actually in the condition of these forty-five letters. Then the zāʾ, hāʾ, yāʾ, and wāw are mentioned, and all of them are indeed in this manner: 'alif-jīm,' 'dāl-wāw,' 'zāy-ḥāʾ,' 'bāʾ-rāʾ-kāf'—these ten letters are originally abjad, and yāʾ is the tenth. All sources are generated from divine letters, as we have explained. Then you have come to know these three names: the Eternal, the One, the Dominant. Then these names are combined: the Beneficent, the Merciful, the Forgiving.

**The mysterious letters in the circle are these:**

And know that the letters written in the circle are all mysteries and encompass concealment and hiding. And those written in the circle of names, like [stylized symbol] the circle of "O God and Noah"—see. Their explanation and the detailed secrets of letters will come soon. And these are from the names that are written in the circle. When you intend the initiating letters, they will be ten, and for this reason we have written yāʾ among the last letters, and these are the primary letters: alif-lām-mīm. And behind them are the letters of the Greatest Name, that is, the letters of the Name of the Essence. When you investigate them, you will find the result of the Holy Name through the science of letters. But these letters that encompass the Greatest Name are those present at the entrance, and in reality all are connected to one another, and through the science of letters something results from everything.

**The Hour will not be established until there is no one on earth saying "Allah, Allah"**

This circle and its letters are among the secrets of Almighty God, which the Commander of the Faithful, our master ʿAlī (may God honor his face), referred to through allusion and hint. Its complete letters, of which this verse is:

**"Alif-Lām-Mīm. The Byzantines have been defeated in the nearest land, and they, after their defeat, will overcome in a few years. To Allah belongs the command"** (Quran 30:1-4)

And he (peace be upon him) said: "The Hour will not be established until there remains on earth no one saying 'Allāh, Allāh.'" And this matter is a very great secret, contained in his book. The secret of the book is in the first chapter, al-Fātiḥah. Their extraction and derivation has been written, and it has also been explained in the Wisdom and the Hekal.
