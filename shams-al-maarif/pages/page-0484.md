# Page 484 (English translation)
**To Become Beloved Among Creation and to Have Abundant Livelihood**

If one writes upon red copper, under the conjunction of the Moon and Jupiter, on Friday during the Hour of Venus, twenty-one times, and keeps it with oneself, then one shall become beloved among creation. If it is affixed to the wall of a house, then there shall be goodness and blessing in the home. Livelihood shall become abundant. The form is as follows:

[Sigil diagram: A cross-shaped figure with angelic names at each end.
Top: جبرائیل (Jibrail)
Right: میکائیل (Mikail)
Bottom: اسرافیل (Israfil)
Left: عزرائیل (Azrail)
In the center of the cross is a stylized calligraphic symbol.]

And the prayer for this letter is:
Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā kabīru yā kabīru yā karīmu bimā awda'tahu harfa al-kāfi mina al-asrāri al-makhzūnati wal-anwāri al-maknūnati an tusakhkhiru lī khuddāma hādhihi al-harf innama amruhu idhā arāda shay'an an yaqūla lahu kun fayakūnu

**The Letter Lam**

The letter "Lam" is the friend of the noble creature. Within its secrets lies that hidden delight. The name Al-Latif (the Subtle) is derived from it.

Properties and Benefits: If one writes it 237 times upon a red copper plate on the night of Friday, the fifteenth of Ramadan, and keeps the inscription with oneself, Allah the Most High shall protect him from all illnesses. He shall deliver him from every hardship, trial, and corruption. His honor shall increase among the people. Call upon Allah Al-Latif: Tarzuqu man tashā'u bighayri hisābin

[Marginal annotation: محمد حسیب صدیق (Muhammad Haseeb Siddique)]

The divine grace shall be bestowed:

If one wishes to engrave the inscription of speech and grace upon the bezel of a gold ring, one should be fasting. Whoever wears this ring, divine grace shall remain with him. The form is as follows, and the prayer is the verse above.

**The Letter Meem**

The letter "Meem" is the most eloquent articulator, favored by celestial destiny.

Properties and Benefits: It is used for both benefit and harm. If one writes "Meem" twenty-four times upon linum wood (tunka) and affixes it to the door of a certain person's house, then the desire shall be fulfilled. It shall be written in this manner:

[Symbolic table: A 4x7 grid, each cell containing the Arabic letter 'Meem' (م).]

If one fills the numbers in Mars and writes some matter upon paper during the Hour of the Moon along with it, then that person shall be drawn and attracted toward it.
