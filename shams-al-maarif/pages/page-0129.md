[Header in a double-lined box: The Experience of Sheikh Shihab al-Din Suhrawardi]

His Eminence Sheikh Shihab al-Din Suhrawardi, may Allah have mercy on him, says: Whoever recites Surah Buruj in the 'Asr prayer shall remain protected from boils and abscesses.

The Blessing of Bismillah:
Whoever writes Surah Fatiha and washes it with water, then mixes that water and drinks it—blessing shall manifest in his home.

[Header in a double-lined box: The Properties of the Letter-based Surahs of Surah Fatiha]

Whoever understands the meanings of its letters, writes them in a glass vessel, erases them with rainwater, and after forty days of fasting drinks it—Allah the Most High shall open for him the door of His apparent and hidden grace. If after five days of fasting he writes the BISMILLAH and the verse "Inna rabbakum Allahu alladhi khalaqa al-samawati wal-ard" up to "Rabbi al-'Alamin" on clean leather on Friday during the hour of Venus and ties it to his head, Allah the Most High shall grant him a world of grace and instill awe of him in people's hearts.

Cure for Forgetfulness:
And if forgetfulness afflicts him, let him wash it and drink it and he shall benefit. Let him write this forgetfulness tablet and keep it in his house; no harmful things shall enter it. This is due to the illumination of his heart, because by "cure" the people of observation mean spiritual power. So if you attain cardiac and spiritual power, it is much better. In these works one must be very careful about impurity. The more careful one is, the better the result will be. This is the image of accord:

The Properties of the Name Latif:
Allah the Most High has four verses in the Noble Quran concerning His Name Latif. One is in Surah An'am, as follows:

لَا تُدْرِكُهُ الْاَبْصَارُ وَهُوَ يُدْرِكُ الْاَبْصَارَ وَهُوَ اللَّطِيْفُ الْخَبِيْرُ

This verse benefits the person who fears an enemy, an oppressive and tyrannical person. He should recite the Name Latif or "Latif" 129 times in the morning. He shall witness a wondrous grace of the Forgiving, the Most High, and Allah the Most High shall protect him from the evil of his enemy. Additionally, the second verse is in Surah Yusuf, as follows:

اِنَّ رَبِّيْ لَطِيْفٌ لِّمَا يَشَاۤءُ اِنَّهٗ هُوَ الْعَلِيْمُ الْحَكِيْمُ

Its property is that whoever is afflicted with sorrow, grief, or pain should recite it with the Name Latif according to the numerical correspondence of the letters. He shall be freed from every sorrow and grief. The third verse is in Surah Haj, the saying of Allah the Most High:
اَلَمْ تَرَ [illegible/cut off]
