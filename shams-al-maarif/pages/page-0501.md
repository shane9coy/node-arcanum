# Page 501 (English translation)
501

The Healing of Prophet Ayyub, peace be upon him:
Huha u dana'i asaba'ut al-shada'i, ajab ya tutha'il... That is: I am God. I heal the sick.
This was the supplication made by Prophet Ayyub, peace be upon him, through which he was healed. Whoever supplicates with these names shall obtain healing.

For victory over enemies:
Ya qahlija... That is: I am a powerful elder. Whoever persists upon this name, God shall grant him such strength that he shall subdue all enemies in battle.

The fire became a garden for Abraham, peace be upon him:
Ya ghiyath man la ghiyatha lahu ya al-shada'i ya man laysa ka-mithlihi shay'un ya bari ya wajjad ya samad ya Allah ya hay ya qayyum ya da'im ya abda al-abad... That is: I am God; I grant security to those who fear. This is the very name by whose blessing God Almighty made the fire cool and safe for Abraham, peace be upon him. Whoever reads this and blows upon a tyrant, his fever shall depart.

The effects of the names of the Muqallat:
There are twelve Muqallat, and each Muqallat is connected to one name: Ajab ya kurth'il wa ya 'usra'il wa ya khasifiya'il wa ya warjiya'il wa ya damaha'il wa ya qasaiya'il wa ya tahtai'il wa ya mu'aza'il wa ya 'iza'il wa ya mughta'il... These names are recited in the presence of kings so that one may remain safe from their evil, and when one intends to travel through forests or rivers and fears thieves, bandits, or enemies, reciting them shall dispel the fear.

The acceptance of the repentance of Prophet Adam, peace be upon him:
Ya tamtai'il wa ya tahtai'il wa ya mu'aza'il wa ya 'iza'il wa ya mughta'il. These names are to be recited in the presence of kings, and by the blessing of these very names God Almighty accepted the repentance of Adam. Whoever sinful reads these and repents, his repentance shall be accepted. If one reads them upon a pani, that is, upon a basil plant, and gives it to smell to the one whose love is desired, that person shall begin to love him.

This name is written upon the wing of Gabriel, peace be upon him:
Ya masha'ta ihhib ya hurqabiyl... That is: These are His, that I am the very essence who spreads mercy upon servants. This name was written upon the wing of Gabriel, peace be upon him, by whose blessing they travel to the east and west in a single glance. If this name is blown upon an epileptic, he shall regain consciousness.

This name is inscribed upon the palm of Israel, peace be upon him:
Ya tahraj wa tahraj ajhab ya rufiya'il... That is: I am God; I am manifest and hidden in all things. This name is written upon the palm of Israel, peace be upon him. Whoever reads it or writes it upon the palm of the hand, all his needs shall be fulfilled, and the earth shall gather beneath his feet—that is, he shall traverse a long journey in a very short time. And when one reads these names standing before the Ka'ba with ablution and takes an oath, let him present it before him.
