# Page 747 (English translation)
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Sabur"

"Sabur" is one who does not rush to do anything before its time. Rather, he leaves matters to divine decree and carries them out according to the established method, and does not advance or delay them beyond their appointed duration, and places everything within it as His divine wisdom wills.

Types of Patience: Patience of the Soul, Intellect, etc.:
There are several types of patience. One is the patience of the soul—that is, patience for the attainment of the blessings of Paradise. Another is the patience of the heart upon those things which Almighty God has deposited within it. The patience of the intellect is upon those matters which rational proof demands. And [there is] the patience of the body upon refraining from [taking revenge] and vengeance.

The Benefit of Patience:
As the Noble Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "One day of fever is the expiation for a whole year of sins."

Allah Does Everything at Its Proper Time; He Never Acts Hastily—Haste Is the Work of Satan:
A servant is not given the name "Sabur" because at the time of haste, the servant cannot remain steadfast in patience. And the Exalted Truth is free and pure from haste, and no one is more patient than He. He grants respite to the sinners in their sins even though He has the power to destroy them, yet He does not punish them in this world, but rather has extended their respite until the Day of Resurrection and the Hereafter.
This name "Sabur" is synonymous with the name "Tawwab," meaning one who gives reward and does not take retribution against criminals. The servant, too, repents either from His mercy and desire, or sometimes repents from fear of His punishment. Repentance, which means "return," is the servant's return to God. And "return" means that he fulfills His commands and obeys Him—and this too is from God's mercy alone, that He grants such success.

What Is the State of a Person When Committing Sin:
When the servant commits a sin, his thought becomes hidden within him, and the person is behind a veil. Then when he repents, that thought departs and he makes a divine return.

Types of Repentance:
There are two types of repentance. One is the original [type]—that is, God granting you the success of repentance, as Allah the Exalted says: "Then He turned to them [in mercy] so that they might repent" (Quran 9:118). This is the original type. And the secondary type is what He commands: "And repent to Allah all together" (Quran 24:31)—meaning, all of you collectively repent in the presence of God.
