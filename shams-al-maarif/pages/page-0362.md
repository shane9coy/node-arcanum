# Page 362 (English translation)
360

Pardon me with Your power and protect me so that I am not destroyed. And (my hope is, O Allah, how many blessings You have bestowed upon me, my gratitude for them to You has been deficient). Do not deprive me, O You who sees the sins and have not disgraced me. O You who possesses goodness that never ends, O You who possesses blessings that cannot be counted. I ask You to send much peace and blessings upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and his companions. Protect me from my enemies who intend evil against me.

O Allah, place fear of him upon him and cast his evil beneath his feet. Whoever intends harm against me, return it upon him, and let it reach him. Allah returned the disbelievers with their anger—they could not attain good. Allah sufficed the believers from being killed, and Allah is the Powerful, the Victorious. They are deaf, mute, and blind, so they do not see and do not speak. On that day they will speak and no excuse will be accepted from them. Indeed, Allah suffices you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Then the sheikh wrapped that paper and gave it to the person, and said: Keep it in your turban, and no harm from them will reach you. Thus it was, and that person never saw any trouble or distress ever after. It is known that whoever keeps these names with him shall be protected from every evil and shall remain safe from every fear. And if he comes before an oppressive ruler, no harm shall reach him from him. If he fights someone, victory shall be his over him. These virtues are famous among scholars, and they are also famous among those who know their value. Allah, the Exalted, supports with His own help whoever He wills, and Allah is of great bounty.
