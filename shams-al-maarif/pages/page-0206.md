# Page 206 (English translation)
The second story is that Yahya's student recited it, but when the client arrived, he lost his senses and his tongue became tied.
He closed his eyes. When he opened his eyes, he kept seeing the client before him until he gathered courage, and then the client left.
But the client did not cause him any harm. Therefore, it is obligatory upon you that you keep your heart strong—this client does not cause harm. There is comfort and
wisdom in the complete Noble Surah, and know that this is from among the specific secrets.

**Prayer of the Comprehensive Secrets:**

This is from among the books of the prophets and saints and their secrets. The prayer is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Say: "Revelation has been sent down to me." O Allah, I ask You, O You who send down revelation from above seven
heavens, that You subjugate for me what I intend and seek, and that You subjugate for me the servants of this blessed Surah, that they obey me in
all that I desire. Indeed, You are over all things capable. O Allah, O You to whom the fugitives flee, and O You in whose pardon the greedy hope,
that a company of the jinn listened. O Allah, I ask You, O You who hears and sees and cannot be seen, and He is in the highest vision.
And they said: "Indeed we have heard a wondrous Quran— that guides to the right course, so we have believed in it, and we will never associate any partner with our Lord."
O Allah, I ask You by the right of those who believed in You from the believers, through their prophets and Your prophet, and through me and through those who ask, that
You subjugate for me the servant of this Surah, that he may be a helper for me in what I desire, and that Exalted is the majesty of our Lord, He has not taken a wife nor a son.
O Allah, I ask You, O You who has not taken a wife nor a son, that You make my heart speak with wisdom and my tongue with knowledge,
and that You be a helper and assistant for me, and that You subjugate for me the hearts of all Your creation.
And that there were men from humans who sought refuge with men from the jinn, so they increased them in overwhelming error, and that they thought as you thought
that Allah would never send anyone. O Allah, I ask You, O Elevator of the heavens and O Creator of creatures and O Former of existences and O Director of time and O Sender down of the Torah and the Gospel and the Psalms and the Quran, and O You who preferred the children of Adam over
all creatures, O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O You who never sleeps, O You who subjugated the jinn and humans for Solomon—peace be upon him—
I ask You, O Allah, that You subjugate for me all Your creation and all things. And I bear witness to greatness in goodness. O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting.
O Allah, I ask You by the great hidden stored name and by the noble light, that You subjugate for me the spiritual essence of this Surah, so that they may attend me and be helpers for me in what I desire. Indeed I have sought intercession with You to You, O You who does what He wills. I adjure you, O great honored radiant spiritual spirits,
by the name that was written upon the heart of Adam—peace be upon him—and by the name with which Allah preferred you above many of the angels. There is no deity except Him, Lord of all creation. Answer me, O spiritual honored magnified radiant spirits,
that you may be helpers for me in what I desire, so that no one from creation may be able to oppose my command. Answer by the One who commanded you, O angels of the Lord of all worlds. O Allah, amen, amen, and be for me a helper in Your servant and the son of Your servant. And I have sought intercession with You, so answer me and protect me,
and the supplicants. Indeed there is no helper for him except You, and no supporter except You, and I fear no one besides You, O Most Merciful of the merciful.
