# Page 370 (English translation)
![Manuscript page 268](../source-text/.shams-al-maarif-pages/shams-al-maarif-Pages-268.jpg)

[Page 268]

Perform the calculation of ruk rifta or farfa, for this number enters into all works, and the name of the student: calculate the jimall kabir together with the name of the student, and also perform the calculation with the name of the sought one. Then observe which of them is dominant. An example: the name of the student is Ahmad and the name of the sought one is Muhammad. You perform the calculation according to the method of the operation as follows: the number of Ahmad is 53 and of Muhammad is 92. Then when the numbers of ruk rifta are applied to them, the student's becomes 273 and the name of the sought becomes 276. When these two are combined, they become 549. Subtract 30 from them, leaving 519. Divide these by 4; when both are combined, they become one hundred twenty-nine with a remainder of 3. Then the divine command is achieved as intended. When the remainder is three, add one to the fifth cell. If the remainder of one of them is one, add one to the thirteenth cell; the purpose will be fulfilled. And Allah the Most High is the most knowing.

Know that with this verse, "And Allah brings forth the hidden what you were concealing, and We said: Strike it with some of them, thus does Allah give life to the dead and shows you His signs that you may understand," a sleeping person will reveal the secrets of his heart. The form of the talisman is this:

When you intend this, write it on your finger, then place it on the chest of the sleeping one, and ask him whatever you wish; by the command of Allah the Most High, he will tell immediately. This is specific to the people of insight.

The Destruction of the Oppressor:

And write the verse, "Thus is the taking of your Lord when He takes the cities while they are unjust; surely His taking is painful, severe," and place it in the house of the oppressor, and he will be destroyed and ruined. Write it on a bone of the elbow and place it in the house of the oppressor. This is its form:
