# Page 346 (English translation)
346

Even if they wish, they cannot speak of what they have caused. They do not utter a word. Every king is a slave to God, and every wealthy one is poor and insignificant before God, and every tyrant is humble before God, with no protector from God. I seek aid against you, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, through God the Almighty. The voices are humbled before the Merciful, so you hear nothing but whispers. And a barrier is placed between them and what they desire. This is the Day when they do not speak, and no permission is granted them so they may apologize. God will suffice you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Kāf Hā Yā ʿAyn Sād. Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf.

If they also wish for what they intended, they do not speak. All kings are slaves to God, and all the wealthy are needy before God. Every tyrant is low before God, and there is no place to save from God. I seek help from God over you, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, through God the Almighty. The voices have bowed down for the Merciful, so you hear nothing but whispers, and between them and between what they desire, a barrier will be made. They will not speak, nor will permission be given to them that they make excuses. God alone is sufficient for you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing.

Deaf, deaf, deaf, deaf, deaf, deaf, deaf. Mute, mute, mute, mute, mute, mute, mute. So they do not speak. So they do not speak. And We placed before them a barrier and behind them a barrier, and We covered them so they do not see. So they do not see. So We have covered them, so they do not see. I have bound your tongue, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, by what God bound therewith the seven heavens—that it should not fall upon the earth except by His permission—and by what God bound therewith the wild beasts from Daniel, peace be upon him, and by what God bound therewith the rock from giving birth, and by what God bound therewith the barren wind that leaves nothing it touches but makes it like crumbled rubble. I have bound the tongues of all humans, every female and male, from the children of Adam and daughters of Eve, from the bearer of this my writing—that they do not speak concerning him except good, or they remain silent. Deaf, mute, blind, so they do not speak. And God repelled those who disbelieved in their rage; they did not attain any good. And God sufficed the believers in fighting, and God is Strong, Mighty.

They are deaf and mute, so they do not see and cannot speak. And We placed a wall between them, and they are blind. We have covered them, so they cannot see. O So-and-so son of So-and-so, I have bound your tongue with that by which God the Most High bound the seven heavens, that they should not fall upon the earth, and with that by which God the Most High repelled the wild beasts from Daniel, peace be upon him, and with that by which He bound the rock from producing offspring, and with that by which God the Most High gave the barren wind, such that whatever it comes upon, except He makes it like rotted wood. I have bound the tongues of all people from the bearer of this writing, that they speak concerning it only with good and virtue. God the Most High returned them with anger, that they may not attain good. God is sufficient for the believers, and He is Strong, Mighty.

Destruction of the enemy and deliverance from the fear of the ruler

This talisman, which is written above, has this property: when there is the intention of the enemy's destruction, or you fear some oppressive ruler, then on the night of Friday at midnight, write this talisman on paper and bind it on a ruin or on a branch of a tree from which no bird flies and sits. By God's will, the enemy's destruction will occur.
