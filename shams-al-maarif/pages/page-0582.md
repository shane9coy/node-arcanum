# Page 582 (English translation)
**Kings, Amirs, and Ministers Are Subjugated:**

If it is desired to subjugate kings or great men, make a tablet of twomithqāls of gold on Sunday. Isolate the letters of the names of the practitioner and the desired person, sum them, and write the letter alif according to its corresponding number and add it to them. Fill them in a square and prepare the talisman. Write the seal and the client's name on the four corners of the talisman, and beside each side write the letter alif thirty times, and on the fourth side write it thirty-one times, so that there are 121 alifs. Then fumigate it with incense and keep it with you; the desire will be attained.

**Inscribing the Letter Alif with the Client's Name on a Knife, They Cut the Spleen and Cure Headache:**

If they engrave the letter alif with the names of both clients on a knife and point it toward one who has spleen problems or headache, he will immediately be cured. And one who is afflicted with epilepsy or madness will also regain consciousness through its pointing. There is also a special combination of this letter for disappearing. Tan the skin of a wolf with henna and bagharī (a type of dye), then write the letter alif along with the names of the clients, the invitation, and the breaking of the fast upon it, and place it on the head like a cap; they will be hidden from the sight of people.

**Its Talisman: Weapons Have No Effect on Whoever Possesses It:**

If one makes a tasxīr of this letter and fills [unclear] with it, and writes it on paper with red light in the hour of the ascendant of Mars; whatever person keeps it with him, no weapon will have any effect on him.

**Method of Knowing What Is in the Heart:**

If you wish that a person tell you what is in his heart, write this letter on the palm of your right hand with your left hand along with your points of articulation, at the time when you are in an assembly and the horoscope is inclined toward him. Then, if that person is sleeping, place your hand on his chest; whatever is the state of his heart, he will express it. And if that person is standing, shake hands with him; he will immediately express the things of his heart. Its talisman is this:

**Its Retreat Is 28 Days:**

The retreat for this letter is 28 days. Purify your outer and inner being, sit in retreat, and recite the invitation and the breaking of the fast 111 times after each prayer. Write the form of the letter alif and place it before you. On the day the client appears, the entire retreat chamber will be filled with light; except that in the air between earth and heaven, something will appear suspended. Whatever glorification and declaration you wish to make to it, do so. Then the name of that person upon whom this effect will occur is necessarily [involved]. Another method of the invitation of this letter is that one should perform the ritual ablution and recite its prayer in retreat, and keep its form before oneself.
