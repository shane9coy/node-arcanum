O angels and spirit, unless you answer and do what I have commanded you, I swear upon you, O Safyanil, the king assigned to the fighting sphere, by the right of Him who knows the secret and the hidden. Answer, O Kasfayil, in the presence of Maymun Abalokh. O Maymun, by the right of the victorious king whose command dominates you, Kasfayil, and by the right of the Eternal, 2 Adrak, 2 Azrial. 2 I have sworn upon you, O angels of the Lord of the Worlds, by the right of Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim, unless you answer me, heeding by the right of Him who said to the heavens and earth, "Come willingly or unwillingly." They said, "We have come willingly." By the right of the True Real King, the trustworthy sovereign, the extractor of man from every distress, and by the sanctity of Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, and his companion the Truthful, unless you have subjugated for me this earthly one to be my helpers in my will, obeying my command, by the right of Ahya Ahya Frsh Bakfush Akash Kshlakh, and by the right of the Singular Self-Sufficient who did not beget nor was begotten and there is none equal to Him, unless you have hastened and answered and none of you remains. Make haste now. God bless you and upon you. Answer and do what I have commanded you, by the right of what I have sworn upon you, and indeed it is an oath—if you knew—great.

At any time, I have sworn upon you in place of this, and if you knew, this oath is very great. And indeed, if they remain steadfast in this way, We shall give them abundant water so that We may test them. And whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, God the Exalted will enter him into severe punishment. Accept, none of you shall be left behind from that which I have sworn to you. And indeed, mosques belong to God alone, so do not call upon anyone alongside God. And indeed, a servant of God stood up calling upon Him, and they were near him when the mountains would have fallen upon them, that he said, "I call upon my Lord alone and I do not associate anyone with Him." Say, "I have no authority to bring you benefit or harm." Say, "None can give me refuge from God except Him, and I find no refuge other than with Him, except that the way is through reaching God and the message." Every disobeyer of God and His Messenger, for him is the fire of Hell, they shall remain therein forever, until when they see that which was promised them—the punishment. Then they shall soon know who is weaker in helpers and fewer in number. Say, "I do not know whether that which is promised you is near, or whether God will extend it for a long time. He is the Knower of the Unseen, and He does not disclose the Unseen to anyone except a messenger He has chosen." [unclear] Before him and behind him are guardians.

O God, I ask You by Your eternity, the purity of Your power, the root of Your sovereignty, the glory of Your throne, the vastness of Your footstool, O You whose kingdom no imagination reaches, O You whose essence every creature seeks refuge from, there is nothing that does not seek refuge from Him, and in His kingdom no one is given refuge except by Him. O God, I ask You—I indeed have no power over benefit or harm except by Your command. O God, I ask You by this promise that You made with Your prophets, and with it You guided Your saints. O God, O Majestic, three times. O Great, three times. O Holy, three times. O God, three times. O You whose is the kingdom of heaven and earth. O You who knows, and nothing is known except by Him. O God, I ask by Your rank, by the very core of Your knowledge, by the face of Your grace, by the kaf of Your greatness, by the lam of Your kindness. O the nun of the three, [unclear] the alif of my outcry, by the dad of my asking.
