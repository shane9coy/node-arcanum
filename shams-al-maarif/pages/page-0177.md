# Page 177 (English translation)
The Angel and the Spirit, Lord of the Angels, who are in the presence of the Divine Sanctuary—the Exalted—the Lord of the Angels who execute what they are commanded, the Exalted. Lord of the Angels who are on earth, seeking [Your pleasure]. O Allah, I ask You by the spirits divided by the Exalted's glory, and I ask You by the spirits entrusted with the breezes of generosity, and I ask You, O Allah, to support me with a spirit from You, so that no power may prevent me from standing to unveil the two manifestations, until I stand in the presence that brought me forth, and the immersion in the lights that revealed me from it, so that I may be strong to face the fixed luminous spirits, and may I live through the movement of these noble lines, for You are the Living, the Eternal, the Light, the Guide, the Pure, the Guardian, the Revealer, the Descender, the Hearing, the Enricher, the Holy, the Exalter, the Strong, the Forbearing.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Allah—none has the right to be worshipped except Him, the Living, the Eternal. He has sent down upon you, [O Muhammad], the Book in truth, confirming what was before it, and He sent down the Torah and the Gospel before, as guidance for the people, and He sent down the Furqan [the Quran]. Indeed, it is We who have sent down the Remembrance, and indeed, We will be its guardian. Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche within which is a lamp, the lamp is within glass, the glass as if it were a brilliant star, lit from a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil almost glows even if untouched by fire. Light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills. And Allah presents examples for the people, and Allah is Knowing of all things.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Ya, Sin. By the wise Quran, indeed you, [O Muhammad], are among the messengers upon a straight path. A revelation of the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate, to warn a people whose forefathers were not warned, so they are unaware. Indeed, the word [i.e., decree] has come upon most of them, so they do not believe. Indeed, We have placed upon their hearts coverings, and upon their ears heaviness, and even if they see every sign, they will not believe. And when they come to you arguing with you, those who disbelieve say, "This is nothing but legends of the former peoples." And thus We have placed upon every city the greatest of its criminals to conspire within it, but they only plot against themselves, though they perceive it not. And when a sign comes to them, they say, "Never will we believe until we are given like that which was given to the messengers of Allah." Allah is most knowing of where He places His message. There will afflict those who committed wrong, a disgrace from Allah and an severe punishment on account to their plotting. Or do they seek to deceive Allah and those who believe, while they are deceiving themselves and perceive it not? In their hearts is disease, and Allah has increased their disease. And for them is a painful punishment because they used to lie. And if they are told, "Do not spread corruption upon the earth," they say, "We are only rectifiers." Unquestionably, it is they who are the corrupt, but they perceive it not. And if they are told, "Believe as the people have believed," they say, "Should we believe as the foolish have believed?" Unquestionably, it is they who are the foolish, but they do not know. And when they meet those who believe, they say, "We believe," but when they are alone with their devils, they say, "Indeed, we are with you; we were only mocking." Allah mocks them and leaves them in their transgression, wandering blindly. These are the ones who purchased error in exchange for guidance, so their commerce did not profit them, nor were they guided. Their example is like one who kindled a fire, and when it illuminated what was around him, Allah took away their light and left them in darkness, unable to see. Deaf, mute, blind—so they do not return. Or [it is] like darkness upon a sea layer, covered by waves, above which are waves, above which are clouds—darknesses, some upon another. When one extends his hand, he can hardly see it. And he to whom Allah has not granted light—then he has no light.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend therein by the permission of their Lord for every matter. Peace it is until the emergence of dawn.

Translation of the Second Prayer: Beginning

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The pure essence of my Lord, who arranged desires before their existence. Pure is my Lord, who destined decrees from His light before their manifestation. Pure is my Lord, whose essence brought forth limits before knowing them. Pure is my Lord, who drew the heavens near with His light and praised them. Pure is my Lord, who moved the spheres with His light and established their locations. Pure is my Lord, who refined spirits with His light and honored them. Pure is my Lord, whose essence composed bodies with His light and unified them. O Allah, I ask You with that light with which You radiant upon the Throne and expanded the lights, and I ask You with that light with which Your radiance encompassed the essence of lights and all those spirits. And I ask You with that light with which You radiated upon the earth, which shaped the bodies.

O Allah, I ask You with the essence of Your light and with the throne of Your lights and with Your knowledge—I ask You with Your light and with Your seat. O Light upon Light, O Turner of all things, O Illuminator of all things, I ask from You...
