# Page 637 (English translation)
All categories of divine [names]: universals, particulars, "sublime," "base," "simple," "compound," etc.:

Among these are all categories of divine [names]. These are the categories of universals and particulars, the sublime and the base, the simple and the compounds, the separate and the essences and the letters, and all of these are mentioned in the Noble Quran. If the seeker knows the indications and practices the discipline, and investigates this secret through the imagination of spiritual discipline, all these secrets will be revealed to him. Know that this is established among the names of the saints of Allah for those competent to interpret it. Whoever mentions this name, it will grant him awe and greatness.

Its properties are: when one engraves it upon silver on Monday night and writes the name of the assigned angel around it, and recites it according to its numerical correspondence to the desired number, and keeps it with himself, Allah the Exalted will elevate his rank. The assigned angel is Jīʾīl, which corresponds to the desired number: 121. If recited in seclusion, the assigned angel will fulfill his need. For the person whose name corresponds to this number, this is the Supreme Name. When one recites it before a ruler, the ruler will honor and respect him. Its talisman is this:

And its recitation of this name is:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ o اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ الْمَلِکُ الْقُدُّوْسُ مُحْيِ الْاَرْوَاحِ وَالنُّفُوْسِ مَالِکُ الرِّقَابِ وَ مُسَبِّبُ الْاَسْبَابِ مَلِکِ يَوْمِ الدِّيْنِ وَ مُقَرِّبُ الْبَعِيْدِ وَ مُجِيْبُ دَعْوَةِ الْمُضْطَرِّيْنَ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ الْوَاحِدُ الْاَحَدُ ذَلَّتْ هَابَ الْمُلُوْکُ وَ صَارَ كُلُّ مَلِكٍ لَكَ عَبْدًا مَمْلُوكًا اَسْئَلُكَ بِاسْمِكَ الْمَلِكِ الْقُدُّوْسِ اَنْ تُمَلِّكَنِيْ نَاصِيَتِيْ وَ تَكْشِفَ لِيْ عَنْ حَقَائِقِ عَالَمِ الْجَبَرُوْتِ لِاَحْظٰی بِالْاَسْرَارِ الرَّبَّانِيَّةِ وَالْاٰيَاتِ الْمَلَكُوْتِيَّةِ وَاَسْوَدُ بِاِشْرَاقِيْ عَلٰی اَبْنَاءِ جِنْسِيْ وَ مَلِّكْنِيْ اَللّٰهُمَّ نَاصِيَةَ عَوَالِمِ اِسْمِكَ الْاَعْظَمِ الَّذِيْ تَعَزَّزْتَ بِهِ وَلَا يُسَمّٰی بِهِ غَيْرُكَ يَا مَلِكُ يَا قُدُّوْسُ يَا مَالِكُ الْمُلْكِ يَا ذَاالْجَلَالِ وَالْاِكْرَامِ اَجِبْ اَيُّهَا السَّيِّدُ الْجَلِيْلُ هٰذَا اِسْمُ الْجَلِيْلُ وَ اَمِدَّنِيْ بِرُوْحٍ مِنْ رُوْحَانِيَّتِكَ يَخْدِمُنِيْ فِيْ حَوَآئِجِیْ

With the recitation of this name, strange subjugation and love are produced.

Know that this name has a strange effect in subjugation and love, and it also has full efficacy in the fulfillment of needs. Write its talisman and keep it with yourself, recite this name according to its aforementioned number, and command the assigned angel, and whatever task you command it, it will fulfill.
