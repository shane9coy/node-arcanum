# Page 130 (English translation)
God is Most Gracious to His servants; He gives sustenance to whom He wills; and He is the Strong, the Mighty. [Surah Shura 42:19]

Whoever reads this verse and the Name "Ya Latif" [O Most Subtle] regularly, wealth will come to him in abundance. The fourth verse in Surah Mulk is as follows:

Shall he who created not know? And He is the Most Subtle, the Aware. [Surah Mulk 67:14]

Its special property is that if any person seeks the position of judge or ruler or similar office, let him recite this verse and the Name Ya Latif every morning and evening, and he will obtain his desire.

[Boxed Header: The Ritual for Deliverance from Every Illness Except Death]

Now we return to the mention of the properties of Surah Fatiha. Let the person write Surah Fatiha and wash with rainwater, and apply it to the patient's mouth and hands, and recite it three times while drinking: "O Allah, grant healing—You are the Healer—and sufficiency—You are the Sufficient—and well-being—You are the Granter of Well-being." Let him recite this three times, and Allah the Most High will grant him healing at that very moment, provided death has not come.

Treatment for Palpitation:
If there is heart palpitation, drink this water and the illness will depart. If it is written in a glass vessel with musk and saffron and washed with rosewater, the illness will depart; drink it and you will be healed.

Treatment for Mental Sharpness:
If the dull-minded person drinks it for seven days, his dullness will end. He will become wise and intelligent, such that whatever he hears he will remember. If it is written in a glass vessel with musk and washed with rainwater, and Isfahani kohl is dissolved in this water and applied to the eyes, weakness of vision will depart and his gaze will become sharp, and all eye illnesses will be eliminated. If white pepper powder and black pepper powder are mixed into this kohl and applied to the eye, he will see spiritual beings; they will do such things with him that he will not understand.

Treatment for Lassitude, Fatigue, and Pain:
Whoever recites Surah Fatiha with a cup [of water] day and night, fatigue, lassitude, and any kind of pain will not befall him. If it is written in a pure vessel and washed with rosewater, and poured into the ear of the person in pain, relief from pain will come. If it is washed with pumpkin oil or butter, and Surah Fatiha is read seventy times and blown upon it, then when it is applied to one afflicted with paralysis or sciatica, relief will come.

Fulfillment of Desires:
If a person of nobility has a need, it is fitting for him to recite this surah in order, with the ablution of tranquility and sincere heart, facing the qibla, seven times—which is essential. First, pray two units of prayer [damaged/unclear] read this surah forty-one times, then ask Allah the Most High for your need. The need will be fulfilled, and this has been tested many times. If a sincere person reads it forty-one times between each obligatory prayer for forty days, his need will be fulfilled.
