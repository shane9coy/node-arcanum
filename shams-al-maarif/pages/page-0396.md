# Page 396 (English translation)
And give me from Your vast treasuries, O You who subject [all things]. Subject my sustenance for me, and make every difficulty easy for me, and soften for me the hearts of Your servants, as You softened iron for David, peace be upon him. O Allah, subject for me the servants of this Surah, that they may fulfill my need, and grant me visitation of the grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. And they say: "When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful?" — a statement from a Lord, the Merciful. Kaf Sin Aalam — "Peace be upon you; You are my Lord, and in Your hand are my hearing, my sight, and my heart." That is my fortress, and You have honored me and disgraced me and raised me, You have remembered me and exalted my rank. Blessed are You, O Light of Lights, and O Giver of Lifespans, and You are transcendent in Your sublimity above the attributes of created beings, and Your rank is elevated above the ways of deficiency and calamities. The earths and the heavens bear witness to this: "To You belongs the supreme glory, the most vast court, and the most satisfying honor." Holy, Holy, Lord of the angels and the Spirit, Illuminator of the dark depths and the wastelands, and Rescuer of the drowned from the sea of destruction and terror. I seek refuge in You from the evil of the darkness when it descends, and from an envier when he envies. And I await the supplications of a broken servant who composed [this]: "Indeed You hear, so You give hope; indeed You answer, and I am standing, waiting. I find no guardian other than You. I ask You, O Allah, by Your name with which You have decreed all good things, and with which You have sent down blessings, and with which You have brought forth [souls] from the darkness, and with which You have opened the remembrance of [all] volitions. I ask You to bless and grant peace to our master Muhammad, and to grasp the garments of Your lights which repel the visions of the unjust and the enviers, making their hands fail and their hopes dashed. And make my portion from You be an illumination that purifies for me my certainty, and reveals for me every secret, O Light of all things and their guidance. You are the One who created darkness with Your light, and every light is from Your light, O Revealer of everything hidden, and to You all matters return, and by You evils are repelled, O Living, O Sustainer, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. In You I seek help, and from Your punishment I seek refuge. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from their evils. If We willed, We would send down upon them from the sky a sign [before which] they would bow." Kaf Aalam — "O Allah, O Sender of clouds, O Destroyer of coalitions, defeat my enemies and their troops and their followers, and grant me victory over them. O Allah, show me Your sanctuary by Your generosity, and convey me to visitation of the grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and subject for me Your creation, and soften for me their hearts and their spirits. O Allah, make every difficulty easy for me, and make the difficult easy, gentle, and smooth. O Allah, grant me a mighty victory, and open for me a clear opening, and grant me lawful, pure, blessed sustenance, by the right of Surah Ya Sin and the Wise Quran, O Lord of the worlds."

[Urdu Translation]: O Allah, I ask You — O the Lord of the First and the Last — that You fulfill my need, remove my pain and sorrow. Grant me from Your vast treasuries. O the One who subjects all things, subject my sustenance for me, make every difficulty easy, soften the hearts of Your servants for me, as You softened iron for David, peace be upon him. Subject the servants of this Surah for me — they fulfill my need. Grant me visitation of the blessed grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. They say: "When will this promise be fulfilled, if you are truthful?" Until the peace of the Merciful Lord — "Peace be upon you; You are my Lord; in Your hand are my hearing, my sight, and my heart."
