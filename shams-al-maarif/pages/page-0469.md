# Page 469 (English translation)
467

Have an image of a fish engraved on its back and throw it into the river. The fish will gather around it so that it can be caught by hand. If he does this every day and night after every prayer, indeed at all times, then he will be counted among those with whom Hazrat Khidr, peace be upon him, will meet.

For obtaining the sacred sciences:
And these sacred sciences will be taught to him so that he can progress spiritually and enter the sacred assembly. He can observe the lights of the veils of beauty, noble feelings, and the stations of the angelic realm.

Ism al-Nāfiʿ (The Beneficial Name): One letter of the Greatest Name is within it. Its chief angel assigned to it is ʿAzrāʾīl. It has four deputy commanders. Each deputy has 201 rows under its command. In each row there are 201 angels. When the reciter mentions it, the angel assigned to him comes. The prayer is this:

Yā nāfiʿu anta alladhī manaʿta al-shubuhāti mina al-qulūbi wa al-bidaʿa ʿani al-ʿaqāʾidi al-māniʿati ʿan idrāki sirri al-ghuyūbi ṣadaʿa ʿankum al-khayru wa al-sharru wa al-nafʿu wa al-ḍarru wa al-fawāʾidu wa al-ʿawāʾidu wa al-shadāʾidu fī kawni al-ṣaghāʾiri al-nāsi asʾaluka manʿa al-balāʾi wa jazīla al-ʿaṭāʾi wa siʿati al-rizqi wa aʿūdhu bika mina al-zalali wa al-mukhālafāti wa al-mawāniʿi wa al-āfāti asʾaluka mahābatan bi-ghayri wāsiṭatin wa-ijʿal lī min kulli ḍīqin makhrajan ḥattā yahīsha bi-khafīdika bi-dhālika min tābidin iʿtibārika fī al-āfāti innaka anta Allāhu mā jiʾa al-shubuhāti

Ism al-Nūr (The Name of Light): The commander angel of this name is Mīkāʾīl. Its sciences have four commanders. Each commander has 258 fixed rows. Each row has 258 angels assigned to it. The prayer is this:

Yā nūru anta al-nūru al-ẓāhiru alladhī ẓahara bihi kullu al-ẓahūri wa anta al-ḥākimu bi-nūrika ʿalā kulli nūrin taʿrifu bawāṭina al-khalqi wa khawāhirahum bi-mā albasastahum min karāmatika wa bi-mā aḥyaytahum min shahādatika wa bi-mā rafaqta ʿalayhim nūran walā yabkī wa-in min shayʾin illā ʿindanā khazāʾinuhu wa khaḍaʿa kullu jalālin li-jalālika wa jabrūti ḥamdhika wa adhhal fī jazīlika wa maddadika wa asʾaluka Allāhumma yā nūra al-nūri wa shāfiya al-ṣudūri wa bāhita man fī al-qubūri an tunawwiranī bi-nūrika al-aʿlā wa jamālika al-abhā sirrī wa jahhrī wa ẓāhirī wa rūḥī wa qalbī wa lisānī wa fuʾādī wa jildī wa nahāḥī wa bi-dhātika innaka anta Allāhu fī al-shiddati wa al-rawāʾi

Ism al-Bāqī (The Eternal Name): One letter of the Greatest Name is within it. Its commander is Khaẓfiyāʾīl. It has four subordinate commanders. Each commander has 113 fixed rows. Within each row there are 113 angels. The prayer is this:

Yā bāqiyu anta alladhī tufnī wa tabqī kulla makhlūqin wa anta alladhī aḥyayta kulla marzūqin bi-faḍlika wa anta alladhī akhrajta mani ijtabaytahu mina al-kufrī wa al-bāqī wa al-qawī asʾaluka bi-sirri baqāʾika fī khalqika an tarzuqanī baqāʾan lā nifādda lahu abadan aw ḥayātan lā mawta baʿdahā sarmadan wa lā takilnī ilā aḥadin ṭarfata ʿaynin walā ilā aḥadin dūnakum warzuqnī tathbīta al-qulūbi wa al-arwāḥi wa al-istiʾlāʾi ʿalā azimati al-ajsādi wa al-arwāḥi innaka anta Allāhu al-wāḥidu al-qahhāru
