# Page 433 (English translation)
433

Between the veils there is a thousand veils of light and darkness. If the dhakir (one who remembers) mentions this name, the subordinate (mawkal) will come to him and grant him forbearance and patience, and it cools his anger and wrath, even if [the wrath] has been cast upon him. But Allah the Exalted grants him the tranquil soul, and all these ranks are obtained through this very subordinate. If this person remains inclined toward this subordinate, the subordinate is his servant along with his names. And the rank of this dhakir with Allah the Exalted will be elevated above the subordinate. Understand this secret well, for there is no more beneficial matter than it. O Allah, nothing can prevent what You grant, and if You do not give, there is no other giver. O Ghaffar! The prayer for this name is:

O Allah, O Ghaffar, You are the Initiator of the magnificent blessings and their great ones, and You are the Creator of the subtle favors and their delicate ones. The downpour has ceased by Your condensation upon all creation, and You are the Disposer of whatever You have decreed among the existent. And how excellent is the Decider! You cover the faults and unveil the griefs, and from between them You manifest sunrise and sunset. You are the Forgiver, the Much-Forgiving, the Pardoning of what You have revealed. O You Who have overcome, and You are the Knowing, the Aware of what You have concealed in the appearances of Your gentleness and what You have hidden in the innermost thoughts of the hearts of the people of Your veils. I ask You by Your ancient power and by Your firm strength to grant me the coolness of Your pardon on the Day of Gathering, and the sweetness of Your forgiveness on the day of the appearance of sorrow and joy. O Allah, establish me upon the constancy of intentions for the unveiling of Your light, for You are Allah, the Light, and the Healer of hearts. O Ghaffar!

[In a rectangular decorative box:]
Subjugation of the subordinate of the name Qahhar—victory over all kinds of enemies

The name Al-Qahhar is a great name. The soul of the one who remembers this name, and all his enemies, become subdued. The name of his subordinate is Damyayil (Damiel). He rules over four officers, each of whom watches over 306 safs (ranks), and in each saf there are 306 subordinates. All these subordinates possess immense power. The subordinate comes to the dhakir of this name and manifests to him whoever person he wishes—whether apparent or hidden. By the blessing of this name, the apparent and hidden enemies are subdued, and no person has the strength or courage to speak because of the awe of the dhakir. The prayer of the name Al-Qahhar is:

O Qahhar, You are the One Who has overcome the tyrants and the Pharaohs with humiliation and debasement, and You are the One Who erased their traces in magic and returned them to the Fire. Yours is the predominant strength and power, and the sublime majesty. You are capable of whatever You will in the present and the past. There is no existent except You, and everything that You have brought forth from creation is subject to Your overcoming. I ask You by the subtle intricacies of Your hidden grace and Your fulfilling beneficence, to make my heart inhabited by all forms of beautification, and my spirit scattered with the lights of true knowledge. And adorn my neck with the delicate subtle secrets of Your names and attributes, so that I may find You as a witness. O Allah, I ask You for the subtle manifestations of Your goodness and the continuous flow of Your beneficence, for the perfection of myself through it in actions, and the perfection through it of my tongue in speech. And You are the Encompasser of all that the worlds contain. O Qahhar!
