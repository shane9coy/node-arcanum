# Page 404 (English translation)
O the one who purifies, O the one who destroys enemies, purify my enemies. O Korah's rebellious ones' destroyer, destroy my so-and-so enemy. O Zarqawn,1 the lovers have been purified, and we are indeed upon that we remember You. But we promise them, and at that time they will all be present before us.

Operation for Love

When one intends someone's love,2 write as follows:

Allāhumma aʿṭif qalba fūlān ʿalā muḥibīna fa-lā yasmaʿu wa-lā yubṣiru wa-lā yantiqtu illā bi-muḥibīna yā jāmiʿa l-nāsi li-yawmin lā rayba fīhi inna Allāha lā yukhlifu l-mīʿāda Allāhumma ijmaʿ baynī wa-bayna fūlānin wa-allif baynahumā kamā allafta bayna l-thalji wa-l-nāri wa-inna kullan lammā jamiʿun ladaynā muḥḍarūna wa-alqaytu ʿalayka maḥabbatan minnī wa-li-tuṣnaʿa ʿalā ʿaynī yuḥibbūnahum ka-ḥubbi Allāhi wa-llaḏīna āmanū ashaddu hubban li-llāhi law anfaqta mā fī l-arḍi jamīʿan mā allafta bayna qulūbihim wa-lākinn Allāha allafa baynahum Allāhumma yā man allafa bayna l-thalji wa-l-nāri allif bayna qulūbi ʿibādika aw bayna qalbi kadhā wa-kadhā yā ʿazīzu yā jabbāru wa-āyatun lahumu l-arḍu l-maytatu l-ʿazīzu l-ʿalīmu Allāhumma allif l-maḥabbata li-ghayri kulli shāhidin wa-l-mutawallā ʿan kulli bāṭinin as'aluka yā Allāhu. Recite this three times.

Translation:3 O Allah, place the heart of so-and-so in love for me; let him not hear, see, nor speak except about my love. O the one who will gather people on that Day in which there is no doubt, Allah the Most High does not break His promise. O Allah, bring me and so-and-so together, create love between him and me as You created love between snow and fire, and there all will be present before us, and I have cast upon you my love so that you may be nurtured before my eyes. They love them with the love of Allah, and those who believe have intense love for Allah. If you were to spend all that is on the earth, you could not have induced love between their hearts, but Allah induced love between them. O Allah, O the one who induced love between snow and fire, cast love into the hearts of Your servants or into the heart of so-and-so and so-and-so. O the Mighty, O the Compeller, and a sign for them is the dead earth.4 The Mighty, the Knowing. O Allah, You are the one who induces love with every unseen matter and the one who takes charge of every secret. I ask You, O Allah!

Then write:

Yā arḥama l-rāḥimīna yā rāḥima l-ʿabarāti wa-kāshifa l-kurabāti anta Allāhu lladhī tuzīlu sahā'iba l-miḥani wa-qad amsati thiqālan wa-tajʿalu zarʿahā hashīman wa-ʿiẓāmahā ramīman wa-turaddu l-maghlūba ḥaythu wa-l-maṭlūba ṭāliban kam min ʿabdin daʿāka innī maghlūbun fa-ntanṣar fa-fataḥnā abwāba l-samā'i bi-mā'in munhamirin alāwāḥun wa-dusurun5

O the most merciful of those who show mercy, O the one who has mercy upon those who weep, O the one who removes hardships, You are Allah, the one who disperses the clouds of afflictions whose evening has become heavy, and You make their harvest as broken straw and their bones as dust, and You make the defeated victorious and the sought one into a seeker. How many servants have called upon You saying "I am defeated, help me!" and You opened the gates of heaven with pouring water. Planks and nails.6

---

1. Variant: Zarqum or Azraq. The name of a spirit or entity.
2. Or: "the love of someone."
3. This is the Urdu translation of the preceding Arabic text, rendered here in English.
4. Quran 29:20.
5. Quran 54:11 — "We split the earth with springs of water. So We paired the mountains: the well-timbered trees bound with iron pegs." This appears to be an allusion to Quranic verses within the magical text.
6. This appears to refer to Quran 54:11 or 54:13.
