# Page 182 (English translation)
Truly your Lord is the Compeller, the Powerful, the Helper, the Strong, the Dominant, the Avenger, Possessor of severe vengeance, humiliation 60 times [unclear] pure, the Chosen, Qasim the one who strikes terror, Possessor of Glory and Generosity, the Great Guardian, the Noble Attorney. He is the One of great power and severe grip, the Possessor of the Glorious Throne. He does whatever He wills. Allah has sealed their hearts, and over their hearing and eyes is a veil, and for them is a great punishment. Allah keeps cutting off their sustenance and leaves them to wander in their rebellion. They persist in their rebellion. They are deaf, dumb, and blind, so they will not return. Or they are like clouds in the sky containing darkness, thunder, and lightning—until humiliation was cast upon them, wherever they are found, except those who have taken refuge with Allah and the people. They have incurred Allah's wrath; upon them is humiliation. Those who disbelieved said to their messengers: "We will surely expel you from our cities, or you must return to our religion." So their Lord revealed to them: "We will surely destroy the wrongdoers, then We will settle you in the land after them. This is for whoever fears My station and dreads My promise." They sought victory, and every rebellious tyrant was frustrated. Truly We help Our messengers and the believers in the worldly life, and on the Day when witnesses are established, the excuse of the wrongdoers will not benefit them; for them is cursing and an evil home. We made examples among them with the destruction of those of great strength, and the example of the former people has passed. This is because Allah is the Guardian of those who believe, while the disbelievers have no guardian—until, when they rejoiced in what was given them, We seized them suddenly, and they were despairing. The plot of the wrongdoers was cut off, and all praise belongs to Allah, Lord of all the worlds. Allah the Exalted has decreed: "Indeed, We and Our messengers will surely prevail." Truly Allah is Powerful and Exalted in Dominion. He is the One who expelled the disbelievers among the People of the Scripture from their homes at the first gathering. He cast upon their hearts seal, so they do not understand. When you see them, their bodies impress you, and they listen to you saying vain things. Have you not seen what your Lord did to the people of 'Ad, that is, the people of Iram, who had structures the like of which had never been created in the land, and with the people of Thamud who carved out dwellings in the rocks of the valley? Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the people of the elephant? Did He not make their plan go astray, and send upon them birds of Ababil that pelted them with stones of baked clay, and He made them like eaten stubble?

**The Destruction of Enemies:**
Whoever reads the supplication containing these attributes on the first or second hour of Saturday will obtain his desire regarding enemies. But fear Allah and do not perform this for the unworthy.

**Fulfillment of Needs:**
Whoever has a pressing need must read this blessed supplication for several days after each prayer. Then let him ask for his need. And whoever is distressed about some need of his and wishes it to be fulfilled quickly should perform ablution, go to a mosque, or go to the graves of the saints and righteous, pray two units of prayer, and intend in it the fulfillment of his need. In the first unit, after Surah Al-Fatiha, recite Surah Al-Ikhlas three times and "Bismillahi Rahmanir Rahim" sixty-five times. In the second unit, do the same and add the Mu'awwadhatayn. When finished with the prayer, recite seventy times Istighfar, then recite seventy times blessings upon the noble person of the Prophet ﷺ. Then with utmost love, recite this supplication seven times, concentrating fully while reading it, until his need is fulfilled. By the One in whose hand is my soul, Allah surely hears this supplication. This is the blessed supplication—read it.

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ اِلٰهِيْ اَسْئَلُكَ بِاسْمِكَ الَّذِيْ فَضَّلْتَ بِهٖ عَلَى الْاٰخِرِ وَالْاَوَّلِ بِالتَّجَلِّي الْمُظْهِرِ لِلنَّسَبِ التَّنَزُّلِيِّ وَ الْاِفْتِعَالِيِّ اَمْرًا وَ جُوْدًا لَا بَاطِلًا مَفْعُوْلًا ذٰلِكَ لِمَنْ اَرَدْتَّ بَلْ

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O my God, I ask You by Your Name which You have preferred above the later and the former, through the manifestation revealing the descents of command and generosity, not falsity or nullity—for whom You will. Rather
