# Page 642 (English translation)
The need will be fulfilled, and the beginning of the retreat should be from Friday, and the recitation should begin after the afternoon prayer. The one who recites this Name will remain in safety and peace. Whoever writes it in a silver ring and wears it on his hand, and recites this Name after each prayer according to its numerical equivalent, Allah the Most High will grant him safety through justice and forbearance. And for the person whose name matches the numerical value of this Name, this is the Great Name, for the sake of whatever need he recites it, it will be fulfilled. And when this talisman is written in wax and kept by a person with him, he will remain safe on sea and land. The talisman is this:

Column 2: لا | 2 | 22 | 27

And the dhikr of this Name is this:

Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. O Allah, protect me from the psychical thoughts, and revive my heart with the light of Your sacred knowledge, and protect me from the murky impurities and the soul's base desires, and keep me away from everything hateful, and bestow upon me every elevation, and reveal, O Holy, O Peace, O Guardian, O Keeper, and master for me the forelock of the servant-king Ba'thayayil, and remove the veil between me and him, and fulfill my needs by the right of Your Name, As-Salam.

The person who performs dhikr with this Name on Saturday at the time of dawn, Allah the Most High will raise his rank and keep him protected from every evil.
