# Page 498 (English translation)
Within half a month, before the 14th date. Write each letter separately in the form of a circle around the ring. The spiritual discipline for it is seven days, by which you can subordinate and utilize the Alawi or lower muqill (subordinate spirit).

The days and stars of these letters, with the divine name:

Sunday 1111 - its day is Monday and its star is the Sun, and the figure is hexagonal, and from the Beautiful Names these two names are **Hayyi Qayyum**; the apparent number and the detailed (mufassal) and with the descent. Monday 222 - its day is Tuesday and its star is the Moon, and the figure is triangular; from the divine names these two names are **Rahman Rahim**. Tuesday 333 - its day is Wednesday and its star is Mars, and the figure is hexagonal, and from the divine names these two names are **Malik Quddus**. Wednesday 444 - its day is Thursday and its star is Mercury, and the figure is square, and from the divine names these two names are **Kabir Mutacal**; from it there are many blessings. Thursday 555 - its day is Friday and its star is Jupiter, and the figure is octagonal, and these two names are **Hamid Dhu al-Quwah**. Friday 666 - its day is Saturday, the star is Saturn, the figure is hexagonal, and these two names are **Fattah Razzaq**. Saturday 777 - its day is Sunday, its star is Jupiter, the figure is hexagonal, and these two names are **Fattah Razzaq**. Sunday 888 - its day is Monday, its star is Jupiter, the figure is hexagonal, and these two names are **Qawi Qahhar**.

Purity of clothes and body, and retreat (i'tikaf) for seven days for each occasion is necessary, and the beginning of every discipline should be made from the very day to which that discipline is attributed. During the days of discipline, keep a fast throughout the day and perform the night vigil (qiyam). Each night the figure should be placed before the stars, and in the morning and evening burn incense according to the star. Recite the two names according to the numbers written, and during the day also burn incense four times: in the morning, at midday, after the afternoon, and at midnight. Perform the recitation in this manner for seven consecutive days.

Conditions for the operations:

Know that the operations depend upon all these things. Patience with [unclear] the book, places, and location, and patience with incense and discipline, and adherence to fasting and prayer—this is the greatest condition of all. If even one condition among all these conditions becomes invalid, the operation will be invalid. I state this subject for you with the utmost clarity so that no matter escapes you.

The first rank is Itqan (precision). In this manner: alif '111', ba' '11', jim '1000', dal '101', and the expansion of numbers is like this: 'a' 'b' 'j' 'd' 'r' 'z' 's' 'd' 'n'. The total number is 1111, and when they are combined with the luminous letters, the total numbers will be 12978, and the divine name Qayyum will yield 174 in total. The detailed form: whether alif, or name, or two expanded: m 'a' y 'y' n 'a' l 'f' r 'w' a 'r' b 'h' a 'z' - there are 18 letters. Thus the number of both names is 174, and their detailed number is 216, and their expansion is 4599; the total numbers for each name is 5188, which is the total that results from the rank and names.
