# Page 466 (English translation)
O Avenger, You are the one who has overcome the tyrants and broken the Pharaohs with annihilation and passing away. I ask You by the secrets of the lights of union in the station of compliance that You fulfill my need and protect me from the glance of vengeance, and make me among the people of generosity and beneficence, and place me with You as one who receives the secret of peace. Indeed, You are God, Lord of Majesty and Honor.
The name "Afuw" (The Forgiving) is a great name. Its vicegerent is Sayakhil. It is not based on four quadrants. Under each quadrant there are 158 pages, and on each page there are 158 assigned vicegerents. The prayer of the name "Afuw" is:
O Forgiving, You are the one who has removed from Your beloved ones the multitude, and You are the one who has eliminated from the seekers of Your court the binding troubles and the slackness, and You are the one who have illuminated their insights from the time of bringing forth obedience to You. Praise and thanksgiving belong to You, and generosity and permanence. I ask You, O God, by the magnificence of Your blessings and the flowing of Your pen and the hidden mysteries of the subtleties of Your writing, that You blot me out through Yourself and revive me for You, and do not make me in need of anyone other than You in Your land and Your sea, and that You grant me immediate permanence and profound understanding and beneficial knowledge. Indeed, You are the Mighty, the Wise.
The name "Ra'uf" (The Compassionate) is a great name. In it there is one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. Its vicegerent's name is Sabahil. It is not based on four quadrants. Under each quadrant there are 286 pages, and on each page there are 286 noble angels assigned. The prayer of this great name is:
O Compassionate, You are the one who has bestowed upon Your beloved ones the life of knowledge and worship, and You have granted them the magnificence of all types of good and happiness, and You have admitted them by the viceregency of the glory of happiness and perfected their essences with knowledge and witnessing. I ask You by the subtleties of Your knowledge and the nobility of Your forbearance, that You make me compassionate toward the servants and make me one who turns toward You on the Day of Calling, and do not make me in need of any of Your creation except Your Prophet through solitude, and that You grant me the station and the settling in the most sacred of lands. Indeed, You are God, the Caller to the servants on the Day of Calling.
The name of God the Exalted "Malik al-Mulk" (King of Kingship), Lord of Majesty and Honor, is the Greatest Name. Its vicegerent's name is Ruhia'il. It is not based on four quadrants. Under each quadrant there are 212 pages, and on each page there are 212 angels. The prayer of this name is:
O Master of Kingship, You are the one who has taken the reins of the necks of creation, and You are the one who has brought them into existence from nothingness and extended to them the attachments, and You are the one who have scattered upon them from Your treasuries and Your reckoning sciences whose abundance has been revealed by the path and the realities. To You belongs the penetration of Will and Determination and the Encompassing of what is intended in the worlds of Your blessings, by the light of worship and purity. You are transcendent in Your Essence and noble in Your attributes. I ask You, O God, by Your permanent sovereignty and Your everlasting majesty, that You make me one who executes Your command in the abysses, able to preserve my soul and preserve Your right in the abysses, and victory over the enemies, and strengthen me with the succession of blessings that I may obtain from You the realities of the secrets. Indeed, You are God, the Finder, the Conqueror.
