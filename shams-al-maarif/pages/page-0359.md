# Page 359 (English translation)
I become heedless of myself, and teaches my ignorant one, and clarifies to me my path toward You, and may it be a companion in love and return. Thus by You is my effort, and upon You is my reliance, and to You is my return, and before You is my prostration. You know the reality of my matter, and before You are my secret and my outward self. Exalted are You above attributes of created beings, and You are purified from defects and misfortunes; Your knowledge is free from being opposed by desires. O my God, I ask You for repentance that erases my slip, and accepts my deed, and reforms my outward self, and purifies my inward self, and gathers my family, and sanctifies my secret, and disciplines my discipline, and purifies my soul, and cleanses me from impurity. Grant me a light from You with which I walk among people, for You are indeed the Bestower of lights and the Revealer of secrets, and everything with You is by measure. O Living, O Eternal, O Possessor of majesty and bounty. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, and his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.

O Allah, You accept the repentance of whoever repents, and You draw near whoever turns to You. You are the remover of the veil of darkness. You know treacherous eyes and what hearts conceal within themselves, and You have power over all things. All affairs return to You, and with You evils are repelled. O Allah, I ask You for a secret from Your secrets, a light from Your light, and a spirit from Your command that makes tranquility inherit my destiny. Grant me from You such success that awakens my heedlessness within me, and makes my ignorant one aware, and clarifies my path toward You. May that same spirit be a companion in love and return. My effort is in You, my reliance is upon You, my return is to You, and my prostration is before You. You know the reality of my affair. Before You are my secret and my outward self. Exalted are You above the attributes of created beings, and You are free from imperfections and misfortunes, and Your knowledge is free from being opposed by desires. O Allah, I ask You for such repentance that erases my slips, accepts my deeds, reforms my outward self, purifies my inward self, gathers my family, sanctifies my secret, disciplines my character, purifies my soul, and cleanses me from impurity. Grant me a light from You with which I walk among people, for You are indeed the Bestower of lights and the Revealer of secrets, and everything with You is by measure. O Living, O Eternal, O Possessor of majesty and bounty. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad ﷺ, and his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.

Among the special properties of Ayat al-Kursi is that if any person fears the vengeance of some tyrant and desires deliverance from it, it is binding upon him that after being pure and wearing fine, clean garments, and being in a clean place where he may seclude himself, after the 'Isha' prayer and before Witr, he should perform in that same place two units of prayer. In each unit he should recite Surah al-Fatiha once and Ayat al-Kursi eleven times. After the salutation, he should recite Surah "Inna Anzalna" (i.e., Surah al-Qadr) and Surah al-Ikhlas three times. Then he should recite this prayer three times once:

O my God, the efficacy of Your ancient speech—show me what is perplexing. O Allah, show me on this night of mine all that I have asked about and what I have not asked, and clarify for me the exit from every affair that I fear and that I guard against, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.
