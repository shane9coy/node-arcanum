# Page 452 (English translation)
The Perfect and General Verses grant you your request without compensation or deserving, and You are the Exalted in the loftiness of Your status without limit. I ask You by the glory of Your noble face and the generosity of Your glory to grant me from Your generous giving and to remove Your trial from me, and make me noble in essence, perfect in attributes, good in deeds, abundant in generosity, and raise me to the pinnacle of oneness and unity, and make me in my standing for You with the most complete preparation. Indeed, You are the Most Compassionate, the Merciful.

Attraction, Drawing, and Obtaining Inner Blessings

In the Name Al-Ba'ith (The Resurrector), there are 4 letters of the Greatest Name. By the blessing of this Name, Allah the Exalted will resurrect creation on the Day of Judgment from the graves. It has great power to awaken souls and bodies. Its officer's name is Hubā'īl. Under him are four commanders. Each commander rules over 573 ranks, and in each rank there are 573 appointed officers. The officer bestows two cloaks upon the reciter of this Name. One is outward and the other is inner. By the blessing of the inner cloak, the reciter draws the entire world toward himself like a magnet, and by the outward cloak, the reciter's spirit is honored by visiting elevated stations. The supplication of the Name Al-Ba'ith (The Resurrector) is this:

"O Resurrector, You are the One who resurrects by Your mercy to hearts and breasts, and You are the One who found the spirit of Your fragrances for the order of affairs, and You are the One who corrected the secrets of the hearts of the people of unveiling with the spirit, and You sent Your messengers and prophets by revealing the secret of power and removing Your trials. I ask You, O Allah, by the expansion of Your authority over the necks of Your friends, and by the purity of Your sanctification in the hearts of Your chosen ones, that You make me return to my actions and deeds, covered by Your power in my states, victorious over my affairs, reaching the pinnacle of achievement in my remembrance, fulfilling the functions of my praise and gratitude, coming to You in my secret and my outward, taking my knowledge and my work, and guide me with Your power in the wonder of perfection and the granting of degrees. Indeed, You are Allah, most merciful to servants and the restorer of their gathering to the Abode of Resurrection."

(Chapter 26: The Sixth Method—The Expanses of the Secrets of Divine Names)

Abundance of Provision, Increase in Intellect and Understanding:

Know that among the Beautiful Names, Al-Ghaniyy (The Self-Sufficient), Al-Shakur (The Appreciative), Al-Mughni (The Sufficient), Al-Razzaq (The Provider), Al-Fattah (The Opener), Al-Kafi (The Adequate), Al-Hasib (The Reckoner), Al-Wakil (The Trustee), Al-ATI (The Giver), and Al-Mughith (The Helper)—their special properties are that by reading them, there occurs such blessing as would not even be imagined. There is abundance and ease in provision. One remains in no need of anything. There is great increase in intellect and understanding, and one attains a station of elevation and trust above all stations.

Practice for Removing Stinginess:

Allah the Exalted writes the reciter of "Allah Al-Aliyy Al-Shakur" among the commanders of generosity, and he performs divinely-inspired gratitude. If a stingy person persists in its remembrance, generosity is produced and his habit of stinginess is corrected.
