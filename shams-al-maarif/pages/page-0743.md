In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Guide for every creature; there is no one who attains the knowledge of his need from You except through turning to You and drawing near to Him in his need. We were guided by You, and You guided the worlds of people with the proofs of clear statement along with the creatures, and You guided the sinners to the knowledge of You, and You manifested to them from the subtleties of generosity, and You guided children in their youth to nursing and nature to their development and turning towards benefit. And You are the Guide of bees and every living soul to the rectification of their states and to seclusion. I ask You to grant me of the excellence of divine success that we may perfect guidance by it, and make me among the followers of Your prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.

Whoever recites this supplication, Allah the Most High will grant him guidance, and out of His bounty and generosity, He will purify him and grant him the success of righteous deeds.

# Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Badī'"

Badī' is the Essence which is unique in Its essence, and nothing resembles It in Its attribute, nor is anything equal to It in any of Its decrees. Badī' is the Lord Most High, and whatever is imagined is not the absolute Badī'. Allah the Most High says:

**بَدِیْعُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضِ اَنّٰی یَكُوْنُ لَہٗ وَلَدٌ**

"He is the Creator of the heavens and earth—how could He have a son?"

### There are five types of divine providence and observation in created things, and they have their times:

The first is relating to the Name, which is observing divine creativity in created things with the subtlety of divine decree and exact contemplation, and it has five divisions. The first is the Intellect, and its divine reality is named knowledge, wisdom, and the subtle divine mysteries and hidden secrets, to the extent that its name has become established in its doctrine.

The second is the time of the Spirit, in which one recites the divine Word and reflects on what divine indications Allah the Most High has placed within one's inner self. The third is the time of certainty, meaning spiritual purification and remembrance, drawing oneself away from the name Badī', and the disclosure of divine realities.
