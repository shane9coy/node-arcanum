He should make his need with the hope of turning [to God], until his observation becomes apparent from the visible to the hidden.

### Proven Istikhara: through the blessing of these names, one will obtain the unveiling of self-respect

And among the Divine Names, these 13 names are suitable for it. These names protect the hearts, and their effect becomes apparent in the hearts. The soul gains honor from them, and the hidden is revealed, and the unveiling of the purpose is obtained. Whatever matter one wishes to know, one should recite them at bedtime on the bed, and the meaning will be obtained and become apparent. There is no practice better than this for this purpose. All sorrows and griefs are removed by them. Whoever adopts the remembrance of these, all creation loves him, and the wonders of secrets are unveiled to him, and the darkness of the heart is removed. In short, the properties of all names are contained in these—the luminous names are these:

Huwa Allah alladhi la ilaha illa huwa al-muhaymin al-kamil al-wahid al-wasi al-barr al-sadiq al-nur al-mubin al-mubdi al-nazir al-mufdi al-mujib al-ghayth

### Within these names is the Greatest Name; whoever remembers it will be among the people of unveiling, accepted in prayer, and the world will be subjugated to him

And among the Latāʾif, this Latifa is suitable for it, in which there is that Greatest Name—verily when supplication is made with it, it is accepted, and when question is asked with it, there is favor. The people of unveiling have a special connection to it, and it is the greatest and most noble among the remembrances. When the reciter is constant in this remembrance, the unveiling of all matters will be granted to him, and goodness in both worlds will be his portion, and he will witness the secrets of existence from among the affairs of the world, and the entire world will be subjugated to him. These names are:

Al-Muhaymin al-Qa'im al-Rabb al-Wahid al-Mujib al-Fa''al al-Khallaq al-Khaliq al-Bari al-Musawwir

### Our master the Great Pole became suspended in the air through the recitation of these names

The person who saw Hazrat Wali Kamil Sheikh Abd al-Qadir Jilani, may God be pleased with him, says that he used to recite the remembrance of these names at midnight, and by their blessing his condition was such that sometimes he would become suspended in the air and sometimes he would disappear from sight. And all these things were from those secrets that he was witnessing, and only his sincerity, truthful intention, strength of certainty, intense courage, and correction of state had assisted him.

### He saw Israfel in his original form

Similarly, the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, saw Israfel in his original form in this manner: the foot of the Throne is placed upon his shoulders, and his feet are below even the lowest earth, and the trumpet whose width is equal to a path of five hundred years is within his mouth. And Jibreel had mentioned the form of Israfel to the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, when he appeared to him in his original form.

### The Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, saw Hazrat Jibreel and Israfel in their original forms

And he saw Jibreel in this manner: he has seven hundred wings, and each wing is so large that it covers [the distance] from the west to the east. The Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, himself supplicated to God to see their original forms, but when they appeared, trembling overcame the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, and he lost consciousness. Then Jibreel appeared in that same ordinary form in which he always used to come. When he regained consciousness, he said, "O Jibreel, have you ever before seen your own form?" He replied, "The first time, I wished to see it at your request. Otherwise, even the angels do not possess the power to see this." He said, "Jibreel, how powerful your arms are indeed."
