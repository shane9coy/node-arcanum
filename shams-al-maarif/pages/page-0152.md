Success in Legal Cases and Fulfilling Needs

The composition of this [Arabic: "khara samtan, may he ascend through it the degrees of the highest"] is such that you write the name on the inner part of your right thumb and go to whichever oppressive person; or if you have a case or work, you will be successful. That oppressive person or ruler will show you great respect and will fulfill whatever need you have.

To Silence an Evil Speaker:
Write this talisman on a piece of paper, wrap it in a wax doll, and place it under the tongue, then you will always remain happy. Whoever sees you will love you, and every evil speaker will be silenced.

Victory in Battle, Without Even Getting Wounded

Regarding this [Arabic: "its secret is: may he be at ease, for it has been dyed for our fighting"], these four letters are to be written. Whoever carves this repeatedly on an iron plate and then writes the same on the inner surface of a repeated Kufi (script) amulet—exactly as on the plate—and places it inside a cap on their head and fights the enemy, will be victorious. Even if they throw themselves into fires and swords, still they will remain safe. No wound will touch them, and they will achieve victory over their enemy.

The Practice of Remaining Safe in Frightening Places

Regarding this [Arabic: "the secret of the moon, and in its treasure: for one of the enveloping ones it scattered"], write it on a tablet and perfume it, then recite this verse and blow (on it):
Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and the earth.

Whoever reads this upon it and goes (to a frightening place), will not suffer any harm in a place of fear and darkness, and will be safe from every affliction.

The Desired Person Will Appear

[Arabic: "and by means of it you will bring the individuals"] contains four letters. When these four Arabic letters are extracted—on a night when the moon is in an airy sign and is in conjunction with Mercury—write on a single pomegranate skin and light the pomegranate seed, which in the terminology of the sciences is also called "nahr al-jahaja." Then if you wish to summon someone from a distance of fourteen days' journey, he will appear within one hour. At that time, recite this verse:
Wherever you may be, Allah will bring you all together; indeed, Allah is over all things competent. And it was only one shout, and then behold, they were all assembled before Us as a gathering.

The name of that person will be heard—since he is famous—so you will learn from him that he is coming, or he will give you some news, and he will fulfill your every need.
