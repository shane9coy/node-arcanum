# Page 255 (English translation)
**253**

His presence Sheikh Abu Musa Sirawi used to complete seventy thousand (recitations) day and night because he had a connection with the Names. Likewise, all the great masters obtained a relationship with the Names of Allah the Exalted, such as Abu al-Qasim al-Qushayri, Dhu al-Nun al-Misri, Abu al-Barakat Sayyid Abd al-Qadir al-Jilani, Ahmad ibn Taghrib, Abu al-Hasan al-Harrani, Abu Abdullah Muhammad al-Din ibn al-Sibagh, Abu al-Abbas al-Qisi, and Abu Abdullah al-Kufi, and there are many more whose number is known only to Allah the Exalted. Whoever desires knowledge of this should reflect upon their words, for it becomes clear that man himself is the Greatest Name, because whoever recognized his self has recognized his Lord. Taj Abu al-Hasan al-Shadhili relates: One day I came to the service of my master Sheikh Abd al-Salam ibn Mshish and sat before him. The Sheikh had a small child; I took him into my lap. Then it occurred to my heart that I should ask the Sheikh about the Greatest Name. The child grabbed my chin and said: "Uncle, you yourself are the Greatest Name, or rather, the Greatest Name is within you." The Sheikh said: "The child has given you the answer; understand it."

[Heading: Chapter: Necessary Conditions for Certain Practices]

To obtain the benefits of these sciences, the conditions are: adherence to time, suitability for the practice, kindling incense, and wearing specific clothing. These conditions are for the weak ones who have not reached perfection. It should be understood that whoever adheres to these conditions should designate a specific place for the practice where no other person enters. The place should be so narrow that only that person can sit in it, with no extra space. It should have no window through which light could enter. It should be so far from habitation that no sound reaches it. Whenever one sits, whether on a prayer rug or on a mat, when intention and resolve prevail, then one sleeps. Most of the time one should be engaged in praise and glorification and recitation.

[Heading: The Meanings of Solitude and Its Subtleties]

Someone asked a great master about solitude, and he said: "Negligence makes one heedless of its meanings. Its form is drawn from its purpose. Whoever adopts solitude has chosen the best and highest work." It should be understood that solitude is natural for the people of solitude and is among the signs of the wisdom of union.

The Noble Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Solitude produces knowledge of God; from solitude comes knowledge of hunger of the world; knowledge of Satan comes from solitude; and from waking comes knowledge of the self." The righteous predecessors reached consensus that lifting the veil and the disclosure of the truthful cannot be attained by anyone who has even an atom's weight of food in their stomach. This is the limit of physical purity. There is disagreement about how long a period is required to achieve this. Some say two weeks, but the most famous opinion is that it occurs in forty days. Indeed, Allah the Exalted also commanded Moses, peace be upon him, for forty days so that his stomach would be purified from the heaviness of food, so that the spirituality of the spirit would become strong, the intellect would become clear, the heart would become firm, and the self would become pure. This is the purity of spirits. The masters among the predecessors set the maximum at sixty days, and within those sixty days, the abstention from carnal desires, the subtleties of majesty, and the secrets of the kingdom become revealed. The purity of intellects occurs in seventy days, and this period is prescribed for those who contemplate. From it comes rebirth, which is specialized with the particular lights. No one among the people of spiritual states can pass through this without having the secrets revealed to them and the veil lifted from them. Its name is dying while alive with persistence, and this is the highest degree of qualities in humanity, in which his knowledge and divine inspiration become manifest. It should be understood that the duration for those who practice solitude, if it reaches one year, the enjoyment of humanity continues in the secrets of lordship without being lost. However, at the beginning of the purification of temperament, the servant requires a solitary retreat of forty-one days with effort and struggle. The people of perfection recognize such a person whose interior is more inclined toward the divine lights, due to their subtlety, because it becomes known to them that their interior has become illuminated and luminous with light, and their sleep is like their waking. Some people call this their unveiling.
