Chapter 88: The Properties of the Name "Ghani" (The Self-Sufficient), Acquisition of Wealth, Riches, and Independence

Whoever frequently mentions this Name, such that its state dominates him, Allah the Most High will make him wealthy. For the people of guidance, its mention is recommended. "Ghani" derives from the Names of Bounty (Isma'i al-Tafaddul). Its literal number is 1070 and its digital number is 1080. The literal number is an imperfect even-odd number. Its (121) 128 intermediaries indicate toward the Lord of Majesty. As for the digital number, it is an excess even-odd number. It is (1171) 274 more than Allah the Most High's Name "Mujib" (The Responsive) in its original form. Its square is very beneficial. Its value and the fulfillment of vows are known only to the scholars of the science of letters. This is the image of its talismanic seal.

Chapter 89: The Properties of the Name "Mughni" (The Enricher), Acquisition of Wealth, Riches, and Independence

Whoever frequently mentions this Name, his need will be easily fulfilled. Whoever writes its square and keeps it with him, and mentions it according to its number, and reads Surah Al-Dhuha and recites this supplication: "Allahumma yassir 'alayya al-yusra alladhi yassartahu 'ala kathirin min 'ibadika wa aghnini bi fadlika 'amma man siwaka" — let him persist in this for forty days. Allah the Most High sends to him, in a dream or while awake, a virtuous person who comes with a very great story. I mentioned this Name to a certain friend, who continued mentioning this Name in seclusion for a period of time. Allah the Most High fulfilled their need and a great deal of gold came to their hands. The responsive voice (Hatif) called to them: "Do you want more, or shall we give you more? If this is sufficient, then well and good."

The Proof of Islam (Hujjat al-Islam) [Al-Ghazali] says in the Revival of the Religious Sciences (Ihya'): "Whoever reads this supplication after the Friday prayer becomes wealthy." The supplication is:
"Allahumma ya Ghaniyyu ya Hamid ya Mubdi' ya Mu'id ya Fa''alu limaa yurid ya Rahim ya Wadud Aghnini bi halalika 'an haramika wa bi ta'atika 'an ma'siyatika wa bi fadlika 'amma man siwaka"
Whoever persists in this, Allah the Most High will make him wealthy. Whoever writes it and keeps it with him, profit will come in their trade. Know that through the mysteries of the Names and Lights, the earth is drawn forth, revelations occur, miracles become manifest, and wisdom flows from the heart.
Allah the Most High has said: "And to Allah belong the beautiful Names, so call upon Him by them." The beautiful Names belong to Allah the Most High, so call upon Him by them. And He has said: "Ask Me and I shall accept." The Noble Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "Supplication benefits those who ask, whether the asking has been revealed or not yet revealed." And he said: "Supplication is the weapon of the believer." Then he said: "Whoever is given the opening of supplication is given the opening of acceptance." Then he said: "Ask Allah for supplication. Allah is pleased with it." He said: "Allah does not grow weary until you grow weary."
Its number is 1100, and it conforms to the Mighty Name and the Generous Name, because Allah the Most High is merciful. Its letter numbers are 8307, indicating toward Allah the Most High's two great Names: Jabbar (The Compeller) and Shakur (The Appreciative).
