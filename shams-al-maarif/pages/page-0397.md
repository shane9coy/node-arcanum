# Page 397 (English translation)
![Manuscript page 325](../source-text/.shams-al-maarif-pages/shams-al-maarif-Pages-325.jpg)

[Page 325]

O Great Being, You are pure. The Lord of angels and spirits. The illuminator of darknesses. The one who splits grains and extracts them from husks. I seek refuge in You from the evil of darkness when it is dominant, and from the evil of the envier when he envies. I pray to You like a broken-hearted captive who knows that You hear and who knows that You accept. I am in distress; I find no one besides You to accomplish [matters]. I ask You by this Name with which You made charity flow and sent down blessings. O One who emerged from darknesses, and with whom You lost armies and volitions. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon the noble essence of Muhammad ﷺ, and to cast upon me the cloak of Your lights, which removes the gazes of oppressors and enviers, even their hands [shall be] crippled. Make of Your essence a portion for me that illuminates blessings for me, and open every road for me. O light of every thing and guidance, You are the One who created darknesses from Your light; all lights are from Your light. O Opener of every difficulty, all affairs return to You and originate from Your essence. O Living, O Sustaining One, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful, I ask You for help; I seek refuge in Your punishment. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from their evil. If We willed, We would send down a sign from the heaven. O One who sends down clouds, O One who defeats armies, defeat all my enemies and their armies and their gathering; grant me victory over them. O Allah, by Your grace show me Your sanctuary and grant me arrival for visitation to the blessed grave of Your Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Make Your creation subservient to me; soften their hearts for me. O Allah, make every difficulty easy for me and turn every bitterness into honey. O Allah, help my absent one; make him visible to me, and grant me the lawful, pure, blessed sustenance from Your Surah Yasin and the Wise Quran, O Lord of the worlds.

"And I entrust my affair to Allah" to the end, 7 times, and the noble ṣalawāt ten times.

"And separate yourselves today, O criminals" to "the straight path" [continues]. "O Allah, I ask You, O God of the first and the last, O Reliever, relieve, O Allah, unite my mind and relieve my distress, and give me from Your treasures, and make all Your creation subservient to me, and ease every hardship for me, and I entrust my affair to Allah; surely Allah sees the servants" 7 times, and the noble ṣalawāt ten times. "And certainly many of you We led astray from among the people; then will you not understand" [continues].

"And I entrust my affair" 7 times, the noble ṣalawāt ten times. "And whoever We grant寿命 to, We reverse him in creation; will you not reason?" "And so the word may come true upon the disbelievers" [continues]. "O Allah, I ask You, O God of the first and the last, I ask You to make all Your creation subservient to me with love and affection, to grant me lawful, good sustenance, to ease every hardship for me, and to make the difficult easy for me, and I entrust my affair to Allah" to the end, 7 times, and the noble ṣalawāt ten times. Then [the verse beginning] "so that he may warn" to "the living" [continues]. "We revive" [to the word] "the eye" [continues]. "O Allah, I ask You, O God of the first and the last, to make my sustenance subservient to me, and to ease every hardship for me, and I entrust my affair to Allah" seven times, and the noble ṣalawāt ten times. Then [the verse beginning] "he has set forth for us an example and forgotten his creation" to the end. "So glory be to Him in whose hand is the sovereignty of all things, and to Him you shall be returned" [continues]. Then [the verse beginning] "In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful" [continues]. "O Allah, I ask You by the right of Surah Yasin and the Wise Quran" 7 times. After this, read this:

"By Your signs which You sent forth and Your guides to the straight path; You do not postpone him who is not an oppressor against the oppressors, O Destroyer of the oppressors, and all of them shall be present, O One who gives life to bones while they are dust. What they sent before and their traces, and every thing We have enumerated in a clear register. O One who gives life to the earth after its death, and brings forth from it grain, from it" [unclear] "they eat."
