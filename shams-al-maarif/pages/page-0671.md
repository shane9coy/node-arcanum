# Page 671 (English translation)
Within every letter there were nine thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine sciences, within every science there were 28 sciences, those were given to Adam:

Thus within each of these letters there were nine thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine sciences, and within every science there were 28 sciences. Allah the Most High made Adam acquainted with all of them, and after him, his successors were granted those sciences which were for the people of the sacred precinct.

On the saint, Paradise and Hell and the Preserved Tablet are revealed:

After the scholars of wisdom, the people of wilayah are awakened. The lowest rank of the saint is that everything from the Throne to the earth is revealed to him, and Paradise and Hell and the Preserved Tablet are all before him, and he knows the reality of all.

Note: The hierarchical ranks of the masters of saints:

Allah the Most High appointed seven successors and placed them in all seven realms. Thus the help of the generous comes from them, and each one of them assists the other, except that the Ghauth takes help from the Elevated Throne. Thus his grace is Alawite, and he is the one who possesses the state of the Theophany of manifestation, and for this reason the world takes its sustenance from him. The Ghauth gives help to four, and these four to seven, and the seven to forty, and the forty to seventy, and the seventy to three hundred and sixty.

Note: From "four" are meant your temperaments, and from "seven" your heart, and from "forty" your forty years of maturity, and from "seventy" the end of your life, and from "three hundred and sixty" your limbs are meant.

The ranks of the states of creation and the emission of grace upon the human heart

The states of creation are these: the first is the natural state, the second is the state of celestial composition, and the third is the rational-soul state upon which humanity becomes knowing, and the fourth is the Perfect Human, the fifth is tawhid, the sixth is opening, the seventh is address. Woe to this—that within each of these ranks there are six states, by the assistance of Allah the Most High. Thus the leaders of the lights of speech flow within it, but the Pole of the world has understood this, and he made the lights of life flow upon the soul and achieved pleasure with the Pole of action. Thus the first rank was existence, and the total number was from his name, so he made it flow upon his servant with the lights. For this reason, humanity has been distinguished with the understanding of the types of creation and the unveiling of numbered decrees and the states of the world, and with the secrets of benefit and harm in both worlds and the shared secrets of the two barzakh in the gathering of this grace upon the human heart. Thus the origin of unveiling and the secret of acceptance become the testimony of the knower, with the lights of the first speech. Thus he understands by it and takes guidance by it.

The heart of Qawsain and revelation occurred from it—the origins of life are four, the four are the origins of all names

And it was this heart of Qawsain, the nearer, and revelation of truth was with it, and the condition of its presence with him was "So he revealed to His servant what He revealed." The origins of life are four, and the four are origins, and the origin of all names is the same, and the celestial sphere stands by the very names themselves. Regarding seventy, this hadith has been transmitted.

The Messenger ﷺ said that the lifespans of my community are between sixty and seventy.

And to recite this name 84 times: one multiplies its number by it according to its count and recites it in accordance with day and night. When one makes it one's method in the remembrance of Allah, the lights are obtained, and if one makes it one's regular litany, Allah the Most High will enrich him with boundless wealth, and Allah the Most High will raise his rank in all circumstances.
