Chapter Number: Fortunate and Unfortunate Hours

It concerns the explanation of fortunate and unfortunate times and hours, and which are suitable for good and evil.

Sunday:
The first hour belongs to the Sun. It is good for lovers, for going to rulers, kings, and officials, and for wearing new clothes. The second hour belongs to Venus. It is very bad. Nothing should be done in it. The third hour belongs to Mercury; it is useful for travel, reconciliation, love, and talismans of acceptance. The fourth hour belongs to the Moon. Commerce should not take place in it, nor is it good for anything. The fifth hour belongs to Saturn. In it should be performed actions of separation, enmity, and hostility, etc. The sixth hour belongs to Jupiter. It is good for requesting needs from kings and officials. The seventh hour belongs to Mars; nothing should be done in it. The eighth hour belongs to the Sun. Important works should be performed in it because it is the morning for important matters and very fortunate. The ninth hour belongs to Venus; actions of love and attracting hearts are correct in it. The tenth hour belongs to Mercury; do whatever you wish in it, it is a good hour. The eleventh hour belongs to the Moon; talismans and designs, etc. should be performed in it because it is a good hour. The twelfth hour belongs to Saturn. Nothing should be done in it because it is unfortunate; nothing should be done in it except actions of causing harm.

Monday:
The first hour belongs to the Moon. It is good for love, silencing tongues, and attracting hearts. The second hour belongs to Saturn. It is good for travel and important needs. The third hour belongs to Jupiter; it is good for marriage, supplication, and obtaining verdicts. The fourth hour belongs to Mars; it is good for meeting any ruler, for catching thieves. The fifth hour belongs to the Sun. It is good for fulfilling needs, silencing tongues, and attracting hearts. The sixth hour belongs to Venus; it is a good hour for the works of talismans. The seventh hour belongs to Mercury; it is for fulfilling needs, silencing tongues, and attracting hearts. The eighth hour belongs to the Moon; it is for marriage and reconciling two enemies [text incomplete/damaged].
