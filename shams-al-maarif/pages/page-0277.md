# Page 277 (English translation)
Chapter 24: The Properties of the Name "Rafi" — "Increase in Rank and Dignity"

By frequently remembering this [name], conquests are achieved and status is elevated. If the one who remembers it is from the people of spiritual practice, a state (ḥāl) will manifest in his movements and stillness. Its numerical value is 1251, and it is an incomplete rectangular compound. Its components point toward the name of Allah the Most High, Al-Muʿtī (The Giver). The letter-divisions of its letters are 45, and they point toward two names of Allah the Most High: Mālik al-Mulk (Lord of Dominion) and Qarīb (The Near), and this is the form of its square:

Chapter 25: The Properties of the Name "Muʿizz" — "Attaining Honor and Fame"

Whoever is utterly humiliated and persists in the remembrance of this name shall receive honor and become generous and distinguished. The remembrance of this [name] is best for strengthening resolve. If one writes it in a square and keeps it with oneself, he will be worthy of respect among people, and every tyrant will begin trembling before him. This name is among the greatest remembrances of the believers. Its numerical value is 124; it is the Spirit Without (rūḥ al-khārij) and an incomplete odd [number]. Its components point toward a letter in the abjad system which is ten — it indicates strength and extension with encompassment, and it is the alif (ا). Allah the Most High pointed toward the great divine names Mālik and Malak (King), because He alone holds power over all things; He is their Owner, and Malak (the King) is He Who gives respite to His friends. The letter-divisions of its letters are 338, and they point toward two great names of Allah the Most High: Allāh and Rabb (Lord). Its square is as follows:
