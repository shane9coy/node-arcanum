# Page 231 (English translation)
[Heading in decorative box: Benefit of the Opening of Sustenance]

After each prayer of every age, recite Surah al-Qālaq fourteen (14) times, and recite the Beautiful Names of God fourteen (14) times, then recite this supplication. Very blessed is whoever persists upon it; the doors of sustenance will be opened for him, and Allah the Most High will give him sustenance from places he could never have imagined. The supplication is this:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by the greatness of Your noble, ancient, treasured Names, by the various kinds and types of the writings of Your lights, and by the glory of the honoring of Your majesty, and by the strength of the might of Your severe power, and by the measure of the ability of Your might, and by the support of the praising and glorifying of Your greatness, and by the exaltedness of the elevation of Your highness, and by the perpetuity of the eternity of the permanence of Your eternity, and by the pleasure of the safety of the grace of Your forgiveness, and by the loftiness of the wonderful, protected dominion of Your sovereignty, and by the prayers of the charities of the spread of Your mercy, and by the shining brightness of the lightning flashes of the thunder of the clamor and tumult of Your majesty, and by the overwhelming might of the blessed, unique Oneness of Your deity and for the surging of the waves of Your ocean surrounding Your divine sovereignty, and by the vastness of the expanse of the arena of the appearance of Your throne, and by the highest heavenly spiritualities upon the glory of Your throne, and by the angels of the spiritual realm who manage the stars of the heavens, and by the longing of the tranquility of the aspirant for Your forgiveness, and by the movements of the sighs and fears of those frightened by Your violent might, and by the descent of the strivers in Your pleasure, and by the glorifying and praising of the worshippers at Your gate—O the First, O the Last, O the Manifest, O the Hidden, O the Standing One. May he be erased by an erasing [with a talisman].

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. By the protection of Mihra, turn away the hearts of our enemies and Your enemies, and crush the heads of the oppressors with the swords of the emergence of the might of Your violence, and shield us with Your impenetrable veils from the glances and flashes of their piercing eyes, by Your power and might. Pour upon us Your pleasure from the pipes and channels of success until the times of night and the ends of day. Immerse us in the completeness of Your grace and mercy, and bind us with the bonds of safety from falling into Your disobedience. O the First, O the Last, O the Manifest, O the Hidden, O the Ancient, O the Standing One, O the Forbearing, O the Knowing. O Allah, the intellects have been bewildered, the imaginings have retreated, the understandings have been lost, the doubts have been confused, the thoughts have failed, and the concerns have fallen short of comprehending how the wonders of Your power have manifested, beyond the reach of the glittering lights of obedience.

O Allah, the Mover of movements and the Initiator of purposes, You split apart the hardest of firm rocks, and from them You cause to flow a helping water for the creatures, and You give life to all animals, plants, and the world, and You know what stirs in their secrets—the speech of signs, the hidden languages of the swimming ants—and those who are great and distant and sanctified and praised and glorified by the majesty of Your glory, the angels of Your seven heavens. Make us among those who, in this blessed hour, called upon You and You answered, who asked You and You gave, who entreated You and You had mercy, and bring us near to what obedience lies before You by Your grace and ancient beneficence.
