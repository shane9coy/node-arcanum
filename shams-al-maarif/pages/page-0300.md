[Heading in decorative box: Chapter 65: Properties of the Name Wājid—fulfilling desires and obtaining the knowledge of gnosis]

Whoever frequently remembers this glorious Name, whatever they seek shall certainly be found. The spiritual travelers also recognize their souls through its blessing. Whoever mentions this Name so much that his state dominates him, then he will find within himself a state that cannot be obtained through the sciences and knowledges. This Name is suitable for him whose name is 'Abd al-Wājid (Servant of the Wājid). Its number is 14. This is an odd-even rectangle because it has nobility. For this reason, it is composed by multiplying the first perfect number by the first even number. Thus it is seven times seven, the number 49. This is the number of the luminous letters and also the number of the bright nights, because these nights are nights of finding (wajd), and dark nights are nights of loss. This number is deficient. Its components are 1, 2, 4, 7, pointing to the letter Yā that is the station in the divine utterance "He hears me, He sees me." For this reason, the names of its letters point to His utterance of connection. This is the talismanic form of the Name Wājid:

[Heading in decorative box: Chapter 66: Properties of the Name Mājid—expansion of rule, gaining the love of people]

When a king mentions this Name, his kingdom expands, his word is obeyed, and all his subjects love him. This is suitable for him whose name is 'Abd al-Mājid (Servant of the Glorious). Its number is 48, for it is derived by multiplying the number of the Name BISM by the smallest number, then multiplying the sum by the smallest number. This number points to the Mīm that the Noble Prophet ﷺ designated as the sign on the day of the Battle of Uhud, by which is meant the collectivity of the kingdom, its expansion, and its permanence. This number is excessive (abundant), and among the odd numbers its corresponding number is 3. Its components point to the names of attachment, for whoever has an expansive kingdom, every seeker's desire will be attached to him. It points to 'Alī with Al-Raḥīm. Its form is this:

[Heading in decorative box: Chapter 67: Properties of the Name Wāḥid—inclination to solitude, obtaining dignity, majesty, and grandeur]

Whoever frequently repeats this Name, his heart becomes free from all besides God. There is inclination to solitude and aversion to public gatherings. It has great power for obtaining honor or position.
