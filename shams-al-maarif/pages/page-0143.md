# Page 143 (English translation)
God the Most High has said in His Book:
"Say: It is guidance and healing for those who believe."
"You say: It is guidance and healing for those who have brought faith."
Its meaning is that in the world of dominion it is guidance, and in the world of the barzakh it is the Helper Ahmad. These call upon the traditions that bring punishment. This is due to their deafness, and in all circumstances they pursue the disclosure and analysis of the essences of punishment along with the genera. Through it, the picturing intellectual faculty is taken away, and the other formal aspects of punishment become connected. It is the punishment of the tribe of Jārith. It relates to the divine throne; it is the first punishment of the stations of numbers through the operation of the delicate letters. It is the punishment of the spirit that has been constructed with philosophical depth. This is because the divine discourse has already come regarding it. Abandoning divine thought and the cause of purification is severe punishment. Because of speech, discourse is prior; in the same way, thought and the understanding of absolute purification and the completions are prior without them, and it is the fire that burns with intensity to set ablaze. As for the inferior letters, they merely cut off the flames, and they desire to remain hungry, and if they become naked due to darkness and fear, they put on clothing, until its firm insistence takes on complete form in the decree of existence, which severs the righteous ones from success in the world: "And for them is a painful punishment." Then the veil is lifted from the eye of human insight, and for him the mysteries of the school of letters become manifest. The reality of the hidden numbers and the inherent nature are also revealed—meaning that it is specifically connected with the عرض of noble prophetic grace, not in such a way that if we intend the barzakhi world, otherwise darkness follows it and punishment cuts it off. By nature, its direction has been accepted by us in common, and this that we have stated, that we are established with the subtle and the dense—understand the reality of this secret. This is true: whoever recognizes his self has recognized his Lord, and nothing is nobler than divine knowledge; whoever understands the secret of this delicate matter has also understood its relation to the self of the subtle and the dense.

Understand this secret: it is connected with the knowledge of the self, and this is the secret of the spiritual exercises that lead to it completely. After this, divine grace and the divine ray will be spread out for you, which will extract you from the imaginary composite portion and bring you into the circle of the Creator of forms. Then this veil will be lifted, and the vast field will become manifest for you. You will progress to the Lote Tree of the Extreme Limit, and you will descend upon entering Paradise of Ma'wā. I have revealed for you the hidden secret of God the Most High for His pleasure, and to it He has guided me. Then you will see the symbol with the eye of insight among the profits of life. I hope that in the Highest Assembly he will be my companion and in the Garden of the Garden of Intimacy he will be my associate.

God the Most High has said:
"Help one another in righteousness and piety."
And as the noble proclamation of the Prophet, upon him be peace, states:
"The faith of a person is not complete until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself."

Know that every goodness is the benefit of the results of good of goods, and its fruit is very sweet—it has become near to the spirit. This is in accordance with the pure proclamation of the Prophet, upon him be peace:
"Whoever establishes a good tradition, he has its reward and the reward of whoever acts upon it until the Day of Resurrection."
