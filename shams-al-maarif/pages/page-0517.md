# Page 517 (English translation)
**The Prophet's (peace be upon him) Saying Regarding the Unseen (Ghayb):**

The Commander of the Faithful, Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) said: "What is the supreme head?" And this is the Unseen (ghayb), by the knowledge of which things are governed and made subservient.

And some say this is it; rather, what Allah has granted to His creation. And the intention by this is the Unseen [corresponding to the number] one hundred sixty (460). And some say: "I believe in the Unseen" is a Quranic verse. *Am 'indahumul-ghaybu yaktubun*— "Or do they possess the Unseen which they write down?"

**He Who Understands Divine Mysteries Speaks in Wonderful Secrets and Enigmas:**

That is, they take help from it according to what Allah wills; it also guides us regarding its Unseen. So glory belongs to that Exalted Essence. When the contemplative one sees these mysteries, he will find wonderful and strange things, and he will narrate wonderful reports. The manifestation of this will occur among the philosophers. So understand these mysteries, for I have sometimes placed the subject first and sometimes last, sometimes near and sometimes far, sometimes expressed it in riddle and sometimes openly, and some things I have not mentioned at all.
