# Page 716 (English translation)
714

By this name, the sick person is healed:
For warding off diseases, write both of these on two tablets, wash them with honey and water, and give to the patient to drink. He will obtain healing by divine command. And if both names are engraved on a silver ring and worn, the tongues of enemies will be silenced and their hearts will be softened, and all desires will be fulfilled for him.

The station of the reciter of this name is among the individuals:
The reciter of this name enters among the individuals. Each one of them has a separate solitary retreat for the name. The name should be recited according to its numerical value in accordance with the conditions of spiritual discipline, and Jibr'il (Gabriel) will be present as the assigned angel. He is among those subordinate to 'Izra'il.
When you look upon an enemy with a gaze of wrath, he will be destroyed instantly, and from the solitary retreat of the name of al-Muqtadir, knowledge of the realities of things and their true nature is obtained.

The mysteries of destiny will be revealed to the reciter:
His assigned angel is Miyka'il. He is among those subordinate to Mika'il (Michael). He will appear to the reciter in a dream or while awake, and the mysteries of destiny will be revealed to the reciter.

The reciter knows the fortunate and the wretched well; every intention of his will be fulfilled:
Whoever comes before him will, upon seeing him, immediately determine whether this person is wretched or fortunate. And when he desires anything, that thing will be obtained immediately. The matters of the afterlife will also be revealed. The form of both talismans is as follows:

Al-Qadir:

Al-Muqatadir:
