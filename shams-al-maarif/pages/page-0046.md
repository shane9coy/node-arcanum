10. Jabhah:
The tenth mansion is Jabhah. Its letter is (y). When the Moon descends into it, spirituality of an intermediate degree between good and evil is produced, and it is useful for such actions.

11. Zabarah:
The eleventh mansion is Zabarah. Its letter is (k). When the Moon descends into it, spirituality of sustenance and seeking needs is produced. Such actions are suitable for it.

12. Sarfah:
The twelfth mansion is Sarfah. Its letter is (l). When the Moon comes into it, intermediate-degree spirituality is produced. Such actions are suitable for it.

13. 'Awwā':
The thirteenth mansion is 'Awwā'. Its letter is (m). When the Moon descends into it, nothing should be done except river travel, because mixed spirituality is produced from it.

14. Simāk:
The fourteenth mansion is Simāk. Its letter is (n). When the Moon descends into it, spirituality that does not assist good actions is produced; therefore, no good matter should be done in it.

15. Ghafar:
The fifteenth mansion is Ghafar. Its letter is (s). When the Moon descends into it, very good spirituality is produced; therefore, all religious and worldly actions should be performed in it.

16. Zabanā:
The sixteenth mansion is Zabanā. Its letter is (ʿ). When the Moon descends into it, mixed spirituality is produced.

17. Iklīl:
The seventeenth mansion is Iklīl. Its letter is (f). When the Moon descends into it, spirituality that is very beautiful for religious works is produced, and it is useful for good and worldly works.

18. Qalb:
