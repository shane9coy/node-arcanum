## 95: On the Properties of the Name "Badi'" (The Incomparable) — The Attainment of Manifestation of Sciences and Wisdom

The remembrance of this great Name is appropriate for one who intends to demonstrate any craft. Whoever engages in the remembrance of this Name shall continually progress in the divine sciences; knowledge flows from his heart to his tongue. Whoever engages in the remembrance of this Name shall continually progress in the divine sciences; knowledge flows from his heart to his tongue. Whoever wishes to become acquainted with any science through the remembrance of this Name—[I had not understood wisdom before.] When I maintained continuity in the remembrance of this Name for a period of time, God Almighty caused wisdom to flow from my heart to my tongue, and those statements began to come upon my tongue which I could neither understand nor know beforehand. Its number is 86; by multiplication from the people of number into the operations of number, the odd numbers are [derived]. Take heed for understanding this strange [and wondrous] one: this number is deficient. Its divisors are 46. It is "the elevated courage." This chapter points toward the province of numerical works. The names of its letters are 181, pointing toward the Name of God Almighty "Al-'Alim" (The Knowing), because creation does not occur without knowledge. Its great and noble square is very beneficial. ## 96: On the Properties of the Name "Baqi" (The Everlasting) — The Preservation of Memory and the Protection of Perishable Things

This great divine Name is proven effective for the preservation of memory. Whatever thing is feared to decay, when it is written during the ascendant of the fixed stars, that thing shall not decay. Whoever engages in the remembrance of this Name shall have no illness throughout his lifespan. Every monarch who engages in the remembrance of this Name, his kingdom shall remain established forever and shall be preserved from calamities. Its number is 113, the people of number, which point toward Oneness and the Cognomen. The names of its letters are 158, pointing toward the Name of God Almighty "Ar-Razzaq" (The Provider), and since Ar-Razzaq is everlasting, there is no worry or toil regarding sustenance. This is its square:
