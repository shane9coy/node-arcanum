It is. Fatimah the first is the praised one, and Fatimah the second has a light upon every heart—this is the station of these secrets.

**Categories of Sacred Spirits:**

The sacred spirits are also divided into three categories. The first is certainty, which is achieved through contemplation of the world of absorption and unification with the luminous realities, and through knowledge is connected to the worshipful spirits of the saints. They are created from the supernal forms and their acceptance, until the intellect connects to the intellect.

**Categories of Sanctification of Souls:**

The sanctification of souls is also divided into three categories. The first: its proof is upon the original foundation, and accepting this secret which has been appointed for it—this is achieved by removing the passions through ascetic practice. The second category: awareness of those essences which God Almighty has deposited in the Preserved Tablet, for such is the nature of the human world, because of the secrets of the realities which God Almighty has placed within it. This rank is obtained through the study of divine sciences, their remembrance, the illumination of their symbols, and the verification of the people of knowledge. The third category is its transformation—this refers to the verification of the first direction with light, but in the direction of the intellect. And this is the third degree, and its method is to completely disconnect from the lower world.

**Categories of Sanctification of the Heart:**

The sanctification of holy hearts also has three categories. One is the sanctification of faith from the darkness of polytheism, and the sanctification of God from hypocrisy. The second is with sincerity in religious matters. Therefore, the sanctification of faith is to consider oneself destitute before the True Lord, and this station is obtained through divine support.

**The Specific Method of Sanctification of Actions:**

Then the sanctification of actions is that you make the Truth your qiblah and do not imitate anyone besides Him; rather, gaze toward all realities. The third category is subsistence with God [unclear: mt]—with every breath, meaning to be occupied in divine remembrance.

**Renouncing the World and Its Pleasures:**

Do not love worldly wealth, for whoever has even an atom's worth of love for the world in their heart, God Almighty will make the obtaining of His sweetness forbidden for them, because this is the claim of the insincere ones, who do not possess this capability. And regarding such people, God Almighty says: **"They love to be praised for what they have not done."**

**Categories of Sanctification of the Body:**

The sanctification of the holy body also has three categories. The first sanctification is to seek lawful sustenance—this is obtained through trust in God and entrusting all affairs to Him. The second sanctification is the purity of the body with humility, until all grossness of the body is removed and only the subtle portion remains. This is obtained through divine remembrance, seclusion, and silence. The third category is the continuity of bodily sanctification and remaining in worship day and night, and taking upon oneself vigilance and service. This is the extreme limit of piety and God-consciousness.

**The Descent of the Lights of Holiness upon the Rememberer:**
