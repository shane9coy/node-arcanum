# Page 777 (English translation)
775

Conclusion

[Brief mention of the guide, teachers, and chains of transmission]

May God deliver you from the state of misguidance and bring you to the station of the gnostics. For among the scholars of the Sufi path and the masters of divine reality, it is correctly established and continuously transmitted that His Eminence the Commander of the Faithful, Imam of the God-fearing, Asad Allah al-Ghalib, Ali ibn Abi Talib—may God honor his face—received the entire caliphate through spiritual instruction from His Eminence the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace. I received this verbal transmission from Imam Ali ibn Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ya'qub al-Kulayni, may God have mercy upon him. He received it from the late master, the master of the masters, and he received it from the divine pole, Sheikh Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Abi al-Hasan Ali ibn Haram. He received it from the master of the path, the mine of verification, Ali Muhammad Salih ibn Aqil al-Hashimi. He received it from the proof of the age, unique in his time, Sheikh Abu Madyan Shu'ayb ibn Hasan al-Andalusi. He received it from Abu Shu'ayb Ayyub Sa'id al-Santaji. He received it from the master of the masters of gnosis, the pole of divine assistance, the unique gathering one, Abu Maysir al-Maghribi. He received it from Abu Muhammad ibn Mansur. He received it from Abu Muhammad ibn Abd al-Jalil ibn Makhlayan. He received it from Abu al-Fadl Abd Allah ibn Abi al-Bashr. He received it from his father, His Eminence Imam Musa al-Kazim, peace be upon him. He received it from his great father, the master of the household of the Prophet, His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, peace be upon him. He received it from his father, His Eminence Imam Muhammad al-Baqir, peace be upon him. He received it from his great father, His Eminence Imam Zayn al-Abidin, peace be upon him. He received it from his father, His Eminence Imam Husayn, the beloved son of Batul. From His Eminence the master of the free ones, Imam Husayn, peace be upon him. He received it from his father, the gate of knowledge, His Eminence the Commander of the Faithful, Ali, peace be upon him. He received it from His Eminence the lord of creation, the seal of the prophets, His Eminence Muhammad ibn Abd Allah, the messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace. Likewise, His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, peace be upon him, also obtained the knowledge of the inner sciences from Qasim ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Bakr al-Siddiq, may God be pleased with him, and he obtained it from the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace.

The chain of transmission for the science of letters reaches my teacher through this path: from Abu al-Hasan al-Basri, the Imam and master. From him, the great divine master Habib al-Ajami. From him, Sheikh Daws al-Ta'i. From him, Sheikh Ma'ruf al-Karkhi. From him, Sheikh Sari al-Saqati. From him, Sheikh of the moment and the path, the mine of spiritual practice and reality, His Eminence Sheikh Nujm al-Ghazali, may God's mercy be upon him. From Sheikh Abu al-Najib al-Suhrawardi. And he transmitted it to the pole of the path, the master of gnosis, Isma'il al-Din al-Shirazi. He transmitted it to Sheikh Abu Abd Allah al-Dulbaqi. He transmitted it to Sheikh Qasim al-Sarbuti. He transmitted it to Sheikh Sarbuti. He transmitted it to the Imam of gnosis, the truthful one, the luminous Imam Jalal al-Din Abd Allah al-Bastami. He transmitted it to my teacher, His Eminence the master of the masters of gnosis, Sirfatani, and to Sheikh Abu Abd Allah al-Shams al-Din al-Asqalani.

The chain of transmission for the science of magical squares is to me from Sheikh Imam, the master of gnosis through God, Abu Abd Allah Muhammad ibn Ali, may God sanctify his spirit. I also obtained it from Sheikh Imam, the scholar Siraj al-Din al-Hanafi. And he obtained it from Sheikh al-Shahab al-Din al-Maqdisi. He obtained it from Sheikh al-Shams al-Din al-Farisi. He obtained it from Sheikh al-Shahab al-Din al-Hamdani. He obtained it from Sheikh Qutb al-Din al-Qayshani. He obtained it from His Eminence the master of the masters, Taqi al-Din ibn al-Khatibi. He obtained it from Sheikh Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Fakhr al-Turuni. He obtained it from Sheikh Abu Abd Allah al-Qarashi. He obtained it from His Eminence Sheikh Abu Madyan al-Andalusi. I also have this chain of transmission from Sheikh Abu Madyan ibn Ja'far al-Shafi'i. He obtained it from Sheikh Muhammad ibn Sari. He obtained it from Sheikh al-Shahab al-Din al-Hamdani. He obtained it from Sheikh Qutb al-Din. He obtained it from His Eminence Ali ibn Abi Talib, may God be pleased with him.

The chain of transmission for the science of divine names and numbers is to me from Sheikh Imam Tahir ibn Askar al-Khwarazmi, upon him be mercy and blessings, Abu Hurayra al-Hamizi al-Qarashi. He obtained it from Sheikh al-Shahab al-Din Ahmad al-Shati. He obtained the science from Nur al-Din. He obtained it from Ibn Abbas, may God be pleased with him.
