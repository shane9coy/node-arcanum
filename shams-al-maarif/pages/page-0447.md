# Page 447 (English translation)
The Most High,扩大知识的主，祢以恩典和容忍向您的朋友们展示荣耀，宇宙之主啊

The Operation of Obtaining Leadership and Government:
The Great Name is very beneficial for seeking leadership and government. Its deputy is named Al-Fi'ail, under whom there are four officers, each of whom oversees 232 ranks, and in each rank there are 232 deputies. The prayer of the Great Name is:
"O Great One, You are He who manifested Your greatness in the hearts of the people of monotheism, and You spread Your majesty with Your knowledge in the minds of the people of abstraction and isolation in You; through You every majesty in the worlds appeared, and to You returns the end of every human being. I ask You, O Allah, by Your encompassing knowledge among Your creation, and by Your effective power in Your creatures on land and sea, that You make me great through knowledge and recognition of the secrets of Your generosity and Your compassion in all times. Grant me a comprehensive opening, a shining light, and a hearing ear, so that I hear only from You, speak only through You, and dwell only in You—for You are the One who exists in every place and is worshipped in every language, in every place and time."

Protection of Life and Property in Land and Sea Travel
In the Name of Allah, the Protector, there are complete properties for containers and protection and safety in travel, and the like. Its deputy's name is Fara'il, under whom there are four officers, each of whom oversees 998 ranks, and in each rank there are 998 deputies. The one who mentions this Name, its deputy clothes him with the person through whose blessing protection of life is achieved in land and sea travel. The prayer of the Name Al-Hafiz is:
"O Protector, You are He who protected every existing thing with Your complete power, and You are He who revived the souls of the prophets and saints in the state of bowing and prostrating, and You are He who gathered the secret of the righteous and the good in the glorifications of Your Face in the station of the praised, and You protected the heavens and the earth and what is in them with Your divine power, and You realized the innermost secrets of the heavenly realms with Your eternal knowledge. I ask You by You in the station of servitude that You grant me moderation between opposites, and establish me upon the best of alignment between equals, and protect my limbs and my mind from the assault of Your wrath at the descent of negligence, and preserve me from squandering Your words and from slipping away from confronting You and meeting You on the day of spreading the good deeds, and grant me generous nobility encompassing the secrets of the Names and Attributes, truly You are Allah, the Knower of the hidden things, and the Giver of goodness to the people of noble qualities."

The Operation of Establishing Constant Blessing:
The Name Al-Muqit is a great Name. It contains one of the letters of the Greatest Name among its letters. Its inner blessing flows in all creatures upon the earth, through which blessing the entire system of the world never deteriorates. The power of sustenance that is present in this Name is beyond comprehension [unclear], its deputy's name is [unclear] who is the overseer of the realm of spirits, of whom each officer oversees 550 ranks, and in each rank 550 deputies are assigned. Through the remembrance of this pure Name, there is an inner virtue through whose blessing, whenever the reciter places his hand upon any edible thing and reads this verse: "Indeed this is our provision, there is no end to it," it shall never be exhausted but rather there is blessing [damaged source]
