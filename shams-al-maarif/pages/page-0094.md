# Page 94 (English translation)
Write them and tie them on [the person], then by the command of God the Almighty, those two will become friends with each other. Or if you desire the love of a certain person, write [them] and tie them on him, then he will love you intensely. This operation should be performed on Sunday during the hour of the Sun or Mercury, and fumigate with agarwood, mastic, mustard, and benzoin. It has many properties, but for brevity we have described only a few. These are the divine names:

Ya Allah, Ya Sami', Ya 'Alim, Ya Sari', Ya Wasi', Ya 'Adl, Ya 'Ali, Ya 'Azim, Ya Mutacali, Ya 'Aziz, Ya 'Afuw, Ya Ba'ith, Ya Fa''al lima yurid, Ya Rafi', Ya Ma'bud, Ya Mani', Ya Nafi', Ya Badi', Ya Kafi, Ya Zaki

This is the shape of the circle/seal:

[A circular seal with a broken double border containing Arabic inscriptions. Inside the circle, divine names and talismanic symbols are arranged in a geometric, star-like pattern. The word "Allah" is at the center. Below the seal, there is a faint handwritten signature, appearing to read "Muhammad Hanif".]

And this is its supplication:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by Your name with which You opened the world of creation and command—by the manifestation for the truth, the revealer of the cause of descent, the Exalted, a command and existence and inward realities comprehended, perception for one You have guided, knowledge for one You have testified, unknown for one You will. What resembles from it in multitude does not damage the unity of what You have wisely decreed, O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord. And I ask You, O Allah, O Hearing, O Knowing, O Swift, O Vast, O Just, O Exalted, O Great, O Most High, O Mighty, O Pardoning, O Resurrector, O Witness, O Exalted, O Worshipped, O Withholder, O Restorer, O Beneficial—I implore You by the secret of the addition connecting the presence of existence. I ask You by what You have spread in the sovereignty of Your omnipotence, by what You have dispersed in the omnipotence of Your sovereignty, by what You have exclusively kept in the worlds of Your divinity, by what You have concealed from the comprehension of minds in the secret of the expanse of Your mercy, by what You have enrolled into the secret of Your secret within the folding of the troubled entity, by what You have distinguished from symbols and faiths of the kinds of the stored qualitative realities in the interior of the interiors of the descent—that You guard me with Your mighty protection from the desires of Satan and his morsels, his promptings, and his cuttings that make good evil, hatred love, and benefit harm, and from the evil of my deeds. I ask You, O Allah, that You grant me by Your immense favor and Your tremendous generosity the rank of my luminous angel of mysteries and honor in the kingdom of the most excellent, and honor me with Your words in trials and tribulations so that I may attain the paths of mysteries, and grant me the kingdom of gnosis, O Most Affectionate, O Most Generous, O Lord of the worlds.
