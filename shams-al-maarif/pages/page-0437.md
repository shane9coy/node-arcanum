[Heading]: Acceptance of Prayer and Salvation from Harmful Animals

The Name Al-Qabid (The Seizer) is a name of swift response because it relates to the Angel of Death. When Allah the Most High sent several angels to take a handful of earth, the earth swore by Allah to all the angels saying "Do not take earth from me," so they all returned. After this, Allah the Most High sent the Angel of Death Azrael. To him also the earth swore an oath, but he did not heed the oath and said "I will carry out Allah's command; I will not listen to you." Thus he took a handful of earth and presented himself before Allah the Most High. Allah the Most High said "When the earth swore an oath to you, why did you not heed its oath, and why did you not return empty-handed like the other angels?" Azrael said "O Allah, I know that Your command is such that there is no remedy against it." Then Allah the Most High said "By My glory and majesty, from this earth I will create a creation whose souls you will be charged to take—not one of the two seizures (the seizure of dust and the seizure of spirit) has a guardian but you." Its officer's name is Sara'il, who sits on the chair of greatness to the right of the Angel of Death, under whom are four officers, each governing 903 rows, and in each row there are 903 officers whose sole duty is the seizure of spirits.

The officer and its four lieutenants come to the one who mentions this Name, terrifying him with awe, and he sees them spiritually. This officer gives his mentioner two gifts: one apparent and one hidden. By the apparent quality, all wild beasts, harmful animals, and humans fear him and flee from him. The effect of the hidden quality is that whoever the mentioner looks at with anger, he is destroyed instantly by light; and whoever he curses for oppression, that person is immediately seized and does not remain alive. The prayer of the Name Al-Qabid is this:

"O Allah, You are He Who seized by the forelock of every creature; You are He Who extended Your provision to every creature; and You are He Who distinguished the secrets of meanings in every provision; Seizer of spirits from bodies at the time of death; and Expander of bodies by Your perfect power at the time of resurrection and the return of bones, even though they are decayed, in the swiftest of times; and You give every one entitled their right that You have decreed for them at the time of Your creation of the atoms. I ask You by the secret of Your friend in the station of glorification, and by the secret of Your greatness upon the stations of the protected ones, that You expand upon my heart and spirit the secret of provisions, and that You remove from my heart the effects of disbelief and hypocrisy. O He in whose hand is the covenant of the pact on the Day of Meeting. O Allah, make me expanded in every seizure, and humiliated before You in the hidden of every excuse; and grant me by Your immense and universal bounty, from the secret of the seizure and from the manifestation of the seizure, a seizure, and from the lights of expansion, an expansion, so that I may attain the effects of Your mercy in the realms and disperse the effects of Your compassion at times of calamity. Indeed You are the Ancient of goodness. O Seizer."
