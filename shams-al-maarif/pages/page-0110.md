On the Properties of the Muqatta'at Letters and the Clear Verses

May Allah the Most High grant me and you the success of obeying Him. Know that the scholars have offered many interpretations regarding the separated letters at the beginning of certain surahs in the Wise Quran. Thus, they have declared that the meaning of "Alif-Lam-Mim" is "I am Allah." Hasan states that Alif is from eternity, Lam is from perpetuity, and Mim is for both conjunction and disjunction—for those who desire conjunction and for those who desire disjunction. In reality, there is neither conjunction nor disjunction. These statements of the scholars are according to convention, but Allah possesses desires; He does not make such statements.

The Reality of the Muqatta'at Letters

Each of the divine names can elevate us in rank, for it is the name of that very Essence which is described with all the sacred attributes. All the names return to Him. Whoever becomes acquainted with its meanings has become acquainted with the meanings of the divine names. These are the singular letters. Understand these indications. You will be praised among all the learned, and reflect upon the acts of worship. These names are the first matter of the apparent and the hidden. Because Allah the Most High placed the ranks of the names within Adam, peace be upon him, and they were not established within the angels. For this reason, the letters appeared upon the tongue of Adam, peace be upon him, with the arts of movements and the varieties of creation—those letters whose forms Allah the Most High placed in the heart. This is the spirituality that manifests in human speech and bodily desire. These letters are in the heart, the tongue, and the gift. This is the meaning of the word of Allah the Most High: **Sād. By the honored Quran. Qāf. By the noble Quran. Nūn. By the pen and what they inscribe.** The letters indicate the verses of the Quran, in which there is admonition for the people of understanding. In every letter there are three stations according to the three movements. They are fatha, kasra, and damma. Among these, the letters of the heights are analogous to elements. Then each of these three is bodily, spiritual, and psychical—they are not blood, nor are they numbers; the nine spheres are they, and the temperaments and senses are also nine. This correspondence has become evident. Now the discussion of the mysteries of numbers and letters follows. In their gathering and dispersion, you will find the exquisite mysteries of the requirement of the Most Merciful. Mercy comes from "In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful." The letter ba of "In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful" gives the name to the world of creation. Reflect upon the beginning of the Quran. You will find this by multiplying six by six. Likewise, there are the surahs of the Noble Quran. The number six within number is perfect—that is, complete—because Allah the Most High created the heavens, the earth, and their inner contents in six days. Then seven: the heavens, the Throne, the Pedestal, and the earth—all combined are these. Now at the beginnings of surahs, each letter has five ranks. These are besides the spatial, the rational, and the itinerant, whose sum is 78.

Chapter on the Types of Letters

There are two types of letters. One is those upon which there are two dots, and one is those upon which there is one dot, such as ta, which indicates the gathering of truth, and nun, which indicates gathering. The letters with two dots are these three: ta, qaf, and ya. From ta is intended the burning of the kingdom; from qaf is intended the form in power; and from ya is intended the form in grace.
