# Page 467 (English translation)
O Equitable One, You are the one who established justice among creatures with the right hand of their creation, in essence and attributes, and You are the one whose bounty reached every creature and they received their share with completeness and dignity. I ask You to grant me justice in speech and actions among the learned and the ignorant. Truly, You are Allah, the Great, the Exalted.

The Name "The Gatherer" contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its leader is the angel Riqaʾil. He is the leader of 619 rows. In each row there are 619 angels. Its supplication is this:

O Gatherer, You are the one who gathered the atoms upon the backs of Your creation on the Day of the Covenant, You did not inform them of the news about them by way of priority and release. And You are the one who brought them out from the theoretical existence that came into being by force and preservation. I ask You for what You placed within it from the realities of attributes and morals, that You gather my scattered ones with You on the Day of Meeting, and that You appear to me with the benefits of the wisdom of Your words. And the leg is intertwined with the leg, and do not disappoint my hope by my turning to You and my standing before You. Truly, You are Allah, the Mighty, the Beautiful.

The Name "The Self-Sufficient" contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its chief is Rimaʾil. This is the command of four commanders. Each commander has 1060 angels. The supplication is:

O Self-Sufficient, You are the One who enriches, and You are the One able to do whatsoever You will, able to force everything and every strong one. And You are the One who takes by the forelock every proud one, and the One who enriches, the Beautiful, who arises for every creature. I ask You by that in which there is success and victory, that You pour forth from Your eternal imagination until I stand before You on the Day of Reliance and Need, and strike against the repelling of what prevents me from You. Truly, You are Allah, the Mighty, the Forgiving.

The Name "The Enricher" contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its chief is Hayʾil. Four commanders are obedient to it. Each commander has 11 banners. Within each row there are 600 angels. The angel of the remembrance comes to its practitioner. The supplication is:

O Enricher, You are the One who administers the affairs of creation and manages them. And You are the One who brings forth their essences from the house of non-existence and disciplines them after Your administration. And You gathered them between them in the nearest intervening realm according to their actions and attributes. You helped the wronged one and hastened to the satisfaction of the wronged one the satisfaction of the seeker. You reconciled opposites, differences, and contraries that have no connection for him to other than himself, neither in his essence nor in his attributes nor in his actions. And You are the One who enriches by Your care whoever seeks the fulfillment of needs. O Turner of hearts and intentions, and Director of affairs toward directions and sides. I ask You to grant me good administration and dealings, and to make me just in equity, gathering between the related and the relative. O Lord of the worlds.

The Name "The Preventer" contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its chief is Miyaʾil. Four commanders are obedient to it. Each commander has 111 banners, in each row there are 111 angels. The supplication is:

O Preventer, You are the one who prevented love of You from the hearts of the wicked. And You are the one who weakened humanity with the [obstacles]. You are [unclear/end of page]
