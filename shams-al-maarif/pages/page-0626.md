# Page 626 (English translation)
624

**The Search for the Greatest Name by the Inspired Voice upon the Heart:**

Imam Khwarazmi relates that the Voice from the Unseen revealed into my heart: "Seek the Greatest Name." So I traveled for seven years in search of it, until I reached the city of Masha'ir and came to the land of Tibet, presenting myself before a blind elder. I asked him about the Greatest Name. He said: "O son! All the names of God are the Greatest Name."

**The Greatest Name Contains All Four Natures:**

I submitted: "You speak truly, but I ask about that Name which is comprehensive and in which all four natures are present." Upon my saying this, he looked toward me and said: "Do you know Ehya, Sharahya, Azarni, E Bani, or Ho, Ba Ho, Yaqofah, Muni, and certain other continuous names that are useful in the science of alchemy?" I said: "Your Eminence, I know them." Then he said: "Come to me." I went to him, and he said: "A person like you has never come to me. Know that the Greatest Name is that Name which every sincere person recites."

[separator marker]

**The Greatest Name Was Inscribed upon Moses' Staff:**

Upon the staff of Moses, peace be upon him, the Greatest Name was written, and with it he would make supplications. It contains the letters of all four natures, and its total letters are twelve. Know then that I show you a grain belonging to this very Name, and the names that emerge with it are also within it. Then the sheikh took out a small box and opened within it a shining paper, upon which this grain was written larger than my comprehension could grasp. I submitted: "O Sheikh! I wish you would explain them to me." He said: "O son! I will tell you its meanings, its significance, and its method of invocation. Thus many things became known to me from that paper which I had not known before.

**God Is as Superior over All Names as Laylat al-Qadr Is Superior over Other Nights:**

Abdullah ibn Ubayd related to me that this Name holds such excellence over the other names as Laylat al-Qadr holds over other nights.

[separator marker]

Khwarazmi says: "I kissed the Sheikh's hands. The Sheikh gave me a blessing and said: 'O son, knowledge of the Beautiful Names is a treasury of divine mysteries known only to the people of God.'

**A Grain Filled with Divine Secrets:**

Then the Sheikh bestowed upon me that grain. I observed in it wondrous and extraordinary things, and indeed this grain is one of the secrets of the Divine that should be kept hidden from people."
