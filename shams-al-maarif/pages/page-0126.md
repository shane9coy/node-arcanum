# Page 126 (English translation)
O you who are mighty in Your majesty, the transgressors of jinn and men who rebel, O severe in might, O great in subjugation, O one who takes revenge from every mighty oppressor, strengthen me with Your victory and clear opening, until I subdue all my enemies. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those whom You have favored, not of those who have incurred wrath, nor of those who are astray. Grant us from Your presence the gifts of the truthful ones, and make us witness the stations of the martyrs and the righteous, and support us with angels of victory and empowerment, as You have said in Your truthful statement: "Your Lord will reinforce you with three thousand angels, descending with a misfortune for you, O Muhammad." And dispose us among all beings and creations, and pour upon us from the abundance of Your blessings that which returns to us from the blessings of the former and later peoples. Do not make us astray nor misleaders, nor gather us among the transgressors. O refuge of those who seek help, help me and reach me with Your subtle kindness, for whoever You have hidden beneath Your subtle kindness has indeed hidden, appeared, and vanished. There is no god but You. Glory be to You, I have certainly been among the wrongdoers. (Hour of war) And place me in Your faithful, fortified, mighty, sufficient, protecting, encompassing shelter, and immerse me in the vastness of Your provision from the treasuries of Your mercy which encompasses all things, and relieve me of every distress, O reliever of the distressed, by Your mercy, O most merciful of the merciful. The oath of the witness, the exterminator, Wahā, Ajala, O subordinate, and witness that Wahā, surely a witness that Ajala is coming, trust in such and such, I swear upon you by the honor of God and by the light of God's face, by the Opening of the Book and by what the pen has inscribed from God to one who is foreign, and expedite the fulfillment of my need, which is such and such. Indeed, His command when He intends a thing is only to say to it "Be," and it becomes.

[Handwritten marginalia between sections: Beginning]

In the name of God, the begin, the most merciful, the especially merciful. All praise belongs to God, Lord of all worlds. Such praise as is more excellent, complete, and superior than the praise of any created being. Immerse me in the sea of such praise until it encompasses my outward and inward. May it accompany me until the Day of Resurrection with honor, awe, and beauty. May it grant me sudden [victory] and protect me from those who cause harm and enemies. May it be such praise as brings contentment and joy, an invaluable treasure through which I have no need of any among the former and later peoples, and may it be a source of honor for me through which I repel the might of tyrants. And He is the Merciful One whose mercy extends over all things, and every existing thing bears witness to Him. With a thousand [confirmations] made in acknowledgment of His favor. For in every heaven there are mysteries and signs, and in His concealment there is both hiddenness and manifestation. I ask You by that secret which You have made clear, and that knowledge which You gave to Ayn [possibly a proper name or "the eye"], that You immerse me in that sea which shall never be separated from me at any time or in any age. And that preparation for me be such praise that I shall never need anything outside it at any day or time. And that it be for me a courage through which I seek refuge in the chambers of return from among humanity. The Merciful One who dealt gently with me regarding what has passed, for His mercy was foremost. Through Him there is between me and him [or "between me and my father"] a gentle mercy that was abundant when I found existence and walked with it. With the finest and most excellent [praise], I ask You in my questions, for the Knowing One is the God of [His] previous mercy which grants permanence. Therefore I do not fear the cunning of any cunning person. And purify me in the intellect and elevate me in the horizon to such purity that I am the master of the Day of Judgment. His glory is very great. [I seek] to be free from need of any associate or any helper [through] knowledge.
