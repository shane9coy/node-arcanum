Having engraved it on a copper tablet, when this tablet is heated with fire in a place, the person or all people will become restless to come to that place.

All people will be ready to meet:

If someone takes possession of the letter 'Shīn' and writes it on musk-scented cloth in the shape of incense called 'lubān' and writes this verse around it:

"Alā yasjudū lillāhi alladhī yukhrij al-khab'a fī al-samāwāti wal-arḍi"

And binds it around the neck of a white rooster and leaves it in a place where treasure is suspected or where magical amulets are believed to be buried, that rooster will stand up and crow three times in that same place and scratch with its claws. This is the picture of the letter 'Shīn':

[A 3x3 magic square containing the Arabic letters Shīn (ش), Yā (ی), and Nūn (ن) arranged in a magic square pattern:
Row 1: ش | ی | ن
Row 2: ی | ن | ش
Row 3: ن | ش | ی]

And the prayer for this letter is:

"Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā shākiru yā shakūru yā shahīdu yā hamīdu bimā awda'tahu harfa al-yaqīni mina l-asrāri l-makhzūnati wal-anwāri l-maknūnati an tusakhkhira lī malā'ikataka khuddāma hādhā l-ḥarfi innaika 'alā kulli shay'in qadīrun"

The Letter Ṣād:

The letter 'Ṣād' is专属 to 'the Truth' (al-ḥaqq), 'the Creator' (al-khāliq), and 'the Maker' (khallāq), and dominates Tuesday.

Properties and Benefits: If you write Ṣād fourteen times on your hand or on a fish on Friday and keep it with you, when you go hunting you will catch much game.

Fish will gather in one place:

If you have its number engraved on a lead tablet, and on the other side draw a picture of a fish and write Ṣād ten times around it, bind this tablet in some cord and throw it into a river or canal, fish will gather there in abundance—you can catch them with your hands.

Theft will never occur:

If you write it ninety-five times in square form. These are its numbers for its preservation: 90 for Ṣād, 1 for Alif, 4 for Dāl, making 95. If you draw lines around it and outside of that write 'Ṣ' fourteen times and keep it with you, theft will never occur in that place. It will be protected from the evil of jinn and men. The form and prayer are these:

[A grid with 3 rows and 4 columns, each cell containing the Arabic letter Ṣād (ص).]

Prayer: "Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā ṣādiqu yā ṣābiru yā ṣāmitun kullu gharībin as'aluka bimā awda'tahu ḥarfa l-ṣādi min al-asrāri l-makhzūnati an tusakhkhira lī khuddāma hādhā l-ḥarfi innaika 'alā kulli shay'in qadīrun"
