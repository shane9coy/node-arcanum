# Page 520 (English translation)
518

When the corrupt and impure tyrants rule the lands... destruction to deaths and speech
When they let their mustaches grow and trim their beards... they become like scorpion-tails in imagination
And they dye their clothes and make them wide... and they mix the unlawful with the lawful
When an Arab comes to them in truth... quickly he will rule without doubt
They will open it without doubt... and many a caller calls with supplication
And Mahmoud will appear after this... and will rule Syria without combat
All the fortifications of Syria will obey him... and he will spend his wealth in every circumstance

When the corrupt and unholy traitors and liars become rulers of the lands, and when they grow their mustaches and trim their beards
Their mustaches will become like scorpion-tails and they will wear various loose-fitting clothes and mix the unlawful with the lawful wealth
Suddenly a sincere person of Arab lineage will appear among them and will quickly seize control of the lands and will conquer it without any doubt
And many supplicants will call out with humility and after this Mahmoud will appear and will become the owner of the land of Syria without fighting
All the forts of Syria will submit to him and in every situation he will spend all his wealth

An army will appear from the lands of the Byzantines toward Aleppo... whose end is like perfection
With Russians, Burghal, and Byzantines... and every rider skilled up to combat
And ships will descend from its west... upon Syria's barren shore
Arabs and Turks will covet their wealth... seeking plunder after combat
And the army of the Armenians will return at evening... upon their heels despite their alliances
And Jaiza will forgive wind and blacks... and Jisnad the tall towers
There will be no Islam remaining in it after this... a dwelling after the times of suffering
And a day upon a rock, what a day... upon which there will be a people of calamity
When they raise the banner and make it firm... and desire combat over the common people
The Merciful will pour upon them a wind... that throws with the eyes and with weight
And we have from it a great day... the young men of men will be killed in it
With a sword like scorpion-tails, sharpened... of Indian steel, well-forged
And the Nile will not be purified soon... and an ugly state will appear in Syria
How many cycles have passed [decorative circle with numeral 9]... and how many revolutions, the excellent example
