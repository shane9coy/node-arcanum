# Page 450 (English translation)
I have lifted my need to another who is not to be hoped for, and when a distant one is neglectful, he does not exact, nor does he who takes refuge in him perish; and the fugitive is safe.
I ask You by Your generosity and the sublimity of the varieties of Your blessings that You grant me a generous gift that serves as sufficiency and sustenance, between sufficiency and generosity—
by the connection of the kāf, O One Who comes—thereby two words are strung together: "Indeed we glorify You much" and "and we remember You much; surely You were Seeing of us."

And I ask You, O most generous of the generous, and O most merciful of the merciful, for the continuation of Your blessings and their permanence upon me of ease and well-being and an ample dominion, O Light of lights, O Healer of breasts.

**Let it be known to the treasurers:**
The name Zaqqīb is a great name. The mention of this name in a house of treasure removes the obstacles to extracting treasure and the attention of light becomes manifest. Its client's name is Ṣamʿāʾīl; he rules over four officers, each officer oversees 312 ranks, and in each rank 312 clients are employed. The one who remembers this name attains very elevated ranks. The prayer of the name Ar-Raqīb is:
O Raqīb, You are the necessary Preserver—by Your preservation for him whose connection I have made to You. And You are the Peace for one whom Your generosity has gathered for him. And You are He Who draws out secrets and reveals sights and meets spirits with lights. I ask You by the greatness of Your power and the majesty of Your proximity that You make me preserved in every visible thing and endowed in every manifest thing. And grant me the recompense of he who accompanied me, and be for Your servant a companion and a helper and a preserver, and regard him with the gaze of affection, O He to whom belongs power and permanence and might and splendor, O Lord of the worlds.

**May prayers be accepted:**
The name Al-Mujīb is a great name. Within it is one letter of the Greatest Name. In this name there is a strange effect of acceptance of prayer. Its client's name is Ṭuhayyāʾīl; he is acquainted with the client of the veil of acceptance and with the gate of indulgence. This client rules over four officers, each of which oversees 55 ranks, and in each rank 55 clients are employed. The client comes to the one who remembers this name and fulfills his need. The prayer of the name Al-Mujīb is:
O Mujīb, You are He Who answers the distress of the hard-pressed, and You are He Who aids the anguished and those turned away from guidance. And You are He Who bestows blessings of Your majesty before wealth and who favors with the continuation of Your generosity before supplication. I ask You by the beauty of Your face that You make me one who answers You in Your times, and one who avoids Your prohibitions, and one who hastens to Your call seeking Your pleasure. And manifest upon my desire what You have taught me and made me complete—surely You are the Compassionate, the Beneficent.

**Solution of difficulties and boundless, measureless expansion in sustenance**
The name Al-Wāsiʿ is a great name. The person who persists in the remembrance of this name—difficult tasks become easy for him. There is a special effect in the practice of this name for obtaining sustenance. Its client's name is ʿIlmāʾīl; he is chief over four officers. Each officer oversees 137 ranks, and in each rank 137 clients are employed. The client comes to his reciter. The prayer of the name Al-Wāsiʿ is:
O Wāsiʿ, You are He Whose dominion and justice and whose glory and forbearance encompassed all states, and You are He whose magnificence and gentleness extended to every rank—
