Chapter 26: The Mysteries and Properties of the Name Muḍhill (Humiliator) – May Enemies Be Humiliated

This name is most glorious. Whoever mentions this name abundantly, his enemies will be humiliated before him. If an animal is not under control, let him mention this name, and Almighty Allah will make that animal obedient. Whoever fasts for three days, the last of which is Friday, and on Friday delays breaking the fast until after the Maghrib prayer, let him perform two rak'ahs, and after al-Fātiḥa let him recite the name one hundred times, and similarly in prostration. Then after the salutation, let him recite the name one thousand times and say: "O Muḍhill, humiliate so-and-so before me!" Then he will certainly be humiliated and will not oppose him in anything.

Latifa (Subtle Point): When the people of insight perceived with the eye of the heart that the name of humiliation and the name of delight and union are close to one another, they understood that union without humiliation is not possible. For this reason, they began to eat dirt in the places of dogs. When they had experienced humiliation in every way, Almighty Allah gave them an honor that is everlasting, and they became free from humiliation before anyone other than Him. They recognized faith by its fruits and observed kindness, and they came to know that there is no humiliator except Almighty Allah. Its number is 707, and within it lies the marriage entry in the category of the odd number according to the feminine position, which is obtained by multiplying the future in the locus. Its 21 + 1096 point to the transcendent direction. With 90 they indicate ṣamadāniyyat (eternal self-sufficiency), and with the thousand they indicate the ghayn (غ). And because actions come to the one who has pure mashī'ah (divine will), this number points to the letters mīm, hā', wāw, and yā'. So ghayn, mīm, and nūn are from the names of Almighty Allah in meaning, and the yā' was deleted because it contains defect. Whoever chooses humiliation before an enemy will be humiliated. The yā' takes the place of wāw, which indicates that whose consequences involve humiliation. The numbers of its letters are 893, pointing to two of the great names of Almighty Allah: Dhū al-Quwwah (Possessor of Power) and Mājid (Glorious). Its square is as follows:

Chapter 27: Properties of the Name Samī' (All-Hearing) – Acceptance of Prayer and Countless Benefits

Let him mention this name after every prayer, and the prayer will be accepted. Whoever mentions this name abundantly, no prayer of his will be rejected. If he engraves it on a ring during the honor of the Moon (in a favorable lunar condition) and mentions it abundantly before wearing it, every word of his will be heard. Its mention is suitable for preachers, orators, and those whose work is enchanting. Its number is 180, and it is an even-even and binary odd. Its 1 + 7 = 8 and 206 point to two of the great names of Almighty Allah: Qā'il (Speaker) and Ḥakīm (Wise). Because the name Samī' is opposite Qā'il, the name Samī' is completed with that inspiration, and the ṣifātī (attribute-based) acceptance continues. In this station, the binding of knowledge is necessary. And because in the position of the Moon, all celestial bodies have a unique attraction, the outflow of the name Samī' is in its place, and the requirements of this degree indicate Samī' as its gratitude, opposite the numbers of its letters. It contains the substance of the name of guidance (al-hudā); in it are nūn, wāw, rā', and the repeated dhāl. Its numbers 206 + 180 = 386 point to the name Rāfi' (Elevator) of Almighty Allah.
