# Page 767 (English translation)
765

There was a report that there would be an angel with such a form. Gabriel said: "If you, peace be upon him, see Israfel, then you, peace be upon him, would know that he also has seven hundred [wings], and upon one [wing] his seven hundred wings are proportionate." So, during the Night of Ascension, the Messenger, peace be upon him, also observed Israfel. When Israfel contemplated the greatness of God, he would become as small as a sparrow, and when he became great, he would encompass the world within himself.

Similarly, when the venerable Shaykh Abd al-Qadir Jilani, may God's mercy be upon him, recited the divine names, light would blaze in his heart at every moment concerning him. At that time he would become great and rise above the wind. In short, in every state there was ascension and elevation for him.
