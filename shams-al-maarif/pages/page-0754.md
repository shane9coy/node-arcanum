# Page 754 (English translation)
754

For the protection of something, and this is also the special state of the disciple. For any oppressor, one should recite these names and the oppressor will be destroyed; these names are:

الصابُّ الْعَدْلُ الْمُؤَخِّرُ الْمُنْتَقِمُ

And recite this supplication:

اللهم يا شديد خُذ حق من من طلسنی و اعتدى و علي و كفّ شره عن الخلق

Or indeed: "Allahumma ahlkahu bi-ajjal jalalin, write 1000 tas, it will become pure, and may God turn him toward al-Khaliq, so that if He wishes He may help him"

اللهم يا شديد خُذ حق منه و اقصم ظهره و اقطع دابرة و انزل و اكفني شرّة

And these are all the names:

هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْقَادِرُ الْمُقْتَدِرُ الْعَزِيزُ الْجَبَّارُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
الْقَوِيُّ الْقَائِمُ الْمُبِينُ الشَّدِيدُ الْقَاهِرُ الْقَهَّارُ ذُو الْبَطْشِ الشَّدِيدِ

Whoever maintains the wirds of these 21 names and has them engraved on a silver ring and wears it, will have dominance over all creatures, and all affairs among people will become desirable to them. Whoever writes these 20 names on black wax and buries it in some place, that place shall never be inhabited.

And whoever propagates these names and has them engraved on a silver ring and wears it, let him write this verse around it in a circle, and give the fumigation of ustarkh al-farri and ladh khara, and wear it. When this person goes to anyone, they will fear only him. These names are: الْمُقْتَدِرُ الْقَوِيُّ الْقَائِمُ And the verse is: إِنَّ بَطْشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ And when this ring is placed in the house of a tyrannical king, his sovereignty will depart and his subjects will become his enemies. And the method of construction is as follows: ال ال ال ص ح م ا ب ی ا ک ف ی ر

**For gaining dominance over enemies:**

These names are الْجَبَّارُ الْعَزِيزُ الْمُتَكَبِّرُ This is for the person who wishes to prevail over enemies; he should write in this manner and keep it with him: ال ال ال ص ح م ا ب ی ا ک و ر پ ر And around it write this verse:

إِنَّا فَتَحْنَا لَكَ فَتْحًا مُّبِينًا عَنْ عِزًّا

Write at sunrise on Tuesday. And if the ascendant is unfavorable or Mars and Venus are in a state of combustion, give the fumigation of al-muhtaliba al-nar and keep it with you. Whoever sees this person will fall into fear of him. King Buqrdan the wise had performed this operation, and by means of this operation great treasures were found for him. And when he died, he had bequeathed this to his son. ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ Whoever mentions this name to such a degree that its worker prevails over it. That person will become extremely eminent and honored in the eyes of people, and there is a strange influence in his spirit. This name is very abundant in fulfillment, and have you not heard the saying of the Messenger ﷺ, for he said: "Make supplication with ya dhul-jalal wa l-ikram."

**Asaf ibn Barachia brought the throne of Bilqis to the court of Solomon by the blessing of these very names**

Asaf ibn Idris al-Rumi wrote in his great book, which he had obtained from the treasury of Harun al-Rashid, that this is the Greatest Name with which Asaf ibn Barachia made supplication, so that he brought the throne of Bilqis to the service of Solomon, peace be upon him. This name is extremely swift in answer, because supplication is made to God Almighty with it. God [text unclear] taught His community from His grace, and explicitly [text unclear] from among the [text unclear], they call this name the Greatest Name.
