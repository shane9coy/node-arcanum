# Page 33 (English translation)
Details of the Book's Subjects

Chapter 1: The secrets and emanations of the letters of the alphabet (dotted letters).
Chapter 2: Contraction and expansion, and the arrangement of works in times and hours.
Chapter 3: Concerning the decrees of the twenty-eight mansions of the moon.
Chapter 4: The decrees of the twelve signs of the zodiac and their indications and correspondences.
Chapter 5: The secrets and properties of "In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful" and its hidden blessings.
Chapter 6: The eternity of seclusion that draws forth from solitude and public attendance.
Chapter 7: Those names with which Jesus, peace be upon him, revived the dead.
Chapter 8: The four rotations, their divisions, and their circles.
Chapter 9: Concerning the evidences of the Quran and the clarifying verses.
Chapter 10: The secrets of Surah al-Fatihah, its invocations, and its famous properties.
Chapter 11: The illuminations and spiritual lights.
Chapter 12: The hidden details of the Greatest Name.
Chapter 13: The corresponding numerical squares (1) and invocations for Surah al-Fatihah.
Chapter 14: The spiritual exercises of dhikr and the accepted prayers that subjugate.
Chapter 15: The necessary conditions for certain works.
Chapter 16: The beautiful names of Allah and their comprehensive and wondrous numerical squares.
Chapter 17: The properties of the sacred letters of Kha-ya-ayn-sad.
Chapter 18: The Verse of the Throne and its manifest blessings.
Chapter 19: Certain numerical squares and the properties of the sublime talismans.
Chapter 20: Surah Yasin and its answered invocations.
Chapter 21: The beautiful names, their words, and the invocation and details for each name.
Chapter 22: The void spaces and those same names.
Chapter 23: The third line and the names indicating the attribute of evildoing.
Chapter 24: The divine secrets of the fourth line.
Chapter 25: The selected properties of the fifth line.
Chapter 26: The secrets and properties of the sixth line.
Chapter 27: The seventh line, concerning the names of Allah the Exalted and the declaration of His blessing.
Chapter 28: The eighth line, concerning the beautiful names of Allah the Exalted and the declaration of their manifest secrets.
Chapter 29: The ninth line, the beautiful names and their details.
Chapter 30: The tenth line, the sublime secrets and properties of the beautiful names.
Chapter 31: The Arabic letters, their celestial servants and assistants, and the seclusions.
Chapter 32: The secrets of the unveiling of the Throne's level.
Chapter 33: The explanation of the secrets of the Bhala (خ) circle, and the conception of similitudes and descriptions.
Chapter 34: The science of astrology, all the praised letters, and the declaration of the famous weights.
Chapter 35: The declaration of the letter-diagram from the rules of Jafr.
Chapter 36: The lights and divine graces, the honored stone and the properties of courage.
Chapter 37: The works of the science of Simiya.
Chapter 38: The elements of the letters, their risings and all their details.
Chapter 39: The exposition of the beautiful names according to the method of the Minhaj (pathway) and detailed method of their utterance.
Chapter 40: The designated prayers to be recited at all times, day and night.

***

(1) Awfaq is the plural of waqf, meaning meeting and touching. Da'wat is the plural of da'wah, meaning wird (ritual recitation) and wird. Talismans refers to both sigils and talismans and wird and wirds together. Jumla means methods.
