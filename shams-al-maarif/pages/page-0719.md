# Page 719 (English translation)
717

So He is First through relation to existence and Last through relation to ascension. So when His reality becomes known to you, then know that He is the First and He is the Last, and He is the Manifest and He is the Hidden.
[underlined] His essential attribute is the Firstness of Divine Unity, and annihilation [fana] will not occur—everyone will undergo annihilation; He is the Last:
His Firstness is an essential attribute for Him, and Unity is through His existence, and His Lastness is an established attribute for His creation, and His perpetuity is for Him after the annihilation of others, just as He was before their existence.
[underlined] No partner can be associated with His Firstness and Lastness:
And because His authority does not require the arrangement of place, number, or quantity, therefore it is impossible for anyone other to be with Him in Firstness or Lastness, because He is such a matter to which the knowledge of the knowers of God reaches its limit. So He is First and Last through the matter He intended and the power He ordained. Firstness informs of His eternity, and Lastness informs of His self-sufficiency from nonexistence.
The saying of Shibli:
Regarding Him, His Eminence Shibli said: Letters exist before numbers, and something exists before letters. In this there is an indication toward the essential footstep of the Lord, Exalted.
[underlined] His essence is unlimited, and His speech is without letters:
Meaning there is no limit for His essence, nor are there letters in His speech.
The saying of Junaid al-Baghdadi:
Someone asked His Eminence Junaid about Divine Unity. He said: It is the individual's isolation of the monotheist, the verification of His oneness, and the perfection of His incomparability, in such a way that He is One—neither did He give birth to anyone, nor was He born from anyone. There is no contrary for Him, no associate, no one resembles Him, and there is no likeness to Him.
{"There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing"}
[underlined] If you are first in turning toward Him and last in servanthood, you will become one brought near:
So O brother, it is fitting for you that in proximity you should be first toward Him, and in the act of servanthood you should be last before Him. Because if you are first in turning toward Him, He will establish your inner reality in the contemplation of the lights of the Firstness of Divine Unity, and if you are last with a heart of servanthood, He will make you among the last of the blessed, and He will reveal to you the realities of the Last.
[underlined] God's existence is non-separated:
The summary of the discourse is that the subtleties of Divine Unity are so delicate and refined that they cannot be expressed in words. So the meaning of First is that He is such an eternal being that no beginning has a limit for Him, nor an end. So His existence has non-separation, and from His being without beginning, this fact becomes known—that anyone other than Him being His associate is impossible. From this cause it becomes known that His Firstness has no beginning, nor does His eternity have termination. He alone is described with the attributes of the perfection of truth. So His incomparability is above any claim of partnership for anyone.
