# Page 759 (English translation)
757

The names of al-Kabir al-Muta'al: Whoever writes it on a deer's membrane with musk and saffron and rosewater, and keeps it with himself, whatever he desires shall be granted to him through it.

And it is for obtaining dominance, awe, majesty, and strength; the half of the influence of the Greatest Name is in it, and it is extremely swift in effect for warding off poison, whispers (waswasa), overpowering lust, and frightening things. The time for its recitation is daily at the afternoon (Asr) hour, and the masters (shaykhs) are with it.

The influence of the litany of these eight names is very great:
And they are these eight names:
**al-Malik al-'Ali al-'Azim al-Ghani al-Muta'al Dhu al-Jalal al-Muhaymin al-Kabir**

The explanation of the name Dhu al-Jalal has already passed. Whoever writes al-Basit al-Fattah al-Jawwad and keeps it with himself, whoever looks upon it will love him. Their mention is beneficial for those skilled in spiritual restraint (ahl al-qabd), and when those devoted to solitude (ahl al-khalwa) mention them, they feel expansion in their seclusions, and they hear dialogues with heavenly subtlety. Only those who are acquainted with the secrets of the names and invocations understand these matters well.

The benefits of this supplication are very great: "freedom from sorrow, grief, and captivity."

This supplication is extremely great and blessed. Whoever performs two rak'ahs of prayer and then reads it at the second hour of Sunday, that is, at the hour of Venus, all sorrow and grief shall depart from his heart. If a prisoner reads it, he shall obtain freedom from captivity, and the following verses from the Quranic verses are of this meaning:
**farihin bima atahum Allahu min fadlihi**
"And they rejoice in what Allah has given them out of His bounty" (Quran 57:27)
and so on. Through its blessing, all desires shall be fulfilled. And the blessed supplication is:
**Rabbi farrhni bima tarda bihi 'anni farahan tahijuni bi-jamil al-sajiri hatta la yatasallata bi-shay'in bayna wujudi illa bima basata wujuduka al-fi'li Rabbi farrhni bi-nayl al-murad min ka baghtatan wa iradati hatta la yakuna li kawni iradata illa iradatuka mahfuzan min 'awarid al-takwin wa-inhashini bi-idhnika bi-mizan al-iftitah fi al-wujud innaka ya basitu al-rizq wa al-rahma ya dha al-jud ya basita ya jawwadu ya fattahu ya razzaqu**

"Lord, gladden me with what pleases You concerning me, a gladness that moves me through beautiful magic, until nothing among what exists shall have power over me except what Your active existence has expanded. Lord, gladden me with the sudden attainment of my desire from You, and my will until my existence has no will except Your will, preserved from the accidents of creation. And raise me by Your permission with the balance of opening in existence. Indeed, You, O One who expands sustenance and mercy, O One of generosity, O Expander, O Generous, O Opener, O Provider."

Through the recitation of this supplication, sorrow and grief and all troubles are ended:

This supplication also has a wonderful effect in removing sorrow. On the day of Sunday, at the luminous hour, after two rak'ahs of prayer, it should be read sixty times—all troubles shall depart.
**Subhana an tanjali fi bayadhi asma-ika min al-bab al-khas alladhi la yuhjabu bi-nurihi wa la yuzlimu lahu bi-shay'in wa la bi-shay'in kharijun 'anhu wa alliq yadayya fi nayl al-ni'ma wa adhiqni dhawqa kulli madhuqin minhu hatta akuna laka wa akuna bika bi-ismika bi-tabyin bayanin fa'alta minaka innaka latif rahim ra'uf karim**
"Praise be to You, that there may shine forth in the whiteness of Your names, from the special door which is not obscured by light, and for which nothing is darkened, and nothing exists outside of it. And release my hands in attaining blessing, and cause me to taste the taste of everything from it, until I belong to You and belong through You by Your name, through the clarification of the statement that You have made from Yourself. Indeed, You are gentle, merciful, compassionate, generous."

And among the Quranic verses, this verse is also of this meaning:
**ma yaftahu Allahu lil-nasi min rahma**
"Whatever mercy Allah opens for people" (Quran 35:2)
and so on. Through its blessing, all troubles shall depart.
