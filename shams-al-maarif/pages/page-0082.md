[The Letters of Bismillah and Their Properties]

There are nineteen letters in total, of which ten are non-repeating, namely: b, s, m, a, l, h, r, h, m, n, a, l, h, r, h, y, m. Among these, the meem appears repeated three times. The lam repeats four times, and the ra repeats twice. Thus, the repeated letters are nine. Among the non-repeating letters, one letter is "ba," which serves for spreading goodness. Its temperament is cold. This is why it is placed at the beginning, and the letter ba is at the end of what remains until the Day of Judgment, and it is an essential letter. Therefore, it is among the mysteries of "the odd by essence" because it has indicated the reality, and it is from this and towards this.

Know that first was the Scripture of Hazrat Abraham, peace be upon him, of which the news was revealed to Hazrat the Chosen Prophet, may Allah bless him and his family, given in the first revelation, as Allah the Exalted commanded: "Read in the name of your Lord who created—created man from a clinging substance." After "Read" comes "ba." With this, Allah the Exalted created hundreds of thousands of angels who glorify Him and sanctify Him.

Protection from Sudden Calamities:

Among the properties of Bismillah is that the Chosen Prophet, may Allah bless him and his family, said: Whoever recites three times in the morning "Bismillah al-A'zam alladhi la yadharru ma'a ismihi shay'un fi al-ard wa la fi al-sama'i wa huwa al-sami' al-'aleem," he will be protected from every sudden calamity until sunrise. Another narration says he will be protected from paralysis. One narration says that a person gave a cup of deadly poison to Hazrat Khalid bin Walid, may Allah be pleased with him, and said: If you are truthful and these words have effect, drink this. At that time many Companions, may Allah be pleased with them, were present. He said "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Raheem" and drank it. Nothing harmed him. One narration says that he recited these words: "Bismillah al-A'zam alladhi la yadharru ma'a ismihi shay'un fi al-ard wa la fi al-sama'i wa huwa al-sami' al-'aleem," then drank it, and he rose from there sound and healthy. All the poison flowed out through his sweat. See how this supreme Name blocked the effect of poison. By the blessing of this supreme Name, the ship of Hazrat Noah, peace be upon him, sailed, and by it Hazrat Abraham, peace be upon him, was saved from the fire—it became cool and safe.

Therefore, if you also desire salvation, when you enter the house say "Bismillah," and when you leave the house say "Bismillah" and then depart. Because in the noble Hadith is the command of the Chosen Prophet, may Allah bless him and his family: When you enter the house and when you leave it, say "Bismillah, idhana dakhalna wa kharajna wa 'alayhi tawakkalna." Also say "Bismillah" when closing the door, for Satan cannot enter that house. When you lie down on your bed, say "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Raheem wa 'ala millat rasul Allah," for by its blessing no harm will befall.

The Chosen Prophet, may Allah bless him and his family, said: Whoever does not recite "Bismillah" during ablution, his ablution is incomplete.
