Chapter: Taksir of the Zodiac Signs

Aries: It has two methods, which most philosophers and sages have adopted, such as Plato and others. The taksir of Aries is as follows: t, m, a, l, d, a, r, b, a, d, n, m, s, a, a, th, r, ay, n. The cube of the numbers was 12; when multiplied by themselves they yielded 184. This was subtracted, resulting in Hayā Tāyīl. Then, without the defining alif-lām, contemplation was performed, and without their subtraction, Zakā Tāyīl was obtained.

Taurus: Its basīṭ is th, and its taksir is kh, m, a, y, s, th, m, a, t, y, n—total 15. Its cube is 25. When derivation was performed, Haya Tāyīl resulted. This is the doctrine of some philosophers, and according to Plato, alif is counted with it, meaning thus: al-thawr. And the composite: the basīṭ is a phonological name, and the composite is a letteral name. a, l, f, l, a, m, th, a, w, r—its numbers are 13 letters, and the composite: a, b, th, l, a, th, r, n, kh, m, s, d, n, m, a, y, s, t, m, a, t, y, n—total sum 23. When multiplied, it yielded 1578. When treated thus, Ghitāyīl was produced.

Gemini: Basīṭ and composite. al-q-l-l is the name. j, y, m, a, w, z, a, y, f—total 10 letters, and the antī operation is applied. But the composite is letteral: th, l, a, th, r, n, th, m, a, l, a, d, n, a, r, b, a, w, m, a, th, a, l, ay, n. Example: ath-rayn. Its cube is 1494. When derivation was performed, Aḥyā Nāyīl resulted.

Cancer: Basīṭ and composite. The basīṭ is: a, l, f, l, a, m, s, y, n, r, a, t, a—a, l, f, n, d, n—14 letters, and the antī operation applies. The rukhī basīṭ has 30 numerical letters, and their cube is 900. Sā'īl Al-Tā'īl resulted. Direct your attention toward its related matters.

Leo: Basīṭ and composite. The basīṭ is: a, l, f, l, a, m, s, y, n, d, a, l, a. The rukhī basīṭ has 211 numerical letters. Its total sum is 418, and derivation yielded Dabāyīl.

Virgo: Basīṭ and composite. The basīṭ is: a, l, y, m, s, y, n, n, a, b, n, d, n, p, a, l, a, s. Total 22 letters. Its basīṭ is 433. Its derivation yielded Alfiyāyīl.

Libra: Basīṭ and composite. Total letters are 18, and the antī operation applies. Its numerical basīṭ and cube are 99, and its derivation is Matiyāyīl.

Scorpio: Basīṭ and composite. Total letters 8, rukhī number 47, and cube 747. Sāyilāyīl Hawayīl resulted.

Sagittarius: Total 15, basīṭ 20, and cube 30. Its derivation is Satāyīl.

Capricorn: Numerical basīṭ 23, its cube is 441, and derivation is Liyyāyīl.

Aquarius: Rukhī composite. Its taksir is of three types. Al-j-r-t (45) through this, Vayāyīl resulted. For the second reason: al-f-l-m, Taghal, 13 letters, and derivation Hayā Nāyīl resulted.

Pisces: These were the qualities of the derivations of letters that I mentioned according to the statements of the scholars. Their rules are very strict, but however you perform them, you will succeed.

Chapter: On the Derivation of Planets and Hours

The seven days of the week revolve around the seven planets, the detailed explanation of which has passed in the commentary of the book.

The Sign of the Sun (Sunday):
