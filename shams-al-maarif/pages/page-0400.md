# Page 400 (English translation)
[400]

He is One and praise belongs to You; You alone are the Creator, and praise belongs to You.
O Allah, there is no deity besides You, and praise belongs to You.
Your proof exists, and so does praise.
There is no Compeller except You, and praise belongs to You.
There is no Dominant except You, and praise belongs to You.
There is no Provider except You, and praise belongs to You.
There is no Omnipotent except You; there is no Hearer except You, no Seer except You, and praise belongs to You, O Allah; You are Sufficient and Guide.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Best Opener.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Turner of trusts.
Praise belongs to You; You alone are the Lord of the heavens and earth.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Reliever of distresses and calamities.
Praise belongs to You, O Allah; You are the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful, and praise belongs to You, O Allah; You are the Best of Creators.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Best Forgiver.
You are the Best Helper.
You are the Best Provider of sustenance.
Praise belongs to You.
O Allah, You are Perfect and Exemplary, and praise belongs to You; You are the Giver and Creator.
Praise belongs to You, O Allah; You cause the day to enter into the night and the night into the day.
O Allah, You are Near and Responsive.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Rewarder and the Punisher.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Lord of Lords.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Cause of Causes.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Exalter of ranks.
Praise belongs to You, O Allah; You are the Creator of the heavens and earth.
Praise belongs to You, O Allah; You are the Commander of commanders.
Praise belongs to You.
O Allah, You are the Helper of the distressed.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Elevator and Heir; You are the Creator and Compeller.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Giver of life; You are Patient and Eternal.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Dominant, the Great Dominant, the Self-Sufficient, the Unique.
You are Most Appreciative and Glorious.
O Allah, You are the Witness and the Knowing.
Praise belongs to You; You are the Light of guidance, the Firm, and the Just.
You are the Messiah, the Mighty, the Compeller, the Proud.
You are the Creator, the Maker.
When you intend [something], put your trust in Allah.
Indeed, Allah loves those who put their trust [in Him].
Whoever puts trust in Allah, Allah is sufficient for him.
O Allah, place love in the hearts of Your servants from among the children of Adam, peace be upon him, and from among the towns of Allah—be they male or female, free or slave, young or old—with love, affection, tenderness, and mercy; draw their hearts and protect me from their evil, from those who conceal [mischief] and hold ill intention toward me, whose disbelief and treachery remove from me.
O Allah, I ask You by virtue of this: I have called out, namely, that You show me Your sanctuary by Your grace, and bring me to visit the blessed grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may peace be upon him.
O Fulfiller of needs, O Acceptor of prayers, O Allah, O Lord of the worlds, O Most Merciful, O Especially Merciful, O Source of Peace, O Guardian of the faithful, O Mighty and Compelling, O Proud, O Everlasting, O Light of every thing and guidance—He who created darkness with His light.
O Most Exalted above every thing, O Allah, I ask You to appoint for me a servant of this noble Surah, who will be my helper in every affair; whatever I intend and seek, accept [his service], and obedience too belongs to him, for he contains secrets within it; and whatever he is given, I will burn it with fire.
You are established by the virtue of this noble Surah over all those affairs which I command you.
Recognize the value of what has reached you and keep it protected from the people of falsehood.

[Boxed heading: Invocation of Surah Yasin, the Great Red]

This is the invocation of the Great Red; the Chosen Prophet, may peace be upon him, said: "Whatever matter Surah Yasin is recited for, it is sufficient for it."
His Honor, upon him be peace and blessings, said: "Whoever recites Surah Yasin purely for the sake of Allah's Self, his preceding and subsequent sins are forgiven."
Remember that the secrets of Allah, the Exalted, in the Quran are in Surah Yasin, and the secrets of Surah Yasin are in four verses of it: (the first) "A greeting, a word from a Merciful Lord" (the second) "And is He who created the heavens and earth not able to create the like of them? Yes, and He is the Knowing Creator" (the third) "His command is only when He intends a thing that He says to it 'Be,' and it becomes" (the fourth) "Glory be to He in whose hand is the dominion of every thing, and to Him you shall be returned."
Upon this verse, say: "Your desire shall be fulfilled."
This is the spirit and supplication of Surah Yasin; its virtue exceeds [that of] millions of secrets.
In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Ya Sin. [repeat] 7 times. "By the Wise Quran, indeed you are among the messengers." "Upon the straight path." 7 times.
