Scholars' opinions regarding the Hour:
Some scholars say that when the aforementioned number is summed and multiplied by the active participle, you will know the age of the world; and this is the method for knowing the conditions of kings and sultans, and these are the extracted secrets. From the result of this craft, which is called "The Tablet of al-Qadir," verification will guide you.

The arrangement of "Bismillah" and the extraction of names from it is as follows:

[Calligraphic arrangement of names and symbols. Above the main calligraphy are handwritten annotations: Ṣ, Ḥ, J]

Bism
Al-Hadi the Merciful the Compassionate

Muhaymin Mujib Khaliq Uthman Alan

It should be known that from these secrets, the purpose is the number that is derived from the name.

How long will a king's reign last:
When you desire its explanation, expand the name of God and remove the duplicates; then look at the name of that king who is upon the throne at that time. If his name falls outside the secret names, then duplicate it, and look at all the letters that are at the beginning. Then sum them and remove the duplicates according to the method of the science of letters, and then sum all of them and form compound words. Whatever has remained from them, look at it—one will know the duration of the king's sovereignty and the end of the realm during his time. And know that each letter is the name of a king, and it is essential that whichever astronomer, caliph, or sultan there may be, some letter from these will be in the beginning, middle, or end of his name. When this is the case, take the name of that king, the essential name, all letters, and the compatible name, and properly distribute and expand, and remove the duplicates. The condition of this king will become known to you, and the entire quality of his government and sovereignty will be known to you through the method of clairvoyance. And the method for knowing the duration of sovereignty is this: take the number of the name and multiply it by the numbers of this king's name along with the equal letters, and remove the thousands and see what remains—that is the duration of sovereignty. And these letters are: Sin Mim Lam Shin Alif Mim Ain Mim Lam Arib, inscribed in "Gift of Kings, Fragrant Glories," which is for the kings and ministers of Egypt, in which the method of signs and markers of the land is also mentioned, and the explanation of all events that will occur in every era until the Day of Judgment is contained within it.
