**The method for determining the nature (mizāj) of a person's name:**

The Indian spheres are hot and dry, whose number is 2640. The method for determining this is: when you want to know the nature of a name with which you are working, see what temperament it possesses. Look at the numerical values of the letters of that name, and which letter's number is greater. For example, in the name Yaʿqūb (Jacob), when we examined it, we found the element of the letter qāf to be predominant, because the letter qāf has a value of 100. The natures of the letters are as follows: first, fiery; second, earthen; third, windy; fourth, watery. These are the natures present in all simple letters. Know that without these elements that appear, nothing can be established. It has also become known that the letters of fire, wind, earth, and water are all present in this table. However, the sum of these elements applies to the good and evil, guidance and misguidance, truth and falsehood of all simple entities, and they are mixed together in both worlds. This explanation is very lengthy. If you wish to perform any action of good or evil in the world, do it in this manner.

**Achieve your purposes by incorporating fiery, earthen, watery, and windy letters according to the name:**

Generally understand it thus: if you have enmity with someone and you wish to destroy them, or wish to make them disappear, or intend to obtain some benefit or advantage from someone, then look at their name to see which element predominates in it. The letters of the element that predominates are dominant in that name; these are called "bodies," so that the rational letters may create a mixture among them.
