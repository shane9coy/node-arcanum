# Page 380 (English translation)
378

If you want to expel an enemy or a bad neighbor from a house, have this talisman (amulet) inscribed on a board of wood or lead on the roof of that house, and bury it under that person's door. Then the enemy or bad neighbor will leave the house, but do not perform this for anyone except the deserving one.

⟨ Chapter: The Operation of Binding People's Tongues ⟩

When you want to bind the tongues of one person or a group of people, write this talisman and place it in the front part of a grain container. You will see a strange effect. This is the form of the talisman:

[A horizontal sequence of occult sigils and cryptic magical characters, including a six-pointed star and various stylized marks]

aṣamtu lisāna kulli nāṭiqin illā bi-khayrin two times 5070 filmah infah ya ʿunqūdu wa-rbiṭi al-al-sinata bi-ḥaqqi al-zurdi ghijlan ghijlan samkhulṭamkhali salsalasala miytakjiyl hayya al-ʿajala al-sāʿata

⟨ Chapter: Remedy for Doubts During Prayer or Ablution ⟩

If doubts occur during prayer or ablution, write these names on paper to repel the obsessive doubts and keep it with you—the doubts will keep leaving.
