# Page 402 (English translation)
O God, make me among those who fear their Lord, and give them the good tidings of forgiveness and a great reward. O my Lord, on the day of my meeting with You, give the good tidings of forgiveness and a great reward. O God, I ask by Your wise and forbearing name and by the honor of Your most noble Prophet ﷺ that You do for me what is befitting me, for truly You are the One worthy of piety and forgiveness.

Then he should make supplication, and it will be accepted immediately.

Then he should say: "Allāhumma sakhkhir lī al-mulka wa al-malakūta lā ilāha illā anta yā dhā al-jalāli wa al-ikrāmi bi-rahmatika astaghīthu yā mughīthu āghithnī"—say it seven times. Then he should make supplication, and it will be accepted immediately.

Then he should say: "Qālū innā taṭayyarnā bikum ʿadhābun alīmun"—up to this point.

The Ritual for the Destruction of an Enemy:

In this verse there is a special property for the destruction of an enemy. Its procedure is as follows: Make an image of the enemy on the ground, take an iron knife without a handle in your hand, recite the aforementioned verse twelve times, and plunge the knife into this image. You will see a strange effect. After this, recite this supplication:

"Allāhumma aḥfiẓnī min balāi al-dunyā wa ʿadhābi al-ākhirati allāhumma lā tubaddil ismī wa lā taghayyir jismī wa lā tufarriq baynī wa bayna nabiyyika ṣallā allāhu ʿalayhi wa sallama wa allāhu khalaqa kulla dābbatin min māin inna allāha ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun"—up to this point.

"O God, protect me from the misfortune of the world and the punishment of the Hereafter. Do not change my name and do not change my body. Do not separate me from Your Prophet ﷺ. God Almighty created every living creature from water; truly God has power over all things."

Then perform prostration and make supplication; it will be accepted. It has great effect for repelling illness and sorrow. "Bārikafiyya ithdhillīn ḍalāla"—say it seven times. After this:

"Subḥāna al-mufarriji ʿan kulli maḥzūnin subḥāna al-munaffisi ʿan kulli madyūnin subḥāna al-muṭahhiri li-kulli mamlūnin subḥāna al-mukhalliṣi ʿan kulli masjūnin subḥāna al-ʿālimi bi-kulli maknūnin subḥāna man jaʿala khazā'inahu bayna al-kāfi wa al-nūni subḥāna man idhā arāda shay'an an yaqūla lahu kun fa-yakūnu"—three times "Subḥāna rabbika rabbi al-ʿizzati ʿammā yaṣifūna"—to the end: "Bismi allāhi al-raḥmāni al-raḥīmi al-ḥamdu lillāhi rabbi al-ʿālamīna nastaʿīnu"—to this point. Then make supplication, and it will be accepted immediately.

Then say: "Yā hādiya al-muḍillīna lā hādiya ghayruk ihdinā al-ṣirāṭa al-mustaqīma ṣirāṭa alladhīna anʿamta ʿalayhim wa malaktahum asrāra asmā'ika wa ajʿalnī yā raḥmānu yā raḥīmu mina alladhīna yakhshawna rabbahum bi al-ghaybi fa-jazāhum maghfiratan wa ajran karīman."

Then make supplication, and it will be accepted at that very moment.

Then say: "Innī āmantu bi-rabbikum fa-ismaʿūn"—up to this point. Then recite this supplication eleven times:

"Allāhumma yā man akrama ʿibādahu al-mu'minīna akrimnī bi-karāmati awliyā'ika al-muqarrabīna wa ʿibādika al-ṣāliḥīna alladhīna lā khawfun ʿalayhim wa lā hum yaḥzanūna allāhumma akrimnī bi-qaḍā'i ḥawā'ijī min anḥā'i faḍlika yā qāḍiya al-ḥājāti wa ajib daʿwatī yā mujība al-daʿawāti bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi al-sūrati al-sharīfati yā dhā al-jalāli wa al-ikrāmi allāhumma akrimnī min faḍlika wa karamika wa afḍal min faḍlika ahlahu wa lā tafʿal bī mā anā ahluhu fī al-dārayni ṣallā allāhu ʿalā khayri khalqihi wa ālihi wa aṣḥābihi ajmaʿīna."

"O God, O You who have honored Your believing servants with honor like that of Your close friends and righteous servants—"
