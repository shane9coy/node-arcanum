# Page 213 (English translation)
Then I raise my hand and ask: Make subservient for me, make easy for me, help me, and provide goodness for my affairs, O Allah, O He whose speech this is—this is His speech—I ask with His great speech, and I request by the promise of Al-Halim through Surah "Qul huwa Allahu ahad" [Say, He is Allah, the One]. O Allah, O He who numbered every thing and made them flow in measure, and causes creation to perish, and He is the Eternal Subsisting One, O He whose attributes cannot be described by those who describe, nor is He described by standing or sitting—this is so that You make subservient for me the servants of this Surah and the names, that they may serve me and obey me, for surely You have power over all things.
O Allah, O you spiritual servants of this invocation, I adjure you by the honored spiritual group who have been appointed with the celestial spheres—He created you from His light and placed you beneath His throne—accept with hearing, administer, I wish, I have adjured you by this invocation of the names and the Surah: bakhi arfaqush kalfahush batatahush kamtahush bahwash fafawsh.
O Ruqaiyal, I have adjured you by the angel appointed with the sphere of the Sun, be present by the right of Allah, the One beside whom there is no god, every thing is perishing except His Self, to Him alone belongs the command, and to Him alone all shall return. O Ruqaiyal, I have adjured you by the presence of the religion—accept, O religion, by the right of this angel whose command prevails over you. O Ruqaiyal, in the matter of memory, that you accept and execute, and you do what I have commanded you. O Jibrael, by the right of Sam, but that you accept, act quickly, and you do what I have commanded you. O Samiel, I have adjured you by the angel appointed with the sphere of Mars, be present.
By His right who commanded between kaf and nun, surely His command is—when He intends a thing, He says to it "Be" and it becomes. O Samiel, by the right of the Red Angel, be present and accept. O Ahmar, by the right of this angel, accept whose command prevails over you. Masaiyal, and by Damliya, that you accept, act quickly, and do what I have commanded you. O Mikael, I have adjured you by this angel with whom is appointed the sphere of Mercury, and by the right of Him whom eyes do not see but He sees, and He is the Subtle, the Aware, the star. O Mikael, by the right of Barqan's presence, accept. O Barqan, by the right of this angel's presence, accept whose command prevails over you. O Mikael, by the right of Asba shara siba, but that you act quickly, accept, and do what I have commanded you. O Sarfiel, I have adjured you, the angel appointed with the sphere of Jupiter. By the right of Allah, the Light of the Heavens and the Earth, accept. O Sarfiel, by Shamurash. O Shamurash, by the right of this angel, accept whose command prevails over you. O Sarfiel, by Rud, by the right, but that you accept, act quickly, and do what I have commanded you. O Aniel, I have adjured you, the angel appointed with the sphere of Venus, by the right of Him who knows the child in the womb, and what increases or decreases in the wombs of wombs.
Accept, O Aniel, by the presence of Zubai, by the right of this angel whose command prevails over you. Jial, and by Sahiha and Sadimun, who is the Lord of angels and spirit—but that you accept and do what I have commanded you. O Kasfiel, I have adjured you by the right of the angel appointed with the sphere of Saturn, by the right of Him who knows the apparent and the hidden. O Kasfiel, accept, by Bakur Maimun Abu Nukh. O Maimun, by the right of this angel whose command prevails over you. Kasfiel, by Aniel, 2 times, Hawaraq 2 times, Hazial 2 times.
O angels of the Lord of the Worlds, I have taken an oath. By the name of Allah, the Rahman, the Rahim, I adjure that you accept. By the right of Him who said to the heavens and the earth "Come, willingly or by force," and they said "We come willingly." By the right of the obedient Angel of the Earth, who brings humans forth from dust. By the honor of the Presence of Jibrael and his friend the Presence of Mikael. O my Creator, make the earthly one subservient for me, let him be my helper in obedience, let him accept my command. By them and by the Quran and "and the scheme of Allah, and Allah is the best of schemers"—surely I wish that one among you be struck with fear and have no helper. But that you act quickly, paralyze, strike with hemiplegia—this one for whom this supplication was made, and for whose sake I have [performed this].
