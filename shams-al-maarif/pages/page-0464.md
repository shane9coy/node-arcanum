# Page 464 (English translation)
[464]

The Supreme Name is the Great Name. Its commissioned spirit's name is Samāʾīl, under whom there are four chiefs. Each chief has 541 hosts under him, and each host has 541 commissioned spirits appointed. The supplication of the Supreme Name is this:

Allāhumma anta alladhī fataḥṭa ṭuruqa l-hudāya wa ʿarrrafta awliyā'a ka-asrāra l-kashfi wal-fatḥi wal-dirāya wa nawwarta baṣā'ira ahl al-ʿirfāni wa khallaṣtahum mina l-ḍalālati wal-ghawāya as'aluka bi-ʿizzi sha'nikum wa quwwati sulṭānikum wa stīlā'i amrika wa burhānikum an tufrighanī ḥīna'idhin li-l-ightishāli ilā fanā'i l-jamʿi wal-kamāli wa hdini bi-aḥsani l-nawāli wa ḥaqqi manāhiji bawāṭina l-wiṣāli innaka anta al-muḥsinu l-faʿʿālu

The Name of Al-Barr contains one letter of the Supreme Name. Its commissioned spirit's name is Qiyāsīl, under whom there are four chiefs. Each chief has 202 hosts, and each host has 202 commissioned spirits appointed. The supplication of this Name is:

Yā barru anta alladhī aḥsanta li-kulli makhūqin bi-qudratika wa anta alladhī agh'nayta kulla nāqiṣin wa akhfayta amrahu fī amrika wa anta al-muʿīnu al-mutafaḍḍalu ʿalā man aqbal ʿalayka bi-khulūṣi l-īmāni rājiʿan ilayka bi-l-qalbi wal-lisāni wa anta alladhī tufīḍu l-nadāta wa tushadiddu l-ʿiqāba ʿalā l-ṭughāti wa taʿfū ʿani l-mudhnibīna wa tubaddilu sayyi'ātihim ḥasanātin dhū l-ra'fati fī ḥaqqi l-zāhidīna wal-raḥmati fī ḥaqqi l-ṭālibīna wal-ʿizzati wal-kibriyā'i fī ḥaqqi l-anbiyā'i wal-rājiʿīna ilā yawmi l-dīni

[Muhammad Ḥabīb Ashraf]

[Eight-pointed star/seal]
