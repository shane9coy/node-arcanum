Section: On the Second Half of the Work

This is called "combination" (tarkeeb), and its meaning is that the moisture should be introduced into the calcined substance in such a quantity that it becomes fully mixed with it, and the moisture and the calcined substance both merge into one. For the calcined substance has absorbed this moisture with eagerness. Then when it is calcined again, it will appear in the form of earth, and if it is then placed in fire, the moisture will not separate from it; rather, it will predominate over it and work upon the essential components, and this moisture will not be able to cause any harm, for the spirit has restrained it in the fire. Had it been vapor, it would certainly have escaped, and when it escapes, it would contend against the spirit so that the effect of fire could not reach those components, and the heat would be eliminated from it, and this heat would merely result from the intensity of temperament. Therefore, when this calcined substance is placed in fire, this moisture will not emerge; rather, it will merge with the liquefied body and will clothe it like a garment. It should be understood that the calcined substance will merge with the liquefied body like a lover meets his beloved, for the moisture and the spirit have both combined to form one thing, and the effect of the aqrab (scorpion) alone will take effect. From these two, an excellent color will be produced, for they are analogous to the dyeing process in which one dissolves the color and then dyes the cloth with it. That is, the substance possessing the quicksilver is the cloth, and the moisture is the water through whose mingling the color penetrates the body.

The Method of Introducing Moisture into the Calcined Substance:

Now I shall tell you the combination through which moisture is introduced into the calcined substance. Among craftsmen of the craft, this calcined substance has many names: (1) Spirit (nafs), (2) Ramad (ash), (3) Ghul, (4) Bayd (egg), (5) Minkahal of the land of Mercury's valley, (6) Turab (earth), (7) Sakhra (rock), (8) Zill, etc. When this calcined substance is ready, place it in the solvent of qameesha, and a sufficient weight of refined solvent equal to its weight should be poured upon it and dissolved. After dissolution, then place it over fire; a black color will appear. This is called "baz-hat" (that which returns). Then, by the aforementioned method, perform hadhaniyah and dissolution so that it remains in fire. A slightly lesser black color than before will appear. This is "nar kham" (crude fire); they call it "nar al-had" (the fire of had). This will melt, and the moisture will appear from it, although it had not melted before. Among craftsmen, it is an established principle that when dissolution and hadhaniyah are performed in this manner four times with certainty, then its color will be white. From the solvent, use less than the amount of dissolved sulfur; perform hadhaniyah and purification by the aforementioned method. When this process is also carried out three times, it will become completely red.

Section: On the Explanation of This Treatise Which a Philosopher Wrote in Reply to His Student When His Student Asked Him About the Noble Stone

The Noble Stone, which is universal, has two types: spiritual and physical.

In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. It should be known that the Noble Stone, which is universal, has two types and two different forms. One is spiritual, the other physical. The first part is that in which the Moon, the follower of Mercury, and Venus are dissolved, and the second part is that in which the Sun, Mars, and Saturn are bound. For this reason, the wise ones call this stone the "microcosm," for whatever exists in the macrocosm—celestial spheres, stars, and so forth—all are present in it. I shall tell you such a combination that you can do it yourself; there is no need to do it in my presence.

The Elixir for Making Silver:

The combination is this: place the first part, as has been described, in a flask over gentle fire; when it begins to heat, remove it. Then smoke will begin to emerge. At that time, replace its head, and then place the empty vessel on top, keeping it inverted. Put it over fire once more, and make the fire intense; then cool it. Take the residue that has adhered to the top of the vessel and remove it, and place it carefully in a container.
