Chapter 18: The properties of the Name "Razzāq" [The Provider]—"The Elixir for the Expansion of Sustenance"

This name belongs to the dhikr of Michael (Mīkāʾīl). Whoever performs the dhikr of this Name, Allāh the Most High will make his food and drink easy, and his sustenance will be expanded. Whoever performs its dhikr abundantly on the night of the middle of Shaʿbān, and has it engraved on a ring and wears it, Allāh the Most High will grant him sustenance for the whole year. Whoever is named ʿAbd al-Razzāq [Slave of the Provider], it is appropriate for him to perform its dhikr, and it is also appropriate for him whose name is Jāmiʿ [The Gatherer].

Its letter-number is 315 and its ordinal number is 28. This is among the Names that are the gatherers of the secret of the category of the Unseen (sirr al-ghayb). Its letter-number is completed by multiplying the numbers of the masters of calculation among themselves. Then, multiplying both of them as one at the end of the total, its benefit is found only in one's own heart. In it is the power of the letter alif, and in it, as in all of them, the little worm of "bayn lā pāweh" is the engraving of alif's formation and the writing of zāy. In it is blessing and help for the seeker of sustenance. Effort is necessary in obtaining it; that number is deficient. Its 311 points to the Name of Allāh the Most High, "Ghaffār" [The Oft-Forgiving]. That is, whoever asks sustenance from someone, it is necessary that he show him compassion—this is related to its ordinal number, which is that of sacrificial production and its equivalent. That number is deficient; its 358 points to the Names of "Musabbib" [The Causer] and "Nūr" [Light], which are among His Names that are united with Magnificence. For this reason, the search for sustenance has destroyed people. One person said to Ḥātim al-Ṣamm: "From where do you eat?" He answered: "The treasures of Allāh are in the heavens and the earth." Its letter-numbers point to "Ḥalīm" [The Forbearing] and towards proximity. This is the description of its square.

Chapter 19: The properties of the Name "Kāfī" [The Sufficient]

If someone writes the Name of Allāh "Kāfī" [The Sufficient] in a good horoscope during Mars, gazes upon it frequently, continues the dhikr of this Name, reduces it to its number, keeps it with him everywhere, and continues the dhikr, then he will receive help in every matter and will be protected from enemies. But its operation will not be fully complete [damaged source: text obscured by watermark] its numbers' similarity to sustenance will be fully complete.
