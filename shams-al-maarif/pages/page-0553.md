[Heading:] The 186 difficult-resolving names of God in the Torah:

Know that from the interpretation of a letter, 26 names are produced, and these are the great names; and there are 186 of them, mentioned in the ancient Torah. For difficult matters, keep them with you. If you wish, take one day's portion: four names in one line, and each name has seven letters. In one letter, as it is in the minor and Connected chapters, and these total seven letters in one line. Write them. The seal, annexed to the minor, and Sarayil, His servant and Lord, the speech of the group of Kajeer, and they are after the minor. The beginning: with the two children, both first names together, both second names together, from the name of the operation. The speech of Kajeer has 47 degrees. In one name, the interpretation: upon the last alif, the degree, by degree, the full degree is desired, then and the number, when the minor concludes, from its number write the word by interpretation.
