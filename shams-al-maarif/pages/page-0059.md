# Page 59 (English translation)
57

**Manzil Shulah:**
Its figure is this: `[Three dots arranged in a triangle: ∴]` Its letter is Qaf. This manzil is of mixed nature. When the Moon descends into it, by the command of God Most High such a mixed spirituality is produced that performs works of good. Works of middling degree should be performed in it. One should not wear new clothes nor make talismans in it. Spiritual healing should not be performed. Solitude is better in it. The child born in it will be "unlucky," "a liar," "ill-tempered," and "depraved." Its incense is costmary and musk.

**Manzil Na'aim:**
Its figure is this: `[Seven dots in a horizontal line: .......]` Its letter is Ra. When the Moon descends into it, such a spirituality is produced in the world that performs lowly deeds. Its temperament is fiery and auspicious. Love, honor, and good deeds are found in it. Alchemical works are prepared in it. In it begins the study of sermons, wisdom, and legal jurisprudence. Talismanic operations are performed in it. This time is suitable for building structures, planting gardens, and wearing new clothes. As long as he wears those clothes, he will remain in comfort and happiness, until those clothes become worn and old. The child born in it will be blessed, auspicious, and successful in all endeavors. Its incense is frankincense.

**Manzil Baldah:**
Its figure is this: `[Four dots in a square-like pattern: ∷]` Its letter is Shin. Its temperament is fiery and inauspicious. When the Moon descends into it, a spirituality of enmity and hatred descends. Therefore, operations of enmity are performed in it. Alchemical works are performed in it, but spiritual healing, agriculture, travel, meeting with kings, marriage, buying and selling, building structures, wearing new clothes, and performing rituals are not good at this time. The child born at this time will be unlucky and deceitful. Its incense is bitter apple and gum arabic.

**Manzil Sa'd al-Dhabih:**
Its figure is this: `[Two dots side-by-side: ..]` Its letter is Sad. Its temperament is earthy, inauspicious, and mixed. In it descends a spirituality of enmity and hatred. Whatever works are performed in it will not have good outcomes. Kings and rulers at this time are angry and harsh. Buying and selling, and those who are betrothed whom one wishes to release are better off. Opening a shop, treasure that has been found, and concealing secrets are suitable in it. The child born in it will be beautiful, blessed, worldly, and deceitful. Its incense is lign aloe.

**Manzil Sa'd al-Bula':**
Its figure is this: `[Two dots side-by-side: ..]` Its letter is Fa. Its temperament is mixed. When the Moon descends into it, by the command of God Most High the spirituality of evil descends in the world and causes corruption. This manzil is both excellent and corrupt. Buying slaves and animals, discussing matters of benefit, revealing schemes of comfort, and digging wells and building terraces are suitable works for it. The child born in it will be blessed and virtuous. Its incense is camphor.

**Manzil Sa'd al-Sa'ud:**
Its figure is this: `[A row of dots, partly obscured by watermark: ........]` Its letter is Ghain. Its temperament is celestial, airy, and mixed. When the Moon descends into it, all previous influences are removed. All works are correct in it. Operations of love, marriage, and meeting with kings and officials should be performed. Spiritual healing should be performed. Working with kings, leaders, and officeholders is best. For operations of love, this time will be most excellent.
