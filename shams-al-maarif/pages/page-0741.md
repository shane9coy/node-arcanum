# Page 741 (English translation)
From the lights of investigation, the lights of knowledge and realities are obtained:
A thousand [investigations] and the realities of knowledge and the lights of the theophanies obtain from this Light, and by diving into the lights of the Holy Quran, it extracts pearls like gold from it. The light of the Quran is divine disclosure itself. Allah the Most High says:

"And We have sent down to you a light that is clear." [Quran 33]

To be characterized with this Name brightens the mirror of the heart with the lights of thoughts through it. Recite this verse:

"Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth." [Quran 33]

Indeed, eat lawful sustenance and abandon comfortable things, keep permanent fasting, and always be devoted to ablution and purity. In this manner, practice spiritual discipline for forty days.

From the recitation of the Quran, light emerges from the mouth:
When he does this, he will see light emerging from his mouth during recitation, and when he raises his gaze toward the Throne and the Chair, he will witness radiant lights, and all the world and the lights of the higher realms will be unveiled to him.

Hadhrat Umar al-Faruq saw the state of the battle of Nahavand through the light of the inner [self] and called out: "Sarayah"—and he was heard:
This story of Hadhrat Umar pertains precisely to this light of disclosure: that from the Holy City of Medina to Nahavand, he said to the army: "Ya Sariyatu al-Jabala" [O Sariyah, the mountain!]

The blessed presence's vision encompassed all the face of the earth:
The blessed presence had been granted disclosure of the light of Hell and Paradise in the circle of Banu al-Najjar, and the land that His presence's community had conquered had passed before his vision. When the seeker recites this Name audibly upon the recitation of this verse "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth," the angel "Izra'il" will be present.

The illuminated heart, increase in honor:
The special property of this Name is that if one engraves it on a gold or silver ring and keeps it with him, and recites this Name according to its numerical equivalent, his heart will become illuminated and awesome dignity and majesty will be attained beyond measure. Its form is this:

And the supplication is this:
"In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Light; You have illuminated the heavens with the light of Your guidance; with the heart in their remembrance, upon the declaration of Your Oneness; the knowledge of the Light, the Clear, the Guide, the Strong, the Firm. O You whose Light has no likeness in all the worlds; that is the confirmed Existence which has no end. O Allah, [I ask of You...]" [unclear] "I have asked of You."
