# Page 149 (English translation)
Pour upon me from the lights an overflowing of the Abode,
Upon me and revive my dead heart with a covering of glory.

And clothe me with awe and grandeur,
And restrain the hand of enemies from me with gentleness.

Indeed, and shelter me from an enemy and envier,
By the right of Shamakh, lofty, safe, elevated.

Indeed, and fulfill, O my Lord, by the light my need,
And facilitate my affairs after hardship that has passed.

And deliver me from every terror and distress,
With the utterance of a wise one, cutter of the land, it has flowed forth.

And grant safety by sea and give me the best of its land,
And spread over me the veil and heal from the malice.

And deafen and mute, then blind our enemy,
And silence them, O Possessor of Glory, with al-Bahusamat.

And in the party of Maʿ with my dissolution and my burning,
I have fortified myself by the Great Name from the malice.

And unite the hearts of all the worlds entirely,
Upon me and clothe me with acceptance by way of the name Shalhimat.

And facilitate, O Possessor of Glory, affairs for me by the bundle of gentleness,
To Him hastened the lizard of the desert and it gave greeting.

And loosen the knots of hardship, O Bahi, it has been agitated,
And O He from whom our sustenance grows by His generosity.

And bless for us, O Allah, in all our earnings,
And by the Name cast them from afar with dispersal.

O Bahi and Yahu and O best of Bazirs ... [handwritten note between columns: in the bread of Hadi]
By you we repel enemies from every direction,
So You are my hope, O my God and my Lord.

And O best of those asked and most generous of those who give,
And O best of hopes unto a nation that has passed.

Light my star by the Name with radiance and delight,
Throughout time and days, O Light of Jalajat.

By the right of the presence of Your Court, and grant me pardon for what it has committed,
To him came Aji, excited, O my God, joyful.

And Jalajulut answered with acceptance, it has trembled,
Noble, he cleared Jalajat, numerous, excited.

By the counting of broom and saffron when it strikes,
And by the Baharat of Tabriz and Mother Tabarikat.

A leader of the lamp of the sea, by land it has illuminated,
And Quddus of Barqhut, by it the fire has been extinguished.

By Tamtam of Mehrash, for the fire of enemies it has surged,
By Hal [unclear] Shal.

By Anukh, Bayamlukh, and Barukh Barasua,
By the erased one, noble, impetuous, it has been revealed.

A ram for Tihram, it has risen and been magnified,
Throughout time and days, O Bahi, it has been agitated.
