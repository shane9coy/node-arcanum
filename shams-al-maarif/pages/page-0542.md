# Page 542 (English translation)
In order to acquire these sciences, one has traveled everywhere:
For this same knowledge, I traversed the entire earth and beheld its wonders, and I witnessed the divine blessings and the great and small Abram, and I entered within them. Thus I saw twenty-six treasures inside the great sanctuary (haram), which the people of Greece had placed there before the flood. I opened those talismans.

In one book, a description of Simiad Qisa was found
And among them, a book was found in which the description of Simiad Qisa was present. I edited its contents and composed a treatise among these talismans, and at the beginning of each problem, I wrote k for the sign of alchemy, so that it may be known that this comes from the Greek word (kimia).

Let it be known that the people of the eighth and ninth centuries and those after them will deny these sciences, although those people have become few, even if one among them seeks a master (murshid) who would show him the path to these sciences. Yet if such a master is found for him, surely God Most High has appointed angels over these sciences, and the philosophers who composed books on these sciences have not done so in vain or without purpose. Rather, the excellence of this knowledge and the reality of purity, asceticism, repeated practice, recitation, lawful consumption, and sincere hope reveal it.

The Investigation of the Word Il:
Let it be known that there are various opinions regarding the addition of the word Il, but we, departing from all of them, mention only its original wisdom, which is this: This word contains two letters. One is alif and the other is lam. This totals four letters, whose number is 51, and these are its numbers which were subtracted from the original numbers. When they were added in this same form, the cube was as it were completed, and the desired name appeared before you. Let it also be known that these letters belong to three natures. Alif is fiery, ya is terrestrial, and lam is watery. The ya is repeated for this reason: that alif is martaba (ordinal) and ya is raddif (following). When it is repeated, it becomes like martaba. Some scribes, due to their errors, have composed these subjects in the books, and some scholars, afflicted by such errors and following the imitation of other books, have gone astray, although in reality this is the error of the scribes. In summary, the addition of these letters alif, ya, and lam to every cube must be done in the extraction.

The Inferior Muqall is with the Superior Alawi:
It must also be known that for every Alawi there is also an inferior Muqall, whose rule is as follows: When you wish to extract a working, look at the name and make the Jummal letters either Alawi for the Alawi, and add the three letters Bari to the inferior letters by combining them. Those letters are: Tayyish. Then call the inferior Muqall with the superior Muqall, and the inferior with the one who possesses blood and weapons.

The Original Law is Followed:
This is the original law, and upon it every working is encompassed. If seven, or five, or three letters result for you.

The Method of Extracting the Inferior Muqall:
The word Tayyish is added at the end of the proper name. I have explained both rules in detail, namely the word Tayyish and Il. The word Tayyish contains three letters. Ta is 9, ya is 10, shin is 300. These total 319 in number. Ta-ya-shin, whose number is 319. This rule is derived from that law whose discourse about it is complete with their proof, and this rule is sound and excellent. That is, the word Il and the word Tayyish are the word of number forty-one or 41, and the word Tayyish is 319. When these are added together, they become 360, and these are the degrees of the heavens.
