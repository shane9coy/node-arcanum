# Page 556 (English translation)
**Chapter on the Virtues of the Craft**

**God taught Adam the names of things along with minerals:**

God Almighty taught Adam, peace be upon him, the names of everything, and also taught him how minerals are extracted from them. Then when these matters became known to him, he was instructed in the craft of gold and silver. Then Adam, peace be upon him, desired to teach this craft to his son Seth. So Adam, peace be upon him, gathered his children and said: "God Almighty has commanded me to teach this craft to the one among my offspring who is the most devout in worship." When Seth heard this, he prepared himself for worship and worshipped God for forty years. At that time, God Almighty sent revelation to Adam: "Seth is My saint; teach him the divine craft." Adam mentioned this matter to Seth, and Seth said: "I fear that because of this I might stop my worship." In short, Adam taught him this craft, and he learned where gold, silver, pearls, rubies, and emeralds are found, and it became known to him that every hard thing can be melted and every brittle thing can be softened and every liquid can be frozen, and he saw that this is an extremely easy matter. And the substances from which these are made are in the sight of people extremely insignificant things that people step on with their feet on the road and, considering them worthless, throw them away.

**Idris, peace be upon him:**

God Almighty took Idris up to heaven, and this knowledge and spiritual discipline, which had then been transmitted from Adam, was the knowledge of astrology. From this Idris derived the knowledge of the craft of artisanship. When he received news of the flood of Noah, he carved this knowledge inside the Egyptian pyramids for fear of the flood, so that it would remain safe from the flood.

**Musa, peace be upon him:**

And it is known that when God spoke with Musa, peace be upon him, Musa complained to God about poverty. God granted him the knowledge of the craft, and He made the Torah its place of worship and religion. He performed the prostration of gratitude in the divine court, and he prayed that God Almighty would make this knowledge a mercy and sustenance for the Children of Israel, and that He would make him more from little. Some people say that this craft was acquired by Musa, peace be upon him, in the house of Shu'ayb.

**Qarun learned from Musa, peace be upon him, how to make gold and silver:**

From Musa, peace be upon him, Qarun learned and filled his treasuries, and pride and self-admiration developed in him, about whose status God Almighty says: "And We gave him treasures whose keys would burden strong groups of men." He said: "I was only given it on account of knowledge with me."
