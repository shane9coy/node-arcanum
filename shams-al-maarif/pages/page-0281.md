[Chapter 32: Revelation of the Hidden Secrets of the Properties of the Name Khabir]

The mention of this name is suitable for one who wishes to know something hidden in a dream or while awake. If its square is written while Mercury is in its dignity and placed beneath the head while sleeping, he will receive information about secret matters.

Whoever mentions it with seven days of seclusion and spiritual discipline, spiritual matters will bring him full news, or they will give him news about kings. Its number is 812. This spirit is the odd one. Its components are 268, pointing to two great names of God Almighty: Al-Khaliq (the Creator) and Al-Wasi' (the Expansive), because only the one who created things and encompassed all things with His knowledge can give news of the reality of things. He is Al-Latif Al-Khabir (the Subtle, the Knowing). Its letter-names are 209, pointing to two great names of God Almighty: Al-Akhir (the Last) and Al-Wahid (the One). This is the attribute of its square:

[Chapter 33: The Properties of the Name Halim - Protection from the Rage of the Ruler and Popularity Among the People]

Whoever recites this name at the time of a ruler's anger, his anger will subside. If its square is written while the Moon is in its dignity and kept with him, his character will be good and every breath of his will be pleasant, and people will incline toward him, and he will remain safe from distress and anxiety. This is its glorious name.

Its number is 88; it is the Spirit of spirits and the odd one. Its components are 93, pointing to the name Quddus of God Almighty. The name Halim is particularly harmonious with the meaning of Karim, and for this reason the Messenger ﷺ used to often supplicate: "O Lord, forgive my people, for they do not know." This is also why the number of your blessed name, O Messenger ﷺ, points to this name. Its letter-names are 153, pointing from one consideration to the name Jalil (the Majestic), and from another consideration to the name Jalil.
