Chapter 35: The Secret Rules of Jafr "Letter-Based"

This knowledge is transmitted with a sound chain of narration from His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, may God be pleased with him, and it is also transmitted from His Eminence Commander of the Faithful, may God honor his face. Before His Eminence Commander of the Faithful, it was with His Eminence Solomon, upon him be peace, and with His Eminence Asaf ibn Barhiya, concerning whom God says: "He said, 'He who has knowledge from the Book.'" And before them it was in the Preserved Tablets (al-zabur al-awwalin) and in the Scrolls of Adam. What is intended by this knowledge is the writing of the conventions of the people of language, and these are the letters: alif, ba, ta, tha, jim, ha, kha, dal, dhal, ra, zay, sin, shin, sad, dad, ta, za, 'ayn, ghayn, fa, qaf, kaf, lam, mim, nun, waw, ha, hamza, ya. These are thirty-two letters in total, of which twenty-eight are Arabic letters and four are non-Arabic letters, and they are original in the script of the pen. And those four letters are: gim, zhe, pe, che. The thirty-third letter is established from the Scrolls of Adam. God the Exalted says: "And He taught Adam all the names" up to "and We taught you what you did not know." In this verse there is an allusion to this very knowledge, and these letters are written in the Preserved Tablet along with the Pen. "He taught man what he did not know" indicates this, namely that he expresses the intention of the heart through the tongue by means of letters. God the Exalted taught this knowledge, and they point to speech, and they arise from the loss of meaning of words. This knowledge was first taught to His Eminence Adam, upon him be peace, and after him it was attained by all the prophets and messengers from among his descendants, upon them be peace, who mentioned it in their books.

Chapter: When you wish to work with this, first take the Name of God and extract its letters from the earth. Begin first with the Greater Chapter (bab kabir) and derive its letters' calculations, its numerical values, and its degrees from which extraction takes place. Thus the "chapters" will be produced: 60 [plus] 60, changing the "transforming powers" (quwat munqalib) to the chapters of fasting. From this comes the extraction of the speech of the pre-Islamic era that matches the numbers: the chapters of 60 from charity, the corresponding number for the days of a year, the hours of day and night. One method: in 700, the hours are from the chapter of leaven (bab khamir), and the chapter of the spoils (bab al-ghanim) belongs to this name, and in it there are twenty-four letters. Corresponding to the number of the mansions (manazil), the total letters of the names are 900. In the same manner from alif to tha, and from alif to ghayn: each mansion is one name. Then divide these letters into twelve "suns" (sam), and by "sun" is meant the thirty-day stay of the sun in each sign of the zodiac. The total results are twelve. The first source is Aries, and this is the original language and the mother of languages. Then Taurus, then Gemini, and so on.

When you have come to know this, when you wish to perform an operation, perform it according to the sign of the zodiac, the time and hour according to the date, and determine the mansion. Take this name according to the consideration of the mansions and letters in which you are, in accordance with the method that has passed through this name, and do not exceed its limit. Then your operation will never go wrong. When you have inquired from the owner of the sign about it and his mother's name, and from the seeker the thing sought and his mother's name, present them according to the initial degrees of the letters of speech on the times of bodies. In accordance with the quantity that has passed through this name in which you are, remain with the body on the same day in which you are; do not exceed this. When the name of the seeker matches some of the types of that section in which you are, and it is above the name of the thing sought and below the name of the seeker, and the name of the thing sought is above the name of the seeker in the types of the chapter, then substitute the portion of the name of the thing sought in it. That is, make its first last and its last first. Then establish the portion of the name of the seeker with the calculations of the letters of incantation. The trouble will be related to the one to whom it is attributed. Then extract the names of this chapter. Begin with the first letter from its letters, and the operation of the letters will be the name of the thing sought. Then make each line a separate incantation (mantra), but first extract from it the divine names. Then extract these letters of incantation, and from them extract the names of the angels, and also extract the names of the jinn. This operation takes place in every supplication, whether beloved or despised. In your operation you should never mention the rewards of the portion of the thing sought, nor speak between them.
