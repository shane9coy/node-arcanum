# Page 223 (English translation)
[111]

O Allah, You are the Sovereign, the All-Powerful, the Dominant, the One, the Light, the Severe in Seizing. O Allah, one of Your servants has acted defiantly and wrongfully toward me, and You are the Ruling Just One. I bring him before You for requital of his wrongdoing. I have made You my advocate against him; send down upon him such [punishment] that heaven and earth would be unable to repel, until he recognizes Your curse and the value of well-being upon himself. Break his skull as the pebbles broke the skulls of the People of the Elephant. Make him destroyed, ruined, and humiliated. For the sake of their sins, seize them—there is no one besides Allah to save them from You.

May Allah destroy them, and destruction be for the disbelievers as well. They were made thus, and their places were not visible. Have you not seen what your Lord did to the People of the Elephant? He made their plotting lead them into error. He sent upon them ABAABIL birds that struck them with stone pebbles, and He made them like eaten straw. Then recite this verse one hundred twenty-nine (129) times: **ALLAAHU LATIIFUN BII'ABADIHI YARZUQU MAN YASHA'U WA HUWA AL-QAWYYU AL-'AZIIZ** O our Lord, O Latif—one hundred (100) times in one breath. From the beginning of "Ya Latif" until the operation is complete, one must remain in continuous [recitation]; do not speak to anyone. If one speaks to someone, one must begin the operation anew. The operation must be performed with sincere intention—not like people say, "We perform the operation, and it may happen or not." The operation will not work in this manner. Understand that it will certainly happen. If this supplication is also added to it, it is most excellent:

**ALLAAHUMMA INNII AS-ALUKA YAA LATIIFAN FAWQA KULLI LATIIFIN YAA MAN 'AMMA LUTFUHU AHLA AS-SAMAAWAATI WA AL-ARDHI. ALLAAHUMMA INNII AS-ALUKA AN TATALAṬṬAFA BIIY MIN KHAFIIYI LUTFIKA AL-KHAFIIYI AL-LATHII ITHA LATAṬṬAFA BIHI LI-AHADIN MIN KHALQIKA KAFAA FA-INNAKA QULTA ALAA YA'Lamu MAN KHALAQ WA HUWA AL-LATIIFU AL-KHABIIRU. ALLAAHU LATIIFUN BII'ABADIHI YARZUQU MAN YASHA'U WA HUWA AL-QAWYYU AL-'AZIIZU.**

[Translation]

O Allah, I ask of You, O most Merciful beyond every mercy, O You whose grace is pervasive among the people of heaven and earth. O Allah, I ask of You to show me mercy through Your hidden grace—Your most hidden grace—which, when You show it to any of Your creation, is sufficient. For You have said: "Does He not know who created? And He is the Subtle, the Aware." Allah is Gracious to His servants; He gives sustenance to whom He wills, and He is the Powerful, the Mighty. Recite this supplication also one hundred twenty-nine times.
