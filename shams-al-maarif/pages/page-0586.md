**The Letter Jim**

This letter is cold, moist, and majestic. Like air, and it is among the letters of the hierarchical degrees. Whoever works with it, its work is done.

**Fevers are Reduced and the Absent is Made Present by its Operation:**
When this letter is written on paper according to the number of names that begin with Jim, and given to drink to one suffering from feverish heat, it will be very beneficial. If one wishes to make an absent person present, then write the letter Jim three hundred times along with the prescribed fasting and the name of the desired [person] on cloth of saffron color, and make it into a lamp, and light it in oil of zanbil, and continue reading the prescribed fasting. There will be no delay in the present arrival of the desired [person] covering such a distance.

**The One Who Has a Jim Tablet Will Be Honored and Venerated**
If three letters Jim are written on a red stone or gold or red copper tablet in the name of the Angel of Conjunction, and three letters are written on each letter, and it is necessary that the stone or tablet be triangular, and the writer keeps it with himself, his honor and greatness will increase, and the entire world will revere and honor him. And if the letters Jim—that is, two names that begin with the letter Jim, such as Judah and Jiw—[are written] on the membrane of a monitor lizard with red light, and kept with oneself, the acceptance of Jim will be obtained.

**For Facilitating Childbirth:**
If the shape of the letter Jim, which is triangular, is written, and Jim is written around it, and the name of the Perfect One is written on it thirty times, and tied around the waist of the pregnant [woman], the delivery will occur immediately. It should be known that the realms of this letter are those which bring coolness to the world, so that the heat of the sun may not burn the world. When these letters are written on a ring, and only the prescribed fasting is written around it, and kept with oneself, and the invocation is read, and [the letter] "Jim" is spoken eighty-three times, thirst will never occur.

**Paralysis and Kidney Stone Illness Are Caused by its Operation**
If this letter is written on paper or clay that was picked up from a filthy place, along with someone's name, and [the text] is written, and that person is given it to drink, that person will be afflicted with kidney stone disease. And if [it is] given in food, and the servant of the letter is made to dominate over it, the person will be afflicted with paralysis disease. And if [the letter] is written on cloth with the name of the desired [person], along with the great and beautiful names, and [the text] is written, and kept with oneself, the acceptance of Jim will be obtained.

**For Discovering Treasure**
If the letter is written on paper along with the prescribed fasting, and one goes to the place suspected of containing treasure and opens the scroll, it will open immediately.

**The Letter Jim is Also Used for Protective Incantation**
In the invocation of this letter, one should remain in a state of purity, enter [the ritual state], and not be negligent in reading the invocation. And the form of the letter should be written and tied to one's head. Whatever name of protection is given to you from among them, remember the time when you read it, and this letter, from which the work is to be done, will be made present
