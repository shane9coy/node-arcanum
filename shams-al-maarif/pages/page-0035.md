# Page 35 (English translation)
**Chapter Concerning the Letter of the Human Soul**

The letter of the Throne is Alif, the letter of the Chair is Ba, and the letter of water and Saturn is Mim. Thus it continues to the Moon as described above.

**Chapter on the Types of Letters:**

There are types of letters. Some of them are written from the right side, such as Arabic letters, and some are written from the left side, such as Roman, Greek, and Coptic letters, among others. Those letters that are written from the right side are called hazz, and those written from the left side are called mant. Understand this.

**The Number of Explicit Letters and Their Secrets:**

The letters are twenty-eight: Alif-Lam-Alif equals twenty-eight. The Moon has twenty-eight mansions of moderation. Since fourteen of them are above the earth and fourteen below, for this reason these fourteen letters are combined with the divine names. These fourteen letters are as you see: Alif, Nun, Mim, Ta, Tha, Ra, Za, Za, Ta, Dha, Lam, Sad, Dhad, Sin, Qaf.

And the other fourteen are apparent with them. They are: Waw, Alif, Ba, Ta, Tha, Jim, Ha, Kha, Dal, Dhal, Ra, Za, Sin, Shin, Sad, Dhad, Ta, Dha, Ain, Ghain, Fa, Qaf, Kaf, Lam, Mim, Nun, Waw, Ha, Ya.

The first letter is Alif. After it come the other letters, such as Ra, Waw, and Ta. If any intelligent person contemplates these letters with careful reflection, he will find them matching their reflections before they exist in action. Understand this. The letter Alif is one in number, and in destiny it is the subtle spiritual force. For this reason, the secrets of lights and utterances come from it, just as they come from the secrets of the letters of actions. There are many secrets and benefits of numbers in the world of humanity. Almighty Allah has arranged them by His power, just as He has granted the secrets of benefit in letters, and just as the effect of supplication and amulets in letters is apparent with various categories of divine names.

Know that there is no need for any specific time for the letters. They are written by those who practice spiritual exercises among their people—let whoever desires do so. And they work with the triangular tablet, and their connection is with the world of transcendent realities.

**Qualities of the Heart Letter**

**Understanding's Elevation, Honor, Victory over Enemies, and Liberation from Captivity:**

The letter Dal has four in number. Whoever multiplies four by four and writes its numerical proportion on the day of Monday, which is the day of the birth of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—the day of the proclamation of the world, Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—is in the honor of the Moon, while the Moon is in the third degree of Taurus in Mars. Let it be purified from malefic influences, and let the time be of the Moon. The writer must be in complete purity and pray two units of prayer. In each unit, recite Surah al-Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, and three times Surah al-Ikhlas, then offer it as a gift. Then write Dal on paper or a tablet. Whoever keeps this with him, Almighty Allah will grant him illumination and understanding. His status will be elevated in both worlds. And when a prisoner keeps it with him, he will be freed from captivity. If one keeps it and confronts an enemy, the enemy will flee. Bind this talisman on your arm, by which you will prevail over it.
