# Page 355 (English translation)
O God, I ask You, O God, O God, O God, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O He upon whom neither drowsiness nor sleep comes.
O God, I ask You to request the sanctity of Ayat al-Kursi. Then ask for your need, and for this task it is obligatory upon Almighty Allah to fulfill it, and he should recite the Noble Salutations upon the Prophet as much as possible, for truly he shall succeed in his endeavor who recites Ayat al-Kursi during the day—Almighty Allah shall protect him until night; and whoever recites it at night shall remain protected until morning.

**The practice of forgiveness of sins and removing anger:**

It is also a special property of Ayat al-Kursi that whoever recites it after each prayer, Almighty Allah shall forgive him all his sins. Whoever recites it at the time of sleeping shall be protected from Satan throughout the night. And if one is overcome by anger, let him recite it and spit to the left—Satan shall flee and his anger shall depart. I shall relate here a most noble and great supplication, but its benefits have been shortened so that if it falls into the hands of unqualified people, they may not obtain benefit from it and harm others. The supplication is:

Allah—there is no deity except Him, He alone persists in existence and eternity. The essences of created beings do not establish their realities beside His essence, nor do their attributes beside His attributes, nor do their names beside His names, nor do their actions beside His actions, nor is there any besides Him. There is no beauty in reality except His beauty, and no majesty except His majesty, and He is eternal in His perfection—the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Everlasting upon His Throne by the permanence of His sovereignty, and all creation is subservient to the perfection of the knowledge of Him. They know that He is One in His kingdom, Unique in His eternity. Mighty is the eternity of His eternality, despite the variation of their intellects and religions—all of them return to the reality of the knowledge of Him. They know that He is the Creator, the Provider, the Giver of life and the Causer of death, and all affairs return to Him. As for the knowers and the verifiers, they have become lost in the reality of the knowledge of Him; what has illuminated their hearts and their visions is the unveiling of the depths of knowledge placed within them. They have become lost in the seas of the love of Him and what He has bestowed upon them thereby. They have dived into the waves of the depths by the seas of the glistening of the turbulence of His power. So they have acknowledged their incapacity to comprehend the knowledge of Him, and they have drowned in the seas of His kingdom. They have known and verified that there is no deity except He. He has guided to Allah until He has established and brought to life their hearts and illuminated their visions and their intellects, so they did not witness in creation anyone besides Him, nor any Lord except Him. They have acknowledged for Him their incapacity—neither slumber takes Him nor sleep—that is, no period of inattention from creation for the created, nor sleep from the comprehension of the known. The command of Allah, when He intends a thing, is only that He says to it "Be," and it is. Glory be to Him in whose hand is the sovereignty of all things, and to Him you shall all be returned. He is far removed from indwelling, change, union, beginning, end, connection, and separation. Nothing is like unto Him. Before all things, and the return of creation and their origination is to Him. He is, in His kingdom, the First and the One, the Unique, the Singular, independent in His Self among the unseen realms from conjectures and understandings. His is whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth and all existing things. There are witnesses for Him, and for His wisdom there are knowers who know that there is no deity except Him. His is whatever is in the heavens—who is there that intercedes with Him except by His permission? The inhabitants of the heavens and the earth glorify Him, and there is nothing but that it glorifies with His praise, but you do not understand their glorification. Every speaker speaks by His permission, and every recluse exists by His knowledge. Knower of all things, and His is over all things, and everything is in need of receiving the power.
