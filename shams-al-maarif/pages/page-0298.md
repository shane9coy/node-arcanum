Chapter 64: Properties of the name Qayyūm "His command is established outwardly and inwardly" — the end of destitution

Whoever remembers this name abundantly, Allah the Most High will establish his outward and inward affair. If he maintains this state, everything will be established with him. The remembrance of this name is prepared for one whose work is Joseph—its hidden subjugation is not hidden. Know that Qayyūmiyyah is specific to Allah the Most High. Allah the Most High says: "Is he who is watchful over every soul for what it has earned?" (Quran 13:33). And He says: "And He is Allah besides whom there is no god; He is the Knowing, the Powerful" (Quran 3:54). In another place it is revealed:

"And He is with you wherever you are. And Allah is Seeing of what you do. Lord of the two Easts and Lord of the two Wests. So wherever you turn, there is the face of Allah. Indeed those who pledge allegiance to you, they are pledging allegiance to Allah. Allah's hand is over their hands. Have they not known that Allah is He Who accepts repentance from His servants and takes the charities, and that Allah is the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful?" (Quran 57:4, 9-11)

The pure hadith: "Indeed charity falls into the palm of the Merciful—I was sick and you did not visit me, and I was his hearing and his sight" (Bukhari, Muslim).

The name Qayyūm of Allah the Most High is explicit with the encompassment of His oneness. Every name among His names appeared with creation from the outward to the inward, and between these two is the barrier (barzakh). Allah the Most High said:

"Alif-Lām-Mīm. Allah—there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting. And We did not create the heavens and the earth and what is between them except in truth" (Quran 3:1-3).

Just as with the name Allah, there is no second god, because creation sees it through His oneness. The name Qayyūm is specifically for the Uncreated—one's work is not in creation. Know that the Greatest Name is in these two verses:

"And your god is one God. There is no deity except Him, the Beneficent, the Merciful" (Quran 2:163).

"Alif-Lām-Mīm. Allah—there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting" (Quran 3:1-3).
