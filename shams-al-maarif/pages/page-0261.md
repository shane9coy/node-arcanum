# Page 261 (English translation)
With kindness, suffice me, so that the souls of the saints may find comfort and the souls of the fortunate may be pleased for You. Expansive greatness and all the kingdom belong to You.

O Allah, I ask You with every name that is in Your knowledge—truly You do not deprive those who ask, and whoever asks with them You do not reject. I ask You to fulfill my need that I desire, and grant me good completion—truly You know what I intend. Before You is the severity of the angels, except [He is] the Powerful.

O Allah, I ask You, and with Bismillahi al-Rahman al-Rahim I implore You, that You put upon me the garment of Your lights that repel the thought of enemies from me with humiliation, and regret comes upon their hands. Grant me recognition of the seal and freshness, and the souls of the purifiers strive, and for that the eyes of the observers become cool, and with it the secrets of the gnostics travel—truly You are the Knower of the Unseen, the Teacher of secrets, the Opener of mysteries, and their Explainer. Praise and glory belong to You, and good and benefit are in Your hand of power.

O Allah, send blessings upon Your prophets, messengers, peace be upon them, the near angels, the righteous, the saints, and all the people of obedience. Convey to them our greeting and good wishes, grant us their intercession, along with our asking.

O Allah, I have directed my hope toward Your great essence, I have made my conjecture excellent in Your great knowledge. You, have mercy upon me and my parents, grant forgiveness for me and all Muslims. O Allah, do not deprive my hope of Your essence, and do not make my good assumption about You false through Your bounty. O Allah, how could we depart from Your gate unsuccessful when You have commanded us to supplicate? O Most Merciful of the merciful.

O Allah, I ask You to have mercy upon me when my final time comes, and my deeds cease, and I don my shroud, and I depart from my place. O Lord of lords, O Cause of causes, O liberator of necks and opener of suffering—harm has befallen me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.

In the name of Allah, He who is the healing, the sufficient, and the pardoning. Surely Allah is the best guardian; He is the Most Merciful of the merciful. There is no deity besides Him.

Then say: "La ilaha illallah" one hundred times, "Ya Latif" 129 times, "Ya Kafi" 111 times, "Ya Halim" 88 times, "Ya Mujib" 55 times, "Ya Salam" 131 times, and "Ya Hafiz" 899 times.

Then after that, ask supplication for whatever religious or worldly need you have—it will be accepted.

This supplication is a very great secret. Understand it, and may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad ﷺ, his family, and his companions.

Chapter 16: The Beautiful Names and Their Useful Talismanic Forms

This chapter is a hidden and concealed pearl that has come from the valley of Safa toward the friends and particular Qalandars of the Sufis, who ride upon the wings of the spirit of inspiration. They obtain understanding of Arabic meanings, numerical divine names, luminous realities, and the secrets of mystical knowledge, contrary to the sciences of mere outward forms.

I say: The divine names in the Quran and Hadith are always the name and the named are the same; the name is derived from the named. Those of unveiling who are illuminated upon the realities of names are very many. When we recited the names of Nadir Quddus, Shakur, and the Beautiful Names, they amounted to three hundred (300). Some say they have reached six thousand (6000) names.

From this disclosure, the purpose is to point toward the brevity of the science of hidden knowledge and the concealed secret, which is good for the seekers. Whoever has a portion of this knowledge destined for him, it is obligatory upon him to sit in seclusion, take up the torch of spiritual wayfaring, collect these radiating lights, and adorn himself from the plenitude of truth.

When those who have desire for this knowledge become acquainted with its wonders at that time, Exalted Allah has said: "No soul knows what comfort has been hidden for them, as a recompense for what they used to do."
