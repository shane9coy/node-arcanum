# Page 459 (English translation)
157

O Qayyum, You are the one who established the pillars of existence and spread in the hearts of Your servants the secret of bowing and prostration, and You connected Your beloved Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, and those who followed him to the Praised Station, and You are the one who takes charge of all matters upon which all things depend, and You are light upon light. I ask You by the secret of Your Qayyumiyyah in Your creation and by the manifestation of Your lordship in the appearances of the flash of Your lightning, that You grant me reliance upon You with the description of health and rectitude, and it is the reliance of the spiritual seeker upon the desired object that is beneficial in the beginning and the return.

[Urdu heading] On the subjugation of the dependent of the Name Al-Wahid

Name Al-Wahid: It is a great name. It contains one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. The name of its dependent is Ḥuṭayʾīl. It rules over four rulers. Each ruler governs fourteen rows, and each row has fourteen assigned dependents. The dependent descends upon whoever mentions it. Its supplication is:

O Wahid, You are the one who created the light of Your love in the hearts of the chosen ones, and You placed the secret of Your love in the receptacles of the mysteries of the prophets, and You are the one who manifested the radiance of Your beauty in the beloved of the people of love and union in the place of splendor and the station of praise, that You grant me the perception of the spirit of Your self in the first and the last, and attraction toward You in the inward and the outward, and do not cause me to belong to any of Your creation, for You are God, the Strong, the Powerful.

Name Al-Majid: It contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its dependent is named Rīyā Tāʾīl. Under him are four rulers. Each ruler governs forty-eight rows, and each row has forty-eight assigned dependents. The supplication of the Name Al-Majid is:

O Majid, You are the one who brought people from nonexistence into existence, and You created everything by Your power, and You are the Lord, the Glorious, the worshipped, and You are the Powerful, the Dominant, and You are the Hidden and the Manifest, and You are the Necessary Existence until the end of all purposes, and You are the Knower of what is in the earths and the heavens, knowing, powerful, wise, seeing. I ask You by the greatness of Your sovereignty and the most beautiful of Your divisions, departure from this house in goodness, and support me with a guide from You, O Lord of the worlds.

Name Al-Wahid: This name contains one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. The name of its dependent is Lamghīʾīl. Under him are four rulers. Each ruler governs sixty-six rows, and each row has sixty-six assigned dependents. The dependent descends upon whoever mentions it. The supplication of the Name Al-Wahid is:

O Wahid, You are the Wahid in Your eternity, and You are the Everlasting, and You spoke to Yourself by Yourself in the stations of the names, and You are the Knower of what is beneath the dust and what is above the highest heavens, the One who ascended upon Your throne by Your power, upon which everything is together. I ask You by the light of Your perception and by the collectivity of Your oneness in the essence of the pulpits of Your sanctity, that You make me accepted and obeyed among Your servants, O Lord of the worlds.
