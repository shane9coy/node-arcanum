# Page 634 (English translation)
This name is most suitable. When it is written according to its numerical value and tied around the child's neck, it will be protected from crying and whimpering.

Whoever wishes to perform the retreat (khalwa) for this name must refrain from showing humility before any creature, attend to the care of his family and children, and not ask anyone for anything. He should maintain complete piety and detachment, and his heart should be content and patient.

**Benefits of "Allah Al-Rahman Al-Rahim": The divine manifestations in religion, this world, and the hereafter are countless:**

All the power within creation and all beneficial things exist solely through the manifestation of these two names, "Al-Rahman" and "Al-Rahim." Allah, Exalted be He, says:
**"Then look at the traces of Allah's mercy, how He revives the earth after its death"**

This pertains to the mercy of generality. Whoever makes the recitation of this name obligatory upon himself, Allah the Exalted will have mercy upon him in this world and the hereafter, and an elevated rank will be granted to him. When it is placed on a silver plate around the neck of a crying child, his crying will cease. Whoever wears it engraved on a ring, Allah the Exalted will grant him affection and mercy. Whoever mentions it according to its numerical value, Allah the Exalted will elevate his rank.

The retreat for this name lasts forty days, provided he remains steadfast in spiritual discipline and continuous remembrance. When he writes its talismanic figure and keeps it with him, and recites the name after each prayer, the assigned spirit whose name is "Jibreel" (Gabriel) will appear. He is the commander of four officers, each of whom commands two hundred ranks. He will appear before the reciter and fulfill his needs. Its talismanic figure is as follows:

And its invocation (dhikr) is as follows:
**In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Merciful over all creatures [unclear: wa-ka-] among existing beings, and You are Al-Rahman. I ask You to subjugate for me Your servant Jibreel to fulfill my need and what I desire. My Lord, I ask You for manifestation upon my existence [unclear: wa-bi-] of my purpose, and reveal to me the solar particles [unclear: in every street] which appear white for the afflicted [unclear: as a found object], and illuminate my heart with the light of the name Al-Rahim so that the spirits of the mighty may submit to me and [unclear: the trembling] of the rebellious souls, and reveal to me the reality of the world of sovereignty and the heavenly realm, and of glory and omnipotence, so that I may attain proximity to You. O Near One, O Loving One, O Merciful One, may it be so.**

Whoever performs the ritual activities along with the supplication and recites these names, Allah the Exalted will ease all his difficulties for him.
