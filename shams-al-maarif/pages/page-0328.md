Its square is this:

[A 15x15 magic square containing fifteen names of God in Arabic script. The names listed in the first row from left to right are: Allah, Latif, Adl, Jabbār, Shakūr, Hakīm, Khabīr, Rahmān, Haqq, Salām, Hayy, Jalāl, Quddūs, Ghanī, Hādī. Each subsequent row is a rightward cyclic shift of the previous row, where the last name of the preceding row becomes the first name of the current row.]

All needs will be fulfilled.

Whoever recites these luminous secrets 66 times and sends blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ 137 times, then whatever he asks from Allah the Most High will be granted to him. For kings, rulers of states, and seekers of ranks, it has special effectiveness. Every king should recite it abundantly, then his kingdom will expand, his subjects will become obedient, his word will be exalted, the people of faith will heed his words, and all will become subservient to him. Among these names is the Greatest Name and the Supreme Treasure; honor it, for it is from the divine mysteries.

Know that each of these names has a specific manipulation. Whoever gathers the letters and numerical values of any of these names at one time, keeps them with himself, and recites that name—Allah the Most High will grant him the station of unveiling, and the veil will be lifted from him. When the number is odd, it demands the action of effect. When it is even, it demands completion.

Some people of Allah say that the essential numerical value in Arabic, its fractions and its forms work like the Greatest Name. In the Holy Quran, there are appropriate verses for each name. We have arranged the names in a different sequence, and I have named it the Subtleties.

[Heading in a decorative frame: Strange Subtleties and Properties of the Holy Quran and the Divine Names]

The First Subtleties: In it are ten names that work for those in fear—granting safety; for those struck by terror—granting comfort; and for prisoners—granting release. They are: Allah, Al-Rahmān, Al-Rahīm, Al-Barr, Dhu al-'Afw, Al-Mannān, Al-Karīm, Dhu al-Tūl, Dhū al-Ikrām.

The Second Subtleties: The subtleties of opening the elevated sciences of the noble rank. Whoever makes these names his recitation and litany, Allah the Most High will open the subtleties for him, there will be blessing in all his affairs, and sciences and wisdom will gush forth from his heart like springs, and the station of unveiling will be attained by him. They are: Allah, Yā Al-'Alīm, Al-'Azīz, Al-Halīm, Al-Khabīr, Al-Mubīn, Al-Qahhār, Al-Hādī, 'Allām al-Ghuyūb.
