# Page 455 (English translation)
**The Subjugation of the Muwakkal of the Name Matin and the Name Wali:**

The name Matin is a great name. It contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its muwakkal is named Fahiẓāʾīl. Under him are four rulers; each ruler oversees 500 rows, and in each row 500 muwakkals are assigned. The muwakkal comes to its dhikr-knower. Its prayer is:

Yā Matīnu anta alladhī raḥimta fī qulūbi ahl al-tawḥīdi wa anta alladhī makkanta awliyāʾaka fī ṭalabi hal min mazīdin wa anta alladhī jaamaʿta al-ʿulūma bi-asrihā fī qāf wa al-qurʾāni al-majīdi asʾaluka bi-al-ilāhiyyati wa bi-saṭi kitābika allāhu ayyāhu an takshefa ʿan qalbī sirra asrāri al-kāʾināti wa an tajdhibanī bi-al-mayli ilayka ilā aʿlā al-darajāti wa an tarfaʿanī ilā dhīnat al-mutaqīna asʾaluka al-quwwata wa al-qudrata al-tāmma an tuthabbitanī ʿalā bābika bi-al-aḥwāli al-sālimati innaka anta allāhu al-ʿālimu bi-al-sarāʾiri wa al-khafiyyāti

**Obtaining a High Rank:**

The name Wali is a great name. Its muwakkal is named Kazāʾīl. Under him are four officers. Each officer rules over 48 rows, and in each row 48 muwakkals are assigned. The muwakkal comes to its dhikr-knower and obtains a high rank for him. The prayer of the name Wali is:

Yā Walīyu anta alladhī aḥnayta quwā al-ʿuqūli wa al-baṣāʾiri wa aẓhart maknūnaẗ al-ḍamāʾiri wa anta alladhī rafaʿta liwāʾa al-ʿizzi fī awdiyati qulūbi ahl al-sharāʾifi anta al-ḥajību wa al-mawlā wa al-ẓāhiru wa al-ḥākimu wa al-qādiru asʾaluka bi-ramzi man ijtabaytahu min al-awliyāʾi wa sirri man aḥbabtahu min al-anbiyāʾi wa bi-nūri qudsika al-mūlahabi bi-jawāmiʿi al-kalimi an tanṣuranī ʿalā aʿdāʾī wa an takūna lī fī al-shiddati wa al-rakhāʾi

**The Subjugation of the Muwakkal of the Name Ḥamīd:**

The name Ḥamīd is a great name. It contains one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. Its muwakkal is named Jiyāʾīl. Under him are four officers. Each officer oversees 62 rows, and in each row 62 muwakkals are assigned. The muwakkal comes to its dhikr-knower. The prayer of this name is:

Yā Ḥamīdu anta alladhī ḥamdta nafsaka bi-mā yalīqu min jalālika wa anta alladhī alhamta ʿalā lisāni nabiyyika wa awliyāʾika wa anta al-maḥmūdu al-muthnā ʿalayka bi-ḥamdi nafsika azalan wa abadan wa anta al-maʿrūfu li-man il-tajāʾa ilayka dāʾimatan sarmadan asʾaluka bi-sirri ḥamdika al-nāzili fī qulūbi ahl wilādika an tarzuqanī qurbaẗ tāmmāẗ wa zulfaẗ ʿāmmāẗ wa ijʿal aʿmālī wa akhlāqī ḥamīdaẗ wa ʿaqāʾidī ṣaḥīḥaẗ wa nafsī bika sāʿīdaẗ wa arzuqnī bi-nūrika [unclear] ḥattā akūna maqbūlan ʿindaka fa-inna nafsī bika [unclear] anta al-ḥaqqu al-dāʾimu wa al-maliku al-qāʾimu
