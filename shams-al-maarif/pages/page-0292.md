Chapter 51: Properties of the Name of the Martyr (Shahid), protection from excess and deficiency, and the rank of martyrdom

One who mentions this Name will attain perfection in rank; if he preserves it, he will be characterized by sincerity of unity and honor. He will be far removed from excess and deficiency in his character. This is from the Most Glorious. It is most appropriate for one who desires and seeks the rank of martyrdom. I have taught this name to certain people, and the rank of martyrdom was achieved by them. Whoever writes it on paper during the first hour of Friday and binds it upon his heart, all will praise him. May Almighty Allah grant him awe and dignity. Its number is 319. This is a primary number because unity is included in its meaning. Almighty Allah has said: "And sufficient is Allah as a Witness." Its letters' names (asmā') refer to 1319, pointing toward that divine inspiration, because the ship sails by the knowledge of Almighty Allah, as mentioned in the Holy Quran. And this is its figure:

Chapter 52: Properties of the Name of Truth (Haq), steadfastness in obedience and knowledge of hidden secrets

Whoever reads this Name frequently, Almighty Allah will grant him holy courage upon obedience. The realities of matters become manifest to him. He becomes aware of hidden secrets. Only fine points are revealed to him. His speech becomes elevated. By means of this Name, Almighty Allah keeps the faithful firmly steadfast. Whoever inscribes its square under the ascending sign of Scorpio upon anything whose preservation is desired, that thing will remain stable in its condition. But before doing so, he should mention this Name to such an extent that its operator becomes dominant over it. And he should write this verse around the square: "Zāban mā yanfa'u al-nāsa fa-yamkuthu fī al-arad" (useless dust that does not benefit people, so it remains on the earth). Its number is 108. Thus it is first, twice even (zawj al-zawj), and an odd number with excess. Its 21 divisions (691) refer to the names of the Patient and the Truthful, because bodily health is the secret of physical well-being, and it is the portal of the spirituality of the spirit (sarā zawj al-rūh). Its 1319 refer to the Great Name (Jalīl).
