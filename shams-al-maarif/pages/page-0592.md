# Page 592 (English translation)
Reply and trust in so-and-so, by the formula Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh al-'Aliyy al-'Aẓīm. Ṭā'.

Translation: "O Allah, increase my desire for You and my inclination toward the things that are with You. Among these matters in which I hold affection for Your remembrance, make me more inclined. Protect me with Your hidden grace and clothe me with the garment of Your light and beauty, by means of which I may obtain assistance in revealing the secrets of this region, by whose nature the mountains were shaken and were shattered by the awe of the Lord of Majesty. Blessed is your Lord, the Mighty, free from what the disbelievers attribute to Him. O servant of the letter Ẓā', appear quickly by the command of God, who is the Lord of the Worlds, possessing glory and beauty. Blessed is He, and Exalted is He. Behold, hearts find tranquility through the remembrance of God. O letter Ẓā', reveal your secrets to me, by the glory of that pure Essence which has neither begotten nor been begotten, nor has any people or tribe. O appointed one, appear and accomplish my such-and-such task for me, by the glory of a thousand Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh."

The game will come to him of its own accord.
When you recite this supplication in the desert, game will come to you from all directions of its own accord, and the game for him is this:

Answer, O Lord Ghalfashā'īl, by the right of Saqṭiyyūs, Ḥazāhīẓ.2 He descends. Answer, by the right of Bimawh al-Waḥā al-'ajal, this hour. Ṭā'.

The Letter Khā':
This letter belongs to the mysteries of creation, and its numerical value is 600, because it is related to the Throne and is of the first rank.

The sick person will become healthy.
Its property is to rectify illnesses. Its composition is as follows: Write this letter with the name of the patient and the effective names on a Chinese vessel, dissolve it with a little honey, and administer it. The patient will recover in a few days.

He will not burn in fire and desire will be extinguished.
If any person recites the universal names during the days of intense heat at the time of sunrise and sunset, heat will not harm him however much he travels, nor will he feel thirst. Those who are people of resolution enter fire by the influence of this letter, and no harm befalls them; rather, the fire is extinguished. Nullifying desire is also a special property of this letter. When it is engraved on a ring with the appointed name and the formula for breaking fast, wearing it will be beneficial.

Know that when the letter Khā' occurs in a Syriac or Hebrew divine name, this ruling applies; and if it appears within the name, the operation will be stronger. The vigil of this letter is extremely magnificent. After each prayer, recite the invocation seventy times. A bright light will appear before you and, having been requested, will assist you. When you say, "Answer, O servant of the letter Khā', and do so-and-so," he will appear immediately. If you wish to make him subject to you and his tribes, sit in seclusion and recite the names and say, "O letter Khā', unless you answer and bring me the prince Hanāyīl." The appointed one will appear. His invocation is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. 5 Praise be to the Clement, upon those who disobey Him. O Allah, O Clement, Creator of the sick, and by Your knowledge I ask You by the status of Muhammad, upon him be peace. O most merciful of the merciful. O Allah, guide me and help me against those who wrong me, and direct me in the fulfillment of needs, and make me one who seeks refuge in Your command, and I seek help through Your word, O Praised One.
