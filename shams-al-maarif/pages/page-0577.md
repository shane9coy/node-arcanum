# Page 577 (English translation)
The Lights by the Right of God the One, the Dominant, the Giver

The Second Article:
These names are very many, and [draw] 11 5811 818. Write these names on the original paper, and throw some red sulphide forward, take the paper in your hand, and point toward any person—immediately they will fall and become unconscious. And if you point toward a pregnant woman, her fetus will be miscarried. And if you point toward animals, [the rider] will fall from the horse. And if you look at an arrow, it will reverse and hit the one who shot it. And likewise, every striker and thief will meet the same fate. The design to be written on the paper is this: [sigil: ۴ م و صه لا و اللّٰه مھو]

And recite this prayer:
ابلا[د] هبواه آه اهیا ول هـ ر غ غ ع لا لا 384 و د د ۱ باش اشهواش ایاش الوحا العجل الساعة تو کوا یا خدام ط ح ح 111175861139911181 these names, and answer and hasten with such and such, by the right of the One Who says to a thing "Be" and it becomes.

The Third Article:
Take the number 111 of thekri and a little vermilion, go into the jungle, place a little red sulphide in front of you, behind you, to your right, and to your left, recite the design seven times. A river will become apparent at this place, and as long as you do not lift both of these, you will not be seen from any direction. This is a very great talisman, and this is the same talisman that most of Pharaoh's sages made. And in the same way, when you wish to dry up a river, take a little red sulphide and throw it exactly into the river. Those who see it will perceive that it has dried up. And if you wish to drive mad any canal, recite this discourse upon it seven times. A spirit called d-f-l-l-i will appear; say to it: "Annul this talisman"—and it will be annulled. This incantation is written upon thekri.

The Fourth Article:
Regarding particular salvation, when a person has become trapped in a place of calamity, it is necessary to write these letters on the earth: ط ط ط, sprinkle a little red sulphide before yourself, stand upon it, recite the aforementioned prayer seven times, and extend your hand toward your destination. The sukul will deliver it and bring you to a place of safety. And if such safety is not available, take a large copper basin, fill it with water, recite the design once, take the paper in your hand, throw a little red sulphide before you, and descend into this basin—you will vanish from people's sight. At that time, go wherever you wish. Draw this design: [sigil: ط ح م و م ح مھھ ططط]

And speak this discourse: ابلداه یا روه لوا 5151512 515 لهه لھلوا اجب طیلقا وافعل کذا و کذا—whatever is spoken will come to pass.

The Fifth Article:
Mix vermilion with paper, make it into paper, and write these names in a knot: الله الله 11011 111111111—make it into a candle.
