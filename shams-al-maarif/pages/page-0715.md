# Page 715 (English translation)
Understand the mystery of the divine will and know that the point of primacy is the reverse of the circle of mystery.

The Station of the Sincere Ones:

This is the solution for the sincere ones, for they are the ones who possess true proximity. Since they obtain knowledge from the affair of the luminous realm, this is the first creation of the substance of mystery—the first creation of the lights from the side of the divine command, and the first creation within the Pole of the Affair. Then the point of extremity, which is the chief, exists for this reason: so that the rank of the sincere ones may be perfected within their stations.

The learned practitioners know these degrees—I have only mentioned them here so that observers may develop eagerness and become acquainted with the virtue and nature of knowledge. Its discourse is that through which the mysteries of the Divine Names are unveiled, and these Names have no concealment. I have written their specific properties in my book "Shams al-Anwar"—refer to it there.

[Boxed Heading: Chapter Three - The Virtues of "Al-Qadir Al-Muqatadir" (The Powerful, The All-Powerful)]

The meaning of both [names] is "the Possessor of Power," but there is emphasis within "Al-Muqatadir," and power is expressed in the sense that with it truth is found. The one who is "Qadir" (Powerful) is whoever wishes to do something and does it, but it is not a condition that he must necessarily do it.

The establishment of the Hour [of Judgment] is according to His will—He establishes it whenever He wishes:

Allah is capable of establishing the Hour, but He will establish it at the time He wills, because He has decreed the time for it in His foreknowledge. This does not create any flaw in His power, for He alone is the Creator and Originator of all things and their Preserver.

Allah Has No Assistant Counselor:

Allah has no need for another's assistance. He alone is the Exalted Lord. Although the servant may possess some divinely granted power, it is not like His power—for the servant accomplishes things only through His power. We have explained this in detail in the Book of Divine Science, and whoever recites this Name with sincere devotion observes that all things exist through divine power, and Allah alone is their Determiner. He alone is the Creator of things along with their actions.

Fire Does Not Burn but Rather Becomes Cool:

Thus fire does not burn except by the command of Allah Most High; it does not possess the power to burn of itself. This matter is not hidden from the spiritual traveler. Nimrod did not burn Abraham, peace be upon him, because the attribute of burning had been invalidated.
