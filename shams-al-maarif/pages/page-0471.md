The People of the Sanctum devised a mode of writing; in this mode they recorded their sciences. In our age, some people place the abjad letters out of their proper order and consider this their own invention, thinking it superior. They are in error. The consequence of this will fall upon them. This is altering the divine arrangement, especially when this concerns the divine names. Exalted God says: "And whoever alters the favor of God after it has come to him—surely God is severe in retribution." Had the secret of these letters not been preserved, the Qurʾān would not have been revealed in these letters. Therefore, the Exalted God swore by these letters, as in: "Alif. Lām. Mīm. Nūn. By the pen and what they inscribe," so that the people of knowledge might understand the value and dignity of the letters. Now we shall explain the properties of the letters.

**Chapter 32: On the properties, magical squares, regencies, governance, and day of the Arabic letters**

**The Letter Alif:** The letter alif is the first and luminous. Its numerical value is also one. Its rank is the foremost. It divides the letters among the elements. On this subject, scholars have written extensive books. The disagreements among scholars have been recorded in writing, but all are agreed that alif is the primary. There are two kinds of magical squares:
It has two squares: the minor and the major. The minor is this: the alif. The major is this: the alif standing upon the square. The Indian square is like the numerical square: a, b, j, d. They have other squares as well. These are not hidden from those who reflect and ponder—for example, the square of alif: alif lām fā'; "a," "l," "f," "alif," "lām," "alif of lām," "fā'"—these are the hearts. Each square has distinct properties and secrets, just as this letter was created first. It is the first number, the first element. The Exalted God appointed for it a day of the week according to its temperament and honor. This letter has two forms that are identical. "A" "A" the minor and the Indian are written in one manner. It is the origin of the intellect. In its primacy, all secrets are hidden.

**The Command of God to Write with the Pen:**
The Exalted God commanded the Pen: "Write whatsoever shall happen until the Day of Resurrection." The Pen placed its head upon the Preserved Tablet, and a luminous point came forth; upon being extended, it became alif. For this reason, it is the primary, and the beginning of God's name is from the word alif.

**The Method of the Operation:**
Whoever carves or writes the alif upon a golden tablet or saffron-colored paper in the hour of the sun on a Sunday, anoints it with perfume, and carries it with him—his awe shall persist. Whoever meets him shall be filled with terror. He shall be protected from all evil. He shall be granted the fortune of charity. He shall worship. The magical square is this:
[Magical square of letters and numbers:] A A A A 111 111
Writing this square upon the hand during the circle of blessings is a correct practice. If afterward one repeats alif with musk upon a copper vessel, hanging that vessel between two trees with a thread, and drinking the water after washing—this is beneficial. Whoever drinks this water shall be protected from every affliction and misfortune, and if it is caused by the evil eye, there shall be healing, and there shall be blessing in the household.
