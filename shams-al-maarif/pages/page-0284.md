# Page 284 (English translation)
And Allah—the Most High—says: "And Allah is behind them, encompassing." Allah the Most High has encompassed them from behind, while in every thing there is His existence and manifestation. And His concealment is wisdom appropriate for those near forgiveness, and it indicates His specialness. Allah the Most High has described this thing with wisdom as He has described it with knowledge. Allah the Most High has said: "And indeed it is in the Mother of the Book with Us, indeed it is sublime and wise." This is from the aid of the spirit, and the odd number is augmented upon its numbers—yet he says: "Ali is the Most High, the All-wise." Its letters' numbers are 200, pointing toward the divine name of Allah the Most High: "Malik al-Mulk" (Master of Sovereignty), and this is its form:

Chapter 38: Properties of the Great Name for inspiring awe in hearts—innumerable benefits.

Whoever repeats the remembrance of this name abundantly, everything becomes insignificant before him; they become frightened upon seeing him. This remembrance is mostly from the Glorious One. It should be performed before kings so that they consider themselves insignificant before it. Its number is 232. This is "even of the even" and "odd deficient" of the quarter of the third. Its letters point toward two great names of Allah the Most High: al-Baṣīr (the All-Seeing) and al-Wāḥid (the One). Its square is in this form:

Chapter 39: Properties of the name Ḥafīẓ (the Protector)—attainment of safety and protection from every kind of fear and harm.

Whoever repeats the remembrance of this name abundantly during travel, Allah the Most High will keep him in His protection until he returns. Whoever writes it on some paper and places it in something while in the mansion of Jupiter, he will remain safe. Whoever repeats the remembrance of this name abundantly will remain safe from every harm. Whoever is afraid during travel, this name quickly provides intimacy, because whoever remembers it remains safe from places of fear and sees no harm. Many times I myself [unclear] was trapped in perilous places; I performed the remembrance of this name and witnessed its strange effect, which cannot be described. Whoever engraves it on a silver ring, let its number be complete, let him diminish the letters in the interior of the seal and wear it. Then even if he sleeps among wild beasts, no harm will befall him. After that, let him say ten times: "Ya Ḥafiẓ, aḥfiẓnī" (O Protector, protect me), "O my Protector, protect me"—and the purpose will be attained. Its letter-form and numerical form are as follows:
