An Extremely Great Beneficial Prayer

From the letters of this talisman, according to the abjad calculation of da-d-zay-jim-tay-yaw, the tenth letter—a highly beneficial prayer spoken orally—is taught, and it is this:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-asmā'ika al-ḥusnā kullahā mā 'ilimtu minhā wa-mā lam a'lam yā hūwa yā wājidu yā aḥadu yā hādī yā bāri'u yā bādi'u yā baṣīru yā badī'u yā bāsiṭu yā bāqī yā jalīlu yā dā'imu yā wārithu yā wadūdu yā ḥayyu yā ḥakīmu yā ḥaqqu yā ḥalīmu yā ṭāhiru yā muṭahhiru ajib da'watī wa-qḍī ḥājatī yā rabba al-'ālamīn

And this statement has passed before: the letters of the alphabet correspond to the numbers of the twenty-eight mansions of the moon, of which fourteen mansions remain above the earth and fourteen below it. When the first mansion begins to descend below the earth, the fourteenth mansion begins to come exactly above. This is always so in this manner. For this reason, there are fourteen dotted letters and fourteen undotted letters. They are: undotted letters are bā', tā', thā', jīm, khā', dhāl, zāy, shīn, ḍād, ẓā', 'ayn, ghayn, fā', qāf, nūn, yā', and alif; and the dotted letters are alif, ḥā', dāl, rā', sīn, ṣād, ṭā', 'ayn, kāf, lām, mīm, hā', wāw, and lām-alif. May you know—and may Allah the Most High grant you and us success—that the undotted letters are the mansions of auspiciousness and the dotted letters are the mansions of inauspiciousness. A letter with one dot is closer to felicity. Letters with two dots are intermediate in inauspiciousness. Letters that have three dots are the greater impurity, such as thā' and shīn. Contemplate this: you shall know that the forms of the mansions are different and do not resemble one another. The moon and sun are both round, and in them too there is a subtle secret whose explanation is impossible, because revealing the secrets of wilayah is disbelief. When the moon enters the linear formation of mīm-mīm, extraordinary signs appear from it, the explanation of which is very lengthy. Some of them will be mentioned later. Walls also have ears, so silence here is most excellent. Understand the signs I have revealed before and contemplate them—you shall find guidance.
