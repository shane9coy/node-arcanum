# Page 354 (English translation)
**352**

The Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting, Who sustains all creation and Who endures forever—neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him. Indeed, slumber and sleep belong only to created beings. His is whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who is there that intercedes with Him except by His permission? Who is there that possesses power over anything that You possess? All created beings are beneath the compulsion of Your greatness. He knows what is before them and what is behind them, while they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him; and He is the Most High, the Most Great.

Our Lord, our Lord—our master, our master—our patron, our patron! You are the One Who gives and withholds. You are the One Who raises and lowers. You are the One Who fulfills and intercedes, and nothing in the earth or the heavens is hidden from You. I ask You by the hidden gentleness of Your providence and the majesty of Your glory that You send blessings and peace upon the Most Great Beloved, the Most Honored Prophet, the Most Venerated Messenger—our master and our prophet Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace. O God, by the honor of his pure and blessed Family, by the honor of all his Companions, by the honor of the Followers and the Followers of the Followers who acted beautifully toward them until the Day of Judgment, I ask You to gather me among their company, beneath their banner, and to aid me with their support. Amen, O Lord of the worlds.

God alone is the one—apart from You, all hopes are cut off and disappointed. Apart from Your threshold, all paths are blocked. O one whose trust is worthless! O God, I ask You by Your magnificent name—Allah, there is no god but He, the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting—by which You endure forever, whom neither sleep nor slumber takes. For You is whatever is in the heavens and the earth. Who will intercede with Him without His command? Who possesses power over what You possess? All creation is beneath the compulsion of Your majesty. He knows what is before them and behind them. Whatever is in the breasts, You are the Knower of it, and You also know what we conceal and what we declare. They cannot encompass anything of His knowledge except what He wills. His throne extends over the heavens and the earth, and You are the One Who encompasses all things with mercy and knowledge, and You have knowledge of all things. O You whose guarding them does not fatigue Him! O God, You give and withhold. You alone raise and lower. You alone hear and see. Nothing in the heavens or the earth is hidden from You. I ask by Your hidden favor and the majesty of Your glory that You send blessings and peace upon the Beloved of Greatness, the Most Honored Prophet, the Most Venerated Messenger, upon him be peace and blessings. By the honor of the pure and blessed People of the House, all their Companions, and the Followers and the Followers of their Followers, I ask You to raise my resurrection among their group and to help me with their aid. O Lord of the worlds!

**Religious and worldly needs will be fulfilled:**

Whoever asks God with this supplication whatever he desires, it will be granted. When anyone has a religious or worldly need, it is binding upon him to perform four units of prayer in the middle of the night. In each unit, he should recite Surah al-Fatihah once and Ayat al-Kursi ten times. After the salutation, he should raise his head toward the sky and recite the supplication written below:

O God, I ask You—O Allah, O Allah, O Allah, O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O One whom neither slumber nor sleep overtakes—I ask You, O God, by the sanctity of this Kursi with You and that You do for me such-and-such [damaged] all my needs and purposes, O King of kings.
