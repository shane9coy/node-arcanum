**An extremely easy and powerful method for gold:**

Take human head hair, as much as possible, wash it thoroughly with soap, dry it, and pass it through fine glass bottle [fragments].
Take one ritl of this hair and one ritl of water of the head in which vitriol has been dissolved, and use it in the process. The characteristic of vitriol is: take yellow copper, grind it finely, soak it in vinegar, make a paste in a bowl, and place it on fire.
Then calcinate it in the same manner until the substance becomes thoroughly red. At that time, purify it with salt of urine, and with the water of the head place it on fire,
and add the hair little by little
until one ritl of hair has completely dissolved. At that time, distill it. The first white water that comes out, keep it separate, and change the receiver and attach another.
Now water will begin to come out red. It will also contain grease, and its color will be like saffron. This should not be touched by hand because this water turns everything yellow. Take one dram of pure cinnabar and set it aside, and grind sulfur and orpiment and soak them in egg yolk, then apply this mixture on this dram. And in a pot, grind cooking salt and place this dram in its middle, and seal the pot hermetically and apply intense heat, and do not fear anything. When it cools down, then in the same manner take sulfur, salt, and orpiment and apply them on the dram, and keep only fresh salt in the pot. Do this five times. After that, grind the cinnabar and put it in white essence, and place it inside a glass bottle, seal it hermetically, and cook on low heat. When it dries, then correct it in red oil until
the cinnabar dissolves in the oil and does not congeal. Then give it to quicksilver in the aforementioned manner; it will be distilled and will become calx.
At that time, dissolve sem al-far in this prepared cinnabar oil, and in sheep fat oil. Apply a little on the finest [gold], and cook it in fire; gold will be prepared.

**The correct method to obtain alchemy:**

Whoever desires to know the science of alchemy correctly must purify himself, keep a fast for forty days, completely abstain from living creatures, and break the fast only on lawful sustenance.
And at all times recite Surah Ash-Shams, Surah Al-Layl, Surah Ad-Duha, and Surah Alam Nashrah, each seven times, and without counting recite the verse "Qul Allahumma Malik al-mulk" forty times, and then recite this supplication:

*Allahumma inni as'aluka bi-qudratika 'ala kulli shay'in wa taskhirika li-kulli shay'in, ya ahadu ya samadu ya witru ya hayyu ya qayyumu an tusalliya 'ala sayyidina Muhammadin an taskhirali al-'ilma alladhi satartahu 'ala kathirin min khalqika wa akramta bihi kathiran min 'ibadika, ya kafiyu ya ghaniyyu ya mughniyu ya fattahu ya hadiyu wa aghnini bihi 'imman siwaka innaka 'ala ma tasha'u qadirun wa bi-yadika maqalidu as-samawat wa al-ard innaka 'ala kulli shay'in qadirun*

When he performs this practice continuously for forty days, Allah the Most High will teach him, either in a dream or while awake, such a method that He will teach him this science.
