# Page 366 (English translation)
Between them is a barrier that they do not transgress.

O Allah, I ask You for health, fear, victory, longing, presence, knowledge, and the permanence of thought. And I ask You for secrecy that is without stain, if it is not established for us with sin and fault. Give us life and with action give us perfect guidance, as You have spread through Your Prophet's tongue, and with it the trial of Your friend Abraham, peace be upon him. So Allah the Exalted fulfilled them, saying: "I will make you an Imam for the people." He said: "And from my offspring," and Allah said: "My covenant does not reach the wrongdoers." So make us from the doers of good, and from his offspring, and from Adam's offspring, and from Noah. I ask You to show us the path of the Imams of the Godfearing. O Allah, I have wronged my soul greatly, and only You are the forgiver of sins. Grant me forgiveness and accept my repentance. There is no god but You. Your essence is pure, and I am among the wrongdoers. O Allah, O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Hearing, O Seeing, O Ancient, O Living, O Sustaining, O Compassionate, O Merciful, O First, O Last, O Manifest, O Hidden, blessed is Your name.

O Great and Honorable One, O Allah, join me with Your name that does not bring loss with sins, and make for me from it such a means that all my needs of mind, spirit, longing, touch, and body may be fulfilled. And place my name among Your names, my greatness among Your attributes, and my actions below Your actions, that are among the degrees of safety. Safety remains for every great one, humiliated, weak, and belittled. May the fault be theirs, and may they become for me among those whom You have tested with Your signs as Imams of guidance. And keep it established with me (whether alive or dead) with me. Whether You take me from among the servants, do not make me among those brought near. Give me knowledge, for surely the unjust cannot attain this logic except after [understanding].

Then Surah Fatiha and "Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad" three times should be recited, and all worldly and otherworldly needs will be fulfilled. We have opened the door of the treasures of charity—anyone whose heart desires may enter. And Allah grants sovereignty to whoever He wills.

After this, recite this supplication:

Yā Allāhu yā Ḥaqqu yā Nūru yā Munīru ftah qalbī bi-nūrika wa ʿallimnī min ʿilmika wa-ḥfiẓnī bi-ḥifẓika wa-asbighnī wa-fahhimnī ʿilmaka wa-baṣṣirnī bika wa-sabbib lī sababan min faḍlika tughnīnī bihi ʿani l-faqri wa-tuʿizzunī bihi mina dh-dhilli wa-tuṣliḥu lī bihi d-dunyā wal-ākhirata wa-tujallīnī bihi n-naẓara ilā wajhika l-karīmi fī jannati n-naʿīmi innaka ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun wa-lā ḥawla wa-lā quwwata illā billāhi l-ʿAliyyi l-ʿAẓīmi

O Allah, O Truth, O Light, O Illuminator, open my heart with Your light. Teach me from Your knowledge. Protect me with Your protection. Grant me sufficiency and give me understanding of Your knowledge. Grant me insight through You. Make for me from Your grace a means that will enrich me from poverty, honor me from humiliation, rectify for me this world and the next, and grant me the vision of Your noble countenance in Paradise of Bliss. Surely You are over all things capable. There is no power nor strength except by Allah the High, the Great.

And whoever reads these words, prayers, and supplications shall obtain from Allah the Exalted the fulfillment of his needs.

Furthermore, regarding the virtues of this supplication, an elder says: Whoever causes this coming supplication to be read, all his intellectual comforts and all his needs shall be fulfilled. That supplication is:

Al-ḥamdu lillāhi alladhī jaʿala lil-ʿālamīna aʿlāma l-ʿulūmi wa-jaʿala ḥamalata l-qur'āni l-ʿaẓīmi khawāṣṣahu wa-aḥbābahu min [...] wa-arāḥa arwāḥa l-qurrā'i min [...] wal-hammi wal-hamzi wa-ṣayyara l-ʿālima ka-mithli l-awzīyati waṣ-ṣāliḥīna [...] lisānuhu mamdūdun wa-ʿāṣimuhu [...]
