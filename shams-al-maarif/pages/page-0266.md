Chapter 5: Regarding the Name al-Quddūs (The Most Holy) of God the Almighty, its Properties and Benefits

Being adorned with good morals:
This name is very great. Whoever frequently remembers it until his state dominates over him, God the Almighty will remove from him every evil desire. His inner kingdom will be of benefit to him internally, such that he pays attention to it. If he writes it in Jupiter on the night of Friday and keeps it with him, God the Almighty will remove from him bad morals and adorn him with good morals, and he will be beloved among people and everyone will praise him. Its mention is suitable for one whose name is ʿAbd al-Quddūs, and it is suitable for one whose name is Isḥāq. Its literal number is 174 and its spiritual number is 170, and this name is among all the great names from the branches. It consists of the anxious number of odd marriage. Its form is like this: its 170+121 refer to the expanded name of God the Almighty. Its numerical flavor contains 241, and these refer to the two names of Allāh and Raqīb. God the Almighty knows best.

[Section 6 Header in decorative border]
Chapter 6: Regarding the Name Salām (Peace) of God the Almighty, Protection from Calamities and Misfortunes

This name is very merciful. Whoever writes this name and keeps it with him will never see any evil. Whoever frequently remembers it will remain protected from all calamities. In its remembrance there are many secrets for nil (annihilation), beginnings, and theophancies (tajalliyyāt). If one who fears another frequently remembers it, God the Almighty will keep him in security.
Its number is 131; these are the first numbers that refer to the complete name of God the Almighty. Its letter-names are 202, which refer to the two great names al-Raḥmān and al-ʿAzīz. Its mention is beneficial for one whose name is Muḥammad; it is the science of the first, just as the heart of Sūrat Yā-Sīn is "Salāmun qawlan min rabbin raḥīm" (Peace, a word from a merciful Lord). This verse is very great, and in it is the Supreme Name of God the Almighty; its entirety is very great. It is among the hidden secrets; whoever writes it in the honor of Jupiter and keeps it with him will be accepted among all creation, and God the Almighty will make his religious and worldly affairs easy.
