So that sleep and drowsiness do not come to you; despite the spaciousness of the earth, make it narrow upon him; let it not be heavenly for him so that it makes him happy, but rather let there be tightness and let harm befall him.

O Allah, three times. O the Merciful (Al-Rahman), three times. O the Compassionate (Al-Rahim), three times. [Centered boxed heading]
Benefits of Ayat al-Kursi

Protection from Satan and Enemy:

Know that Ayat al-Kursi has 170 letters. It has five sections and twenty-eight chapters. Whoever recites it in the morning, he will remain under Allah's protection from Satan and the ruler. Whoever recites it at midnight, in a place far from habitation, a number of times equal to its letters—namely three hundred and thirteen (313) times—then supplicates, it will be accepted. And if he recites it a number of times equal to the combined count of the companions of Badr and the companions of Talut—namely three hundred and thirteen (313) times—and supplicates, it will be accepted. If any person fears an enemy and intends to destroy him, let him recite Ayat al-Kursi according to the number of its letters, then recite this supplication:

O Conqueror, O Severe, O Possessor of Mighty Seizure. O Allah, just as You have been gentle with Your gentleness above all the gentle ones, and You have been elevated above all the great ones with Your greatness, and You know what is beneath Your earth as Your knowledge of what is above Your Throne—then the promptings of hearts are like the overt to You, and the overt utterances are like secrets in Your knowledge—every creature has submitted to Your greatness, and every possessor of authority has humbled himself before Your authority, and the affairs of the world and the hereafter have all come into Your hand. Make for me from every worry and grief in which I have been in the morning and the evening, a relief and an exit. O Allah, indeed Your forgiveness of my sins and Your overlooking of my mistakes and Your covering of my ugly deeds have made me bold enough to ask of You what I do not deserve from You, regarding what I have fallen short in. I call upon You feeling safe and ask of You with intimacy, for indeed You are the one who does good to me, and I am the wrongdoer against myself in what is between me and You. You lavish blessings upon me while I harbor hatred toward You through disobedience. I have not found anyone more generous and more compassionate toward a wretched servant like me. But trust in You has moved me to be bold before You. O Allah, by Your grace and Your goodness upon me, indeed You are over all things capable.

O Conqueror, O one of severe grip, O Allah, just as You, with Your gentleness, are more gentle than all the gentle ones and are elevated above all the great ones with Your greatness, You know what is beneath Your earth as Your knowledge of what is above Your Throne. The promptings of hearts are manifest before You, every ruling authority is humiliated before You, all affairs of the world and the hereafter are in Your hand. From every distress and grief in which I have been morning and evening, make relief for me. O Allah, my forgiveness of my sins, Your overlooking of my mistakes, and Your covering of my ugly deeds have made me bold regarding deeds for which I should not dare ask of You—regarding what I have fallen short in. I call upon You for the sake of security and ask of You with familiarity, for indeed You are the one who shows kindness to me while I myself am sinful in what is between me and You. You show affection to me through blessings while I show hatred to You through sins. I have not seen anyone more generous than You toward a base servant like me. But trust in You has moved me to dare to approach You. O Allah, bestow upon me Your grace and Your kindness, for indeed You are over all things capable.
