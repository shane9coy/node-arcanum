He is the Most High, the Great. Recite it for twenty-one days, or at most for forty days, and the purpose will be achieved before the forty days are over.

[Centered heading in a double-line decorative box]
The Supplication "Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum" and its Secrets

The method of this practice is that whatever intention you have, recite both of these names one thousand (1000) times, and recite this prayer three times, seven times:

O Allah, O Ever-Living, One to whom life is attributed has none besides Him who is not attributed to Him—for the one who attributed a son to himself—exalted are Your names and You are far above all designations. That [exaltedness is] above [any] example, partner, equal, consort, and minister. You are the Ever-Living eternally, and the Self-Sufficient. There is no life eternal except Your life; thus life spread forth from Your life. You are the Ever-Abiding; Yours is the everlasting permanence after the annihilation of creation. And as Yours is permanence, so for Your servants is annihilation. Your command, O God, is executory; Your decree has no deficiency. The individuals have passed away, and the severe ones have perished—those who associated polytheists with the existence of Your permanence and the eternality of Your life. O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, I ask You by this eternal life to grant me a life connecting to bliss, and I revive my soul among creation with a life by which I have support and felicity. And grant me felicity through the delicate subtleties of Your name Allah, the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting, and veil me with a thin veil from the fine subtleties of Your name Allah, the Ever-Living, until You erase wretchedness from me and admit me into the circle of felicity. Allah erases what He wills and establishes, and with Him is the Mother of the Book. O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O One by whom the heavens and earth stand in length and breadth with what You know and what You do not know, and You know it best, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

O Allah, O Ever-Living, One to whom life is attributed has none besides Him who is not attributed to Him—for the one who attributed a son to himself—exalted is Your very self. Your names are pure, and You are far above designations. Your very self is pure from any example, partner, equal, consort, and minister. You are eternally alive, and You are the Self-Sufficient in Your eternal life. Life spread forth from Your life, and You are the Ever-Abiding. For You is everlasting permanence after the annihilation of creation. As for You, there is permanence, and for Your servants is annihilation. Your command is executory; there is no opposition to Your decree. Truly, the individuals have passed away, and thought has perished. Because of Your permanence and the eternality of Your life, [they remained] alongside [them], O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting. I ask with this eternal life that You grant me a life connecting to blessings, and keep my soul alive among creation in such a way that there is for me support and felicity. Grant me felicity through the favors of Your name Allah, the Ever-Living, the Self-Subsisting, and gracefully adorn me with the delicate subtleties of Your name, until You remove wretchedness from me and admit me into eternal felicity. Allah erases what He wills and establishes, and with Him is the Mother of the Book. O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Sustainer of all, O One with whom the heavens and earth stand firm in length and breadth—those we know and those we do not know, and You know them best—by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

And recite this prayer as well:
O Allah, if my sustenance is in the heaven, then send it down; and if it is in the earth, then bring it forth; and if it is near, then make it ready for me; and if it is abundant, then bless me in it and convey it to me wherever I am—and do not convey me to it wherever it is—and bring it to me from Your bounty and generosity by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and all his companions.
