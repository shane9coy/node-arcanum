# Page 224 (English translation)

دَعوةُ سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ اَكْبَرُ
وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ ط

Invocation: "Glorified is God, and praise be to God, and there is no deity except God, and God is the Greatest, and there is no power nor strength except through God, the Sublime, the Great" ṭ

If you wish to practice this spiritual exercise (riyāḍah), then recite it 544 times after each prayer, and 545 times after the ʿIshāʾ prayer. The total for ten days will be 2,723. Recite it three times after the witr prayer, and after finishing it, recite this supplication:

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. By it my tongue becomes sincere, and by it my heart is steadfast. I ask You, O Provider of crawling creatures, Stabilizer of mountains, Mover of winds, and Runner of seas, O Light of lights, You know every light by Your great grace. The illuminator of every light—One, Unique, Absolute, Eternal, Everlasting. Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He has not taken a son. I have called upon You by Your noble name: "Near" [Qarīb]. Praise be to God. O ʿAynāʾīl, by the path of guidance and worship for God, Lord of the worlds. The First, the Last, the Manifest, the Hidden. Every soul He guides. O ʿAynāʾīl, you are among the noble angels, and I am among the best of humanity, by the grace of God and prostration to Him. I swear to you by the right hand of the Throne, and by the Lote Tree of the Extreme Limit, and by the face of ʿIzrāʾīl, the Seizer of the creation of the heavens and earths. I call upon you from the depths of the sea, and by the winds that move upon it, and by the clouds that cause them to rain, and the mercies that descend and the decrees that descend as a servant from before you, who obeys my command. His movement is from the branches of the earth—I send them forth willingly and as the best of woodcutters. They worship none but God, and I place my trust in you, the One, the Unique, there is no partner for Him in His sovereignty. O servants of the tree: the first of it is forty branches, distributed from four limbs [or members]. Its fruits are glorification, sanctification, and declaration of God's oneness. Its glorification is: "Glorified is God, and praise be to God, and there is no deity except God, and God is the Greatest, and there is no power nor strength except through God, the Sublime, the Great"—
