[Heading in a decorative box]: Chapter: The Nature of the Balances

Know that in this art, knowing how to extract the nature of the balance is extremely necessary. Thus, when you intend to perform any operation, first construct its letters and sum them all, and observe the letter-ranks, because each letter has a rank equal to six letters of the degree, and twelve letters of rank equal one letter of rank. In this same way, determine the balance of all letters, and the example of the balance is as follows:

Know that the name of the first balance is the Balance of Equality (Mizan al-Misawat); from it, the nature of these equality letters is known, which you need during the time of operation, and the condition of the preservation letters is also known, which is needed among the people of Islamic law. From the second balance, the relations of the form letters are known according to the calculation of degrees and subdivisions, etc. And the third is the Great Balance (Mizan al-Kabir). It has many special properties and holds great benefits. The Balance of Love and the Balance of Patience are known from it. The weights for making the elixir are also known from it, and its reality is such that only through it can one attain; it will also be mentioned in the exposition of alchemy. Learn it from there, and one of its great special properties is that on whatever seal you write it, the effect will become manifest, and it is extremely beneficial for the purposes of Islam and for good and evil.
