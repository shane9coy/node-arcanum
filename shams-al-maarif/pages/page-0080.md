**Entry to Paradise, Release from Hell, Expansion of the Grave, and Attainment of Felicity on the Day of Judgment:**

The first virtue is that of Basmala. In the noble Hadith it is stated that whoever comes on the Day of Judgment, in whose record of deeds three hundred times "Basmala" has been written, which he recited with certainty, God Almighty will grant him release from Hell and admit him into Paradise. In the Gospel it is stated that God Almighty said to Jesus, peace be upon him: "Begin your prayer and recitation with Basmala, for whoever recites it will be protected from the terrors of the Resurrection. When he dies, God Almighty will ease the pangs of death for him. His grave will be expanded and will be made spacious for him as far as the eye can see. He will be raised from the grave in such a state that his body will be fair and white, and his face will be radiant. God Almighty will also take his accounting very lightly and easily. His deeds will be heavy [i.e., weighty in his favor]. There will be much light for him on the Bridge [Sirat] until he enters Paradise, and he will be called in the field of the Day of Judgment with felicity and forgiveness." Jesus, peace be upon him, asked: "O God, for whom are these virtues?" God Almighty replied: "These are for you and for those who follow you, and these virtues will be for Muhammad, peace be upon him, and his community." Jesus, peace be upon him, taught Basmala to his companions, but when Jesus, peace be upon him, ascended to heaven, those who came after him fell into misguidance and abandoned it, taking the world instead of religion. The sign of faith was removed from the hearts of the Christians, monks, and people of the Gospel.

**Basmala is a Cause of Blessing and Completion:**

Until our Noble Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, was sent, and from that time it began to be written in the chapters of the Holy Quran, in registers, in all books and epistles. God Almighty, the Lord of Glory, swears by [unclear: His] honor that for whatever thing a believing servant recites Basmala, God Almighty will bestow blessing upon it.

**The Blessing of Basmala:**

The Noble Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, stated that for the person who recites Basmala with sincere heart, the mountains seek forgiveness for him, but their seeking forgiveness is not heard. The Noble Prophet Muhammad, may God bless him and his family, stated that whoever says Basmala, Paradise says to him: "At your service and your happiness, O Allah, this Your servant has recited Basmala, so transform his evils into goodness." On the Day of Resurrection, the nations will see the greatness of this community and say: "Glory be to God! May God make the deeds of the community of Muhammad, peace be upon him, heavy." Then God Almighty will say to His Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him: "The reason for this is that these people used to begin every speech with Basmala." In Basmala there are three of God's names—very great and noble names. If they are placed in one pan of the scale and all the heavens and earths are placed in the other pan, the pan of Basmala will become heavy and sink.

**Basmala is a Cure for Every Illness:**

God Almighty has made Basmala a means of protection from every illness and from Satan, and by its blessing this community will obtain bounty, delight, and benefit. We should seek nearness to Him through this, that is, through the Glorious and Honored Lord.
