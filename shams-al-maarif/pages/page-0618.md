# Page 618 (English translation)
616

Know that the secret of memorization is joy, and the result of memorization is tranquility and unveiling. The seeking of names and the verses of knowledge are through the names, for their relation is to knowledge.

Note: It has been transmitted that faith (amanah) is the knowledge of secrets. His presence (peace be upon him) said: "Faith has been sent down into the hearts of people, and faith has been placed upon the backs of humans."

**Types of Covenants:**

Knowledge is that faith was placed within the intellect at the time of Alastu bi-Rabbikum (the primordial covenant "Am I not your Lord?"), and the second covenant is called the covenant of the mind, and the third is the covenant of the souls, and the fourth takes the form of elective composition, and the fifth is the compulsion of the manifestation of rulings. From response, in the drops that which shows the oneness of God becomes manifest. The sixth is in the first audition with the continuity of its connection, and the indication from the covenant of the world of particles is the manifestation of the knowledge of unveiling, as has been said. The beginning of the reality of the Pen is an indication, and the beginning of the reality is a ruse, because in it God, exalted be He, has placed happiness and misery, and for this reason His presence, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "Each is made easy for that for which he was created"—meaning, the work for which a person was created is easy for him. Then taking the covenant from souls was solely for the manifestation of the ruling. This is the gathering of the senses and the submission of the heart with the sultan of power and might, and its composition lies in the manifestation and unveiling of elective will, and the manifestation of rulings with the perfection of the command is the sending of messengers and with those rulings they brought, and the reality of the secrets of the ruling and its unveiling lies within the provisions.

**Chapter:** Know that the condition for the retreat (khalwa) of these names is only one. When you wish to perform the retreat of any of these names, begin with fasting and vigilance, and make this supplication your regular practice:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ o اِلٰهِيْ اَسْاَلُكَ نُوْرًا تُبَیِّضُ سِحْنَائِيْ وَ یَمْحُوُ زَلَّاتِيْ وَ یَقْبَلُ عَثَرَاتِيْ وَ یُصْلِحُ ظَاهِرِيْ وَ یَجْمَعُ شَمْلِيْ وَ یُقَدِّسُ سِرِّيْ وَ یُنِیْرُ اَمْرِیْ وَ هَبْنِيْ مَعْرِفَةَ مَا اُلْزِقَ بِهِ عَلٰی آتِيَاءٍ جَلَسِيْ اِنَّكَ مُنَزِّلُ الْاَنْوَارِ وَ كَاشِفُ الْاَسْرَارِ وَ کُلُّ شَيْءٍ عِنْدَكَ بِمِقْدَارٍ

Translation: "O God, I ask You for a light that illuminates my face, erases my slips, accepts my errors, rectifies my outward appearance, gathers my scattered affairs, sanctifies my secret, illuminates my affair, and grants me knowledge by which I may gain superiority over those who sit with me, for You are the one who sends down lights and reveals secrets, and all things are with You in due measure."

**Causes of the Revelation of Secrets:**

Whoever persists in this remembrance, God, exalted be He, will place awe of him in the hearts of people, and will grant him the unveiling of the states of the names. And when reading the invitation, do not look around—read with contemplation.

**Precautions during the Days of Invitation:**

During the period of invitation, one should abstain from garlic, onions, and the like out of love. Sleep very little. Subsist only on what barely sustains you with bread. At the time of dawn, recite istighfar (seeking forgiveness) abundantly. Also recite Surah Ya-Sin and Tabaraka Alladhi. After the evening prayer, offer two units of optional prayer. Sleep first, then recite the Holy Quran and the noble name abundantly.
