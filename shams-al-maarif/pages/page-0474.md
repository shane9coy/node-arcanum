# Page 474 (English translation)
472

Benefits: It carries amazing properties. The effect of poison is very quickly neutralized by it. It should be engraved on pure silver ten times. Write Surah Hud once around it. Wash with water or Zamzam water and give to drink to the poisoned or snake-bitten person. By the grace of God, relief will come immediately.

The shape is this:
[Diagram: A diamond-shaped sigil divided into four triangular segments by a cross. Arabic letters occupy the vertices: 'د' (Dal) at the top, 'ب' (Ba) at the left, 'ا' (Alif) at the right, and 'ہ' (Ha) at the bottom. In the center, a stylized word appears, likely 'یا' (Ya).]

That no harm comes from anything:
If this is engraved on silver and placed around a child's neck, no harmful thing will come near it. No flies will land on it. Crying will also stop. If you fill the space between it and the letter "b" in a square shape and engrave it on a ring, and write the [name] fourteen times around it and wear it, no snake will come near. One will have knowledge of ingesting poison. Whoever has swallowed poison, place this ring in their mouth and keep them silent, the effect of the poison will be removed.

The kohl that eliminates boils:
Make its numerical compound into a mixture, write it on camel skin and burn it to make kohl. It will eliminate the white swelling of the eye. Write this letter "Jim" by combining it letter by letter with someone's name: that is, one letter from the name of the seeker and one letter from the name of the desired [person], write the letter Jim in between. Hang it facing the north wind. Read the letter Jim seven times and blow upon it, and say: "Through this letter Jim, send the servant of so-and-so person to this place." Perform this operation on Monday in the prevailing moon, and every purpose will be achieved. Its prayer is like the letter Jim; read it.

The Letter Jim:
Jim, the letter of divine luminous nature. It holds the third degree of heat and the first degree of moisture. Its dryness predominates. Its star is Mars; its day is Tuesday. The sage has said: this is the third letter. Its shape is triangular. Both chords converge at the point of equilibrium. This is the only form of Jim. There is no other form. Some ignorant people have created several other forms; they are completely wrong.

Its shape is this:
[Diagram: A triangular seal containing a magic square or arrangement of Arabic letters: 'ا' (Alif), 'ب' (Ba), 'ج' (Jim), and 'د' (Dal) within nested segments.]

Benefits: Write this shape on a piece of bread circle and write this verse around it: "And when you killed a soul, then you accused [someone else] for it" [Qur'an 8:31]. Give it to whoever is suspected of theft. If he is the thief, he will not be able to swallow this piece. Otherwise, he will swallow it. If someone writes Jim on the thumbnail of the left hand and goes to the king or ruler, he will fulfill his need. The ruler will not turn away from his shape. The Jim compound tied as a strip, written on the hour of Friday, tie it to a tree from which flowers and fruits have ceased, then it will become fruitful. It will begin to give flowers and fruits. Engrave the number of this Jim compound in a triangle, and engrave it on a sheet of parchment, and write this prayer seven times around it: whoever has it set in a ring and uses it, and always keeps it with him, will be loved. This is the prayer of this letter Jim:

اللّٰھُمَّ اِنِّیْ اَسْئَلُکَ یَا جَبَّارُ یَا جَمِیْلُ یَا جَامِعُ بِمَا اَوْدَعْتَہٗ حَرْفَ الْجِیْمِ مِنَ الْاَسْرَارِ الْمَکْنُوْنَۃِ وَالْاَنْوَارِ الْمَخْزُوْنَۃِ اَنْ تُسَخِّرَ لِیْ مَلَائِکَتِکَ الْجِرَامَ خُدَّامَ ھٰذَا الْحَرْفِ بِالطَّاعَۃِ فِیْمَا اَطْلُبُ مِنْکَ بِہٖ وَتَنْصُرَنِیْ عَلٰی مَنْ جَحَدَنِیْ وَتَرْزُقُنِیْ بِہٖ مَا اَشْتَھِیْ وَمَا اُرِیْدُ وَتَجْبُ لِیْ بِہٖ مَا اُرِیدُ وَتَجْبُ لِیْ بِہٖ مَا تَشْآءُ وَتَرْضٰی [O Allah, I ask You, O Compeller, O Beautiful, O Gatherer, by what You have deposited within the letter Jim of the hidden secrets and the concealed lights, that You subjugate for me Your honored angels, the servants of this letter, in obedience in whatever I ask from You through it, and that You support me against whoever denies me, and that You grant me through it my desire and my wish, and that You answer for me through it what I want, and that You are pleased with whatever You will and are pleased with].
