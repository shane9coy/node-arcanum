# Page 64 (English translation)
[Heading: (Chapter: On the poetic proverbs of the Arabs concerning the lunar mansions)]

Some have said: Sheikh al-Kindi was before me. I read before him. Then he said: I read before Abu Mansur Khawqani. He said: Abu Muhammad al-Sulwi narrated to me, saying: The Arabs say that when al-Sharṭayn rises, pomegranates ripen, gardens become verdant, blisters of travelers heal in the sun, neighbors treat one another with gentleness, and the poor sleep anywhere. When al-Buṭayn rises, debts are repaid. When al-Thurayya rises, the confused become enlightened and dogs become emaciated. When al-Han'ah rises, people's misfortunes are removed. When al-Han'ah rises, honor becomes plentiful. When al-Zira'an rises, the rays of the sun become visible and water flows from the lands. When al-Nathrah rises, sea creatures become weak. When al-'Awwa' rises, life becomes settled and the wind becomes excellent. When al-Simaks rise, the wealth of kings increases. When al-Fargh al-Muqaddam, al-Fargh al-Mu'akhkhar, and al-Nasr rise, water flows beneath every step. When al-Zubanā rises, dates are taken from the trees. When al-Iklil rises, words become trivial. When al-Qalb rises, every difficulty becomes easy. When al-Shulah rises, speech comes quickly to the elderly. When al-Na'aim rises, goodness is attained. When al-Baldah rises, food is prepared with ghee and flour, and dishes are prepared with butter and ghee. When al-Dhibh rises, the earth becomes verdant. When al-Shulah rises, grass and vegetation become fresh. When al-Fargh al-Muqaddam rises, serve and do not regret. When al-Fargh al-Mu'akhkhar rises, act quickly and do not delay. We have mentioned the poetic proverbs of the Arabs concerning the lunar mansions, and God Almighty knows best.
