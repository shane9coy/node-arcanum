[Chapter 44: The Manifest and Hidden Properties of the Name Raqīb for Protection]

This is the Name of Majesty and the Great Secret. Whoever mentions it frequently shall remain protected in all his movements and stillness. Likewise, he shall remain protected in his states and his dealings. Its magnificent square is written in the honor of the Moon. Whoever keeps it with himself shall find protection and innocence in both manifest and hidden [realms]. Know that whoever recites this Name Raqīb 14,444 (fourteen thousand four hundred and forty-four) times daily with the lights of accordance, purification, and ascetic practice, for forty days beginning with Friday morning, until its operative force overcomes him—then the helpers of the Name shall be with him. Thereafter, wherever this person goes, if there is a talisman, the talisman and its operative force shall be nullified. Its number is 312. This is an odd excess number. Its divisors are 526, pointing to two great names: Hayy and Matīn. This is the square form of the Name Raqīb:

[Chapter 45: The Properties of the Name Mujīb as the Greatest Elixir for the Acceptance of Prayer]

This name is of great majesty and the Supreme Great One; it is extremely beneficial for the acceptance of prayer. It should be recited combined with every supplication. Whoever writes its square on the first Friday in the hour of the Sun and keeps it with himself, every supplication of his shall be accepted, and whatever he requests at the time of sunset shall be granted. Its number is 55. This is a deficient number. Its divisors are 17, pointing to the names Hādī and Hākim of Almighty Allah, for [the number 17] in overcoming obstacles means manifestation. This number points to perfection along with the divine attributes. Its limit relates to the name of Prophet Sāliḥ, upon him be peace [the text is cut off here].
