O Allah, I ask You with every name that You have taken for Yourself, or that You have revealed in Your Book, or that You have taught to someone among Your creation, or that You have chosen in Your knowledge of the unseen. And I ask that the Wise Quran be the joy of my heart, the light of my eyes, and the removal of my restlessness. Remove my sorrow and grief and complaint.

Ibn Mas'ud (may Allah be pleased with him) requested from the Prophet (peace be upon him): "Shall we learn this supplication, O Messenger of Allah?" He (peace be upon him) said: "Yes, learn it, but do not teach it to the ignorant." I have heard from some righteous people that they used to supplicate in this manner. This supplication is very effective:

Allāhumma ij'al mā tashā'u muwāfiqan li-mā ashā'u kay lā yaṣīra mā ashā'u mukhālifan li-mā tashā'u fa-man anā ashā'u bi-khilāfi mā tashā'u law jāhada l-'abdu wa shā mā kāna illā mā tashā'u fa-l-ṭif binā wa-mā tashā'u wa-in illā an yashā'a Allāhu rabbu l-'ālamīna

"O Allah, make what You will consistent with what I will, so that what I will does not become contrary to what You will. So whoever I will against what You will, even if the servant strives, it will only be what You will. So be gentle with us and what You will. And there is no will except what Allah, Lord of the Worlds, wills."

Properties of the Ma'asar Najm [Seal of the Star], needs will be fulfilled, expansion of sustenance will occur

Know that I have seen many people who, in the last part of the night, would take this band bearing the same shapes in their hands and supplicate to Allah the Exalted, and they would place this symbol on their heads and call out. And whoever writes this Ma'asar Najm and hangs it around his neck, his supplication will be accepted.

In the Sacred Precinct of the Grand Mosque of Mecca, I saw a woman with disheveled hair holding this symbol in her hand and supplicating:

Yā rabbin bi-hādhā wa-fīhi mina l-asmā'i l-karīmati wa-l-asrāri l-'aẓīmati illā mā ataytanī min ghayri kulfatin wa-mashaqqatin innaka anta l-fa''ālu li-mā tashā'u wa-anta 'alā kulli shay'in qadīrun

"O Lord, by this and in it are the noble names and the great secrets, unless what You have given me without hardship or difficulty, indeed You are the Doer of what You will and You have power over all things."

Before her speech was even finished, Allah the Exalted sent down a dining tray from the sky, in which there was some sustenance, and in it was a piece of paper on which was written: "If you supplicate to Allah the Exalted saying 'Take me to my home,' we will take you to your home. Because you have supplicated with this Greatest Name, for whatever is asked with it is granted." This symbol is very beneficial for every purpose. Allah the Exalted is the one who grants success through His bounty and generosity.
