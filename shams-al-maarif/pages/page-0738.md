# Page 738 (English translation)
736

I ask You by Your mercy and protection, I ask You by Your will regarding all things, that You cut off from me everything that cuts me off from You and that veils me from You, O God, O Gatherer. I ask You to gather upon my perceptions and my essence with holy safety, and that You manifest Yourself to my spirit for the duration of Your protection, and that You grant me success in Your service and my presence before You, truly You are God, the Gatherer of all good, there is no god but You.

Whoever performs this dhikr, the generous Allah will grant him good in this world and the next.

Chapter: On the virtues of the name "Māniʿ" (The Preventer/Withholder)

Māniʿ is one who removes the causes of destruction and harm from within bodies and religions. Whoever understands the true meaning of protection has also understood the meaning of māniʿ. The summary is that the meaning of prevention is intending protection, and the meaning of protection is intending prevention. That is, every protector is a māniʿ, and every māniʿ is a protector, but when māniʿ is absolute, it is used for all causes of prevention.

In some narration, this is the Greatest Name, and it has three letters. Its punishment is severe, and its appointed angel is Shamāʾīl, and it is from those appointed over the original withholdings, who prevent the people of Hell from entering Paradise and prevent the people of Paradise from entering Hell, and they do not allow disbelievers to meet the faithful. The triangle is its most magnificent sigil.

It is unparalleled for stopping storms and rain.

To stop storms and rain, write this figure and hang it in the air, and recite it according to the numerical value of the Name of the Creator (Bārī), and manipulate it according to the method of the people of secrets as you wish. We cannot explain more than this. If a person has an enemy and wishes to stop him, let him recite this name according to its numerical value—God will protect him from that enemy's evil.

Chapter: On the virtues of the name "Al-Ḍārr An-Nāfiʿ" (The Harmful the Beneficial)

Ḍārr and Nāfiʿ is that from which both good and evil, benefit and harm proceed, and all these things are attributed to God Most High, whether through the mediation of angels, humans, and minerals, or without mediation. Do not understand that poison kills or causes harm while an angel or human or satan or any creature or star brings goodness and benefit. Rather, understand that these are means through which God has subjugated them—upon this they are established.

When this act is performed with sincerity, along with the eternal power, through the complete letter of the Pen, from the book, for the sake of the common people—benefit and harm are in the grasp of Allah Most High. He provides whom He wills.

When a person perceives the eternal power with its attributes of harm and punishment, it will remove harm from his essence.
