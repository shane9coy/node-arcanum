# Page 557 (English translation)
**Qarun Sank into the Earth**

Then Musa (Moses), peace be upon him, demanded zakāt from him. He refused because he realized that the amount of zakāt would come out of his abundant treasure. Musa (Moses), peace be upon him, cursed him for this, and he sank into the earth along with his treasures.

**This Knowledge Came to Abraham, Solomon, David, and All the Prophets**

Abraham, Solomon, and David, and all the prophets, peace be upon them, practiced this craft because all of them were poor, and God enriched them through this craft. For God grants this craft only to whom He chooses among His creation, so that lawful sustenance may be theirs in this world, and their hearts may be pure. For them, this craft is mercy, and a source of regret for the disbelievers—such as Qarun, Pharaoh, Haman, Shaddad, and others like them.

**The Minerals of the Stars**

Know that Saturn is the highest of all stars, and its mineral is lead. After Saturn comes Jupiter, whose mineral is tin. Then Mars, whose mineral is iron. Then the Sun, whose mineral is gold. Then Venus, whose mineral is copper. Then Mercury, whose mineral is quicksilver. Then the Moon, whose mineral is silver, as has been explained previously.

**Light and Its Effects**

Know that light is like the outward ray (shu'ā'), and the ray is the inward light. Thus, for every light there is a ray, and for every ray there is light. The ray is the observed reality. The barzakh (intermediate realm) of the reality of light is within the World of Images (ālam almithāl), just as the ray is the intermediary for that spirit. Here, first the ray manifested, then its [light manifested], and then light manifested upon the ray's subtle and dense [aspects]. For this reason, the entire lower world is between light and ray. Thus, the head of form (hay'a) is the ray, and the head of the pattern (namūna) is light, and for corporeality the outward [aspect] is the head. Thus, the ray is inward from form, and light is outward from the servant. So outwardness became bound up with form in the servant. Because of light, the inward of bodies is the ray of form with the form of the composite figure. Thus, at that time, form is attributed to the ray from the side of the living [soul], and also to the light from the side of the living [soul]; but the living [soul] is light with the reality of understanding, and with the world of souls is the reality of light, and such is the human species for the sake of understanding. Similarly, jinn and fulūsh (earthly spirits) are for the sake of the living [soul]. And just as the inner commentary is for the sake of the quality of understanding, so too is the form of mixture for the human body.

**Protection from Satan through Eating Plants**

Know that in plants, the moderation of the ray has not occurred according to the measure of light, except in those in which moderation has occurred and whose temperament has become inflamed. From this, wholesome nourishment is produced for the body, and the generation of fine blood also comes from such food, which has the capacity for the mixture of blood's subtlety, and the Devil finds no access in it.

**No Leprosy through Eating Vegetables**

And light is present in the entire body, by which leprosy and other diseases are eliminated.

**Luminosity is Acquired through Drinking Vinegar**

The form and shape whose light is according to the measure of the intellect produces desire. Moderation occurs because of the power of imagination, which does not know [how] to dry the moisture of light, for light becomes absent more from moisture and density.
