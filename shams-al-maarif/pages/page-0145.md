Chapter 16

The Great Name and its Definitions

Know that the Great Name has many indications and properties. I am stating them clearly so that every seeker of secrets may benefit from them and understand their meanings and wonders. This name brings relief to every apparent pain and ailment. Swift recovery is obtained through it. It is a strong fortress for protection.

Protection from the Torment of the Grave
Among its properties is that if it is written and buried with the deceased in the grave, the deceased shall remain protected from the torment of the grave.

Great Acceptance and Protection from the Harm of Rulers
If it is written and kept with oneself, there shall be great acceptance. When one goes before any king or emir, one shall be protected from their harm. Its bearer shall prevail over every enemy. Whoever harbors enmity toward him, upon that person shall come wrath. This name is useful for nullifying sorcery, for opening the mesmerized (the affected person), and for the prisoner.

Epilepsy Shall Depart:
It benefits the one afflicted with epilepsy. It is also useful for the expulsion of jinn from the body. If it is hung upon [the afflicted person] and the jinn does not flee, it shall be burned by the blessing of this Great Name.

Obtaining Love:
If any person writes it on a silver ring during the first hour of Friday, and the one writing it is fasting, then wears it, whoever casts their gaze upon it shall love him. His need shall be fulfilled. If he goes with it before kings and rulers, he shall succeed; however, the ring must be worn on the right hand. If going to war, wear it on the left hand. If this ring is placed in a deserted house, it shall become inhabited.

Marriage Shall Occur:
If a person whose marriage is not taking place wears this ring, his marriage shall occur.

Safety from Bandits:
If any person fears bandits, let him keep it with him. If it is affixed to the banner of an army, that army shall be granted victory and triumph.
