# Page 599 (English translation)
The method of solitary retreat is this: write the letters "Bism" on the wall of the retreat chamber, and after each prayer recite the da'wat forty times; after iftar the muwakkal will attend and fulfill the service. After each prayer he shall say this word forty times:

ajib yā khādim ḥarf al-nūn wa a'ṭinī rūḥan min rūḥāniyyatikum yā khadīmīn fīmā urīdu

And this is its da'wat:

bismathi allāh al-raḥmān al-raḥīm o allāhumma malikan min mulūkika amlik bihi mulkan tāmman laka al-mulku yā dhā al-jalāl wa al-ikrām yā mu'min yā muhayyim yā mu'ṭī yā māni' yā mālik al-mulki malliknī khādim hādhā al-ḥarfi aw amzijhu bi-rūḥāniyyatī yā arḥam al-rāḥimīn * ajib yā nūn wa abṭil ḥarakāt al-kunūz wa ajlib lī al-arzāqa wa alqi maḥabbatī fī qulūb al-khalā'iq ajma'īn allāhumma al-mughnī lafaḥatan min nafḥatikum yā mun'im tamahhak allāhu al-ni'am allāhumma an'im bi-al-ni'am al-tāmmati yawma tamūru al-samā'u mūran ahni'an bi-na'īmin wa hanī'an yā mun'im bi-ḥaqqi ahdinā al-ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm al-muqtabadīn bihā m wa īdh ḍiryām wa la'ah salṭam al-wahhāb ajib yā mun'im bi-ḥaqqi jibrīl wa mīkā'īl wa isrāfīl wa bi-quwwati al-malak mihāyīl akrim allāhumma ḥarf al-nūn ḥattā takūna bayna al-'awālim min al-muqarrabīna hayyā wa rji' ilā karāmatikum min allāhi al-karīm ihbiṭū al-bard hā'ulā'i al-'ummār min makān kadhā wa kadhā al-wahā al-'ajalu

And its iftar is this:

ajib yā sharāḥīl bi-ḥaqqi lam ḥamjathā alladhī ṣamīḥu yā lāmārah ahyā ḥajamshaṭ lamayā' bi-nūr al-anwār wa munawwir al-abṣāri ajib bi-idhni allāhi fīka bi-ḥaqqi hādhā al-ḥarfi

Through the utilization of this letter every blessing can be obtained.

The Letter Nun:

This letter is luminous and dark. Mars is its cold and dry. When it is written on a mirror thirteen times and this verse is also written with it: allāhu nūru al-samāwāti wa al-arḍi to the end; then whatever spiritual essence of a star he commands will accept it. And when it is written on the gemstone of a ring and the hālūh is also written with it, then if he goes toward a woman, the muwakkals of her husband will turn away from it and be afraid.

For pain of colic and stomach pain, relief will come.
The one suffering from colic and stomach pain should keep it with him—God willing, there will be a cure.

Fish will gather around it:
When this letter is written on a silver plate and the name of the muwakkal is also written on it, and this plate is thrown into the river, the fish from all sides will gather around it. And if anyone tries to catch fish, fish will come from all directions. And if he catches some and puts them in a house and leaves, fish will gather upon it.
