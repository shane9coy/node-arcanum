The Narration of His Holiness Dhul-Nun al-Misri:
--------------------------------------------
His Holiness Dhul-Nun al-Misri, may God the Most High have mercy upon him, said: I saw a young man praying excessively in the Sacred Kaʿba. I went to him and said: "You are praying very frequently." He said: "I am asking permission from God the Most High to depart." I saw a letter fall upon him on which was written: "This letter is from the Mighty, the Forgiving to His truthful and grateful servant. Permission to depart is granted to you. All your past and future sins have been forgiven."

All Prayers Are Accepted Through the Greatest Name
------------------------------------------------
It is narrated from His Holiness Anas ibn Malik, may God be pleased with him, that the Master of the Worlds, His Holiness Muhammad the Chosen, may peace be upon him, passed by a man who, after finishing his prayer, was supplicating this prayer:
"O God, I ask You by virtue of the fact that all praise belongs to You—there is no deity except You, O Compassionate, O Beneficent, O Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Living, O Self-Subsisting."
The Noble Prophet, may peace and blessings be upon him, said to his noble Companions, may God be pleased with them: "Do you know what supplication this man made?" They all said: "God the Most High and His Messenger, may peace be upon him, know best." He, may peace be upon him, said: "He supplicated with the Greatest Name with which any supplication is accepted and whatever is asked is granted. These are ten Names for which no action is better, as they are beneficial in times of severe difficulty and calamity. They are:
"The One, the Unique, the Self-Sufficient, the Creator of what He wills, the Hearing, the Seeing, the Powerful, the Omnipotent, the Strong, the Subsisting."
This is a list of the supplicatory Names each of which has been called the Greatest Name:
"O God, I ask You by virtue of the fact that all praise belongs to You—there is no deity except You, O Compassionate, O Beneficent, O Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Living, O Self-Subsisting. O God, I ask You by virtue of the fact that You are God, there is no deity except You, the One, the Self-Sufficient, who did not beget nor was begotten, and there is none comparable to Him. There is no deity except You. Glorified are You—indeed I was among the wrongdoers. O Most Merciful of the merciful, O Hearer of supplications, O our Lord, O our Lord. I ask You by the name of God upon which there is no deity except He, the Lord of the Mighty Throne. 'Alif-Lām-Mīm.'"
