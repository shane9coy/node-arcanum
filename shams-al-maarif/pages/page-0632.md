# Page 632 (English translation)
I write it and keep it with myself. When someone has an epileptic seizure, when you see them fallen in this affliction, stand before them and say: **"O servants of these names, answer and rely upon the branch of these."** And point with your finger toward those people. They will all be seized by the affliction of epilepsy. And when you want to rouse them, blow once into the ear of each of them, and they will become conscious. And whoever is diligent in reciting this, they will be safe from Hellfire.

**Its operation obtains subjugation:**

When you have a need from a king or emir, fast on Thursday with devotion and break your fast with almonds and dates, recite the number of the desired one 1011 times, and also recite it when going to sleep until sleep comes. Then when morning comes, recite according to the number mentioned, write it on paper with musk, saffron, and rose water. At the time of writing, raw ambergris incense should be burned. Tie this paper to your head and go to them. The need will be fulfilled. And if one writes the number of its letters of transition in reconciliation and keeps it with oneself, one will be beloved and accepted.

**It is beneficial for the payment of debts of the debtor, the need of the poor, and all purposes:**

When the sun is in the first degree of Aries, write it 360 times and keep it with yourself. If he is a debtor, Allah the Almighty will pay his debt, and if he is needy, God will make him generous. Writing Basit is equal to its number of Basit, but write it at least 70 times. When she writes it 70 times and keeps it with herself—the woman who cannot conceive, or ties it to a tree that bears no fruit—the woman will become pregnant and the tree will bear fruit. And if she writes it 100 times and puts it in the water that is given to grapes, fruit will become very abundant. And if she writes it on a stone and puts it in the water that is given to date palms, they will bear much fruit. And when they write it on a lead tablet in a triangle and attach it to the hunter's net, the game will be abundant. And if they write this same triangle and keep it in the house, no vermin will remain. And if they write it on a gold or silver tablet and put it around the child's neck, it will remain safe from every affliction. And whoever keeps it engraved on a silver ring with himself and recites it 31 times after every prayer, his sustenance will become easy. Its triangle is this:

It has come in the noble hadith that on the Day of Judgment, whoever has "Bism Allah" written in their book of deeds 800 times and is a truthful Muslim, Allah the Almighty will free him from punishment.

**There is a specific peculiarity of acceptance in al-Raḥmān:**

In the name al-Raḥmān there is a wonderful peculiarity of acceptance and attraction of the desired. Whoever maintains its litany should take the person whose love they desire, separate their letters and combine them with the letters of al-Raḥmān, and write the sum of all of them. Then let him mention this name according to the number of the total letters, and the desired will be obtained. When he writes this name 50 times with musk and saffron and keeps it with himself, he will inspire awe and be accepted. Its peculiarity for acceptance of supplication is well-known. And its system is Fiṭrā'īl, under whom there are five officers, and each officer is an officer of 70 ranks. When the reciter mentions this name in seclusion according to its number after every prayer, the servant descends upon him and fulfills his need. And when he writes it on a silver watch—sleeping on silver—with the name of the Mukil, then enters seclusion and recites 100 times after every prayer, the Mukil descends upon him, he will see his vision, and when he calls them, they will be present. Its design is this.
