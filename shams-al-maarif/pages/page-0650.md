The name of his client (mowkal) is Riti'il, and the mention of this name is as follows:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You, O Sustainer of those in perplexity and the Eternal of eternities before the passing times and the safety that perishes, O Compeller, O Holy One, O He Who is the First and the Last and the Manifest and the Hidden, O Creator of creation, O Determiner of time and the moment, transport me from this small and perishing sea and the perishing creation, and carry my spirit with Your noble angels who are near and virtuous, and remove my nature from the natures of humanity, O Eternal of eternities, O Destroyer of creation and the world, O He Who in His kingdom is a Compeller, there is no god but You, the One, the Vanquisher, the Mighty, the Compeller. I ask You to support me with the worlds of this name, so that they may vanquish every arrogant one. O Allah, O Compeller, answer, O King Rutfa'il, and trust in such and such by the right of His mighty name.

And recite the verse to the end; the purpose will be achieved.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Mutakabbir" (The Proud)

[End frame]

Know that the "mutakabbir" (one who claims greatness) is he who considers everything in comparison to his own self as insignificant, and attributes grandeur solely to his own self, and looking at others is like a king looking at his slaves.

**Understanding the attribute of greatness within oneself is ignorance for a human being:**

Thus, this pure essence belongs to God Almighty; whoever besides Him considers greatness for himself is ignorant. The absolute "Mutakabbir" is only the essence of God Almighty, and one should know Him in this way.

**The attribute of Allah's greatness is manifest in every world:**

When God Almighty created the high heavens and the low earths, He first manifested the light of existence as the veil of false deities. Then this light circumambulated and remained in agitation; the left wind stirred, and it spoke with wisdom and eloquence with divine favor. Then upon it, Allah the Most High revealed the lights of mercy, which were established for it in the world of divine oneness, and with which You have witnessed the realities of divine actions. Thus, with every particle that has settled, this is from the attribute of divinity, and this attribute is manifest in religion and the world and outward in the creation. This is not an attribute that is manifest in one world and hidden in another.

**Allah gives the vision of His greatness to the servant with whom He intends goodness:**

When God Almighty intends goodness and well-being for a servant, He gives him the vision of the attribute of greatness. In the world, He helps him with mercy. At that time, that servant becomes intoxicated with this divine blessing.

**The one who shows arrogance is an enemy of Allah:**

In the divine revelation of Allah the Most High: "I have sent down My wrath upon the arrogant ones in the earth—those who said 'I' and followed their desires."
