# Page 589 (English translation)
587

And it has an unshakable effect in love.
Through this operation, the beloved will appear immediately.
When this is written on a blue cloth and placed in a lamp with the name of the desired one and the azīmat is recited, the desired one will appear immediately.
And when a dull-witted person makes this letter into a name 45 times and writes it with a tablet and keeps it with himself, understanding will be granted to him.
And the vice-deputy: let him engrave this letter on a gold or silver ring, and when the moon is in the house of Saʿd and his fast is firm, let him wear it—the love of people will be established in their hearts.

To prevent bad dreams:
There will be no bad dreams if this letter is written with incense and placed at the head of the bed; there will be benefit.
And if this letter is combined with the name of the desired one and kept with oneself, the desired one will become restless from love.

And its azīmat is: let him recite it 45 times after each prayer, except for the condition of seclusion.

بِسْمِ اللّٰہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ ○ ھَبَۃٌ مِّنْ مَّوَاھِبِکَ یَا وَھَّابُ یَا رَزَّاقُ یَا فَتَّاحُ یَا عَلِیْمُ یَا زِیَّادَۃُ یَا شَھَادَۃُ یَا غَایَۃَ قَصْدَاہُ یَا مُنْتَھٰی اَمَلَاہُ یَا مَلْجَاَ الْمُخَالِفِیْنَ اَنْتَ الْاَوَّلُ وَالْاٰخِرُ وَ الظَّاھِرُ وَ الْبَاطِنُ سُبْحَانَکَ لَا اِلٰہَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ ھَبْ لِیْ یَا ھَاھِیْھْمَہ اللہُ یَا ھَا ۲ سَاۃُ اَجِبْ یَا وَاحِدُ عَزِیْزٌ ھَنَا ذَاھَا اَجِبْ اَیُّھَا الْمَلِکُ وَافْعَلْ کَذَا وَ کَذَا الْعَجَلَ یَا حَرْفَ الْھَاءِ اَمَدَّنِیْ بِالْمَحَبَّۃِ عِنْدَ الْخَلْقِ ھَنَا بِلَا حَوْلَ وَ لَا قُوَّۃَ اِلَّا بِاللّٰہِ الْعَلِیِّ الْعَظِیْمِ

Translation: "In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful—[He is] a gift among Your gifts, 'O You who give gifts, O You who provide sustenance, O You who open, O You who know, O You who increase, O You who testify, O You who are the goal of my intention, O You who are the end of my hope, O You who are the refuge of those who oppose—You are the First and the Last and the Manifest and the Hidden. Glory be to You; there is no god but You. Grant me, O Muhrim, [perform] for me obedience and accomplish such and such a task, O Letter Hāʾ—grant me love among creation, here, by the power of 'there is no strength and no power except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great,' O My Deputy, appear now and do my work for me immediately.'"

And its ikhlāṣ (sincerity formula) is:

اَجِبْ اَیُّھَا الْمَلِکُ هُهِيَاتِيْلُ بِحَقِّ دَلْحٍ اَهْلِيْلَكٍ سَلْمُوْخٍ یَاہٍ اَجِبْ وَ تَوَكَّلْ بِكَذَا وَ كَذَا اَلْوَحِي اَلْعَجَل اَلسَّاعَة

THE LETTER WĀW:
The letter of love, and it removes the constriction of the heart and opens [it], and removes tightness.

Through this operation, wealth will increase immediately:
Let him write this, blow on it at the time of breaking the fast, and give it to the owner of wealth—abundant property will be produced.
