# Page 489 (English translation)
Being involved with ʿAlī and belonging to the lowest level of Hell's nethermost layer. Exalted Allah says: "And if they had hastened on, We would have surely outrun you" (Qur'an 21:109). Whoever has understood this secret can never wrap himself in the cloak of deceit and trickery, nor will he share in despair of Allah's mercy like Iblīs. This state becomes accessible to him when he has performed ablution and purified himself from worldly impurities. He has deprived his self of worldly pleasures. His tranquility is written as divine decree states: "Alas, my grief that I neglected my duty to Allah, and verily I was among the mockers" (Qur'an 39:56). O Allah, we seek refuge in You; protect us and keep us safe from this destruction and negligence.

[Boxed Heading: Fourth, the Throne of Grace]

The culmination of spirits and the ascent of the great deeds of spirits is through the reach of secrets; the acceptance of deeds lies in the will of the Rewarder. The reality of being free from want regarding rights extends to the Throne of Grace. With it stands the existence of all things.

[Underlined Heading: Infallible prophets and protected saints]

Regarding the prophets, peace be upon them, their infallibility is immunity from sins. Regarding the saints, protection from sins. Regarding those who repent, mercy. Regarding the deniers, punishment. Whoever has connected his outward deeds to the arena of the Throne of Grace has illuminated the subtleties of his lights. He has illuminated that person's heart with the reality of his love. It has become known that the culmination of formal deeds extends to the Throne of Grace. The Throne for every superior and inferior deed is this.

[Underlined Heading: The secret of the deed is revealed here]

Here the secret of superior and inferior deeds becomes manifest, and the agents themselves come to know whether their deed has reached acceptance at the Throne of Grace through the manifestation of insight or not. For this manifestation of acceptance, there is neither any register here, nor the form and shape of deeds, nor writings. Rather, all attributions—whether formal or spiritual, whether superior or inferior—are terminated. Here the deed remains through actual state, order, witnessing, and spiritual ecstasy.

[Boxed Heading: Fifth, the Throne of Glory]

Exalted Allah states: "Allah, there is no deity except Him, the Lord of the Throne of Glory" (Qur'an 27:26). He manifests the elements and the light of insight with realities as they were before their existence, and after they came into being when they traversed the boundaries of the kingdom. When he witnesses this, he will see wondrous craftsmanship bestowed upon him as a favor. This is for those whom Allah has blessed with proximity. This is the limit of the Throne of Glory.

[Underlined Heading: The culmination of deeds of the heart and mind]

There is also a culmination of deeds and intellectual worlds toward things, and pure speech ascends toward it. Through its mention, the secrets of the Speech of Truth are obtained without the written letters and known bodies. This is sufficient regard; it annihilates his own existence. It is the Speech that illuminates his lights and manifests to the people of insight. The revelation of the realities of their lights to those who show reverence. This is the Throne of Glory, which is from among the names of the Lord of Majesty. The joy that is granted to the people of vision and that with which they are adorned from the lights of the people of honor.
