# Page 391 (English translation)
391

O Allah, I ask You, in whose hand is the dominion of all things. I ask You by the light of the secrets of the radiance of Your Names and with the light of the luminosity of the effulgence of Your Throne, and by all that You have deposited in the Preserved Tablet (Lawḥ Maḥfūẓ), which is from the secrets of Your Names, and by all that You taught to Adam, peace be upon him, and inspired to him, and You have said in Your luminous Speech: "And He taught Adam the names, all of them" — and I ask You by Your Glory, Beauty, and Perfection and by Your Most Generous and Most Great Light, You Who are the Most Luminous and Most Holy, and by that which You have deposited in the Hidden of the hidden, that is, from these Noble Names.

[Heading:]
Chapter: The Great Talisman for Glory, Status, Acceptance, and Love

The explanation of these Names that were inscribed under the knowledge of Joseph, peace be upon him. These Names possess great efficacy in matters of status, acceptance, and love. Through their means, desires are attained with kings, princes, and ministers. This is a characteristic of this talisman.

The Accepted Prayer of Needs

I mention a noble practice. Once I was performing prayer in a mosque where there were also those friends who lived and studied with me in the service of Shaykh Abū 'Abdallāh Haytī. When I saw them, I approached them to greet them with "Peace be upon you," but I observed that they were standing and occasionally gazing at the sky and sometimes at their hands. When I drew very near to them, I heard them reciting this prayer:

Allāhumma yā Mujīb al-Da'wāt wa yā Qāḍī al-Ḥājāt wa yā Mufarrij al-Kurabāt min fawq sab'i samawāt wa yā Fātiḥ Khazā'in al-Karāmāt wa yā Qāḍī Ḥawā'ij al-Sā'ilīn wa yā Sāmi' al-Aṣwāt wa yā Ghāfir al-Zallāt wa yā Muqīl al-'Atharāt wa yā Munzil al-Barakāt wa yā Man aḥāṭa 'ilmuka bi-kulli shay'in wa as'aluka an tuṣalliya wa tusallima 'alā sayyidinā Muḥammad wa 'alā āli sayyidinā Muḥammad wa an taqḍiya ḥājatī wa hiya kadha wa kadha bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi al-asmā'i yā Huwa Huwa Huwa Huwa yā Huwa subbūḥun quddūsun rabbu al-malā'ikati wa al-rūḥi as'aluka yā Rabba bi- [damaged source due to watermark] al-asmā'i al-rafī'ati wa bi-mā fī hādhihi al-da'wāti min al-barakāti an taqḍiya ḥājatī.
