Sixth Treatise:
He should engrave this on a piece of wood and fumigate it with frankincense, and after reading this speech seven times, place it in a place where protection is needed, such as a treasury or garden or house. Any person who comes that way will see a man standing, and will flee from fear of him. This man's hand will appear to be a flame of fire. When this wood is removed, the talisman will be dissolved. Ninth Treatise:
Go into a forest, draw a circle around yourself, sit within it, and place ash (raqq) in front of you outside the circle, and read the speech. Write these names on a piece of paper and attach it to a piece of wood for the oven. All the birds and wild animals of the forest and common animals of that kind will gather. Among them, whichever has wood and bones—I will grant leave to depart. To grant leave to depart means: remove the paper from the wood.
