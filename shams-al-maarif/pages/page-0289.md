**Chapter 46: Properties of the Name Al-Wasi' (The Vast/Encompassing) - Longevity of Life, Expansion of Sustenance, and Acquisition of Knowledge**

Whoever frequently mentions this honored name and noble secret, Allah the Most High will grant expansion in his sustenance and knowledge. His life will be long. This name is from the greatest names (asma' jalila). The heart of its practitioner never feels distress. Allah the Most High creates expansion and spaciousness in all his affairs. Whoever frequently mentions this name, Allah the Most High expands his sustenance and his chest (heart). Whoever writes its square in the state of increased sanctity, reads Surah Fatiha, and mentions this name according to its numbers, and keeps the talisman with him, all his difficult affairs become easy and sustenance is expanded. Kings, princes, and nobles, if they frequently mention this name, their dominion will be vast and their command will be executed. Its number is 137. The seven points toward liberation from every distress. The letter sin is within the arrangement of all names. The letter waw is in the midst of expansion and vastness. The letter alif points toward encompassment and manifestation. For this reason it is a comprehensive number, which is for the manifestation of the names or for that station which is their last in regard to manifestation, which is the kingdom. When this number is arranged in their form, what is the tongue of expansion? This points toward Hazrat Muhammad, peace be upon him, and is compared with the saying: "My vastness encompasses the heart of My believing servant" - meaning My vastness is in the heart of My believing servant. This number is from "Al-Ahad" (The One). Although apparent worship requires freedom, in the sense that for everything its manifestation is interposed between the manifestations of all things - but Allah the Most High is far above anything inhabiting anything or anything inhabiting His essence. This is a subtle indication understood only by those with taste. Note: Whoever has witnessed greatness says: "I saw Allah before seeing anything. I am the interior of greatness and the exterior of vastness." For this reason greatness is called "izar" (waist-wrap/clothing). Understand, these are the subtleties of tawhid (divine unity). Its letters point to the 137 names toward the Angel of the Spirit (Malak al-Ruh), for the expansion of its encompassment. Its square has this form:

**Chapter 47: Properties of the Name Al-Hakim (The Wise), Benefits of Knowledge and Wisdom**

Whoever frequently mentions this name, Allah the Most High will grant him knowledge of the hidden sciences, meanings, subtleties, indications, and inspirations. This is from the illustrious names. Its talisman should be written in the first hour of Wednesday in the dignity of Mercury, kept with oneself, and this name should be mentioned - then he will be adorned with the morals of the wise and attributed with their customs.
