# Page 388 (English translation)
He left, and after completing forty fasts, he returned and said, "Master, the forty fasts are complete." The sheikh said, "Now you are also worthy of this virtue." The sheikh went into his cell and came out with a letter, examining it carefully. He kissed it, placed it upon his head, and gave it to that person. The sheikh also gave him some instructions, which he accepted gladly, kissed the sheikh's hand, and departed.

After he left, I went to the sheikh, kissed his hand, and said, "Master, what was that letter you gave him?" He said, "O Ahmad, this is a secret of Allah the Most High, about which Allah has informed those people with whom Allah the Most High desires good. I said, "Will you inform me about it?" The sheikh gave no answer. I thought to myself, "I will ask him at another time." Then after six days, I came to his service and asked about that letter, but he gave no answer. I asked him repeatedly until a full year of my insistence passed. Then one day the sheikh said, "O Ahmad, why do you wish to inquire about it?" I said, "O my master, I wish to know these names so that I may practice them properly." The sheikh said, "If this is your intention, then keep forty days of fasts and abstain from animal products (that is, eat nothing from animals). Then inform me thereafter." I said, "Very well." Then sitting in seclusion, I kept the fasts, and by Allah the Most High's help, after completing them, I came to the sheikh's service. I kissed his hand and said that the fasts are complete. The sheikh said, "Now you are worthy of this virtue." He entered the cell, and after a long time came out holding a similar letter.

The sheikh kissed it and said, "O Ahmad, do you know what is in this letter?" I said, "I do not know." The sheikh said, "In it are those names that were written upon the staff of Moses, peace be upon him, and these same names were written upon the staff of Shuaib, peace be upon him, and these were upon Joseph's shirt, peace be upon him. These same names were written upon Daniel's turban, peace be upon him, and these were with Abraham, peace be upon him, when he was cast into the fire. These were with Jesus, peace be upon him, and he informed his disciples about them, among whom was Simon. By their blessing, they would cure the sick. Those who possess these names—wild beasts and creatures fear them. Allah the Most High protects him from the harm of jinn and humans. The tongues of evil-speakers and enemies' weapons are sealed against him, so that if this person goes to war, no enemy can overcome him, and the enemy's army is defeated. Whoever has headache, eye pain, or any bodily illness should write these names on a membrane of deer or mina and keep it with him. Let him write Surah al-Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, Surah al-Ikhlas, and the Mu'awwidhatayn in a Chinese vessel with musk, saffron, and rose water and drink it, then Allah the Most High will protect him from all illnesses and ailments. If he writes in the same manner we have described and goes to a ruler, king, or minister and says this supplication in his heart:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi l-asmā'i an ta'qida lisāna fulānin ibni fulānatin shāhatil-wujūhu wa ta'inatil-wujūhu lil-ḥayyi l-qayyūmi wa qad khāba man ḥamala ẓulmān

"O Allah, I ask You by the right of these names that You seal the tongue of so-and-so son of so-and-so. All faces have bowed to the Living Self-Subsisting One, and surely he who bore wrongdoing has failed."

Then let him blow upon himself and go before him, his need will be fulfilled, and he will be protected from his harm. Let him keep these names with him, and he will obtain honor among people, and whoever sees him will honor him. All judgments of the world are made by these names. The proofs and divine wonders come into the hands of unworthy people who do not know their value.
