# Page 330 (English translation)
If this whispering arises in the heart, one should say: "I have believed in Allah and His Messenger ﷺ," and the whispering will be removed.
It is narrated from Lady Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her, that she said: The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: Whoever experiences this type of whispering should say three times: "I have believed in Allah, the Most High, and His Messenger ﷺ"—and the whispering will be removed. Muslim narrated from Uthman ibn Abi al-As, may Allah be pleased with him, that he said: I said to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ: "O Messenger of Allah, Satan intervenes between me and my prayer and makes my recitation uncertain for me." The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "That satan's name is Khanzab. When you feel this whispering, recite AthazhibiLlah and spit three times to your left." Uthman ibn Abi al-As said: "I did so and the whispering left me." Khanzab is written with a dotted kha, a silent ba, then za, and with a pointed ba. Scholars have differed in determining the vowel of the kha. Some say it is with fatha, some with zabar, and some with pesh.

Abu Dawud narrated from Ibn Zamil, who said: I said to Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with both of them: "What is this matter that comes to my heart and goes out?" Ibn Abbas said: "What matter? Like this." He said: "I will not describe it." Then he said: "There are some doubts." Ibn Abbas said: "No soul has been spared from them." Allah, the Most High, revealed this verse: "If you are in doubt about what We have revealed to you..." Then he said to me: "When whispering arises in your heart, recite this: 'Huwa al-awwalu wal-akhiru wal-zahiru wal-batinu wa huwa bi kulli shay'in 'alim'—He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He has knowledge of all things." Some scholars say that whoever experiences whispering during wudu, prayer, and the like should recite La ilaha illallah, because Satan flees upon hearing dhikr of Allah, and La ilaha illallah is the chief of dhikr. For this reason, the great masters teach this same dhikr to their disciples and say that the most beneficial remedy for whisperings is the abundance of dhikr of Allah.

Sheikh Ahmad Khuari says: I complained to Abu Sulayman al-Darani about whispering and sin. He said: "When you want to remove whispering from its root, at the moment the whispering arises, become very happy, because your happiness will drive the whispering away, since Satan finds a Muslim's happiness most unbearable. If you become sad, the whispering will increase." Sheikh Taqi al-Din says: This saying confirms the saying of some scholars. Their saying is that one of complete faith and sincere devotion is afflicted with whispering, because a thief goes only to the place where wealth is. Abu al-Darda, may Allah be pleased with him, said: Whoever reads the verse "Fa-in tawallaw faqul hasbiya Allah" seven times every day, Allah, the Most High, will help him in all his worldly and religious affairs. In another narration, a man fell and fell [damaged text: (he has a convulsion)] will die—list from Sa'd—the thigh of a man was torn open. A man came to him and said: "Place your hand where the pain is and say: 'Fa-qul hasbiya Allah la ilaha illa huwa 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbu al-'arsh al-'azhim.'" That man recited this and his thigh was healed. One of the special properties of this verse is that if one writes it and keeps it with oneself, then goes before any ruler, his need will be fulfilled.
