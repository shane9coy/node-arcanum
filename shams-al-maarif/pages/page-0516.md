# Page 516 (English translation)
514

The call (of awareness) belongs to the spirit, and the third call (comes) from the heart and intellect, and the fourth call comes from the secrets. And until the servant reaches the "ain,"1 it does not deceive you with this thing. It informs you of hours and degrees and minutes and seconds, and from the third and fourth degrees up to the unlimited. Then the rest continues. Every moment it says, "I am returning to my origin," and such is the speed of the wind, and the most delicate of all these is the breath that warns you at all times, whether it enters or exits. And these are divine inspirations (lahatif) exclusively for God, knowledge of which is heard through divine secrets and symbols. As God Almighty says:
اِنَّ اللّٰهَ يُسْمِعُ مَنْ يَّشَاءُ ۖ وَمَا اَنْتَ بِمُسْمِعٍ مَّنْ فِی الْقُبُوْرِ
"Verily, God makes hear whom He wills, and you cannot make hear those who are in the graves."

Poetry:
لَقَدْ أَسْمَعْتَ لَوْ نَادَيْتَ حَيًّا وَلَكِنْ لَا حَيَاةَ لِمَنْ تُنَادِيْ
"Verily you would make hear if you called to one who is alive, but there is no life for him whom you call."

The governance of scholars is goodness with God's creation:
And the scholars say: when God Almighty desires to do good to a community, He gives the rulership to its scholars, and the scholars (give it) to its kings. A certain person asked a philosopher, "Who is a king?" He said, "He who has captured his desires and has become subservient to the pleasure of his Lord."

فَكُنْ كَاتِمًا اِنْ نِلْتَ بِالْعِلْمِ مَرْيَمًا فَكِتْمَانُهَا عِنْدَ الْحَكِيْمِ مِنَ الْفَرْضِ
"If you have obtained a rank through knowledge, then conceal it, for its concealment is obligatory in the view of the wise."

The statement of scholars regarding the unseen (ghayb):
Know that the cycle of God's sovereignty in the unseen is (as follows). God Almighty says: عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ "Knower of the unseen and the witnessed." Some scholars say that speaking about the unseen is not permissible because it is God's secret with which He honored Adam, peace be upon him.

The statement of mystics (ārifīn) regarding the unseen and secrets:
And a mystic says: These secrets have also been granted to a group of people of understanding, whose number only God Almighty knows. And He said: وَمَنْ يُّؤْتَ الْحِكْمَةَ فَقَدْ أُوْتِيَ خَيْرًا كَثِيْرًا "And whoever is granted wisdom, then surely he has been given abundant good."

God, blessed and exalted, has described in His Book the conditions of the former and latter peoples, and there is no secret such that it is not in it. He says: وَلَا رَطْبٍ وَلَا يَابِسٍ اِلَّا فِي كِتٰبٍ مُّبِيْنٍ "And no moist and no dry but it is in a clear Book" — that is, the Preserved Tablet in which everything is written, as God Almighty says: مَّا فَرَّطْنَا فِي الْكِتٰبِ مِنْ شَيْءٍ نٓ ۚ وَالْقَلَمِ وَمَا يَسْطُرُونَ "We have not neglected anything in the Book" — and the Pen and what they inscribe. "We have written in the Book every thing in detail."2
