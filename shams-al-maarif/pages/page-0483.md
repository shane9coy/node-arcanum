# Page 483 (English translation)
481

Over all things is He capable

**The Letter Qāf:**
The letter Qāf is a "warm-talking" letter. Sometimes dryness is also in it.
Properties and benefits: Its properties are related to strength. Its names which begin with the letter Qāf are "Qawī" (The Powerful), "Qā'im" (The Self-Subsisting), "Qadīm" (The Ancient), "Qadr" (The Decree).
If Qāf is inscribed twenty-one times on an iron plate and tied to the arms, then one can lift the heaviest of weights. Allah the Most High has made the letter Qāf a source of power, such as bringing weakness to falsehood, bringing the complete world to destroy the oppressors [unclear].

**Every creature fears it:**
If its numbers are written on lion skin in the first hour of the day of Mars or the Sun and tied to the arm, then beasts, birds, jinn and wild animals, the jinn kings and human kings shall remain fearful of it.

**No appointed jinn can frighten it:**
If one writes Qāf without a dot and sits inside the cell of the ascetic practicing spiritual discipline and performs the operation, then no appointed jinn or any other can cause them any trouble or frighten them.

**For lifting heavy weights:**
Those names of Allah the Most High which begin with the letter Qāf. If inscribed on a silver ring during Mars and worn, then one will be able to lift the heaviest of burdens. Its figure is this:

[Table: A rectangular grid of 3 rows and 6 columns. Every cell contains the Arabic letter Qāf (ق).]

The supplication is this:
O Allah, I ask You by Your powerful, self-subsisting name, O Self-Subsisting One, that You subjugate for me Your noble angels, for truly You have power over all things.

**The Letter Kāf:**
The letter Kāf is a "warm-talking" [letter], fortunate.
Properties and benefits: Write the letter "K" four times on any vessel and place it on the spleen; there will be healing.
