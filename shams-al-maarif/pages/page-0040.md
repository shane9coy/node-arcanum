It is dry. The air is warmer. This is the nature of blood. The nature of phlegm is colder. The nature of black bile is cold and dry. These are eight balanced [humors]. Among them is the ratio of one to seven. The heavens are also seven. The heaven of Saturn, the heaven of Jupiter, the heaven of Mars, the heaven of the Sun, the heaven of Venus, the heaven of Mercury, and the heaven of the Moon. The days are also seven. The earths and the heavens are also seven, and every sphere is contained within it. After this comes the ratio of one to five. The prayers are five, and the pillars of the declaration of faith are also five. Those are three realms. The realm of this world, the realm of the barrier, and the realm of the afterlife. After this comes the ratio of one, and that is the intellect. Thus, in the number sixteen there are eight pairs and eight odd numbers. Every pair meets every odd number, and every odd number meets every pair. Its example is like this: one and one make two. Three and three make six. Three is odd and six is a pair, and in the same way consider the pair.

[Heading: The Attainment of Good Deeds and Blessing in All Affairs]

The numerical form of the letter dal with a triangle, which is also called Hindi, which will be explained later, contains wonderful secrets. The method is to make a grid of boxes and write their letters according to abjad instead of numerals. When you wish to perform this operation, keep a fast for two weeks and eat nothing except dry bread. Remain occupied in the remembrance of God day and night; this operation depends upon purity and asceticism. Take a clean and gilded plate and engrave the design on it [unclear]. Before engraving, face the qiblah and perform two units of prayer, in which after Surah Fatiha you recite Ayat al-Kursi once and Surah Ikhlas. Perform this operation on Thursday in the hour of Jupiter when the Moon is conjoined with both Jupiter and the Sun. The ascendant of the time should be Cancer, and every Thursday burn mastaki and white sandalwood with frankincense. Whoever keeps this engraved tablet with him, God Almighty will make his religious and worldly affairs easy for him and will grant him success in good deeds.

[Heading: Expansion of Sustenance]

God Almighty will make the means of sustenance easy for him and will grant him blessing in everything. Whoever writes this design and keeps it in his shop or treasury, his wealth and sustenance will have blessing and abundance. Regarding blessing, its properties will be explained, God willing, later.

[Heading: Protection from Thieves and Robbers]

Whoever writes this design on deerskin at the time of sunrise on Thursday and keeps it with him, he will be protected from the evil and mischief of thieves and robbers and from every kind of wickedness.

[Decorative Box Header: The Secrets of Numbers and Their Benefits]

The science of numbers is the first matter of the sciences. Now I will briefly tell the secrets of some numbers and the properties that God Almighty has deposited within them, whose benefits and harms people should also know, and I will inform them from the list of these. I will also write here the secrets of those letters that are at the beginning of the first twenty-eight surahs of the Books of God. No one knows this except the special servants of God. The divine names and the secrets of the Greatest Name that I have explained in this book can also be compared to this science. Whoever reads them and understands them will derive benefit.
