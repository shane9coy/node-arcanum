[Heading in decorative cartouche]
The operation of becoming hidden from people's sight

[Urdu text below heading]
Some practitioners say: whoever makes a squat on the ground, draws a circle around himself with his finger, kisses his hands, and says: "O servants, these are the names and verses by their right upon you, except what you have hidden from the people and from all creation" – then becomes silent and speaks nothing, will remain hidden from everyone. And whoever frequently recites these verses and maintains this practice will witness the wonders of Divine creation, the description of which makes the tongue powerless. Know that whoever writes these names repeatedly with determination and steadfastness in the honor of the sun and keeps them with himself, his rank will be elevated and his secret will be revealed. These are from the hidden mysteries. Those names are: Allah, Latif, Malak, Kayyin, 'Alim, Muhaymin, Hafi, Tayyib, Salih, Hayy, Qayyum, Nur, HADI
