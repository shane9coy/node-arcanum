Indeed You are the Knower of the Unseen—the True saying, and to Him belongs sovereignty. Amen. Then: O Guide, O Rightly Guided, O Knower of the Unseen, O Knower of Secrets, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds.

Whoever reads this supplication twenty-five times, having performed two units of prayer, will have success in every endeavor and will remain protected from harm.

This supplication is the Greater Red [Kibrīt Aḥmar]:

This supplication is the Greater Red. Experience it and see—it is one of the great names of God, and the entire secret lies in swift answered prayer. Among the verses, these verses are appropriate for it:

"And with Him are the keys to the unseen" etc.

The name Khabīr [the Aware]: Whoever mentions it for seven consecutive days, a representative [mukīl] will stand before him as a guide and inform him [of secrets].

The name Mubīn [the Manifest]: Whoever reads it one thousand times daily, observing a fast, with ritual purification and ablution—all spirits will attend him. Whichever he wishes he may keep with him, and whomever he wishes he may dismiss. This name should be recited at sunrise when the temperament and spirit are balanced, and this person will obtain wisdoms that no other obtains.

And these are names of the Exalted One that display wondrous and strange effects:

"He is Allah, there is no god but He, the All-High, the Knower of the Unseen, the Wise, the Aware, the Guardian, the Watcher, the Manifest, the Guide"

Whatever you find difficult to remember will become easy by the blessing of these names. It is most sufficient; better than reading along with it:

"The True saying, and to Him belongs sovereignty" etc.

These are the names of the people of proximity and connection. Whoever mentions them obtains great sciences and is honored with the request for blessings.

He understands the speech of animals:
He understands the speech of animals. These names have special efficacy for revealing hidden things.

Their recitation during the hour of Jupiter, while chewing a ball of honey. Reminding the forgotten is an effect of this star, as is reminding of ancient love, readiness for its protection and care, and befriending rulers and artisans—all of this lies within its effects. Beware that the Moon not be malefic; let it be benefic.
