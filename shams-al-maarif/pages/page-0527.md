# Page 527 (English translation)
**How Many Days Will the King Rule, and When Will He Die**

The second method is as follows: when you wish to know the circumstances of a king's reign, how many days he will rule. Take the numerical values of the letters of his name according to the Jabal calculation, then look at the letters of the aforementioned name: if it is quadrilateral, meaning it has four letters and the first letter is alif, subtract two from its number. Then take the remaining numbers and multiply them by themselves twice (that is, by their fifth, meaning by multiplying them internally twice). Subtract the past centuries from the resulting number and see what remains. If there are thousands in it, drop the hundreds of the date that you have, and if there are hundreds remaining from the thousands, move them backward to the hundred's place where those before them were. If they equal the years of the date, subtract from it in the same manner; if something less remains, move it backward to the previous place. Whatever number this reaches, that is the decree—that is, he will rule for such a time—and upon that which precedes it, say that this is the desired duration. For example, his name is Ahmad ibn Daniyyal. Ahmad has four letters, and the numerical value of these four is 53. Subtract 2 from them; 51 remains. Multiply these by themselves, giving 2,601. The duration of his birth is Wednesday, the day of Jumada al-Akhirah 685. Subtracting the external multiplication yields one year remaining. Then subtract 600 from 1,000, leaving 400. Then we moved it backward and combined it with the place before it, which is 3, and before that is 50, making 846 together. But now it is not known whether these are 45 years, or months, or days. This is clarified by inference from the gift, that this duration is 40 years and 6 months. Another rule is that if the numbers refer to the earlier ones, wait for them to be counted, then give the same number of months after them. If his lifespan is not completed, understand that these are years—that is, if he does not die in 6 days or 6 months, understand that he will die in 6 years. And Allah knows best!

And if the name is quinque-literal, meaning it has five letters and one letter is repeated in it, such as Mikaram, apply the same rule that you have already done, meaning subtract two from its numbers, then multiply the remainder by itself, and observe the external multiplication. If the letter repeats three times, repeat the external multiplication three times. Then proceed after that in the aforementioned manner; the desired meaning will be obtained. And if it is a three-letter name and it does not contain single letters or the mashqatah letter, multiply the numbers of the name by themselves, and subtract 300 from the external multiplication according to the date you have, but here until the years of the date are less than what remains. Then move it backward and combine it with the hundreds remaining from the external multiplication. If these become greater than the hundreds of the date, subtract the hundreds of the date from them until they become less. Then combine these with that number which is the lesser and lower; the desired result will be obtained. For example, his name is Hafiz, with 118. Subtract two from it, and multiply 116 by themselves, giving 12,960. Then the previous remainder became 856, and after subtraction it became 356, making a total of 13 days and 6 or 5 months. His reign will be 8 months and 20 days. If the name begins with a mashqatah letter and a repeated letter, multiply the letters by their fifth multiplied by two, as stated. Then extract a ruling upon it for an equal or greater number, and what is in the repeated portion. The remainder will be one century or less. Move it backward and combine it with the number before it. For example, his name is Furqan. Its total number is 400, and the total remainder is 288. We subtracted three complete centuries from it, meaning 700; 100 remains, and these are less than the date. Move the aforementioned name backward to this place, and before it was 7, these are years, and before these the 4 were months. If you also subtract the days and hours of the current year from the date, because the time in question is when you subtract from it the days that have passed.
