# Page 780 (English translation)
Blessed is he who saw me, and blessed is he who saw one who saw me.

Sheikh Abu Abd Allah Sufali, may his secret be sanctified, wrote an excellent exposition of this noble hadith in his treatise. The Chosen Messenger of God, peace and blessings be upon him, said:

*Tūbā li-man ra'ānī wa tūbā li-man ra'ā man ra'ānī*

That is: "There is happiness for that person, and he is fortunate, in whom the blessings of my gaze and my witnessing had effect. And there is also happiness for that person in whom the gaze of that person had effect, in whom my gaze had effect. In this same way, this chain continues among the friends of God. Therefore, whoever is affected by the gaze of any saint, that too is an effect of the Prophetic gaze. And the effect of every person corresponds to his state. And this is the reason why the influence of the masters flows upon their disciples and will continue to flow until the Day of Judgment, God the Almighty willing, because the chains of lights are like the chains of commands—indeed, they are more delicate and subtle than them."

O you who have come into possession of this book of mine, know that I have explained and expounded in the chapters of this book whatever the Almighty Lord inspired in me. And everyone who did a favor to his inner self regarding my condition revealed in it upon my tongue subtle points of doubt and unveiled knowledges and the garden of jacinth and the narcissus garden and the shining myrtle garden and the luminous pearl and the agate lamp and the luminous brilliances and the spiritual theophanies and the maryami luminous form of light and the theurgic wisdom and the Solomonic courage and the sacrifice of the invitation and the Mosaic staff and the garment of Adam and the tablets of glass and the ark of Noah and Noah's garments and the Night of Destiny and the solid magic and the Nazarene jewels and the triple prayer and the olive radiance, the flame neither eastern nor western, and the tree of the veil and the divine veil and the musk fragrance and the spiritual smoke and the Arabian lights and the Indian president and the Aphlihi president and the alchemy of letters and the bejeweled sciences and the prophets of the people and the Indian numbers and the Greek and the Indian figures and the Furqanic secrets and the Roman آثار (vestiges) and the calligrapher's properties and the divine names and the Aliyan locations and the Arifian worship and the Qudsi words and the Alofian invitation and the electric revelations and the zawiyyan lights, individual unveilings and the underground treasuries and the Asafi talisman—I have set them in motion. So I have written them in this book, and in it are the Greater Healing and the Red Cinnabar and the Topaz Zahra and the Green Chrysolite and the Hidden Jewel and the Hidden Pearl and the Greatest Name and the Mention of Lights and the musk Jafri and the ambergris Ashhab. From its study, the secrets of guidance and the landmarks of ultimate ends will be revealed to you. So there is happiness for that person who circumambulates around its Kaaba and stands upon its Arafah of gnosis.

Verse:
*Its meanings are beneath the letters, as if it were [unclear] an illuminating verse*

*Ma'aniha tacht al-horuf ka-annaha* [unclear] *ayatun tushriq*

That is: beneath its letters are the realities like the lights of the moon, which I have placed in the knot of the bride; I have placed subtler collections than these, and I have revealed some of their secrets. And were it not for the fear of the dissemination of secrets, I would have made them manifest.

Moreover, understanding the command of the Presence, peace and blessings be upon him, is also necessary. He said:

*Iffsha al-sirri al-rububiyyati kufrun*

That is: "The disclosure of the lordly secret is disbelief." And the command of the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib, upon him be peace, is:

*Khatibu al-nasa ala qadri uqulihim*

That is: "Speak to people according to the measure of their intellects."

God the Almighty says:

*Wa in qila kun wa illa jannadat nakharatina wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-aliyyi al-azhim*

And if it is said: "Be," otherwise our decaying corpses will assemble, and there is no power nor strength except through God, the High, the Mighty
