# Page 104 (English translation)
102
And the Manifest, I ask you to fulfill my need by the right of Him by whom I have sworn upon you. O Allah, I ask You by the light of lights and the knower of secrets: You are Allah, the King, the Compeller, the Mighty, the Dominant. To You belong praise and thanksgiving and glory and blessings. I have believed in You; there is no deity except You. I ask You, O Allah, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Lord, I ask You, O the Encompasser, O the Knowing, O the Powerful, O the Seeing, O the Vast, O the Originator, O the Hearing, O the Sufficient, O the Everlasting, O the Thankful, O Allah, O the One, O the Forgiving, O the Clement, O the Seizer, O the Expander, O the Living, O the Self-Subsisting, O the Sublime, O the Great, O the Protector, O the Praiseworthy, O the Bestower, O the Upright, O the Swift, O the Watcher, O the Aware, O the Responder, O the Cause of Death, O the best of masters and the best of helpers, O the Preserver, O the Near, O the Responder, O the Strong, O the Firm, O the Doer of what He wills, O the Great, O the Exalted, O the Benefactor, O the Most Compassionate, O the Truthful, O the Resurrector, O the Generous, O the Truth, O the Manifest, O the Light, O the Guide, O the Opener, O the Much-Forgiving, O the Severe in Punishment, O Possessor of Glory, O Possessor of Bounty, O the Provider, O the Hidden, O the Holy, O the Source of Peace, O the Giver of Faith, O the Guardian, O the Mighty, O the Compeller, O the Proud, O the Creator, O the Maker, O the Former, O the Bringer Forth, O the One, O the Eternal, O He who did not beget and was not begotten and there is none equal to Him, O Allah, O the Creator, there is no deity except You. I ask You by the right of these names with You and their honor with You, that You subjugate for me the spiritual essence of this day and this hour and this moment and the four directions. Indeed, You are over all things powerful. I swear upon you, O spiritual essences, that you be helpers for me in what I seek. Answer, O Mahlub, and do that which is between You and me, He who said to the heavens and the earth: "Come, willingly or unwillingly." They said: "We have come willingly."

[Heading] Translation

In the name of Allah, the most great, the most merciful. O Darayil, I have taken an oath from you and from your helpers. I ask you to fulfill my need, by means of Him by whom I have taken an oath from you. O Allah, I ask You, O Light of lights and the knower of secrets, You are Allah, the King, the Compeller, the Mighty, the Dominant, the Possessor of praise and thanksgiving and glory and blessings. I have accepted faith in You; there is no deity except You. I ask You, O Allah, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Lord, I ask You, O the Encompasser, O the Knowing, O the Powerful, O the Seeing, O the Expander, O the Originator, O the Hearing, O the Sufficient, O the Everlasting, O the Provider, O the Thankful, O Allah, O the One, O the Forgiving, O the Clement, O the Seizer and Expander, O the Living, O the Self-Subsisting, O the Sublime, O the Great, O the Protector, O the Praiseworthy, O the Forgiver, O the Upright, O the Swift, O the Watcher, O the Aware, O the Acceptor of prayers, O the Striker, O the best of masters and the best of helpers, O the Preserver, O the Near, O the Acceptor of prayers, O the Strong, O the Firm, O the One who does all that He intends. O the Great, O the Exalted, O the Benefactor, O the Best of creators, O the Truthful, O the Resurrector, O the Great, O the Truth Manifest, O the Light, O the Guide, O the Opener, O the Much-Forgiving, O the Severe in Grip, O the Possessor of Glory, O the Bestower of bounty, O the Provider, O the Hidden, O the Holy, O the Source of Peace, O the Giver of Faith, O the Guardian, O the Mighty, O the Compeller, O the Proud, O the Creator, O the Maker, O the Fashioner, O the Initiator, O the One, O the Carefree, the Being who did not beget anyone nor was begotten by anyone, and no one is His equal. O Allah, O the Creator, there is no deity except You. I ask You by the right of these names which are with You, make the spiritual beings of this hour my helpers. O He whom I seek, by His right who said to the heavens and the earth: "Both of you, whether you will or not, come in submission." Then they said: "We have come willingly."
