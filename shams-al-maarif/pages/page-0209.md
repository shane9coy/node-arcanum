# Page 209 (English translation)
207

"Yet once they see a sign, they flee it. But when they beheld it, lo! it smote them. Say: The Quran is a warning. And no one knows the forces your Lord let loose upon them, but that is a stern lesson and warning. And they all respond to the names of Allah and submit to them."

I adjure you, O you pure spirits, by the right of what I have recited to you of the names of Allah the Almighty and His verses—let not one of you hold back. Answer, and listen, and attend, and enter into all earthly things. Answer, O company of earthly ones, by the right of what I have recited to you. Answer by the right of the names of Allah the Almighty. Answer willingly for the names of Allah, Lord of the Worlds. Answer—let not one of you hold back.

And regarding the rulers of this land whose command prevails over you—I have made them stand, and they attend before me. O Ruqayyal, you are present, and my helpers and all the tribes are present and under your command. Accept and become my helpers in the matters I command, by the right of Him whose name I have recited to you by the authority of Allah the Great. O Allah, be my helper and assistant. Muska'il, by the right of Bashir, Allah, and all the tribes of all times. O Jibra'il, by the right of the name written upon the heart of the moon, and by the right of Allah the One, the Conqueror, accept and by the right of the matters that are not permitted, regarding this land whose command prevails over you, accept—Jibra'il and Allah, upon whom is the Highest—become my helpers upon this. I desire it. O Ahmar tribe, regarding this land whose command prevails over you. O Shimma'il, accept—your helpers, your subordinates, your tribes, and all the obedient ones, all accept and do what I desire from you, by the right of Sabuhh Quddus, who is the Lord of the angels and the spirit. Accept, become obedient ones and believers in His names. O Mika'il, by the right of the verses and the wise remembrance, accept, and regarding this enemy who created the heavens and the earth, and He is the Knower of all things, by Barqan's right, regarding this land whose command prevails over you. O Mika'il, accept—your helpers, your tribes, and your subordinates accept, by the right of Him who said to the heavens and the earth: "Come willingly or by compulsion." They said: "We came willingly." O Israfi'il, by the right of the land of Maiman and the remaining five prayers, accept, by the right of this land whose command prevails over you. Israfi'il, O Israfi'il, accept and become my helpers—your subordinates, your tribes, and the obedient among us, let none of you fall behind, by the right of these great names and the Greatest Name of Allah: La ilaha illa Allah. You are my helper and assistant. O Ayni'il, by the right of the day of Friday and its remaining tribes, who gather the people on that day in which there is no doubt. O Zuba', accept regarding this land whose command prevails over you. Ayni'il, accept—your helpers, your subordinates, the tribes, and those who are subject to your command. O Kusi'il, by His books who subjected what is between the heavens and the earth, regarding the land of Quddus, Hannan, upon the Most High, who encompasses all existence—accept by His right, whose command prevails over you. Kusi'il, you and your helpers, your tribes, and subordinates attend. O earthly ones of the higher realm, spiritual spirits, all accept—become my helpers upon them, I desire from you through them. Accept regarding Him who will exercise dominion through the divine names. Come with obedience, hear my address, and among yourselves
