# Page 190 (English translation)
138

The gates of heaven have been opened. (Verse:) Help and victory from Allah are near—when heaven was opened, the gates became [visible]. O Victory, O Provider, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, and upon our master Muhammad ﷺ, his family, and his companions, may blessings and peace be.

[Section (Fourteenth): The Mention of Dhikr, Invocations, and Subjugations]

Know that each of the divine names has distinct properties, which have been expounded by Shaykh ʿAbd al-Raḥmān Bastāmī, and he has designated these for the friends of Allah—namely, when anyone has a need from their Lord, for indeed He is the very Essence whose hand holds the treasures of all things. Then one should bathe on the day of Thursday, that is, the night of Friday; sit in seclusion at the place of prayer; pray the Maghrib prayer; recite Ayat al-Kursī continuously until one prays the ʿIshāʾ prayer; and perform as many supererogatory prayers as possible. When the final prostration of the Witr prayer arrives, let him read three times:

يَا اللهُ يَا رَبُّ يَا رَحْمٰنُ يَا رَحِيْمُ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّوْمُ بِكَ اَسْتَغِيْثُ

Then let him ask for his need, and it shall be fulfilled.

Benefits of the Verse of the Throne:
The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: Allah, the Most High, created a white pearl, then He placed within this pearl the ḥajar al-shahab—meaning Ayat al-Kursī—and by His honor and His majesty He swore that whoever recites it after each obligatory prayer, for him the eight gates of Paradise shall be opened, through whichever one he wishes to enter. Whoever recites Ayat al-Kursī upon leaving his home, his need shall be fulfilled, his sins shall be erased, and devils shall stay far from him. Allah, the Most High, shall appoint for him angels who will protect him from every illness, affliction, tribulation, and from jinn and humans, etc. I have made the design of Ayat al-Kursī eight by eight digits, within which are the realities of the doors of Paradise and all the realities of the Throne. Whoever [wishes to use this talisman]—it is auspicious on the greater auspicious [time]; on Thursday, after sunrise, when the moon is connected with Jupiter, with the connection being one of affection and rays—at that time, with pure body and pure clothes, outside the inhabited area, with courage and resolute inner intention, let him engrave it on a tablet of pure silver, fumigate it with the fragrance of aloes and ambergris. Let him also fast on that day, and he shall see strange effects by the hidden grace of Allah, the Most High, which will astound the intellect.
