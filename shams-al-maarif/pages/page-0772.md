[Section 1]

**The work of this great talismanic figure that produces effects**

Whoever possesses this talisman shall not be deprived of its benefits. If there is an obstacle in some endeavor, if there is litigation, if victory and triumph are desired, if one wishes to be saved from the harm of oppressors, if one wishes to make enemies feel defeated, or for any matter whose desire arises in the heart—let him write this talisman and keep it with him or bind it upon his arm. God willing, victory and triumph shall be achieved in every way. If any enemy wishes to bring calamity, he shall be defeated under the influence of this talisman. If any girl whose marriage is hindered also wears it, by the grace of the Exalted Lord, her betrothal shall occur very soon. It is also extremely beneficial for bringing the absent to presence, and a woman's pregnancy shall remain protected from miscarriage. If any sick person wears it, he shall be granted cure. In truth, however much this talisman is praised, it falls short, and its blessings are very numerous.

[Section 2]

**Work: for removing fever, trembling, and impotence**

With the blood of a stork, write this blessed talisman on four pieces of cotton thread, and every sensible woman and ordinary female shall also write this verse:

**لَا يَصْلَىٰ نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ وَسَوْفَ تَعْلَمُونَ**

"And he shall not enter the Fire of blazing flame, and you shall soon know"

And keep it with oneself; fever, trembling, and every type of illness shall depart. Its recovery has been firmly established through reason, and concerning it, whoever is asked shall not refuse to provide proper correction. Take the hand of a woman who spins thread on a spinning wheel, write these words, and have her swallow them. God willing, improvement shall come very soon. The aforementioned talisman is as follows:

[Section 3]

The person who is overcome by oppression and injustice should recite Surah Al-Inshirāḥ up to verse thirty, eight times, and place a black peppercorn pressed between pigeon feathers into the fire. Thereafter, upon every piece of paper, let him write this talisman and one verse from these verses, and having engraved it upon a silver tablet, let him place it around his neck or bind it upon his right arm. God willing, every need shall be fulfilled, and through these verses there shall be consolation from God the Exalted.
