# Page 144 (English translation)
142

"He who adopts a good method, for him there is a reward, and there is also a reward until the Day of Resurrection for him who acts upon it."

For me, upon this matter, there is a covenant and pact: that he who is just with pure sincerity and is victorious with the cloak of fidelity, from behind this established law they remove veils and hope for the secret of the straight path. That they close their eyes from the desire of the tongue and see with the eye everything that is hidden, and it becomes known that the world has upper and lower parts. Their total is within the parts. Their number is in the millions. Upon these numbers your dense essence is included, and relative to your celestial form, it is like a single number. In the composite form, the mention of subtleties and grossities has come before in the section on the subtle and gross. He who has attained knowledge of this reality has reached the secret of gratitude and the flow of love. As for souls, which have been mentioned before, the great divine spirit stands with them, which is for the apparent, good, and beloved. Thus this part of the composite world bears the gross. When you intend to double it, divide the total number which is in the fingernails. Whatever emerges from it, add it as a number. That is the original result. Add it as rare letters, then enter it into these parts. By these things the reality has become clear of those which have been named. I have clarified it for you with a hidden secret and complete knowledge.

Allah, the Exalted, maintains steadfast those people in the world and the Hereafter who brought faith with the firm word. May Allah, the Exalted, keep its reality established for us in the world and spread His mercy upon us, for He is the generous, the knowing, the merciful. This is the reality of the doubt of the expanded souls; in the same way, in the lights of wisdom there is also the reality of the realities of worship. Maintain faith and certainty in it and confirm its realities. May Allah, the Exalted, have mercy upon you with His vast mercy and beneficence and generosity, for He is the one who bestows favors with His universal beneficence. May Allah, the Exalted, guide whoever He wills to the straight path. [unclear handwriting]

May Allah bless our master Muḥammad, his family, his companions, and grant them peace.
