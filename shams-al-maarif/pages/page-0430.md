# Page 430 (English translation)
By Your majesty for repelling the error of the proud, You are the Lord of the mighty, the Compeller, the Comrade of the righteous, the Good of the weak and the great, the Reformer of the affairs of creation, the Revealer of the truths, the Hearer of the subtle and the minute. I ask You, O Jabir of every broken thing, and the Helper of the friends through the burdens, and the Lifter of every small and insignificant thing, by the secret You placed in the mountain of Your mercy of the majesty of Your power and the greatness of Your forgiveness and the sources of Your love, that You make me trusting in You in all my affairs, looking to You in all the inner aspects of my actions and my words, and make my reins in Your hand and my submission to You and my refuge and my success in coming to You, O He whose majesty is beyond comprehension and perception, and whose greatness is beyond assertion and denial. I ask You by the surplus of Your grace and the abundance of the gifts of Your blessings, that You grant me the happiness of every happy one in the House of Joy and keep away from me the misery of every wretched one in the House of Deception, and specify me with the witnessing of the martyrs and every martyr at the throne of Your lights on the Day of the Promise. Indeed, You are Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, the Near of every far one, and You are nearer to him than the jugular vein.

Chapter 22: The Second Method of Names and Forms and Their Properties and Benefits

Know that among the Beautiful Names, al-Ghaffār (the Much-Forgiving), al-Ghafūr (the Forgiving), al-Shakūr (the Grateful), al-Qādir (the Powerful), al-Tawwāb (the Accepting of Repentance), al-Ḥamīd (the Praiseworthy), al-Samīʿ (the Hearing), al-Baṣīr (the Seeing), al-Wadūd (the Loving), and al-Shākir (the Thankful) are all in one sequence. In this method, beyond them there are secrets for doing good, seeking forgiveness, manifesting virtue, reforming vice, concealing faults, easing bitterness, and softening the heart. Whoever is afflicted with sins, Allah the Most High will transform his sins into good deeds and forgive him all his sins. Every person who makes the litany of these names will have an effect upon his heart, and he will derive a lesson from everything. Among them, the names al-Ghaffār, al-Shakūr, al-Qādir are the best litany for the forgiveness of sins; the state of the one who remembers them improves. The names al-Tawwāb, al-Ḥamīd are very close to the cause of action; by their remembrance, every task becomes easy. By the remembrance of al-Samīʿ and al-Baṣīr, understanding and intellect increase, and many lights and honors are obtained. Mysterious secrets become comprehensible, and revelations about the realities of things occur. The power of hearing and sight increases in the one who remembers them.

Recovery from brain trauma and afflictions:
When the Persians attacked Khurasan, an affliction befell Shaykh Muḥammad Ghassānī in his garden. He came to me and said: I had instructed him with the remembrance of these two names, and he continued reciting them. Within a few days, he derived great benefit from them. From that time, he remained with me until his death.

Love in people's hearts:
Allah the Most High puts love for the one who remembers the names al-Wadūd and al-Shākir into the hearts of people. The one who remembers achieves success in whatever task he intends.
