**The reciter of this Name will obtain special nearness to Allah and awe:**
From the properties of this Name, every person should recite it according to its numerical value and fulfill the conditions of spiritual discipline, and he will obtain awe and nearness to Allah. This Name should be recited according to its numerical value with seclusion and spiritual discipline, and it should be recited as follows:
**SUBBUHUN QUDDUSUN RABBUL-MALA'IKATI WAR-RUHI**
And one should observe silence during the recitation of the Names, for the Messenger, peace be upon him, said: "Guard your lips from vain and superfluous speech, for it is with them that you recite the Holy Quran." In this, the Messenger indicated the keeping of the lips pure. When this Name is written on white paper with musk and saffron, and a person keeps it with him and multiplies its recitation at the time of recitation, he will be pure and accepted. And when a sinful person engraves it on a silver ring and wears it and recites the Name, Allah the Most High will purify his heart.
Know that this Name belongs to one letter of the Greatest Name, and its number should be multiplied within the tablet and then recited; the result will be obtained. Its form is as follows:

**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Salam"**
Know that the meaning of Salam is that He is pure in His Essence from being a created thing, and He is pure in His Attribute from the qualities of creation, and this Name belongs to Allah, the Most High, alone. True safety, in reality, comes from Him and leads to Him, as the Messenger, peace be upon him, said:
**ALLAHUMMA ANTA AS-SALAMU WA MINKA AS-SALAMU WA TABARRUKTA BI ISMIKA AS-SALAMU WA TA'ALAYTA YA DHA L-JALALI WAL-IKRAMI Ta**
The chest of the reciter of this Name opens for Islam.
Know that safety proceeds from the Name Salam of Allah the Most High, and Islam is divine guidance that extends specifically to the Messengers. As for the right of Islam...
