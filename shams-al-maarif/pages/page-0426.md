# Page 426 (English translation)
424

By the Eternal, the Heavens of the heavens, and by His firm secret, the foundations of the directions. Make me, by Your abundant grace, among those who orbit around Your command with Your might and Your strength, my God. You are the Everlasting, the Eternal, far removed from anyone approaching You so that they would be perceived by the senses, or departing so that they would disappear from the senses. Grant me the life of Your essence and illuminate the sanctification of Your attributes with the noble, universal, divine sciences related to the Primary Knowables and thereafter with Your light in the innermost depths of the innerness of the specific, non-impossible entities. O Allah, You are invoked by every tongue and You are the Responder at every time. I ask You to glorify me and purify me by Your support and the strength of Your attraction from oppositions and following desires, and turn me by the right hand of Your glorification away from desire for the world. Make me among those consumed by longing for none but Your most noble presence, and extract me by Your universal grace and Your radiant light from the Book of Your innerness as a complete verse of perfection, through which I am complete in essence and attributes. Engrave in the created beings a spiritual vision and reveal from the face of my spirit the secret of a covering from which a veil is never removed from my sight, when You manifest upon me after the lifting of the concealment of the letters and the evidences of the Known. Suffice every described one with Your generosity and beneficence, O He Who splits the grain and the seed, and O Originator of atoms in the highest heavens. You are the Manifest, the Subtle, the Omnipotent, O Holy One.

O Holy One, You are absolutely sacred; by Your grace You are truly manifest. You are the discoverer of the subtleties of knowledge that are upon the realms of spirits. With You, all the hidden and the apparent have become purified. Such light have You taken—Your light in the essence, hiddenness, and the spirits of others are the secrets that are manifest, which are from the movement of hearts. I ask You, with the greatness of proofs and glory, O Divine, standing upon Your refuge, by the mystery that You have placed within the curtains of Your sanctity and by the light that You have placed within Your related worlds, and with it You have inscribed the traces of Your majesty and what is beneath the sheet of Your greatness, and with it what You have hidden within the garment of Your grandeur, and along with it what You have made known for the station of Your saints and prophets.

O You whose essence, with His ancient knowledge, created the heights of the heavens, and whose firm secret established the foundation of the directions: encompass me with Your universal grace, those who orbit around Your command with my strength and power. O Allah, You are Everlasting and Eternal, far removed from anyone being near to You or being perceived by them, or being distant from them and hidden from them. O Allah, grant me the life of Your essence and the illumination of the purification of Your attributes, and all the noble, universal sciences which are with the divine attributes related to the Primary Knowables and thereafter with Your light within the hiddenness of the depths of the specifics that are not impossible. O Allah, You are called by every tongue and at every time You are the Acceptor. I ask You that You overwhelm me with Your support and the intensity of power, and protect me from following desires, and turn me away from desire for the world with Your grandeur, and You will draw me towards Yourself from everything other than You, extract me with Your universal grace and Your radiant light from the Book of Your intimate ones, as a complete verse through which I am complete in essence and attributes, and lift the covering from the face of my spirit until the veil is removed from my sight, for when the veil is lifted, the mysteries of the letters and the evidences of the Known become visible. O Generous One, suffice me, the described one, with Your generosity and beneficence. O He Who splits the grain and creates the lofty heavens, You are the Hidden and the Manifest, the Omnipotent and the Holy.
