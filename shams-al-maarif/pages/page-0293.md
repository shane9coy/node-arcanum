(Chapter 53: Properties of the name Wakeel [Agent/Attorney] "Expansion of sustenance and ease and success in all affairs")

Whoever repeats this name frequently, Allah the Most High will be sufficient for him, and He will make him independent of the means of sustenance, and He will grant him sustenance in such a manner that he will not even imagine it. If he is a truthful agent of mercy, he will exercise influence in the world. This name is suitable for one whose name is Muhammad. Its numerical value is 66, and it is an even-odd rectangle. This name is among those that are specifically associated with the Noble Prophet. For this reason, Allah the Most High has also placed his name in the Holy Quran as Mutawakkil [the one who relies on Allah]. This name corresponds to the name Jami' [Gatherer] by virtue of its collectivity and its being specifically associated with the presence of the Noble Prophet. The numerical value of Allah is 66, and the numerical value of Wakeel is the same. The sum of both is 132, which equals [unclear] the name Muhammad. Its components, 78, point toward the name of Allah the Most High, the Wise [Hakim], because an agent who is not wise cannot act appropriately according to the situation. On this subject, someone has composed poetry:

إِذَا كُنْتَ فِی حَاجَتِکَ مُرْسَلًا
فَاَوْكِلْ حَکِیْمًا وَلَا تُوْصِہٖ

"When you are someone sending [an emissary] for your need, send a wise one and do not give him any instruction."

Its distinguished form is a triangle, and this is its figure:

[A 3x3 magic square combining words and numbers, each row sums to 66 in Abjad value:
Wahid (One) (19) | 26 | Tayyib (Good) (21)
Badhuh (20) | Habib (Beloved) (22) | 24
25 | Hay (Living) (18) | 23]

The components of both these names point toward their origin. This is the name Ahab [Loved ones], which is the specific name of our Noble Prophet. But when one of them is increased and combined with the other, they become 94. This is the blessed name of the Noble Prophet, just as their sum is the name Ahab. The names of its letters, 108, point toward the name of Allah the Most High, the Sustainer [Qayyum], because in agency, inner steadfastness is necessary.

(Chapter 54: Properties of the name Qawi [Strong] "Obtaining outward and inward strength")

Whoever repeats this name frequently will have greater strength to bear outward and inward burdens, and his spirit will be strong. This name is suitable for those who engage in remembrance. It is suitable for one who desires assistance in bearing burdens, and it is also suitable for one whose name is Musa [Moses] or one whose name ends with [unclear]. If he persists in the remembrance of this name, he will never be afflicted with misfortune on a journey. Its numerical value is 116. Its remembrance points toward Qayyum. Its numbers point toward [damaged]. Whoever establishes a connection with this name will not be rendered helpless by anything; rather, with the help of the Hidden of Hidden ones, he will accomplish many such matters that [unclear].
