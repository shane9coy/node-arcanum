# Page 195 (English translation)
195

Upon my right and upon my left, and grant me encompassment, and they cannot comprehend anything of His knowledge except by what He wills. O You whose knowledge encompasses all things and who has enumerated all things in number—I ask You for encompassment of what is between the fingers and the exiting from the two loins, covered by the amulets of protection, drawn by the subtleties of divine care that raises by the refined acts of nurturing care that gathers the lights of guidance to all sources and abundant in benefits and the attaining of desires, immersed in the seas of Your mercy, attributed to the purity of Your presence, turning to the fulfillment of Your knowledge, crowned with the crowns of honor, adorned with the morals of safety. I ask You, O He whose Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, O He whose power and will encompass all things; expand for me my sustenance and relieve my distress, and forgive my sin by Your generosity and Your nobility, and enter me into the mystery of the extension of Your greatest and most magnificent name, and they cannot comprehend them, and He is the Most High, the Great.

O Allah, I ask You, O Allah, O Living, O Eternal, by the right of this noble verse and these sublime names, that You give me victory over whoever wronged me, and subdue whoever subjugated me, and whoever intended for me evil and treachery and betrayal. How swiftly descends Your severe retribution, and how swiftly descends Your glorious subjugation, over every stubborn tyrant and every rebellious Satan who transgressed against the servants and tyrannized in the lands and strived for corruption. O Lord, I seek refuge in You, O my deity, I ask You by the right of this noble verse and these sublime names, that You look upon me with a gaze of mercy, and make me among Your righteous servants upon whom there is no fear and who do not grieve. Our Lord, accept from us, indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing. Our Lord, pour upon us patience and stabilize our feet, and grant us victory over the disbelieving people. Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself, indeed You are the Bestower, and there is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Great.

O He whose Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth, turn away from me what distresses me of oppression and jealousy, and heal my heart with Your support, O Healer of broken hearts, and mix joy with rejoicing in my part and whole, O Strong One; distinguish between the weak, and raise above my head a banner by which the world testifies that I am wronged. Grant me, O Allah, the reward of the wronged, indeed You know what is unknown, O Self-Sufficient, repel from me what prevents me from poverty, O Allah, O Exalted, O Great, You are exalted in great glory, and magnify me by Your immense greatness, and deliver me from the oppressive people, and extend to me Your proximate angels, and subject for me the hearts of all Your creation by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy, and they cannot comprehend them, and He is the Most High, the Great—O Allah, I ask You, O Allah, O Living, O Eternal, that You deliver me and whoever seeks refuge with me from the oppressive people, and enter me into the treasuries, In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful. Their locking is "Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds." Their key is "There is no deity except Allah; Muhammad is the messenger of Allah."
