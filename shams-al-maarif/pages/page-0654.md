**Whether a child is wretched (shaqī) or blessed (saʿīd) and of happy fortune:**

It is written that whether the child is wretched or blessed, the angels of divine oneness meet with him. And if he is among the people of the right hand, the angels of capability also come to him, and Allah the Most High places wisdom and the trust within him.

**At the birth of the blessed one, light fills from the earth to the heaven:**

Likewise, at the time of the birth of the child, light upon light fills from the earth to the heaven, and the angels raise their voices with recitation of divine oneness and magnification. This circle is specific to the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous.

**At the birth of the wretched one, the earth and heaven become dark:**

When Allah the Most High withdraws from a child his natural light and the lights of wisdom, at his birth the heavens and earth become filled with darkness, and the devils raise a great clamor, and the fire flares up for the sake of divine disobedience—not for the sake of pleasure, but due to the default of compelling wisdom and for the sake of all the righteous.

**The four centers of the lower world (sufliyyāt):**

The centers of the lower world are also four: fire, air, water, and earth. So the center of heat is the sphere of the Sun, and the center of cold is the sphere of the Moon, and the center of moisture is the sphere of Jupiter, and the center of dryness is the sphere of Saturn.

And the components of natures, from among the spheres, each one is drawn toward its respective sphere and intermingles with the newly formed ones, and they are the elements of nature which are called the centers of the lower world.

**Notice:** Know that the realities of letters are names, and names are essences. So you are the bearer of the trust, that is, of the names, and their conditions are that you fulfill them with righteous deeds, that is, with prayer and fasting. And their key is ablution, and their establishment is that for each limb there is a gate. So one should establish them to such a degree that the gates of Hell are closed and the gates of Paradise are opened.

**The reason the gates of Paradise open:**

For this reason, His presence (peace be upon him) said: Whoever performs ablution properly, then says:

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

For him all the gates of Paradise are opened; let him enter through whichever one he wishes.

**The explanation of the sciences: they are forty-one sciences:**

The key to all the gates of Paradise and this connection with the divine realities. So within your inner lights are: the World of the Secrets and the World of the Unseen and the World of the Dominion and the World of the Dominion of Dominion and the World of Disclosure and the World of Splitting and the World of Joining and the World of the Branches and the World of Creation and the World of the Secret and the World of Solitude and the World of the Manifest and the World of Divinity and the World of Alchemy and the World of the Exalted and the World of Meanings and the World of Light and the World of Sanctity and the World of Power and the World of the Intellect and the World of the Soul and the World of the Heart and the World of the Throne and the World of the Chair and the World of Beneath-the-Earth and the World of Saturn and the World of Jupiter and the World of Mars and the World of the Sun and the World of Mercury and the World of the Moon and the World of Fire and the World of Air and the World of Water and the World of Earth and the World of Forgetting and the World of the Forgetting-of-Forgetting and the World of the Preservation of All—these are forty-one sciences, and they are greater than all three worlds.
