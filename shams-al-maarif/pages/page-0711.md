# Page 711 (English translation)
**Disagreement Among Scholars Regarding the Greatest Name:**

There are three opinions among scholars regarding the Greatest Name: first, that whichever name is answered when one supplicates during distress is the Greatest Name. Second, people have differed greatly regarding the Greatest Name—some say the Greatest Name is "Allah" and this is the most correct opinion; some say it is "Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām" (Possessor of Glory and Generosity); some say it is "Yā Latīf" (O Subtle); some say it is "Salāmun qawlan min Rabbin Raḥīm" (Peace, a word from a Merciful Lord); some say it is "Hanan Manān Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām" (Merciful, Compassionate, Possessor of Glory and Generosity); some say it is the beginning of Surah Hadid; some consider it to be the end of Surah Hashr; some consider it to be "Yā Wudd" (O Loving); and they say this verse is in Surah Hajj:

wa alladhīna hājarū fī sabīl Allāhi thumma qutilū aw mātū la-yarzuqannahumu Allāhu rizqan hasanā

And some say the luminous letters are these three connected to the Greatest Name; some consider it to be the Name of Perfection; some say it is the repetition of "Ism Allah" (the Name of Allah); some mention the name "Al-'Alīm" (the Knowing); and some say "Al-'Aliyy al-'Azīm" (the Most High, the Great); and some call it the testimony of "Lā ilāha illā Allāh" (There is no deity but Allah). All of these are narrations transmitted with authentic chains of hadith, and in one hadith it is stated that the Messenger of Allah (peace be upon him) said: "Supplicate with Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām"—this is definitive proof.

**The Greatest Name in Syriac and Hebrew:**

In Syriac this name is also "Bahniyār Hayimāh Hijr Hawshā" and in Hebrew it is "Hawayā Sharobiyā Hāwāh Rabbānī Ambānāt Al Shdā'ī" and it occurs in three places in the Noble Quran: in Surah Al-Baqarah, Surah Āl 'Imrān, and Surah Tā-Hā; and according to some, the Greatest Name is "Huwa" (He) and according to others it is "Yā Huwa".

**The Greatest Name is the Pole of Names:**

The third matter is that the Greatest Name is the pole of all names—that is, all names receive aid from it, just as the entire world receives aid from the qutb (pole) or ghawth; and for this reason supplication is answered. All spirits and assigned angels obey it.

The practitioner must eat lawful (halāl) food and undertake spiritual discipline, because this is the name with which the life of the world is connected and from which it takes aid. When the practitioner completes the ritual, the assigned angels will descend upon him and a lofty rank will be attained—that is, he will reach the station of the elite (awrād). The name of his assigned angel is Natshā Ta'īl, and he rules over four officers, each of whom has under him 70 rows of 60 angels.

This is the station of those who become established among the People of the Divine Shadow.

**This Name Has Special Effect for Love:**

The special properties of these two names are very numerous. For love, one should write them within Mars or in the sextile during the dignity of the Sun, keep them with oneself, and great acceptance will be obtained. If written on a gold tablet, the same effect applies. When connected with the name of the desired person and written during a fortunate ascendant, and kept with oneself, love and great acceptance will be obtained. If written on a flag and displayed, victory will be attained. If the spiritual traveler persists in this name, he can influence whatever he wishes. The dhikr (remembrance) of these two names is as follows:
