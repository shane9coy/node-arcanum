# Page 232 (English translation)
In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

**Prayer of Affection and Love**

In the name of Allah, who is most beneficent and most merciful. O Allah, I ask of You in the manner of the records of the imprints of Your lights, the sums of Your glory's supremacy, the severity of Your power's intensities, the authority of Your might, the bond of Your wisdom, the praise of Your wisdom with eternal covenant, the loftiness of Your elevation, the perpetuity of Your eternity with the decree of the Eternal, the contentment of Your kingdom's favor, with Your sultan's sublime, elevated, and lofty, with the goodness of Your mercy, with the sifalat, with the lights of Your radiant veils, with Your glory and uniqueness, with the sixty seals of Your encompassing, with the fields of Your throne's Founder, the purity of Your spirituality, with the elevation of Your throne, with Your spiritual administrators of properties, with the celestial bodies of the spheres, the satisfaction of those who desire Your sovereignty and satisfaction, with the movements of those who fear Your heights, and with the best of the family in Your pleasure, and with Your obedience, the glorification and magnification of the worshippers, I ask: O the First, O the Last, O the Manifest, O the Hidden, O the Ancient, O the Great.

With the talisman of Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim and the secrets of the stars and the poor, make black the hearts of our enemies and Your enemies, and with the swords that cut the drainage of Your sovereignty's conquest, constrain the children of darkness. With Your power and might, cast upon us the moments of shining eyes with Your lofty curtains, and day and night cast upon us Your refuge through Your success, plunge us in Your mercy and goodness with the bonds of safety, confine us so that we do not stand in Your disobedience. O the Exalted, O the Last, O the Manifest, O the Hidden, O the Ancient, O the Great, O the Knowing.

O Allah, difficulties have become bewildered, works have become weak, sorrows have become distressed, exhaustion has transformed, thoughts have become changeable, hearts have become incapable of perceiving states, which appeared from the sword of Your might's wonderful banners. For the radiance of Your obedience, there is no inclination without You, O Allah, O the One who gives movement to movement, O the Origin of gifts, O the One who splits the pieces of hard mountains and makes water flow from them, the Helper of all creation, the One who revives all animals, various races of people, and the world, the Helper of all creation, all animals, various races of people, and the world, the One who revives them, along with His secret of the recitation of hidden signs that make the tongues of infants speak, and they are the ones who declared division, glorification, and sanctification, and declared the tahlil and takbir with the glory of the throne of the all, with the majesty of the angels of the lofty heavens. O Allah, in this blessed hour, make for us that whoever asked You for supplication, accept it; whoever asked You, give him; whoever supplicated to You with humility, have mercy upon him; forgive his sins with Your grace and ancient favor.

Then recite this prayer seven times.

**Arabic Text:**

*O Allah, deal with us as You are worthy of it, and do not deal with us as we are worthy of it. Indeed, You are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness. Glorious are You—we cannot count praises upon You. You are as You have praised Yourself. Majestic is Your face, glorious is Your honor, and magnificent is Your praise. Allah does what He wills by His power and decrees what He intends by His glory. O the Ever-Living, O the Self-Subsisting, O the Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor. There is no might nor power except through Allah, the Exalted, the Great. And may Allah, the Most High, send blessings upon our master Muhammad, upon his family, and grant him peace.*

**Urdu Translation/Closing:**

O Allah, deal with us in such a manner as You are worthy of, and do not deal with us in such a manner as we are not worthy of. Indeed, You are worthy of forgiveness and piety. Your sacred essence is pure—we cannot praise You in the way You have praised Yourself. Your essence possesses greatness, Your rank is magnificent, Your praise is very lofty. Allah does whatever He wills by His power, and by His glory He decrees whatever He intends. O the Ever-Living, O the Self-Subsisting, O the Creator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor. Power and strength belong to Allah, who is Exalted and Great. May Allah the Most High send blessings upon our leader Muhammad ﷺ, upon his family, and grant him peace.
