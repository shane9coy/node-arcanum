# Page 445 (English translation)
O Clement One, You are the one who pardons whoever turns to You their lapses and their errors, and forgives whoever approaches You with a heart [sincere] in their circumstances. You grant reprieve from punishment to whoever associates others with You in Your dominion until the Day of Judgment. You accept from whoever repents to You with their entirety and good deeds. You bring back whoever deviates from the path of righteousness through Your grace to the paths of guidance. You elevate whoever holds fast to Your noble rope in the beginning and the end. You open for whoever knocks at Your door and save him from error and transgression. I ask You by the light that reaches the hearts of the noble ones—who if they aligned their souls with justice and fairness—to grant me knowledge mixed with wisdom, and to admit me through Your mercy into the entrances of peace, and to distinguish me with knowledge, O Knower of what is in the hearts of all worlds. O Clement One, [be patient] with whoever has incurred the punishment of the furthest extent of punishment until the Day of Judgment.

Obtaining great power and the practice of making kings and rulers obedient:

The name al-'Azīm contains twelve letters from among the letters of the Greatest Name. The deputy of this name is called Ḥurfāyīl. Under him are four officers, each of whom rules over 120 rows, and each row contains 120 deputies. The deputy comes to the one who practices dhikr and grants him great power. His value and status become elevated throughout the entire world. Kings and rebellious people become humbled and obedient before him. The supplication of the name al-'Azīm is as follows:

O Great One, You are the one who magnified Yourself by the greatness of Your authority, and You are the Most Exalted by the perfection of Your splendor. You are above all things by knowledge, power, and beauty. You are the one who bestows every blessing with greatness, light, and majesty. Eternal subsistence belongs to You, eternal perfection, and everlasting permanence. Magnify Your status visibly in hearts and souls, and illuminate Your blessing as prevailing in spirits and bodies. Your essence is manifest upon every creature, and the light of Your face is upon every recipient of sustenance. O Allah, I ask You by the greatness of Your status in existence, the majesty of Your blessings in the visible world, and the vastness of Your mercy that turns toward every witness and witnessed. Grant me a good life after which there is no death, and grant me the vision of the majesty of Your face in the horizons, with which there is no difference, and [with it] gather every benefit and reject harm. O Allah, I ask You by the greatness of Your rewards that You make me great in status before You and before whoever You love from Your allies, and before whoever has no power that is permanent away from You, and [the following are] the attributes: O Lord of all worlds.

The treatment of anger and the fulfilling of needs:

The name al-Ghafūr contains twenty-two letters of the Greatest Name. In overcoming the anger and wrath of rulers and authorities, this name has many special properties and effects. The name of its deputy is Ṣiyāyīl. He rules over four officers, each of whom rules over 1,388 rows, and each row contains 1,388 deputies. The deputy of this name comes to the practitioner and fulfills his need. The supplication of the name al-Ghafūr is as follows:

O Forgiving One, You are the one who overlooks their attributes and their actions until they behold nothing but You. You are the one who illuminated their hearts and their intellects until they look only toward You. You have erased their intellects [damaged] in the carpet of knowledge and adorned it with the garments of wisdom. You caused Your servants to grow through gentleness by the grace of the secret of faith and iḥsān in encompassing all worlds.
