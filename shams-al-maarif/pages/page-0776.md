# Page 776 (English translation)
A Poem on Celestial Love

Glory be to Him by whose love is saved whoever seeks it—let not whoever is displeased thereby be prevented; how can it encompass matters while there are veils and signs?* How can an embracer here, apart from the cause of his Lord, stand at the door, absent yet worthy of a gift, when a time has come for him to erect a miracle? And through him is attained the gaining of radiance, but you died as a martyr, and blame was turned back upon you by his love, so you became one joining the unfortunate. Woe to the isolated ones—for what was missed by my missed one bit, perhaps he rests, and from those near me after distance there was confusion. And whoever entrusted to me the weaving of life, my household rebuked and wondered; a close one grew old with me out of jealousy. What softness! Was permission granted and it gleamed, seeking its aspects. Alas! Have you descended from yourself, and they benefited in firm resolutions. And after me I turned away from your course, and it has passed, and it shaped matters from which one will read—No! And your intellect was returned to me. Already your coal has overflowed and overturned the forehead, and trial met it but did not make the deed loathsome, and your love turned aside, nor was there strength. There is truth in the essence, and it overcame others, so do not prevent your eyes by it—a mystery. ۝

Whatever there is no strength for your love—neither for your purpose, nor sorrow from your love—neither by yourself nor your humiliation, neither hope for you nor news expected for a matter between them. So whoever has in his hand the reform of the West, hearts became sound for him. Whoever diminished for me, the bonds of curtains were acquired without sins, a divorce is finalized. So do not find fault, my betrayer, and your lot is due to your nature—from my wishing for them, nor die in anger. You were accustomed, there was at your side a return to hearts, and your love for the beholders—how it irrigated whoever shakes within the fortress, and you were made safe, your companion over whom you had authority—from my wishing for illumination, and daughters accepted from you with kindness toward them. I commanded seeking forgiveness except while you are present, and the lords of the Wests—how the suns of spirits have risen from the fifth of the shades.*

You named it, He accepted it as a preference and made it | and granted it success in existence with verification, all of it.
So something extended in love alongside God | is realized between a sought thing and its cause.
And this moon in verification is a garment | and I say that people, after his grace,
So the sea of God—the people of virtue, certainly | and the people of virtue are most worthy of its distinction,
And none recognize virtue except its possessors.
And God knows best.
