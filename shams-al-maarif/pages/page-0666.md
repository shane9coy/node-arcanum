And they comprehend the reality of the spiritual sustenance because this sustenance has no need of an intermediary in its seeking.

Seeking sustenance from God—whether outward or inward—He alone is the Provider:

Allah the Exalted says:

فَابْتَغُوْا عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ الرِّزْقَ

"That is, seek sustenance at the very place of God."

Whoever's station is established in the domain of the Names and Acts, their sustenance will be from the world of composition; and whoever's station is with the Names of Attributes, their sustenance will be from the malakuti realm; and whoever's foot is established in His station with the Names of the Attributes of the Divine Essence, their sustenance—that is, their daily provision—will be directly from God. Thus, to this same [principle] Lord Abraham, peace be upon him, referred when he declared:

اَلَّذِیْ خَلَقَنِیْ فَهُوَ يَهْدِيْنِ ۝ وَالَّذِیْ هُوَ يُطْعِمُنِيْ وَيَسْقِيْنِ ۝

Translation: "He who created me, He guides me; and He who feeds me and gives me drink."

And his intention in this statement is nothing other than removing intermediaries.

The amount of sustenance was determined two thousand years before the creation of earth and heaven

Allah the Exalted created the sustenance of creation and its amount two thousand years before He brought forth the heavens and earth. Then He commanded one of His winds to scatter this sustenance throughout the world. Thus, to some He gave abundantly so that they are awash in plenty, while to others He gave only very little.

A question and answer with Wahb ibn Malik

A man said to Lord Wahb ibn Malik, "From where do you eat?" He gestured toward his mouth. The man said, "Everyone knows that." So he said, "He who created this mill is the same one who sends the flour for it."

وَلِلّٰهِ خَزَائِنُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ

Translation: "For Allah are the treasuries of the heavens and the earth."

And whoever desires to become a practitioner of this Divine Name, it is binding upon them to occupy themselves with tawhid and turning toward God, and to know that Allah the Exalted has apportioned their sustenance, and to begin its litany with constancy. In their seclusion and public hours, it is appropriate according to the prescribed number; and after the recitation, they should say these words: اَللّٰهُمَّ ارْزُقْنِیْ يَا رَزَّاقُ And this person should constantly practice contemplative meditation in both public and private. Its special properties are very numerous, and its assigned angel is Jibreel (Gabriel), under whom are many officers.

Whoever combines the Name Fattah (the Opener) with this [Name] and recites [them], Allah the Exalted will ease sustenance for them, and every locked door will open for them. If it is engraved on a silver tablet and kept with them, whatever the number [of letters] corresponds to the Name. And if placed in a house or shop, there will be blessing and benefit in it, and blessing will not depart from their hand. When this Name matches the name of any person and they make it an assigned practice, there will be expansion in their sustenance, and only good from the unseen realm will befit them, and all difficulties will be resolved. This wealth, due to remaining in their hand, accompanies the obtaining of every thing.
