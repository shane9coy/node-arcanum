Chapter: The Spiritual Influence upon the Human Body

For such purposes, there are regulations, and meanings become established within them; it consists of four parts. From here, too, the luminous form and the portion of silver and from among the trees, Hijra, and from the wilderness, the Great Word becomes manifest—provided it is practiced with purity and piety. The seven stars are separate except for those who mention the sanctity of God the Most High with them. The one who exercises influence—that is, the one who performs the operation—locks the glorification of each star upon its own mine, for which reason God the Most High subordinates all the effects of that star to him. If you wish to lock these effects, take whichever name is approved for you, and after the abjad and taksir, sum its numbers to the zāhir. Continue until it appears at the end of the first line; then take the letters from it and extract them. Its secret will become known to you. Its restriction is written according to the method of the diagram; learn from it.

And the characteristic of taksir is that, just as the signs of abjad and the repetitions are removed, the lines remain, and within them the properties of the letters are gathered and enter into one another, and the products of the numbers remain each in its own place, which God the Most High has placed within it—and each one has its own particular effect. The glorification of the Mighty One is indicative of the life of all things, and this is what the scholars have mostly agreed upon regarding the properties and benefits of spending and religion, and it is the extraction of reality and rafīqa. The characteristic of rafīqa combined with ismīya, for example: when it is combined in 35, the letters of the Name of Life will be within 35 words—though in writing there are only six, because there are two letters of the knot, and both of these names contain a shadda. So when you multiply seven by five, you will have 35.

This is a subtle point from among the letter-sciences, and its efficacy is very strong; whatever is desired is fulfilled through it. Thus, from the pronoun, 42 letters were obtained, because we said: alif-lām-mīm-yā—and all of that is the descent of the Divine Name. Alif-lām-lām-alif-mīm-ḥā-yā... removing all repetitions leaves 8 letters: alif-fā-mīm-yā-rā-ḥā... When the Hayy of al-Qayyūm is descended, 17 letters emerge: alif-fā-lām-alif-mīm-qāf-alif-yā-yā-alif-waw-alif-mīm-mīm-yā... After removing the repetitions, the letters that remain are those of al-Qayyūm. Then multiply 7 by 6 to get 42, and this is the combined form of both names. Throughout all the lines, and after the interpenetration of the pronoun, 12 letters remain, which are: alif-bā-tā-jīm-ḥā-sīn-shīn-ṣād-ḍād-ṭā-ẓā-ʿayn-ghayn-fā-qāf-kāf-lām...

Then from these very letters are formed those names by which the desired purpose is actually pursued, and those names are these:

Yā ḥayyu, Yā ḥakīmu, Yā ḥalīmu, Yā ḥamīdu, Yā ḥannānu, Yā ḥasību, Yā ḥafīẓu, Yā ḥafiyyu, Yā ḥāfiẓu, Yā khallāqu, Yā khabīru, Yā zakiyyu, Yā zāhedu, Yā salāmu, Yā ḥāfiẓu, Yā shāfiyyu, Yā shakūru, Yā muṣawwiru, Yā ḍā'u, Yā ẓāfiru, Yā ghaffūru, Yā fattāḥu, Yā quddūsu, Yā kāfiyyu, Yā maliku, Yā mujību, Yā nūru, Yā wāṣilu, Yā Allāhu, Yā Dhā al-manni

Now, by God, their operator remains—only then, when you add these names to the supplication of some Divine Name among them, as the people of spiritual practice do, for some purpose that accords with the indication of the name, its effect will manifest immediately, God the Most High willing.
