# Page 736 (English translation)
**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "al-Muqsit" (The Just)**

Know that al-Muqsit is the one who causes the rights of the oppressed to be obtained from the oppressor through justice, whether in this world or in the Hereafter. And Allah, the Exalted, is the one who establishes justice and equity, and in truth the attribute of justice is found in Him alone—not only is He the Sustainer, but He is the Praiseworthy.

It is narrated that once the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was [unclear] sitting. Then he laughed until his blessed teeth became visible. It was asked: "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, why do you laugh?" He ﷺ said: "Two people from my ummah will stand before Allah the Exalted—one of them will say: 'O Lord, he has taken my right, so give me my retaliation from him.' The Lord will say to the other: 'Pay your brother's right.' He will say: 'O Lord, I have nothing left to give as retaliation.' The oppressed one will say: 'O my Lord, then place his sins upon me.'"

**He who pays the price shall receive a place in Paradise; the price is to forgive the oppressor:**

Then the tears of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ flowed, and he said: "Indeed that day will be very heavy. Indeed on that day there will be a need for a person to bear his burden." He said: "Then Allah the Exalted will say to the oppressed one: 'Raise your head from here and look toward Paradise.' And He will say: 'For the one who paid its price.' He will ask: 'Who owns its price?' He will say: 'It belongs to you.' He will say: 'What do I have?' He will say: 'Forgive your brother's offense and go to Paradise.'"

Then the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Fear Allah so that reconciliation may occur among you in goodness, for Allah the Exalted will establish justice among the believers on the Day of Resurrection."

**What is the definition of justice:**

Someone asked the Messenger ﷺ about justice. He said: "It is another name of a Lord upon which who is capable?" It has a special property for removing anger, especially when combined with the name al-Ghafur (the Forgiving). When fighting, say thus:

**"Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismik al-'afuww al-muqsiṭ illā mā aṭfa'ta 'annī ghaḍabā ṭ"**

His anger will be dispelled. And if a person writes its impression and keeps it with him, all the people who are angry or displeased with him will become pleased. Its form is this:
