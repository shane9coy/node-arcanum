**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Mumin" (Believer)**

Know that in etymology, "mumin" means one who affirms Islam, and in technical terminology, it refers to that essence to whom all works are attributed. The locus of Islam is the chest, that is, the breast, and the locus of faith is the heart, that is, the world of the Throne.

**The heart is the throne of God, the center of His manifestation and grace:**

Because the heart is the place of divine manifestation and heavenly love. God the Most High says:
"Those are they in whose hearts He has inscribed faith" (Quran 58:22).

And this is the place of the spirit. The original matter is that no change has occurred in the realm of divine government; rather, it is the locus of faith, and faith is affirmation by the tongue, belief in the heart, and action by the limbs.

**There is great disagreement among scholars regarding the details of faith—what things must one have faith in:**

There is much disagreement among people regarding faith, and its reality is this: that you believe in God, and in His angels, and in His books, and in His messengers, and that you also believe in the good and evil of fate, and in benefit and harm. And know with certainty that whatever things the noble Muhammad (peace be upon him) brought faith in are true, and the Balance is true, and the Pool (Hawḍ) is true, and intercession is true, and punishment is true, and the Hour is certainly coming—there is no doubt about it—and the Lord Most High will raise the dead from their graves.

**The effects of faith on asrār, intellects, spirit, soul, heart, and bodies:**

The faith of the asrār (secrets/mysteries) is with knowledge, and the faith of the intellects is with science, and the faith of the spirit is with unveiling (kashf), and the faith of the souls is with verification (taḥqīq), and the faith of the hearts is with sincerity (ikhlāṣ), and the faith of the bodies is connected with actions. This light of faith is born from the mercy of faith, and from it love is born. When the light of faith manifests upon the souls, expansion is produced from it; and when the light of faith manifests upon the bodies, standing with the reality of service is produced from it.

**The stations of faith:**

It is fitting for the dhākir (one who engages in remembrance) that when he mentions this name, he abandon the lights of the adhkār (remembrances), and remove the heart from causes, and attain elevation to the station of the intellects, and turn away from all else (mā siwā), and return to the pure singular essence. The first station of faith is intuition (firāsa), because it is a matter that is produced by the entry of the light of faith into the heart. The second station of faith is the vision and witnessing of faith—this is attained by those who traverse the spiritual path in the highest degree.

**What is the original reality of faith:**

Faith is that intuition is a single drop that crowds upon the heart and cuts off doubt from it. Unveiling (mukāshafa) is a light that dissolves in darknesses and produces sight upon the eyes. Faith submerges in the lights, and it is the preservation of divine will and divine courtesy in the world, and it is the reference point of ethics. Such faith—that is, the light of the heart's core and the witnessing of the solitude of the friends of God—is the perfection of whoever possesses the quality of calmness (tamakkun), and this is the reality of faith.
