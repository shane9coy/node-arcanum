# Page 406 (English translation)
406

This prayer is written for sinking ships, but one must fear God Most High and not act unjustly without proper cause. Write the fifth section separately on one plank of the ship and recite "Khawf al-nizami al-tibisi" five times, and the ship will sink very quickly. Attach this incantation with hot coal and seal it into the ship's hull. However, the ship will move with such force that it will either sink or break apart.

[Boxed Heading: Procedure for the Protection of Ships and Vessels]

Write the coming disaster on a red brick and place it in the vessel—no disaster or misfortune will befall it, and it will reach the shore safely and intact. The verse is: "Illa rahmatan minna wa mata'an ila hin" and "Wa idha qila lahumut-taqaw ma bayna aydikum wa ma khalfakum la'allakum turhamun" – this grants abundance of sustenance for the span of a month. Read it 35 times and make a supplication; it will be accepted. Recite in the sea: "Subhan al-mufariji 'an kulli mahzun, subhan al-munaffisi 'an kulli mdayyuni, subhan al-mutahhiri li kulli maghsuri, subhan al-'alimi bi kulli maknun, subhan man ja'ala khazainahu bayna al-kaf wa al-nun, subhan idha arada shay'an an yaqula lahu kun fa yakun" up to the end. "Subhan rabbika rabb al-'izzati" three times from beginning to end; "Bismillahi al-rahmani al-rahim, al-hamdu lillahi rabbi al-'alamin" up to "Nasta'in" – read this, then make a supplication and it will be accepted immediately. Say this seven times: "Ya hadiya al-mudillina" – three times "La hadiya ghayruka" – "Ihdina al-sirata al-mustaqim, sirata alladhina an'amta 'alayhim" – "Allahumma aj'alni mina alladhina an'amta 'alayhim wa mallaktahum asrara asma'ika ya rabbu ya rahmanu ya rahim, ghayra al-maghdubi 'alayhim wa la al-dallina" – "Amin ya mubin" seven times.

"Show me the straight path—the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor. O Allah, make me among those upon whom is Your favor, and whom You have made owners of the mysteries of Your Names. O Lord, O Merciful, O Compassionate—not the path of those upon whom is Your wrath, nor of the astray. Amen, O Manifest."

"Allahumma subhana al-maliki wa al-malakuti la ilaha illa anta ya dha al-jalal wa al-ikram, ya hayyu ya qayyumu, billahi ista'tantu, ya mughithu aghithni" – read four times. Make your request and supplicate, and it will be accepted at that very moment.

[Boxed Heading: Procedure for Exiling Enemies]

"Allahumma inni as'aluka bi-ismika al-'azimi al-a'zam wa nabiyyika al-mukarrami sayyidina muhammadin salla allahu 'alayhi wa sallama an taf'ala biya ma anta ahlahu wa la taf'ala biya ma ana ahlahu innaka ahl al-taqwa wa ahl al-maghfira" [unclear] [Arabic and Urdu text partially obscured by "FREE AMLIYAAT" watermark] This procedure is for exiling enemies. When the name of him and his mother is taken and this is read one thousand times, then God willing, the enemy will be exiled.
