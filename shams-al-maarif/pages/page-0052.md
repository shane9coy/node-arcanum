**Friday:**

The first hour is that of Venus. In it are performed the attractions of love, betrothal, and marriage rites. The second hour is that of Mercury. In it talismans and all kinds of operations are performed. The third hour is that of the Moon. In it nothing should ever be done because it is a very evil hour. The fourth hour is that of Saturn. It is better for this hour for digging wells, constructing springs, and similar works. The fifth hour is that of Jupiter. In it write amulets for subjugating women and great men. The sixth hour is that of the Sun. In it are written amulets for confronting rulers and for satisfying needs. The seventh hour is that of Venus. In it are performed the attractions of love, marriage proposals, and marriage rites. The eighth hour is that of Mercury. Whatever work you do in it will be brought to good completion. The ninth hour is that of the Moon. It is most acceptable for separation and relocation. The tenth hour is that of Saturn. The eleventh hour is that of Jupiter. The twelfth hour is that of Mars. Whatever work you wish, do it in it, including travel and the like.

**Saturday:**

The first hour is that of Saturn. Perform whatever operations of love and the like you wish in this hour, for this is the only auspicious hour of Saturn. During these days of the first part of the month, the second hour is that of Jupiter, which is good for reconciliation among people. The third hour is that of Mars, which is for hatred and malice. The fourth hour is that of the Sun. It is for going to kings and meeting and corresponding with them. The fifth hour is that of Venus. The sixth hour is that of Mercury. In it write amulets for contracting marriage. The seventh hour is that of the Moon. In it there is no good. Nothing should be done in it. The eighth hour is that of Saturn. In it perform the operation of causing enmity and shedding blood. The ninth hour is that of Jupiter. In it perform whatever auspicious operations you wish; you will succeed. The tenth hour is that of Mars. In it are operations of evil, corruption, and fighting. The eleventh hour is that of the Sun. In it perform operations for acceptance, love, and reconciliation between husband and wife. The twelfth hour is best for gaining benefit from kings, ministers, and great men. Know that whoever recognizes the appropriate time for performing good and evil operations will obtain whatever he intends and will succeed, for this is the foundation of the knowledge and its gate through which one enters it. I have explained these matters to you that the experts of this knowledge have mentioned, so that practicing it may become easy for you. I have made for you a table by which you will be able to recognize all works of fire, earth, air, and water.
