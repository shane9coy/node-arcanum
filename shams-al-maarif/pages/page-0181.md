# Page 181 (English translation)
[170]

...they cannot earn a livelihood and they do not return to their families. Indeed, You are the Compeller, the Magnificent, the Withholder, the Helper, the Dominant, and the Victorious, the Prevailer, the Just, the Avenger, the Destroyer, the Strong, the Abaser, the Delayer, the Exalter, the Abaser, the Harmful, the Breaker—Lord of Majesty and Bounty, the Protecting Friend, the Mighty, the Trustee, the Glorious, the Encompasser, Possessor of Might, the Strong, Possessor of severe punishment, Possessor of the magnificent Throne, Doer of what He wills. Allah has set a seal upon their hearts and upon their hearing, and over their eyes is a veil. And for them is a great punishment. Allah mocks them and extends them in their transgression, wandering blindly. Deaf, dumb, and blind—they do not return.

Or like a rain from the sky within which is darkness, thunder, and lightning. [Qadir (tughri)] Humiliation has been struck upon them wherever they are found, except by a rope from Allah and a rope from the people. They have earned the anger of Allah, and struck upon them is poverty. And those who disbelieved said to their messengers, "We will surely drive you out of our land, or you will surely return to our religion." Their Lord revealed to them, "We will surely destroy the wrongdoers, then We will surely settle you in the land after them. That is for whoever fears My station and fears My threat." They sought victory, and every stubborn tyrant was frustrated. Indeed, We will support Our messengers and those who believe in the worldly life and on the day the witnesses stand—the day when the wrongdoers will not benefit from their excuse, and for them is cursing and for them is the worst dwelling. We destroyed those mightier than them in strength, and the example of the former peoples has passed. That is because Allah is the protector of those who believe and because the disbelievers have no protector. And when they rejoiced in what they had been given, We seized them suddenly, and behold, they were in despair. The root of the people who wronged has been cut off, and praise is to Allah, Lord of the worlds. Allah has decreed, "I will surely overcome, I and My messengers. Indeed, Allah is Strong and Mighty." It is He who drove out those who disbelieved among the People of the Scripture [to the believers] (tughri). Your Lord is Most Generous (tughri). Have you not considered, O Muhammad, how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant? [End of Surah]

Place them in darkness and distress, so they remain in humiliation and far from cursing—let them not be upright in secret or in public. Let them not obtain the benefit of honor and reward. Let them not see help or My strength. Send upon them punishment, from above them and from beneath their feet. Turn them into various factions, so some fear others. And clothe them with the garment of Hellfire. Burn their hearts with steadfastness; make them drink hot water upon which floats the dust of all their possessions. Send down from the heavens hot wind upon their hair, so they become distressed. Or make their water deep, so they have no strength to seek it. Do not set right their wealth, and make them among those who incur losses, like one bound in fetters. Let them not raise their heads, and make them among the common people, and let them not be among those who find salvation. Give them such illness that they have no strength to eat or drink. Let them not find comfort on earth. Create obstacles before them and behind them. Build a wall on their right and left. Place a stone above their heads and a pit below them, so they derive no pleasure. Let no peace come to their eyes. Do not send down goodness for them. Place a collar on their necks. Bind them in chains. Place shackles on their feet. Shake them with earthquakes. Nay, let there be collars on their necks; let enemies be behind them. Give them fear in their homes, so they find no prosperity. Do not let their speech be [continued] so they find no guidance. Overturn their souls, so they do not see. Corrupt their spirits, so they do not find prosperity. Blacken their faces, so they cannot see. Make their ears deaf, so they cannot hear. Make their eyes blind stars, so they cannot see. Create for them such a harsh and tight siege that they are called out to, yet they cannot hear the voice. Let them find no light, and let them not return to their homes.
