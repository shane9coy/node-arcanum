# Page 769 (English translation)
763

The rule of combination, alteration, interaction, and mixing of natures is this: it is applied to all the Divine Names.

Whoever mentions a Name and the Secondary Names, such as Al-Hayy (The Living), Al-Hakim (The Wise), Al-Alim (The All-Knowing), Al-Hannan (The Most Gracious), Al-Muid (The Restorer), Al-Habib (The Beloved), Al-Hafiz (The Protector)—let him write them in the third hour after sunrise, and the effect will become known. And when you examine the ranks (numerical values) of these Names, you will find that after the letter Ha in Hayy, it is the first rank of the tens. Look at how Al-Zahir (The Manifest) has appeared in Al-Hakim, which has the numerical value 20, and in Alim (The All-Knowing) the letter Lam has appeared, which has the numerical value 30.

For protection from fever and thirst:

And whoever writes the letter Ha eight times in this fashion—Ha Ha Ha Ha Ha Ha Ha Ha—on the eighth day of the month, in the ninth hour, on the day of Wednesday, and also writes these Names: Al-Hayy Al-Hakim Al-Hannan, and keeps them with him, will be protected from fever and thirst.

For the protection of gardens and fields:

With this composition, very good protection is achieved for the garden and crops, when it is written and hung on the tree. And similarly, if the tajmir (numerological combination yielding 28) of these eight Names is performed and filled in a glass vessel, they work very well for attracting hearts and attracting love. The composition for this is as follows: take the first letter of the desired name and write it. Then write the letter Ha, for example if the desired person's name is Zayd, then write in this fashion: ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa ZaHa. Then write these Names over it: Talatil, Hamail, Habsail, Halfail, Hatail. And let the circular seal be at the end, surrounding it on all four sides, and give the incense of dhikr (remembrance) and place it in some high place where sunlight does not reach, directed toward the desired person. And you should perform the dhikr of the Secondary Names together with the Spiritual Names in this manner:

"O company of the spiritual beings, by the right of what is in your names and the names of Allah: Al-Hayy Al-Hakim Al-Halim Al-Hannan Al-Hamid Al-Habib Al-Haqq, unless you have made for So-and-so acceptance, mercy, forbearance, and grace in the heart of So-and-so, until no comfort satisfies his life, nor does he find rest, and he remains always wandering, confused, hungry, thirsty, following the traces of So-and-so and seeking him as the thirsty one seeks water, by the Surah of Al-Rahman and the Opening of the Quran and the Garden of Ridwan and the offering and the grace, and may his heart be lifted—O Allah, verily this is constant, eternal, for the eternity of lives and ages and years and times. No, no—let there be no sky shading him, nor any earth bearing him. Answer, willingly, by the names of the Lord of the Worlds: Al-Wahhaj Al-Ajal, the Hour draws near."
