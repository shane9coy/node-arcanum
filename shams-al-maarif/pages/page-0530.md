The Forms of Witr Strings:

1- The witr of Aries: a b j d h w z h t y k l m n s a f s q r sh t th kh dhal dhadh dh gh.
2- The witr of Taurus: s a t d r j t n s w q dhadh r t f a s m k y r w h r b j d.
3- The witr of Gemini: a t h th n s q dhadh p a l k r w h r b j.
4- The witr of Cancer: w q sh b a s a s a s a b j d h w z h t y k l m n r j a.
5- The witr of Leo: k t m a b j d h w y q r a s m w d r j y k l m n s f s.
6- The witr of Virgo: b q h n s d h a b j d h w z h t y k l m n a f s t w a r t th q k l r a f l b.
7- The witr of Libra: k s a l f t h s a k l m n a q s t th w a r t th q k l r a f l b.
8- The witr of Scorpio: s s r t h w k l m n s a f s q r sh t b j d h a s a b j d h.
9- The witr of Sagittarius: s q r sh t th kh dhal dhadh dh gh a b j d h z.
10- The witr of Capricorn: a b j d h w z h t y k l m n s a f s q r sh t th kh dhal dhadh dh gh.
11- The witr of Aquarius: s d h a sh q k l m n s a f s q r sh t th kh dhal dhadh dh gh a sh a m y d h r z k l.
12- The witr of Pisces: a b j d h w z h t y k l m n s a f s q r sh t th kh dhal dhadh dh gh.

These are the incantations of the twelve signs. If you wish to proceed with operation, separate the letters of the need (question) and then divide them by 12 after placing "ayn-mim-kaf" on each letter, and observe. If one remains, join it with the witr of Aries; if two remain, join it with the witr of Taurus; and if it is Libra, divide by the natures and count the letters to ascertain the result. By this method, the entire state of good and evil becomes known. When a person comes to you asking about some judgment, join the witr of Aries with the letters of the questioner, count the letters, and give the answer. If a person comes asking about love and affection, or request, or wisdom, join the letters of the witr of Aquarius for them. Likewise, for each matter, work from its appropriate sign.

The Great Rule

Each sign has a letter that takes the place of the letters, and its composition is as follows: when you wish to perform an operation, first ascertain the reality of the ascendant. Then take the letter of this ascendant and join it with the number, and such is the letter for each sign:
* The letter of Aries is "b" and the number is 21 and the afaq is pentagonal.
* The letter of Taurus is "t" and the number is 89 and the afaq is triangular.
* The letter of Gemini is "y" and the number is 32 and the afaq is square.
* The letter of Cancer is "t" and the number is 25 and the afaq is hexagonal.
* The letter of Leo is "h" and the number is 21 and the afaq is hexagonal.
* The letter of Virgo is "r" and the number is 14 and the afaq is square.
* The letter of Libra is "r" and the number is 14 and the afaq is octagonal.
* The letter of Scorpio is "w" and the number is 90 and the afaq is decagonal (90).
* The letter of Sagittarius is "h" and the number is 11 and the afaq is triple.
* The letter of Capricorn is "b" and the number is 10 and the afaq is square.
* The letter of Aquarius is "b" and the number is 12 and the afaq is octagonal.
* The letter of Pisces is "p" and the number is 13 and the afaq is decimal.
