Destruction of the Tyrant King:
Some people have used this [seal] against tyrant kings, and they were destroyed. When you wish that a boat or ship should not travel on the river, or if it travels it should sink, then write the Seal of Nobility on a piece of wood with the water from a bath-house drain, and take some of that water into your mouth, and rinse your mouth with it in the boat and ship—it will not travel. When Caliph Ma'mun wished to raise a storm in the Tigris River, he would tie this seal in an elevated place with a white silk thread. From all directions, waves would turn toward it, and people would come close to drowning. Then they would realize that this was the feat of the Caliph, and they would cry out and raise an uproar. At that moment, he would send for that talisman, and the river's storm would subside.

For Removing Illness:
When you wish to remove someone's illness, write this seal on their forehead and recite the 'Azimah over it—the patient will find relief. When you intend to free a prisoner, take some blessed earth; let the prisoner remove it from his collar, then turn it over, and recite the 'Azimah—he will obtain release.

Summoning the Absent:
When you wish to call someone from a distance, write this seal on his footprint if possible; otherwise, write it on a piece of paper along with his name, and after giving it the breath of life, hang it in the air—he will come quickly.

Know that the actions of this Name are correct, but ascetic practice, fasting, and renouncing the world are necessary conditions for the purpose to be achieved. His Eminence Ali, may God honor his face, said: "The Quran has an apparent and a hidden meaning. The apparent is the luminous [letter] which is seen, and the hidden is the 'Azimah." When a pot is boiling over, write this seal and place it on the pot—the boiling will subside.

The Operation for Summoning the Absent:
When you wish to call someone who has become absent, write this seal on a sheet of paper, and around it write "By the heaven and the star" (wa al-samā'i wa al-ṭāriq) in the form of separate letters. Then hang it in front of the sun at the appropriate hour while the Moon is in an airy sign. Recite the 'Azimah seventy-one times—that person will appear immediately.

Causing Pain in the Enemy's and Tyrant's Eyes:
When you wish that the enemy or tyrant should suffer pain and agony in his eyes, make a wax figure resembling the form of the person you desire. Engrave this seal upon it, and pierce both eyes of that figure with two needles. Then place it in a black pot, sprinkle some whiteness like that of figs upon it, and cast bath-house filth upon it. While performing this operation, the victim will immediately clutch his eyes and shriek, "Woe, fire has seized my eyes!" He will suffer in both eyes and be unable to see anything. Due to the intensity of the pain, he will seek help. Do not leave him in that state for more than seven days, otherwise the victim will die. Then when you wish to restore that person to health, remove that figure and cast it into water—the person will recover and his eyes will be healed. If you wish that someone should not sleep, write his name on wax and bury it in a ruin or some other desolate place—he will not sleep as long as it remains buried. When you wish to cause harm to someone, remove it from the burial and discard it, then keep it away from the earth. If you desire something regarding someone and write "murder" upon it, write the victim's likeness upon it, then pour a little water into a glass bottle. Seal its mouth, and bury it in the earth. Then light a fire beneath two stones and place this glass upon it. The victim will be afflicted from all sides by pain, suffering, misfortune, and illnesses.
