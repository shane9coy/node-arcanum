# Page 757 (English translation)
In my face there is a ray from Your awe that snatches the eyes of the enviers from among the jinn and humans, blinding them and preventing them from casting the arrows of envy. In my paper write: "And protect me from them with the veil of light whose interior is light. And I ask You by Your name the Light and by Your face the Light, the light by which every light shines, O Light of Light. I ask You to fortify me with the light of Your name as a veil that protects me from every tyrannical aggressor and stubborn oppressor, silencing regarding me every deficiency, by separating between the eyebrows. And blind them, for You are the Light for every light of Yours, O God, O Living, O Manifest, O Light of Light. God is the light of the heavens and the earth." End of the verse.

Whoever performs two prostrations of prayer and then recites this supplication forty-one times, Allah the Most High places his awe and grandeur in the hearts of all creation.

By the recitation of this supplication, enemies are defeated:

When this devoted person prays for the defeat of enemies, it will be accepted. And whoever recites this supplication in darkness with eyes closed around the cheek, his heart will be filled with observations of the luminous unseen. And if he persists in it, the realm of the unseen will become complete in his sight.

This dhikr is extremely beneficial for the people of understanding and the masters of hearts. Its writer and bearer display an increase in their powers, and enemies are defeated by it, because subduing enemies and silencing tongues and producing hot temperaments such as yellow bile are specific properties of the soul, and its effect in winning hearts is also very lasting and effective.

By the recitation of this supplication, phlegmatic diseases are healed:

Whoever is pregnant will overcome diseases of black bile, especially phlegmatic illnesses. Where shall we fully explain everything? A hint suffices for the intelligent.

By the recitation of this verse, sustenance will be abundant and the chest will be illuminated with spiritual sciences

Whoever writes this verse "Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth" during the hour of Venus and keeps it with himself, his chest will open for the sake of knowledge, and Allah the Most High will grant him abundant sustenance, and power and awe will become manifest upon him. And this supplication should also be written with it during the eighth hour:

"My God, look upon my existence, the sun of my witnessing from You in the worlds and the lights, so that I may walk with it walking of those who are informed of the news of the Dominion realm, joyful and delighted. And reveal to him the meaning of the creative word, so that it may act for me in every creature and its reaction by the universal word by Your permission, by which You have subjected what is in existence to it without any darkness following. Indeed, You illuminate the all by Your all, and illuminate the lights by Your light, whose breasts emanate from Your name the Manifest Light and the Living, the Sustainer. Everything is perishing except His face; His is the command, and to Him you shall be returned."

The one who recites this supplication will have the light of wilayah and success in every endeavor with the correct method.
