Chapter 77: Properties of al-Walid, obtaining wilayah, and awe in people's hearts

This is the great name at the head of the ancient saints, poles, masters, caliphs, and disciples, and it is also suitable for a person who has subjects and is responsible for people's affairs. Whoever mentions this name frequently will have awe among all creation. Whoever writes its square on paper with luminiferous light and mentions it according to its numerical value, if he desires wilayah, it will be obtained. Its number is 47, the first number. Chapter 78: Properties of the Supreme Name, ease in difficulties, success in lawsuits and disputes

Whoever mentions this magnificent name and goes to kings and princes will have his purpose fulfilled. This name is suitable for a person who faces disputes and lawsuits. If it is written on an iron plate during the ascendant of Saturn and he mentions it according to the name's number, every enemy and opponent will be defeated. Whoever mentions this name frequently, all his difficulties will become easy. Its number is 541, an odd deficient number. Its 21 and 41, two great letters, point to ta and mim. These two letters are a sign indicating the acceptance of stations upon the person and of the Most High's elevation. This is a square number from the multiplication of the complete number of "complete in itself." The names of its letters point to the great names of Allah the Most High: the Most Generous, the Omnipotent, the Guide.
