# Page 66 (English translation)
There are eighteen stars within it and seven stars outside its figure. The figure of this sign is like standing twins, each of whom has placed both hands upon the shoulders of the other. All the stars of this sign face northward. The legs face east and the back faces west. The fourth sign is Cancer. It has six stars within and four outside. Its shape is like a crab. Its front part faces east and the rear part is connected to Gemini toward the west and south, as if they are carrying it. Among its stars is also the star Heart of the Lion (Regulus). The fifth sign is Virgo, also called al-Aẓrāʾ (the Virgin). It has sixteen stars. Seven stars are outside its figure. Its figure is like a winged maiden who has inclined her head toward the north, and among its stars is the star Simāk al-Aʿẓal. The sixth sign is Libra. Eight stars are within its figure and nine outside. Its shape is like a right angle. The sign of Scorpio has twenty-one stars within and three outside its figure. Its figure is upright. Among its stars is one bright star, Heart of the Scorpion (Antares). The eighth sign is Sagittarius, also called Rāmī. Thirty-one stars lie behind Scorpio. Its shape is a composite of a human and a horse. The body up to the neck is that of a horse, and from the neck upward the half-body is like a human holding a bow and arrow, which is inclined toward the earth. The tenth sign is Capricorn. It has twenty-eight stars. Half its body is a goat's and the rear half is like a fish's tail. The eleventh sign is Aquarius, also called Dālī. It has forty-two stars and three stars are outside its figure. Its figure is like a man holding a pitcher, pouring water upon his feet. The water flows from behind his feet toward the south. The twelfth sign is Pisces. It has thirty-four stars and four stars are outside its figure. Its figure is like two fish whose tails are joined. All these stars together number 346 stars. Aries is the first of these signs. Taurus is also a celestial sign. Regarding the signs, it is said that they are in the middle of the heavens. "Signs" refers to the middle of everything. All these signs are in the heavens, and toward the direction of Capricorn's heart there is a star by which the qiblah direction is determined.

Chapter: Description of the Cities Associated with Each Sign
[/Decorative rectangular frame]

These cities are associated with Aries: Babylon, Persia, Azerbaijan. With Taurus: Hamadan, Ghor, Kurdistan. With Gemini: Gurjan, England, and Suan. With Cancer: the land of the Kingdom of China and eastern Khorasan. With Leo: Turkestan, Tatary, and Bilād al-Aqsūr. With Libra: the Land of Rome, Africa, Egypt, and Abyssinia. With Scorpio: Hejaz, Yemen, and the surrounding regions. With Sagittarius: Baghdad and Isfahan. With Capricorn: Kerman, Oman, Bahrain, and Basra. With Aquarius: from Kufah to the Hejaz. With Pisces: Tabaristan, Bahrain, Mosul, and Alexandria are associated.

[Decorative rectangular frame containing heading text]
Chapter: Description of the Division of Time
[/Decorative rectangular frame]

Time has four parts. The first is spring, and according to some, the first part is the summer season, and some have assigned autumn to it. Its beginning occurs when the sun enters Aries. This is the spring season. The beginning of the summer season occurs when the sun goes to the head of Capricorn. The beginning of the winter season occurs when the sun goes to the head of Capricorn. The beginning of the autumn season occurs when the sun enters Cancer, and according to some, each of these has a different season.
