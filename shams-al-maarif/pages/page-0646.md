### Through contemplation and dhikr of this name, one attains the rank of the Abdals:
He will obtain from its guardian such charity and blessings that their number and end are beyond reckoning. The dhikr of this name is extremely noble; let every person be diligent in reading it, for he will attain the rank of the Abdals, and the realities and hidden matters will be revealed to him. And if any person combines the letters of someone's name with this name, fills it into a square, and keeps it with him, that person will never be separated from him. If he engraves it on iron or silver and keeps it with him, his enemies will be subdued.

### The manifestation of Allah will descend upon him:
If he desires that manifestations come upon him in dreams, let him write this name on paper at an auspicious time, place it beneath his head, and sleep after reciting the name according to its numerical value. Allah the Most High will display His manifestation upon him. Its talismanic square is as follows:

And its supplication is this:
"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Glorified are You, how great is Your glory! There is no deity except You, Lord of Lords, and Master of servants. You are the Guardian, the Bestower. I ask You, O Allah, by the secrets of Your wisdom in hearts and in hidden matters, and by the light of Your manifestation upon the righteous and the good, that You clothe me with majesty and acceptance among the sons of my kind, and that You reveal to me the secrets of the Guardian. O Guardian, You are the Knower of what shall be. You have turned the intellects and tongues away from describing [You], for as belongs to You, and You are too glorious and too great to be comprehended by Your essence. I ask You that You grant me a portion from the refinements of Your Guarding Name, and that You grant me a servant of this name, the first of the elevations of the strange [knowledge] from the divine sciences. O Allah, O Guardian."

Whoever serves this dhikr, Allah the Most High will make hearts subservient to him and he will attain the goal of his desire.

[Centered heading in a decorative frame:]
### Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Aziz"

Know that "Aziz" is applied to that Being who is incomparable and peerless, and who is dominant and victorious over all.

### Properties and Regression:
This name's original rank is among the divine names of Allah the Most High, which are for making a claim of honor, connected to the rank of the prophethood of the Messenger of Allah, may peace be upon him, and it is the light of life. In it lies all the speech of ignorance that remains alongside their rank, and for this reason Allah the Most High has not invalidated it—though only God knows the secret of whose rank it establishes.
