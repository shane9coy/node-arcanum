# Page 120 (English translation)
[Not] of those who have incurred wrath, nor of those who are astray. Amen. O Allah, do not make us straying nor misguiding, nor expelled from Your gate, nor separated from Your face by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.

O Allah, I ask You by Your name with which You opened the mystery of the world of creation and command, which is the means of revealing the truth for the cause of descent, and which is elevated in the forms of command and contemplation, subtleties and weight. By the truth of You, You gave support to whoever You willed—nay, it is known to whoever You provided for and for whoever You willed, they have stations, for whoever You willed and by the wisdom You established—their unity cannot be diminished. O All-Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Lord, O Allah, I ask You by this communion which is the connection between the Presence of Existence and Contingency, which is suitable with divine proof for the manifestation of the adornment of decrees and the secret of striving, and especially it is not hidden from the servant and the men of spiritual sciences, and the establishment of certainty is by the proof of special mercy, and that which occurs in desire which is coupled with witnessing and declaration, and that which is the proximity of those who obtain nearness around the middle axis. O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Opener, I desire from You light, ease, help, power, protection, success, concealment, consideration, beautiful praise, sustenance, blessing, hope, and good assumption of You, and despair of other than You.

In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. It is by Your command that it occurs, with Your blessing and from You is blessing. Your name is very blessed and Your rank is very elevated. There is no deity except You. We have brought faith in You and we have submitted for You and we have placed our trust in You. O Allah, grant us victory with Your light, O Master of the Day of Recompense. Illuminate our eyes with Your light. The proof is the light of lights, O Guide of the astray. Show us the path of truth—the Guide of the blind is not (like this). All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds. Make us independent of other than You, O Self-Sufficient, O Generous, O Allah, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate. The testimony to Your essence, O Most Merciful, O Peace. The speech of the Lord, the Most Merciful: "The Master of the Day of Recompense is He. We worship only You and we ask help only from You. I ask You that truly You are Allah in the realities of the explanation of works, and truly You are Allah in all states of splendor and transformation, and truly You are Allah with the attributes of oneness and eternity, who is pure from opposition, investigation, likeness, and assistance, and truly You are Allah that there is none like unto You, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, his family, and his companions, and that in the right of ihdinā al-ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm, ṣirāṭa alladhīna anʿamta ʿalayhim, I ask You to fulfill my need, and upon me bestow blessings which contain good of religion and world for me, with sustenance protected from calamities, with the qualities of gifts, O One who bestows blessings repeatedly, O He whose essence is truly the People of Piety and the People of Forgiveness. O Allah, do not make us in the life of this world humiliated and base, nor among those upon whom is wrath, nor among the astray. O Allah, do not make us straying and misguiding, nor those who are banished from Your gate, nor despairing of Your mercy, with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, his family, and his companions.

This is the last prayer; it should be read during very great and difficult matters.

This prayer is [unclear] in the reaching of blessings.

In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, praise spoken by the praising ones, Lord of the first and the last, praise that brings me satisfaction and benefits the worlds. The Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate, entered the regions and the trial of Moses, the one who spoke [to Allah]. Shajan al-Mitham, and it is the times of the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. These are two noble ones.
