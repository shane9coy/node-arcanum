# Page 475 (English translation)
**The Letter Ha:**

ح has a silent cold temperament. Its day is Thursday, the star is Jupiter.

Benefits: Very effective for thirst and travel. Highly effective in ending love, pain, and anger. If you write it on your palm, wash it with water and drink it, your thirst will end. Those with heat-related ailments should perform this practice for three days and will be healed. If someone writes it on the skin of a cheetah [unclear] and burns it, makes kohl from it, and applies it to the eye, they will see the spirits unveiled. If the letter ح is written 64 times with musk or around a Chinese bowl and blown upon, healing will occur. The form is this: ح ح ح ح ح

**For heat, burning, and obtaining comfort:**

Write it in a glass cup, wash it with water and drink it—the heat and burning in the chest will go away. The heart will obtain comfort and joy.

**For expansion of sustenance and love:**

Make a square of its numbers and inscribe the talisman on a plate shaped like a دانگ when Jupiter is in its exaltation and the Moon is in its own lot of fortune. Its sustenance will expand and people will love him. Bind this tablet on the head of someone suffering from migraine due to yellow bile—the pain will end immediately. No one will quarrel or fight with him. If a sage [unclear] binds the tablet to his chest, he will make peace with hostile armies and there will be no war. The supplication is this:

O Allah, I beg You, O Living One, O Wise One, O Praiseworthy One, O Beloved, O Compassionate One, O Guardian, O Truth, O Preserver—by means of the secrets of the letter Ha which You have placed—that You subjugate for me the servants of this letter, they will obey me in that which brings Your pleasure; indeed You are over all things competent.

**The Letter Kha:**

خ has a cold temperament like ح but its special properties are distinct. Some scholars say they are identical.

Benefits and properties: On a Chinese plate upon which no grease has been applied, write the letter خ 600 times, wash it with the sweat of Bid Musak (saffron water), and give it to the patient—it is beneficial for heart palpitations and anxiety. Its numerical value is 600. [unclear] Kha appears on the other side; the number is 600.

**For a coward to become brave:**

Divide the numerical value of the letter خ and copy the square; make a circle of خ around it. By wearing it, a coward becomes brave, strong, and courageous of heart. He displays acts of bravery. If a child wears it, he will not be afraid in his sleep. He will be protected from the evil of jinn and humans. Inscribe this talisman on a blacksmith's axe, tap the coward on the back or strike the child, and he will become brave and courageous.

**For madness to depart:**

If you write the compound number [damaged] on a wooden tray with saffron and rosewater on a Tuesday during the first hour of Mars, write 600 خ on one side of it, wash it with rainwater and give it to the mad person for three days—he will become healthy. The symptoms of distress will end.
