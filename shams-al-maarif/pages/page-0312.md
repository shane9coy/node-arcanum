Chapter 90: Properties of the Name of the Preventer, protection from every fear and harm

Whoever frequently recites this name, Allah the Most High will keep him safe from every fear. Whoever fears harm from someone, let him recite this name, and Allah the Most High will protect him from all harm and evil, and the tyrant will forget hearing him. Its recitation benefits the sick and those afflicted with mania. Its number is 161. This number is the product of the perfect, the origin, and the future imperfect. Its 21 and 31 refer to the pure names; these three should be combined and used. Its square is written at the beginning of the inner [book] in the dignity of Mercury. This is its form:

Chapter 91: Properties of the Harmful Name, operation for the enemy's illness or destruction

This name should be written and recited in suitable times for causing illness to someone. Let there be complete attention and contemplation. Its literal number is 1001, which is a prime number, and its verbal number is 1100, which is an odd imperfect number. Its 21 and 11552 refer to the names of the Self-Sufficient and the Glorious. Know that harm occurs according to knowledge and comprehension. Whoever has more knowledge will have his regard fulfilled and will be perfect in effect. Whoever frequently recites this name until his operation becomes dominant over him, he can change one person's harm into benefit. In some circumstances, great benefit and reward is obtained from such things. If any person has an enemy who has become afflicted with worldly diseases such that if he remains in that condition he will die by night, this person struck him with such a blow that his blood decreased, and from this he obtained health, even though the striker did not do this action with that intention. And the reality of benefit and harm is from Allah the Most High. Abu Abd Allah al-Kufi says: whoever writes this name on a clay tablet in the first hour of its placement, at the time when Mars is in combustion, and looks at the tablet and recites this name according to its numbers with the intention of causing harm, his purpose will be attained.
