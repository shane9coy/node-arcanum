# Page 150 (English translation)
O Shamkhā, O Shamkhīthā, you are Shalmaḫā,
By Ṭā, Hā, Yā, and Ṣād—be for us,
By Kāf and Hā, then ʿAyn and Ṣād,
For it is my chief one—my helpers and servants—
By Qāf and Nūn, then Ḥā Mīm after it:
Three rods arranged after a seal,
And a Mīm of erasure, truncated, then a greeting,
And four like fingers arranged,
A Hā of union, then a curved Wāw,
Its end like the beginning—a seal.
So this is the Name of God, may His glory be glorified,
And this is the Name of God—O ignorant one, believe!
So take these noble names and conceal them;
By them are the covenant, the pact, the promise, and the meeting.
And if its bearer is in fear, he will be safe,
And if he has fallen from the jinn, he will stand.
So receive and do not fear, and judge and do not be afraid,
For from them are four letters from the Torah,
And five from the Quran—they are their completeness.
So no serpent shall you fear, nor scorpion shall you dread,
Nor shall you fear a sword, nor shall you fear a dagger.
O keeper of the Name whose mention is glorified,
And pray to God, O Lord, morning and evening,
I have sought intercession, O Lord, by Your most beautiful names.

And O Ṭalmaḫā—lo, the winds have quivered,
By Ṭā Sīn Mīm for happiness have they come,
Sufficient for us from every terror that has encompassed us,
By Āl-Shaday I swear—I did not burn,
And in the Chapter of the Smoke, a letter has commanded,
Upon its head like a spear it stands upright,
And in its midst, with jarr, a soft one has been joined,
It points to the good things and sustenance gathered,
Like a cupping vessel, wound from the secret,
Fivefold of pillars, and for good it has contained,
And its names among creation have risen.
And do not doubt, lest the spirit and body perish,
For in it are names whose night has contained,
And with musk and camphor truly it is sealed,
So receive and do not fear kings for what they contain,
Scorching hot water—the garden of help has been cut off,
And abundant for sustenance—you are safe from misfortune,
And four from the Gospel—ʿĪsā ibn Maryam,
To every creature, eloquent and mute,
And no lion comes to you with roaring,
Nor from a spear, nor has a sword pierced,
By it are averted all misfortunes and harm,
Upon the family and companions whose mention has contained,
By Your most beautiful names when they are gathered.

Note: In some manuscripts, the description of the location of the Gospel is written thus:
It should be known that I have not written the blessed name of the Holy Prophet, upon him be peace and the blessing of God, here. What is the reason? I will explain:
That the Holy Prophet, upon him be peace, are witnesses. If their name were mentioned in this place, then God Almighty would bear witness to these names, and to such a person who performs ghusl (ritual bathing) with these sacred names, his need will be fulfilled. In this are the six letters of the Torah: Shammakh, Shamakhtha. The letters of the Gospel are these... [The rest of the line is partially cut off or illegible]
