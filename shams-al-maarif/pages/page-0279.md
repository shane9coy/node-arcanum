Chapter 28: The Secrets and Properties of the Ism Basir (All-Seeing Name) and the Revelation of Hidden Mysteries

This name is very glorious. Whoever frequently recites this name, Allah the Exalted will show him hidden matters. If he is a seeker (of knowledge), no religious or worldly matter will remain concealed from him. Its numerical value is 302. This is an even-odd rectangle, pointing to "two minus six and three hundred" and toward Monday. This is its reason. Its 21 and 154 point toward the eternal name of Allah the Exalted, because Allah the Exalted is Basir (All-Seeing) even before the existence of things. Its square figure is this:

Chapter 29: The Properties of the Ism Hikam (Name of Decrees) — "May the Decree Be Executed"

Whoever frequently recites this magnificent name, his decree shall be executed. Its recitation is suitable for the ruler and the governor. It is from among the hidden secrets. Its numerical value is 68. It is both even-of-even and odd. Its numbers are deficient. Its 21 and 58 point toward the divine name of Allah the Exalted, Hikam (Decrees) and Siddiq (the Truthful), although all of these derive from its requisite number. The numerical values of its letters are 3 for one reason and 18 for another, thus pointing toward the names of Allah the Exalted, Alim (the Knowing) and Fadil (the Most Excellent). These three names are more apparent in consideration than the first three names. Its figure is this:
