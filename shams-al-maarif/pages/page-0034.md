**Chapter 1**

**Letters of the Alphabet and Their Mysteries and Significations**

I speak, and may God the Most High grant success and guidance. The desires of those who seek fall into two categories: religious and worldly. Each of these has numerous subdivisions according to different aims. The scholars of this art have written extensively before the composition of this book regarding the knowledge of prices, the discovery of conditions and circumstances, and the operations of austerities and talismans. This knowledge is very extensive. Many people have directed their attention toward it, especially those who have found benefit within it. However, I have intended to avoid those matters upon which the ancient philosophers have discoursed and from which many people derive benefit. Although much benefit may be obtained from them in this world, they cause great loss in the Hereafter. The matters I mention to you will grant you benefit in both this world and the Hereafter.

Chapter: Regarding the exposition of the letters of the alphabet, for these are the foundation of speech and upon them its structure is established. We know that the effects of the unpointed letters are as they are in the alphabet, which contain the mysteries. In a hidden manner, help passes from the higher world to the lower world. The world of the Throne helps the world of the Chair; the world of the Chair helps the sphere of Saturn; the sphere of Saturn helps the sphere of Jupiter; the sphere of Jupiter helps the sphere of Mars; the sphere of Mars helps the sphere of the Sun; the sphere of the Sun helps the sphere of Venus; the sphere of Venus helps the sphere of Mercury; the sphere of Mercury helps the sphere of the Moon; the sphere of the Moon helps the sphere of fire, by means of which the sphere of moisture is taken; the sphere of moisture helps the sphere of cold; the sphere of cold helps the sphere of cohesion; the sphere of cohesion helps the sphere of air. If the air helps you, it helps your dust, for the dust helps the sphere of Saturn. Therefore, for Saturn, the letters in the simple forms are jīm. Its collective number is three (3) and its detailed number is fifty-three (53). Likewise, for mīm it is forty, for yā' it is ten, and for jīm it is three. Saturn also has three letters. In the connected forms, its letter is jīm, and its number is three. In the simple forms there are five, whose letter is hā', and its proportion is the fifth part. The number of the sphere of Jupiter is six (6), and its letter is wāw, and its proportion is the sixth part. As for the sphere of Venus, its letter is ẓā' (z) and its proportion is the seventh part. Regarding the sphere of Mercury, its number is eight and its letter is ḥā' (ḥ). Its proportion is the eighth part. Regarding the sphere of the Moon, its number is nine (9) and its letter is ṭā' (ṭ). Its proportion is the ninth part. The triangle of Saturn (1) is very famous among all people.
