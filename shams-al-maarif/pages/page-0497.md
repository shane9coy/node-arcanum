3124- The second degree is **Thawab al-Mu'awin**, whose nature and numbers are 314. The third degree is **Thawab al-Musta'mil**. Its expansion is 118. The fourth degree is **Thawab al-Sibbah**, in which no grass grows. Its expansion is 1314.

#### The Method of Exercising Power over Entities:

These are the important balances. If you wish to exercise your power over all entities—whether for good or evil, attraction or repulsion, or to unleash a monkey, wind, or rain—then expand the letters of this type and see which temperament is dominant. Increase the proportion of whatever element is dominant. Then if you wish to perform the operation during the day or at night, add the expansion of the night or day to it. Then also combine the expansion of the hour in which you wish to operate, and include the stellar balance as well. Then when all these balances are gathered together with the name of the person upon whom you wish to perform the operation. If their numbers are of one genus, arrange the names in quatrains; but if they are contrary, arrange them in septuplets, as I have explained previously. For example, suppose you wish to perform an operation on a person whose name is Ya'qub. You expand it thus: y, 'a, q, u, b, n, a, d, n, m, l, y, k, s, t, u, a, t, n, y—21 letters in total, and its numbers are 1856. Then combine with these the balances I have mentioned. Work according to the elements and see which element is dominant. Perform the operation accordingly—for example, if earth is dominant, the operation should be pressed into the earth, or a grave, or the threshold of a house; if fire is dominant, it should be buried near fire or burned in a lamp; if air is dominant, it should be hung in the air or tied on a mountain; if water is dominant, it should be thrown into water or buried. For good works, incense and perfume are lit; for evil works, foul-smelling things are burned.

#### The Method of Determining the Good and Bad Effects of Names:

When you wish to know the good and bad conditions of names, determine this from the balance of each name of the Lord of the Word. Here is an example: Sunday is associated with the Sun, and gold is produced from the earth for it. When you have expanded its letters and then multiplied the letter-numbers with the name according to the number of days, one figure remains. The Sun is for Sunday, and when the name Zahab (gold) is expanded, there will be 778 letters, and when these are worked through with the complete operation, only one will remain. When you take the name by which God created this difficulty, it too will correspond to the number of the stars and the essences. All similar matters are weighed in this balance. Whatever corresponds is correct, and whatever contradicts is wrong. Return it to the balance and restore each letter to its place, and delete any extra letter. This operation should be performed with words and letters.

### Chapter: The Clear Knowledge and Understanding of Hidden Secrets
[End of boxed section]

What is meant by this is the mention of the Oneness of the One, encompassing all letters, their degrees, days, heavens, and the Beautiful Names. These are the nine degrees of this importance.

That is, for each name there are 9 letters, and each degree is associated with one day, one star, and two portions. These are the divine names in numerical form: **Subbuh**/111 **Quddus**/222 **Nur**/333 **'Alam**/444 **Dust**/555 **Bint**/666 **Lawh**/777 **Lat**/888 **Khatl**/999

When you wish to perform a complete operation, take one of these degrees and extract from it the name of the Most Beautiful, the Detailed, and the Expanded. Add the numbers of all letters to it, and the numbers of both names are [illegible] in them. When all numbers are complete, you should perform the operation with your fingers. Accordingly, prepare a talisman and on the skin of the body [illegible] with stone and saffron and rose. This operation assists in every type of endeavor.
