**The angels mention the name [of God] around the seed for forty days so that Iblis cannot pass through it:**

(The angels circumambulate inside the womb and breathe the name of God upon the seed. This continues in this manner for forty days, and during this period Satan cannot approach the seed.)

**One should approach one's wife for intercourse with ritual purity and cleanliness:**

For this very reason our master, upon him be blessings and peace, commanded us: When we go to our women, go with wudu and cleanliness, and say "Bismillah" and recite this supplication:

اَللّٰهُمَّ جَنِّبْنَا الشَّيْطٰنَ وَجَنِّبِ الشَّيْطٰنَ مَا رَزَقْتَنَا اَللّٰهُمَّ ارْزُقْنَا وَلَدًا صَالِحًاؕ

The specialization of the angels of the Throne in assisting upon the seed is due to the fact that the name ar-Raḥmān of Allah the Exalted is inscribed upon the Throne.

**God named the womb by deriving it from His name ar-Raḥmān:**

Raḥm is derived from ar-Raḥmān, and for this reason the Messenger ﷺ said: Allah the Exalted says, "I am ar-Raḥmān, and I have created the raḥm. I derived its name from My names. So whoever connects with it, connects with Me, and whoever cuts it off, cuts off from Me."

**The angels protect the seed for forty days because it becomes mature at forty years of age and comes to know God:**

[Note: Faint handwritten text "Muḥammad ..." is visible under the previous heading.]
The angels protect the seed for forty days for this reason: that after birth, it needs forty years to become mature.

**Admonition: At how many months does a child emerge from the womb and remain alive:**

When the child becomes four months old in the womb, it begins to move and is swift to descend. The physicians say that a seven-month-old child, upon being born, remains alive, but when it is born at eight months, it does not remain alive. There is a discussion about this issue among the astronomers and the philosophers. The philosophers say that when the child completes seven full months, it moves to emerge, and if it emerges, it remains alive; in the eighth month it does not move, and for this reason its movement becomes heavy. The truth lies in the souls, because the nature in the hearts of souls turns toward warding off pain, which arises in the stomach. For one day and night, and then after that it becomes still in order to find ease. If the child also moves in the eighth month, then it takes the place of two conditions. For this very reason the child becomes extremely weak in this month and does not remain alive.

**In the womb, which star's influence affects the child in which month:**

The physicians say that in the first month the influence of the star Saturn falls upon the child, then Jupiter's, then Mars's, until in the eighth month Saturn's turn comes. Its nature's dominance over it is very strong, and for this reason the child, upon being born, does not remain alive. The first opinion is more correct.
