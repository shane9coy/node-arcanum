# Page 84 (English translation)
Names of Your Power: whoever supplicates with them, I shall protect from the evil of what He created upon the earth and what emerges from it and what descends from the heaven and what ascends therein. Indeed, You are the Subtle, the Aware, the Guardian, the Knowing.

The name Bari is highly beneficial for removing Bari and all types of swelling. Similarly, there is the Ism al-Ba'ith [Name of the Resurrector]. The properties of both will be described in their respective places.

[Heading: Properties of the Letter Seen]

When Almighty Allah created this letter from the world of command, He sent down with it nine thousand eight hundred and eighty (9880) angels. This is the first letter of the Supreme Name. The Supreme Name has one apparent [aspect] and one hidden [aspect]. From its apparent [aspect], the heavens and earth are established. In its hidden [aspect], the elevated [realms], the Throne and the Seat are established. For this reason, the letter Seen is the first [letter] of the word Silence. The rank of the Seat belongs to it, since the letter Ba relates to Divine Power and it is that which conceals hidden things. The meaning of the letter Ba is "from you to me." It says: "From Me is [all] and with Me is [all]."

[Heading: Virtues of Surah Yasin]

Surah Yasin is one of the Names of Wisdom. Whoever knows it should write it, wash it with rainwater, face the qiblah, drink it alone—then Allah Almighty will cause him to speak with wisdom upon his chest as if it were inscribed there. This name is in the middle of the aforementioned surah. Its letters have the number sixteen. From these, two letters have four dots [each] and two letters have dots below. Within it are the Sacred Words, whose first letter is Seen and last letter is Mim. In the divine names Salam [Peace], Sami' [All-Hearing], and Sari' [the Swift], the letter Seen also appears. These names are specific to those who show great humility in supplication.
