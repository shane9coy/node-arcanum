# Page 409 (English translation)
407

[Top Margin]
K... times recite

[Section 1]
O Allah, subjugate for me the kingdom and the sovereignty; there is no god but You, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Living, O Self-Subsisting; I seek help through You, O Helper, help me—recite 40 times. Then the prayer will be accepted. After that, recite 37 times: "O Respondent, answer my call and fulfill my need by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful"—recite K times; the prayer will be accepted. After that, recite: "O Allah, I ask You by Your great and greatest Name, and I answer Your call, O Honored One, our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that You act toward me as You are worthy of, and do not act toward me as I am worthy of. Indeed, You are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness." Then the prayer will be accepted. Then recite: "And He has set forth for us a parable" [unclear] "indeed, He is Knowing" up to there. Then recite: "O Allah, lighten for us the burden of sins and grant us the livelihood of the righteous, and avert from us the evil of the whisperings of night and day, and free our necks from the Fire, and our fathers and mothers by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful, O Mighty, O Forgiving, O Generous." O Allah, lighten for us the burden of misdeeds, grant us the livelihood of the righteous, avert from us the evil of whisperings of night and day, free our necks from the torment of Hellfire, and free the necks of our fathers and mothers as well, O Most Merciful, O Mighty, O Forgiving, O Generous. Then the prayer will be accepted. Then recite:

[Section 2]
"[He is] the one who made for you from the green tree a fire, and then you kindle from it" [illegible] up to "In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of all the worlds"—recite to the end. Then the prayer will be accepted. After that, recite 7 times: "O Guide of the astray, there is no guide besides You. Guide us to the straight path—the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, and You have subjugated the secrets of Your Names, O Lord of all the worlds—not the path of those who have earned wrath, nor of those who are astray. Amen, O Manifest" [illegible] times. Recite: "O Allah, subjugate for me the kingdom and the sovereignty; there is no god but You, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Living, O Self-Subsisting"—recite 3 times. "I seek help through You, O Helper, help me"—recite 4 times. Then the prayer will be accepted. Then recite 37 times: "O Respondent, answer my call and fulfill my need by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful." Then recite four times:

[Section 3]
O Allah, I ask You by Your great and greatest Name, and I answer Your call, O Honored One, Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that You act toward me as You are worthy of, and do not act toward me as I am worthy of. Indeed, You are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness. O Allah, grant us the good of this world and the blessings of the Hereafter, and turn to us in forgiveness before death, and do not torment us after death, and make easy for us the agonies of death, O Hearer of all voices. O Allah, deliver us from doubt in exile, from illness in hardship, and from wretchedness at the time of the blast. O Allah, grant us safety and grant safety to our religion, and do not take away our faith at the moment of death, and do not put us to trial at the time of death. O Allah, make me one who multiplies Your remembrance, one who fulfills Your right, one who hopes for Your promise, one who fears Your threat, one who hopes in every state [unclear]. Relieve my distress and remove my grief and fulfill my need, O Decider of needs, and answer my call, O Respondent to calls, by the right of these chapters and forgiveness, and by the guidance [and for all the Muslim men and Muslim women and believing men and believing women. Indeed, You are Hearing, Near, Respondent to calls by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.]

O Allah, I ask You through Your great and greatest Name, I make intercession through Your noble Prophet, upon him be peace, that You treat me with what You are worthy of, and not treat me with what I am not worthy of. Indeed, You alone are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness [illegible/faded text]
