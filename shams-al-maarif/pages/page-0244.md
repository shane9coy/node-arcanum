Chapter: The Reality of Tawhid and Its Testimony

Know that the reality of the testimony of tawhid is that which God Almighty has given as testimony for His own Essence—indeed, He is Himself the Witness of His own Essence, and among His creation, He has made whomever He wills His witness. Regarding their creation, He took [this testimony] for their destiny beforehand, in the sense that He alone is the Knower of His own testimony for Himself, which He gave in truthful testimony, to the extent that the testimony of the truthful believers and monotheists is accepted. Every sect among them will recognize it, will affirm His oneness, and will bear witness to His eligibility and lordship. Thus this is evident from the verse "Allah has borne witness" [shahida Allah]. This testimony is obligatory, because it is given through the contemplation of His greatness and the manifestation of the signs of the unseen. Those people are created precisely for this eligibility.

By "the great ones" [awliyā al-ʿaẓm] are meant the scholars who are the people of realities in the realities of tawhid. They are united with the whole and the individual. They affirm the oneness of Ahmad, the Eternal. They know the meanings of the Divine Names and the realities of the Attributes. They witness the unseen. In the presence [of divine vision], they are the praise of God. In the state of presence, their station is [elevated], and in the seat of truth, near the Omnipotent King, they have lofty ranks.

Ibn ʿAbbās, may God be pleased with him, states that God Almighty took this testimony from them two thousand years before their creation. One narration states that it was twelve thousand years before. Each year has three hundred and sixty days, and according to our calculation, each day equals one thousand years.

It is transmitted from the masters that one should not enter the ocean of wilayah (divine friendship), because it causes division. Rather, upon hearing the verse "Allah has borne witness" [shahida Allah], one should be pleased with the ocean of the oath, because this is the praised station [maqām maḥmūd]. For one who exists in his identity with mysteries for speculative reasons, he is the first among the first and the last among the last.

Then, after this, he enters the ocean of the mysteries of "There is no deity but He" [lā ilāha illā huwa], which pertains to the Essence.

Know that in the mighty Quran, there are three categories: one category indicates the Essence and Attributes of God Almighty and tawhid; one category indicates religious matters [sharʿiyya]; and one category indicates knowledge of the matters of the afterlife. It is not hidden that the knowledge of the indication of the Essence and Attributes of God Almighty is accompanied by the tawhid, wilayah, and sanctification of its people. This equals one-third of the Quran. This also indicates the Divine Name of promise and warning.

Chapter: The Recognition of the Gnostic

The sign of one who has recognized God is that if he is not illuminated by his mysteries, his knowledge also will not be found. God Almighty has given precedence to the best of people over others in this state.

Chapter: Attaining Proximity to God Almighty

When you are granted the presence [wuṣūl] that God Almighty shows you the luminous effulgences of your station, then restrain your limbs from languor and laziness. Expel from yourself greed and pride. Keep the soul free from hope and desire, and from the urgency of action.

Chapter: Attaining Divine Success

The essential matter is that we all, as servants, are in need of the divine success of God Almighty. Therefore, God Almighty desires to grant guidance, so that we may remain complete for His work. And the being of God Almighty with [us] is [the goal], for which four principles have been established. The most essential of all is the primordial covenant [ʿahd fiṭrī]. God Almighty has placed four things for humanity in Islam and faith: forbearance, contentment, and verification.
