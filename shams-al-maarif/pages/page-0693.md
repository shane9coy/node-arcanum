Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Raqib"
Raqib is the essence which protects and watches over the most hidden and the most insignificant of things, and He is eternal. Neither time can compass Him nor space, and this attribute belongs to none but God Almighty.

[Heading]
God has appointed guardians over His creation in every place:
When the Almighty created the creatures in heaven, He placed for them an Ana (ego) as Raqib (guardian) within Tawhid (monotheism). Then He transferred them to the realm of the grave, and there also He appointed a Raqib over them. Then He brought them into the worldly realm of nature and made amanah (trust) their Raqib. Then He took them toward the resurrection, and there also He made them Raqib. God Almighty says:
إِلَيْهِ يُرْجَعُ الْأَمْرُ كُلُّهُ
Translation: "To Him shall all matters be returned."
The ritual retreat for this name is performed with external and internal purity and sitting in darkness. Day and night one should be occupied with reciting this name and with dhikr and devotional practices; the muwakkal (assigned spirit) will be present and fulfill one's needs.

[Heading]

And its dhikr is this:
بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ط اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ الرَّقِيْبُ الْمُرَاقِبُ لِاَعْيَانِ تَفَاصِيْلِ الْاَفْئِدَةِ فِی الْمَوْجُوْدَاتِ وَ تَفَاصِيْلِهَا يَا اِلٰهَ الْعِبَادِ اَنْتَ الْمُلَازِمُ بِدَوَامِ النَّظَرِ لَهَا فَلَا تَغْفُلُ لَمْحَةً مِنَ اللَّمَحَاتِ وَ اَنْتَ الْحَافِظُ لِنِظَامِهَا عَلٰی اَکْمَلِ الْحَالَاتِ فِی التَّحْلِيْلِ وَ التَّرْکِيْبِ وَ الْحَرَکَاتِ وَ السَّکَنَاتِ اَسْئَلُكَ بِسِرِّ الرَّجْلِ عِلْمِ حَبِيْبِكَ الْقَدِيْمِ اَسْئَلُكَ اَنْ تُنَوِّرَ ظَاهِرِيْ وَ بَاطِنِيْ بِنُوْرٍ مِنْ عِنْدِكَ وَ اَنْ تُلهِمَنِيْ اَنْ اَجْعَلَ بِمَرَاقِبَةِ لَمَحَاتِيْ وَ لَحَظَاتِی بِمَا تُصْعِدُنِيْ بِهِ لَکَ حَيْثُمَا وَ لَمَّا تَرْضَاهُ عَيْنُ مَحَبَّتِنَا اَللّٰهُمَّ اَيِّدْنِيْ مِنْكَ [unclear] الْمُلَاحَظَاتِ بِنَوَاكِی السَّوَاقِی وَ کَمَالِ اَنْجِلَاءِ حِفْظِهِ مِنَ الْاَمْرَاضِ فِی الْقَلْبِ وَ الْجَسَدِ وَ مِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا رَقِيْبُ اِذَا حَسَدَ ط

"In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the Raqib (Watcher) and Muraqib (Observer) of the essences of the particulars of hearts within existing things and their particulars. O Lord of servants, You are the one who adheres with perpetual vision over them, so do not neglect for an instant of instants. And You are the Preserver of their order in the most complete states in analysis and composition and motions and stillnesses. I ask You by the secret of the foot of the knowledge of Your ancient beloved. I ask You to illuminate my external and internal with light from You, and to inspire me that I make by the observation of my glances and my sights that by which You elevate me to You, wherever and whenever the eye of our beloved does not approve. O God, support me from You [unclear] the observations with the deficiencies of the beverages and the perfection of the healing of its protection from diseases of the heart and body and from the evil of an envier.
