This is the master of all means/intermediaries, and the greatest proof in it is the story of Abraham, peace be upon him—that the time for meeting his son Ishmael, peace be upon him, was thirty years. And they knew that it is in the Book of God. And the gnostic knows that according to the decree of God, the arrangement is as follows: he who writes a book, he alone is the special servant of God Almighty. God Almighty says: "And it is sufficient for you." By the recitation of these attributive names of God Almighty, the bewilderment of people is dispelled. And when you write these words, your knowledge will be complete, and you will reach the goal among the stations of gnosis.

To cause harm to someone, the seal of "Dhar" (the Harming) is effective; and to bring benefit, the seal of "Nafi'" (the Beneficial) is useful. The divine name "Dhar" does not only harm; rather, it is also beneficial. The mention of this name is: God Almighty will remove all necessities through it. And if He wishes to harm someone, He is well able to do so through this name.

The summary of the discourse is: whoever engraves these two names on silver and keeps them with him will be saved from all calamities and misfortunes. And whoever engraves the name "Nafi'" for the purpose of increasing benefit, from God and His creation all kinds of benefit will come to him—indeed, the desires of his heart will be fulfilled. And for the purpose of love and people's intercession, it should also be written on silver in a propitious hour. This is the virtue of both seals.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Nur" (Light)
Light is the apparent one that has made all things apparent, because the apparent in itself is that which is divinely subjugated. It is for this reason that it was given the name "Nur" (Light). And since existence is in any case, this fact is evident: existence is apparent and luminous, while non-existence is dark and darkness. And in existence there is a light that is the light upon the inner of the world and is apparent—it is the original light of essence and tranquility.

[Large calligraphic stamp text: Nurī Salām]
