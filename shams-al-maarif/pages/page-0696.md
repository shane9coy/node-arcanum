Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Hakim"

The meaning of the name Hakim is found in this Quranic verse:
"Whatever is in the heavens and the earth glorifies Allah, and He is the Mighty, the Wise."

The special property of this name is that whoever persists in it—whatever difficult task he intends—will achieve it. Its prayer is:

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, O my Master, O the Finder, O my Master, O the Eternal, O my Master, O the Knowing, O my Master, O the Wise—Your wisdom is perfect; there is none who can thwart Your command, none who can reverse Your decree, and none who can contradict Your judgment. So from Your saying: 'Blessed art Thou and Exalted,' O Possessor of Glory and Honor—Allah is He who created you, then provided for you, then will cause you to die, then will give you life—then the pervading wisdom within creation. I ask You, O Wise one, by the wisdom and what it contains of wondrous crafts and the perceptions of mercy and the complete blessings, that You open for me the treasuries of Your mercy from the seas of Your overflowing grace through Your complete blessings, and cause me to advance in the footsteps of servanthood for Your obedience, O Lord of the Worlds."

Whoever recites this prayer constantly, extraordinary feats and words of wisdom will flow from his tongue, and Allah Most High will elevate his rank.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wadud"

"Wadud" is He who wishes goodness for creation. This name is close to "Rahim," and it is the pure essence of God Most High. Whoever seeks divine proximity through this name will no longer be in need of creation, and because of the remembrance (dhikr) from the solitude of this name, his relatives will love him. It is fitting that in solitude he continually recite istighfar and perform the litany of this name thus: "Ya Wadudu Ya Rahim." Whoever recites this name should incline toward "Glory be to the Merciful, the Loving." This name is an elixir. For love, one should engrave this name and the name of the angel surrounding it inside a ring, and having recited this name, wear it. Will there be love?

For abundant love, write and keep with you:

Whoever desires complete love, it is incumbent upon him to separate the letters of these names with the beloved's name, and then combine their numerical values; the desire will be fulfilled. The names are:

Al-Wadud Al-Rahim and Al-Ghafur Al-Rahim.

Its prayer is:

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, O Wadud, You are He who has revealed the secret of love and affection in the hearts of the people of faith, and You have manifested with the standing and eternal light in the secret to the spirits, thus You have united the bodies, and You have manifested with Your name Al-Wadud over sustenance by subjugating all of Your creation, just as You have united the wild [animals] in—[Text cut off at bottom edge]
