# Page 352 (English translation)
**Obtaining love, acceptance, rank and status:**

Know that there is a great virtue in Ayat al-Kursi for love, acceptance, and obtaining rank and status. When you intend to use it for this purpose, write this talisman that follows on a deer's membrane with musk, saffron, and rosewater, and write Ayat al-Kursi around it. Fumigate it with Indian oud, ambergris, and cross-shaped oud. The form of the talisman is this:

**For needs to be fulfilled through rulers:**

Know that [Ayat al-Kursi] is also very beneficial for creating love and affection in hearts and for going before kings and ministers. Write it and keep it with you, and recite this supplication:
