Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Basit"

[Heading]
The Beautiful Allegory of Contraction and Expansion

The name Basit is the same one that will spread the souls within the bodies on the Day of Ist (the Day when souls were first spread), and this is the sacred essence of Allah the Most High alone. Its visionary meaning is that Allah the Most High contracts with stillness and expands with movement. Thus, this is the universal contraction. On the Day of Ist in the pre-eternal past, on the day when the people of division—that is, the disbelievers—He had contracted their hearts from acquiring the realities of faith, and He had expanded the hearts of the people of the right hand toward acquiring the lights of faith, and He had opened their breasts for accepting Islam. And He contracted the back at the time of death on the Day of Resurrection, and He contracted the night with stillness and movement, and He expanded the day with the manifestation of movement, and He contracted the inner reality in the world of non-formation, and He expanded creation in the mercy of the worlds.

[Heading]
The Gates of Mysteries Open for the One Who Remembers This Name:

The method of attaining proximity through these two names is that one should restrain one's soul from desires, the body from the forbidden, the tongue from speech, the eyes from forbidden things, the ears from backbiting, the hands from the forbidden, the heart from sins, the intellect from thoughts, the spirit from turning toward glory, and the head from the disclosure of divine mysteries. Then when you establish a connection with the name Basit, Allah the Most High will open for you the gates of light.

[Heading]
The Five Senses Will Be Illuminated and All Celestial and Terrestrial Matters Will Be Revealed:

Then at that time your five senses will become seeing and hearing, and your tongue will speak with remembrance, and your heart will be illuminated with the light of divine intuition, and Allah the Most High will make you aware of the realities of the kingdom (malakut). And when Allah the Most High reveals His lights upon you, at that time you will witness the realities of celestial and terrestrial matters, and authority (tasarruf) will also be obtained. Recite it 824 times after each prayer, and recite its dhikr 72 times; the assigned angel (mawwal) will be present, whose name is Bamtayil, and he is appointed over the expansion of souls. The reciter will see him in sleep and waking, and by his means the reciter will obtain many charitable gifts and blessings. And this square on which [unclear] is written, if one places it in water for seven consecutive days and drinks it, it will be beneficial. Or if this figure is engraved on a silver tablet and the dhikr and the name of the assigned angel are written around it, and the tablet is kept with oneself, there will be safety. And whoever has a name whose numerical value matches the number of this name should engrave this square on a ring and keep it with himself, and be consistent in the recitation of the name. He will become awe-inspiring among people and wealthy, and his heart will never experience contraction. And if he combines it with the name Wadud and remembers (them) together, Allah the Most High will grant him expansion in sustenance and felicity, and all his affairs will become easy.
