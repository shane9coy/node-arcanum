# Page 594 (English translation)
This is the secret that God Almighty reveals to His friends.

Protection from Fire:
This is the effect of the amulet of the letter that when you write it with the letter ṭā' on your palm and recite al-battā and then take fire in your hand or insert your hand into fire, your hand will never burn.

The one who keeps this amulet will become understanding, the fever will end.
The person who carries this amulet with him will have increased understanding. Whoever has been suffering from fever for many days should keep it with him, and the fever will depart from him. And if this letter is written on a piece of sulfur and thrown into someone's house over fire, the entire house will burn.

The Dull-Witted Becomes Intelligent:
When the dull-witted person recites this letter 10 times every day, his intellect will increase.

The Operation for Destroying an Enemy:
If you take soil from under someone's feet and make a complete likeness of them, then recite the letter ṭā' over it 81 times, and recite the 'azīma, that is, the declaration and the دعوت (incantation), and blow upon it, immediately an earthquake will occur in that person's house and their house will be destroyed. Solitude is its condition for the day. After each prayer, the دعوت must be recited 9 times. The client of the red light will present himself to you and will obey. The name of this client is Ṭayāṭīl. In the manner described, busy yourself with the دعوت. Its دعوت is:

بِسْمِ اللّٰہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِo طَلَبْتُ مِنَ اللّٰہِ الْمَعُوْنَۃَ عَلٰی مَظْلُوْمِیْنَ حَتّٰی یَنْبَسِطَ اِلَی اللِّقَآءِ بِطَرْدِ مَنْ ظَلَمَنِیْ اَجِبْ یَا طَا بِنَظَائِلِ عَظْمَۃِ ذِی الْقَوَّی الشَّدِیْدَۃِ ھَیًّا ھَیًّا یَا اَللّٰہُ یَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِیْنَ طَلْطِیَاطُ 2 یَا طَا یَا طَاطٍ عِیْطُوْا طَفْلًا طَھْیِیْطٍ طَیْطُوْطِ اَلْوَحَا اَلْعَجَلَا اَلسَّاعَۃِ طَرَدَ مَنْ یَّقَابِلُنِیْ بِحَقِّ ھٰذِہِ الْاَسْمَآءِ اِذْطَرَدَ بِکُنْ مِنْ ذِی الْقُوَّی مَظْلُوْمِیْنَ عَجِّلْ یَا خَادِمَ الطَّآءِ وَاِلَّا اَشْکُرُکَ اِلٰی عَلَّامِ الْغُیُوْبِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّۃَ اِلَّا بِاللّٰہِ الْعَلِیِّ الْعَظِیْمِo

Translation: "I ask God Almighty for help to obtain my desire, so that He may create the letter ṭā' for me to drive away my oppressors. O Ṭayāṭīl, by the glory of the one with great power, obey me. O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, drive away those who fight against me. By Ṭabṭīl, drive away the enemies by these names, so that I may achieve my purpose. The one with a large throat has been found. O client of the letter ṭā', do my work quickly. Otherwise, I will complain of you to the Knower of the Unseen, and there is no virtue or power except with God the Exalted, the Great."
