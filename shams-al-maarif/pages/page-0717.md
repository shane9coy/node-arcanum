# Page 717 (English translation)
715

Its remembrance is this:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the All-Powerful, the All-Authoritative, who created by Your power whatever You found among the authorities, and You determined the ability that You invented and established by Your power—and whatever You established with it, its explanations and its establishment—and You are independent of needing assistance from anything among the created beings. You are the All-Powerful who can, by Your power, over all creatures without correspondence or treatment through treatments and instruments. I ask You, O Powerful One, by the encompassment of Your power over the great and the small, that You make for me strength for what brings me closer to You from You, and do not cut me off ever from You, and help me by Your grace wherever there is from the attachments, and do not test me with the change of action and affirmation. Indeed, You are Allah, the All-Bestowing, the All-Powerful, the All-Authoritative.

[Decorative Frame: Chapter: The Merits of the Name "Al-Muqaddim Al-Mu'akhkhir"]

Al-Muqaddim (the One Who Brings Forward) and Al-Mu'akhkhir (the One Who Delays) is He who makes distant and near—for whoever He brings near becomes the one brought forward, and whoever He makes distant becomes the one delayed.

<u>The prophets and saints are among those brought near by Him, while the deniers are among those delayed:</u>

Thus He has brought His prophets and saints near to Him with direct proximity and delayed His enemies with distance, placing between Him and them the veil of the unseen.

When a king makes someone his close confidant, people say that the king has brought him forward. This advancement occurs sometimes in place and sometimes in rank. This necessarily involves addition to its opposite, and within it there is necessarily an intention which is favor—by the addition to it of being brought forward, there is meant that which was brought forward, and being delayed means that which was delayed. And the intention of Allah alone suffices, because when you reflect upon their advancement and delay among the attributes, you will honor them. This is upon the basis of their action, so their honor is with their knowledge. Thus it becomes known that Allah, Exalted, is alone the One Who Brings Forward and Delays. In this verse He has made explicit:

Indeed, those for whom the good has already gone forth from Us... [the verse]

And He said:

And if We had wished, We would have given every soul its guidance.

Thus He brought the believers forward and delayed the disbelievers. The seeker becomes aware of these two attributes. When the seeker recites the name Al-Muqaddim, a laylat al-muwakkal [assigned angel] appears to him, whether in a dream or while awake, and traverses all regions for him.

<u>From the remembrance of this [name], creation loves [the practitioner], and rank is elevated:</u>

Whoever writes it and keeps it with himself, all creation will love him, and an elevated rank will be his portion. And let the person recite the name Al-Mu'akhkhir whose soul's quality may or may not be diminished—the effect of the divine intellect will descend. In this manner, he recites it according to the number of the name. The assigned angel descends upon him and assists him. Whoever writes these two names together with the intellect and keeps them with himself, and writes his own name and keeps it with himself, will obtain great light.
