Fourth Chapter: Description of the Name Mālik, its Properties and Benefits

This mention is suitable for kings. Its square is 3×3; here too the author has used the word "square" for what is meant to be a "triangle."

Whoever engraves it on a gold plate with the verse "Qul Allāhumma Mālika al-Mulk" and keeps it with him, will become awe-inspiring among people. And for whoever bears the name ʿAbd al-Mālik (Servant of the King), this mention is highly appropriate. The form of the talisman is as follows:

[Table 3: 3×3 magic square with numbers]
| 29 | 24 | 27 |
| 28 | 30 | 32 |
| 23 | 26 | 31 |

When it is in the dignity of the Sun, if one writes its triangular numbers on a sheet of gold, places it in a ring, sets a ruby seal on top, and then wears it, then before whichever ruler he goes, that ruler will bow before him, will not be able to look at him, and will treat him with great honor. The sage Plato had prepared this very talisman for Dhū al-Qarnayn (Two-Horned Alexander), by whose effect lions would flee from before him. Its form is this; its number is 29. These are from the realities of letters. These names are from the Written Names, suitable in rank according to the number of the station, and it consists of Nūḥ (the single Noon). Its components are 44, which refer to the name Bāqī (the Everlasting) of Allah the Most High, and its letters' numbers are 182, which refer to the name Mujīb al-Daʿwāt (the Answerer of Supplications).
