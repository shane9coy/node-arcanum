Whoever writes the square of the name Mughannī on a deer's membrane and keeps it with him, the joy of the heart will be his portion, and God will ease all affairs for him. This talisman is written for every purpose; it is a very hidden secret.
The mention of these two great names is as follows:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ہ اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ الْغَنِيُّ فَمِنْ وَحْدَانِيَّتِكَ بِالذَّاتِ الْمُنْفَرِدُ فِي تَنْزِيهِ
الذَّوَاتِ وَالصِّفَاتِ الْمُغْنِيُّ عَنِ التَّحْقِيْقِ فِي الْاَزَلِ وَ الْاَبَدِ الْاَحَدُ الْفَرْدُ الصَّمَدُ اَسْئَلُكَ بِفَنَاءِ ذَاتِكَ
فِي تَنْزِيْهِ صِفَاتِكَ اَنْ تَكْشِفَ لِي هَذَا اَحْوَالِ الْمُحْدَثَاتِ وَعَنْ نَفْسِي ذَاتِي بِالتَّوْحِيْدِ اِلٰى ذَاتِكَ وَتُظْهِرَ
صِفَاتِي بِنُوْرِ صِفَاتِكَ يَا هُوَ ہ اَللّٰهُمَّ اَنْتَ الْغَنِيُّ اَغْنِيْنِي عَنْ خَلْقِكَ مِنْ عِبَادِكَ بِالْعَرَضِ الْفَانِيْ
وَ اَهِّلْنِي مَنْ جَلَّتْ بِالْبَقَاءِ لِلدَّارِ الْجَمَالِي اَلْمُغْنِي اَغْنِ الْبَدَنَ بِوُجُوْدِ الْمَالِ وَ اَحْسِنْ لِي فِي الْاٰخِرَةِ

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Hu. O God, You are al-Ghanī—therefore from Your oneness in essence, You are the unique one in the sanctification of essences and attributes; the self-sufficient one beyond verification in eternity and perpetuity; the One, the Unique, the Self-Sufficient. I ask You by the annihilation of Your essence in the sanctification of Your attributes, that You reveal to me these states of contingent beings, and from my own self through unification to Your essence, that You manifest my attributes by the light of Your attributes, O Hu. O God, You are al-Ghanī—enrich me from Your creation among Your servants by means of the perishing accident, and qualify me from the majestic One with permanence for the dwelling of glory. O al-Mughannī, enrich the body with the existence of wealth, and grant me goodness in the Hereafter.
