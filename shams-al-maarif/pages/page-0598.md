I desire light, through which I may contemplate the revelation of the secret of the Lām-mīm. So be obedient to me, O Lām, for I have called upon you, O Allah, O Allah, O He Who is Allah—there is no deity but Allah. Answer, O King, and come to the aid of one who has transgressed and rebelled among kings and servants. Answer by the One before whom the lofty mountains crumble in awe, and at whose fear the skins of people shudder—Lord, Eternal, Sustainer, before Whose greatness all things prostrate, and before Whose glory all things submit—and He is the Subtle, the Aware. There is no deity but He; His are the most beautiful names and the highest attributes. Vision cannot comprehend Him, yet He comprehends all vision. And He is the Subtle, the Aware. There is no deity but He, Lord of the Mighty Throne. Be One, O Lām, and hasten the destruction of the unjust. Glory be to Allah the Mighty—there is no deity but He. Whoever obeys Him shall be saved, and whoever disobeys Him, He shall make him as scattered dust. O Lām, by the night and nights, and by Sīriyāl and Tufriyāl, answer by the Mighty Throne and the Wide Footstool. Soften to me your side toward what I have called upon you, and grant me power over whoever among the spirits has disobeyed me, by the authority of Him Who says to a thing "Be" and it is. Hāyā, O good one, in seeking and in action—such and such. Hāyā, O you who are present from the spiritual spouses, by your Lord, beside whom there is nothing greater, and there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Mighty.

Its incense is laban, udh, and loban.

**The Letter Mīm:**

This letter is connected to three realms: the realm of the physical world, the realm of the divine intermediate, and the realm of the divine command.

**I am revealed with the secret of the divine intermediate:**

When one writes this forty times along with this verse forty times and keeps it with him, Allah the Most High shall illuminate hidden matters for him, and the revelation of the realm of the physical world and the divine intermediate shall be granted to him. The verse is: "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah" and "those who believe" to the end; and when this letter is written with the seven names—forty names—along with them and kept with one, awe and wisdom and forbearance of the upper and lower divine realms shall be granted.

**Everything shall be subjugated:**

Whoever writes this letter on the wall of his private chamber and gazes upon it forty times daily while reciting this verse: "Say: O Allah, Lord of sovereignty" to the end—Allah the Most High shall make his command prevail throughout the world. And if this letter is engraved forty times on a silver ring along with the name of its ruling spirit and worn, all the people of the world shall love him, and all of creation shall feel affection for him.
