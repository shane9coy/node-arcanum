Be mindful of God and do not act wrongfully against anyone, for those who rise for the sake of sin have become bewildered by Him. The procedure with this prayer for causing discord among these people is as follows: Write this talisman on a tile of fired clay on the day and hour of Mars, and after fumigation with the leaves of tongue, myrtle, and dried vine leaves, place it in vinegar of grapes. When the clay has softened in it, add a little tar and warm oil, and place it in the gate of the house where those people gather.

[Header:] The operation for stopping caravans by sea or land travel

Know that it consists only of the letters shīn, ṣād, and ʾālif. Its nature is fiery, hot and dry in the third degree. Its special property is to stop ships from sea travel and caravans from land travel. The talisman should be written on a lead tablet in an inauspicious hour and buried on the road; a strange effect will become manifest. Its light is the tongue of a chameleon and the head of a bat. Its manner of writing is as follows: q q q q q q q faṭirast ḥattā bayyin wa qifūhum ʾinnahum masʾūlūn [Bottom line partially obscured by watermark]
