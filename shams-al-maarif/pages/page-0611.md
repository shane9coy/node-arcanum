# Page 611 (English translation)
**To Cause Madness:**
When you wish to seize someone's reason, make their likeness and write this letter upon it, then place it in their house. Their reason will be ruined.

**Its Operation Will End Anger and Thirst:**
Write this letter to dispel anger and thirst, keep it with you, and when you wish to use its service, recite the invocation one hundred times after each prayer. The assigned spirit will be present. Its talisman is this:

[Diagram: A rectangular talisman box. Outside each of the four corners is a stylized letter Dal (د). On the top and bottom borders, there are two pairs of vertical lines with curved, hooked ends. Inside the rectangle, on both the left and right sides, the letter Dal (د) is repeated in two groups of three (د د د). In the center of each side's column, there is a stylized vertical mark.]

And its invocation is this:

**The Letter Ḍād:**
This letter is red. Whoever writes it in red on the corner of their turban and keeps it with them will become formidable, and everyone will believe his words. And when this letter is written with hedgehog fat and buried in the doorstep of a person's house, mosquitoes, flies, lice, and wasps will gather abundantly in that house. If you wish to start a fire in any building, mark one of its corners with ink and it will ignite.

**The Spirit That Will Be Consumed by This Operation**
When this invocation is recited over jinn, their jinn will be consumed. Its sigil is this:

[Diagram: A rectangular talisman featuring the letter Dad (ض) repeated six times along the top edge and six times along the bottom edge. Each corner of the rectangle is marked with a stylized script character. In the center is a grid-like sigil resembling a hash symbol (#) with stylized versions of the letter Dad (ض) extending from each of its four external corners.]

Note: The author did not mention its invocation.

**The Letter Ṭā':**
This letter is a horseshoe [unclear], and when written with lion skin or [unclear] fat and buried in a house, all harmful creatures will gather there.
