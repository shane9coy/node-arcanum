# Page 631 (English translation)
631

The Esoteric Inner Luminous Interpretation of "In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful":

Your saying "In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful" is the absolute world unrestricted, and the mention of the First Origin, that is al-Rahman, was made first because His mercy has preceded. The sin is among the noble, the foundations, and the greatest of names, and from it power emerges with the ba of "Bism" to the outside, and the world of the unseen and the witnessed comes into existence. And with the ba together with the sin, the world of the heavenly divine realm becomes manifest, and with the ba alone the resting of lights occurred, and with the ra and the mim mercy became manifest, and from the mim and the nun the decree of both Qiblas became manifest. When the companion bestowed upon you the secret of inspiration through graciousness and beneficence, at that time you said "All praise belongs to Allah" upon this matter which has preceded upon you in the arrangement and composition, and it is that the Glorious and Exalted Truth has His own praise. For this reason He inserted the alif and the lam, and al-hamd is from the name of al-hamid, and it is from the secret of "Bismillah." So you have indeed said: "In the Name of Allah is my eternal beginning and the first void." When you said "Allah," for this reason He praised Himself—subhanahu—and the secret of glory is also the secret of glory, and the spirit and al-Rahman are the secret of the heart, and al-Rahim is the secret of majesty. When you said "All praise belongs to Allah," it is in the world of composition, and when you said "al-Rahman," now this manifest al-Rahman will come from "Bismillah," and this is the manifest heart, because this is the location of the establishment of lordship and the secret of mercy according to possibility. When we said "the worlds," this became manifest as al-Rahim because all existents have become manifest in the disclosure of their glorification with the light of lordship and the gentle lights. For this reason these bodies have praised—the worlds which are compelled from the human realms and the divine secrets. This is the oneness of eternal glorification, but mercy has become manifest in the world for your sake, as it had become manifest in the eternal world. Thus you said "al-Rahman," meaning that Essence which has become established upon the heart regarding this matter which He inspired upon us through His being spoken in His praise.

The Great Name is Within "Bismillah":

This is the reason that the Great Name has been deposited within "Bismillah," and when it descended, the heavens quaked and earthquakes occurred in the earths, and the angels became occupied in the recitation of glorification to the extent that idols also fell upon the ground. This "Bismillah" is written upon the forehead of Adam and Israfel, and upon Gabriel's wing and upon Azrael's palm, and upon Moses' staff and upon Jesus' tongue, and upon Solomon's ring. From this verse separation occurs in every form. According to Imam al-Shafi'i, it is one verse from every surah.

The Mention of Some Blessings and Benefits of "Bismillah":

We mention some of its special properties for blessing. When a patient recites it according to its numerical value for seven days, he attains health from illness. When it is recited fifty times before an oppressor, one remains protected from his evil. If recited in the same quantity for the fulfillment of needs, requests are granted. If recited fifty times upon sleeping, one remains protected from every harmful thing. If recited one hundred times over three days and blown upon a patient, he recovers. If a prisoner recites it daily according to its numerical value, Allah the Exalted releases him from imprisonment. If one recites it one hundred twenty-three times in the seventh hour of Friday while supplicating for any religious or worldly matter, it will be fulfilled. If it is written in a vessel according to the numerical value of its expansion and given to a slow-witted person to drink, his intellect becomes sharp. If it is blown upon flowing water and cast into a garden, it brings blessing to its fruits. Whoever recites it one thousand times daily for forty days, Allah the Exalted grants him unveiling of graciousness, and He inspires upon him the most hidden things, and whatever occurs in the world becomes known to him entirely. Whoever recites it two thousand five hundred times after each prayer observes all the secrets of the world and becomes aware of things before they become manifest.

Now we [provide instructions] for some matter or important purpose: Fast for four days as Allah has decreed, and after the prayer of salawat, pray twelve units, and in each unit recite Ayat al-Kursi, al-Ikhlas, and the two mu'awwadhat forty times. When you finish the prayer, recite this expansion one thousand times and the durood Sharif one thousand times. Then after that, whatever you wish—signs from the unseen will become manifest, and you will be blessed with mercy in the world and among people.
