And the invocation for this letter is:

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I have brought forth by the majesty of Divine Sovereignty and by the glory of Greatness and Pride, and by the One, the Unique, the Ever-Subsisting, the Eternal, who does not die. Majestic One who manifested unto the mountain and made it crumble, and Moses fell prostrate in swooning. I have brought forth my desired beloved—there is no beloved besides Him—the Near, the Responsive. Answer, O letter of Jīm, by means of the righteousness and glory and exultation that is in you; your rank is most noble. Answer, obedient, by the right of the Sun and its burning heat. Jīm, I have made you my helper, and I adjure you by the Lord of servants, in whose hand is the command and the judgment, and there is no power nor strength except through God, the Sublime, the Magnificent. Ṭā, answer O Ṭahiyāʾīl, and do such and such by means of this letter.

Translation: "I have sought by the rank of Divine Sovereignty and by glory and greatness and pride, and by the One Unique Essence, the Great, the Ever-Subsisting, the Everlasting who does not die. Majesty manifested upon the mountain of Ṭūr with Its theophany and leveled it, and Moses fell unconscious. I call upon my desired beloved—besides Him I have no beloved—and He is Near and Accepting of supplication and Responsive. O letter of Jīm, by the goodness and glory and rejoicing that is within you, my rank is most great and noble. Accept by the right of the Sun and its heat; I have made you my helper, and I swear to you by the Lord of servants, in whose hand is command and judgment, and there is no virtue nor power except through God, the Great and Most High. O Ṭahiyāʾīl, appear and fulfill my command by this letter."

The letter quickly brings the client and fulfills the need, and its emergency invocation is:

May He turn with mercy toward the needy one who is present. Hallowed, Lord of the angels and the Spirit. Answer, O king Ṭahiyāʾīl, the One, the Swift—the Hour.

The Letter of Dhāl

Sorcery and delusion were perfected by God Most High along with this [letter]—the talisman of Arjaha.

The operation of Dhāl is successful for love:

Write it on a fresh board with the divine names such as Dāʾim and Diyān, etc., and write four Dhāls at the four corners of the board. Then the Exalted One will make each one of them [unclear/watermarked] and keep them with you. And among its secrets is this: if you wish to captivate someone with your love, write this letter and wash it with water, recite the divine names over it, and blow upon it. Then give it to him to eat—he will never forget you.
