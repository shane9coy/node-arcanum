# Page 399 (English translation)
397

Praise belongs to You, O the Bestower, O the Originator, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You cause the night to enter into the day and You cause the day to enter into the night, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Near, the Responsive, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Oft-Returning, the Bestower, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Lord of Lords, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Cause of causes, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Elevator of degrees, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Creator of the heavens and the earth, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Master of masters, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Succor of the seekers of help, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Resurrector, the Heir, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Creator, the Compeller, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Rightly Guided, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Patient, the Ancient, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Subduer, the Dominant, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the One who Possesses, the Unique, the Self-Sufficient, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Thankful, the Glorious, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Possessor, the Majestic, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Light, the Guide, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Judge, the Just, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Protector, the Mighty, the Compeller, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Supreme, and to You belongs the praise, my God. You are the Creator, the Maker.

The end of Surah Hashr: "But when you have decided, then rely upon God. Indeed God loves those who rely upon Him." The end of the verse: "And whoever relies upon God, then He is sufficient for him."

O God, incline toward me the hearts of Your servants among the children of Adam and the daughters of Eve, from every male and female, free and enslaved, young and old, with permanent love, affection, tenderness, and mercy. Bring their hearts to me, protect me from the evil they conceal and intend for me, and repel from me their cunning, their harm, and their evil. O God, by the sanctity of what I have recited, I ask You to show me Your sacred precinct by Your generosity, and to grant me the visitation of the grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may the blessings of God be upon him and his family, O He who answers prayers, O the Responsive to supplications, O God, O Lord of the worlds, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Peace, O Guardian, O Protector, O Mighty, O Compeller, O Supreme, O Provider, O Opener, O Knowing, O Expander, O Elevator, O Honorer, O Humiliator, O Hearing, O Seeing, O Subtle, O Forbearing, O High, O Great, O Thankful, O Preserver, O Sustainer, O Reckoner, O Majestic, O Generous, O Watcher, O Responsive, O Vast, O Hearing, O Gatherer, O Rich, O Enricher, O Everlasting, O Light of all things and its guidance. You are He who created the darkness with Your light. O Exalted above all things in His exaltedness. O God, I ask You, O God, to subjugate for me the servants of this noble surah, that they may be my helpers in all that I desire and seek. By its right upon You and its obedience to You, answer and respond. By the right of what is in it of mysteries, whoever falls short I will burn with fire. Hasten, hurry, the moment. And whoever does not answer the caller of God, then he is not beyond (God's) power on earth.

The end of the verse: "Answer and rely upon that which I have commanded you, by the right of this noble surah upon you and its sanctity before you. Hasten, hurry, the moment."
