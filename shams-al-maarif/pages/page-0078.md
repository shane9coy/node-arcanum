# Page 78 (English translation)
**The Ritual for Having One's Need Fulfilled:**

And whoever reads Bismillah with true intention and spiritual discipline for fourteen days, one thousand times daily—and in one tradition it is said that he should read it one thousand times after each prayer—he will see the spiritual angels and converse with them, and they will speak with him and will have dominion over everything he intends. And whoever separates its letters in this manner: *Mīm-Hā-Alif-Lām-Rā-Alif*, and whoever has a need, write his name and his mother's name, then go to him: whatever matter he has will be fulfilled.

**The Weight/Duress for the Return of One Who Has Fled:**

And if someone's servant or slave has run away, let him write Al-Raḥmān seven times along with that servant's or slave's name, bury it in his house, and place a heavy stone upon it. And let him recite this supplication:

*Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ḥaqqi bismillāhir-raḥmānir-raḥīm wa bi-ḥaqqi ismik al-raḥmān an tarudda hādhā l-'abda min al-ābāqi yā rabba l-'ālamīn*

Then that slave will come and will never run away again. And whoever writes Bismillah on a steel knife whose handle is also steel, and blows upon it three hundred and twenty-one times, then slaughters a chicken with it and cuts off its head and releases the headless chicken—when the headless chicken walks along the path, at that moment let him take up the head and bury it at the threshold of the house; then all insects and jinn will flee from it. And if he dips that chicken's head in olive oil and anoints his chest, his illness will be removed. And if a woman who is menstruating keeps it with her, it will benefit her.

**That Weapons Have No Effect in the Battlefield:**

And whoever writes Al-Raḥīm one hundred and ninety times on a banner and goes to the battlefield, no weapon will have any effect upon him.

**Relief from Headache; Obtaining Love:**

If one writes it twenty-one times on paper and binds it to the head or hand, headache will be relieved. If one writes this name on seven almonds with a brass needle and gives them to someone to eat, that person will love him greatly—but it should be written during the hour of Venus on Friday. And according to the numerological value of its letters, let him recite this name and blow upon the almonds.

**Treatment for Epilepsy:**

If on Monday at sunrise he writes *Mīm-Hā-Raḥmān* in this manner upon a mirror and looks at it frequently, he will be relieved from epilepsy. If he writes *Al-Raḥīm* in this manner on a silver ring weighing three and a half *māshas* and keeps it with him, Almighty God will grant him majesty and sweetness.
