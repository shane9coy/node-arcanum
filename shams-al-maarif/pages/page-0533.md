Chapter: On the Extraction of Letters, Among the Properties of Magic Squares

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Know that first the exposition of the extraction of letters is presented; thereafter, the complete exposition of the planets, the lunar mansions, the magic squares, and the properties of letters along with their servants and attendants will follow.

Know that the alif is the root of origin, and it is the same as the central point: "His command, when He intends a thing, is only that He says to it 'Be,' and it is" (Qur'an 36:82). From the number and the dot, the gaze turns toward the dot; the dot began and extended by the power of God, taking the form of the alif.

The alif holds the rank of the root of all letters:
Since from this root, the alif among the letters of the extended horizon exists, and all letters originated from it. Its detailed exposition will appear with the Mubashshir; rather, the exposition of the alif has been stated in the Ishārāt.

Know that the alif is the pattern of creation among all letters, and the exposition of its realities is very extensive. But to whoever is worthy of this science, we write here: The alif is the first letter and the first number, and it is the origin of all existing things, and this letter emerged from the presence of God, the Exalted, and it is the key of these divine words, and this is the reason why it is [designated for...].
