# Page 194 (English translation)
I ask You to grant me a good and blessed life, O Ever-Living—Your life by which life has expanded and flourished in every living being. O Ever-Living, grant me a good life in which nothing hateful ever befalls. O Self-Subsisting One, O You by whose overwhelming power the entire worlds stand, here I am before Your Self-Subsistence, upon the carpet of fear, draped in humility, covered by hope, cast upon my back in bearing sins and transgressions, trusting in my expectation [of Your mercy]. Verily You have said—and Your saying is truth—"Call upon Me and I will answer you." And I seek none other than You, and I hope for no one besides You, my Lord. None shall deliver me from what I am in except You, seeking response, relying upon the outward manifestation of sincerity from Your Self-Subsistence. O Dominant One, subdue whoever wishes to subdue me with a subjugation that prevents him from acting for himself, by Your grace upon me, O You whom slumber nor sleep does not overtake. Whoever intends harm against me, conceal me from him and deny him slumber and sleep, and constrict upon him the earth however vast it may be, so that not prosperity delights him but rather adversity harms him. And disgrace him with the worst of disgraces, for surely You, O Allah, Lord of the heavens and the earth and what is in them and what is between them, do not overlook what is hidden. And do not, O Allah, grant dominion over me to my enemies nor to whoever drives me away. Here I am, Your wronged servant, Your poor and weak servant. Judge, O Allah, and cast upon me the gentleness of Your covering as a veil through which I may enter with Your allies upon the carpet of Your sanctity and intimacy, O You beside whom none intercedes except by Your permission. I have sought intercession through the revelation upon the tongues of the prophets, peace be upon them, and through Gabriel from Your creation, that You may protect me from all hateful things, calamities, and harms. I ask You, my Lord, to grant me victory over whoever has wronged me, to defeat for me whoever confronts me, to subdue whoever fights me, to forsake my enemies and to close them wherever they gather, to curse them and expose them wherever they disperse, to cut them off wherever they connect, and to make them heard through words of contempt and tried with humiliation, and that from vengeance they receive no requital and are not spared, neither in secret nor in public, and that they gain no honor or glory, and are unable to achieve victory or patience. And send upon them a punishment from above them and from beneath their feet. He knows what is before them and what is behind them. O Knower of the hidden things, O Forgiver of the sins, O Eradicator of the faults when they are hidden, frustrate my weakness and conceal me, and grant me victory over my enemies as You granted victory to Your prophets over Your enemies, and make them retreat backward. And humiliate them with chains and shackles upon their necks. Seize their hearts so they do not understand, and seal their ears so they do not hear, and blind their eyes so they do not see, and seal their mouths so they do not speak. And enrage them at their place so they cannot proceed forward nor return to their families. Verily You are the Compeller and the Supreme, the Withholder and the Helper, the Strong and the Conqueror, the Subduer and the Exalter and the Humiliator, the Avenger and the Destroyer, the Severe and the Frustrator, the Delayer and the Preventer, the Withholder and the Abaser, the Bringer of harm and the Breaker. Possessor of majesty and honor. O Allah, I ask You by Your great and most great Name, and by the glory of Your noble Face, and by the right of these noble Names and lofty Names, that You protect me from before me and from behind me, and from above me and from beneath me, and from my right and from my left [damaged source / obscured by watermark]
