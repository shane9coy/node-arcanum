# Page 267 (English translation)
Chapter 7: Explanation of the Name "Al-Mūmin" of Allah the Exalted and its properties; all needs will be fulfilled

It should be known that this Name is very great in majesty and clear in proof. Whoever mentions this Name frequently, all their needs will be fulfilled and all prayers will be accepted. Whoever suffers from the affliction of obsessive thoughts (waswās), if they write its charm (mizāj) on a gold or silver tablet and keep it with them, their affliction will depart. Whoever mentions it frequently, their tongue will be protected from lying. If its charm is written when Jupiter is in its exaltation (mushrif) and kept with them, great acceptance will be obtained and many benefits will be bestowed. For one whose name is "Abd al-Mun'im," its dhikr is recommended. Its number is 136. It is outside the pattern of 7 degrees. This number is deficient. Its names (of letters) are 134, which point to the Name "Al-Ṣamad" of Allah the Exalted. The names of its letters are 399, which point to the Name "Al-Raḥmān" of Allah the Exalted. This is its form:

Chapter 8: Properties of the Name "Muhaymin" of Allah the Exalted; protection from the evil of rulers

This Name is among the comprehensive Names (asmā' jāmiʿa). Whoever constantly mentions it will be granted knowledge of the hidden (bāṭin) and its secrets. Allah the Exalted will deposit awe and dignity within his very essence. Whoever writes its charm's image when the Pleiad (thuriyā) is in the exaltation of Saturn and keeps it with them, and mentions the Name according to its numbers, will be protected from the evil of kings and rulers. Whoever persists in its dhikr, Allah the Exalted will make him aware of hidden perfections. This Name is among the encompassing Names (asmā' iḥāṭa). Only those know its worth upon whom the realities of the Names have been unveiled. Its number is 145. This number is [unclear] rectangular. All of this is from the hidden multiplication of the letters of ghāliba until it appears in its manifest until it appears in the soul's manifest. From this, encompassing became correct in it. This number is deficient, which points toward the return of the palm tree of Zahra (nakl zahra). The names of its letters are 33, which point toward the great luminous principle of Ahlah and Fāṭir. It is narrated from the Commander of the Faithful, Umar ibn al-Khaṭṭab, that someone asked him about the meaning of this Name. He delayed answering for a while, during which a Bedouin woman of eloquent speech came before him complaining of an ailment in her thigh, saying: "O Commander of the Faithful, I seek my right upon my thigh, and my muhaymin is present upon it; can you send some treatment by a conjurer?" At that time, Umar explained muhaymin in the sense of "witness." Its charm is in 8 degrees. It is from the hidden secrets, and with five it begins, with seventy it enters. The saying of Allah the Exalted is [unclear], that for it there is an individual of nature who overcomes individuals of the world of day and night and marriage for the world of expansion and beauty. This is its form:
