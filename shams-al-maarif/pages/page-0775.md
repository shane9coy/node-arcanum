# Page 775 (English translation)
My work, that I hope for Your beneficence and I have disobeyed, I return seeking Your forgiveness. O Allah, if You guide by Your mercy with which You began with the obedient until they complete their obedience, that You bestow it upon the sinners after their disobedience, for You are the Benevolent, the Generous, Owner of immense favor.

O Allah, O One who gives respite and does not neglect, and conceals until it is as if He has forgiven. You are the Rich and I am the poor in need of You, and You are the Mighty and I am the insignificant before You. O Allah, look upon us with the gaze of pleasure and join us from the register of the people of roughness and boasting to the register of the people of purity, and grant us good faith-keeping.

O Allah, we ask You by the right of Your beautiful names upon You and their virtue and their blessings are to You, and by the rank of whom You have chosen from Your creation and selected for Yourself and connected his name with Your name and brought him to Your holy presence and deposited in him the secrets of Your knowledge and made him the seal of Your prophets and messengers, and he is Your servant and beloved and intimate friend and saved one and close friend, our master Muhammad, upon him be peace and blessings. I ask You by his standing with You, by his sanctity with You, that You favor us with Your success to understanding Your knowledge and Your way.

O Allah, indeed You accepted the repentance from the late night worshippers when they remembered a single prostration and prostrated to You a single prostration, and we have not ceased affirming Your lordship and confessing Your oneness. We have never prostrated except before You, nor raised our needs except to You. O Allah, bestow upon us from Your generosity and have mercy upon us with Your mercy and shelter us with Your gentleness and treat us with Your judgment and grant us success in Your service, and forgive us and our parents and all the Muslims, by the rank of our master Muhammad, upon him be peace and blessings, and his companions and followers, and by them are the lights of hearts and the keys of the unseen, the companions of subtleties, and make for us from Your presence a helper.

O Allah, protect upon us our knowledge and our deeds. O Allah, grant us good turning to You and attentive listening to You and understanding from You and insight in Your command and remaining in Your obedience and persistence in Your will and hastening to Your service and good conduct in dealing with You and submission to You and contentment with Your favor.

How have I succeeded with You in prayer with one who disobeys You in Your privacy? Were it not for Your forbearance, how could one who forgets You at moments of desire call upon You? Were it not for Your favor, how could eyes sleep when on every night You say "Is there any repenting, is there any seeking forgiveness, is there any asker?" and I did not give him his request. How can one be cut off from You when You have not cut off from him these messages? How can the eternal be sold for the fleeting? The past days are few.

O Allah, O beloved of every stranger, O companion of every grieved one. Is there any one who turns to You that You have not sufficed him? Or is there any seeker whom You have not given hope in Your mercy? Or who is he who reached You without You making for him a connection to the cord of Your remembrance, and You did not grant him companionship? Or any caller who called upon You whom You did not answer? And it is narrated from You that You said "And I decreed upon no one a decree [unclear]"
