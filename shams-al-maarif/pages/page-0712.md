# Page 712 (English translation)
I advance, O You who have no limit that may be known, and He is the most knowing. I ask You by the right of Your great, most great name, and by the light of Your noble, most generous face, and by what the Pen has inscribed, and by what You redeemed with it the sacrificed one Ishmael so he was saved, and by what You saved with it Yunus in the belly of the whale, and in the darknesses of its innards, so he glorified and sanctified, and indeed he returned and said, "There is no god but You; glory be to You, truly I was among the wrongdoers." I ask You by what You raised with it Idris, and by what You saved with it Noah from drowning, and by what You spoke with it to Moses and saved him from Pharaoh, and by what You saved with it Abraham, Your friend, and by all, by blessing, I ask You, the Living, the Sustaining, and by what You showed gentleness to Isa, and by what You chose with it Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and You answered their supplication and their request in it. By Your name, the Living, the Sustaining, I ask You that my requests succeed, and that You subjugate for me the angel and the angelic realm, and that You cause the clouds of Your hidden grace to flow according to my wish, and I accomplish my needs by Your name, the Living, by which You saved whoever You saved, and destroyed with it whoever You destroyed. There is no god but You; glory be to You, truly I was among the wrongdoers. O Living One, O Sustaining One, I ask You to make my heart alive with the light of Your knowledge forever, and grant me success in Your obedience eternally, and multiply for us our sustenance and bless us in it, and show gentleness to us wherever You have decreed it over us, O Living One, O Sustaining One, O Most Merciful of the merciful. Peace be upon you, a saying from a Lord, Most Merciful. O Huwa, O Latif, O Wadud, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. Ṭa.

By the mention of these names, desires are fulfilled and the subordinate spirits become obedient.
The mention of these two names is [to be made] every day, for the sake of everything, so that desires are fulfilled quickly and success is achieved with complete joy.
By this mention, all spirits and subordinate beings become obedient, and all desires and perfection are attained.

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wājid"]

Wājid is He than whom no thing can escape the power of existence; therefore it is a necessary divine attribute. Accordingly, God—exalted be He—is alone, and is One, and Wājid absolutely. There is no one besides Him. Whoever wishes to mention this [name] must understand that Allah—exalted be He—alone gave existence to things from non-existence. Let him mention [this name] in its retreat according to its number, and let him also say "Hayy" or "Hayy, Hayy" along with it. Its attached angel is Iṭā'īl. He will appear to the reciter in dream or waking and will guide him to the realities of existence.
By his mention, veils are removed,
and he will remove the veil. His dhikr is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Ṭa. O Allah, O Wājid, You are the One who found every apparent and hidden thing in the treasuries of Your unseen, by every glorious power, and from the secret of existence in the hidden secret. And by the command in everything, and You brought forth from the kaf and nun. I ask You to bring forth things from non-existence to existence, without incapacity for creating anything. And O Mūjid, O Mūjid, O Living One, O Sustaining One, O Possessor of Majesty and Honor. Ṭa.
