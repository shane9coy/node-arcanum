# Page 159 (English translation)
kā-hā-yā-ʿayn-ṣād ṭā-hā ḥā-mīm ʿayn-sīn-qāf ḥasbunā allāhu wa-niʿma al-wakīlu asʾaluka bihā wa-bi-l-āyāti kullihā wa-bi-l-asmāʾi kullihā wa-bi-l-ismi al-ʿaẓīmi minhā yā man lam yalid wa-lam yūlad wa-lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥad an tuṣalliya wa-tu-sallima ʿalā sayyidinā muḥammadin wa-ālihi wa-ṣaḥbihi ṣallā allāhu ʿalayhi wa-sallama

"O Allah, I ask You, for all praise belongs to You alone; there is no god but You, O Compassionate, O Munificent, O Creator of the heavens and earth, O Possessor of glory and majesty, O Ever-living, O Sustainer, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Allah, O He who calls me, O Best of heirs, O Forgiving, O Near, O Hearer, O Knower, there is no god but You; Your sacred essence is pure. Verily I am among the wrongdoers. O Most Merciful of the merciful, O Hearer of supplications, O our Lord, O our Lord, I ask You by Your name. You are the One besides whom there is no god, and You are the Lord of the Mighty Throne. Allah is sufficient for us and He is the best disposer of affairs. I ask with all the verses and all the names, and I ask by the Greatest Name, O He who has no offspring, was not born, and has no equal. And that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad, upon his family, and upon his companions, may Allah bless him and grant him peace."

And whatever need you ask for shall be fulfilled by the command of Allah the Most High.

The Shaykh Abu ʿAbdallāh Muḥammad ibn Ismāʿīl al-Sulmī, may Allah the Most High have mercy upon him—who was one of the great masters possessing karamāt (miraculous gifts) and among the greatest of the Gnostics—says: "In a state of contemplative meditation (mujāhada), I once saw a luminous form whose face was that of the "Head of the Eye" (rās al-ʿayn), and within its interior was the Name of Allah, and the names containing letters revolved around it. When that form became firmly established in my mind, I transferred it to paper, and I thought to myself: 'I shall extract fifteen names from it.' I began to write them down, and indeed these nineteen names issued forth from this glorious Name. Its benefits are exceedingly magnificent. Whoever verifies them shall examine wonderful mysteries, and the marvels of the upper and lower worlds shall be revealed to him. Whatever he asks from Allah the Most High shall be granted to him. Whoever has a religious or worldly need should, at the midnight hour, perform two cycles of prayer and then engage in the dhikr of these names."

16,730 times, with utmost presence, in an empty place, with humility and submission, and facing the qiblah at least seventeen times, then whatever need exists, Allah the Most High shall grant it acceptance and it shall be fulfilled. Particularly, if someone desires religious knowledge or yearns for the revelation of luminous secrets, Allah the Most High shall soon open the way for him. The divine sciences and luminous knowledges that are revealed to the great masters shall be revealed to him, and these stations are attained by the victorious. Whoever looks upon them fourteen times every day and makes dhikr of Allah the Most High, Allah the Most High shall inform him of the mysteries of knowledge and hidden subtleties. Divine sciences and sacred mysteries shall be granted to him. Allah the Most High shall cause the lights of divine grace to flow from his heart and shall keep him safe from harm in all his movements. He shall place the crown of awe and majesty upon his head. Whoever writes them and places them within anything during travel or residence, that thing shall remain safe from all calamities. If he binds it upon his right arm, he shall be safe from the tricks of enemies. If he goes before tyrannical kings, awe of him shall descend upon them; they shall not raise their voices before him. And this is a practice for subduing kings. This is from the glorious mysteries of Allah the Most High.
