# Page 610 (English translation)
Enter into seclusion and make your request; it will be fulfilled, if God the Exalted wills.
Letter Khā'
This letter is cold and watery in temperament. When you write it on the palm of your hand with the izmār and rub it with the ashes of the sun, and sprinkle where people gather, they will disperse.
For closing a business
When you write this on a sheet of lead and bury it in a place, buying and selling will cease there. And when you write it on your own palm and recite the izmār and close your fist.
For frightening someone
If you wish to frighten someone, go before him and open your fist in front of him at the appropriate moment—he will be frightened. His ta'wīdh is this.

The invocation (da'wat) of this letter is:
In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, deliver me from every worry of the world and its torment, swiftly to the good things. O Hidden One, You are the Hidden, O Knower of the hidden matter, and He knows the hidden matter and He knows it. I ask You, O Knowing One, by what is in the consciences, to turn me toward happiness and guide me in my affairs, O Knowing One. I ask You to clothe me in light by which I may testify to the secret of the Khā', until I may fulfill my need, O Knowing One. Come, come, hasten, hasten, O Khā', by the Creator of the Khā', Gayūm, I ask You to send me the servant of the letter Khā' who may inform me among Your creation, O You who know the可见的和隐藏的 [the visible and the hidden]. There is no god but He; to Him belong the most beautiful names. And by the letter lām: There is no power nor strength except through God, the Most High, the Most Great.

Its izmār is this:
Execute by the truth of Hū, Tā, Hā, 'Abd, al-Awwal, Karkū, Kas, Kh Kh Kh, J 2, Khafaj, Yā, Hā, Yā, Mūh, al-Awhā, al-'Ijl, al-Sā'a.

Its total effect is this.
Letter Dāl

This letter is cold in temperament. When you have attained mastery in the meaning of speech, write this letter with the desired outcome on a piece of white silk and write the name of its mother upon it, while you are in this gathering and its purpose is ready. Light it in your lamp and recite the izmār; the desired outcome will be present.
