Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Baqi" (The Eternal/Permanent)

Baqi is that whose existence is never cut off, and He alone is the Necessary Existent by His Essence. However, when this is attributed to time, it would be extremely far-fetched due to probability. Thus, the name Baqi should be used, and when attributed to the past, the name Qadim (Ancient) should be used. Baqi is that whose decree of existence has never occurred in the past, and this same is called Azali (Pre-eternal) and Abadi (Eternal). The Necessary Existent by His Essence encompasses all these things, and these various names are due to His attribution to His past, future, and presence, because all these—past and so on—are expressions of time, and they enter into change and motion, because by His Essence He is divided into past, present, and future, and He enters into the humble sphere of time due to change.

That which is not humble has no relation to past, present, or future time. That which is free from change does not enter into time; there will be neither past nor future in it, nor will the expressions of past and future be appropriate in it, and in it there are such matters that necessarily exist within time, which come into being little by little according to distance. Thus, that which has passed in time is called the past, and that whose occurrence is awaited is the future, and that interval before the creation of pleasure is non-existent, and after its creation it remains Baqi.

There is great benefit for the spiritual traveler in this name; rather, the traveler should know that I am the Ever-Abiding. When spirits crowd in solitude, he should recite this name combined with the name of Jabbar. [illegible/faint text at the very bottom]
