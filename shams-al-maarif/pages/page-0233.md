# Page 233 (English translation)
231

Send prayers and blessings upon the companions.

[Boxed Title: The Honored Circle and Its Great Secrets]

Making the Absent Person Appear:

This honored circle is called the Circle of Lights (Dāʾirat al-Anwār); it has wondrous secrets. Behold it with the eye of insight. When you wish to summon someone who has disappeared, write this circle on paper. Write the name of the one you wish to summon and the name of his mother. Then place this circle against the eastern wall and insert the needle at the letter Alif. Recite the Azīmat seven times. Fumigate with frankincense, saffron, black seed, musk, sandalwood, and labanī incense. If the desired outcome is not achieved and delay occurs, then insert the needle at the letter Bāʾ and transfer it from one letter to another—that is, do this with all the letters in this manner. During this time, also fumigate with perfume. Continue reciting the Azīmat until among these letters, at the time of that letter, its attendant (khādim) will appear. Always summon this operator with that same letter. This operation can be performed at any time. If the desired person is a traveler, then in the same manner insert the needle at each letter, recite the Azīmat seven times, and write the distance of the journey—indeed, he will appear. After each recitation of the Azīmat, recite the Names (Asmāʾ) or Malakū or Qayyūm 260 times. By the will of Allāh the Exalted, your purpose will be achieved.

This is the form of the circle:

[Circular talisman/diagram titled "Dāʾirat Sharīfah Dāʾirat al-Anwār". It consists of concentric rings divided into 28 radial segments, each containing an Arabic letter of the alphabet and associated names or formulas. The central circle contains several lines of Arabic script for a specific petition. The central text includes placeholders for names like "Fulān bin Fulānah" (so-and-so son of so-and-so) and magical commands such as "Aḥriqū qalb" (Burn the heart...), "Al-Wāḥā al-Wāḥā al-ʿAjl al-ʿAjl al-Sāʿah al-Sāʿah".]

The Azīmat is as follows:

Bi-smi Llāhi al-Quddūsi al-Muqādiri al-ʿAliyyi salfaʿu huwa al-Qāhiru rabbu hayṭulūʿi jazʾun rabbi
ʿalayhimu al-dāʾiratu wa-al-zamāni ʿaqbārun armāṭun wa-al-zamāni alladhī lā yaḥūlu mulkuhu wa-lā yazūlu ṣāḥibu
al-ʿizzi al-shāmiḥi wa-al-jalāli al-bādhikhi wa-bi-ismihi daʿawtukum yā dāra al-arwāḥi al-rūḥāniyyati al-mulaqisiyyīna ʿalā
ṭabāʾiʿi hādhihi al-ḥurūfi an tawakkalū fīmā amartukum min jalbi fulānin bin fulānata ilā fulānin bin fulānata bi-ḥaqqi
