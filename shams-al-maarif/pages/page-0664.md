# Page 664 (English translation)
By reciting this, innate knowledge and hidden treasures have been obtained:

There is a story that a fortunate man was of slow intellect. In seclusion he began to recite this name; God Most High revealed upon him innate knowledge, and in a dream the guardian taught him all the sciences. Whoever recites this name shall not be in need of any creature, and hidden treasures shall be his.

A servant of God in Jerusalem asked God for bread and for wheat-flour pudding (aṣīda ṭīda), and said: "If You do not give it, I shall break the lamps of Your house."

One day I was sitting in Jerusalem when I saw a man standing who began to say: "By Your honor and glory, if You do not feed me bread and aṣīda at this moment, beware! I shall break the lamps of Your house." When I saw him, I said in my heart: "This man is mad." Then that man fell asleep, and suddenly I saw another man coming with bread and aṣīda in his hand, who woke the sleeper and began to eat bread with him. I ran after him and said: "Who are you and why have you come?" He said: "I was sitting at home eating bread when the Calling Voice summoned me: 'Go to the mosque; there one of our friends is sleeping—wake him and eat bread with him.'" I said: "By God Most High, my forgiveness has come, since the Messenger of God ﷺ said: 'Whoever fed bread with a friend, his forgiveness shall come.'" Then I remained in that man's company for a long time; I came there but did not find him.

When a servant with sincere heart obtains [something] through the remembrance of this name, he shall see all the world serving him, and whoever performs this remembrance abundantly, the Creator shall open for him the doors of His mercy. The recitation of this name is with its number [unclear], and when a person recites it fully and keeps it with him, the divine theophanies shall descend upon him abundantly. If a slow-witted person writes it, drinks it, he shall be granted intellect and understanding. Its talisman is this:
