# Page 628 (English translation)
Seven shall come and acknowledge submission:

At this time seven persons will come to you and greet you. These seven are servants of the Mala'ikah 'Alawiyyah (the Elevated Angels), and they will say to you: "O Angel of Fortune, we are your servants. Whatever you command, we shall fulfill with heart and soul." It is therefore obligatory upon you to assign each of these seven to a specific day in rotation, and to employ the one whose turn falls on that day. The names to be recited during the khalwah (spiritual retreat) are as follows:

Bismillahi al-Rahmani al-Rahim. Ta'allahumma inni as'aluka bima yasta'iluka bihi Jirayl 'inda 'arshika al-'azhim an tusakhkhira li mala'ikatika al-kiram khuddam hathihi al-asma'. Ta'allahumma sakhkhir li Kasfiyayil wa Dardiyayil wa Sarfiyayil wa Shamkhiyayil wa Tawtiyayil wa Rawfiyayil wa Samkhiyayil wa Tafiyayil wa Jibra'il wa Mika'il wa Isma'il wa Srufiyayil. Ijibu ayyuha al-muluk wa al-ru'asa' wa ijibuni 'ala qada' hawa'iji bi-haqqi ma ta'lamuna min 'azim sirr Allah wa bi-haqqi hatha al-ism al-'azim al-a'zam Allah Allah Allah bi-'ilmika wa qudratika 'ala al-khalaiq wa bi-ismika al-'azim al-kabir al-muta'al Allah Allah Allah al-ism alladhi faddaltahu 'ala sa'ir al-asma'. As'aluka an tusakhkhira li hathihi al-arwah an tatiyani fi nawmi aw yaqazati innaka 'ala kull shay'in qadir. Ta Ya Allah.

After each name, mention the comprehensive name of each angel three times. When you wish to obtain proximity to God through this [method], recite "Al-Khabir" (the All-Aware) sixty-six times after each prayer. If you wish to recite it according to the conditions of the khalwah, multiply these numbers together and recite [the result], that is, 4,356 times. When this khalwah is completed, its associated spirit will appear and perform obedience for you.

**The Hebrew Khalwah:**

The second khalwah is to recite this name sixty-six times after each prayer for sixty-six days. If you fast while reciting it, that is even better. This khalwah is called "The Hebrew Khalwah." Its spirit will appear before you, and it is the commander of sixty-six ranks of spirits. The seal, that is, the name-square, is as follows:

Another method is to engrave this seal on a golden ring on Sunday, write the spirit's name around it, enter the khalwah, and recite the name 4,356 times according to your need. The angel Kafhiyal (Kafhiiyail), who is the spirit of this name, will prostrate before the Divine Court with the tasbih (prayer beads) placed upon his head, and in prostration he will speak as follows: "251 El 2 Elohim 2 Anta ta'lam" (You know). God Almighty will say: "Fulfill the need of My servant." Then this spirit will appear before him.

**Whoever ruler sees the operation completed with anger and vengeance shall perish**

This person, during the recitation of the name, will fall wrapped in his clothes, becoming unconscious and oblivious. If that person gazes at any ruler with anger, the ruler will immediately perish, and at that moment the operator's heart will say "Be present."
