# Page 543 (English translation)
The quantity of hours:
The quantity of hours is calculated according to day and night, meaning the sky has 360 degrees, and they are called degrees because the twelve signs are divided into thirty each.

Determination of degrees from the Holy Quran:
The scholars have derived this from the Holy Quran, as He said: "Rafee' al-Darajat" (Exalted in Degrees), the number of Rafee' is 360, and Darajat is the plural of degree, and when we combine both names we get 360, because this science has a special connection to the heavens. And when we added the divine letter in Tawi, this will be what we subtracted from its number of 360. 51 subtracted leaves 309. Thus the lower should be increased upon the higher. Similarly in Tawi there is a transmutation. Its number is 41, increased, and everywhere decreased. This too is increased. Then these are all the rules upon which operation is performed, and they are verified and tested. If the seeker wishes to be bound by the constraint of destiny, then it is necessary to perform Jafr Dani, and if they wish to reach the high ranks of free will, then let them perform whichever system they wish. When some number emerges that we wish to divide, and this situation often arises in arranging the conjunction, for example 100 emerges, whose letter is Qaf, and we wish to divide this letter by three. So without extraction, place it on the tongue, and similarly when the number 300 emerges, whose letter is Shin, either in the lower or in Tawi, divide it by five. I cannot explain this rule more than this, and this is the same rule applied to all the letters of the number language, such as Zal and Dha and Ghayn and Tha. The number of the letter Zal is 700. When we divide it by four or five or seven for the extraction of Tawi, the extraction for four letters would be thus: Fiya Nil, and if divided by seven, it becomes Dhiya Nil, and similarly measure the remainder.

Methods of extracting divine names:
There are various methods for extracting divine names. Among them is this one: take the extracted original number, and from among the divine names, whichever name has the same number as the extraction should be taken. And this name is the Greatest Name for this operation.

Second method: The second method is this: take the first letter from the name of the questioner, and see which divine name has this letter at its beginning. Then take that name, for example: the letter Alif is the first letter in the questioner's name, and from the divine names, the name of Allah has the first letter Alif. So this name is taken, and if the first letter is Ya, then the name is Latif for Allah. Thus the operation is completed in this manner.

And know that each letter has its own realms, which no one attains except the person whom God has granted a share. And when the realms are revealed to you, at that time when you gather the letters and conceal them, the officer will immediately appear before you and fulfill your need, and will remain occupied in prayer and seeking forgiveness for you until the Day of Resurrection.

A strange and tested method of bringing the beloved:
Take the name of the desired one and its number, and see if they correspond to any divine name. When the name emerges, make the officer of that name, and recite in the name of the Exalted, and command the questioner with the divine name for bringing the desired one; the desired one will appear.
