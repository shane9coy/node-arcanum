Obtaining Glory and Knowledge of Hidden Secrets:
The Name Al-Muʿizz contains two letters from the Great Name of God (Ism Aʿẓam), and its secrets are revealed only to one who understands and invokes them. All the properties of this Name are manifested through the muwakkal (spiritual deputy); when one performs spiritual service, all secrets become unveiled. Know that between the Beautiful Names and the letters of beauty, there is a relationship like that of body and soul—that is, when the muwakkal of a Name becomes known, its meanings also become clear. The name of its muwakkal is Raghbāʾīl. It rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 117 ranks, and in each rank there are 17 assigned muwakkals. If a muwakkal speaks openly with the dhākir (one who remembers/cites God) and shows him the path of guidance, that person becomes fortunate in both this world and the afterlife. The supplication for the Name Al-Muʿizz is this:

O Muʿizz, You are the one who has honored Your allies and made Your prophets capable of bearing Your trials and Your blessings, and You have subdued things by the sovereignty of Your power and by Your procurement. I ask You by Your noble and precious honor, and by Your great and capable generosity, and by Your right upon Your creation, both the great and the small, that You make me honorable among creatures through independence from them and through turning to You. O Allah, make me honored at the gate of truth through steadfastness and presence, so that I may be near You, and expand my honor in the hearts of the people of faith, so that I may attain Your compassion at the emergence of the proof and the evidence. O Hannān, O Mannān, O Lord of the worlds.

Obtaining Glory and Humility of the Rebellious:
The Name Al-Mudhill is the opposite of Al-Muʿizz. Between these two is the Greater Heart—the first gives honor and the second gives humiliation. It has great effect for the oppressed and the weak. One who recites the formula of Al-Mudhill and performs the seal of the lion (mihr sīnghar)¹ or says: "Muḍhill aḍilla man ẓalamani"—O Humiliator, humiliate the person who has wronged me—Allah the Exalted will humiliate him. The name of its muwakkal is Abāʾīl. It is a very great angel. Under it are four officers, each of whom oversees 770 ranks, and in each rank there are 770 muwakkals—all of them are powerful and severe. They are all under the command of Israel (Isrāʾīl). Their task is to humiliate the rebellious. When the dhākir mentions the Name Al-Mudhill, the muwakkal comes and enriches him, and he is granted boundless joy and delight. The supplication for the Name Al-Mudhill is this:

O Allah, You are the Humiliator of tyrants, the Witness, the Severe, the Painful, the One, the Great, the Dominant, the Exalted above all bodies and spirits, the one purified from companions and children, the creator of the compulsion of enemies and the subjugation of proud tyrants. O Allah, You are the best of schemers. I ask You by Your Name before which foreheads have bowed, and by which You brought down fortifications, and by which You cast terror into the hearts of the wealthy, and by which You destroyed the people of harshness. I ask You to send down to me a grain's weight of the subtleties of this Name, which flow through all my organs, both total and partial, until I am able to do what I will with whom I will.

¹ mihr sīnghar: a magical seal/configuration; literally "seal of the lion"
