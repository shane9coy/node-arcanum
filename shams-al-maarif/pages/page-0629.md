And when you wish to love someone or want to silence someone's tongue, then after reciting this name, say these words:

أَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا السَّيِّدُ كَهْيَائِيلُ إِلَّا مَا أَمَرْتَ أَحَدَ قُوَّادِكَ بِمُجِيئِهِ وَيَفْعَلُ كَذَا وَكَذَا

Translation: "O master Kayyāʾīl, I adjure you to order one of your commanders to come to me and do such and such a task for me."

Whoever's name has a numerical value corresponding to the numerical value of this glorious name, when he performs the remembrance according to its number, his desire will be fulfilled. The remembrance of this name is:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ ه اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ بِحَقِّ إِسْمِكَ يَا اَللَّهُ ٣ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ أَحْيِنِيْ حَيَاةً طَيِّبَةً أَعِيشُ بِهَا عَلَىٰ خَاطِي وَ بَحْرِ مَحَبَّتِكَ وَ أَلْبِسْ مَهَابَةً عِنْدَ الْغَوَالِي الْعُلْوِيَّةِ وَافْتَحْ عَيْنَ قَلْبِي وَ بَصَرِي بِنُوْرِكَ حَتَّىٰ يَنْفَتِحَ قَلْبِي لِتَلَقِّى الْأَسْرَارِ وَ تَنْطِقَ بِمَكْنُوْنِ جَوَاهِرِ رِقَائِقِكَ وَ أَفِضْ عَلَيَّ مِنْ بَحْرِ فَيْضِكَ الْأَقْدَسِ وَ سَهِّلْهُ عَلَيَّ حَتَّىٰ أَصِلَ إِلَىٰ سَاحِلِ اللُّطْفِ وَ غُدِّيْنِ أَغْدِيَةً لَطِيْفَةً أَجِدُ حَلَاوَتَهَا أَيَّامَ لِقَائِكَ يَا لَطِيْفُ ٣ اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ بِتَفَرُّعِ نَسِيْمِ نَفَحَاتِ أَسْرَارِكَ وَ كَشْفِ سِرِّ اسْمِكَ الَّذِي أَلْقَيْتَهُ لِتَلَقِّي غَطَشَ أَكْبَادِ رَوَاِي خَوْضِ بِرِّكَ وَ قَاصِدِي مَنْهَلِ سِرِّكَ يَا مَنْ لَهُ الْإِسْمُ الْأَعْظَمُ وَ هُوَ أَعْظَمُ يَا مَنْ لَا لَهُ حَدٌّ يَعْلَمُ وَ هُوَ أَعْلَمُ يَا قَدِيْمُ أَسْأَلُكَ بِسِرِّ إِسْمِكَ وَ بِمَا جَرَى بِهِ قَلَمُكَ وَ بِمَا أَلْهَمْتَ بِهِ عِيْسَىٰ وَ بِمَا نَاجَيْتَ بِهِ مُوْسَىٰ عَلَىٰ طُوْرِ سَيْنَاءَ وَ نَادَيْتَ بِلِسَانِ الْقُدْرَةِ أَنَا اللَّهُ بَلْ ٢ اَلُوهِيْمُ وَ بِحَقِّ مَا أَنْزَلْتَهُ عَلَىٰ نَبِيِّكَ مُحَمَّدٍ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ عَجِّلْ بِنُجْحِ مَطَالِبِي وَ تَسْهِيْلِ مَا رَيْنُ وَاكْشِفْ لِي عَنْ عَالَمِ الْمُلْكِ وَ الْمَلَكُوْتِ وَ أَجْرِ مُرَادِي فِيْمَا يُرْضِيْكَ مِنَ الْقَضَاءِ وَ اكْشِفْ لِي عَنْ أَرْوَاحِ الْمَلَكُوْتِيَّاتِ الْمُسْتَمِرَّةِ مِنْ سِرِّ إِسْمِكَ الْجَامِعِ لِلْأَسْمَاءِ وَ الصِّفَاتِ الَّذِيْ تَسَمَّيْتَ بِهِ فِي كُلِّ اللُّغَاتِ وَ سَبَّحَتْ لَكَ الْمَخْلُوْقَاتُ يَا اَللَّهُ ٣ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ يَا نِعْمَ الْمَوْلَىٰ وَ نِعْمَ النَّصِيْرُ يَا اَللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ أَنْ تُسَخِّرَ لِي خَادِمَ هَذَا الْإِسْمِ كَهْيَائِيلَ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by the right of Your name, O Allah. 3 O Living, O Self-Subsisting, grant me a good life by which I may live in Your pleasure and the sea of Your love, and grant me majesty among the supernal nobles. Open the eye of my heart and my sight with Your light until my heart opens to receive the secrets and speaks the hidden jewels of Your subtle essences. Pour forth upon me from the sea of Your most holy effusion and make it easy for me until I reach the shore of gentleness. Provide me with delicate provisions whose sweetness I shall find in the days of my meeting with You, O Subtle. 3 O Allah, I ask You by the branching of the breeze of the breath of Your secrets and the unveiling of the mystery of Your name that You cast to receive the darkness of the livers of those who wade in Your grace, and my quest is the spring of Your mystery, O He to whom belongs the Most Great Name, and He is the greatest, O He who has no limit to knowledge and He knows best, O Ancient, I ask You by the mystery of Your name, and by what Your pen has written, and by what You inspired in ʿĪsā (Jesus), and by what You conversed with Mūsā (Moses) upon Mount Sinai, and You called with the tongue of power: "I am Allah," but 2 Ĕlōhīm, and by the right of what You sent down upon Your prophet Muhammad, upon him be peace, hasten the success of my requests and the facilitation of what is difficult for me. Reveal to me the world of Dominion and the Celestial Kingdom, and carry out my desire in what pleases You of the decree. And reveal to me the eternal celestial spirits from the mystery of Your comprehensive name for the names and attributes that You have called Yourself by in every language, and the creatures have glorified to You, O Allah. 3 O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O best of masters and best of helpers. O Allah, I ask You to subjugate for me the servant of this name, Kayyāʾīl. Indeed, You are over all things powerful.

Through this, knowledge, dignity, sustenance, and honor increase.

Whoever persists in this remembrance, Almighty Allah will increase him in dignity and in his knowledge [unclear/handwritten addition: He will increase] and in [damaged source] He will grant blessings in his sustenance. And whoever writes it and keeps it with himself, it will be a refuge for him from every affliction and calamity.
