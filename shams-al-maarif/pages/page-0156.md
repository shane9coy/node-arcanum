"Answer me and fulfill my need and grant my wish and what I have hoped for with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy."

"O Allah, You have made me self-sufficient through Your knowledge. Surely, when anyone from among the Arabs and non-Arabs seeks refuge, they grant him refuge, and the Lord of Arabs and non-Arabs is One. I have sought refuge with You, so grant me refuge and do not leave me disappointed. Indeed, I hope for acceptance from You, so grant me priority and fulfill my need. Grant my desire and request through Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy."

May Allah the Exalted accept your supplication through His grace. Make your intention good and seek only what is lawful.

[Boxed Heading: Supplication of the Greatest Name]

Benefit: Some say that in this supplication there is the Greatest Name. Recite the supplication thus:

"O Allah, dissolve this knot and remove this difficulty, inspire me with good ease, protect me from evil decree, grant me a good heart, and protect me from an evil end. O Allah, my argument is my need, my provision is my poverty, my means is the severance of my stratagems, my companion is my tears, and my capital is my inability to help myself, and my treasure is my helplessness. O Allah, a drop from the seas of Your generosity suffices me, and a particle from the current of Your pardon suffices me. So grant me sustenance and have mercy on me, grant me well-being and pardon me, fulfill my need and relieve my distress and remove my sorrow and grief through Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad, upon his family, his companions, and grant them abundant peace."

Translation: "O Allah, dissolve this knot and remove this difficulty. Inspire me with good ease, protect me from evil decree. Grant me a good heart, protect me from an evil destination. O Allah, my repetition is my destitution, and my means is the severance of stratagem. My tears are my torment. My wealth is my lack of resource. My treasure is my helplessness. O Allah, a drop from Your seas of generosity will make me self-sufficient, a thread from Your treasury of pardon is enough for me. So grant me sustenance and pardon me, fulfill my need, remove my distress, open my sorrow and grief through Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And may blessings and abundant peace be upon His Eminence our master Muhammad, upon his family, and upon his companions."

[Boxed Heading: The Established Supplication for Deliverance from Sorrow and Distress]

His Eminence Ibn Mas'ud, may Allah be pleased with him, narrates that the Messenger, upon him be peace and blessings, said: "Whoever is afflicted with sorrow and grief should recite this supplication. Allah the Exalted will remove his sorrow and grief and give him the same in joy." The supplication is:

"O Allah, I am Your servant and the son of Your servant and the son of Your female servant. My forelock is in Your hand. Your judgment of me is just, and Your decree of me is executed. I ask You by every name that belongs to You, by which You have named Yourself, or revealed in Your Book, or taught to any of Your creation, or kept exclusively in Your knowledge of the unseen with You, to make the Noble Quran the spring of my heart, the light of my sight, the illumination of my chest, the remover of my sorrow, the dispeller of my grief, and the companion of my Paradise."
