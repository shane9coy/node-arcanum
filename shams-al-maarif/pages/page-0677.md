Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Mu'izz Al-Mudhill" (The Honorer and The Humiliator)

Honor and humiliation are in the hand of God:
In truth, the true and knowing reality of honor and humiliation is He. He grants whoever He wills kingdom and sovereignty, and takes away from whoever He wills. He gives honor to whoever He wills and gives humiliation to whoever He wills. Kingdom and sovereignty belong to the one who has freed himself from the humiliation of base desires by dominating passion, and who has removed arrogance from his heart. Such a person will obtain contentment and will witness the presence, until he becomes independent of all creation.

Self-knowledge leads to knowledge of the Lord:
And it will be in accordance with this saying of the Noble Prophet, may blessings and peace be upon him:
"He who knows himself has known his Lord."
"He who recognizes his own soul has recognized his Lord." And God grants him kingdom and informs him: "O tranquil soul, return to your Lord. He is pleased with you and you are pleased with Him." And the person who, at the time of being blamed by creation, extends his hand toward creatures—God Almighty will impose upon him greed, until this person will not be content with the amount of subsistence, and he will attain the state of being led gradually to destruction, until his soul becomes base and he falls into the darkness of ignorance. This is indeed God's perfection—He does as He wills.

The believers receive nearness and pleasure; the disbelievers receive rejection:
With nearness and pleasure there is honor for the believers, and there are pushes and distance for the humiliated. With distance, there is humiliation for the disbelievers. God Most High has given honor to the prophets of the world together with knowledge, and to the martyrs together with elevated ranks, and He has humiliated the polytheists by keeping them far from His friends.

This is a special property of the Name of Glory (Al-'Aziz): that any person who recites this name forty-one times every morning, having performed ablution, will breathe upon himself, and there will be no fear of the oppressors for him. God Almighty will help and assist him, and no person in the world will be able to oppress him. The sweetness of its recitation is against his enemies, and oppression will not be his companion.
