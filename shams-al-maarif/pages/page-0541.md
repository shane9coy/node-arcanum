**The Allocator of the People of Heights (Ahl al-ʿUlwī) is the Client (Muwaqqil):**

This is the Allocator (muwaqqil) of the People of Heights. Analogize for others similarly.

First, collect the letters of the ascendant (ṭāliʿ), the auspicious hour (sāʿa al-saʿīda), and the day. Then give them binary arrangement, the method of which with the cube (kaʿba) is as follows: expand the original letters, then derive their numerical values and multiply them; whatever results from the multiplication is the cube. However, there are differing opinions among scholars on this matter—for example, one opinion states: take the number, convert it to its corresponding letter, and append the word "ayyi" at the end. The Client will appear standing before you.

Know that in the invisible realm (ʿālam al-ghayb), no name has manifested without having a body in the visible world (ʿālam al-shahāda). That is, when the author composes, arranges, and writes it, it manifests in the same form with the same name. Understand this secret.

And the second method is: when there is a remainder, its letter is hāʾ, and when the word "ayyi" is added to it, the Client appears.

Know that these are general principles, for when many letters appear before you, simply prioritize the letters according to their ranks.

**The Opinion of the Majority of Scholars:**

The majority of scholars say: first take the units letters. Then the tens letters, then the hundreds letters, then the thousands letters. And if the thousands are repeated, follow the same rule as for names, for which journeys are undertaken to know them. Plato expressed things very obscurely. But he concealed the result in the letter ʿayn-lām-fāʾ (ʿilf).

**The Method of Determining the Name of the Client:**

And all scholars have explained this, and we have explained this rule in detail. If the letters of thousands are repeated with you, then look at how many times the letters have been repeated, and expand them over the letters of the thousands number. For example, when you have seventy thousand repetitions—that is, seventy thousand—first write ʿayn-yāʾ (the sign for seventy) and after it ghayn (the sign for thousand), and after that add the word "ayyi," so that you have nine thousand four hundred and ninety-nine combined. So first write ẓāʾ, then tāʾ, and arrange it in this manner. The words "āyīl" and "hīlūf" and so on are repeated until the units rank reaches the tens rank. Then also add one ʿayn and then proceed with it. Such a letter that is minor according to the rule of numbers—for example, when we had three thousand nine hundred and ninety combined, we derived in this manner: Mīkāʾīl.
