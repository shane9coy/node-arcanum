Chapter: Composing hearts and establishing love between husband and wife
---
These names are written on a turban for a husband and wife who are separated. Write on Friday at the time when the Imam sits on the pulpit to deliver the sermon and the adhan begins, using saffron, rose water, and clove. Then wrap the amulet, apply perfume to it, and place it in the turban on which both of them sleep. Love will be produced between them, and the names are:

Tasum 2 Aysum 2 Alum 2 Kalum 2 Hayum 2 Qayum 2 Dayum Subhan man bi-dhikrihi tatma'inu al-qulub Iṭma'in qalbu fulanata binti fulan aw fulani ibn fulanata lamma aslahta bayna Muhammadin salla Allahu alayhi wa sallama wa al-ansar Allahumma ya man adkhala mahabbata Yusuf fi qalbi Zulaikha wa ya man adkhala mahabbata Musa fi qalbi Asiya binti Muzahim adkhil mahabbata kaza wa kaza fi qalbi kaza wa kaza Allahumma ya man adkhala mahabbata Muhammadin salla Allahu alayhi wa sallama fi qalbi Khadijata binti Khuwaylid wa A'ishata binti Abi Bakrin adkhil mahabbata kaza wa kaza kama adkhalta al-layl fi al-nahari wa al-nahara fi al-layli wa al-dhakara fi al-untha law anfaqta ma fi al-ardi jami'an ma allafta bayna quluubihim walakinna Allaha allafa baynahum innahu azizun hakim wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-aliyy al-azim

Pure is He by Whose remembrance hearts find peace—grant peace to the heart of so-and-so daughter of so-and-so, or so-and-so son of so-and-so—just as You established reconciliation between Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the Ansar. O Allah, O He who placed the love of Joseph in the heart of Zulaykha, and O He who placed the love of Moses in the heart of Asiya daughter of Muzahim, place the love of so-and-so in the heart of so-and-so. O He who placed the love of Khadija daughter of Khuwaylid and A'isha daughter of Abu Bakr in the heart of Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, just as You placed the night into the day and the day into the night, and the male into the female. If you were to spend all that is in the earth, you would not compose their hearts, but Allah Himself composed them. Truly He is Mighty and Wise, and there is no power and no strength except by Allah the Exalted the Magnificent. If you wish, write it in the first hour of Friday.

Chapter: The operation of opening locks
---
Someone asked Abu al-Hasan al-Shadhali about the name of the mother of Moses, upon him be peace. He answered: There are various narrations regarding it, but in my view the correct one is that you should fast for seven days with this supplication, speaking to no one. Each day give charity to seven poor people, and fumigate the place of prostration morning and evening with frankincense and agarwood. After each prayer, recite this supplication seven times, then whenever you read this supplication from your heart with the intention of opening any lock or chain, at that very moment it will open. The supplication is:

Bisimillahi al-Rahmani al-Rahim Rabbi halna binti Zakariyya al-mu'mina al-siddiqa ummi Musa alayhi al-salam wa billahi al-aliyy al-hakim al-kabir al-mutakabbir al-muhaymin al-azim al-Rahman al-Rahim alladhi yufattahu bihi al-aqfal wa istdarati bihi al-ardu wa al-aflak fa bi-haqqi bihi al-alqabi iftah hatha al-qufl bi-idhn Allah ta'ala

If one desires to incline someone's heart, write it in this manner [damaged source]
