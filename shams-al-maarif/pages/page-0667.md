And its dhikr is this:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Guarantor, the Provider, the One who bestows sustenance to all creatures without limit, the One who gives provision to all. Glorified be You, Provider of the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth with sustenance, and You have sustained them with subtle spiritual gifts. Provider of the inhabitants of land and sea, and Provider of the bodily instincts. Provider of the embryo in its mother's womb with delicate nourishment and refined drinks. I ask You to pour forth upon me sustenance from all directions, and expand my chest, and grant me victory by unveiling to me the subtleties of divine provision, and make them for me a strength in Your generosity, O Generous One. Grant my heart the subtle sciences of knowledge and make them my sustenance, and aid me, O Lord of the worlds, O Provider. And aid me with it and revive my heart forever, O Allah, O Provider.

Whoever reads this supplication, Allah the Most High will expand sustenance for him.

[Bordered heading: Chapter: The Merits of the Name "Fattah"]

Fattah is the One who opens every locked gate and decides all cares and concerns.
There are two types of opening: one is of knowledge, and the other is of secrets, which is attained only by the saints.
But there are two types of opening: one is the opening of knowledge, and the other is the opening of every deep and subtle matter. And Fattah is the One who removes from the eyes of His saints the locks of wisdom and opens the gates of mercy for the believers. Allah the Most High said to His Prophet: "Indeed, We have given you a clear opening." In this opening, the servant's portion is that he should be patient until the entanglements of divine matters and the subtle insights of transcendent wisdom are revealed to him, and Allah makes him understand things that other people cannot comprehend, and the matters of knowledge from the Divine Source and the law of prophethood and the secrets of books are all opened to him.

The secrets of the knowledge of divine inspiration and prophethood are revealed to its reader:

This name is from the Most Noble Names. Whoever wishes to engage with it must first conduct an accounting of his soul, and it is essential to obtain the fruits of sincerity along with it. At that time, the secrets will be revealed to him. The meaning of the name Fattah has been explained in the section on the name Wahhab. The one mentioned previously has the number 489. It works day and night after recitation. Allah the Most High will show all places in one hour. A particular property of this name is that whoever writes it on the day of Friday, keeps it with them, and continues to recite it—the observation of divine matters will be revealed to him [unclear] that which he needs.
