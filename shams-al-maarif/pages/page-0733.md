# Page 733 (English translation)
**Three States of Human Being:**

Majesty and Wisdom are the initial states for jinn and humans, and they are also the middle states, as well as annihilation (fana'), transference, and subsistence (baqa'). Among the people of states, the one who is in the beginning will have the attribute of majesty manifest upon him; the one who is in the middle will have expansion appear upon him; and the one who is in the ultimate state will have the states of Husayn manifest upon him through ascetic practice.

**The Story of Ibn Jalaal:**

It is narrated from Ibn Jalaal that he said: "I was riding a camel. The camel's foot sank into the sand. I said 'Jal Allah' [Glory be to God]! The camel also said 'Jal Allah'! The camel had the power of speech from the spirit for this occasion. First, because the camel intended God, and the hadith of the Noble Prophet ﷺ serves as evidence for this: 'If you were on a mountain, you would attribute to Allah.' And second, because the initial states of majesty were descending upon the camel at that time; due to exhaustion, the camel could not bear them. So Allah the Most High made the reality of the situation speak through it, and it spoke with divine majesty.

**Whoever Surrenders His Outer and Inner to Allah, Allah Protects Him Himself:**

Whoever comes to know the divine favor submits his heart and soul to it and bows his head in submission trusting in every action of Allah. So God protects him from visible and invisible enemies.

**The Mother of Moses, Peace Be Upon Him, Trusted in Allah and Moses Remained Safe:**

Have you not seen that when the mother of Moses, peace be upon him, trusted in God, how she found deliverance among her enemies, and the enemy like Pharaoh raised him, even though on that very day before Moses's arrival, he had killed seventy thousand children?

It is reported in some narrations that when a servant intends righteousness, Allah the Most High says: "Return to your Lord and submit to Him." And when he intends sin, Allah the Most High says: "Do you take him and his offspring as allies instead of Me?"

**The Outcome of Surrendering Oneself to Allah:**

Thus it is proper for you to entrust all affairs to God, because when you do not turn your inner self away from Him, He will protect your outward actions from the cunning and deceptive one, and people will fear him, and there He will give you protection.

**The Mother of Mary Entrusted Her Pregnancy to Allah, and from Her Pregnancy Mary Was Born, and from Her Jesus Was Born:**

Behold the mother of Mary, when she dedicated her pregnancy to Allah, how Allah the Most High gave her excellence, and Mary was born through her, and then from her Jesus, peace be upon him, was born—who in the final age will descend upon the eastern minaret of Damascus, will break the cross, will kill the swine, and will truly [damaged/watermarked] the jizyah, and after slaying the Antichrist (Dajjal) will rule the world.
