# Page 689 (English translation)
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Preserver, the Guardian of all created beings that You brought into existence in the diversity of creation, the purity of all that exists in the natural and the return. You brought together opposites and similar things, and You perfected creation according to every measure of created beings in gathering and detail. I ask You by Your power over creating manifestation—

**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Ḥasīb"**

The meaning of Ḥasīb is "sufficient" (kāfī), as in this verse: "A reward from your Lord, a sufficient gift" — meaning gift of the meaning of sufficiency and enough. Taking account of actions and intentions is the meaning of it. Thus, Ḥasīb means the performer of the matter of sufficiency, and this name belongs solely to the divine Essence, exalted be He.

**There are three states in which one stands in need of sufficiency:**

Because sufficiency is in need through three states: one is existence, the second is the perpetuity of existence, and within existence, the perfection of its good — apart from God, exalted be He, there is none. Reflecting even slightly upon human existence makes clear how God, exalted be He, has provided for its sufficiency.

**After the gradual creation of the sperm, nourishment through milk from the mother's breast:**

First, the gradual nourishment of the sperm, although it was merely water and a summary of life-giving substances, that is, food. The Lord, in His delicate craftsmanship, managed its affair and created within its heart the substance of motion, for every motion that comes from mercy is mentioned. The sperm grows contrary to the natural temperament, then for it He created milk in its mother's breast and inspired within it that this is its food, and He created hunger within it and taught it that when hungry, it should cry, until the wet nurse's attribute of compassion moved and she gave milk.

**Progressing through the stages of creation degree by degree:**

Then gradually it was made accustomed to eating food, and Allah, exalted be He, created within this child along with it an intellect as well, which grows with it, so that it may come to recognize the things of this world and learn various things on its own. Then Allah, exalted be He, showed it the path of guidance so that what had been destined for it might be revealed before it, and He made its heart the locus of the practice of dhikr al-kāfī (sufficient remembrance), and the intellect the locus of the practice of providence, and for the believing faithful He made it a means of salvation. Thus, He did not place upon any soul a path upon Himself such that it would not need another to make a living — it produces the means of its own livelihood.

**He alone is sufficient for every newborn child:**

Thus, how obligatory is this upon the servant, and what relationship does it have with this name, that it should utterly turn away from creation and take excellent care of its innate dispositions.

When you have an enemy, then entrust his action to Allah and keep it with yourself, and go to the enemy while reciting this name.
