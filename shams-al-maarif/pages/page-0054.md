# Page 54 (English translation)
(Chapter)

On the Concealment of the Spirits of the Letters

Without which no operation can be completed

It is this: when you wish to perform an operation, examine the letters of that day for the seeker and the sought number, and divide them into groups of three. If fewer than three remain, then the last letter is the concealment of that letter. The masters of the divine names must know this without fail, because it is useful in most operations. These are the concealments of the spirits of the letters (mowakkalāt). The spirit of the letter Alef is Ṭuqṭāyīl, and its concealment is Hadhībūb Salfā Mukhtaj. The concealment of the letter Bāʾ is Yas Yakh Hulīj Marīj. The spirit of the letter Jīm is Mählīj Silk Bahloh. The concealment of the letter Dāl is Sahlʿatak. The concealment of the letter Hāʾ is Mahṭaʿ. The concealment of the letter Wāw is Mahlīw Salīmūʿ Barāh. The concealment of the letter Zāʾ is Saʿīdah Pūāh Ṭuqṭum Mahrīṭ. The concealment of the letter Ḥāʾ is Līlā Ṭalaḥ. The concealment of the letter Ṭāʾ is Shumḥṭ Salīsaṭ Ṭamh. The concealment of the letter Yāʾ is Madakh Takhīṭ Sūīd Yū. The concealment of the letter Kāf is Sīūdah Nuqaṭ Madīḥ. The concealment of the letter Lām is Ghalīṭ Ṭaʿmish ʿIlūm. The concealment of the letter Nūn is Madīj Kalīl. The concealment of the letter Sīn is Ḥaṭaṭ Muṭaliʿ Ḥamlṭ Bajasam. The concealment of the letter ʿAyn is Lījaṭyīm ʿAn Fawādir. The concealment of the letter Fāʾ is Kayṭam Yaraṭsh Halīṭ. The concealment of the letter Ṣād is Sahmhand Hamīsh. The concealment of the letter Qāf is ʿAdʿatīr Laṭmhash. The concealment of the letter Rāʾ is Satahat Lahīl Dhoūm. The concealment of the letter Shīn is ʿAlṭīn Muhfāʿil Muhmalā. The concealment of the letter Tāʾ is Parmīlū Ḥafīṭ. The concealment of the letter Thāʾ is Muhfīṭ. The concealment of the letter Khāʾ is Ṣahʿaj Mūhīsal. The concealment of the letter Dhāl is ʿAlamṣ Sahdʿ Sahlaṭ. The concealment of the letter Ḍād is ʿAlʿalam Ḥiṣ Ṣahdʿ Shahlaṭ. And the concealment of the letter Ẓāʾ is Nawwadh Wadhakh Lahmosh Lahmosh. You should know that the spirit of the letter Dhāl and the letter Ẓāʾ is one individual, and its concealment is singular. The spirit of the letter Ghayn is Salmat Kalkat Lahyūdh Wanaʿmah. These are all the explicit designations.

And Allāh, Glorious and Exalted, knows best what is correct. And may Allāh bless our master Muḥammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
