# Page 649 (English translation)
[Page 649]

A compulsory system has been established for the connection of every world:
That is, just as there is a system of the higher world (عالم علوی), and for it there is a world that serves it with the force of compulsion (جبر). With each world of his own there is a world that administers it through its providence, and whatever is subjugated—he has bound the angels and spirits within creation through divine wisdom.

The rule of this Name is forty days, and when arrogance or haughtiness arises within you, then contemplate upon this Name; arrogance will depart.

Through the use of this Name's square (مربّع) and reduction (تکسیر), it becomes greatly honored:
Whoever writes this Name in a square and keeps it with himself according to the method of reduction, he will be a person of standing and rank among people, and the rulers will honor him. And whoever engraves its square upon silver and writes around it the entire speech (کل کلام) and also writes the dhikr, and keeps it with himself, then when he meets kings, they will approach him with honor and reverence. And if he has enemies, let him mention this dhikr; the enemies will be destroyed. Its dhikr is this:

بِسْمِ اللّٰہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ اَللّٰھُمَّ اِنِّیْ اَسْئَلُکَ بِاسْمِکَ الْجَبَّارِ اَنْ فُلَانًا عَبْدَکَ اَذَانِیْ وَ تَجَبَّرَ عَلَیَّ وَاَنْتَ جَبَّارُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضِ اَسْئَلُکَ اَنْ تَجْبُرَہُ وَتَقْھَرَہُ بِالْمَحَبَّۃِ وَالْمَوَدَّۃِ لِیْ یَا جَبَّارُ یَا اَللّٰہُ

And if you wish, say thus:
اَجِبْ اَیُّھَا الْمَلِیْکُ وَ تَوَکَّلْ بِفُلَانٍ بِحَقِّ ھٰذَا الْاِسْمِ

And recite this Name, and in this verse many of these Names have appeared:
ھُوَ اللّٰہُ الَّذِیْ لَا اِلٰہَ اِلَّا ھُوَ عَالِمُ الْغَیْبِ وَالشَّھَادَۃِ
until its end—these are all the Beautiful Names (اسمائے الحسنیٰ) except one Name of God which is non-derivative.

And in the method of operations, this Name belongs to the Gate of the Spirit (باب روح). And there is one square for this Name: when it is written with musk and saffron and rose-water, and the writer is fasting, and he recites the verse in accordance with its numerical value, and takes the name of the client (مؤکل)—the client will be present. This client is in the company of Azrael (عزرائیل). When you wish to bring him forth, recite the Name in accordance with its numerical value; he will appear. Under this client are four officers, and each officer commands eighty ranks. He appears in the form of the reciter and manifests his sign. His talisman (نقش) is this:
