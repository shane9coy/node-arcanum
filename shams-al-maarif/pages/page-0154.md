# Page 154 (English translation)
152

The glorified, the great form of your noble greatest name, that you may invoke blessings upon our master Muḥammad by the number of every letter that the pen has run, that He may fulfill my need, and it is such and such.

The name of this is Ism Barakat, and write it beautifully:

[A 7x7 magic square grid containing various esoteric symbols such as stars, crescent moons, cross-like shapes, small boxes/dots, and the Arabic letter 'Meem' distributed throughout.]

The Blessed Wafīq:
This is the blessed wafīq. On its letters write seven letters from the alphabet letters which are the letters of the Fawātiḥ al-Fātiḥa. Each of these letters is a divine name from among the names of God, and those letters are: fā, jīm, shīn, thāʾ, ẓāʾ, khāʾ, dhāl.

Chapter:
Chapter: Allah the Exalted is sufficient, and may He grant me success. And know that when your intention is to inquire about the condition of a sick person or a missing person, then first determine on which day he fell ill, or on which day the missing person traveled. Take his name and his mother's name using the method of extended numerical computation (Ḥisāb al-Jummal al-Kabīr), then in the lunar month however many dates have passed, make the numerical values as large as possible, then add further to those numbers on top of them. Divide the total sum of all numbers by nine, then see what remains. If three or less remains, then look at both tablets; whichever of the two it falls into—I have named one the Tablet of Life and the other the Tablet of Death. In whichever tablet the calculation falls, from that one issue the decree regarding death or life. In the same manner, the condition of husband and wife may also be known—whether they will remain together in harmony or in discord—and who among them will die first, by calculating according to the mentioned rule. From the Arabic months, the dates that remain, add them as an increase, and also add their names to them, and divide according to the rule, and place them before the Tablet of Life and the Tablet of Death. If it falls in the Tablet of Life, then they will remain together. If one of them falls in the Tablet of Death, then there will be discord between them or one of them will die. Similarly, when a ruler comes to a city, determine the day on which he entered the city. Calculate with the ruler's name along with the past dates according to the mentioned rule, and place the remainder that remains before both tablets. By its light, whatever becomes apparent regarding death and life, issue the decree accordingly. And similarly is the case of a pregnant woman regarding what she will give birth to and whether the child will remain alive or die.

The procedure is as follows: Calculate the pregnant woman's name, her mother's name, and the name of the day on which you are, along with the past lunar dates. If it falls in the Tablet of Life, then issue the decree of life; and if it falls in the Tablet of Death, then issue the decree of death. In this same manner you may inquire about the seeker and the sought and every difficult matter, and Allah the Exalted knows best. The description of both tablets is as you see:
