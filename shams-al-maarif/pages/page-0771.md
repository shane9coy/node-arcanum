# Page 771 (English translation)
By His complete words, and by resurrection and revival, and by the angels who do not eat or drink—their food is glorification and their drink is sanctification—by Bahya Sharahiya and Dani Aspat and Al Shaddai. I swear by you by the Living, the Eternal, and the Creator of earth and heaven, who said to the heavens and earth: "Come willingly or unwillingly." They said: "We come willingly." I swear by you by God that you must answer and attend my gathering, or release what I have mentioned for you in the fastest time and the most complete hour.

**The operation of the letter Alif with various benefits:**

And regarding the vow of the vice-regent of the letter Alif: Badus, Khalifa, Firdus, Awhan, Hermes, Haris. Write the letter Alif and recite the Azimat seven times, the desired will appear. And if with the name of an absent person, write with saffron on the abjad clay tablet and give fumigation, recite the Azimat and throw to the wind, the desired will appear quickly. And if you wish to reconcile two people between them, write it with the king's name on Thursday at sunrise, give fumigation and recite Azimat seven times over it, and put this paper in burning fire and say:

**"I have burned the heart of So-and-so son of So-and-so"**

And if the intention is to bring the desired person, take the earth from beneath their feet and write the names in it, also write his name and his mother's name, recite Azimat seven times facing sunrise at dawn, and at the end say these words:

**"O shining, exalted sun, by the One who placed you in your sphere and is the Creator of the heavens and earths, make So-and-so son of So-and-so beloved to me until he becomes obedient to my hand and has no escape from me"**

And if you wish to burn it at night, write these and perform at sunset, the desired will appear immediately.

**Chapter: The benefits of this figure and the method of the double prayer:**

Whoever wishes to use this blessed figure on Sunday when the Moon is in Aries at 3 degrees from its dignity—that is, its sign of exaltation—and is free from malefic aspects, and the hour is also lunar, should pray two rak'ahs and then write it. In the first rak'ah, after Surah Fatiha, recite Ayat al-Kursi one hundred times, and in the second rak'ah recite Surah Ikhlas one hundred times. Then write this figure and keep it with you. Understanding, memory, and wisdom will be granted to him, and his value and status will be elevated throughout the upper and lower worlds. If this figure is bound on a prisoner, he will be freed quickly; if attached to a battle standard, victory will be achieved; and if this figure is properly positioned and battle is fought against the enemy, there will be victory. Its form is as follows:

[A 4x4 magic square grid containing letters of the word 'Buduh']
| ح | و | د | ب |
|---|---|---|---|
| د | ب | ح | و |
| ب | د | و | ح |
| و | ح | ب | د |

There is a strange secret in the letter Alif for this: namely, whoever writes letters instead of numbers, when the Moon is in its own sign—that is, Taurus—and remains pure with kohl, and with glorification and declaration of oneness, fasting, and purification of the inner self, God Most High will maintain his blessings and there will be expansion in his sustenance, and every person will submit to him, and his fortune will remain forever. We have explained its properties in our book Ilm al-Hadi. And God knows best.
