And after reading this prayer, blow on it seven times:

"In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. In the name of God, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, before whom faces humble themselves and voices bow in reverence, and hearts tremble in awe of Him. I ask You to bless and grant peace upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and companions, and grant peace, and fulfill my need in Fulan. O God, surely You know that if he repents from what he is upon, guide him and grant him success, and if You know that he will not repent, then send down upon him a tribulation devoid of Your pleasure and Your wrath, and destroy him, O the Conqueror, O the Prevailer, O the Powerful, O the All-Powerful, O God."

And let him read this prayer seven times—either the oppressor will desist from his oppression, or he will be destroyed. And let everyone write the complete Basmala eight times within a circle, and write "Muhammad is the Messenger of God and those who believe with him" up to the end of Surah al-Fath around it, and fumigate it during an auspicious time with good incense, then keep it with himself. Then he will be awe-inspiring and venerable among people, and whoever sees him will love him instinctively, and by the command of God the Most High, he will succeed in all endeavors.

**Obtaining Martyrdom:**

And whoever writes the complete Basmala when the moon is in the sign of Pisces and the ascendant is auspicious, writing it on deerskin according to its numerical values and tying it upon his head—he will live an excellent life and die as a martyr, and he will see no evil in his life, wealth, or children.

**The Secrets of the Basmala and the Greatest Name**

Know that "Bismillah" is the Venerated Name, and "Allah" is the Greatest Name. "Ar-Rahman" and "Ar-Rahim" are Its attributes. He is the Merciful of the religion and the Compassionate of the world. "In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate" comes before "Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds." Know that the complete praise of God lies in "Master of the Day of Judgment." This is the manifestation of lordship. He is the King of kings and the Master, who manifests dominion and conquest over souls and encompasses all within His kingdom. Thus His saying applies: "with a sovereign endowed with power." And all secrets are within "Bismillah Ar-Rahman Ar-Rahim." The Basmala is the name from which all words were obtained from God the Most High for all things. "Bismillah" corresponds to sunrise and emergence; toward the First Origin, "Ar-Rahman Ar-Rahim" points toward the Second Origin. In the Basmala are the secrets of beginning and end, and within it are the degrees of divine unity. Understand it thus: "Say: He is God, [the] One" has the place of "Bismillah," and "God" has the place of "There is no god but He," "One" [unclear] has the place of "Ar-Rahman," and "And the angels and those endowed with knowledge" has the place of "Ar-Rahim." Likewise is the relation to the realm of collision. That is, from God's saying "And those upon whom God has bestowed favor from among the prophets, and the truthful, and the martyrs, and the righteous," the correspondence is from compassion to mercy, to the knowledge of God here, the return is made to the knowledge of God.
