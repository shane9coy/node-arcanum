# Page 365 (English translation)
O Living One, O Sustainer, You are God besides whom there is no god. Glory be to You, truly I am among the wrongdoers. I ask You by Your unity that You establish me toward You, and I ask You by Your life for the life of the heart and its safety, likewise in religion and world and hereafter and in what is between them, and preserve all that for me. O You whom nothing is burdensome in His preservation, O Exalted, O Great, until I meet You while You are pleased with me. O God, upon the best state from You, and the most comfortable condition without hardship or punishment, not in religion nor in the world nor in children nor in wealth nor in the world nor in the hereafter, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

O Living One, O Sustainer, You are God, there is no god but You. Glory be to You, I am among the wrongdoers. I ask You with Your unity that You keep me firm toward Yourself. I ask You by Your life for the life of the heart and its safety, likewise I ask for safety in religion and world and hereafter and in what is between them, and protect me over all that. O You whose protection is not burdensome. O Exalted, O Forbearing, so that I may meet You while You are pleased with me. O God, upon the best state from You, and the finest condition without hardship or punishment—neither in religion, nor in children, nor in wealth, nor in the world, nor in the hereafter—by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. May God the Exalted grant you and grant you the success of obedience to Him, and illuminate our hearts with the light of His recognition. I recited the final verses, then the Verse of the Throne, the beginning of the Chapter of the Cow, and from the beginning of every surah until "Huwallahu Ahad," and "Qul Allahumma Malika al-Mulk" until "bighayri hisab"—and this supplication is to be recited [unclear].

O God, I ask You for the soundness of fear, the dominance of longing, the completeness of knowledge, and the permanence of thought. And I ask You, O God, the secret of secrets dwelling within the lights, until we have no place for sin or defect. Give us life and guide us to action by these words that You have expounded for us through the tongue of Your Messenger, and with which You tested Abraham Your friend, so he completed them. He said, "Truly I am making you a leader for mankind." He said, "And from my descendants?" He said, "My covenant does not encompass the wrongdoers." So place us among the humble, and from my descendants and from the descendants of Adam and Noah, and I ask You by us as a way to the Imams of the God-conscious. O God, I have wronged myself with great wrong, and none forgives sins except You, so forgive me and have mercy on me and turn to me in repentance. There is no god but You. Glory be to You, truly I am among the wrongdoers. O God, O Forbearing, O Knowing, O Hearing, O Seeing, O Willing, O Powerful, O Living, O Sustaining, O Compassionate, O Merciful, O He Who Is, O He, O First, O Last, O Apparent, O Hidden. Blessed is Your name, Possessor of majesty and honor.

O God, bless with Your great name which nothing harms alongside sins, and make for me from it a direction through which needs are fulfilled for the heart, the intellect, the spirit, the longing, the soul, the body, and cause my names to bow under Your names, my attributes under Your attributes, and my actions under Your actions, to the rank of safety and the removal of regret and the descent of honor in actions. And be for me in what You have tested me with, among Your verses of guidance, from Your scholars, and suffice me until You eliminate [unclear] Your wealth among me, and give me life until through me You give life to whom You wish among Your servants, and place me as a treasury of spirits and from the special ones of the God-conscious, and make me a leader whom the wrongdoers do not attain. They are deaf, mute, blind, so they do not return—they meet.
