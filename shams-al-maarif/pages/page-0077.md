# Page 77 (English translation)
Allah the Exalted will place love for him in the hearts of people. The manner of writing it is as follows: Ab Sam Al L A H Al Rahman Al Raheem. Write it one hundred and eleven times. And if a person of narrow means writes it on a henna leaf with musk, saffron, and rose water, and gives it fumigation of costus, galbanum, and frankincense, and keeps it with him, Allah the Exalted will expand his sustenance. If a debtor keeps it with him, Allah the Exalted will pay his debt for him. He will remain safe from all evil.

**For the sick to be healed:**
Whoever writes it in a glass or any glass vessel, and you wash it with Zamzam or sweet well water, and give it to drink to a sick person, he will be healed.

**Ease in childbirth and progress in trade:**
If you give it to a woman suffering from labor pains, the child will be born immediately. If you write it thirty-five times (35) on a paper and hang it in the house, neither Satan nor jinn will enter it, and there will be more blessing in the house. If you hang this paper, profit will increase and the shop will become very successful. Customers' eyes will become blind. And whoever writes it one hundred and thirty times on the first of Muharram and keeps it with him, no evil will befall him or his house throughout his life.

**Treatment for those whose children die and for pregnancy:**
Know that if a woman whose children die or whose pregnancy does not continue, it is necessary that after she becomes pure from menstruation, she writes "Bismillah" one hundred and ten times, and after three days ties it to her waist. And understand that by the command of Allah the Exalted, she will surely become pregnant and give birth to a righteous and long-lived child, and she will not feel any discomfort of pregnancy. Then she should open the two-rupee coin for this talisman and keep it. Whoever has children who do not survive should write it eighty-one times and keep it with him, and his children will live. Its effectiveness has been tested and found correct, and Allah the Exalted has power over all things. If it is written one hundred and one times and buried in a field, the field will flourish abundantly and remain safe from all calamities. If written seventy times and placed in a deceased person's shroud, it will be safe from the torment of Munkar and Nakir, and there will be light for him until the Day of Resurrection. If written on money and tied to the waist, and then one waits for fruit, there will be very abundant game. If written once and placed under the bezel of a ring, and then that ring is washed in milk and given to drink to a person who has been bitten by a venomous creature, all the poison will leave by the command of Allah the Exalted. If its letters are written separately and kept with one, great honor will be attained.

**The meanings and secrets of the letters of Bismillah:**
Because from "Ba" is the existence of Allah, His hearing, generosity, and power; from "Alif" is the eternal name; from "Lam" is kindness; from "Ha" is sweetness; from "Alif" is the divine command; from "Lam" is the sovereignty of speech; from "Ra" is mercy; from "Ha" is wisdom; from "Mim" is sovereignty; and from "Nun" is blessing. If the "Ba" of Bismillah is written twenty-one times and these letters of this verse are recited... In this manner: S L A M A L Y K M M R J F Y A L R H A L M Y N. And if given to someone bitten by a venomous creature to drink, he will be immediately healed by the command of Allah the Exalted. Write Bismillah and look at its "Mim" every day for forty days, and continue reciting until "Qul Allahumma Malikal Mulk," and he will be granted goodness and blessing, and whatever he has will increase. Write "Qul Allahumma Al-Rahman Al-Rahim" fifty times, and after that recite Bismillah sixty-three times over it, and keep it with you, and if you go to a king or any ruler, he will be well-disposed toward him, and no trouble will befall him.
