|Prayer for the Fulfillment of Desires|

Know that one of the leading scholars of the city of knowledge (i.e., the companions of the Prophet), the door of prophethood, asked His Eminence Ali, may God be pleased with him, saying: "Pray for me a prayer for the fulfillment of my need." He, may God be pleased with him, said: "Recite six verses from Surah al-Hadid beginning with 'Whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth extols God' up to 'the Knowing of what is in the breasts.' Recite the last verses of Surah al-Hashr. Then say these words:

'O God, O You Who is thus and Who never ceases to be thus, besides whom there is no one thus, make for me in my affair an opening and a way out.'

Then mention your need. Verily He will fulfill your need. God knows best, and He is the Most Generous and Most Bountiful.
