Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Jāmiʿ"

Jāmiʿ is He who gathers together affairs and contradictions and opposites. Thus Allah Most High will gather many people upon one earth. This is the gathering of similars (one genus), and the gathering of opposites (the contrary of one another) is that He will gather together heaven and earth and stars and minerals and plants and animals and so forth, although each thing among them differs from the other in form and appearance and color and taste. Allah Most High has gathered the angels upon the earth and has gathered them in the entire world, and He has gathered flesh and blood and veins and sinews and so forth in all animals.

The gathering of opposites in one place—only Allah can do this:
And the gathering of opposites is that He has gathered heat and cold and moisture and dryness within the body, although these are contrary to one another and bear hatred toward each other. And Allah is the greatest of all gatherers.

From its mention, unveiling is obtained:
Know that the human is the gatherer of appearance and insight, and when a human establishes a connection with this name, unveiling is produced for him, and the eye of his heart opens, and he sees the opposites and so forth. The one who performs the retreat for this name is granted unveiling.
The retreat must be with the rectification of faculties. The supplicant will be present. Seventy thousand officers are under its command, and the garment of perfection will be bestowed upon the reciter, and it will fulfill his needs. Its officer is named Jiyāʾīl. It will appear to him according to the reciter's status. It has the quality of bringing back lost things.
Let him write its talismanic figure and place it in the location, and afterward let the supplicant read the name and say:

Yā jāmiʿa l-nāsi li-yawmin lā rayba fīhi ajmiʿnī ʿalā kadhā wa-kadhā

That thing will return. And when you wish to gather two people together—for example, if a husband is angry with his wife and you wish that reconciliation occur between them—then work with this name according to the mentioned rules. Its form is this:
