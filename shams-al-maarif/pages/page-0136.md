# Page 136 (English translation)
Chapter 11

The Divine Lights and Mysteries of the Sovereign Realm (Malakut)

Know that Allah, the Most High, seventy thousand years before creating souls—according to those years whose one day equals fifty thousand years according to our reckoning—wrote a book, the knowledge of which belongs to none but Him. However, the presence of the Prophet, may the blessings and peace of Allah be upon him, has informed us that Allah, the Most High, wrote a book seventy thousand years before creating the earth and heaven, which is with Him upon the Throne. In it is written: "My mercy has preceded My wrath." This is indeed the greatest truth upon which the people of intellect—those whom Allah, the Most High, has guided to the straight path and whom He loves—have built. It is because of this truth that they recognized Allah, and because of monotheism they became drowned in the oceans and rivers of blessings. These are the ones about whom Allah, the Most High, has said: "These are the ones who are the heirs of Paradise-Firdaws; they shall abide therein forever." They shall abide forever alongside the realities of the highest stations. They do not tire from contemplating their states, and their testimony regarding the Divine Mercy is based solely upon this truth from which came the strength that you comprehend these composite letters.

And know that when Allah, the Most High, seated the bearers of true monotheism upon the Divine Throne, He adorned them with the garment of light and the crown of wisdom. And He informed them, in the station of Divine pleasure, of the realities with absolute perfection, and He granted them His power in the realities of the spiritual world and the luminous sciences. Then the people of solitude saw upon their mats in their field of station, within their sacred vision and divine directions, the vast expanse of the sacred presence's realm of the divine kingdom.

Above it is the world of divine manifestations and the higher realms. Thus they made this very path their purpose and beloved, and they made this supplication: "O our Lord, grant us such a secret of grace that we may become acquainted with the secret of secrets, and may the realities of thought be revealed to us, for doubt surrounds us and causes division." When Allah, the Most High, knew their gentle union and original reality, He opened before them the pages of that book whose realities and knowledge are renowned. He made them aware of the mysteries of the clear explanation, and the design of the head of that circle became inscribed upon their heads, by virtue of which they became acquainted with many mysteries. But that luminous circle expanded and spread, and with its trust it slaughtered many strangers. There, another circle appeared which had both an outward and an inward aspect. Thus, outwardly it contained 567 letters, and inwardly 231. The proportion of 130 is eternal. This is the proportion of the divine kingdom (Malakut). The proportion of 231 is everlasting, and this is the book of the divine kingdom. From their discourse, they were granted perfect knowledge and understanding, divine effusion, and the sacred spirit. Therefore, this also remained with them, and that proportion continued to make the truth evident before them. Then they completed it for the administration of the world and prepared it as a gift for the realm of Islam. When you intend it, seek the verification of the secret of the Lord; then complete knowledge, action, and the head (secret) shall come to your hand. The reason for this is that the head of the divine purpose upon the luminous throne of the Divine Essence hides a secret and light among the luminous individuals. Indeed, it is a renowned light among the states; the reality of the stations has taken refuge in it. Understand that most people, upon reaching this station, have been defeated, falling back down in confusion to a lower degree. Whoever looked upon the book of action witnessed the effusion of the head of the divine purpose, and whoever studied the head of the book witnessed divine knowledge. There is no degree beyond this for advancement except within this same reality, and it encompasses those lights and actions.

Now I shall relate to you an example by which it will quickly become clear. Allah alone is the one in whom we place our bright purpose
