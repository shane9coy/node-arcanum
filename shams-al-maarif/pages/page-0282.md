Chapter 34: The Properties of the Great Name for Obtaining Eternal Honor

This is the Great Name, the Splendid One and the Greatest Magnet. Whoever persists in the remembrance of this Name, Almighty Allah will grant him eternal honor, and he will be regarded as great in the eyes of people. His evil deeds will not be known to anyone. If he possesses truthful sincerity, he will observe the Command of the Lord throughout all the world. Love for him will be implanted in the hearts of people. Its number is 1020. It is the Husband of Husbands and is in excess of it. Its square division triangle yields 9. Its numbers are 1191, increased from the original. The square of Gavla and Dada Handri is for this, that is, the Comprehensive Detail exists for the matter of selection; it is an indication. Thus He is the Holy Essence who is Hidden through the intensity of Manifestation. Therefore it is an indication of the Name of Probability, and it is toward the Light of Lights. The descent of Ya is for the determination of Manifestation. The reliance upon all the numbers of this Name is upon its components, which are extracted just as they emerge from abbreviation. But through doubling, perfection is obtained. Allah the Most High has said: "Cast forth the hearing while He is the Witness." Its letters' numbers are 331. The great names of glorification and Hallah allude to it. These are its square's attribute.

Chapter 35: The Properties of the Name Ghafur for the Treatment of a Ruler's Anger and Griefs

Whoever frequently remembers this Name will obtain respite from every fear. This Name has special effectiveness in cooling the anger of kings. Its remembrance is appropriate for those who remain in the service of kings. It is also appropriate for those who are overwhelmed by sorrow and grief, or who are in distress. Its number is 536. It is odd, deficient. Its numbers are 1335, alluding to the name Samasar, because Allah the Most High is Odd and Oddness is Affirmation. This Name is from the deficient ones. Its numbers are with its letters. Its letters' numbers are 122, which allude to two of the great Names of Allah the Most High: Dhī al-Arsh and Mājid. This is its square's attribute.
