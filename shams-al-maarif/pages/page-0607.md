# Page 607 (English translation)
605

The design is this:

[Diagram: A horizontal rectangle containing two inner horizontal lines. In the center is a grid-like symbol resembling a hashtag '#'. In each of the four corners within the inner rectangle are four small vertical dashes '////'.]

And its invocation is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful ۝ O Lord, I implore You for a spiritual aid to strengthen my particular and universal powers, so that I may subdue the soul of every tyrant among beings and particulars, until my soul becomes truly myself, and draws back to itself its sovereignty with a contraction by which my powers fall, until there remains no being with spirit in both forms except that the fire has extinguished it by their appearance, as You have said, "O Mighty One, the servant of the letter Ra' will certainly fulfill [his promise]." And by the special virtue thereof, until I may accomplish with it my task and my desire. Amen, O Allah, O Strong One, O Possessor of Strength and Severe Might, O Guide, O Light, O Living One, O Sustainer, Yehowih 2, Yamowih 2, Rehiya Sharhiya, wa adonai asbatut ali shaday, Yehowih 2, Iyahu 2, Huwihi 2, my face and my glory and my majesty, Shashahya, Bahiya, ya bah Yahu, ya ilah al-aliha, raf'i jilaluhu, hiya ya ba la jalat, bil-qalb, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-'aliy al-'azim, Ta

And its incantation is this:
Respond, O Lord, Harba'il, by the truth of Natiq Hayyum Qayyum, Ra'uf Talhalikh, Yimowih 2, Yahu 2, al-Waha al-'Ajil

The letter Shin for peace among enemies:
This letter is for making peace between two enemies. Write it in a favorable hour with the name of the desired person and the ascendant, and keep it with yourself; the desired outcome will be achieved.

For enmity:
For hatred, write it on a lead tablet with the ascendant and bury it in some place. And if this letter is written with several Sethite names such as Shamit etc., and kept with oneself, proof and honor will befall him, and its solitude is 28 days. The invocation and the ascendant are needed here until the client is present; its name is Harba'il. Its talisman is this:

In the center is a large cross '+'. The branches of the cross are labeled with letters: 'ش' at the top, 'ی' at the left, and 'ن' at the right. In each of the four quadrants formed by the cross, there is a letter 'ش' and a small circle 'o'.]
