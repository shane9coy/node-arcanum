# Page 148 (English translation)
**The Operation of Intense Love:**
When you intend to love someone, write the auspicious design on a glass with musk, saffron, and rose water. Write the name of the desired one and his mother's name on it, then give it to him to drink, and that person will never tolerate being separated from you. If giving it to drink is not possible, then sprinkle it on his clothes, and the desire will be fulfilled.

**The Operation of Expelling Sinners from a House and Causing Discord and Separation:**
And when you wish to separate those people who gather for sin, or you want to expel a man from his house, or you want to create separation between two people, write this design with musk, rose water, and mint water on a lizard, and bury it in that house. That house will immediately fall into ruin. There will be enmity between them and they will separate from one another.

**The Operation of Reconciliation Between Wife and Husband:**
When you intend reconciliation between a wife and her husband, make two wax figurines. Place a piece of the Kaaba in each one. Recite the 'Azimat twenty-one (21) times over them, and they will reconcile. When you wish that you have status among people and that your greatness is established, write the design with musk, saffron, and rose water, wash it, and place it in a bottle. When you intend to go before great people, take a little of the water from it and apply it to your face; whoever sees you will love you.

This is the attribute of the seal: ★ A L M ★ 9 9 9 9 LA ★
And the design for evil is this: 1 1 9 9 9 9 LA

Sheikh Muhammad al-Kabras wrote that he found this design in the Jami' al-Sufiya at a certain person's house in the following manner:
"LA SHAMKHAL HAL ISRA'IL BALUBA'IL MAHTAKURUN TAWAKKALU YA KHUDDAM HATHIHI AL-ASMA' AL-MUBARAKA BI-KADHA WA-KADHA"

"And let him mention whichever of good or evil he intends, and the desire will be fulfilled."

**The 'Azimat Recited in All Affairs, with the Greatest Name:**
This is the 'Azimat that is recited in all operations. It contains the Greatest Name, and this is the famous 'Azimat.

"I begin with the name of Allah, my spirit by which it is guided | Towards revealing the secrets of its inward, it is enfolded"
"And I pray in the second with blessings upon the best of His creation | Muhammad, who removed error and wrongdoing"
"O my God, I have indeed sworn by Your name, calling | By AJ AHWAJ JALJALUT MAHALHAT"
"Pour upon me from the lights, O Lord, its abundance | By the secret, and revive my dead heart with SALSALAT"
"Let it reach the secret of the pious heart, freeing it from impurity | By the eternal one, the secret in it arose and shone"
"And it revives my heart after its death through remembering Him | By the wisdom of our great Master, it has risen"
"Glory be to You, O Allah, O best of creators | And O best of makers, and the most generous of those sent"
"May it convey my purpose and all my desires | By the light of the radiance of AT-IM and the spirit, which has risen"
