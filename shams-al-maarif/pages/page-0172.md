[Inside a rectangular frame:]
Chapter: The Saqiṭāt of Surah al-Fātiḥa and its Wafags

[Urdu text:]
Know that the saqiṭāt of Surah al-Fātiḥa are seven letters: fā, jīm, shīn, thā', ẓā', khā', dhāl, whose sum is fajsh ẓākhdh, and the divine names attributed to them are: from fā – al-fard (the One), from jīm – al-jabbār (the Compeller), from shīn – al-shahīd (the Witness), from thā' – al-thābit (the Stable), from ẓā' – al-ẓahīr (the Manifest), from khā' – al-khabīr (the Aware), from dhāl – al-zakī (the Pure). These seven letters are the seven seals for the seven days, and Allah the Most High is the bestower of good success.

Those seven seals are these:
