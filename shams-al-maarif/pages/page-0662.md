# Page 662 (English translation)
Until the point when this reality is known, only then should it be described with an attribute. Because it does not accept analysis. Thus, in this same capacity, the world is found through God, the Exalted.
[Heading:] All other things are contingent; God alone is eternal:
Thus, it is as though the Truth created us from pre-eternal existence, and the third known is the entire universe—the celestial orbs, the earthly realms, the air, the water, and all things. And the fourth known is an allusion to creation. God, the Exalted, says:
"And He has made subject to you whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth, all together, from Himself."
It is related in a tradition that God, the Exalted, created beneath every particle of the Throne creatures equal to this world. Thus He says:
"And none knows the hosts of your Lord except He."
"Your troops are known to none save Him."
[Heading:] The reader of this name receives unveiling and perception:
Whoever seeks proximity through this name will, by its virtue, have unveiling and perception granted to him. He should recite it in seclusion according to its numerical value; when the count is complete, his subordinate, the Muqtāʾīl, will appear. This is the commander of four officers, and under each officer are 36 ranks of subordinates.
[Heading:] If written and kept by a woman on Monday whose pregnancy is prone to miscarriage, her pregnancy will not be lost
Among its properties is that when it is written on a piece of paper, a woman whose pregnancy is prone to miscarriage should keep it with her—her pregnancy will not be lost. The name of the subordinate and its dhikr should also be written around it. When this name equals the numerical value of any person's name, it will be the Greatest Name on their behalf. And its dhikr is this:
"In the name of God, the Beneficent, the Merciful: ṭāʾ. O God, You are the Former of forms and the Former of the subtle details of wonderful works, and the Former of the varying forms of the ideal world whose images are invented and whose compositions—O Inventor of its model, I ask You—and the Former of the superior images with their forms and realities, of the beautiful and the ugly, and the beautiful—all from Your grace. You are the Inventor of pairs and the Bringer-forth of bodies. I ask You by the secret of Your extension in the superior and inferior worlds, that You send upon me Your beneficence and Your blessings. O God, You are the Bestower of the Most Generous toward creatures by the blessing of bringing into existence. I ask by the right of this established secret, that You extend to me sustenance from Your sustenance, through which You unveil for me the realities of the corporeal images. O Creator, O Fashioner, O Former, O God, O Provider and Originator and Restorer. Answer, O Muqtāʾīl, and fulfill my need."
