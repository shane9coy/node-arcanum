[Heading enclosed in decorative brackets: Chapter 8: The Four Seasons and Their Specific Angels]

May God the Most High grant me and you success in His obedience and in understanding the mysteries of His names. Know that the essence of this book depends upon this chapter; in it are great mysteries. When you intend to perform the operation with these blessed names of divine grace and the names of angels that administer the order of the world, and when you work with the names of the winds and the celestial bodies, you should know that there are twelve months in the year. These are divided into four types, and each type consists of three months: the hot season, the cold season, the spring season, and the autumn season. Each of these seasons has a beginning day. The first beginning is of the spring season, which begins in March. The second beginning is of the hot season, which begins in June. The third beginning is for the autumn season, which begins in September. The fourth beginning is for the autumn season, which begins in December.

[Heading enclosed in decorative brackets: The Angels Who Administer the World]

This chapter is an exposition of the names of those kings who administer the world. The name of the angel in charge of the East is Rifāʾīl. The name of the angel in charge of the West is Dardiyāʾīl. The name of the angel in charge of the North is Ismāʾīl, and the name of the angel in charge of the South is Raḥāʾīl. The angel of the East is associated with the hot season. The angel of the West is for the cold season. The angel of the North is associated with the spring season, and the angel of the South is for the autumn season.
