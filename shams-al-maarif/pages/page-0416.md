# Page 416 (English translation)
And the moon and the stars and all things for our master Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, I ask You to subjugate for me the servant of this chapter by the right of Your most beautiful names, O Allah, O Swift, O Near, O Responsive, O Extender, O Loving, O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, I ask You by the light of Your face which has filled the pillars of Your throne, and by Your power with which You have power over all Your creation, and by Your mercy which has encompassed all things: there is no god but You, O Helper of those seeking help, help me. 41

And fulfill my need, O Allah, O Merciful, O Compassionate, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. O Allah, place my love in the heart of the servant of the chapter—those whom He loves have been deeply filled with love, they love them as the love of God, and those who believe are most fervent in love for God, He loves them and they love Him. Absolutely not, do not obey him and prostrate and draw near.

Friday's Practice:

"And that they worship Me—this is a straight path." Q. Truly this Qur'an guides to that which is most upright. O Allah, I ask You, O Forbearing, O Knowing, O Knower of the unseen, O Light, O Most High, O Subtle, O Guide, that You subjugate for me the servant of this chapter. Answer, O White One, hearing and obedient, by the right of the Sovereign who has overcome you, whose command you must obey, and by the right of Jakhṭayīl, to whom ascends the good word and righteous deed which he elevates. I swear upon you, O Jakhṭayīl, and you, O White One: glory be to the One who frees every indebted one, glory be to the One who relieves every grieved one, glory be to the One who liberates every imprisoned one, glory be to the One who made water flow in seas and springs, glory be to the One who made His treasures between kāf and nūn, glory be to the One who, when He desires a thing, says to it "Be" and it is. 31 times

O Allah, subjugate for me the servant of this chapter as You subjugated the sea for Moses, and the fire for Abraham, and the mountains and iron for David, and the jinn and mankind and the devils for Solomon—peace be upon them—and the sun and the moon and all things for our master Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace. I ask You to subjugate for me the servant of this noble chapter, and by the right of Your great and most great name, and by the right of Your most beautiful names, O Allah, O Swift, O Near, O Responsive, O Extender, O Loving—5 times

O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, I ask You by the light of Your face which has filled the pillars of Your throne, and by Your power with which You have power over all Your creation, and by Your mercy which has encompassed all things: there is no god but You, O Helper of those seeking help, help me and fulfill my need in this hour by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. O Allah, place my love in the heart of the servant of this chapter—those whom He loves have been deeply filled with love, they love them as the love of God, and those who believe are most fervent in love for God, He loves them and they love Him. Absolutely not, do not obey him and prostrate and draw near.
