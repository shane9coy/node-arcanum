# Page 314 (English translation)
Abdullah Tarani says: When something is hidden from a person or he has lost his way, if he mentions the Name with sincere intention and firm resolve along with its numerical value, Almighty Allah will show him the way and whatever he desires will be fulfilled. Whoever frequently mentions it, Almighty Allah will illuminate his outward and inward. If he maintains this state, it will manifest from his inner being onto his face, and in solitude he will begin to be illuminated by its light until the place of solitude is filled with this light. In the mention of this Name, there are secrets for the people of guidance and lights for the people of manifestations. Whoever in a dark place closes his eyes and mentions this Name until his working principle dominates him, he will witness the divine lights that fill his heart. This noble Name is suitable for spiritual unveilings in its practice. If one combines with it the Name Badi' and mentions it in solitude with fasting, spiritual exercises, sincere intention, and purity of heart, he will have no need of a lamp. This is the mention of the people of insight among the people of Allah. Its square is of great nobility. Its worth is known only by those with pure hearts. This is its square:

Chapter 94: The Properties of the Name Hadi - Goodness in All Matters and Desires Fulfilled

For every traveler on the path, reciting this Name in his practice is effective like camphor. This is from the noble Names. Its square should be written as follows: whoever has weak memory should keep it with him and frequently mention the Name, then there will be improvement in all outward and inward affairs. If he engraves this square on a silver ring in the exaltation of the Moon and keeps it with him, he will be granted success in all good deeds. If it is placed around the neck of a child, he will never cry and will begin to drink milk. Whoever has lost his way, if he mentions this Name, he will find the path; whatever task he intends, it will be accomplished. Whoever enters darkness and says "Hadi Ahdani," he will find his desire. Among its secrets for the people of spiritual states, it is from the words and dhikr of Israel, peace be upon him. If on Monday during the first hour when the Moon is increasing in light, one writes it on a tablet four times and fumigates it with its leaves, and reads it fifty times every day, it will neither increase nor decrease nor deteriorate. It has special efficacy in its mention for kings and nobles. Any king who mentioned this Name so much that his working principle dominated him, all countries and inhabitants will obey him. Among the travelers on the path, whoever wishes to advance toward the higher world, mentioning it is also beneficial for him. Its number is 20, the Zabih al-Ruh fard lakh (125,000). Its 21 and 22 refer to the Name Hasib of Almighty Allah. The numerical values of its letters, 125, refer to the Pure Name, because understanding the lost path is essential in guidance.
