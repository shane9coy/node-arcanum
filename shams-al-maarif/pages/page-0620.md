# Page 620 (English translation)
618

**From His manifestation, 28 letters were created:**
It is for this same reason that when Alif sought the letters with its luminous manifestation, then 28 letters descended with mercy, and these 28 types were completed in the essence of letters; rather, this is in the manifestation of Divine Power. Then a second manifestation occurred, which is specifically associated with their description.

**One letter's approach to Allah**
So these letters went forth with their essence, calling upon the upper and lower realms. This is the manifestation of the Will. Then from among all these letters, one letter became the closest to the Divine Presence, so that by its means there would be no reality of authority over the other letters.

**The rank of Alif is elevated above all letters:**
So Israfel was with them in proximity to this manifestation, because this letter Alif has an elevated rank above all letters, and it stands with the forehead of destiny and is extended with the forehead of creation. Then one letter was distinguished for the One Who Encompasses, which is Maqbul al-Sirr (the accepted of the secret), and after the division, it gathers all in the very gathering. This is the letter "A," and Allah the Most High has made it the chief of mysteries, and with it He bestowed favor upon our master, the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him: "Did We not expand for you your breast?" (al-Sharh: 1).

**Types of movement:**
Then when Alif manifested, it became deserving of being described with movement, and then after this with stillness, due to its connection in the beginnings and ends, the completion of purposes and the finalities are directed toward it. The movements also have three types: raf' (raising), nasb (raising), and khaff (lowering)—that is, before, above, and below—and striking and definition, and this is not the place of emphasis.

**The mysteries and symbols of the word Allah:**
From this Alif, the first Lam appeared as silent, and then the second Lam as moving, and after it the Alif became silent, and after it the essence of encompassing, meaning "H," appeared, and the mystery of movement and stillness was gathered in it. For this reason, it is the Hidden of the Hidden, and the mystery of the expanded breast is also in it. So from Alif there is an indication toward the Essence, and from the first Lam toward the covenant of the pact, and from the second Lam toward the multiplicity of nature. From Lam-H there is an indication toward the additional covenant, and it is the acceptance of the religious rulings in the world.

**Explanation of the circulation of Alif:**
Because in it is the mystery of Alif, then "A" is the intermediary for all affairs. On the Day of Resurrection for all the first and the last, thus this wisdom has circulated in 14 letters, among which Alif is the first of all and also the last.

**The letters of Alif are 14, and the earth and heaven are also 14:**
The explanation is that both Alifs and both Lams are four letters. Multiplying them by three gives 12, and "H" is one letter, making 13, whose sum is 14. The seven heavens and the seven earths together are 14. Alif is between them, subtle, firm, and wise. All are established by the remembrance of the Divine Name; rather, every particle in the depths of the world is established by this very Name of Allah. Everything prostrates to Allah and is established by Him.
Thus Allah the Most High says: "And to Allah prostrates whoever is in the heavens and the earth" [Al-Ra'd: 15]
