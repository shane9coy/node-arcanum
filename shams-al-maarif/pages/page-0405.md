Saying "Allahu Akbar," strike the hand three times and recite this:

O Allah, I indeed ask You, O He whose power is dominant and whose sign is radiant and whose vengeance is cutting and crushing for every tyrant—through the power by which You have taken control of their souls, and had You taken it away they would become still. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, and grant blessing and peace. O Allah, show me Your sufficiency against whoever has wronged me, O He who breaks the tyrants and the arrogant ones, and cuts off the根基 of the Pharaohs and the mockers. How quickly Your severe punishment descends, and how quickly Your noble domination comes! Against every stubborn tyrant and rebellious Satan who has transgressed against me with defiance and rebelled in the lands and committed corruption therein. O Allah, by You I seek help against whoever has wronged me. I ask You, O my Master, to grant me victory against whoever has oppressed me, and to defeat whoever has confronted me, and to overpower whoever has fought me, and to abandon my enemies and to scatter them, and to make them drink scalding water, and to make them fuel for Hellfire, and to send upon their bodies burning coals from the sky, so that they become smooth ground, or so that their water becomes deep—they shall never be able to seek him. You are the Irresistible, the Proud, the Seizer, the Helper, the Strong, the Victorious, the Conqueror, the Humiliator, the Avenger, the Destroyer, the Severe, the Punisher, the Delayer, the Withholder, the Seizer, the Harmer, the Crusher, Possessor of severe might, Possessor of mighty strength.

O Allah, strike whoever wronged me, for Allah seized them because of their sins, and they had no protector from Allah. So they became in their homes, kneeling. O Allah, seize him as the mountain seizes, and may Allah utterly destroy him. The command of Allah has come, so do not hasten it. There is no god but Allah.
