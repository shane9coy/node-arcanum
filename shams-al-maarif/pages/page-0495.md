[Underlined heading] Rules for performing any operation:

When you wish to perform any task, take the letters of the name of the person with whom you wish to do it, perform the numerical expansion, and see which part is desired; bring it accordingly and perform the operation of doing or health or illness. For example, the name of the desired one is Muhammad. When its letters are expanded numerically, they will be 22 letters. In this manner: 'M' 'Y' 'M' 'H' 'A' 'Y' 'M' 'Y' 'M' 'D' 'A' 'L' 'A' 'R' 'B' 'A' 'H' and their numbers are 600. Then after that, bring the numbers mentioned above and bring letters from the Speech of God in the same manner, whether the purpose is health—that is, well-being—or for a sick person. At that time, perform the expansion of the word of negation and affirmation—that is, La ilaha illa Allah—with this expansion, and complete the operation by adding to it.
