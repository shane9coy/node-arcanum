Ḥaḍrat al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī, may the mercy of Allah be upon him, states that the verse "And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion" means "In the name of Allah," and the verse "And He imposed on them the word of piety, and they were most entitled to it and its people" also refers to "In the name of Allah."

The Miraculous Effects of the Basmala:

Whoever writes "Bismillah" with faith in its greatness, he will be recorded among the intimate ones with Allah the Most High. It is narrated from Ḥaḍrat ʿAkrimah, may Allah be pleased with him, that first there was only Allah, and there was nothing else, so Allah the Most High created light. After light, He created the Preserved Tablet and the Pen, and commanded the Pen to write upon the Tablet whatever would happen until the Day of Resurrection. Thus, the very first thing the Pen wrote upon the Tablet was "Bismillah," so Allah designated it as the source of safety. This is for the one who recites it. The angels and all the inhabitants of heaven recite it. It was revealed first upon Ḥaḍrat Ādam, peace be upon him, and it was "Bismillah." At that time, he said: "Now my offspring will be protected from punishment if they continue to recite it." Then afterward, it ascended to heaven. Then it was revealed upon Ḥaḍrat Ibrāhīm, peace be upon him, when he was in the catapult. By its blessing, the fire became a garden. Then after him, it ascended to heaven.

Then it was revealed upon Ḥaḍrat Sulaymān, peace be upon him, at which time the angels said: "Now the kingdom of Sulaymān is complete." Then Allah the Most High commanded that all the causes and treasures that are in the air and [all things] marked with the signs of the Sunnah should go to Ḥaḍrat Sulaymān, peace be upon him. Accordingly, all presented themselves before Ḥaḍrat Sulaymān, peace be upon him. Ḥaḍrat Sulaymān ascended the pulpit and recited before them the verse of faith, namely "Bismillah." All the people were pleased and said: "We all bear witness that O Sulaymān son of Dāwūd, peace be upon them both, truly you are the messenger of Allah the Most High." Then it was raised toward heaven. Then it was revealed upon Ḥaḍrat Mūsā, peace be upon him, and by its blessing, Ḥaḍrat Mūsā, peace be upon him, subdued and conquered Pharaoh, Qārūn, and their armies. Then after them, it ascended to heaven. Then "Bismillah" was revealed upon Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā, peace be upon him. Allah the Most High sent revelation to him saying: "O son of Maryam, you know what this verse is." Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā, peace be upon him, said: "Yes, O my Lord, I know." He said: "This verse is of safety. The one who recites 'Bismillah' by day and by night, walking and moving, all [the rest of the text continues on the next page] the slanderer, on the Day of Resurrection, whoever has it in his record of deeds, we will forgive his previous and subsequent sins."

When this deputy of Allah arrived at their door, he saw that rain was falling and they were waiting for him outside. At that time, a rainbow had appeared.
