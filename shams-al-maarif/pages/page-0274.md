For this reason, the manifestation of knowledge is from the holy realm and the angelic spirits, which are specialized for the instruction of the prophets, and the most excellent among all of them is our noble Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

He to whom revelation was sent with humility—exalted is Allah—said: "Taught him one of severe strength" (Quran 53:5), meaning the One of mighty power taught him. And since the spirit of Jesus, upon him be peace, is under the influence of Gabriel, upon him be peace—that is, upon him be peace—he was the most noble of the prophets in the subtleties of sciences and the delicacies of words.⁽¹⁾

The science of letters is also from among these sciences of his. Therefore, the name Nun has special suitability with this subject. Thus, from Nun is meant the science of universals: Sin from the form of the comparative (asmā al-tafdīl), and Alif from the superlative (ajall). Its number is 150, and this is the name "the Knower" (al-ʿālim), while the science of particulars is manifest. Regarding this it was said "the Knowing One" (al-ʿalīm), and this points to it. Its name "the Knowing One" is written, its number is 141, its letters' names are 292, pointing to the name "the All-Seeing" (al-baṣīr). And while the verse is attributed to "the Knower" (ʿālim) for the reversed word (muqlab), there is complete connection with it. Sometimes it is said that there is confusion in obtaining all meanings together, and this is what he considered: that knowledge in "knowledge of the essence of a thing" is the obtaining of the form of a thing. The meaning of "knower" (ʿālim) in any case is that it refers to the person upon whom the essence of a thing has become manifest; the manifestation is connected with everything. Its interior is from those secrets concerning which exaggeration is not correct, because among them are sciences that extend until after the particularities of all existing things. Exaggeration concerning them can only be with one of two matters: either it is with the immersion (fī bahrat al-istighrāq), so he is called "the Knower" (al-ʿālim), then it indicates descent for the enjoyment of subtle realities; or it is with immersion (fī bahīrat al-bahr), so he is called "the Knowing One" (al-ʿalīm), the one who holds knowledge of subtleties. "The All-Knowing" (al-ʿalīm) is the one who holds knowledge of all particulars and manifest things.

Exalted Allah said: "And above every possessor of knowledge is one who knows" (Quran 12:76). The one called "possessor of knowledge" (dhī al-ʿilm) knows the universals of matters, and the "knower" (ʿālim) knows the subtleties of matters; "the Knowing One" (al-ʿalīm) is the one who holds knowledge of apparent and hidden matters. Some scholars have taken the word in this place as meaning "knower" and understand the superiority of knowledge as multiplicity of information. They say that the verse "And above every possessor of knowledge is one who knows" is with particulars. This is not correct, because if this were the meaning, why would Exalted Allah say to Moses, upon him be peace: "Our servant Khidr, upon him be peace, at the meeting place of the two seas, who holds more knowledge than you" (Quran 18:60-65), while Khidr, upon him be peace, did not hold more knowledge than Moses, upon him be peace? This is because Exalted Allah said regarding Moses, upon him be peace: "And We wrote for him upon the tablets admonition for everything and a detailed explanation of everything" (Quran 7:145). The meaning of this is that they know the interior of information as they know its exterior. Therefore, their station was at the meeting place of the two seas—that is, the sea of the apparent and the sea of the hidden—it was attributed to Moses, upon him be peace, that they are upon knowledge given by Allah, which no one else knows. O you who know! You also strive so that this knowledge is given to you. And to this knowledge our noble Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is pointed by the verse: "And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge" (Quran 20:114)—meaning: O my Lord, increase my knowledge. The excellence of knowledge is well-known; understand its meaning. Allah, exalted, gives success.
