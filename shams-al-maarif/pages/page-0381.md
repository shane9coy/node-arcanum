# Page 381 (English translation)
This is the form of the names.

[Circular talisman featuring calligraphy in a circular border around a complex interwoven geometric central motif]

(Chapter: Protection of Wealth and Commerce)

Whoever fears thieves, robbers, and the like regarding his wealth or merchandise, let him write these names on paper and place them in the chest of the merchandise or both; then Almighty Allah will protect him. This is the image of this form:

[A square 4x4 grid talisman on the left side of the text, containing various Arabic letters or short words in each cell, with decorative calligraphic extensions from the top and sides]

(Chapter: Protection from the Evil of Enemies)

Whoever fears the evil of an enemy who harbors malicious intent against him, and desires to be protected from the enemy's evil, should perform two supererogatory prayers after the prayer of the desired one. In the first unit (rak'ah), after Al-Fatihah, he should recite Surah Qul or "Say: O disbelievers." In the second unit, after Surah Al-Fatihah, he should recite "Qul a'udhu bi-rabbi al-falaq" and "Allahumma ya kafi aknini sharra fulan bin fulanah" and mention the name of whoever he wishes. Let him write these names and place them in his turban; verily Almighty Allah will be sufficient for him against what he fears.

(The Operation to Stop Urination)

Surah Al-Kawthar is a very effective incantation for stopping urination. When you wish to stop someone's urination, write it upon an eggshell during the inauspicious hour, writing his name and his mother's name, wrap it in yellow or black cloth, and bury it near the fire. Verily the urination will be stopped continuously. If on the seventh day it is dug up and not removed, the patient will die, and no one will be able to restore his urination until the Day of Resurrection. This is the form of the names that are written on his back. Its incense consists of sal ammoniac, bitter colocynth, and black pepper.
