His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, peace be upon him, states that we have the Red Jafr and the White Jafr and the Comprehensive Jafr. As for the Red Jafr, it is that which is explained in this noble verse: **O Prophet, fight against the disbelievers and the hypocrites and be harsh upon them** (meaning: fight the disbelievers and hypocrites and be strict with them). And the White Jafr is this: **We will gradually lead them on from where they do not know** (meaning: soon We will raise them degree by degree in a manner they will not recognize). And the Comprehensive Jafr is this: **Allah erases what He wills and keeps firm what He wills, and with Him is the Mother of the Book** (meaning: Allah abolishes what He wishes and maintains what He wishes; with Him is the Mother of the Book, that is, the Preserved Tablet).

After Imam Ali, peace be upon him, the perfect caliphs from among his descendants were also knowledgeable of these secrets.

**The response of Imam Ali al-Rida to the Abbasid Caliph:**
When an Abbasid caliph wrote to His Eminence Imam Ali al-Rida, may Allah be pleased with him, requesting his allegiance, he said: "You, O king, know our right and you know that the Jafr will not indicate [the validity of] your allegiance. Most of the scholars have been kept unaware of this science of the Jafr because it contains divine wisdom and sacred interests. And the Imam mentioned the ministers of the seven continents, and all the events that will occur until the Day of Resurrection have been made manifest."

**Seven kings have measured the earth:**
This division of the seven continents is not a natural division of the world, but rather these lines are the same ones that the former kings—who traversed the earth—established, such as Ifridun the Nabatean, Awaj the Himyari, His Eminence Solomon son of David, His Eminence Alexander, and Ardashir son of Babak, the Persian kings, who in their time measured the earth and determined its length from east to west and its breadth from north to south. The earth, together with all that is within it—mountains and rivers—is in comparison to its heavens like [something] within a desert.

**The number and magnitude of the stars of heaven:**
Its mention in the Book of Allah is: one thousand and one stars, each one of which is very large, far from its place. Each one of them is a thousand times larger than the volume of the earth. The blessed name of His Eminence Muhammad, the Chosen One, may the blessings of Allah be upon him and his family, and his noble Attribute are written in the celestial column.
