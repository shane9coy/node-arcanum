# Page 491 (English translation)
491

**The Dhikr Practitioner Advances:**
The first point is that the advancement of the dhikr practitioner lies in revealing the encompassing and protective circles. From the density of the great constituent parts of the compound world, and the spiritual power's intervention in the intellectual realm, and the undertaking of truthfulness, and the imitation of the operations of practical manuscripts, and the increase of the heart's power that emanates from the heart—this is the intellectual power that generates good effects in the very essence of the celestial bodies. In the terminology of the chapters, this is called "effective courage" (himmath mu'aththarah). Ponder this and understand it. The third [ego] is that of noble execution. This corresponds in the form of similitude towards the lower arrangement. And establishing wisdom, or destiny, or position of the whole with it—with intervention, until the movement of the interior becomes through it, and this is the movement of the exterior.

**Several Methods for the Proximate Ones:**
The first method is for attaining the presence of the Lord of the Worlds through the methods of the proximate ones, in which there are the lights of both lamps and the hearts of both knowing ones, and they are lamps for both darknesses and shadows. This is the reality for which the devoted ones are faithful. In our joining of this writing, that the lights of hearts and the spiritual causes of the absolutely elevated are one reality. Allah the Exalted says: "Indeed this is in the former scriptures, the scrolls of Abraham and Moses" "This reality has been mentioned in the first correct scrolls by Abraham and Moses."

**Obtaining the Perfection of the Interior:**
He who has pierced it is manifest—the jewels—that one who has become perfect in the states of the interior. With the words of truth and abandoning the works of the dense. With the works of the malakuti realm, as Allah the Exalted says: "He has made successors on the earth, and variations in your tongues." He who has completed this reality, the light that he possesses is like the light of the moon. In the realm of barzakh, the Lord's light is with him due to the subtlety of his light, with the movement of the elevated. The elevated songs become audible: For him, the words of the elevated become audible to hear. With the instrument producing sounds (various sounds emanate from it), it is made audible to him with sound. This is a very great sign among his specific signs, for which the devoted ones are sympathizers.

**Miracles Issue from Prophets, Charisms from Saints:**
Miracles issue from prophets, charisms and signs from saints. He says: "and variations in your tongues"—of voices. In truth, within this very realm, communion with the Throne-bearing angels becomes possible. For the people of such states, the reality of the creation of creatures, the universe, and animals is revealed, and the establishment of tawhid within them that has been deposited within them—this is the ultimate limit of revealing the secrets of hearts. But the revelation of the reality of taqiyya in the spiritual manuscripts is that they be connected to the nasut [material world] with the luminous bright mysteries of wisdoms. These words and expressions have been attempted to be stated, but the path of indications for it is extremely subtle. If one khalifa were sent down upon it, they could achieve dominance over the entire universe. Which are exclusively reserved for Allah's grace, for which there is no meaning and no response. Their knowledge and practice is a portion of light, which [a person] may be included in, by Allah's grace.
