# Page 585 (English translation)
583

It will be obtained in every creature.

The virtue of the letter Waw is very powerful, and its deputy is Hazayil. When you intend to perform its service, after the spiritual discipline, write the letter and place it upon your head, and recite the invocation thirty-eight times after each prayer. Its spiritual discipline lasts forty days. The appointed deputy will appear and say: "Your service has been fulfilled." Burn incense, go forth, light candles. Whenever you say "O servant of the letter," the deputy will immediately appear.

The form of the sigil of Badhuh is as follows:

[4x4 magic square containing Arabic letters]

| Bāʾ | Dāl | Wāw | Ḥāʾ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ḥāʾ | Wāw | Bāʾ | Dāl |
| Dāl | Bāʾ | Ḥāʾ | Wāw |
| Wāw | Ḥāʾ | Dāl | Bāʾ |

No thief may enter:

Write this sigil upon a stone and affix it to a wall; no thief will enter it. When you enter a village and enchanted water appears before you, write the letter Waw upon a piece of wood and cast it into that water. It will dry up.

To blind and silence robbers:

Take a handful of earth, breathe upon it while invoking the letter Waw, and throw it upon the robbers—they will all be blinded. For silencing the tongue, it is also beneficial if this letter is written along with the verses appropriate for binding speech.

With its help, treasure is obtained:

The letter is also employed for extracting treasure. One should speak thus:

"Respond, O servant of the Bāʾ, and be my helper in what I desire... O Allah, I ask You, O Lord of Lords, O Provider of creation without measure, to subjugate for me the spiritual essence of this letter, that it may fulfill my needs, for You are the Most Wise, stronger than the strong, and in You I seek aid, and You are the One sought for aid, and upon You is reliance. There is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great. Respond, O servant of the letter Bāʾ, Yahiyoub, mover of spirits and stabilizer of unions. Expel: Yahiyoub 2 and Karkoub 2, Liʿawat 2, Miqoub 2 and Sayoub 2. Respond by the right of him whom You chose, Ayyoub, and by the chosen, the beloved, upon him be peace, for the righteousness that belongs to Allah I have summoned you and taken your forelock by the One who said: 'To whom belongs the sovereignty today?' To Allah, the One, the Conqueror, the Giver, the Giver, the Giver, who provides for whom He wills without measure."

And its breaking of the fast is as follows:

"Respond, O servant of the letter Bāʾ, the master Hazayil, by the star of the shining sphere of light, the possessor of blessings and majesty..."

From this, this secret has been revealed to me: that the creature who seeks livelihood through this, the deputy of this letter shall subjugate itself to me, so that it may fulfill my need. I direct you to this divine light and seek aid from you alone, for You are the Helper and in You alone is trust, and there is no power over good or evil.
