# Page 127 (English translation)
He is. Everyone who, in his authority and spiritual realm with his power, overpowers the proud, the arrogant, the rebellious, and the tyrants with great victory, strength, and grip—for those who have become rebellious and disobedient. He destroys the disobedient and the rebellious who associated partners with His majesty and greatness. Oh, seize those who have perished with the disobedient. I ask you to subjugate the hearts of your creation for me.

Oh one who melts iron, oh one who softens iron for David, peace be upon him, oh evil spirit, oh King of Kings of all the worlds, make me the owner of So-and-so's forehead. Until all the enemies come into my possession. Oh Most Merciful of the merciful, we worship only You and seek help only from You. Indeed, I have made intention for my sight and understanding. Oh Being whose greatness made all the proud and arrogant bow, and before whose glory the rebellious of jinn and humans were humiliated. Oh one with firm grip, oh one with great vengeance, oh one with all majesty, exacting vengeance from the base. Assist me with your own help and with clear victory, until I gain victory over all my enemies. Grant us from yourself the gifts of the truthful ones and place us among the presence of the martyrs and the righteous, and help us with support for our success and victory, as You said in Your true statement: "Your Lord will help you with three thousand angels."

Grant us authority over the universe and governance. Pour Your blessings upon us as You have poured them upon the early and the late. Do not make us among the misguided and those who mislead. Do not place our fate among the rebellious. Oh Guardian of the worthy, nurture me with Your hidden grace. Indeed, You have covered over Your grace, and one who made a mistake found safety. There is no deity but You; blessed is Your pure essence, and I am among the wrongdoers. Admit me into Your realm of mercy, which is a lofty, sufficient protection and a fortified refuge, and grant me abundant sustenance from the treasuries of Your mercy—that mercy which encompasses all things. Oh Opener of difficulties, open every difficulty for me. With Your mercy, oh Most Merciful of the merciful.

I adjure you by the honor of God, by the light of His essence, and by the Opening of the Book, and by that with which the command was issued from God—that you accept and hurry to fulfill my need. Indeed, His command is that when He intends a thing, He says to it "Be," and it becomes.

If one recites the Surah of Glorification three times. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: Whoever recites Surah Fatiha three times and Surah Ikhlas three times, he will remain safe from everything except death. When Imam Hasan and Imam Husayn, may God be pleased with them, became ill, His Honor wrote a supplication, and God Almighty sent revelation: Recite Surah Fatiha forty times over water, then wash their hands, feet, mouth, and entire body with it, and God Almighty will grant healing. Scholars say: Whoever writes Surah Fatiha on a pure vessel and gives it to drink to a patient, God Almighty will heal him. Whoever suffers from frequent forgetting, let him write Surah Fatiha in a vessel, erase it, drink it, and the affliction of forgetfulness will cease. Whoever recites it abundantly, his heart and inner self will become purified from all the dangers of the soul and satanic inspirations. Whoever writes it on a glass vessel, washes it with oil of balsam, and uses it for massage for spinal pain and backache, will find relief; it will also benefit the brain and paralysis, and it is beneficial for every illness and disease. Whoever writes Surah Fatiha in a gold vessel during the first hour of Friday with musk, saffron, and rose water, washes it with rose water, stores it in a bottle, and when going to any ruler, applies a little of it to his face, will achieve success and honor. Whoever goes to any ruler and recites Surah Fatiha will remain safe from his evil. Imam Sha'bi, may God have mercy on him, suffered from back pain and said: Treat it with the foundation of the Holy Quran—that is, Surah Fatiha. He wrote it, recited it, drank it, and found relief. Ibn Abbas, may God be pleased with him, said: Everything has a foundation, the foundation of the Holy Quran is Fatiha, and the foundation of Surah Fatiha is Bismillah.
