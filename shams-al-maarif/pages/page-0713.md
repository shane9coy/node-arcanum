# Page 713 (English translation)
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Majid"

"Majid" is like "Majid" [the Glorious], however "Aalim" is used in the sense of "Aleem" [the Knowing], and its dhikr is as follows:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful

O Allah, You are al-Majid [the Glorious], al-Majid [the Glorious], the Doer of what You will. And the severe promise [is Yours]: I ask You to fulfill my need, O One who brings the living forth from the dead and brings the dead forth from the living. Your command is between al-Kaf and al-Nun, and You say to a thing "Be" and it becomes. The Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Creator of all things from non-existence, without precedent, without an advisor, and without a manager. Glory be to You, there is no god except You, al-Latif [the Subtle] al-Khabir [the Aware]. You are al-Wahid al-Majid [the One, the Glorious]. I ask You to bring forward the good things before me and to grant me my share according to Your action concerning my secret in perfection, and I ask You—and You are al-Wajid al-Mawjud [the Finder, the Found]. Grant my need and subjugate for me the servant of the Name, King Mahyatiyal, upon him be peace. Indeed, You are over all things Omnipotent.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wahid al-Ahad"

Know that in technical terminology, "Wahid" refers to the first number, and "Ahad" is that which cannot be divided into parts—for example, every odd number, and likewise a point cannot be divided. And Allah the Exalted is truly "Wahid" [One] in the sense that it is impossible for Him to be divided among anyone.

Allah is without equal; there is nothing like unto Him. That thing whose substance cannot be composed and whose plural cannot be formed—verily that thing is matchless, for there is no second of its kind in its genus. Then this matchlessness may be with respect to time, in which case it would mean that in another time its like could be created; or it may be with respect to some qualities but not all qualities. Thus absolute oneness belongs exclusively to God Almighty, and "ahadiyyah" [uniqueness] comes to be used in the sense of specific distinction in His description, just as it is said: "Huwa Allahu Ahad" [He is Allah, the One]. And this is a negation-of-multiplicity attribute.

The sign of a subtle mystery and revelation is that its meanings create extraordinary perception within the intellect:

I tell you a subtle mystery and noble revelation, which is this: whichever name is subtle and great, its sign is that its meanings within the intellect will be extraordinary and of such a kind that its comprehension and perception will be beyond the reach of understanding. So know that such a name which is thus is close to the Greatest Name. Just as the name "Ahad" is not known from any other direction except Allah. This name is from the ancient divine names in eternity, because besides it there is no other existent. Through it He created the paradigms of the entire cosmos.

The one who is closer to the center will receive assistance more quickly; to him there will be greater revelation and acquaintance:

The entire cosmos consists of how many circles, within which are points—that is, centers. The closer one is to the point [center], by that much one hears inspiration from the unseen more quickly. Sometimes one hears it, and one's speech is what one obtains, and one has acquaintance with many things.

Now I conclude the exposition of these false notions. It has been established that true uniqueness belongs only to the Pure One [God], as the matters of sovereignty and felicity and vicegerency are from Him, and all these have a voice in Him—which is encompassed by His knowledge.

Thus He has said [Qur'an 112:1-2]: "He is Allah, One. Allah, the Eternal."
