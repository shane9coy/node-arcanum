# Page 612 (English translation)
**The child will remain protected from every misfortune**

When this letter is written and hung around children's necks, they will remain protected from every misfortune. And when it is written with sincerity on a hexagonal tablet and buried in a house, its inhabitants will be in agreement. This invocation should be read 300 times daily in seclusion; the assigned spirit will appear. Take a covenant and pact from him. This letter belongs to the Pure Ones. It causes sinking into the earth and killing and destruction. Its talisman is as follows:

[A rectangular diagram with a decorative, scalloped border. The letter 'ط' (Tah) is placed at each corner and at the midpoint of each side. A vertical line divides the rectangle into two equal halves, with the letter 'ط' at the top and bottom of this line. Inside each half, the letter 'ط' is written seven times in a symmetrical pattern: a row of three at the top, one in the center, and a row of three at the bottom.]

And the invocation for this letter is:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ ط ظَهَرَتْ قُدْرَتُكَ اللَّهُمَّ فِی الْآفَاقِ وَحَصَلَ مَا ظَهَرَ عَلَی الْآخَفَاقِ وَصَلَّ مَنْ ظَهَرَ بِالْإِصْدَادِ وَالْإِلْدَادِ أَسْئَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ بِمَا أَوْدَعْتَ أَلْبِيَاءَكَ وَ أَوْلِيَائَكَ مِنَ الْأَلْفَاظِ اللَّطِيْفَةِ الطَّاهِرَةِ الْعِظَامِ أَنْ تَظْهِرَنِيْ عَلَی كَشْفِ سِرِّ الطَّاءِ حَتَّىٰ أَضْرِبَ مَنْ تَطَاهَرِيْنَ عَلَی خَلْقِكَ بِالْأَذَی وَ الْفَوَاحِشِ بِمِيْزِ الْأَعْرَاضِ وَ الدَّلَالَةِ الْمُخَالِفَةِ الْأَمْرِ هَيَا بَا طَلَاهُ تَمَثَّلْ لِیْ حَتَّىٰ أَرَاکَ وَ أُخَاطِبَكَ أَجِبْ بِحَقِّ مَنْ قَالَ أَنَا اللَّهُ الَّذِيْ لَا إِلِهَ إِلَّا أَنَا وَ أَسْئَلُكَ يَا رَبِّ بِالْأَسْمَاءِ الْحُسْنَىٰ أَهِيًا يَا طَا بِحَقِّ وَ طَهَيَائِيلَ وَ طَهُورِيَا لِتَقِلْ إِظْهَرْ بِالْأَسْرَارِ النُّورَانِيَّةِ وَالْآيَاتِ الرَّبَّانِيَّةِ الْعَجَلَ الْوَحَا أَقْضِ حَاجَتِیْ بِحَقِّ الْوَاحِدِ الْقَهَّارِ وَ بِأَلْفِ لَا حَوْلَ وَ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيْمِ

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, Ţ. Your power has become manifest, O Allah, throughout the horizons, and what has appeared upon the earths has been accomplished. May He who has appeared with opposition and enmity be cursed. I ask You, O Allah, by what You have placed within Your prophets and saints of the noble, pure, fine expressions, that You manifest me in the disclosure of the secret of the Ţā', until I may strike those who act unjustly toward Your creation with harm and abominations by the scale of reputations and contrary indication. Come forth, O Ṭālāhū! Appear to me so that I may see you and address you. Answer by the right of Him who said, "I am Allah; there is no god except Me." And I ask You, O Lord, by the Most Beautiful Names, "Ahiyā, O Ṭā!" By the right of Wa-Ṭahayā'īl and Ṭahūriyā, speak: "Manifest with the luminous secrets and the divine signs! Come quickly, Wahā!" Fulfill my need by the right of the One, the Subduer, and by a thousand "There is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Exalted, the Great."

Its evocation is as follows:

Answer, O Hijjiya'īl, by the right of Hamṭarūsh, Sa'diya'īl, Satūl, Shimūh! Ṭā, ṭ, ṭ, ṭ, ṭ, ṭ, Yā[h], Yāmūhū! Come quickly, Wahā, this very hour!

**The Letter of Ghayn (غ)**

This letter is of red color. When it is written on a single paper along with the names of richness, and tied to one's head, sustenance will be granted, and everyone will love him.

**Desired: May he appear immediately:**

It is required that this be written with someone's name while the Moon is in its mansion, and after reading the Khulāṣa, one should blow upon it and bury it beneath a heavy stone. The beloved will come and be present, overwhelmed with longing.
