(Chapter 79: The Virtues of the Name Bār, Obtaining Blessings and the Acceptance of Prayer)

Whoever engages in frequent remembrance of this glorious Name shall have an abundance of blessings and pass his time in happiness. If he writes it on a silver tablet and keeps it with him, whatever supplication he makes shall be accepted. In this Name there is safety for travelers by sea and land. Whoever the traveler frequently remembers this Name, Almighty Allah shall ease his desires and purposes for him, his journey shall be easy and conducted with wellbeing, and his family and dependents shall remain safe. When a storm intensifies and the ship begins to sink, if the sailors frequently remember this Name at that time, they shall be protected from drowning and the wind shall become calm. If a drunkard, usurer, or person addicted to immoralities remembers this Name, Almighty Allah shall grant him the success of repentance. If a physically disabled person performs the recitation of this Name 700 times each day, Almighty Allah shall grant him the success of repentance and he shall refrain from his ugly deed. Its literal number is 402 and its Burhī number is 202. The first number is an even-odd compound which points, together with the Names Badīʿ, Fāṣim, and Ilā, toward al-Tajī, and they are worthy of seeking aid from Saʿīd without the article alif-lām. These numbers are from the surplus. Its components are 16 and 14, and its migratory names point toward Infak. The second number is also an even-odd compound, and this number is deficient. Its components are 204, and it points toward the angel Mīlā and Jāhil. Its square is depicted as follows.

(Chapter 80: The Virtues of the Name Tawwāb, Acceptance of Repentance and Removal of Evils)

Whoever the sincere person frequently remembers this Name, Almighty Allah shall make it easy for him to return to his origin. Every sincere person should remember it day and night without fail. This Name also has the property of removing roughness from the body. Its number is 415, an odd deficient rectangle. Its components point toward the saying "Huwa al-Ḥakīm" (He is the Wise), because there is wisdom in repentance, and they also point toward the Ṣaḥāḥ, because returning to the origin is returning to the practice of purification, where lights are radiant. Thus, the one who repents is as one who immerses himself in the river of light, wherein he becomes pure. Almighty Allah has said: "Indeed Allah loves those who repent" (inn Allāha yuḥibbu al-tawwābīna). The numbers of its recitation point toward the names and outcomes of the viziers. Its square is of great majesty. Only the people of Illuminationist Wisdom know its worth and rank.
