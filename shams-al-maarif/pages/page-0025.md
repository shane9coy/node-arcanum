# Page 25 (English translation)
25 ❖ The creature has knowledge of God, and is also merciful ❖ Types of praise; one should praise on every day of joy ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Ghanī (The Rich) ❖ Memory becomes strong through the sweetness of this name ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Mubdi' al-Mu'īd (The Originator who Returns) ❖ Through its sweetness presence (ḥuḍūrī) is attained; through its contemplation every task is accomplished ❖ Through it acquired knowledge (ʿilm ladunī) and blessings are obtained ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Ḥayy al-Mumīt (The Living Who Causes Death) ❖ Life and death are in God's possession ❖ Eternal life is attained through divine remembrance ❖ Through its remembrance a robe (khilʿa) is given ❖ Write on gold, silver, or deer's membrane ❖ Doors of goodness will open ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Ḥayy (The Living); the wisdom of life becomes manifest ❖ The method of dhikr of Ḥayy ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Qayyūm (The Sustainer) ❖ God established the intellects; the establishment of day is from the hours ❖ This same method in human creation is "the manifestation of the human" (numūd-i insān) ❖ The science of divine names is better than all sciences ❖ Scholars' disagreement about the Greatest Name (ism aʿẓam) ❖ The Greatest Name in Syriac and Hebrew ❖ The Greatest Name is the Pole of Poles (quṭb al-anām); it has a special effect of love ❖ Through its recitation-remembrance, spiritual guidance and charges (mawākīl) are obtained ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Awwal (The First) ❖ Through its remembrance curtains are lifted

❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Wāḥid (The One) ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Wāḥid al-Aḥad (The One the Unique) ❖ God is without form ❖ The noble head and the sign of unveiling (kashf) ❖ It will be near every center; its unveiling will increase ❖ Division of the circles ❖ The other seven circles ❖ The station of the truthful (ṣiddīqīn) ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Qādir al-Muqaddir (The Powerful the Ordainer) ❖ The establishment of the Resurrection is by His will ❖ God has no advisor or helper ❖ Fire does not burn it; it becomes cold ❖ The paralyzed one obtains healing ❖ The station of this dhākir is the elite (afrād) ❖ The secrets of destiny will be revealed to this dhākir ❖ The dhākir of this knows the fortunate and the wretched ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Muqaddim al-Mu'akhkhir (The Advancer the Delayer) ❖ Prophets and saints approach and fall short ❖ Through its remembrance creation will love ❖ The secret of this dhākir will become luminous ❖ Chapter on the virtues of the name al-Awwal al-Ākhir (The First the Last) ❖ God is First in existence and Last in permanence ❖ He created all; no one created Him ❖ His personal attribute is oneness (waḥda) ❖ In the hereafter, His majesty has no partner ❖ The saying of Shiblī ❖ His essence is without limit; His speech is without letters ❖ The saying of Junaid al-Baghdādī ❖ This God is the establishment in priority and the Lord in finality of origin
