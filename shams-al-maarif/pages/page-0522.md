# Page 522 (English translation)
And the woman whom he opens by measure, and he divides her wealth as a measured portion:
Her place shall be twenty years, and twenty multiplied in bounty.
There the lying Dajjal shall come to the people of Syria with kingdom and wealth.
With him a great mountain of thr (bread pudding), and his form is a novelty unprecedented.
His dwelling upon the earth shall be decreed for seven months, the number of perfection.
And the Messiah shall kill him in the land of Lydd, and creation shall rejoice in the manifestation.
And He shall kill his followers in every corner, and there shall remain no place for them therein.
And Gog and Magog shall come like a scattered flock from the side of the rising.
So neither the Euphrates river shall suffice them, nor the Seyhun and the heavy Tigris.
Nor the river of Syria and the Nile of Egypt, and the Sea of Suwayd emptied of water.
And they shall graze the vegetation, so no vegetation shall return, and they shall strip the leaves of the mountains.
And the sun shall rise from the west, with an inclination so that its heat may melt the hard stone.
It shall remain for three full days, and its heat shall burn the trees of the mountains.
And the bottom of the sea shall surely appear, and the wild beasts and birds shall perish.
And the clouds shall cease, so there shall be no clouds, no dew that returns, and no bounty.

During these days, the lying Dajjal shall make an exodus toward the people of Syria with kingdom and wealth, and with him there shall be a mountain of food, and his form shall be youthful and new. He shall dwell upon the earth for seven months. Then Jesus, peace be upon him, shall kill him in the place of Lydd, and shall show mercy to creation. With destruction and the Dajjal, Gog and Magog shall soon come—swiftly in a furious rush—and they shall drink so much water that neither the Euphrates River, nor the Seyhun, nor the Tigris shall suffice them, nor the river of Syria, nor the Nile of Egypt shall suffice, and the river of Suwayd shall also become empty of water. And grass shall grow so abundantly that there shall remain neither grass nor leaves upon the mountains. Then the sun shall rise from the direction of the west, whose heat shall melt the heated stones, and the sun shall remain in this state for three days, and its heat shall burn the trees of the mountains. The bottom of the sea shall become dry and appear, and the wild beasts and birds shall all perish, and clouds and rain clouds shall all cease.

And no virtue shall remain, nor alms, nor grace that returns, nor bounty.
And no child shall honor his parents, and the maternal aunt shall be made bald, and the paternal uncle shall be ashamed.
And [damaged source] upon the earth, as the (world) changes, and the conflagration by the thousands.
And Mecca and Medina shall be destroyed, and Jerusalem and the weighty deed.
And the Dome shall depart, and Hell, which ejects its worms from every [damaged source].
