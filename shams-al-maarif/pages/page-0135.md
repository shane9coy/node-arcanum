# Page 135 (English translation)
[135]

Creator of all things, and He is over all things a Guardian. Ṣādin qāf nūn fā. So Allah will suffice you against them, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Nor does He become weary of preserving them, and He is the Most High, the Most Great. And you cannot harm Him at all. Indeed, my Lord is over all things a Guardian. So Allah is the best Guardian, and He is the most Merciful of those who show mercy. For him there are successors from before him and from behind him, who guard him by the command of Allah. And We have placed guardians over them. That is the ordaining of the Mighty, the Knowing. And a guard from every rebellious Satan—Allah is a Guardian over them, and you are not over them a guardian. For every penitent one there is a Guardian. Rather, it is a glorious Quran in a preserved tablet. Trust, O Mayṭūrūn, so that they make the tongue of the owner of wealth mute.
