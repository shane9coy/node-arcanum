# Page 451 (English translation)
Your power has encompassed what Your knowledge has encompassed. I ask You, O Vast in Forgiveness, to forgive my sins and cover my flaws, and make me firm in matters and standing upon the subtleties of light and the residue, encompassing what is in the depths of hearts. And bring me out from darknesses to light, O Vast one.

The most beneficial invocation and duty:
The name al-Ḥakīm (The Wise) is a great name. It contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its client's name is Dardāyīl. It rules over four officers. Each officer oversees 78 ranks, and in each rank 78 clients are assigned. The prayer of al-Ḥakīm is:
"O Wise One, You are the One who perfected the foundations of existence with Your attributes, and You are the One who spread the light of Your knowledge in the hearts of Your beloved. Yours are the consequences of the actions You have revealed. I ask You by the secret of Your light in Your forms and by the life of Your spirit in the spirit of Your generosity, that You grant me supreme wisdom and knowledge of the most noble of things, until I know the end of the names and the limit of eternal subsistence. Verily, You are Allah, the Beneficent."

The operation for generating love:
The name al-Wadūd (The Loving) is a great name. Its client's name is ʿĪhayl. It rules over four officers. Each of these officers oversees 20 ranks, and in each rank 20 clients are assigned. All of these are under the command of Jibrāʾīl (Gabriel). They create affection among different people. The one who mentions this name, its client adorns him with two gifts: one external and one internal. From the internal comes love and kindness, and from the external, love for him is generated in everyone's heart. The prayer of al-Wadūd is:
"O Loving One, You are the One who declared the secret of love and affection in the hearts of the people of secrets, and You are the One who illuminated the seekers with the light of lights, and You manifested with everlasting glory and eternal light over the spirits—so You united the similitudes and You manifested the human being with the completion of the rarities of expression. And You increase generosity for the people of guardianship and tribulations by Your everlasting compassion for the people of certainty through knowledge and excellent care. I ask You, O Allah, by Your beautiful blessings and Your abundant favors, that You make me among Your friends who are enjoying Your bounty and favors, who remember You and are grateful for Your blessings and are returning to You, and You granted the life of eternity and You paired me with You in the presence of the light of Your face and Your generosity with the best of support, until I do not turn except to You, nor do I dwell except toward You, nor do I live except from You. So You are the provider for the people of gratitude, and You are the completer for whoever turned to You with tranquility."

The operation for knowing hidden good and secrets:
The name al-Majīd (The Glorious) is also one letter of the Greatest Name. Its client's name is Rifāʾīl. It rules over four officers. Each of these officers oversees 59 ranks, and in each rank 59 clients are assigned. Every one of them is a client of nobility, and they show the one who remembers [this name] matters that he did not understand before. The prayer of al-Majīd is:
"O Glorious One, You are the One who glorified Your essence with the magnifications of Your attributes, and You are the One who magnified Your side for us, O Rare One."
