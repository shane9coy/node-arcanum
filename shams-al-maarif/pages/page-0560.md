He may say: "Say: 'Become stones or iron'" (Qurʾān 17:50) – here "stones" refers to the fuel of Hell, and "iron" refers to chains and collars. For those whose manifestation will be in the ruling Sovereign, that is, in Hell – for this earthen body which has not been illuminated by the lights of the unseen; rather, it has remained solidified like a stone, and it has concluded that there is no way to perceive the observances.

The seventh reason is that Eternal Life sent forth its grace through Its Power, suitable for the sake of the Eternal One in preserving purity and in being made manifest, to reveal grace from Life for the sake of knowing the realities. This is the knowledge which the great one possesses. From the fact that something is perceived, there is a primary relation and a primary intellection – God, the Exalted, says:

"Yet they do not comprehend anything of His knowledge except what He wills. His Seat encompasses the heavens and the earth." (Qurʾān 2:255)

Thus, when the particle illā (except) has occurred together with the word of God in the verses, it is still attributed to His specific knowledge, to which it is connected. Knowledge belongs to Him and is His attribute, and the attribute is identical with His very Essence, and knowledge is His disclosure of everything other than Himself – both with respect to action and with respect to the truth.

**The Majesty and Splendor of Wisdom: The Revelation of Unveiled Mysteries:**

Then He sent forth from knowledge the grace of the realities of existing things and the former decrees, along with their manifestations, and wisdom, and tranquility, and discourse, and the words of mysteries and delights that belong to the field of universals and are established in the cosmos – through the subtle favors from the perspective of His generosity and forgiveness. Then He sent forth grace upon grace upon logic, so that it might be the cause of hearing His Mighty Book and taking a place in the oath, and of the unveiling of realities and wisdoms. For this very reason, when the Lord, Exalted be He, deemed it fitting to manifest His portion upon His servants, He guided them to hearing His Speech. Then He brought them through His knowledge to a grace of bounty and gift, by means of which the perception of the cosmos occurred in eternity, and they will witness the cosmos in destiny, and the manifestation of knowability in the Eternal became necessary – for if this were not the case, then in the Hereafter no one would possess the power to look toward His Great Essence. Thus you have found Him along with His perception, so He is the one who perceives and the one who is perceived: "There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing." (Qurʾān 42:11)

Then He sent forth from the brow a grace upon the Eternal Speech, and for this reason benefit occurred in speech. Then He speaks with such speech which is His Eternal attribute and is utterly separate from the speech of created beings.

**Whose Grace Is in What:**

Thus, the summary is this: in speech there is the grace of majesty; in the brow there is the grace of hearing; in hearing there is the grace of intention; in intention there is the grace of knowledge; in knowledge there is the grace of power; in power there is the grace of life; in life there is the grace of essence; in the essences there is the grace of the Divine Breath; in reason there is the grace of life; in spirit there is the grace of power; in soul there is the grace of knowledge; in heart there is the grace of intention; in human being there is the grace of hearing; and in his composition and majesty there is the grace; in form there is the grace of speech. And since this benefit reaches – it is both actually and potentially debtor in both ways – therefore it has become debtor by becoming connected to Him; it is debtor in the beginning and also debtor in the end.

Some of the great masters say: whoever wishes to attain divine wisdom must necessarily mention the Name ʿAlīm (the Knowing) with frequency. And some have said: he should perform the dhikr of this Name ʿAlīm upon the Qurʾān. And some mention the Name Ḥakīm (the Wise), or with the vocative particle, meaning "Yā ʿAlīm" and "Yā Ḥakīm" – he should perform dhikr in this manner until he becomes drunk with it, until the divine decree descends upon him and its description resounds in his ear: the guiding attribute, or that al-Khiḍr, peace be upon him, will teach him. And such an elixir will be obtained that even the Book of Knowledge will not change color from it. (Continued on page 559)
