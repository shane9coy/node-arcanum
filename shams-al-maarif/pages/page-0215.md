# Page 215 (English translation)
215

Nor any tyrant but he became distressed and humiliated, nor any king but he became humiliated. I swear by the One who gave the sun its fortune in the heaven, and indeed it is a great oath if you knew it. Accept the voice of the caller, O Maimun. Indeed those who are with your Lord, they do not show arrogance from worshipping their Lord, and they glorify Him, and they prostrate to Him alone.

The prayer is as follows:

O Allah, I ask You by Your primordial primacy which has no beginning, and by Your finality which has no end, O Most Generous, O Possessor of endless generosity that shall never cease forever, O Possessor of vast mercy that cannot be encompassed, O One who penetrates into hearts, intuitions, and thoughts—nothing is hidden from You. The All-Seeing who sees the people of insight and guides them to His majesty, employs them, inspires them to remember Him, grants them success, and teaches them the knowledge of His noble name. He opened for them the gate of mercy, so they called out, "O Merciful!" Then they remained steadfast upon the steadfastness of communion. He called out to them in the depths of the night, the caller of response, when you cry out to your Lord, and He answered you, "O my God, O my Master, O my Lord."

Uncover from our hearts the veil of heedlessness and from our eyes what attaches to them from bewilderment, until we know from Your knowledge what You have taught us and we act with it as the spiritual beings act by the secret of Your name, O You who created fires for those who disobey You and adorned the gardens for those who obey You. I have sought access to You, O Allah, by Your most beautiful names and Your complete supreme words, that You fulfill my need and that You subordinate for me the servant of these two noble generous magnificent names, that he come to me every day with a gold dinar from the depths of the earth, which I find beneath my head, and I seek help with it for fulfilling my needs and my interests. O Allah, O Lord, O Most Compassionate, O Most Merciful.

Protect us, O Allah, O Possessor of the generous essence and the magnificent names. I ask You for sustenance that is precious, unopposed by any seeker, not demanded by any supplicant. O Allah, if my sustenance is in the heaven, then send it down; and if it is in the earth, then bring it forth; and if it is distant, then bring it near; and if it is near, then make it easy; and if it is nonexistent, then cause it to exist; and if it is withheld, then establish it; and if it is little, then multiply it. And bless it for me, O Allah, and bring it to me from Yourself, and take charge of my affair concerning it, and make my hand raised with giving and do not make it lowered with begging, by Your mercy, O Provider, O Opener, O Knowing, O Magnificent, O Generous, O Merciful. Answer my prayer by Your grace and generosity. Indeed You are over all things Omnipotent, and with Your servants You are Kind and Aware.

There is no power and no strength except by Allah the Most High, the Magnificent. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
