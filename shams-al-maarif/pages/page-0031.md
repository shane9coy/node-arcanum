**Purpose of Composition**

Efforts are for the sake of truth, and there is a system for reality. For the spirits there is refreshment with divine knowledge. The means is courage and is desired by all. The distribution of all affairs has proceeded from Allah the Most High. Felicity is connected to the bright sun of perfection. It is attained through following the commands of the Sharia. The people of action hold the highest rank in the station of the Highest. Even higher than this is the station of the guiding investigators.

In Islam, there is no position for a scholar who does not bring benefit to others. As if, in reality, those who do not bring benefit are not alive. Certainly, far from felicity is the person who has not honored the commands of the Sharia and has introduced defects into the conditions of the investigators of the people of the Qibla.

I have studied the words and writings of the great and famous elders whose wisdom has spread throughout the world and from whose blessing all creation receives benefit. They wrote about the definition of the Divine Names and Attributes, concerning the letters of dhikr and prayers. For this reason, my friends requested that I clarify the difficulties of their complexity and reveal the buried treasures. Therefore, I accepted their request and decided to compose this book. I acknowledge that I cannot reach the level of the elders of the predecessors and the guiding learned investigators. I pray to Allah the Most High with the utmost humility that He may help me through the spiritual blessing of the elders of the predecessors.

My testimony should be in accordance with that of the investigators and stated with the tongue of confirmation. I seek help from Allah the Most High and trust in His essence alone. The purpose of this book's obligation is knowledge—that in the ocean of the honor and greatness of the Divine Names, Allah the Most High has hidden the pearls of wisdom and the divine subtle mysteries, and how one should work with these names and prayers and the letters and verses of the surahs connected with them. In this book I have established sections so that every seeker may be guided to his desired goal, which brings him before Allah the Most High without hardship and difficulty, and also to convey the sciences from which worldly benefit is derived.
