# Page 210 (English translation)
This is what I want, O earthly ones, the spiritual group. Come forth in my presence for the Kingdom of the Divine. There will be one voice and they will be silent. O servants, there is longing. The Messenger came to them and they used to joke with him. Come forth, obey the tribes, and whoever among you falls behind, the angels will burn them with the flames of fire. Indeed, we touched the sky and found it filled with stern guardians and flames. Indeed, we used to sit first for hearing. So now whoever finds hearing, it will prevent him from arrogance, and indeed we do not know whether evil is intended for the people of the earth or their Lord has intended good for them. And indeed among us are the fortunate, and we are distinct groups. Indeed, we have believed that we cannot humiliate Allah on earth, nor can we make Him flee. Indeed, when we heard guidance, we believed in it. So whoever believes in his Lord does not fear being seized by another. And indeed among us are Muslims and among us are wrongdoers. So whoever accepts Islam has found guidance, and the wrongdoers will be the fuel of Hell. O spiritual souls, I swear to you, do not change His word. Among the names of Allah and His verses, you have been recited. None of you should fall behind. Prepare, listen, come forth, and enter all the earth. O earthly group, accept. I have recited upon you everywhere the names of Allah the Most High. Accept for the sake of the names of Allah. Accept while obeying the names of Allah, for He is the Lord of the Worlds. Accept, none of you should fall behind. O earthly group of souls, accept while obeying.

Respond, O assembly of earthly spirits, obediently by the right of what I have sworn upon you, and indeed it is an oath whose magnitude you would not know if you were aware. And if they had steadfastly followed the way, We would have given them abundant water to drink, that We might test them therein. And whoever turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, He will admit him to an ascending torment. Respond, let none of you fall behind by the right of what I have sworn upon you. And indeed the mosques are for Allah, so do not invoke anyone alongside Allah. And indeed when the servant of Allah stood calling upon Him, they almost became upon him in compact mass. Say, "Indeed I only invoke my Lord and do not associate anyone with Him." Say, "Indeed I do not possess for you harm or guidance." Say, "Indeed no one can protect me from Allah, nor will I find besides Him any refuge except as a communication from Allah and His messages." And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger, then indeed for him is the fire of Hell, therein abiding forever. Until when they see what they are promised, then they will know who is the weaker helper and the fewer in number. Say, "I do not know whether what you are promised is near or whether my Lord will appoint for it a term." Knower of the unseen, so He does not disclose His unseen to anyone, except whom He has approved of messengers. Then indeed He sends guardians before him and behind him. O Allah, I ask You by the permanence of Your supremacy, by Your eternal existence, by the measure of Your power, by the foundation of Your blessings, and by the establishment of the identity of Your sovereignty, O One whom doubts do not mix with Him in His kingdom, O One by whom everything seeks refuge, and there is nothing among His creation but it seeks refuge by Him, and none is given refuge in His kingdom. I ask You, O Allah, indeed I do not possess for myself benefit or harm except by Your permission. O Allah, I ask You by the right of the One with whom You promised all the prophets and with whom You made the saints unique for You. O Allah, O Majestic, O Great, O Creator of the earth, O O Allah, O Creator of whoever is in the heavens and the earth, O One who knows and no one knows about Him besides Him. O Allah, I ask You by Your subtlety and by the firmness of Your knowledge and by the right of the might of Your glory, O O Subtle One, and by the kāf of Your greatness, O there is no deity except You, by Your existence and by the alif of Your divinity and by the ḍād of Your light. O Allah, I ask You, O O Creator
