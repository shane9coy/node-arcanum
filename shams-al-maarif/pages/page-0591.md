# Letter Rāʾ

This letter is cold in nature and has strange and wonderful effects on animals. This letter has not appeared in the Divine Names except at the end of the names Zaki and the name of the Spirit. On the day when the Moon is opposite Jupiter, the Shaykh jurist should write this letter and keep it with him; honor and wealth will be his portion.

### He will be protected from harmful animals

If the number of this letter is written on a camel blanket at the time when the Moon is in the same sign, and he keeps it with him, he will never tire. And if he sleeps in the wilderness, no harmful animal will come near him.

### Rain will fall through this practice

When you need rain, write this letter on the skin of a black goat, place it on the head of a stallion, and read the Invocation of the Thunder of Sights with presence of heart, and pray to God for rain, and say these words: *uḥḍur ayyuhā l-sahāb wa l-maṭar* "Come, O Cloud and Rain!" By Divine Power rain will come that very day. Many people have tested this.

### For blessing in sustenance

It is also a special property of this letter that when it is written on something, blessing enters it. When the Moon is in its domicile, write it on money and draw a circle around it. Then put it in a purse; blessing will be in that purse. And if this letter is written with a signet ring and kept with one, sustenance will be granted from fate. If the circle of the letter Rāʾ is written with pure musk and saffron together with the name of the desired one and kept with one, the desired one will become his seeker. Its figure is this:

[A 3×3 magic square containing the following numbers:
Row 1: 5 | 10 | 3
Row 2: 4 | 6 | 8
Row 3: 9 | 2 | 7]

### Through its recitation everything will be accepted

The invocation of this letter is extremely magnificent. After every prayer, recite it 21 times; the assigned spirit will be present and will accept whatever you say. For it, you should illuminate (the tablet) with saffron, musk, and frankincense. When you intend to undertake its spiritual retreat, break fast (begin the practice) with seclusion and an oath, and engrave the letter Rāʾ on a ring and wear it on your hand, and begin the litany of the spiritual journey. The assigned spirit will come and make a covenant with you and fulfill your need. The invocation is this:

*bi-smi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīmi* *zidnī yā llāhu khawfan ilayka wa raghbatan ladayka fa-innamā aḥabba ilayya dhikruka wa tāmilī bi-ḥīlī laʿallaka wa albisnī nūran wa jamālan astaʿīnu bihi ʿalā kashfi asrāri n-nuqṭati allatī min janbihā tarkkabat l-jibālu wa qad kudhibat min haybati rabbi l-ʿizzati subḥāna rabbika rabbi l-ʿizzati ʿammā yaṣifūna ātuṣ ṣaḥili t-tadbīri li-ḥarfi r-rāʾ bi-samāhi rayyāhu hud barazūhī zawdin barūhī bi-amri llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīna jabrāʾīl jamīlun subḥānahu wa taʿālā alā bi-dhikri llāhi taṭma'innu l-qulūbu hayyā bihā tahā ʿalā ʿaliyyin yā burhānu amānin ʿajjil wa farā yā bānī wa ikshif lī ʿan amrika hādhā bi-ārāʾi bi-ʿizzati man lam yalid wa lam yūlad wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥad*
