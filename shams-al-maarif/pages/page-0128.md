[The Experiment of Allamah Ibn Qayyim on Healing through Surah Al-Fatiha]

His Eminence Allamah Ibn Qayyim states that the best cure is Surah Al-Fatiha. He narrates his incident saying: "I had been living in Makkah Al-Mukarramah for a considerable period. I contracted an illness from which I did not recover with any physician or medicine. I decided that I would treat myself with Surah Al-Fatiha. Then I treated myself with Surah Al-Fatiha and I recovered. I saw its very great effect."

Whenever I saw any person suffering from any illness, I would tell him to treat himself with Al-Fatihah, and he would be cured. Whoever recites it nineteen times and goes to an oppressor, he will remain safe from his evil. Whoever writes Surah Al-Fatiha on the number of its special properties on the day of Friday and keeps it with himself, he will remain safe from the attacks of jinn and humans.

[Removal of the Effects of Poison]

Whoever recites this supplication three times daily, poison and the like shall cause him no harm:
بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ خَیْرِ الْاَسْمَاءِ بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ رَبِّ الْاَرْضِ وَ السَّمَاءِ بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الَّذِی لَا یَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِہٖ شَیْءٌ فِی الْاَرْضِ وَلَا فِی السَّمَاءِ وَھُوَ السَّمِیْعُ الْعَلِیْمُ

Whoever writes the Surahs of the Abjad letters on a glass china plate and erases it with rainwater and gives it to drink to one who has ingested poison, poison shall have no effect. This has been tested many times. The Noble Prophet ﷺ has stated: "Whoever places his hand upon his head and recites this verse, for him there is cure from all illnesses except death."

The verse is this:
ھُوَ اللّٰہُ الَّذِیْ لَا اِلٰہَ اِلَّا ھُوَ عَالِمُ الْغَیْبِ وَالشَّہَادَۃِ ھُوَ الرَّحْمٰنُ الرَّحِیْمُ

Recite up to the end of Surah Al-Hashr.
