# Page 250 (English translation)
I supplicate, whose beginning is anger, whose strength is weak, and whose means have diminished. O You who grant the supplication of the wronged, who besides You finds no one to relieve their affliction—besides You there is no deity, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy! Remove from us every thing that has come upon us as affliction from our enemy and yours, Satan the accursed. O Lord of the Worlds! Indeed You are over all things capable. O Helper, O Allah! Help repeatedly, O You who grant refuge, who has no beginning, O Eternal One whose end is not known, O You who revive the dead, O You who are present with every soul—as for what each has earned, my Allah! Allah is the Mighty, the Compeller; there is no deity besides You; You are the One and Only deity. I ask from You, with the Perfect Names, for Your safety, forgiveness, well-being, and for lasting security in religion and this world and the Hereafter. And I supplicate regarding the people of the body, the people of offspring, and all Muslims. O Lord of the Worlds! Indeed You are over all things capable, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. Have mercy upon me with Your mercy, and remove from me whatever affliction has befallen me, and grant me beautiful deliverance. O Lord of the Worlds, keep Your good opinion of me. And Allah the Exalted is the bestower of good.

Chapter:

Know that the head of the letters is inscribed in the breasts of the angels, and the head of the matter is written in the pages of the mysteries of the wise, and the head of alchemy is hidden in the treasuries of the intelligent. The head of discernment is concealed in the hearts of the saints, the head of the divine names is the mirror of insight of the prophets, and the head of speech is in the throne of perception and comprehension. If you understand these sacred indications and the heavenly subtleties, you will obtain an abundant portion of the attributes of the world of descriptions.

Some important matters of the chapter:

Know that for every supplication there is a divine name and also a door through which one enters it. "Glory be to You" is that upon which the spirit ascends to the heights of spirituality; through this door of "I seek refuge" one obtains virtue, and the stations of religion are three: the station of Islam, the station of faith, and the station of spiritual excellence. And the ranks of the world are also three: the Garden of Intellect, the Garden of Paradise, and the Garden of the People of Excellence. The divisions of excellence are also three: one applies to the station of Islam, verification to the station of faith, and realization to the station of spiritual excellence. When the traveler progresses in the station of Islam, then the effects of each divine name become manifest to him in his self, body, all faculties, organs, and states. And he sees all of them through the decrees and effects of these names, and every form of remembrance brings him complete pleasure, for example gratitude for blessings and patience in calamities. These organs enter the Garden of Works, which is the totality of the journey of those who enter with the attribute of faith, just as Abraham, peace be upon him, created it in this manner—that this faith is a garden. Its trees are "Glory be to Allah," "Praise be to Allah," "There is no deity but Allah," and "Allah is most great." Its cultivation is in the station of faith with verification, and to know the realities of these names and their meanings is with the knowledge of the spiritual ones. Its embellishment is indicated by the Prophet's blessed guidance: "Acquire the character traits of Allah the Exalted" (that you adopt the attributes of Allah the Exalted, meaning let one who acquires a trait acquire it according to the meaning of the divine name, and act in accordance with it). This insight enters the Garden of Inheritance, and this garden is higher than the Garden of Goodness—that is, it is the inner aspect of the first garden, from which the world of the kingdom and the heavenly kingdom is obtained. And it is to this that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him and his family, pointed with his saying: "The place of each of you in the Garden is [determined], and also in the Fire. When he dies and enters the Fire, he inherits the place of the people of the Fire." If you wish, read this verse: "That is the Garden which you are made to inherit on account of what you used to do." Its enumeration in this reform is with verification through imagination and manifestation.
