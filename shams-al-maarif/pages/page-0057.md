# Page 57 (English translation)
The Station of Dhirāʿ (The Arm):

Its form is this: * * Its letter is Lām. This is the station of Hawā. When the Moon descends into it, it sends down upon the world the most virtuous spirituality by the command of God the Exalted. At this time, it is best for beginning sciences, performing good deeds, meeting scholars, gaining benefit from worshippers, preparing talismans and amulets, and meeting kings, honored people, and brothers.

The child born in it will be fortunate. Its incense is celery and flax seed.

The Station of Nuthrah (The Nostril):

Its form is this: ::::: Its letter is Ḥāʾ. Its temperament is cold, mixed with fortune. When the Moon descends into it, spirituality descends by God's command and creates enmity, hatred, and destruction. This time is best for making talismans against enemies and rebels, making evil prayers, and bringing calamities, because this is an unlucky station. It is suitable for evil works as we have described. The child born in it will be very unfortunate. Its incense is costus and pomegranate peel.

The Station of Ṭarf (The Glance):

Its form is this: * * Its letter is Kāf. This is an unlucky station. When the Moon descends into it, the same spirituality descends upon the world as mentioned above. No talisman or craft should be made in it. One should not go to meet officials. One should not start love affairs. One should not recite any operation. It is best to remain completely separate at this time. This station is extremely unfavorable for all operations. The child born in it will be unfortunate. Its incense is not saffron as mentioned above [Handwritten insertion: eastern]

The Station of Jabhah (The Forehead):

Its form is this: . . . Its letter is Yāʾ. Its temperament is cold and unfortunate but closer to capability.

When the Moon descends into it, operations of love, proximity, satisfaction, and relocation should be performed in it. Having new clothes sewn and wearing them is good in it.

The child born in it will be legitimately and virtuously fortunate, but he will also be cunning and deceitful. Its incense is hab al-lās and saffron.

The Station of Kharṭān (The Two Twigs):

Its form is this: : : Its letter is Kāf. Its temperament is warm and dry. When the Moon descends into it, spiritual healing, talismanic operations, treating patients, farewell for commerce, buying and selling, going to kings and leaders, traveling, taking up residence, undertaking major ventures, and wearing new clothes are best in it. The person born in it will have defects, but he will also have enmity, cunning, and deception. Its incense is sweet pomegranate rind.
