Or the invocation is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, Your power is dominant over Your enemies, and Your strength and Your majesty are established toward Your friends. O Allah, I ask You to grant me ability upon the shore of Your nearness, and nearness to You, O Allah, O One near to my heart, by halq until what was bitter becomes joyful and settles in the mold of Your nearness. And strengthen me with Your power, O Strong One; strengthen me with Your powerful strength until security becomes manifest; let nothing flee by Your pleasure and Your exaltation, O the sought-one. So let the Qaf draw near to You, and let the Qaf tremble so that it does not settle in it. Answer, O Qaf, and hasten to me the response before the descent of the decree. Q, and by the Noble Quran, the standing Qaf, a little without negligence in the response. Answer, and place your trust in such-and-such by the command of the Dominant, the Powerful, the All-Able through domination. And dissolve, O Qul: cease from complaint and be still from standing until You fulfill my need and my occupation. It became strong, and it struck: Har shaqq shaafaq. Haya by the sovereignty and the angelic realm, and by the breath of Israyil, and by the handful of Izrayil, and by the cry of Jibra'il, and by the taking of spirits. Do not settle until You fulfill my need by the glory of Allah, so that Allah may decree a matter that was destined to happen. Glory be to Him by His decree, and you are settled by the light of Allah—had I not said, "Stop, wait a little until you see from them a power that is the strength of Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Strong." Answer, and peace be upon you, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings.

Its incense is the peel of fox and its nails, and this is:
Ajab bi-haqq 'iltak 'atluqa mahlut 'inj 'ayimawah qahar yuh. Answer and do such-and-such.

The letter Ra:
Saturn is in the fifth degree. Its properties: it relieves headache. The number of its stages should be written on the skin of a hernia patient with the name of the person, and also write the name of its first (stage), and place it beneath a bamboo stick, and continue reading the incantations. Pain will begin in the customary head, and weeping should occur after a little time.

There will be expansion in sustenance:
If you write this letter and keep it with you, and recite the name "Ya Rahim" (O Merciful), sustenance will become ample. And when this letter is written on a copper tablet while the moon is in its mansion, and kept with you—see the book of Athbat.

The growth of gardens will be rapid:
Know that this letter, after its service is performed, also benefits in making trees fruitful quickly. When it is written and placed in the basin with which trees are watered, the trees will grow very quickly and become fruitful.

For removing epilepsy:
[damaged source] The manner of its service is that you enter with the left hand and recite the invocation, then say: "The muwakkal is present." And the muwakkal will hover in the chest of this muwakkal. [damaged source] He will point to the patient with his right hand—immediately he will become healthy. The name of this muwakkal is Dhahmar 'A'il.
