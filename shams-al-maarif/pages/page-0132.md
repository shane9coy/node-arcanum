# Page 132 (English translation)
He made Abraham, peace be upon him, a friend; He spoke with Moses, peace be upon him, and honored Muhammad ﷺ. O Lord of the Day of Resurrection, we worship You alone and ask help from You alone. Show us the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, and do not show us the path of those upon whom Your wrath has descended, nor the path of the astray.

If after three days a white dog appears before you, marked with signs, and begins to spread within the house until it fills the entire house, a person will emerge from within it and ask: "What is your need?" Then say: "I desire the Supreme Name and its talisman." He will set certain conditions with you. Likewise, recite this after the morning prayer three times, and after Zuhr, Asr, and Maghrib recite it ten times, along with this supplication:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ ۝ اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ حَمْدًا يَكُوْنُ لِيْ رِضَاةً وَلِعِزِّ مَرْضَاةِ جَلَّ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ الَّذِی دَحَی الْأَقَالِيْمَ وَ اصْطَفٰی مُوْسٰی الْكَلِيْمَ يُحْيِ الْعِظَامَ وَ هِيَ رَمِيْمٌ فِيْهِمَا اِسْمَانِ عَظِيْمَانِ شِفَاءٌ لِكُلِّ دَاءٍ وَ سَقِيْمٍ وَ طَرِيْقِيْ لِجَنَّاتِ النَّعِيْمِ وَ نَجَاةٌ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْجَحِيْمِ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّيْنِ لَيْسَ لَهُ فِيْ مُلْكِهِ شَرِيْكٌ وَ لَا مُنَازِعٌ وَ لَا مُعِيْنٌ ﴿إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ﴾ بِالْاِقْرَارِ وَ نَعْتَرِفُ بِالتَّقْصِيْرِ وَ أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَّا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ الْحَقُّ الْمُبِيْنُ وَ أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ سَيِّدَنَا مُحَمَّدًا رَّسُوْلُ اللّٰهِ الصَّادِقُ الْأَمِيْنُ وَاللّٰهُ مُكَوِّنُ الْأَكْوَانِ وَ عَالِمُ خَفِيَّاتِ الْإِضْمَارِ وَ مُكَوِّرُ اللَّيْلِ وَ النَّهَارِ حُجَّتَيْنِ لِكُلِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ وَ وَجْهَيْنِ إِلَى الْآخِرِيْنَ وَ الْآتِيْدِيْنَ مِنَ الْأَجْنَاسِ الْمُخْتَلِفِيْنَ ﴿وَ إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِيْنُ﴾ بِكَ عَلٰى كُلِّ حَاجَةٍ مِنْ أُمُوْرِ الدُّنْيَا وَالدِّيْنِ اَللّٰهُمَّ يَا مَالِكَ مُلُوْكِ الْعَوَالِمِ كُلِّهَا اَجْمَعِيْنَ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّيْ كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِيْنَ رَبِّ نَجِّنِيْ وَ اَدْرِكْنِيْ بِرَحْمَتِكَ يَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِيْنَ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ وَ نَجِّنِيْ مِمَّا أَخَافُ وَ اَحْذَرُ وَ اِهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيْمَ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ اَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوْبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَ لَا الضَّالِّيْنَ ۝

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, Lord of all worlds. Such praise as brings Your pleasure and fulfills Your honored desire, O Lord of all worlds, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, who spread out the regions and chose Moses for prophecy. He gives life to bones though they have become dust. These are two great Names, a cure for every illness and ailment, a path to the Gardens of Bliss, and salvation from the punishment of Hell. Master of the Day of Judgment, there is no partner in His sovereignty, no disputer, no helper. "You alone do we worship" with affirmation and we acknowledge our shortcomings. I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah alone, without partner; to Him belongs the sovereign authority, the manifest truth. And I bear witness that our master Muhammad is the messenger of Allah, the truthful, the trustworthy. And Allah is the creator of all existences, knower of the secrets of inner thoughts, the roller of day and night. Two proofs for all worlds and two faces for the later ones and the followers from various nations. "And You alone do we ask for help" for every need of worldly and religious matters. O Allah, O Lord of the kings of all worlds, all of them together, there is no deity except You. Glory be to You; I was indeed among the wrongdoers. My Lord, deliver me and reach me with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful, O Lord of all worlds, and deliver me from what I fear and dread. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not that of those who have earned Your wrath, nor of those who are astray.

If after completing the spiritual exercise of the Fatiha, Surah Fatiha is read every day twenty-five times after the Fajr prayer and ten times after Zuhr, this spiritual exercise and fasting are not required. Perform this seven-day spiritual exercise and after your supplications, recite this prayer as well.
