# Page 554 (English translation)
Bāb — Jīm — Dāl — Wāw — Zāy — Light of the Gate

These letters are not in the operation, nor are they in the Small Gate. The letters of the Small Gate are: Bā, Ḥā, Dāl, Ḍāl, Ghayn, and Qāf.

And know that "interior letters" refer to those letters that are repeated in a name; "exterior letters" refer to those letters that are not contained in it; and "parallel letters" refer to those letters that are found in the line of the name's letters from which the name is composed. The interior letters are always four letters, and the exterior letters are also the same number, and the parallel letters do not exceed three. This rule is throughout the fifty [names], and the names of angels emerge from exterior and interior letters together with the parallel letters, and the exterior letters of the gates are ten from here: forty letters, just as in the Small [Gate], and these are the qualitative degrees.

The Quality of the Letters

Heat — Cold — Dryness — Moisture

Alif, Hā, Ṭā, Mīm, Fā, Shīn, Dhāl — Bā, Wāw, Yā, Nūn, Ṣād, Tā, Ḍāl — Jīm, Zāy, Kāf, Sīn, Qāf, Thā, Ẓāl — Dāl, Ḥā, Lām, ʿAyn, Rā, Khā, Ghayn

Number of the Great Gate: 8545
Number of Names: 721,929
Number of Letters of Names: 677,376
Exterior Division from the Gates: 725

And of the twelve signs: 725, for each night and day: 24, from the Small Gate: 792, for each name: 17,426, letters of names of operation: 3,633,855, exterior division from the twelve signs.

Know that for each night and day there are 24 hours, and the hour is from the Small Gate, and the Gate has twenty-eight letters. The corresponding numbers of the stations and the complete letters of the names are arranged as: Alif, Bā, Tā, Thā, alif, etc.

And know that each day yields 24 Gates from the Small Gate. In a month there are 720 Gates, and in a year there are 8,240 Gates. Each Gate has 8 letters, and for the night and day: 672 letters. Thus in a month there are 20,160 letters, and in a year: 241,920. Then this was divided into eleven types, and the place of the Sun's name is in the first [sign]: it enters every sign with 30 days. First of all it enters Aries, and this is the first sign and the first season, and the first Gate emerges from the operation of the Small Gate. The result of both: first, here there are 30 days. 73.

May God bless our master Muḥammad and his family and grant him peace.
