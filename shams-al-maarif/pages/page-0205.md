# Page 205 (English translation)

And the gain from every good and the safety from every evil. Do not leave for me a sin except that You have forgiven it, nor a worry except that You have relieved it, nor a debtor except that You have healed him, nor a debt except that You have paid it, nor anything corrupt except that You have set it right, nor anything scattered except that You have gathered it, nor anything absent except that You have returned it, nor a need from the needs of this world and the hereafter except that You have fulfilled it. Ease is from You, and the outcome of our affair, and the vastness of forgiveness is through Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy.

Translation
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I seek the causes of Your mercy and the purposes of Your forgiveness. Safety from every evil and the gain from every good. O Allah, leave no sin for me but that You forgive it, and no grief but that You relieve it, and no sick person but that You give him healing, and no debt but that You pay it, and no corruption but that You set it right, and no division but that You unite it, and no absent one but that You bring him back, and no need from the needs of this world and the hereafter but that You fulfill it, with ease from Your side and a good outcome. O One of vast forgiveness, by Your mercy. O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. Then lift the hands from prostration.

Fulfill my need, O One Who Fulfills Needs, O Lord of the First Ones and the Last Ones, O Maker of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of Glory and Generosity, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, the best of His creation, and his family and his companions, and grant them peace.

O Fulfiller of Needs, fulfill my need. Lord of the first and the last. O Maker of the heavens and the earth. O Possessor of glory and generosity, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And our master [may Allah bless him and grant him peace], who is the best of creation—send blessings and peace upon his family and his companions.

(Surah of the Jinn: Description of Its Spiritual Exercise and the Subjugation of Its Client)

Know that when you intend this spiritual exercise, fast for three days—Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Eat no animal substance. Keep loban and jandri incense burning day and night. During this mentioned period, recite the noble Surah one thousand (1000) times; recite three hundred thirty-three (333) times each day. Your prostration should be completed in the middle of this duration. At that time, its client will appear. Its height will be short and its hands long. This client will come before you and sit and say, "Peace be upon you." You should keep your heart firm, for its awe is very intense. This is the king of the Muslim jinn who believed at the blessed hand of the Messenger [may Allah bless him and grant him peace]. Three men will stand behind him. If you remain steadfast, you will succeed and your need will be fulfilled. If you hesitate or show stubbornness, they will turn back and your practice will be void. Therefore, it is necessary to keep the heart firm and not fear. Its name is Hūr Hif. Say to it: "My right over you is established; you see how much poverty and distress I am in. I ask you to help me so that I may perform pilgrimage with lawful provisions and provide for my family. Allah will give you your reward." Know that if you keep your heart firm and the chief of this category of jinn that we have mentioned—may He be blessed—is pleased, He will direct one of his companions who are behind him to command something; they will run faster than lightning and come to you. Then he will give you something and say, "This will be your portion; you will obtain what you desire from him."

It is transmitted from Shaykh Salih Abu 'Abd Allah Husayn ibn Mansur that this matter occurred through his hand, and then he gave thanks to Allah for it.
