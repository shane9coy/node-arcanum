# Page 623 (English translation)
621

He established the twelve hours of mercy in accordance with truth.

**The Completion of the Word Belongs to Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah:**
Thus, when la ilaha illallah alone cannot complete pure tawhid. Its completion comes with Muhammadun rasulullah, and from its twelve letters the circle of day becomes perfect, and wisdom becomes complete with mercy which we have explained. Without it, one's tawhid would be incomplete. This is not merely a word but the most excellent word and saying, as comes in the hadith.

**The Word is Written on the Throne in White and Green Light:**
In opposition to these twenty-four letters are twenty-four worlds. Their total is contained within the letter alif. This word is the reality of the upper and lower world and of both. The luminous realities of the Throne are its attribution. As if on the Throne one line is written in white light and one line in green light—that is, these two words: la ilaha illallah muhammadun rasulullah are written. Understand this: it comes in the noble hadith that whoever says la ilaha illallah, a column of light emerges from his mouth and reaches beneath the Throne, glorifying until the Day of Resurrection. We have witnessed this, because this is the attribution in the kingdom, the ascension in the malakut, and the ascent in the jabrut. For its sake no door remains closed; all realities become manifest through it. "To Him ascends the good word and the righteous deed He exalts" [Qur'an 35:10]. To Him ascend the pure words and He elevates the righteous deed.

**Reciting the Litany One Thousand Times Daily Opens the Sustenance:**
It is related that whoever recites la ilaha illallah one thousand times daily after the noon prayer, the Provider Allah Most High will make sustenance easy for him. Similarly, whoever recites it at the time of sleeping, his spirit will remain prostrating beneath the Throne.

**Its Litany Weakens the Self and Protects from Trials:**
Whoever recites it at midday with the same count, his self will become weak. Whoever recites it at the sight of the moon, he will be protected from illnesses. Whoever recites it with presence of heart for the intention of blessing any work, that work will be blessed. Whoever recites it one thousand times upon entering a city, he will be protected from calamities and trials.

**Spiritual Advancement Through Its Remembrance:**
Whoever recites it contemplating the advancement of stations, his purpose will be achieved. It is narrated from the Prophet, peace be upon him, that whoever recites this word, forgiveness will be his.

**Forgiveness is Certain for Reciting the Word at Death:**
Moreover, the Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Whoever's last words from this world are la ilaha illallah, forgiveness will be his." Whoever has a need, it is incumbent upon him to recite la ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lah with presence of heart and supplicate, and his need will be granted.

**The Statement of the Mystics:**
Some mystics have said: whoever recites it with its count, in return Allah Most High appoints for him a perfect gift of His own; "Is there any recompense for goodness except goodness?" [Qur'an 55:60]—for la ilaha illallah.
