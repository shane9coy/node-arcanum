[BOX: Conjuration of the Regent of the Name "Salām" (Peace)]

The Name "Salām" is the Greatest Name (Ism al-A'ẓam). Allah the Most High protects whoever recites it in all places. The name of its regent is "Darmiʾāyīl." Under it are four regents, each of whom oversees 131 sections, and in each section 131 assigned servants are appointed. They are under the influence of the Honorable Gabriel. The regent of this name comes to the reciter. The prayer of this name is:

Yā salāmu 'anta al-salāmu wa 'ilayka ya'ūdu al-salāmu salāmuка al-tāmmu rāfiʿataka ʿalā al-'awliyāʾi wa al-'anbiyāʾi
wa al-'atqiyāʾi wa 'anta al-muḥīṭu bi-ʿilmika al-qadīmi wa bi-ṣafāʾiḥi al-ṣafā fī qulūbi al-'aṣfiyāʾi 'as'aluka bi-sakīnatika
al-nāzilati ʿalā al-barā li-mūsā wa bi-ʿizzatika al-ẓāhirati ʿalā al-jinābi al-ʿīsawiyyi wa bi-mā jaʿalta fī bāṭini
dāʾirati al-hawā wa ẓāhirī maʿālimi al-manāʾi 'an tajʿala qalbī qābilan li-tawārudi al-wāḥidiyyati fārighan min shawāghili
al-muwḥidiyyati ghāliban bika 'ilayka fī jamīʿi al-'awqāti al-sarmadiyyati wa 'arzuqnī bi-luṭfika al-muqīmi wa 'iḥsānika
al-qadīmi ḥusna al-ẓanni bikaāffati al-muslimīna li-'anāla sirra subūḥiyyatika alladhīna jaʿaltahum bihā fī maqāmi al-yaqīni
wa ajʿalnī mutabarrikan bi-daqāʾiqi nefāʾisi al-'awwalīna wa al-'ākhirīna fa-'arzuqnī al-riḍā bimā qaddartahu lī fī ʿilmika
wa yassirhu lī bi-'amrika yā man zayyana subula qulūbi al-'awliyāʾi bi-maṣābīḥi al-khawāṭiri iftaḥ lī 'abwāba al-mushāhadati
bi-maṣābīḥi al-baṣāʾiri yā salāmu yā salāmu

"O Peace, You are Peace, and peace returns to You alone. Your peace is complete. Your grace is upon the saints, the prophets, and the righteous. You encompass all things with Your ancient knowledge, and with the pure tablets You place purity and goodness in the hearts of the chosen ones. O Allah, I ask You for the tranquility that descended upon Moses, peace be upon him, and by Your evident glory upon the sacred presence of Jesus, peace be upon him, and by what You have gathered within the inner circle of desire and what appears among the signs of the knowledge, make my heart receptive to the flow of the Oneness, free from the occupations of monotheism, returning to You through You in all eternal times. Grant me through Your abiding grace and ancient favor the good assumption of all Muslims, so that I may attain the secret of Your Supremely Holy One, whom You have placed in the station of certainty, and make me blessed through the subtle refinements of the treasures of the ancients and the moderns. Grant me contentment with what You have destined for me in Your knowledge, and ease it for me through Your command, O You who have adorned the paths of the hearts of the saints with the lamps of thoughts. Open for me the doors of witnessing through the lamps of insight. O Peace. O Peace."

[BOX: Conjuration of the Regent of the Name "Mu'min" (Believer)]

"Al-Mu'min" is a Great Name. The name of its regent is "Matiyāʾīl." Under it are four rulers, each of whom oversees 130 sections, and in each section there are 130 servants. Whoever recites this name according to its numerical value, the regents come to him and fulfill such unlimited needs, and his life will transform into one of light and honor. Indeed, all goodness is in the hands of Allah alone, who gives to whom He wills. [watermark: FREE AMLIYAAT] The prayer of this name is:

Allāhumma 'anta al-mu'minu alladhī 'athbaita al-'īmāna fī qulūbi 'ahli al-ʿirfāni wa 'aẓharta al-'īmāna ʿinda
