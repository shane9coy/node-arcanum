Virtues of Surah Fatiha and Verses Attributed to Ali

These verses have been transmitted from the book Kanz al-Muqribin, which is a composition of Allama Ibn Sab'in, and are attributed to Ali, may God be pleased with him.

[Arabic poetry arranged in three columns]
[Right Column]
When you are seeking sustenance
And safety from opposition and treachery,
Persist in its recitation after the night prayer
Until ninety, followed by ten,
And glory that the nights do not change,
And safety from the calamity of every evil,
And from hunger, nakedness, and severance,
For a long duration in every age.

[Middle Column]
And the intention succeeds from ease and difficulty,
For the Opening of the Book, indeed in it...
And in the morning, noon, then afternoon,
You obtain what you wish of glory and rank
By an extraordinary measure of deficiency flows,
And you do not need anyone for anything,
And from the violence of one who forbids and commands,
So behold, if you do so, you will reach it.

[Left Column]
And you achieve swiftly what you desire,
For what you hoped, a secret, any secret.
Persist in it at the sunset of every night,
And greatness of awe and sublimity of rank,
And you will not be in want, and joys eternally,
And you will not suffer anything hateful or harmful,
And you will reach your aspirations truly,
By what suffices you over Zayd and Umar.

These verses are for the abundance of sustenance.

[Centered heading in a double-lined box]
Spiritual Practice of Surah Fatiha and Its Benefits

Its method is that you should seclude yourself in a dark room wherein, aside from God the Most High, no one can see you. Beginning from Sunday, keep a three-day fast. With this condition of the spiritual practice: that you should break your fast with bread and olive oil. Do not eat bread until your stomach is full. After every prayer, recite Surah Fatiha one hundred times, then recite this supplication:

My Lord, immerse me in the depths of the ocean of Your Oneness and the tumultuous waters of Your Uniqueness, until I emerge to the freshness of Your mercy. And upon my face, may there be flashes of the Quran from the signs of Your mercy—majestic through Your awe, mighty through Your strength, glorious through Your glory. And clothe me with the robes of glory and acceptance, and make easy for me the dissolving mergings of union and meeting. And crown me with the crown of honor, and create affection between me and Your beloved ones, O Lord of this world and the next, O You who took Ibrahim as an intimate friend, and spoke to Musa with direct speech, and honored Muhammad, may God's prayers and peace be upon him, with honor. Peace be upon a believing Lord, the Merciful. O Lord of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship; You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path—the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have incurred wrath, nor of those who are astray.
