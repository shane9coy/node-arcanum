# Page 764 (English translation)

The benefit of these names is also the same—by performing their taksīr, every difficulty will be resolved, and they will receive divine inspiration.

These names are also appropriate from among these names:
Al-Sharīʿu al-Qarību al-Laṭīfu al-Khabīru

Whoever performs the taksīr of the name Al-Sarīʿu al-Qarību Ṭā-Hā and keeps it with himself, no task will be difficult for him, and whatever he wishes to subjugate, it will be subjugated for him immediately. This dhikr is suitable for the People of Retreat, because whenever they are in need, Almighty Allah inspires them with matters of elevation, and among the Quranic verses, this verse is appropriate for it:

"And with Him are the keys of the unseen" Ṭā-Hā

The People of Kashf should perform the wird of Al-Laṭīf al-Khabīr. This supplication is also beneficial:

Whoever performs the wird of these two names Al-Laṭīfu Khabīru, no matter will remain difficult for him. Their dhikr is suitable for the People of Mukāshafāt, and this is an extremely beneficial supplication.

Yā man wujūduhu aṣlu li-kulli mawjūdin Ṭā-Hā, wa ḥaṣala min wujūdihi ismun yulīqu bihi wa huwa miftāḥuhu al-khāṣṣu fī ḥaqīqati al-wujūdiyyati wa sitruhu al-muqābalu, fa-mā fī al-kawni jawharun fardun min jawāhiri ajzā'i al-ʿālami al-ʿulwiyyi wa al-suflī illā wa maqālīdu aḥkāmihi mutaʿalliqatun bi-asrāri asmā'ihi wa ijtimā'ihā bi-raqā'iqihā fī sirri ismika alladhī satartu bihi jamīʿa khalqika fa-lā yaẓhuru lahum illā mā lānati al-afʿālu fa-asmā'uka yā ilāhanā lā tuḥṣā wa maʿlūmātuka lā nihāyata lahā as'aluka ghumṣatan in yakhruja hādhā al-nūru ḥattā adʿū ilā al-kamāli al-awwali fa-lā taṣarrufa bihi fī al-kawni al-kamālu naṣaruhā tabqā al-nafsu ʿaynan bi-al-wuqūfi ʿalā ʿubūdiyyati al-nafsi innaka anta Allāhu al-ʿAzīzu al-ʿAdlu al-Laṭīfu al-Khabīru al-Ḥakīmu al-ʿAdlu al-Mujību Ṭā-Hā

Whoever recites this supplication ten times, Almighty Allah will protect all his affairs from waswās, and this verse is appropriate for it:

"Thus do We relate to you some of the news of the messengers" etc.

After the recitation of these names, the chest becomes dilated, sustenance is expanded, and the jinn will flee:

From among the divine names, these names are appropriate for it: Al-Mughīthu al-Qawiyyu al-Ḥasību. Whoever is constant in it, Almighty Allah will solve his difficulty, dilate his chest, and whatever he asks from God, God will give him; and he will be completely saved from highway robbery, warfare, and river storms, or the king's anger subsiding or a need being fulfilled—in short, whatever task he makes supplication for will be accepted. However, it is obligatory upon him to perform it after purifying himself and offering two rak'ahs in a place of retreat, and presence of heart is also essential. This supplication is also beneficial. Let him recite it on Sunday at the fifteenth hour:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka madadan rūḥāniyyan taqawwa bihi quwāya al-kulliyyati wa al-juz'iyyati ḥattā aqahara bi-quwwati nafsī kulla nafsin qāhiratin fa-tanqabiḍu lahā raqā'iquhā inqibāḍan yatadawwalu ʿanhā fa-lā yabqā fī al-kawni dhū rūḥin illā nāru al-qahri akhmadat ẓuhūrahu yā shadīdu yā dhā al-baṭshi al-shadīdi yā qahhāru as'aluka bi-mā awdaʿta ʿIzrā'īla min quwā asmā'ika al-qahriyyati fa-al-faʿalati lahu al-nufūsu bi-al-qahri an tajʿala dhālika al-nūra fī hādhihi al-sāʿati ḥattā layluna lī kullu ṣaʿbin
