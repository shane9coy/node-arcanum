**Benefits of Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim:**

Now we return to the benefits of Bismillah. When this verse was revealed, the people of heaven were extremely pleased. Upon its descent, the Throne of Allah began to shake, and countless angels descended with it, whose number is known only to Allah the Most High. The faith of the angels increased, and the celestial spheres moved. Before Its glory, rulers were humbled.

**The Practice for Fulfilling Needs, Protection from the Evil of Satan, and Becoming Wealthy Within One Year:**

Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim was written on the forehead of Adam, peace be upon him. Even five hundred years before his creation, it was written on the forehead of Gabriel, peace be upon him. When he came to make the fire a garden for Abraham, peace be upon him, he said to the fire, "In the name of Allah, O fire, become cool and safe for Abraham, peace be upon him." Bismillah was written in the Syriac language on the staff of Moses, peace be upon him, because had there been no Bismillah, the Nile River would not have split from the striking of the staff. Bismillah was written on the tongue of Jesus, peace be upon him. When he spoke in the cradle, he would recite Bismillah over the dead, and they would come back to life. Bismillah was written on the ring of Solomon, peace be upon him. Among the special properties of Bismillah is that it is written at the beginning of every surah of the Noble Quran. Among its properties is that whoever reads it seven hundred and eighty-six (786) times in accordance with its numerical value and letters, consecutively for seven days, whatever intention he holds shall be fulfilled—whether for obtaining good, warding off evil, or for the advancement of trade and so forth. Among its properties is that whoever recites it twenty-one times before sleeping shall be protected from Satan that night. He shall also be protected from theft, sudden death, and every misfortune. When he comes before an oppressor and recites this number of times, Allah the Most High will instill fear of the reciter in the oppressor'S heart, and he shall be protected from his evil. And if at sunrise he faces the sun and recites Bismillah three hundred times and an equal number of blessings upon the Noble Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, Allah the Most High will provide him sustenance from a place he never expected, and before even one year passes, he shall become greatly wealthy. For the purpose of love, if a person recites Bismillah seven hundred and eighty-six (786) times, blows on water, and gives it to drink, he will come to love very intensely.

**The Practice for Sharpness of Mind:**

And if a slow-witted person drinks the same water on an empty stomach in the morning, his mind will become very sharp, and whatever he hears will be retained in memory.

**For Rain of Mercy, Love in People's Hearts, Deliverance from Poverty and Debt:**

Among the properties of Bismillah is that when it is recited sixty-one (61) times with the intention of seeking rain, Allah the Most High will send down rain upon that place. When it is recited two thousand five hundred times for forty days after the morning prayer with sincere intention and humility of heart, Allah the Most High will reveal to him the profound mysteries and secrets, and whatever is going to happen in the world will become known to him in dreams beforehand. However, ascetic practice and abstinence are conditions for this. He will see wondrous things and achieve success. If he needs to go to a ruler, he should fast on Thursday and break his fast with dates and olive oil. After the Maghrib prayer, let him recite Bismillah one hundred and eleven times and continue supplicating for his need until sleep overtakes him. Then on Friday morning after the Fajr prayer, let him write it one hundred and eleven times with musk and saffron and rosewater on paper, and let him burn incense of agarwood and amber. By Allah, whoever keeps this with him shall never face destitution. He shall be in people's eyes like the full moon of the fourteenth night. He shall obtain honor and obedience from everyone. Whoever sees him shall love him and fulfill his need.
