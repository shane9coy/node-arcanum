# Page 734 (English translation)
This is that once the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was walking on a road. He heard an Arab man praying thus:

"Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismika al-'aẓīm al-a'aẓam al-mannān mālik al-mulki dhū al-jalāl wa al-ikrām"

(The Greatest Name by which every supplication is answered):

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "This person has prayed with the Greatest Name. When supplication is made with it, it is accepted; when something is asked, it is given. Whoever desires to draw near through this Name must perform the 'azīma and recite this Name according to its numerical value.

Karbiyāʾīl, Dunyāyīl, and Harmiyāʾīl will be present in service. Each of them has 70 rows under his command, who obtain help over the kingdom, and all the secrets of creation are revealed to him, and power over the entire world is attained by him. The figure of this Name is 25 by 25 and possesses great special properties.

Its talisman is beneficial for many purposes:

When this figure is written and Sūrat al-Ḥadīd is written around it, in the honor of the Sun (Sharaf Shams), and Sūrat al-Shams and Sūrat al-Mulk, recite over it, then keep this talisman with yourself. Weapons will have no effect upon it. For the purpose of silencing, write this talisman with the name of the desired person, recite Sūrat Yāsīn over it, and keep it with yourself for three months. For rulers, for establishing dominance, for resolving difficulties, and when written on silk and placed upon one's head, terror and acceptance are obtained, and government becomes established. If written on a single page and placed in a house, there will be goodness and blessing, and people will turn toward it.

From binding it, a pregnant woman's fetus will not miscarry:

A woman whose fetus is miscarrying should keep it with herself; the pregnancy will remain stable and safe. For reconciling two men, dissolve it and give it to both in food or water, and the desire will be fulfilled. In the same way, extrapolate all its benefits for whatever purpose one wishes to write it according to its number or in the ascendant; it will give benefit. I have seen some scholars who wrote it on silver thread and also drew the figure of the desired person upon it and hung it. It gives [unclear] for every purpose, but it will not become corrupted.

Write on silk and keep it in your lap; beauty and adornment will increase, desires will be fulfilled:

If written on a silk cloth and kept in your lap, your beauty and adornment will increase. Likewise, it has special properties in it for the fulfillment of needs and so on. Write the divine names around it and give it fumigation with perfume so that the operation may be complete.
