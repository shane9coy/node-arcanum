Chapter 23: Numerical Attributes and the Third Method

Knowledge and Divine Secrets are Gifts:

One should know that among the Asma al-Husna (the Beautiful Names), al-'Alim al-Hakim al-Basit al-Karim al-Wahhab al-Tawwab al-Nasir al-Badi' 'Allam al-Ghuyub contain various properties and secrets. If the reciter performs the dhikr of these names in this way, Allah the Most High grants him knowledge and divine secrets such that no one in this age has information about them. He receives blessings, good character, and gifts of wisdom, and divine assistance is obtained in every task. The names al-Hakim al-'Azim are also highly beneficial names. Whoever wishes to obtain wisdom or knowledge should sit alone, with his head resting on the ground, facing the qiblah, and perform their dhikr. Allah the Most High will grant him wisdom and knowledge, and his desire will be fulfilled. If in the dignity of Mercury, when Jupiter is conjunct with it, he engraves its square (four by four) on green jade and keeps it with him, Allah the Most High will make him eloquent in wisdom, and whatever he writes his gaze upon will be memorized.

The Practice for Abundance of Sustenance and Countless Benefits:

The names of Allah the Most High, al-Basit 'Allam al-Ghuyub are glorious names. Through their dhikr, forgetfulness is removed and abundance in sustenance occurs. Whoever on the fourteenth of any month engraves the square (four by four) of al-Basit on a silver ring with a golden inlay and wears it, joy is produced in his heart and sustenance is expanded. These names contain secrets whose explanation is impossible.

Abundance of Sustenance and Advancement in Trade:

Through the dhikr of the names of Allah the Most High, al-Karim al-Wahhab, abundant sustenance and advancement in trade occur. Their reciter is never poor. Whoever engraves both these names on an agate ring and wears it on the left hand, sustenance comes to him easily and people's hearts incline toward him. Whoever takes their letters by numerical reduction and writes them in gold, silver, or saffron during the dignity of the Sun, places them in a bag of money, and continues spending from them, money will never be exhausted—provided that all the money is not taken out at once.

Victory in War and Battle:

The names of Allah the Most High, al-Tawwab al-Nasir contain a great secret. Through the dhikr of both these names, the gaze of Allah the Most High falls upon one with favor. He prevails over his enemies, especially in the battlefield. Whoever performs the dhikr of these names, no harm will reach him. If he writes the numerical values of these names on white silk during an auspicious hour and hangs it on his banner, there will be victory and triumph. From the Holy Quran, this verse and similar verses are appropriate for it: "Then they shall not be able to harm you in any way. You are the victors."

Acquisition of Sciences, Arts, and the Summit of Wisdom:

The dhikr of the names of Allah the Most High, al-Badi' 'Allam al-Ghuyub is appropriate for whoever wishes to write a book in any science. Through their dhikr, his task becomes easy, especially the three complete arts, which are most difficult. Whoever keeps the duration of al-Badi' near him obtains eloquence and hyperbole. Their dhikr is very appropriate for those who compose poetry. Whoever recites 'Allam al-Ghuyub along with Hakim and 'Alim, every intention of his is fulfilled, and the regret of longing flows from the heart. If any person on the first Friday of the dignity of Jupiter writes on deerskin parchment or keeps it for seven nights in the house and keeps it with him, Allah the Most High grants expansion through understanding of knowledge. Finally, for forty days, every day with tasbih (glorification) he performs the ward of the name 'Allam al-Ghuyub, then all the conditions of creation become known to him and information about the unseen will be obtained.
