⟨ The Operation for Always Remaining Wealthy ⟩

Some scholars say that whoever wishes to obtain wealth, poverty relief, and great treasure should inscribe the following verse on a gold or silver plate during the first hour of Thursday and keep it with him. If one wishes to complete the operation, one should observe a forty-day fast, breaking the fast only with lawful animal food. The verse is this:

قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ مَالِكَ الْمُلْكِ تُؤْتِي الْمُلْكَ مَنْ تَشَاءُ وَتَنْزِعُ الْمُلْكَ مِمَّنْ تَشَاءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَنْ تَشَاءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَنْ تَشَاءُ بِيَدِكَ الْخَيْرُ إِنَّكَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ تُولِجُ اللَّيْلَ فِي النَّهَارِ وَتُولِجُ النَّهَارَ فِي اللَّيْلِ وَتُخْرِجُ الْحَيَّ مِنَ الْمَيِّتِ وَتُخْرِجُ الْمَيِّتَ مِنَ الْحَيِّ وَتَرْزُقُ مَنْ تَشَاءُ بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ

Recite Surah al-Watiin after sunrise one thousand times daily, then make this supplication: اللہُمَّ يَسِّرْ عَلَىَّ الشَّيْئَ الَّذِي بِيَسَّرْتَهُ عَلَى كَثِيرٍ مِنْ عِبَادِكَ فَاغْنِيَنِ بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ O Allah, make easy for me that which You have made easy for many of Your servants, and by Your grace make me independent and free of want from all besides You. Place this talisman in a pure pouch with forty rupees. Then, whenever you need to spend money, recite the verse a corresponding number of times, take rupees from it, and spend them. The rupees in the pouch will never decrease, however much you spend. This is specific to men of wealth, but sincerity of intention is a condition for it. We have opened the gate of fortune for you; now its acquisition lies in your hands.

⟨ Verses for Obtaining Ministry and Governorship ⟩

These are the verses for obtaining ministry and emirate:

وَاجْعَلْ لِي وَزِيرًا مِنْ أَهْلِي هَارُونَ أَخِي اشْدُدْ بِهِ أَزْرِي وَجَعَلْنَا مَعَهُ أَخَاهُ هَارُونَ وَزِيرًا

And appoint for me a minister from my family, Aaron my brother; strengthen me through him. And We made his brother Aaron a minister with him.

⟨ The Elixir Operation for Love and Obedience ⟩

This operation is an elixir for love and obedience.

وَأَلْقَيْتُ عَلَيْكَ مَحَبَّةً مِّنِّي وَ لِتُصْنَعَ عَلَى عَيْنِي لَوْ أَنْفَقْتَ مَا فِي الْأَرْضِ جَمِيعًا مَّا أَلَّفْتَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِهِمْ وَلَكِنَّ اللَّهَ أَلَّفَ بَيْنَهُمْ إِنَّهُ عَزِيزٌ حَكِيمٌ وَأَلَّفَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِهِمْ

And I cast upon you love from Me, and so that you may be formed under My eye. If you were to spend all that is in the earth, you could not bring their hearts together, but Allah brought them together; indeed He is Mighty, Wise. And He brought their hearts together [damaged source/watermark]
