# Page 88 (English translation)

Every person writes it on [unclear: ہیان] parchment and shows [it]; in fevers and cold, if [unclear: ڈالے] one binds it to a person, the troubles disappear. The reason for this is that there is an intellectual power in numbers which points toward the spiritual world, and the power of letters points toward the physical world, and in this context, the spirituality of letters appears with physical subtleties, while numbers are specific to spiritual subtleties. Every person who understood the mysteries of the Messiah knows the secret of this bell sound which is revelation.

[Heading] The Connection of the Letter Meem with Revelation:

It was asked of the Noble Prophet ﷺ: "How does revelation come to you?" He said: "Sometimes it takes the form of the sound of a bell, and sometimes an angel comes in the form [of a person] and speaks to me. Whatever he says, I memorize it." A bell is called a ghanta or ghonghara. You see that when they are on the necks of camels or horses and they walk along the path, how they make sounds and their voice comes from very far away. This is the quality of a person which is in the sound of a bell, and the voice of revelation appears sharper than it. The bell resounds with meem; the "correspondence" is similar to intensity and fear. Have you not heard the saying of the Noble Prophet ﷺ about the body, power, and obedience of Israfel, peace be upon him—that despite being of such great creation, he placed the leg of the Throne upon his shoulder and his eyes are fixed upon the Tablet, and he has placed the Trumpet in his mouth whose length is a distance of five hundred years. Both his feet extend below the seven earths. He looks toward the Throne, waiting for the command: "When will it be, so that I may blow into the Trumpet?" And meem is last in order because the Trumpet is for terror and distress, screaming and frightening, and blowing—that is, puffing—is not possible without pressing both lips together. In the same way, meem also only emerges from the joining of the lips. Without joining them, the letter meem cannot emerge at all. For this reason, along with the ghonghara, one sees the shriek of the bell; the sound of the bell comes from the power of the voice. From this, you will understand the difference between the sound of a ghonghara and the sound of dragging a chain on stone, which Moses gave as a simile for revelation. The sound of the bell is spiritual motion, and the sound of the chain is physical motion.

[Heading] The Two Aspects of the Letter Meem:

The letter meem has two aspects: one celestial and one terrestrial, which is also called "meem thaniya." Since the mysteries of spirituality, intellectual concepts, and the mysteries of physicality are contained in meem, therefore its numbers are related to the celestial realms and its letters are related to the terrestrial realms. The letter meem is pronounced from the outer part [of the mouth], and between its two heats there is one moisture. In this manner meem [unclear: بسلائم] second, or third meem, and both meems are hot or cold, and their correspondence and union is from these two heats. If there were no moisture between these two, this correspondence would not occur—understand this and verify it. And from the letter meem alone, the Great Name "Bismillah" is derived, and the letter meem is the demonstrative pronoun in the blessed name of the Messenger ﷺ.

[Heading] The Circle of Meem and Its Properties:

Whoever writes the letter meem, its form, and its correspondence, and recites upon it what will be mentioned, then keeps it with himself, and goes to kings and great officials of courts, will become beloved to them. People will hold him dear; he will prevail over his enemies. If he comes before a lion, it will become weak and lowly before him and fear him. If he goes to war and battle, he will overcome his enemies. And whoever finds victory and triumph under his feet—anyone standing before him will incline toward him. It has many other useful properties. "And Allah speaks the truth, and He guides to the way." Meem is the form of its circle.
