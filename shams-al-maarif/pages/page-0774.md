The winds in the atmosphere of the sky and the knowledge of their courses, and He created night and day, and made the darknesses and the light and the illuminations in the eyes and the rivers, and He caused the trees and the fruits to grow, and He fixed the mountains upon the surface of the earth and the foundation, and He subjected everything with the power of the Incomparables, and He gathered the opposites, and He decreed for all creatures annihilation. So glory be to Him who is the Creator who created creatures most excellently and perfected the fashioned things without trials or instruments—rather, His command when He wills something is to say to it "Be," and it becomes. PRAISE

O You who are illuminated by the light of His glory, and the celestial spheres revolve by His destined decrees, and the necks of rulers and kings have submitted to the might of His sovereignty, and by all that Your knowledge encompasses, and glory be to Your forbearance, and by Your most beautiful names and Your supreme attributes and Your countless signs, by Your name in which the absent and the present travel, and by Your complete words which neither the righteous nor the wicked can surpass, and by the light of Your generous face, I ask You, O God, with an absolute request beyond which there is no goal, no end to the goal, and no named division, that You bless our master Muhammad, Your trustworthy servant and Your clear messenger, the seal of the prophets and messengers, and his family, his wives, and his honored household, and all the prophets and messengers, and all the people of Your obedience altogether, and protect us

O God, from the evil of what You created and produced and brought into being, and from the evil of what enters the earth and what emerges from it, and from the evil of what descends from the heaven and what ascends therein, and from the evil of every creature whose forelock You seize—surely my Lord is upon a straight path.

O God, grant us from knowledge its most beneficial, and from action its most elevated, and from sustenance its most expansive, and from speech its most truthful, and from certainty its most firm, and from virtues its most complete, and from patience its most beautiful, and from wisdom its most just, and from piety its most enduring, and from guidance its greatest, and from livelihood its most blessed, and from the gaze its most honorable, and from mercy its most generous, and from blessings its most encompassing, and from well-being its most beautiful, and from worship its most excellent. O God, so give good news to the Creator and that which has brought us to a beautiful return, and grant us safety at the Great Terror, and do not test us at the revolution of the rising place, and do not disgrace us before the assembled witnesses in that gathering.

O God, surely our sins have preceded us to You, and we have presented what we have advanced and what we have delayed in the preserved tablet. They await us, and we await mercy. Where and how You revived every thing, and how You caused every living being to live.

O God, due to us is what we await of Your mercy, and safety from what we fear. Do not take us to task for what we have advanced, and forgive us what we have committed.

O God, grant us of beautiful certainty what will make the clear hardships easy for us, and provide us with good assumption through which we are confident of attaining the desire, and protect us from the injustice of the unjust and the malice of the envious who are astray.

O God, give us the reward of those who return to You in repentance, and generously reward the humbly devoted, and record us among the righteous, and admit us by Your mercy among Your righteous servants.

O God, do not place in our hearts malice toward those who believe, and employ us in what will please You concerning us, and appoint for us from beside You a guardian who desires forgiveness.

Nor for what is more honorable than you have inspired—You are the one sought for refuge by the question before the question, the Giver, with a tongue: "Surely for us every"
