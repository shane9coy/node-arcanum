# Page 167 (English translation)
There is no god but He; He gives life and causes death. Then know that there is no god but Allah, and seek forgiveness for your sin. He is Allah, there is no god but He.
(End of Surah al-Hashr) Allah, there is no god but He, and upon Allah let the believers rely. Lord of the East and the West, there is no god but He; so take Him as protector.

Translation: There is no deity but He, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. There is no god but Allah. He is the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. Alif-Lam-Mim are disconnected letters. There is no god but Allah; He is the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. He is the Essence who forms shapes within the realms of mercy. There is no god but Allah. He will gather you on the Day of Resurrection. He is Allah, your Lord; there is no god but Him. He is the Creator of all things. So follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord. There is no god but Him. Say: O people, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all. They have been commanded to worship only one deity. If they turn away, then say: Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but Him. Until when death came upon him, he called out to Allah, the Most High, and it is true. There is no god but He, and He is the Lord of the noble Throne. He is Allah; there is no god but Him. Praise is due to Him alone. O people, remember the blessing of Allah upon you. Indeed, when it is said to them: There is no god but Allah, they are filled with arrogance. To Him belongs sovereignty; there is no god but Him. So where are you turning away from the truth? The Quran is a revelation from Allah, and He is the Mighty, the Knowing. He is the Forgiver of sin, the Acceptor of repentance, the Severe in punishment, the Possessor of power. There is no god but Him, and to Him is the return. He is your Lord, the Creator of all things; there is no god but He. He gives life and causes death. Know that there is no god but He, and seek forgiveness for your sin. He is Allah; there is no god but Him, and upon Allah let the believers rely. He is the Lord of the East and the West; there is no god but Him; make Him your disposer of affairs.

[Eight-pointed star / Rub al-Hizb]
