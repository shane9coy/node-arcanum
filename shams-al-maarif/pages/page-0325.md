# Page 325 (English translation)
325

During times of difficulty, one should make it one's means of approach. One will remain protected from all fear and distress. Hazrat Ali (may God honor his face) used to pray in times of hardship and distress as follows: O Allah, O Kahiya'ṣ and O Ḥam'asaqa, forgive me and have mercy upon me. And he used to say: Whoever among you supplicates with this name, it will be accepted and the need will be fulfilled. The form of the talisman is as follows:

[Diamond-shaped talismanic grid composed of Arabic letters (k, h, y, ʿ, ṣ, ḥ, m, ʿ, s, q) and numbers arranged in a 4x4 diamond pattern. The numbers are: 48, 51, 155, 114 (top row); 94, 44, 47, 34 (second row); 94, 23, 34, 56 (third row); 19, 53, 74, 22 (bottom row).]

Whoever writes Kahiya'ṣ and Ḥam'asaqa as an enslaved letter talisman on a silver plate, let him observe such properties that language is incapable of describing. It has an extraordinary effect for economic prosperity. The form of this talisman is as follows.

**The Most Effective Talisman of the Great Magnet and Red Khabrit**

[A 10x10 square table containing a repeating sequence of Arabic letters from the groups 'Kaf-Ha-Ya-Ain-Sad' and 'Ha-Meem-Ain-Seen-Qaf'. Each row is a cyclical shift of the letters.]

This is his supplication: In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by Kahiya'ṣ Ḥam'asaqa that You suffice me from every great matter and that You turn away from me every misery. O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, I ask You that in the matter of Kahiya'ṣ and Ḥam'asaqa, You help me in every great task of mine and remove every misfortune and calamity from me. These five verses are suitable for this adaptation. They are as follows:

kama'in anzalnahu mina al-sama'i fa-ikhtalata bihi nabatu al-ardi fa-asbaha hashiman tadrūhu al-riyaḥu wa-kana Allāhu
