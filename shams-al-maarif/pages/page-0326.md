[Quranic verses from Surah Al-Ghashiyah and Surah Qaf] He has power over all things; He is Allah, there is no god but He, the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed; He is the Compassionate, the Merciful. On the Day of the Approaching [the Day of Judgment], when hearts reach the throats, choking them. What is [the benefit] for the wrongdoers? [The Day] when every soul will know what it has brought forth. So I do not swear by the retreating stars that hide [in the daylight]. Qaf. By the Quran containing the reminder. Rather those who disbelieve are in arrogance and dissension.

Its properties are very numerous. Sheikh Zayn al-Din says: It liberates the prisoner from imprisonment. Its virtues are very great. It corresponds to the planet Venus; it is fitting for it. We have composed it for these noble verses, and Venus is a fortunate star. In felicity and temperance, it takes the place of Sa'd al-Akbar. It works with its nature to increase affection, the sweetness of the pure life, the increase of love, and joy. He added its degree to the Siraji [text]; its corners are ten divine names, five from Surah Al-Fatiha and five from Surah Al-An'am. Understand them. Some scholars have said: When you wish to reveal the existence of an absent person, recite the five verses with presence of heart fifty times and invoke him; he will appear immediately. As for the letter Sad, it is the blessed name of the Prophet ﷺ. Its numerical value is 14, half the number of the moon. It is the Greater Veil, useful for every purpose.

Tongue-Binding:
When you fear a tyrant or king, pick up five pebbles from the ground. On the first, recite the letter Kaf; on the second, the letter Ha; on the third, the letter Ya; on the fourth, the letter 'Ayn; and on the fifth, the letter Sad. Throw the first pebble to your right side and say: "Fa hiya." Throw the second pebble to your left side and say: "La." Throw the third pebble behind you and say: "Wa la." Throw the fourth pebble in front of you and say: "Inna al-thuluth." Then place the fifth on your head and say: "Kaf Ha Ya 'Ayn Sad Ha Mim 'Ayn Sin Qaf. Hold back your tongue, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, by the right of the Greatest Name and by the right of these noble names. Kaf Ha Ya 'Ayn Sad Ha Mim 'Ayn Sin Qaf. [They are] deaf, dumb, and blind, so they do not return. They do not see." Allah the Exalted will bind his tongue. And this is a hidden secret. When you fear an enemy in a place and wish to be unseen, draw a line with your finger behind your back and make a circle around you on all four sides. Read this eleven times, then become silent and speak to no one. Even if all the people of the world attack you, they will not be able to see you. Some gnostics say: Recite these verses seventy times when going before a ruler, judge, or tyrant. When you reach sixty, close the first finger of your right hand while saying Kaf; close the second finger while saying Ha; close the third finger while saying Ya; close the fourth and fifth fingers while saying Sad. Likewise, close the fingers of your left hand with the letters of Ha Mim 'Ayn Sin Qaf; then both hands will be closed. Go before the king and open the palm facing him; that king or ruler will become kind. This is its form:
