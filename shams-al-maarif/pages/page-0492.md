# Page 492 (English translation)
[492]

**The Descent of the Seventh Throne**

Regarding which the Noble Prophet ﷺ stated: Our Lord descends upon the lowest heaven in the last part of the night. The Throne of Souls is Beyt al-Maʿmūr. Its circuit is above Beyt al-Maʿmūr. All these Thrones are in reality mysteries. Upon them lies the extremity of the World of Secrets and the Hidden Reality. For this reason, the veil has been attributed to night and day. Whoever understands the mysteries and secrets of the veil, the secret of the acceptance of prayer will also become known to him.

**What are the Seven Veils:**
These are the seven veils: (1) Sitr al-Mulk (2) Sitr al-Murakkab (3) Sitr al-Dawāʾir, in which is the fourth movement. (4) Sitr al-Ghayb—this is from the East. (5) Sitr al-Jabarūt al-Awsaṭ—this is the Interworld. (6) Sitr al-Nafs—this is pure imagination. Its connection is with dispensation. (7) Sitr al-Qalb—this veil is in the most subtle intention and action. Sitr al-ʿAql connects the scattered.

**Which veils are called Ḥijāb:**
In terms of order and the arrangement of letters, and with respect to light and elevation, all these veils are called ḥijāb and attraction. Between the sciences and knowledges of the righteous, the reformer, the subtle and the dense— whoever has lifted these veils has known the reality of the Hidden Sanctum. Spiritual subtleties have been unveiled to him. He will meet face to face; there will be success. His reaching has extended to this particular Throne. This is the extremity of the breaths of humans—this is the light of strong sakīnah and the prophetic theophanies, light and mercy and reaching.

**The beginning of the manifestation of miracles and extraordinary feats:**
At this very station, the manifestation of miracles and extraordinary feats attains the rank of violation of customary practice. From this station manifestation is found. From the oceans of gnosis to the shores of guidance—if you can swim, then hurry. If you can walk, then make haste in walking. These are the true pearls of allusions that emerge from the veils of expression. These are the sublime realities that manifest from the lowest abodes. Their value is very little; buy them at a cheap price. The wealth of the capital of works that you have accumulated—spend it in making the lap of the common people wide. That is, spend it in the service of the poor and the needy.

**Causes to avoid regrettable failure:**
So that the laughter of regret and failure may not be heard saying "Alas, we have done nothing." Allāh the Exalted says:
**"Our Lord, bring us out of this [world] that we may do righteous deeds different from those we used to do. Did We not give you life long enough for whoever would remember to remember, and did there not come to you a warner?"**
O Allāh! O Lord, return us to the world so that we may perform righteous good deeds other than those sins we committed before. Allāh will say: "Did I not give you such life and respite that within it you could understand what My worship is? And did I not send the warners among the prophets to your time?"

**What benefit will come from regretting failures?**
May Allāh keep us all safe from failure. May Allāh accept the service ofḤaḍrat Muftī ṣāḥib. Āmīn. By the rank of the Noble Prophet, Āmīn.
