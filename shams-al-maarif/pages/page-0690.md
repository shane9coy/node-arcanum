Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Muqit"
[End decorative box]

It creates strength in bodies from food:

Muqit is the one who creates sustenance, and upon his table the superior spirits also feed, and in the interior of the affair, he is Muqit with various kinds of foods, and he is the one who satisfies the body.

It gives strength to the soul through dhikr:

He gives nourishment to the spirits and bodies in every way, so that the foundation may be firm and the soul may remain established. Whoever person performs the dhikr of this name, whatever he desires is granted to him. If it is inscribed on a silver ring and worn by the person who needs strength, Allah the Exalted will assist him. And when inscribed with the name Razzāq and placed in a house, there will be abundant blessing in it.

For protection from psychological ailments:

For those people who suffer from psychological ailments, this name is very beneficial, and this is its dhikr:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are al-Muqīt, who created every thing and gave it its sustenance, and made for it within it the remedy, and brought forth types of foods and drinks, and made the cause of the forms, and manifested categories of sciences and secrets, and made them beautiful for the spirits. And make for me strength for the righteous acts of obedience that bring nearness to You, O Lord of the earths and the heavens.
