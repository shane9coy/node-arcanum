Chapter 18: The Blessings of Ayat al-Kursi and Its Innumerable Benefits and Properties
[/Decorative heading box]

Know that every name has meanings that indicate, and among the Books of Allah, the greatest of names is Ayat al-Kursi, for Allah the Most High has said:
"His Kursi extends over the heavens and the earth." [Quran 2:255] And in Alif-Lam-Mim, there are great meanings, for from the Alif is "Allah," from the Lam is "lahu" [to Him belongs], from the Mim is "Malik al-Mulk" [the Sovereign of Dominion] is meant.

Know that when this verse was revealed upon the Noble Prophet ﷺ, seventy thousand angels descended with it because of its greatness. This verse grants nobility, majesty, sublimity, and exaltedness. This verse is the Lady of the Quran. It has 50 letters, 50 words, and 7 sections.

Whoever recites this verse according to the number of its letters and seeks intercession from any king, it will be accepted. If any person is in difficulty and he recites this verse at midnight with ablution, facing the Qiblah, according to the aforementioned number, with utmost humility and reverence, then makes supplication, it will be accepted.

If he recites it 225 times at midnight, he will remain protected from enemies and the enemy will be destroyed. If he recites it 313 times, every religious and worldly difficulty will be eased, and the doors of hidden knowledge will be opened.

The Acquisition of Knowledge and Wisdom:

Among its properties is this: if its letters are written separately in a Chinese vessel with musk, saffron, and rose water, and he drinks it for as many days as the number of its words, while fasting and breaking his fast with it, then Allah the Most High will deepen him in the arts of wisdom. However, it must be begun in the month of Nissan (a month of the Roman calendar). If he washes it with the water of Nissan and drinks it, it will be most beneficial. At the time of breaking the fast, he should recite Ayat al-Kursi seven times and blow [upon himself]:

"O Allah, I ask You by the right of this noble verse that You inspire me with Your innate knowledge. O Allah."

When you seek any scholar, mention this verse; your purpose will be fulfilled and there will be success. I have taught this method to certain friends, and they acted upon it. Even before the aforementioned number was completed, Allah the Most High manifested unveiled sciences upon them, and they achieved their purpose and desire.

A particular property is that when you wear new clothes and at the time of wearing you say these words:

"O Allah, just as You have clad me in new garments, make me beloved and happy, and grant me an extended lifespan."

By the effect of this verse, they will seek forgiveness for him as long as he wears those clothes, until they become worn out. If Surah "Inna Anzalnahu" [Quran 97] is recited with it, it is much better.

[Decorative heading box]
Healing from Headache and Every Type of Pain
[/Decorative heading box]

Among the properties of Ayat al-Kursi is that when you visit a sick person and inquire about the patient's condition: if the illness begins from the head, place your hand upon his head and recite Ayat al-Kursi; there will be recovery. If there is pain throughout the patient's entire body, recite Ayat al-Kursi upon him. Writing it in a vessel with musk, saffron, and rose water and giving it to drink is most effective. The verses of healing should also be written with it. The verses of healing are these:

"And He heals the breasts of a believing people. And healing for what is in the breasts, and guidance and mercy for the believers." [Quran 10:57] "In it is healing for the people." [Quran 16:69]
