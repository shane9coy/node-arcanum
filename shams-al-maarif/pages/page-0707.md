# Page 707 (English translation)
Through its recitation, presence is attained, and what is missing is found.
Reciting it in accordance with its number produces the power of presence. One recitation is that whoever has lost something should mention it according to its number, and it will return. This is among the blessed names' remembrances. Allah the Exalted should be written on a silver king, and when worn, his status will be elevated and his rule will endure forever.
Through its square, every affair is completed:
Whoever keeps the square of these two names with him gains power in all affairs. He should establish its practice. Its form is this

And the remembrance of this name is:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Originator and the Restorer; You originated creation without precedent or example, without prior form or model, without evidence or number. I ask You to manifest upon me the lights of the secrets and the subtle spiritual realities that You created for the select of the subtle and dense corporealities, and You brought them from nonexistence and made them existent without being constrained by comprehensive knowledge, and You restore whatever You will of the various restorations of existents. I ask for the penetration of Your power in creation according to the details of Your wisdom, that You manifest in my heart the subtle lights of Your essence, through which I witness the realities of Your secrets, and that You reveal to me the sanctuaries of Your holiness, and that I may be in Your proximity and nearness. Verily, You are Allah, the Originator, the Restorer.

Through its reading, goodness, divine knowledge, and blessings' doors are opened:
Whoever persists in the supplication of these two names, Allah the Exalted opens for him the doors of good and divine sciences, and grants him guidance to the straight path. And whoever persists in this remembrance, Allah the Exalted will open for him the doors of divine knowledge, charity, and blessings, and will open his chest and guide him to the straight path.

[Boxed Heading: The Excellence: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Muhyi Al-Mumit" (The Life-Giver, The Death-Giver)]

Life and death are solely in the grasp of Allah's power:
The meaning of these (these two) points to the direction of creation and annihilation. When He wills creation, His action bears the name "Life," and when annihilation occurs, it is attributed to "the Death-Giver." There is no other creator of death and life.
