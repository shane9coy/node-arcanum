# Page 336 (English translation)
On Saturday, in the first hour, write this: its attendant is Awn Muʿīl al-Ḍarūrat, and its lord of the hour is Maimūn Abānūh; its name is Shtṭṭṭkūsh. It also has nine letters. This composition of letters is for Abraham, because there is great power in the number. The form of the talisman is as follows:

[A square talisman diagram consisting of nested squares with text in the margins and sectors.
- Along the four outer sides, the word 'شططططکوش' is repeated.
- The next layer of text within the outer square contains names: 'معیل بالضریت' (top), 'میمون ابانوح' (right), 'سید الہبب' (left), and 'شمر الہب' (bottom).
- The central square is further subdivided. Text within includes 'مدد' (top), 'یا ھو' (left), 'جبرائیل' (right), and 'صمد' (bottom).
- The very center contains several illegible numerals/symbols, including characters resembling '۸۴' and '۵'.]

It has been transmitted that its ritual protocol is as follows:

Allāhumma yā qawiyyu lā qawiyya illā llāhu taʿālā al-laylu wa-l-nahāru al-qādiru ʿalā mā yashāʾu wa-yurīdu wa-lā yakhfā ʿalayhi shayʾun mina l-ashyāʾi lā yakhāfu ʿiqāban wa-lā yarjū thawāban al-qādiru bi-qudratihi al-raḥīmu bi-raḥmatihi qad saʾaltukum ayyatu-hā l-arwāḥu bi-ismihi al-raḥmān al-raḥīmi wa-bi-l-rūḥi l-amīni jibrīʾīla wa-l-malaki l-ʿaẓīmi l-rafīʿi mīkāʾīla wa-l-malaki l-muwakkali bi-l-nafkhi isrāfīla wa-l-malaki l-marhūbi alladhī tartaʿiddu minhu l-qulūbu ʿizrāʾīla wa-ḥamala-ti l-ʿarshi ajmaʿīna illā mā amartum man yaqḍī ḥājatī wa-yataṣarrafu fī marḍātī bi-ḥaqqi nabiyyi llāhi sulaymān ʿalayhi l-salāmu wa-bi-ḥaqqi qawlihi taʿālā fa-inna ʿifrītan mina l-jinni anā ātīka bihi qabla an taqūma min maqāmika wa-innī ʿalayhi la-qawiyyun amīnun innahu min sulaymān wa-innahu bi-smi llāhi l-raḥmān al-raḥīmi allā taʿlū ʿalayya wa-atūnī muslimīna Allāhumma innī asʾaluka bi-hādhihi l-arwāḥi l-rūḥāniyyati l-kirāmi ʿalayka an tuskhatira lī l-ʿafārīta l-arbaʿata bi-qudratika wa-jalālika la-hushtashin mashhashin qaṭūshin la-hanūshin kashkashin nūshin la-shaḥshalūṭin jafjaḥin ajībū wa-tawakkalū wa-l-ifʿalū mā tu'marūna

O Allah, O the Strong—there is no strength except through Allah, the Exalted—the Creator of night and day, the Powerful over all that He wills and intends; nothing among all things is hidden from Him; He fears no retribution nor hopes for reward; the Powerful through His power, the Merciful through His mercy. I have asked you, O spirits, by His name, the Compassionate, the Merciful, and by the Trustworthy Spirit, Jibrāʾīl (Gabriel), and the great, exalted angel Mīkāʾīl (Michael), and the angel charged with insufflation, Isrāfīl, and the awe-inspiring angel at whose fear hearts tremble, ʿIzrāʾīl, and all the bearers of the Throne, that you command whoever will fulfill my need and act in accordance with my desires—by the right of the Prophet of Allah, Solomon, upon him be peace, and by the right of His exalted statement: "Then a demon from the jinn came to me"—up to His saying "they come to you in submission." O Allah, I ask You by these noble spiritual essences that You subjugate for me the four demons by Your power and majesty. Come, Hashatash, Mahshash, Qatush, Lahanush, Kashkash, Nush, Lashahshalut, Jafjah. Answer, trust, and do what you are commanded.
