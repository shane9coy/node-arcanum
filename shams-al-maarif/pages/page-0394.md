# Page 394 (English translation)
Glory be to You, the Reliever of every grieving one; glory be to You, the Liberator of every indebted one; glory be to He who made His treasuries between the kāf and nūn. O Reliever, relieve [my distress], O Lord of Needs, O Answerer of Supplications—subject to my service the servants of this noble chapter, let them obey me and fulfill my command. And grant me the visitation of the grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and make every difficulty easy for me. And subject to my service all of Your creation and Your provision. And turn toward me the hearts of Your servants—both free and enslaved, small and great, male and female—and unite their hearts for me with permanent love and affection. And grant me abundant fortune and a long life, and open for me the doors of Your mercy, and grant me lawful provision, and be for me a helper, a supporter, a protector, and a protector. O Allah, I indeed ask You, O God of the first and the last, that You subject to my service all of Your creation with permanent love, affection, and compassion, just as You subjected the sea to Moses, peace be upon him. And make soft for me their hearts, their spirits, their bodies, and their limbs, just as You softened iron for David, peace be upon him, so they do not speak except by Your permission, their forelocks are in Your grasp, and their hearts are in Your hand—exalted is Your praise and holy are Your names. There is no god but You and no lord besides You. By Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, I indeed ask You by the right of this noble chapter that You subject to my service my provision and turn toward me the hearts of Your servants, and bring to me their spirits and their bodies, by Your right and by the right of Your creation and the light of Your face, and by the right of Your prophets and messengers and the proximate angels, and by the right of Sūrat Yā-Sīn and the Wise Quran, and by the right of "Alif Lām Mīm. That is the Book—there is no doubt in it—a guidance for the righteous, whom Allah has—not there is no god but He—the Ever-Living, the Sustainer [of existence], and by the right of Alif Lām Mīm Ṣād, Hā Mīm, Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf, Ḥā Mīm, and the clear Book, and by the right of Ṣād and the Quran of remembrance, and by the right of Qāf and the glorious Quran, and by the right of "By the Turā, and a book written upon an unrolled scroll," and by the right of Nūn and the Pen and what they inscribe, and by the right of the mighty Quran in which You said—and You are the most truthful of speakers—"And We send down from the Quran what is a healing and a mercy for the believers," and by Your most beautiful, great names, and by the right of the mighty Throne and the Kerubim and the proximate angels upon our Prophet, and upon them be prayer and peace, and by the right of the heavens and earths and what is in them, and by the moving planets, and by the heaven of the constellations [seen by all], and by the heaven and the morning star, and by the right of "By the dawn when it shines," and by the right of "By the fig and the olive and Mount Sinai," and by the sanctity of the Sacred House and the Holy City, and by the sanctity of Your prophets and Your chosen ones and Your righteous servants, O Lord of all worlds, O Best of helpers, O Answerer of supplicants, O Lord of Needs, O Answerer of supplications, O Pardoner of stumbles, O Lord of good deeds, O Averter of evils, O Forgiver of evils, O Revealer of distresses. O Allah, show me the service of the special ones of this noble chapter through the visitation of the grave of Your Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and subject to my service all of Your creation, and make soft for me their hearts and their spirits with love, affection, and compassion toward me, and ease for me my provision and make light for me every difficulty, by the sanctity of Yā-Sīn and the Wise Quran. Amen, amen, amen. And relieve me, amen.
