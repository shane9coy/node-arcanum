# Page 714 (English translation)
**712**

"And He creates what you do not know." 1

**The Division of the Circles Among Them: Felicity and Wretchedness:**

This is the circle of the ethereal sphere, which encompasses the realm of the material world, and it is the circle of the Throne, and the circle of the Footstool, and the circle of the Zodiac. The purpose [of the circle of the Zodiac] is that there are nine heavens within it. One is the heaven of Saturn, one of Jupiter, one of Mars, and [the heaven of] the Sun, Venus, Mercury, and the Moon. Then beneath them are the circles of fire, that is, of air, water, and earth. The mountains are driven as pegs within the earth, and Mount Qaf surrounds the earth. Then beyond it is the encompassing ocean, and beyond that is white earth. The form is as follows:

Some say that paradise is within this earth, and these are eight circles. Each circle among them has great expanse, and the circle of the afterlife is separate from them, and it is the sign of resurrection and the awakening.

**The Seven Circles Belonging to Me:**

In this [same scheme] there are seven circles: the circle of the sphere, and the circle of the world, and the circle of messengership, and so on. Within it there are very many verses, and within each verse there are central circles. In it there is also the circle of reality and the circle of the pole, that is, the circle of the saints. This circle is very large. Its first part is the dense circle, then the circle of the soul, then the circle of the center. From it primacy is manifested. When the cycle is completed, the otherworldliness and primacy are also completed, and they are for the sake of God, Blessed and Exalted. He is the First and He is the Last.

Then I have given you such a sign so that you may be of the realm of the material world. In the afterlife, look upon His circle and say: within the circle there are four circles. One circle is of the lights of the pole, which has been written in the name of the clear circle, that is, kāf. The first name of the lights of the pole is primacy, and it is the head of the system of this knowledge. Then the first of the pole is without purpose, and the light of every body is the word—all of this is their reality and [divine] reality is [the source]. Then
