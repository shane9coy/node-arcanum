# Page 608 (English translation)
Its izāmah (invocation) is as follows:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Summon me, O Allah, by Your gentleness with overflowing blessings, as You have favored Your creation with kindness and beneficence. And that You may appoint for me a servant of the letter of separation, whom I may direct as I wish in matters of benefit—these blessings which You have granted me. O Allah, through the turning of success and action and the increase of voluntary offering: hāyā bāshī samāmī basā bihin shahriyā, by the right of Sahā, hasten for me by the secret of the great sphere, through the preservation of the wind, and by the Lord of Moses and Jesus and Dhū al-Kifl, and Ayyūb and Muḥammad, the chosen one, peace be upon him: shaf shaf shaf shaʿshaf, answer me, O Shīn, by the Lord of the Worlds.

The letter Tha (ث):

Its nature is that of fish, and it is a displaced/preternatural Alif.

To prevent nocturnal emission and repel enemies:

Its special property is that when a person suffers frequent nocturnal emission in dreams, then let him write this letter in accordance with its numerical value along with ikhlāṣ and this verse: "Blessed is He in Whose hand is the sovereignty," and keep it with himself—he will be protected from nocturnal emission. And when you write this letter on a sheet of lead and throw it upon the house of any person, that person will very quickly leave it and depart. And if you write this letter in a bowl made from the rind of a gourd and give it to one suffering from stomach pain to drink, it will seem pleasant to him and the pain will cease.

To silence an enemy's tongue:

For silencing the tongue, write this and bury it at the threshold, or dissolve it and administer it to drink. But when that person wishes to speak, his heart will become sealed from within, and he will be unable to speak. Its ritual retreat is 28 days, and its client's name is Thuriyāʾīl, whose light is like that of the sun. Having recited ikhlāṣ and ṣalawāt, make a fumigation with frankincense and mastakā.
