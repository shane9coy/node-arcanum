# Page 351 (English translation)
**The Operation and Talisman for Creating Love Between Two Enemies:**

Among the properties of Ayat al-Kursi is that it is written to create love between two individuals, as we have mentioned: begin the operation at an auspicious hour. Take a sheet of paper and write the names of both enemies upon it. Then take laban (benzoin) equal to the size of a black peppercorn, forty pebbles, and forty grains of white pepper. Divide these into two portions and place two grains [of each substance] into the incense. Recite Ayat al-Kursi until it is completed forty times. After every five recitations, say this supplication:

*Transliteration:* tawakkalū yā khuddāma hādhihi al-āyati bi-al-qalā'i al-mahabbati bayna fulānin ibni fulānata aw fulānata binti fulānata bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi al-āyati ʿalaykum wa-barakātihā ladaykum wa-bi-ḥaqqi man qāla li-al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi i'tiyā ṭawʿan aw karhan qālatā ataynā ṭāʿiʿīna

"O servants of this verse, take upon yourselves the task of placing love between So-and-so son of So-and-such, or So-and-such daughter of So-and-such, by the right of this verse upon you and its blessings with you, and by the right of the One Who said to the heavens and earth: 'Come forth, willingly or unwillingly.' They said: 'We come willingly.'"

"O servants of this verse, take upon yourselves this task of placing love between So-and-so son of So-and-such or So-and-such daughter of So-and-such, by the right of this verse and its blessings with you, and by the right of *man qāla li-al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi i'tiyā ṭawʿan aw karhan qālatā ataynā ṭāʿiʿīna*."

When you have completed this, write this talisman on the same sheet of paper and also write this supplication alongside it:

*Transliteration:* Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā ḥayyu yā qayyūmu yā man lā tarāhu al-ʿuyūnu wa-lā tukhāliṭuhu al-ẓunūnu wa-lā yanʿathuhu al-nāʿithūna yā man amruhū bayna al-kāfi wa-al-nūni innamā amruhū idhā arāda shay'an an yaqūla lahu kun fayakūnu as'aluka an tulqiya al-mahabbata wa-al-mawaddata bayna fulānin ibni fulānata wa-kaẓā bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi al-āyāti yuḥibbūnahum ka-ḥubbi Allāhi wa-alladhīna āmanū ashaddu hubban li-Allāhi law anfaqta mā fī al-arḍi jamīʿan mā allafta bayna qulūbihim al-āyatu wa-innahū li-ḥubbi al-khayri la-shadīdun wa-alqaytu ʿalayka mahabbatan minnī wa-li-tuṣnaʿa ʿalā ʿaynī Allāhumma yā man khalaqa fī al-samā'i al-rābiʿati malakan niṣfuhū min thaljin wa-niṣfuhū min nārin fa-lā al-nāru tudhību al-thalja bi-lithqhi al-nāri wa-huwa yunādī bi-lisāni al-iqtidāri subbūḥun quddūsun rabbi al-malā'ikati wa-al-rūḥi Allāhumma yā man allafa bayna al-thalji wa-al-nāri allif bayna ʿabdika fulāni ibni fulānata wa-fulāni ibni fulānata innaka ʿalā mā tashā'u qadīrun

"O Allah, I ask You, O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O He Whom eyes cannot see and doubts do not mix with, and whom describers cannot describe, O He to Whom the command between kāf and nūn was given—surely His command, when He intends a thing, is only that He says to it 'Be,' and it is. I ask You to cast love and affection between So-and-so son of So-and-such and So-and-so son of So-and-such by the right of these verses: '*They love them as the love of Allah; and those who believe are most intense in love for Allah*.' O He Who created in the fourth heaven an angel whose half is of snow and half is of fire, so neither does fire melt the snow with all the fire's heat, nor does snow extinguish the fire, and that angel calls out with the tongue of power: 'Holy, Holy, Lord of the Angels and the Spirit.' O Allah, O He Who created love between snow and fire, create love between Your servant So-and-so son of So-and-such and So-and-so son of So-and-such. Surely You have power over whatsoever You will."
