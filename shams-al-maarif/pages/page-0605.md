# Page 605 (English translation)
603

The Qaf and the Action: Indeed all is present, and the person who recites this, the Exalted Lord will grant him power upon power.
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I ask You, O You who placed the kiss upon the necks of His servants so that they are fearful of His sovereignty, O You who alone possesses glory, permanence, greatness, and majesty, for all His creation trembles from fear of Him and submits under His command. O You whose allies on the Day of the Great Terror are secure—I ask You, O Generous One, by the power with which You looked upon the sky so it rose, and upon the earth so it spread, and upon the mountains so they stood firm, and You looked upon the springs so they burst forth, and upon the rivers so they flowed, and upon the hearts so they humbled themselves and trembled, and upon the mute tongues so they spoke. They said: "You are Allah, the One, the Conqueror." That You clothe me in light by which I am self-sufficient for revelation, and that You subjugate for me the servant of the letter Qaf, the angel Samsama'il, and by the great heavenly Name, strike him among Your creation with Your commanding word. Cover me with Your generosity, and the tongue of my hair is beloved—increased is the love of it, and gone is the hastening gold in my desire—comfort for the best. I ask You, so let it not be incumbent upon me. He is the support of all things, and O its Conqueror. YaH. And from the secret of these words: that You give me dominion, and Hiya, and with the unseen—"there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Exalted, the Great."
The 'Azimat for it is this:
Respond, O Tai'il, by the truth of Sa' Sa'ma' Alti' Sa' Libahi Yimawah YaH. Peace, a saying from the Merciful Lord.
Respond and do so.
[Handwritten signature: Muhammad Hanif Sabiri]
The Letter Qaf
When these letters are written with it below, like the image, on a piece of silk fabric, and placed beneath the gemstone of a ring—whether red agate or ruby or the like—and worn, acceptance will be obtained.
The enemy will be defeated:
For the defeat of enemies and the silencing of their tongues, write on a piece of paper 100 times the name of the enemy, and hang it in the air. When you intend its privacy, make a vow and break the fast—recite after every prayer 100 times, and write the form of this letter and hold it in your hand on your head. The servant will present himself and perform the service. The seeker will see him illuminated like the sun in the privacy. It is obligatory for the seeker to sit in the privacy facing the qibla. The talisman of this letter is this:
