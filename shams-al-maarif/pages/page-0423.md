# Page 423 (English translation)
421

O Allah, there is no god but You, the Most Supreme, the Most Holy, the First without beginning and the Last without end, and You are the Revealer of the needs of whoever holds fast to You in misery and hardship, and the Responder to whoever calls You from the depths of their heart and who commands it in the dark night. And You are the Standing, the Powerful over fulfilling the needs of those who come to You, who turn to You in hardship and ease. I ask You by Your supreme light and Your most noble majesty and Your support for the people of encompassing knowledge and direct vision, and by the voice of the greatest and mightiest bell, which is Your trustee in the station of glory. Remove from my heart the effects of Satan's misdeeds, and replace for me my spirit and heart with the throne of Bilqis, which is the secret of the vile nature, and replace me with Your complete light and Your universal grace—no one is delivered from among creation except by being drawn to You, from the effect of the desire of illumination, and from the darkness of his glorious garment. O He to whom belongs greatness, pride, majesty, and splendor! I ask You by Your invincible might and the trace of Your wonderful knowledge, for protection that shines from the pavilions of Your generosity, and the preservation of the corners from the protection of Your fortress, and comprehensive care from the sanctuary of Your sacred space, and the revelation of Your mysteries, and descending mercy from the world of Your holiness and Your firm decrees, that You suffice me against all besides You, and have mercy upon me with mercy that gives me life, and through which You manifest the apparitions, and deliver it every morning with the best of righteousness and success, and descend with the subtleties of Your gentleness and the gifts of Your grace upon my face, removing the darkness of a veil that You will not see me when a verse descends—and by all the verses in the heavens in the core of Your manifestation, and whoever has been purified in private audience. Make me, by the abundance of Your grace and the spirit of Your action, one who looks toward You, and by Your grace is capable, and upon the path of Your face is victorious and a helper. O He to whom belongs glory, splendor, praise, and generosity! O Lord of the worlds. ◌

This person should recite this prayer at midnight; then their supplication is immediately accepted, and all their needs are fulfilled.

The subjugation of the officer of the Name "Merciful" and its secrets:
The Name Merciful is a great name; there are great secrets in it. The officer of its numbers is Homayriel, and he is chief over four officers who rule over 658 kingdoms, and in every rank there are 658 angels. Angels descend upon the reciter of this name, and the hearts of all creation incline toward this reciter. He is related to Michael; he is the king over the emission of mercy and is swift to answer. The officer descends upon the reciter. It should be understood that the spirits are from the realms of Michael; their worth is among the matters of the afterlife. This world is ephemeral and insignificant compared to the afterlife. If worldly benefits are obtained from these names, it is as if one has settled for the satisfaction of a single atom. The saints, the righteous, and the perfected have mastery over these divine names; they take help from whomever they wish, for these names should not be disclosed to unworthy people, and only God is the giver. The supplication of the Name Merciful is this:

O Merciful One, You are the Merciful of the brothers, and You are the Sovereign over every day that is in a matter, and You are the One who pours forth Your gifts upon the people of this world and the next, and You are the One who makes things easy with Your honorable disposition for whoever has qualified to come to You in spirit and in public. And You are the Most Generous, the Most Compassionate, the Judge, possessor of strength and favor, possessor of overwhelming strength and victorious power, by Your clear, decisive secret in land and sea, and by Your radiant truth in the secrets of the hidden and the manifest, and by what You have deposited of gentlenesses—indeed, victory and triumph are fulfilled for me, wherever I rise with it, by Your help, from Your most secure majesty.
