Chapter 37: The Work of Simiya and Its Stages

This knowledge was narrated by His Excellency Asaf ibn Barhiya from His Excellency Solomon, peace be upon him, and the unique treatment of this art has passed examination.

Know that the requirements of this knowledge are according to reason, and it has been transmitted from the great perfect masters such as Khwārazmī, and Sayyid Bahāʾullāh, and Asaf ibn Barhiya, and His Excellency Solomon, peace be upon him, and al-ʿIlāj has compiled it in eleven treatises.

Know that the essence of this knowledge is spiritual light. Now if you intend to do this, catch a black cat in which there is not even one white hair, and fast for three days, and each day turn towards the qiblah and recite the Prayer of Requirements twenty-one times, and take a double-edged knife in your hand and have this cat slaughtered in a large vessel. Another way is to cut open its belly, but do this work with such caution that not a single drop of its blood goes out, otherwise the operation will be ruined. The vessel should be earthen, and while slaughtering, keep reciting the aforementioned prayer. After that, obtain a hijāzī or nawwāl or matʿar (type of wood). Remove the legs with blood, meat, and skin, and also the aforementioned bones, seal them in a pot, and make a ghul ḥikmat (ritual ash). Place it on the fire, using maghāq wood, and kindle such a fire that all the meat, skin, and bones burn to ash. At that time, sit above this vessel and receive the smoke that emerges from inside this pot, so that whoever this smoke touches becomes blind, and there will be no treatment for him afterwards. When this smoke ceases, then grind all of it finely and keep it in a small vessel close to you, because the essence of this knowledge is this ash. When you sprinkle this ash before you and recite the aforementioned prayer, whatever you indicate will become manifest. When you become the owner of this ash, you will come from disbelief and darkness into light.

First Treatise

Cap of Deer's Membrane:
Make a cap of deer's membrane and write this talisman upon it, and wear it on your head, and recite this Prayer of Requirements:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismika al-qadīmi yā dā'imu yā abadu yā wāḥidu yā aḥadu yā fardu yā ṣamadu yā man lam yalid wa lam yūlad wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥadun yā rabba al-arbābi yā ʿazīzu yā wahhābu yā khabīṭu yā fīhālu yawma al-ṣṣāfi as'aluka an tuskhiṭira lī wāhidan bna khuddāmi ismaka yakhidhumunī fīmā urīdu innaka ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun

For this prayer, much spiritual discipline is required. When its effect becomes apparent, make ash and cast your ash before you and recite this prayer until the hidden shadow disappears.
