# Page 702 (English translation)
702

And He gave him the power to establish himself upon His oneness. Then He created the soul and commanded it also to oneness. Then He created the heaven and earth and commanded them also to oneness. These are the strange and wondrous matters of oneness, until they became bewildered and lost in the river of wonder, until He gave them a light from His light of power. Then they affirmed oneness, and in the same way, the spirit and bodies and much else—the heavens, earth, mountains, rivers, air—all things are established in their respective places and stations.

**From the marvels of this power, He created the power of darkness and light, hearing, seeing, speaking, etc.:**

For this reason, night is dark and day is bright, Paradise is radiant and Hell is blazing, ears hear and eyes see and tongues speak and senses move. This is due to traversing the path of His blessings and being established in accordance with His commands.

**By multiplying this [Name] and washing and drinking it on an empty stomach, the doors of power open for him:**

From the solitude of this Name, power is generated in all the senses and limbs of the seeker. Whoever is weak should write it by the method of multiplication and drink it on an empty stomach for twelve days. The doors of power will be opened for him. Then, solely in solitude, after each prayer, he should mention it according to the prescribed number. The assigned angel will descend upon him abundantly:

Muqawwi kulli dha'ifin haqqa fulānin

Under him are four assigned angels, and his name is Suway'il. He comes to the seeker in a dream or while awake, fulfills his needs, and eases illness. His practice is written:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Strong, the Severe, the Firm, the Self-Subsisting Who maintains all things by Your power, and Your rank is the novelty of the penetration of power to manifest created things. I ask You by the intensity of Your power over the creation of existents and the formation of newly-made things with the detailed penetration from the lowest of the low to the highest of the high. I ask You to strengthen my heart's power to address the spiritual spirits, and my intellect's power to compose created things and form them. And strengthen my heart with Your love, and my limbs to obey You, and the sincerity of my secret in Your known matters. Make me among the people of Your honor, and support me against whoever intends me with evil and harm, and return his trickery upon him with abandonment and helplessness. O Allah, do not grant him respite, and hasten his punishment before he surprises me, and seize him before he seizes me, O Allah, O Strong, O Firm.

**Whoever recites this Name is protected from the evil of the envious oppressor:**

Whoever recites this dhikr, Allah the Exalted protects him from the schemes of the envious and the evil of the oppressors, and as long as any person of resolve practices its solitude, Allah the Exalted keeps him steadfast and grants him the power to converse with the assigned angel.
