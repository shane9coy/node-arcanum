# Page 549 (English translation)
Let it be known that the planets, the constellations, and the tablet (بصب لاد کلب - the plate of the heart of the throne and the throne of Solomon son of David, and the area of the Gate of Intellect) are for the purpose of the "pir of transmission." Banu Harith bin Hamrayan Shahanshah Hurdagan, and these are the servants of the Throne and the (those who cause flickering in the eyes) and the increasers of the intellect, the revealers, and the Throne (the expanders). And the knowledge of the stars is analogous to the discourse of Talisha, and this chapter is like the example of Kuthayyir. Know it, and it is 25 degrees above one and 50 degrees. In one name, if you apply its ending to qal in degrees and decrease by doubling, and the beginning should be with the number of the first name from the ridge, and with 60 it should be reduced to the last names, the name after the second name. When the maqtul (killed) is prior, its rule is this:

Chapter: Knowledge of the 70 arteries of the planet Mercury. This is from the discourse of Shihab. And the chapters of the Great and Small Mercury are above. Like the example of 180 and 33 above, and the gathering is 156 above, and their application and negation are according to the analogy of likeness.

Chapter: Knowledge of the Small Mercury from the discourse of Taila Tald, and this is a small chapter and section, and these are 404 degrees above in the tablet. The name according to the analogy is Noah son of Adam, peace be upon him. Applied and negated. Make both ends to twenty.

Chapter: Knowledge of the small that is called the Small Chapter, and it is the discourse of Tayyish, and these are the names of the angels of the seventh heaven. The letters of the Small Chapter are obtained according to the numbers of the degrees, and these are written in one name from ten to forty degrees in the manner that has been explained to you. Decrease in the same way. The name will equal 110 names. Do likewise wherever you wish.

Chapter: Knowledge of the names that are attributed to the Small Chapter. From the discourse of Tayyish, these are the names of the angels appointed over the sphere of the Moon. And the letters of the tablet that enter from the Small Chapter, and these are in forty degrees, one name suitable for the description of history.

Chapter: Knowledge of the love of King Misha, which is the royal love of the treatise of Abd al-Qadir. This is from the Small Chapter. According to those who mention it among similar chapters.

Chapter: Understanding the explanation of what has been written from the discourse of Tayyib with the Small Chapter, and these are the names of the angels of the fifth heaven. By the tablet, the letters from the Gate of Intellect, and these are external to this sphere. Applied and negated according to the analogy of the history of the name of the Small Chapter.

Chapter: Knowledge of the names of the angels appointed over the sphere of the Sun. In the chapter of decrease, from the letters of the maqtul entering, according to the analogy, this is called from ten leaves.

Chapter: Knowing the love of Azrael, peace be upon him, and these are the names of the angels of the fourth heaven. By the tablet from the letters of the Gate of the Maqtul in ten leaves.

Chapter: Knowing the love of Joshua son of Nun, peace be upon him, and this is the same as the royal love of the treatise. With the discourse of the middle, and these are the names of the angels of the third heaven. According to the tablet like the tablets of Mars and Venus, and these are four degrees. It returns to their names at the end to 20. Its analogy of history is called. From the Small Chapter, above the tablet.

Chapter: Knowing the tablet of Adam from the Small Chapter. Apply the heavy letters with the discourse of Raqaf. The name will be at 44 degrees, and it returns in ten names. Similarly should be done until the end of the chapter. This chapter will return from the leaves of the discourse of Asaf bin Barkhiya of joy.
