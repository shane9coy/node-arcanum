The Properties and Benefits of Some Divine Names

Whoever recites the Name al-Mū'min of God, may He be exalted, one thousand one hundred and twenty (1120) times every day, God, may He be exalted, will protect him from slander and plague. Whoever inscribes the Name al-Muhaymin on a ring during the ascendant of the Moon and wears it shall be protected from the satans of the jinn and mankind. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-'Azīz of God, may He be exalted, will be honored before God, may He be exalted, and among people. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Jabbār will be awe-inspiring among all people. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Mutakabbir, his command will be executed. Whoever engraves the Name al-Khāliq on a silver ring during the sign of the fiery zodiac and reinforces it [unclear: with brass rings], it will become记忆力 [a perfect memory] by the decree of God, may He be exalted. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Bārī of God, may He be exalted, God, may He be exalted, will make him acquainted with the mysteries of creation and subtle signs. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Musawwir of God, may He be exalted, God, may He be exalted, will cast awe among his enemies. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Ghaffār, his sins will be forgiven and erased. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Qahhār of God, may He be exalted, his carnal desires will be conquered. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Wahhāb, whatever he asks from God, may He be exalted, will be granted to him. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Razzāq, God, may He be exalted, will ease for him the means of sustenance and grant him sustenance in such a manner that he does not even imagine it.

Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Fattāh of God, may He be exalted, God, may He be exalted, will open for him the outward and inward means of good. Frequent remembrance of the Name al-'Alīm of God, may He be exalted, grants the faculty of eloquent speech along with wisdom. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Qābiḍ, every kind of obstruction and blockage is removed. Frequent remembrance of the Name al-Bāsiṭ of God, may He be exalted, expands the heart. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Khāfiḍ of God, may He be exalted, and supplicates against an oppressor, the oppressor is seized at that very moment. God, may He be exalted, elevates the status of one who performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Rāfi' and grants him a high rank. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Mu'izz of God, may He be exalted, God, may He be exalted, will grant him honor in this world and the Hereafter. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Mudhill, God, may He be exalted, will humiliate all the rebellious people for him. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-Samī', every one of his prayers will be accepted. Whoever writes the Name al-Baṣīr of God, may He be exalted, one hundred times in a glass vessel, erases it with rainwater and applies it to his face, God, may He be exalted, will strengthen his intellect and fortify his heart. Remembrance of the Name al-Hakam is beneficial for the propagation of knowledge. Whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-'Adl, justice will be his portion in all matters. Frequent remembrance of the Name al-Latīf removes every hardship and illness. Whoever inscribes the Name al-Khabīr on the bezel of a ring during the first hour of Friday and keeps it in his mouth, thirst will leave him. If he washes it in water and drinks it, every hidden matter will become known to him.

Frequent remembrance of the Name al-Halīm pacifies the impatient and the angry. God, may He be exalted, will protect whoever performs frequent remembrance of the Name al-'Azīm from every fear and terror. Frequent remembrance of the Name al-Ghafūr fulfills all needs. By the grace and generosity of God, may He be exalted.
