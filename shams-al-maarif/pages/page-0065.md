# Explanation of the Twelve Constellations, Their Connections and Indications

Know that there are twelve constellations and twenty-eight lunar mansions, which Allah the Most High has mentioned:
"We have indeed made constellations in the heaven and adorned it for the observers."
"We have made constellations in the heaven" - Blessed is He who made constellations in the heaven.
Then He says: "By the heaven possessing constellations." And it is stated: "And the moon, We have appointed it stations." Buruj is the singular form. Buruj also means fortress, and it is also called a fortress. The saying of Allah the Most High: "Even if you were in firmly-built castles" - "Even though you were in fortified castles, death would surely overtake you."

Hadrat Hasan al-Basri (may Allah be pleased with him) said: These constellations in the heaven were all created at once and will all pass away at once. Some scholars have said in their interpretation of the verse "Blessed is He who made constellations in the heaven" that the constellations are the positions of seven stars, and they are twelve in number. They are: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces. Aries and Scorpio are the houses of Mars, and Taurus is the house of Venus. Gemini and Virgo are the houses of Mercury, and Cancer is the house of the Moon. Leo is the house of the Sun, Pisces is the house of Jupiter, and Capricorn and Aquarius are the houses of Saturn. These twelve constellations are divided into groups of three each according to the four natures, each of which is called a triplicity (mithaliyya). Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius are the fiery triplicity. Taurus, Virgo, and Capricorn are the earthy triplicity. Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius are the airy triplicity. Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces are the watery triplicity. This is a chart in which the constellations, lunar mansions, and Roman months are shown in detail. This is its form:

Scholars have differed in their opinions regarding the meaning of constellations. Some say they are palaces in the heaven, and they cite the saying of Allah the Most High "even if you were in firmly-built castles" as their proof. Some say they are stars. Some say they are celestial spheres. Some hold the view that they are the gates of the heaven, which are called bukka. From Hadrat Ibn Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him) there is an explicit text that the constellations we are mentioning are twelve. Allah the Most High has divided them through subdivision and triplication, and there is also their division over seven stars, as we have explained. Their division has been made similar to that of the five stars. The first constellation is Aries, which has five stars; its forward part is towards the west, its rear part towards the east, and its tail is turned towards the south. The first segment is on (A) in the form of its back and is connected with the stars of the Pleiades. The second constellation is Taurus, which is one star - "your servant" - which is a dim star; I see such a sign appearing that is near its chin. It has become divided into two pieces; its rear is towards the east, its front towards the west, and its feet are also towards the south. These are its stars. In audibility
