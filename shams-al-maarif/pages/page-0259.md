# Page 259 (English translation)
By certainty filled with longing, and reveal to me every hidden secret, O Ever-Living, O Eternal, and grant me gently that which the spirits of the saints find rest in and the souls of the blessed become content with. To You belongs the most vast glory and the most complete dominion. O Allah, I ask You by every name that has preceded in Your knowledge, surely You do not withhold from the one who asks with it a seeker, nor do You reject one who asks with it disappointed. I ask You to fulfill my need in what I desire, and to accompany me with a good ending. Surely You know what I want, and to You belong the keys of matters, and You are over all things capable.

O Allah, I ask You and I implore You by the Name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, that there flow upon me from the garments of Your lights that which repels the eyes of enemies, humiliated, and their hands weakened, and that You make for me everything I attempt a joy—that which the spirits of the knowers find rest in, and the gazes of the seers are fixed upon it, and the secrets of the gnostics whisper with it. Surely You are the Knower of the unseen and its Teacher, the Revealer of secrets and its Explainer. To You belong praise and commendation, and in Your hand is goodness and victory. O Allah, bless Your prophets and Your messengers, and Your near angels, and Your righteous friends, and all the people of Your obedience, and convey to them our salutations and greetings, and grant us their intercession through our merits.

And O Allah, I have turned my hope toward Your generous face, and I have made my good assumption in Your great forgiveness, so have mercy upon me and upon my parents, and forgive me and the Muslims, and do not turn my hope from Your face disappointed, nor make my good assumption of Your forgiveness false. O Allah, how can I leave Your door disappointed when You have commanded us to supplicate to You, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, I ask You to have mercy upon me when my term concludes, my deeds cease, I don my shroud, and I depart from my dwelling. O Lord of lords, O Cause of causes, O Separator of necks, O Revealer of punishment. Affliction has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.

In the Name of Allah, the Healer. In the Name of Allah, the Sufficient. In the Name of Allah, the Grantor of Well-being. Alif Lam Mim Sad. Kaf Ha Ya Ain Sad. Hha Mim Ain Sin Qaf. Ta Sin Mim. Ha Mim Qaf Nun. And Allah is the best Guardian, and He is the Most Merciful of the merciful. There is no deity except Allah.

In the Name of Allah, the Beginning, who is Most Merciful, Most Compassionate. Power and strength belong to Allah, who is Exalted and Great. O Allah, send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, as You sent blessings and peace upon Abraham and his family. You are Praiseworthy and Glorious. There is no deity but You, and I am among the wrongdoers. Allah is my Allah and He is sufficient, and He is the best Guardian. Allah is sufficient for me; there is no deity but Him; in Him I have placed my trust, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne. Seven times.

In the Name of Allah, by whose name nothing in the heavens or the earth is harmed, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Three times. O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Creator, O Returner, O Maker—whom He wills, I ask by the light of Your essence, He who filled the pillars of Your throne. And by Your power which You have placed over all Your creation, and by Muhammad, who is given precedence over everything—there is no deity but You, O Relief of the seekers. Grant me victory. Three times.

In the Name of Allah. O Great One. O Ever-Living. O Eternal. O Compassionate. O Generous. O Merciful. O Compassionate. O Beautiful. O Gentle. O Generous. O Doer of good. In short, I ask by all of them that You make this one and gather all good for it, and pour Your grace upon it, and a luminous halo, and the divine Name, so that I may with readiness of these directed emanations of Yours subjugate souls and spirits. For it, for them—
