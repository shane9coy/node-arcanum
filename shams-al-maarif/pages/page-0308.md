Chapter 81: Properties of the Name "Muntaqim" (The Avenger) - 'The Destruction of the Tyrant'

Whoever invokes this name frequently and makes an imprecation against any tyrant, that tyrant will be immediately destroyed. This is among the lunar names that belong to the Malak (Angel) Izra'il, upon him be peace. Its number is 630, and these are even-odd-future-excess. Its extended numbers 1252 point toward the saying "Huwa Qawi Tahir" (He is the Strong, the Pure). The names of its letters are 858, pointing toward two great Names: Dhu al-Tuwal and Badi'. Its square is the division of al-Falaq (Morning), the value of which the People of the House and Glory know. This is its form:-

Chapter 82: Properties of the Name "Afuw" (The Forgiving) - 'Excellent Moral Characteristics Beneficial in This World and the Hereafter'

Whoever invokes this name frequently, Allah the Most High grants him noble moral characteristics, and He does not hold the one who invokes this name accountable for his sins. If anyone commits a sin and fears punishment from the ruler and others, let him invoke this name according to its number, then Allah the Most High grants him safety from it. Its invocation is appropriate for one whose name is Yusuf. Know that the names Ghafur, Ghaffar, and Afuw are related names suitable for removing distress and pain, and are especially appropriate for matters of this world and the hereafter. Pure is His Sacred Essence, Who placed mysteries in the names. The author of al-Muntakhab says that invocation of this name is not shameful, and one never feels fear or anxiety from it. He is preserved from the vicissitudes of time. Its number is 156 and 156; the literal number is an odd alms-offering. Its extended numbers 130 + 121 point toward the name 'Asim and Fadil. Its number is indeed even-even-odd-excess. Its extended numbers 235 point toward the saying "Kun Liyakun" (Be, and it is). The names of its letters are 225, pointing toward two great Names of Allah al-Wahid (The One). Its square is magnificent, the value of which belongs to the People of Taste, and only the Masters of Disposition know it. This is the form of its sigil:-
