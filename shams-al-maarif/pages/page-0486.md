# Page 486 (English translation)
Letter Lam-Alif (لا):

The letter "La" is airy, though it also contains some earthiness.

Properties and Benefits: If it is written 71 times on a copper plate and hung around the neck of an animal, it will remain protected from the evil eye and from all ailments.

If there is a danger of something being lost, write this letter along with this verse inside it: "Wa-lā yawadduhu hifẓuhumā wa-huwa al-ʿAliyyu al-ʿAẓīm" [the Throne Verse, Qur'an 2:255]. It will remain protected. This may not be written in any form other than the Arabic shape. Its prayer was mentioned under the letter Alif; read that one.

Letter Yā' (یا):

The workings of the letter "Yā'" are similar to those of the letter "La"—follow the same analogy. It has no special prayer. This letter is used only for calling, such as "Ya Allah," "Ya Raḥman," "Ya Raḥīm." This section is complete. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds!
