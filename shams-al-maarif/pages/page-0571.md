Types of Kohl (Antimony):

Kohl is of two types: that which is beneficial for health, applied with the tongue, and habb al-mustanfi.

Al-barak is the common type: Yemeni, Bahri (sea), and Nile blue. When it is crushed, it separates into layers. The best type is the Yemeni, then after that the white Bahri which has red lines appearing in it.

Glass has several varieties and is made from sand and alkali. Among all of them, the Syrian is the best, which rivals crystal in clarity, and there is a yellow hard type that has glittering particles. Color grinders use it, and some pieces are small and rival zinc in quality, and this is the best.

Night of Yemen has several varieties of pike (antimony ore) as well. White Yemeni, linear, and Tabarzadi, and Syrian white. It is mixed with earth and some greenness is also found in it, and the Egyptian is yellow. The soft, useful white is suitable.

Types and Properties of Lapis Lazuli (Kis):

Lajvard is white lapis lazuli, and al-qandast is green lapis lazuli. Al-qantar is yellow lapis lazuli.

Suri is red lapis lazuli, and it is for painters. The finest Suri is useful for red color, and they extract it from Cypriot mines. Its origin is pike (antimony ore) and lapis lazuli, which flowing water carries to pools. Then it solidifies from the heat of the sun.

The philosophers manufacture it when needed, and its preparation is as follows: Dissolve and clean white excellent pike, and also clean the lapis lazuli. Then mix copper vessels with it so that it becomes a component. Then clean it and pour it into a copper flask and place it on the fire, and add one-twentieth of its weight of sal ammoniac. Then remove it and let it cool until it solidifies.

An even finer preparation than this is: Dissolve yellow lapis lazuli in water and add orpiment equal to the glass, and leave it for several days. Then clean it and purify it. Another preparation is: Dissolve the glass and clean it, mix saffron into it and cook it, then the red foam will disperse, and sometimes grape stalks also serve in its place.

The preparation of al-qantar is: Dissolve the glass in water and clean it, add a quarter of its weight of diluted bile water to it, and it will solidify.

The preparation of Suri is: Dissolve the glass in water, add orpiment, and heat it. Then cool it, and it will freeze and be useful for red color. This is even better than the natural (type).

Types of Borax:

Among Armenian borax, there is a blue borax and a manar (lighthouse) borax. They are white like common salt, which mostly appears at the roots of walls, and there is a Ravandi borax whose color is bright red. This type is the best.

Making Borax from Sal Ammoniac:

One type of sal ammoniac is borax, and its preparation is as follows: White salt of alkali, one part; white borax, three parts. Dissolve both of these and mix them together, and upon it sprinkle the hand of Ibn al-Far two times, then from your lips twelve times, cook it and solidify it, make pills from it, dry it in the sun, and use it.
