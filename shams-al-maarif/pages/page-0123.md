# Page 123 (English translation)
121

In the name of the Cursed one of the believers, O You who have bestowed upon the clouds of the sciences of contracts upon all of creation entire, O You who have no partner in His kingdom and no helper for Him. We acknowledge our inability to fulfill the right of Your worship; by [our] inability, O God, we seek help for what You have commanded from the lofty ones by Your rights in every time and hour, O Possessor of great success, O Possessor of vast grace, O You who gives life to bones when they are dust. Guide us to the straight path—the path of the people of the correct religion, the path of the people of steadfastness and right guidance, the path of those upon whom You have looked with the eye of Your care, they are the people of determination and the sound heart, the path of the people of sincerity and submission, the path of those who held fast to guidance and rejoiced therein, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed blessings from among the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. And send reinforcements of the angels of victory and empowerment, and grant us influence among the existents and the originated beings and the creation, not among those who are wrath upon them nor the astray. Amen. Do not make us astray nor misleaders, nor expelled from Your door, and resurrect us in the gathering of the God-fearing by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And may God bless our master Muḥammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.

In the name of God, the Beginning, who is most beneficent, most merciful. All praise belongs to God alone, who illuminates the eyes of the knowers of divine truth with the lights of knowledge and certainty, who attracts all investigators with the emotions of proximity and completion. He is the One who opens the threads of the hearts of the monotheists with the keys of divine unity and attracts them with the emotions of nearness and desire. He is the Essence who adorned creation in the best form, and the universe also made the beginning of the birth of faith from the lowest thereof. Then He made its lineage from despicable water—He is the Most Merciful, the Ever-Merciful, the All-Knowing, the Wise, the Eternal, the Ancient, the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. He inscribed the verses of divine unity upon the chests of the people of teaching with the pen of His power, and He elevated the chests of the people of guidance in the degrees of the secrets of knowledge. And sufficient for you are the Companions of the Cave and the Companions of the Rock. God, Exalted, spoke to Moses, peace be upon him, and honored His Noble Prophet, may the blessings of God be upon him and his family, with the virtue [of the verse] "And We have certainly given you the seven oft-repeated [verses] and the Great Qur'an." He is the Owner of the Day of Recompense. He is the one who destroys the tyrants and the rebellious, and annihilates the transgressing evil-doers. He is the one who cuts off the heads of the Pharaohs and demolishes the people of innovation and the atheists. God, Exalted, is the Lord of the Worlds, full of blessing. O Essence who adorned the universe with the garment of creation and sent wisdom to the station of honor. O Essence who spread the majesty of the contract of contracts upon all of creation. O Essence who has no partner in sovereignty and no helper. We worship You. We acknowledge that we cannot fulfill the right of Your worship, and we seek help from You for the establishment of those rights which You have endured and commanded in every age. O Essence of great success, O Essence of universal grace, O Essence who gives life to bones when they are crumbled to dust. Grant us guidance to the straight path, which is the path of the people of the correct religion and the path of the people of steadfastness and right guidance. Show us the path of those upon whom You looked with the eye of Your favor—they are the people of determination and the sound heart. Show us the path of the people of sincerity and submission, the path of those who held fast to guidance and rejoiced therein. Show us the path of those upon whom You bestowed blessings—the path of the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. Assist us with the angels of success and completion. Grant us influence among the existents. Do not show us the path of those upon whom wrath was incurred, nor the path of the astray. O God, do not make us astray nor leading others astray, and by Your favor and generosity, with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful, and may God send blessings upon our master Muḥammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace. Amen.
