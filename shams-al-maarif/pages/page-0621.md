They prostrate in the sun. Therefore the first Alif indicates the essence (dhāt) and the second Alif indicates the attribute (ṣifāt), and the "Hā'" points toward the inner aspect of the divine names.

**Investigation of the Degrees of the Word "Allah":**

Know that Alif is the intellect among creatures, because the intellect precedes everything other than itself. Similarly, Alif also precedes all letters. The first Lām is the spirit (rūḥ), because its rank is after the intellect. But after it comes the second Lām, which is speech (naṭq), because its rank is after the spirit, and due to the spirit it becomes the attribute of life (ḥayāt). Then within the Lām there is a relation to the heart (qalb), because it is derived from the soul (nafs). For this reason, the second Lām is derived from the first Lām. But after this comes the "Hā'" which is the fifth letter, and it is the essence (dhāt) from which interpretation is given with seclusion (khalwa), and this is the hidden (khafā'), meaning the aerial, from which absence (ghayba) is intended.

**The Prophet ﷺ explained this secret:**

He explained this secret in this manner:

*Khalaqa Allāhu al-khalqa fī ʿamā'in thumma ayyana habā'an*

This is the secret of the first Lām, and ʿamā' is the realm of traces (ʿālam al-dhar). Some of the knowers of God say that Lām is one secret. From the secret to the secret, others have said there is one secret between Alif and Lām. And some have said there is an entire secret between Alif and Lām, so he who says this "Hā'" will come to know the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden.

**Chapter:** And since the inner aspect (bāṭin) is the Greatest Name, it precedes all that is in divine unity, because Almighty God says: *Huwa Allāhu*—this purport has already passed, that the Hā' points toward the inner aspect of divine unity. Therefore, from this cause, the beginning of divine unity connects to its end, because His saying is: *Huwa al-awwalu wal-ākhiru wal-ẓāhiru wal-bāṭinu*—He is the First and the Last and the Manifest and the Hidden. The name "Huwa" is composed of two letters: Hā' and Wāw—this is the concealed secret.

**God has declared the inner aspect to be the locus of heat:**

Almighty God has appointed the inner aspect as the locus of heats, among which one is the heat of longing (shawq) and one is the heat of temperament (ṭabʿ). So upon these, Almighty God had mercy while maintaining those heats in equilibrium.

**Investigation of "Huwa" and its warm effect:**

Therefore, when the knower of God says *Huwa Huwa*, they gather within him in the heat of burning and longing, and going out with the breath, they go toward it; then the breath returns inside carrying the coolness of air, and *Huwa Huwa*, though in appearance it is verbal, in the inner aspect it is a warm attribute. Within God, the inner aspect is air and the manifest aspect is Alif. The bringing of Alif in divine unity is a secret, but from "Huwa" if both Wāws appear, it is produced from the conjunction of seclusion (khalwa). The breath finds its exit with heat. In *Huwa*, the Wāw is final, but in *Huwa Allāhu* the Hā' is between it and divine unity, and this divine unity is of His essence, and it precedes the glorification of beings. His glorification is accompanied by Jalla Jalaluhu because His saying is: *Wa huwa maʿakum aynamā kuntum*—And He is with you wherever you are. Endowment of life is also prior in meaning to the inner aspect, because His saying is: *Huwa al-awwalu wal-ākhiru wal-ẓāhiru wal-bāṭinu*—So He is the inner aspect of the manifest and the inner aspect of the inner aspect. Thus, the Hā' is the cause of the delicate life (laṭīfa). Therefore, it is with the breath of the delicate spirit (rūḥ laṭīf), and the Hā' is the complete heat—understand this. And God grants success!

**Seclusion (Khalwa)** And it has also become known to me here that ecstasy (saramastī) is the cause of benefit. This is joined with both of them, but it is prior. When I enter within your breath—exalted is He—your inner aspect is joined with it at that time, serving as an intermediary here.
