# Page 221 (English translation)
219

The Provider, the Giver of Sustenance, the Hearing, the Seeing, the Knowing, the Just, the Gentle. The Good, the Self-Sufficient, the Enricher, the Great, the Generous, the Bestower, the Sustainer, the Vast, the Grateful. The Possessor of Grace and Generosity, O Allah, I ask of You what is Your right and what is due to Your favor, O One of Ancient Favor, O You whose favor is upon every human being, O Lord of this world and the hereafter, O You of True Promise, there is no deity except You. Truly I am among the wrongdoers. O Allah, make sustenance easy for me in my situation, and O my Beloved, make [me beloved]. O Allah, by the virtue of Surah Al-Waqi'ah, by the virtue of Your Great Name, and by the virtue of the sacred status of our master Muhammad ﷺ, accept my prayer, and may blessings and peace be upon him, his pure and chaste family, and his companions.

And by the virtue of Fatah, the Sustainer, the Conqueror, the Abolisher of Idols, the Best of Providers, and Basit, the One who enriches the poor, the Accepter of repentance, the Seeing, who does not punish for sins, make lawful sustenance easy for me; gather between me and him through Your lawful [means]; and make Your lawful his portion for me. O Possessor of Glory and Honor, in this very hour, O Allah, O the Sufficient, O the Guardian, O the Advocate, make me self-sufficient through Your hidden grace, O Generous, O Merciful, O Allah, make the lawful sufficient for me, and protect me from the unlawful, make obedience sufficient for me through [avoiding] disobedience, and make Your grace sufficient for me besides all others. O Allah, O Lord of this world and the hereafter and their Merciful, O Lord of the Worlds. O servants of this noble Surah, take upon yourselves all these tasks that I have commanded you and that I have entrusted to you, by the virtue of Aviah Sharaih, Adiabi, Asaba'ut, Al Shadi.

O Allah, I ask of You that You send blessings upon our chief Muhammad ﷺ and upon his companions, and abundant peace.

[Heading in decorative box: Riḍāfat: Ya Hafiz, Ya Basit, Ya Wadud, Ya Mabin—Noble things will be obtained]

When you perform withdrawal and spiritual discipline with these names, then you [should be] in an empty house far from habitation, observing all conditions. The incense [should be] of udhiyah, ladan, frankincense, musk, sandal, and dhooni.

After reciting the aforementioned Surah fourteen times, if you desire haste, then for seven days recite these names according to their numerical values after each prayer. When the seven days are completed, fourteen mawakkils will come before you and greet you. You should not greet them, nor should you fear them. If you fear, then there is the fear of death, and all your effort will be wasted. They will sit before you, ask you of your needs, and promise to fulfill them. You may ask them for whatever you wish, but you must not ask them for anything at all, until when much time has passed they will depart. Strengthen your heart and fumigate with incense—this incense should be musk, 'id udhiyah, ladan, and termis. When you do this, keep your heart firm. They will gesture to you with their hands. They will still say the same things, but you must never respond, even if they continue speaking with you. When some time has passed, they will depart from you. After that, a person will come to you; a chair will be spread for him, and he will sit upon it. He will greet you, so respond to his greeting, and sit respectfully before him. He will ask you about your desire. You should be humble with him, for he is the one who commands nobility. Then he will ask: "O servant of Allah, what is your desire and need?" So say: "I ask from you assistance and a servant from among your servants, such that whatever I wish he fulfills." Then he will give you some wazifa (spiritual assignment). Take it from him. It will never be lost. Among the blessings of Allah, be grateful to Allah for this blessing. When the condition is that you conceal your secret, then you will achieve your purpose.
