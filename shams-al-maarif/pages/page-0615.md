I ask You to subjugate this point to me, O form جامع (comprehensive) of the honored Baduh, by my obedience, by means of the names that are within the Preserved Tablet, and by the right of the four lines, and by the letters of the alphabet within them, O spirits of the realm of spirit, by the right of the Basmalah, obey me, and the letters of the alphabet that are within it, O He who is the possessor of the majesty and greatness of the King of Kings."

Chapter: The Operation of Letters for Any Purpose
In the second method of the incantation of these letters, it is known that the composition of the effects and the knowledge of the letters are from it. So when you wish to perform an operation with these letters for the purposes of love, acceptance, obedience, binding of tongues, attraction, marriage, the majesty of sorcery, and the opening of signs, then sit in a secluded place for three days to be preserved from the appointed spirit. Write down the selection of the letter you wish to operate with. In the first week, a luminous form of a group of spirits will appear to you, and the spirits will become visible to you. At that time, speak these words:

"O servants of these names, reveal yourselves to me to the extent of my circumambulation; may God bless you."

Then if this light continues to increase daily and the sound of the glorification of the appointed spirits reaches you, know that within twenty-one days four appointed spirits will come to you and greet you, and each of them will have a noble Quran in his hand.

Taking the covenant of obedience from the appointed spirits:
Say to them: "O masters, I desire obedience through the divine names, and take the promise from you." They will accept your obedience with heart and soul and will say: "As long as you remain steadfast in divine obedience, we are obedient to you."

The operation for obtaining treasure:
Know that when you intend to extract any treasure, write the letters on four pomegranates and crush them in your cell, then place one pomegranate in each of the four corners of that place and depart. Behold, when you enter seclusion for any jinn and recite the letters bearing their lights for that jinn, the jinn will immediately be subjugated and present. When you need something, write the twenty-eight letters on twenty-eight pages along with their lights on one page and pierce it with a needle. When that jinn [unclear] is rendered, extract it and bring it forth, and it will eagerly and amicably attend to your work—until you have performed this with all the letters.
