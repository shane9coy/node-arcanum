[Decorative Box: The practice of remaining hidden from oppressors]

The verse: "They ask you about the mountains. Say: My Lord will dismantle them, dismantling them completely, and leave them a level plain. You will not see in them any crookedness or unevenness" (Ta Ha 20:105). It is in every Surah Al-An'am that it is blown away by the wind and a person becomes hidden from the sight of oppressors. The saying of Allah the Exalted is: "Vision does not comprehend Him, but He comprehends all vision, and He is the Subtle, the Aware" (Al-An'am 6:103). Eyes cannot see Him, yet He sees all, and He is the Subtle, the Aware. Write Surah Ash-Shu'ara (Poets) and tie it around the neck of a white hen—the treasure will become known.

Dispersing enemies
Surah Al-Munafiqun contains the effect of dispersing enemies. Surah Al-Fath contains the effect of gaining success, causing water to flow, and producing blessing in fruits. The weak and humiliated person who recites it every day will obtain honor and strength; the defeated will gain victory; the distressed will experience such expansion that they will not even imagine it.

[Decorative Box: The practice of attaining victory and dominance in war]

Whoever writes it on a clean membrane with saffron, rose water, and musk, and ties it on his right arm, will obtain honor and strength and remain victorious over enemies. For this reason, writing Surah Ar-Rahman on it is preferable. If he places it precisely on his body, he will remain victorious over defeat. Whoever writes it in a wooden bowl and washes it, then washes his face with that water, will obtain honor, dignity, and awe among people. Sa'ud says: I have come to know that whoever recites Surah Al-Fath on the first night of Ramadan within the supererogatory prayer, Allah the Exalted will protect him from every affliction. Ibn Qutaybah says: A person from among the people of Makkah narrated to me that once he faced distress, so I mentioned it to an elder. He said: Write on a slip of paper:

"Indeed We have given you a clear victory, that Allah may forgive you your former and your later sins, and complete His favor upon you, and guide you to a straight path, and that Allah may help you with powerful help. He is the One who sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers, that they may increase in faith along with their present faith. And to Allah belongs the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is Knowing and Wise. That He may admit the believing men and the believing women to gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever, and that He may expiate from them their misdeeds—and that is in the sight of Allah a great achievement. And that He may punish the hypocrite men and hypocrite women, and the polytheist men and polytheist women, who think evil thoughts about Allah. Upon them is a round of evil, and Allah is angry with them and has cursed them and prepared for them hell—and what an evil destination. To Allah belongs the soldiers of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is Exalted in Might and Wise. Indeed, We have sent you as a witness and a bringer of good tidings and a warner, that you may believe in Allah and His Messenger and honor him and revere him and glorify Him morning and afternoon. Indeed, those who pledge allegiance to you, [O Muhammad], they are actually pledging allegiance to Allah. The hand of Allah is over their hands. So whoever breaks his oath breaks only what is upon himself, and whoever fulfills what he has promised Allah—He will give him a great reward. Among the bedouin Arabs, there are those who consider what they spend as a fine and wait for calamities to befall you, but upon them be calamities. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing. Among the bedouin Arabs are those who believe in Allah and the Last Day and consider what they spend as means of nearness to Allah and the mercys of His Messenger. Indeed, it is a means of nearness for them. Allah will admit them into His mercy. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful. And the first forerunners [in faith] among the Muhajirun and the Ansar and those who followed them with righteousness—Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him, and He has prepared for them gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein forever. That is the great attainment. And among the bedouin Arabs around you are hypocrites, and among the people of Medina; they are stubborn in hypocrisy. You do not know them, but Allah knows them. And We will punish them twice, then they will be returned to a great punishment. And [there are] others who have acknowledged their sins; they mixed a righteous deed with another that was not good. It may be that Allah will accept their repentance. Indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful. Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them increase, and pray for them. Indeed, your prayers are a comfort for them. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing. Do they not know that Allah accepts the repentance of His servants and receives charities and that Allah is the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful? And say, 'Work, and Allah will see your work and His Messenger and the believers, and you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He will inform you of what you used to do.' And there are others deferred until the command of Allah—whether He punishes them or whether He accepts their repentance. And Allah is Knowing and Wise. And [there are] those who call upon a deity other than Allah that does not harm or benefit them, and they support one another in clear hostility to Allah. And those who hoard gold and silver and do not spend it in the way of Allah—give them tidings of a painful punishment. On the Day it is heated in the fire of hell and burned will be their foreheads, their flanks, and their backs. [It will be said]: 'This is what you hoarded for yourselves, so taste what you used to hoard.' Indeed, the number of months with Allah is twelve months in the register of Allah the day He created the heavens and the earth; thereof are four sacred. That is the correct religion, so do not wrong yourselves therein. And fight the polytheists collectively as they fight you collectively, and know that Allah is with the righteous. And [know] that if any should plead after the stipulated period—truce which you have reached, then [you must give] what you have taken, and desire not the disbelievers as friends. But if they break their oaths after their treaty and defame your religion, then fight the leaders of disbelief—their oaths are nothing—so they may cease. Will you not fight a people who broke their oaths and determined to expel the Messenger and attacked you first? Do you fear them? Allah has more right that you should fear Him, if you are believers. Fight them; Allah will punish them by your hands and will disgrace them and give you victory over them and heal the breasts of a believing people and remove the anger of their hearts. And Allah turns in mercy to whom He wills; Allah is Knowing and Wise. Or do you think that you would be left without Allah testing you with those who fought and strove in your cause and among your brothers and among yourselves, and that He would make you taste the crushing loss? But Allah has promised us the true victory and help. If we hold fast and fear Allah, our Lord will forgive us our sins and admit us into gardens beneath which rivers flow. But whoever disbelieves among you, he will have strayed from the sound path. And Allah has promised us Paradise. But whoever denies it, the fire of hell awaits him. A promise of Allah, and Allah does not fail His promise, but most of them do not know. And whoever desires a reward in the world—with Allah is the reward of the world and the Hereafter, and Allah is Hearing and Seeing. Indeed, those who disbelieve in the signs of Allah and kill the prophets without right and kill those who order justice among the people—give them tidings of a painful punishment. They are those whose deeds have become worthless in this world and the Hereafter, and they will have no helpers. Have you not seen those who were given a portion of the Scripture? They are invited to the Book of Allah to settle their disputes, then some of them turn away in aversion. Because they claimed that the Fire will not touch us except for a few numbered days. And what they used to fabricate has deceived them in their religion. But how [will it be] when Allah has prepared for them a punishment in the world and the Hour, and they will know that the truthful are those whose witnesses are truthful and that the liars are those who are declared liars? And whoever should desire occupation in it—occupation is made lawful for him, and whoever should desire against it occupation—then whoever desires occupation against a near ally, then indeed Allah is the releaser, the firm, and His decision. And when there comes to them some matter of safety or fear, they broadcast it. But had they referred it back to the Messenger or to those of authority among them, those among them who are competent would have examined it. And if not for the favor of Allah upon you and His mercy, you would have followed the devil, except for a few. So fight, [O Muhammad], against the disbelievers. Indeed, their oaths are nothing to them. Do they fear that they will be seized upon by Allah and His Messenger? But the ones who fear are those who say: 'We are with you,' the ones who have believed in Allah and His Messenger. So if they obey you, they are rightly guided. But if they turn away, it is only that Allah has sent the Punishment upon a sinning people. And the heavens are torn apart. And the earth is split open. And the mountains crumble into pieces. That they may know that the resurrection is indeed established, and that the Hour is coming, in which there is no doubt, but most of them do not believe. And your Lord is the Knowing, the Knowing. And when they come to you, they greet you with a greeting with which Allah does not greet you and say: 'This is nothing but invention.' Rather, they are a people who are ignorant. And if they had only referred it to the Messenger or to those of authority among them, then those who can correctly investigate it would have known it. And if not for the favor of Allah upon you and His mercy, a party of them would have certainly conspired to mislead you. But they did not mislead except themselves, and they did not harm you at all. And Allah has revealed to you the Book and wisdom and taught you what you did not know. And the favor of Allah upon you is great. There is no good in most of their secret conversations except for the one who orders charity or goodness or reconciliation between people. And whoever does this seeking the pleasure of Allah, We will give him a great reward. And whoever opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear to him and follows other than the way of the believers, We will give him what he has chosen and land him in hell—what an evil refuge. Indeed, Allah has forgiven you your former and your later sins and completed His favor upon you and guided you to the straight path. And Allah would certainly grant you overwhelming victory. It is He who sent down His servants with the religion of truth, that it may be manifest over all religion, even if those who associate partners with Him despise it. O you who have believed, shall I guide you to a transaction that saves you from a painful punishment? [It is that] you believe in Allah and His Messenger and strive in the cause of Allah with your wealth and your lives. That would be better for you, if you but knew. He would forgive you your sins and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow and pleasant dwellings in gardens of perpetual residence. That is the great attainment. And [also]He would give you other things you desire—relief from distress and an escape from poverty and the joy of hearing the good news of victory and opening and relief. And sufficing [Himself] against the commands of the people. The victory from Allah and an imminent conquest, and give good tidings to the believers. O you who have believed, be supporters of Allah, as when Jesus, the son of Mary, said to the disciples, 'Who are my supporters for the cause of Allah?' The disciples said, 'We are supporters of Allah.' A group of the Children of Israel believed and a group disbelieved. So We supported those who believed against their enemy, and they became the dominant party."

Tie it on your right arm. When he did so, Allah the Exalted granted him all blessings. Whoever recites it: "Indeed, I open, I trust, and I entrust my affair to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seeing of the servants"
