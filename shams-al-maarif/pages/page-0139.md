He has attained dominion over the heavens, and His envelopment is like the rolling up of paper with lines written for the preserved tablet. Whatever is external and manifestly evident, yet after it come His desires. The earth has been changed into the non-earth, as it has been changed in length and breadth. The spirits have sunk into these seas until each one was enveloped by the eye. The obtainers take the sparks. This is like the tree and the honored outcome, which is the elevated Tuba. From these, guidance is not obtained, though one strives, but it is for the bond of salvation and success. This is the collection of seclusion that appears and the embellishment of the names of creation. So understand this gentle Tuba. It is a secret in the saying of Allah the Exalted. The declaration of the Most Holy is:

"Say, 'I only advise you with one thing—that you stand for Allah, in pairs and alone, then reflect. There is no madness in your companion.'" (Saba':46)

And reflect upon contemplation with the letter of gentleness. Then reflect upon the secret of divine unity for the burning of human decrees. And the consciousness of divine unity becomes correct only after the mixture of discernment, and the secret of good tidings is from eternity, and benefit is eternal. Whoever has witnessed the secret of odd and even has become acquainted with the heavenly blessing, and for color and expression, felicity is in the heavenly places. Know that in the remaining rank, the quantity of the Day of Resurrection is fifty thousand years, and the Day of Allah which is in the third time—such as the Noble Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, explained its quantity in his saying, that it equals two units of the dawn prayer. This is for one upon whom the secret of divine unity has been opened. Whoever said "La ilaha illa Allah" (there is no deity but Allah)—for one who did not say this, the duration of the Resurrection is fifty thousand years. Whoever associated partners with Allah for the sake of seeking His unity has a duration of a thousand years. Whoever confessed divine unity over the billions of the worlds, his quantity equals two units of the dawn prayer. He will become acquainted with the eternal secret over the unseen.

"All praise belongs to Allah who has taken away grief from us. Indeed our Lord is forgiving and grateful."

Know that every world is established from every life, heavenly and earthly, and life has been placed in water. In it is the secret of creation, and in the components of water is the principle of difficulty. Water is a veil between two components. Thus through the secret of creation, life and governance were obtained. He is upon the secret of life in every apparent thing, and within is the secret of creation. Therefore the place of life is. With the secret of building is the secret of majesty, its interior is with the place of building. This is the secret of life, and its appearance is with the understanding of majesty. For the understanding of majesty, the secret of subjugation has become clear, and with the place of life, Cancer has become the rider. Thus the understanding of majesty entered between the Tuba of visions, and the result is subjection, and with the building, for the essence of intellect, the glass of life's light became illuminated. The ray of light became illuminated, thus from the image of the mineral came the building and the understanding of majesty came, because this is the secret of lordship, and from the station of lordship comes deityhood. For the manifestation of understanding, the theophany of realities occurred. With the witnessing of intermediaries and divine unity is the original wisdom. With this same consideration, the declaration of Allah the Exalted is:

"Say, 'Have you considered if Allah made upon you the day perpetual until the Day of Resurrection?'"

Thus if the understanding of creation has prevailed over the darkness of divine unity, then it is for non-disposal with wisdom, and if it has prevailed for the circuit of life, then it is for the interior of divine unity. Regarding this is the declaration of Allah the Exalted: "Say, 'Have you considered if Allah made upon you the night perpetual...'" (Al-Qasas)

"And by His mercy He made for you the night and the day, that you may rest therein, and that you may seek from His bounty, and that you may be grateful."
