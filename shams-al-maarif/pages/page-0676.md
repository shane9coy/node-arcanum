# Page 676 (English translation)
By Your wisdom, You have established reward and the heart's protection, expansion, and the revelation of hidden matters, and steadfastness upon the unveiling of concealed beauties and generous matters. Grant me a fine thread from the fine threads of Your blessings, so that every atom of the atoms of existence may speak to me with expansion. O You who expand! O Allah! I ask You to subjugate for me the servant of this Name, that he may be a helper in my affairs. O You who abase! O You who raise! O Most Loving!

The person who always recites this prayer, the means of expansion become easy for him and his difficulty ends.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Khafid Al-Rafi'" (The Abaser, The Exalter)

Al-Khafid is He who humbles the disbelievers with vengeance and honors the believers with felicity, raises the rank of the friends for nearness, and shows the enemies as low with remoteness.

[Heading underlined]
Lowering and raising degrees, humiliating the honorable, making the honorable humiliated:

Indeed, the lowering and raising of ranks is in the power of God alone. He is the One who raised the heavens and made the earth low. He spread out the earth and raised the heaven upon it, and He is the Creator and Owner of all. The person who purifies himself from passions, sensations, and the animal qualities and raises himself—upon him the mysteries of this Name will be unveiled. By practicing this Name, its reciter obtains awe, dignity, and general acceptance. Among its properties is that the person who, after practicing its spiritual discipline, recites it before a ruler or tyrant, God will humble that ruler. If one keeps the imprint of this Name during difficulty and goes to face the opponent, one will be victorious. And the person who recites it according to its numerical count after every prayer and asks the world from its guardian angel, it will immediately come and fulfill desires.

And the person who recites "Al-Rafi'" according to its numbers, his value and honor will be raised among creation, and the mysteries of "Exalted in Rank" will be unveiled to him. Its apparent quality is fitting for it, and in Al-Rafi' there are two letters of the Greatest Name, and its properties are very many. Among them is that when a person is in distress, let him write the imprint of Al-Rafi' and keep it with him and recite it.

[Heading underlined]
By the recitation of Rafi', ease is produced in sustenance:
