# Page 579 (English translation)
**The Ninth Discourse:**

Write these names on a blade with saffron (rahoo). Then take a piece of some person's chicken and apply a little saffron (raykah)—sprinkle this on the piece of chicken. Also give this blade to that person and tell them to slaughter it with the blade, and recite the incantation upon your tongue. It will appear in the form of a chicken, and while crowing it will fly up from that person's hand into the air. On one side of the blade you should write this:

11111996111h 9k

And on the other side you should write this: 88 89 2118h 11h 9c

And the incantation is: Ya shara shara shara pas asha'ish ajib wa 'ajjal bi-kaza wa kaza barak Allah fik wa 'alayk amin.

**The Eleventh Discourse:**

Write these names upon a copper glass and fill it with water, then recite the incantation. The water will appear as [coins] if approved. If so, cut [them] in the shape of paper and recite the incantation upon them. All the stamped [coin-shaped pieces] will appear, and for the art of inscription [unclear: the mentioned] on one side write this: 02 g 9 h and on the other side write this: **Hikma 5 m 89**; and upon the glass write this: 1111 h 111111 h 1113 9 h 9. And speak this incantation: Sahibiya nas is ajib wa 'ajjal rabb.

(Raykah) mentioned—when placed, everything we have described becomes manifest. Recite it and verify. There will be guidance. Let it be known that for practitioners of the occult sciences, a period of fasting and spiritual discipline is required. When you perform this according to the conditions of seclusion, you will achieve success and become effective. And God is the one who grants success.
