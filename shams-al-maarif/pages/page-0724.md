# Page 724 (English translation)
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Wali Al-Muta'al"

Allah, without partnership with any other, is the absolute ruler and operates within the world: The name Wali does not appear twice in the Holy Quran except in the sense of goodness. He is the owner of all things and their ruler by His will; He is the one who operates and disposes over them. Thus Allah and Allah the Exalted, without partnership with anyone, is the ruler and disposer of all things.

The name Muta'al appears in the Holy Quran in this verse: "Al-Kabir Al-Muta'al" (The Great, The Exalted). Its meaning is "the one of lofty rank," and among its meanings is a form of exaggeration indicating supreme excellence.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Barr"

"Barr" means truth/righteousness, and "Barr" refers to the one from whom all happiness and blessings originate. One who does good to mother, father, teacher, and relatives is the beloved of this name; and a servant is also called "Barr" according to the measure of his good deeds, especially when he does good to his parents or teachers.

A question and answer regarding a certain person with Moses: It is narrated that when Moses, peace be upon him, spoke with his Lord, he saw a person near the foot of the Throne, and seeing him there, he was amazed and asked: "O Lord, how did this servant become worthy of this rank?" The answer came: "This person never felt envy toward anyone and treated his parents with good conduct." This is the description of "Barr" for the servant.

The specific effects of the Name Barr upon servants: And the first of God's blessings upon a believing servant is that He made him one of those safe from trials and granted him the acceptance of inspiration. Then along with this, He granted him the acceptance of prayers, and when the intensity of passions and natural impurities befell him, He gave him the success to return to the endurance of faith.

Another of His blessings is the sending of messengers and books. Then He also granted acceptance to those messengers and books, and He inspired the servant to remain established in practice, and then He protected him from desires. So how numerous are His blessings and favors!

His blessings upon servants in the intermediate realm (Barzakh): And His blessings in the Barzakh are that the spirits of believers and martyrs will fly about within Paradise in the form of green birds, eating and drinking until the Day of Resurrection. Then He will soften the graves with great blessings and keep them firm upon the Straight Path so that they do not fall into Hell from that station, after they have taken faith with the immortals from the right side and obtained the Quran from ahead and practiced the Sunnah. Thus their goodness is that He will give them to drink from the basin of Kawsar a drink after which no one will be thirsty.

Then He will favor these servants and show them His vision; they will remain in Paradise forever. But this is the blessing that He has chosen the path of entrance into Paradise; then He will bestow this great favor and enter them into Paradise with grace.
