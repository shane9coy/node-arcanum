# Page 432 (English translation)
O Creator of ailments and diseases, You are the one who aids in the ordering of the realities of things by Your power, and You are the one who gathers between the forms of things and their secrets in Your land and sea. I ask You by the subtle intricacies of Your hidden grace and the fine details of Your perfect knowledge, that You illuminate my heart with light from You in the station of clarification, and that You grant me access to every hidden secret lodged in the hearts of the prophets and saints. Indeed, You are Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, the Most Bountiful, the Generous, O Creator.

The subjugation of the steward of the Name al-Musawwir—all needs are fulfilled

The Name al-Musawwir is a noble name. In this name there are properties of the forms of knowledge being inscribed upon the heart. From it emanates divine pride. The name of its steward is Arqal. He rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 336 ranks, and in each rank there are 336 stewards. The world of knowledge and the depiction of creation is their task. All of these are under the command of Gabriel. When the reciter recites this name according to its numerical value, its steward comes to him and fulfills his needs, producing the power of spiritual influence within imagination and removing the veil from the spirituality of the cell. However, all these matters are conditional upon adherence to austerities, consumption of lawful food, and the purity and cleanliness of inner thought. The traveler, while attaining these degrees, begins power from the world of spirits, and the chain of lights is established from the world above. The conditions of the rituals have not been stated in one place in the books; the practitioner should select and use them from various books. When a servant intends, Allah shows him the straight path. The supplication of the Name al-Musawwir is this:

O Allah, You are al-Musawwir who gathers the scattered things and brings together the dispersed ones, from which You bring forth forms wonderfully composed, managing all kinds of secrets of the earth and heavens. You have determined sustenance and originated essences and arranged attributes. I ask You by the right of the secret lodged in the hearts of Your prophets, and by the spirit of Your secret existing in the spirit of Your saints, and by the wondrous acts of Your grace in Your decrees, and by the subtle inspirations in Your creations, and by the marvels and rarities of Your wisdom in Your handiwork, that You make my form protected, adorned, and prepared for acquiring forms of knowledge corresponding to the forms of existence. And make me a bearer of the secret of the Quran, described with the lights of the secret of al-Furqan, and bring me forth with the divorce of the tongue, and adorn my inner being with the light of oneness and tawhid, and bestow upon me the garments of abstraction and isolation, until I become isolated with You in the station of rectification, O He in whose hand is the balance for the manifestation of justice and perfection, proof and demonstration, and authority for the flowing of the secret of arrival and connection, O Musawwir.

The subjugation of the steward of the Name al-Ghaffar—the decrease of anger and wrath

The Name al-Ghaffar is a magnificent name. In it there is powerful strength for the transformation of souls and cooling of anger. The name of its steward is Jirmiyyal.
