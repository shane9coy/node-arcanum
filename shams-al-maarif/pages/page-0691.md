# Page 691 (English translation)
Your secrets are what connect me to the vision of their realities, by the mystery of Your Essence, O God

The doors of sustenance remain open for the one who recites this name. Whoever is diligent in this remembrance, Allah the Most High will open for him the doors of sustenance and will ease his difficulties.

From non-existence to existence You preserved the power of existence of what is in every state among opposites; You guaranteed it in every state through the power of the divine expansive acts, and You sufficed it in the state of bondage through the beneficial cosmic compositions. O Allah, by Your sufficiency and by the pure work of the seven compositions, protect me from the evil of whoever harms me, or whoever intends me with harm, or whoever disputes with me with evil. O Allah, place me in the good fortune of Your sufficiency and in Your protection, and make me, by the good fortune of success, worthy of drawing near to You, dwelling in the sacred enclosures of Your holiness, O Lord of the worlds.

From this [prayer] one obtains expanded sustenance, protection from evils, and the rank of wilayah:

Whoever reads this supplication, Allah the Most High makes sustenance easy for him, protects him from all evils, and clothes him in the robe of wilayah.

[Table/Boxed Heading: Chapter: On the Virtues of the Name "Jalil"]

Jalil is He who has been from eternity in majesty and perfection. The one who remembers this name will be honored; it is beneficial for melancholic ailments:

Whoever maintains constant remembrance of this name will attain a great rank, and in both his private and public life he will be held in great honor and high status among all creation. Its assigned angel is Isma'il; whoever suffers from a severe melancholic illness, let him write this name and give it to drink, and benefit will occur. Its dhikr is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Majestic One, whose Essence is glorified beyond any resemblance to anything of the majesty of bodies, and whose greatness is sanctified beyond any representation by anything of the qualities of creatures. You are only described with the majesty of grandeur, sovereignty, and the power strengthened by life, knowledge, and divine omnipotence in earth and heaven. To You belongs the perfection that no perfection can match, and to You belongs the majesty that the angels of the shining curtains cannot rival. By the awe of Your great majesty and by Your noble majestic name, I ask You to clothe me in awe and majesty so that I may be glorified among creation, so that I may attain the beautiful delight and joy from the gatherings of the perfection of Your qualities. O Allah, adorn me with the light of Your awe and greatness until I may subdue my enemies, and guard my eyes with the garments of darkness, and deliver me from the evil of the enviers, and extract me from ignorance, and fulfill my need, for truly You are over all things powerful.
