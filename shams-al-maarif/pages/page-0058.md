# Page 58 (English translation)
**Manzil al-Aʿwā**

Its form is: [five circles in a horizontal row: ○ ○ ○ ○ ○] Its letter is mīm. Its temperament is dry, mixed, and inauspicious. When the Moon descends into it, by the command of God Almighty a spirituality descends that excites desire to frenzy. It inclines men toward the love of women; one desires to go to them. This time is suitable for beginning the study of sciences, but it is not suitable for alchemy, confronting enemies, litigation, or meeting with officials. In it, wearing new clothes and donning them is preferable. The child born in it—if a boy, he will be contemptible; if a girl, she will be fortunate. Its incense is frankincense and dhikr.

**Manzil Simāk**

Its form is: [one circle: ○] Its letter is nūn. It is a terrestrial sign. When the Moon descends into it, by the command of God Almighty a spirituality is produced that creates enmity and corruption. At this time, preparing poison and every work of corruption is preferable. One should not begin major operations in it, and buying and selling in it is also bad. The child born in it will be a liar, a deceiver, and one who comes to a bad end. Its incense is frankincense, dhikr, and a grain of harmal. God Almighty is the most knowing of all.

**Manzil Ghafar**

Its form is: [three circles in a horizontal row: ○ ○ ○] Its letter is ʿayn. When the Moon descends into it, by the command of God Almighty a spirituality of love, compassion, and comfort is produced. It benefits from kings. Medicines are effective. Antidotes to lethal poison are prepared that dispel their suffering. It is preferable for attaining mature and lasting results in alchemy. Talismans are constructed in it. The child born in it will be sincere, a deceiver, and fraudulent. Its incense is frankincense and dhikr, and nothing else.

**Manzil Zabānā**

Its form is: [two circles in a horizontal row: ○ ○] Its letter is ṣād. Its temperament is blessed and mixed. When the Moon descends into it, talismans are written for iron wounds and dog bites, for one whose body suffers such illness that treatment is unavoidable, and for those against whom enemies make futile efforts; talismanic figures are written for them. The child born in it will be fortunate and blessed in all his endeavors. Its incense is fenugreek—a certain herb—and nothing else.

**Manzil Iklīl**

Its form is: [three circles in triangular formation: ○ (above) ○ ○ (below)] Its letter is fāʾ. Its temperament is mixed with felicity and inauspiciousness. When the Moon descends into it, a spirituality descends in it that produces discord, hatred and enmity, and evil. Travel, marriage, purchasing slaves, and planting trees are not preferable, for its outcome will not be good. Seeking need, tin-plating, and wearing new clothes are not preferable in it. The child born in it will be unlucky and will become great. Its incense is black pepper, saffron, and aloeswood.

**Manzil Qalb**

Its form is: [a series of dots: ........] Its letter is lām. It is the divine felicity. When the Moon descends into it, a spirituality descends that announces all felicity. This time is preferable for: faithfulness and trustworthiness, breaking covenants and promises, treating love and affection, cutting trees, agriculture and farming, fighting and quarrelling, administering medicine to animals, attacking, and conquering fortresses. The child born in it—whether boy or girl—will be ill-omened and deceitful. Its incense is sand of the desert.
