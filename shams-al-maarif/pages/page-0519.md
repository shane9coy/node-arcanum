# Page 519 (English translation)
He has sent down. My Lord, by the command of the Lord of Majesty, soon among us, successors will appear who become kings. The total number of all of them is thirty-three. And indeed they will all perish, and the caliphate will remain sealed for twenty-four years. And when the time of their removal comes, there will be many contenders, and an army of nine thousand will come whose number will be like the grains of sand. And many people will flee from fear of death. So no fortress will save them and no refuge.

And how many dwellings there are in that place—We overturn our departure like speech.
And how many departed from a direction that blew with sorrow—
Though they were from those of the field.
And a measure of the future will come after this,
And defeat will return with the people of the north.
Oh my grief for Aleppo and my sorrow—
What deeds they will encounter!
And in its battles there is something wonderful
That will befall them with great misguidance.
So neither scarcity of provisions will unite them,
Nor will their destruction be prevented except by annihilation.
And a great star appears in the sky
With a tail like the high wind.

And how many places will be abandoned from this place—like an eternal journey in speech. And many great ones will go with the wind, though they were from those of the field. And the measure will come after this, and defeat will return with the people of the north. So grief is for Aleppo and sorrow is for what kind of war will occur among the people of Aleppo. And in its attacks there will be something wonderful from which a burden will descend upon the people of Aleppo. So in their community no burden will remain, and after the abundance of disagreement they will be in destruction. And in the sky a great star will appear whose tail will be like elevated wind. So all these are signs.

And those are truly signs of the Franks—
They will possess the coasts and the heights.
With an army they will fill Mecca—
As clouds rise over the mountains.
And their homes will be stained with the blood of people
Who came to them fleeing from battle.
And the White Sand [Ramla al-Bayda] will truly be conquered—
So destruction for the coasts and the sands!
And after Jerusalem, that is a great day
For which the angels weep with intense lamentation.
And the river of Canaan remains mighty,
And is not able to provide pure water.
So destruction for Aleppo and Homs,
And what they encounter from the injustice of the oppressor.
So destruction, then destruction, then destruction
For the people of Syria from the dominion of misguidance!

English dominance will occur, then they will become masters of the river shores and mountain peaks. An army will come to the city of Mecca in such numbers, like clouds ascending over the mountains, and all the walls will be stained with the blood of Muslims when they flee from battle and come inside it. The city of Ramla al-Bayda will also be conquered. There will be destruction for the coasts and sands. And the day after the conquest of Jerusalem will be very heavy; when they see it, the angels will weep and wail bitterly. The water of the Jordan will become so foul that clear water will not be available to anyone. Then grief is for Aleppo and Homs, and for the injustices that will descend upon them. So a hundred regrets—destruction for the people of Syria at the hands of the misguided king!
