# Page 749 (English translation)
Allah manifests one name each year:

Every year Allah the Most High manifests with one of His names. So according to this calculation, one cycle of the names is ninety years. Thus, according to the Hijri calendar, ten cycles of the names were completed in 990 AH. Now one thousand and ten years remain. In the year when the name of Love or the Seizing One manifests, much killing, plundering, and destruction will occur in the world; and in the year when the name of Life manifests, there will be an increase in life and birth; and in the year when the name of Victory or the Provider manifests, there will be abundance of good and blessings. We cannot explain more than this, and this is sufficient.

And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
