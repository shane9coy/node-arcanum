# Page 431 (English translation)
[431]

The color of the Name is white and the garment is yellow. When the reciter mentions this Name, the familiar comes to him and fulfills his need. The prayer of this Name is:

"O Allah, You are the Great, the Great, the One Whose knowledge encompasses [all things]. You have brought things into existence and invented their forms after the unfolding of the Names. You are the One Who gathers their realities in the apparent [world] of the earth and heaven. I ask You, O Allah, by the majesty of Your eternity, by the subtleties of generosity, and by the secrets of Your truth, through the two parts of Your Heart—You are the Great without limit, described with the choicest of characters, the Self-Subsisting through the eternal, primordial gift and the perfect distribution on the Day of Meeting. You are greater than every great [thing], and You have made the angels messengers for every prophet and warner, the Lord who is established upon the Throne, Who was over the water. I ask You by the Qaf of Your power and the Kha of Your address, which take shelter in the worlds of Your purity. And I ask You to make me free of everything except You, waiting for You and for what does not contain Your pleasure, and to spread my existence in the station of presence, and support me with majesty and light. Indeed, You are the Commander of all things, O Great."

The Subjugation of the Familiar of the Name of Creator

Allah the Exalted's Name al-Khāliq (the Creator) is a great Name, and it is eternal because Allah the Exalted has always been the Creator. The name of its familiar is Ḥatiyā Tāʾīl, which is under the influence of Michael. Under it are four officers, each of whom oversees 731 ranks, and in each rank there are 731 familiars. These familiars are tasked with providing sustenance with ease and forming the child in the mother's womb. The familiar comes to the reciter of this Name and fulfills his need. The reciter should not fear anything because its terrors are dead. The prayer of the Name of Creator is:

"O Allah, You are the Creator, the Maker, the Former, the All-Powerful in Your knowledge. You brought things into existence, and You are the Inventor of their forms before the unfolding of the Names. You are the One Who gathers their realities in the essences of the earth and the hidden [realm] of heaven. I ask You by the majesty of Your eternity, by the subtleties of Your generosity, and by the secrets of Your mercy, through the two parts of Your Heart, that You make me standing by You, turning to You, returning to You, ruling by You. And grant me the vision of the亲近 ones nearest to You, and bestow upon me patience to the station of servanthood, and raise me to the pavilions of the glory of Lordship. Indeed, You are Allah, the One, the worshipped, O Creator."

The Subjugation of the Familiar of the Name of Creator and Harming Enemies

The Name al-Bārīʾ (the Maker) is a great Name; its meaning is the Essence that creates creation anew, without prior pattern, and will resurrect them. In this Name, besides the apparent, there is a secret of the Essence. Whoever has been separated from worldly position or covenant will obtain it again through the remembrance of this Name. Its familiar is Allah Mayʾīl, which is under Azrael. Under it are four rulers, each of whom rules over 213 ranks, and in each rank there are 213 assigned familiars. All these beings are experts in colds and catarrh. The familiar comes to the reciter of this Name and fulfills his need. In this Name there are methods from the giant [class] for killing or sickening an enemy.
