# Page 385 (English translation)
385

O servants of these names, perform the task of placing love and affection in the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so, such that they shall not be separated from it day and night, and shall not oppose in any matter—by the favor of these names and their sanctity.

Fifth paper, written on Thursday: "تَوَكَّلُوْا يَا خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ بِحَقِّ الْفَلَكِ الْمُؤَكَّلِ عَلَيْكُمْ الطَّالِعِينَ أَمْرَةً عَصْحَلَعِيَالِ هَطِيْلِ أَهْطِيْلِ كَالِ اِيْجِهِ عِيْلِ حَلْحَلِ هِيْهِيْلِ گان"

"O servants of these names, you are assigned to this matter by the authority of the heavens over you; attend to it and, obeying its command, perform it."

Sixth paper, written on Friday: "يَا خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ بِجَلْبِ وَجَذْبِ قَلْبِ فُلَانِ إِلَى مَحَبَّةِ فُلَانِ وَأَلْقُوْا بَيْنَهُمُ الْأَلْفَةَ وَالْمَوَدَّةَ بِحَقِّ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ"

"O servants of these names, work to draw and attract the heart of so-and-so toward the love of so-and-so, and cast between them affection and friendship—by the right of these names."

Seventh paper, written on Saturday: "تَوَكَّلُوْا يَا خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ الْمُبَارَكَةِ وَحَرِّكُوْا رَوْحَانِيَّةَ الْمَحَبَّةِ وَالْأَلْفَةِ بَيْنَ فُلَانِ بْنِ فُلَانَةٍ وَفُلَانَةِ بِنْتِ فُلَانَةِ بِالْمَوَدَّةِ التَّامَةِ الدَّائِمَةِ بِمَحَبَّةِ فُلَانِ بْنِ فُلَانَةِ بِحَقِّ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ عَلَيْكُمْ وَطَاعَتِهَا لَدَيْكُمْ"

"O servants of these blessed names, take upon yourselves the task of moving the spirituality of love and affection between so-and-so son of so-and-so and so-and-so daughter of so-and-so, with perfect and permanent friendship, by the love of so-and-so son of so-and-so—by the right of these names over you and their obedience incumbent upon you."

May the desired outcome always remain present:
Whoever writes the following verse with a person's name and keeps it, they shall never be separated from it as long as this verse remains written with them. The verse is: "وَهُوَ الَّذِي أَنْشَأَكُمْ مِنْ نَفْسٍ وَاحِدَةٍ فَمُسْتَقَرٌّ وَمُسْتَوْدَعٌ قَدْ فَصَّلْنَا الْآيَاتِ لِقَوْمٍ يَفْقَهُونَ"

"And He it is Who produced you from a single soul, a resting-place and a repository—We have made clear the signs for a people who understand" (Qur'an 6:98).

This is its talismanic design.
