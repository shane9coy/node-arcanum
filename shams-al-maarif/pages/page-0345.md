# Page 345 (English translation)
Some have removed it, and there is none equal to Him. O Remover, You have seized the veins of the head and eyes. I adjure you by the Master Joseph, peace be upon him, son of Jacob, peace be upon him, and by his torn shirt, and by the Torah of Moses, peace be upon him, and the Gospel of Jesus, peace be upon him, the Psalms of David, peace be upon him, and by the Noble Quran and Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who is the lamp and light and the Lamp of the Lord, the God most worthy of worship. O Pain, flee from the bearer of this my book, by the right of La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun rasul Allah, and by ten thousand ten thousand La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-'Ali al-'Azim.

Among the properties of this amulet is that it is written for the affliction of children's crying and hung above them: Surah al-Fatiha four times, beginning and end, and the Ayat al-Kursi three times, and:

"Allah is dominant over His affair, but if they turn away, say: 'Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; in Him I have placed my trust, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.' O child, be silent, be silent by the honor of the Lord, the God most worthy of worship. The voices have become humble for the Most Gracious, so you do not hear except a whisper, whisper, whisper. Faces have been humbled for the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence. Lost is he who bears injustice. Many faces that day will be radiant, laughing, joyful. Do you then wonder at this discourse and laugh and not weep, while you are heedless? Prostrate to Allah and worship Him. O child, be silent by the right of the King, the God most worthy of worship, and the Lord, the Beloved. Allah encompasses them from behind. Rather it is a Noble Quran in a Preserved Tablet. I seek protection for the one upon whom this my writing is hung from the evil of what He created. I have made a fortress with La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun rasul Allah, and by the right of Bismi llahi l-Rahmani l-Rahimi. Say: 'I seek refuge in the Lord of the Dawn from the evil of what He created, and from the evil of the darkening night when it falls, and from the evil of those who blow upon knots, and from the evil of [unclear] when they envy, and I have made a fortification with the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence, Who does not die. O Allah, drive away harm and distress from the bearer of this my writing.' I have made a fortification with La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun rasul Allah, by the right of Bismi llahi l-Rahmani l-Rahimi, and by ten thousand La hawla wa la quwwata illa bi-llahi l-'Aliyyi l-'Azimi."

An effective practice for tongue-binding:

This verse is also very useful for tongue-binding. The arrangement is that one should recite this verse at the place of tongue-binding and press one's tongue against the palate.
