# Page 668 (English translation)
668

The transfer of this key is this:

[Table: A 4x3 grid with Arabic letters and numbers.
Row 1: ح | ن | ف | ال
Row 2: 79 | 32 | 7 | 72
Row 3: 23 | 82 | 19 | 6
Row 4: 400 | 5 | 44 | 81]

And its dhikr is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Opener upon the supports with whatever keys of the paths You wish. The One Unique in the secret of Your name, the Opener, the Victor, from severe dangers. The Judge between servants with the subtleties of wisdom in the upper world and the kingdoms. You rule with what You wish and choose in Your creation. I ask You by Your secret that flows through the sevenfold of the Malakut world, sent into the hidden mysteries of its secret until it reaches the Nahut, the one returning in its prostration in the space of the world of Jabrut. And that You open in my heart through witnessing these secrets and realize it with the realities of lights. And make me worthy of connection by the secret of the life of that dawn, with the majesty of the secrets of Your attributes. O Allah, support me with Your mighty, victorious help over every opponent and adversary. O Allah, subject for me Your servant Tamkhayil, servant of this name. Indeed, You are over all things powerful.
