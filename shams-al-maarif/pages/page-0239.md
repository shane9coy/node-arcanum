# Page 239 (English translation)
237

He is the One in whom one places trust. Pure is He whose glorification everything makes along with His praise. Your essence is pure; there is no deity except You. And with Your praise, O You whom all glorify, reach my call, for I am distressed. Then after this, recite one hundred (100) times seeking forgiveness. It should be recited from the Friday morning prayer until the Friday prayer. The trustworthy Gabriel taught this supplication to the Noble Prophet ﷺ:

Allahumma astur bil-'afiyati fi al-dunya wal-akhirah

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: Whoever recites the words "La ilaha illa Allah al-malk al-haqq al-mubin" one hundred (100) times daily, for him the doors of sustenance will open, his poverty will be removed, he will enter Paradise, he will be protected from the trial of the grave, the world will remain humble before him, and from each word he speaks, Allah the Exalted will create angels who will glorify Allah and pray for the forgiveness of his sins. Allah the Exalted is All-Hearing and All-Seeing, and He guides to the straight path.

The translation of the book Shams al-Ma'arif, Part One, is complete.
