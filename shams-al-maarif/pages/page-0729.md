# Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Afuw" (The Pardoning)

Afuw is He who pardons evils and overlooks sins, and this has been mentioned in the explanation of the names al-Rahman and al-Rahim; however, this one is more eloquent, because ghafar comes for the covering of sins, while afuw means to uproot from the root, and He uproots from the root. The servant's share in this name is that whoever wrongs him, he should pardon him and treat him kindly.

### Allah Does Not Punish the Sinner Immediately but Waits for Repentance:
Allah Most High is unconditionally the Most Beautiful (Muhsin), and He does not hasten in the punishment of the disobedient, until He accepts their repentance and pardons them through His grace and generosity. The magical square of this name is for protection from the evil of enemies. Its form is this:

# Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Ra'uf" (Most Merciful)

Ra'uf is one who possesses extreme mercy; its details have passed in the explanation of the name al-Rahim, and its workings and meanings have passed under the name al-Wadud.

### Through the Recitation of This Name, Love and Revelation Are Obtained:
Its property is that when it is composed with someone's name and written, great love will be obtained, and through the solitude (khalwa) of this name, revelation will be granted. The angel assigned to this name is Jeremiah (Armiyayil), who is among those subordinate to Michael, peace be upon him. The assigned angel will appear to him according to the worthiness of the reciter. Its form is this:

| Fa | Waw | Ra | Lam |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 199 | 32 | 79 | 7 |
| 33 | 202 | 4 | 78 |
| 5 | 77 | 34 | 201 |

And its dhikr (invocation) is this:

Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim. O Allah, You are al-Ra'uf al-Rahim, al-Mawjud al-Hay al-Qayyum, Lord of vast mercy, who multiplies good deeds and elevates ranks. I ask You for vast mercy. O Allah, O Rahman, O Rahim, I ask You to grant my purpose and do not disappoint my hope, and grant me enjoyment in witnessing Your essence, and adorn me with the beautiful attributes forever as long as my life endures. O Allah, deliver me from what I fear and dread, from everything apparent and hidden. O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Lord of the worlds.
