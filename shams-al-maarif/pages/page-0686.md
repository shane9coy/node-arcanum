In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the Greatest and the Most Great. Bodies terrestrial do not become great, nor do spirits celestial, for each of these has a spatial extent, numerical configurations, physical breadths, and limited natural bodies of composite nature. But Your greatness, O God of the worlds, O Lord of the first and the last, is a greatness of majesty, and splendor, and perfection, and sovereignty—the light of Your divine essence and the comprehensiveness of Your educative power and the sublimity of Your greatness is akin to the domination of absolute oneness. I ask You, O You who are thus, to make my heart attentive to Your greatness, that it may remain in awe before Your majesty. O God, You are the Forgiving, the Clement, the Grateful. Is not my essence among Your greatness, before which every stubborn tyrant submits, and by which the evil of every enemy is vanquished and their cunning repelled from me? O God, O Great One.

Jas was thus protected from the harm of enemies by reciting this supplication to his Lord. And when the seeker (salik) recites it in seclusion, God Almighty protects him from the harm of enemies.

[Heading] Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Ghafoor" (The Forgiving)

The meanings of Ghafoor have passed in the entry for Ghaffar. Whoever wishes to remove the anger of a king, this is the appropriate method. He should go to whichever king or ruler and recite this name, and blow upon olive oil 601 times, and write an amulet and the name of the celestial deputy (múakkil) upon a piece of paper, and stand before him—God will elevate his status in that person's sight. It is likewise beneficial for reconciling between two enemies. Its details have passed in the entry for the name Ghaffar.

[Heading] Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Shakoor" (The Grateful)

Shakoor and Shakir have the same meaning. Shakoor is the essence which gives great ranks and blessings for the smallest of deeds. Recite it with the numerical configuration of Mabsut. The celestial deputy will be present and a spiritual meeting will occur. Its name is Tahiyya'il. Write its talismanic form upon a gold or silver tablet for obtaining blessings and the perpetuation of favors, and keep it with you—sustenance will be expanded. Its talismanic form is as follows:
