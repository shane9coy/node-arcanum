In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the One who puts forward and puts back. Your decree has preceded in Your creation. You distribute mercy upon every existing being that You have brought to life, from the noble and the insignificant. And You have decreed wretchedness upon whoever You have kept distant from all good. I ask You by the flow of the pen of clarification and editing, and by the perfection of beautiful forming and decreeing, and by the encompassing of Your knowledge in [the act of] blackening, that You place me among those brought forward to You by good means of attainment and the fulfillment of needs. And do not place me among the causes of delay in [Your] providence and among the people of clay and depletion. O Allah, advance me and support me against whoever opposes me. And fulfill with weakness and abandonment whoever intends my harm. And fortify me with victory, O One who puts forward, O One who puts back, O Lord of the worlds.

The chest of the one who remembers this [dhikr] becomes illuminated, his name becomes bright, and he performs righteous deeds:
Whoever performs the discipline of this dhikr, Allah the Exalted opens his chest for the light of faith and illuminates all his affairs with wisdom, and grants him the success of righteous deeds.

[A heading within an ornamental border]
Chapter: The Merits of the Name "First and Last"

The First is He who is first in relation to any thing, and the Last is also He who is last in relation to any thing. These two names are contradictory to one another. It cannot be conceived that one and the same thing could be both first and last in relation to one and the same thing through attribution.

Allah is First in terms of existence and Last in terms of progression:
If you consider the order of existence and observe the chain of beings, you will know that He is the existing One by His very essence, and all existing things derive their existence from His existence. And when you observe the order of the spiritual travelers' wayfaring, you will find Him as the Last, because the final stage of the realm of permanence is that toward which the gnostics advance, and there is no knowledge comparable to His knowledge that is attained before it or will ever be like it. Thus He is Last by the attribution of wayfaring, and First by the attribution of existence. From Him is the primal origin, and to Him is the return of all things, and "First and Last" refers to Him alone. Rather, when you consider the existing beings, you will see Allah the Exalted before them, for their existence came into existence only from the existence of God.

Therefore He created all existing things, while no one created Him.
For this reason it has been said in theology that "the culmination from Himself" — meaning that when you consider the stations of the gnostics, you will find Him as the Last, for He has said:
Indeed, unto your Lord is the return.
