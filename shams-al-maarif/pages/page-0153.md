# Page 153 (English translation)
Return, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, to your place by the power of Him who says to a thing "Be" and it becomes, by the power of Him whose command is between al-kāf and al-nūn—indeed His command, when He wills a thing, is to say to it "Be" and it becomes.

Read until the final surah, then say:

Rely, O servants of these names, upon restoring So-and-so son of So-and-so, or restoring So-and-so daughter of So-and-so, by the right of what I have recited to you of His names, and God the Most High.

Chapter: The Operation for the Fulfillment of Every Need

Verse: And with our seal, the attributes of good are magnified—

At the end of "and with our seal, the attributes of good are magnified," there is this metrical seal. When this is written after the qasida, the word for fulfilling the need is written alongside it, then it will be beneficial. It is extremely useful for: opening the gates of prisons, releasing bonds, facilitating affairs, easing women's childbirth, sealing the tongues of enemies, freeing prisoners and captives, seeking sustenance, blessing the oppressor, and for the anger and wrath of rulers. Whoever for whom it is intended—if his name is written and it is placed in his house, there will be great blessing. If one wishes to make this seal destructive—that is, writing the first part and then the following one twenty-seven times—then it will cause all manner of grief and sorrow. Likewise, obsessive thoughts, headaches, and terrible dreams will appear. Blood will flow from the body. This is written for destroying business, preventing the marriage of women and men, and for sea and land travel. It is written on red paper. That which you desire is written alongside the name, and it is pressed under a heavy thing. If someone is bleeding, give it a yellow-colored fumigation, seal it in darkness, and bury it at the place where water flows toward the east. Before this, wrapping it in red silk—if the person for whom the operation was performed has blood flowing from several places, he will perish. These are operations for destroying enemies and people, and they should be performed in the hour of Mars.

Chapter: The Solution of Difficult Affairs and Innumerable Properties

Verse: For解开一颗珍珠之结并摧毁所有障碍—

Regarding "For解开一颗珍珠之结并摧毁所有障碍," the explicit letters of this sacred name are extracted and written on a tablet of the fist—that is, an iron tablet—on the fourteenth day, when the Moon is in the sign of Pisces, free from malefic aspects. And the Sun is ascending toward the sign of Cancer, and the Ascendant is in the house of Jupiter. It will accomplish the work of red sulfur and the great antidote. Whoever carries it with him, all his affairs will become easy. God the Most High will protect him, his heart will become strong, and he will exercise dominion in the world. Angels will obey him. When he walks upon the earth, it will bow beneath his feet, and the wonders of God will manifest. Distant things will seem to him like nearby ones. Spiritual beings will speak with him and give him news of hidden matters, and Lanka will bow and tremble—those things which confound the wise will be resolved. One of its special properties is that for whatever purpose it is written—whether for taking or giving, burning or using—the affair will be accomplished. This is sufficient; understand it well and be trustworthy with it. This is God's promise. If there is certainty of betrayal, even so that these secrets remain hidden—
