# Page 435 (English translation)
433

Strive to occupy yourself with dhikr (remembrance of God). The Most High God gives, and there is no deity besides Him. The prayer for the name Al-Fattah (The Opener) is:

"O Opener, You are the One who opens the locks of breasts with the keys of eternal providence, and You are the Rich, the Generous, and You are the noble Savior through Your blessings for whom You will—of those whose keys of good things and treasures are in Your hand, and You are the Facilitator of difficult matters, and in Your hand are the finest pearls of light, and You send forth the spirit of generosity to the innermost recesses of the hearts of those with sealed breasts, and by Your providence every locked matter is opened, and by Your command the righteousness of every supplicant and petitioner is revealed. I ask You, O Opener of all good and repel all harm, that You make me standing before You, receiving from You, holding from the pools of living knowledge and the eternal gifts, and with good expectation for the emergence of the existence of Your ongoing grace, constantly watching for the attainment of the perfection of Your bounty, continuously looking forward with awareness of perceiving the effects of Your glory. Open for my heart and my insight the doors of revelation and witnessing, and support me in receiving the light of Your face when You unfold the treasures of what is in Your mercy and forgiveness, O Ancient of benevolence, O Most Compassionate, O Most Munificent, O Lord of the worlds."

[Decorative box centered on the page]
The subjugation and acquisition of knowledge of the angelic deputy of the name Al-Alim
[End box]

The name Al-Alim (The All-Knowing) is a great name. Two letters from the letters of the name Al-Alim are included in it. This is an ancient name, because the Most High God has always been Knowing. The dhikr of this name is especially appropriate for whoever seeks knowledge of the Unseen. The Most High God does not inform anyone of His Unseen except a chosen messenger and no one else. The name of its deputy (angel) is Tagh'ayil. Under him are four officers, each of whom rules over 150 ranks, and in each rank there are 150 appointed deputies. The deputy comes to the one who practices the dhikr of this name and fulfills his need. This matter comes about through the dhikr-practitioner's own effort. The prayer of the name Al-Alim is:

"O God, You are the Knower of what is in the breasts of the worlds, and You are the Knower of what is in the innermost secrets of the veils, and You see what is hidden in the essences of the worlds, and You encompass what is in the movements of the thoughts of all creation. I ask You by the hidden things stored in Your mercy and by the radiance of the breezes of Your compassion and by the magnificence of the great blessing You have, that You make my knowledge encompassing of all things—their apparent and their hidden, their exalted and their glorious, their beginning and their end, their opener and their seal—so that I may be drowned in the expansion of the mysteries of Your Oneness and the indication of the finest points of Your bounty and the seeking of intercession with You for my certainty and my faith. I shall not place my hope in anyone other than You, O Knower of the hidden things and the secrets, and O Gatherer of scattered things in the hearts. Grant me existence, and opening, and revelation, and oversight upon the name of what is in thoughts and prohibitions, for You encompass all that exists by knowledge and generosity, and You are the Judge over secrets by expansion and witnessing, O Lord of the worlds. And may God bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace."
