# Page 358 (English translation)
It is the comprehensive sacred formula for the acceptance of the places where knowledge and influence flow forth. His throne encompasses the heavens and the earth. The preservation of both does not weary Him, and He is exalted and supreme. O Allah, expand our knowledge and understanding with Your divinity, for with this light we may exercise influence in the universe, and power and might belong to You. I bring to You my poverty and my humility, so do not disappoint my hope in You, while You are the one of vast generosity and great nearness. I ask You with the diverse natures of spiritual souls and the varieties of the mysteries of the great kingdom and the sovereign, whose manifestation caused the liver-strings of the lovers to be severed—whose love's evidence is manifest, yet they have vanished in the fields of their inner polishing and the lights of their tongues. They called out: O You whose throne is more expansive than the heavens and the earth—the preservation of both does not burden Him, He is exalted and supreme. O most generous, O most merciful, O most tender, O most patient. O You besides whom there is no deity. He is the Living, the Sustaining One; drowsiness and sleep do not overtake Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. Who is there who may intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them; they encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His throne encompasses the heavens and the earth; the preservation of both does not weary Him, and He is exalted and supreme. O Allah, I ask You regarding this magnificent verse and the great names, that You illuminate our hearts and expand our sustenance. O You who beautify the hearts, O companion of hearts, O coverer of faults, O opener of difficulties, O forgiver of sins, O knower of the unseen—You have knowledge of my dwelling, and my Allah is with me in solitude and protects me from my slips, and delivers me from my outward affairs. Allah, You know my courage and are acquainted with my intention. You are the knower of my hidden affairs. The Master of the kingdom.

O my Lord, You are the one who answers me in my distress; You are my ultimate desire and my refuge in times of severity. You are my companion in my solitude, the one who has mercy upon my weeping and lamentation, the one who grants me respite from my bitterness, the one who accepts my prayer. If I have fallen short in those deeds which You commanded and have engaged in those actions from which You forbade—yet You supported me with Your honor and greatness and concealed with Your veil. So O greatest of the great, O Creator of all, O Master of the purpose and the Day of Recompense, You know what is hidden in the heart. You are the one who ordains every coming matter. If You complete my state with Your favor, then I ask You to accept intercession on my behalf and have mercy with Your mercy—for everything is expansive. O most merciful of those who show mercy. I ask You by virtue of this magnificent verse and the great names, that You send blessings and peace upon Sayyidina Muhammad, and upon his family and his companions, and grant me my request. O Lord of the worlds.

Forgiveness of Sins:

It is also a special property of Ayat al-Kursi that when a person has committed many sins and wishes to repent from them, he should on the night of Eid perform ghusl (ritual bath) and be clean from face to feet, then perform wudu (ablution), and at midnight offer four rak'ahs of prayer. In each rak'ah, he should recite Surah al-Fatiha once and Ayat al-Kursi seven times. After giving the salutation, he should recite istighfar (seeking forgiveness) seventy times and this durood sharif seventy times:

O Allah, bless our master Muhammad with a blessing by which You deliver us from all terrors and calamities, and by which You fulfill for us all our needs, and by which You purify us from all evil deeds, and by which You raise us to the highest ranks, and by which You cause us to reach the ultimate purposes from all goodness in life and after death—a blessing which I have stored for the Day of the Great Terror—and by his rank, O Allah, bless his family and companions and grant them peace.

O Allah, grant us through this verse the goodness of this world and the hereafter and all virtuous deeds, and by Your favor may all our needs be fulfilled. Remove our illnesses, make us pure, keep all calamities away, and fill us with health and well-being.
