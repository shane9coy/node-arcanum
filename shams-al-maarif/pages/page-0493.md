# Page 493 (English translation)
493

The reality of those [celestial] houses that have been mentioned is that they are the eternal letters of continuity. God the Most High says: "For every announcement there is a place of establishment, and you will surely know." In this [state], the present and future become equivalent: whoever has lifted the veil from their sight and illuminated their insight will be addressed with the truth of the self. The word "burūz" [manifestation/emergence] is used for this reason. That person keeps their intellect present among the created things, and the present and future appear alike to them. They observe the flash of actions. These were the celestial maidens whose mention you have read. This matter is concluded. God is the one who grants success.

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: The Celestial, Terrestrial, and Intermediate Kings and the Division of Letters]

There are twelve houses in the heavens. They are divided according to the four temperaments. Thus, three houses are hot and dry, fiery; and the remainder are similarly divided, and upon these same houses the division of letters is based. Their discussion is the royal ones and the created ones.

The categories of the houses:

Fire [signs]: three houses hot and dry: (1) Aries (2) Leo (3) Sagittarius
Earth [signs]: three houses cold and dry: (1) Taurus (2) Virgo (3) Capricorn
Air [signs]: three houses hot and moist: (1) Gemini (2) Libra (3) Aquarius
Water [signs]: three houses cold and moist: (1) Cancer (2) Scorpio (3) Pisces

What has been stated above regarding the houses and letters, memorize it well and understand it. These are mutually arranged, and keep this circle well in memory.
