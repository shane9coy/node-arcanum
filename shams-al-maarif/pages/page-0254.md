Four letters and the number 55—if these two are added together, they become 60. So in an empty place, with presence of heart, let him perform the dhikr seventy (70) times. If he supplicates, his need will be fulfilled.

Know that some names have only a single specific property, while others share their properties with other names—meaning there are several names for one meaning. In this lies the mystery of dominion and sovereignty with God the Exalted. Know that among the names, each has a property that is not found in others. Among these are the "source names," and the third meaning lies in the oneness, in which there is a strange mystery and the light of the command. Its sacred essence is He, the Knowing and the Wise.

Know that the properties of every name derive from its derived form, and its definition is according to its requirement. This is the hidden mystery that reveals itself to the knowers. For one upon whom the door of this knowledge has opened, he has succeeded alongside all of the family of Muhammad, peace be upon him, and God alone grants the knowledge that opens.

[Framed Title: Chapter: The Subtle Properties of Divine Names]

Know that among the divine names, whichever name has odd letters, it is for separation; in like manner, it also has the capability to bring about separation. And whichever has even letters, it is for creating affection, for marriage and love. Likewise, every name has letters and numbers, and for every number there is a specific procedure. One who has gathered these manifestations, the mystery has been unveiled to him. Among the names, every name has a spiritual number that relates to the temperament of its body. I do not elaborate on this because it involves great length. If I were certain that these secrets would not become manifest to every unworthy person, I would certainly expound here upon many strange and wondrous matters. And one for whom the decree of sustenance has been given, he appears as the drawing, sincere one and like a ruby. God the Exalted has said: "And God gives sustenance to whom He wills without account, to whom He wills from among His servants."

If you are among those who are astonished, there is a wonder in this; and if you are empty, you are being called. These are the pearls of the station of inspiration, which have become manifest through the files of expressions and signs. So obtain these precious truths with delight, otherwise there will be regret, although at that time regret will be of no benefit. Understand this matter: know that the conditions for practice with names and dhikrs are very many. However, I shall expound upon those conditions among them that are necessary for the perfection of practice, and I shall also mention those that are necessary for the most excellent works.

[Framed Title: Chapter: Conditions Necessary for Every Practice]

Among its conditions are: prayer in congregation; to exert oneself properly; correct belief; and perseverance in physical and spiritual purity. Then, according to qualification and preparedness, there is the spiritual discipline of contemplation regarding the practice of these names. For the knowledge of such mysteries, there must be perfect certainty and complete trust in their teachings. Then there is the connection that has already been mentioned.

One who desires complete spiritual authority must obtain it along with all these names, so that each name may grant him its power. With it, he should obtain the manifestation upon every description, and have complete satisfaction with everything. Whoever wishes to exercise authority over a name should direct his attention toward the presence of that name, and be prepared to accept whatever descends upon him in every place. He should be filled with the lights of that name until nothing else remains within him beside the lights. When that person reaches this station, the intimacy of that name's approach is obtained. When he has established a connection with one name, he is not empty from either—the name will either lead him to a knowledge that becomes manifest from the reality of that name at that time, or the light that becomes connected with it will be connected with that name as a mode of employment, and following them is obligatory, as Noah, peace be upon him, used to follow these names when God the Exalted was connected with the name of Hud, and in generosity and purity he was [text incomplete] a scholar from the whole...
