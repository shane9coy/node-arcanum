On the Production of Poison and Poisonous Medicines:
Some medicines are only poisonous, and some are only antidote to poison. But those medicines which are from the interior of the ray, from which the absence of the poisoned matter occurs—according to correct statement, those which are the excitants of bodies, whose matter is within the interior of the ray of light. Then they are overcome by bodies through their dense composition. So this medicine is one that causes separation, mixed with the soul in the apparent body, and transports it towards the upper world.

On the Prophet and Messenger Having Special Knowledge:
Its disclosure is not available to anyone except messengers, nor does this poisonous medicine have effect upon food, because they are fully acquainted with its complete quality.

Poison Does Not Affect Prophets:
Do you not know that Muhammad (peace be upon him) consumed poisoned meat, yet due to the divine lights, it had no effect upon him whatsoever? Most of the saints use such pleasures that ordinary people consider extremely harmful. The reason for this is that the disclosure of the causes of existence occurs to them.

Investigating Heavenly Matters Through Observation:
They investigate heavenly matters in the rank of earthly ones, and they compare the shadow wholly as it opposes the part, and the whole in which they are subjugated, and the keys of good are in their possession.

Radiant Heavenly Causes:
Know that the causes of radiant heavenly matters are rays, and the rays of earthly causes are united with light, and this is the reason for which animals have need of nourishment. "And there is nothing that does not glorify Him with His praise." So the luminous grace is very lowly. Whoever has understood these hidden ranks has also understood the secret of the divine craft. So due to the subtlety of luminosity, there is vicegerency in dense bodies, and due to the power of the ray, a revolution has occurred from one world to another, and due to its coincidence, the assertion of the elevation of parts has occurred in bodies.
