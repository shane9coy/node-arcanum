**The specific operators in the world are these individuals:**
The great ones who are in the world are: Adam, Abraham, Ishmael, Jesus, Nur Afaz, the hope of the Prophet's presence ﷺ, and after him the four Qutbs. They govern every region, because the spirituality of the Haatif assists them, and no work can be accomplished without it—for the Haatif is the comprehender of all books and the unifier of all communities, and it is the manifestation of the sphere of Qasur, the truth, and the existence of the world. With the reality of the lights' signs and since Qasur is in its letter Lam, which has been inscribed on a particular tablet, and they came upon the mystery of the ancient books and it became manifest.
Along with the Compendium, because of its being the source of realities: the reality of realities, the heart and stone, and the axis of Qasur, the truth and the existence of the world—and for this reason it came to contain the mortgaged books, and eternal revelation and the world's halo occurred. The detailed account of its guarded pen will come later, and it is also mentioned in our books of subtle meanings between signs and in the books of the Fatha. We mentioned this page solely for the purpose that you may become illuminated by its nobility and become acquainted with the original fundamental categories. If we were to describe it in detail, the discussion would become very lengthy. Therefore we describe the page briefly, and this is the pearl whose value and rank the marks of the perfect are aware of.
Know that when I traveled to perform the pilgrimage to Jerusalem, it occurred to me to visit the great ones of Damascus and Aleppo. Accordingly, I was en route.
**A tablet page in the gift of the Abdal:**
A person from among the Abdal came before me, and after greetings he said to me: "O Ahmad, I wish to give you a gift from which great benefit accrues." I said: "Venerable one, what is that gift?" He said: "One day I was in seclusion engaged in spiritual practice and occupied with my functions when suddenly a tablet appeared before me on which were written several lines, circles, letters, and names. Then a supplicant came before me and that tablet—he gave it to me but did not tell me anything. I did not know what benefits this tablet had nor what work it was for. Because of this my anxiety and restlessness increased. Then in that very state sleep overcame me, and I was blessed by the visitation of the Lion of God, Ali Murtaḍa, may God honor his face, and I saw that he was standing and he preceded me in greeting. I responded to the greeting. Then he said to me: 'Where is that tablet?' I said: 'It is present.' He took it from me, kissed it, and said: 'Do you know what mysteries of reality and knowledge are in this tablet? All the knowledge that you have compiled is contained within it.'"
**The tablet's name is Decree and Predestination:**
And I named this tablet "Decree and Predestination." In it are the mysteries of the Letter Alif, and the secret of the Greatest Name is within it, and the substance of the lights of the heart and Decree are all within it. Then he gifted that tablet to the Commander of the Faithful, placed his hand upon the Name of the Essence, and said: "This is the secret of the Greatest Name and its reality." Then he departed, saying: "O Ahmad, I am bringing this tablet to you." I accepted it. [unclear] scholars have also mentioned its benefits in the books.
