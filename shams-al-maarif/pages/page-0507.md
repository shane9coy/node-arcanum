# Page 507 (English translation)
505

I saw it and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "I saw it in this form in the Preserved Tablet, and Gabriel [damaged source] showed it to me in this very form."

At that time I submitted: "Your presence, if permission is granted to me, I shall explain it publicly." He commanded: "There is no harm." At that moment I woke from the dream and began to contemplate this circle. Now what I saw became clear to me—that this circle encompasses all secrets. Its results and effects are rare, its secrets are awaited and gathered. I have certainly mentioned the letter Alif in such a place and in this manner. And I pray to God that He guides me at the end and grants great reward and favor. Surely He is the great Forgiver. And I also pray that He grants every seeker of knowledge benefit through it with beneficence and generosity. The form of this great circle is as follows:

The ability to extract judgments shall be possessed by whoever can perform expansion of construction:

In this circle is the state of all the transformations of the world and events, and the rise and fall of kingdoms. All battles that occur between kings can be known, and all kings' names can be extracted from it. But only the person who knows the rules of expansion and discrimination can perform this extraction—one who knows how to multiply every root in its principles, because when you expand the numerical values of letters and determine which zodiac sign each letter belongs to and which fortune it relates to, then you will know what events will occur with that fortune. In this circle is the collection of all those matters that were established in the Jafr al-Mustakhraj al-Layib, and we have made it the source of Jafr. Indeed in this Jafr there are 528 verses, each of which has 18 tables, and each table has 28 cells in length and breadth, and all letters are diacriticalized. The properties of this circle are extremely majestic. If any person writes it and keeps it with him, awe, majesty, and acceptance shall be his portion. If he writes it on a silver tablet with gold ink and keeps it, great acceptance in all affairs shall be his. If he writes it on a military banner and displays it, that army shall never be destroyed or defeated. If he writes it on a ship's sail and keeps it with him, he shall be protected from every harmful person

Know that the judgment of every matter depends upon the letter Alif, and it has an unparalleled share in efficacy. The divine attribute names are contained in His name al-Qayyum. [unclear] It is obtained. The dependence of Alif upon the name of Allah—those letters from which its component parts are named and the rule is [established]. And Alif is from the beginning, and the name of Light holds a very elevated rank—it has the status of four, and with the letter (M) for it, there are the manifested and hidden worlds and the center below the intellect. The person who with this verse [damaged source—appears to be the beginning of Surah Al-Fath: Indeed We have opened for you a clear opening...]
