# Page 420 (English translation)
**The Effective Practice for Acceptance of Prayer:**
After performing two units of prayer, invoke Allah the Most High through the intercession of these three Names for one hour in this manner: "Ya Allah, Ya Allah, Ya Rabb."
Great lights shall become manifest thereby. Allah the Most High shall open the insight of his heart, and every religious and worldly supplication shall be accepted. If one constructs a square of 8 by 8 from their numerical values and engraves it upon a ring of three and a half mashas of gold and wears it, then Allah the Most High shall grant him awe, majesty, and greatness from His Presence, and his inner being shall be illuminated by the Divine Majesty.

**Revelation of Hidden Matters:**
If one recites the Name of Allah daily while keeping vigil at night, Allah shall inform him of hidden matters and enter him among His close servants. There are several stages in the recitation and application of each Divine Name. The first stage is that the Name should be recited according to its numerical equivalent, and these same numbers should be multiplied into the letters and written. The second stage is that the numbers of the Name should be multiplied within themselves, recited in this manner, and written. The third stage is that one should recite all the Divine Names for one hour. However, the superior method is to recite the Name according to its numerical equivalent without excess of anyone.

**The Mysteries of the Names Rahman and Raheem:**
The Names of Allah the Most High, Rahman and Raheem, are very numerous. Through recitation according to their numerical values, the mysteries of mercy descend upon hearts. Whoever has a hard heart that does not soften should recite both these Names. Allah the Most High shall transform the hardness of his heart into softness; his heart shall turn toward obedience and worship of Allah. If one recites these Names before an oppressor, Allah the Most High shall create softness in the heart of that oppressor and protect him from his evil, and the one who recites shall receive goodness.

**For Love:**
If one writes the letters of both Names, Rahman and Raheem, properly in a magic square of 8x8 on the day of Friday during the hour of Venus and keeps it with him, then whoever sees him shall love him greatly and obey him. If the numbers of both Names are written in a square upon a silver ring and left for seven nights under the light of the stars, and one recites both Names 535 times, then upon wearing this ring, whoever sees that person, Allah the Most High shall place love for him in that person's heart.

**Properties and Benefits of the Names Al-Malik and Al-Quddus:**
Al-Malik Al-Quddus (The Sovereign, The Holy)—both Names are very great. If a person is not valued, respected, or honored by people, and he is not renowned among them, then through the blessing of the recitation of these Names, Allah the Most High shall grant him fame, increase his worth, purify his inner being, and remove the rust from his heart.

**The Practice for Always Remaining Prosperous:**
If one writes the numbers of these Names in a four-by-four grid and engraves them on brass on the day of Thursday during the hour of Jupiter while the Moon is free from any malefic aspect, and keeps it with him, then through the grace and generosity of Allah, his honor shall increase, his dominion shall be established, and victories shall be attained. Whoever maintains constant recitation of the Name Quddus, Allah the Most High shall remove doubts from his heart, he shall acquire piety, and his pure character shall be renowned everywhere.
