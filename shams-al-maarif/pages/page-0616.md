# Page 616 (English translation)
**614**

If you want to meet this person, summon them with the same letter, and when you wish to perform the operation of summoning treasure, then write the four letters of alif, ya, mim, and dal on four eggs. Bind them in the neck of a pigeon or chicken and leave them in that place.

**Knowledge of the location of treasure:**

Where there is treasure, that animal will dig with its feet and beak. After emptying those eggs, perform the operation. And when you wish to make someone stay with you so that they are never separated from you, write this form and every letter along with its numerical values, make one form following another, and take it with you and bury it in the place where the desired person is. They will remain with you forever. The form is as follows.

[A rectangular frame containing letters and symbols. Above the frame are the letters: alif, ba, ha, waw, zay. Inside the frame are three rows of text:
Row 1: alif, ba, ha, ba, dal
Row 2: ha, ha, 'ayn, 'ayn
Row 3: waw, zay, 'ayn, jim, ha, ha]

And this is also a rule: even if a long journey is undertaken for its sake, it should still not be revealed. When you wish to perform an operation, take the first and last letters of that operation and write their numerical values, then perform the aforementioned operation. If it is for good, then do it in the regular manner, and if it is for evil, then perform it in reverse. We shall not explain further in greater detail.

**Chapter: The treasure will become visible**

Take twenty-eight eggs and write on each egg one letter from the sum of the numerical values of this noble chain, then place all the eggs in a glass vessel and a leaden bowl and set them aside. Seat a hen upon these eggs and recite some words and give it water to drink from that glass. Continue until its chicks emerge. Among them there will certainly be some chicken, and the head of that chicken will be turned upward. Guard it carefully, and when it matures, slaughter it and take its blood. Whoever applies this blood to their eye, treasures will become visible to them, and they will see the lower spirits. And when you write on its three membranes the numerical values along with the four letters and bind them in the neck of the chicken, the chicken will indicate the location of the treasure. These letters work for love, acceptance, silencing of tongues, subjugation of hearts, and similar purposes.

**Types of letters and the use of their operations:**

For these purposes, masculine letters should be written. For summoning the absent, use the letters of hawāhīyah. For causing fear and throwing animals, use the terrestrial letters. Use the aqueous letters for repulsion and purification. When it is the turn to call upon a sick person, treat them with the letters contained in the name of that disease along with its numerical values. Write the numerical values in regular order for good purposes and in reverse order for evil purposes.
