# Page 32 (English translation)
**The Name of the Book**

I have named this selected, unprecedented, and elevated book "Shams al-Maʿārif wa Laṭāʾif al-ʿAwārif" (The Sun of Knowledges and Subtleties of Mystical Knowledges) because it contains the most subtle operations and confirmations. Whoever receives this book of mine should protect it from unqualified people and consider its use in improper contexts forbidden (ḥarām). Whoever acts against this [principle], Allah the Most High will withhold from him the benefits, advantages, and blessings of this book. Read this book in a state of purity while remembering Allah the Most High, so that you may succeed in those matters which you desire. Only perform those operations by which Allah the Most High is pleased. Indeed, this book is for the friends of Allah (awliyāʾ), the righteous (ṣāliḥīn), the people of obedience and action, and the people of desire and those who act. Act with certainty and eagerness. May your certainty be true and your faith solid along with its realities. Indeed, actions are based on intention (niyya), and when intention is established for you in actions, then remain established with it along with sincerity of intention.

The saying of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him and his family, is:
[Overlaid signature: Muḥammad Ḥanīf Fārūq]

"Supplication should be made with light, certainty, and trust."

The pure saying of the Noble Messenger ﷺ is:

"When any of you supplicates, let him be firm in supplication and have certainty regarding acceptance."

Do not be impatient for the acceptance of supplication. Continue supplicating and await acceptance— Allah willing, the supplication will be accepted.

This book consists of forty chapters. In each chapter are countless benefits, and within them are hidden sublunar indications, symbols, and mysteries. Contemplate them with reflection and deliberation. The arrangement of the chapters is as follows:
