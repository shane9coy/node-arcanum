# Page 428 (English translation)
428

O Lord, You have manifested security and safety, and You have granted steadfastness to those for whom residence in the House of Divine Pleasure has been established, and You have given them protection from the changes of fortune and preserved them from the wiles of Satan who casts doubt upon the soundness faith by what You have armed them with from the weapons of faith and proof, and You have purified them from the anxieties of the impulses of calamities, and You have raised them above accepting the accidents of negations. I ask You, O God, by all that is in Your unseen of scientific realities and eternal subtleties, to make me safe from the fear of formal contemplation in the station of benefit and harm, until I turn to You with an empty heart, pure of soul, and confident in the promise of the Lord. I ask You, O God, to make for me something to hold onto so that I may be safe from the creation, and draw me to You with guidance to the path of life and direction to the way of salvation, O He who gives much and accepts little, loves goodness, and bestows favor upon the people of faith and goodness. I ask You, O God, by the witness of humanity and Your intercessor on the Day of Gathering, and by Your beloved whom for the greatness of Your signs for Your servants on the Day of Approaching, to uncover benefit and repel harm, and protect me from every affliction, honor me with the best of endings, and descend upon me with Your compassion a secret to soften—for You are the doer of good to every person, the one who bestows generosity and goodness, O Faithful.

O God, You are the Faithful One who placed faith in the hearts of the people of gnosis, and You manifested faith at the time of security and safety. You granted it steadfastness, for whom dwelling in the House of Divine Pleasure became apparent. You protected them from the changes of time. You shielded them from the whisperings of Satan, which cause defects in the soundness of faith. You gave them pardon, proof, and purification. You purified their desires from dangers, and raised them above accepting the accidents of negations. O God, I ask You, by virtue of all those pure realities and eternal subtleties that are in Your unseen, that You keep me safe from the fear of formal contemplation in the station of benefit and harm, until I turn to You with an empty heart and pure soul, indeed grasping firmly Your promise. O God, I ask You to make for me something by which I may be safe from the creation, and guide me to the path of life and make me firm on the way of salvation, O He who gives much and accepts little, who loves goodness, who bestows favor and grace upon the people of faith and goodness. I ask You, by virtue of the Master of Humanity and Your intercessor on the Day of Resurrection, and Your beloved whom You made a messenger on the Day of Judgment—who uncovers benefit and removes harm—I ask You to protect me from every affliction, honor me with the best ending, and with Your mercy remove hidden matters from me, for indeed You are the one who does good to every person and bestows generosity and grace, O Faithful One.

[Boxed heading]
The subjugation of the vicegerents of Ism al-Muhaymin and the increase in ranks and elevation of degrees

Ism al-Muhaymin is a great name. Its vicegerent's name is 'Itayyal. Under it are five rulers, each of whom oversees 145 divisions, and in each division there are 145 vicegerents present together. It is under the influence of Gabriel, peace be upon him, and is a manifestation of the mysteries of divine power. Allah the Most High taught it the mystery of permanence. His follower, upon asking about its worship, is concerned with it—so its rank increases and elevation of degrees becomes manifest from the unseen, and no matter what important task it may be, without doubt one should recite this supplication of Ism al-Muhaymin.

O God, You are the Guardian over Your creation, You have taken possession of their terms, You accept and answer their desires, and in all other states You are the striving one for their secrets, in the tablets of the Decree You connect their bonds with constancy and fulfill their ambitions.
