Every Need Will Be Fulfilled:

And among the properties of Ayat al-Kursi is also this: when you face an urgent need, go to the mosque and perform two rak'ahs of prayer. In the first rak'ah, recite Surah al-Fatiha once and Ayat al-Kursi seven times, and in the second rak'ah similarly. After giving the salam, stand in the mihrab and say this seven times: Yā Rabbī Yā Qāḍī al-Ḥājāt.

Then recite this supplication:
Allāhumma aghninī bika 'amman siwāka 'alā al-muttabi'īna 'an kulli ḥaqqin yad'ū ilā kulli ẓāhirin 'uluwin 'uluwan aw bāṭinin amrin wa ballighnī murādī wa arfa'nī ilā darajati muntahāya wa ashhidnī al-wujūda bi-al-ri'āfati wa al-surūri bi-a'ẓami sirran al-tanzīli ilā al-nihāyāti wa al-jūdi ilā al-badāyāti ḥattā yanquṭi'a al-kalāmu wa tasukuta ḥarakatu al-anāmi wa taṣīra bi-qaṭ'i laqẓati al-ghayni wa yanūba al-wāḥidu 'ani al-ithnayni. Allāhumma nayyir 'alayya min al-nūri alladhī yassartahu 'alā kathīrin min 'ibādika wa ayyidnī bi-dhālika bi-nūri ḥujajātī yakhṭafu bihi baṣaru kulli ḥāsidin mina al-jinni wa hab lī al-darajata al-'uliyā li-kulli maqāmin wa aghninī 'amman siwāka bi-ḥayyi tuhibu bihi faqrī annaka anta al-ghaniyyu al-ḥamīdu. Allāhumma innī as'aluka an tughniya faqrī wa tuhayyi'a amrī wa tujabbira kasrī wa an taqḍiya ḥājātī wa hiya kadhā wa kadhā.

O God, make me independent through Yourself from all besides You. Make me independent from every material and spiritual pursuit on the path of following. And deliver me to my desire. Exalt me to the rank of my ultimate end. Manifest me in existence with compassion and joy, along with the greatest secret of the revelation to the ultimate ends and generosity to the beginnings—until speech is cut off and the movement of creation falls silent, and through the cutting of the point of the letter ghayn, make me solitary, and let the one suffice for two. O God, illuminate upon me with the light that You have made easy for many of Your servants. Strengthen me with that with the light of my proofs, by which the eye of every envier among the jinn is snatched away. Grant me the highest rank in every station. Make me independent of all besides You through the living one by whom You give my poverty. Truly You are the Rich, the Praiseworthy. O God, I ask You to enrich my poverty, make my affair easy, repair my brokenness, and fulfill my needs, which are such and such.

This verse is also good to read for its meaning: Al-am najidka yatīman fa-āwā. Wa wajadaka ḍāllan fa-hadā. Wa wajadka 'ā'ilan fa-aghni. Understand this allusion.

The Practice for Fulfilling a Need Through a Great Ruler:

This is also one of the special properties of Ayat al-Kursi: when you have some need with an important person and you desire its fulfillment, fast that day—and it is better if you remain silent. Then break your fast with something sweet. After the Maghrib prayer, sit in the same place and recite Ayat al-Kursi until 'Ishā'. Do not speak about worldly matters until you go to pray 'Ishā'. Recite Ayat al-Kursi seventeen (17) times in this manner: with each recitation of Ayat al-Kursi, recite this supplication:
Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā ḥayyu yā qayyūmu yā dā'imu yā waddūdu an tulqiyya al-maḥabbata wa al-mawaddata fī qalbi kulli wāḥidin wa an tulqiya 'alayya ḥullatan bi-al-jūdati wa al-maḥabbati

—and mention your own name. Then say: Ḥattā yakūna ṭaw'an yadīhi wa lā yukhalifannī ṭarfan 'aynin. Yā ḥayyu yā qayyūmu yā waddūdu wa bi-ḥaqqi asrāri hādhihi al-āyati. Yā karīmu yā jawwādu. Allāhumma ayyid bi-jalbi qalbi fulānin ibn fulānatin wa ḥarrikū rūḥāniyyata al-maḥabbati wa al-mawaddati li-kay [unclear] yajidū lahum kutiba al-nūru. Allāhumma āmīn.
