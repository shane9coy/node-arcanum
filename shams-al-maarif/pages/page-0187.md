# Page 187 (English translation)
189

Give me such sustenance that makes me independent and removes poverty. Remove the anxieties of Satan from my heart. Indeed, You are the One Who is asked, the King of kings, the Great Giver, the Sustainer, the Almighty, the Extender, the Uplifter, the Bestower of honor, the Giver of humiliation, the Self-Sufficient, the Enricher, the Generous, the Bestower of gifts, the Giver of sustenance, the Requested, the Vast, the Appreciative, the Possessor of grace and bounty. O Allah, I ask You by Your right, and by Your generosity, and by Your favor and by Your beneficence. O You whose favor is above every favor and whose beneficence is above every beneficence, O Lord of this world and the hereafter, O You of the true promise, there is no deity except Your pure essence. Indeed I am among the wrongdoers. O Allah, make lawful sustenance easy for me from Your provision and make it my lot. O Allah, accept my prayer, Surah Al-Fatiha, by the right of Your great supreme Name, and by the right of our master Muhammad ﷺ, accept it, and may blessings and peace be upon the Prophet ﷺ, his family ofMuhassin and Tahir, and all his companions. O You who open and give victory, O Provider, O Able, for the sake of him who seeks, gather between me and him what is lawful, and make him independent from what is unlawful, make this my lot in this hour, O You of greatness and honor, O Allah, O Sufficient, O Majestic, O Guardian, O Advocate, help me with Your hidden grace, O Generous, O Merciful, O Merciful, O Allah, suffice me with what is lawful and protect me from what is unlawful, and grant me obedience in comparison to my sin, and by Your favor, O Allah, O Compassionate of this world and the Merciful of the hereafter, O Lord of the worlds, I ask You that You send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad ﷺ and his family, such blessings as You are worthy of and they are worthy of. O Lord of the worlds, and power and strength are from Allah, the Great. And that You open for me the doors of Your sustenance. O Allah, I ask You [unclear: requesting] otherwise I will not take. By the right of these secrets, I ask that You make my sustenance easy for me as You made it easy for Your vast creation.

[Boxed heading: Blessed Benefit: The Practice for the Fulfillment of Needs]

Whoever has a need from Allah the Most High, it is obligatory that before the Fajr prayer, he prays two rak'ahs in this manner: in each rak'ah he recites Surah Al-Fatiha once and Surah Al-Ikhlas three times. Then when he finishes the prayer, he sits on his two feet in such a way that he is slightly raised, and he recites Istighfar:

Astaghfirullah al-'Azhim alladhi la ilaha illa huwa al-Hayy al-Qayyum wa atubu ilayhi wa as'alhu al-tawbah wal-maghfirata li wa li walidayya wa li jami' al-muslimeena wal-muslimat

He recites it one thousand times with eyes closed. Then whatever supplication he makes with humility will be accepted.

Whoever recites these verses and writes them and keeps them with him will receive sustenance without limit. These verses are:

Indeed, sustenance and acceptance of supplication

Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim: Ta, Mim. And from what We have provided them, they spend. Ta, Mim. Every time Zakariyya entered the prayer niche, he found provision with her. He said, "O Maryam, from where is this for you?" She said, "It is from Allah." Indeed, Allah provides sustenance to whom He wills without reckoning. "And provide for us, and You are the best of providers." Say, "Shall I take as a protector other than Allah, the Maker of the heavens and the earth, while He feeds and is not fed?" "And We made the people who were oppressed inherit the eastern and western lands which We had blessed." "So He gave you refuge and strengthened you with His help and provided you with good things that you may give thanks." "Our Lord, that they may establish prayer, so make hearts..."
