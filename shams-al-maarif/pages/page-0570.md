Melt silver and cast pellets upon it until its color becomes full and appears golden. The composition of the effective dissolution of orpiment is as follows: grind separately and mix together tar, talking (a mineral), and qit (a substance). Then dissolve the mixture by combining them, and grind a dinar weight of antimony into fine pieces, place it in sour wine three times, filter it, and until noon following the mentioned traditions, mix it in and divide it into 1/8 portions. Then pour this onto pieces of copper, that is, orpiment, while warm, and extinguish it in sweet water. Then perform the appropriate procedure with the aforementioned antimony and offer thanks to God. Silver will be prepared.

And another composition is this: Mars 1 part, central (sinneral) 5, poison 30, period 5, qira 4, which 5 — when it is pure, it will be prepared. The composition of Mars antimony is this: one ounce of iron filings, one dirham of Cypriot orpiment green, one dirham of zuhalmaz, one dirham of red sulfur — dissolve all of these by mixing in egg yolk and keep it in fire throughout the night until it becomes red here. Then grind it with oil and nazar wa nushuri and sahaga and let it cool. It will become 'mushab' (transformed).

The composition of ru'yat zahar (orpiment viewing/preparation) is this: one ounce of copper, grind it and drink (consume) salt and white qara, then pour little by little approximately one and a half ounces upon it. It will become correct.

[Heading: Chapter on the Types of Antimony]

Types of Roots, Herbs, and Minerals:

This has three types: botanical, animal, mineral. Botanical has six types: spirits, bodies, stones, vitriols, salts, sublimate. Spirits have four types: mercury, naphtha, sulfur, orpiment. Bodies are: gold, silver, copper, iron, lead, tin, antimony. Stones are 15: marcasite, litharge, red earth, riting (minium), leon, shazanj, milak, surma, hirak, bhis, glass. Vitriols are 5: white borax, mixed borax, nitre, red borax, rhubarb borax, Egyptian borax. Salts are 11: pure salt, marcasite salt, Tabrizi salt, Indian salt, naphtha salt, Indian salt, qili salt, mountain salt.

Good mercury is that whose color is white and thin, and when it is placed in cloth and wrung, it comes out, and nothing remains in the cloth. Naphtha has two types: one mineral that is ancient and burned. Its production is in Samarkand. And one type of it is yellow. This does not work in mansaf (a vessel), and this is the naphtha of grace. When you melt it, it melts.

Types of Orpiment:

Zarnikh, that is orpiment, comes in six types. Red: this is rare. Yellow: this sticks like cedar. White, hard like elephant tusk. Found in soil, black pebbly, qurnisiya — four types. Hazel, ruby, smaller, apni, very red, iron-black. Stithia also has several colors. Black in which there are eyes (specks) and some black pieces. This is male. Bright — two types: asghari and hamari al-jazid. Tanukhiya also has several types. Green pieces, yellow, small, mahmudi, green kuhli, qushwardi, white, al-azraq, Indian. Ritinaj is a green stone. Striated, from which many beads are made — this is found as lapis lazuli, Egyptian, kuhli, and ghrasani. But the best is kuhli. Lapis lazuli is of three kinds: the one in which there is redness and flaking — in it the layer of lapis is green.

Ritinaj: this is found in Anjar. It turns the color of copper and iron red. It is not more valuable than copper. Talk: two types, white and yellow. It is extracted from silver mines.
