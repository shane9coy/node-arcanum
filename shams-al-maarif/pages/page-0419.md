Chapter: The Beautiful Names, Their Methods of Practice, and the Invocations for Each Method

Allah the Most High has deposited in His names the mysteries of His grace and generosity, justice and majesty, mercy and forgiveness. These are the manifestations of everything. That is, these names are the manifestations of mysteries. When a seeker mentions any name, at that time its mysteries and symbols become manifest.

**The Importance of the Book and the Complete Knowledge of the Author:**

We have before this book composed fifty books in this science and art, which are known to the people of reliability, but in all of them only one part of this science could be explained, which only the knowers can understand. In this book I have mentioned those methods and indications which can lead the seeker to his desired goal. I have explained these methods in this book. It contains invocations and remembrances. I pray to Allah the Most High that He protect these mysteries from the unworthy. Indeed, He is the possessor of grace and the doer of supererogatory deeds.

**The First Method of the Beautiful Names and Its Benefits:**

"He is Allah, there is no god except Him, the Merciful, the Compassionate, the King, the Holy, the Peace, the Guardian, the Mighty, the Compeller, the Great" to the end of Surah al-Hashr. In these names are hidden mysteries of sincere monotheism, increase of faith, and awakening of the light of certainty. Those who remember them obtain elevated spiritual stations, the heart becomes alive, there is inclination toward obedience to Allah, and the attributes of Allah the Most High are bestowed upon the servant.

**The Benefits of Three Names:**

The remembrance of the name of Allah the Most High, "Allahu la ilaha illa huwa Rabbi," is very noble. For those who maintain divine communion in seclusion, this remembrance is an elixir—they obtain communion in their seclusions, and it brings them divine lights and greatness and majesty. It produces humility and submission before Allah in them. "La ilaha illa huwa" is the most excellent remembrance of complete spiritual wayfaring. Among common people, whoever is constant upon this remembrance, special blessing descends upon them; Allah the Most High gives them every good and protects them from every evil and sickness.

**The Remembrance for Removing Obesity:**

Whoever has become very overweight and feels sluggish in movement, the remembrance of these names is beneficial for him. His remembrance will cause him to feel decrease and lightness in his body. Whoever writes these three names in a ten-by-ten square while the sun is entering Aries and keeps it with himself, Allah the Most High will grant him strength of certainty, increase in faith, and sincerity in deeds.

**Protection from Jinn and Treatment of Fever:**

Whoever is troubled by jinn should keep them with himself, and the jinn will immediately flee. Those with fever should keep them with themselves, and the fever will immediately subside. If one engraves this on a sugar tablet, washes it with water, and drinks it, there will be relief very quickly.
