# Page 535 (English translation)
535

When joined together, Heqq produced Tamthi'il. This was the apparent method, and the esoteric [method] is as follows: multiplying 9 by 5 gives 45. Ashi'il was produced. Then returning to the original [number], Darvi'il was obtained, and it became known that each of these letters has muwakkalat assigned to it, serving as their operators, and they are luminous bodies that manifest in solitude.

Chapter on the exposition of the letter Ya. Its number is 10, its basit is ya, and its gheiras is ya-alif. When multiplied, 209 results. When joined, Haqqthi'il was produced. This is the method of the methods, and the inferiorities—that is, the esoteric method—is this: they are ten in number, multiply them by themselves, 100 results. Waqti'il was produced. Then we returned to the apparent number without joining. Ikhti'il was produced. Then we came to the apparent [number], Dari'il occurred.

Chapter on the letter Kaf. Its number is 20, its basit is kaf, and its gheiras is kaf-alif. When the basit was performed, there were 36 letters. Multiplied, 309 results. Datri'il was produced. And by the esoteric method, the original number 20 multiplied by 66 results. Nisa'il was produced. Returning to the original without subtraction, Nisa'il was [again] produced.

Chapter on the letter Lam. Its number is 30, its basit is lam, and its gheiras is lam-alif-mim-alif. Their basit is 181. When joined, Taaqi'il was produced. Then in the esoteric direction, it appeared in thought—Sa'il was produced through derivation. Then returning to the original, Ahya'il appeared.

Chapter on the letter Mim. Its number is 40, and its basit is ya-mim. Subtracted, it becomes 39. Multiplied, 381 results. Joined, 361 results, and A'il was produced. This belongs to the spine of the methods, but the inferior [method] is this: the original number was multiplied into the esoteric number, [Khai'il] was produced. The word of the people of the middle [region]: [Kha'il]. Then returning to the original, Qita'il was produced.

Chapter on the letter Nun. Its number is 50. Subtracted basit, it becomes 53. Multiplied, 425 results. Subtracted in this way, 375 remains. Hati'il was produced. This is the apparent derivation, and the esoteric is as follows: when multiplied, 2500 results. Through derivation, Lakni'il was produced. Returning to the original number, Baiki'il occurred—this manifests in the methods.

Chapter on the exposition of the letter Sin. Its number is 60, and its basit is sin-ya-nun, and its gheiras is 30. Multiplied, 300 results. Joined, 158 results, and Fasa'il was produced. This was apparent, and the esoteric is as follows: when we multiplied the original number, Laqa'i'il appeared, and then from the original, Fati'il was produced.

Chapter on the letter 'Ayn. Its number is 70, its basit is 'ayn-ya-nun, and its gheiras is 36. When multiplied and joined, Risma'il was produced. And the esoteric [method] is this: 'Ayn is 70, multiply them by the number of its letters, that is three, results in 210, and Ghara'il was produced—returning to the original number, Dari'il was produced.

Chapter on the letter Fa. Its number is 80, and its basit and gheiras is fa-alif-lam-alif—36 letters result. After multiplication, it was joined—Rati'il was produced. And the esoteric [method] is this: multiply the original number by 3, Wasq was produced, and Sati'il appeared. Then returning to the original number, Ra'il was produced. Let it be known that each of these muwakkalat holds a wondrous rank, and they have wondrous and strange names.

Chapter on the letter Sad. Its number is 90, and its basit and gheiras is 92. When multiplied, 409 results, and the kacba of letters becomes correct. When extracted, Wasi'il appeared. And when the original number was multiplied by the extracted letters, 20 results, and the third muwakkal from the original number, Dari'il was produced.

Chapter on the letter Qaf. Its number is 100, and its basit is qaf-alif. When multiplied, 356 results. When extracted, Ra'il, the muwakkal, was produced. This was the apparent form. Afterwards, we returned to the original and multiplied its number—300 was obtained. When this was derived, and from the apparent number, the muwakkal Laqa'i'il was produced.
