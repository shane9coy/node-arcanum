# Page 551 (English translation)
Extract a name that is of the family of the first Hamaj. Then [take] forty names from each chapter for those clients who are appointed upon the Klif and are from the offspring of Nari Sama'. Thus this is the expansion of the Jifni and the outlet of the limited ones, from the descendants of the Khagan of the first Hamaj. Then take forty names of the Hamtas and extract the names of the fifth Khirea, which are from the swift-penned offspring of the Shahanshah ibn Tarij. At the end of the chapter of the desired, speak with the speech of the Qabiz.

The Speech of the Masrat of the Metaphorical [or Allegorical]:

The speech of Masrat and its metaphorical [form] and the explanation of its outlet. This outlet is a commentary on Zahira; it will become one name. Then make it renowned in the city with the attribution of the singular up to the extreme radius with its outlet, and the letters except for the name of Awn in the direction of Taswib to its Ghayira. And make its extraction with scarcity from its outlet upon its difficulty. Thus this becomes one name. Then make it profound and reverse all that which emerges from it; these are its lineage and your helpers. Then in this manner, make the extraction from the books of Hamaj of Masrat and the books of Zahira.

Commentary on the Small Chapter:

The outlet of the names is with the letters of invitation above seven letters, and its intention is the intermediary. The royal bond of the outlet is with seven letters from the number of twelve Satrbayl, and it is Awn. Take the intention reversed. Then make the extraction of the names of God, because the intention is recorded above the five with the letter of invitation repeated in the manner of Muzmul, and these are called the previous and the conjoining, and they are three hundred total. Habba and W-Saf and Khazin are upon them, and Imar Dig Tarasiyara thirty, of which seven are clients; Salt al-Huda and Dastavar Harran, and Abla Marg al-Batur twenty-one, of which seven are Jamīl and Dastavar Harran are clients, and Abla Marg Khtartha ninety, and Afras and Dastavar Harran are clients.

Commentary on the Connected Chapter: The Great Chapter:

And these are the privation of the offspring of the connected Shahanshah, the outlet to al-Husayn, the ancient and the greatest, and they are Kaji and Sarista and Akhlat and Mustada and Samai. Take its name with one letter of the letters of invitation from the midst of the row of Masaji with its number, and reverse the letter of Jalal. That is the same. And whatever letter becomes evident upon this pattern of invitation—because that is the same—take the name of Mutqallas. Then extract its offspring from before with the letters of invitation upon the method of difficulty, and they are five, like the offspring of Intention. The names are concluded by opening Jarab, and they are called Wasar and Akhlat and Atro and Mustada and Samai. Thus by opening, the offspring of Wasar are twenty-one. Salt of them is Dastavar Murakkab and Harran are clients, and Taiyyib and Ham are the offspring of Mustada, of which Tykrasy is upon and Salt Muqara and Hariba are clients. Husayn Hamish's offspring are Samai and Farahad and Muluk and Qamala and Harasa and Taurah and Shalandah, and the kunyah of all of them is Abu al-Qahtan; these are his offspring.

I, Jayakal, am the most incapable deputy agent. From them [come] sixty names with letters, sixty when they appear in seven languages, and you have come to know the outlet of each language, and you have also learned the names of Awn. And when you have also learned the invitation, then take the name of the Owner of System at that time, and its quantity will also become known to you from all seven languages, and those seven languages are these.

The names of the seven languages:

Arabic, Hebrew, Syriac, Greek, Hindi, Roman, and Persian. Thus when these names have become known to you, those which are upon the Klif are first, because they are offspring, thus this is the attribution of the Owner of System. The knowledge of the books of the Seal of the Gate is now known to you, and I have explained it. Thus all the degrees of the chapter's letter are the beginning of God; the alteration of the letters is upon the bonds of their times, which are anger and moisture. Thus by the method of inscription and elevation, the first degree is from it, and for its one letter there will be one name. Then at its end upon the first, place the pronoun of the degrees, and extract the names of the angels with the letters of Zahira in the middle of the name. The names of these angels are the clients whose names you have constructed. Thus place the names upon the pronoun, and the angels of the names have been extracted from between the columns, because the guardians of the names are from the degrees. Mahamaj Nigar
