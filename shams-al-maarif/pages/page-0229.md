Kings gaining victory in battle

We have opened this chapter, wherein if you conceal your secret you will achieve your purpose. If any king intends to fight and wishes to overcome his enemy, when going to the battlefield let him perform ablution and pray two units of prayer. Then let him pick seven pebbles from the ground and take them up; with each pebble let him recite one of these letters: "fa", "qaf", "tha", "jim", "kha", "zay", "shin". Let him place these pebbles on the palm of his left hand. Then let him take one pebble with his right hand and recite the noble verse ten (10) times: "SUMMUN BUKMUN 'UMUYUN FAHUM LAA YARJI'UNA", and then throw it before him. Then let him take the second pebble in his right hand, recite the second verse upon it ten times, raise his hand upward, and recite "AFAHASIBTUM ANNAMAA KHALAQNAAKUM 'ABATHAN WA ANNAKUM ILAYNAA LAA TURJA'UNA"; then throw it behind him. Then let him take one pebble, recite the third verse upon it ten times, raise his hand upward, and recite "WA JA'ALNAA MIN BAYNI AYDIIHIM SADDAN WA MIN KHALFIHIM SADDAN FA AGHSHAYNAAHUM FAHUM LAA YURSA'UNA"; and throw it to his right side. Then let him take one pebble and recite the fourth verse upon it ten times, raise his hand upward, and recite "YAA MA'SHAR AL-JINNI WA AL-INS I INISTATTA'TUM AN TANFURUU MIN AQTAR AL-SAMAAWAATI WA AL-ARDHI FA ANFURUU LAA TUNFURIUNA"; then throw the pebble to his left side. Place the remaining three pebbles upon your head and enter the battle. By the command of Allah the Almighty, no harm shall ever befall you.

Among its properties is this: when you fear your enemy or are in a secret place, take seven pebbles from the ground, for victory will be expedient for you at that time. Then recite over the pebbles as has been mentioned previously—the pebbles you cast from your right, left, front, and back, as we have described earlier. Sit upon the ground with knees bent. Close the fingers of your right hand. Then make the fortress of refuge upon the fingers of your left hand, close your hand, and then fall silent. You must keep your mouth firmly closed. Even if these enemy people come to him and search for him, they will not see him. Allah the Almighty will have concealed him from their eyes. And Allah the Almighty is the most knowing.
