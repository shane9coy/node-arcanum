# Page 725 (English translation)
Remember it, and one favor is that He has made His servant a slave of His word, for He has subjugated all things in the world to him. As He says:

"And He has subjugated to you what is in the heavens and what is on the earth, all of it, from Him"

All these divine blessings are what He has bestowed upon His servants.

Imam Hasan's statement to his mother Fatimah, may Allah be pleased with her, that he should not become undutiful:

It is narrated from His Eminence Imam Hasan, may Allah be pleased with him, that for several days he did not eat with his mother, Her Eminence Lady Fatimah al-Zahra, may Allah be pleased with her. She said: "O son, why do you not eat with me?" He said: "I am afraid that your gaze may fall upon something and I eat it, then I would become undutiful." The mother said: "O son, do not fear at all. You have permission to eat." Then he ate.

The Muslim ummah will be a witness over all nations; the repentance of this ummah hides those sins from the angels so they do not write them:

This is also His favor that He has made you a witness over the nations on the Day of Resurrection, and He has concealed great deeds along with seeking forgiveness from the angels.

Kindness to all becomes a means of spiritual unveiling:

It is binding upon a person that whatever creature asks kindness from him, he should show every amount of kindness to it. Especially with the poor and the needy, and he should also be kind to his own heart with thought and sincerity, so that the things of the heart of the divine kingdom may become a cause of unveiling. And being kind to one's own self by abandoning its desires is goodness. Various forms of spiritual exercises become a cause of knowing the Lord, because when you are kind to souls with righteous deeds, even to the extent that their qualities become manifest upon you. This is what the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, indicated in this saying:

"Whoever knows himself has known his Lord"

Your self-reform and guarding the rights of others will make you a saint and will grant you recognition of the Lord:

Your kindness to your own soul is that you stand firm upon the divine rights and fulfill the trust that has been placed upon you, and standing in its service is obligatory upon you, for this is the essence of the religious laws and the divine names. Simply this conduct among existing things will be the cause of unveiling the mysteries of divine power, and through it you will become free from the servitude of the world and the darkness of the body.

Opposing the lower self and obeying the rational soul leads to witnessing the lights of Allah:

It is binding upon a person to abandon the use of those things that are beloved to the lower self, for not using them is as if destroying the lower self. Therefore, take action through reason so that their end may be discovered and knowledge may be yours, and the occult sciences and noble realities may become manifest upon you. For this it is necessary that having witnessed the reality of light, you establish your way. Know that these virtues that have been mentioned are all like the root of roots. If you have become acquainted with their reality, the essence is to conduct yourself with all reality and truth along the path of spiritual wayfaring. Therefore, do not be heedless of them. Take courage to witness His lights.
