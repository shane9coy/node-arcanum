# Page 186 (English translation)
186

O Allah, there is no deity except Him, the Lord of the Great Throne. O Allah, I indeed ask You by Your exalted Name, which You have manifested to whom You will among Your allies and inspired to Your chosen ones among Your beloved ones—I ask You, O Allah, that You bring me my sustenance from Your presence, by which You will suffice me against others, and with which You will repair my broken state, and sever the bonds of Satan from my heart. For indeed, You are Allah, the Compassionate, the Beneficent, the Peace, the Judge, the Bestower, the Provider, the Opener, the All-Knowing, the Withholder, the Extender, the Abaser, the Lifter, the Honorer, the Humiliator, the Self-Sufficient, the Enricher, the Noble, the Giver, the Provider, the Subtle, the Vast, the Thankful, Possessor of Grace and Blessings and Generosity and Honor.

O Allah, I indeed ask You by the right of Your creation, and the nobility of Your nobility, and Your grace and Your beneficence, O He whose grace is above every grace and whose beneficence is above every beneficence, O Master of this world and the Hereafter, O True in His promise—there is no deity except You. Glorified are You, indeed I was among the wrongdoers.

O Allah, make lawful sustenance easy for me from Your provision, and make it my portion. O Allah, answer my supplication by the right of Surah Al-Waqi'ah, and by the right of Your great Name, the Most Great, and by the right of our master Muhammad, upon him be peace and the blessings of Allah and his family, the good and the pure, and upon all his companions, and by the right of a clear opening, an opening judge, a provider, an able one, a sufficiency, the best of providers, one who suffices the wretched and the poor, the oft-returning, who does not take to task for crimes.

O Allah, make easy for me sustenance that is lawful, good, and gather between me and it from Your lawful provision, and make it from my portion in what is lawful, not what is unlawful, O Possessor of Glory and Honor, in this hour, O Allah, O the Sufficient, O the Majestic, O the Guarantor, O the Trustee.

Suffice me with Your lawful provision against Your unlawful provision, and with Your obedience against Your disobedience, and with Your grace against all others besides You, O Allah, O Compassionate of this world, O Merciful of the Hereafter, O Lord of the Worlds. I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad, and to open for me the doors of Your provision, O Opener. And I ask You by the right of Surah Al-Waqi'ah and its secrets, that You ease my provision for me, as You have eased it for many of Your creation, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds.

O Allah, send blessings upon our master Muhammad and upon our master Muhammad, a blessing for which You are worthy and he is worthy, O Lord of the Worlds. And there is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great.
