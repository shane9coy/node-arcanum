Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Hadi"

Hadi is He who created the creatures and guided them toward knowledge of His essence, and He has attributed guidance to His own essence. Thus He has said:

Inna al-huda huda Allah

"Indeed guidance is Allah's guidance."

And whoever walks upon it, He guides him. When Allah the Most High brought existence forth from non-existence in the first creation, there are two divisions:

Fariqun fi al-jannati wa fariqun fi al-sa'ir

Those who come from non-existence into existence are of two kinds: the People of the Right (Ahl al-Yamin) and the People of the Left (Ahl al-Yasar):

One group is called the People of the Right, and one group is called the People of the Left, and each of them inclines toward the Creator, and upon this Allah the Most High bears witness with His saying:

Fa-hada Allah alladhina amanu

That is: "Allah the Most High has guided those who believe" to accept the oneness of God, and He is the True Guide, and upon Him is the figurative application of guidance to idols—rather in the original reality He has guided them toward that very essence, upon which they walk, and all things are according to former deeds, which He has caused to be distant.

[Underlined] Regarding self-awareness, the decrees of qada and qadar become separate:

That every action is according to His decree and predestination; its attribution is that it is not empty of command and knowledge.

La yu'salu 'amma yaf'alu wa hum yu'saluna

And whoever obtains proximity through this name becomes a hadi (guide), and becomes good for the sake of the people of good. (The noble name)
