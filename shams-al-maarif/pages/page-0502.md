# Page 502 (English translation)
**THE APPOINTED ANGEL WILL INFORM HIM IN A DREAM**

Whoever desires to know something through a dream should write this name on his thumb and place it beneath him, then sleep. And while falling asleep, he should recite these words: "Ajib ya khadim hazha al-ism wa akhbarini 'an kaza"—"Answer, O servant of this name, and tell me about such and such." However, he should recite the name so much that sleep overcomes him. In the dream, a person will correctly inform him about the matter. If it is not known on the first night, it will be on the second, or certainly on the third.

It is transmitted from some of the elders that they supplicated to God Almighty for thirty years and it was not accepted. Finally, when God Almighty knew of their sincere intention, their need was fulfilled. "Ya muhijj ajib samsamayyal"—meaning, I am that pure essence who sees even in the deepest darkness. Whoever recites this and breathes upon his agriculture, the crops will not spoil, and by virtue of this name, a person remains protected from drowning. And this name is written on the palm of Kasfayyal, the appointed angel.

**BY THE BLESSING OF THIS NAME, SOLOMON RECEIVED RULE:**

"Ya malita ya tardiyyal"—by the blessing of this name, God Almighty returned to Solomon, peace be upon him, the kingdom of the jinn and his ring.

"Ya sam'ufiya ya qamla ajib ya tutiyyal"—meaning, I will revive old bones. This name makes the blind see. When its letters are separated and bound for pain in the mathematics of the mouth or teeth, there will be relief; and if the afflicted one writes it on a piece of bread and swallows it, his suffering will be eased. And if these names are engraved on a ring and the seal of that ring is pressed into clay and it is buried in a field, neither locusts nor insects will consume that crop.

**GOD GAVE THE BELIEVER VICTORY OVER THE DISBELIEVER:**

"Ya muti' ya tiyyal ya saghir ajib ya alhayyal ya huwa man la ya'lam ma huwa illa huwa"—this name is like a ram and its meaning is: I am God, the sole, the eternal. By the blessing of this name, God granted the believers victory over the disbelievers and hypocrites. "Ya sam'itaya ya nuriyyal ya 'amithaya"—meaning, I am the hearer, the knower. I move the sun from east to west.

**THE ENEMY WILL BE DEFEATED:**

Whoever recites this name and breathes upon a handful of dust and throws it toward the enemies while saying: "Shahati al-wujuh" three times, the enemy will be defeated.

**DELIVERANCE FROM DISTRESS IS OBTAINED:**

"Ya man yufni al-akwan wa al-mulukut wa yabqa huwa ya man la ilaha illa huwa al-awwal wa al-akhir wa al-zahir wa al-batin"—
"O You who annihilate all worlds and kingdoms while You alone remain, O You beside whom there is no god, the First and the Last, the Outward and the Inward." Whoever recites this will obtain deliverance from every hardship.

"Ya muti' ya al-kusha ajib ya darijayyal"—meaning, I am the one who removes every hardship and sends down the months and secrets.
