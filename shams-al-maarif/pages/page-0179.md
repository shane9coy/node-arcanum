# Page 179 (English translation)
177

Al-Ḥafīẓ, Al-Muqīt, Al-Barr, Al-Tawwāb, Al-Razzāq, Al-Wahhāb. Our Lord, accept [this] from us. Indeed, You are the Hearing, the Knowing. Our Lord, pour upon us patience and make firm our feet, and grant us victory over the disbelieving people. Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. Indeed, You are the Giver. Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers. My Lord, cause me to enter a sound entry and cause me to exit a sound exit, and grant me from Yourself authority that helps. Our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and prepare for us guidance in our affair. My Lord, descend [upon us] with a blessed descent, for You are the best of those who descend. My Lord, I seek refuge in You from the whisperings of devils, and I seek refuge in You, my Lord, from their attendance. My Lord, grant me authority and join me with the righteous, and make for me a tongue of truth among the later ones, and make me among the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss. Our Lord, upon You we have relied, and to You we return, and to You is the destination. Our Lord, do not make us a trial for those who have disbelieved, and forgive us, our Lord. Indeed, You are the Mighty, the Wise.

Mujīb al-Duʿāʾ (Acceptor of Prayer)

Nūr (Light)

Note: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Lord, how pleased You are with Your servant's repentance, whom Your power has drawn [to You]. You have made him taste the sweetness of Your forgiveness with absolution. Therefore, the servant gains boldness to sin and rejoices in sins, being immersed in desires. He is far from [understanding] the subtleties, being absorbed in the vicissitudes of grace and favors. He is the one who gathers all graces and perfections, attains illumination through the lights of guidance, achieves the perfection of the outward and the acquisition of the inward, and, having dived into the sea of Your mercy, stands in the field of Your presence—[he who] seeks the garment of knowledge from You, wears the crown of miracles, is adorned with excellent character, and whose spirits are filled with obedience. O Lord, I ask You for sincere repentance, so that through it I may be granted the first rank, and [unclear]. Grant me the attribute of the strivers, the annihilation of the worshippers, the persistence of the prostrators, the happiness of the believers, and the perfection of the complete, so that with Your angels my realms of love may be attained, and my subtleties may gain proximity to the observation of You, so that I may remain in Your mighty hand, continue to dive in the sea of Your mercies, and stand observing You. Indeed, You are the Merciful, the Compassionate, the Forgiving, the Knowing, the Granter of Requests, the Generous, the Acceptor of Prayer, the Ever-Living, the Helper, the Good, the Near, the Accepter, the Provider, and the Bestower. O our Lord, accept from us, for indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing. O our Lord, pour patience upon us, keep us firm-footed, and grant us victory over the disbelieving people. O our Lord, do not skew our hearts after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. O our Lord, we have wronged ourselves. If You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers. O our Lord, admit us to a place of truth and admit us out with truth, and ordain for me from Yourself authority and a helper. O our Lord, grant us mercy from Yourself and prepare for us guidance in our affair. O Lord, descend upon me a blessed descent, for You are the best of those who descend. O Lord, I seek refuge in You from the whisperings of devils, and from their coming to me. O Lord, grant me knowledge and action and join me with the righteous. Make for me a tongue of truth among the later ones, and make me among the inheritors of the Garden of Bliss. O Allah, we have placed our trust in You, we turn to You, and to You is the return. O Allah, Lord, do not make us a fitnah for the disbelievers; forgive us, for indeed You are the Mighty and the Wise.
