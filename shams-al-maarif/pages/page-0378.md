# Page 378 (English translation)
Blessed Verses

He said: "Do not fear; you have escaped from the wrongdoing people. Do not fear; indeed you are the uppermost. Indeed the messengers do not fear before Me." [One of] two men among those who fear said: "Enter upon them through the gate, and when you have entered it, then you are the victorious. And upon Allah rely, if you are believers." Come forth, O So-and-so son of So-and-so, as the preacher comes forth to the pulpit and the sultan [comes forth] to the army. I have bound the tongue of whoever disputes with him, and the tongue of every speaker—they shall not speak concerning the bearer of this book of mine except with good, or they shall be silent. [They are] deaf, dumb, blind, so they do not return. Deaf, dumb, blind, so they do not see. I have made the bearer of this book of mine victorious, supported over everyone, as Allah aided His prophet Muhammad—may the blessings of Allah be upon him and grant him peace—with the angels, and Jibril [Gabriel] at his right, and Mikail [Michael] at his left, and Israil [Israel] from behind him. And the names of Allah surround him. "Faces have become blackened, and faces have prostrated to the Living, the Self-Subsisting." What has blown, O what has blown, O what has blown, until it has wiped away darkness. "[Come forth] successful, victorious, supported by the One, the Unique, the Single, the Self-Sufficient, who did not beget nor was begotten, and there was none equal to Him."

O Moses, advance and do not fear; indeed you have escaped from the unjust people. Do not fear; indeed you are elevated. Indeed the messengers do not fear before Me. Two men among those who fear Allah—upon whom Allah bestowed favor—said: "Enter through the gate. When you have entered it, you have become the victors. Put your trust in Allah if you are believers." O So-and-so son of So-and-so, come forth as the orator comes forth to the pulpit and the king [comes forth] to the army. I have bound his tongue; the tongue of whoever disputes with him and [the tongue] of every speaker shall speak concerning the bearer of this book only with good, or they shall remain silent. They are deaf, mute, and blind—they shall not be able to see. I have made the bearer of this book aided and victorious over everyone, as Allah the Exalted aided His prophet Muhammad ﷺ with the angels, with Jibril at his right, Mikail at his left, and Israil at his back. The beautiful names of Allah encompass him, and many faces have bowed low before the Living and Self-Subsisting in the utmost unity. Come forth, O Allah's essence, who neither gave birth nor was born, and none is equal to Him.

The method for subjugating the amazing and strange love talisman

The amazing and strange talisman is a wondrous thing, which the scholars have kept hidden out of fear that it might fall into the hands of the ignorant, lest corruption occur in the world. This talisman will be [effective] in all endeavors. If you wish to subjugate someone, there will be a severe reckoning on the Day of Resurrection before Allah the Exalted. When you intend a lawful action for it, then take a little akkīr wood or white sandalwood and seed of cardamom, warm it thoroughly, and fumigate well. Begin writing on the first hour of Tuesday; all purposes will be solved. Its beginning is:

In the name of Allah, the Holy, the Pure, the Exalted above every evil [unclear]
