# Page 515 (English translation)
515

وَاللَّهُ مِنْ وَرَائِهِمْ مُحِيطٌ بَلْ هُوَ قُرْآنٌ مَجِيدٌ فِي لَوْحٍ مَحْفُوظٍ

Since in this verse the accidental letters are also eighteen and they are combined, and the letters of the Greatest Name are in the four pillars of the grain, and the remaining letters are in the grain of the celestial sphere. Consider all of them in the same way as we have considered the letters of the pronoun, as we have explained from the beginning. The indication of understanding is that its number and quality are of the humble type. Then we mentioned the path, then the dust, then the standing, and these are the nine letters which are the original numbers, and all the informations of valor emerge from them. Then the three names, which are a hundred times light among its letters, I have explained. Then I have gathered the other names, among which are "Original," "Merciful," "Forgiving." And indeed all the letters which are placed in the grain of the celestial sphere are the accidental letters and they unite the beginning and the end. And know that these names in the grain are Mahmud, Muhammad ﷺ, and Ibrahim and others—their details will be explained soon, and the details of the accidental letters are here, and as many names as have been placed from the grain of the celestial sphere. When you look at these initial letters, they are ten, and for this same reason we have placed the letter Ya after all of them, because below them are the letters of the Greatest Name, and it is the Name of the Essence. And when you investigate the verses, you will find them to be a summary of the Greatest Name. May God grant us and you the success of His obedience through the science of letters, and may He guide us upon His path.

The measurement of the circuit and revolution of the heavens:

The circumference of the celestial sphere from the equator is three hundred and sixty degrees, and each degree is twenty-five farsakhs, and a farsakh is three miles, and a mile is a thousand paces, and a pace is four cubits, and a cubit is twenty-four fingers, and a finger is equal to four hairs of a donkey's tail.

Knowing the climes is necessary—that the first clime is the clime of the heart, and the second clime is that of Saturn, and the foreigners are its east. The second clime belongs to Jupiter, and it is the clime of Mars, and the south is its scholars. The third clime is Shughaf, and it is the clime of Mars, and the foreigners are its princes. The fourth clime is of love, and it is the clime of the Sun, and the foreigners are its kings and rulers. The fifth clime is Egypt, and it is the clime of Venus, and the south has its poets. The sixth clime is of the rope, and it is the clime of Mercury, and the foreigners are its wise men and scribes. The seventh clime is of the heart, and it is the clime of the Moon, and the foreigners are its ministers.

The prophets are the owners of the gates of the climes:

Then know that each of these climes has a gate. So the gate of the first clime is Green Life, and it is the gate of Abraham, peace be upon him. And the second gate is Red Understanding, which is the gate of Aaron, peace be upon him. And the third gate is Green Power, which is the gate of Moses, peace be upon him. And the fifth gate is Green Mercy, which is the gate of Joseph, peace be upon him. And the fourth is Green Command, which is the gate of Jesus, peace be upon him. And the seventh is Red Action, and it is the gate of Adam, peace be upon him. So the key of the first gate is triangular in shape, and the key of the second is square in shape, and the key of the third is hexagonal, and the key of the fourth gate is octagonal, and the key of the seventh gate is hexagonal. Understand these gates well, because only he understands them who understands the mysteries of the unseen and is wise. O you who are concerned, know that the Creator of the world is true. He has taught you these mysteries, and revealed them for the good of the lights. So from the distinction of day and night it becomes clear to you that these changes of the stations and so on lay the stain of sorrow upon the field of life. So the one who gives news tells you of the outward secret, and you are in need of the outward stations, because every flowing line will pass away, and I too have become with my establishment. And my declaration is the Resurrection, and my declaration is the spirit, and my declaration is the subtleties, and my declaration is the indigo.

Peace.
