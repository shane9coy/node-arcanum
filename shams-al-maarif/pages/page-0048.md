# Page 48 (English translation)
# The Book of the Sun of Knowledges (Shams al-Ma'arif)

## — 28. AL-RISHĀ —

The twenty-eighth lunar mansion is al-Rishā. Its letter is *shīn*. When the Moon resides within it, there emanates from it a very noble and pure spirituality that assists in the pursuit of knowledge and the acceptance of prayers. Every virtuous deed flourishes within it.

### Mysteries and Properties of Letters

You have come to know what properties Almighty Allah has placed within the letters. From these same letters the Divine Discourse is composed. The praise of the Divine Names likewise proceeds from them. Therefore, that which exists within the inner reality of these letters is precisely that spirituality which descends from the lunar mansions. Thus, in the Holy Qur'an, those verses of mercy are, for those deserving mercy, angels of mercy—they are auspicious—while those verses of punishment are, for those deserving punishment, angels of punishment—they are inauspicious—and those verses containing promises and warnings are, as it were, mingled spiritualities. All these matters pertain exclusively to human beings, not to angels and others, because angels are pure goodness—that is, there is not even a particle of evil within them—nor are they subordinate to humans. Many human beings are also pure goodness, such as the believers, while many others possess a mixture of qualities, and several are pure evil, such as the disbelievers. Those of mixed quality are persons who perform both good and bad deeds. Regarding the sinful believers, Almighty Allah has stated:

**"And others who have acknowledged their sins, having mixed righteous deeds with wicked ones. Perhaps Allah will accept their repentance."**

[Note: Some handwritten corrections appear over the Urdu translation of the verse.]

### Spiritual Quality of Letters

These are the esoteric matters concerning letters. All cycles converge upon a single point, for the letter is composed of a point, though the method of formation differs. Thus, each lunar mansion, each spirituality, and each letter is gathered within a single point. If observed for forty days across several mansions, their influences and properties become manifest. The final letters are the letters of spirituality, and they are the repositories of felicity and inauspiciousness. Were there no distinction in properties between the letters and the cycles of the heavens, human beings would not be able to discern the causes of felicity and inauspiciousness or the causes of need, and all these branches have emanated from Adam's offspring alone.
