# Page 529 (English translation)
Chapter 34: Zā'iraj 'Istinkāq' 'Letter Proportions' 'Signs' 'Mansions' 'Balances'

Zā'iraj is of three kinds
Know that the science of zā'iraj has three types, one of which is called "borrowed subject matter" (mawḍūʿ mustaʿāra), and this is like the form of ghazal poetry and it is also called "center" (markaz). The second type is "rhymed subject matter" (mawḍūʿ muqaffā), and this is derived from equal rows and hexagonal numbers, and it is like rhymed poetry or rajaz or rhymed prose. The third type is "symbolic subject matter" (mawḍūʿ ramzī), which has different rules and answers appear in the form of poetry.

First rule, which is called "center," is as follows: First, reduce the name of the seeker as we have mentioned before. Then combine it with the letters of the question, which are called "preferences" (tarajiyyāt) and which will be mentioned later. Then combine the letters and count them all with numbers. Then according to the four balances (mawāzīn arbaʿa), the details of which will come later, make two more divisions, and keep the numbers that are yours as "gift." So if there is excess externally, then you have surplus; be grateful for it. If there is something else, continue until all numbers are exhausted and construct speech from all the letters. If any word is deficient, add to it from your own side. This is the lowest rank, and most of the scholars have acted upon this rule, such as Imam Muhammad (may God have mercy on him) and the Caliph in the time of Ma'mūn. And this rule is very easy for divination and contemplation.

Second rule:
The second rule is this: reduce the letters of need to the same and combine them with the letters of the pole (qutb). Then sum all those totals. Then divide the number, and from the outcome extract also by the hexagonal good division or by other extraction. Then divide the number by the four balances, which are arranged according to the calculation of temperaments, and keep the remainder as "support." Do the same in all operations.

Third rule:
Then there is another rule, which is this: transform the letters of need and the letters of the one having need, and extract them well with the letters of the pole name. Then drop them on the external temperaments, and where the letters are completed, that is the point of exit. Understand this, and be grateful for the balance based on two examples, and the letters must be preserved so that the extraction proceeds in accordance with poetry or prose. The form of this operation is this: collect all the letters of the name of need, the one having need, the day, the hour, the ascendant, the setting, the medium, the odd number, and the intermediate sign between these two, and the solar balance and the musical balance. Then apply to them the process that will be mentioned according to this rule: perform letter division, and write separately in one place the letters that have not been repeated; the name of these letters is "letters of grace" (ḥurūf faḍla). Multiply all of them together, and drop them on the external temperaments; wherever the letters fall, that is the point of exit. Begin preserving the letters from there, with whatever arrangement you wish (you may choose), the poetic form will emerge, and the easiest and longest is the best. If any letter is needed, add it from your own side; the answer will be sufficient.

And the gathering of all:
The first letters of the words mentioned above are as follows. When you wish to perform any operation, first combine the letters of the mansions in devotion, and perform the operation according to need; this form is worthy of implementation.
