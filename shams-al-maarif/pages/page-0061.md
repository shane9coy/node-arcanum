Chapter: Knowledge of the Foundations of the Lunar Mansions

Mansion of the Two Indicators (Sharā'iṭayn): It contains two separate stars. One is toward the south and the other toward the north. These two stars are the horns of Aries. Among the two, the brighter star is called Naṭaḥ. When they appear in the middle of the sky, they appear to be about ten hand-widths apart. There is also a small star that sometimes appears ahead of them.
Its shape is: [Two dots side-by-side: * *]

Mansion of the Belly (Baṭīn): It contains three stars that are small and dim. This is the belly of Aries. Thurayyā is its buttocks and the Two Indicators are its horns. Its shape is: [Three dots in a triangle: * * *]

Mansion of Thurayyā (Thurayyā): It contains seven stars, six of which are bright and one is very small. People use it to test the sharpness of their eyesight. Its name was derived from tharwat (wealth), meaning abundance of external and internal fortune. It has other names, among which Najm is also one. Some scholars say that the verse of the Glorious Quran "By the star when it descends" refers to this very Thurayyā, because Arabs mostly call even a few stars collectively Najm (star). The Messenger of God ﷺ named it Najm. It is related that when Najm rises, crops become safe from calamities and so on, and by Najm is meant Thurayyā, which is ahead of and superior to Sa'd and Muqaddam.
Its shape is: [A cluster of seven small dots/stars] One star called Kaff al-Khudhayyib is near Thurayyā, and two Sara'ātayn which are situated below the Two Indicators, and there is one red shining star called 'Uyūq behind which are three stars called the 'A'lām (standards). These are the subsidiaries of the Mansion of Thurayyā; since they are near Thurayyā, we have mentioned them.

Mansion of Aldebaran (Dubrān): This is also the buttocks of Aries. It is called Dubrān for this reason—that it turns its back toward Thurayyā. Some say it is five stars that are in the hive (hūdaj) of the bull; they call them "honey" (shahd). Its shape is: [A V-shaped pattern of five dots] Some say it is a red star. Its shape is: [One single dot/star] Al-Fanīq is also a name for it, because it is like a great mountain. Ahead of it are some small stars called Qalāṣ. All of these together appear like the head of a bull. This mansion is behind Thurayyā. [A cluster of small dots representing stars]

Mansion of the Veil (Haq'ah): In the Mansion of Haq'ah, three stars are close together. This is the head of the linear figure of Gemini, like three fingers joined together. Its shape is: [Three dots close together: * * *] It has also been said that this is the grain that is in the animal's side. When asked about the ruling concerning a man who divorced his wife with a number of divorces equal to the count of the stars of the sky, Ibn 'Abbās, may God be pleased with him, said: "The Haq'ah of Gemini suffices for him"—meaning three divorces have occurred.

Mansion of Hana'ah (Hana'ah): It contains five stars, two large ones and three small ones between them, and apparently they are two feet. Their shape is: [Five dots/stars in a slightly curved row] It was named Hana'ah because it has an influence of love; when it rises, every person becomes kind to his friend.

Mansion of the Forearm (Dhirā'): Some say this is the arm of Leo. They are two shining stars between which many small stars appear, like the claws of a lion. To the eye, there appears to be about one hand's width between these two stars. Among the two arms of Leo, one is extended (mabsūṭ) and the other is closed (maqbūḍah)—the arm that is not on the back of the arm. The rear arm is above the Mansion of Simāk. It is also called the Soaring One of the Winking Eye (Suhayl al-Ghumayṣā'), meaning such cotton from which the stain of antimony has been removed to such an extent that it was rolled but still could not be united with it. Some say it is called Dhirā' for this reason—that it is the forearm of Thurayyā. [Two dots/stars with faint surrounding marks]

Mansion of Nathrah: There is a slight distance between these two. It is the nose of the two nostrils in the form of pieces of cloud. It has also been said that these are the nostrils. [The text below is partially obscured]
