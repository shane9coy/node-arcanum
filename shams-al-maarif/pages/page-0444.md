# Page 444 (English translation)
One outer and one inner. Through inner blessing, knowledge of God the Most High descends, and through outer blessing, every difficulty becomes easy. The prayer of the name Al-Latif is:

"O Latif, You are the one who has shown subtlety to Your servants and connected them to the various types of bliss, and You accompany those veiled in curtains and deliver them from the vicissitudes of misfortune, and You have mercy on those seeking salvation unto You with Your all-encompassing mercy, and You draw them to the lights from the darknesses. You know the hidden things of all things and their subtleties, and You bestow Your goodness upon Your servants with all forms of righteousness and reveal their realities. I ask You, O Allah, by the subtlety of Your gentleness, and by the overflow of Your grace, and by one particle of the ocean of Your generosity, and by the strength of the authority of Your army and Your generosity, to make me pure in words and deeds, refined in state and wealth, and grant me from the blessing of Your subtlety a blessed portion, and help me to accept the effects of Your grace, and make for me from it an abundant and pure share, and support me with Your planning so that I may obtain from the ocean of Your generosity an overflowing abundance. Indeed, You are the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful."

Expansion of Sustenance and Deliverance from Distress

The name Al-Khabir contains one of the letters of the Greatest Name. This name has an extraordinary and potent effect for removing difficulty and tightness. Its trustee's name is Siyayil, who rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 812 ranks, and each rank has 812 trustees who are concerned with worldly life and provisions of sustenance such as rain and vegetation. Upon the reciter of this name, a trustee descends and transforms him into a person of outward and inward blessings. Through outward blessing, knowledge of hidden matters is obtained, and through inward blessing, the secrets of hearts and thoughts become known. The prayer of the name Al-Khabir is:

"O Knower, You are the one who informed Your close servants of what You kept secret of the mysteries of Your prophets' minds, so no swift news escapes You, nor any hidden effects, nor any protected states, nor does anything hidden from You run through the kingdom of Your dominion, nor does any particle move in the stillness of one who is still, nor does any mustard seed settle in the nature of one who moves, except that You know its apparent and its secret, its public and its beginning and its end. Yours is its good and for whoever You will through it. I ask You, O Allah, by the secret of Your omnipotence that descends in the hearts of the righteous and the good, and by the great strength of Your apparent power in the minds of the people of mysteries and lights, to make me by Your beautiful choice one who knows what flows in my heart and spirit from the various mysteries, and one who receives from the essence of Your lights. O He to whom is my return, and from You comes the unveiling of the prophets' wonders. O Lord of the Worlds, O Knower."

Acquisition of the Knowledge of Alchemy and Stones

The name Al-Halim contains one of the letters of the Greatest Name. Whoever seeks knowledge of alchemy and the Philosopher's Stone, there is specific excellence in this name for him. Its trustee's name is Iyayil. This one rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 812 ranks, and each rank has 812 appointed trustees whose task is the fulfillment of worldly blessings. The trustee also grants the reciter two blessings: one outward blessing through which he gains fame and respect among people, and another inward blessing through which he attains knowledge of the reality of knowledge.
