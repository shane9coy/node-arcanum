# Page 752 (English translation)

As we have mentioned, keep its image with yourself and maintain the continuity of dhikr, and know that all lights are obtained through the presence of the heart and repetition—merely performing dhikr once or twice will not produce the effect. The entire effect lies in repetition. And thus it is extremely swift in effect for obtaining the noble sciences. Every person makes it his habitual practice, and Allah the Most High opens the doors of blessing upon him, and He makes the people of knowledge and virtue subservient to him, and He honors him with the unveiling and secrets of these names.

And those six names are:
الْعَلِیْمُ الْحَکِیْمُ الْخَبِیْرُ الْمُبِیْنُ الْہَادِیْ عَلَّامُ الْغُیُوْبِ

And for their dhikr, the time of dawn is suitable. At this very time, the command of our Lord descends toward the heaven of the world, and He says: "Is there anyone who calls upon Me whose prayers I may accept? Is there anyone who asks forgiveness of Me whom I may forgive? And is there anyone else who asks of Me upon whom I may show mercy?" In the ninth hour of the night, which is attributed to the Moon, recite this dhikr.

This is a special supplication, extremely beneficial for the rectification of hearts and the understanding of sciences. Whoever begins it in the last third of the night and continues reciting until morning, and after sunrise, after finishing the prayer, busies himself with dhikr of Allah and seeking forgiveness—Allah the Most High will provide all means of good for him. And whoever writes this and keeps it with himself, the attributes of beauty and good fortune will become manifest upon him, and whoever asks Allah the Most High for knowledge and gnosis, it will be granted to him. And that supplication is:

Ilahi as'aluka bi-ismikal-maknun alladhi faṣṣalta bihi fawāṣila at-tafṣil fi al-mawjuda

All things You have made distinct in detail; in its praise You made the word of justice apparent, so languages diverged and names appeared, and actions confronted each other, and species became varied, and genera became mixed, and the heavens were ordered in ranks—each within its own orbit, they glorify You, and by the force of Your justice they are guided. Draw toward Yourself the darkness of my body with a gentle drawing, and pour upon the light of Your care a gentle expansion. You are the One who disposes freely, and I am the one who disposes bound—until I may receive from You what is in the realm of existence, a meaning from the meanings of Your knowledge, and through it find comfort in the strangeness of the world, a comfort that dispenses with every companion and saves me along with everything by which one intercedes among all the worlds—until the forms of existing things draw near to my heart with their sights humbled and their insights compelled toward that noble shore. And every existing thing before the presence of my witnessing keeps its meaning concealed, bound to Him by Your decree which cannot be repelled nor driven back. Surely You judge with truth and cannot be judged. O You who judge with truth, You are the Truth, and Your names are the Truth, and Your actions are the Truth, and Your knowledge is the Truth, and the interweaving of all things with Your knowledge is the Truth—and there is nothing except the Truth. So let the Truth become clear to me regarding the proportion of what I understand, until I know what I did not know. Surely You are the Knower of hidden things.

خَوَاصُ سُوْرَةِ وَالَّیْلِ [unclear] رَبِّ فَدَا تُجِیْنِیْ [unclear] الْمَلِکِ

And those works in which there is power and unveiling are accomplished, God willing, through this.
