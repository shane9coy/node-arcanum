[Heading in a decorative frame: The subjugation of the angel of al-'Azīz and obtaining honor]

The name al-'Azīz contains one letter from the letters of the Most Great Name. Whoever persists in the remembrance of this name will obtain honor. The name of its angel is Munyā Ṭayl. Under him there are four rulers, and under each ruler there are 94 squadrons, and in each squadron there are 94 symbols. All of these are under the influence of Gabriel, peace be upon him. Upon the mention of this name, the angel descends, and great honor is bestowed upon him from Allah the Most High. The prayer of al-'Azīz is this:

O You who are Mighty, You are the stable in Your eternal might, the responsive in Your right, the one who stands by the might of Your power for the people of knowledge and recognition. And You humble by Your force and sovereignty the people of humiliation and transgression. O You who are mighty, by the manifestation of every hidden thing in existence, whenever it may be, I ask You by the might of Your might and the majesty of Your glory and the expansion of Your tenderness, and by the blessing of Your goodness in Your signs and Your sovereignty which has no resemblance nor similitude nor counterpart, and by Your gathering light, the radiant, the precious, that You make me beloved to You, and by Your obedience make me one who sees clearly for every [seeker], and by the company of Your saints make me one honored and honored by Your teaching. O You before whom minds have become bewildered from comprehending the majesty of His greatness, and tongues have become weary from fully praising the light of His mercy, and phantasms have departed from the limitations of the essence of His existence, and hearts have become troubled from the epiphanies of His beauty and majesty—grant me the vision of the good that You have deposited in the eastern and western parts of the earth upon the jewels of their realities and the treasures of their knowledge, and single me out for the acceptance of Your light and the majesty of Your glory, for indeed You are Allah, the Strong, the Effecter, the Great, the Exalted. O Mighty one.

[Heading in a decorative frame: The subjugation of the angel of al-Jabbār—protection from calamities and afflictions]

The name al-Jabbār is a great name. Upon the mention of this name, no one can compel [the practitioner] by force, and no affliction can ever reach him. Its remembrance is tried and proven for kings and rulers, for no other ruler can overcome whoever performs its remembrance, however powerful he may be. The name of its angel is Ṣūfā'īl. Under him there are four rulers, and each ruler oversees 206 squadrons, and in each squadron there are 206 symbols. These are under the command of Isrāfīl. Upon the mention of this name, the angel descends and fulfills his needs. Fortunate is the person who recites it day and night. The prayer of al-Jabbār is this:

O Jabbār, You are the one who repairs the broken and takes vengeance upon every stubborn tyrant among all the tyrants, the Able one in all the dominions. [Bottom of page damaged/illegible]
