You wrote it before all things while it was still a speck, and when you called upon the opening of the Clear Book, to make for us from every distress an opening and from every worry an exit: O One who brings relief to the distressed, O Exalted in Rank, O Best of Places of Refuge, O Most Mighty Place of Resort, O Generous in Forgiveness and Generosity, O Provider of sustenance to the crawling creatures in solid stone, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, and may Allah bless our Master Muhammad and his Family and grant them abundant peace.

In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I seek forgiveness from You and I repent to You. I have repented to You for every sin. Then I return to You. I seek forgiveness for every deed that I performed with the intention of Your essence, then I associated others with You in it. I repent for every sin that I committed in the darkness of night and in the day. I have humbled myself before Allah the Almighty, but as a humiliated and defeated servant. I have brought faith in Allah, who is the Lord of Forgiveness and Appreciation. I am pleased with my Prophet, upon whom may Allah the Almighty send blessings and peace, who is Your beloved, Your chosen, and the best among Your creation. They are Muhammad, upon whom be blessings and peace, the messenger to all. There is no deity except Allah. He is the truth upon whom the rights are established in the revealed books. All praise belongs to Allah. Gratitude for Allah's blessings is obligatory, and the reward for His bounty is that He is thanked. Allah is the Greatest. Glory belongs to Allah, and the authority belongs to the One for whom the obligation of Allah's forbearance and His greatness is binding. All are thankful, and every sin is forgiven. Glory belongs to Allah's essence; He is pure morning and evening, and the authority and power belong to Allah alone. He is the Elevated, the Forbearing. The acknowledgment of power is with Allah. If He wills, He is the Thankful One. O Allah, we are people of Your heart, turning to You, to Your hidden treasure of the Book, the blessing of faith. By Your names, the realities of Your glory, and by this name of Yours, everything in Your earth and Your heaven was established. Indeed, You are Allah the Self-Sufficient, who neither begets nor is begotten, and there is none comparable to Him. O Allah, I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, the leader of the prophets and messengers, peace be upon them, whom Allah the Almighty created before all things. He was a pearl, or rather a light placed within his chest. That You may change every difficulty of ours into ease. O Dispeller of hardships, remove every pain and grief from us. O Ocean of the highest ranks, who gave refuge and gave honor to those who take vengeance. O Generous One of forgiveness and abundance, O Provider of sustenance to the worms in solid stone, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds, and may Allah send abundant blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, upon whom be blessings and peace, and upon his family and his companions, may Allah be pleased with them.

**A Proven Prayer for the Opening of Sustenance**

And this prayer is extremely effective for seeking sustenance. Its method of reading is as follows: read Surah al-Waqi'ah once, then read this prayer:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You, O Allah, O Allah, O Existing, O One, O Odd, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Extender, O Self-Sufficient, O Enricher, by a delicate subtle grace, by a fragmenting breaker, of radiant splendid beauty, O Allah, to whom belong greatness and majesty, a fragmenter, and its manifestation, and the beauty of the revealed, O radiant, by the Exalted One, O humbler, O fragmenter, the Allah who has subjugated by the light of every luminary, O fragmenter, O Fragmenter, answer, O servants of the beauty of the great and supreme Allah, by the subjugation of the hearts of creation and the bringing of sustenance. There is no fear—may You grant salvation—the permanent love established by the light of the consumed face, the veil for the strength and essence of the necks, for the great one.
