# Page 782 (English translation)
780

Poetry

And in the inscriptions of temples that have been recorded:
Here am I, concerning the secret of eloquent speech.
Read my book, which has through you made clear a witness
That guides you from it through the knowledge of a scholar you know.

And at times this title is written upon "discovery" and "manifestation." Know that this book of mine is such that falsehood does not pass through it, neither from before nor from behind—just as the Exalted Lord says:
*"Falsehood does not approach it from before it nor from behind it; a revelation from one wise, praiseworthy."*
Yes, whatever matter is in it, behold and take it, for this matter is exactly as you have seen it. I swear by God, I have made it manifest for you therein and have left nothing to keep you waiting. So if you deny these matters, know that the Lord of the Ka'ba is its guardian. It is fitting for you to become one of the discerning, for "God is his helper" who is a sincere discerning person, and "understanding is his witness." And there is extreme regret and sorrow upon this sincere one who lies sleeping in the slumber of negligence, day and night, and has fallen behind his compatible companions. His loss and regret are evident, and his name is removed from the register of the intimate ones. May God keep us and you safe from disgrace, humiliation, and expulsion. Indeed, He is the gracious, generous, and merciful-doing One, and the requiter of good deeds. I pray to God that He grants those truth-seekers the success to open the doors of our mercies and to lift the curtains of our hearts, and whatever we have written is sufficient for the one whom God gives success.

*There is no power and no strength except through God, the Most High, the Most Great. And may God bless our master Muhammad, the seal of the prophets and the leader of the messengers, and upon his family and all his companions, and the followers until the Day of Judgment, whenever the rememberers remember Him and the negligent are heedless of His remembrance. And praise belongs to God, Lord of the worlds.*

All thanks are due to God for this.
O noble, successful, merciful One—a thousand thousand thanks, for today, on the date of 9 Ramadan Sharif at 2 a.m., on the night of Friday, 1423 AH, corresponding to November 15, 2002 CE, the translation of this book was completed.

*Praise belongs to God therefor.*
Muhammad Awal Rasuli

*Finis.*
