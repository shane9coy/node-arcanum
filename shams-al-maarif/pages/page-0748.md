**Veneration:**

The sins of the nafs are: remaining established in the realm of desire, having attachment to things of comfort and pleasure, and the repentance for these is to completely sever worldly ties, following the hadith "Poverty is my pride," meaning the fire of contentment, and to become God-fearing. The sins that are worse than these are: pursuing miracles and being self-sufficient in various forms of communion with the river.

**Seventy Sages Asked Moses, Upon Him Be Peace, How God Grants Forgiveness:**

It is related that seventy sages came to Moses, upon him be peace, and asked him about God's forgiveness. He said, "I only know what my Lord has taught me." Then when Gabriel came to him, he asked him about it. Gabriel went up to heaven and said, "O Lord, Moses has asked: What is divine forgiveness?" He said, "O Gabriel, God's forgiveness is this: that a servant sins, then repents, then sins, then repents—My command regarding this servant is that I forgive him, and for each sin I give him a virtue in return."

**Definition of Repentance:**

Whoever among the people has understood repentance has indeed repented. Know that repentance emerges from every bad character trait and enters into every good character trait. The good character trait is only that which the Lawgiver, upon him be peace, declared good. This repentance sometimes occurs through removal from the sins of the heart without any admonishment, because God Almighty Himself guides him toward Himself, and this person becomes immersed in the sea of obedience and worship. Through this name, drawing near to God is attained such that poverty and wealth, in every state, the servant remains patient. There is no particular invocation for this name, but it is extremely beneficial for those who have lost a child or suffered personal loss—to write it and make it (a talisman). God will rectify his condition and make difficult tasks easy for him, God willing.
