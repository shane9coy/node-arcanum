# Page 115 (English translation)
Then elsewhere it is declared: "Beautified for mankind is the love of desires" [Quran 3:14]. The meaning of "h" [the letter shin] relates to obtaining adornment from the fruits of trees. The letter shin indicates honey (shahd) and desires (shahawat). Allah the Exalted says:
"Allah bears witness that there is no deity except Him, and [so do] the angels and those of knowledge—" [Quran 3:18]
Mahashadat is what is called visual witnessing. Regarding the witnesses (shuhada'), it is declared: "They are alive, obtaining provision from their Lord" [Quran 3:169]. And it also indicates drinking (shirb). Allah the Exalted says: "They will drink from a cup whose mixture is camphor" [Quran 76:5]. Then He says: "A spring therein, named Salsabil" [Quran 76:18]. And the letter shin also indicates healing (shifa'). Allah the Exalted says: "And We send down from the Quran what is healing and mercy for the believers" [Quran 17:82]. The Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said: "The healing of my nation is in three things: a verse from the Book of Allah, or a lick of honey, or a cupping session." And healing (shifa') indicates all that is good, and all that is good is innocence. Allah the Exalted says: "Upon it they purify themselves" [Quran 47:15]. Then He says: "So they became apparent" [Quran 47:15]. And the letter "ta" indicates the desirable (maghdub) — from this comes the name Zahir. The letter "ta" indicates innate nature (fitra) and manifestation (zuhur). Allah the Exalted has said: "The fitra of Allah upon which He has created mankind" [Quran 30:30]. And He says: "Originator of the heavens and the earth" [Quran 6:14].
Then there is the divine declaration: "Do you see any flaw?" [Quran 67:3]. Allah the Exalted says: "Fruit and palm trees and pomegranates" [Quran 55:68]. "Fa" will have fruit; they will be with their spouses in shade. Then He says: "And fruit of whatever kind you select" [Quran 56:20].
"Ta", "Za", and "Jim" are barred letters and possess a watery temperament, and the temperament of the moon is all that is good, and Junaid's temperament is supremacy. The letter "Zay", "Shin" are cold, dry, earthy, and watery in temperament. The letter "Dad" is moist. The letter "Ta" is hot, dry, fiery in temperament. Its stars are the Sun and Moon. These have seven names. The first is Thabit, which writes the destinies of servants. Then the wicked jinn are: "Wali, Zahir, Fard, Shahid" [Quran - various verses]. And the letter "Ta" has appeared in the names of Allah the Exalted as Ba'ith (Resurrector) and Warith (Inheritor). He is in every particle of the world, and the allusion continues to the meaning that is in the name of Allah the Exalted, al-Warith. In the alphabet letters, there is no letter with three diacritical marks except these two letters: "Ta" and Shin, because Shin encompasses all others besides itself. "Ta" has pervaded everything, and its special property depends specifically on all the lower world of bodies. It is a cold, dry letter. It has the temperament of earth and mountains. The letter "Ta" is dry, in which the letter of heat intervenes. This is the fifth degree of heat. Its form is concealed in the letter Alif. Its names are Qa'il and Fatir. The letter Shin is cold. Its secret is the head-shin, and among its virtues is that in the alphabet letters there is no letter that has three marks and three dots except the letter Shin, and Shin is the جمع of Dhāt Raḥmah Ahlā and Ishrat. Shin is placed in "Allah bears witness" (shahida Allah), in which there are three glories: the good tidings of angels, the good tidings of the people of knowledge, and the good tidings of those besides the people of knowledge.

All the lights of divine unity (tawhid) are gathered in the Throne (Arsh), regarding which the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said that when anyone pronounces the word "La ilaha illallah" (There is no deity except Allah), this word goes to the Throne, and the Throne trembles from it. Allah the Exalted says from the Throne: "Remain established." It responds: "Forgive the one who uttered this word."
The proof of this is that Allah the Exalted desired to be accessible through the intention of the servant. He could not be revealed to their intellects. Therefore, He created the Throne for the servants and attributed it to His lordship: "The Most Merciful rose over the Throne" [Quran 20:5]. Among the servants, the Throne is like the royal courts among kings, and there are means to reach the king, but despite this, there are forms of lights and temples of glory and majesty. Do you not know that the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, said that Allah the Exalted wrote a book and placed it upon the Throne...
