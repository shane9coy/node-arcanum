[Heading in a decorative frame: A great chapter, the properties of Bismillah and Surah Fatiha, healing diseases from the Holy Quran]

May Allah the Most High grant us the success to obey Him. Know that every person who understands the secret of the verse "And We send down from the Quran what is a healing" has understood that it provides relief for physical diseases outwardly, and he knows that it is also a healing for heart diseases. Accordingly, regarding this very matter, the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "There is healing in three things: in the verses of the Holy Quran, or in cupping, or in drinking honey." The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: "The Holy Quran is a pearl; understand that Allah the Most High has placed what treasures and wonders are within the coverings of its letters, and in its deep ocean." Some among the mystics have said: "Bismillah is to you like 'Kul' (Say)." Imam Hasan relates: "Whoever chooses to write Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim in an excellent manner out of reverence for Allah the Most High, will enter Paradise." It is narrated from Abu Sa'id al-Khudri that he said: "I heard from Ibn Abbas that he said: 'Everything has a foundation, and the foundation of all books is the Holy Quran. The foundation of the Quran is Surah Fatiha, and the foundation of Fatiha is Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim. When you are sick, take the foundation; you will find relief. In it is healing for every illness.'"

Whoever recites Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim 786 times upon any talisman and breathes upon it, that talisman will be immediately invalidated. Whoever recites it this number of times and then makes a supplication, it will be fulfilled. Some scholars have said: "Whoever recites Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim 150 times every day, Allah the Most High will make him knowledgeable about the mysteries of knowledge and hidden realities." Understand this: whoever mentions Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim frequently, will be granted dignity in the higher and lower worlds. In it, Allah the Most High has placed His mysteries; in it is the Greatest Name. This is the thing that the Upper Pen first wrote upon the Tablet. Through it, the kingdom of Solomon, upon him be peace, was established. Through it, the tree of the world is sustained, and all its mysteries are manifested therein. Whoever writes Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim in this manner and keeps it with him, will extinguish fire. Whoever writes it on a piece of paper and binds it upon the place of pain from headache or migraine, will find immediate relief.

It is narrated from Abdullah ibn Umar that whoever has a need should fast on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday, and on Friday perform ghusl (ritual bath) and go to the mosque, give some charity on the way, then after finishing the prayer recite this supplication:

"O Allah, I ask You by Your Greatest Name, by Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, besides whom there is no god, the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, He is the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, I ask You by Your Name with which You filled the heavens and the earth, and I ask You by Your Name, Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, before which faces prostrate and voices bow in awe, and hearts tremble in fear of Him, that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad and upon his family and companions, and grant peace, and that You fulfill my need—"

Mention your need by name and it will be fulfilled at that very moment. Abdullah ibn Umar used to say: "Do not teach this supplication to the ignorant, otherwise they will curse one another and it will be accepted."

From the letters of Bismillah ar-Rahman ar-Rahim, 19 Names are formed corresponding to its letters; they are the noble names:

Allah, ar-Rahman, ar-Rahim, ar-Rabb, as-Salam, al-Mu'min, al-Muhaymin, as-Sattar, al-Hayy, al-Muhyi, al-'Alim, al-Hannan, al-Mannan, al-Hayy, al-Qayyum, al-Hadi, as-Subbuh, al-Quddus, al-Hasib

These great Names should be placed (inscribed) on Wednesday morning, then ask Allah the Most High and He will give it to you. Among these Names is the Greatest Name. Keep its practice hidden so that no harm befalls anyone.
