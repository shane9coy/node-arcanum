Chapter: The best practice to avoid every fear and harm

Here the names are mentioned which were written on the staff of Moses, peace be upon him, by virtue of which all wonders and marvels appear. These names should be written on a deer's membrane with the influence of the Sun at its highest or Jupiter at its highest, musk water or rose water, dhatura water [unclear] rose water and saffron. While writing, fine perfume should be burning. Then take a staff and hollow it out and place these names inside it. Then seal it with wax. Then when there is a battle in any direction, or thieves and robbers have surrounded you, or you have encountered a wild harmful animal, then strike the staff on the ground three times and recite this prayer:

**اَللّٰھُمَّ اِنِّیْ اَسْئَلُکَ بِبَرْکَۃِ ھٰذِہِ الْاَسْمَاء الۡعَظِیْمَۃِ الَّتِیْ کَانَتْ عَلٰی عَصَا مُوْسٰی ابْنِ عِمْرَانَ عَلَیْہِ السَّلَامُ وَ ضَرَبَ بِہِ الْبَحْرَ فَانْفَلَقَ فَکَانَ کُلُّ فِرْقٍ کَالطَّوْدِ الْعَظِیْمِ اَنْ تَحْبِسَ عَنَّا مَا ھٰھُنَا**

O Allah, I ask You by the blessing of these great names which were upon the staff of Moses, son of Imran, peace be upon him, and with which he struck the sea, and it split, and each section became like a great wave, that You may withhold from us what is feared here.

O Allah, I ask You by the blessing of these great names which were upon the staff of Moses, son of Imran, peace be upon him, and with which he struck the sea, and it split, and became like great mountain pieces, that You may withhold from us—mention the name of the thing which is feared—and at the end of the speech say: **وَ قِفُوْھُمْ اِنَّھُمْ مَّسْئُوْلُوْنَ** "And stop them, for they shall be questioned." The practice of walking on water and flying in the air

When Sheikh Abu Abdullah Hannī passed away and the one washing him removed his clothes to wash him, a letter was found among them. I opened it and looked at it, and in it were these noble names, and this text was written: **عجلہ لَا حَوْلَ وَ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰہِ الْعَلِیِّ الْعَظِیْمِ وَ صَلَّی اللّٰہُ عَلٰی سَیِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَّ آلِہِ وَ صَحْبِہِ وَ سَلَّمَ** "Hasten—there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great—and may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, his companions, and grant them peace." I used to often request these names from him during his lifetime, asking him to give them to me, and he would say: "Ahmed, do not worry. These names will come to you without difficulty." Now when I saw this letter, I reflected deeply and said in my heart: "This is the karamah (saintly miracle) of the friends of Allah the Most High—that after his passing, they speak to me through the tongue of their spiritual state, saying: 'Here, these names have come to you without difficulty.' I had been searching for them for a long time. Now see, this was written in it: 'O my brother, whoever has this blessed letter in his hand, in which are the great names—by virtue of those who carry them, they will be protected, and all their needs will descend by the grace of Allah along with Islam.
