# Page 762 (English translation)
This mention is extremely beneficial for beginners, because through it they are granted understanding to comprehend difficult meanings, and among the Divine Names, these names are appropriate for it: **Al-'Alim Ash-Shahid Al-Muhsi Al-Hakim** (The Knowing, The Witness, The Reckoner, The Wise). Whoever recites these names will be granted understanding of those things that he could not comprehend, and he will be granted understanding of that which he did not know. These names illuminate the heart and, by their means, dispel anxiety and fear—so that in their solitude they find comfort and within their inner selves they gain strength. And this supplication is most great in benefits.

**Allahumma ya man bashara nisbat al-'ulum ila 'ilmihi nisbatayn la shay' ila shay'in la yatanahi azharta al-huruf bi'l-qalami fa-kana laha tasrifun fi alwah al-mulkut fa-qama laha maqam makharij al-huruf min al-khalqi wa al-sadri wa al-lahati wa al-lisani fa-kullu ismin sadara 'anhu jinsun la bi-qalamm tarkibihi siwa minaka fa-laka wa kullu naw'in sadara 'anhu murakkabun fa-nawwahu israil azhartahu bi-quwwatin fi ahadi kulliyyatihi min juz'iyyat taraqibihi as'aluka bi haza al-sirri al-khafi alladhi waqafa ahlu al-'aqluna dahu wa taqaddama ilayka al-birru bi-birri aw da'watin fihi ya muhayminu yawma imkani wujudihi as'aluka kashf hijabi al-ghaybi hatta ughayyina al-lahaba bima fihi bi-maqamihi hatta al-ruhu al-baqi ya hayyu ya ya huwa ya anta ya baqi ya bari'u ya musawwir anta huwa**

*The mention of five names:*

And among the Divine Names, these names are appropriate for this supplication, and these names consist of five recitations, which the people of the path recite in various ways, and their effect is that they alert the heedless and gladden the hearts of the heedless. They encourage beginners. For those of guidance, mysteries are revealed through them. In short, each person receives benefits according to their capacity and worthiness. And if they are engraved upon their appropriate receptacle and then washed, or kept nearby, and the practice of dhikr is maintained, great knowledge is obtained. However, in every practice, abstinence from prohibitions, purity and piety, consumption of halal, and presence of heart are essential. And being pleased with Divine Majesty, and considering oneself as a humble, helpless servant in His presence, takes precedence.
