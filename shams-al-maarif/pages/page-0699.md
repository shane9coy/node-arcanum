# Page 699 (English translation)
Mention, this is its dhikr:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the one who sends forth toward liberation in all circumstances, or I found all things from the delicate ease of flowing water, and You sent every spirit to its body by Your mighty, overpowering command. You made known through the subtlety of spirits in dense bodies what You chose from corruption and righteousness. When the effusion of every subtle reality becomes complete and reaches its limit, You will prepare every resurrection and rising. You will send forth from the graves those stations for the attainment of what the secrets of hearts contained, due to what has preceded from the flowing of the Pen upon the Preserved Tablet, hidden and concealed. I ask You by the secrets of this great resurrection and what is within it of the hidden mysteries of the ancient command, that You send forth to me from the secrets of Your gentleness what will repel from me the weight of Your trials, and what will obligate for me the hidden mercies of Your mercy and the sleep of Your protection, from the delicacies of Your mercy... and purify my heart with the description of Your divinity, so that it may gaze upon the essences of the secret of Your life and mercy, O Allah, O Sender.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Shahīd"
[End border]

Shahīd also reverts to 'Alīm (the All-Knowing), because within knowledge there is a knower of both the hidden and the witnessed. The hidden refers to the interior, and the witnessed refers to the apparent. One should maintain continuity with this name in seclusion with complete lawful [means], and after each prayer, recite it for forty days in a number equal to this count. Then every supplicant who calls will be present, the inner secrets will be revealed, and the veil will be lifted from the reciter. Spiritual people will appear to him in dreams and waking. This is its dhikr:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O The Witness over every particle, by what You have made manifest in the world of the hidden and the witnessed, by what has flowed by the Pen of specification upon the pages of the Preserved Tablet. Your witnessing over every particle in all existing things, and by Your power over existing things, and by what has preceded in the knowledge of the hidden of wretchedness and felicity, and by what has preceded in the hidden knowledge—make me witness by Your bounty the specification of the stations which are the stations of the witnesses. And make me a witness to that, and verify me by the realities of known things, O Allah, O Witness over every soul for what it has earned, O Allah, O Witness.

Blessings in wealth and sustenance, the chest is illuminated by the light of the interior:
Whoever has adopted continuity in the recitation of this dhikr, Allah the Exalted facilitates for him matters of good and grants blessings in his wealth and sustenance, and He opens his chest by the light of the interior. And Allah knows best!
