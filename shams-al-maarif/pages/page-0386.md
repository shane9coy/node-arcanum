# Page 386 (English translation)
(Chapter: The Return of the Fleeing Person)

This is for the return of a person who has fled, even if he is imprisoned in chains. By the blessing of this operation, he will escape from there as well. Perform this ritual in the following manner: if it is a man, take a shirt or a tunic or a piece of cloth; if it is a woman, take a veil or a tunic. Insert a needle between the thighs and tie it to the leg. When the horse turns around the needle, summon the person who has fled—in the same way, the fugitive will turn back and return to the place where this talisman is hanging. Allah the Most High will create a means for this prisoner's release; it will be by the blessing of this talisman. The form of the talisman is as follows:

[A talisman featuring three concentric circles.
Inner circle text: "لوحا / فلان بن فلانہ / لهذا / القول".
Between inner and middle circles: Names of four angels: جبرائیل (Jibrail), میکائیل (Mikail), اسرافیل (Israfil), عزرائیل (Azrail), interspersed with other Arabic script.
Outer ring and surrounding: Various Arabic words and phrases radiating outwards in eight lines. The names 'طه' (Ta-Ha) and 'يس' (Ya-Sin) are visible within the outer ring. Clusters of script, including "يا بدوح", are arranged around the outer circumference.]

(Operation for Lulling a Sick Person or a Crying Child to Sleep)

When you wish to lull to sleep a sick person or one who is in severe pain, write these names and place them beneath a pillow or cushion; at that moment, sleep will come upon him. He will not wake as long as you do not remove these names from beneath the pillow or cushion. This also benefits children who cry excessively. The names are:

وَلَبِثُوا فِي كَهْفِهِمْ ثَلَاثَ مِائَةٍ سِنِينَ وَازْدَادُوا تِسْعًا وَتَحْسَبُهُمْ أَيْقَاظًا وَهُمْ رُقُودٌ وَنُقَلِّبُهُمْ ذَاتَ الْيَمِينِ وَذَاتَ الشِّمَالِ هَلْ تُحِسُّ مِنْهُمْ مِنْ أَحَدٍ أَوْ تَسْمَعُ لَهُمْ رِكْزًا

"And they stayed in their cave three hundred years, plus nine. You would think them awake, though they were asleep. And We turned them about, to the right and to the left. Do you perceive from among them any one, or do you hear from them a sound?"
