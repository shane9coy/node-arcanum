# Page 480 (English translation)
**Letter Dhad:**
Dhad is voiced and dry in temperament. Its actions related to God are similar to its prayer. These are its specific numbers.
**Properties and Benefits:** The number of times it should be written is only 18. If it is written on goat's hide and placed in a house, that house will collapse. Those dwelling in it will become distressed and bewildered. If there is a very high official, he will be deposed.
**For Destruction:**
If you want to destroy someone, combine their name with dhad and bury it at such a distance from the fire that it does not burn but remains affected by the heat. Wounds will appear on the body of the intended victim. After a few days, he will die.
**The Spirit Mahfoor Will Appear:**
If you transfer its letter-numbers into a talismanic composition and write on panther skin and place it around a child's neck, he will never be afraid. If someone dissolves 18 dhads (ض) in walnut or red date pulp and writes on a glass vessel, write these letters in the form of a madman. Write the name of the fleeing spirit Mahfoor in the middle—he will appear very quickly. Its prayer is the same as written in the section on Sad.
**Letter Ta:**
Ta is a voiceless, hot, dry letter. Its lunar form has very many numbers.
**Properties and Benefits:** It is used for killing, pillaging, and destroying bandits. If you engrave it on red copper on Monday during the first hour and engrave the form of Mars on the other side, and throw it into a well, its water will dry up. The form is this:

**For Punishing the Tyrant:**
If you want to kill a tyrannical transgressor, make an image of that man inside it, write "ța" on the heart. Then on an iron knife whose handle is also iron, write the letter "ța" 18 times in one line. Do this on Tuesday during Mars's hour. Drive this knife into the place of the heart where ța was written. That man will die. Recite the prayer that is in the section on Sad. Its name is evident.
**Letter Za:**
Za is a voiceless, airy letter. It completely eliminates poison.
