# Page 357 (English translation)
355

Intercede for me and have mercy on me with Your mercy that encompasses all things, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy.
And I ask You, O Allah, by the right of this noble verse and the great Names, to send blessings upon Muhammad and upon his family and companions, and to grant peace, and to give me my request and what I have asked of You, O Lord of the worlds.

Allah—there is no deity except Him. He is the Unique, characterized by permanence and eternity. The essences of created beings do not persist with His essence and attributes, nor does their reality persist with Him. Nor do their attributes, Names, and actions persist with His attributes, Names, and actions—and there is nothing besides Him. In truth, His beauty and glory exist in their perfection, eternally. He is the Living, the Sustainer, eternally upon His throne, with the permanence of His kingdom. All creation is obedient and submissive to the perfection of His knowledge. They know that He is unique in His kingdom, and in the unveiling of their intellects He is one in the elevation of His eternal glory; and through the unveiling of His manifestation, all of them turn to the reality of His knowledge. They know that He is the Creator, the Provider, the One who gives life and causes death, and all affairs return to Him. But the gnostics and researchers were bewildered by the reality of knowing Him.

They dove into the waves of His ocean of power. They acknowledged their helplessness and were drowned in knowing Him. Then they dove into His ocean of the heavenly realm. They came to know and verified that there is no deity except Him—this is evidence that He is the Living, the Sustainer. So He revived their hearts, illuminated their eyes and hearts. They observed Him in the universe—there is no deity except Him, and He alone is the Lord. They acknowledged this for Him with humility. He is not overtaken by drowsiness nor sleep. No languor comes upon Him from the unveiling of knowledge, nor does sleep come from the comprehension of knowledge. His command, when He intends to create something, is that He says "Be" and it becomes. His sacred essence is pure. In His hand of power lies the ownership of all things, and all beings shall return to Him. His essence is free from incarnation, change, unity, essential union, extreme connection, and separation. Nothing is like unto Him. He is more perfect than all things, and the return of all creatures and their being obedient is unto Him. He is unique in His kingdom with the People of Permanence and unique in His essence. The perfection and mysteries of souls are His alone—whatever is in the heavens and the earth. The entire universe is His manifestation and is knowing through His knowledge. Indeed, He is the Lord of the earths and the heavens. Who is there that intercedes with Him except by His permission? Whatever is in the heavens and the earth belongs to Him. Every thing declares His glorification and sanctification, but you cannot understand their glorification. Every possessor of speech speaks by His command, and the speaker speaks by His permission. He is the Knower of every thing. He is independent of every thing. Every thing is in need of Him and is helpless before Him. Before Him every thing is humiliated. He knows what is before them and behind them. Your sacred essence is pure. We have no knowledge except what You have taught us. Indeed, You are the Knower, the Wise. He knows what is in the dry and the moist, and no leaf falls but He knows it. No grain is in the darknesses of the earth, nor any wet or dry thing, but it is in a clear Register. They cannot encompass anything of His knowledge except what He wills. His knowledge encompasses every thing. Allah encompasses them—rather, it is in the Preserved Tablet, the Noble Quran. His power encompasses the heavenly kingdom. So all shall return to Him. O our Lord, You have encompassed every thing with mercy and knowledge. Our intellects have been dissolved. They have vanished, and all souls within their bodies have become bewildered. They became intoxicated by the light of His radiance, and they drowned in the sea of wonder. For the awareness of the unveiling of forms in their visions, intellects were lost, and behind the veils of intellects is the manifestation of the knowledge of the glory of Allah—which manifests with the manifestation of the one whose manifest is the manifest of power and whose hidden is the hidden of the command.
