# Page 412 (English translation)
410

And by the right of Bismillāh al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm, Ya Sin, and the Wise Quran, the manifest.¹

I adjure you, O assembly of spiritual beings, by the glory of Allah and His Messenger, and by the light of the Face of Allah, and by the right of the Names of Allah, by the right of Al-Ḥamdulillāh Rabb al-'Ālamīn. O Living, O Sustainer, O Sufficient, O Healer, O Guide, O Gentle, O Everlasting—answer, O Rūqīyāʾīl, and you, O al-Mudhahab, answer obediently, by the right of Al-Ḥamdulillāh Rabb al-'Ālamīn, and by the right of the Living Sustainer, and by the right of the King who overwhelms you with His command, ABJAD, and by the right of ḤNṬṬQL,² and say, "The truth has come and falsehood has vanished"—the final verse of Ta²

I adjure you, O Rūqīyāʾīl, and the King al-Mudhahab, by the right of the worshipped King. Glory be to the One who releases every debtor, glory be to the One who liberates every prisoner, glory be to the One who relieves every grieving one, glory be to the One who made water flow in seas and springs, glory be to the One whose treasures are between Kāf and Nūn,³ glory be to the One who, when He intends a thing, merely says to it "Be," and it becomes—the final verse of Ti²

O Allah, subjugate for me the King Rūqīyāʾīl, as You subjugated the sea for Moses, peace be upon him, and the fire for Abraham, and the mountains for David, and the wind, the jinn, and humanity for Solomon, and the sun, the moon, the stars, and all things for our master and prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. I ask You to subjugate for me the King Rūqīyāʾīl, that he may fulfill my need, by the right of Your Great and Most Great Name, and by the right of Your Most Beautiful Names. O Allah, O Swift, O Near, O Responsive, O Extender, O Loving, O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills—I ask You by the light of Your Face that filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your power with which You have power over all of creation, and by Your mercy that encompassed everything, O Resort of the supplicants, help me,³ times, and assist me in this work of mine in this hour, and fulfill my need, O Allah, O Resort of the supplicants, help me, O Compassionate, O Merciful, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, subjugate for me the King Rūqīyāʾīl—he created them with love, as Allah's love, and those who believe have a more intense love for Allah; He loves them and they love Him. No! Do not obey him, and prostrate and draw near.⁴

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, O One who makes the Nile flow, O One who subjugates the past, O One who split the sea for the Children of Israel—subjugate for me what I desire; indeed, You are the Doer of what You will. O Allah, I ask You to make easy for me what I intend. O Best of helpers, O Best of those sought for refuge. And by the right of Al-Ḥamdulillāh Rabb al-'Ālamīn—assist me in all my affairs with Your power. O Compassionate, O Merciful. Surah Ya Sin, the Wise Quran, the master of the messengers, the beloved of the Lord of the Worlds, peace be upon him.

By the name of Allah the Compassionate the Merciful. Ya Sin, the Wise Quran, by the authority of the Quran, O assembly of spiritual beings—I have adjured you by the glory of Allah, the glory of His Messenger, the majesty of Allah the Exalted, "There is no deity but Allah, Lord of the Worlds," by the authority of the Quran. O Living, O Sustainer, O One who sustains, O Sufficient, O Healer, O Gentle, O Everlasting—accept and respond to me.⁵ [unclear] By the right of the Lord of the Worlds, for the sake of the believing community, and by the right of that dominant King whose command is upon you—all of you⁵ [unclear] and today's⁵ [unclear] and the people of the rank—what are the spiritual beings? And today you have all⁵ [unclear] entrusted. By the authority of the oath: "Falsehood has vanished"—glory be to the One who releases every debtor, the One who brings from darkness to light, the One who brings forth the true.⁶
