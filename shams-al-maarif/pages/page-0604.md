The invocation for it is:
In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You, O He who does what He wills and chooses and judges what He wills—judgment belongs to Him and to Him you shall return—there is no repeller of His judgment and no delaying His decree and no restitutor for His servant from disobeying Him except by His success and His mercy. I ask You, O Allah, for the lordly actions and the shining Rahmanite lights, O He to whom belong the bounties and blessings—there is no god except You. Make this matter of mine one of guidance, and grant me the answer, O my Lord. Falasaw fi balasaw bi faṣʿar baliʿawiyaw sarayawf shalahawf baturayal fahar fahawf rafiq al-fawz bil-jinnat bahliyawf failfar fashahawf shafa shafa shafshaf shaff shafiʿ shafiwa safiṣaʿaṣawf fa yadif jins hasf bi-amrika fahanan. And indeed You are Suwwa and Murawa, and I walk—There is no harm in my affair and no failing in the standing triumph. Alif between me and such-and-such, and do for me such-and-such, with an alif. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi al-ʿaliyyi al-ʿazim. Ta.

The incense for it is: pepper and vinegar and colocynth. The amulet for this is:

Answer, O Samtaṭṭayil, by the right of the Sultan Saṭṭal Kayṭam Liṭam Liṭash. 22. Hanak. Answer and reveal the barrier between me and the calf—the Hour. Ta.

The Letter Sad
This letter is among the letters of the Supreme Name, and its properties are: when this letter is written on a piece of silk cloth together with the Name of the Client and the incense.

There shall be good and blessing; may he be protected from every harmful thing:
Then this should be placed beneath the bezel of a ring and worn—good and blessing, increase in livelihood, and may he be protected from harmful things. And work with it like the letter Mim. The amulet for this is:
