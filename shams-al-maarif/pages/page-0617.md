Chapter 39: Explanation of the Beautiful Names—Elucidation and Detail

The Beautiful Names are very numerous. The number of the Beautiful Names is not confined within a specific count, but among them the most excellent are those which Allah, Blessed and Exalted, has mentioned in His Book. We have previously made mention of them in summary, but now we shall do so in detail; however, before this, it is necessary to explain the manner of their application.

The recitation of the Names is of two types:

The recitation of a Name is of two kinds: one is that a particular Name be recited for some need—its conditions shall be explained later; and the other is that it be done in the manner of the people of love (ahl al-mahabbah). For this, the requirement is istikmal (perfection), which introduces one into solitude (khalwah) and grants permission to recite the Name. This matter shall not be obtained merely by looking at our book.

Four conditions for obtaining benefits:

For the person who recites for the fulfillment of a need, these four conditions are obligatory:

First: One should examine which Name among the Divine Names is appropriate for that need. For example, if the need is for love, let him recite the Name al-Wadud (the Loving); and if there is a need for the subjugation of hearts, let him recite the Name al-Rauf (the Compassionate). Let him recite it after each prayer according to its numerical equivalent; and if one intends to destroy someone, let him recite the Name al-Muntaqim (the Avenger), or al-Qabiz (the Seizer), or Dha al-Batsh al-Shadid (Possessor of Mighty Force), in accordance with spiritual discipline (riyadah), or by jimāl and number.

Second condition: The recitation in accordance with spiritual discipline and the numerical value of the Name.

Third condition: That one sit in solitude with gathered concentration, with the entire being directed toward the action. Whatever sign is destined shall be fulfilled.

Fourth condition: That one add the numerical value of one's own name to that of one's desired object, then find a Name that corresponds to your name and the sign. Use that.

A second method:

Another method is that if the person is among the people of spiritual knowledge (ahl maʿrifah), then the Names appropriate to him should be called upon: for example, Ya Zakki (O Pure One) or Ya Fattah (O Opener), and so forth; and if he is among the people of righteousness (ahl salahat), then let him call upon such Names as Tayyib (the Pure), and so forth.

For obtaining the degree of wilayah (sainthood):

The method of practice of the Names through which one attains dominion over the world and unveiling (kashf), so that all jinn and humans serve him, and presence (shuhud) and purity are achieved, and the degree of sanctified perfection is bestowed—this is the result of the people of purity (ahl al-safa). Allah, Exalted, says: "And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them" (Qur'an 7:180). Therefore, call upon Allah through them; and had there been no veils over the Names, their rays would have illuminated the entire world. Know that perceiving the realities of the Beautiful Names is not within the faculty of reason.

Ninety-nine Names are sufficient for entering Paradise:

His presence, upon him blessings and peace, said: "Allah, Exalted, has ninety-nine names. Whoever memorizes them shall enter Paradise."
