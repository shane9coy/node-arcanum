# Page 390 (English translation)
It is obligatory upon you. Then there are such special properties mentioned that if I were to tell them to you, you would walk upon water and your feet would not get wet; rather, by the blessing of these names, you would fly through the air.

By the blessing of these noble names, the men of the Unseen remain hidden from people's sight. When you recite them and say, "O servants of these names, take me to Mecca the Noble," they will take you there in one hour, and similarly they will bring you back. Its properties are countless. If there were no fear of it reaching ignorant people, I would have described it. This talisman was written beneath the staff of Moses, peace be upon him. Obtain it and keep it safe.
