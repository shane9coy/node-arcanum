{Chapter: The Best Treatment for the Spleen}

Whoever suffers from spleen pain should write this talisman on paper and place it on the abdomen above the area of constipation, directly on the spleen. Then place a small iron ring on top of it, add a little ash into the ring, and put some charcoal on it. The closer this fire is to the bandage/wrapping, the more this person will suffer. It will become evident that fire has been ignited in his abdomen. He should bring the ring as close to the abdomen as he can bear. By the command of God the Exalted, all corrupt matter will exit through the path of defecation, and that patient will become healthy. The form of the talisman is this:

[A circular seal with a double border. The outer border contains a ring of Arabic script. The inner circle is divided into four quadrants by a cross, each containing a word in Arabic:
Top-Left: Al-Halim (The Forbearing)
Top-Right: Al-Ghafur (The Forgiving)
Bottom-Left: At-Tawwab (The Acceptor of Repentance)
Bottom-Right: Al-Wahhab (The Bestower)
Additional script is written around the cross within the circle.]

{Chapter: Treatment for Forgetfulness and Absent-Mindedness, and the Method for Expelling Enemies and Bad Neighbors}

If forgetfulness and absent-mindedness have become very severe in your constitution, and you wish that what you hear will be remembered, then write these letters in a glass vessel, and on the day when it takes effect in your memory, and while drinking from it, recite the verse "fa-fahhamnaha sulaymana" (The Prophets 79).
