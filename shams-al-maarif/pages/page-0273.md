Chapter 19: The Properties of the Name Fattah—Difficult Matters Become Easy

Whoever frequently mentions this name, Almighty God will open his path for him. For seekers on the path, its mention is appropriate at the beginning, and for those who have reached the goal, it is also appropriate at the end of the spiritual journey. Its square is constructed as 5×5 with red ink, which should be kept with oneself—every difficult matter becomes easy for him, and he will not be distressed by any need. However, this is achieved through fasting, spiritual exercises, and glorification along with two units of prayer after the prescribed prayers. He should recite glorification before and after the recitation, and also recite this glorification in the bowing position. After the prostration, in the first unit he should recite Surah Al-A'la and in the second unit Surah Al-Mulk. Then he should make supplication and every need will be fulfilled. Its number is 889. It is among the divine names close to the Quranic names, and it is a singular rectangular form from the perspective of word and mercy. It is obtained from 137×7, and this number is deficient. The 125×7.1 points to the name Fattah al-Ali (The Opening/Conquering of the Most High) because in the concept of opening, the meaning of value is implied. Its number is 127, and with the letter alif, it points to the name of Almighty God al-Musi (The Chosen One). Its letters total 417, pointing to two of the great names of Almighty God, Mutin (The Firm) and Majid (The Glorious), and this is its square.

Chapter 20: The Properties of the Name Alim—Knowledge of Hidden Matters

Whoever frequently mentions this name, Almighty God makes him aware of the intricacies of matters and the psychology of sciences. If a person inscribes it on a piece of quicksilver in the ascendant of Mercury and keeps it with himself, Almighty God makes him eloquent with wisdom and grants him knowledge of the subtle wisdoms. If he inscribes it on a silver plate in the ascendant of Jupiter and keeps it with himself, Almighty God grants him understanding of the religious sciences. For one whose name is Isa (Jesus) or Sultan, its mention is appropriate. This is its form; its triangle is also constructed:
