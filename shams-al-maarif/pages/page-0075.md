# Page 75 (English translation)
73

Allah, Exalted, has said in the Noble Quran: "But whoever pardons and makes reconciliation, his reward is upon Allah" (3:159). That is: whoever forgives and reconciles, his reward is upon Allah, Exalted. And when written for good, light an excellent fragrance; and when written for evil, light a foul odor. If the moon is in an airy sign at that time, hang this figure in the wind. If it is in a fiery sign, bury it in fire. If it is in an aqueous sign, bury it in water or in a place near water, and if it is earthy, bury it in earth or beneath the threshold of its door.

**The Practice of Summoning Someone:**

And if you wish to summon it to yourself, bury it in your gate. However great a man he may be, he will answer you and heed your word. And if you wish to summon it, seal it in a cedar tube with wax and cast it into flowing water, and recite this supplication upon it one thousand four hundred and forty-eight times. The supplication is:

"In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by all Your beautiful names, the praiseworthy, the building, which when they are upon a thing, it becomes humble and submissive; and when good deeds are sought through them, they are attained; and when evil deeds are turned away through them, they are turned away; and by the perfect words, which if all the trees on earth were pens and the ocean were ink, with seven oceans added after it, the words of Allah would still not be exhausted—verily Allah is Mighty, Wise. O Sufficient, O Guardian, O Compassionate, O Gentle, O Provider, O Loving, O Self-Subsisting, O Knowing, O Vast, O Generous, O Bestower, O Extender, O Possessor of Grace, O Enricher, O Enricher, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Self-Sufficient, O Helper, O Most Gracious, O Most Bountiful, O Most Generous, O Most Kind, O Avenger. O Allah, suffice me with Your lawful provision over Your unlawful, and with Your obedience over Your disobedience, and with Your grace over all besides You, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And I ask You, O Allah, by Your name beside which there is no god but He, the Majestic, the Merciful, the Gentle, the Mighty, the Provider, the Forgiving, the Protector, the Trustee, the Helper, the Responsive, the Near, the Hearing, the Swift, the Generous, Lord of Majesty and Generosity, Lord of Grace and Beneficence."

**Correcting Noble Character and the Inner Self:**

You should know that whoever reads these names or keeps them with him, his character will become noble. People will be kind to him and will give him gifts abundantly. Strange and wondrous meanings will be revealed to him. His outward and inward selves will become good, because this supplication contains the Greatest Name, by which whatever is supplicated will be accepted and whatever is asked will be given. This is a very great noble practice. Whoever recites these names at midnight will have strange secrets revealed to him. He will obtain his desire happily and with abundant sustenance. The continuity of this dhikr opens hidden secrets. Whoever continues to read them, the affairs of the celestial realm will be revealed to him, and his angels will be subjugated. These are words of perfection containing wondrous mysteries. Whoever makes the dhikr of these names with the name Al-Kafi of Allah, Exalted, and desires something, that thing will be obtained for him in such a way that it will not even occur to him in his imagination, and every joy and rank will be discovered. If he desires advancement, Allah, Exalted, will grant him advancement through his dhikr. If someone has lost something and he makes the dhikr of Al-Kafi and Al-Jami', that thing will return to him. Through the dhikr of His name Afuww, great pains and sorrows will be dispelled. Through reading His name Ra'uf, the heart obtains luminosity and tranquility. Whoever reads this name in seclusion with fasting, such actions will appear upon him that if he takes fire in his hand, it will not harm him; and if he blows upon a raw piece of wood, it will lose its sap. It is better to add Ya Halim, Ya Ra'uf, Ya Mannan, Ya Jawad, or Ya Ghaffar. If these names are written in the hour of the moon and he confronts an enemy, victory will be his, proof of which is many [unclear]
