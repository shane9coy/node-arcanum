# Page 778 (English translation)
778

My chain of transmission for the science of letters and talismanic correspondences goes back to the Master and Imam, the scholar of scholars, Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Qastallani. He received it from Sheikh Abu Abdullah Muhammad ibn Ahmad al-Qarishi, who received it from the Master and Imam, the teacher Nasr al-Din Shu'ayb ibn Hasan al-Ansari, continuing until reaching one of the seven Peshwaramis, who was from among the nobles of the seven gates, and they from the great teacher and master of the learned, Umar ibn Hariri, who would seize a lion by the ear and lead it wherever he wished, and the lion would be completely obedient to him. And whoever looked upon his face would immediately become alive again. Thus Sheikh Abu Madyan also came to serve him, and upon gazing upon his face, he immediately became alive again. Then when they placed his garment upon their eyes—may God protect them—and they from the Master and Imam, the Pole of Poles, Abu al-Abbas ibn Ali Sa'id al-Mustaki Arsuzi, he from the great saint Ali Muhammad ibn Nur, he from the Imam of the world, Abu al-Fadl Abd Allah ibn Bishr, he from his father Abu al-Bishr Hasan Hajuwairi, he from the presence of the Master Sarri al-Saqati, he from the presence of Anas ibn Malik, may God be pleased with him, he from the presence of the Messenger of God, may God bless him and his family.

And when my fortunate days arrived, destiny brought me to the service of such a venerable sage who is himself a man of reason and whose son is a man of reason, and Diya' ibn Fikhr, and Satara ibn Jud, and Nuzul ibn Fikhr, and Nujayyib ibn Nujayyib, and Nusayyib ibn Nusayyib, the combiner of both sacred laws, who grasps the rope of greatness with both ends, who clung to the Sacred Law and the Reality, who acquired the manners of the spiritual path with both outward and inward aspects, and who is among those who have obtained the essence of divine proximity and among the sincere servants. The Imam of the researchers, the divine Imam, the Imam of the exacting, the summit of the mystics, the lamp of the travelers, the luminous scholar, the spiritual mystic, the humble human being, the proof of the monotheists, the remnant of the predecessors, the pillar of the successors, the possessor of noble qualities and weighty compositions, and the possessor of unveiled miracles and saintly exploits, the moon of the felicity of the heavens of happiness, Sheikh Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Muhammad al-Qarishi al-Hanafi—may God cause them to repent and make Paradise their dwelling—and he looked upon him with great attention, and ghee and the lamp of Qarib al-Hif Abd Allah and the most wretched of creation, holding fast to the hem of the generosity of God, Ahmad ibn Yusuf al-Qarishi, gave him instruction. May God Almighty rectify his condition and grant him a blessed end.

And likewise I have also seen Imam Ali ibn Sina, and he took his chain of transmission from Sheikh Mahmud al-Wasiti, and I have sat in his service and taken a chain of transmission from him and heard hadith. And he saw Sheikh Muhammad al-Jazairi and took from him a chain of transmission and heard hadith. And he saw the great chest, the presence of Sheikh Aziz al-Din Ali Muhammad Abd Allah ibn Musa ibn Sulayman Hajuwairi, and staying in his service, heard hadith from him. And he heard hadith from the most eminent, the Master and Imam Abu al-Hasan Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Abd al-Wahid al-Maqdisi, and he saw Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Ibrahim Abi Musa and stayed with him and heard hadith, and he saw Muslim ibn Ibrahim ibn Abd Allah and sat with him and heard hadith, and he heard hadith from the presence of Anas, the Companion, may God be pleased with him. They used to say that when the Messenger of God, may God bless him and his family and give him peace, came to Medina, the mother of Anas ibn Malik took my hand and brought me to the presence of the Apostle, and she said: "O Messenger of God, this is Anas, an intelligent and clever boy who also knows reading and writing. Keep him with yourself so that he may serve you." The Apostle, may God bless him and give him peace, accepted me. And this supplication is also from the presence of Anas:

He said: The Messenger of God, may God bless him and give him peace, said: "Indeed among the servants of God there are some who, if they were to swear by God, He would fulfill their oath."

There is consensus on its authenticity. And it is also transmitted from the presence of Anas:

He said: The Messenger of God, may God bless him and give him peace, said: "Help your brother whether he is the oppressor or the oppressed." I said: "O Messenger of God, I help him when he is oppressed, but how do I help him when he is the oppressor?" He said: "You prevent him from oppression—that is your help for him."

There is also consensus on its authenticity. I transmit both these hadiths through twelve intermediaries from the presence of the Apostle.

I was honored by the visitation and sweetness of the presence of the Apostle, may God bless him and give him peace, in Mecca the Honored, and I took permission for private audience and for the permission to use his names. For more private audience, this is the supplication; one should make it a regular practice:

O Living, O Sustainer, O Lord of Majesty and Honor, O Ultimate of Ultimates, O Provision of Provisions, O Causer of Causes
