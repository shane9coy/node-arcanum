# Page 411 (English translation)

And upon His Family and Companions and may He send blessings.
O Allah, there are no particles in the heavens, nor in the earth, nor in the mountains hidden, nor in the seas drops, nor in the eyes tears, nor in the souls dangers, except that they indicate Your Essence and bear witness to You. In Your kingdom and in the act of taskhīr (subjugation), I ask You for the subjugation of all things, that You grant me success for that which You are pleased with. You alone are the helper, and upon You is trust. The power and strength belong to Allah, who is the Exalted and the Wise. May Allah the Most High send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad ﷺ, his Family, and his Companions.

[Boxed heading: The Second Supplication of Surah Yā-Sīn and Its Numerous Benefits]

The righteous used to supplicate with it for the most extremely difficult matters, and their supplication was immediately accepted. It is as follows: One reads Yā-Sīn and repeats the word "Yā-Sīn" seven times, and after reading up to "lā yubṣirūna" (they do not see), one recites this supplication:

Allāhumma yā man nūruhu fī sirrihi wa sirruhu fī khalqihi, akhfini 'an a'yuni l-naẓirīna wa qulūbi l-ḥāsidīna wa l-bāghīna kamā ḥafiẓta l-rūḥa fī l-jasadi, innaka 'alā kulli shay'in qadīr.

O Allah! O You whose light is in His secret and whose secret is in His creation! Hide me from the eyes of the watchers and from the hearts of the envious and the wrongdoers, just as You protected the spirit within the body. Verily, You are capable of all things.

Then after reading up to "fāghshaynahum" (and He covered them), one says this:

Allāhumma ikfini bi-qaḍā'i ḥawā'iji.

O Allah, fulfill my needs and grant me honor (13 times).

Wa akrimni bi-ṭā'atika.

And honor me through Your obedience (8 times).

One reads and mentions the name of one's need. Then after reading up to "taqdīru l-'azīzi l-'alīm" (the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing), one repeats it fourteen times and says:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka min faḍlika l-wāsi'i l-nāfi'i an tughniyani 'an jamī'i khalqika.

O Allah, I ask You from Your vast, beneficial bounty that You make me independent of all Your creation (14 times).

Then one reads "salāmun qawlan min rabbin raḥīm" (peace, a word from a Merciful Lord) and repeats it ten times. After that, one says:

Allāhumma sallim-nā min āfāti d-dunyā wa fitnatihā.

O Allah, protect us from the calamities of the world and its trials.

And repeat in the same manner. Then one says:

Wa laysa alladhī khalaqa s-samāwāti wa l-arḍa bi-qādirin 'alā an yakhluqa mithlahum balā qādirun 'alā an taf'ala lī kadhā wa kadhā.

And is He who created the heavens and the earth not able to create the like of them? Yes, He is able (13 times).

And mention the name of one's need. Then say: "Wa laysa alladhī khalaqa s-samāwāti wa l-arḍa bi-qādirin 'alā an yakhluqa mithlahum" and read up to the end of the sūrah, then make supplication—it will be accepted at that very moment. And Allah is capable of all things.

Know that the seven "mubayyana" (clarified divisions) of noble Surah Yā-Sīn are based upon the method of "malūk" (kingship) and all that is good, which I have established as a daily devotional practice. Value it and protect it from ignorant people, for the secret of Allah the Most High is in the Quran, and the secret of the Wise Quran is in Surah al-Fātiha and Surah Yā-Sīn.

[Boxed heading: Sunday's Wird (Devotional Practice)]

Bismillāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm. Allāhumma yā mūjriya l-jibāli wa mūsakhkhirā l-fīli wa fāliqu l-baḥri li-banī Isrā'īl. Allāhumma sakhkhir lī mā urīdu, innaka fa''ālun limā turīdu. Ilāhī as'aluka an tusakhkhir lī mā urīdu, yā khayra nāṣiran, yā khayra man yaḥullu l-ṣa'bata. Allāhumma ajibnī 'alā kulli amrin bi-qudratika, yā raḥmānu yā raḥīmu, bi-ḥurmati sūrati Yā-Sīn wa l-qur'āni l-ḥakīmi wa bi-ḥurmati sayyidi l-mursalīna ḥabībi rabbi l-'ālamīna, ṣallallāhu 'alayhi wa sallama.
