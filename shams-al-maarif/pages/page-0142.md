# Page 142 (English translation)
140

"So when I have fashioned him completely and breathed into him from My spirit, they fall down in prostration to him."

That is: when I prepared him and breathed My spirit into him, they fell into prostration for him. That is, both the Last and the First, like the efflux and the first grace—with these three relations, he is the complete world of the supreme or lower realm. For in the world there is no one who has received one grace, no one who has received two graces, and no one who receives three graces. This is the world of the Supreme Pole. For this reason the world is the realm of guidance for the second, since the original grace of the Throne is sought—it does not change the realities of its opposites, nor does it degrade those souls that are grave offenders, because the world in its human reality and the secret of its composition are realities for the Supreme Throne. This world is in the kingdom; for it, the human world manifests the reality of the established Throne, that is, the world of Dominion. In the realities of the Supreme Spirit, the Throne's secrets are present, but also the reality of the First and the Last—that is, the forms of the Day of Resurrection—are found. At that time, it manifests its complete essence and reality. Whoever has walked from the straight line toward the crooked line has gone to prison. When the crooked line is given relation to the crooked and both are extracted, they will meet. Similarly, when the straight is given relation to the straight, they too will both meet. Therefore, whoever perfected it at the time of transfer to the world of the interval has progressed with all the realities of the Thrones.

The one who fulfills this first grace is connected to the Supreme Throne, which is not for the lowest of the dominions. It is encountered in the punishment of misguidance and in correspondence. He perfected this bodily constitution. But he transfers from punishment toward Mount Jali. This is the punishment of the reality of humanity, just as the first punishment is the reality of the dense physical form that carries it, and it is given punishment, and from it proceeds grace—that is the Vast Throne.

This image causes the punishment of descent and the corruption of Jinn-like bodies. Thus, from the perspective of the reality of truth, all dualities appear toward the inner wisdom. Then inner forms come to it. Now when it desires something, its volition goes toward rebellion. It becomes one who commits fornication; it reaches the limit of punishment, and what is evident from the outer forms is shown to it. Until the reality of these forms is extinguished by it. At that time, it returns to the nutritive faculty. Then it sees another form, and its beauty becomes severalfold, and its desire becomes rebellious. What appears in it experiences severalfold suffering, punishment, and various forms of humiliation whose seeing it does not desire or wish for. Then by the command of their density and subtlety, those whom God Most High wills remain. Until all these forms of his are extinguished. This is the secret of the saying of God Most High:

"Every time their skins are roasted, We shall replace them with other skins so that they may taste the punishment."

In accordance with the nutritive faculty, their skins are of various kinds, and at the station of liberation their various forms are changed until these forms become disordered. Thus it is the very essence of beauty in the manner in which He placed it in the Throne. Its punishment becomes severalfold. The divine address is well applied to this punishment. The guidance of God Most High is pure:

"So We have set between them a wall with a gate within it; within it is mercy, and its exterior from before it is punishment."

Now when you intend to see the reality of the absolute beauty that meets you—how it has become toward the eye of ugliness, while there is nothing in it except difference in essence and attribute—this is the mirror of the Primordial Existent for it. He perceives beneficence as beautiful, and the light of essence transforms by His grace every veil of his, and the world turns its face toward it, from which we derive certainty for it, and we have set the subtleties of thought toward it for the line of specialization. And from the friends of God there is a chain from the Throne of the Wise—for the will of the essences, not—so that the pure truth may be told of the absolute secret of the light of the Originator, which is praised for the straight path.
