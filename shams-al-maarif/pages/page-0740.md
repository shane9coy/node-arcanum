# Page 740 (English translation)
For them He has placed in their hearts the mystery of destiny and free will. Thus it is evident, and this mystery belongs to the power and the stationary Light of the All-Knowing. And this is that the realities of the world have not been established except through the practice of the known path, through some courage and in some manner. Whether the practice is intellectual or religious.

The eight types of light that are the realities of the Divine Throne—He has borne the Throne upon Himself. These are:

The manifestation of wisdom and the reality of witnessing the Divine is like the treasury of Lordship, and its light is divided into eight types. The light of the heart, the light of faith, the light of the soul, the light of the spirit, the light of the intellect, the light of the head, the light of the heart, and the light of unveiling. These are the eight lights, and for each light there are many mysteries, because these are all realities of the Divine Throne, and from them comes the secret of these eight who bear the Throne.

"And the Throne of your Lord will be borne above them on that Day by eight [angels]." (69:17)

That is, on the Day of Resurrection, eight angels will be carrying the Throne of your Lord. Thus the light of the heart takes assistance from the light of faith, for faith is from the light of majesty. So whoever has received the grace of the Divine light has accepted all the religious hardships and the commands of servanthood. Concerning the rank of these people, Allah the Most High says:

"Indeed in that are signs for those who observe closely." (15:75)

The eye of the heart became illuminated by the light of faith, then everything became manifest:

When the eyes of their hearts confronted the Divine light, Allah the Most High disclosed to them the entire realm of the Kingdom, both in summary and in detail, and they became acquainted with the secrets of the composition of the world and with whatever Allah the Most High has deposited within their dispositions.

The light of Allah appears luminous in every particle:

In each and every particle they beheld the light of the eye of truth, and this is their reality. It is established upon one of the Divine lights—whosoever has seen this light sees the walls burning from its rays, as if suspended from the sun. And this person sees light within his heart and body.

The lights of the soul and spirit:

The light of the soul comes from the light of the spirit. So whoever has established his soul upon obedience and worship and has achieved deliverance from the darknesses of temperaments, until his light has derived benefit from the light of the spirit, [attains] absorption in witnessing within Paradise. This is the sincere one whom Allah the Most High grants the unveiling of the lights of the realities of the realm of divine power for his soul and spirit. And this person sees in the heavens the different kinds of angels of divine administration carrying the pure word [also] upon the heavens.

The light of the intellect and the explanation and division of the lights:

The light of the intellect comes from the light of the head, whose intellect is directed toward knowledge. It abandons everything and calls out to its Lord and Creator, until it sees with the head and observes the capacity for wisdom, and sees how He who is occurring in the upper and lower world is established thereby, and how it can behold His reality in both summary and detailed form. The light of the head comes from the lights of the Quran, whose head has become illuminated by the lights of the Quran along with the sciences, by the One Who guarantees the truth. This is the reality that the Lord Most High has manifested for the Quran alone.
