Section: Obtaining Perpetual Love

When you desire that a certain person love you with a love that remains steadfast forever and endures until death, then write these names on seven pieces of paper. Upon it, write your own mother's name, the name of the desired person, and also his/her mother's name. The composition should be done with a basil (rayhan) pen and musk ink. Each day, burn one piece of paper. On the first Sunday, write: ʿaṭaftu qalba fulānin bni fulānatin ʿalā fulānin bni fulānatin bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi l-asmāʾ 212311x511636546236 a ṣ-ṣiḥr [I have burned] in it the heart of so-and-so with love. These are the talismans which should be written on seven pieces of paper according to the days of burning. Its copy is this:

The Talismans Which Are Written on the Seven Leaves According to the Seven Days

a- [Series of seven stars and stylized symbols] ghirt sflāyīd bā zikā ṣiyāḥ ʿallamah [Symbols]
khnh [Symbols] mh mh [Symbols] ḥdm [Symbols] mh mh [Symbols]
aḥbṣṭ ḥṭlībān ḥṭīl ʿadīl kān lahu ʿalā jabal hayhīl kān

b- [Series of six stars and stylized symbols] [Numbers: 161359 121392 7711293] [Symbols]
habt [Symbols] ṣāmāh ṣallū [Symbols] ṭābat ḥamlit maʿa ṣiyām rubʿ inna kasil 1990

On the second paper, write on Monday in this manner: aḥraqtu qalba fulānin bni fulānatin ʿalā maḥabati fulānin bni fulānatin wa-alqaytu baynahumu l-maḥabbata wal-mawaddata bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi l-asmāʾ ḥān maḥall khnh ḥṭ ṣh sbḥānah qah minnah qad ḥay [I have] burned the heart of so-and-so in the love of so-and-so, and by the blessing of these names I have placed between them love and affection. On Tuesday: aḥraqtu qalba fulānin bni fulānatina wa-aḥlaltuhu wa-jadhabtuhu ilā maḥabati fulānin wa-aḥraqtuhu bi-n-nāri ka-mā taḥruqu hādhihi l-asmāʾ tawakkalū yā khuddāma hādhihi l-asmāʾ bi-mā amartukum bihi hayā l-ʿajala l-wāḥid l-wāḥid as-sāʿata [I have] burned the heart of so-and-so and dissolved it and attracted it to the love of so-and-so, and I have burned it with fire just as these names burn. O servants of these names, do the task which I have commanded you. On the fourth day, Wednesday: tawakkalū yā khuddāma hādhihi l-asmāʾ wal-falaqṭuriyyāt bi-l-qalāʾi l-maḥabbati wal-mawaddati fī qalbi fulānin bni fulānatina ilā maḥabati fulānin bni fulānatina balā laylan walā nahāran walā yanʿamu lahu qarārun walā nazlun walā yakhluquhu amran bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi l-asmāʾ O Falqṭuriyyāt and O servants of these names, place love and affection in the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so.
