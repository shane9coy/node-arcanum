# Page 247 (English translation)
...I ask of You with complete power, and make me indifferent to all Your creation with my mortality. Bestow upon me Your grace with praise and servitude, and for my need, show favor upon me.

This talisman is effective when it is complete.

[Boxed Heading: The Best Prayer for Sight and Vision]

Hasan bin Salem says that my grandmother was blind. A man came to her and said, "I will tell you something that is among the names of Allah the Exalted, by which Allah the Exalted will restore your sight." My grandmother said, "May Allah the Exalted forgive you." He said, "Allah al-Shakir—recite this prayer." Then rub your hands on your mouth and eyes. She did so, and Allah the Exalted restored her sight. She saw an elder standing before her, then he disappeared. My grandmother mentioned this prayer before her death. This prayer begins from the beginning of Surah Hadid to "Alim bi dhat al-Sudur" and ends with Surah Hashr.

Through the blessing of Ali, may Allah honor his face, someone asked about such a prayer that the Prophet, peace be upon him, taught specifically to him. Ali said, "I had no idea that anyone would ask me this." Then he said, "When you have a need, recite from the beginning of Surah Hadid up to the verse "Ila al-Sudur" and from "La inzalna hadha al-Quran" to the end of Surah Hashr, then say: 'Allahumma ya man huwa kaza wa kaza, wa bi fadlika kun kaza wa kaza.' Then ask that prayer, and it will be accepted."

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: A Proven Prayer for Deliverance from Sorrow and Grief]

His Eminence Sheikh Abu al-Hasan al-Shadhili, may Allah be pleased with him, says: One night I was in extreme sorrow and grief. At that very moment, I received inspiration to recite this prayer:

"Allahumma mananta 'alayya bi al-tawhid wa al-ta'at, wa ahatat baya al-shahwa wa al-ghafla wa al-ma'siya, wa tarahqitani al-nafsu fi bahr al-hawa wa al-zulma, fa hiya mudhlima wa 'abduka mazlum mahzun mahmum maghmum, qad i-tqamahu al-hawa wa huwa yunadika bi nida' al-maghmumi al-mahbus 'abdi dhi al-nun, wa yaqulu la ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min al-zalimin, fa istajib li kama istajabta lahu, wa hdini bi 'izzi al-mahabba fi mahall al-tafrid wa al-tawhid wa al-wahdaniyya, wa anta al-Latif al-Hannan al-Mannan, wa laysa li illa anta wahdaka la sharik laka, wa la tukhlif wa'daka li man amana bika, fa innaka qulta wa qawluka al-haqq, fa istajabna lahu wa najjaynahu min al-ghamm, wa kadhalika nuji al-mu'minin, wa sall Allahu 'ala sayyidina Muhammad wa 'ala alihi wa sahbihi wa sallam."

"O Allah, You have shown favor upon me with monotheism and obedience. Lust, negligence, and sin have surrounded me. The ego has thrown me into the sea of passion and darkness, so it is dark, and Your servant is wronged, grieving, worried, and distressed. Passion has devoured him, and he calls upon You with the call of the distressed, imprisoned servant—the one of the whale—and says: 'There is no deity but You; glory be to You; surely I was among the wrongdoers.' So answer my prayer as You answered his, and guide me with the honor of love in the place of solitude, monotheism, and uniqueness, for You are the Gentle, the Most Kind, the Bestower of favors. I have none but You alone; there is no partner for You; You do not break Your promise to those who believe in You, for You have said—and Your word is truth—so We answered him and saved him from grief, and thus We save the believers. May Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace."
