# Page 176 (English translation)
You have fulfilled our hope, and You know us well with Your complete forgiveness. Pardon us whose distress You have not remedied. His concern is very great, and the cause of the power of one whose distress You have not removed has ceased. That is a bounty which You have removed from Your door, and regret is for one whom You have removed from Your presence. If Your mercy is for the righteous, then where shall the sinful go? O Allah, hide us in Your veil and forgive us with Your generosity. O Allah, give us well-being from Your grace. O Allah, if we are not capable of repentance, You are capable of forgiveness. O Allah, we have obeyed You in our words such as accepting faith, and we have abandoned great evil—polytheism—with You, and we have also abandoned lying against You. We have left that which is between these two, so do not put me to shame before You. O Allah, our sins are very small compared to Your pardon, though they are very great compared to Your prohibition. If You intended our humiliation, then [unclear] and if You intended our honor, then [unclear], and do not take from us that with which You gave us greatness. O Allah, You will burn with fire that face which knew You. O Allah, You alone are our refuge. Rather, when hopes narrow, we are happy with Your remembrance and take pride. We appeal only to Your forgiveness and are needy before You. Our pride is in You alone, O Allah, have mercy upon us as You have shown us Your way, and pardon our slips. Advance our desire, and do not deprive us of what is with You because of our sins, nor cast us out because of our flaws. O Allah, if we have sinned in unfaithfulness, remind us that we have worshipped You, for we know that You are our Lord who forgives and does not care. O Allah, before complaint You know my state, and You are able to fulfill hopes and to avert affliction. O Allah, who removes errors, erases evils, and changes them into good deeds. Grant us refuge from Yourself and with Your remembrance give us [unclear]. Work with us according to Your command. Grant us the ability to give thanks. Forgive us, our parents, our shaykhs, and all believing men and women, all Muslim men and women, those who are alive and those who have died. And all praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

Second supplication:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Glorious is the Lord of His Light, the Lord of desires before they exist. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who determined the decrees before their manifestation. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who governs the ages before their occurrence. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who drew near the angels and distributed them. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who set the celestial spheres in motion and designated them. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who refined the spirits and ennobled them. Glorious is the Lord of His Light who assembled bodies and harmonized them. I ask You, O Allah, by Your Light with which You manifested upon the Throne, who expanded the lights, and I ask You by Your Light with which You manifested upon the forms, who established the spirits, and I ask You by Your Light with which You manifested upon the Chair, who gathered the similitudes. And I ask You, O Allah, by the light of Your Face, and the light of Your Throne, and the light of Your Pen, and the light of Your Spirit, and the light of Your Form, and the light of Your Chair. And I ask You, O Light of lights, O Light of every light, O Illuminator of every light, to make in my heart a light, and in my sight a light, and in my hearing a light, and in my tongue a light, and in my bones a light, and in my flesh a light, and in my skin a light, and in my hair a light, and from my right a light, and from my left a light, and from before me a light, and from behind me a light, and from above me a light. And I take refuge in You lest I falter beneath me and that darkness should overcome me in the Light. Indeed You are the Light of lights, the Light of the亲近的 ones and the righteous. Hallowed, most holy, Lord of majesty and glory.
