# Page 588 (English translation)
588

**The desired person will love the seeker:**
When the names of the seeker and the desired person are written on a silk cloth along with the letter Dal and its substitute letters, and kept with you, the desired person will feel love. If this letter is written 26 times within a square, and the letter Dal is written around it, then it is placed beneath a cushion of perfume, that person will wear it and become wealthy.

**The one who keeps this with him sees the wonders of God:**
If this letter is written 26 times and this verse is also written opposite it: "Muhammad is the messenger of God and those who are with him" on a cloth, and the name of the spirit and its substitute letters are also written with it, and kept with you. He will see the wonders of Divine Craftsmanship, which are endless. The service of this letter is extremely great and the spirit is its obedient servant. When it is desired to treat it, observe the 28-day retreat and recite the summoning after every prayer—the spirit will be present and will obey. Its talisman is this:

And its summoning is this:
**In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I call upon a great Lord who sees the secret and the proof, the Judge of the Day of Judgment. Adam through Your grace and the grace of Your angels, answer me, O King Samhāʾīl. Glory be to You, there is no god but You, that You subjugate for me this, O my Lord. Subjugate for me the letter Dal through the eternity of blackness and through Your perpetuity, let Him decree my affair for me, and through Your guidance upon me and upon him this is the request of those who do not delay. Or I ascend what ascended, and he is the Good One. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not the path of those upon whom wrath is incurred, nor of the astray. And do not hesitate, O Dal, by Fa, there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Sublime, the Great.**

Translation: "I call upon this Lord who sees both the hidden and the manifest, who passes judgment on the Day of Resurrection. O Lord, You created Adam through Your grace and exquisite craftsmanship. O spirit Samhāʾīl, be present. O Lord, pure are You, there is no god but You, subjugate this spirit for me through the eternity of Dal, through perpetuity, for the arrangement of my affairs, and through Your favor upon me and upon it. Guide me to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed blessings—not that of those upon whom wrath has fallen, nor of the astray. O spirit, be present quickly, do not hesitate at all, O Dal, through a thousand times 'there is no power nor strength,' O spirit be present quickly. May God grant you blessing."

And its fumigation Idhānī is this: Cubeb pepper and spikenard, and whatever you request through this letter you shall obtain. And its substitute letters are these:
**Al-Ḥayyu al-Qattuf, buried [unclear]. Answer me, O King, may God bless you.**
