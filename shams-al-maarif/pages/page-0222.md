# Page 222 (English translation)
Presidency of the Name of the Essence, that is, Allah Allah

After it, the noble verse "Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth" is also [included]. The instruction is that you should remain in seclusion for fourteen days, observing the conditions of seclusion. During those days, abstain from animal products and remain separate from people. After each prayer, this Name should be recited one thousand (1000) times and the verse fifty (50) times. During dhikr, perform this fumigation twice daily. Recite this Name ten thousand times and also perform fumigation with incense. On the fourteenth day, you will know that the entire house has been filled with light, and you will perceive yourself as being immersed in the place of light. Hold your heart firm; this state does not last more than three hours. After that, the officer of peace will come to you. Do not fear him at all, for he is very blessed. He will greet you—respond to his greeting and sit respectfully, for he is an officer of great majesty and grandeur, who constantly repeats "Allah, Allah." He will give you a knowledge and then take his leave. You should thank him for this. This is from the grace and bounty of Allah. Most High Allah will surely give you paradise through His grace and bounty; indeed, He has power over whatever He wills.

[Text in a decorative box:]

The Invocation of Ya-Latif

May sorrow and grief depart, may enemies be destroyed

When need arises in any matter, perform two units of prayer, then recite Surah Al-Inshirah, then recite "Ya Latif" sixteen thousand six hundred forty-one (16,641) times—this is its number of greatness. Then whatever supplication you make for the removal of sorrow, grief, and the like will be accepted. But if the destruction of an oppressor is desired, then after reciting the aforementioned number, supplicate with this plea for aid:

O Allah, You are the King, the Omnipotent, the Subduer, Possessor of might and severe punishment. O my God, a servant among Your servants has wronged me and acted tyrannically, and You are the Just Judge. I have brought my complaint against him before You, and I have placed my trust in You for the removal of his oppression from me. Send down upon him an affliction that the inhabitants of the heavens and earth cannot avert, until he recognizes the value of Your blessing and well-being upon him. And make him firm with the firmness of the stone upon the companions of the elephant. Strip him, clothe him, sever him, destroy him, and annihilate him, "So Allah punished them for their sins, and there is no protector for them from Allah" [Quran 71:4].

And He sent upon them birds in flocks, striking them with stones of baked clay, making them like eaten stubble." [Quran 105:1-5]
