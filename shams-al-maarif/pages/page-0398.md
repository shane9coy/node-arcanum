# Page 398 (English translation)
In it are gardens of vines, and We caused springs to flow therein. Will they not give thanks? O You who is glorified by every tongue, O You who created all pairs from what the earth grows, and from themselves, and from what they do not know. O You who made the sun run to its resting place—that is the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing. O You who ordained for the moon stations until it returns like the old palm stalk. O You who subdued the cattle for us and We made them subservient to them, so they eat from them. And O You who created humans from a drop of fluid, then behold, he is an open disputer. O You who gives life to bones while they are dust. O You who first created them and He knows every creation. O You who made fire from the green tree—behold, you kindle from it. O You who created the heavens and the earth with precision, able to create the like of them. O Creator, O Knowing, O You who when He intends something says to it "Be" and it becomes.

And through our noble Messengers, who guide the astray to the straight path—You have granted us pure dominion. O Destroyer of towns, all are present before Him. O You who gives life to the dead and We have inscribed what was sent forth before them and their traces, and We have numbered everything in the Clear Imam. O You who revives the earth after its death—from it He brings forth grain; then from it they eat. O You who made among them gardens of vines, and We caused springs to flow therein, so that they might not give thanks. O You whose glorification is made in every tongue. O You who created all pairs from what the earth grows, from their own selves, and from what they do not know. O You who made the sun that proceeds to its resting place—this is the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing. O You who ordained for the moon stations until it returns like the withered old branch. O You who created cattle for us, and We made them subservient to them—some of them they eat. O You who created humans from a drop of fluid, yet he is an open disputant. O You who gives life to bones when they are crumbled dust. O You who first created them—He knows all creation. O You who made fire from the green tree, and behold, you kindle from it. O You who created the heavens and the earth—that is sufficient for creating their like. O Creator, O Knowing, O You who when He intends a thing says to it "Be" and it becomes.

Then recite this supplication:

O Allah, to You belongs all praise, my God—there is no god except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, O Sovereign of sovereignty—there is no god except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no one except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no authority except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no helper except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no conqueror except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no god except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no proof except Yours, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no tyrant except You, and to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no subduer except You, so to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no provider except You, so to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no power except Yours, so to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no hearer except You, so to You belongs all praise, my God, there is no seer except You, so to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the Sufficient and the Guide, so to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the best of helpers, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the Turner of hearts, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the God of the heavens and the earth, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the Revealer of distresses, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the Compassionate, the Merciful, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the best of creators, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the best of forgivers, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the best of helpers, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the best of providers, and to You belongs all praise, my God, You are the Sufficient, the Healer. O Allah.
