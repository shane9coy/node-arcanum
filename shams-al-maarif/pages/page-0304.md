# Page 304 (English translation)
Chapter 72: Properties of the Last Name — "The Form of Ascent and Descent"

One who mentions this luminous name and name of divine essence can bring about ascent or descent, but mentioning it should be combined with the First Name. Know that whoever desires to elevate someone's rank must necessarily depict that person's form on a tablet with great artistry; placing it before himself, he should continue studying it while observing it with presence of heart until his state predominates over it. At that time he will see this one [the person being worked upon] mentioning the forms along with himself. One should persist in this state, and one's need will be fulfilled, especially if one is among the People of States. Further elaboration on this is not possible; through the mysteries of this First Name, every matter can be understood. One must be an understander. If your certainty is complete, the gate of sovereignty will open to you, and you will witness the mysteries. His sacred essence is He who granted the revelation of the Sami mysteries to the gnostics and those who attain divine lights. The tasatturi number of this name is 1448 and the rukni is 845. The tasatturi number is even-odd; 121 and 1458 are witnesses. These two names, Muttaqi al-Ruh and Ghalib, point to it. The original upon it is the Necessary Name; it is greater than its parts; its components are greater than the original, for they point to the name Muttaqi. This is a great and majestic name; people of ambition hold it. It is from the hidden mysteries. This is its form:

Chapter 73: Properties of the First Name — "The Completion of All Purposes and Desires"

Whoever persists upon this name will attain precedence in all purposes. Allah the Most High will fulfill every longing of his. Its tasatturi number is 43 and its rukni number is 37. Thus 43 are the attention of the original, for thirst is among the meanings of "first," not stinginess. The explanation of 37 has already been given in the Name of Allah. Its letters' names, from the standpoint of "first," point to the name Halim and descent. Its design is this:

Chapter 74: Properties of the Last Name — "Obtaining Allah's Support and Victory Over Enemies"

Whoever mentions this noble name frequently will remain after his enemies, and all their property and possessions will come into his hands. And Allah the Most High will grant him support through it. For know that whoever performs the litany of this name, He grants him such power and support that describing it is impossible. Some of its strongest effects: when it is in Mars, write on a red copper tablet the name of some tyrant along with [the name], place it beneath a burning stove, continue mentioning this name, and place it in someone's lock — then the tyrant will perish at that very moment.
