# Page 333 (English translation)
Open the heart of so-and-so with the love of so-and-so, by Allah, the Mighty, the Proud, the Great, the Guardian, the Magnificent.

In another narration it is that the name of the mother of Moses, peace be upon him, is Qaydarah; the opening of the lock and chain is this: Ṭawm 2, Aym 2, Ḥaym 2, Qayūm 2, Dāʾim 2, Dayūm 2:

Allāhumma yā man fataḥa l-samāʾa bi-l-maṭari l-ʿazīzi, iftaḥi l-qayda wa-l-aghlāla wa-l-qulūba, innaka ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīrun.

O Lord of the fulfillment of needs, who showed tenderness to the mother of Moses, peace be upon him, to a hundred degrees, and He is the Mighty, the Wise, the Merciful, the Compassionate, the Rahman and the Rahim. He is the Essence with whom locks are opened, whose light illuminates the entire world, with whom possibilities open. Open this lock and chain. The full supplication is as follows:

Ṭawm 2, Aym 2, Ḥaym 2, Qayūm 2, Dāʾim 2, Dayūm 2: Allāhumma yā man fataḥa l-samāʾa bi-l-maṭari l-ʿazīzi, iftaḥi l-qayda wa-l-aghlāna wa-l-qulūba, innaka ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīrun. Allāhumma ashhī ashhī ashhī wa shayhī wa daydūḥa wa bahkhin wa ṭāḥūla muʿahhala lahu wa makāʾida wa salāmun wa mā yūḥā mukhal-khalata dāma akhrāʾuhu julūduhā khāʾūzata būha dhah bidīḥan wa khālībun jālūlata mardūdata fa-inna ṣaḥḥa ṣaḥḥa ṭaffa laffa kuhf sahaffa fayʾilun yā layṭan wa layāṭiyyan li-jazīrati illā mā tawakkaltum wa ajabtum wa aṭaʿtum Allāha wa rasūlahu wa qudratahu wa sulṭānahu, iftaḥū hādhā l-qufla wa-in kāna mina l-ḥadīdi fa-burrūhu wa-in kāna min maṣfarin aw nuḥāsin aw ʿūjin fa-ajīrūhu bi-ḥaqqi hādhihi l-asmāʾi ʿalaykum.

If you desire the composition of hearts, write: "Open the heart of so-and-so with love and affection towards so-and-so."

(Translation) O Allah, who opened the heaven with rain, the Mighty, open the chains and the hearts. Truly, You are over all things capable. O those entrusted by Allah and His Messenger [obscured by stamp: black circular seal containing illegible text] whose command among whom I accept. By His power and sovereignty, open this lock. If it is of iron, melt it. If it is of copper or aloe wood, break it. By these Names, open the heart of so-and-so for the love of so-and-so.

Section: On the countless properties of the Seal of Solomon, peace be upon him

Know that whoever keeps it with him remains protected from sins, keeps clothes and body pure, keeps the tongue silent, abandons sins, consistently performs acts of obedience, and has faith in the essence of Allah Most High—this seal is of obedience. Some call it one Bakhūkh (?) (Hebrew: lo li-lah tikhatu?); the Seal of Solomon, peace be upon him, had four precious stones. On the right side of each ring was written: Lā ilāha illā Allāhu lam azal. On the left side: Anā Allāhu l-ḥayyu l-qayyūmu. On the third side: Anā Allāhu l-ʿazīzu lā ʿazīza ghayrī wa ʿazīzun mina l-hayʾati khātamī. And on the fourth side, Ayat al-Kursi, and with it was written: Muḥammadun rasūlu Llāhi ṣallā Allāhu ʿalayhi wa sallama.

Merit: It has also been said that on the Seal of Solomon, peace be upon him, these Names were inscribed:

Lā ilāha illā Allāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lahu, anā Allāhu taʿazzaztu bi-l-mulki wa-l-suṭṭān, ayyil ayyil, anā Allāhu taʿazzaztu bi-l-jabarūti wa-l-amkāni, bāh bāh bāh, anā Allāhu ḥayyu qayyūmu lā anāmu, ayyih ayyih ayyih, anā Allāhu khabīrun qādirun aṭāʿanī kullu shayʾin, aṭūkhin 3, anā Allāhu l-raḥmānu l-raḥīmu, dhā hījin dhā hījin, mā ʿurijah, lā ilāha illā Allāhu ḥiṣnī man dakhalahu amina min ʿadhābī, taḥaṣṣantu bi-l-qāderi hādhā l-khātami wa bi-dhī l-ʿizzati wa l-jabarūti wa iʿtaṣamtu min aʿdāʾī bi-dhī l-mulūkūti wa l-quwwati wa bi-dhī l-ʿizzati wa l-mulūkūti wa fawwadtu amrī ilā l-ḥayyi alladhī lā yamūtu, mā hīya man
