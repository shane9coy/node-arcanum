# Chapter 100: The Properties and Benefits of Kāf-Hā-Yā-'Ayn-Sād

O sincere seeker, O desirous one, you should know that Allah the Almighty will bring you to the eternal elixir of felicity and the everlasting state of eternity, because the knowledge of the divine names is noble luminous knowledge and a sacred spiritual secret. Great saints and mystics such as Imam al-Ghazali and Fakhr al-Din al-Razi have given complete attention to it. In reality, this knowledge is from the sciences of divine origin (luduniyya) which has been attained by those who have luminous attentions, and the lovers' burnings are aware of it. The master of joy and the center of the valley of the lights of the chosen Prophet ﷺ said: Knowledge is like a treasure which the saintly scholars know. When they explain it, the ignorant ones deny it. So O my pure brother and loyal friends, this knowledge is a hidden door, a concealed secret, red sulfur, and yellow sapphire. Its allusion is clear to the mystics. Understand it well and acquire it if you can comprehend it, for it contains wisdom-filled words and admonition for the learned. Allah the Almighty is the one who solves all difficulties. Do not think this is merely a knowledge spoken by the tongue and written with the pen; rather, each of its letters is composed of luminous letters and dark letters.

O wondrous composition and strange arrangement, it reveals the exoteric sciences, sacred understandings, and spiritual mysteries. The talismans of divine treasures have been unlocked; in it are Sufi unveilings and devotional attentions, noble teachings of the pure spiritual way, the most complete and discriminating matters, and true spiritual breaths. It contains the comprehension of the brides of secrets, luminous traces, Sufi allusions, Sufi expressions, prophetic intimations, and divine clarifications. In it is the revelation of letter sciences, numerical quantities, numerical omen, sciences of divine origin, and divine subtleties. You can reach the divine presence and individual unity in your spiritual journey without difficulty, so investigate this knowledge and understand it. This is the favor of Allah the Almighty, which He gives to whoever He wills, and Allah is the possessor of great favor.

This is His nature: He gives wisdom to whichever of His servants He wills, and upon whoever of His saints He wills, He sends down secrets. If the conditions and times are worthy of mental control and analogy, then through the aid of reason, investigation proceeds. There is relief from occupations, the occupied one receives a reward, and through guidance, souls become radiant. May your life's hours be more than the times of pure clarity. May days pass fully and completely, and may greatness be attained. May your spiritual understanding be illuminated, and may your luminous comprehension achieve its desires. Its hidden secret, its concealed name, its magnet of attraction, and its attractive sapphire. Its light rises, its understanding shines, its desired presence, its spreading excellence, its manifest beauty, its elevated subtleties, its land of knowledge, its nearness of secrets, its east of lights. Opposite to it are its names; its high rank, its wondrous name, and its strange number. Its secret is in the verses, and its portion is in the recitations. Its precious subtleties and sacred knowledge. Its books are concealed; only the pure can touch it. This state is clear to the saints, and only with special caution can one attain its knowledge. Only the wise give the command to practice it. Only the noble find it. The verse:

**"Al-Ḥusn has chosen for me her beauty,**
**so every lover has attained her desire."**

Her beauty has made me like the devoted lovers, loving her and desiring her.
