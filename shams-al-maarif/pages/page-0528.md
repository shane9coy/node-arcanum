# Page 528 (English translation)
From the remaining of the رباعی (Quaternary), 101 leaves 19, and some months have four days. From 19, 15 remains, and these are the days of the fixed period. Thus the fixed period of its sovereignty is 7 years and 15 days. In the same manner, calculate all things. And God knows best.

Know that the name of the رباعی (Quaternary) in the pattern of احمد (Ahmad) never makes an incorrect judgment.

All these rules are universal and completely correct. They have been derived from the principles of جفر (Jafr). The author solved them in 12 registers, and very rare matters have been known through them. Then, after him, other scholars have also derived benefits from it according to their respective capacities. For this very reason, I have named this rule "Za'ir" (Beneficial Visitor). This knowledge has been derived from the science of numerical fractions (علم کسری اعداد), which has many different methods. May God bring you to its ultimate limit and grant you knowledge of its realities and secrets—indeed, He has power over all things.

And may God bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions.
