# Page 113 (English translation)
From this, the head of movement is obtained. Stillness is also among the mysteries of movement. For this reason, it is the Manifest and the Hidden, just as Allah the Exalted says: "He is the First and the Last." This is the gate that opens it. The letter Alif is an indication of the Essence, and all the saints of the world are for the covenant of faith in order to accept the religious rulings, in which the head of Alif is found. The letter Ha is for all matters on the Day of Judgment, because on the Day of Judgment all the first and the last will be present. With this divine wisdom, these fourteen letters circulate. You will find Alif alone at the beginning and end of this Name. Similarly, with expansion, the letters are Alif-Lam-Mim-Alif. The presence of the Noble Prophet, upon him be peace, said: "He alone is the Manifest, and nothing is above Him, and He alone is the Hidden, behind whom there is nothing." Since the collection of fourteen letters is from it, therefore the seven heavens and the seven earths and whatever is between them of the physical and spiritual realms are established by the Divine Head. In every particle of the world is the mystery of the Divine Name, and for this reason it bears witness to His oneness. Allah the Exalted said: "Do you know one who shares His name?" Then He said: "Say: Allah, then leave them to their immersion in play." That is, say to the disbelievers: your Lord is Allah—abandon the experts.

The Imam of the World, the Great Scholar, Fakhr al-Din al-Khwarazmi, said in summary within the Sacred Mosque of Makkah: Allah the Exalted revealed His Name according to his condition, so for him that is the Greatest Name, just as "the Most Merciful of those who show mercy" was specific for Prophet Ayyub, upon him be peace, as he said: "My Lord, indeed affliction has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of those who show mercy." The Name "the Bestower" is for Prophet Sulayman, upon him be peace, when he made this supplication:

"My Lord, forgive me and grant me a kingdom that no one after me shall have. Indeed, You are the Bestower."

The "Jealous" is for Prophet Zakariya, upon him be peace. Accordingly, Allah the Exalted accepted his supplication and Prophet Yahya was born, and Allah the Exalted granted Prophet Sulayman, upon him be peace, the kingdom, and Prophet Ayyub, upon him be peace, was cured of his illness. Therefore, every person who supplicates with whichever of the Divine Names according to his need, it will be accepted, and he will obtain his desire.

Among the masters, it was their practice that when a student or disciple came to their service, they would recite the Names from Allah the Exalted before him, and while mentioning the Names, they would look at his face. Then, whichever Name they found him suited to, they would teach that Name to the student and disciple. From this, his need would be fulfilled. From its continuity, gates of unveiling would open for him, because in it are the Name and its effect—there is great influence in it.

The knowledge of the Greatest Name is among the noblest of sciences. The Greatest Name is a hidden precious pearl and a concealed treasure. It is from the coverings of those books. The curtains of glory and the veil of majesty are cast over it. Its extraction belongs not to the power of the Dominion; the circle of the Celestial Kingdom revolves around it. Their example has been given, and they are those difficult matters of the religion which only the great scholars know.

This is the matter of its greatness: that it controls the noble aspects of its honor, that it sends this described noble luminous power when it is praiseworthy, and when its greatness is extolled—if the types of negation and sanctification are various for it.

Its hearing is sufficient and best for you. It is according to the seriousness of the effects, so that the noble tongue becomes associated with its mention. Or it is that courage is increased for him who follows it and recites it. He presents it before those who return to it. He is one who loves the knowledge of the Names, whether he is a body or a scholar who supplicates with it. They are the noble Names and the honored attributes. They are the coolness of the sun and its radiant majesty. With it, eyes are brightened and the narrations are good. This is a strange thing: that the supplicant supplicates with it and it is not accepted. This Greatest Name is not devoid of any worship behind the worships. In its light shining, it will be, and it will be the leader of all the other Names. This is the proof that it is the Greatest Name. The saying of Allah the Exalted is:

"To Allah belong the beautiful names; so call upon Him by them."

And for Allah the Exalted are the beautiful names—so supplicate to Him by them. The virtue of these beautiful names is from Allah the Exalted; Allah the Exalted arranged it in all remembrance, which is the proof that it is the best of the names. For this reason also it has been said that the Names are of the Name—
