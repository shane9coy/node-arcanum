# Page 219 (English translation)
And what is intended by the noble verse "They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them and do what they are commanded"—

The Operation of Love and Reconciliation:

"They are before you and behind you, and the voices have been lowered for the Compassionate. You hear the soft voice. O servants of these names and the noble invitation, you are with the Beloved of the drum of hidden and explicit grace. Take the work upon yourselves and you shall not speak the speech of the Slain One of Luminous Light except for what the Compassionate commands and what one has spoken well." O servants of this Surah, make such-and-such person firm in command near me, that he may hear my word, obey my command, fulfill my needs, and whatever I ask from him, he shall give it all for the sake of this noble verse. Allah the Exalted commands them, and they do not disobey Him, and they do only what He commands them.

Among its special properties is the seeking of love, and also reconciliation between two people in permissible matters. One should act for permissible purposes and not for forbidden ones, because whoever uses it at an improper time will suffer harm and his operation will be invalidated.

When you intend to bring reconciliation between two enemies, you should recite upon some food item, and after the Surah, also recite these words upon it and blow:

*Arabic text: "Place your trust, O servants of this noble Surah, with permanent love and affection between so-and-so son of so-and-so, by the right of this Surah upon you and its obedience with you."*

O servants of this noble Surah, take upon yourselves the work of permanent love and friendship between so-and-so son and so-and-so between them. Then give that food item to both of them to eat, and they will reconcile with each other and never be separated.

Every desire will be fulfilled

And among its properties is that when you recite it fourteen times after Asr and the Beautiful Names of Allah once, then maintain continuity of its recitation every day, and after your recitation in this manner, read it for forty days. (Truly, its appointee will come under your control and will be your helper in whatever task you wish.) Understand this matter and fumigate with "white sandal and black grain."

This is the supplication of this noble Surah:

*Arabic text: "O Allah, I ask You, O Allah, O Creator, O Finder, O Unique, O Self-Sufficient, O Odd-Numbered, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O Extender, O Self-Sufficient, O Enricher, Mahmahib, Mahmahib, of hidden grace, by ṣa'ṣa' with light of radiance, humble yourselves, and by Allah to whom belongs greatness and majesty, ṣamṣamun, of beauty, radiant ṭalhob, of might, lofty, O Hū, Mahlahūb, by Allah who has subjugated every light with His light, by ṭahṭahūb, lahūb, answer, O servants of this Surah and servants of the Name of Allah the Great and Most Great, with the subjugation of hearts of creation and the drawing of sustenance, and place your trust by the spirituality of love, with permanent love, in the name of Allah whose light has burned the curtains and necks have humbled before His majesty and mountains have crumbled before His awe and thunder has glorified Him in praise and the angels from fear of Him. He is Allah besides whom there is no god, the Lord of the Great Throne."*
