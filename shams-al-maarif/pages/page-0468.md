O You who have veiled the hearts of enemies from seeing the stations of the noble and righteous ones, I ask You by Your standing imagination and the purity of Your eternal pole, to protect me from the cunning of Satan, and to admit me into the house of security and safety, and make me hopeful by the right of whoever possessed in the gardens. O Strong in proof, O Great in status and beneficence, O Lord of the worlds.

The name Al-'Azīz: This name is very great. Its effect is unparalleled. It is the ruler of victorious conquerors. Every leader's rule is over 1001 rows. In every row there are 1001 angels. The supplication is:

O You who cause harm, You are the avenger against the people of denial and ingratitude, and You are the conqueror of whoever rebelled and broke covenants, and You are the one who humbles whoever defiled Your religion. I ask You by the greatness of Your compassion and by the permanence of Your might, to repel from me the harm of standing before anyone other than You, and grant me the vision of Your face, and let me see none but You, and grant me complete repentance from You, so that I may win by the covering of Your pleasure and success by the goodness of Your imagination, O Lord of the worlds.

Chapter 30: The tenth method of the beneficial secrets of the Divine Names

These ten Divine Names—Al-Ḥaqq, Al-Quddūs, Al-Khabīr, Al-Hādī, Al-Ḥayy, Al-Qayyūm, Al-Awwal, Al-Akhir, Al-Ẓāhir, Al-Bāṭin—contain within them subtleties, good character, love, purification of the soul, and many hidden things: revelation of the unseen, unveiling of hearts, and the special properties of graves. Upon their recitation, blessings descend, purity of the outer and inner is achieved, and enemies are destroyed. Their mention is in the source of salvation. The answer to every question becomes known to them through inspiration. Their sustenance will be expansive. The rivers of wisdom will flow from their heart and tongue. No hidden thing will be concealed from them. Their desires will be fulfilled by the grace and favor of Allah the Most High. Their heart becomes illuminated and filled with light. By this inner light, every wonder of earth and heaven and every other hidden and strange creature becomes under their observation.

The name Al-Ḥaqq: This great name is beneficial for every purpose. If its numerical square of 139 is drawn as a talisman and worn around the neck, every ruler and king will be awed by it. They will prevail over every enemy.

Istikhārah (Seeking divine guidance):

The names Al-Khabīr, Al-Mubīn, Al-Hādī—if someone wishes to know something and recites these names one thousand times then sleeps, Allah will inform them in a dream. An angel will tell them. After every hundred repetitions, they should recite this:

Show me the news within them, help me, O Knowing One, guide me, O Guide.

After completing the count of one thousand, continue reciting these names until sleep comes. If nothing is known on the first day, let them continue this practice for three consecutive days, Allah willing, knowledge will be obtained. Let them write these three holy names or dissolve them, or mix honey with fresh rose water, and wash with it, then drink from a Chinese bowl three times. Divine understanding and wisdom will be obtained. They will become a master in their craft.

The names Al-Ḥayy Al-Qayyūm: From their taste, the gates of pleasure open. The heart receives life, the heart engages in remembrance, and the soul gains strength. Inspiration alone is its lantern on this path. They should recite these after the remembrance of Sūrat Al-Fātiḥah, Yāsīn, and Ad-Ḍuḥā. In this [practice] lies complete pleasure, remembrance, expansive sustenance, obedience and sincerity remain established. The outer and inner become illuminated.
