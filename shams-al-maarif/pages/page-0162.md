# Page 162 (English translation)
He who keeps me Muslim until morning, and his reward will be with Allah.

The blind man said, "I will." The blind man took his hand and brought him to his home and entertained him very well. At night, when this blind man got up for the call of nature, he heard that the person who was his guest was repeatedly reciting this supplication. Allah the Most High placed in the heart of this blind man that he should also memorize this supplication. So he memorized it. He performed ablution and prayed two units of prayer, then continued reciting this supplication. Before morning came, his sight had returned and his eyes had become bright. He searched for this guest in every way but could find no trace of him anywhere. It became known that he was a saint of Allah.

The supplication is this:

Allāhumma rabba l-arwāḥi l-fāniyati wal-ajsādi l-bāliyati as'aluka bi-ṭā'ati l-arwāḥi l-rāji'ati ilā ajsādihā l-fulti'imi bi-'urūqihā wa bi-ṭā'ati l-qubūri l-mushaqqaqati 'an ahlihā wa da'awātika l-ṣādiqati fīhim wa akhdhika l-ḥaqqa minhum wa qiyāmi l-khalqi kullihim min makhāfatika wa shiddati sultānika yantaẓirūna faḍlaka wa yakhāfūna 'adhābaka as'aluka an taj'ala n-nūra fī baṣarī wal-ikhlāṣa fī 'amalī wash-shukra fī qalbī wa dhikraka fī lisānī bil-layli wan-nahāri mā abqaytanī yā Allāhu yā rabba l-'ālamīna wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhi l-'aliyyi l-'aẓīmi wa ṣallallāhu 'alā sayyidinā muḥammadin wa ālihi wa ṣaḥbihi wa sallama taslīman kathīran āmīn

Translation: O Allah! Lord of the perishable souls and decaying bodies, I ask You through the obedience of the souls returning to their bodies united with their veins, and through the obedience of the graves split open from their inhabitants and Your truthful supplications among them, and Your taking of truth from them, and the standing of all creation due to Your fear and the severity of Your sovereignty, who await Your grace and fear Your punishment, I ask You to make light in my sight, sincerity in my action, gratitude in my heart, and Your remembrance in my tongue, by night and day, as long as You keep me alive. O Allah, Lord of the Worlds, and there is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great, and may Allah send abundant blessings upon our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace. Amen.

[Ornate Heading: Chapter: The Five Noble Verses, Their Properties and Benefits]

Know that among them is the Greatest Name [of Allah]. Each one of them contains ten Qāfs. They have a strange story. It is that a minister of a king became envious of the king in proportion to the king's attention towards him. One day, the king told the executioner that when the minister came and the king gave him a signal, he should immediately cut off his head. But what happened was that a saint of illuminated conscience came from before the minister and the minister's anger changed into love, and he gestured to the executioner to go back. Exactly this happened—when the minister came, the king signaled repeatedly but did not order his killing, and when the minister approached the king, his enmity transformed into love.
