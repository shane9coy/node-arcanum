Then, after each prayer, recite Ayat al-Kursi twelve times and read this supplication:

O Allah, You are the One of fierce seizing, the painful, the mighty seizing, the One of absolute domination, exalted above all opposites and equals, the One sanctified from having a spouse or offspring. I ask You for domination over enemies and for crushing the tyrants. You plot against whom You will, and You are the best of plotters. I ask You by Your name at which hearts and foreheads have humbled, by which You cast terror into the hearts of enemies, and by which You extinguished the people of wretchedness. I ask You to aid me with a subtle portion from among the subtle aspects of this name, which flows through my limbs for the sake of the universal and the particular, until I am enabled to do what I will to whom I will, so that no oppressor reaches me with evil, and no arrogant one assaults me. Make my anger subservient to You and my purpose conjoined with Your purpose. Blind the sight of my enemies, harden their hearts, and place between me and them a veil whose inner aspect contains mercy and whose outer aspect from Your side is punishment. Surely Your seizing is fierce, the punishment is painful. And такова taking of your Lord when He seized the settlements while they were wrongful. Surely His seizing is painful, fierce. And by what He has made clear from the verses of the Qur'an, Allah seized them for their sins, and they had no protector from Allah. Surely the seizing of your Lord is fierce; thus He seized them with an ever-increasing seizure, and the roots of the wrongdoing people were cut off. And praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds. O Allah, by the blessing of these verses and by the secret of what I have invoked You with, I ask You to subdue my enemies and whoever intends evil against me, for He is the Compeller above His servants. [handwritten insert: the Compeller (al-Qahhar)] So-and-so son of so-and-so, for I seek refuge in You against his neck and suffice me from his evil, and turn away from me his treachery and his cunning, O Lord of the worlds.

O Allah, O One of fierce seizing, O One of painful punishment, O Mighty, O One of domination—Your essence is exalted above all opposites and equals, sanctified. I ask You for domination over enemies and the destruction of tyrants. You erase the cunning of whom You will, and You are the best at breaking and erasing cunning. I ask You by Your name at which hearts became humble and foreheads bowed, by which terror was cast into the hearts of enemies, and for which You decreed wretchedness for the people of misfortune. I ask You to grant me assistance with a subtle essence from the subtle aspects of this name, which pervades my limbs both in universal and particular ways, so that I may have power to perform every act I desire. Let no oppressor be able to reach me with evil intent. Merge my anger with Your anger; conjoin my purpose with Your purpose. Extinguish the sight of my enemies and harden their hearts. Place a veil between me and them whose inner aspect contains mercy and whose outer aspect brings punishment from Your side. Surely You are one of fierce seizing and painful punishment. Thus is the seizing of your Lord when He seizes the settlements while they are wrongful. Surely Your seizing is painful and fierce. And these are the amazing verses of the Noble Qur'an concerning it. The Most High Allah seized them for their sins. O Allah, I ask You by the blessing of these verses, that You subdue my enemies and those among them who harbor evil intentions against me, and Allah is dominant over His servants.

[handwritten insertion: He is dominant over His servants. So-and-so son of so-and-so will achieve dominance, and no one will be able to save him from Allah's domination. Therefore,] seek Allah's protection from the evil of so-and-so son of so-and-so, and his evil will end. By their blessing, we will dominate the enemy with speech. By the blessing of this, Allah the Most High will keep him safe, preserve him, and place him in a state of triumph and victory if Allah wills—and this will indeed be good.
