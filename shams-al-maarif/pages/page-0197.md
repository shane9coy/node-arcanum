# Page 197 (English translation)
O You, the Being whose throne encompasses the heavens and the earth, O You whose power is very vast and whose majesty is over all things—indeed, I ask that You expand my sustenance for me, remove hardship from me, forgive my sins with Your generosity and grace, and instill within me the assistance of the Supreme Name. The protection of these two is not heavy upon Him, and He is Exalted and Great.

O Allah, O Hayy Qayyum, I ask You in the right of this noble verse and the Exalted Names that You help me against the person who wronged me. Take vengeance upon him who took vengeance upon me, and upon him who intended deceit, treachery, and ill-will against me. How swiftly Your grip descends—it is very severe—upon every tyrannical rebellious one and every rejected Satan! Upon him who rebelled in corruption and strove for corruption, I ask You for help.

O Allah, I ask You in the right of this noble verse and the Exalted Names that You have mercy upon me and make me among Your righteous servants—those upon whom no fear shall come and who shall not grieve.

O our Lord, accept our prayer. Truly You are the Hearing, the Knowing.

O our Lord, grant us patience, keep our feet firm, and help us against the disbelieving nation. O our Lord, do not distort our hearts after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. Truly You are the Forgiving. All might and power belong to Allah.

O You whose throne is vaster than the heavens and the earth, turn away from me that which brings grief and harm, and with Your help, transform my heart. Grant joy to this subdued heart among the humbled. O You who strengthens after weakness, strengthen my heart after weakness. Raise a banner for me upon my head that the whole world may see. Truly I am wronged—O Allah, grant me the reward of the wronged. Truly You know what I do not know.

O Self-Sufficient, remove from me that which prevents me from poverty.

O Allah, O Ali, O Azeem—how supremely elevated You are in majesty and greatness. Make me with Your humble creation. Deliver me from the nation of wrongdoers. Help me with Your beloved angels. Make all the hearts of Your creation subservient to me, O Arham Ar-Rahimin. The protection of these two is not difficult upon Him, and He is Exalted and Great.

O Allah, I ask You—O Allah, O Hayy, O Qayyum—that You deliver me and my dependents from the oppressors. Enter me into the treasuries of Bismillahi Ar-Rahmani Ar-Rahim, beneath whose shade is Al-Hamdu Lillahi Rabbi Al-Alamin, and whose key is La Ilaha Illa Allah Muhammadun Rasulullah.
