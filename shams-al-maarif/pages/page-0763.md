O my God, exalted is Your glory, exalted is Your majesty, exalted is Your holiness, exalted is Your secret, O Lord of beautiful names, O Lord of great deeds, O Most High above all ascending-places of every ascension. Verily I have come to Your noble supreme Name, and every ladder for ascent has its ascent by Your Name and its beginning. I have adorned myself in Your Names, so the adornment has appeared in Your deeds, until the universe has shone with the radiance of Your manifestation. And every monotheist only exists by what has appeared to him of Your manifestation, and the secret of what has darkened in it from the knowledge of Your Names turns away, and he knows by what has attached to it those who have learned Your knowledge in its primacy from his creation by You. You are the lifter of degrees; for each one, his upbringing is by You, and his nearness is from You. I ask You by the right of the secrets of Your Names and the special properties of Your knowledge to lift my existence to the heaven of my glory by You upon an ascension of Your providence, and Your strong Name has become beneath me, and Your preceding Name before me, and Your guiding Name behind me, and Your preserving Name at my right, and Your impenetrable Name at my left. So I do not see You in the lap of Your Names, looking upon those other than myself, as the unseen looks upon the seen. So we do not reach the soul except by the influence of what You have guided me to, nor do the beneficial actions reach from me except by what Your arrow of protection strikes me with, which You throw at whoever has thrown evil at me, O Lord of Israel and Izrael and Azrael and Jibreel. And there is no power nor strength except by God.

Whoever has recited this prayer until morning, upon him will appear such a manifestation of divine majesty that all the hidden sciences will become manifest to him. And his sign is that within his heart there will arise dread and fear, especially on dark nights, but this dread and fear will pass after a short while. And this prayer is also extremely beneficial. One should recite it in the fourth hour on Sunday, which is attributed to the Moon and has a cold temperament; it has a strange effectiveness in summoning enemies and instilling love.

The love produced by this practice never ends. It is very beneficial for heat-related diseases:

This love is never destroyed, and it is an excellent treatment for diseases of hot imprisonment, and the prayer is this:

My Lord, receive me with the light of Your hidden Name, a reception that fills my existence, outwardly or inwardly, until You erase all the shares of the forms within me. You are the guardian of my existence from my existence, a secret that the pen of Your decree has concealed from every depositor in a resting place and a deposit, within a deposit. So nothing is hidden from God of what has been absent from the observers, by You. And look upon me, O lover, with the light of Your concealed written Name, until I see the absolute perfection in the divine realm and the verified good, O Possessor of perfection, O Depositor of lights in the hearts of His righteous servants, O Swift, O Near, O Responder, O Bestower.
