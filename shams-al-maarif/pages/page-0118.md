# Page 118 (English translation)
118

O Mighty, O Victorious, O Eternal, O Inheritor, O Compeller, O Creator of all that exists after the annihilation of creation, by that which You intended and by that ordaining which You decreed, O You whose essence has no beginning and no end, O You from whom there is no severance. On that Day when Allah will not humiliate His Prophet, peace be upon him, nor the believers. Surely on that Day humiliation and evil shall be upon the disbelievers. O You of severe punishment, O You of severe seizure, surely Your seizure is severe. Surely they shall be wretched and in Hellfire. There shall be for them horrific voices and groaning. Surely the Zaqqum tree is the food of the sinful, like boiling water that scalds the belly. O Honored, O Dominant who has no equal, all authorities belong to You alone, You are the Absolute Eternal Dominant. No inheritor can inherit Your honor. O You who said "He is the Truth," surely Hell surely flames and strips the skin from heads, neither shaded nor able to protect from the flames. O Inheritor, You are the One to whom all affairs return, You alone shall annihilate all creation and call out: "Whose is the sovereignty this Day?" Then He shall declare: "It belongs to Allah, the One, the Mighty." Whoever has any habit, whether outward or inward, returns to You. O Allah, pour down destruction, ruin, and punishment upon so-and-so, and take vengeance. On this Day, call not upon one destruction but seize upon many destructions. O Compeller, You are the One whose command, when it proceeds, no excuser's excuse can repel. You are the One who erased the densities of bodies and the personal faculties and powers. This is an obligation, however, upon one who performed your incantation and whom You have made a means for Your selfness and made him a quality of Your eternity for the manifestation of Your wrath. Surely You are One of power, might, and Lordship. By Your greatness which You have chosen, and by Your great awe and Your importance, and by Your lights of gnosis, no one knows Your majesty besides You. Your sovereignty is very great. Indeed in all dominion of the spiritual and angelic realms there is movement, its measure is by Your command, O You of excellent thousand goodness, by which You have chosen good and evil. O Eternal, Glorious, and Exalted, manifest Your severity of power in so-and-so, and also Your wrath, by which his faculties become mute before me and his spirit becomes cold before me. Surely Hell is the promised place for all of them, and surely for Hell We have created many jinn and humans. O Creator of the heavens and earth, I ask You by that power with which You created the upper and lower worlds, and by that First Word with which You created earth and heaven by Your truthful saying. Then He turned toward the heaven when it was smoke, and He said to earth and heaven: "Come, willingly or unwillingly." And they said: "We come willingly." And do the same for me.

And mention your need, and the desired object will be obtained.

And Allah is the One who grants success.
