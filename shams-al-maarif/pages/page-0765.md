# Page 765 (English translation)
765

Destroying and humiliating by it every arrogant one is in Your power and strength, O Lord of compulsion, O Compeller, O Lord of the worlds

By the blessing of this prayer enemies will be destroyed:
Whoever reads this prayer eighty times at this hour, then curses any oppressor, that oppressor will immediately be destroyed. But before this, ten units of prayer with five salutations should be performed, and in each unit one should recite Surah al-Fatihah together with verses of this subject:
"Wa kadhalika akhthu rabbika ida akhatha al-qura wa hiya dhalimatun"
"And thus was the taking of your Lord when He took the cities while they were wrongdoers"

And from the Beautiful Names, these names are suitable for it: Al-Qahir, Al-Qadir

And this prayer is most excellent. Let him read it at the second hour on Sunday:
"Ta'layta ya man taqassara kullu fikrin 'an wasfi hasara maghani asmaihi fa kullu rif'atin wa kullu 'uluvin fa min baynika al-rif'atu wa al-'uluvu suduruhu dhähiran wa batinan taqaddasa majdaka ya man istanara 'arshuhu wa bahura kibriyaahu as'aluka bi al-sifati allati la ta'allaqa laha bi mawjudin siwakka ya man lahu al-'azamatu wa al-kibriya'u ya dha al-jalali wa al-ikram ya man lahu al-jamalu wa al-baha'u wa al-kamal as'aluka al-uns bi sirri muqabalati al-qadri a lam tara mahbub al-nari wa khashyati al-dhikri hatta yajtaniba wa fayyin bika fala yataharraku dhu tabi'in bi mukhalafati al-asghari li-'azamatika wa 'atisha li-kibriya'ika innaka jabbru al-ardi wa al-sama'i wa qahiru al-kulli bi qahrika ya mujib"
"Exalted are You, O He whose glory all minds fall short of describing the essence of the abodes of His names—for every elevation and every highness, elevation and highness issue from You. Their breasts are narrowed outwardly and inwardly. Holy is Your glory, O He whose Throne is illuminated and whose majesty shines forth. I ask You by the attributes that have no attachment to any existing thing besides You, O He to whom belong greatness and majesty, O Possessor of majesty and honor, O He to whom belong beauty and splendor and perfection. I ask for intimacy through the secret of confronting fate. Have you not seen the beloved of the Fire and the fear of Remembrance until he avoids it and swears by You, so that no one of nature moves in opposition to the lesser for Your majesty and thirsts for Your majesty? Surely You are the Compeller of the earth and heaven and the Conqueror of all by Your compulsion, O Responder."

Whoever reads this prayer seventy times at this hour, Allah the Most High will make him famous throughout all the world with good reputation. From the verses, "Hatta iza ista'asa al-rusulu" (Until when the messengers despaired) etc. is suitable for it, and from the Beautiful Names: Al-Hayyu Al-Qayyumu Al-Hafizu Al-Sani'u. And the time of the last third of the night is suitable for it, and by this a request will be fulfilled.

This prayer suffices for every important matter

"Ilahi bima adraktahu suradatu al-jalali min masuni asmai ka wa ya badi'u as'aluka bi taqdisi al-kurubiyyina wa bi haybati munajatati al-saffina wa tasbihi al-muqarrabina ya subbhu ya quddu su rabba al-mala'ikati wa al-ruhi ya man ansa al-arwaha fi al-arsakhi wa sawwara al-fajra al-murakkabati bi nuri al-takhkhsisi wa ruhi al-asmai hatta anwaruhu fi kulli maknunin bi nurin tanharu bihi a'yunu al-hamidiyna mina al-jinni wa al-insi hatta tanqabizu quwahum bayna al-ibsasi 'ayni al-khuffashi min nuri al-shamsi fala yastati'un muqabilin bihi Halatun minka dhatuka al-nuru wa sifatuka al-nuru ismuka al-nuru wa fi'luka al-nuru wa 'arshuka al-nuru wa kursiyyuka al-nuru wa qalamuka al-nuru wa lawhuka al-nuru wa mala'ikatuka anwarun fi sami'i al-nuri wa subhana wajhika al-baqi li-nurin muta'alliqun bi al-'ilmi fi zahrhi nurun wa kullu qa'imin bina nurun la ya'ma ilayhim min asmai ka munghamisun fi al-nuri fa ijma' hijri [likely intended for fa ij'al hissi] wa basari wa batini wa zahiri fi kulli amrin yatala'alu nuran 'ala nurin anta al-'aliyu al-kabiru al-muta'ali wa anta 'ala kulli shay'in qadir"
"O my God, by what the pavilions of glory have encompassed from the sanctified aspects of Your names, and O Originator, I ask You by the sanctification of the cherubim and by the majesty of the supplication of the ranks of the righteous and by the glorification of the near-ones: O Holy, O Most Holy, Lord of the angels and the Spirit, O He who gave companionship to spirits in the depths and formed the dawn of composite beings with the light of specialization and the spirit of the names, until His lights shine in every hidden thing with a light through which the eyes of the praisers from jinn and humans are dazzled, until their powers contract between the closed eyes of the bat from the light of the sun, and they cannot face it. From You is the essence, the light, the attribute is light, Your name is light, Your action is light, Your throne is light, Your footstool is light, Your pen is light, Your preserved tablet is light, and Your angels are lights among the hearing of light. And glory be to Your everlasting Face, to which light is attached by knowledge; upon its back is light, and every one standing by us is a light—the eyes do not become blind to them. From Your names, immersed in light, so make my hearing and my sight and my inner and outer being in every matter shine with light upon light. You are the Exalted, the Great, the Most High, and You have power over all things."

Whoever reads this prayer starting from dawn until the dawn of the next day, certainly the help of [unclear] will be at the end of every [illegible].
