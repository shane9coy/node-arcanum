# Page 236 (English translation)
And he who has not told anyone, nor has anyone heard from him, nor is there anyone equal to Him. I ask You that You subdue for me the servants of this noble Surah, so that they obey my command which I desire—indeed, You are the one who does whatever He wills.

O servants of this Surah, I have adjured you by Him in whom you believe, but that you accept quickly. At that time, three muwakkals (attendants) will present themselves to you. Their faces will be shining like the moon on the night of the full moon, which the eye cannot gaze upon steadily. They will say to you: "Peace be upon you, O righteous servant, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings." They will say: "We are the muwakkals of this noble Surah." You should say: "What do you want from me?" Then greet them as well. At that time, give this response: "I desire from you the honor, glory, and reverence of this noble Surah—that you serve me and obey me in the matters I command you. And it is obligatory upon me that I ask from you that need by which the Lord is pleased."

They will say: "We have heard and will obey." They will say: "We also have two conditions from today: that you will not commit any sin from this time forward, and you will not speak falsehood. You must not eat garlic, onions, or fish. Keep a fast every Thursday perpetually. Bathe every Friday. Recite Surah al-Ikhlas ten thousand (10,000) times and donate its reward to deceased Muslims. Every week, go to the cemetery and recite Surah al-Ikhlas eleven times and donate its reward to the souls. Do not break the Thursday fast except when it is a festival day, then break the fast." You should say: "Yes, that Allah guides to the straight path, and Allah is a witness to this." You accept these conditions, and then they will depart with a handshake. They will say: "From today, you are our brother. Whatever need you ask, we will fulfill."

You should say: "Each one of you, give me a sign by which I may recognize you." One of them will say: "My name is Abd al-Wahid. When you recite the Surah and say 'O Abd al-Wahid,' I will come immediately. I will take you to Makkah al-Musharrafah in a single moment." The second will say: "My name is Abd al-Samad. Call me in this manner, and whatever food, drink, gold, silver, and every lawful thing of the earth you ask for, I will bring to you." The third will say: "My name is Abd al-Rahman. Recite the Surah and say 'O Abd al-Rahman,' and I will answer by the command of Allah the Exalted. I will hide you from people's eyes and bring you news of cities." Then give thanks to Allah the Exalted for this blessing. Say to them: "May Allah give you the best reward. Guard this and keep it hidden from ignorant people—for this is a trust from me."

The Invocation of Surah al-Humazah and Its Properties

When you intend this practice, purify your body and clothes in an empty house and sit in seclusion. Recite one hundred (100) times istighfar (seeking forgiveness) and one hundred times durood sharif, then perform two units of prayer. In each unit, recite Surah al-Fatihah once and Surah al-Ikhlas five hundred (500) times. In the second unit, do the same. Perform ghusl with frankincense. Then place your head upon both your knees and, with pure intention and presence of heart, recite Surah al-Humazah once. You may see the form of any person you wish, or you may transform into the form of a swordsman. Recite Surah al-Inshiqaq so that your need may be fulfilled.
