They used to reduce [their food] by an amount equal to the pit of a date. Some people did not reduce [it], but rather delayed [eating] until they would eat once every seven days, ten days, or forty days. Some people would place a wet piece of wood equal in weight to their food; as it dried out, they would correspondingly reduce their food. Ḥaḍrat Suhayl—may God be pleased with him—says: "Whoever remains hungry for forty days, the signs of divine power become manifest in the celestial realm. We have revealed the mysteries of spiritual practice and clarified the path. Understand it well."

[The Composition of the Prayer of Kafaliyah]

Whenever your soul desires, pray six rakʿahs and sit down, then recite this:

Subḥān Allāhi wa-ḥamdu lillāhi wa-lā ilāha illā Allāhu wa-Allāhu akbaru wa-lā ḥawla wa-lā quwwata illā billāhi l-ʿAliyyi l-ʿAẓīmi

Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa-ʿalā ālihi wa-aṣḥābihi wa-azwājihi ummahāti l-muʾminīna kamā ṣallayta ʿalā Ibrāhīma wa-ʿalā āli Ibrāhīma innaka ḥamīdun majīdun

Then after that, say the Takbīr and, after performing prostration, recite Sūrat al-Fātiḥah seven times and Āyat al-Kursī seven times, and say this:

Lā ilāha illā Allāhu waḥdahu lā sharīka lahū lahū l-mulku wa lahū l-ḥamdu yuḥyī wa yumītu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr

Then recite this ten times:

Allāhumma innī asʾaluka bi-maqāʿidi l-ʿizzi min ʿarshika wa muntahā l-raḥmati min kitābika wa-bi-ḥaqqi ismika l-ʿaẓīmi l-aʿẓami wa majdika l-aʿlā wa kalimātika l-tāmmati

Then ask for whatever need you have and raise your head. Slaughter an unblemished lamb such as is [offered] in sacrifice. If no lamb is available, a goat or sheep is also sufficient. However, one should slaughter while in a secluded place facing the qiblah, and at the time of slaughter speak these words: Allāhumma hādhā minka wa ilayka fa-ijʿalhu fidāʾī wa taqabbal minnī (O God, this is from You and to You, so make it my ransom and accept it from me). Then dig a pit in that same place and bury its filth in it. Divide the meat into portions and distribute [it] among the poor and the needy, or cook excellent food and feed them, and distribute seven dirhams among seven needy persons. He will then be protected from whatever evil he fears.
