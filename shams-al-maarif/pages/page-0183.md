# Page 183 (English translation)
[Composed] for one whom I have made witness [to that which is] unknown, for whom You will, through what resembles it, a multiplicity that does not diminish the unity of what You have perfected in its definitive form, O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord. And I ask You, O Allah, by the mystery of the connection binding between my presence of obligation and possibility, requiring the manifestation of the greatest attribute by the ambiguous Name for the essences of those of lesser rank, a merciful manifestation from beginning to end, by the vastness of the knowledge of the Rahmaniyya which is infinite, or by stability and permanence from the particular effusion of the Rahmaniyya that elevates the witnessing of the affirmation of approach by the unknown proximity of the Qahiriyya from You, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Opener, O Knowing. I ask You for illumination, insight, assistance, success, protection, guardianship, concealment, perfection, the bringing of sustenance and blessing therein, hope, good opinion of You, and despair of other than You. And I ask You by the right of the chain, by creation not command, and by perfection, and by Your generosity and the blessing of the kingdom of the sanctity of Your Name, and blessed is Your majesty, and there is no god besides You. In You we believe and to You we submit, and upon You we rely. O Allah, by Your light and by the light of Your Name, increase us beyond other than You, for it is not in You, O Allah, the witnessing of the Self, O Merciful. Peace be upon the word from a Lord, Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by the right of this blessed supplication that You fulfill my need, which is such and such.

I begin with the name of Allah the Most High, who is most merciful and most compassionate. O my Lord, I ask You by Your Name with which You have opened the heavens and the earth. With this manifestation which is deserving of the proportions of descent, which is for the binding of truth in the visible and the hidden as You willed, and indeed You know the manifest from the known things—multiplicity in His unity does not create any obstacle, for which You have issued a decree. O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord. I ask You by the mystery of the connection which is between obligation and possibility, which is in accordance with the manifestation of the greatest attribute with the ambiguous Name for the proof of divinity in general, and specifically in the beginning and end, from this summary upon which the mercy became general and vast. Its stability and proof is the particular mercy which elevates the witnessing of the affirmation of approach from the unknown proximity of divinity. O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Opener, O Knowing. I ask You for heavenly light, divine assistance, success, protection, guardianship, concealment, perfection, good sustenance, hope, good opinion of You, and despair of other than You. I ask by the right of "Bismillah" of Your command of creation, the completion of Your generosity, and blessing from You. Your Name is blessed, Your majesty is most high, there is no god besides You, we have faith in You and submit to You, we trust in You. O Allah, O Light, turn us towards the light of Your Name and remove us from other than You. O Allah, I bear witness for You, O Merciful. Peace be upon the word from a Lord, Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You by the right of this blessed supplication that You fulfill my need.

And mention the name of your need wherein is the pleasure of Allah and seek that which is lawful.

Prayer for the Fulfillment of Needs

It has been transmitted that if a person [performs this], the need will be fulfilled before completing the deed. The supplication is:

O Allah, I ask You by virtue of You being Allah—there is none who can humiliate me with the humiliation of the date-palms, and I ask You by virtue of You being Allah in every state of the servant's states, the mean and the ignoble, and by virtue of You being Allah, the Sanctified, with the meanings of oneness and self-sufficiency, from cunning and force and whitening and purification, and by virtue of You being Allah, there is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing.
