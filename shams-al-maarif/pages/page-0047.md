# Page 47 (English translation)
19- Shula:
The nineteenth mansion is Shula. Its letter is lam-alif. When the moon descends into it, a mingled spirituality is produced from it. Therefore, one should not perform any worldly works during it.

20- Na'im:
The twentieth mansion is Na'im. Its letter is ra'. When the moon descends into it, a mingled spirituality is produced from it that purifies the hearts and gladdens the soul. Both religious and worldly affairs are excellent during it.

21- Baldah:
The twenty-first mansion is Baldah. Its letter is sin. When the moon descends into it, a mingled spirituality is produced from it. It neither benefits nor harms any work, and it is not favorable for worldly affairs.

22- Sa'd al-Dhabih:
The twenty-second mansion is Sa'd al-Dhabih. Its letter is ta'. When the moon enters into it, a mingled spirituality is produced from it. At this time one fears no movement or harm.

[Bracketed note: A faint circular stamp is visible between sections 22 and 23 with the text "Muhammad Munir Hanafi"]

23- Sa'd Bala':
The twenty-third mansion is Sa'd Bala'. Its letter is tha'. When the moon enters it, a continuous-flowing spirituality is produced from it. All good works are best performed during it.

24- Sa'd al-Sa'ud:
The twenty-fourth mansion is Sa'd al-Sa'ud. Its letter is kha'. When the moon descends into it, an extremely noble and steadfast spirituality is produced. It proves to be a helper and assistant for all good works. Good works are appropriate during it.

25- Sa'd al-Akhbiyah:
The twenty-fifth mansion is Sa'd al-Akhbiyah. Its letter is zal. When the moon descends into it, a noble spirituality is produced from it which assists all good actions. Therefore, perform whatever good deed you wish during it.

26- Farq al-Muqaddam:
The twenty-sixth mansion is Farq al-Muqaddam. Its letter is dad. When the moon enters into it, a spirituality helpful for good works is also produced from it. Therefore, perform whatever good work you wish during it.

27- Farq al-Mu'akhkhar:
The twenty-seventh mansion is Farq al-Mu'akhkhar. Its letter is dad. When the moon enters it, a mingled spirituality is produced from it. Silence is preferable during this time.
