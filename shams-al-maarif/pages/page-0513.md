# Page 513 (English translation)
And there remain from it Afwādh of its count; Hāʾ has not been built and Jahar after it.
And it breaks ʿAmm and Ibn al-Zawja; and the Jahmī is the builder of a rotting corpse.
What a speaker, how excellent he is—possessed of a strong and precise character.
The severance of the forearms is a sign, and wide-chested, with a mole upon it.
And its judgment is with the ape in the Pyramids; and judge for it with the spouse in the Days.
And after it: Sīn, then Lām, and Alif; and Ghayn has no helper left for it.
And after it: Yā, Waw, Lām, Mīm, Qāf—to say: the entire count is but perdition.
The Aqraʿ fights Nā with Bayn; and the Turkūwātayn rule twice.
There remains no ʿAyn, Dāl, or Fā; it has made Syria entirely a homeland.
And the Thiqāh in the Shoobā is other than its need—contradicting and contradictory and Qājiyā.
And War descends upon the land of Syria; and with him a gathering of people.
And another: my heart is upon the bright one; free from any transaction and hypocrisy.
And whoever lives shall see a حقّاً مفوتاً [truth missed]—this, and if joy remains from it.
And the Nile for the destruction of Egypt; and the sea is drowning with every affliction.
And this is not for you therein except: our kings have been versified so that they may be falsely attributed.
It suffices to repel the qualities of every face; and that one in the small is many and unique.
And among our children there is disagreement in the letters; and few of them if they appear to be lacking.
So how many wars and Jilāf and Bayn—and the intention is to display what is in it like this.
And praise be to God, the Sublime the Powerful; for He is but to Him belongs the Revealer of secrets.
And praise be to God, the Great possessing constancy; and thanks be to God the Most High, indeed.
