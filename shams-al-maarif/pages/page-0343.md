# Page 343 (English translation)
343

It is that when any person faces a difficult matter, he should perform ablutions at night and offer a two-rak'ah prayer. In each rak'ah, he should recite Surah al-Fatihah once and Ayat al-Kursi three times. Then after the salutation, he should recite Ayat al-Kursi seven times and make this supplication:

Allāhumma anta tasma'u kalāmī wa tarā makānī wa ta'lamu sirrī wa 'alāniyatī, lā yakhfa 'alayka shay'un min amrī. Ad'ūka du'ā'a al-bā'is al-faqīr al-mustaghīth al-mu'tarif bi-dhambihi wa al-taqsīr. Wa as'aluka mas'alata al-miskīn wa abtahilu ilayka ibtihāla al-'abdi al-da'īf al-mudhnib al-ḥaqīr. Ibtihāla man khaḍa'at laka raqabatuhu wa fāḍat ilayka 'abratuhu wa aḍalla laka khadduhu wa raghima laka anfuhu. An tuḥyiya qulūbanā wa tashraḥa ṣudūranā wa taj'ala masā'inā khāliṣatan li-wajhika al-karīm wa sababa al-fawz ilā al-janati. Wa waffiqnā li-mā huwa maḥḍu riḍāka wa okhtim lanā minka bi-khayr. Wa aj'alnā ghadan ma'a alladhīna an'amta 'alayhim min al-nabiyyīna wa al-ṣiddīqīna wa al-shuhadā'i wa al-ṣāliḥīna wa ḥusna ulā'ika rafīqan. Wa okfinā mā ahammana min umūr al-dunyā wa al-ākhirati wa lā tushmit bīna al-a'dā'a wa lā tulzim al-ḥāsidīna wa lā taj'al muṣībatanā fī dīninā wa lā taj'al al-dunyā akbara hammana wa lā mablagha 'ilminā. Wa lā tusal-liṭ 'alaynā bi-dhunūbinā man lā yakhāfuka wa lā yarḥamnā. Wa matt'i'nā bi-asma'inā wa abṣārinā wa aḥyinā ḥayātan ṭayyibatan wa oftaḥ lanā abwāba al-khayr wa arzuqnā wa anta khayru al-rāziqīna. Rabbanā aghfir lanā wa li-ikhwāninā alladhīna sabaqūnā bi-al-īmān. Wa lā taj'al fī qulūbinā ghillan lil-ladhīna āmanū. Rabbanā innaka ra'ūfun raḥīm. Rabbanā aghfir lanā dhunūbanā wa isrāfanā fī amrinā wa thabbit aqdāmanā wa onṣurnā 'alā al-qawm al-kāfirīna. Rabbanā ātinā fī al-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fī al-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā 'adhāba al-nār bi-raḥmatika yā arḥama al-rāḥimīn.

O Allah! You hear my speech, You see my place, You know my secret and my open affairs. Nothing of my matter is hidden from You. I supplicate to You with the supplication of the distressed, poor one seeking help, who acknowledges his sins and shortcomings. And I ask of You as the poor ask, and I plead to You with the pleading of the weak, sinful, humble servant. The pleading of one whose neck has bent toward You, whose tears have flowed to You, whose cheek has become humbled before You, and whose nose has become covered with dust. [I plead] that You revive our hearts and expand our chests, make our efforts sincere for Your noble Face, and make [this] a means of salvation to Paradise. Grant us success in what is pure Your pleasure, and seal our fate with goodness from You. Make us tomorrow with those upon whom You have bestowed favors—the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous. And those are the best of companions. And suffice us in the matters that concern us in this world and the hereafter. Do not make our enemies gloat over us, do not burden us with the enviers, and do not place affliction in our religion. Do not make this world our greatest concern, nor the extent of our knowledge. Do not empower over us, because of our sins, one who does not fear You and does not have mercy upon us. Bestow us with benefit from our hearing and our sight. Give us a good life and open for us the doors of goodness. Provide for us, for You are the best of providers. O Allah, forgive our brothers who preceded us in faith, and do not place in our hearts any malice toward those who believe. O Allah, indeed You are [Most Compassionate] [Most Merciful]. O Allah, our Lord, forgive us our sins and our excess in our affair. Stabilize our feet and grant us victory over the disbelieving nation. O our Lord, grant us goodness in this world and goodness in the hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy.
