# Page 56 (English translation)
54

A most virtuous spirituality descends by the command of God Most High to set right the affairs of men. Affairs pertaining to women are not set right by it. The works of talismans and alchemy are superior in it, and every excellent craft is properly executed in it. It is good to begin every science in it. Engraving rings with seals and the like, treating the sick, and travel are also effective in it. The child born at this time lives life with felicity and good fortune. He becomes beloved of creation. His incense is frankincense, saffron, and mastagi. And God Most High knows best.

**Manzil Thurayyā:**
Its form is this: **. . .**: Its letter is jīm. When the Moon descends into it, hot and cold spirituality descends in the world by the command of God Most High. In it, the works of talismans, operations for women, and the preparation of medicines are superior. This time is also very good for travelers. Merchants gain very great profit in it. It is also auspicious for kings. Contracting marriage and purchasing concubines and slaves are better in it because at this time the Moon is in a state of equilibrium. Whatever work you do in it will have a good outcome. The child born in it will live a life of felicity and good fortune. He will hate evil. His end will be good; whoever is born in it will hate immorality and vice and love righteous people. Its incense is linseed and black seed.

**Manzil Debrān:**
Its form is this: **.**: Its letter is dāl. This is an earthly fortune. When the Moon descends into it, such spirituality descends in the world by the command of God Most High as spreads in the earth enmity, hatred, and corruption. At this time one should avoid beginning works and seeking dominion, and works of talismans and crafts should absolutely not be done. No work is superior in it. Yes, however, it is good for burying the dead, hoarding wealth, concealing secrets, digging wells, and building palaces. Apart from these, no work is superior in it. Whoever is born in it will live a long life. Its incense is sweet pomegranate peel and lisān al-dhikr.

**Manzil Haq'a:**
Its form is this: **.**: Its letter is hā'. When the Moon descends into it, it is mixed with felicity and inauspice. In it one should prepare antidotes to poisons and practice medicine. Alchemical works should not be done. Trees should not be planted in it, new clothes should not be worn, and marriage should not be contracted, because at this hour the outcome of work is not good. Its incense is frankincense, nad, lubān, jāwī, and mastagi. And God Most High knows best.

**Manzil Hni'a:**
Its form is this: **. . .**: Its letter is wāw. This is a fortunate influence. When the Moon descends into it, works of kindness and love should be done. Burn fine perfume. Go to kings and leaders. Strive in all desires and needs. Meet slaves who have fled and perform whatever works you wish. Contract marriage, take medicine, purchase concubines and slaves, acquire horses, plant gardens, build houses, travel—in short, whatever work you do. All works are good in it. Whoever is born in it will live a life of good fortune and [unclear] will be. Its incense is qaṭrab,1 kāfūr, white sandalwood, and shab'.2

(Footnotes:)
(1) Qaṭrab is a small plant/herb.
(2) Shab' is the name of a type of grass.
