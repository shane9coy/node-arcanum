# Page 53 (English translation)
When the moon is in a fiery sign, you should perform those operations which are suited to the works of fire. The same applies to the remaining signs. When a person in need comes to you, write his name, his mother's name, and the name of his desire as separate letters, then observe which element predominates among those letters. If it falls in a fiery, airy, earthy, or watery sign, perform the operation suited to it. Continue until the moon enters the sign which is desired. If the time is suitable, there will be success. The rule for determining the moon's sign is this: however many dates of the lunar month have passed, add five to them; then divide by fives starting from the sign in which the sun is. Wherever the number ends, in that sign is the moon.
