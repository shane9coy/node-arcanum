In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, Your mercy encompasses all things, and there is no deity except You, O Most Merciful of the merciful. You have ordained all things and perfected them with Your wisdom, and You have directed servants with the mercy of generality and the mercy of specificity. Glory be to You, You are Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. A sovereignty-possessing, eternal, singular, divine encompassing of bounty. Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Raḥīm" (the Merciful)

The exposition of the name al-Raḥmān has passed, and the exposition of the name al-Raḥīm remains. These two noble names share one derivation, but there is specialization in their usage. When we observe the things that are the effects and manifestations of mercy—such as rainfall, sustenance, reproduction, the passage from one form to another, the descent of the world and natures, and the deficiency of directions—indeed, all of this is mercy that joins together with the blessing of generality and specificity. Allah, the Exalted, says:

The worldly mercy is that which is in the world, while the mercy of the Hereafter is separate from it, and it is held in trust for the Day of Resurrection. Thus, for those who are people of means, the effects of mercy are manifest upon them, so that they may succeed in the Hereafter. For the people of spiritual knowledge, spirituality is established.

The good of religion, the world, and the Hereafter—all beauties are contained in the Basmalah:

The good of both religion and the world is gathered in "In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful." Thus, the Basmalah was first revealed upon His servant Adam, peace be upon him. Then upon Idris, peace be upon him, and then upon Solomon, peace be upon him. Accordingly, Allah, the Exalted, has said:

And Allah, the Exalted, had gathered for Solomon both goods—the good of the world and the good of the Hereafter—within it were sovereignty and prophecy. Thus, the secrets of spirituality were manifested upon him, and He made the entire creation subservient to him, and He granted him the secret of the Greatest Name from among mercy. Accordingly, the Chosen One (peace be upon him) says:

For the letters of "In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful," the Most Merciful of the merciful of this world and the Hereafter descends His mercy upon him who accepts the form of creation, the glimpse of whose mercy is beyond the limits of mercy's pretense.

The pure Master (peace be upon him) has commanded that upon every child whose teacher has made the reading of the Basmalah obligatory, he should read the supplication of teaching upon his inheritor. The debt shall be discharged, and whoever reads this name al-Raḥīm after every prayer in accordance with its numerical value, all its excellences shall be granted to him.
