# Page 401 (English translation)
339

Glory be to the One who liberates every indebted one. Glory be to the Holy One who frees every prisoner. Glory be to the Knower of everything hidden in the treasuries of His sovereignty, between the kāf and nūn. Glory be to He who, when He wills a thing, says to it "Be" and it becomes. 21 times. Glory be to Him, and bless and exalted be He. Glory be to your Lord, Lord of Glory, above what they describe. 21 times. In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.

Read it and ask for your need, and say this 7 times: "O Guide of the astray, there is no guide besides You. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor." O Allah, subjugate for me the kingdom and the malakūt. There is no deity except You, O Possessor of Glory and Generosity. O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, by You I seek help. O Helper, help me. Recite this 40 times and the supplication will be answered. Then recite this 36 times: "O Responder, answer my call and fulfill my need, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy, not among those upon whom wrath is incurred nor among those who are lost."

Then say: "O Allah, make me among those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not among those upon whom wrath is incurred, and whom You have made masters of the secrets of Your names. O Lord, O Merciful, O Compassionate. And cite for them the example—the companions of the town, as a clear sign—until: 'Glory be to the One who relieves every sorrowful one. Glory be to the One who frees every prisoner. Glory be to the One who liberates every indebted one. Glory be to the Holy One who frees every prisoner. Glory be to the Knower of everything hidden. Glory be to He whose treasuries are between the kāf and nūn. Glory be to He who, when He wills a thing, says to it 'Be' and it becomes.' 21 times. 'Glory be to your Lord, Lord of Glory.' 21 times. In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds. 'You have bestowed favor upon them'—up to this point, recite 21 times: 'O King of the Day of Judgment, "You have bestowed favor upon them"—up to this point.' Recite repeatedly the supplication, or: 'Guide of the astray' 7 times. 'There is no guide besides You. Guide us to the straight path, "You have bestowed favor upon them"—up to this point.' "O Allah, make me among those upon whom You have bestowed favor and whom You have made masters of the secrets of Your names. And make me, O Lord, O Merciful, O Compassionate, among those who fear their Lord in the unseen. So give them glad tidings of forgiveness and generous reward. O Allah, give me glad tidings on the Day of Your meeting of forgiveness and generous reward."

Then supplicate and the supplication will be accepted immediately. "Not among those upon whom wrath is incurred nor among those who are lost. O Manifest." 7 times. Then recite this 4 times: "O Allah, I ask You by Your great, most great name and by Your noble, chosen prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, that You do for me what I am worthy of. Indeed, You are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness."

Holy is His essence, who takes upon Himself the task of collecting payment from the debtor. He liberates every prisoner. He is the Knower of every hidden thing. His treasuries of sovereignty are between the kāf and nūn. Holy is His essence—He, when He wills a thing, says to it "Be" and it becomes. Holy is the essence of your Lord, who is Lord of Glory. O Guide of the astray—there is no guide besides You. Show us the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor. O Allah, subjugate for me the kingdom and dominion. There is no deity besides You. O Possessor of Glory and Generosity. O Ever-Living, O Sustainer—I seek help from You. O Helper, help me. O Acceptor, accept my supplication and fulfill my need. O Most Merciful of the merciful—do not show the path of those upon whom wrath was incurred, nor the path of the astray. O Allah, make me among those upon whom You bestowed favor and made them masters of the secrets of Your names. Holy is His essence—the one who removes sorrow from the sorrowful, who grants liberation to every prisoner. His treasuries are between the kāf and nūn. When He intends something, He says to it "Be" and it becomes. Holy is the essence of your Lord, besides whom there is no guide.
