# Page 706 (English translation)
Praise be to the Knower of the hidden matters and their counter [Glory to the One who knows the hidden matters and counts them].
And it will appear in dream or waking.

[Heading with underline]
Its recitation from a ring/script strengthens memory:

Its inscription is beneficial for correcting memory. Write it and tie it around the waist. For three weeks, or engrave it on a silver tablet and hang it around the neck; understanding and intellect will open.

Its dhikr (remembrance) is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Counter of all existing things before their existence in forms and similitudes, and You are the Knower of the measures of atoms and the Throne and the Chair and the lofty veils and the number of stars and the weights of the heavy heavens and the weights of the earth and mountains and the drops of seas and rains and all animals and the leaves of trees and the number of sand and stones and the sustenance of humans and jinn and the number of what emanates from them of breaths. I ask You by Your knowledge that counts all creatures, of what You have taught us on earth and in the heavens and what we do not know of the secrets of the unseen, that You cover my nakedness and secure me from frightening matters and forgive my evil deeds and multiply my good deeds and gather me with Your allies and prophets and messengers and elevate my ranks. And I ask You to inform me of the realities of existing things. O Allah, O Counter of existing things, O Lord.

Whoever constantly performs this remembrance, Allah the Most High makes him knowledgeable of the realities of things.

[Heading in decorative frame]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Mubdi' al-Mu'īd"

Mubdi' is the One who brought things from non-existence into existence. [unclear] And Mu'īd is the One who brings from non-existence into existence. Allah the Most High created things the first time, then will recreate them. Therefore Allah the Most High is the Creator and to Him is the return.

The muwakkal (assigned spirit) of the name Mubdi' is Uṭayyā'īl.
