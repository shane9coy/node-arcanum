# Page 434 (English translation)
[The heading: The Talisman of the Spirit of the Name al-Wahhab, Obtaining Honor and Rank]

The Name of Allah, the Most High, al-Wahhab (The Bestower) is a great Name. This Name is very beneficial for the person who desires honor in this world and the Hereafter. By the blessing of this Name, Prophet Sulayman (Solomon), peace be upon him, obtained sovereignty, and by the same Name's blessing he received that ring which had not been given to anyone before him. Whoever becomes acquainted with the secrets and mysteries of the Name al-Wahhab, he attains his desire. The name of its spirit is Satil. Under it are four officers, each of whom has 45 rows under his supervision, and in each row 45 spirits are assigned, all of whom are under the command of Mikayil (Michael). The spirit of this Name comes to the one who remembers it and fulfills his needs. The supplication for the Name al-Wahhab is this:

O Allah, O Wahhab, You bestow generously and grant magnificently, and You guide Your servants to the Abode of Bliss without stint. I ask You by the secret of secrets deposited within the letters of al-Qasim, and by the gifts of Your subtle grace extending in al-Qasim, and by that which You have spread of the mysteries of Your generosity toward the resolute determinations of the fundamentals. And make me one who returns to You with good intention, preserving the right guidance. O You Who are on the watch, calling servants to the Resurrection. O Wahhab.

[The heading: The Talisman of the Spirit of the Name al-Razzaq and Abundance of Sustenance]

The Name of Allah, the Most High, al-Razzaq (The Provider) is so blessed because Allah, the Most High, has always been the Provider. The name of this Name's spirit is Hurqaniyil. This is the spirit who provides sustenance to the servants by nurturing creation and nature. Whoever has become acquainted with the name of this spirit and has appointed him over his farm or garden has found it beneficial. Under this spirit are four officers, each of whom oversees 308 rows, and in each row 308 spirits are assigned. This spirit comes to the one who remembers this Name and fulfills all his needs, and he receives sustenance from the unseen. The supplication for this Name is:

O Allah, You are the Provider for all that You have brought into existence from Your generosity, and You are the Guarantee, in essence and attribute, from the life of Your Self-Sufficiency. And You are the One who sends down their sustenance from the depths of Your knowledge through Your angels and Your generosity. I ask You by the hidden treasures of Your protection that You make my existence a place for goodness and a means of blessings in actions and attributes. And grant me beneficial knowledge that affects the pure hearts, and a state encompassing the human conditions, and a giving hand for the pleasing gifts. And make me always, by virtue of that, in the manner of gathering and detail, connecting to Your servants. I find nothing but perfection and completion, and make me attain through the mysteries of Your Unity and the special favors of success and firm guidance. Indeed, You do what You will.

[The heading: The Talisman of the Spirit of the Name al-Fattah, the Key to Blessing and Success]

The Name of Allah, the Most High, al-Fattah (The Opener) is a noble Name. Allah Himself is al-Fattah. By the blessing of this Name, the secrets of the hidden realm are opened. The name of its spirit is Kitayil. Under it are four officers, each of whom oversees 489 rows, and in each row there are 489 spirits. All these spirits hold the keys of blessing in their hands; their task is to bring good and blessing.
