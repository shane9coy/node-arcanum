# Page 770 (English translation)
**Chapter: The Manipulation of Heavenly Letters in the Human Body**
**and**
**The Manipulation of Spiritual Numbers in the Human Soul**

**All beings are composed of the four natures; this is the foundation of creation's dispensation:**
All beings are composed of the four natures, each with its distinctive qualities, yet together they form the singular unity established upon the four natures from which God, Exalted is He, composed every thing and upon which He established the fundamental dispensation. He referred these powers back toward the material world alongside the divine matter. The philosophers have discussed this matter at great length; however, I shall relate to you its summary and conclusion, which has been placed among these enumerated letters, and the Arabic and Hindi languages, with all their dialectal variations, are dependent upon these very twenty-eight letters, apart from the letter alif, for it is included within these twenty-eight. The twenty-eight letters correspond to the twenty-eight stations. For each station there is one letter, and among these twenty-eight letters there are four natures, and each letter has a distinct property. The very first letter is alif, because it is the beginning of every word and is composed of the word (kalima); it proceeds from the human essence, and the letter of the intellect is alif, and this letter and the letters following it exist, and this letter is toward the original thing. Among the letters, alif is the unit among numbers, and numbers derive from the mysteries of sayings, just as letters derive from the mysteries of actions and passions.
You should know that there is no particular time specified for the operations of letters, for when God wills, their effect becomes manifest, and numbers operate upon creation because they accord with the authorities of the supernal realm.

**Each letter has a supernal trustee and a terrestrial trustee:**
Each letter has trustees, both supernal and terrestrial, and they have incantations (azīma) and also fumigations. So when you wish to obtain some benefit, at that time make a square figure in a gazelle membrane, write upon it with musk, saffron, and rose. On Friday, in the hour of Venus, in a clean and pure place of solitude, fumigate it with frankincense, aloe, sandalwood, and moist aloe wood. Then write within this figure the interior (bāṭin) and the name of the desired object. And take the names of the trustee of the letter alif, its assistants, and its deputy. Then make an image of your desired object from white wax, write upon it the name of the desired object twice, and also write the names of the trustees. Place this image before you and illuminate it seventy times while reciting the incantation. The incantation is:
