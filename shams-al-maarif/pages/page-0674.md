He sent Israfel, and the earth swore an oath by him and turned him back. Likewise, Michael also returned. Then Allah the Most High sent Azrael. Azrael came to the earth, and the earth also swore an oath to him. He said by his power and might: "Did He not send me, He in whose grasp is my life?" The earth said: "Yes! He has sent you too." Azrael said: "Then why would I disobey Him? I shall not disobey Him." He took a handful of dust from the earth and went up to the heaven, and he would recite glorifications with the Name of the Seizer.

**THE COMMAND TO TAKE SOULS WAS ALSO GIVEN TO HIM:**

Allah the Most High said to him: "You have accepted this; swear also to accept the souls." Thus, from that time he became the one who assists in taking souls. When you recite this Name and threaten any supplicant, he will immediately come with submission. Under him are four officers, and under each officer are many soldiers. If a person recites this Name for any tyrant, that tyrant will perish. And when this talismanic figure is engraved on a ring and recited according to its number and blown upon, and this supplication is written around it and kept with oneself, all of creation will be shielded from his evil. Its talismanic figure is this:

And its dhikr (remembrance) is this:

Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīmi Ṭ Allāhumma anta qābiẓu al-samāwāti wa bāsiṭu al-arạ̄īni wa al-jamīʿu bi-qabḍatika wa ʿaẓamatika wa qudratika qaddarta al-ashyāa bi-qawāmihā Ṭ al-Khabīru al-Fahīmu yā man qabạa wa basata al-anhāra wa abadda al-nūra al-muḥaqqaqa bi-al-ḥayati al-arḍi wa al-samāwāti al-muẓhiru bi-quwwati al-tadbīri khafā al-tadbīru fī baṣṭi al-ḥarakāti wa qabḍi al-sakanāti wa sāiri al-mawjūdāti as'aluka an taqbiḍa qalbī wa jawāriḥī bimā yub'iddunī ʿani al-maʿāṣī wa an lā yaḥjubanī ʿan nūri janānī wa ikhlāṣī wa aqbiḍ ʿannī sharra kulli muʿānidin wa mutakabbirin wa ḍarra kulli ḥāsidin mutaḥayyarin wa ij'al faṭḥī ʿinda wafātī masrūran lā maqtūlan wa lā maghbūnan Allāhumma absuṭ rizqī wa yassir lī amrī wa mā qaddartahu lī abadan li-abadin Allāhumma nawwir qalbī wa absuṭ yā bāsiṭu yā ḥayyu yā qayyūmu yā bāri'u lī bi-asmā'ika innī as'aluka bi-sirri al-sabbatayni wa birri al-munṣaḥayn an tuskhiru lī ʿabdaka Qaṭsāyīl khādimu hādhā al-ismi bi-ḥaqqi ismika al-qābiḍi wa bi-ḥaqqi al-malā'ikati al-muqarrabīna wa an tarzuqanī wa al-banīna nūran min anwārihā hādhā al-ismi yā Allāhu yā qābiḍu Ṭ

Whoever recites this Name, Allah the Most High makes his difficulties easy and grants him strength.
