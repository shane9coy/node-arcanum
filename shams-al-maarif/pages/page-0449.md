# Page 449 (English translation)
Obtaining Glory, Majesty, and Grandeur:
The one who recites the Names al-Mu'izz dhu al-Jalal wa al-Ikram, Allah the Exalted clothes him in the garment of glory, majesty, grandeur, and dignity. If he goes before any ruler and recites these Names, reverence for him will enter that ruler's heart. Let every person make a literal and numerical talisman of the Name al-Mu'izz and have it engraved on a red ruby gemstone and wear it in a ring; then he will never fall into humiliation, but discipline is necessary for every endeavor.

Obtaining Defense Against Enemies:
The Name of Allah the Exalted, al-Hasib, holds the command of the Greatest Name to repel enemies. Its attendant's name is Mitat'il, and beneath it are four officers, each of whom oversees eighty ranks, and in each rank there are eighty assigned attendants whose task is to help creation. The reciter of this Name is approached by the attendant who fulfills his need. The supplication of the Name al-Hasib is:
"O al-Hasib, You are He who gathers the scattered things to manifest oneness, and You are He who divided all individual essences in the station of adjustment, and You harmonized between the scattered contents of hearts for the unification of secrets and the realities of matters. I ask You by the secret of Your concealed knowledge and by the expansion of Your command in the depths of Your knowledge, that You grant me provision without reckoning, and that You admit me to Paradise, and that You open for me the doors of jinn and discourse by Your secret, victorious, O Lord of the worlds."

Obtaining Worldly Honor:
The Name Jalil contains the lights of majesty and the manifestations of reverence and dignity, by which the heart becomes illuminated and secrets are revealed. Its attendant's name is Ahdiya'il. Under him are four rulers, each of whom oversees seventy-three ranks, and in each rank there are seventy-three assigned attendants. The reciter of this Name attains great honor in this world and the hereafter. The supplication of the Name Jalil is:
"O al-Jalil, You are He who described Yourself with the attributes of majesty, and You are He who prepared for Your loved ones the stations of union, and You are He who revealed to the seekers of Your mercy the paths of perfection. I ask You by the majesty of sovereignty and power and forbearance and the beauty of glory, and by praise and forbearance and the perfection of strength and power and recognition, that You grant me the vision of Your expansive beauty in the breasts of meanings, so that I may attain the ultimate of happiness and joy in the station of nearness, and I may draw from the splendor of Your delight a secret from among the secrets intermingled in the auspicious meanings, and grant me perfect, complete strength by which I may attain the strength of absolute joy and happiness, O al-Ali, O al-Ghaffur."

God's Grace and General Forgiveness:
The Name al-Karim contains two letters of the Greatest Name. Its servant's name is Murka'il. He rules over four officers, each of whom oversees eight hundred and seventy ranks, and in each rank there are eight hundred and seventy assigned attendants. The supplication of the Name al-Karim is:
"O al-Karim, You are He who bestowed upon the saints the robes of knowledge and union, and You are He who pardoned those who disobeyed You and urged them toward repentance, the most beautiful of stations, and You are He who fulfilled Your covenant to those You promised and drew near for them the terms—for indeed the generous one, when he has power, pardons, and when he promises, fulfills, and maintains [damaged source] if he"
