# Page 200 (English translation)
[200]

"And indeed you are not taken by slumber nor sleep, and the earth straitens upon him however vast it may be—no attendants, but rather his gaze is swift. O God."
3 times: "O Allah, O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate" 3 times: "O Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of sovereignty, O Possessor of majesty and generosity."
"I ask You, O Allah, that You may pour upon me from the pearls of the secrets of hearts among Your servants, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful."

O Allah, I ask You that truly You are Allah, that You are the Essence beside which there is no deity—You are the Unique, the Single, the Sustainer, the Guardian. You are that Essence whom neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes. I ask You to send blessings upon our master Muhammad ﷺ and to grant me from the treasures of Your mercy. And O You beside whom lies all good, sustenance, blessing, and Your grace of existence and favor—that You may enrich me with Your good and Your grace. O Allah, three times: "O Most Merciful, O Most Compassionate, O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Lord of sovereignty, O Possessor of majesty and generosity." I ask You with the light of Your generous Essence—the light with which You have filled the pillars of the Throne—and with Your power which You have placed over all Your creation, and with Your mercy which encompasses everything, that there is no deity besides Your Essence. Your Essence is pure. Truly I am among the wrongdoers, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful. I ask You and request this supplication: that You may grant me perpetual blessing, goodness, and sustenance, and bestow upon me from Your vast treasures that which renders me heedless of all besides You. O You whose Essence, when He intends a thing, says to it "Be"—and it becomes. Truly You have power over all things. O Allah, 3 times: "O Most Merciful" 3 times: "There is no deity besides me; I am the Giver of treasures, the Bestower of favor and grace, the Generous, the Munificent."

O Allah, O Munificent, grant me abundant wealth, blessing, sustenance, and honor by Your grace. O Giver of blessings, bestow blessing and goodness upon me, and by Your grace render me heedless of Your infinite bounty. Make me so wealthy that thereafter there may be no poverty for the servant. Truly You are Allah—that Essence beside which there is no deity except You. You have given Your generosity and liberality. You are the Most Generous, the Munificent. You give sustenance and You accept, and You are the Munificent. O Allah, You are with all things. You are the Eternal, the Ever-Living, the Compeller, the Great. By Your great attribute, grant me greatness. O You who are greater than every great one, the Greatest. O Allah, I ask You by the truth of this Your great, noble name—which, when supplication is made with it, is accepted, when a thing is asked with it, is granted—and by the truth of all the beautiful names which I know or do not know, and by the truth of the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms, and what they contain, and by the truth of the Wise Quran and what is in it, and by the truth of the name with which You established the seven heavens and what is in them. Your prophets, upon them be peace, Your saints and Your chosen ones, Your near angels, and Your prophet ﷺ, his family, and his companions—peace be upon them. By their truth I ask You that You may help me with abundant good, courage, sustenance, and Your grace and mercy. O You who bestow generosity, by Your generosity. O You who do favors, by Your favor. O You who are generous, by Your generosity. O You who are the Giver, by Your giving. O You who are the Bestower of blessings. O Allah, three times: "O You who establish all the worlds, by Your Purity. O You who establish the heavens and earth—every one is obedient before Your grasp." Clad in the cloak of modesty and with the stature of hope. O Allah, I present to You that You are the Seizer and the Extender, and You are the best of those who suffice. Whereas You have said in Your Book: "Ask Me and I will accept." O Allah, I ask You and request that You may grant me wealth, mercy, and boundless sustenance. O Allah, three times: "O Bestower of blessings, O Possessor of all goodness." Call upon Him that it may be granted swiftly. Pour upon me and provide ample, pure, blessed sustenance—concerning which I know not, nor do I have knowledge—

O Allah, You have power over all things. O Allah, I seek from You that blessing which manifests from Your oneness with sincere devotion. I seek forgiveness for my sins.
