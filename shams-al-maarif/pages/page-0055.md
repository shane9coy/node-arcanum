**Chapter 3**

Commands of the 28 Lunar Mansions

The method for determining the lunar mansion is as follows: On the first day of each lunar month, the Sun and Moon are in the same line. One should consider on which day one wishes to know the Moon's mansion—whether it falls within the first third, second, or third third of the month, or within the first half or second half. One adds the number of days elapsed from the beginning of the Arabic month to this third or half, and the lunar mansion will be determined. For example, the Moon was in the Mansion of Sharaṭayn on the night of the lunar month's full Moon. Today is the 18th day. We wished to determine in which mansion the Moon is. We counted the mansions from the first day, and seven mansions were completed upon reaching Dhirāʿ. We learned that the Moon is in the line of Dhirāʿ.

Through this circle, the mansions, signs, and stars are clearly known:

**Explanation of the Forms of the Lunar Mansions and Their Commands**

**Mansion of Sharaṭayn:**

The form of the Mansion of Sharaṭayn is this: ** Its letter is Alif. It is malefic and fiery. When the Moon descends into it, there is an increase of corruption and bloodshed in the world. The wise men would not make any movement at this time, but rather would lie down and rest. Some people also see disturbing dreams during this time. Therefore, sleeping at this time is also not advisable. If you wish to perform any operation, perform the operation of subduing the enemy, which manifests precisely at this time, or corruption and harm. Its incense is black pepper and sindūr.
