# Page 442 (English translation)
[the eyes and what the breasts conceal, and You are over all things powerful. To You matters are referred, and by You evils are repelled.]
O Allah, I ask You for a secret from Your secret, and a command from Your command, and a light from Your light, and the taking charge of the secret by Your decree. And grant me from Your nearness a victory with which I may triumph over those who wronged me. And I ask You for success from You that awakens my heedless one, until my ignorant one knows, and illuminates my path toward You, and makes mercy my companion. My striving is from You, and my reliance is upon You, and to You is my return, and before You is my place of prostration. You know the reality of my affair and the hidden secret. Exalted are You above the attributes of created beings, and You are purified from deficiencies and slips. My God, I ask You for repentance that erases my errors thereby, and accepts my deeds thereby, and reforms my outward self. For You are the Light of lights and the Revealer of secrets, and everything with You is by measure, O Possessor of majesty and honor.

[Small decorative star-shaped rosette symbol]
