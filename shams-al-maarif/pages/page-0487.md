# Page 487 (English translation)
Chapter 33: The Inner Quality of the Throne and Its Types

Allah the Most High has adorned the Name with His Most Mighty Name. He has spoken concerning Himself: "He established Himself upon the Throne" (al-Istawa 'ala al-'Arsh). Allah has made the Throne the center of His Essence, for it is the limit of known bounds, the terminus of established thought, and the ultimate of concealed mysteries. This limit is the ultimate end; beyond it lies the Boundless (La-Makan). The example of the seven heavens and earths is similar to this arrangement. The Word arranges according to this order. The cycle commands according to this arrangement.

The Throne was Created First in Order

This should be understood thus: in accordance with the degrees of the realm of the Throne, order is the first invention, and the Chair (Kursi) is the first origin and essence of the realm of invention. The creation of the Throne is the first origin. The creation of the Chair is its branch. The Throne is the point of origin of the first creation, and the Chair is the invention within the Throne. Just as the beginning of a line proceeds from a point. Its relation is to that which is found in these things.

The Throne is the First Creation among Upper and Lower Creations:

When it has been established that the Throne is the first of Allah the Most High's creations—both upper and lower—the Throne is the example and the limit of every creation. Therefore the Throne has been called the Encompassing (Muhit) Far (Ba'id), although there is no difference of nearness or distance in its luminosity. It is the beginning of the creation of the hidden (Batin). The beginning of this creation was commanded by that Noble Essence itself. The celestial orbs are contained beneath it, meaning the divine theophancies—such as the Chair and others—which are the second example for the intellectual forms. From this it is known that in every heaven there is a throne. Accordingly, every hidden thing has a throne; their denial has not been made. Only the beginning and creation of the Throne and Chair have been mentioned. Wherever the beginning of the creation of a realm may be, within it will necessarily occur the invention of the realm of creation. In this there is wisdom: that which He has decreed is the reality which He has foreordained.

The Believer's Heart is the Throne:

Thus it has been established that in every heaven there is the Throne of Allah, just as the believer's heart is the Throne of Allah. "The hearts of the believers are the Throne of Allah" (Qulub al-Mu'minin 'Arsh Allah). Therefore every spirit is close to the beginning and creation. Similarly, for every throne a Chair is necessary. In the seven layers of the upper realms there are seven thrones and seven chairs.

The First Throne is the Throne of Expression (Itlaq)

Everything that enters the earth or emerges from it is within the knowledge of Allah the Most High. As a result of one motion following upon birth, He creates every moment, and that which descends manifests the mercy of Allah. This is His disposing, hidden within the command of the hidden (Batin). It becomes manifest. That which progresses within it emerges from its circle. My purpose in this is that Pure Essence.

[Subheading partially obscured]: He Knows All the Secrets and Mysteries of Bodies:

Allah knows, with regard to all bodies, the aspect in which there is transgression therein—the Most High knows this. He knows every thing that consists of subtleties and realities, secrets and spirits within bodies, and He also knows those which emerge from the celestial orbs. The Most High alone knows their greatness. It is known through the motion resulting from the Sphere of Intellect and the celestial orbs.
