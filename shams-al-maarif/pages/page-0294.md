# Page 294 (English translation)
His 171 points to the name al-'Aziz (the Mighty) of Allah the Exalted. Because when it is with the number of power, it will be complete. The first number points to Moses and the second number points to Jonah, peace be upon him. Let it be known that the person who is closer to the Strong Name will also receive divine attention. It is for this same reason that Moses, peace be upon him, was also weak. And see how much similarity there is between these two: Moses, peace be upon him, was placed in the darkness of the Ark and cast into the sea, and Jonah, peace be upon him, remained in the belly of the fish. Chapter 55: Properties of the Name al-Matin (the Firm/Steady), Obtaining Power, and Ease in Difficult Matters

Whoever frequently recites the Name al-Matin, his weakness will be transformed into strength. However heavy the task may be, it will not be difficult for him. For whoever has had his strength cut off, his recitation is beneficial. If it is combined with the Strong Name, it is extremely swift in effect, especially for the person who does the work of carrying burdens. Its number is 500; this is outside the progression and an odd excess number. Its 121 exceeds the original because in it there is a reference to the Divine Name. In firmness there is safety from the defect of power; it is for this reason that it ends with nun, which is the existence with which manifestation occurs. Allah the Exalted said in the Noble Quran: "Indeed, the best one to hire is the strong and trustworthy"1 And He said: "Indeed, We offered the trust to the heavens and the earth and the mountains, but they refused to bear it and were afraid of it, but man bore it"2 — because although they had power, they did not have firmness, and the trust is [a matter of] morality from power. Then He said: "But man bore it; indeed, he was unjust and ignorant"3 — he did not have the power to bear such a burden on his self, just as in truth the notification is with the power [when there is no reality]. Its letters' numbers are 88, pointing to the two great honored names, al-Mujib (the Responsive) and al-Razzaq (the Provider). Its square is this:

Chapter 56: Properties of the Name Wali (Friend/Protector), Obtaining Wilaya (Divine Friendship), and the Practice of Angelic Presence

Whoever frequently recites the Name Wali, friendship (wilaya) is granted to him. This recitation is specifically for the presence of angels called Cherubim. Whoever recites it with the meaning that "Glory be to Him and Exalted, may He make a friend" — He will raise him — will be granted from Allah the Exalted the station of Great Friendship (wilaya 'uzma). And whoever loves the one who recites this Name, his affair will be accomplished. Its number is 46; this is the first outside the ladder of progression and an odd excess number. Its 21 exceeds the original, pointing to this meaning because whoever has broken his covenant with Allah, his portion has been taken up: "the matters that were forbidden to others have been made permissible for him."
