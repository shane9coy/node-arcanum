Chapter 75: Properties of the Manifest Name, "Information about hidden matters and treasures"

Whoever frequently remembers the head of the Manifest Name of this name of supreme power, God Almighty will reveal hidden matters to him and the treasures of the inward will come into his hands. If he is in a state of incompleteness, let him complete it through repetition and then confront his enemies—he will obtain victory and triumph over them. Its number is 1106, even, odd, deficient. Its (2) 3/8 23 indicate the two divine names al-Mughnī (the Sufficer) and al-Bāsiṭ (the Expander). Know that the divine names al-Nūr (the Light), al-Bāsiṭ (the Expander), and al-Ẓāhir (the Manifest) are like unveiling. Whoever wishes to know something through a dream should remember these names with complete purification until their state dominates over them; then they will see that matter in a dream. Chapter 76: Properties of the Hidden Name, "Every person will love and revelation will be obtained"

Whoever frequently remembers this lordly name and the head of the Eternal Lord will obtain safety from that which they fear. Their heart will expand and they will receive the light of the inward. Whoever persists in its remembrance, their angels will begin to remember with them. At that time, wherever this person goes, the people there will treat them kindly and obey them; whoever sees them will love them. In this name there are many secrets for the people of divine unity. Shaykh Zayn al-Dīn states: whoever writes its numbers in a glass vessel while the moon is at its brightest, and remembers this name until the state dominates over them, then washes it with rainwater and drinks it—the seeker of unveiling and the secrets hidden within it will be revealed to them in a dream or while awake. If they possess completeness, the veil will be completely lifted from their inward and clear revelation will be obtained. Know that all the divine names of God Almighty are paired; unveiling will elevate [one] to the degrees of subtleties. Every inward in comparison to what is more inward than it is manifest. In this name there is a shadow for which the command of perfection is [designated]; it is more inward than the good ones. Thus, whose inward is for [mere] information is not for verification. The ethical lights of the inward are the same—many things will be revealed to them by its light. So, as the image is specific to it, the additional inward is the knowledge of limit and extremity; all things are not the limit of its inward. Its number is 82, even, odd, deficient.
