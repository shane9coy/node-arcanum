# Page 745 (English translation)
The one who practices the dhikr of this name, by placing his hand on the patient, the patient recovers: This effect is produced in the practitioner of dhikr: whatever patient he touches, immediately recovers. This is from among the dhikrs of the name "Al-Quddus" (The Holy), and for the disciple of this [name], whoever writes it and keeps it with himself, and whose numerological value matches his name's numerical value, this Great Name will work for him. He can obtain from it whatever he wishes, whether great or small. Its design is as follows:

And its dhikr is this:
 Bismi'llahi al-rahmani al-rahimi. Ta'Allahumma anta al-baqi fa-la intiha'a li-wujudik, wa anta al-samadu al-qayyumu al-awwalu, wa anta al-hayyu al-baqi fi al-azali ba'da zawali al-asbab wa al-'ilal. Allahumma inni as'aluka bi-hayatik allati la yamutu abadan, wa bi-baqa'ika alladhi la yanqui wa la yafna, wa bi-'ilmika al-muhiti bi-kulli shay', wa bi-qudratika 'ala khinnaqi kulli shay'in an tuhyiya qalbi bi-raf'i al-hijab. La tan'am bi-hayatika abadan, wa abqi 'ala tilka al-hayata mubtahijan sarmadan, ya ghayata al-maqsudi wa al-mithal, ya muntaha al-amaal, ya dha al-baqaya, ya dha al-jalali wa al-ikram. Anta Allahu al-baqi, la ilaha illa anta.

Whoever recites this dhikr, the Most Noble Lord will compensate him and will open for him the doors of goodness and beauty.

[Section: The Virtues of the Name "Warith" (The Heir)]

Inheritance and ownership belong solely to Allah. When there is nothing, He will be. Warith is the one to whom the inheritance of the owners returns after their destruction, and Allah alone is He—because He alone will remain after all creation, and to Him alone will all things return, and He alone will be the one to say: "Limani al-mulku al-yawma"—that is, "Whose government is today?" Then He alone will answer: "Lillahi al-wahidi al-qahhari"—to Allah, the One, the Conqueror. Because many people thought that we are the owners of the kingdom and the rulers of the empire, Allah refuted their notion, until certainty became manifest before them.

Ghazali, may Allah have mercy upon him, has provided an explanation of this name in his book Maqsad al-Sani; refer to it and you will understand. And the virtue of this name is to obtain positions and ranks—just as it is in knowledge—so according to its numbers, make its design and keep it with you; then in every matter, the assigned guardian will be present and will fulfill the need.
