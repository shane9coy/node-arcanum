# Page 361 (English translation)
[359]

By the sanctity of these noble, blessed, sublime letters—ḥ ḥ ḥ ḥ ṭā yā kāf lām mīm nūn ʿayn sīn ṣād yā—I place in their shelter him upon whom I have recited these names. The Sufficient God—the Sufficient God—Allah will suffice them, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. There is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Mighty.

O my pain of half the head, and the throbbing pain—go away from his condition, just as the blessed and honored letters by whose sanctity the Merciful's Throne was established—place them in the position upon which I have mentioned these names. The Healer God, the Sufficient God—them He is sufficient, and He is the Hearing and Knowing. Power and strength belong to Allah, who is Exalted and Supreme; it has been tested and found correct and sound. The properties of Ayat al-Kursi are countless and beyond number. This is the greatest verse of the Holy Quran; I have already mentioned its many great properties. Once I was in the service of my teacher, Sheikh Abu Abdullah al-Andalusi. We were discussing certain sciences when a person came to the Sheikh, panting. He greeted him, kissed the Sheikh's hand, and began weeping profusely. The Sheikh asked, "O man, why do you weep?" He replied, "I fear my enemy; the enemy is after me, I do not have the strength to confront him. I have come to you—please help me and deliver me from this difficulty." Upon hearing this, the Sheikh said, "Do not worry; tomorrow this will become clear to you." The Sheikh took a paper and wrote upon it this supplication:

I seek refuge in Allah from the accursed Satan, and may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, his companions, and grant them abundant peace.

Then he wrote Surah al-Fatiha, Ayat al-Kursi, and certain chapters, and wrote this verse:

"Do not fear, for you are among the secure. Do not fear lest you be overtaken, nor be afraid. Do not fear, for I am with you both—I hear and I see. Do not fear; you have escaped from the wrongdoing people." Two men among those who fear [Allah]—upon whom Allah has bestowed favor—said: "Enter upon them through the gate. But when you have entered it, you are sure to be victorious. And upon Allah put your trust, if you are believers. Do not fear, for you are the Most High." O Allah, protect me with Your Eye that never sleeps, and shelter me with Your Pillar that cannot be assailed, and forgive me by Your power so that I may not perish—and You are my hope, my Lord. How many blessings You have bestowed upon me, yet my gratitude for them was scant, yet You did not deprive me. O You who saw me in sins yet did not disgrace me, O Owner of the bounty that never ceases forever, O Owner of the favors that are never enumerated forever, I ask You to bless our master Muhammad, his family, his companions, and grant them abundant peace, and that You protect me and guard me from my enemies and whoever intends evil for me or harm. And let Your might, O Allah, be turned back upon him. Place his blindness between his eyes and his evil beneath his feet. Whoever intends for me evil or harm or treachery—it returns upon him. Place it connected to him. And Allah turned back those who disbelieved in their rage—they attained no good. And Allah was sufficient for the believers in battle, and Allah was Powerful, Mighty. "Deaf, dumb, blind—so they do not see. So they do not speak. So they do not converse. This is a Day when they will not speak, nor will permission be given them; then they will not apologize." Ṣād Qāf Nūn Fā. "Then Allah will suffice them, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing."

Do not fear, for you are indeed among the secure. Do not fear either of you, for I am with you both—I hear and I see. Do not fear; you have escaped from the wrongdoing people. Among those who fear [Allah], two men said—upon whom Allah bestowed favor—"Enter upon them through the gate. But when you have entered it, you are sure to be victorious. And upon Allah put your trust, if you are believers." Do not fear, for you are indeed the Most High—for you are indeed the Exalted One.
