And (the formula of) Al-Akhir (The Last) is this:
Bismillahi al-rahmani al-rahim. O Allah, You are the First, the Eternal One, there is no end to Your existence. You are the Everlasting, the Cause of causes, the one who makes things last and the one who brings [all] existents into being, and the one who delays each of them until an appointed term. I ask You, O You by whom all things tremble, whose existence is not due to creation by another, to awareness, to selection of every living thing in its life, and to the cessation of existence of all things and to return to Him after its annihilation. I ask You to efface me by Your imagination, O First, O Last, O Manifest, O Hidden, O Lord of the Worlds.

[Section on the merits of the name "Al-Zahir al-Batin" (The Manifest, The Hidden)]

These two attributes are contradictory, because the manifest becomes manifest from one aspect, and [it would be] due to a certain relation [that something] is neither manifest nor hidden.
Due to the relation of the senses, [something] is hidden; due to the relation of reasoning, [it] is manifest.
Thus, if you comprehend Allah the Exalted through the relation of sense perception, He is hidden from them, and if you seek Him through the method of reasoning and transmission, He is manifest. On this issue, people have engaged in much discourse. But we are content with the indications of the scholars.
Thus, the manifest is derived from His power, and the hidden is manifest through His wisdom.
Allah sometimes demands from you outward worship, sometimes He demands inward worship.
The Exalted Lord has sometimes required from you worship together with the manifest and the hidden, and sometimes only with the manifest. Thus, the worship that is together with the manifest and the hidden is this:
"And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, making religion sincerely His." [Quran 98:5]
Translation: "And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, making religion sincerely His."
Outward worship means the bodily works that are manifest, and inward worship means the sincerity of the heart. And the worship that is from the hidden alone, it is gratitude for [divine blessings] and [the remaining text is unclear]

And He has said:
"Do they not reflect upon themselves? It is Allah who created the heavens and the earth and what is between them..."
