Chapter: Mention of Jafr. His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, peace be upon him, said:

[Urdu text]:
These are some names whose meaning is their numerical values, and their reduction (takhsīr) and multiplication (ḍarb) in the apparent form is known according to the principles. I will not explain this, and I have mentioned this explanation in this book merely for the purpose that this book may remain superior to other books and excel them in virtue. The method of revealing their secrets is the same that we have already explained, and the secrets of the science of Jafr that are written in the original are these:

[Arabic text]:
In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Mūsá, Shu'aib, Samī', Shīth, Ḥizqīl, Qābīl, Ṭūs, Mabāṭ, Nā'ilus, Ṭarābulus, Ṭarsūs, Ḥalab, Ḥimṣ, Dimashq, Tifāriqū, Aḥramū, Ū Muḥammad, Aḥmad, Mūsá, Ilyās, Yūsuf, Muḥammad al-Mahdī, the Clear Sovereignty, God is the Guardian (wakīl), Mūsá, Bilqīs, Sulaymān, Jalīl, Nakham, Qābiḍ al-Muṣ, Kāhiya'aṣ, ṬāHā, Him Him, Must, Himṣ, Nun, Al-Qalam, "And what they scribe." The purpose from him: Muḥammad, 'Uthmān, Ṣāliḥ and Ṭāliḥ. "The command belongs to God; He gives victory to whom He wills" "when your Lord said to the angels: 'Indeed, I will place upon the earth a successor.'" "O David! Indeed We have made you a successor in the earth, so judge between the people with truth." "The command belongs entirely to God; He honors whom He wills and humbles whom He wills, and He is sufficient for me and enough."

[Handwritten note below Arabic]: Prostration

[Urdu text]:
You should know that these names and verses are derived from the same one whose mention we have made earlier. I have collected many methods, and one method is this: that the letters of the name of the ruler (ṣāḥib salṭanat) which are among the names of the secrets—whichever name agrees with this knowledge, take it, then expand (basṭ) them, then add the Name of the Essence (dhāt) to it, then multiply everything and count. Whatever results from the exterior multiplication is the purpose (marād).

[Heading]:
Explanation of the Sacred Name regarding Migration, Union, the Killing of 'Umar and 'Uthmān, etc., and other Events of the World

[Urdu text]:
And know that the principles of the Sacred Name are eleven letters, and they correspond: first, from the Migration to the passing of the Messenger, may God bless him and give him peace, and London with its principles are twelve, and these include the killing of His Eminence 'Umar and the turmoil of the Council (Shūrā); the principles of the Name of the Council (Shūrā) correspond from the killing of His Eminence 'Uthmān. And with another method, the beginning is from the explicit phrase of the Name, and the occurrence of tribulations upon the Muslims is apparent. When you multiply the principles of the beginnings by the Name, the exterior result will be the killing of the tribulation of al-Zubayr at its time. Then multiply the beginnings by the original Name, and there will be two hundred thirty-two (232), and from the letters, this is the killing and the end of the dynasty of the Banū Umayyah and the time of vengeance for their sovereignty. Then when we multiply the original letters of the secrets by the remaining letters, this will be the exterior multiplication. Thus in 187 AH, due to earthquakes, many people in Egypt, Syria, and other countries perished, and many cities and fortresses were destroyed, and Jabal Akbar Afākiyah broke and fell, and this happened in the time of al-Mutawakkil the Abbasid. And when you sum the letters of the secrets, the exterior will be three thousand thirty-two, and from the letters, this is the sign of the appearance of the Qarāmiṭa and the people's distress and discord, and the one whose true name of discord appeared. Then when you sum the thirty-two, four hundred twenty will result, and this was the time of the sovereignty of al-Ṣaffāḥ, Ghayz al-Wāl, the treachery, and the Saljūqid dynasty. And when you multiply the letter M, the letter of secrets, by the celestial letters, five hundred seventy-seven will result, and this was the time of the termination of the Fāṭimid dynasty and their destruction. And the phrase that is multiplied by the beginnings will be nine hundred forty-five, and the sign is five hundred thirty, and from the letters, this is the occultation, and this is the time of al-Taṭahhur al-Dīn, and this is the time of the purification of the Muslims. And when you multiply the principles of the beginnings with the letters of secrets, the product will be 827, and this was the year in which Sultan Jalāl al-Dīn Khuwārazm Shāh
