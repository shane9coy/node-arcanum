# Page 622 (English translation)
**Types of Breath:**

The breath of exhalation is contraction and the breath of inhalation is expansion. So "A" (آ) enters with the breath of life and "H" (ہ) exits with the combustion of heat. Thus the heat of "A" is frozen by the coldness of "H," and this cycle continues until the end of time, and through it one meets with Allah, "and to Him you shall return." From understanding this secret you will know that all beings exist only for the sake of God.

**Revelation of the Secret of Earth and Heaven:**
Chapter: Know that the Name of Majesty (Ism Jalalat) is also the Supreme Name (Ism al-A'zam), and its retreat and seclusion is 66 days. In these, after each prayer you should recite this Name 66 times, and it is obligatory to cultivate within yourself all the external conditions and noble character traits. You should become entirely angelic in character and continuously perform divine remembrance in your heart until a special state from the dhikr descends upon you and you lose awareness of yourself, and your courage becomes elevated. At that time the gates will open before you and earth and heaven will be manifest before your eyes. You will meet the spirits of the prophets and saints, and the unseen matters will come to you in dreams. This is the state of the first retreat. From this retreat you will attain the rank of the dhikr-performers and will be honored with the knowledge of the realities of the divine names.

**That Which Protects from Divine Punishment:**
Since there are 12 letters in لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ, for this reason it is the fortress of God. As it is related in the Hadith Qudsi: "Allah the Most High says: لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ is My fortress. Whoever enters it is safe from My punishment."

**On the Zodiac Signs of the Heavens and the Movement of Celestial Bodies:**
Some have expanded it (the formula) in this manner: ا ل ا ل ا ہ ا ل ا ل ل ہ. These are all 12 letters, and they are the number of the zodiac signs. Thus, by their blessing, the heavens and celestial bodies are revolving, and whatever action takes place within them is quickly accepted. This same Word serves all these things, and this Word is specific to human speech, while other species of the world have this movement by the wisdom that God the Most High has appointed for the celestial spheres.

**The Word is the Treasury of All Existing Things:**
And this same Word is the essence of all existing things—that is, animals, plants, and minerals. From this same Word comes the perfection of the four seasons and the 12 months, and according to the count of these letters there are 24 hours, and from these 24 letters, each single letter has a month established with it. So by means of these letters mercy and blessing descend, wisdom flows, worship occurs, there is advancement in everything, and goodness increases.

**The Division of Hours:**
Or know that the detailed account of the original realities is as follows: Allah the Most High appointed the 24 hours of day and night from His subtle grace. Then He divided them by His grace and wisdom such that 6 hours are for heat, 6 hours for cold, 6 hours for autumn, 6 hours for spring, and this formula assists them. This same Word is the support for the sought-after monotheism. There are 12 designations in لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ. The Beloved's monotheism (Tawhid Habibī) is the chief of all.
