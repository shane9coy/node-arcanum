This is its دعوت (invocation):
In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, teach me the knowledge that You taught to Your friends, and inspire it within me so that it becomes clear. And benefit me through it as You benefited the special ones among Your creation—for You are the One sought for aid, and upon You is reliance. O God, act gently toward me with Your hidden gentleness, until I may seek help in the sciences that You extracted for the people of Your obedience and those who act righteously from among this slips. And turn affectionately toward me, and turn the hearts of creatures toward me, O Most Affectionate, O Most Merciful, O Most Loving. Subjugate for me Your servant, the assistant of the letter عayn, and steady my heart for dealing with him, and send him to me to teach me the knowledge of Your noble friends and prophets, O Knowing, O Mighty, WAḤĀ. OAYN, by LMYʿYṬ and GHFYḌ, and ʿAYN and ʿNQWʿY. Do for me what I love, and do for me what I commanded You, by the right of the elder ancient pure elder, ʿMWʿYṬ, and by the right of the manifest signs. Answer, O servant of this letter. May God bless you and upon you. And I establish upon you, O helper who descends by the goodness of God's greatness and His signs and His names, and by the right of the One to whom belongs the might of omnipotence and to whom belong the beautiful names, and whose light is not extinguished, and whose throne does not waver, and whose chair does not shake. WAḤĀ, WAḤĀ, by the glory of God. WAḤĀ, by the right of the One who knows the secret and the hidden. God, there is no deity except Him—ṭ. To Him belong the beautiful names. God, there is no deity except Him, the Lord of the magnificent Throne—ṭ. And there is no power and no strength except through God, the Exalted, the Magnificent.

This is its اضمار (incantation):
Answer, O Sharahīl. May God bless you and upon you, by the right of YʿẒMW ʿDLQY, Irdīf. SḤY, O YMWH, ʿĀ ṬWRW. I call: I am God. IBYL, AḤLHH. I love and trust. WAḤĀ, the quick.

One should know that when one finds the sciences of this letter by means of an invocation that fulfills its purpose, especially the science of alchemy—

Letter الف (Alif):
This letter is warm and is between two letters.

Letter الف (Alif) continued:
The one suffering from paralysis will find rest. When it is in the script on paper, write this letter according to its numerical value and wash it with all kinds of trees in which this letter appears from among the letters R B Q N F S, and massage the paralytic with it seven times; he will be healed within seven repetitions.

The child who does not speak will speak. For a child whose tongue has not loosened, write this letter together with its اضمار when it is in the script on paper, and place it around the child's neck; the tongue will loosen.

If the intention is domination, write the دعوت and اضمار and the letter seven times and keep it with yourself in solitude; the entire world will become subservient, and great and mighty works will be accomplished.

If there is a fire for some purpose and it should not be extinguished, then by means of this letter it will be extinguished. The procedure for this should be as follows: write its دعوت in the script and together with its اضمار write and throw it into the fire; the fire will become quiet.
