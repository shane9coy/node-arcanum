# Page 163 (English translation)
The king approached the minister and placed his hand on his shoulder, saying: "I ask you one thing—tell the truth and hide nothing." The minister replied: "Speak, Your Majesty, I shall speak only the truth." The king said: "Do you know that every day I intend to kill you, but when I see your face, all my anger dissipates and enmity turns into love? So tell me, what is the reason for this? Also tell me this: I have forgiven you, and no enmity toward you remains in my heart. Do you have any recitations and practices with which you make supplications? Tell me these." The minister said: "Your Majesty, the matter is this—I had a teacher who was a jurist; he taught me the Quran. One day he said to me: 'I wish to give you a gift. Keep it well, protect it, and read it constantly, day and night—for you shall remain safe from all your enemies and from those who intend harm against you. These are five verses of the Quran. In each verse, ten instances of the letter qaf (ق) have been gathered together. Whoever reads them before sunrise and sunset, all people will be kind to him. If a sultan or ruler reads them, his rule will endure. His subjects will be pleased with him, and his awe shall be established over his servants. God Almighty will maintain his position. If any person in need reads them and makes a request, it will be fulfilled.'"

When the king heard this from the minister, he was greatly astonished. He was very pleased with the minister and praised him greatly. He asked the minister to write down those verses. These five verses are:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ o اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الْمَلَاِ مِنْ بَنِيْٓ اِسْرَآءِيْلَ مِنْۢ بَعْدِ مُوْسٰى اِذْ قَالُوْا لِنَبِيٍّ لَّهُمُ ابْعَثْ لَنَا مَلِكًا نُّقَاتِلْ فِيْ سَبِيْلِ اللّٰهِ قَالَ هَلْ عَسَيْتُمْ اِنْ كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِتَالُ اَلَّا تُقَاتِلُوْا قَالُوْا وَمَالَنَآ اَنْ لَّا نُقَاتِلَ فِيْ سَبِيْلِ اللّٰهِ وَقَدْ اُخْرِجْنَا مِنْ دِيَارِنَا وَ اَبْنَآئِنَا فَلَمَّا كُتِبَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْقِتَالُ تَوَلَّوْا اِلَّا قَلِيْلًا مِّنْهُمْ وَ اللّٰهُ عَلِيْمٌۢ بِالظّٰلِمِيْنَ o

"I begin with the name of God, the most merciful, the especially merciful. Have you not considered the assembly of the Children of Israel after Moses, when they said to a prophet among them: 'Appoint a king for us that we may fight in the path of God'? He said: 'What, if fighting is ordained for you, will you not fight?' They said: 'And what reason have we that we should not fight in the path of God, when we have been driven forth from our homes and our children?' But when fighting was ordained for them, they turned back, except a few of them. And God is knowing of the wrongdoers."

"The second verse is this:

لَقَدْ سَمِعَ اللّٰهُ قَوْلَ الَّذِيْنَ قَالُوْٓا اِنَّ اللّٰهَ فَقِيْرٌ وَّنَحْنُ اَغْنِيَآءُ سَنَكْتُبُ مَا قَالُوْا وَ قَتْلَهُمُ الْاَنْۢبِيَآءَ بِغَيْرِ حَقٍّ وَّ نَقُوْلُ ذُوْقُوْا عَذَابَ الْحَرِيْقِ o

"God Almighty has heard the speech of those who said: 'God is poor and we are rich.' We shall certainly write what they have said, and their slaying of the prophets wrongfully, and We shall say: 'Taste the punishment of burning.'"

"The third verse is this:

اَلَمْ تَرَ اِلَى الَّذِيْنَ قِيْلَ لَهُمْ كُفُّوْا اَيْدِيَكُمْ وَ اَقِيْمُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اٰتُوا الزَّكٰوةَ فَلَمَّا كُتِبَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْقِتَالُ اِذَا فَرِيْقٌ مِّنْهُمْ يَخْشَوْنَ النَّاسَ كَخَشْيَةِ اللّٰهِ اَوْ اَشَدَّ خَشْيَةً وَ قَالُوْا رَبَّنَا لِمَ كَتَبْتَ عَلَيْنَا الْقِتَال لَوْلَا اَخَّرْتَنَا اِلٰى اَجَلٍ قَرِيْبٍ قُلْ مَتَاعُ الدُّنْيَا قَلِيْلٌ وَالْاٰخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لِّمَنِ اتَّقٰى وَلَا تُظْلَمُوْنَ فَتِيْلًا o

"Have you not considered those to whom it was said: 'Restrain your hands and establish prayer and give zakah'? But when fighting was ordained for them, [damaged source] a party of them feared people as, or more than, the fear of God, and they said: 'Our Lord, why have You ordained fighting for us? Why not delay us to a near term?' Say: 'The enjoyment of this world is little, and the hereafter is better for him who fears God, and you shall not be wronged even a thread's worth.'"
