# Page 184 (English translation)
I ask You to bless and grant peace upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad and upon everyone who loved Muhammad,
that You fulfill my needs which are for me containing good of this world and the Hereafter, preserved in care, preserved from calamities,
by the specialities of divine protections, O You who respond with blessings, O You who in truth are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness
and good deeds. O Allah, indeed it is the request of a servant who honors You by revealing his humility, for indeed You are the Knower of the unseen
and the Witness of the reality of requests before their actual fulfillment. So complete it with a beautiful ending, O Best of those Requested,
and may Allah bless our master Muhammad, the beloved of hearts, and his family and companions, and grant them peace.

O Allah, I ask You—indeed in all states of generosity and grace, Allah remains in every circumstance, and indeed with the attributes of absolute oneness,
You are free from all similitudes and examples and the like. Indeed You are Allah, there is nothing like unto Him; He is the Hearing, the Seeing.
I ask You to send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and upon his family and upon all who love him,
fulfilling my need for which there is good for me in this world and the Hereafter. Preserve me from calamities by the specialities of Your protections,
O You who give blessings without limit, O You who in truth are the People of Piety, the People of Forgiveness, and Good Deeds. O Allah, this is the
request of a servant of the glory of Your Lordship. By revealing his humility, for indeed You are the Knower of the unseen, the Witness of the reality
of matters before their actual occurrence. O Best of those Requested, complete it with a beautiful ending, and may Allah send blessings upon
our master Muhammad, who is the beloved of hearts, and upon his family and his companions, may Allah be pleased with them, and grant them peace.

A beneficial prayer for every work

then something other than You became mixed with it. And I seek Your forgiveness from every sin I committed in the darkness of night and day,

one and Your generous one among Your creation, Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, who came with the truth through the message,
