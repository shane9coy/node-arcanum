[Heading in a decorative rectangular box]: Chapter 25: The Fifth Method of the Beautiful Names and Its Selected Secrets

**The Operation for Obtaining Honor Among Common People and Kings:**

Know that among the Beautiful Names, al-'Aliyyu al-'Azhimu al-Jamilu al-Kabiru al-Jalilu al-Nuru al-Bahiyyu Dhu al-Jalali wa al-Ikram are such names that no person, regardless of how elevated and noble a rank they hold, can achieve near kings what the reciter of these names achieves. People will endeavor to fulfill all the words of the one who recites these names. Whoever sees him will fear him and will honor and respect him.

**Popularity Among People, Comfort, and Increase in Beauty:**

The one who recites the name al-'Aliyyu al-'Azhimu always remains honorable. Whoever has lofty courage, all people love him and feel affection for him. His sustenance becomes abundant and he lives in comfort. All people listen to him and accept his words, and all his purposes are fulfilled.

Whoever writes these two names according to their numerical and literal correspondence on white silk during the honor of the moon and keeps it with himself, the divine favors of Allah the Exalted shall be upon him, and people will honor him immensely.

The name al-Jamilu of Allah the Exalted is appropriate for the Compassionate. If its image is written and placed around the neck of an enemy, his beauty increases immensely. Whoever persists in the recitation of this name will become one possessed of beautiful character and excellent habits and qualities.

**Obtaining Acceptance, Victory Over Enemies, and Protection from Harmful Animals:**

Allah the Exalted grants the reciter of the names al-Kabiru al-Muta'alu garments of awe, dignity, and greatness. He elevates courage and the spirit. Whoever writes their numbers on a ring in gold during the honor of the sun in Mars, whoever looks upon him will love him, and Allah the Exalted will cast his awe upon the hearts of his enemies.

Jinn and humans fear the reciter of the name al-Jalilu from afar, and all harmful animals fear him.

**Strength in and Increase of Vision:**

The name al-Haqq illuminates the heart of the reciter of the names al-Nuru al-Bahiyyu, and this name is immensely blessed. Whoever recites only the name Nur, Allah the Exalted illuminates him and informs him of His lights. If the numbers of these two names are written on a mirror and kept with oneself, or in darkness—if weakness or a film has come into someone's eye, his vision becomes strong.
