# Page 727 (English translation)
[Top Center: 7]
[Top Right:] To You in this world and the hereafter, O Most Merciful of the merciful

[Boxed Heading:] Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Tawwab" (The Acceptor of Repentance)

Al-Tawwab is He who accepts repentance from His servants and presents His signs and blessings before them, so that they are moved to return to repentance. And He accepts repentance through His grace and generosity. As He says:
"Huwa alladhī yaqbalu al-tawbah 'an 'ibādihi wa ya'fū 'ani al-sayyi'āt"
Translation: "He is the One who accepts repentance from His servants and pardons the evil deeds."

When is the time for acceptance of repentance:
Repentance is accepted until the death rattle begins. Through repentance, all sins, small and great, are forgiven. When there is the intention not to repeat them, and whatever was taken wrongfully from people is returned to them.

The repentance of sinners from their sins:
This is from the supplications of the friends of God (awliyā). Write it and give it to a grave sinner, and he will repent from his sins. And whoever is an agent of this name, when he looks toward any sinner, that person will immediately repent. The muwakkil (attendant spirit) of this name is Ṭay'īl, and it is recited with the name of seeking forgiveness (istighfār). Assistance is sought through it against the Khawārij (external enemies). Its seventh station is from beneath the feet of the Throne (ʿArsh), and seventy rows of angels are under its command. They ask forgiveness for the one who remembers (dhikr).

The doors of sustenance open for whoever keeps the dhikr of Al-Tawwab, and tightness of livelihood is eliminated:
Whoever is in difficulty should recite istighfār abundantly. And whoever recites this name, Allah the Exalted will open the doors of sustenance for him, and he will be wealthy, not in need. When its talismanic figure (naqsh) is written and someone reads it, the doors of charity and blessings will open for him. Through many methods it has reached us that when there is tightness of sustenance, recite istighfār and Allah the Exalted will create ease. The figure is this:

And its dhikr (recitation) is this:
"Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm. Allāhumma anta al-Tawwābu 'alā al-ʿuṣāti idhā nadamū wa anta awwābun 'alayhim bi al-faḍli idhā rajaʿū fa aẓhartu lahum al-dalīla wa al-āyāti wa nashartu lahum min janābika al-ḥasanāti wa araytahum ʿawālima al-ʿaraqāti fa taṣaffaḥa lahum aṣḥābu al-tawbāti as'aluka Allāhumma yā muqaddira al-nawāzili bi al-iradāti wa musabbibu hādhihi al-asbābi yā mūwajjihahā yā Rabba al-arbābi as'aluka li aṣḥābi hādhihi al-awrādi wa tajʿalahum ʿindaka min ghawā'iṣi al-aḥbābi ḥattā lā yabqā baynī wa baynaka ḥijābun wa aghfir lī khaṭī'ātī wa zalālātī yā Tawwāb."
