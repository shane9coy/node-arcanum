# Page 657 (English translation)
How the difference between the commanding soul (nafs ammāra), the reproaching soul (nafs lawwāma), and the tranquil soul (nafs muṭma'inna) came to be:

We have come to know that subsistence (baqā') is indeed in the supernal realities (al-'ulwiyyāt) and not in the inferior realities (al-sufliyyāt). The soul that became pure and transparent in the dominant luminous realm was the tranquil soul (muṭma'inna), and the one that became rebellious in the dominant realm of darkness was the commanding soul (ammāra), and the one that became rebellious in the dominant luminous realm emerged as the reproaching soul (lawwāma).

Some humans are like monkeys and pigs:

There are some people whose hearts God, Exalted be He, created like the hearts of beasts. These are the people who remain immersed in carnal desires like monkeys and pigs, because God, Exalted be He, has transmuted their souls, and this is the marker of transmutation, as He says in this verse: "Those are they whose hearts God has sealed" (83:3). And this connects the supernal realities to the human body with which the heart is established—a light with which torment has been inflicted—and in accordance with this verse "But when they waxed arrogant regarding what they had been forbidden" (6:146), the True Exalted One is averse to accepting their faith. Due to the darkness of the soul, when the Divine Speech reached them, they heard but did not accept. Thus they became rejected, as God, Exalted be He, says:

"And We have placed coverings upon their hearts lest they understand it, and in their ears heaviness."

Translation: "And We have placed coverings upon their hearts so that they do not comprehend it, and in their ears We have placed deafness."

And the apparent state is this:

"Then your hearts became hardened after that, being like stones or even harder."

Translation: "Then your hearts became hardened after that, so they are like stones or even harder."

Thus this is the creation of the hidden self, and these are the sins of the Most Beautiful Name of God, and from this the attribution of souls has been derived. Accordingly, He says:

"No misfortune befalls on the earth or in yourselves except that it is in a Book."

Translation: "No affliction strikes in the earth or in yourselves except that it is written in a Book."

Some souls are blessed, some are wretched:

Know that the souls of the people of felicity were created in the mode of "expansion" (sadr basṭ), and the souls of the people of wretchedness were created in the mode of "contraction" (sadr qabḍ). The hearts of the people of felicity were shaped in the body of faith, and the hearts of the people of wretchedness were shaped in the body of disbelief. The bodies of the people of felicity were compelled to service, and in the bodies of the disbelievers the innate disposition of wretchedness has been placed along with negligence.

The blessed soul shall be in 'Aliyyīn and the wretched soul shall be in the lowest depths:

Whoever conforms to the people of felicity shall be in 'Aliyyīn, and whoever wretchedness has preceded shall be in the lowest of the low (asfal al-sāfilīn) and shall enter among the wrath of God. Thus for the people of felicity it has been revealed:

"So whom God desires to guide, He expands his breast for Islam."

And regarding the people of wrath He says:

"And whom He desires to lead astray, He makes his breast constricted and narrow"—the verse.

All of this is the divine wisdom in the predestination of the composition of the inferior realm, and there is no deficiency in it from eternity to eternity. Human capacity cannot comprehend felicity, wretchedness, and the like. And God knows best of the truth.
