# Page 490 (English translation)
He who is called Dhū al-Jalāl. Its fruit is nonexistence. He who has intoxication of joy. Its outward aspect is the eye of generosity, to the pure one the ecstasy of intoxication.

Barzakhī rays emanate from this place:

From it emanate subtle electric waves and Barzakhī rays emanate. This throne connects the two poles. It shows the paths of both sides. It encompasses both circles. Its lights cast radiance upon spirits with a special effect of illumination [unclear]. The weight of lights, in truth in my view, is their weight belonging to God alone, which are specific to it.

The names of the paradises:

(1) Dāʾirāt al-Naʿīm (Circle of Bliss) (2) Jannat al-Khuld (Garden of Eternity) (3) Jannat al-Baqāʾ (Garden of Perpetuity) (4) Jannat al-Kirāma (Garden of Generosity) (5) Jannat al-Naẓar (Garden of Vision) (6) Jannat Asmāʾ (Garden of Names) (7) Jannat al-Khiyām (Garden of Pavilions) (8) Jannat al-Firdaws (Garden of Paradise)

Establishing connection with the Great Throne:

Whoever has freed himself from the densities of the corporeal soul and has bound himself with the strength of spiritual ecstasy, has avoided things similar to those being killed, which are connected with the lowest degrees of the lower world, has kept his soul distant from all evils, has adopted nobility and purity, has spent life in purity and cleanliness—then his connection with the Great Throne is established. In the realm of the path of spiritual discipline, dividing himself, he passes safely through the path of the realms of reality, joins their group, and obtains the reality of the desired goal.

God the Exalted says: "Is he who was dead and We gave him life and made for him a light by which he walks among the people like one in utter darkness?" Another place He says: "That is the grace of God; He gives it to whom He wills; and God is of infinite grace."

[Centered rectangular box containing the following text:]

The sixth is the Throne of Administration

[End box]

In truth, this is the Divine Throne which is the center of subtle sciences. It is the possessor of pure intentions and the mode of expression, the place of manifestation. This is specifically the place of acceptance of supplication. The theophanie of the sovereignty of the Lord are formed into luminosity. Here alone the reality of the most sincere sincerity is manifested. Here alone His face is raised and elevated.

The soul's relation with the spirit occurs:

The ruling of action is obtained. Here alone are the bearers of secrets. Here alone the lower souls are connected with the angelic realm, and the pure and clean secondary spirits are inscribed in the luminous scrolls. The Tabā is spread upon the trees of true theophanie. The proportion of three is moderate; the proportion of three is from spirits. The three are spiritual tens which illuminate in various fields like the attribution of spirits. Their spiritual movements have created an environment upon spirits.

Knowledge of the Ṣaḥāʾif (celestial tablets) is obtained:

One sentence among these elevated stations is that which, upon reading and through the power of practice, becomes a school of mastery along with external progressions through which knowledge and meanings become known and hidden, and their manifestation is made in the reality of the essence. That is the light of the hearts of the people of [unclear—possibly knowledge].
