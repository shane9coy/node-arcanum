These luminous names: kahaz tahatef halashtato hilishtato malishtato hawaz, by the right of Nahhub, by Lashalfi
Every thing flees and there is no likeness, so every living thing answers his call: mazqash hashowat, and by the engraving, every thing submits
Lah ashlifubo azfalfato hadi shahla ghaghoos atamtanbikh, you are by the command, every thing and planting
Mahshtalanalif, whoever hears your name, spirit and his staff, except that he is struck by thunder and burned: lashflanikh, haitala
Ahtamtami hi, answer, O noble spirits, servants of these great letters, by the right of what I have sworn upon you, trust, O Touta'il, and you O Alha'il, and you O Tafya'il, and you Amsa'il, by the subjugation of the servants of these noble letters, let them fulfill my needs and let them come to me, such as what I have named for you in this circle: the summoning of so-and-so son of so-and-so, wherever you may be, may God bring you all together, surely God over all things is powerful, and He over their gathering when He wills is capable: Hayahiya al-Waha al-Waha al-Ajal al-Ajal al-Sa'a al-Sa'a, by the right of what I have recited upon you of these noble, blessed, mighty names, and by the right of what I have recited upon you: Halam.

In the name of God, who is holy, pure, and exalted, He is the dominant and the Lord, He is the Lord of passing ages and the one who brings together compassion and favor. He is the essence whose kingdom never perishes, great in honor and great in glory. With the spiritual spirits, by His name, I have called to you, and by the natures of these letters, you are bound—meaning, whatever task I command you, fulfill it: bring so-and-so to so-and-so. By the luminous names, O noble and sacred letters, the rulers shall accept my command and fulfill it, by the One whom I adjure you, O Touta'il, O Alha'il, O Tafya'il, and O Ahsa'il. By the subjugation of the servants of these letters, the work shall come to my people; let them fulfill my needs and bring my desire, which I have named for you in this circle, and that is for the attraction of so-and-so son of so-and-so, wherever you may be, God Almighty will bring you all. Surely God Almighty over all things is powerful, and whenever He wills, He has power over gathering them, meaning by what I have recited before you these blessed, sacred, noble names, and by the sake of what I have recited before you.

O seeker, let it be known to you, and may God Almighty grant you and me the success of His obedience, and grant the secrets of His names. Doubtless this chapter is very great; its practice is only in lawful matters and following good. Surely this is from the books of the righteous and the saints; fear God if you practice any of it in forbidden matters, for with God Almighty there will be a severe grasping for you. I have removed this matter from my neck; he who obtains this great benefit, now it is upon your neck, and God Almighty knows best.
