# Page 753 (English translation)
**The Properties of Mars:**

In Mars there is the power of domination, assistance, and generating radiance, and it affects with such swiftness that its actions exceed even the power of Saturn, and it exerts a very great influence in corruption. It has a remarkable effect in producing hot diseases, in making lines flow, and for the evil eye. When you perform its operation, understand this matter well.

Among the divine names, these names—**al-Shahīd al-Muhṣī al-Ḥalīm** (The Witnessing, The Reckoning, The Forbearing)—when accompanied by their recitation, whoever asks any need from God with sincerity, be it any need, it will be fulfilled immediately. Therefore, value this gift that has been conveyed to you. Within the Name of al-Ḥalīm there are strange and wonderful secrets. Whoever inscribes it upon red copper on Sunday for some purpose and keeps it with himself, that purpose will be fulfilled quickly.

This is a supplication of supreme greatness. Whoever recites it in the last third of the night after two units of prayer, with presence of heart and an empty body, Allah the Most High will clothe him in a garment of honor, and by its blessing help comes abundantly and one overcomes enemies. For kings this remembrance is extremely beneficial; by its means their kingdom becomes extensive and their authority remains established.

With its supplication, this verse should be recited:

**"Al-ḥamdu lillāhi alladhī lam yattakhidh waladan"**
(Praise be to Allah who has not taken a son)
to the end,
and these names: **Yā 'Azīz Yā Jabbār Yā Qahhār** (O Mighty, O Compeller, O Dominant) should be recited.

And the supplication is:

**"Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm. Awaqifnī mawqifa al-'izzati wal-kamāli wal-bahjati wal-jalāli ḥattā lā ajidnī dharratan walā rafīqatan illā wa-qad ghashiyahā min 'izzi 'izzika mā yamna'uhā mina al-dhulli li-ghayrika ḥattā ushāhidu dhulla man siwāya li-'izzatī bika mu'ayyadan bi-rafīqatin mina al-ru'bi yakhḍa'u lahā kullu shayṭānin marīdin wa jabbārin 'anīdin wa abqa 'alayya dhulla al-'ubūdiyyati fī al-'izzati naqā'an yanbasuṭu lisānu al-i'tirāfi wa yaqbiḍu lisānu al-da'wā innaka anta Allāhu al-'Azīzu al-Jabbāru al-Mutakabbiru al-Qahhāru"**

And what is appropriate: from the verses of the Quran:

**"Qul al-ḥamdu lillāhi alladhī lam yattakhidh waladan"**
to the end of the verse should be recited.

Whoever recites this supplication at that time with an empty body and presence of heart, Allah the Most High will make him dominant over his enemies. Within these names there is the effect of awe and grandeur, of subjugating enemies and inspiring terror in their hearts, and of making everything obedient to the one who recites them. These names disperse the armies of enemies and gather their opponents, and whoever recites them, Allah the Most High removes from him all manner of evil and mischief, softens the hard hearts for his sake, and grants him the power to bear every burden of sickness and hardship. And whoever in fear recites them remains fearful of all armies, yet no heavy task becomes difficult for him, and all creation becomes obedient to him, and he sees himself humble and insignificant before the court of Truth.

**By its remembrance, every manner of victory and success is obtained.**

...he whose rank will be elevated and who will not be humiliated, and before enemies he will not be defeated—he will be strong and every person... for the destruction of some tyrant... in the assembly... with the intention of gathering, in the hour of Mars, upon a copper tablet... whose words he will see and from which happiness will be obtained.
