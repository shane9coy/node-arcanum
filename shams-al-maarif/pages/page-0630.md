[Boxed Heading]: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Rahman"

This name is derived from mercy (rahmat), and mercy desires its object of compassion, and every object of compassion is in need of its compasser, and the compasser is indeed the Rahman (The Merciful), and He is the Rahman of this world and the hereafter. That is, Allah the Exalted. The inner reality (batin) of Rahman is Rahim (Merciful), and the outer reality (zahir) is divinity (al-lahut), and divinity is the inner reality of Rahman.

For this reason, Allah the Exalted says: "Say, invoke Allah or invoke the Rahman." [Qur'an 17:110]

[Subheading]: The application of "Rahman" is exclusively reserved for Allah:

For this reason, this name is not applied to anyone except the Essence of the Exalted, while the name "Rahim" is also applied to others. Thus, it is mentioned regarding the honor of our Prophet: "Indeed, He is compassionate and merciful toward the believers." [Qur'an 9:128] Angels are also created beings, but created beings can be called "Rahim" (merciful), not "Rahman" (the Merciful). "Rahim" is said of one in whom mercy and compassion predominate. The Prophet ﷺ says: "Indeed, Allah has mercy only upon those of His servants who are merciful" — meaning Allah the Exalted has mercy upon His merciful servants.

Know that the mystery of Rahman and Rahim is extremely subtle, and it is that it consists of very many divisions, one of which is Ba. It relates to power (qudra) because it draws things from their origins, and this is the established principle. For the sensory world, through power, every statement has come into existence from truth: "By Me you are caused to be subtle, and by Me you are created, and by Me you were sent down, and by Me you were breathed into" — meaning, "It is only with Me that you have obtained knowledge, perception, and standing." For the mystery of "Inni Man" — "Fa-bi ya-sma'u wa bi ya-yubsiru" — "I hear with Me and I see with Me."

[Subheading]: The attributes of B-S-M-K

Ba, Sin, and Mim are original names. Thus, names are manifestations of the inner reality of power, just as Sin is from beneath, indicated by the signs of the manifestation of power, and Mim is apparent in the midst of the place obtained, among things that are within the place of the one who calls for help from the inner reality and in place — this is the world of Malak Malakut, for it is the lower manifestation of the inner reality of power. Power is from the name al-Qadir (the Powerful), and it is derived from the attributes of Safa (purity), which have heights and elevations — that is, from Aliyy (the Most High), from which it is derived. Mim is from the covering (gillaf), and covering is that which encompasses. Therefore, this name is derived from al-Muhit (the Encompasser). For this reason, it precedes the effects of power and the lights of the descent (hobut) of all with Aliyy.

[Subheading]: The Greatest Name is Allah:

The Greatest Name is Allah — its resolution has been established. Since power is an attribute of the singular mode, therefore the Alif indicates toward the direction of the Essence. Since "Lam" indicates toward power, therefore it stands opposite the Alif. "Lam" is from the mysteries of Khaf, and since Lam is from the mysteries of Sin, therefore Sin is from the mysteries of things. Since Ha encompasses the mysteries of Hayat (life) and Mim is included in the mysteries of the womb (kunn), therefore Ha stands opposite Mim. When you reflect upon all this, you will know that the right [hand] is connected to ten pillars — this is the apparent twelve horizons, within which are the Name of the Essence, the power of the spheres, and Aliyy together. Then when you complete them for the manifestation of the kunya and the manifestation of mercy, the lights of these fifty-eight names will be found especially from the name Rahman. Although the higher and eternal world is based upon this, yet their Mim — which is certainly mercy — is from the blessed right (basid sa'id), and the degrees of the Word are connected, that is, to Rahim, so that the divine exclusivity may appear upon the eternal.
