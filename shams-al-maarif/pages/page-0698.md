Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Ba'ith"

Ba'ith is the One who accepts the prayers of the distressed and removes their anxiety, and He is God, may He be exalted.

Types of those who pray to God:
Those who pray to God are of four types:
(1) One whose prayer is in a state of desperation. This prayer is accepted.
(2) The second prayer is with the tongue of indifference, for which the one praying did not exert full effort. Sincerity must accompany this prayer so that patience may be granted over hardships.
(3) The third is the person who is struck by extreme poverty and destitution, and his prayer was accepted by God, may He be exalted.
(4) The fourth is the person who prays to God that he may obtain worldly abundance and honor.

This person is rejected because he has wasted his time on something that was never worthy of God's acceptance.
It is better to pray in such a way that God bestows blessing upon his sustenance, grants him success in good deeds, and aids the believers.

The Strange Incident of Hazrat Umar Khurasani:
It is related from Hazrat Umar Khurasani that he wrote: One year I was going for pilgrimage. On the way I fell into a well, and I said to myself that I would not cry out to anyone except God. Then some people passed by and I did not cry out. Then more people came and said that the mouth of this well should be covered so that no one falls in it, and they placed a stone over its mouth and covered it with dirt and left. I remained quietly trapped inside the well. Then, shortly after they left, a wild animal removed that dirt and stone and hung its tail inside the well and sat down. I grabbed its tail and came up, and gave thanks to God. A voice called out saying: "You cried out to Him, so how should We save you from that which you did not even imagine?"

Be Content with God's Decree:
The seeker on the path should hold firmly to the Sacred Law and be content with God's pleasure. He should not object to anything and should have patience over the first shock. How excellently God, may He be exalted, spoke concerning Hazrat Moses, peace be upon him:
**"So they said: O God, O Healer"**

And its special property is that when one has a [toothache], one should mention it. God will grant release from it. And if one reads it together with the name Fatah, his deputy Hatiyayil will descend and fulfill his need.

The Place is Blessed by Its Deputy's Name:
When his deputy's name is written and placed in a place, there will be great blessing and benefit. The arrangement for it is as follows:

Letter arrangement:

Row 1: Ba' | Alif | 'Ayn | Tha'
Row 2: 2 | 23 | 41 | 10
Row 3: 30 | 5 | 58 | 8
Row 4: 60 | 45 | 31 | 4
