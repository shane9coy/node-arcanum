# Page 367 (English translation)
The tyrant wrongs (others), and when the wronged one supplicates against him and complains about him to a king of great ambition, the kings rise in anger.

At His anger, water and air, night and day, the sun, the moon, and the stars, heat and cold, trees and stones, clouds and the Ever-Subsisting stand, and death and life stand at His gate like a servant standing for his master. There is no deity except Him, the Living, the Ever-Subsisting. He renewed existence day after day and annihilated past generations nation after nation, and He stilled the movements of those on earth and those in the sky without any sign from them. He satiated the people of extravagance and starved the people of fasting, and He annihilated all those figures while He remains eternally subsisting. Sleep and slumber do not overtake Him—[He is] above all that is above and below, in length and breadth. He decreed salvation and success, and sin and obligation upon His servants—those content with that obligation. His is what is in the heavens and the earth; all creatures necessarily belong to the stronghold of the Strong [or: the Strong One], and the believer is in His fortress and the hypocrite is in His prison.

And when the Day of Resurrection comes, every parent will be preoccupied with their child, and their father. No one intercedes with Him except whom He has chosen by His grace. Who is it that intercedes with Him except by His permission? [He is] the Creator of water and fire, of dust and air, and He made them the four elements—so fire, dust, air, and water are but like a seed in [comparison to] water, and fire, dust, air, water, and the Chair are but like a star in the sky, and fire, dust, air, water, the Throne, and the Chair are but a man with twenty dirhams, and the totality is in His grasp like an atom in the knowledge of the prophets—and the prophets know what is before them and what is behind them, but they do not encompass anything of His knowledge except what He wills.

He created the Throne's creation in four massive forms, prostrating beneath the majesty of their Lord, and their feet are upon the rocks—they resemble in faces a lion, an eagle, a rooster, and a wild goat.

And no companion will ask about another in [the day of] bliss. His Chair encompassed the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him. He revealed the verse of the Chair, fifty words from the greatest of the Mighty Quran—nothing like it has been heard by Moses—and it protects souls, spirits, wealth, children, dwellings, and livestock, and it cures the blind, the leprous, the paralytic, and the sick. Its station is great, its angel is ancient, its path is straight, and its virtue is universal. And He is Allah in the heavens and on earth, and He is the Most High, the Great.

All praise belongs to Allah, who established the signs of knowledge for the worlds. He made the realities of the Quran into His special [properties] and friends. He gave rest to the souls of the pious from hardship, suffering, and weariness, and He made the world like a garment of lights. The perfect grain of it was revealed to the virtuous people. The one who obeys Him is good, and the one who disobeys Him is evil. The tyrant cannot escape from the supplication of the wronged one—he complained about him to the king of great valor, before whose presence kings stand. At His anger, water, air, night, day, the sun, moon, and stars fear; heat, cold, trees, stones, and clouds fear; death and life stand at His gate like a servant for the master. There is no deity except Him; He is the Living, the Ever-Subsisting. He renewed existence day after day and destroyed nation after nation. The movements of those in the heavens and earth stopped without a sign—they became still. He filled the bellies of the extravagant and kept the fasting ones hungry, and He annihilated all those people, while the Everlasting, the Truth, remains. He is at the pinnacle of glory and elevation; for those people, the truth proved victorious. Beneath it, in length and breadth, He obtained salvation and success, and He commanded the fulfillment of those obligations that exist in the heavens and earth. All creatures necessarily belong to the Strong One, the believer is in His fortress, and the hypocrite is in His prison. At that time, on the Day of Resurrection, every parent will be preoccupied with their child and their father. No one intercedes with Him except by His permission. Who can intercede with Him except by His permission? The Creator of water, fire, dust, and air, and He made them the four elements—so fire, dust, air, and water are but like a seed in the ocean, and fire, dust, air, water, and the Chair are but like a star in the sky, and fire, dust, air, water, the Throne, and the Chair are but a man with twenty dirhams, and the totality is in His grasp like an atom in the knowledge of the prophets. The prophets know what is before them and what is behind them, but they do not encompass anything of His knowledge except what He wills. He created the Throne's creation in four massive forms, prostrating beneath the majesty of their Lord, and their feet are upon the rocks. They resemble in faces a lion, an eagle, a rooster, and a wild goat. No companion will ask about another in the abode of bliss. His Chair encompassed the heavens and the earth, and their preservation does not weary Him. He revealed the verse of the Chair, fifty words from the greatest of the Mighty Quran. Moses never heard anything like it. It protects souls, spirits, wealth, children, dwellings, and livestock, and it cures the blind, the leprous, the paralytic, and the sick. Its station is great, its angel is ancient, its path is straight, and its virtue is universal. He is Allah in the heavens and on earth, and He is the Most High, the Great.
