Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Mujib" (The Responder)

Mujib is the One who fulfills the requests of those who ask, responds to the cries of those who cry out, and removes the distress of the distressed—and all these attributes belong to God, the Exalted.

God feels shame at sending His servant away empty-handed:
God, the Exalted, feels shame at turning His servant away empty-handed. It is therefore fitting for the servant that he fulfill His commands with his heart. He should not neglect any command. And it is fitting for the knower (ʿārif) that he observe all the inner realities and apparent matters, for the motion of all of them is one.

For the acceptance of supplication:
In the acceptance of supplication there is also a strange and particular quality of the Name. It delivers the desired object and grants blessings. If one wishes to incline the heart of any king or the like toward oneself, let him take down that king's picture and place it before himself in seclusion, and begin to recite the Name, and write this image on a fresh brick and keep it with him—the purpose will be achieved. And if he engraves it on a silver tablet and keeps it with him and continues to recite its remembrance, then whatever supplication he makes to God will be accepted. Its form is as follows:

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the Responder to the supplication of the supplicant when he is sincere in his supplication, and the quickener of the desperate with response before they ask, for You know the need of those in need through what has preceded in Your ancient knowledge from the decreed matters and the execution of what You have decided from the established decrees and the swiftness of Your command in the quarters of the earth and the layers of the heavens. I ask You to respond to my supplication and to hasten the fulfillment of my need, to remove from me the evil of my adversaries, to grant me security from my terrors and my fears, to subdue whoever intends harm against me, and to elevate my ranks to my ultimate aims. You are the ultimate end of my aims from all my directions and all my orientations. O God, O Near, O Responder.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wasie" (The Vast/Encompassing)

The meaning of the name Wasie is vastness. Sometimes it is connected to an attribute, and sometimes to the Creator. From the perspective of the world, its attribute is as follows: it is very vast, for it encompassed the realities of sovereignty with its existence, and beneficence and bounty are attributed to it, and its beneficence encompasses the entire cosmos.
