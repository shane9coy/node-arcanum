## Strange Operations for Creating Love and Hatred Among People

It is narrated that His Eminence Ali, may God honor his face, asked a Jewish man for a number in which all quotients from one-half up to ten are contained without remainder. He said: "If I tell you, will you accept faith?" The man said: "Yes." He said: "Multiply the days of the week by the dates of the month, then multiply the product by the months of the year, and you will obtain the answer." That is, the sum total is 2520. Its half is 1260, its third is 840, its quarter is 630, and its fifth is 504. Its sixth is 420, its seventh is 360 [text appears incomplete here], its eighth is 280, and its tenth is 252. Understand this divine knowledge, for it is the grace of God Almighty. God Almighty gives His dominion to whom He wills, and God the Great is the possessor of grace.

The luminous letters are fourteen. This is their total, that is, they are collected in this manner: "Nuṣṣ ḥakīm qāṭi' lah lā sirr ḥadd kalām." They have two categories: superior and inferior. Seven letters are superior, collected in "Qūwāl daq fī ghab makhṭūm." The inferior seven are "Qūwāl ḥaʿsh ḍahā laẓhar." Each luminous letter has corresponding darkness letters.

Regarding luminous letters, they are collected in "Sam'ak al-naṣḥah ḥaṭṭah," and "Sarā jamī'a man ẓunūbika saṭara sikr" knows them.

Know that when darkness letters are removed and added to someone's name, and a candle is placed while the Moon is in Scorpio, then buried in an old grave, sorrow and grief will be inflicted upon that person's heart from all sides, and severe distress will befall him. But fear God Almighty and do not afflict anyone with suffering without cause.

## Fulfillment of Needs:

It is written that someone said: When a need arises, take the numerical value of one's name and one's mother's name and the numerical value of the desired thing, then combine them in one place. For example, if a child is ill, one should go to the name of God Almighty, by whose decree it will be fulfilled.

Know that when you wish to see someone, take incense, or rather the earth, of that person along with his name and the letters of his fortune, then perform the extraction. Add it to his hour and leaf, and his temperament will spontaneously turn toward you. This is a very subtle secret against others.

His Eminence our Master Imam Ja'far al-Ṣādiq, may God grant him peace, said: When you face a difficulty, first perform the calculation from the numerical values of the desired thing, then perform i'tikāf [spiritual retreat]...
