The remaining time is appropriate for its reciter, and this name is such that whoever performs its litany, the door of "nearness" opens for him, and he becomes acquainted with the secrets of the inner reality.

The hadith of the Arab in the collection is appropriate for it, when he said during the bowing:

**رَبَّنَا لَكَ الْحَمْدُ حَمْدًا كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ مِلْءُ سَمَوَاتِكَ وَ أَرْضِكَ وَ مِلْءُ مَا شِئْتَ مِنْ شَيْءٍ بَعْدُ**

"O our Lord, to You belongs all praise, abundant good blessed praise, the fill of Your heavens and Your earth, and the fill of whatever You will after that."

So the Messenger of God ﷺ said: Who is the one saying these words? The Arab said: O Messenger of God, it is I. He said: I saw seventy angels writing it down.

The incident of the hadith of Zayd ibn Harithah:

In the hadith of Zayd ibn Harithah it is narrated that a Kurdish bandit surrounded him and intended to kill him, and said to him: O Zayd, prepare yourself for killing. He said: Give me time so that I may pray. He said: Many besides you have prayed, and none of them derived any benefit from it. Zayd performed ablution and prayed two units, then raised his hands and recited this supplication:

**اَللَّهُمَّ يَا وَدُوْدُ يَا ذَا الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيْدِ يَا مُبْدِيُّ يَا مُعِيْدُ يَا فَعَّالٌ لِمَا يُرِيْدُ اَسْئَلُكَ بِنُوْرِ وَجْهِكَ الَّذِي مَلَاَ اَرْكَانَ عَرْشِكَ وَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ الَّتِي قَدَرْتَ بِهَا عَلَى جَمِيْعِ خَلْقِكَ وَ بِرَحْمَتِكَ الَّتِي وَسِعَتْ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ يَا غِيَاثَ الْمُسْتَغِيْثِيْنَ اَغِثْنِي۔** (3 times)

"O God, O Most Affectionate, O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O He Who Brings Forth, O He Who Returns, O Doer of what He wills, I ask You by the light of Your face which filled the pillars of Your throne, and by Your power with which You had power over all of Your creation, and by Your mercy which encompassed everything—there is no god except You, O Helper of those who seek help, help me." (3 times)

The bandit wanted to strike, but hearing a noise from the unseen, his hand refrained from killing. While he was thus, a rider came galloping and called out to the bandit: Beware, do not kill this person—come before me and I will bring you to the place of execution. The bandit turned toward the rider. The rider cut him in two with a single blow and said to Zayd: When you recited the supplication the first time, Gabriel called out: Who will help this helpless one? I said: I am going. I was at that time on the seventh heaven. Then when you recited the supplication the second time, I was at that time on the heaven of the world, and when you recited it the third time, I came to you, killed him, and lo! O Zayd, whoever recites this supplication and makes a request, it will be granted immediately.

**The supplication is answered when accompanied by the Greatest Name:**

Then when Zayd came to Medina, he mentioned this incident to the Messenger of God ﷺ. He said: O Zayd, God has put into your mouth that Greatest Name of His by which whoever makes a supplication, his supplication is answered.

From the same category comes this extremely beneficial supplication: whoever writes it on red paper and keeps it with him, charity and blessings will come to him without his even being aware of it. Whoever recites it during the last portion of the night until the break of dawn, whatever need he asks from God, his supplication will be answered.

**When the supplication is answered, light is seen issuing from the reciter's mouth:**

Truly, from the perfection of these words, light will appear from the mouth by which the area around him will be illuminated. For dispelling grief, obtaining safety, or acquiring good fortune, when he intends to learn [his fortune], let him recite it—the Exalted Lord will soon fulfill his purpose, and this supplication is:

**اَللَّهُمَّ يَا اَسْرَعَ التَّكْوِيْنِ لِكَلِمَتِكَ وَ اَقْرَبَ الْاِسْتِجَابَتِ لِدَعْوَتِكَ اَسْئَلُكَ بِمَا ظَهَرَتْ اِلَيَّ**

"O God, O Fastest in bringing into existence Your word and nearest in answering Your call, I ask You by what has appeared to me..."
