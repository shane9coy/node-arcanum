# Page 180 (English translation)
This prayer is for the destruction and ruin of tyrants and rebels:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Exalted are You, O He who broke the arrogant and the proud, and cut off the roots of the Pharaohs and the mockers, and struck humiliation upon the tyrants and the rebellious. How quickly descends Your severe punishment, and how quickly descends Your glorious subjugation upon every stubborn tyrant and rebellious devil who rebelled against the servants and transgressed in the lands and sought corruption therein. In You do I seek refuge, my God. Grant me victory. To You do I complain against those who wronged me, and I ask You, my Lord, to grant me victory over those who abandoned me, and to defeat those who confronted me, and to subdue those who fought me, and to abandon my enemies and defeat them wherever they gather, and to curse them and expose them wherever they separate, and to crush them wherever they unite.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. You are exalted, O He who broke the tyrants and the proud. You have cut off the roots of the Pharaohs and the mockers. You have struck humiliation upon the rebellious and the defiant. How quickly descends Your severe grip! How quickly descends Your subjugation upon every stubborn tyrant and rebellious devil who rebelled against the servants and transgressed in the cities and sought corruption therein. O Allah, I seek help through Your power. I complain against those who wronged me. I ask You, my Lord, to grant me victory over him who fights me and defeats my opponent and gains victory over him who fights me. Humiliate my enemies. Where they gather, defeat them. Curse and disgrace them where they separate, and crush them where they unite.

And make them wander in confusion toward injustice, and upon misguidance they kill, and from blessing they argue—they cannot benefit by piety or openly, nor can they gain any reward or wage, nor can they obtain any victory except through patience. And send upon them a punishment from above them and from beneath their feet, and clothe them in discord,1 and cause some of them to taste the violence of others, and make them fuel for Hellfire. And another: Keep them from uprightness, and give them abundant water to drink,2 and make their wealth upon the earth barren and sterile, and send down upon their gardens a reckoning from the heaven so that it becomes trampled ground, or its water sinks into the depths so they will never be able to seek it, nor will its condition ever improve for them. And make them among the greatest losers in deeds, and do not raise for them a banner, and make them among the frustrated, and do not extend for them any support, and make them among the frustrated. They cannot eat or drink, and they find no rest on land or mount,3 and place before them a barrier and behind them a barrier, and upon their right sides a堵住4 and upon their left sides a barrier, and upon their heads a rock and beneath their feet a crushing weight, so that they cannot advance by foot nor find sustenance, and no good comes to them. And place shackles upon their necks and drag them in chains and fetters upon their feet, and shake them with earthquakes and shackles upon their necks and enemies at their heels, and confine them in their homes so that they may not prosper. And confound their speech so that they may not be guided, and confound their spirits so that they may not bear witness, and make their souls despondent so that they may not see, and blind their eyes so that they may not understand, and deafen their ears so that they may not hear, and blind their eyes so that they may not see, and seal their mouths so that they may not speak, and transform them upon their places so that they may not—
