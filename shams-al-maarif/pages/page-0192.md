# Page 192 (English translation)
Obtaining Blessings, Protection from Calamities

Know that this talisman is sufficient and the practice of princes and kings. Whoever keeps it with them, their honor, awe, generosity, elevation, exaltation, and chieftaincy increase. Their rank becomes elevated, blessings descend, they remain protected from calamities, and all their needs are fulfilled. In it are secrets for those worthy of guidance and lights for those of renunciation. By the blessing of this talisman, desired success is achieved. There will be increase in religion and sincerity, the etiquette of repentance, success, strength of preservation, victory, obedience, affection and love, protection and sufficiency, security and safety, kingdom and sovereignty, the abundance of livelihood, the expanse of emirate, joy and happiness, the increase of status and rank. The removal of illness, sudden death, and pain and suffering is obtained. Similarly, the subtleties of sciences, the subtleties of the details of dreams, the conversation with wisdom, realities, and knowledge are informed. Because its nature has the effect of increasing wealth and rank, many children, and the removal of illnesses, pains, and sorrows. Whoever traces this talisman on a copper tablet when the moon is in a quadrant, and has already recited the 'Azimat 1289 times before tracing, Allah the Almighty will blind the eyes of every rebellious tyrant.

Benefits of the Seal of Ayat al-Kursi

If one maintains a sincere state, one will become invisible to all people. Whoever, seeing a favorable augury in the exaltation of Jupiter, traces it on a gold or silver tablet and, wearing it around the neck, goes to the battlefield, will be victorious and triumphant. No envier's envy or any enemy's malice can harm him. He will be beloved to kings, sultans, ministers, and women. Whoever sees him will honor and revere him. It is essential that it be fumigated with perfume every Thursday, for wherever perfume is fumigated, there will be good and blessing. Wherever this talisman is in a house, Allah the Almighty will remove every plague, trial, and illness from that house. If this talisman is placed upon an epileptic person, he will immediately find relief. If this talisman is placed in water and given to drink to an unconscious person, his unconsciousness will be lifted at that very moment. If the evil eye is washed off with this talisman and given to drink to the afflicted person, he will immediately find relief. Likewise, it is useful for warding off thieves, robbers, snakes, scorpions, all wild beasts, and all harmful things. This is a great attraction and noble secret. By its means, Abraham, peace be upon him, was saved from Nimrod. Jonah, peace be upon him, was saved from the belly of the fish, and by its means, wild beasts, birds, jinn and humans, and the wind were made subject to Solomon, peace be upon him. In it is the Greatest Name of Allah, by whose blessing Muhammad, may peace be upon him, continued to have access to the disbelievers. Whoever recognized its value became indifferent to many things, because many of its special properties are beyond counting. Whoever uses it without purification, Allah the Almighty will afflict him with some illness.

The practice of this should be done by one who engages in spiritual discipline and struggle, whose spirit has become strong with the light of warriors. Know that there is a strange effect in obtaining the good of this verse. To remove illnesses, write it for the sick or the thirsty, diarrhea, urinary incontinence, treatment of limbs, eyes, and barrenness, it should be written on pure paper. For one with epilepsy, brain disorder, or distressed condition, write it on a pure stone and hang it. To go before kings, rulers, chiefs, and armies, write this talisman on something pure and honorable. To drive away enemies from a house, write it and bury it in the house; then thieves, robbers, fire, snakes, and harmful creatures like scorpions will also be driven away. This is a very superior thing, because in it are the Greatest Name and the talisman, etc.
