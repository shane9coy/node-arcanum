# Page 174 (English translation)
Chapter 14

**Invocations and Accepted Supplications**

Abu 'Abdullah Muhammad ibn Idris al-Razi states that a book was found among the market and brokers' records from the treasury of Harun al-Rashid, in which it is written that Asad ibn 'Asim states: A person from among the ascetics of Kufah would, on the day of 'Arafah or al-Tarwiyah, perform ritual ablution, wear two white garments, then depart from his home and perform prayer, then make this supplication, and then he would continue observing the people in Mecca or 'Arafat. The supplication is this:

*ahiya sharahiya nur hune ya wajid hayy fard quddus rabb jibril wa mika'il wa israfiyl wa 'izra'il wa as'aluka bismika wa anta la tukhayyibu man da'aka allahumma an tusalliya 'ala sayyidina muhammadin wa 'ala ali sayyidina muhammadin*

Make your request according to your need. By reading this supplication, anxieties are removed, and whatever you desire of food and drink will be brought to you—it comes by the mercy of Allah the Exalted. If you intend to do this, fast for five days at dawn with solitude. Give three dirhams in charity, then recite these divine names and supplicate, and it will be accepted. Another narration states that a person from among the ascetics of Kufah would, on the day of 'Arafah, perform ablution, wear white garments, ascend upon a mound, and recite this supplication. The supplication is this:

*allahumma inni as'aluka bismika wa anta la tukhayyibu man da'aka bismika al-rahman al-must'an al-muhaymin al-kabir al-muta'al al-tahir al-batin al-mufarrid al-mahmud al-fattah al-muqaddir al-fayyaz as'aluka an taqdiya hajataya allahumma hawwin 'alayya al-safar wa atwi liya al-bu'd*

Translation: O Allah, I ask You by Your names—and You do not disappoint one who supplicates to You by the names al-Rahman, al-Must'an, al-Muhaymin, al-Kabir, al-Muta'al, al-Tahir, al-Batin, al-Mufarrid, al-Mahmud, al-Fattah, al-Muqaddir, al-Fayyaz—I ask You to fulfill my need. O Allah, make travel easy for me and shorten the distance for me. And whatever need you have, state it, and it will be fulfilled by the command of Allah. These are twelve divine names, the Divine Light, excepting the thief. And if your desire is not fulfilled by this supplication, then the deficiency lies with you, not with the supplication, for this supplication is most perfect. A person does not incur sin who, while giving sincere counsel, adhering to lawful sustenance, fasting and prayer, with spiritual discipline and sincere intention, supplicates to Allah.

**Unlawful Food Prevents Acceptance of Supplication:**

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: Whoever makes supplication while his food, drink, and clothing are unlawful, how can his supplication be accepted? Therefore, make the use of lawful things obligatory upon yourselves, and your supplication will be accepted, for this is the supplication of Magnificence and Fortitude.
