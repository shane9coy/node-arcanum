# Page 178 (English translation)
I say that You place light in my heart, and create light in my sight, my hearing, my tongue, my bones, and my flesh. I seek refuge from above my hair, from my right and left, from before me and behind me, from above me and below me, from the matter that deception be practiced against me, so cover me with Your light, for You are the Light of Lights, the illuminator of the亲近 ones and the righteous. Holy and pure is the Lord of the angels and the Spirit who are present in the Holy Presence. He is the Lord of those angels who do what they are commanded. He is the Lord of those angels who walk upon the earth. O Allah, I ask You with those spirits who were given virtue along with the ten nights, and I ask with those spirits who were assigned with the breaths of time. O Allah, I ask You to support me with Your spirit—for me nothing is strong, nor should the binding of a thousand veils prevent and obstruct me from the binding of my nature—so that I stand in this presence with which You have connected me, and immerse me in those lights from which You have raised me, so that I become strong, the spirits of luminosity are arrayed against me, and with the lines of nobility I live, for You are indeed the Eternal, the Sustainer, the Manifest, and the Revealer, the Opener, the Hearing, the Softening, the Holy, the Sublime, the Strong, and the Great.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. There is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Eternal Sustainer. He sent down upon you the Book with truth, confirming what came before it, and He sent down the Torah and the Gospel, guidance for the people, and the Criterion. Indeed, We have sent down the Reminder, and We are its guardian. Allah is the light of the heavens and the earth. The example of His light is like a niche containing a lamp, the lamp is within a glass, the glass is as if it were a glittering star; it is lit from a blessed olive tree, neither eastern nor western, whose oil would almost glow even if fire barely touched it. Light upon light, Allah guides to His light whom He wills, and Allah sets forth for the people examples, and Allah has knowledge of all things.

Ya-Sin. By the Wise Quran. Indeed you are among the messengers upon a straight path, a revelation from the Mighty, the Merciful, so that you may warn a people whose fathers were not warned, so they are heedless. Indeed the word has come to pass upon most of them, yet they do not believe. Indeed We have placed chains upon their necks, and they reach to the chins, so they are with heads raised. And We have placed before them a barrier and behind them a barrier, and We have covered them, so they do not see. It is the same to them whether you warn them or warn them not, they will not believe. You warn only one who follows the Reminder and fears the Most Gracious in the unseen; so give him glad tidings of forgiveness and a generous reward. Indeed We give life to the dead, and We record what they have sent before and what they leave behind, and We have numbered all things in a clear register. And cite for them the example of the companions of the city, when the messengers came to it, when We sent to them two but they denied them, so We strengthened them with a third, and they said, "Indeed we have been sent to you." They said, "You are only like us, and the Most Gracious has not revealed anything; you are only lying." They said, "Our Lord knows that we have been sent to you, and we are only clearengers to you." Indeed we do not have except clearenger upon you and upon what you associate with Him. And they said, "Our lot has been made to depend upon us," and they returned with their plotting. Have they not seen that We made their land shrinking, and when they are gathered, they are the ones in the wrong? Have they not seen that We drive the water to a land that is barren, and We bring forth thereby grain from which they and their cattle eat? Have they not seen? Rather, they do not fear the Hereafter. And when they see you, they take you only as a jest, saying, "Is this the one whom Allah has sent as a messenger? Indeed he would have nearly misled us from our deities, had we not been steadfast in relation to them." And they do not recognize the gratitude due to them. The Most High is the Sovereign of the throne, He sends inspiration by His command to whom He wills among His servants, saying, "Warn that there is no deity except Me, so fear Me." He is the Lord of the east and the west; there is no deity except Him, so take Him as protector. Rather, they say, "Magic! Or he has made it up." Say, "My Lord has made it clear to me; you are in clearenger about it." And those who have no knowledge of it say, "Why does not Allah speak to us, or a sign come to us?" Thus said those before them, "A heart like theirs." Allah does make clear the sign for a people who believe with certainty. Indeed your Lord is truly the one who will gather them on the Day of Standing, about which there is no doubt, but most people do not believe. And your Lord is the Best to come to and the Best to ask. And He is the One who makes you laugh and makes you weep. And He gives you death and gives you life. And He created the spouses from yourselves, and through them He made for you wives and sons and grandchildren, and He provides you from the good things. Is there in that a sign for those who believe? And if they say, "We have obeyed our command and our affair belongs to Allah," they would be nearer to the truth, but they do not say it. And they say, "If only we had been with those who were right, we would have been among those who are safe." So today those who disbelieve have despaired of your religion, so do not hold them in awe, but hold Me in awe, and today I have completed for you your religion and have completed upon you My blessing and have approved for you Islam as religion. But whoever is compelled by hunger with no inclining to sin, then indeed Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. They say, "We have obeyed Allah and the messenger," then they turn away; yet half of them, or rather most of them, do not fear Allah. And when a chapter comes down saying, "Believe in Allah and fight with His messenger," those with affluence among them ask you for exemption, saying, "Leave us with those who sit." They prefer to be with those who remain, and their hearts have been sealed, so they do not understand. But the messenger and those who believe with him fight with their wealth and their lives; for them are the good things, and they are the successful ones. Allah has prepared for them gardens beneath which rivers flow, to abide therein; that is the great achievement. And some of the desert Arabs came making excuses, asking to be granted exemption, and those who lied to Allah and His messenger sat idle; soon there shall be for those who disbelieve among them a painful punishment. Upon the weak and the sick, and upon those who do not find what they would give, there is no blame upon them if they are sincere to Allah and His messenger; and there is no excuse against the good-doers; and Allah is Forgiving, Merciful. Nor upon those who, when they came to you requesting mounts, and you said, "I do not find what to mount you on," they returned with their eyes flowing with tears from grief that they did not find what to give. The excuse is only for those who ask permission of you when they have no need; they would have been content to be with those who remain, and Allah has sealed their hearts, so they do not know. They ask you for exemption at the time of departure; say, "You shall not depart with me, nor shall you fight an enemy with me; indeed they said before, 'We are not going to fight, and they remain.'"

And this is also a strange prayer:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Lord, there is no one who has made him glad with the repentance of a servant whom Your hand of sin has attracted, and whom the cold of Your pardon has thrilled, and the sweetness of Your forgiveness, so that he becomes in the morning after his audacity in committing prohibitions and his joy in acquiring sins and his drowning in the requital of desires, cut off from disputes, occupied with returning festivals, drawn by the rays of caring mercies, faced by the rays of merciful mercies that gather the lights of guidances to all excellences and abundant benefits and attaining precious things, and immersed in the seas of Your mercy, standing in the plain of Your Presence until the completion of Your knowledge, crowned with crowns of honor, adorned with the morals of safety, and mixed with the spirits of the foremost. O Lord, I ask You for a sincere repentance—join me with it in the first rank of those who repent, and adorn me with the attributes of the worshippers, and the splendor of the praisers, and the purity of the travelers, and the annihilation of those who bow, and the persistence of those who prostrate, and the tranquility of the inheritors, and the perfection of the complete ones. Be You, Exalted, with me before Your angels, and draw near to the seekers through the witnessing of You, as You turn my heart between the fingers of Your gentleness, purified from Your mercy, and let a moment of the lights of Your splendor and Your witnessing fall upon me. Indeed You are the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, the Great Forgiver, the Clement, the Bestower, the Generous, the Ever-Forgiving, the Compassionate, the Righteous, the Merciful, and You are the Loving.
