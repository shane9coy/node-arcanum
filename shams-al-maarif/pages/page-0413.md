# Page 413 (English translation)
...remains from every grief-stricken one, the one who removes grief. Pure is He who made water flow in rivers and springs, whose treasures are between kaf and nun. Pure is He when He intends something, He says to it "Be" and it becomes.

O Allah, make Rufa'il subservient to me, as You made the sea subservient for Moses (peace be upon him), fire for Abraham (peace be upon him), iron for David (peace be upon him), wind, jinn, and humans subservient for Solomon (peace be upon him), and the sun, moon, stars, and all things subservient for our Noble Prophet Muhammad (may Allah bless him and grant him peace). I ask You to make Rufa'il subservient to me, who fulfills my need by the blessing of Your Greatest Supreme Name, and by the blessing of Your Beautiful Names: O Allah, O Swift, O Near, O Responsive, O Expander, O Loving. I ask You by the light of Your Essence which filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your Power which You have decreed over all Your creation. Help me and fulfill my need in this hour, by the right of these verses.

...by the right of the Compassionate and Affectionate One, and by the right of this Master whose command prevails over you.
