Chapter 57: Properties of the Name "Hamid" — Greatness among People of Good Character

This is a supreme and exalted Name. Whoever recites this Name frequently, his characteristics become praiseworthy and he attains greatness among people. If one writes it in a glass vessel and drinks from it, he is healed from every ailment. It is suitable for one whose name is Mahmud that he should make this Name an object of research — that creation should praise him and that unveiling should be attained alongside him. You are all the tongue of Mahmud. Muhammad ﷺ is the manifestation of Praise and the opener of the books of existence. Just as you ﷺ stated: "Allah, the Exalted, created my light first." Thus the Noble Prophet ﷺ is entirely Praise. Allah, the Exalted, inaugurated the books of existence through you. You are the matter of the tongue — meaning every deed not begun with praise is incomplete — and you are Praise itself. For this reason, the deity of all the prophets, upon them be peace, was Praise, which was the deity of the Noble Prophet ﷺ: "All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of all worlds." Thus you ﷺ are the opener and the sealer. In this manner, Allah, the Exalted, began the book of trials through you. Likewise, the books of deeds will also begin through you. Accordingly, you ﷺ said: "When I supplicate, I begin with that from which I began before." For this reason, Surah Al-Hamd was专属 to the Noble Prophet ﷺ, which is the Opener of the Book and the treasure beneath the Throne — the Surah for which no one has been opened. Understand these luminous particles: "Huwa al-Tayyib" — when this word is comprehended, it will be understood here. Its number is 62, which is the luminous spirit. Its components are 34 in total. Chapter 58: Properties of the Name "Muhsi" — Sweetness in Contemplative Devotion

This is the Name of one who is Clement in essence and Glorious in proof. Whoever recites this Name frequently attains the sweetness of contemplative devotion. For one for whom the Name "Haseb" is appropriate, the recitation of this Name is also appropriate. Its number is 148. The forty are for attaining completeness, and the hundred are for recitation. Some scholars say its perfection is encompassing. Its number is both even composite and odd deficient. Its components are 118. They indicate the Name "Hai" of Allah, the Exalted. According to the Glorious in proof, they indicate the Name "Malik" (King), because the aspects of sovereignty and perfection are better than satiation. Allah, the Exalted, is better.

Notice: Know that the relationship between the Name "Rahim" and the Name "Hamid" is very deep; their luminous emanation is becoming clear.
