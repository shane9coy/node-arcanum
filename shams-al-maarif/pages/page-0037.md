"Indeed, the eyes are not blind, but blind are the hearts that are within the breasts." "Indeed, the eyes are not blind, but rather those hearts are blind which are within the chests." And that which is between the letter and the heart—there is the place of love. It also has a luminous eye, with which it perceives the seeking and searching. It strives in the search for something sought. Because of its subtlety, this part is connected to sincerity very quickly. Upon this, the disclosure of the world of the kingdom and all the universe and divine creatures takes place, and the long beauty is known to those who love beauty. The first letter also has a luminous eye, with which it perceives the mysteries of sensations and the modes of compounds. It sees the realities of the letters and what Almighty Allah has concealed within them of the mysteries of the divine names and their gnostic realities. For this reason, He has affection for the servants of Allah. This is for the glorification of Almighty Allah along with His knowledge. Almighty Allah bestowed upon it this blessing that the mysteries of sensations were disclosed to it. These are all the eyes of hearts, but they differ in matters, and all are different. This has been explained in our book "Mawaqif al-Basar wa Lataif al-Sarair" [The Stations of Insight and the Subtleties of Secrets].

Mention of the Types of Revelation

In the divine books, there are three spirits: one is the Trustworthy Spirit, the second is the Holy Spirit, and the third is the Spirit of Command. The Trustworthy Spirit's revelation indicates the first letter of the heart—that remembrance is a veil between speech and tongue. It holds the first rank among all types of divine inspiration and revelation. Then after it comes the Holy Spirit, which comprises those lights that descend upon the second letter of the heart from the Mixed Light, establishing the insight of the tongue and thought. The divine lights of wisdom, the angelic lights, and the inner subtleties manifest upon it, and this is the second rank or portion of the letter of the heart's core, which is the place of the Holy Light. Hearing and intellect are also within it alone. Almighty Allah said to His Noble Prophet ﷺ:

"Indeed, you cannot make the dead hear, nor can you make the deaf hear the call."
"Indeed, you O Muhammad ﷺ cannot make the dead hear your voice, nor can you make the deaf hear your call."

Here, death does not mean sensory death but rather the death of disbelief and misguidance. From deafness, what is meant is not the physical deafness of the ears because their auditory faculty existed. Indeed, what is meant by "hearing" is that which is related to the heart—that is, the faculty of intellect—and this is the place where the Spirit of Command descends, which points toward the complete command and the universal reality. Only Muhammad ﷺ is specifically designated for this revelation. We have expounded upon this subject with great detail in our book "Mawaqif al-Ghayat fi Asrar al-Riyadat" [The Stations of Ultimate Goals in the Mysteries of Spiritual Disciplines] along with the benefits of hearts, lights, and insights. Look at it therein, God willing, Almighty Allah. You will find in it a firm foundation of power. Almighty Allah said:

"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds—the Most Merciful will appoint for them affection."
"Indeed, those people who have believed and done good deeds—soon the Most Merciful will place love for them (in hearts)." That is, creating affection in their hearts will be the work of Allah. He is Almighty Allah Who will do this work because those people have produced in their hearts the love of Almighty Allah through various kinds of divine remembrance and the recitation and study of the Noble Quran, and those who glorify Almighty Allah
