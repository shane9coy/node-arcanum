# Chapter 5

## The Properties and Blessings of Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim

Allah the Almighty grants me and you the success of His obedience and gives understanding of the mysteries of His Names. Allah the Almighty has placed great mysteries in Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim, which whoever learns it shall not burn in the Fire, and the Fire cannot harm him in any way. Whoever writes it on a page and keeps it with him shall also not burn in the Fire. All scholars have agreed that every important matter should be begun with Bismillah, due to the Noble Quran, because Abu Hurairah may Allah be pleased with him narrated from the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, that the honored Messenger said: "Every matter begun without Bismillah is incomplete." In one narration it states that it will be cut off. In another narration it states that it will be deprived of blessing.

It is narrated that the books revealed from Allah the Almighty consist of one hundred and four scrolls. Sixty scrolls were revealed to Seth, peace be upon him. Thirty scrolls were revealed to Abraham, peace be upon him, and ten scrolls were revealed to Moses, peace be upon him, before the revelation of the Torah. The revealed books are the Torah, the Psalms, the Gospel, and the Noble Quran. The sum of the meanings of all books is the Noble Quran. The sum of the meanings of the Noble Quran is Surah al-Fatihah, and the sum of Surah al-Fatihah is Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim, and the sum of Bismillah is in the letter ba, which means: "Bi ma kana wa bi ma yakun" — meaning: "That which was was with Me, and that which will be is with Me." It is narrated that when Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim was revealed, the earth perspired from its revelation, and the angels of Hell said that whoever reads Bismillah shall not enter Hell. It contains nineteen letters, and the guardian angels of Hell are also nineteen. May Allah the Almighty protect us all and keep us in safety.

It is narrated from Jabir, may Allah be pleased with him, who said: When Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim was revealed, clouds fled toward the east, the wind was stilled, and the sea disappeared. By the command of Allah the Almighty, animals bowed their necks and became silent, and meteors were thrown at the devils from heaven. Allah the Almighty swore that when His Name is blown upon a patient, he will be healed, and whenever His Name is pronounced over something, blessing will be given to it. Ibn Mas'ud said that whoever wishes for salvation from the inmates of Hell should recite Bismillah abundantly, because its letters are nineteen — with each letter one will obtain salvation from an angel. Whoever remembers Bismillah abundantly will be granted awe in the higher and lower worlds. By the blessing of this very Bismillah the kingdom of Solomon, peace be upon him, was established. Whoever writes it one hundred times and keeps it with him, people will hold him in awe. It is narrated from Ibn Umar that whoever has a need should fast on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday, then on Friday perform ghusl and go to the congregational mosque, give some charity to the poor, then after the Friday prayer recite this supplication:

"Allahumma inni as'aluka bismillah al-rahman al-rahim alladhi la ilaha illa huwa al-hayyu al-qayyum la ta'khudhuhu sinatun wa la nawm alladhi malat lahu al-wujuha wa khasha'at lahu al-aswat wa wajilat al-qulub min khashyatihi, as'aluka an tusalliya wa tusallima 'ala sayyidina Muhammadin wa 'ala alihi wa sahbihi wa sallim wa an taqdiya hajati" (mention your need by name).

Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with him, says that this supplication is a means of steadfastness for trees bearing fruit and a gift — every supplicant's supplication will be accepted by this supplication. The sacred proclamation of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, is that the difference between Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim and the Greatest Name of Allah is as much as the difference between the white and black of the eye [illegible]
