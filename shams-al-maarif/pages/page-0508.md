# Page 508 (English translation)
506

Write the letter Alif in accordance with its numerical value, and also write the name of the person whose love you desire, under a favorable ascendant; then fumigate it with aloe and sandalwood, and keep it with you—that person will be favorable toward you.
**For strengthening memory:**
If you write the letter Alif one thousand times with musk and saffron at the time when the Moon is in the first sign of Aries, and then wear it as an amulet on the right chest, his memory will become strong. There is another treatment with the letter Alif: if you engraves it on a golden tablet while the Sun is in its place of dignity, or write it with musk and saffron on deerskin and keep it with you, strength and dominance will be attained. And if you engraves it on a silver ring, recite Surah Yasin upon it and wear it, great awe will be obtained, and all tongues will be silenced from speaking ill of him.
**For expansion of sustenance:**
If you write this talismanic design on deerskin and write the letter Alif around it, and recite this name of the Provider along with that essence. God will give him sustenance from a place where he would not even imagine, and if you write it on white paper and attach it to a shop or house, good fortune and blessing will increase in it.
**For love:**
Whoever writes this square and writes this verse around it:
"They love them as they love Allah, but those who have faith have intense love for Allah. If you were to spend all that is in the earth..."
Let him write it on Sunday or Thursday, then wash it and give it to drink to whichever woman—the husband will be pleased with her. Likewise, if two persons have enmity between them, their enmity will be dispelled. The figure of the love talisman is as follows:

Know that the encompassed letters of Alif are 111, and the Name of Truth (al-Haqq) is Sufficient, and this is its highest name, and the letter of the plane is the Greatest Name. And know that in every cell where a word is written, it is Alif. So now I explain it to you. Alif, Lam, and Mim are the luminous letters of hope, and they are the first letters of the Greatest Name. Alif-Lam-Mim—these are the key of the Holy Name, because they are unique and singular, and they are composed of three letters which are its craft and its origin. Alif, Lam, and Mim are also singular, and they are the collection of the craft, the material, and the principles—and this too is singular.
Then when you examine the nine letters of these principles and drop the repeated letter, five letters will remain. That is: Alif, Lam, Mim, Ha, and Ya—these too are singular, and with these five the entirety of this science is accomplished, and this too is singular. So consider that the principle of singularity is necessarily and intrinsically linked with these principles. And when it is collected within the principles of origins, one Alif, Lam, and one Mim is produced from it—and this is the Name of Allah, whose numerical value is 111. It is said: Alif-Lam-Mim consists of this name, and this is His name, whose number comprises the Beautiful Names, and that is Allah. Page 109.
