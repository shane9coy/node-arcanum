# Page 636 (English translation)
[Huwala]

**The Mention of the Seven Climes and Their Names:**

The first clime is the light of divine love. This is the station of the king, because the Exalted God says, "Whatever my earth and my heaven have not preceded, and the heart of my believing servant feels shame." The second clime is suwayda (black bile), and it holds the position of a minister in the heart. The third clime is shughaf (phlegm); this too is the place of a minister. The fourth clime belongs to fibb (gallbladder); this is the station between suwayda and shughaf. The fifth clime belongs to the conscience; this is the seat of the head. The sixth clime is ghilaf (envelope). The seventh clime is the enclosure of the heart, and each of these climes has one gate. Thus, the gate of the first clime is Sirr al-Hayat (the mystery of life), and the gate of the second is Sirr al-Ilm (the mystery of knowledge), and the gate of the third is Sirr al-Qudrah (the mystery of power), and the gate of the fourth is Sirr al-Iradah (the mystery of will), and the gate of the fifth is Sirr al-Rahmah (the mystery of mercy), and the gate of the sixth is Sirr al-Hikmah (the mystery of wisdom), and the gate of the seventh is Sirr al-Amal (the mystery of action).

**The Veils Are Cast Upon Each Clime:**

There are forty veils for these climes, and these are the same veils that exist between the servant and God. For removing these veils, a forty-day spiritual discipline has been prescribed, because each day of discipline removes one veil, and the seeker travels through these years, observing their wonders. Whatever animals, plants, and minerals are within them all pass before his gaze.

**The Types and Names of the Veils:**

The detailed description of these veils is as follows: The first veil is of dust, then of water, then of air, then of fire, then of dryness, then of moisture, then of heat, then of coldness, then of poverty, then of oppression, then of blood, then of black bile, then of falseness, then of attachment, then of negligence, then of wrath, then of difficulty, then of thickness, then of sediment, then of desire, then of claim, then of longing, then of hope, then of pride, then of courage, then of separation, then of prosperity, then of the anterior, then of subsistence, then of fatwa, then of worship, then of asceticism, then of good, then of them, then of trust, then of fear, then of the antecedent veil. Thus these are all forty veils, and there are also the veils of the seven gates, and all these veils are removed through four types of spiritual discipline.

Each type has ten veils. Thus, the first ten veils are removed by Nur al-Hayat (the light of life), and the second ten veils are removed by Nur al-Ilm (the light of knowledge), and the third ten veils are removed by Nur al-Qudrah (the light of power), and the fourth ten veils are removed by Nur al-Iradah (the light of will).
