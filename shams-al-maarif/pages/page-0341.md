Among its properties is that when a person intends to travel and begins to leave the house, he should say:
"A thousand, a thousand, a thousand. Say: 'He is Allah, One' (the Verse of Sincerity), the Throne Verse. I seek protection with it for wealth, children, and family. A thousand, a thousand, a thousand. Say: 'He is Allah, One,' the Throne Verse. From my right and my left I seek protection with it from everyone. I have put on the veil of Allah, the Glorious, the Most High, and I have taken refuge with Allah, the Ancient, the Eternal, and I have girded myself with the sword of the Commander of the Faithful, Ali, peace be upon him, and I have wrapped myself in the garment of Aisha, Mother of the Believers, and I have entered the treasuries of 'In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.'"

"By Say: 'He is Allah, One' and the Throne Verse, I protect my wealth, my children, my right and left. From everyone I have put on the veil of Allah which is Glorious and Most High, and with Allah, the Ancient, the Eternal, I take refuge. I have girded myself with the sword of the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him. I have put on the garment of the Mother of the Believers, Lady Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her. I have entered the treasuries of 'In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful,' whose complete form is 'Praise be to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.'"

If he desires to achieve closeness (to God), let him recite Surah Al-Ikhlas three times and the Throne Verse once, then blow on his right and left. By this, all misfortunes and dangers will be removed. He will remain protected from every evil until he reaches home. If he recites it in the morning, he will be protected until evening; if he recites it in the evening, he will be protected until morning. If he recites it eleven times over the head of an epileptic and blows, he will find relief. If there is any ailment in the body, it will immediately depart. If he reads it once after each prayer, all his sins will be forgiven. Among its properties is that if he recites it before any ruler or oppressive governor, there will be improvement.

"O Allah, O Living, O Sustainer, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor, I ask You by the right of this noble verse and the great secrets within it, that You bridle his mouth against me and silence his tongue so that he speaks only good or remains silent. Your good is before you, and your evil is beneath your feet."

"O Allah, O Living, O Sustainer, O Originator of the heavens and earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor, I ask You by the right of this noble verse and the great secrets within it, that You bridle his mouth against me and silence his tongue so that he speaks only good or remains silent. Your good is before you, and your evil is beneath your feet."

"O Allah, place a barrier between me and so-and-so son of so-and-so, as You have placed a barrier between the heaven and earth, and seal his mouth against me. The bridle of the Omnipotent [unclear: for the wild beasts] for Daniel, peace be upon him, by the right of these noble names."

"O Allah, place a barrier between me and so-and-so son of so-and-so, as You placed a barrier between the heaven and earth, and seal his mouth against me. As the bridle of the Omnipotent was placed on the wild beasts for Daniel, peace be upon him, by the right of these noble names. He will be protected from his evil, and Allah the Exalted will bridle his tongue on your behalf, and it will be better for you—for this is a tested practice."

It is also [a property] that when you are in a gathering and wish to protect yourself from someone's evil, recite the Throne Verse three times and blow in their direction.

The noble properties and benefits of the Throne Verse:

Among its properties is that when you fear someone and harm has come to you from him, then after the prayer of the desired one, pray two units of the opening prayer (Fatihah) along with the Throne Verse, and prostrate three times, and recite "And no burden-bearing is weary for them" (here appears to be a fragment) and "And He is the Exalted, the Magnificent" seven times, and make this supplication:

"O Allah, place a barrier between me and so-and-so son of so-and-so, as You have placed a barrier between the heaven and earth, and seal his mouth against me. The bridle of the Omnipotent [unclear: as the bridle] was placed on the wild beasts for Daniel, peace be upon him, by the right of these noble names."

"O Allah, place a barrier between me and so-and-so son of so-and-so, as You placed a barrier between the heaven and earth, and seal his mouth against me. As the bridle of the Omnipotent was placed on the wild beasts for Daniel, peace be upon him, by the right of these noble names. He will be protected from his evil, and Allah the Exalted will bridle his tongue on your behalf, and it will be better for you—for this is a tested practice."
