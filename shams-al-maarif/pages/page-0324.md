# Page 324 (English translation)
322

If he keeps it with himself, he will obtain many benefits. Hazrat Imam Ali (may Allah honor his face) states that the day before the Battle of Badr, he met Hazrat Khidr (peace be upon him). I said to him: "Teach me a prayer by which I may overcome my enemies." Hazrat Khidr (peace be upon him) said: "Recite this prayer:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّیْ اَسْئَلُكَ بِحَقِّ الم و المص و المر و الر و كهيعص و طه و طسم و طس و یس و ص و حم و حم و حمعسق و حم و حم و حم و حم و ق و ن و ز یَا مَنْ هُوَ یَا مَنْ لَّا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَ اِغْفِرْلِیْ وَانْصُرْنِیْ اِنَّكَ عَلٰی كُلِّ شَیْءٍ قَدِیْرٌ

This comprehensive secret and radiant light is inscribed as a talisman on a gold or silver plate or leather during the first hour of any Thursday. Upon it, the word كهيعص is written five times. Then recite the prayer: اَللّٰهُمَّ یَا هَادِیْ یَا كَرِیْمُ یَا عَلِیْمُ یَا بَاطِنُ یَا اِلٰهَنَا اقْضِ حَاجَتِیْ and mention the name of your religious or worldly need, and it will be fulfilled. In كهيعص there is a hidden secret: the ک (kaf) denotes هادی (the Guide), the ہ (ha) denotes باری (the Creator), the ی (ya) denotes عالی (the Exalted), the ع (ain) denotes علم (knowledge), and the ص (sad) denotes صادق (the Truthful). Likewise, it has been narrated from Hazrat Abdullah ibn Umar and Ibn Abbas. Hazrat Abdullah ibn Abbas used to pray in this manner:

یَا كَافِیْ یَا هَادِیْ یَا بَارِیْ یَا عَلِیْمُ یَا صَادِقُ افْعَلْ لِیْ کَذٰلِک

Some say that it is the Greatest Name (Ism al-A'zam). When you desire acceptance and the fulfillment of needs from a great person or a specific individual, inscribe the talisman that appears on deerskin, place it beneath the prayer rug, burn incense of agarwood, and place your head toward the qiblah—every need will be fulfilled, and Allah the Exalted will grant victory over enemies. Hazrat Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (may Allah honor his face) composed these verses regarding it:

عِشْرُوْنَ حَرْفًا بِاتِّقَانٍ جُمِعَتْ      خَمْسٌ وَ خَمْسٌ صُوْرَتَيْنِ تُكُمِّلَتْ
تَرَی الْبِرَّ فِيْهَا اِنْ تَشَاكَلَتْ مَعْدِلا     یَرَاكَ اِذَا اِنَّهَا مَعَانٍ تَنَوَّعَتْ
فَمِنْهَا قَضَی الْحَاجَاتِ قَدْ شَاعَ ذِكْرُهَا     وَ مِنْهَا ارْتِقَالُ الْحُطَمِ اِذْهَنِ جُرِّبَتْ
تَكَلَّمَ اَهْلُ الْعِلْمِ فِيْهَا بِاَسْرِهِمْ     وَقَالُوْا خَصَّتْ بِلَا التَّبَرِ الَّذِیْ الْتَمَتْ

For acceptance among the people and the fulfillment of all needs:

Whoever inscribes it on a ring during the first hour of Friday when the moon is at its peak and wears it on his finger, great acceptance will be achieved. Abu Ya'qub al-Kindi wrote this talisman on yellow silk for attaining great acceptance among all creation, when the ascendant was Jupiter; by the power of Allah the Exalted, they obtained great profit. The form of this talisman is as follows:

[Two 5x5 magic squares follow. The first contains cyclic permutations of the letters of "حمعسق" (Ha-Mim-Ain-Sin-Qaf). The second contains cyclic permutations of "كهيعص" (Kaf-Ha-Ya-Ain-Sad).]

[Left Table]
| ح | م | ع | س | ق |
| م | ع | س | ق | ح |
| ع | س | ق | ح | م |
| س | ق | ح | م | ع |
| ق | ح | م | ع | س |

[Right Table]
| ک | ہ | ی | ع | ص |
| ہ | ی | ع | ص | ک |
| ی | ع | ص | ک | ہ |
| ع | ص | ک | ہ | ی |
| ص | ک | ہ | ی | ع |

For obtaining love and acceptance, the talisman will be subdued:

Whoever inscribes it on silver during the elevation of Venus and keeps it with himself, love, chastity, and acceptance will be attained. If inscribed on a ring and worn, and one approaches someone whose help is needed, if his numerical value and properties are combined, there will be immense acceptance. It is narrated from Hazrat Abu Hurayrah that the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said: "Indeed حمعسق is 'Thus He reveals to you and to those before you' (kadhalika yuhee ilayka wa ila alladheen min qablik)—it is Allah."
