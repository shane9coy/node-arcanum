# Page 334 (English translation)
Whoever desired harm against me, "There is no power and no strength except through God, the Exalted, the Mighty, and sufficient for us is God, and He is the best Disposer of affairs." Say: "O God, Owner of sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will without reckoning." (Arabic text: تک۔)
"There is no deity except God; He has no associate." He says: "I am God, with the kingdom and sovereignty and with honor and hosts, I am the Conqueror. I am God, the Living, the Sustainer of all, the Compeller, the Powerful. Everything obeys me. I am God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. 'La ilaha illa Allah' is my fortress—whosoever enters it shall be saved and protected from My punishment. I have made these Names of the Seal My fortress. I have taken refuge with the Possessor of power and strength against my enemies. I have entrusted my affair to the Truth, who does not die. I have struck him with it who intends harm against me. Along with 'There is no power and no strength except through God, the Exalted, the Mighty,' God suffices us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs."

Chapter: It has been transmitted that these Names were written on the torque of Solomon, upon him be peace—they are extremely blessed and specifically for kings. These Names are:

"Il, Il, Il, I am God, I have glorified myself with glory and power and possibility. Ya hi, Ya hi, Ya hi, I am God, the Living, the Sustainer, I do not sleep. Ah, Ah, Ah, I am God, the One, the Conqueror, until the Compeller—nothing of mine is lost. Anukh, Anukh, Anukh, I am God, the Mighty—there is no mighty one besides me—from confusion and the equal. Dha hu waj, fay hu waj, dhay hu waj. There is no god except God; He is my fortress. Whosoever enters it is safe from My punishment. And I have fortified myself with the Possessor of glory and omnipotence and sovereignty. And I have taken refuge with the Possessor of glory and omnipotence. And I have trusted in the Living who does not die. And I have struck whoever struck me with evil and guile and deception or a false claim, with no power and no strength except through God, the Exalted, the Mighty. And I have taken refuge with God, and I have trusted in God and in His beautiful, hidden, noble, magnificent Names. Ah, Ah, Ah, 'Ad, the day of Talum, Qayyum, Daymum. And by the right of Hama'saq, and by the right of Kaf Ha Ya 'Ayn Sad, and by the right of the Ha Mim suras and what is in them of the noble verses. I have taken refuge with them and by the glory of God, by which He created Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace."
