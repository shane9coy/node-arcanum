# Different Names of the Liquid:

First, they mention one component and say that this is what the expert says... The component, as long as it has not turned white, remains thin, and some vapor remains upon it. As if it contains fat, and they have given it many names: Bā' Mu'aṭṭar, Bāh Aṭīb. Because the philosophers say this thing is formed from the external volume: Bahr Nasr Mayn, Mā' al-Kawākib, Saṭr Amīn, Rahn, Khall, Hayūlā—and all these are liquids and compounds. Then when the fire is intensified, the white, shining, and lustrous water became luminous. Its brightness... intensifies the fire, and when placed in the furnace, it melts rapidly from the heat, as if it will burst forth from breaking the glass. If it is moved, various rays are produced in it. They named it Ziyaq Naqī. Its effect is colder. Then the name: for the component, when the oil becomes blackish and luminous, this is Ziyaq Sharqī (Eastern Quicksilver), and its effect is warm and dry, and the color takes on a mercurial nature, but the name "passive" belongs to Ziyaq Gharbī (Western Quicksilver). When it is dissolved, the spirituality becomes pure and acquires a colored quality, and for the sake of whiteness or redness, the earth is cooked or matured, and Earth, Air, and Fire are dissolved in the water of Quicksilver to such a degree that they become like dust. The rays dazzle the eyes, and they move like the moon. When the light moisture of the earth is removed from it with gentle fire, this is the wisdom. When they are mixed together, they all become one water and cannot be separated from one another. The expert says that when you see in any book "correction" or "preparation" or "sublimation" or "digestion" or "multiplication" or "analysis" or "distillation," understand that all these words refer to one and the same meaning, and all occur within the great immortal water.

# The Soul Colors the Spirit, and the Spirit Colors the Body:

The one that colors is Ziyaq Sharqī, and the subdued soul is it. The soul holds the spirit, and the spirit holds the body, and this is the secret of the elite. Thus it is seen that oil which changes, when its spirit and essence return toward their earthly components, after separating from them, and both combine into one form, each of them inclines toward its origin with desire and speech. So when all of these gather, they return to one another.

Many times Khall is called Earth, Body, Gold, Silver, Copper, Lead, Bronze, and Iron. When you see these names, do not be troubled—these are all names for one and the same thing. And sometimes they call Ziyaq "Bāh Ahl." This is the specific leaven of the Earth, which is burned with fire, and this is the contrary matter. When it is such that the fire does not completely consume it, they purify it with water several times until it becomes white and hard. At that time they say that Ziyaq has become rose-wool, and in the sulfur of the people there are three things: one is the composing power, the second is the excipient, and the third is the digestive [power].

# Types of Fire:

And there are seven types of Fire: thick-skinned fire, and the fire of the knot of fire, which refers to Ziyaq, and the element which is lit in houses, and the fire of nature, and the fire of the knot.

# The Saying of the Master Dhul-Nun al-Miṣrī:

The Master Dhul-Nun says that fire has seven levels and three cycles. Ten to complete. And some say that every natural power... among them, the keys are in your hands: the capital and the mine and the table. Except for the attachment of speech, it produces. Fruit is produced from food. Can mine be dissolved first? Your power cannot be in the root, for the child cannot eat hard food—milk alone is known. And the gradually growing power is what nourishes it, and the components of the body eat... until it reaches its youth, and afterward it returns to decay and deficiency.
