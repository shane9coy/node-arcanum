**Chapter 83: Properties of the Name Ra'ūf — "Softening of Heart and Spirit," the Operation of Humbling the Oppressor**

The heart of whoever recites this Name becomes soft and the spirit becomes gentle; creation becomes kind to him. When he meets an oppressor, that oppressor becomes gentle before him. Whoever persists in the recitation of this Name until his heart becomes predominant — whoever sees him will love him and approach him with gentleness. Its numerical value is 286 according to one method and 287 according to another. For this purpose, one draws a figure in this form: with the alif of knowledge associated with the one, directed toward the length of the Giver; which is abbreviated before the four. It establishes in it the letters of the Name, which are apparent from the degrees of the numbers, as they are in both numerical figures. These numbers are deficient. Its components, 218, indicate the two Names Samī' wa Mūṣil, because in Paradise the spirit is in perfection and in prayer it is that which inherently causes gentleness. Its explicit number is 302; the couple of spirits are odd and excessive. Its components are 350, indicating the two great Names, Ṣādiq and Wārith. Its definition is the square of al-Qadr, whose value only the people of the inner reality know. This is the figure of its talisman.

**Chapter 84: Properties of the Name Mālik al-Mulk — "Obtaining Sovereignty and Government"**

Whoever recites this Name abundantly and seeks sovereignty and government — it shall be granted to him. Its number is 212; the couple of spirits are deficient. Its (211 + 666) indicate the Name Qayyūm of God the Exalted. This number is the Name Nun with two and the Name Jim with four. Its half is Walī and its quarter exists in the Munajjim. If a king recites this Name, his sovereignty shall endure. Its glorious square is of a value that only the people of the inner reality know, and this is its form:

**Chapter 85: Properties of the Name Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām — "Whatever Is Asked Is Given," Protection from Thieves**

This Name is from the glorious ones; in some places it is recorded that this is the Supreme Name. Whoever recites this Name abundantly, whatever he asks God the Exalted shall grant him. It is related in the noble Hadith: "Make abundant supplication with Dhū al-Jalāl wa al-Ikrām." If it is written on a chest of wealth in the first hour of Thursday, it shall be protected from thieves. Its method of operation is that every day one supplicates according to its numbers and continues the recitation of the Name; then difficult affairs shall become easy for him. Its number is 1100; the couple is odd and excessive. Its total is 4000, whose origin — exceeding the negation — is 154. Its Names are Rabb and Mun'im. This is the form of its square.
