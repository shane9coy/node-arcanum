Poetry

"And the star after setting is steadfastness, and the sun after setting rises."

"For the star there is steadfastness after setting, and the sun rises after setting."

"From the year three because it is the beginning of wars. O Salih, Salih, and submit the judgment to God. Yusuf, ignore this. O Musa, accept and do not fear. With peace, surrender. O Jahijan, [Moses] spoke. O Muhammad, sleep. O Mustafa, prostrate, for the time has come. O Mahdi, the last of times."

"And a spirit and bliss and glory that He has granted, and honor and glory, and kings show respect."

"Smiling from Afan, the family of Shamakha, Salih whom you have perfected among the skulls of the enemy."

"Until for the friend of God there is continuity therein, because she has a kingdom that destroys the enemies."

"He has a time at the time of man A'yar, upon him the banner of victory stands with victory."

"And after the completion of glory, the glory of their station, the time of palm trees will overcome you, say [the time of] injustices."

"Muhammad the Mahdi, the mother of his book, noble for the family of the House, the breaker of the disbelievers."

"His banners flutter with victory always, extending before the army, ever [brandishing] swords."

"So live a time among creation with influence, and the people will not be a burden upon you on the Day of Injustices."

"And lasting for you is the establishment as long as you prevail, by the right of what is therein also of knowledge."

The honored Muhammad ﷺ said that the kingdom and dominion are among Quraysh, and he said Islam is glorious—that is, it will prevail until twelve caliphs from Quraysh, who are 530 and 395 caliphs. A certain person asked Imam Najmi ibn Aqaba about the corruption of the world and the cause of ruin, and he responded with this poem:

"I saw among the secrets a strange state and resemblances that my speech will reveal."

"By what the Merciful has truly revealed, it will be by the judgment of my Lord of glory."

"So in Baghdad there will appear soon from the caliphs kings of battle."

"Their number is ninety-nine persons who will not disperse, all with hope."

"Their standing will be twenty years and four months of nights."

"When humiliation truly comes upon them, the lands will perish without doubt."
