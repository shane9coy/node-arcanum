# Chapter

## The Relationship of the Twelve Signs with Lā ilāha illallāh

Since these stations are dependent upon the twelve signs, so that divine wisdom may manifest within them, for this same reason there are also twelve letters within the six *tasbīḥs*, and those letters belong to Lā ilāha illallāh. In this manner: lām, alif, alif, lām, hāʾ, alif, lām, alif, alif, lām, lām, hāʾ. These are the twelve letters that correspond to the twelve signs. However, some signs are fixed and some are mutable. Likewise, among these letters, those that are purely affirmative are fixed, while those of negation are mutable, turning toward the matter of non-existence. Non-existence is that from which existence emerges. These letters are separate from the orbit of the moon, because the moon in opposition to the fixed stars is closer to the earth, and these letters are closer to us than the moon, because they are placed within the innate nature of every human being. The mention of letters upon the stations has already come earlier, so there is no need to repeat it. With the waxing of the moon, everything increases, and with its waning, everything decreases. This is the wisdom that Allāh the Exalted placed within it, and it is the knowledge that He arranged. Do you not see how heedlessness and the like increase? Allāh the Exalted placed the secret of guidance within the seven stars. His saying is:

**"Indeed, I will appoint upon the earth a successor."**

"Truly, I am the one who will make a vicegerent upon the earth." And He said:

**"Appointing the angels as messengers"** — "That is, Allāh is the one who makes the angels as envoys."

And the powers of the seven stars are derived from the inner power of these six *tasbīḥs*, and the sacred realities of Lā ilāha illallāh give them assistance.

## The Temperaments of the Letters:

These letters possess the four temperaments: hot, moist, cold, and dry. The hot letters are these seven: alif, hāʾ, ṭāʾ, mīm, fāʾ, shīn, dhāl. The moist letters are also seven: bāʾ, wāw, yāʾ, nūn, ṣād, tāʾ, ḍāʾ. The cold letters are likewise seven: jīm, zāy, kāf, sīn, qāf, thāʾ, ẓāʾ. The dry letters are these: dāl, ḥāʾ, lām, ʿayn, rāʾ, khāʾ, ghayn. The explanation of this is that fire is the combiner of heat and dryness. Air is the combiner of moisture and heat. Water is the combiner of moisture and coldness. And earth, that is dust, is the combiner of dryness and coldness. Likewise, there are four *ṭabāʾiʿ* (humors): yellow bile, blood, phlegm, and black bile. Yellow bile possesses the temperament of fire and is dry. Blood possesses the temperament of air and is hot and moist. Black bile possesses the temperament of earth, being cold and dry. Phlegm possesses the temperament of water, being cold and moist. All these matters are evident, just as some names written down dispel fever. Cold names should be written, such as *al-ʿaql* (the intellect) and *al-sayyid* (the master). The sum of this number is 273, and every word requires — those names whose temperament is bilious benefit cold ailments.
