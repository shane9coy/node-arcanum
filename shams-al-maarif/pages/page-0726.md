**Paradise is beneath the feet of the mother:**
Know that Paradise is beneath the feet of mothers. From these matters, the virtue of birr (righteous conduct toward parents) is intended. Begin the practice of this name in accordance with honoring your parents, following the apparent Sharia, and do not neglect them in the outward and inward. With God, this is a matter of great distinction.

**How Hazrat Bayazid performed obedience to his mother:**
It is narrated from Hazrat Bayazid Bastami, may God be pleased with him, that he said: During the time when I was this many years old, I would not sleep at night. One night, my mother made me take an oath that I would lie down with her on the bed. So I lay down, and my hand was pressed beneath her head, and I did not fall asleep. In that state, I recited "Qul huwa Allahu ahad" ten thousand times, which was my daily recitation, and I did not withdraw my hand out of concern for her waking.

**What is the degree of respect and obedience to the murshid (spiritual master) of the path:**
Know that placing goodness toward your murshid holds a great rank, and this is the cause of your survival. It is obligatory upon you not to conceal anything from your murshid. Thus it is narrated from Taj al-Arifin, Hazrat Sheikh Abu Bakr Qurshi—and I was at that time on the pilgrimage journey—and one of his disciples came laughing and said: "Hazrat, what should I do with him?" He said: "Let him remain without breaking the fast." I said: "O Sheikh, this is something about which they are asking you." He said: "If he conceals even one matter from me, he will never attain any special quality."

In the recitation of this name, observing the rights of God and His elders, treating them with goodness, reciting the Quran and prayers, fasting, and keeping company with righteous people are [necessary]; and it is obligatory not to object to anyone.

**The patron (mukall) will teach how to create the elixir whether in sleep or waking**
For this, secluded retreat and prolonged ascetic practice are required. Whoever recites this name according to its numerical value, the patron will appear in sleep or waking and teach the knowledge of the elixir.
And whoever recites this name after every prayer, God the Most High will cause wisdom to flow from his tongue. This is the inscription of this name: write it and keep it with you; there will be blessing. The form is this:
