[Decorative box containing: Second Prayer of Ayat al-Kursi]

When you are in a place of fear, or among people whose evil and harm you fear, then recite Ayat al-Kursi twenty-one times,
and after that recite this supplication:

O Allah, protect me with Your Eye that does not sleep, and shelter me with Your Shelter that cannot be breached, and forgive me by Your Power, so that I do not perish while You are my hope. I have believed in the treasuries of Allah—response is obtained through the remembrance of Allah; its gate is "There is no god but Allah." Its chapter is "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah." Its heaven is "There is no power nor strength except through Allah." In the name of Allah is light, and in the name of Allah is joy, and Ayat al-Kursi revolves upon us as the wall revolved around Muhammad, the Messenger. It has no lock and no key. From the evening prayer until the morning, by the permission of the King, the Opener, the Splitter of dawns, with a thousand thousand "There is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Great." You are the King whose necks are humbled before Your glory, and the lofty mountains crumble from Your awe. Yours is the supreme sovereignty and the magnificent dominion and the celestial and the divine realm. Yours is the might and the omnipotence. You are adorned with blessings and the cloak by Your greatness—all creatures—and the angels, the close ones, the spiritual ones, and the cherubim serve. Lord of the first and the last. O my God, I ask You to protect me, to watch over me, and to look upon me with the gaze of Your mercy—surely You are the most merciful of the merciful. I am concealed from my enemies by Allah, and I have entered under the shelter of Allah, and I am adorned with the cloak of Allah, and I grasp the firm handhold with which there is no breaking. And Allah encompasses them from behind—rather it is a Noble Quran on a Preserved Tablet.

O Allah, protect me with Your Eye that does not sleep. Take me into Your side that does not become constricted. Forgive me by Your Power so that I do not perish, where You are my hope. We have entered into the treasuries of Allah with continuous divine remembrance. The gate of evening [prayer] is "There is no god but Allah." Its spirit is "Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah." Its heaven is "There is no power nor strength except through Allah." In the name of Allah is light; in the name of Allah is joy. Ayat al-Kursi revolves upon us as the wall revolved around Muhammad, the Messenger. For it there is no lock and no key. From the evening prayer until the morning, by the command of the King, the Opener, who splits the dawns, with a thousand thousand "There is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Great." You are the King whose necks are humbled for Your glory, and from Your awe the lofty mountains have crumbled. For You is the lofty sovereignty and the entire kingdom and the divine realm. For You is might and omnipotence. You have brought blessings without measure. All creatures are subservient for Your glory. The angels, the亲近 ones, and the spiritual cherubim circle in attendance. He is the Lord of the first and the last. O Allah, I ask You to protect me, to watch over me, to look upon me with the gaze of Your mercy—surely You are the most merciful of the merciful. With Allah I have made myself hidden from my enemies; I have entered into the shelter of Allah; I have wrapped myself in the cloak of Allah; I have grasped with firm reliance, for which there is no separation. And Allah encompasses them from behind—rather it is in the Preserved Tablet as a Noble Quran.

The prayer given next is also [associated] with Ayat al-Kursi. After reciting the complete Ayat al-Kursi, recite this supplication:
O Allah, I ask You by Allah, Allah, Allah—there is no god but You—the One, the Unique, the Sustainer, the Living, the Eternal, whom slumber does not seize nor sleep—I ask You to bless our master Muhammad, and his family, and his companions, and bless and grant peace.
