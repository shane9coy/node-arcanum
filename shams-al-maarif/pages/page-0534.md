# Page 534 (English translation)
As for the letter alif, its letters are ا, and among the divine names, Kafi is appropriate for it. Thus we multiplied it by the letters of definition, meaning the spoken letter (111 × 3 = 333); this number is its strength. In the lower manifest things, along with letter compositions, and if you direct the entire letter toward the number of detail, that is, gather it in 333, then they will be 444. So 444 will be manifest. If you follow the method of the letters, you will reach a high rank, and the secret of expansion is also contained in this. Thus we explain: alif, its letter expansion is ا ل ف ل ا م ف. If the expansion of these letters is as the number d, t, l, a, th, w, n, th, m, a, n, n, a, j, h, the number of these letters would be 291, which we multiplied by 361. Thus after this calculation, 330 remain. The derivation of this, whose name is Ka'b, is wind and all. This derivation is without the number of detail, but with the condition of the rational, such that the total number is 111. Its celestial patron is Michael.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter ba. Its number is 2, and its expansion is ب ا ل ف ل ا ر. Its numerical expansion is ا ر ح د ا ث ن ي ن ا ر ح د ث ل ا ث و ن ث م ا ن و ن. Thus the number of letters is 23; these were multiplied within themselves and became 149. Then its Ka'b was made rational, which is Danniyyal; this was removed from it, leaving 13. Thus the Ka'b was completed, and the people became Tayya Fina'iyyal.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter jim. Its number is three, and its letters are jim, ya, mim. Its expansion is like this: ي م ا ي م ا, and its extension is ث ل ا ث ث ق ر و ا ب ث ن ر ح ث ر ح ا ر ح د ا ر ب ث ن ن ر ث ر ہ ا ر پ ث ن. These total 38 letters. These were multiplied within themselves and yielded 254. From this and its 5, we subtracted and 313 remained. Then the Ka'b became fruit, and the people became Tayya Tiyaṭa'iyyal was created. Another method is that the three letters of jim were multiplied within themselves and became 9, and the letter of 9 is ṭa; we combined this with jim, and the people became Tiyaṭiṭa'iyyal. Then he made the first rational, and the patron became Dasi'iyyal.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter dal. Its number is 4 and its letters are dal. When these were broken down and extended, these letters resulted: ا ر ب ع ہ ا ر ح د ث ل ا ث و ن ا ر ح د ا ر ب ث ن. Total 43 letters; these were multiplied and became 409. From this was subtracted 151 and 258 remained. Thus the Ka'b became Ṭiya'iyyal. Another method of derivation is that these were multiplied by the number of the letter dal, becoming 16. Then these were multiplied by the amount of expansion, becoming 48. This was made rational and Qina'iyyal became.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter ha. Its number is 5 and its broken form is ه ا ل ف ا, and its extension is ر خ م س ا ح د ث ا ث و ن. All letters were added together (becoming 22); these were multiplied and yielded 294; this was subtracted, leaving 154. The Ka'b of letters and the hidden patron became Ṭawi Tiya'iyyal. This was the explanation of the manifest, but in the hidden sense, this number was made rational and Miya'iyyal became. These are the four methods of hidden derivation, and without this, derivation is given to Diya'iyyal.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter waw. Its number is 6, its expansion is waw, alif, waw, and its extension is س ت ہ ا ر ح د ث ل ا ث ن ث م ا ن و ن س ت ہ ا ر ح د س ت ہ. Total 33 letters; these were multiplied and became 309; from this was subtracted 25 and 284 remained. Tiya'iyyal became patron, and in another way, when we multiplied these, they became 36; then these were made rational and Nuriyyal became the patron created. Then he returned to them and Ṭiya'iyyal became.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter zay. Its number is 7, its expansion is zay, alif, and its extension is س ب ع ہ ا ر ح د ث ل ا ث و ن ث م ا ن ن. All were added together and became 19; then multiplied and became 187; from this was subtracted and Liqa'iyyal became the patron created. This was the explanation of the upper manifest things, and in the hidden sense it is like this: we made the fourth into a Ka'b and multiplied this within it, becoming 112. Ḥisa'iyyal became the patron created, and when the first number was combined, Liiyal became.

Chapter on the explanation of the letter ḥa. Its number is 8, its expansion is like this: ا ا ل ف ا, and when extended, became 43, and from multiplication, 218 resulted. From this was subtracted 158. Tiya'iyyal became manifest as patron, and from the hidden calculation it is like this: the number of the letter ḥa, eight, was multiplied by two, becoming 16; these were multiplied. Wafiya'iyyal's return, or the patron's rational return, Azrayil resulted. These are three methods.
