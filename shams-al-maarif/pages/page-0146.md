A king laid siege to a city and built walls around it, but the fortress could not be conquered. Some people mentioned to the king a saint who was renowned for piety, asceticism, and knowledge. The king came to him and requested a prayer, saying, "This city has not been conquered for a long time." So the saint took a piece of paper, wrote upon it this Great Name of God (Ism al-A'zam) in full, and gave it to the king, saying, "Place this upon your head and attack the disbelievers." And so it was done—the fortress was conquered within one hour, and much booty fell into the hands of the Muslims. The king sent some of that booty as a gift to the saint, but he did not accept it, saying, "I possess the Supreme Treasure (Qimat al-Kubra)."

## A Tale of a Person's Inability to Be Killed Due to the Great Name:

Among the properties of the Great Name (Ism al-A'zam) is that a person from the household of Ja'far al-Mansur was summoned by the king to be killed. When that person appeared at court, the king ordered his neck to be struck. The executioner struck with the sword, but it had no effect. This happened even the third time. Then the king ordered that his clothes be removed and he be searched to see what he had on him.

When they searched him, this very Ism al-A'zam was found written on a paper on his person. Everyone was greatly astonished that God Almighty had shown favor to him because of this secret of His. These are seven letters (huruf), and they should be kept with great care. These letters were written upon the Holy Ka'ba. These letters are useful in various operations (7). All desires are obtained through them, and treasures are also extracted by them.

When you intend to perform an operation with these, write this name with saffron, tie it around the neck of a white chicken, and release it where you suspect buried treasure or wealth. At that place, it will scratch and dig and call out, and wherever it gives a sound, there will be the buried treasure and wealth.

When you wish to demolish fortresses and devastate enemies, carve this seal of good upon wax on one side and transfer the seal of evil to the other side, and bury it in the very middle of the door threshold. Then sprinkle the water that emerges from there.

When you wish to expel a person from a city, write this name with the person's name and his mother's name, and bind it in a bird's feather with yellow thread, take it in your left hand, and release it behind your back. When releasing it, say:

هَرَبَ فُلَانُ بْنُ فُلَانَةٍ مِنْ هَذَا الْمَكَانِ بِحَقِّ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ

"Let so-and-so son of so-and-so flee from this place by the right of these names."

### The Operation of Expelling from a Place:

When you intend to transfer and remove someone, write the seal of evil upon paper, wash it with flowing water from a bath (hammam), and sprinkle it in whatever hour and place you desire. While sprinkling the water, say:

تَوَكَّلُوا يَا خُدَّامَ هَذِهِ الْأَسْمَاءِ بِكَذَا وَكَذَا فَأَصْبَحُوا لَا يُرَىٰ إِلَّا مَسَاكِنُهُمْ

"And also: كَمَا هِيَ الْعَجَل" (As the calf [stood still]).

When you wish to rain stones upon someone, write the verse of evil upon a clay tablet and embed it in the roof of the place. Write this verse upon it as well:

وَأَمْطَرْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ حِجَارَةً مِنْ سِجِّيلٍ

"And We rained upon them stones of baked clay."

Write this name as T T T T T T and fumigate it with the incense of evil—you will see a strange thing.

### To Start a Fire in a House:

If you wish to burn someone or set fire to his house, write the seal of evil in an unlucky hour with that person's name and place, and entrust it to a suspended agent. Burn the wax, and when you request a prayer from them, their great intention will be fulfilled and their word will prevail.
