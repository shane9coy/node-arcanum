# Page 460 (English translation)
Chapter 28: The Beneficial and Useful Secrets of the Beautiful Names and the Eighth Method

Dominion over Enemies:
The names from among the Beautiful Names—al-Qahhār (the Overwhelming), al-Shadīd (the Severe), al-Fa'il (the Doer), al-Muntaqim (the Avenger), al-Mumīt (the Causer of Death), al-Qā'im (the Subsisting), al-Qawiyy (the Strong), al-Qādir (the Powerful), Dhū al-Batsh al-Shadīd (the Possessor of Great Might), al-Muqaddir (the Ordainer)—are included among the obligatory practices of the determined ones. Their properties and benefits are these: that by their recitation, dominion over enemies is achieved; they are destroyed; the houses of oppressive people become ruined; their strength weakens; disagreements occur among them; corrupters are destroyed; and dominion over rebellious people is achieved. Allāh the Exalted grants the reciter of these names strength, majesty, honor, and dignity.

A Successful Practice for Love:
Whoever recites al-Qahhār and al-Shadīd—the task he intends will quickly reach completion, and every person will feel love and fear for him. If their figure is drawn in the form of a square root on clean cloth and bound to the arm, no one will be able to compete with him. Whoever competes will be defeated and subjugated. If he makes an image of their numerical values and binds it to his forehead, whoever sees him will love him.

A Practice for the Destruction of the Oppressor and Enemy:
Allāh the Exalted's names al-Muntaqim and al-Shadīd possess potent properties for removing the evil of oppressors and sowing discord among them. If on the day of the week, after sunrise, one recites these names according to their numerical values and invokes a curse against an oppressor, that oppressor's neck will bend at that very moment. Allāh the Exalted takes vengeance upon that oppressor, and the oppressor is destroyed. If the letters of these two names are written separately on combustible material at the door of an oppressor on the day of the week, blessings will be taken from that oppressor, and he will become entangled in misfortunes.

The Elimination of Lust, Arrogance, and Splenic Swelling:
The one who recites al-Mumīt—his lustful desires and habits of arrogance and pride diminish. Whoever writes this name on date [illegible] pits, and on each pit recites it eight times, and from among those pits makes an image in the form of the person to be defeated, and performs funeral prayer over it and says "This is so-and-so person," that person will be destroyed—but let him not do this unlawfully or without deserving it, otherwise there will be severe reckoning with Allāh the Exalted. The ruler of Banū Thaqiifa was destroyed in this manner. This is an incident from the time when he waged war against the Companions. If one draws a line with blue-colored polish on the spleen and anoints it, there will be relief.

Increase in Physical Strength:
Whoever recites the names al-Qawiyy and al-Qādir, great strength will be produced in his limbs. However much burden he lifts, no strain or difficulty will be felt. Whoever engraves their numerical figure on a silver ring and wears it, Allāh the Exalted will grant him immense strength for carrying burdens.

Allāh the Exalted's names Dhū al-Batsh al-Shadīd and al-Muqaddir—if the oppressed one recites them, Allāh the Exalted takes vengeance upon his oppressor.

All Needs and Heart's Desires:
In Allāh the Exalted's name al-Ākhidh (the Seizer) there is one component of the Greatest Name. Its assigned angel's name is Jibrā'īl (Gabriel), whose power is twelve [illegible].
