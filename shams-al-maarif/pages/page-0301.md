If on Sunday during the first hour one sits facing the qiblah with the ritual ablution and performs its dhikr and places it upon one's head, then Allah the Most High grants him glory, majesty, dignity, and greatness. The number of this Name is 13, and the numbers of its letters are 58 in one calculation. The first number points toward the Name Ḥayy (the Living), because in absolute oneness the meanings of the Name of Allah are contained. For this reason, in the Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ after the Name of Allah comes the Name Ahad. This Name also points toward ʿAlī because it has superiority through numerical anomaly, and the second number points toward the Name Munass, because with the Oneness of Truth every person obtains companionship and loses fear. Whoever performs this Name frequently experiences frequent loss of fear, and this is its form.

Abu ʿAbd Allāh al-Kūfī says in his book Kanz al-Asrār: These Names are very great and glorious. They are: Allah, "Ahad," "Wāḥid," "Javvād" (the Most Generous), "Wahhāb" (the Bestower), "Ḥayy" (the Living), "Mūjid" (the Creator), "Dāʾim" (the Everlasting), "ʿAlī" (the Exalted), "Mujīb" (the Responder), "Wadūd" (the Loving), and "Hādī" (the Guide). Whoever places this in the inner square within the Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ and keeps it with him will observe the strange and wonderful works of Allah the Most High, the description of which is impossible, because each Name empowers itself through the power of greatness, from which the heart obtains strength with the spirit of divine knowledge and sacred subtleties. If the person of spiritual state is constant in this dhikr, Allah the Most High will expand his sustenance—both outward and inward—and whatever he asks from Allah the Most High will be granted to him. This is among the greatest dhikrs and has many benefits. For kings and great men its design is written in the Dignity of the Sun, and by its blessing they achieve victory over enemies. For judges and rulers it is in the Dignity of Jupiter, for ministers and secretaries in the Dignity of Mercury, and for shaykhs and the poor it is written in the Dignity of Saturn. Understand that this is among the hidden mysteries, and likewise it is the Hidden Jewel. Its design points toward the Honored Stone (the Black Stone of the Kaʿbah), in which is the Greatest Name. Whoever reads these Names one hundred times with the intention of destroying some tyrant, Allah the Most High will destroy him.
