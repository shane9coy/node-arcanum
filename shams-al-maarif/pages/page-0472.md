Write with a clear silver stick at the time of sunrise when the sky is neither cloudy nor dusty, and touch it—healing will occur. Write Fa-Alif by dissolving saffron in rosewater on red silk, bind it to the waist—coldness will cease.

Beneficial for headache:
Writing this jali three times and holding it in the hand is beneficial for headache. If it is inscribed with saffron on a copper plate when the moon is in Pisces and Saturn is in an auspicious position, engrave it on a red copper plate and write 111 alifs around it in a circle; after giving fumigation of fanoun, bind it in the armpit area and use it. If placed in a well, its water will dry up. Recite this supplication with these letters:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismika al-a'ẓami alladhī qāma bihi al-samawātu wa-al-arḍu yā awwalu yā ākhiru yā ẓāhiru yā bāṭinu yā azaliyyu yā abadiyyu ilāhanā āmanna as'aluka bimā awda'tahu fī maḥūfi al-alifi mina al-asrāri al-makhzūnah wa-al-anwāri al-maknūnah yā Allāhu yā aḥadu an tushakhkhiṣa lī malā'ikataka al-kirāma khuddāma hādhihi al-ḥurfi al-sharīfi al-shakli al-nūrānī bi-al-ṭā'ati fīmā amartuhum bihi mimmā laka fīhi riḍān wa-anzil 'alā malā'ikatin min malā'ikatika al-'ulwiyyīna wa-al-rūḥāniyyah al-muzajiyyīna yatataṣarrifūna bi-amrika fī ṭā'atī wa-lā ya'ṣūnaka amrān innaika 'alā kulli shay'in qadīr

"O Allah, I ask You by Your Greatest Name, by which the heavens and earth are established: O First, O Last, O Manifest, O Hidden, O Eternal, O Everlasting, our God, I believe—I ask You by what You have placed within the folded alif of hidden secrets and concealed lights: O Allah, O One, that You subjugate for me Your noble angels, servants of this honored letter of luminous form, to obedience in what I have commanded them by that in which lies Your pleasure, and send down from Your supreme angels and the spiritual ones who are joined together, who act by Your command in obedience to me and do not disobey You in any matter—indeed, You are over all things powerful."

Letter Ba

This letter has the quality of cold and dry. It holds the first degree among the earthly spheres. Its day is Saturday. Its star is Saturn. Its metal is lead. Its practical form is a long alif—that is, an alif standing with ba. The foundation of every letter is the dot. Ba cannot be written with all three letters together. It has very many properties.

For correcting corrupted blood:
If someone engraves it on lead on the day when Saturn holds the third or sixth aspect over Jupiter, writing it thus: b b b, and keeps it near himself, he will be protected from spleen diseases. If b b is written on two degrees of copper (uncooked), healing occurs. One of its letters is very large, called bat saghir, bat kabir, and bat laff. Bat numerically is like the letter ba. Some scholars have called it "silent," saying these are letters that are also silent, which cannot function in the affairs of the world and human life, such as these letters: t, b, r, t, th, r, j, d, kh, r, d, kh, l, w, d, d, kh. Do not make supplications for extraordinary things over them, because their occurrence from them is difficult. The nature of the science of cold and dry quality is cold and dry.
