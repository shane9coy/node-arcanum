# Page 188 (English translation)
People incline towards them, and their sustenance from fruits—perhaps they will be grateful. We have certainly established you in the earth and made for you therein livelihoods. Little you are grateful! All [people] We support, these and those, from the gift of your Lord. The gift of your Lord is not restricted. There is not a thing except that with Us are its treasures, and We send it down only in determined measure. We have certainly established him in the earth and given him from everything a means. So he follows a means, and your Lord is better and more lasting. They have their sustenance morning and evening. We have certainly written in the Psalms after the Reminder that the earth will be inherited by My righteous servants.

So the bounty of your Lord is better and He is the best of providers—that God may recompense them for the best of what they did and increase them from His favor. And God provides whomever He wills without limit. Say, "Do you consider that God has given me sustenance?" Rather, what God has given me is better than what He has given you. Or who begins the creation then repeats it, and who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? We intend to favor those who were oppressed, that We may make them leaders and inheritors. O my Lord, indeed I am, for whatever You have sent down to me of good, in need. Have We not established for them a sacred sanctuary, secure, to which fruits of every kind are brought as provision from Us? But they say, "Follow what God has revealed." Continue in God's provision and worship Him and be grateful to Him—to Him you will be returned. How many a creature does not carry its provision; God provides for it and for you. He is the Hearing, the Knowing. Have you not considered that God has made subject to you what is in the heavens and what is in the earth and has showered upon you His favors, [both] apparent and unapparent? Yet there are people who say, "What is it that God intends to give us by this?" Say, "Eat from the provision of your Lord and be grateful to Him." Whatever God opens to people of mercy, no one can withhold. And whatever He withholds, no one can release thereafter. He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise. Whatever you spend of something, He will replace it, and He is the best of providers. God is never unable to do anything in the heavens or on the earth. He is certainly Knowing, Powerful. Indeed this is Our provision—it has no end. This is Our gift, so give freely or withhold without count. What is with you will be exhausted, but what is with God remains. God is He who created you, then provided for you, then will cause you to die, and then will give you life. Whoever fears God—He will make for him a way out and provide for him from where he does not expect. And God provides for whoever He wills without count.

[Urdu translation:]

In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. What We have given them, they spend it. When Zecharias entered the sanctuary, he found provision there. He said to Mary, "O Mary, from where did this come to you?" Mary said, "It is from God; He gives provision without limit to whoever He wills, and He has given us provision, and You are the best of providers." Whatever God opens for people of mercy, no one can withhold it, and whatever He withholds, no one can send it forth thereafter. God is the Creator of the heavens and earth—"He feeds and is not fed." We made this people inheritors who were considered weak, in the east and west of the earth, in which We placed blessing. Thus We gave you a place and helped you with Our help, providing you with pure things so that you may be grateful. O our Lord, so that they may establish prayer and turn the hearts of these people towards them, and We gave them the fruits as provision so that they may be grateful. We gave you power in the earth and made livelihoods therein. We provide them from your Lord's gift, and there is no deficiency in your Lord's forgiveness. We have provided for them equipment for every purpose. It is established that We gave him power in the earth and provided him with every means; so they followed a means, and your Lord's provision is better and more lasting. For them is their sustenance morning and evening. We have written in the Psalms after the Reminder that our righteous servants will be heirs of the earth, and your Lord's favor is that He is the best of providers.
