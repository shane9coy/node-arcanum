# Page 539 (English translation)
Chapter: The Method of Determining the Stations

There are 28 stations in all, the first of which is Shaṛīṭ. It is both basīṭ (simple) and murakkab (composite). The basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-shā-rā-ṭā-yā-nūn, and the murakkab (letteral) is alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-shīn-rā-ā-ṭā-yā-nūn: 16 letters, and the operation is performed on them. Their total is 35, their káb is 838, and their istinkāṭ is Muʿāyīl.

**Al-Buṭayn:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-bā-ṭā-yā-nūn, and the letteral is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-bā-ṭā-yā-nūn-rā-yā-nūn: 8 letters, and the operation is performed on them. The basīṭ ratī is like this: alif-ḥā-dāl-thā-lā-ath-thā-nūn-yā-nūn-thā-sīn-ʿawwal-thālaatha ʿashara thālāth ʿashara thamāniya 'ashara thamānūn: in total 47 letters, and their káb is 249. Its istinkāṭ is Muʿāyīl.

**Al-Thurayyā:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-thā-rā-yā, and the murakkab is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-thā-rā-yā alif-lām-fā: 15 letters, and the operation is performed on them. The basīṭ ratī is like this: alif-ḥā-dāl-thā-lā-ath-thā-nūn-rā-yā-nūn-ʿayn-mīm-sīn-mīm-alif-yā-waw-mīm-alif-thā-yā-nūn-ʿayn-thā-rā-hā-waw-alif-ḥā-dāl: in total 25 letters, and their káb is 325, and the istinkāṭ of the Thurayyā is Ruḥayyāʾīl.

**Al-Dabarān:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-dāl-bā-rā-alif-nūn, and the letteral murakkab is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-dāl-alif-lām-bā-alif-rā-alif-lām-fā-nūn-waw-nūn: in total 910, and the operation is performed on it. The basīṭ ratī has 32 letters, and when it was vocalized, Ruqiyāʾīl was produced.

**Al-Haqʿah:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ letteral is like this: alif-lām-hā-qā-ʿayn-hā, and its basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-hā-qā-alif-fā-ʿayn-yā-nūn-hā: 15 letters, and the basīṭ ratī reached 272, and when istinkāṭ was performed, Muʿāyīl was produced.

**Al-Hanʿah:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is like this: alif-lām-hā-nūn-ʿayn-hā: 15, and the murakkab is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-hā-alif-nūn-ʿayn-yā-nūn-waw-alif-kāf-lām: 15 letters. The ratī has 36 letters. When it was vocalized, Waḥmāʾīl was produced.

**Al-Dhirāʿ:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is alif-lām-dhāl-rā-ʿayn, and the letteral murakkab is like this: alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-dhāl-alif-lām-rā-alif-alif-fā-ʿayn-yā-nūn: 17, and the letteral ratī has 38 letters, and their káb is 388, and when it was vocalized, it was Ḍaḥyāʾīl.

**Al-Nathrah:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is nathrah, and the murakkab is alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-nūn-waw-nūn-thā-alif-rā-alif-hā: 15 letters, and the letteral basīṭ has 30 letters, and their káb is 900. When istinkāṭ was performed, Jāʾīl resulted.

**Al-Ṭarf:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. It has 33 letters, and their káb is 310, and when istinkāṭ was performed, Bāḥyāʾīl resulted.

**Al-Jabha:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. It has 33 letters, and káb is 29, and when istinkāṭ was performed, Duʿāʾīl was produced.

**Al-Zubra:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. It has 22 letters, and their káb is 304. When istinkāṭ was performed, Ṣarākhīl appeared.

**Al-Ṣarfa:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. It has 25 letters, and káb is 225. Bāḥiq is Duʿāʾīl.

**Al-ʿAwāʾ:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. It has 16 letters, káb is 281. Bāḥiq is Hindiyāʾīl.

**Al-Samāk:** It is both basīṭ and murakkab. The basīṭ is alif-lām-rā-shā, and the murakkab is alif-lām-fā-lām-mīm-ghayn-yā-nūn-fā: 14 letters. Its káb is 225, and when it was vocalized, Raḥmatyāʾīl was produced. And God knows best.
