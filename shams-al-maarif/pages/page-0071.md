# Page 71 (English translation)
**Chapter: Description of the Days of the Stars**

Sunday is the day of the Sun, Monday is the day of the Moon, Tuesday is the day of Mars, Wednesday is the day of Mercury, Thursday is the day of Jupiter, Friday is the day of Venus, and Saturday is the day of Saturn.

**Chapter: The Conjunction of the Stars**

The meaning of conjunction is that when one star is in one sign and another star is within its sight [aspect]. Conjunction [taqarub] is when two stars are together in one sign. At this time, by the command of Allah the Most High, their effects become evident. When Saturn approaches Jupiter, wars and conflicts become abundant in the world and kings of the earth die, and when Mars approaches Saturn, these same effects occur. When Saturn approaches the Sun, it also happens thus. When Saturn conjoins Venus, famine and scarcity become evident, and when Saturn conjoins Mercury, the condition of scribes improves. When it conjoins the Moon, oppression occurs at the hands of rulers, and when Jupiter conjoins Mars, the qualities of justice and righteousness appear in the world.

**Chapter: The Natures of the Planets**

Know that the Moon is cold, feminine, nocturnal; it has accidental heat because its light comes from the Sun's light. It is the king of joy and adornment, and it is sometimes like the benefic Mercury. Mercury is nocturnal, feminine, sometimes masculine, sometimes benefic and sometimes malefic. Its heat is moderate. It is the king of speech, writing, and reading. Venus is feminine. It is a cold and more temperate benefic, the resort of beauty and joy. It is the king of marriage and happiness. Lust, adornment, the hearts of the masses, women, beauty, frivolous play, and laughter—all these are its special properties. The Sun is masculine, hot, and dry. Its temperament is choleric. It is benefic with qualification and malefic with inversion. Its essence is gold. It is the king of sciences, religious law, spiritual paths, and joy. Mars is hot, dry, and masculine. Its temperament is choleric and its essence is iron. Its function is fighting. Among the limbs, the head and stomach; killing and plundering; and women and the like are related to it. Jupiter is masculine, moderate, spiritual, and benefic. It is related to blood. Its essence is tin; its taste is sweet and its color is white. It is the king of the stationary wind that is in the heart. The soul and sovereignty are related to it. Saturn is masculine, cold, dry, and entirely malefic [uncertain: possibly "the greatest malefic"]. Its temperament is melancholic. Its essence is lead, its taste bitter, and its color black. It is the recluse, that is, the king of the joints of the limbs. Building, poverty, oppression and tyranny, and criminality are related to it.

I say: People believe that everything that happens in the world happens because of these very stars and celestial spheres, and that they are the administrators of the world. The proof of Allah the Most High regarding this is His saying "فَالسّٰبِقٰتِ سَبْقًا اَمْرًا" [As for those who hastened forward, they will have a prior affair], and we say that this is not the case. Because when the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, went during the Mi'raj and examined all things, he informed us about the conditions of the signs and planets. Thus, this much is acceptable, and the rest is rejected form, meaning it is invalidated—that toward which attention cannot be directed. Rather, proofs and arguments establish that Allah the Most High created them, and "فَالْمُدَبِّرٰتِ اَمْرًا" [And the ones who manage affairs] refers to the angels, as has been explained by Ibn Abbas, may Allah be pleased with him, that Allah the Most High appointed some angels over sustenance, some over wind, and some over rain, and they perform all actions only by the command of Allah the Most High, because He alone is the Powerful, the Sustainer, the Knowing, and the Wise. His sacred essence manages the entire cosmos: "To Him belongs creation and command. Blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds." And Allah has the most complete knowledge.
