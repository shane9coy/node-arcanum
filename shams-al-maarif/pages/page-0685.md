It states that the illuminations of bodies have been manifested and the method of composition has been arranged in the same way as the stages of arrangement. There is the intoxication of intellect, the intoxication of soul, and the intoxication of heart. The form that flows in perception and discernment along with the intellect—with its intoxication—joins the form of knowledge with attribution, and the intellect shares in it. In its virtual state, the meanings and perceptions are divided from the realities of the divine names. Thus the increase of intellect joins with the increase of soul, and when the increase of soul becomes greater, its power of desire becomes greater, and the insight of the soul becomes complete so that the intellect may obtain illuminated knowledge from it. The increase of intellect occurs with the illuminations of the lights of the Essence, and the illumination of the soul occurs from the light of all Attributes.

The mention of this name is unaware of the errors and mistakes of people, and there is no need for seclusion for it. Its characteristic is that when it is engraved on a silver tablet and worn on the hand of a recluse, it becomes a protective talisman. When written on something and placed around the neck of a child or on the hand, it protects him from all evil.

The mentioner of this name is given the knowledge of the elixir:

The seeker who maintains continuity upon it, his trust in it teaches him the knowledge of the elixir, and its name is Hatimiyail. This name benefits every apparent and hidden disease. Its dhikr is this:

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Azeem" (The Great)]

The name Azeem belongs to the categories of divine names, and three things are included within it. That is, one thing is that which is large such that the eye can encompass its largeness, like an elephant. Another thing is that which the eye cannot encompass but the intellect can, such as the earth and sky. One thing is such that even the intellect cannot encompass it due to its greatness, and that is God, the Exalted.

There are great secrets within the names Ali and Azeem; these two should be recited together:

When the mentioner reads this name, it is appropriate that he also combine with it the name Ali, because within these two names there is a great secret. When the seeker intends to enter seclusion, first let him wear pure garments. 80 [unclear] This name should be recited after each prayer according to its number. Until wonders become apparent [unclear] from its benefits, countless blessings will surround him, whose beginning is manifest. When he engraves it in a gold or silver ring, his enemies will be conquered by its blessings, his worth and status among friends will increase, the support and victory of God the Exalted will increase, and all his purposes will be solved.
