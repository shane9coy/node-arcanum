# Page 661 (English translation)
The cosmic sign is established in four signs; in the first sign is the Muhammadan Reality.

Know that the cosmic sign is established from four signs. The first sign is the Eternal (Azali) and it is the Divine Essence of God. The second sign is the Everlasting (Abadi) and it is the spirit, and within it is the first existing being, which is the Muhammadan Reality, and the Eternal Sign and the inner nature. Thus it is connected to the Sign of Truth. Its retreat is upon this verse:

أَوَلَا يَذْكُرُ الْإِنسَانُ أَنَّا خَلَقْنَاهُ مِن قَبْلُ وَلَمْ يَكُ شَيْئًا ط

**The human is a microcosm; the rest of the universe was created for the human.**

This is the microcosm which they call "human," and all the rest of the universe was created for its sake. This is the result of "knowledge of the Truth" and it is the active spirit of the universe. Both this world and the hereafter, and both parties, each of them is equal for the human. In this state there is no knowledge among them, because every sect knows well the deficiency of its own state. Thus this is an indication, but for the believers it is happiness and misery, Paradise and Hell, Heaven and Purgatory.

Thus the Eternal Sign is the Reality of Eternity, and this is written in this decree: لَمْ يَكُ شَيْئًا مَّذْكُورًا. Then the third sign is the Eternal Sign, and it is the Reality contained in this decree: أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ قَالُوا بَلٰى. Then the fourth sign is that which is explained in this decree:

وَنُقِرُّ فِي الْأَرْحَامِ مَا نَشَاءُ

Translation: "We keep it fixed in the wombs for as long as We will."

And He has said:

هُوَ الَّذِي يُصَوِّرُكُمْ فِي الْأَرْحَامِ كَيْفَ يَشَاءُ

Translation: "He is the Holy Essence Who shapes you in the wombs however He wills."

**Note:** Know that knowledge is of four kinds. The first is God Almighty, Who is characterized by absolute existence, because He is not known through anything or by means of anything; rather He exists, and His existence is not known per se.

**No one can know the essence of Lordship:**

But one who wishes to be illuminated by the attribute of the Giver and the attribute of the All must know that the knowledge of the Essence's reality is cut off. It cannot be known through proof or demonstration, because He is pure and independent of anything; He holds no expedience in anything, nor can any thing hold expedience for Him, by means of which He could be perceived.

**He is incomparable:**

He is such that nothing is like unto Him, and within the knowledge of His Essence there is no entry through contemplation. One should not ponder upon the Essence of God. The second known thing is that it is the reality which is the intermediary for all of God Almighty.

**For this, knowledge is eternal; there is no coming into being or ceasing to be:**

Until the eternity and coming-into-being and non-existence of the universe become known, nothing can be perceived whatever it may be. [damaged source - text obscured by watermark]
