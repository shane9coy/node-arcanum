# Page 461 (English translation)
He is the ruler of ten rows, and in each row there are 13 appointed officers. The officer comes to the reciter and fulfills his needs. The prayer of the Name al-Ahad is this:

"O One, You are He who unified Yourself by Yourself in the stations of all things, and You are He from whom not even the weight of a speck escapes, neither on earth nor in heaven, and You are the Knower of what is beneath the earth and what is in the highest heavens. The Merciful established Himself upon the Throne. I ask You by the light of Your oneness and the radiance of Your uniqueness to make me united with the witnessed [Reality], distinguishing by knowledge and recognition. Indeed, You are God, the One, the Judge."

The Name al-Fard is a great name. Its officer's name is Jayayil. Under him are four rulers. Under each ruler are 284 rows, and in each row are 284 appointed officers. The prayer of the Name al-Fard is this:

"O Unique, You are He who became unique in Your dominion through oneness, and You are the Everlasting, the Eternal through as-Samad. Unto You I turn and by You I take refuge, and upon Your grace and generosity I depend. There is no partner for You in Your dominion, nor any minister, nor any administrator, nor any adviser. And You have power over all things. I ask You to flow through my hands and my tongue the fulfillment of the needs of creation, and to protect me by Your grace from mortal sins and stumbling blocks. Indeed, You are the Lord of goods and the repelaler of doubts."

[One who becomes heedless of the people of the world:]

The Name as-Samad is a great name. Its officer's name is Azariya Kil. Under him are four chiefs. Each chief rules over 134 rows, and in each row are 134 appointed officers. The prayer of the Name as-Samad is this:

"O Self-Sufficient, You are He to whom recourse is made in needs and the refuge sought in calamities and adversities, and You are He who enriches and prevents from Your grace the returns of benefits. I ask You by Your self-sufficiency from Your creation and their neediness toward You, to make me serving the purpose of the servants in weighty matters, and to flow upon my tongue and my hands the fulfillment of needs, and to protect me from mortal sins. Indeed, You are the guide to goods."

The Name al-Qadir contains one letter of the Greatest Name. Its officer's name is Sa'ayil. Under him are four chiefs. Each chief is the leader of 305 rows, and in each row are 305 officers. The prayer of the Name al-Qadir is this:

"O Powerful, You are He who cast Your power into the hidden essence of essences, and You are He who manifested Your will through the gathering of dispersion with good deeds, and You are the Gatherer of the scattered. I ask You, O God, by the greatness of signs, to make me powerful upon repelling sins. Indeed, You are far exalted above association and directions."

The Name al-Muqtadir is a great name. Its servant is Janayil. Under him are four rulers. Each ruler is the ruler of 44 rows, and in each row are 44 officers. The prayer of the Name al-Muqtadir is this:

"O All-Powerful, You are He who surrounded between Your beloved ones unto the Abode of Good Pleasure, and You are He who clarified the mirror of whoever turns toward You for the manifestation of the secret of security and safety. I ask You by the greatness of Your power to grant me the reaching unto Your secret [text cuts off]
