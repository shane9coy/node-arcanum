# Page 652 (English translation)
He knows all things

The one who mentions this name attains an elevated rank:
The one who mentions this name attains an elevated rank because he comes to know the elevated rank. For the souls that determine the degrees for the sake of the self, because the world is a single form that becomes known according to its lights and according to the heart, since divine science and transcendent sciences are in accord with their existence, and their practice is the cause of their acquisition.

God, the Exalted, created the earth and the heavens:
Know that God, the Exalted, created the seven heavens and causes them to rotate among luminous manifestations and states of perfections, and after creating the seven earths, He placed within them the treasury of His blessings.

He appointed four centers each for the upper world and the lower world:
Just as there are four centers of the transcendent sciences, there are also four centers of "sovereignty." Thus, the first center of the transcendent sciences is the Intellect—that is, those are the sources of the intellects—and the second center is the Spirit—that is, those are the sources of the souls—and the center of the heart is that which are the sources of hearts. So the center of the Intellect is the Great Throne, the center of the Spirit is the Pen, the center of the Chair is the Expansive Throne, and the center of the Heart is the Preserved Tablet. And after creating the earths, He appointed for them the treasures of His blessings, the layers of the eye, and the shades of His mercy, and from among them He made each earth the bearer of a type of punishment for the people of sins.

He made man the microcosm:
[Note: Handwritten name "Muhammad Hanif" is overlaid on this heading.]
Know that He placed all these things within you, and for this reason He named you "the microcosm." Some investigators have recited an excellent verse:
وَ تَزْعَمُ اَنَّكَ جِرْمٌ صَغِیْرٌ وَ فِیْكَ انْطَوَى الْعَالَمُ الْاَکْبَرُ
Translation: "You think that you are a small body, yet within you the greater world is folded."

The division and number of hours; the sovereignty of day and night; the gradation of man's creation:
This collects eighty-nine thousand modes. It makes twenty-four parts into fifty, which is divided into twenty-four hours, and they are divided into the seven [planetary spheres]. Thus, at that time, so to speak, the twenty-four hours are divided over day and night. God, the Exalted, placed your heart's modes upon the order of the lower [modes]. For each sign there is a sun, then the darkness of the heart lies upon it. Thus your physical birth is of this same manner, whose initial state God, the Lord Exalted, has described:
مِنْ مَّاءٍ مَّھِیْنٍ ؕ ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَاہُ نُطْفَةً فِیْ قَرَارٍ مَّکِیْنٍ ؕ ثُمَّ خَلَقْنَا النُّطْفَةَ عَلَقَةً
If [the verse continued]—these seven modes are of sovereignty, and after them are six modes of non-sovereignty, whose quality is such that God, the Exalted, gives knowledge of those layers of the Throne to the upper and non-upper layers.
