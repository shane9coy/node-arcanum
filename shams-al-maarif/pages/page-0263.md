**First Chapter: Explanation of the Name of God, Its Properties and Benefits**

This is unanimously the Greatest Name, and it is the name of the Word of the Exalted Essence. Its meaning refers to sovereignty. This is the Comprehensive Name. This is the Name of Essence, and all other names are its attributes, but it is not the attribute of any name. Whoever mentions it with frequency, no person of awe and greatness will be able to look upon him. Whoever writes it on something pure under the authority of Saturn and recites it, every wicked Satan will burn. Whoever mentions it in severe cold will be protected from the pain of cold.

If inscribed on a ring of nisf (silver alloy) and worn, fever will depart immediately. If inscribed on the first Friday of the month when the Sun is in Leo, write it and mention it 317 times. Then whatever branch you place your hand upon will dry up. But it is necessary to be a person of spiritual state— whoever recognizes its worth will become independent of everything besides it. Because this is the Greatest Name with which whatever supplication is made is accepted, and whatever is asked is given. Thus for this reason its external powers point toward the Name al-Mujīb (the Responsive). And it is the first name among the Five Names that is comprehensive of realities and encompasses its subtleties. Its reflected form is majestic— whoever writes it and keeps it with them, no task will be difficult for them, and all hardships will become easy. This mention belongs particularly to the believers among the People of Solitude, and its name is Muhammad [unclear] then if appropriate, O God, O God [unclear]
