# Page 779 (English translation)
When desire overcomes you in seclusion, make ablution and recite the name Ya Tahir (the Pure) or Ya Tahir, Ya Tahir well. And if corrupt thoughts are stirred in seclusion, recite Ya Latif (the Gentle). And if thoughts of food and drink prevail or hunger afflicts you, recite Ya Qawi (the Strong). And if you become a drunkard, recite Ya Fattah (the Opener). And if philosophical thoughts and satanic whispers seek to corrupt your understanding, remove them with Ya Hafiz or Ya Hafiz (the Preserver). And if some matter arises in seclusion that causes anxiety and restlessness, then turn your attention to Ya Basit (the Expander). And if you wish to attend to some matter of the world, recite:

Ya Qawi Ya Aziz Ya Alim Ya Qadir Ya Sami Ya Basir

and begin the recitation of each name after making ablution.

Our Sheikh Hazrat Abdullah Qarshi is among the famous masters of Arabia and Egypt. He says: I have met many great masters and have received benefit from fourteen hundred masters.

And he says that one day I went to Hazrat Abu Muhammad Saqlani, and he said to me: I will teach you something such that when you need it, you may use it. I said: Please do so. He said: When you need it, recite this supplication:

Ya Wajid Ya Ahad Ya Wajid Ya Jawwad, anfahna minaka bi-nafkhati khayrin innaka ala kulli shayin qadir

He says: Since I began reading it, I have achieved success through it alone. And he says: I saw that the Day of Resurrection had been established, and all the stations of creation and the forms of the actions of the prophets, peace be upon them, as they will appear to their doers—all of this was shown to me, and the conditions of the grave and the intermediate realm were revealed to me, and the realities of the magnificent Quran were also disclosed to me, and I became aware of its secrets.

From our Sheikh, the Imam of the knowers of God, Allama Abu al-Hasan Khazai—may God sanctify his secret—also issued the excellent and unique, and many strange accounts are famous about him. He was highly skilled and authoritative in the science of letters and names, and possessed complete knowledge of hidden realities.

On which date Laylat al-Qadr of Ramadan falls:

These are the very words of one who said: From the year I reached maturity, my Night of Power has never passed without my observing it. And he said: When the first night of Ramadan is a Sunday night, then Laylat al-Qadr will be on the 27th; and when the first night is a Wednesday night, then Laylat al-Qadr will be on the 27th night; and when the first night is a Thursday night, then Laylat al-Qadr will be on the 25th night; and when the first night is a [unclear] Saturday night, then Laylat al-Qadr will be on the 19th night; and when the first night is a Saturday, then Laylat al-Qadr will be on the 23rd night.

And in the science of letters, he has the compositions of Hakim al-Mutin. Among these is the book Luma, and the book Shams Miftah al-Qulub, and so on. He used to frequent the bathhouse, and in 538 AH his death occurred there.

Among his statements are these few sayings: He says: If there is no gentleness and moderation, the signs of God remain unknown.
