Chapter 98: Properties of the name Rashid — improvement and betterment in all affairs

Whoever mentions this noble name abundantly, the outcome of all his actions will be good. Whoever writes its temperament and keeps it with himself, Allah the Almighty will correct his outward and inward condition, and he will not be put to shame in any matter. Its number is 152, the abjad is odd and deficient. Its 150, the letters point to the divine name "Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad" (Say, He is Allah, the One). Chapter 99: Properties of the name Sabur (The Patient) — steadfastness from Allah the Almighty against calamities

Whoever mentions this name, Allah the Almighty will grant him patience over calamities, and whatever endeavor he begins, he will not be incapable of completing it. Its mention is appropriate for those engaged in spiritual struggle (al-mujahadat) when they perform [unclear: al-khalwa] for thirty [days]. Its square is written under the sign of Capricorn. See — upon this name all the names are concluded by which Allah the Almighty removes grief and sorrow. Thus the saying of the people of Paradise has been expressed by Allah the Almighty in this verse:
اَلْحَمْدُ لِلهِ الَّذِیْ اَذْھَبَ عَنَّا الْحَزَنَ ؕ اِنَّ رَبَّنَا لَغَفُوْرٌ شَکُوْرٌ ۙ الَّذِیْ اَحَلَّنَا دَارَ الْمُقَامَۃِ مِنْ فَضْلِہٖ ۚ لَا یَمْسُّنَا فِیْھَا نَصَبٌ وَّ لَا یَمْسُّنَا فِیْھَا لُغُوْبٌ

"Praise be to Allah who has removed sorrow from us. Surely our Lord is Forgiving, Appreciative, who has admitted us to the Abode of Permanence by His grace, wherein no fatigue touches us nor does any weariness touch us therein."
