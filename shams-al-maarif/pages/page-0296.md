Chapter 59: The Properties of the Mabda'i (Originative) Name, the Revelation of Difficulties and Hidden Secrets

This is a luminous and Syriac name. Whoever engages in frequent recitation of this name, hidden matters will become manifest to him. Allah the Most High makes him eloquent with wisdom. Whoever wishes to accomplish a difficult task, the recitation of this [name] is appropriate for him. It is also excellent for whoever wishes to attain advancement in noble knowledge or intends to write luminous verses. Its number is 56. This name holds the same station relative to the friend (wali) name as the advocate (wakil) name holds relative to the name of Allah. For this reason, the Mubin (Manifest) name has united these two, combining wilayah (friendship) with ibtida' (beginning), whose meaning is "manifest" — everything becomes apparent through it. Its letter-names are 205, pointing to the divine name 'Alim (the Knower) of Allah the Most High. Its square is this:

Chapter 60: The Properties of the Mu'id (Returning) Name, Finding Lost Things, the Return of the Runaway, and Release from Forgetfulness

This is a noble, spiritual, and intellectually joyful name. The lost items of whoever engages in frequent recitation of this name will return. Every defect will be corrected. Whoever writes its square under the sign of the inverted zodiac and hangs it in a place where wind blows, the runaway person will return immediately, or if he is a traveler, he too will return by divine power. Some knowers say that whoever engages in frequent recitation of this name, forgetfulness will depart and forgotten things will be remembered. Its number is 124. It is the Ruh al-Fauh (the effusive spirit) and Fard al-Nafs (the individual soul). Its letter-names point to the divine name Malak (King) of Allah the Most High, because only He who is the complete owner of everything can bring it back after it has gone. For this reason, Allah the Most High has combined it with the Malak name. This number also points to the letter Qaf, because in it there is elevation (isti'la) which terminates with the beginning of the abandoned [i.e., the qaf ends the alphabet as the beginning does the alef, if we consider it as tarki ibtida']. Its letter-names are 33, pointing to the two names Malak (King) and Qayyum (the Sustainer). Its square is this:
