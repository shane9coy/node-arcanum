hādhihī al-birru ḥattā aṣrif'hu ūrīd hayā yā ḥā ḥalaf'tu 'alayka ḥayākh ḥaṭūj ḥaythu ilá ḥaḥiḥ ḥiḥ ḥiḥ ḥiḥ ḥiḥ
ḥawājat'in ḥawājiḥ ḥiwāḥ fafī al-ḥāli qaḍay'ta ḥājatī bīḥaqqi khalītumū hāhunā al-sā'ati wa asri' bil-ījābati wa
taṣarraf fīmā ṣarraftuka al-waḥā al-'ajala bi-alfi lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhi al-'aliyyi al-'aẓīmi ṭ

Translation: "Pure is He who deals gently with the sinners. O Allah, O Forbearing One, my forbearance is Tasīm, and You know it well. I pray to You by the merit of the Master Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, and the Master Moses, the one who spoke with Allah, that You grant me Your assistance, and help me against the enemies who are in authority, and grant me mastery in fulfilling my needs, and make me guided by Your command, and grant me success in the secrets with word and deed, so that I may have mastery over it as I wish. O Client, fulfill my need at this very moment, and at this very moment execute what I command, quickly, now, by the merit of a thousand 'There is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Exalted, the Mighty'—how great is His glory and how mighty is His sovereignty, O Client, by the merit of Ṭufaylīl, be present at once and execute my command."

And the incense for it is this, and the oath for it is this:

daḥlajin wa dahlajin yumaththalā mā a'ẓama mathānahu wa a'azz salṭānahu ijib ayyuhā al-maliku ṭufyāīlu wa if'al kadhā wa kadhā fī hādhihi al-sā'ati al-'ajala al-waḥā

THE LETTER ṬĀ'

This letter is the meeting point of two structures, and the honorific manifestation of this letter exists both in the world of the supernal and the infernal, and this letter flies over the entire world.

Everyone will fear the one who keeps its tablet:

When this letter is written seven times on a tablet on Sunday, five times, while the Moon is in its own sign, and the暗 [implicit] and the client are also written, then whoever keeps it with him, the entire world will tremble from fear of him. And if a wild beast approaches, it will be at peace. And whoever writes this letter in this same procedure and places it around a child's neck, no harmful creature will come near him.

No fatigue on a journey:

The number of this letter is 9. When it is written in the form of 9 on a deer's membrane with first-quality sandalwood, and kept with oneself, then however much one travels, one will not become fatigued. When this letter and the暗 [implicit letters] of the surrounding area are written and hung in a house or shop, there will be blessing in it.

Hidden causes will be obtained for the one who keeps this talisman:

Whoever writes this and keeps it with him, Allah the Most High will grant him hidden causes, and when it is placed beneath someone's head, he will see true dreams.
The full benefit is obtained.
