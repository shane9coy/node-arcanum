And he said: Whoever fears encountering something beyond their capability should mention this name—God the Exalted shall grant them safety. And whoever reads Ayat al-Kursi when departing from a house shall remain protected from every evil until their return; and if one gives some charity to the poor, this too is a cause of safety.

A caravan passed through a jungle where a man was sleeping carelessly while his horse grazed. The caravan travelers woke him from his sleep and asked: "You sleep in this dangerous jungle? Do you not feel fear, though there are many wild beasts in this jungle?" He said: "I am ashamed to fear anyone besides God the Exalted."

Whoever has verified this name through practice, God the Exalted protects them in all times and movements—just as it is narrated from Abu Ali Daqqaq, may God be pleased with him, that a thousand ashrafis came to a certain saint, so he petitioned God the Exalted: "Ashrafis have come to me, and You know that I have no need for them, but I am unable to protect them. Therefore, I return them to You—whatever is needed, I shall take from You." So he distributed all the ashrafis among the poor and the destitute. Then whenever he had need, he would ask from God the Exalted, and God the Exalted would grant him according to his need, until God the Exalted gave him double what those ashrafis had been worth. And God the Exalted is the Giver.

Chapter 40: The Properties of the Name Muqit; the Mention of the People of Spiritual Connection with the Righteous

Whoever frequently mentions this name shall have their station with truth and patience; nothing they need shall be lost to them. This is the practice of the people of spiritual connection with the righteous—when they continue mentioning this name until a state overwhelms them, they do not feel hunger. The Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, pointed toward the practice of this name when he said: "I am not like you—I am fed and given drink by my Lord."

Its number is 550; this is an even-odd, terminal, deficient number. Its names point to two names of God the Exalted: Wahid and Matin, each having the number 550—as it is derived from the divine name al-Quddus. The names of its letters point to two names of God the Exalted: Sami' and Basir.
