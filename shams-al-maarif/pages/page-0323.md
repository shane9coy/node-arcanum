Some scholars of realities say that the words that are spoken over these letters are half luminous and half dark. These luminous letters are: A, H, T, S, S, K, R, M, N, A, Q, L, Y. Whoever inscribes these luminous letters of the square of the Lights of mystical secrets upon a silver ring on the first day of Rajab and wears it—if he is afraid, he will become secure; if he meets a king, the king will honor him and fulfill his need; if he is touched upon the head while in a state of anger, his anger will depart; if a thirsty person places it in his mouth, thirst will be removed; if he washes with rainwater and drinks, his memory and understanding will become strong; if a virgin girl wears it, many people will be inclined toward her for marriage; if this ring is placed upon an epileptic, he will be cured at that moment; whatever woman suffers from labor pain, if she wears it, a child will be born immediately; whoever has an image carved on this ring and blows upon one affected by sorcery, by the command of Allah the Exalted, the falsehood of magic will be dispelled from him. The Muqatta'at letters of the Divine are: Alif-Lam-Mim, Ra-Alif-Kaf-Ha-Ya-Ain-Sad, Ta-Ha, Ta-Sin, Ya-Sin, Sad-Qaf-Nun. Whoever inscribes them upon a silver ring in the divine arrangement, namely Alif-Lam-Kaf-Ha-Ya-Ain-Sad-Ta-Sin-Ha-Mim-Qaf-Sad-Nun, in the ascendant of the sign of Taurus, and keeps it with him, all his needs will be fulfilled, and he will see such delicate divine matters whose explanation is impossible—when Allah the Exalted gives success. Sheikh Abu al-Hasan al-Harrani says: We have repeatedly seen the properties of these letters in the book Durr al-Zahra. Some scholars of knowledge say that they saw several lines written by the hand of the Imam Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf al-Zuhri, for he used to write these letters for the protection of every property and equipment. It is mentioned that Caliph Uthman ibn Affan used to say:

"Allahumma ihfaz al al-Muhammad sal Allah alayhi wasalam bi al-nasr wa al-afiyah bi al-mim wa kaf-haya-ayn-sad wa hami-asq nun wa al-quran al-majid wa al-qalam wa ma yasturuna"

"O Allah, protect the family of Muhammad, peace be upon him and his family, with victory and well-being, by the Mim, Kaf-Ha-Ya-Ayn-Sad, Hami-Asq Nun, the Glorious Quran, the Pen, and what they inscribe."

When Imam Malik rode upon a boat on the Tigris and Euphrates, he would recite these letters which are found entirely in the Quran. Someone asked him about the reason for this, and he said: Whatever thing I recite them over or write them upon—whether in the wilderness or water—that thing remains protected and is saved from drowning or destruction. Some observers, when they intended a sea voyage, would write these letters on a potsherd or paper and keep it with them; when the sea storm began, they would cast that potsherd or paper into it, and by the command of Allah the Exalted, the storm would cease. Some righteous ones used to keep these letters with them during travel. When someone asked them about the reason, they said: Its blessing has become evident.

**Protection from Wild Animals and Insects, Victory over Enemies:**
From the scholars of the West, wherever property is protected. Increase in sustenance occurs. Protection from thieves, enemies, wild animals, and insects are among their subjects. Regarding some of them, the elders related that their daughter was put to sleep by Allah—or rather, was made invisible. This was due to the blessing of practicing it. Then one was found with her, and he recited "Alif-Lam-Mim, Hami-Asq Nun, the Pen, and what they inscribe." At that moment, the jinn fled and did not return. Whoever enters any place with these luminous letters should come after reciting durood sharif.
