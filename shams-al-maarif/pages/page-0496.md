# Page 496 (English translation)
Through the Sarākhiṭh

If this method becomes known to you, then you will have recognized the Sarākhiṭh, and the more complete your authority becomes, the more everything will obey you. Even the infidelity of stones will accept your command.

As for subjecting rain or wind, so that whenever you wish it rains and whenever you wish it stops—similarly, whenever you wish you make the wind blow and whenever you wish you stop it—their scales are specific to them. Once you have come to know them, your dominion will extend over all the world, and your final success is assured. If you are wise, the scale of the manifestation—that is, rain—is: alif-rā-bā-ʿayn-waw-nūn-tā-sīn-ʿayn-hā-mīm-alif-thā-yā-nūn. These are 15 letters, and their numerical value is 1279. Now combine with it the scale of attraction, which has been mentioned previously, and perform the operation. The scale of the riyāḥ—that is, wind—is mīm-alif-tā-yā-nūn-mīm-alif-thā-rā-hā-ʿayn-waw-thā-mīm-alif-nūn-yā-mīm. These are 18 letters. Combine the scale of attraction with it and perform the operation.

The Method of Repelling Harmful Things

For repelling the hawāmm—that is, harmful things—employ the scale of repulsion. The geometric number of Bahrām is: ʿayn-mīm-sīn-tā-waw-sīn-tā-hā-alif-ʿayn-waw-alif-rā-bā-ʿayn-waw-nūn. There are 15 words total, and this is the method of summoning them.

Subjugating Aquatic Animals

As for the aquatic—that is, the river animals—their scale is: alif-rā-bā-ʿayn-hā-sīn-tā-waw-alif-ʿayn-dāl-rā-thā-lām-alif-tā-waw-nūn-thā-mīm-alif-nūn-yā-hā-mīm-alif-thā-yā-nūn. These are 26 letters. Following the method of the scale of ṭīr, add whatever you wish of good and evil, and from the numbers we have mentioned, produce their external correspondences—for example, from the decimal produce the tenth, and from the seven produce the seventh.

The Degrees of the Elements

When you wish to know the makhraj of the element of barī, which is 1125, and when you need to perform an operation with it for some purpose, take its makhraj and add it to the scale of operation. This is the rule for all numbers in all operations. Know that each of the four elements has several degrees, and each degree has a separate scale.

The Types of Elements

Fire degrees: The first degree of the element of nār is the station of fire. Its letters number 28, and its numerical value is 555.

Air degrees: The element of air has several types. The first: "Mā yanfaʿu al-nāsa fī al-barri wa al-baḥr" (What benefits people on land and sea). Its geometric number is known, and its letters number 138. The third degree is "Bawwāh al-ʿishq wa al-muḥabba" (The expounder of love and affection). Its geometric number is known, and its letters number 88. The fourth degree is "Hawā' baʿḍ al-ẓuhūr" (Air of partial manifestation), and its geometric number is known, and its letters number 438. The fifth degree is "Hawā' bāzār mufsid" (Air of the corrupt marketplace). Its geometric number is 600 and its numbers are known, which the calculator extracts.

Water degrees: Water has five degrees. The first degree is "Mā' al-ʿadhb al-Furāt" (The sweet water of the Euphrates), its geometric number is known, and the letters number 4409. The second degree is "Al-Muqallada al-baʿr al-khinīn" (The dung-beetle), its geometric number is known, and its letters number 73. The third degree is "Hanā' al-zuhālī" (The delight of the refuse), and its geometric number is known. Its letters number 4972. The fourth degree is "Mumāz al-mūdak" (The winking one), which has no right to this [i.e., is improper]. Its geometric number is known, and the letters of the beginning number 102. The fifth degree is "ʿInāya al-tafṣīl ʿalā al-insān" (The detailed attention to humanity). Its geometric number is 500 and its numbers are known.
