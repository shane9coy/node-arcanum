# Page 392 (English translation)
O God, O Acceptor of prayers, O Fulfiller of needs, O Opener of hardships, O Opener of the treasuries of afflictions, O Fulfiller of the needs of supplicants, O Hearer of voices, O Forgiver of slips, O Remover of hardships, O Sender down of blessings, O You whose knowledge encompasses all things—I ask You to send blessings and peace upon Muhammad ﷺ and upon his family, and that You fulfill my need. O, regarding the Names: HaHa Hay Hay 'uw 'uw Yaah Yaah Aah Aah Sabbouth Qaddous [All-Glorious], Lord of us and of the angels, I ask You, O Lord, whatever names are in this petition, that You fulfill my need.

I saw that before his supplication had even finished, his need was fulfilled. Afterward, he turned toward me and offered greetings, and apologized, saying, "I was supplicating to Allah the Exalted, and Allah has fulfilled my need." I said, "I heard your supplication, but you were looking at a piece of paper. What was it?" He said, "I will tell you. In fact, when we were in the service of His Holiness the Shaykh, and you had studied with the Shaykh for a long period, during that time the Shaykh said to me, 'If you have any need, state it.' I said, 'Yes, please teach me such a supplication that when I recite it, Allah the Exalted accepts it immediately.' So he gave me a paper on which these Names were written."
