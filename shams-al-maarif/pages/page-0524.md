# Page 524 (English translation)
They will order the breaking of the cross and the killing of the pig:
They will order the breaking of the cross and the killing of the pig, and they will also kill those who eat pork, and Sufyan will be killed beneath a tree near the suburb of Damascus.
The Emergence of the Dajjal from Tabaristan:
Then the Dajjal will emerge from Tabaristan from the eastern side and will come to Isfahan. There one thousand Jews will be obedient to him, and he will be blind in one eye, and written between his two eyes is "kāfir" which every Muslim will read. The Dajjal will rule on earth for forty days. One day of his will be like a year, and one day like a month, and one day like Friday, and the remaining days will be like all other days. A person asked the Messenger of Allah ﷺ regarding that day which will be like a year: "O Messenger of Allah ﷺ, will prayers of one day be sufficient for that day?" He ﷺ said: "No, rather they will be performed according to the estimation of the time."
The Emergence of Ya'juj and Ma'juj and Their Condition:
Then after the Dajjal, the emergence of Ya'juj and Ma'juj will occur. They will first pass through the Sea of Tiberias and drink all its water; indeed, they will drink up all the rivers of the world. Then Allah the Most High will send upon them an illness by which they will all die in one night, and for seven years people will light fires from their arrows and bows. Many reports have been transmitted regarding these matters, but this is not the occasion to mention them. We have revealed the summary of all matters, so understand it. His Eminence Ibn Abbas may Allah be pleased with him states that the duration of the world is upon the tablet of Sab'ah, and they call it the Indian days. There is no doubt regarding the duration of the world except upon the tablet of Sab'ah—that in each of the cycles, Allah the Most High sent one prophet. In the first cycle there was Adam upon him peace. In the second, Idris upon him peace. In the third, Noah upon him peace. In the fourth, Abraham upon him peace. In the fifth, Moses upon him peace. In the sixth, Jesus upon him peace. And in the seventh, our master upon whom be the light—prayers of Allah and peace be upon him and upon them and upon all his family, all of them.
There Will Be a Renewer in Every Century:
It is narrated that His Eminence upon him be prayers and peace said: "At the end of every century, Allah the Most High will send for this nation such a person who will reform all its religious affairs," and in other words, "I have left it pure and clean."
