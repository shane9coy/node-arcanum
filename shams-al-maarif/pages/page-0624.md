# Page 624 (English translation)
Divine proximity is attained through the Word:
On the Day when the appointed time approaches, no form of remembrance will be known greater than "Lā ilāha illallāh." The knowledge of "Lā ilāha illallāh" itself is nearest.

The presence of Uthmān ibn Affān before the station of prophethood and the establishment of faith in the heart:
It is narrated from Uthmān ibn Affān, may Allah be pleased with him, that he said: One day I was in the service of the Noble Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, when suddenly he raised his gaze toward the sky and Jibrīl (Gabriel), upon him be peace, arrived and said: "O Muhammad, Allah the Most High commands you to act with justice and to bear witness to 'Lā ilāha illallāh' with kindness." Uthmān said: When I heard this matter, the root of faith became established in my heart, and this is indeed justice. He said: I asked the Messenger, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, about sincerity. He said: "Standing with compassion is sincerity."
Allah the Most High said: "O you who believe, fear Allah and be with the truthful" — meaning: O believers, fear Allah and join the company of the truthful. The truthful are those who say "Lā ilāha illallāh."

All knowledge of the first and the last is contained in the Word:
It has been transmitted that all the knowledge Allah the Most High taught to creation is contained within "Lā ilāha illallāh." The knowledge of the first and the last — all of it is within "Lā ilāha illallāh."

All prophets taught the Word:
All prophets came to manifest and teach this very Word, and Allah the Most High says in His Book [unclear]: "Then know that there is no deity except Allah." The Messenger, upon him be peace, said: "The best of all remembrances is 'Lā ilāha illallāh.'"

The dhikr of the Word ascends to heaven without the help of angels:
All deeds are carried up by angels, but "Lā ilāha illallāh" ascends on its own. Some interpreters said in their commentary on this verse: "When the sun is wound round and when the stars fall" — meaning: On the Day of Resurrection, "Lā ilāha illallāh" will manifest its theophany upon the person whose final utterance it was.

The Word is the key to Paradise:
The key to Paradise is this: the Word "Lā ilāha illallāh Muhammadun rasūlullāh."
Know that all deeds and acts of worship on the Day of Resurrection will bring honor to their performer, but "Lā ilāha illallāh" and this [unclear] alone will remain with their owner.

The Field of Resurrection will be illuminated by the one who remembers the Word:
The people of the Resurrection will shine with such brilliance from their light that the entire Field of Resurrection will be illuminated by their light. There will be glad tidings and comfort in the Hereafter. So the saying of one will not be accepted if it is not accompanied by "Lā ilāha illallāh."

The Prophet Yunus recited the Word in the belly of the fish:
The Prophet Yunus, upon him be peace, recited this very Word while in the belly of the fish. Know that this supplication [unclear] has remained with the angels for the fulfillment of needs.
