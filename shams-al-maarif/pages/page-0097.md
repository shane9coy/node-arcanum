Chapter 7: The names with which the Lord Jesus, peace be upon him, would resurrect the dead

May Allah, the Most High, grant us and you understanding of His mysteries and success in obeying Him. Know that this chapter is extremely venerable and great in dignity. Khawarazmi, may Allah have mercy upon him, says: "I have been searching for this name for many years. At last, I found it with one of the people of the earth. They kept many names of this kind written in their possession in the Himyari script, so that those unworthy could not read them." Khawarazmi says: "Whoever fasts for seven days and on the seventh day writes these names on deer parchment with rose water and saffron, then calls upon the angels of one-quarter of the year and their assistants, and the assistant calls upon the name of the wind, then states his need—it will be fulfilled. And if this practice is performed beside running water and placed in the sun, then the angels of wind readings and his assistants, the angels of the air and the planet, take the name, it is superior, and by the command of Allah, the Most High, the need will be fulfilled."

Khawarazmi says: "When I met this venerable man, I asked him about the Greatest Name. He said: 'Every single name of Allah, the Most High, is a Greatest Name.' I said: 'Indeed this is true, and I know many of them among them.' Then this venerable man asked me about 'Ya Qahhar Huwa Ya Qawiy' concerning Joseph, peace be upon him. I gave him the answers as they were. The shaikh thought that I was unaware of these secrets, but when I told them to him, he said: 'Come to me. No one like you has come to me.' He drew me nearer, then he continued mentioning names one after another. I asked him about the names that were upon the staff of Moses, peace be upon him. He said: 'O son, these are the greatest of all names.' Some of them were written in foreign words and some in Syriac words, so that no one could understand them. Most of their virtues and properties have been related by Nuwaiy ibn Abdallah, may Allah be pleased with him, that the excellence of these names over other names is like the excellence of the Night of Power, just as the excellence of Friday over all other days. Khawarazmi says: 'I had seen these names written in the Himyari script with a person in the city of Qazwin.' Whoever knows the virtues of these names should keep them protected from ignorant people and remain in fear of Allah.

Remedy for palpitations and anxiety of the heart:
This is very beneficial for one suffering from palpitations and anxiety.

Remedy for ophthalmia (nazarbod):
Nuwayri ibn Abdallah says that whoever fasts for three days and writes this verse on the white membrane of a deer with saffron and applies it to one suffering from ophthalmia—the effect of ophthalmia quickly disappears. In another narration, whoever on the day of the week, with purification and fasting, while being in his own home—its benefits are that by the command of Allah, he will achieve his purpose. And it was by these that the Lord Jesus, peace be upon him, by the command of Allah, the Most High, would resurrect the dead, and treat those with leprosy and vitiligo. These same names are written upon the heavens and the earth. The people of knowledge have agreed upon this interpretation along with it.
