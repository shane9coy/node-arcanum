This is the invitation of Surah al-Fatiha.

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds. Praise that surpasses its most beautiful and most complete and most excellent form—the praise of the praisers—and I plunge into the ocean of the light of that praise, a plunging that illuminates me, pure in outward and inward, with righteousness and awe and empowerment until the Day of Recompense. And by means of it I seek for myself a protection that preserves me and guards me from those who cause harm and from enemy adversaries who cause harm. Praise that becomes for me contentment and joy and felicity, so that with it I do not lack any one among the first and the last. And may it be for me a means and a stronghold by which I bring down the authority of the oppressive tyrants—the Compassionate, whose mercy encompasses everything, and by which every existing thing bears witness to the beneficence He has poured forth. And every beginning has in Him of the secrets and the manifest matters and His absent ones is a sign of goodness and clear instruction. I ask You by this righteousness that You have made clear and which was pure to the sight, that You associate me in this ocean with a self that never departs from me in all times and lives. And may it be for me a provision and a support such that after it I lack nothing in any time or place or cause, and a means by which I take refuge from the plots of jinn and men—the Compassionate, the Merciful, who has shown gentleness to me. Indeed His mercy preceded me from eternity past.

Behold, I turn through its signs abundantly, and I have obtained knowledge and supremacy by the sweetest of invocations and the most fragrant of stars. I ask You, O my Lord, to grant me enjoyment of Your blessing and the permanence of Your favor, by Your preceding mercy—so that I do not fear any plot from any schemer. May He make manifest to me generosity and noble character from every one of vile description. O Master of the Day of Recompense, He whose status is too great to look upon any associate. O evident cause of soundness and guidance—a clear proof. He has decreed upon those in the dominion and the angelic realm, by His standing power for all the tyrants and the arrogant ones, the Severe in seizure against the wrongdoing oppressors, the One who overcomes by the severity of His overcoming and His might and His seizure upon whoever rebelled and transgressed from among the tyrants and the rebellious. The One who crushes whoever shares with Him in His greatness and His majesty. By it I extinguish all the destroyable ones.

I ask You to make the hearts of Your creation subservient to me, O Turner of hearts, O Softener of iron for David, peace be upon him, by the "dawh." O Reposer, O Originator, O Master of the kings of all the worlds together, all of them. Give me dominion from the forelock of such-and-such, until it becomes for me more beneficial than religion. There is no god but You. Glory be to You; truly I was among the wrongdoers. And attain me by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

You alone do we worship, and You alone do we ask for help. I have reserved You for every hardship and every tribulation, O One whose hand has protected all who go forth.
