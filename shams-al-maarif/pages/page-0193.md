# Page 193 (English translation)
The Story of Burning the Demon and Jinn, and an Easy Practice

One of the sheikhs among the masters says: I was in a house in the city when night came. I saw that a black-skinned person whose eyes were shining like flames of fire appeared before me and began approaching me. From him came a sound like that of a serpent. I was very frightened by him. I began quietly reciting Ayat al-Kursi. My fear departed. Then I lay down in a corner of the house, and in a dream I saw that the Caller was saying to me: "We have burned the demon." I said to him: "With what did you burn it?" He said: "With the verse wa-lā yu'ī-hū hi-faẓu-humā wa-huwa al-'Aliyyu al-'Aẓīm. We burned it with this verse. When you became afraid of this demon, Almighty Allah placed its recitation in your heart. When you would recite one word of it, he would also recite it with you. But when you recited this last word, he could not say it, and Almighty Allah burned him. This verse is very great and noble, useful for all troubles and ailments. Whoever recites it at bedtime shall remain in safety until morning, and whoever recites it in the morning shall remain in safety until evening. It has wonderful properties. Its talisman is very great, and it is as you see it here."

The supplication of Ayat al-Kursi is as follows:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are Allah, the True King beside whom there is no god but You, the One, the Unique, the Incomparable, the Ancient, the Preserver, the Sufficient, the Living, the Sustainer, the Preferred King, the One Who maintains all things, the Sublime, the Magnificent. I ask You by the majesty of Your glory, by which You ward off from me the harms and by which I acquire the benefits. And by the righteousness with which Your servants have pleased You—by it Adam, peace be upon him, and You taught him all the names—I rescue, O Allah, [damaged] from [unclear] what comes between me and the unjust people. You are indeed the Master and the best Helper, and You are the Supporter, and I am Your servant. Therefore let it not be said: He is the god. O Allah, O You whom neither slumber nor sleep takes hold.
