Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "al-Adl" (Justice)

The one who remembers Justice sees from the Throne to the depths of the earth:

Justice is what produces the action of equity, which is contrary to oppression, and whoever does not know equity will not know its action either. This is the rank of the close ones (maqābīrīn), that they perceive the realities of things and cast their gaze from above the heavens down to the depths of the earth, and they observe equity in everything. God the Most High says:

مَا تَرٰی فِیْ خَلْقِ الرَّحْمٰنِ مِنْ تَفٰوُتٍ ہ

And this matter comes from establishing truth and justice.

He maintains the four elements in equilibrium:

God the Most High created existents in the station of equilibrium and established simple and composite bodies. Thus, the four elements from among them are fire, water, air, and earth. Then He established the heavens in diverse orbits and gave the earth a place in the lowest of the low, and upon it He placed water, upon it He placed air, and upon air He established the heavens for the administration of the world.

Man is the microcosm:

Whoever has understood the secret of composition and knows that man is composite also knows this: although man is a small body, the entire world is present within him.

Justice should be considered in every affair:

Know that this is the portion of the world in justice: that one incorporates justice into one's morals and attributes, and performs every matter with equity, and one also establishes justice in one's rights and obligations. God the Most High says:

اِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَ الْبَصَرَ وَ الْفُؤَادَ کُلُّ اُولٰٓئِکَ کَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْـُٔوْلًا ہ

Translation: "Surely hearing, sight, and the heart—all of those shall be questioned."

The retreat for this name is this, and Azrael is its guardian. Recite it according to its numerical value. If one writes it upon something and keeps it with oneself, God the Most High will cause equity to be done through him. And whoever remembers it after every prayer, God the Most High will grant him steadfastness and justice. Its sigil is this:
