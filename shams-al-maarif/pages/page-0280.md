# Page 280 (English translation)
**Chapter 30: Properties of the Name of Justice, Deliverance from the Oppressor and Obtaining Justice**

The name with the number Qāhir—one who conquers—if any person pronounces a curse against any oppressor along with this, the oppressor shall be seized at that very moment. If the ruler frequently makes mention of it, God Most High shall grant him the success to act with justice toward his subjects. Whoever bears the name Abd al-Mu'min, the mention of this [name] is appropriate for him. Its number is 104. The first four [components] indicate the duration and the expanse of dominion, because the narrowness of realms and the diminishment of wealth result from nothing but injustice. This number derives from the numbers of Saturn of the zodiacal signs and the particle increment. Its components, 104, point toward the name of God Most High, al-Ghanī (The Self-Sufficient) and [His name] Walī (The Protector), for whoever has fulfilled [his covenant] has enacted justice among his subjects, has corrected himself, and has delivered his subjects from the oppressor. God Most High has said:

**"O David, We have appointed you a successor upon the earth; therefore judge among the people with truth, and do not follow desire, lest it cause you to go astray from the way of God."**

A seat was spread for His Holiness Hejaz, then he said: "This is the first step"—by which he meant that whoever is a ruler is not entitled to grant precedence to anyone according to his own desire. He said: "When people are not equal among themselves, this is injustice," and this is its design:

| ع | د | ل |
|---|---|---|
| ل | ع | د |
| د | ل | ع |

**Chapter 31: Properties of the Name Latīf, Deliverance from Hardship, Pain, and Captivity**

This name is most swift in answering when uttered in times of hardship to ward off affliction. The mention of it is appropriate for prisoners and those suffering severe illness, and it is also appropriate for whoever is under the tyranny of an oppressive ruler or is captive to his own soul. If he frequently makes mention of this name, he shall find deliverance from them. The mention of it is appropriate for whoever bears the name Sālih. Its number is 129. This number is a perfect odd number; it is 3 with its number 43, and this [43] is deficient. Its components, 47, point toward the name of God Most High, Walī (The Protector), because in Latīf there is necessarily a light reaching unto the name Mahdī, for in it there is a return to the word of primordial nature (kalimat al-fitra). For this reason, its total number is three. The letters' names point toward the name Mustaqill (The Self-Sufficient). This is the description of its square:

[Design: A 4×4 grid. The first row contains the letters of the word "Latīf," and the remaining cells contain numbers whose sum totals 129.]
| ل | ط | ی | ف |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 31 | 79 | 11 |
| 32 | 21 | 26 | 50 |
| 9 | 76 | 2 | 42 |

[Note: The sum of the numbers in the second, third, and fourth rows is 129, which is the abjad number of the name "Latīf." The third cell of the fourth row is faint due to a watermark, but according to the calculation of the total it appears to be 2.]

Know that the names of God Most High that reside in glory—when recited—manifest in the heart of the ruler effects of awe and dread. Whoever makes mention of it, and there befalls him some ailment in his self or body, shall immerse himself in the dhikr of this name, and lo, he shall be removed from whatever thought occupied him. Then in this practice [unclear due to watermark] let him make mention of this name, and that thought shall appear before him in form and shall speak to him, telling him how he [may be delivered].
