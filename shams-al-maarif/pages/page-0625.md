The phrase that fulfills every need when recited seventy thousand times:

Whoever has a pressing need should sit in seclusion and begin reciting "There is no god but Allah" and complete seventy thousand repetitions. Even before this number is finished and one rises, whatever need exists will be fulfilled. Remember this.

Chapter: Every word of Allah indicates His essence:

There is another division of the Name of Majesty, which is that when you write this Name of Essence, it becomes bound to speech by the Name of Divine Unity. For example, if you delete one lam from it and combine, it becomes "Allah"; if you delete both lams it becomes the name "Aah"; and if you delete one lam and the ha, it becomes the Syriac supreme name, that is, "Al."

Allah is the collector and bearer of all secrets:

There is no other name besides this one that can be spoken in this manner, and this name is called "the collective name" because it is the collector of all secrets and is invoked in every state—for instance, you say, "O Allah, have mercy on me," or "O Allah, give me intellect," or "O Allah, help me," or "O Allah, remove my distress." Therefore, it is the collective of all names because it takes the place of all of them; that is, whatever name of God a person finds, all of them relate to this Name of Essence, and the meaning of their relation is exactly what we have stated here.

Chapter: For the healing of every illness:

This noble name dispels illnesses. Write it according to its number 66, wash it, and give it to drink—Allah the Exalted will grant healing. Giving it to those afflicted by evil spirits is also beneficial.

For binding and burning jinn:

When you wish to bind a jinn, write its letters on that person's fingers where the jinn will be bound. If you wish to burn that jinn, write the letters of this Majesty separately on a dark-colored cloth and burn it while making (the person) smell it. This same procedure should be followed for burning, killing, or driving away.

If this name is engraved on a gold ring on Sunday during the ascending sign of Aries, and whoever wears it—Allah the Exalted will elevate his status among all creation, provided he also continues its remembrance daily according to its number. And if he engraves it on silver during the waxing moon and wears it while maintaining remembrance, Allah the Exalted will elevate his status.

His Messenger ﷺ said: Allah hears and answers every prayer of His servant:

Our Lord Muhammad Mustafa ﷺ said that when a servant says "Ya Allah," Allah the Exalted says "Labbayka abdi"—meaning, "Yes, O My servant, I am here." So whatever you need, He knows well the interests of hearts. He is the Lord of all things and He alone knows the truth. His decree and providence belong to Him, and He is very close to His servant with His power. He is not excluded from the majesty of His words. Thus it is established that there is no description equal to the descriptions of the glories of the Attribute of Majesty, for it encompasses all secrets. Therefore, for anyone to comprehend it is very difficult, and this is impossible.
