# Page 374 (English translation)
First, towards the east, meaning there, tie it with a red thread. If not, the hand will burn and the amulet will be made. Then that person will perish, and there will be interrogation and accountability regarding wealth before God the Most High. This practice should not be performed for more than seven days; otherwise, that person for whom the practice was done will perish. When intending to release it, open the amulet and wash it with water. If for a regular purpose, looking in a mirror, write Surah Al-Ikhlas with three other surahs and Surah Al-Fatiha in a pure vessel and have him drink it; then by God's command he will immediately recover. The form of the practice is as follows:

(A practice for causing separation among people)

لَعَلَّ رَبَّهُ إِن طَلَّقَكُنَّ أَن يُبْدِلَكُنَّ أَزْوَاجًا خَيْرًا مِّنكُنَّ مُسْلِمَاتٍ مُّؤْمِنَاتٍ قَانِتَاتٍ تَائِبَاتٍ عَابِدَاتٍ سَائِحَاتٍ ثَيِّبَاتٍ وَأَبْكَارًا (At-Tahrim: 5)

This verse is for the divorce and separation of women. Write it with ink and then kohl in a blue-colored bowl, and write the names of those whose separation is desired, including them and their mothers. Then wash it with dirty canal water and sprinkle it in the house where they gather together; such enmity will arise between them. The impressions of this are as follows:
