In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful

Chapter 38: The Letter Alif

You should know that for the purpose of performing the practice for the letters and carrying out their service, and for understanding their properties and secrets, the letter Alif precedes all letters in creation and is unique in number, because the mysteries of the spoken word issue forth from it, just as the remaining letters are manifest from the mysteries of bounty. And know also that there is no specific time fixed for forming the shapes of the letters; whoever has been destined by Allah the Most High in their fate to be adorned with the properties of these letters, these [practices] will be completely effective for them.

This letter Alif possesses a special efficacy for elevating the perfect saints and the intimate ones who are near [to God] to elevated ranks. Whoever has undertaken the research of its apparent and hidden knowledge can bring the entire world under their domination, and this blessing is equivalent to the paradise of bliss for them.

Know that the letter Alif is the essence of the world and the ultimate attainment of the final perfection; rather, it is the reference point of the entire world. Its establishment is through the Eternal Name, and this is the same letter that is the first letter of the Greatest Name, that is, the Name of Allah, and it occurs at the beginning of Surah al-Fatihah. This letter is a self-subsisting light. Its shapes are of two kinds: one triangular and upright, with these attributes of perfection, and the other without the upright, which is called "shapes of good" [i.e., incomplete forms]. For the slow-witted, write the letter Alif one thousand times on silk cloth and bind it upon their chest; their intellect will be opened, and whatever they hear will be remembered. And if the original number of this letter is 111, write this letter in accordance with them along with your own name and the name of your beloved, and keep it with you. The Lord the Most High will make your beloved merciful to you, and however difficult the tasks may be, all will become easy.

For Creating Love:
If you combine this letter with the names of the lover and the beloved and write it on a piece of cotton thread during the hour of the Sun, and the lover keeps it with himself, there will be nothing but love between them. Benefit from this. If you engrave the form of the letter Alif on a golden ring at the time when Mars is in Pisces, and perform the incantation and the jalalah that will be mentioned below, rulers and kings will hold it in the utmost reverence. The form of this letter is:

For Obtaining Treasure:
When you need to extract wealth from some treasure and you wish that no obstacle should appear, write the letter Alif and its minister, Buzurg al-Qadir, and keep it with you, and extract wealth according to your need. And if this letter with its minister is placed inside an inscription on a stone, the owner will remain safe from any disaster. The words are: "Ya Khaddam hatha al-Harf ihfazoo hatha al-Mal"

And if complete attention is given to worldly perfection and the letter Alif is written upon it along with the names of its ministers that follow, then bury it in the place where it is to be hidden—in a crack in any wall, and that place will become famous. The place will become ruined and uninhabited. And if it is desired to perform an operation for love in which no defect occurs, then combine the name of the seeker and the desired one, and write the letter Alif with its minister, mixing its number with it, and write on white paper with black ink when the Sun is in Leo, and place it on your turban and display it. Also write the seal of the letter Alif and keep it with you; the beloved will become maddened with love within an hour.
