# Page 382 (English translation)
[Operation: Prayer for making peace among enemies]

Write this talisman (figure) and tie it around the neck of the person you wish, or place it under the pillow on which he sleeps. Peace will be established between them, even if swords have already been drawn between them. When all other means have been exhausted, then write this talisman and fumigate it with aloe wood, red sandalwood, and rose, and write this incantation:

*Trust in, O servants of these names, to place love and affection between so-and-so son of so-and-so, by the right of these names. O Compeller of hearts, create love between the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so and so-and-so daughter of so-and-so, by the right of Him who said to the heavens and earth: "Come willingly or unwillingly." They said: "We have come willingly." And I have cast upon you love from Me, and so that you may be made in My sight. Trust, O cluster, to place love and affection between the hearts of the haters, seated facing one another. Trust, O servant of this day and this hour, to draw the hearts of the haters toward love, by the right of these names, by the right of Aywish, Aywish, Baduh, love and friendship. O Baduh, cast love after hatred, and unity after separation. Ahya Sharahiya, Adwnay, Asbawt, Al, Shaddai. And indeed it is a mighty oath if you knew.*

O servants of these names, by the right of these names, be appointed to place every kind of love and affection between so-and-so son of so-and-so. O You who place love in hearts, place love by His grace between the hearts of so-and-so son of so-and-so and so-and-so daughter of so-and-so. He who said to the heavens and earth: "Come willingly or unwillingly"—and they said: "We have come obediently"—I have cast upon you love from Myself, so that you may be prepared before My eye. O cluster, your attention is directed toward the hearts of the hostile enemies. O servant of this day and this hour, by the right of these names, draw the hearts of the hostile enemies toward love. Cast love after hatred and unity after separation. Indeed this oath, if you knew, is great. Come, O unique, hurry, the hour.

[Operation for bringing down a wall upon a tyrant or enemy and destroying him]

Write this talisman, which will be mentioned below, and place it in the wall of the tyrant or enemy; that wall will collapse, and the tyrant and enemy will be destroyed. His people will be scattered. Fear God Almighty and use this only for the tyrant, for he harms people with his hands and tongue. Never use this for the undeserving, for on the Day of Resurrection you will be asked about it. Whoever forgives and makes peace, his reward is with God Almighty. The talisman is this:
