Chapter: The Divine Names and the Shaytānī Names
[/Bordered box]

Know that the shaytānī names are those names whose secrets no one other than God the Most High or those scholars can know. Shumshakha Khathāw, Shumshū Latīyā, Ajīb, or Kasfiyā'īl—its meaning is that He is the Living and the Eternal, to whom sleep and drowsiness do not come, through whom all things in heaven and earth exist. He is the forgiver of sins. This name is written on the Gospel of the Angel Mūkal, the guardian. Or Hūmū, or Ajīb, or Luwā'īl—that is, I strike and I revive, and I have mercy upon the believers. Whoever reads these names shall be safe from terror, remain protected from every illness, and obtain healing. And if he anoints an arrow with this name, that arrow shall not miss its mark. Shilbakhsū Lā, or Shumūlībā, Ajīb, or Mu'arī'āyīl—that is, I am He who established the heavens without pillars. If one reads this name while riding, he shall never fall from his mount, and he shall ride along safely. God shall ease the entire path for him. Ease shall come from difficulty, and if one recites this name, all sorrow and grief shall depart. And this is the same name by whose blessing the Throne-bearers raised the Throne, and by whose blessing the agonies of death are made easy. Or Nūr Shubā, Ajīb, or Mihkā'īl—that is, I am the Essence from whom no one is hidden. I shall revive you after death. Whoever reads this name in a state of distress, God shall grant him respite. Or Hijjatī, Ajīb, or Samistānīl—that is, I am the Essence that revives. By the blessing of this same name, Abraham, peace be upon him, used to revive the dead. Ajab, or Karamī'īl—that is, I am the God who nourishes children in their mother's womb. By the blessing of this name, God makes every difficulty easy. Let whoever writes this keep it with him. All his affairs shall be made easy by God's command. Or Samṭī' al-Nūr, Ajīb, or Rūḥiyā'īl—that is, I am the God upon whom nothing in the East and the West is hidden. Whoever reads this name and asks God for what he wishes, his need shall be fulfilled. Ma'hama Yaftaḥ, Ṭabīḥ, Ajīb, or Sharaṭī'īl—that is, I am the Lord of all creation and the one who grants respite to the deniers. And let whoever writes this name on his shop and ties it there; no harm shall ever befall his shop, as when a dog bites and he shall triumph over his enemies. Or Ṭaymū Hīnj, Ajīb, or Kurafiyā'īl—that is, I am the God who forgives sinners.
