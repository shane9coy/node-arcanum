# Page 121 (English translation)
119

And [be pleased] with every possessor of the Day of Judgment, who has no rival in sovereignty, no equal, no minister, and no advisor. Rather, He existed before the existence of the world and all the worlds together. You are my protection and my provision from all the satans, and my help against the farthest and the nearest, and I have directed them toward the different types. You alone we worship with affirmation, and we are absolved of sins and transgressions, and we repent to You from sins, and we bear witness that there is no deity except God, alone, with no partner, no substitute, and no likeness—the Possessor of Glory and Generosity. And we bear witness that our master and our lord, Muhammad, is His servant and His messenger—may God bless him and grant him peace. And You we ask for help in every need and matter among the affairs of this world and the hereafter. O Guide of the astray, direct us to the straight path—the path of those whom You have favored, among the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous—not of those who have incurred wrath, nor of those who are astray. Amen. In the name of God, Lord of the first ones and the last ones, Creator of whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth, Sender of the prophets and messengers, the One who believes in the truth—the Omnipotent, the Dominant, the Magnificent, the Sufficient, the Merciful, the One Lord in all the worlds, the One worshipped in every place, the One acknowledged in every language, the Excellent, the Ancient, the Master of what He made, the Subduer of all His creation—the Holy One, before whom necks submit and the lofty firmaments bow, and faces humbled before the Living, the Self-Subsisting. And failed is he who bears wrongdoing. O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O One who advances, O One who postpones, O First, O Last, O Apparent, O Hidden, O Governor, O Most High [marginal note: O Watcher, O Compassionate, O Near] O Avenger, O Pardoning, O Compassionate, O King of kings, O Possessor of Glory and Generosity, the Subsisting, the Self-Subsisting, the Everlasting, the Eternal. Behold, by the remembrance of God hearts find peace. [marginal note: O God, O Living, O Self-Subsisting] O Living, O Self-Subsisting, You see me, You hear my speech, my supplication, and my complaint. You are my goal, my request, my hope, and I am the one in need of You. And You are the Knower of secrets and private conversations, and nothing in the earth or the heavens is hidden from You. You are the Lord of the Mighty Throne. I ask You for beneficial knowledge, upright religion, sincere certainty, and profound wisdom. O Generous, O He. I ask You for the unveiling of the curtains of the unseen with what is in it, until I witness the Everlasting Spirit. —end. You, O Self-Subsisting, O Light of the heavens and the earth and what is between them, and Lord of the Mighty Throne—I ask You to bless our master Muhammad and to grant him peace, and to unveil for me the secrets of Your names, and to subjugate for me all Your creation with obedience, and my heart for You with worship, and to grant me the lights of Your guidance, the knowledge of Your secrets, until I may be delighted by the brilliant that appears from Your grace, O Gentle of gentleness, O Most Merciful of the merciful.
