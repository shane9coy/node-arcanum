# Page 226 (English translation)
224

And peace be upon him.
O Allah, if my sustenance is in the heavens, then send it down; if it is in the earth, then extract it; if it is near, then make it easy; if it is abundant, then bless it; and send it to me wherever I am, and do not send me to it wherever it is, but rather by Your grace and generosity bring it to me where I am. By Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, the Exalted, send blessings upon our Master, upon him ﷺ, his family, and his companions.

**The Second Prayer of "Ya Latif": May fear and distress be removed:**
After the morning prayer, after reciting "Ya Latif" 129 times, recite this prayer:
"Allaahumma musakhkhiras-samawaati wal-ardayni was-sab'i wa man fihinna wa 'alayhinna, sakhkhir li kulla shay'in wa min 'ibadika mimma fi barrika wa bahrika hatta laa yakuuna fi al-kawni shay'un mutaharrikun aw saakinun, saamitun aw naatiqun illaa sakhkhartahu bi-ismikal-Lateefil-maknuun, ya Allaah, ya Hayyu, ya Qayyuum, innamaa amruhu idha araada shay'an an yaquula lahu kun fa-yakuun, ilaahii juuduka dallanii 'alayka wa ihsaanuka qarrabanii ilayka, ashku ilayka ma laa yakhfaa 'alayka wa as'aluka ma laa ya'izzu 'alayka, idh 'ilmuka bi-haalii yughnii 'an su'aalii, ya mufarijran 'anil-makruub, farrji 'annii maa anaa fiihi, ya man laysa bi-ghaa'ibin fa-astanzirahu wa laa bi-naa'imin fa-uuqizahu wa laa bi-ghaafilin fa-uthakkirahu wa laa bi-'aajizin fa-umhilahu, ya 'aaliman bil-jumla, ya ghaniyyan 'anit-tafseel, kafa 'ilmuka 'anil-maqaal wa kafa karamuka 'anis-su'aal, inqata'a ar-rajaa'u illaa minka wa khaabat al-aamaalu illaa fiika wa suddat at-turuqu illaa ilayka, ya Allaah, ya Samii', ya Baseer, ya Qareeb, ya Mujiib, ighfir li wa arhamni bi-rahmatika, ya arhamar-raahimiin. Wa yassir lii rizqii wa sakhkhir lii jami'a khalqika innaka 'alaa kulli shay'in qadiir. Wa sallallaahu ta'aala 'alaa sayyidinaa Muhammadin wa 'alaa aalihi wa ashaabihi wa sallama."

O Allah, You who have subjected the seven heavens, the seven earths, what is within them and what is above them—subject for me all Your creation that is in Your land and in Your seas, so that nothing moving or stationary, silent or speaking, may remain in the universe that You have not subjected through Your Hidden Name of Latif. O Allah, O Ever-Living, O Sustainer of all existence, indeed His command when He intends a thing is only to say to it "Be," and it becomes. O Allah, Your generosity has shown me the way to You, and Your beneficence has brought me close to You. I complain to You of what is not hidden from You, and I ask You for what is not difficult for You—for Your knowledge of my condition renders my asking unnecessary. O Allah, O Reliever of distress, remove from me the troubles in which I find myself. O You whose essence is never absent that I should await You, nor sleeping that I should awaken You, nor heedless that I should remind You, nor helpless that I should grant You respite. O Knower of the summary, O Self-Sufficient from detail—Your knowledge suffices speech and Your generosity suffices asking. Hope is cut off except from You; aspirations have failed except in You; all paths are blocked except to You. O Allah, O All-Hearing, O All-Seeing, O Near, O Answerer of prayers—forgive me and have mercy upon me by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. Make my sustenance easy for me and subject all Your creation to me, for indeed You have power over all things. And may Allah, the Exalted, send blessings and peace upon our Master Muhammad, his family, and his companions. And all praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
