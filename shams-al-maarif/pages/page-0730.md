# Page 730 (English translation)
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Al-Ghanī Al-Mughnī"

Definition of Ghanī (Self-Sufficient), Who is Called Ghanī, Who is He:

Ghanī is one who is not dependent upon anyone else. He is free from all attachments in his essence and attributes, because one whose existence depends upon something external is poor and needy, and such a Ghanī (Self-Sufficient One) does not exist except Allāh, Glorious and Exalted. The Lord Most High alone is Ghanī (Absolutely Self-Sufficient), and by His own self-sufficiency, He makes whomever He wishes "Ghanī" (self-sufficient). Among humans, those who are Ghanī are dependent upon the Giver, that is, the One who makes them self-sufficient. Thus, this one is Ghanī in the sense of being heedless of other than God, in such a way that God assists him and removes need from him. The True Giver is one who has no need of anything in creation, and whoever is in such a state that he is needy but that to which he is needy is present with him, then he is figuratively Ghanī. The self-sufficiency of other than God extends only to this limit, because mere absence of need is not sufficiency; rather, need must be completely eliminated.

Ghanī is Only Allāh, the Exalted:

So the Lord Most High alone is Ghanī, as He has said:
"And Allāh alone is the Ghanī, the Praiseworthy"
"And besides Allāh, all are poor."

The Messenger ﷺ said: Poverty is in the Heart, Not in Wealth:

His Eminence Muhammad ﷺ said that poverty is not due to having much wealth; rather, poverty is in the heart. You will see that a merchant may possess so much wealth that even if he spends it throughout his entire life it would never be exhausted, yet he nearly gives up his life over the smallest coin. The highest degree of sufficiency is to be content with whatever one has, because sufficiency belongs to the people of the heart, and only the one whom God grants sufficiency becomes sufficient. Sometimes a person is extremely impoverished, yet people consider him a great wealthy man. It is said:
"The ignorant one thinks them wealthy due to their abstinence."
The man whose character is like that of an animal is extremely worthless, and throughout the entire world he is called base and poor.

One Who Recites These Names Becomes Dignified, a Patron of the Poor, and Wealthy in Heart:

Whoever performs the specific litany of these sacred names should adopt vigilance over the heart along with dignity and consideration. Their temperament becomes extremely noble. If you wish, recite both names together; otherwise, recite them separately. The operator (mowlakhal) of these names will be present. The name of the operator of the name Ghanī is Ṣafā'īl, and the name of the operator of the name Mughnī is Mūnthā'īl.

In these two names there are two ṣirāʿ (mystical formulas) of invocation. The first ṣirāʿ is with tashdīd, and the second without tashdīd. For the sake of love, inscribe the form of the name Ghanī in the natal chart (ṭāliʿ) and write the name of its operator around it, and let a person keep it with himself. The heart of the person for whose sake he performs the operation will turn toward him. If an impoverished person keeps it with himself, his sustenance will become abundant and he will attain the greatest richness.

When any ruler inscribes it in gold or silver in a natal chart and keeps it with himself, he will rule with great splendor. When a poor person inscribes it, God will make him wealthy. If it is written and placed in a box, God will grant blessing within it. If one keeps it with musk, He will expand his sustenance. Whoever repeats the name Ghanī and Allāh will seal the gates of [want?] upon him.
