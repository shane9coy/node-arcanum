And this is its invocation, and it is useful for all purposes:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful 18 There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, there is no god but You, O Allah, by the glory of Your presence, for nothing is hidden from You on earth or in heaven. He is the One Who fashions you in the wombs as He wills. There is no god but He, the Mighty, the Wise. I ask You, O Most Gracious, O Most Merciful, by the right of Your ancient speech, and by Muhammad, peace be upon him, the master of the messengers, that You show me the servant of the letter Lam-Alif. I will not be able, by Your light, to unveil the veil between me and You. Answer by the right of the Possessor of glory and blessings, there is no god but You. Magnify me and give me sight of my affair, O Allah, twice. You are my goal and my need is to You, O Allah, O Lord. Your call is Your word: You are Allah besides whom there is no god; to You do necks submit, and You have power over all things. Hasten for us the glory of the blinking of an eye, and reveal to me, no no no no no no. It has risen in distress and delay. Make rational. Be quick with this, by 'Alif, La'. There is no power except by Allah, the High, the Great.

The usage of this is that when you enter seclusion and seek it, it will appear. The duration of seclusion is 28 days.

Chapter: There is no specific time designated for serving these letters and paying their due; you can do so at any time. When you go into seclusion for them, divide the number of its letter by 30/30; whatever remains is the number of days of seclusion. The luminous days are those [mentioned]. And when any need arises, use that same letter for that need—for example, use Ta for repelling enemies and removing harm, Jim for attraction (obtaining something), Mim for love, and Sin for enmity. Hold fast to piety and consuming the permissible. Change your daily clothes in accordance with the colors of the planets. Sitting in a clean and pure place, busy yourself with supplication until the light comes before you. And at night-time, keep the invocation on your tongue; the client will appear. Its form will be like the shape of the moon. It will address you and fulfill your need. This invocation is useful for every need:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful 18 What Allah wills—there is no power except by Allah, the Great. Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we shall return. Allah is my Lord; I associate nothing with Him. Allah is the protector of those who believe, He brings them out from darknesses into light. Allah suffices me; there is no god but He. Upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Magnificent Throne. In the name of Allah—our door, blessed be our walls. Ya Sin, our ceiling. We seek help from Allah for the secret of the support of the point, from Huwa Hiya Halawa'i 'ajilina ya nukta al-wujud. I ask You, O Allah, O ancient of beneficence, O resolver of doubts, O eternal of eternities, O He who rolls the night upon the day and rolls the day upon the night. I ask You to subjugate for me through this point, and make it eager. Come, O Gatherer—Asli Wahida ya Ya Hu 7 Hayawut 7 Haya 7 wa Ahiyah 7
