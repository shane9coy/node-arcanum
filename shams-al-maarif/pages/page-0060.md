# Page 60 (English translation)
Any child born in it will love the righteous witnesses. Its incense is aloe wood and sandalwood.

Mansion of Sa'd al-Akhbiya:
Its figure is this: [Sigil: four points in the form of a cross] Its letter is dhal (ذ). Its temperament is airy. When the Moon enters it, the spirituality of trials and corruptions descends. There is separation and wars in it. Works are not completed in it. Even if they are completed, they are not good. Medical treatment of patients, spiritual treatment, works of talismans and alchemy etc. should not be done in it. Any child born in it will be a sinner and a transgressor. Its incense is frankincense, henbane, and black pepper. And Allah the Exalted is the most knowing.

Mansion of Far' Muqaddam:
Its figure is this: [Sigil: two vertical points] Its letter is dad (ض). Works of love, desire, and opening of hearts are performed in it. It is suitable for every ancient matter, diseases, and spiritualities. One will succeed in making talismans, preparing beneficial operations, and meeting kings and chiefs. Any child born in it will have a good end. Its incense is frankincense, cloves, and saffron.

Mansion of Far' Mu'akhkhar:
Its figure is this: [Sigil: two vertical points] Its letter is dha (ظ). Its temperament is completely fortunate. When the Moon enters it, the spirituality of blessings and virtues descends by the command of Allah the Exalted. At this time, one should avoid confronting enemies in war. It is beneficial for bloodletting, the act of bathing, and the act of a man's hand being cut. It is good for going to the bathhouse, cutting hair and having letters written, and selling medicine. Any child born in it will be a transgressor and a traitor. Its incense is black pepper and other spices.

Mansion of Rashad:
Its figure is this: [Sigil: four horizontal points] Its letter is ghayn (غ). Its temperament is watery. At this time, the spirituality of good endings descends by the command of Allah the Exalted. In it, talismans, all good works, the art of alchemy, and spiritual treatment are better. In it, travel, wearing new clothes, moving from one house to another, salary, and meeting chiefs are good. Any child born in it will be blessed. Its incense is colchicum.

[Heading: Chapter: Distribution of the Signs of the Zodiac over the Mansions]
Aries has two-thirds of Risha and one-third of Sharitayn. Taurus has two-thirds of Sharitayn, Batayn, and Thurayya. Gemini has one-third of Thurayya, and Dhibran and Haqi'ah. Cancer has Han'adh, Zira', and two-thirds of Nasrah. Leo has one-third of Nasrah and Tufrah, and two-thirds of Jabhah. Virgo has one-third of Jabhah, Khiratan and Sarfah. Libra has one-third of Tufrah and Zebana. Scorpio has Zebana and Aklil. Sagittarius has one-third of Aklil, and Qalb and Shulah. Capricorn has Na'im, Baldah, and one-third of Dhabih. Aquarius has one-third of Dhabih, and Bula' and Dhibh, and one-third of Sa'd. Pisces has one-third of Sa'd, and Akhbiya and Far' Muqaddam.
