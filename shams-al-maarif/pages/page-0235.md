# Page 235 (English translation)
The purity of intention, sincerity, the desire for the afterlife, and companionship with those upon whom God Almighty has bestowed blessings—[unclear] the truthful ones, the martyrs, and the righteous—hold a very elevated rank, and they are excellent as friends. The Sheikh said to me: "O brother Abdul-Wahid, everything good and blessing that I possess comes from Surah Al-Ikhlas." Upon hearing these words of the Sheikh, I smiled. He said: "Why did you smile? Do the ascetics laugh at this?" I said: "No, I swear by Him that I have never laughed at the attributes of God Almighty—for He is the Lord of glory and majesty—but the reason for my smile is that I have come to you from the lands of the Maghreb solely for this purpose." He said: "Is that the matter?" I said: "By the Lord of the Kaaba, that is indeed the matter." He said: "You have been with me for all this time and have not even mentioned remembrance to me. You are a very patient person." The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever intends us, his right becomes obligatory upon us." I am a follower of the Sacred Law; my practice is in accordance with it. Since you have intended me, your right has become obligatory upon me. You are among the people of knowledge and have come to me from very far away. I was not aware all this time of what the reason was for your staying here. This matter indicates the abundance of your intellect and the excellence of your knowledge."

I kissed the Sheikh's hands. The Sheikh kissed my forehead and said: "Tomorrow I will present it to you and explain its method." Due to joy, I did not sleep the entire night. After performing the morning prayer and making tawaf of the House of God, I went to the Sheikh's residence. The Sheikh was present in the house. When I approached, I kissed his hand. He said: "Do you know what I said to you and what I indicated?" I said: "I do not know." He said: "My murshid, Sheikh Abdul-Wahid Khwarazmi, taught me for fourteen years that for whatever matter I wish, after sending the noble blessings upon myself, upon him, and upon the noble essence of the Prophet ﷺ, I ask God Almighty for the unveiling of whatever word I desire, and by the blessing of these Names, the unveiling comes to me as to whether I should do that matter or not. In a dream, I saw my murshid, and he said: 'O Abu Abdullah, you inquire about Abdul-Wahid—or the matter of Qaslat al-Qasd—is about the sacred riyada. So perform it. He is a very virtuous person. Tell him: he is also worthy, but make a covenant with him that he will protect it. He will keep it hidden from unqualified people. If he does not keep his intention correct, severe affliction will befall him through his association with it. Thus, we ask God Almighty for safety. Convey my salutations to him as well.' Abdul-Wahid says: Upon hearing this, I wept profusely and performed a prostration of gratitude in the court of God Almighty." The Sheikh took a covenant from me near the Black Stone that I would not reveal this secret before unqualified people, and he instructed me regarding the fear of God Almighty. Then the Sheikh gave me a paper upon which this sacred riyada was written:

Whoever intends to undertake the invitation of Surah Al-Ikhlas should first obtain sincerity of the heart. After performing ghusl and wudu, let him wear clean clothes and sit in a secluded place where there are no people. He should not speak to anyone. One virtuous person should remain present to serve him. For the riyada, observe a fast on the first Thursday of the new month. After commencing the seclusion, observe fourteen fasts. Avoid eating things that possess spirit. Let him break his fast with bread, salt, and olive oil. Every day, let him recite the noble Surah five thousand (5000) times. After each prayer, recite one thousand (1000) times, and at half of the night, recite one thousand (1000) times. Let him continue this practice for fourteen days, so that the total will reach eighty-four (84) thousand. During the remaining time, let him recite supplications, remembrance, or the noble blessings. Let him recite it day and night, and fumigate him with the fragrance of sandalwood and camphor. However, on the night of Friday, in the last part of the night, after completing the practice, let him recite Surah Al-Ikhlas sixteen thousand (16,000) times and then recite this supplication:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā wājidu yā fardu yā aḥadu yā ṣamadu yā man lam yattakhidh ṣāḥibatan wa-lā waladan yā man lam yalid wa-lam yūlad wa-lam yakun lahu kufuwan aḥadun. As'aluka an tūsakhkhira lī khuddāma hādhihi al-sūrati al-sharīfati an yūjībūnī ilā mā urīdu innaka 'alā kulli shay'in qadīrun. Aqsimtu 'alaykum yā khuddāma hādhihi al-sūrati bi-mā ta'taqidūnahu illā mā 'asja'tum bil-ījābati

"O Allah, I ask You, O One, O Unique, O One, O Eternal, O He who has not taken a spouse nor a child, O He who does not beget nor is begotten, and none is equal to Him. I ask You to subjugate for me the servants of this noble Surah so that they may answer me regarding what I desire. Indeed, You have power over all things. I swear upon you, O servants of this noble Surah, by what you believe, except that you hasten in answering me."
