# Page 340 (English translation)
[340]

May You cause [his heart] to soften as You caused the sea to be subdued for Moses, peace be upon him, and soften their hearts for me as You softened iron for David, peace be upon him. They speak only by Your command, their foreheads are in Your grasp—You turn them wherever You will. O Turner of Hearts, O Knower of the Unseen, extinguish the anger of So-and-so son of So-and-so alongside "There is no god but Allah." I have extinguished their anger, and draw their affection and love for me by the love of Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. When those women saw Joseph, peace be upon him, they were greatly astonished, they cut their own hands, and said, "By Allah's oath, this is no mortal—he is a noble angel." Power and might belong to Allah, who is Exalted and Great.

**Protection from Every Kind of Fear and Misfortune**

Know that this is also a special property of Ayat al-Kursi: if you are in the place of an oppressor and other people are also present, gather them all in one place and draw a circle around all of them, and you also enter within it. Then recite Ayat al-Kursi seven times and recite this supplication:

**"And their protection is not burdensome for Him, and He is the Exalted, the Great. And We have preserved this by the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing. And We have preserved it from every rebellious satan. And We have preserved it from every accursed satan. Surely We have sent down the Reminder, and surely We are its guardians. For him there are successor angels before him and behind him who guard him by the command of Allah. But it is a noble Quran in a preserved tablet. So We delivered him and his family from the great distress. And We delivered him from grief. And thus We deliver the believers. And you are not over them a guardian. Every soul has a guardian over it. So if they turn away, then say, 'Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god except Him; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.' Allah is the Guardian over them, and you are not over them a guardian. Guardian, Guardian, O Guardian, O Amin! Preserve us, O Allah! Protect us with Your eye that does not sleep, and shelter us with Your shelter that cannot be breached. O Allah, O Allah, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds."**

"The protection of both of them does not burden Him, and He is Exalted and Great. This is by the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing, and protection from every satan. We have protected it from every accursed satan. Surely We have sent down the Reminder [the Quran], and We are its protectors. There are guardian angels for a person, walking before him and behind him, who protect him by the command of Allah. Rather, it is the noble Quran in the Preserved Tablet. We delivered him and his family from great distress. We delivered him from grief, and thus We deliver the believers, and you are not a guardian over them."

"If they turn away, then you say, 'Allah is sufficient for me—there is no god besides Him; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne. Allah is Guardian over them, and you are not a guardian over them.' O Guardian, Guardian, O Protector, O Amin! Guard us, O Allah! Guard us with Your eye that does not sleep, and protect us with Your protection. O Allah, O Allah, O Allah, O Lord of the Worlds."

After this, all of you remain silent. Then even if an army and host come upon you, they will not be able to cause you any harm, and Allah will protect you from them. Know that this noble verse was sent down from beneath the Throne. When this verse was sent down upon the Prophet, may peace be upon him, seventy thousand angels came with it. This verse delivers from the fear of jinn and humans. When anyone reads it in a state of fear, they will be safe from every evil, for this verse is one of protection. It has many [seventy-two] special properties. For fear of the length of the text, I conclude my explanation here.
