# Page 393 (English translation)
391

In the understanding without limit, O Allah, You are the Living God who never dies forever, O Allah, You are the Eternal God, the Worshipped, O Allah, You are the Honored and Blessed God, O Allah, You are my Lord, possessor of majesty and honor. O Allah, I ask You by the sanctity of Surah Ya-Sin and by the right of this blessed supplication, that You show me Your sanctuary, and grant me the visit to the grave of Your prophet Muhammad, upon him be peace, and make easy for me every difficulty, and subjugate for me the servants of this surah that they may be helpers for me in whatever I desire of all good. O Allah, subjugate for me Your creation and Your provision. O Allah, incline the hearts of Your servants, of every male and female, free and slave, small and great, with eternal love, affection, and kindness, and grant me abundant good fortune, and subjugate for me the hearts of Your servants, and grant me lawful, pure, blessed provision, and be for me a helper, a reliever, a protector, a supporter, and a trustee. Glory be to the One who releases every indebted one. Glory be to the One who relieves every grieved one. Glory be to the One whose treasures are between the kaf and nun. Glory be to the One who, when He intends something, says to it "Be" and it becomes.

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, I ask You and make supplication to You. O Allah, You are Allah beside whom there is no god, You are One, You have no partner, and verily Muhammad, upon him be peace, is Your servant and messenger. O Allah, You are the Clear Truth. O Allah, You are the Firm Helper. O Allah, You are characterized with sovereignty. O Allah, You are the True God. O Allah, You are the Light of the heavens and the earth. O Allah, You are the Light of this world and the hereafter. O Allah, You are the One, the Unique, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. You are the Mighty, the Compeller. You are the Self-Sufficient, the Master of all. O Allah, You are Allah, the Exalted in beneficence. O Allah, You are Allah, the Creator with Your words. O Allah, You are free from every defect. O Allah, You are Allah—that Essence who neither gave birth nor was born, and none is equal to Him. O Allah, You are Allah whose speech cannot be matched and in whom there is no doubt. O Allah, You are Allah, without "how" You are the First, and without end You are the Last. O Allah, You are Allah and above all gods. O Allah, You are Allah—that Essence who does not die forever. O Allah, You are the Eternal God. O Allah, You are the Honored and Blessed. O Allah, You are my Lord, possessor of greatness and honor. O Allah, I ask You by the sanctity of Surah Ya-Sin and this blessed supplication, that You show me Your sanctuary, and grant me the visit to the grave of Your prophet Muhammad, upon him be peace, and make easy every difficulty. Subjugate the servants of this surah for me—that they may be my helpers in this matter which I desire. O Allah, subjugate for me Your creation and provision. O Allah, instill in the hearts of Your servants, men and women, free and slave, young and old, eternal love, affection, and kindness for me, and grant me abundant fortune. Subjugate for me the hearts of Your servants—that they may grant me lawful, pure, blessed provision, and be for me a helper, protector, and trustee. Blessed is the Pure Essence who pays the debt of the indebted, and relieves every grief and sorrow. Blessed is the Pure Essence whose treasures are between the kaf and nun. Blessed is that Essence who, when He intends something, says "Be" and it becomes.
