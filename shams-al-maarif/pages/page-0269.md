Chapter 11: The description of the name al-Mutakabbir, the protection of place and city, and the subjugation of the rebellious becoming submissive

Whoever writes this name on the wall of a city's wall or gate or garden chamber in 94 places during the seventh hour of Friday, God, exalted be He, will protect that city, place, or garden from every evil. Whoever writes it on a triangular seal with green brass during the dignity of Mars and keeps it with them, every rebellious person will be humiliated before them. Whoever frequently invokes this name, all the rebellious and Satans will submit before them and everyone will heed their words. Its number is 664. This number is from bicomposite and odd deficient numbers. The numbers 700 point to the two great divine names of God, exalted be He—al-Ḥukm and al-Khāliq. This is the description of its remedy:

Chapter 12: The description of the name al-Khāliq, properties and benefits—useful for craftsmen and merchants

This name is suitable for merchants and craftsmen. Whoever writes it on a ring at such a time when some sign is ascending from the triplicity of fire, then wears it and loves his wife, she will become pregnant. Its number is 731. These are the first numbers that point to the letter dhāl; therefore it is necessary that the creation be humiliated before the Creator. Its letters have 904 names, which point to the two great divine names of God, exalted be He—al-Awwal and al-Ākhir. This is the description of its temperament:
