# Page 645 (English translation)
The Virtues of the Name "Muhaymin"

Its meaning is that it is the essence which is established upon the actions of His creation and their life and death and then resurrection. This name is the collector of salam (peace) and is the proof of the apparent and the hidden, and its five letters include dominion and subtlety.

**The Secrets and Mysteries of the Name Muhaymin:**

The mim is from the letters of dominion, and mim is their gate, and the ha is the apparent ruler. Within the ha there are two letters with which the name "Hu" (He) is connected, and Hu means the reality of the self. The ya is the turning of alif, which is created from Hu, and ya is one of the letters of action, and the second mim points toward the supreme dominion, and the nun points toward the reality of knowledge, because it is its inner aspect, and upon it the kingdom was completed, meaning upon the nun. In short, this name is the collector of all these mysteries.

**How Intellect, Spirit, and Soul Have Been Released from One Another:**

Allah the Most High has placed Muhaymin upon the mysteries of intellect, and He has made intellectMuhaymin upon another, and He has made spirit Muhaymin upon soul, and He has made soul Muhaymin upon movements, and movements are Muhaymin upon stillness, and they are Muhaymin upon the letters, and they are Muhaymin upon the lower realm, and they are Muhaymin upon the mysteries, and through this the world has been released, and He has released the things with one another, although all receive assistance from Him, and the first letter is Muhaymin upon the second, meaning alif upon ba, and ba upon ta.

**The One Who Remembers This Name Will Be a Friend of Allah:**

Thus, the name through which you attain progress will be Muhaymin upon another name, and the Names of the Exalted Lord are Muhaymin upon the names of the non-Lord. Whoever gains power through this name must necessarily observe propriety in all actions. This name is among the remembrances of the friends of Allah, because the one who remembers this name becomes greatly knowing and also possesses much fear of God.

**The One Who Remembers This Name Quickly Attains Lofty Degrees:**

Muhaymin is the one who has granted the truth with the pinnacle of intellect, and turned the truth away with the head of heads, and given felicity with the head of public knowledge, and caused the truth to be acted upon with the head of guidance and guidance. The one who seeks divine proximity through this name genuinely desires to progress to other degrees, and degree by degree he ascends swiftly. The recitation of this name with the head and aloud is necessary.

**The Various Contemplations of This Name with Qualities:**

The contemplation of the head you shall perform with intention, and the contemplation aloud with life, and the contemplation of spirit with beauty, and the contemplation of soul with fear, and the contemplation of heart with knowledge, and the work of the body with action. These are the keys to the sciences of purity. When you wish to complete these stations, recite this name day and night in seclusion.

**Through the Remembrance of This Name, the Gates of Various States Open and It Attains the Station of Honor:**

Thus, at that time the gate of reality shall open for the sake of reality with intention, and the gate of sincerity with guidance, and through the contemplation of spirit the gate of peace, and through the contemplation of heart the gate of knowledge shall open, and all this reality shall be obtained through the remembrance of this name.
