Its form is this

[A large square grid, approximately 28 rows by 28 columns, containing individual Arabic letters in each cell. The letters appear to follow a repeating cyclical pattern shifted in each row, forming a talismanic letter square (naqsh). Due to the resolution and repetitive nature, the individual letters within the grid are not transcribed cell-by-cell.]
