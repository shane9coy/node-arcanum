I say that love is the purity of affection, this is the eternal proof with the entrance into the heart. That is the unification of love; it is compassion, it is the beloved, and with it the affair of the intellect occurs with full strength of intention, and only Allah the Most High gives the success.

In this Name there is a strange effect for the subtleties of dreams and knowing hidden matters. Their number is 57. {Chapter 50: On the Virtues of the Name "Ba'ith" (One Who Sends/Resurrects), The Best Practice for Health, Life, Courage and Strength}

This Name is the elixir and the head of light, suitable for one whose courage has become low. From an abundance, let him mention this Name, then courage becomes strong. Some knowers of the arcane have said that this Name has a strange effect for the preservation of life, health, and strength. When you intend it, do not delay but firmly begin the mention of this Name and with courage mention this Name until your state overcomes you. Allah the Most High will grant you life with such a spirit and complete strength that there will be no equal to your courage in existence. In the first hour of reading the Name, first inscribe it on a tablet, then mention this Name 451 times and keep it with you, then you will be protected from every evil action by the command of Allah the Most High.
