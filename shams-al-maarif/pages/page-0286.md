Chapter 41: The Properties of the Name Hasib and Its Innumerable Benefits Along with the Fulfillment of Needs

Whoever engages in frequent remembrance of this Name, all his needs are fulfilled, and whatever he asks from Allah the Most High is granted to him, because this Name points to the Supreme Name. If a person fears the outcome of some calculation, let him engage in frequent remembrance of this Name, and Allah the Most High will, through His blessing, deliver him from every fear and dread. Whoever, during the ascendant of Venus or in the first hour of Friday, engraves this [Name] on a ring of Hasib with the sign of Cancer and wears it, and performs the remembrance of this Name along with its numbers, then everyone upon whom his gaze falls will love him, obey him, and will be inclined toward him in every matter. He will be granted majesty, honor, greatness, and rank. Its number is 80. This belongs to those Names that revert to a single letter, just as this Name reverts to the letter ha', because the original calculation is the boundary between the two letters. Through Hasib, disputes are settled among them. It also has the meaning of al-Kafi [the Sufficient], because sufficiency is sufficient without need of anything else—Allah the Most High is Glorious. This number belongs to the nine added [numbers]. Its (181672) points to the Name of the Savior, because in "sufficiency" there are the meanings of "sign toward good." Allah the Most High has said:

"Wa kafa bina hasibin"
That is: "And sufficient are We as Reckoners."

Then He said:
"Thumma nunajji alladhina ittaqaw"
"Then We shall deliver those who were conscious of Allah."

A poet has spoken well, whose interpretation is: "The inner reckoners have calculated with great precision, then they bestowed favor and embraced." This is the quality of kings—that they act gently with their servants. My heart says to me, and my tongue confirms it, that whoever dies as a Muslim, Allah will not burn him—that is, he will not be cast into the Fire.

And it points to the Name Hasib, because He who takes account from you—by His superiority over you, or by displaying His justice—establishes all things. For this reason, the letter ba', from which everything is derived, appears at the end of the Name Hasib, and it also appears in the Name Ba'i, because He who took account from you has fulfilled it. Especially when He knows what is yours against Him and what is His against you: among things that are counted, there is calculation; among things that exist, there is weight. Thus completeness is necessary for it—truth has come in every confrontation. Regarding which, among bodies and species, one has 143, which points to the Name Mubin. For this Hasib, there is what points to the Name Hasib. Because in it is worn that "the sufficient intercessor is weighed with completeness," for one who is negligent in calculation—Allah is all forms of light. These 143 are what point to it, that in which there is sufficiency for all the worlds. This is the assemblage of Names—that is, the Name of Allah; through His favor, His lights are sufficient, and the table is complete.
