The First Manifestation: it is both simple and composite. Its simple [letters] are 14, and its gradational [letters] are 23. When combined with al-haqq.

The Second Manifestation: it is both simple and composite and known, with 24 letters, and the cube of the well-traveled one, which is 128 with al-haqq, then the result is mushtāyil.

The Third Manifestation: it is both simple and composite. The simple is numerical and the composite is literal, as we have already explained.

The operation: when you wish to create an animal, take the first letter of that animal's name, and construct it with the letters of the same element to which that letter belongs, and perform the operation of creating the animal as we have explained.

The theory of earthly animals is that, as the spirit called sab' has the letter b as its manifestation, hayyiz has the letter l, and zāy is for the intermediary with the letter b as well, and ḥā for the serpent. Likewise, all animals should be understood. And

the theory of minerals is that l is taken from alif and dhāl from zahab. In short, the first letter of each mineral's name should be taken. Then, having decomposed and expanded it, use it with whatever talisman you wish. Do you not know how the prophets, peace be upon them, manipulated the elements—for example, Noah, peace be upon him, in water, Abraham, peace be upon him, in fire, Solomon, peace be upon him, in air, and Moses, peace be upon him, in earth?

The vision of the realm of Malakut:

Regarding the human species among animals, we have already explained that it manipulates these eight letters, in the celestial realm and in the terrestrial, and in all four temperaments. And this is the form of the world, which they call hayūlā. If the veil of desires were not over the human heart, it would see the realm of Malakut with the eye of the visible.

The guidance of the Messenger, may God bless him and grant him peace:

Rather, the Messenger, may God bless him and grant him peace, said that if the satans had not surrounded the heart of the son of Adam, they would have seen the Malakut of heaven and earth. Therefore, for the sake of these very necessities, the rules of ethical refinement and spiritual discipline were established. Thus, the Messenger himself, may God bless him and grant him peace, also practiced spiritual discipline in the Cave of Hira, and regarding these very meanings, the Messenger said:

The hadith about the person who practices forty days of seclusion, whose tongue will speak wisdom:

Whoever devotes forty days solely for God, rivers of wisdom will flow from his heart to his tongue, and God Almighty will open for him the gates of unveiling. Make these principles well-embedded in your heart so that success may be attained through them.

Its details are as follows: write both the name of the seeker and the name of the sought in this physical table that follows. Both will be correct or they will be opposing. In both cases, if the operation is of good, then place the name of the seeker first and the name of the sought last, so that the sought becomes the seeker. Pay attention to its masculine form, and some say that this is not necessary. Only the two names should be combined. In any case, combination is necessary. In short, extract the letters of both names and place them in a square, and perform the extraction of the common people in this manner. Then, if you wish, combine the letters in groups of three or four to form words, and if you wish, extract the name by placing the letters, then combine the letters and read them and burn them, and from every five letters extract the takābīl.

This is the meaning of jālat:
This word is Syriac, which means "burning," and both have the same meaning. Therefore, it was used so that the seeker may attain success.
