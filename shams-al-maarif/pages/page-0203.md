# Page 203 (English translation)
The Invocation of Surah al-An'am and Prayers for Fulfilling Needs

Perform ablution and wear clean clothes, and abandon all worldly matters completely. Begin the ritual with utmost humility and submission on Sunday after the Dhuhr prayer. First, perform two rak'ahs of the prayer for fulfilling needs. In the first rak'ah, after al-Fatihah, recite Qul huwa Allahu ahad three times. Complete the second rak'ah in the same manner. Then write your need on a piece of paper and place it before you. Then turn toward the qiblah. Do not look around or speak to anyone. First, recite durood sharif forty-one (41) times: Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammadin wa barik 'inda manzili mubarak jiyyah (41) times.

Wa uffawwidhu amri ila Allahi inn Allaha baseerun bil-'ibadi wa hasbuna Allahu wa ni'mal-wakeel

Then recite Surah al-Fatihah thirty times and Ayat al-Kursi ten (10) times. Then take the Noble Quran in your hand and, with good intention, hold your need in your heart and recite this prayer:

Bismillahi al-Rahmani al-Raheem. Haadha kalamu rabbinna amanna bima anzalta wa ittabo'na al-rasula faktubna ma'a al-shahideen. Allahumma anzaltahu bil-haqqi wa bil-haqqi nazala. Allahumma 'az-zam raghbati fihi waj'alhu nuran li-basari wa shifa'an li-sadri. Allahumma antiq bihi lisani wa zayyin bihi surati wa jammil bihi wajhi wa jasadi warzuqni tilawatahu bi-ghayri riya'in wa sum'atin wa 'ala ta'atika anaa al-layli wa atraf al-nahari waj'alhu hujjatan lana la 'alayna wa nabbihna 'an nawmatil ghafilina qabl al-mawti bi-rahmatika ya arham al-rahimina.

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. This is the speech of our Lord. We have believed in what You have revealed and followed the Messenger, so record us with the witnesses. O Allah, You have revealed it with truth and with truth it has descended. O Allah, increase my desire in it and make it a light for my sight and a healing for my chest. O Allah, with it make my tongue speak, adorn my form with it, beautify my face and body with it, and grant me its recitation without hypocrisy or showing off, and grant me success in obeying You throughout the night and the edges of the day. Make it an argument for us, not against us, and awaken us from the sleep of the heedless before death, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. Allah will fulfill his need. Then begin Surah al-An'am with sincere heart. When you reach Al-Fawz al-Mubin, recite Wa uffawwidhu amri ila Allahi inn Allaha baseerun bil-'ibadi forty-one (41) times.

Recite the same first durood sharif forty-one (41) times. While reciting "Habbt," when you reach Tad'oonahu tadarru'an wa khufiyatan, recite Iyaka na'budu wa iyaka nasta'in forty-one times and durood sharif forty-one times. Then when you reach Fa-qad wakkalna biha qawman laisoo biha bi-kafireen, recite Uffawwidhu amri to the end forty-one times, and Amanna bima anzalta to the end, and the same durood sharif forty-one times. Then after that, recite this prayer:

Ilahi man dha alladhi da'aka fa-lam tujibhu wa man dha alladhi sa'alaka fa-lam tu'tihi wa man dha alladhi ista'jara bika fa-lam tujirhu wa man dha alladhi tawakkala 'alayka fa-lam takfihi wa aghuthana bika ya Allah (3 times). Bika astagheethu ya Mugheethu aghithni wa iqdi hajati ahlaha wa mustahiqqaha fa-innaka ahl al-taqwa wa ahl lahu maghfirah.

O Allah, who is there who called upon You and You did not answer him? And who is there who asked from You and You did not give him? And who is there who sought refuge with You and You did not grant him refuge? And who is there who placed his trust in You and You did not suffice him? O Allah, I cry to You for help (3 times). By You I seek help, O Helper, help me and fulfill my need, its owner and its deserving one, for indeed You are worthy of piety and worthy of forgiveness.
