# Page 216 (English translation)
Remove the veil of heedlessness from our hearts, O my Lord, and open from our eyes that which has prevented them from taking heed, until we obtain knowledge of Your knowledge—then teach us, and with it we shall dispose [of it]. The spiritual ones who are with Your sacred Name's secret, O You who created Hell for the sinners and adorned Paradise for the obedient—I make intercession with You, O Allah, by Your Beautiful Names and Your perfected, lofty Words, to fulfill my need. Make the knowledge of these two noble, generous, forbearing Names subservient to me, so that every day one of them brings me a gold dinar from the buried treasures of the earth; I place it beneath my head and with it obtain help in fulfilling my need.

O Allah, O Lord, O Most Gracious, O Most Merciful, protect us. O Allah, O You of noble essence and glorious Names, I ask of You the sustenance of the dominant, unasked-for Seeker, of the unasked-for Petitioner. O Allah, if my sustenance is in the heaven, send it down; if it is in the earth, bring it forth; if it is distant, bring it near; if it is near, make it easy; if it is nonexistent, create it; if it is cut off, make it constant; if it is little, increase it and bless it. O Allah, come for my sake, and be my manager in this, and with knowledge and gift raise my hand, and with urgency do not bring it down. By Your mercy, O Provider, O Opener, O Knowing, O Great, O Generous, O Merciful, accept my prayer with Your grace and generosity—indeed You have power over all things and are kind and aware with Your servants. Power and strength belong to Allah, who is exalted and great. And may Allah the Exalted send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions.
