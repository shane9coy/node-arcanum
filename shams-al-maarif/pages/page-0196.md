# Page 196 (English translation)
Grant me majesty through Your glory, so that troubles are veiled from me, and grant me ease, and through the secret with which Adam, peace be upon him, called upon You and You taught him all the names. O Allah, pour Your blessings upon me which stand between me and creation, for You are the Master. If I am one of the servants, then You are our Master; if I am Your servant, it is said to us: "Yours is the kingdom." O Allah, I ask You who neither slumber nor sleep befall, to grant me a pure life wherein no evil ever occurs. O Eternal One, O You whose qaher governs the entire cosmos, now I stand before You, upon the ground of Your threshold, taking shelter in the foundation, hopeful, resting upon pillows of evils, empty, exhausted, and modest. Truly You have spoken, and Your word is truth: "Ask Me, and I shall accept." I ask of You and place hope in none besides You, holding the certainty that You alone shall extract me from this state in which I am, seeking Your acceptance. I speak with sincerity: O Compeller, compel him who seeks to compel me. Restrain his exercise of qaher from me; Your beneficence shall be upon me. O You whom neither slumber nor sleep overtake, restrain from me the one who intends evil against me; despite the vastness of the earth, make it narrow for him, such that his joy transforms into grief, nay, rather harm comes to him who inflicts this suffering, and occupy him with immense tasks, for nothing is hidden from You. O Allah, Your recompense is the Lord of the heavens and earth and all things. O Allah, do not make me subject to the dominion of my enemies or those who wish to harm me. I am Your wronged, poor, and weak servant. Grant me Your blessings along with the capacity of Your allies for sanctity and intimacy. O You beside whom none may intercede except by Your command, yet I am an aspirant to intercession through the revelation sent upon the prophets, peace be upon them, and for the sake of the welfare of Your creation, protect me from all reprehensible things, calamities, and harms. O my Master, I ask You to help me against him who wishes to oppress me, defeat him who wishes to fight me, and exercise qaher against him who opposes me. Do not help my enemies nor abandon them to themselves; rather, wherever they gather, scatter them. Curse and disgrace them wherever they are dispersed; wherever they meet, shatter them into pieces and annihilate them. Make them wander in darkness; let them fall into destruction.

Let them find no refuge from punishment, whether hidden or manifest; let them gain no benefit from honor or pride, no assistance or my strength. Send punishment between them and their followers. He knows what lies before them and behind them. O Knower of hidden things, O Forgiver of slips, O Merciful toward errors, have mercy upon me; grant me intellect; veil my shame; help me against my enemies, as You helped Your prophets against their enemies. Restrain them; place collars around their necks; seize their sight so they cannot see; deafen their ears so they cannot hear; blind their eyes so they cannot see; seal their mouths so they cannot speak; disfigure them in their places so they cannot walk, nor return to their households. Truly You are the Compeller, the Proud, the Seizer, the Helper, the Strong, the Dominant, the Compeller, the Exalter of the humiliated religion, the Avenger, the Destroyer, the Severe, the Quick Helper, the Swift in qaher, the Lover of killing, the Eraser of stubbornness, the Possessor of greatness and honor.

O Allah, I ask through Your supreme name, Your name, Your honored Prophet, this noble verse, and the exalted names, that You protect me from before and behind, above and below, from my right and left sides; grant me encompassing protection. No thing can enter Your encompassing knowledge except what You will. The One whose knowledge has encompassed all things and numbered everything with precision—I am within this encompassing, seeking refuge in it, between two fingers, and with two states as their disclosure. Contrary to observation is that one's heart is drawn away; among the snares of intelligibles it is broken, and gently reaches the path of guidance. You have conferred distinction and abundant benefit and share. O Allah, instead of mercy send down wrath upon them.
