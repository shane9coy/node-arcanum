# Page 457 (English translation)
And blessings come quickly from Allah the Most High.

**Cure from Diseases:**

The Name Shafi (The Healing) of Allah the Most High has a special effect for obtaining cure from illnesses. The one who remembers it, Allah the Most High keeps him safe from every defect, flaw, and disease. The one who remembers it remains safe from every illness and diseases do not enter his body. If one recites the Name Shafi 422 times over a patient and blows, and recites Surah Fatiha seven times and then recites this supplication and blows, the patient obtains cure. The supplication is:

*Allāhumma ishfi anta al-shāfī lā shifāʾ illā shifāʾuka lā yā Allāhu shifāʾan lā yughādiru saqaman walājalan*

"O Allah, cure [him/her]—You are the Healer—there is no cure except Your cure. O Allah, grant a cure that leaves no sickness or pain."

I commanded Mahmud ibn Shaddad to recite this practice. He had been afflicted with leprosy. All the physicians turned away from him in disgust and refused to treat him. Through the blessing of the remembrance of this Name, he recovered within fifteen days. He became as if this accursed disease had never befallen him at all. If one washes the numerical inscription of the Name Shafi or [unclear] with Zamzam water or rainwater and gives it to the patient to drink, Allah the Most High grants him cure.

**Name al-Muḥsī** is a great Name; it contains one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. Its attached servant's name is Thiyāʾīl. Under it are four rulers. Each ruler is an officer over 148 ranks, and in each rank are 148 assigned servants. The supplication of the Name al-Muḥsī is:

*Yā muḥsī anta alladhī aḥsayta anfāsa al-khalāʾiqi wa anta alladhī faqalta min awliyāʾika subula al-ʿalāqiqi wa anta alladhī waṣalta ahl al-maʿrifa ilā al-nūr al-ʿaẓīmi alladhī huwa fawqa niʿmati al-aḥdāqi wal-ḥadāqi wa anta al-ḥāfiẓu li-jamīʿi al-makhlūqāti alladhī tuḥṣī aʿmālahum wa ājālahum wa anfāsahum fī jamīʿi al-awqāti ḥattā yaghība amrun zāʾighun walā yaṣiḥu ʿinda saʿyi masāʿi asʾaluka Allāhumma yā dhā al-faḍli al-ʿaẓīmi an tarzuqaniyya al-iḥṣāʾa wa ḥifẓa ḥaqāʾiqi al-asmāʾi wal-wuṣūla ilā sirrihā*

"O Counter, You are He who has counted the breaths of all creatures, and You are He who has opened for Your allies the ways of connection, and You are He who has brought those who possess knowledge to the great light which is above the blessing of pupils and eyes, and You are the Preserver of all created beings who count their deeds, their lifespans, and their breaths in all times, until no deviant matter remains hidden nor succeeds in the striving of efforts. I ask You, O Allah, O Possessor of immense grace, to grant me enumeration, the preservation of the realities of the Names, and access to their secrets."

**The Subjugation of the Servants of the Great Names**

The Name al-Mubdiʾ (The Originator) of Allah the Most High is a great Name. It contains one letter from the letters of the Greatest Name. Its attached servant's name is Kayāʾīl. Under it are four rulers. Each ruler is an officer over 56 ranks, and in each rank are 56 assigned servants. The supplication of the Name al-Mubdiʾ is:

*Yā mubdiʾ anta Allāhu alladhī aẓhart sirra al-waḥdati fī qulūbi ahl al-tawḥīdi wa rafaʿta liwāʾa al-majdi fī ṣudūri ahl al-tajrīdi wa naṣabta rāyata al-maʿrifa fī fayāfī ʿuqūli ahl al-tafrīdi asʾaluka Allāhumma bimā abadtahu fī qulūbi khātimi al-anbiyāʾi wa bimā thabattahu fī khātimi al-awliyāʾi wa bimā nasharta fī dhātihimā min raqāʾiqi al-alāʾi wal-naʿmāʾi an turaddaniyya ilayka fī al-ibtidāʾi wal-intihāʾi wa an tuḥyiyaniiyya fī al-sarrāʾi wal-ḍarrāʾi*

"O Originator, You are Allah who manifested the secret of Oneness in the hearts of the people of monotheism, and raised the banner of glory in the breasts of the people of disattachment, and planted the standard of knowledge in the deserts of the intellects of the people of singling out. I ask You, O Allah, by what You have implanted in the hearts of the Seal of the Prophets, and by what You have established in the Seal of the Saints, and by what You have spread within them both of the subtleties of blessings and favors, to return me to You in beginning and ending, and to revive me in ease and hardship."

**The Supplication of the Name al-Muʿīd:**

The Name al-Muʿīd (The Restorer) of Allah the Most High is a great Name. It contains 2 letters from the letters of the Greatest Name. Its servant's name is Khayyāṭīl. Under it are four officers, each an officer over 124 ranks, and in each rank are 124 assigned servants. The supplication of the Name al-Muʿīd is:

*Yā muʿīd anta alladhī daʿawta al-khalāʾiqa fī al-asbābi wal-arḥāmi ilā hayākili al-ajsādi* [text damaged/cut off]
