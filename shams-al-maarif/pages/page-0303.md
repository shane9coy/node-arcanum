Section 70: The Properties of the Name Muqitadir (مقتدر) — "Obtaining Victory and Dominance over the Tyrant"

Whoever frequently mentions this name of the All-High and Eternal, all his affairs will become easy. This name is appropriate for those who wish to accomplish better work than others. Its talismanic image should be written with the head-letter and the agent [letter]. For obtaining omnipotence, victory, and elevation, these are the names of the Strong, the Mighty, the Victorious, and the Powerful. Whoever makes an imprecation against a tyrant using these names—at the end of the month, in the seventh hour of the night, sitting bareheaded on bare earth in a dark place—after first performing two units of prayer, and at the end of each prostration says one hundred times: "Take my right from So-and-so son of So-and-so" (khudh haqqī min fulān ibn fulān), then the same effect will appear that he desires. The condition for the one making the imprecation is that he imprecate only to the same degree that the tyrant has wronged him, and the oppressed person himself should make the imprecation. If someone else makes the imprecation on his behalf, it is also permissible. If his image is engraved on a ring and worn, every rebellious person will fear him and tremble before him, and glory will be manifest from his face. This name has the number 744. It is odd and deficient. Its [divisions] are 12:71:1176, indicating two of the great names of Allah the Most High: Al-Ghālib (The Victorious) and the remainder. Following this number comes the name Mudabbar (The Disposer of Affairs) with four, Al-Quddus (The Holy) with six, Nur (Light) with eight, Ma'a al-'Ālī (With the Most High) and Al-Wājib Al-Wujūd (The Necessary Existence) and Al-Bārī (The Creator) with twelve each, and so forth. This is the image of its talisman.

Section 71: The Properties of the Name Muqaddim (مقدم) — "The Acceptance of Every Supplication"

Whoever frequently mentions this name will have influence in the realm of divine power. If he keeps its square with him and mentions the name according to its numerical equivalent, and supplicates for any person, it will be accepted immediately. This name is from the hidden secrets. Its number is 184, and the words [comprising it] are even, odd, and deficient. It points to the name 'Alī with 1089+171. This is the image of its talisman:
