# Page 781 (English translation)
That is, we have treasures for everything with us, but we send them down with proper measure. Had I wished, I would have explained it quite openly, but:

Verse:
Man ammanūhu 'alā sirrin fa-namma bihi lam yūli'ūhu 'alā al-asrār mā dāma-hu
Translation: "Whom they once informed of a secret and he disclosed it, they will never again make him aware of their mysteries."

Whoever desires the ascension of the reality of the soul toward the Paradise of the Heights, it is necessary for him to study my book many times, for this book is a good companion and a loving, compassionate friend and a good associate for the people of the spiritual path, a compendium for the people of divine inspiration, and an excellent refuge for the travelers. Because I have not discussed in it the desires of the soul, but rather it is a flame that I obtained from the light of felicity and security, and I illuminated it from the valley of the Mount of Light (Tur). It was a gift appearing upon the attributes of the Presence of the Prophet, may peace be upon him. Now that I have taken the path of wayfaring in the valley of verification through the concurrence of the companion of divine assistance, with the utmost strenuous effort and full striving, and with auspicious fortune and firm resolve. Indeed, in this book there is advice for the one who keeps the heart and listens with attentive ears.

Some philosophers say:
Man lam yuharrikhu al-'ūdu awtāruhu wa al-rabī'u wa azhāruhu fa-huwa fāsidu al-mīzāji wa-qad yahtāju ilā al-'ilāji
That is, one whom the oud, its melodies, spring, and its flowers do not move is of corrupted temperament, and his treatment must definitely be undertaken.

Verse:
Mā dharra shamsu al-duhā fī hīni ṭāli'atin an lā yara ḍaw'uhā man laysa dhā baṣar
Translation: "What fault is it of the rising sun of illumination that one lacking sight cannot see its light?"

Therefore, whoever understood its symbols and opened its talismans succeeded with the hidden knowledge, the preserved secret, the Greatest Name, and the Most Exalted Remembrance.

Thus, if you wish to enter the garden of the Sandīyah, the meadow of the narcissus, the garden of the Ashrafiyyah, the grade of the armoozī, the heavenly gaze, the kingdom of delights, the paradises of the Garden of Eden, the sacred pools, the luminous Names, the spiritual mysteries, the Merciful invitations, the mystical delicate insights, the spiritual matters, the path-based knowledges, the Throne-based indications, the prophetic creations, the kamalī determinations, the vocal glad tidings, the Davidic Psalms, the lunar sciences, the Mosaic arrangements, Solomon's ring, Luqman's homilies, the Meccan eloquence, the scribal excellence, the Merciful realities, the Kaaf Al-Akl, the Al-'Aliyy evidences, and the Ahmadī benefits, then it is incumbent upon you to remove the veil from your heart's eye of insight, so that your spirit—which is the clear Books of Allah, His ancient head, and the ancient treasure—may become pure. Allah the Exalted says:

"And in yourselves; then do you not perceive?" (Quran 51:21)

Therefore, whoever looked upon its first book, which is the same, then it is not the same.
