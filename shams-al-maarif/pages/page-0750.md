# Page 750 (English translation)
Chapter 40: Accepted Prayers that Should be Read at All Times

I begin this discourse with the name of Al-Alim (The Knowing) and Al-Hakim (The Wise). Whoever dedicates himself to the remembrance of these two names, Allah the Most High shall grant him success in this purpose, and the secrets of wisdom and craft shall be revealed to him.

These two names are those through which unveiling and knowledge of secrets are obtained, and they are attributed to Israel, peace be upon him. Or it is appropriate for Jibreel (Gabriel), and it is appropriate for Hadi (the Guide of Israel), and these are the names: هَادِي خَبِيرٌ مُبِينٌ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ

"Whoever reads these names, the secrets and knowledge shall be attained by him."

**The Method of Istikhara (Seeking Divine Guidance):**

Whoever wishes to know the outcome should engage in fasting and staying awake at night while remembering these names, and upon each pillar he should say:

أَهْدِنِي يَا هَادِيُ خَبِّّرْنِي يَا خَبِيرُ بَيِّنْ لِي يَا مُبِينُ عَلِّمْنِي يَا عَلَّامَ الْغُيُوبِ

"Guide me, O Guide! Inform me, O Knowing One! Make clear to me, O Manifest One! Teach me, O Knower of the Unseen!"

Then he should mention his need by name, and this practice should be done at midnight, then he should sleep. The reality of the matter shall become known to him in a dream.

Whoever desires rulership and that people obey him should make the name Al-Hadi his litany and repeat it abundantly. And if he wishes to make someone his obedient subject, he should combine it with his name and keep it with him. The desired outcome shall be achieved.

The form of the talisman is as follows: He should trace on the body the letters ا ل ل ع و ق ل ف د ب ی م گرس in a diagonal line from the bottom left to the top right, so that the first row appears at the end (of the tracing). Then he should write it on clean paper, but he should not write the last row, because that is the original row; writing it will cause sorcery. ر ع ی ہ ب ا ل و ا د

Then he should fumigate it with perfume and keep it with him, and repeat the name Al-Hadi abundantly—in the morning, in the evening, in every state, and upon every pillar he should say:

يَا هَادِيَ مَنِ اسْتَهْدَى اهْدِ فُلَانَ بْنَ فُلَانَةَ وَاجْعَلْهُ طَوْعَ يَدَيَّ وَ مُجِيْبِيْ مِنْ نَاصِيَتِهِ وَ قَلْبِهِ

"O Guide, whoever seeks guidance—guide So-and-so son of So-and-so, and make him compliant to my hands and responsive to me, from his forelock and his heart."

And let him begin this practice in the first hour of Thursday, and let him write this talisman on the back of amber. The desired outcome shall be achieved. The form of this talisman is as follows:

Then let him read this prayer:

يَا رَبِّ صَفِّنِي مِنْ كُدُورَاتِ الْأَغْيَارِ صَفًّا بِنَ صِفَةِ بِيَدِ عِنَايَتِكَ وَ قُرْبَةِ إِلَيْكَ وَ احْفَظْنِي مِنْ نَقْصِ التَّكْوِينِ حَتَّى يَنْجَلِيَ فِي مِرْآةِ قَلْبِي وَ مُنَوَّا نَفْسِي كُلُّ اِسْمٍ انْطَبَعَ فِي قُوَّةِ جِبْرَائِيْلَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ وَ انْقَرِيْ بِهِ عَلَى لَفْظِيْ مِنْ فِي اللَّوْحِ الْمَحْفُوْظِ مِنْ اَسْرَارِ اَسْمَائِكَ وَ مَجَامِعِ رَسَائِلِكَ اِذْ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ اسْتَنَارَتْ تَمِدُّ مِنْ اَنَائِهَا طُرُقَهَا لِلَّهِ وَ اَنَّ الَّذِينَ لَهُمْ لَمْ جَامِعِ هَذِهِ الرَّقَائِقِ فِي رُقْعَةِ الْاَدَمِ الْجِبْرَائِيْلِ الْعَالَمِ الْعَلِيْمِ الْعَلَّامِ يَا ذَا الْكَرَمِ الَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِالْقَلَمِ عَرِّفْنَا لَوَائِحَ الْقُدُسِ وَ اِلْهَامِ

"O Lord, purify me from the impurities of (worldly) others, a purification by the attribute of Your care and nearness to You, and protect me from the deficiency of creation until what is imprinted in the force of Jibreel, peace be upon him, becomes clear in the mirror of my heart and my soul, and every name that is impressed upon the strength of Jibreel, upon him be peace, is recited by my tongue from the Preserved Tablet, from the secrets of Your names and the collections of Your scriptures—since every enlightened soul extends from its depths its paths toward Allah—and that those who have collected these subtle mysteries in the tablet of Adam, Jibreel, the knowing scholar, the omniscient one: O Possessor of generosity who taught by the pen, show us the sacred tablets and inspiration."
