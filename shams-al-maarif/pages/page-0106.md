# Page 106 (English translation)
[106]

[Heading: Exposition of Some Beautiful Names and Their Properties]

Al-Rahman, Al-Rahim—these two names are great and majestic. Their prayer benefits the distressed, and they are security for the fearful. Whoever engraves them on a silver ring during the last hour of Friday and wears it shall see no evil. Whoever frequently mentions these two names, all his affairs will be made easy. For mercy is that thing concerning which God, Exalted is He, said: "He who befriends you, I will befriend him; and he who cuts you off, I will cut him off." If you reflect carefully, you will find that Muhammad may the peace and blessings of God be upon him and Al-Rahman are the culmination of beauty and the sum of all excellences.

Whoever writes the name Al-Rahman in a vessel during the lunar ascensions, then erases it with rainwater and gives it to a person whose heart is hard to drink, God, Exalted is He, will transform him with gentleness. And this is its figure:

[Heading: Protection from Calamities and Afflictions; Removal of Hardness of Heart]

Whoever writes the name Al-Rahim in the lunar ascensions and keeps it with him, God, Exalted is He, will protect him from all calamities and afflictions, and the hard-hearted will become soft. This is the figure of the name Al-Rahim:

[Heading: Removal of Carnal Desires; The Stubborn Made Submissive]

Whoever frequently mentions the name Al-Quddus of God, Exalted is He, God, Exalted is He, will remove from him carnal desires. Whoever frequently mentions the name Al-Malik of God, Exalted is He, the stubborn will become submissive before him and obey. Whoever frequently mentions the name Al-Salam of God, Exalted is He, God, Exalted is He, will protect him from all calamities. Such a state will overcome him that if he takes a snake or scorpion in his hand, no harm will befall him. For this purpose, [damaged/unclear source text] this supplication is also beneficial. Whoever writes it and keeps it with him, and goes to an oppressor, he will remain safe from his evil, and God, Exalted is He, will admit him into His protection.
