أَجِرْنِي وَ خَسَسَانِي وَ تَخْذُلْنِي فِي خَطَائِىَ فَدُبِكَ الْأَعْلَى يَا اللّٰهُ يَا تَوَّابُ ط

**[Boxed Heading: Excellence: The Virtues of the Name "Muntaqim" (The Avenger)]**

Muntaqim is the Most High One who grants respite to the disobedient and the rebellious with torment and punishment, and before the torment He gives them respite and opportunity, and He instills fear in them thereby so that they might repent. And this method of vengeance is more painful than swift punishment. And the best method of vengeance for a servant is that he takes vengeance upon his greatest enemy—his own self—and vengeance should be taken when it commits a sin or introduces defect into worship.

**The Method of Vengeance by Ḥaḍrat Bāyazīd: He Did Not Give Water to His Self for One Year:**

It is narrated from Ḥaḍrat Bāyazīd that he said: One day my self became sluggish in reciting the Là dāwū (i.e., Lā ilāha illā Llāh), and I was drinking a great deal of water. So I punished my self by not drinking water for a full year, until I nearly perished without water.

**The Practitioner of This Name Can Take Vengeance Upon His Enemy:**

The minister (wazīr) of the heart for this name is here, so that people raise objections against the saints and take vengeance upon them. When someone has wronged you, recite the name according to its numerical equivalent with seclusion and spiritual discipline, and command the deputy (mūkal) to destroy him. The name of the deputy is Alīyākāʾīl. He appears in a dream or while awake, and reciting it together with the name of the Omnipotent (Jabbār) has great efficacy for destruction.

**The Jinn Who Approaches Its Image Burns:**

And the jinn also flee from this name, whose composition is as follows: At the time of writing, wait until it comes to your memorization, which should be related to the first letters of this name. And engrave the image of this name on a lead tablet, and also write the deputy's name around it, and give it to the one who deals with jinn—he will not approach it, and if he approaches he will burn. And if with cold copper you add the name of some disease inside this name along with someone's name, that disease will befall that person. Its form is this:
