And to Him belongs greatness and the honorable Lord whose essence endures. **"Indeed, you are to die, and indeed they are to die."** Before its importance, all things are destroyed. Its number is 707. This is an odd deficient number. To it point 721 and 170, names of al-Mu'ahhal and Badī', because truly the non-existence of every thing is from Him, just as Allah the Exalted says: **"Originator of the heavens and the earth."** He is the Creator of the heavens and the earth. This number points to the names of al-Fātiha, the Most High, and the names of majesty. The name of the word "creation" is Malīk. When you look at its letters literally, when you consider it from the directional aspect, its number is 152—this is an odd excessive number. To it belong 121 and 135. This is the number of "Sattār, Kun fayakūn." The names of its letters number 308, pointing to the name of Allah the Exalted, al-Razzāq, because the establishment of every thing is through Him, from whom its true existence is.

Know that Ḥayy and Qayyūm are very great names. They are perfect remembrance for presence. These two names are the mention of the pure Islamic religion and the form of all angels. Whoever, in the first hour of Friday, sits facing the qiblah and copies these two and keeps them with him, Allah the Exalted will revive his heart. If he is unknown, his name will become famous. If he is poor, he will become wealthy. If his concord is 189—fourth—make it compound and write it and keep it with him, then he will have a strange unveiling. Its form is this:

His Eminence al-Kattānī—may Allah be pleased with him—says: I saw the Noble Prophet ﷺ in a dream. I said: "Pray to Allah the Exalted for me that my heart may not die on the day when all hearts will die." Then the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Recite this supplication every day: **'Yā Ḥayy yā Qayyūm, bika astaghīth, lā ilāha illā ant'**—O Living, O Sustainer, I seek help from You; there is no deity except You." Know that whoever writes the name Ḥayy in a square and places it in the first mat in the place of honor of the sun, and keeps it with him, Allah the Exalted will revive his heart and expand his sustenance for him, and protection will be over his family, his life, and his wealth. Whatever he writes it upon will be preserved. And whatever matters he desires to accomplish, he will excel above them all, the description of which is impossible—briefly, this is the Greatest Name.
