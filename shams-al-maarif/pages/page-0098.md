# Page 98 (English translation)
By reciting these [names] continuously day and night, you will progress to the stations of "Exalted upon all things" (Aliyya Haal Allah).

This is the form of the circle:

**Description of the Circle / The Need Will Be Fulfilled:**

It is narrated from Abu Hurairah, may Allah be pleased with him, that when Jesus, peace be upon him, raised the dead, he would perform two units of prayer and then, in prostration, recite this supplication. He would recite these names: Yā Qadīm, Yā Dā'im, Yā Ahad, Yā Wāhid, Yā Samad. Muqatil ibn Sulayman said: "I sought these names by which Jesus, peace be upon him, raised the dead for forty years, until I found them with a learned man." These are the same names that have been mentioned. He says: "Whoever recites them one hundred times in the morning prayer, then makes any supplication, it will be accepted. Whoever intends to destroy a tyrant should recite these names while seated in the same place after the morning prayer, without speaking to anyone:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ يَا قَدِيْمُ يَا دَائِمُ يَا فَرْدُ يَا وَتْرُ يَا أَحَدُ يَا صَمَدُ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّوْمُ يَا كَرِيْمُ يَا رَحِيْمُ يَا سَنَدُ مَنْ لَا سَنَدَ لَهُ يَا مَنْ إِلَيْهِ الْمُسْتَنَدُ يَا مَنْ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ

After reciting one hundred times, whatever need you ask from Allah the Exalted will be fulfilled. Especially if you curse a tyrant, it will have immediate effect. If you intend to make a talisman from these names, make them in the shape of a circle on a white shirt, write these names inside it, perfume it with incense, and keep it with you—your desire will be fulfilled. Allah the Exalted knows best. The supplication of the circle is:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ بِحَقِّ إِسْمَاعِيْلَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ حِيْنَ صَعِدَ عَرْشَكَ وَبِحَقِّ اسْمِكَ اللَّه اللَّه اللَّه أَنْ تَسْتَجِيْبَ لِي مَلَائِكَتِكَ الْمَلِكِ كَسْفَيَائِيْلُ وَدَرْدَيَائِيْلُ وَسمخَائِيْلُ وَدَرْدَيَائِيْلُ وَسَمْكَائِيْلُ وَطَهْرَيَائِيْلُ وَكَرْمَالِيْلُ أَجِيْبُوا أَيُّهَا الْمَلَائِكَةُ الْكِرَامُ وَالْأَرْوَاحُ الْمُطَهَّرَةُ الْمُخْتَصِيْنَ لِلَّهِ بِالْوَحْدَانِيَّةِ بِحَقِّ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ الْعَزِيْزِ الْمُقَدَّسِ الَّذِيْ فَضَّلَهُ عَلَى جَمِيْعِ الْأَسْمَاءِ وَكُلُّهَا عَزِيْزُهَا وَجَلِيْلُهَا
