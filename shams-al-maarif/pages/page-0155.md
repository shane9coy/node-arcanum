# Page 155 (English translation)
The Supreme Talisman for the Cure of Illness:
It benefits every disease of the body. Write it on some paper, mix it in water, and make the patient drink it. If you write it on a plate of sugar, erase it with rose water, and the sick person applies it to his body, Allah the Exalted will grant him healing. This is the impression of the talisman.

The Working for the Release of Prisoners:
The prisoner should take clean earth and keep it with him. Every Friday during the first hour, he should spread this earth and pray two rak'ahs. He should then take this earth and keep it with him. He will be released quickly. This has been tested many times and found to be correct. This number is like the triangle of harmony.

The Elixir for the Fulfillment of Desires:
Some masters have said: If someone has a need that is not being fulfilled, he should stand facing the qiblah in a mosque and supplicate to Allah the Exalted, saying these words:
اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّیْ قَصَدْتُ بِجَنَابِكَ وَ قُلْتُ بِبَابِ جَنَابِكَ النَّجَاتَ وَ لَكَ سَأَلْتُ وَ بِمُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ سَلَّمَ وَ آلِهِ اِلَيْكَ تَوَسَّلْتُ بِأَنْبِيَائِكَ وَ اَصْفِيَائِكَ اَسْتَشْعَرْتُ فَاقْضِ اَللّٰهُمَّ حَاجَتِيْ وَ نَفِّسْ كُرْبَتِیْ
"O Allah, I have intended Your Court. I stand at the gate of Your Court seeking salvation. I have asked only You. I have taken Muhammad, peace be upon him and his family, as my intercessor. Through Your prophets and Your chosen ones I have sought intercession. O Allah, fulfill my need and relieve my distress."
Then mention the name of his need. After that, pray two rak'ahs. In each rak'ah, recite Surah al-Fatihah, al-Kafirun, al-Ikhlas, and al-Mu'awwidhatayn. In the final prostration, say these words:
وَ اَیُّوْبَ اِذْ نَادٰی رَبَّهٗ اَنِّیْ مَسَّنِیَ الضُّرُّ وَ اَنْتَ اَرْحَمُ الرّٰحِمِیْنَ فَاسْتَجَبْنَا لَهٗ فَكَشَفْنَا مَا بِهٖ مِنْ ضُرٍّ وَّ اٰتَيْنٰهُ اَهْلَهٗ وَ مِثْلَهُمْ مَّعَهُمْ رَحْمَةً مِّنْ عِنْدِنَا وَ ذِكْرٰی لِلْعٰبِدِیْنَ
"And Ayyub, when he called upon his Lord, saying: 'Affliction has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.' So We answered him and removed what was with him of affliction, and We gave him his family and the like of them along with them, as mercy from Us and a reminder for the worshippers."
Then raise his head from prostration, and standing facing the qiblah, recite this supplication:
اَللّٰهُمَّ عَلِمْتَ الْعَجْزَ عَنِ الْبَدَلِ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنَّ الْعَرَبَ وَالْعَجَمَ اِذَا اسْتَجَارَ بِهَا مُسْتَجِيْرٌ اَجَارُوهُ وَ اَنْتَ اِلٰهَ الْعَرَبِ وَالْعَجَمِ وَقَدِ اسْتَجَرْتُ بِكَ فَاَجِرْنِیْ یَا اَرْحَمَ الرّٰحِمِیْنَ فَاِنِّیْ حَالَتُ وَاغْلَبْ لِيَ الْإِجَابَةُ
