# Page 722 (English translation)
And do they not reflect in their hearts that Allah, the Exalted, did not create the heavens and the earth and what lies between them except with truth?

And the worship of manifestations outwardly is this inwardly:

"Do they not look at the camels, how they were created?"

And since Allah, the Exalted, has joined the people of the inner reality with the theophanies, He has also joined for them the outward proximity, and this vision is not only in acts of worship, but for them the secrets of inward proximity are also joined. And this is one of the secrets of outward and inward belonging exclusively to the people of sincere devotion, as He has said:

"Alif, Lam, Mim. That is the Book—there is no doubt in it... the successful ones..." up to

There are elevated theophanies upon those who believe in the unseen with sincerity:

Know that this group is those whose Lord, the Exalted, has praised with faith in the unseen. These are the people of sincere devotion in truth, and upon them come the theophanies, and this unseen is the subtlest thing in the worlds of governance, and within this unseen are those transcendent causes about which the messengers gave tidings. Regarding faith in the unseen, this is the rule: nothing can be perceived by the senses except through something subtler than it, and nothing can be perceived with something less subtle than it. And Allah, the Exalted, created the intellects and gave them the rank of special sincerity. He made the flow of His subtle aspects for the creatures of the worlds of divine mysteries through this luminous special distinction. Then after this He spoke to it, and within it are two powers: one is the power of hearing, and the other is the power of response, with the intellect's obedience. And these are its power of hyperbole and its power of memory.

To obtain luminous theophanies, one must recite these names with Surah al-Ikhlas:

The one who remembers these names should, with piety and humility and silence, in seclusion, with fasting and prayer, recite both names, and also recite Surah al-Ikhlas one thousand times daily, and recite the four names with concentration of heart:

"He is the First and the Last and the Manifest and the Hidden"

until the luminous theophanies manifest upon him and the assigned jinn incline toward him.

He will lift the veil of the unseen and the desire will be obtained:

This will lift the veil of the unseen, and the one making the request will speak with you for the purpose of reassurance when you recite these names in seclusion. This will be granted to you for seven weeks. When you need to know some subtle secret, write both diagrams and write the name of the aforementioned assigned one around them, and recite the name according to its numerical value. Then pray for the revelation of that secret, and tell no one. The desire will be obtained. The form of both diagrams is as follows:
