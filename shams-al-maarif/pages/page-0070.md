[Heading: Chapter on the Bodies of the Celestial Bodies]

Know that the body of the Sun is one hundred sixty times greater than the Earth, and the body of the Moon is thirty-nine times greater. Likewise for Venus and Mercury. The body of Jupiter is eighty-two degrees greater than the Earth. The body of Saturn is ninety-seven degrees greater. Some of our scholars have said that the body of the Sun extends twelve degrees from the front and the same amount from behind. The body of the Moon extends twelve degrees from the front and the same amount from behind. The body of Mars extends eight degrees from the front and the same amount from behind. The body of Venus extends seven degrees from the front and the same amount from behind, and the body of Mercury is also of similar magnitude.

[Heading: Chapter on the Stars Completing the Circuit of the Seven Heavens]

Know that the Moon completes the circuit of the sky in twenty-nine days and one-third of a day. Mercury in eighty-eight days, Venus in two hundred twenty-four days and one-quarter of a day, and the Sun completes the celestial circuit in three hundred sixty-five days. Mars in six hundred eighty-seven days, Jupiter in eleven years, and Saturn completes it in twenty-nine years.

[Heading: Chapter on the Positions in the Signs]

Know that the Moon's position in each sign is two days and three nights. Mercury's position in each sign is fifteen days, Venus's position in each sign is twenty-five days, Jupiter's position in each sign is one year, and Saturn's position in each sign is thirty months.

[Heading: Chapter on the Dignities of the Celestial Bodies]

Know that the Moon's exaltation is in Taurus, Mercury's exaltation is in Virgo, Venus's exaltation is in Pisces, the Sun's exaltation is in Aries, Mars's exaltation is in Capricorn, Jupiter's exaltation is in Cancer, and Saturn's exaltation is in Libra. The Pleiad (Thurayyā) is the lamp of the world in the heavens, because no other place in the heavens has stars gathered together more than in it. For this reason, the astrologers gave it the position of the jewel, and they also gave it the name Thurayyā.
