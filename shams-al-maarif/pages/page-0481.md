# Page 481 (English translation)
[481]

**For Fame**

If one desires outward fame, let him write on white silk in the first hour of Friday [unclear] gathered, and write the Name "Azim" [Great] four times around it, give incense of aloes and sandal, and keep it with oneself. His name will become famous. People from distant places will come to meet him.

For this purpose, let him write with musk and saffron dissolved in rose water on his/her first wing [arm], and write this verse around the figure:

*Asha Allah an yaj'ala bainakum wa baina alladhina 'adaitum minhum mawaddatan wa wallahu qadirun wa wallahu ghafurun rahimun*

"May Allah Most High place affection between you and your enemies, and He will make your enemies become friends."

And this verse *Wa-uthkuru ni'mat allahi 'alaykum*, and bind it on your right arm. The Lord Most High will make his enemy into a friend. The name of this letter is evident. Pray for it in the aforementioned manner.

**The Letter 'Ayn:**

The letter of speech 'Ayn has a watery constitution. It is a source of knowledge and wisdom.

Properties and Benefits: Write the letter Ayn seventy times on paper in the first hour of Friday. Write around it those names of Allah which begin with 'Ayn. They are: 'Alim, 'Aziz, 'Alam, 'Ali, 'Azim, 'Afuw, 'Adl.

**Knowledge and Wisdom Will Be Obtained:**

Look at this talisman four times daily. Allah Most High will grant him wisdom and the apparent and hidden sciences. Whatever names a man mentions, waves of wisdom will flow from his heart to his tongue. The secrets and rarities of knowledge and wisdom will become manifest in his speech.

**For Increasing a Woman's Beauty:**

If one fills the number of the letter 'Ayn in the figure of Mars, and writes around this figure on white silk in green silk with musk and saffron dissolved in rose water, and gives incense of aloes, and binds it on the woman's arm, she will become extremely beautiful and lovely. Everyone who sees her will be enchanted by her. Its form is this, and pray with the aforementioned names.

**The Letter Ghayn:**

Ghayn is the letter of speech 'Nun. It is at the highest degree of fiery constitution. Its names are: Ghani, Ghaffar, Ghafur, Ghadhib. This letter Ghayn indicates the highest good fortune.

Properties and Benefits: If one writes it twelve times on a tin tablet and keeps it, Allah Most High will grant sustenance from a place where one would not even suspect. One will live a life of prosperity. Creation will be obedient to his command. This letter is written only in Arabic script. The scholars know that it is related to the knowledge of the unseen. They give this proof: this word is derived from *Yu'minuna bil-ghayb* [They believe in the unseen]. This supports it.

**Abundant Wealth Will Be Obtained:**

If one writes the figure of its number in twelve hours of day and night, and writes around it those names which begin with it: Ghani, Ghaffar, Ghafur, and so on. Then give incense of aloes and keep it with oneself. If he is poor, he will become wealthy once, and then will never be in need of anyone.
