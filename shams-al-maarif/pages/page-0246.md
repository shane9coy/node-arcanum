Chapter: The Proven Supplication for Times of Distress

Whoever is faced with some affliction should, on the night of Friday, after midnight, perform a complete ritual bath, sit in seclusion, and not speak to anyone until after the Isha prayer. In the final prostration of the Witr prayer, let him say "O Allah, O Lord, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Creator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of majesty and honor" one hundred times, then make his request—it will be fulfilled.

The Proven Supplication for Fulfilling Needs

Imam Tirmidhi narrates from Anas ibn Malik that the Noble Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: Whoever has a need from Allah, Most High, or from any servant, let him perform two units of prayer and then ask with this supplication.

There is no god but Allah, the Clement, the Generous. There is no god but Allah, Lord of the Mighty Throne. There is no god but Allah—glorious is He—Lord of the heavens and the earth and Lord of the Noble Throne. O Allah, I ask You for the things that necessitate Your mercy, the certainties of Your forgiveness, and the gain from every good deed, and for safety from every sin. Do not leave for me any sin except that You forgive it, nor any worry or grief except that You dispel it, nor any distress except that You relieve it, nor any need except that You fulfill it by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

This supplication is of great benefit for the fulfillment of needs. There is no god but Allah; He is the Clement, the Generous. There is no god but Allah; He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne. There is no god but Allah; glory be to His sacred essence; He is the Lord of the heavens, the earth, and the Noble Throne. O Allah, I ask You for the means of Your mercy, the certainties of Your forgiveness, and I ask for gain from every good deed, and I desire safety from every sin. Do not leave any sin for me but that You forgive it, nor any grief and worry but that You dispel it, and O Most Merciful of the merciful, fulfill every need of mine with Your mercy.

Perform two units of prayer, then with sincere intention glorify Allah the Most High, recite istighfar (seeking forgiveness), send blessings and peace upon the noble essence of the Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and then recite this supplication.

O Allah, O Gatherer of the scattered, O Bringer forth of vegetation, O Reviver of the decayed bones, O Answerer of supplications, O Fulfiller of needs, O Reliever of distress from above seven heavens, O Opener of the treasures of generosity, O Lord of the necessities of all worlds—Your hearing hears all voices, Your knowledge encompasses all things, and I ask You, O Allah, by Your power over all things and by Your self-sufficiency from all creation and by Your praise and Your glory, that You graciously grant my need.

O Allah, O Gatherer of the scattered, O Grower of vegetation, O Reviver of the decayed bones, O Acceptor of supplications, O Fulfiller of needs, O Reliever of distress from above seven heavens, O Opener of the treasures of generosity, O Lord of the necessities of all worlds—Your hearing hears all voices, Your knowledge encompasses all things.
