# Page 262 (English translation)
The Number of the Beautiful Names

First I will mention them in their collective form, then I will explain them individually. I say that Imam Tirmidhi has narrated from Abu Hurairah رضي الله عنه that the Noble Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "Allah the Exalted has ninety-nine names. Whoever enumerates them shall enter Paradise." And they are these:

Huwa Allahu alladhi la ilaha illa huwa al-Rahmanu al-Rahimu al-Malku al-Quddusu al-Salamu al-Mu'minu al-Muhayminu al-'Azizu al-Jabbaru al-Mutakabbiru al-Khaliiqu al-Bari'u al-Musawwiru al-Ghafaru al-Qahharu al-Wahhabu al-Razzaku al-Fattahu al-'Alimu al-Qabidu al-Basitu al-Khafidu al-Rafi'u al-Mu'izzu al-Mudhillu al-Sami'u al-Basiru al-Hakmu al-'Adlu al-Latifu al-Khabiru al-Halimu al-'Azimu al-Ghafuru al-Shakuru al-'Aliyyu al-Kabiru al-Hafizu al-Muqittu al-Hasibu al-Jalilu al-Karimu al-Raqibu al-Mujibu al-Wasi'u al-Hakimu al-Wadudu al-Majidu al-Ba'ithu al-Shahidu al-Haqqu al-Wakilu al-Qawiyyu al-Matinu al-Waliyyu al-Hamidu al-Muhsi al-Mubdi'u al-Mu'idhu al-Muhyi al-Mumitu al-Hayyu al-Qayyumu al-Wajidu al-Majidu al-Wahidu al-Ahadu al-Fardu al-Samadu al-Qadiru al-Muqaddiru al-Muqaddimu al-Mu'akhkhiru al-Awwalu al-Akhiru al-Zahiru al-Batinu al-Waliyyu al-Muta'alu al-Barru al-Tawwabu al-Muntaqimu al-'Afuwwu al-Ra'ufu Maliku al-Mulki Dhu al-Jalali wal-Ikram al-Muqsitu al-Jami'u al-Ghaniyyu al-Mughni al-Mani'u al-Darru al-Nafi'u al-Nuru al-Hadi al-Badi'u al-Baqiyyu al-Warithu al-Rashidu al-Saburu

These are the ninety-nine names; whoever memorizes them shall enter Paradise. The Noble Prophet Muhammad ﷺ enumerated them and specified them with remembrance, because these names contain attributes that are of the rank of Paradise. Therefore He said: "Whoever enumerated them shall enter Paradise." The Noble Messenger of Allah ﷺ has one hundred blessed names, which are not merely masculine, because they are the means to the degrees of Paradise. There is none among creation besides the presence of the Noble Messenger ﷺ who knows them with certainty.

And it is not known to me that any person entered the realm of struggle (i.e., the spiritual combat) and emerged from it with disqualification, but rather he burned and died from the fire of regret. Then the one who wished to go there had his humiliation erased. Regarding this, the poet said:

Let the one whose life was wasted weep upon himself,
And he has no share or portion in it from them.

So upon the self let tears flow, whose owner whose lifetime was wasted, for he obtained no portion and no arrow from it. Thus there is extreme regret and sorrow upon the one who, in his negligence and lethargy, fell behind from the Friend (i.e., Allah) and from his lords' companions. The loss became manifest with Allah the Exalted, and indeed his name was erased from among the intimate ones. Understand this and you will obtain guidance.

Chapter: The Explanation of "La ilaha illa Huwa" — Its Properties and Benefits

If you say: Which is the best kind of speech among these names? The answer is that the Noble Messenger of Allah ﷺ did not count it among the names; rather this is a mode of supplication and prayer. So these names were not brought independently; rather "Huwa Allahu alladhi la ilaha illa huwa" is the beginning, and it is one name that indicates one Pure Divine Essence. And the name "Huwa Samad" is the most specific of these names, because the reality is attributed to it alone, because reason cannot comprehend anything beyond it.
