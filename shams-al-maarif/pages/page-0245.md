Chapter: The Method of the Effect of the Name and Action

Sitting in an elevated place, empty your heart of physical qualities, and the effect of the Name will manifest upon you. That delight will be yours, which will leave you astounded and amazed. You will understand that you too are a part of the Supreme Divine, and spiritual love will manifest upon you. Such a light will become apparent within your own self that you will be unable to bear it, and your mind will become helpless and your comprehension insufficient. From this station you will experience descent. Then you should strive until you reach there, until affection for that station develops within you and no descent occurs.

Chapter: The Operation of Resolving Difficulties

It is narrated from Muqātil ibn Sulaymān that whenever a person faces a difficult matter, it is obligatory upon him to perform ablution and enter the furthest part of his house, then perform two units of prayer with complete solitude. After the salutation, recite the Ṣalāt upon the Noble One, then recite this supplication:

Allāhumma innaka malīkun muqaddirun wa annaka ʿalā mā shāʾa qadīrun. Allāhumma in kānat dhunūbī salafat wa ikhtalafa wajhī wa ʿaẓẓimat makhaṭāʾitī wa ḥālat baynī wa bayna qaḍāʾi ḥājatī fa innī asʾaluka bi jalāli wajhika wa ʿaẓīmi ʿafwika wa atawajjahu ilayka bi nabiyyika Muhammadin ṣallā allāhu ʿalayhi wa sallama an taghfira lī wa tarḥamanī wa tufarrija ʿannī [A circle is hand-drawn around the word "ʿannī"] call out loudly: Muhammadum yā Aḥmadu yā Abā al-Qāsimi innī atawassalu wa atawajjahu bika ilā allāhi taʿālā liyaghfira lī wa yarḥamanī wa yaqḍiya ḥājatī wa yuḥawwij al-jībi wa yufarrija karbī wa hammī wa ghamī.

O God, truly You are the Sovereign, the Omnipotent, and truly You have power over whatever You will. O God, though my sins have passed beyond bounds and my attention has strayed, my sins are great which have come between me and the fulfillment of my need. I ask You by the majesty of Your Face and Your great pardon, and I direct myself toward You through Your Prophet Muhammad, upon him be peace, that You forgive me, have mercy upon me, and relieve my distress. O Muhammad, O Aḥmad, O Father of Qāsim, upon him be peace, I seek intercession through you to God the Exalted that He forgive me, have mercy upon me, fulfill my need and all needs, and remove our distress, sorrow, and grief.

If tears come at this time, it is a sign that the supplication has been accepted—the prayer will be answered. Otherwise, perform the operation two or three times, and the supplication will be accepted.

The Tested Supplication of Imam Muqātil

Another tested supplication is narrated from Ḥaḍrat Muqātil, through which Ḥaḍrat ʿĪsā, upon him be peace, would revive the dead. When you wish to recite this supplication, after the dawn prayer, in that same place, recite it one hundred times:

Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīmi wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhi al-ʿAliyyi al-ʿAẓīmi Allāhumma innī asʾaluka yā qadīmu yā dāʾimu yā fardu yā aḥadu yā witru yā ṣamadu yā ḥayyu yā qayyūmu yā dhā al-jalāli wa al-ikrāmi an tafʿala [Text cut off at bottom of page]
