# Page 463 (English translation)
461

There are 801 muwakkils in each row. The prayer of the Last Name is:
"O Last One, You are the one who brings out the lifespans of every creature to its appointed time, and You are the one who has delayed from the heart of every seeker of You what he has concealed of Your wrath and Your punishment, and You have saved by Your encompassing light at the end of his lifespan and the fear of his sin. I ask You by the subtleties of unified knowledge in the mystery of Your oneness, and by the subtle essences of stored knowledge in Your authority, that You make me knowledgeable of the outcome of my affair, and grant me generous, encompassing generosity concerning the subtleties of the realities of my secret and my public [affairs], O Lord of the worlds."

The Manifest Name is a Great Name. The name of its muwakkil is Siyāyīl. Under it are four rulers. Each ruler is the chief of 1106 rows, and there are 1106 muwakkils in each row. If a muwakkil descends upon the one performing [the ritual], the prayer of this Name is:
"O Manifest One, You are the one who made the manifest things appear and announced the hidden things, and You know more than them. You spread forth all existing things and know what is concealed. You gathered all beings for the preservation of Your protected secret. I ask You by the marvel of Your creation and the finest subtleties of Your compassion and mercy, that You make me manifest in every affair, and ordain for me from Your decisive command a matter, and strengthen me by Your power, and bring me forth from my distress as ease. Indeed, You are Most Compassionate, Most Merciful."

The name of the muwakkil of the Hidden Name is Hayāyīl. It reacts according to four principles. Each principle is the chief of 62 rows, and there are 62 muwakkils in each row. The prayer of the Hidden Name is:
"O Hidden One, You are the one who concealed the secret of prophethoods within the authorities, and You revealed among them the secret of unveilings and the realities of descents in the hearts of the masters of private chambers, through the concealed things of inner thoughts and the innermost insights of the sacred rites. That You grant me complete insight and general unveiling concerning the hidden mystery of his affair, and that You assign to me by Your steadfast power the most eminent thing from the unseen of all hidden things—a protected secret. And make me mighty with You and with those who turn toward Him, and a root for their hearts and their secrets—an unrewarded reward. Indeed, You are Allah, who manifests all types of beings by the kāf and nūn."

The Protector Name is a Great Name. In it is one letter of the Greatest Name. The name of its muwakkil is Himāyīl, under whom are four chiefs. Each chief has 47 rows under him, and there are 47 muwakkils in each row. The prayer of the Protector Name is:
"O Protector, You are the one who took charge of the affairs of creation and completed their essences by raising the ego. And You connected every creature to what You created for him of noble gifts. O Allah, I ask You for the greatest authority, the highest wisdom, the purest light, and reaching the Farthest Mosque. And grant me vision of the realities of things through the unveiling of the stations of the prophets and the most pious. Indeed, You are generous in good and blessings."
