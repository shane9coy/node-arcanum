# Page 509 (English translation)
**Detailed Explanation of the Greatest Name:**

The detailed explanation of the Greatest Name is this: when you take the primitive letters, they will be 99, and this is the number of the Beautiful Names, and this is the secret of life. So when the secret of majesty and glory became apparent to you, this also became clear—that there is such a connection between Alif-Lam-Mim and the Sacred Name, and this is the very matter by which the distinction between the essential and the attributed, the subject and the predicate, the prior and the posterior becomes apparent in the manifest, as we have explained the derivation of the numbers of the Beautiful Names from the letters. And this is the reason for which the Sacred Name indicated this number—that is, the numbers of the letters of the Sacred Name are 66, and when these letters are multiplied by the primitives, meaning Alif-Lam-Mim, they become 198. Then when these are divided into two parts, they become half, namely 99, and this is the number of the Beautiful Names which were hidden in the essence of the primitives, meaning Alif-Lam-Mim, and this was the secret regarding the explanation of the Sacred Name.

**Explanation of the Sacred Name of Allah:**

Know that the Sacred Name has four letters. When you remove the repeated letter, three remain: that is, Alif, Lam, and Ha, and these are the principles. When the Sacred Name was multiplied by these, the extraction from the process of doubling [unclear] yields 150, and the Sacred Name has two categories: one is Alif-Lam-Mim, and the other is Alif-Lam-Ha. Then when the aforementioned numbers were divided into two parts, one part became associated with both categories, and these two categories were specified with the number of the Beautiful Names, namely with 99. Another reason is that when you add both sides of this Sacred Name together and divide by the four letters, and multiply the extracted portion by its numbers, the number of the Beautiful Names will become manifest. Another reason is that when you add the symbols and the primitives which are within the circle, and the letters of the Four Names, and the letters of both these names which are outside the circle, the total of the numbers will be the same as that which was summed from the letters of the circle. Know that this Sacred Name is superior to other names for three reasons: first, because it contains the meanings of creation and manifestation, and among the Beautiful Names, these names are synonymous with it: *La ilaha illa huwa* (There is no god but He), *Al-Khaliq* (The Creator), *Al-Bari* (The Maker), *Al-Musawwir* (The Fashioner), *Al-Muid* (The Restorer), and others.

**Second Category: The Description of the Sacred Name:**

The second category of description contains meanings of greatness, domination, might, glory, sovereignty, oneness, and inspiring fear and intimidation and fright, and among the Beautiful Names, these names correspond to these meanings: *Al-Malik al-Wahid al-Qahhar al-Mutakabbir wa al-Jabbar* (The King, The One, The Overwhelming, The Magnificent, and The Compeller), and others.

**Third Category:**

The third category is that this Name contains meanings of grace and mercy, forbearance, inducement, hope and desire, and faith, and among the divine Names, these names correspond to these meanings: *Al-Rahman* (The Merciful), *Al-Salam* (The Peace), *Al-Mumin* (The Believer), *Al-Muhaymin* (The Guardian), *Al-Wahhab* (The Bestower), *Al-Basit* (The Expander), *Al-Halim* (The Forbearing). Then this Sacred Name has four categories, just as it contains four letters. Thus the sum of these letters is also divided into four categories: Names of Majesty, Names of Attributes, and Names of Grace.

**The State of the World is of Three Types:**

The states of the entire world have three categories: the state of execution, the state of creation, and birth, and coming from nonexistence into existence, and these divine Names are specialized with it: the declaration *La ilaha illa huwa al-Hayy al-Qayyum* (There is no god but He, The Living, The Self-Subsisting), and this is the glorification of the divine Names: *Al-Khaliq al-Bari al-Musawwir al-Badi al-Fattah al-Mubdi al-Muid al-Baith al-Warith Malik al-Mulk* (The Creator, The Maker, The Fashioner, The Incomparable, The Opener, The Originator, The Restorer, The Resurrector, The Heir, The King of Sovereignty).
