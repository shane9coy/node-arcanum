**The Third Method:** One method is to extract the name of Awn and its qubbah, and also recite the name that has been derived from the pronoun, and assign them to the task. It will immediately present itself and accomplish the work. This is the method of the sages, and this is the form of derivation from the composite figure: mention the composite figure, take its sides and area, derive the derivation, add the word "Il" at its end, and conjugate it according to the days.

**A Great Benefit:**
That is, establish the client of the derived Awn for the day. When you wish to know the area of the composite figure, subtract 77 from its area. If one remains, know that it is fire; so establish it for this operation, and having derived al-Alawiyyah, establish it upon it, and its day is Sunday. If two remain, it is air; establish it for the operation on Monday. If four remain, this is Barqan. Continue until the seventh day. When this has become clear to you, then whatever work you wish to do, derive the derivation of the client, establish its horse, number, epithet, and the four elements, and speak them aloud. If the composite figure is triangular, speak the middle and area. If it is square, speak the side, middle, and area. In the hexagonal figure, speak without a middle; rather speak the epithet and conjugate. The same applies to subsequent calculations. The more you speak aloud with knowledge, the greater your authority will be. Likewise, if you write the letters of the first line of the composite figure and add the word "Il" after three or four letters, the client will be prepared. In the same way, add the word "Aysh" after three, four, or five letters when summing the lower line; the lower client will be prepared. Derive the names of Awn from the length and width of the composite figure. God is the bestower of success.

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: Determining the Time of the Ascendant]

Divide the number of the composite figure by 12. If one remains, it is Aries; if two remain, it is Taurus. By this measure and standard, likewise, if you wish to derive the hour, divide by 7 and determine the star. Whatever star appears, you should perform the operation in its hour. When you wish to determine the lunar mansions, divide by 28 and begin the operation on the mansion upon which the derived number falls.

**Method for Determining the Temperaments:**
If you wish to determine the temperaments, there are several combinations. The procedure is to weigh the letters and observe which letters are dominant and at what degree and rank. The judgment will be based upon whichever element's letters are dominant. The second combination is that when taking the qubbah in the original operation, divide the total number by 4. If one remains, it is nar (fire). If two remain, it is "air." If three remain, it is "water." If four remain, it is "earth." This is an extremely easy method. Consult the ascendant, the planets, and the days, as I have written previously in the sections. This was my final statement regarding this topic.

[Boxed Heading: Chapter: Determining Incenses]

The description of great incenses is this: divide the number of the ascendant's verses by 2. If one remains, the incense is from animals. If two remain, it is from plants. If three remain, it is from plants. Now to learn its method, sum the four cycles of the composite figure and divide by 4. If 1 remains, it is fire; if 2 remain, it is air; if 3 remain, it is water; if 4 remain, it is earth.
