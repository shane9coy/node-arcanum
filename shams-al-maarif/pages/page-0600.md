# Page 600 (English translation)
**For the expansion of sustenance:**
When this letter is written with the Names of the Exalted One and kept with oneself, the gates of the Provider will open for him.

**For protection from theft:**
When this is written fifty times on a stone with ifṭār and the servant, meaning the client, is told: "O servant, protect this property with this letter," the property will remain safe. And when he wishes to go to his house where the property is, let him write this letter on a stone and place it in that place, and continue reading the da'wah. Then however much property there was, it will begin to double.

**After performing this operation, no other operation is necessary:**
When you have fulfilled the service of this letter, then you will have no need of other operations. And when you wish to nullify talismanic water, at the moment it appears in a little clay, write this letter on a lead tablet or stone slab, and write ifṭār around it, and place it in that water, and that water will disappear.

**For destroying a place:**
If this letter is written on clay and that clay is tied around a rooster's neck in such a way that when the rooster crows, the clay will fall and the place will be destroyed.

**For the protection of treasure:**
[Note: Handwritten marginalia above this heading reads "In the mosque"]
When you wish that no treasure disappear from before you, then write da'wah and ifṭār on its gate, and the treasure will remain established.

**Flowing sand will stop:**
If this letter is written on a lead tablet the size of a palm and thrown into a river of sand, the tablet will float. The service of this letter is majestic, and its client's light appears like the sun. He will make a covenant with you that its da'wah should be read fifty times after every prayer, and similarly ifṭār as well. The name of its client is 'Aṭāʾīl. If he delays in coming, summon him with the letter Nūn and he will appear immediately. Then he will fulfill whatever task you wish to take from him. His ta'wīdh (amulet) is this:

[A rectangular frame contains a central symbol resembling a '#' sign with double horizontal and vertical strokes. To the left and right of this central symbol are three vertical columns, each containing three small crescent-shaped marks opening upwards. Outside the four corners of the box are small clusters of dots.]

Its da'wah is this:
Nūru Allāhumma qalbī wa nuṭqī wa naẓarī wa jawāriḥī wa badanī bi-nūri maʿrifatika alladhī nawwartu bihi ahla l-ṭāʿatika yā munawwira l-shamsi wa l-qamari yā munawwira kulli l-nūri yā hādiyan li-nūri kulli shay'in wa bi-hādhā l-ālati alladhī fa-laqta l-ẓulumāti bi-nūrika asʾaluka an tunawwira lī l-anwāra yā man yujību l-muḍṭarra idhā daʿāhu wa takshifu
