Chapter 16: The Name Qahar, the destruction of the unjust

Whoever recites this name in seclusion and curses an oppressor, he shall be destroyed immediately. Whoever inscribes its square in the honor of Mars and keeps it with himself, no one shall overcome him and he shall remain dominant in courage as well. For seekers and men of spiritual exercise, its keeping is necessary so that their ego may be subdued and protected from desires. Whoever is named Abd al-Qahar, this mention is appropriate for him. Its literal numerical value is 311 and its ordinal value is 358. Its letter-names point to two great divine names of Allah Most High: Fatir and Muqsit, and this is the description of its square:

Chapter 17: The properties and benefits of the Name Wahhab - expansion of sustenance

Whoever persists in its remembrance shall witness great sustenance and his sustenance shall expand. If he inscribes its talisman in the honor of Saturn on paper and keeps it with himself, his soul shall be protected from all heart's desires. Whoever is named Abd al-Wahhab, this mention is appropriate for him. Its literal numerical value is 14 and its ordinal numerical value is 502. Its letter-names point to two great divine names of Allah Most High: Fatir and Muqsit. Whoever mentions this shall receive from Allah Most High whatever he asks, and whoever is named Sulayman, this mention is also very beneficial for him. This is from the secrets and devices and the mystical correspondences; its "witr" (odd number) is in the heart, and its "tashaffu'" (intercession) is in its number. For this reason its ordinal value is 14 and its literal value is 13. It points to the people of the Name of the Absolute because it contains the meanings of secrets and diversity. For this reason it conforms to the One, and the people of taste consider the imperfect odd number. Its 14 letters point to the letter ha', because it signifies the meaning of the cause of expansion of sustenance. Its 121 and 142 point to the divine name of Allah Most High, or toward guidance, or toward peace.
