They love them as they love Allah, and those who have brought faith are most firm in their love for Allah's sake.

For victory and conquest, let these words be recited:

And there is no victory except from Allah, and Allah will give you a mighty victory. Enter upon them through the gate. If you have entered it, then you shall surely be victorious. And upon Allah put your trust, if you are believers.

O Allah, O Self-Sufficient, O Bestower of sufficiency, grant me sufficiency through Your lawful [means] above Your unlawful, so that I shall not fear poverty after it. Guide me, for I am lost. And teach me, for I am ignorant.
