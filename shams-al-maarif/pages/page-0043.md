The self has one letter, and it is Y. The fiery letters are four: M, M, M, T. The airy is only one letter, Q. The earthy are six letters: J, Ḥ, D, R, Kh. Now the dominant among them in heat and dryness are the earthy and fiery. From their divine names this expedition is prepared. You must:

"I swear upon you, O Samsamāʾīl, by the One who created you, fashioned you, and made you a light in his sphere, unless you become my aid—I have subjected you to So-and-so son of So-and-so—and attack me wherever I wish in vengeance against So-and-so. Let him lose his senses, and let him exult in the heat of Mars in the heat of his temperament; let the heat of fire be kindled in him by the pressure of his wealth, and let it grip his belly and his heart, and let his reason be destroyed by it. Let the angels of punishment descend upon him and the scorching wind, and let the fires and headaches and all pains be stirred up, by the right of Mars and what is in it of misfortune and fire, and by the right of your dominant, prevailing, flourishing, vengeful mansion against the oppressive tyrants and the rebellious sinners. Let the spirituality of this tyrannical, arrogant, rebellious giant be sent against him, and let them inhabit his body with the torment of diseases, and let them dominate his inner self with force, anger, and vengeance. For I have sworn upon you by the Powerful, the Glorious, the Pure, the Living, the Eternal, the Light of Lights, the Giver of Secrets, the First, the Last, the outflow of lights, and by the right of fire and flame, and by the right of the red planet, and by the right of God, the One, the Conqueror. Answer, obedient and swift, by the names of the Lord of the Worlds."

**The Mansions of the Moon**

**1- Shartayn:**

Among the mansions of the Moon, the first mansion is Shartayn. Its letter is Alif. Its first letter is a vowel—[it] is airy. Its star is Mars, and its servant is Aḥmar. It is a letter of national excellence. When you strike it in its likeness, it becomes a plane, and that letter (Alif) is supremely high, and you should know that this letter holds great power in the praise of all letters, since it is like the father of the elements.

**Operation and Its Expedition for Love:**

The properties of this [mansion] for love, as we have explained, are these: you should write it in a fortunate hour. If the name of the person for whom you wish to perform the operation falls among its corresponding letters, and you combine it with the operation, it will be very excellent and very powerful in all operations. This is its expedition:

"I swear upon you, O Samsamāʾīl, and upon your servants and your helpers from the upper and lower [realms], and upon all the servants of the letter Alif, unless you hear and obey—you have stirred up So-and-so—and by the right of what I have sworn upon you, and by the right of the letter Alif and what God has placed in it of secrets which none knows except those who know God Most High, and by the right of Abjad and what is in it of properties, unless you answer with obedience as I have called you, O Light! For I am made powerful over you."

The shape of its talisman is as you see; understanding it, you may write it.
