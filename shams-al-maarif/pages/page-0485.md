# Page 485 (English translation)
485

He will come to him and will not leave him for one hour. The form of the square is this:

[4x4 grid containing numbers:
Row 1: 9, 12, 17, 2
Row 2: 16, 19, 10, 13
Row 3: 4, 18, 11, 7
Row 4: 11, 6, 5, 8]

And the prayer is this:
"O Allah, I ask You, O Owner, O King, O Faithful, O Guardian, O Great" — let him mention all those names whose first letter is Meem, and these are 40 names. After these, let him say:
"I ask You by what the letter Meem has called upon from the hidden secrets and the concealed lights, that You subjugate for me the noble angels, for You are over all things capable."

Letter Nun:
Nun is a speaking letter, red in color. It inclines towards moisture. Its officers are like Ha Mim, but they come like 'Ayn. If it is written on the forehead of the afflicted, his grief will be erased.

Properties and benefits: Among its letters, in the world there are three letters which are the secret of the limit of Allah, and they are of the Greatest Name, which are known through Ta Ha and Ya Sin, as Allah said: "And your Lord, so magnify" and "All are in an orbit." If their letters are separated and written, they will be read both from the beginning and the end. Similarly, there is Meem and Nun and Waw. These are also read in this manner, therefore many hidden secrets are concealed within them. The prayer is this:
"O Allah, I ask You, O Light, O Beneficial, by what the letter Nun has called upon" — etc., until the end, read in the aforementioned manner.

Letter Ha:
The letter "Ha" is aerial, fragile, and due to its dryness it is earthy.

Properties and benefits: If at night daily one writes "63" together with "He is Allah, there is no god but He, the Knower of the unseen" etc., and ties it around his neck, no fear will come to him. If its numbers are written in a square and tied around the child's neck, he will remain protected from every disease.

Success in intention:
If this letter is written 41 times on a dead bone and kept with oneself, Allah will make him successful in intentions. The name Bari of Allah is derived from this letter. The prayer is this: "He is Allah, there is no god but He" — with both of these, pray in the aforementioned manner.

Letter Waw:
The letter Waw is dry, slightly moist. All its actions are like those of the letter "Ra." Deduce from this by analogy. The prayer is this:
"O Allah, I ask You, O Giver, O Inheritor, O Protector, O Friend, O Beloved, O Finder, that You subjugate for me Your angels who will execute my command according to what You are pleased with, for You are over all things capable."
