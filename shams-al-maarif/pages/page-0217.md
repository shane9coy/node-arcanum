# Page 217 (English translation)
The Spiritual Practice of "Ya Karim Ya Rahim" and Two Ashrafis Daily

Know that when you intend to perform this practice with this blessed grace, begin it in the week that starts on the first day of Eid.

For seven days, abstain from animal products. Each day, recite both these names—Ya Karim Ya Rahim—as much as possible, especially after each prayer, counting one thousand times.

When these seven days pass and the second week begins, continue doing the same. Fast on the days of the white [i.e., the 13th, 14th, and 15th of the month]. The 15th will fall on a Friday night. On that night, after performing ritual bath and wearing fine clothes, perfume yourself.

Then after Isha prayer at night, sit facing the Qibla. Continue the remembrance of Allah and send blessings upon the Noble Prophet ﷺ one thousand times. Then recite both names Ya Karim Ya Rahim one thousand times. Then conclude with blessings upon the blessed essence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, along with: Allahumma salli ala Muhammadin wa ala alihi wa sahbihi wa sallim al-salata ala rasul Allah ﷺ.

When you complete your recitation, read Ayat al-Kursi and Surah Al-Ikhlas three times and Surah Al-Fatiha once. Be careful not to fall asleep while reciting, otherwise the practice will be ruined. After reciting the blessed durood, also recite this phrase:

Allahumma atihi al-waseelata wal-darajata al-rafi'ata wa ba'thahu al-maqama al-mahmuda alladhi wa'adtahu wa awridna hawḍahu wa saqina min yadihi shurbatan la naz'a ba'daha abadan.

O Allah, grant him the means of intercession and the elevated rank, and raise him to the praised station which You promised him, and bring us to his pool and give us to drink from his hand a drink after which we shall never thirst forever.

And after each prayer, recite the Azimat seven times:

Allahumma inni as'aluka bi-Yuqa'il ya Tuzail ya Shahrayan as'aluka bi-hurmat Kashfeel Burwayhim Nahr al-Nayl Atajil Azasil wa as'aluka bi-hurmat Jibril wa Mika'il wa Israfil wa izzzati al-Nayl wa bi-hurmat Sayyidina Muhammad ﷺ wa bi-haqqi ya Kareemu ya Raheemu an tarzuqani kulla yawmin dinaranusta'inu bihi ala qurbi wal-hajji ila bayti Allah al-haram.

O Allah, I ask You by Yuqa'il, Tuzail, and Shahrayan. I ask You by the sanctity of Kashfeel, Burwayhim, the Nile River, Atajil, and Azasil. And I ask You by the sanctity of Jibreel, Mika'il, Israfil, and the honor of the Nile, and by the sanctity of our master Muhammad ﷺ, and by the right of Ya Kareemu Ya Raheemu, that You grant me one dinar each day to help me with my sustenance and for Hajj to the Sacred House of Allah.

O Allah, I ask You with Yuqail, Tuzail, and Shahrayan. I ask You by the sanctity of Kashfeel, Burwayhim, the Nile of Egypt, Atajil, and Azasil. And I ask You by the sanctity of Jibreel, Mika'il, and Israfil, and by the sanctity of our master Muhammad ﷺ, and by the right of Ya Kareemu Ya Raheemu, that You grant me two ashrafis daily, so that with them I may obtain my sustenance and go for Hajj to the Sacred House of Allah.

When the time of morning prayer comes, after reciting it, join your hands and continue reading durood until drowsiness overtakes you. Then sleep. The servant of both names Ya Karim Ya Rahim will come to you in a dream and ask what you desire—worldly things or the Hereafter. Tell him that you desire the worldly, from which you may gain assistance in the affairs of the Hereafter. Then he will take a pledge from you: to take ritual bath every Friday, to go to the cemetery, and to recite according to their numbers for the helpless and poor after each prayer. If you accept these conditions, that agent will give you two ashrafis. In this way, a lineage of wealth will be produced from you, through which you will become independent. But secrecy is essential in these practices. You will achieve your goal. If you omit mentioning it even one day, it is possible that the daily wealth will cease to come. Give thanks to Allah and continue giving to the poor and needy.
