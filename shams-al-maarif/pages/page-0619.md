Keeping Secrets Hidden:
When you read in this manner, the secrets will be revealed to you on the carpet. They should be kept hidden, and no person should come to you in this seclusion—not even from among humans; rather, all will flee from you. Recite these words frequently as well, and make your living from lawful sustenance. Abandon hot temperament along with moist things, and maintain consistency in prayer and sexual intercourse.

Spiritual Encounters:
The coming and going of spiritual beings will begin, both in sleep and while awake. Some spiritual beings will be luminous, and some will be like lightning, and some will have a light like a mirror, and some will be in the form of green birds whose mouths are like those of humans.

Each Name Has Squares and Triangles:
This was the explanation of spiritual discipline. For the remaining names, there are squares and triangles, and each has separate properties. When you intend to work with any name, on an auspicious day and under a favorable astrological sign, engrave its square upon its mineral. The details are mentioned in the explanation of the name.

Chapter: The Detailed Virtues of the Name "Allah"
This name is unanimously the Supreme Name, and its benefit is that one should recite it with the Beautiful Names. I say that whoever desires to remove the pleasure of veils from his heart, and to pass away from contemplation of the world through annihilation in the reality of tawhid, in accordance with his intellect for which he intended, and in accordance with the command which was desired from him, and to purify his personal essence from divine attributes—to proceed with the power of action on a sufficient day, and near the position of the poor scholar, and the position of the chief of scholars, and let the blessed remain near the truth. Therefore, for this reason, there is lamentation in the night, and they are counted among the sixty early ones. Allah the Exalted says: "Indeed for you in the day is lengthy occupation" That is, for you there is coming and going within the day.

Research on Whether the Name Allah Is Derived or Underived:
Scholars have differed on whether the name Allah is derived or underived. Some say it is underived, and they say that the proof of its being underived is that Arabs derive all other names but this one has not been derived by them, and they say that Arabs write in their books "bismika allahumma" (in Your name, O Allah), and in support of this Allah the Exalted says: "Do you know any other named after Him?"

No One Has Known God Except God:
In support of this, His Holiness Junayd, may God's mercy be upon him, said: I swear by God, no one has known God except God. "Allah the Exalted says: 'So glorify the name of your Lord, the Great'—declare the sanctity of your Lord's name." And I also say: I swear by God, no one has known God in both worlds, rather Allah the One is the reality of this name—not relation. And some people say this name, meaning Allah, is derived, taken from "ulaha" whose original is "walaha," meaning the jinn whose worship was intended, and this is the lexicon to which refuge was sought during the time of ignorance. In summary, the Supreme Name has four letters; when you extract them, the first two letters become "la" from "alif," and the movement of the alif is with the hamza, to whatever it goes, for the necessity of speech.
