# Page 395 (English translation)
And grant me from the vastness of Your glorious verses—indeed His command, when He wills a thing, is to say to it "Be" and it becomes—and by the right of Surah Ya-Sin, O Messengers, 7 times.

My pure essence is the remover of every distress from the servant, the one who erases and pays every debt from the one who gives loans. Blessed is His pure essence, Who placed [His treasures] between the Qaf and the Nun. O Remover of Distress, 7 times, O Fulfiller of Needs, O Acceptor of Supplications, make the custodians of this noble Surah subservient to me, that they may obey me and heed my words. Grant me the sustenance of visiting the blessed grave of my prophet Muhammad, may peace be upon him. Make every difficulty easy for me. Make all Your creation and Your sustenance subservient to me. Place love in the hearts of free and enslaved, young and old, men and women, from among Your servants. Create in their hearts love and everlasting affection. Grant me a great share and a long duration. Open for me the doors of Your mercy. Grant me lawful sustenance. For me, make the helper Nasir and Amin come forth. O Allah, I ask You, O the God of the former and the latter peoples, subjugate all this creation for me with everlasting love, affection, and tenderness—just as You subjugated the sea for Moses, upon him be peace. Soften for me their hearts, their souls, and their limbs—just as You softened iron for David, upon him be peace. They walk by Your command; their foreheads are in Your grasp; their hearts are in my hand of power. Your praise is very great and Your names are very holy. There is no deity except You. By Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, I ask You concerning this noble Surah, that You subjugate my sustenance for me. Soften the hearts of Your servants. Make their souls and bodies subservient to me by the light of Your essence and by the intercession of Your prophets and messengers, upon them be peace, and the angels drawn near—by the right of Ya-Sin. In the Wise Quran: Alif Lam Mim. "That is the Book, no doubt in it, a guidance for the righteous." Alif Lam Mim. "Allah—there is no deity except Him, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of existence." Alif Lam Mim. Sad. "Kaif Ayin Sin." "Ha Mim. Ain Sin Qaf." "Ha Mim. And the Clear Book." "Ha Mim. And the Quran containing the Reminder." "Qaf. By the Noble Quran." "Nun. By the Pen and the inscribed Book." "Ya Sin. And the Wise Quran." "Sad. And the Quran containing the Reminder." "Ta Ha." "Ta Sin Mim. Ta Sin Sin Mim." "Alif Lam Mim. Sad. By the Pen and what they inscribe." And by the right of the Wise Quran, concerning which You said—and You are the Most Truthful—and We send down from the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers. By the right of Your great names, the Great Throne, the Chair, and the angels drawn near, concerning the heavens and the earths and those in them—the walking stars: "Dhat al-Buruj" [The Constellation]. "Mashhud ila al-Sama" [Witnessed to the heavens]. "Wal-Tariq" [And the Morning Star]. "Hafiz" [Guardian]. "Ilā al-Fajr" [Until the Dawn]. "Idha Asra" [When he traveled]. "Wal-Tin" [And the Fig]. "Wal-Zaytun" [And the Olive]. "Lil-Qayyum" [For the Self-Subsisting]. "Bayt al-Haram" [The Sacred House]. "Bayt al-Maqdis" [Jerusalem]. And by the right of my prophets, upon them be peace, the saints, and my righteous servants.

O Lord of the worlds, O Best of helpers, O Acceptor of the supplications of those who ask, O Fulfiller of needs, O Acceptor of prayers, O Heart of majesty, O Remover of calamities, O Forgiver of sins, O Remover of distress. O Allah, by Your generosity, show me Your mercy. Lead me to the holy chamber of Your prophet Muhammad, may peace be upon him. Make Your creation subservient to me. Soften their hearts for me with love and affection. Make sustenance easy. By the right of Surah Ya-Sin and the Wise Quran, make every difficulty easy, remove every distress, and grant me from Your vast treasures—for the command belongs to Him, and when He says to a thing "Be," it becomes—and by the right of Ya-Sin "al-Mursalun" [O Messengers].

Say Ya-Sin 7 times, and [recite] "Wadhrib lahum mathalan ahl al-qaryah" [Strike for them an example—the people of the town] until "Inna ilaykum mursalun" [Indeed We have been sent to you] until "Amantu wa afawwu amri ila Allah" [I have believed and I entrust my affair to Allah] 7 times. Then read durood sharif ten times, then recite this supplication: "Qalu inna tatayarna bikum fa istaghnu" [They said, "We augur ill of you, so suffice yourselves"] until [the end].

"Allahumma inni as'aluka ya ilaha al-awwalin wa al-akhirin an tusakhkhira li jami'a khalqika wa yahawwana alayya kulla 'asirin wa afawwu amri ila Allah" [O Allah, I ask You, O God of the former and the latter peoples, to subjugate all Your creation for me and make every difficulty easy for me, and I entrust my affair to Allah] until 7 times, durood sharif ten times. "Qulna ya naru kuni bardan wa salaman ila al-dalal al-mubin" [We said, "O fire, be coolness and peace upon Abraham"] until [the end]. Then recite this supplication: "Allahumma inni as'aluka ya ilaha al-awwalin wa al-akhirin an tusakhkhira li jami'a khalqika wa yahawwana alayya" [O Allah, I ask You, O God of the former and the latter peoples, to subjugate all Your creation for me and make easy for me]—until [the end].
