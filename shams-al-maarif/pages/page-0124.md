# Page 124 (English translation)
O you spiritual spirits, luminous essences, radiant with spiritual favors, and the divine Nufās devoted to the subtleties of letter manipulation and the fine points of its knowledge, formed and appointed for the subjugation of hearts and spiritual spirits, spiritual of numbers, and the knowers of its hidden secrets—answer, O great spirits and noble angels, Jibrāʾīl and Mīkāʾīl and Isrāfīl and Rūfayāʾīl. Be devoted to the service of whoever calls you, and be my helper and deliver the answer to God and His Messenger. Verily Ishaiya Adonai is my lord: Asbāʾ ūth al-Shaddāy, inspire my desire, fulfill my need, and undertake my service, by the right of God the Opener, the Provider, the Forbearing, the Bestower, the Most High, the Mighty, the Excellent, the Subtle, the Great—KAHYAS HAMAʿSAQA, answer, O Green King. Blessed are you and blessed upon you.

When the fourteenth night arrives, a Mezare spirit will come to you and stand before you. Then you yourself greet it, for it is a great king. Praise him with whatever attributes you wish. Then it will take a covenant from you and will fulfill whatever task you command it. The covenant it takes from you will state that you shall not lie, shall not consume unlawful wealth, shall not use anything unlawful, and shall not disobey God the Most High. If you remain steadfast in these matters, it too will remain steadfast with you. It will appoint for you a servant who will fulfill all your needs and fumigate you with fragrant substances such as Udd Qumārī, Balawī, Jad, Mustaghri, and raw ambergris.

Operation for love and every need

For love: Keep a fast on Thursday, and at the time of breaking the fast read Sūrat al-Fajr fourteen times; also read the same amount at the time of Fajr. Supplicate God the Most High that the heart of the desired one may turn toward you. You will witness a strange vision. For the fulfillment of needs: Keep a fast on Thursday, sit in seclusion, and read this supplication abundantly with presence of heart. Your need will be fulfilled. Its spiritual discipline is nine or seven days. For love, the supplication of Sūrat al-Fātiḥa—since I have written it here—because the sum of its numbers is 9250. Bread with "In the name of God" [written on it]: eat one hundred [pieces] at both the time of eating and drinking, that is, at ifṭār and ṣuḥūr. Keep this transcription before you. You will attain all happiness. By the blessing of Sūrat al-Fātiḥa, spiritual beings will serve you.
