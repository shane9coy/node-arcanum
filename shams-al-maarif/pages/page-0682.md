# Page 682 (English translation)
680

Its dhikr is as follows:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Just One, You have acted justly in giving precedence to existing beings, and You have judged with truth. Or rather, You have caused the decrees to pass away in the created things, and You have placed everything in its proper places in the best arrangement. And the designation of attributes—the Names preceded [the creation] by virtue of the beautiful order of the constituent parts placed for the decrees and the subservient dominions. And You have placed the earth and what is in it of minerals, gems, plants, and all that is in the elemental sublunar worlds, and what is in the restraining seas of various kinds and species of created beings. I ask You, O Allah, by the Knowing and the Teacher, to revive my heart and reveal to me the secrets of the celestial arrangements, and do not grant me success except for every deed that brings me closer to You through justice and righteousness, and subjugate for me the servant of this Name, who fulfills my need, O Allah, O Wise, O Just, O Subtle, O Aware.
Whoever persists in the dhikr of this Name will observe the marvels of the divine craftsmanship.

[Section: The Virtues of the Name "Latif"]

"Latif" is He who knows the subtleties of all things, and this perfection is not attained by anyone in all the world except Almighty God. It is said that in actions, Almighty God is the Possessor of Power over the delicate intricacies within things. His subtlety is beyond all limits.
Exalted God, after creating existing beings, cast the light of these Names upon His special believing servants, because the divine grace of "Latif" is exclusive to the servants who are attributed to Him.
The spiritual retreat of this Name brings the reciter to divine grace and grants him proximity to God. When the spiritual traveler performs the dhikr of this Name, he will see that psychic dangers and desires have been eliminated behind him, and his recitation is with its numerical value, which is 16,641 times. The angel of the unseen imagination descends upon him, and this angel takes permission from Almighty God in this manner: "O Divine, Your servant has called upon me and desires the fulfillment of his need." But in waking vision, he descends upon him according to his capacity and brings him aid.
The reciter's affairs will open and expansion will be obtained:
This Name rules over the first period and the realms of Saturn, and for every need, good or ill, you will recite it, and it will work for you. For one whose affairs have become blocked, recite it with its number, and expansion will come to him.
The account of Muhammad ibn Mansur: When the father of Muhammad ibn Hanifiyyah passed away, he came to me and desired to learn the Divine Names. Through divine revelation, I came to know that he would be crucified. When I saw this, my self was not inclined to teach him. Having performed the istikhara [divine guidance prayer], I ordered him to recite this Name 90,000 times.
