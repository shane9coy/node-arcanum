Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Basir" (All-Seeing)

Basir is He from whom nothing remains hidden, not even a single particle, and through whom every knot of matters and so forth is removed; He is pure and holy. Just as forms are imprinted upon the human eye, they are also imprinted within His essence. The human eye is constrained and limited. It does not observe the hidden realities and secrets. It does not see the spirits, souls, or dangerous thoughts. Within it, vision is kept only for two purposes: one, that it may witness divine attention, and the second, that it may recognize this: that it is a divine mirror. Then it should be amazed at its own movements and stillness. One should hold the belief that among things there are analogies through indication, rather, through their inscriptions, because divine attributes do not change. And the One whose authority is absolute, God is the Unique, the Sustaining.

The reciter of this name becomes balanced and tranquil:

The seclusion of this name makes the one of seclusion balanced, with connected movements and stillness, to the extent that this person cannot make any movement outside of balance. The reciter of this name perceives the power of observation within his eye and tastes the sweetness of faith in spiritual contemplation. It is binding upon him that he protect himself from his dangerous thoughts in both the outward and inward.

The eye of the heart of the reciter of this name becomes illuminated and sees the realities of the world:

And God Almighty opens the eye of the heart of the reciter of this name, to the extent that he sees the realities of things, and the noble, powerful Guardian in your arm becomes present for him and fulfills the need. Whoever enters seclusion and recites the remembrance after each prayer according to its numbers, God will protect him from every evil and open his eye of spiritual sight, and whatever work he intends, success will be his and the work will be completed.

When you write this name in a vessel with musk and saffron, and write the name of its Guardian around it, and dissolve rose-water and pure stone and camphor in it, and apply it to the eye of one who has eye pain, he will find relief.

And whoever, on a moonlit night, stands facing the moon and recites Surah Al-Fatiha seven times, and recites this name according to its numerical value, then salutes the moon and keeps his gaze upon it and recites this supplication, all his desires will be fulfilled:

O God, I indeed ask You by the right of Your name, O All-Seeing, that You have shown me and given me health by the right of Your greatest name, O God, O All-Seeing.

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the All-Seeing through the subtleties of the essence of corporeal existents, like the sight of the essences through the hidden aspects of imagined existents. Thus You see the details of accidents and existents within possible existents.
