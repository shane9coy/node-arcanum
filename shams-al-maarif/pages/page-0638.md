Chapter: On the Virtues of the Name "Quddus" (Holy)

Know that the meaning of Quddus is "purified from defects," and it describes His Essence with perfection and sanctity. For the servant, the meaning of Quddus is purity and cleanliness, and it is also applied to purified things in these same meanings, such as Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem).

When Allah the Most High created the angels who bear the Throne, He appointed for them a particular dhikr. Similarly, for the angels who circle the Chair (Throne), He appointed a particular dhikr, and for the angels who are assigned to the Pen and the Tablet, He also appointed a particular dhikr. A dhikr was appointed for every angel.

The dhikr of all the angels of the heavens is Quddus (Holy):

Thus, the dhikr of the angels of the seven heavens and the Highest Heavens is Subbuh (Glorified), and the dhikr of those near the Chair (Throne) is Subbuh Quddus (Glorified, Holy), and the dhikr of those of the Tablet is Subbuh Quddus Rabb al-Mala'ikah wa al-Ruh (Glorified, Holy, Lord of the Angels and the Spirit). The meanings and subtleties of the name Quddus operate in the realms of Jabrut (the world of divine attributes). They call Quddus that elevated rank of the Divine Essence whose lights transcend perception.

The knowledge of the higher world will be attained:

And the property of this name is that when it corresponds with the numerical value of someone's name and they perform its dhikr, or recite it combined with the name Sami' (the Hearing One), and they persist in it, the unveiling of the higher world will be attained by them. And if they persist upon Subbuh Quddus Rabb al-Mala'ikah wa al-Ruh, the unveiling of the malakuti (the world of divine dominion) and Jabrut (the world of divine attributes) will be attained by them. This is the dhikr of those who bear the Throne. They recite it once and once they recite La hawla wa la quwwata illa billa'h (There is no power nor strength except through Allah). The angels recite only the name of sanctity, and it is the dhikr of the angelic chiefs.

Notice: The station of the name Quddus is Sidrat al-Muntaha (the Lote Tree of the Extremity)

Know that the station of the Holy Spirit is in Sidrat al-Muntaha, and it manifests the right side of revelation among the pure spirits for the creation of faith, and it inspires the friends of Allah, and it has five ranks: head, intellect, spirit, soul, and heart, because the human world in its original constitution is pure from everything other than tawhid (divine oneness). Therefore, Allah the Most High has manifested His secret in the very essence of proximity, and manifested His intellect with the lights of witnessing, and manifested His spirit with the primordial creative lights, and manifested His soul with the realities of sacred numbers, and manifested His heart with the light of faith, because these are the subtleties of divine mysteries.

Types of Purity:

There are three types of purity. One is purity of the bodies with temporal refinement. The second is purity of thought. The third is the rank of the head, which is free from desires so that it becomes conformable to manifestation. The perfection of purity is sanctification—that is, being immersed in the river of divine greatness and drowning in pre-eternity. This rank belongs to the sincere ones, the prophets, and the intimate friends of Allah.
