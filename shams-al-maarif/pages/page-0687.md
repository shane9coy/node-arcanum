# Page 687 (English translation)
With Thy sacred attributes, do Thou grant me to be among Thy grateful servants, and by the bounty of Thy blessings, among those who praise and remember Thee; so accept Thou my deed with Thy abundant grace, and illuminate my heart with the light of Thy sanctity, that I may be of Thy household, and gather for me the choicest of blessings and the removals of evils in life and in death. O God, O Most Appreciative, I ask Thee to enable me to invoke it by Thy grace—surely Thou over all things art Powerful.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Alī"

"Alī" is He than whom there is no rank higher, and sublimity is either sensible, as in degrees, or it is among intelligible stations, as with the distinction between cause and effect, and between the complete and the incomplete. When, therefore, you understand these intellectual degrees, you will also have come to know that the division of degrees is not possible upon disparate intellectual ranks. The spiritual keeper of this Name grants the reciter an elevated degree of divine favor. Its trustee is Qayyāil.

The reciter of this Name has every matter fulfilled for him:

When the reciter reads it with great intensity after each prayer, the trustee comes forth and fulfills his need. And whoever writes it and keeps it with himself is granted awe and acceptance. And when the triangle of the Great Name is placed within its square and kept with him, if he is a ruler, his entire army obeys him.

On a silver tablet, the engraving and placing upon a girl's neck hastens marriage:

And if it is engraved upon a silver tablet and placed upon the neck of a girl for whom marriage proposals do not come, by its blessing marriage will occur very quickly. And its dhikr is:

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, Thou art the Most High, the Supreme, whose sublimity resembles not the sublimity of created beings, nor doth Thy light equal the light of existing things, of the earth, and of the heavens. Thy ancient throne, which hath encompassed all created beings, and Thy mighty, sublime throne above the degrees of the supernal heights—every existing thing is in the hand of Him whose power is over the atoms. As for the sublimity of Thy essence, it is free from condition and place, and holy above what hath been found in ages and times, for it is the sublimity of majesty and glory, the sublimity of greatness and perfection. I ask Thee by the sublimity of Thy mercy over all blamed things, and by the exaltedness of Thy divinity above the greatest of majesties, and by the oneness of Thy singular uniqueness upon the honor of the purification of perfections, that Thou place my worth near Thee for the beauties of acts of obedience, and make me sincere for the vision of Thy noble face at all times until death. O God, make me among those under Thy protection, and ward off the eye of every obstinate adversary, and send down the compulsion of Thy sublimity upon whoever intends harm from every envier and aggressor. O God, take my heart to obedience unto Thee and to the love of Thy chosen ones, and take my soul to the attachment of the sublimity of Thy sanctity, and make me worthy of Thy guardianship among Thy messengers and Thy prophets. O God, O Most High.

Whoever persists in this dhikr, God Most High elevates his worth and status, and fills the hearts of people with love for him.
