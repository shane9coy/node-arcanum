# Page 290 (English translation)

From his heart, rivers of wisdom will flow. Whose practice is conditioned with purification of the soul. Whoever mentions this name frequently, the realities and secrets of meanings will come to his understanding. On a board of twelve sections, in the dignity of Mercury, whoever traces it and keeps it with him, the sciences of wisdom will come to his understanding. Mentioning it is recommended for the sages. Its number is 78. This is an odd-even composite. Its letters point to the Name of God Almighty, the Name of Kingship. It is "Huwa Awwalu Zahiqat al-Hikmah" and its letters according to one consideration are 257 and according to another consideration are 213. According to the first consideration, they point to the Name of God Almighty "Alim" (the All-Knowing) and its apparent meanings; and according to the second consideration, they point to the Name of God Almighty "Bari" (the Creator), because these are the meanings of preparing spirit to accept form-matters, which are from those decrees that wisdom necessitates.

"That is why the wise one desires expansiveness for things, and the commanded one sees constriction from the decree. May God Almighty not give light to him, for him there is no light, and whoever is given wisdom, for him is much good, and people of intellect alone acquire admonition." It has been known that every dhikr gives strength to its reciter, but he must be acquainted with its reality, and such people are very few. Its square is this:

(Chapter 48: On the properties of the Name "Wadud," it is an elixir for the subjugation of hearts)

This name possesses a magnetic quality—people who frequently mention this name have love for it in their hearts. This is from the great dhikrs. Whoever places the Name Wadud and Habib in a triangle, writes the Name Jawad in its centers, makes a triangle within the square's interior, and keeps it with him—however, whoever upon whom his gaze falls will love him. Whoever writes this talisman on the first hour of Friday or in the dignity of Venus, keeps it with him, and persists in its recitation will witness the strange and wondrous effect of attracting desires of creatures. If he writes this name on white silk and keeps it with him, everyone will love him—but purity is a condition. Some gnostics have stated that if one recites this name until its state overtakes him, then whoever sees him will incline to him from the heart and love him. God Almighty will revive his interior with the spirit of love and adorn his exterior with the secrets of affection. You, O such-and-such a person, some scholars of the secrets of letters have placed it in this form: whoever writes this triangle in the dignity of the Moon on the first hour of Wednesday and keeps it with him, and continues reciting this name profusely—however, whoever sees him will love him.
