# Page 108 (English translation)
108

He remains protected from the evil of wicked people through this mention. Whoever frequently mentions the Great Name of God, Exalted is He, will become very great in the eyes of people. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Hafiz (the Protector) will be preserved by God, Exalted is He, from all calamities. Through frequent mention of the Name Muqit (the Sustainer), the bitterness of hunger will not be felt. Through frequent mention of the Name Hasib (the Reckoner), every need will be fulfilled. Through frequent mention of the Name Jalil (the Majestic), he will become distinguished in greatness in his time. Through frequent mention of the Noble Name of God, Exalted is He, there will be protection in every affair. Through frequent mention of the Name Raqib (the Watcher), good fortune appears at the conclusion of all affairs. Through frequent mention of the Name Mujib (the Responsive), prayers are answered.

The gracious spirits:

Whoever frequently mentions the Name Wadud (the Loving) of God, Exalted is He, all gracious spirits become [favorable]. If the king frequently mentions the Name Mujid (the Glorious), there will be advancement in his kingdom. Through the mention of the Name Ba'is (the Resurrector), every kind of good is obtained. Through the mention of the Name Shahid (the Witness), spiritual vision is granted during contemplative meditation. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Haq (the Truth), his word becomes elevated. If one inscribes the square of the Name Wakeel (the Advocate) on marble during the ascendant of Scorpio and places it in his house, all snakes, scorpions, and harmful creatures will depart from it. Through the mention of the Name Qawi (the Strong), the heart becomes strong and enduring love is achieved. Through frequent mention of the Name Matin (the Firm), one is delivered from weakness. Through the mention of the Name Wali (the Protecting Friend), God, Exalted is He, makes him His friend. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Hamid (the Praiseworthy) and inscribes it on paper according to its numerical value, then washes it and gives it to a sick person to drink, God, Exalted is He, will grant him recovery. Through the mention of the Name Muhsi (the Counter), one remains protected from all evils. Through the mention of the Name Mubdi (the Originator), whatever he does turns out correct. Whoever writes the square of the Name Mu'id (the Restorer) during the ascendant of Scorpio and hangs it in the air, and continues reading the Name day and night—if he takes the name of whoever has run away or disappeared, by the command of God, Exalted is He, they will return to the place from which they departed. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Muhyi (the Giver of Life), God, Exalted is He, illuminates his heart with the light of gnosis. Whoever mentions the Name Mumit (the Cause of Death), his carnal desires depart. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Hayy (the Living) and writes it one hundred and twenty (120) times during the dignity of Venus and buries it in the doorway of his house, the dwellers of that house will remain protected from all illnesses. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Qayyum (the Self-Subsisting), he finds sciences and divine knowledges within his heart. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Wajid (the Finder), self-sufficiency and piety are attained within his own heart. Whoever mentions the Name Majid (the Glorious), his remembrance and rank become elevated. Whoever mentions the Name Wahid (the One), [people] feel awe towards him. Whoever mentions the Name Ahad (the Unique), God, Exalted is He, makes him the most fearless of all. Through the mention of the Name Samad (the Eternal), God, Exalted is He, grants spiritual insight and the power of mystic knowledge.

Through the mention of the Name Muqtadir (the Powerful), all spiritual beings become subservient. Through the mention of the Name Muqaddim (the Promoter), God, Exalted is He, grants the ability to manipulate causes. Through the mention of the Name Mu'akhkhir (the Deferrer), benefit comes to him whose door is closed and covered. Whoever mentions the Name Awwal (the First) excels in every good deed. Through the mention of the Name Akhir (the Last), every good is obtained, and this is a protected secret and hidden knowledge. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Zahir (the Manifest), God, Exalted is He, reveals hidden mysteries for him. Whoever mentions the Name Batin (the Hidden) obtains every desired thing and every need is fulfilled. Through the mention of the Name Wali (the Governor), awe is inspired among people. Whoever mentions the Name Mutacali (the Most High), God, Exalted is He, grants him great spiritual power. Upon the one who mentions the Name Barr (the Source of Goodness), the mercy of God, Exalted is He, is abundant. Whoever writes the Name Tawwab (the Acceptor of Repentance) and breathes over rosewater and gives it to a drunkard to drink, and reads this Name frequently, the drunkard will repent from wine. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Muntaqim (the Avenger), God, Exalted is He, takes revenge on his enemies. Whoever mentions the Name Afuw (the Pardon) [unclear] is granted security. Through the mention of the Name Ra'uf (the Merciful), gracious spirits become [favorable].

Whoever frequently mentions the Name Malik-ul-Mulk (the King of Dominion), God, Exalted is He, will grant him such dwellings that he becomes their owner.
