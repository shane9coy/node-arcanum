# Page 93 (English translation)
God, there is nothing whatsoever like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. I ask You to bless and grant peace upon our master Muhammad and upon the family of our master Muhammad. And I ask You to fulfill my need, and whatever good is in the world and the hereafter, [which shall be] protected by care, protected from calamities by the specialities of divine protections. O Most Generous with goodness, and O You who in truth are the People of Piety and the People of Forgiveness. Ṭ

This has also passed in the preceding chapter, and this is its final statement. Trace it and derive whatever you wish from it. By the command of Allah the Almighty, you will obtain all your desires.

Chapter related to this statement:

A great saint said: I was in seclusion, I saw a diagram in which there was a circle within a circle. Inside it was written the Greatest Name [of God], and from it all kinds of rays were emanating. This [circle] contains the Name of Glory [Allah]. When that luminous diagram came to my mind, and then when that diagram vanished from the unseen realm, at that very moment I wrote it on paper and focused my thought so that I might extract from it the seventy names. I began it and took one from it, then I sought forgiveness from Allah the Almighty and praised Him, but I was not given permission for it. Therefore, I stopped that thought. These nineteen names are derived from the Name of Glory, and the twentieth is itself the Name of Glory. Its benefits are as many as the one who knows its method of use will forever see. Whenever any person intends any religious or worldly matter, they should perform complete ablution, face the qiblah, and in some corner perform two units of prayer with presence of heart, submission, sincere intention, and supplication in the middle of the night. Then after that, recite the names one thousand six hundred and seventy (1670) times with presence of heart, and supplicate Allah the Almighty, and it will be answered.

Especially if they supplicate for obtaining some knowledge, Allah the Almighty will open upon them the paths of knowledge through His Name the All-Knowing, and upon them will be the lights of unveiled spiritual perception.

Countless benefits of the Tablet of Light:

Some benefits of this diagram are such that they can be described, and some are such that they cannot be described. One of its benefits is that whoever writes this circle and places it among their belongings will, by the command of Allah the Almighty, be protected from the seventeen harmful influences. Whoever writes it and ties it on their right arm and goes to their enemies, Allah the Almighty will protect them from their evil and will render the enemies utterly helpless. The enemies will become kind and obedient. All their desires will be fulfilled. They will be safe from every evil. In this diagram there are wonderful mysteries. Whoever writes it with rose water, musk, saffron, and camphor, and gives it to a bodily or spiritual patient to drink, will, by the command of Allah the Almighty, be healed. Its bearer will have great strength in body and spirit. By the blessing of the names, people will fear them, and they themselves will feel powerful. Whoever recites these names seventy-seven (77) times as a daily litany after the morning prayer will obtain such benefits that they are beyond description.
