# Page 364 (English translation)
A Great Benefit (Needs are Fulfilled at the Hands of Rulers)

It is very useful for going to rulers, kings, ministers, qadis, governors, and officials. It is inscribed on a tablet of gold, silver, or copper in the dignity of the Sun or the dignity of Jupiter. The engraver should be fasting. When keeping it with oneself, fumigate with frankincense, labanum, jawi, mastic, our wa-lawr, and saffron. The form of the inscription is this:

Immediate Fulfillment of Needs:
One elder says: I had a need for which I prayed for years, for many long years, yet I did not despair. Finally, one night as I slept, a speaker said to me in a dream, "O person! This prayer is placed at your head; recite it and ask in supplication, and it will be accepted." I awoke from my sleep, and I saw at my head a tablet on which several disjoined letters were written. I gathered them together, and from them a prayer was formed. My need was fulfilled. The prayer is:

بِخُشُوعِ الْقُلُوبِ عِنْدَ السُّجُودِ لَكَ يَا سَيِّدِي بِغَيْرِ جُحُودِ
وَبِكَ يَا اَللَّهُ يَا جَلِيلُ فَلَا شَيْءَ يُدَانِيكَ فِي غَلِيظِ الْعُهُودِ
وَ بِكِبْرِيَائِكَ الْمُكَلَّلِ بِالنُّورِ إِلَى عِزِّكَ الْعَظِيمِ الْمَجِيدِ
وَ بِمَا كَانَ تَحْتَ عَرْشِكَ حَقًّا قَبْلَ خَلْقِ السَّمَاءِ وَ صَوْتِ الرُّعُودِ
أَنْتَ اَللَّهُ إِذْ كُنْتَ لَمْ تَزَلْ قَطُّ إِلَيْهَا عُرِضَتْ بِالتَّوْحِيدِ

With the humility of hearts in prostration to You, O my master, without denial, and with You, O Allah, O Magnificent—for nothing compares to You in the severity of covenants, and with Your majesty crowned with light, toward Your great, glorious honor, and with what was beneath Your throne, true before the creation of the heavens and the sound of thunder. You are Allah, as You have ever been—for it was to You that supplication was presented with sincere monotheism.

After this, recite: "O Allah, I ask You to send blessings upon our master Muhammad, upon whom be peace and blessings." Then ask for your need. God, the Exalted, willing, you will not sleep without fulfillment, and He will fulfill your need—and this prayer is like "Ilahi Amin."
