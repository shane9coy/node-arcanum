Chapter: Supplication for the Destruction of the Tyrant
[/Heading]

This blessed supplication belongs to Imam Muhammad ibn Idris al-Anwari, may Allah have mercy on him. Angels tremble from this supplication. The supplication is this:

"O Allah, O Most Affectionate, O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, O Possessor of Might that cannot be overcome, and Kingdom that cannot be subdued, O He upon Whose light the pillars of His Throne stand, O Helper, help me" three times. "Indeed, You are over all things Powerful."

And in another transmission it is like this: "O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, I ask You by the light of Your Face which filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your Power with which You had power over all of Your creation, and by Your Mercy which encompassed everything. There is no god except You, O Helper of those who seek help, help me."

"O Allah, O Most Affectionate, O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills—[You are] one possessing Might that does not perish, O Possessor of such Kingdom that does not pass away, O He upon Whose light the pillars of His Throne stand, O Helper, help me" three times. "[You are] the One over all things Powerful."

"O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills, I ask You by the light of Your Self which filled the pillars of the Throne, and by Your Power with which You have power over all of Your creation, and by Your Mercy which encompasses everything. There is no god except You, O Helper of those seeking help, help me."

One who reads this three times, it is said: An oppressed person read this supplication. At that very moment, a thunderous sound came from the sky, and a person came upon a horse holding a weapon, and he was about to kill the oppressed person. He killed him instead and said: "O Zayd, when you made supplication to Allah the Most High the first time, I was upon the seventh heaven. Jibril called out: 'Who will help this oppressed one?' I said: 'I will.' When you made supplication the second time, I was upon the heaven of the world. When you made supplication the third time, I came to you. By reading this supplication and making supplication, his supplication will be accepted."

Then when they came before His Presence, upon him be peace, and narrated it to him, he said: "Allah the Most High has taught you His Greatest Name, by which whatever supplication is made is accepted."

[Heading in a rectangular box]
Chapter: Explanation of the Verified Istikhara
[/Heading]

When you wish to know the outcome of a matter, after the Isha prayer perform two rakats of prayer. Perform ablution and pray in this manner: in the first rakat after Surah al-Fatiha, recite Surah ad-Duha; in the second, Al-Watiheen; in the third, Al-Inshirah; in the fourth, Al-Qadr; in the fifth, Az-Zalzalah; and in the sixth after al-Fatiha recite Surah al-Ikhlas. After finishing the salam, write on a piece of paper:

"My God, the Lord, the Exalted, the Most Affectionate, the Generous, the Mighty, the Compeller, the Self-Exalted—from His servant, so-and-so, the poor, the humble, the needy, the wretched, the indigent, the poor who has found nothing for his need except You, he demands and yearns by the Kingdom of such and such a need."

"O Lord, the Exalted, the Generous, the Most Affectionate, the Possessor of Might, the Compeller, the Self-Exalted. [This is] from this humble, needy, wretched, indigent, poor supplicant—his need is such and such."
