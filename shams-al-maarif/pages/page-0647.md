**The scholars are the heirs of the prophets:**
For this reason the scholars are the heirs of the prophets, who inherit from them the honor of prophethood.

**Types of life:**
The reality of this in the life of the nation and in faith is the life of the hearts. Service is with the attraction of the Divine, and faithfulness is with the love of God, and the life of bodies is with the establishment upon Israfel. When the servant progresses in these stations, he will be called "permitted." Every person should investigate the reality of this name. It is obligatory upon him to patiently endure the mystery of servitude upon the honor of Lordship, with submission.

**Destroying one's religion by honoring a wealthy person because of his wealth:**
The Honorable Muhammad ﷺ said: Whoever honors a rich person merely because of his wealth, his two-thirds of religion will be destroyed, because in a person there are three things: the tongue, the hand, and the heart. If he shows humility with his tongue, then a third of his religion is lost, and if he shows humility with his heart, his religion is completely destroyed.

**The one who remembers this name receives sustenance from where he does not expect:**
Whoever remembers this name, it is obligatory upon him that he do so with sincerity and not mention any other name, and abandoning all desires, sit in seclusion and not disclose his need to anyone. This name is among the invocations of those who rely on God, and whoever persists in it, Almighty God will give him sustenance from where he does not even imagine.

**The one who remembers it receives an honorable livelihood:**
Whoever inscribes its square in a silver ring and wears it, and persists in remembering this name, Almighty God will grant him an honorable livelihood.

And for those whose name's numerical value corresponds with this name's numerical value, this name is the Greatest Name for them. If he remembers it, Almighty God will open for him the gates of honor and awe, and he will be feared among all kings and rulers. The remembrance of this name is:
