**How enemies can be harmed or destroyed through its practice:**

And the servant of the Name of Justice is Sharakhayil. When you have an enemy, enter into seclusion and recite this Name according to its numerical value, and the confiédent will appear. Whatever you desire, use it for your purpose. Write its square and its talisman, fumigate it, keep it with you, and maintain the recitation of the Name as your regular practice. Any person you see will become obedient, and if a king performs this practice, the greatest rebellious ones will be obedient to him. Whoever writes and carries with him the description of these two Names in the form of a supplication and prayer, and recites them, every undertaking will become easy and all enemies will be humiliated. The supplication is this:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ o اَللّٰھُمَّ اَنْتَ الْعَزِيْزُ الَّذِيْ لَا يُشَابَهُ عِزُّكَ عِزَّةُ كُلِّ عَزِيْزٍ وَّ عَظِيْمٍ وَّلَا يَصِلُ اِلٰی كِبْرِيَآئِكَ عَنِ الْمُلُوْكِ الْاَمْلَاكِ فِيْ جَمِيْعِ خَلْقِكَ اَنْتَ الْعَزِيْزُ بِحُسْنِ الطَّاعَةِ لِاَوْلِيَآئِكَ وَالْمُذِلُّ بِخُذْلَانِ الْفَخَاصِيْنِ لِقُلُوْبِ اَعْدَآئِكَ اَسْئَلُكَ بِمَوَارِدِكَ النَّافِذَةِ بِالْقَهْرِ الرَّبَّانِيِّ الَّذِيْ لَا يَمْنَعُهُ جُوَاسَةُ الْبَشَرِ الْاِنْسَانِيِّ اِلَّا مَا حَمَلْتَهُ فِيْ حِفْظِ حِمَايَتِكَ وَاَمَّنْتَهٗ فِيْ مَقَامِ سِرِّ وَاجْتَبَيْتَكَ اَنْ تُجْزِيَنِيْ وَ تُذِلَّ مَنْ ظَلَمَنِيْ وَ تَعَاجِلَ بِالْجَذْلَانِ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ وَّ حَاسِدٍ وَّ مُعَانِدٍ وَّ اَنْ تُقَوِّيَنِيْ بِقَوِيِّ لُطْفِكَ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا مُعِزُّ يَا مُذِلُّ سُبْحَانَكَ اِنِّيْ كُنْتُ مِنَ الظّٰلِمِيْنَ

In the Name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the Mighty One whose might is incomparable to the might of any mighty or great one, and no kings or rulers among all Your creation can attain to Your greatness. You are the Mighty through the excellence of obedience to Your friends, and You humble through the abandonment of the arrogant to the hearts of Your enemies. I ask You by Your all-prevailing sources of divine compulsion, which no human audacity can prevent except what You have placed under the protection of Your safeguard and secured in the station of Your secret knowledge and chosen ones—that You reward me and humble those who wronged me, and hasten the destruction of every satan, envier, and adversary, and that You strengthen me by the strong gentleness of Your grace, O God, O Honorer, O Humiliator, glory be to You, surely I was among the wrongdoers.

Whoever serves faithfully in the recitation of this supplication, God Almighty will elevate his rank and status, and he will succeed in the destruction of the oppressors.

**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Sami' (the All-Hearing)"**

**For curing deafness:**
The discourse is such that no matter is empty from His hearing. He even hears the movement of an ant, and He hears the supplications of those who make supplication. The special property of this Name is that if a person has deafness in his ear, he should write it on Tuesday and wash it with rose oil, put it in the ear, and the ear will become sound.
For seeking knowledge, they combine the Name Basir with this Name and recite it in seclusion. Its confiédent is Lamnayil, who will do for you whatever task you desire. And when you recite it in seclusion with the intention of hearing the speech of spiritual beings, you will hear it. At the time of magic (sahar), with devotion and certainty, make mention of this Name, and its mention is this:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ o اَللّٰھُمَّ يَا سَمِيْعُ اَنْتَ الَّذِيْ تَسْمَعُ جَمِيْعَ النَّوَاجِيْنِ مِنْ غَيْرِ اُذُنِ ضِحْكاً عَلٰی اِخْتِلَافِ اَصْنَافِ اللُّغَاتِ فَلَا يَخْفٰى عَلَيْكَ شَيْءٌ بِمَا هَجَسَ فِي الضَّمَائِرِ وَ مَا نَطَقَتْ بِهِ السِّنُ اِلَّا لَھَا مِنْ اَحْصٰی جُمْلَةَ مَجْمُوْعِ الْمَسْمُوْعَاتِ الَّذِيْ اَحَطْتَ بِجَمِيْعِ الْمَوْجُوْدَاتِ وَ تَسْمَعُ دَبِيْبَ النَّمْلَةِ السَّوْدَاءِ عَلَی الصَّخْرَةِ الصَّمَّاءِ فِي اللَّيْلَةِ الظَّلْمَاءِ اَسْئَلُكَ اَنْ تَسْمَعَ دُعَائِيْ وَ تُسَخِّرَ لِيْ عَبْدَكَ فَلَمْنَائِيْلُ بِحَقِّ اِسْمِكَ السَّمِيْعِ وَاَنْ تَعْلَمَنِيْ اِلٰی کَذَا وَ کَذَا يَا رَبَّ الْعٰلَمِيْنَ وَ تُعَامِلَنِيْ بِلُطْفِكَ الْخَفِيِّ وَ تُمِدَّنِيْ بِرِيْحٍ مِنْ رِيَاحِكَ وَ اَنْ تُسَلِّطَنِيْ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ فِی الْاَرْضِ وَ فِی السَّمَاءِ

In the Name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, O Hearing One, You are He who hears all who call out, without an ear, mocking at the diversity of languages and categories, so nothing is hidden from You of what stirs in the consciences and what the tongues articulate—except that for it there is from the comprehensive enumeration of all things heard that You have encompassed all existing beings. And You hear the crawling of the black ant upon the bare rock in the dark night. I ask You to hear my prayer and to subjugate for me Your servant Lamnayil, by the right of Your Hearing Name, and to teach me thus and thus, O Lord of the Worlds, and to treat me with Your hidden gentleness, and to assist me with a wind from Your winds, and to grant me mastery over everything on earth and in heaven.
