And its greater [form] is that these noble angels may be subjugated for me to fulfill my need, which is such and such, by the grace of God, verily He is pleased.

O Allah, I ask You with Jabra'il, peace be upon him, when he ascends the Throne and by Your name—Allah, that You subjugate for me from among Your angels Kufayil, Dardayil, Jayil, Shimayil, and Karumiyayil, the obedient ones. O noble angels, O pure spirits who acknowledge the oneness of Allah the Most High, accept my words. O Allah, by the virtue of Your Name the Wise, You have granted it precedence over all Your great Names. That You subjugate for me these angels so that they may fulfill my need, wherein lies the pleasure of Allah the Most High.

Mention your need by name and avoid envy.

The prayer of Bilam son of Beor, may his need be fulfilled:

Bilam son of Beor—whenever he supplicated with this Name, it was accepted. But when Moses, peace be upon him, cursed him, Allah the Most High removed this prayer from him. Allah the Most High describes him in this verse:

"And recite to them the story of him to whom We gave Our signs, but he detached himself from them" (Al-A'raf: 175)

Guard this Name well, for many people have busied themselves with it, and many scholars have attained great ranks through the virtue of this prayer. They achieved their desires and goals. And Allah guides whoever He wills to the straight path.

May Allah the Most High grant me and you the success to obey Him. Know that every person who writes this seal in a state of purity and keeps it with him, Allah the Most High will set right his outward and inward affairs, and grant him the success of obedience. This person will prevail over his enemies. Whatever ruler sees him will be filled with awe of him. Whoever ties it on his head, every arrogant person will be humbled and humiliated before him. May Allah the Most High grant him awe with outward and inward power. Whoever fights his enemy with this seal will overcome him, and victory will be his by the command of Allah the Most High. Whoever goes to war with this seal, Allah the Most High will grant him victory over his enemies, and no harm will befall him. If any king or ruler keeps it with him, his army, commanders, and nobles will obey him. He will be supported and aided by the command of Allah the Most High. Whoever keeps it with him will receive sustenance from such a place that he would not even imagine. This seal also has the effect of winning hearts, love, and affection. Whoever works with it can accomplish anything. Its form will appear with the section on "Works" that follows.

Know that whoever has even one of the mysteries of the letter Mim revealed to him in its encompassing and correspondence will witness the wonders of the universe.

Increasing Memory:

Whoever needs to improve their memory should write it on Wednesday or Thursday in a state of purity, facing the qiblah, and also write the blessed name of the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, forty times. Also fumigate it with incense. Then wash it with water, mix it with honey, and drink it for forty days. Or say: "O Allah, by the blessing of what I have drunk, make memorization and understanding easy for me." Allah the Most High will open his outward and inward [ faculties ]. And say: "Indeed, the one who witnesses these mysteries one by one—they are all within this world of the hidden—they are within its inward, because the letter Mim is connected to them, for this is obtained by many." Whoever writes this seal and ties it on his right arm and goes to his enemies, Allah the Most High will grant him steadfastness and humiliate his enemies. And whoever goes to his enemy with this seal, whom he fears for his evil, or an oppressive tyrant, then that [enemy] will be humiliated and debased. The effects of these seals will be fulfilled quickly, for they contain wonderful mysteries. Whoever writes it and fumigates it with incense, then hangs it in the sun such that from morning until evening it remains in the sun [damaged source]
