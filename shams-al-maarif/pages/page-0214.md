"Blessing upon you, accept it, and do what I have commanded you; and the rest of what I have adjured you by, and that is truly a great oath if you only knew."

[Boxed Heading]
Riyāḍah, or "Ya Karīm" or "Ya Raḥīm"
[/Boxed Heading]

Know that when you intend to perform this practice with this noble invocation and spiritual discipline, take retreat in an empty house that is free of people and distant from noises. Your clothes and your body should be pure. Observe fasting throughout the duration of your retreat and spiritual discipline. Break your fast with bread and vinegar if possible. The duration of the retreat should be seven days. Begin your practice on Sunday and finish on Saturday. If you intend a shorter duration, your spiritual discipline shall be three days: begin on Tuesday and end on Thursday. Recite both Names fourteen thousand times daily without interruption. Those two Names are "Ya Karīm" or "Ya Raḥīm." The method is: every day after the morning prayer, recite Sūrat al-Kāfirūn 21 times, then recite the Name, then recite the oath specifically three times. Then remain occupied with reciting these two Names, "Ya Karīm" or "Ya Raḥīm," at all times. When Friday night arrives, recite both Names, then recite one thousand times the noble ṣalawāt upon the blessed Essence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ, circumambulate both Names one thousand times, then recite one thousand times the noble ṣalawāt upon the blessed Essence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. After twelve [hours], recite the oath while seated, having first performed two units of prayer, and you should face the Qiblah while praying.

The Obtaining of Gold Ashrafī Daily through the Blessing of the Names

When you reach the station "Ḏhālik" upon the Heavens of Paradise, perform prostration with the utmost sincerity, and in the prostration recite this supplication: in this manner, forty-one times, reciting the oath after each time, and perform prostration. And at midnight, recite supplication in your prostration, and maintain one practice for seven days in this way: recite the two Names along with two units of prayer, and continue reciting the oath, and continue reciting the noble ṣalawāt upon the blessed Essence of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. When the time of midnight of Sunday night arrives, a spiritual entity will come to you in a dream or while awake, and it will say: "O servant of God, what do you desire?" Say: "I desire the grace of God and yours, that a dinar come to me every day." It will better your circumstances and will take from you certain conditions—for example, that you visit the cemetery every Friday, recite both Names after every prayer, and give charity to the needy poor. So make these binding upon yourself. Express gratitude for it; may God accept your striving and forgive us, but grant us leave. After this, you will find one ashrafī beneath your pillow at nighttime. "Value this gift that we have presented before you."

The incense for this practice is ʿūd, qāṭī, jādī, and māhimmasūr. Keep incense burning as long as you practice the spiritual discipline. Know that the assignees of these two Names are from among the believing angels. They bring neither wealth nor cause anyone harm. But taqwā is binding upon you, and the oath is this:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā munfikha jimāghi l-ʿālī ʿalā kulli burākh anādīka yā jibrā'īlu fa'mur munādiyan mina l-samā'i bi-nidā'in min qibalika yā samā shalwūth shalwūth mā samiʿaka ʿabduk illā khashiʿa wa khajīʿa wa lā jabalan illā tazāzaʿa wa lā malakan illā khashiʿa bi l-nadī zayyana l-shamsa fī ufuqi l-samā'i wa innahu la-qasamun law taʿlamūna ʿaẓīmun ajibi l-dāʿiya yā mahmūnu bi-ḥaqqi inna alladhīna ʿinda rabbika lā yastakbirūna ʿan ʿibādatihi wa yusabbiḥūnahu wa lahu yasjudūna.

"O God, I ask You—O He who blows into the highest skull upon every temple, I call upon You—O Jibrīl, command a proclaimer from heaven to proclaim by Your command: O heavens, Shālwūth, Shālwūth! No servant hears you except he becomes humble and abased, and no mountain trembles, and no angel is humbled by the proclamation with which the sun is adorned in the horizon of heaven. And surely it is a mighty oath, if you only knew. Answer the one who calls, O Mahamūna, by the truth of: 'Indeed, those who are with your Lord are not too proud to worship Him, and they glorify Him, and to Him they prostrate.'"
