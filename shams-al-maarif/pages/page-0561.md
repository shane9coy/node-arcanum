# Page 561 (English translation)
**Recipe for Making Gold and Silver:**
Take red clay, and when it is prepared with red Abalaq and red spirits and red souls and carried out with excellent arrangement, one māsha of it can color two hundred māshas, and it is used with assistance.

لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَ لَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَ هُوَ عَلٰی كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ

Prepare it with abundant recitation and dhikr of this.

**Materials for the Alchemical Silver Recipe:**
Its preparation is as follows: take an excellent and strong head of Sabirin, and if you prepare it yourself for this recipe, it is better. Take one ratl of it and half of white kali salt, culinary salt, chalkhān, impure residue, yellow iron vitriol, gas, and gabrak—each of these should not be kept separate but [mixed together], and one-fourth the weight of the white yolk of chicken egg, that is, the yolk that has been thoroughly cooked. Mix all of these together. Then take hair from a human head that is completely black and has been cleaned of grease. Soak them in spring water for one week, place them over a gentle fire in the sun, and two or three of the aforementioned items should also be added to it. The hair will dissolve quickly. Distill this, and keep the clear, excellent distillate safely with you. Then dissolve the silver and add an equal weight of jist to it, mix both together, and add sangtābah, making the silver one part and one part of qasdiyah. First dissolve the silver and pour jist upon it, and combine both. Then grind them very fine, place them in a bowl, and cover with another bowl on top. After performing "philosophical sealing," place it on Sunday, Sunday, and Sunday on the bottom, and perform sublimation (which is universal) until it becomes completely the form of the lowest [substance]. Thus all spirit and body will be collected. Now add half of the weight of the fifth part (which is universal) that has been removed. Continue until spirit and body become united. Then purify it without books, and after you desire, dry it in the sun or over fire for three consecutive days, and continue until it becomes apparent that it no longer accepts water. Place it in water until the drinking process is complete. This is "mrāj jānī." When the operation is complete to this point, seal it in a glass vessel and press it in Indian clay, and have the lead changed every week. The medicines will dissolve in the form of white water. Now at this time, however much gold you desire, you can whiten it with this water. When you place a piece of copper in this water, it will become white and its color will never change, even if it is melted a thousand times. If you dissolve and sublimate this water in the same manner a few times, it becomes the perfect elixir.

**One ratl of this elixir will turn 200 mithqāls of copper into gold**
One ratl of it is sufficient for 200 mithqāls of copper, and it will turn quicksilver into silver, meaning this elixir will turn every metal into silver. According to Allāmah Maslahat, there is no doubt in this recipe. And if in place of silver, gold or bell-metal or lead is added, and the remaining preparation is done as before—that is, the portion of dahiyā and the water recipe, replacing iron vitriol with red sulfur and yellow iron vitriol, and instead of white alum, adding the yellow of the yolk—and adding some blood along with the hair, and the remaining entire preparation and all additions are carried out as before, the red recipe will be prepared.

**It will turn quicksilver into gold**
It will turn quicksilver into gold. This recipe was obtained for me from a faqīr. It is extremely correct. Take the fourth of the cooked yolk, a small amount of red emerald, a moderate amount of kohl, and kholān—grind all of these very fine, dissolve them in soap solution, and cook it over a medium fire. Then dissolve lead and pour a moderate amount of it upon this—it will become gold. Alas! I will soon reveal to you in detail the secrets of all these items after making a hundred [copies of this] gold.
