# Page 410 (English translation)
Good sustenance and the blessing of the Hereafter, accept my repentance and turning toward You before death, and do not punish me after death. Make the agonies of death easy for me. O You who hears all voices, O Allah, protect me from every distress and hardship at times of severity, and protect me from difficulties at the time of conclusion. O Allah, grant safety to me and to my religion. Do not take away my faith at the time of the death rattle. At the time of death, do not let me forget the Word of Truth. O Allah, make me one who remembers You frequently.

Make me one who supports the Truth for Your sake, one who hopes in Your promise, one who fears Your warning, and one who is pleased with You in all circumstances. Remove my grief and fulfill my needs, O Fulfiller of Needs. Accept my prayer through the blessing of this Surah. Grant forgiveness to me, my parents, all believing men and women, and all Muslim men and women. Indeed, You are the Hearer, Near, who hears prayers. By Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful.

The prayer requested before one's own is accepted. Many people have tested this and found that when they asked Allah with hope, it was accepted immediately. I have read it many times on various occasions, and its blessing became manifest through Ahmad. But all these things are with sincere intention. It is related in a Divine Hadith: He said, "I am with My servant as he thinks of Me." When the need is fulfilled, let him praise Allah the Exalted with all the words of praise that are in the Wise Quran. Whoever needs abundant sustenance should read it seven times daily. Some scholars of the religion say that whoever is constant in this practice, Allah the Exalted will open for him the best doors of sustenance. Along with this, reading Surah al-Fatihah seven times, Surah al-Qalam, Surah al-Mulk, Surah al-Sharh, and Surah al-Nasr will produce wonderful results. Know that the special properties and benefits of this Surah are countless. Likewise, the special properties of every verse are magnificent and contain many benefits.

Fasl: The Spiritual Discipline of "Salamun Qawlan min Rabbin Rahim" and the Subjugation of the Assigned Angel

[Heading box ends]

Its procedure is as follows: beginning from Sunday, observe a complete fast for forty days with full adherence to the discipline. Recite the verse eight thousand four hundred and twenty times each day. Sleep only briefly at night. The solitude should be such that no sound from anyone reaches you. Burn the fragrance of Indian frankincense and labdanum day and night. The clothing should be white and pure. Bathe at least every third day and apply fragrance. Recite the assigned portion completely once each in the morning prayer, the Duha prayer, and the Maghrib prayer. When you pass that day in this state, an angel will come to you and say, "O sincere one, while I have been passing day after day watching you endure such hardship, you have become exhausted. Give your soul rest. Take this much from me." One should not pay attention to him, no matter how much he offers—never accept it, and continue with the dhikr. When the forty days are complete, your cell of solitude will be filled with light. Every day you will see written on the wall the verse "Salamun Qawlan min Rabbin Rahim." During this period, you will also see fine dreams. A king riding a horse will come to you, and a great multitude will be around him. That king will say to you, "Peace be upon you." You should stand in his honor. Respond to his greeting and say, "As you have honored me, may Allah the Exalted also honor you." Then the king will say to me, "I desire from you a sign by which I may present you to me at the time of need." He will take a covenant and pledge from you and give you a ring, with the condition that you shall not speak falsehood or utter any word that is not the Speech of Allah. Whatever need you have, he will fulfill it, however difficult or far away it may be. The oath is this, and it should be read three times each day:

"Allahumma laisa fi al-samawati dharratun wa la fi al-ardhi ghamaratun wa la fi al-jibali madarratun wa la fi al-bihari qataratun wa la fi al-uyuni lahzhatun wa la fi al-nufusi khatratun illa wa hiya bika wa ilayka dalalatun shahiddatun wa fi mulkika musakhkharatun as'aluka bi-taskhirika li-kulli shay'in ya man yusabbihu lahu ma fi samtika wa anta al-musta'an wa alayka al-tuklan wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah al-aliyyi al-azhim wa salla Allahu ala sayyidina Muhammad"
