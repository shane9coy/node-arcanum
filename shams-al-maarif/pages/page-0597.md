# Page 597 (English translation)
You desire the hearts 51, O 35, behold if it were not for Him I would speak to you speech that declares His chosen ones in obedience to Him. Answer, may God bless you and protect you and watch over you, and peace be upon you and the mercy of God and His blessings, and there is no power nor strength except in God the Most High the Magnificent. Ta.

Translation: I spoke in speech with the Honored One of God, and I spoke with praise of God and thanks to Him, and there is no help except from God the Mighty the Wise. O God, I ask You, O Lord of the Kingdom, O One of Glory and Greatness, O Pure Essence whose command is between kaf and nun, O Pure Essence, when He intends to create something, He says to it "Be" and it becomes. I ask You by Your word Kun (Be), O Creator of all the worlds. Pure is He whose mention brings tranquility to hearts. He knows what is in the conscience and the inner thoughts of hearts. Know that if He were not, I would speak to you such speech that would be entirely sincere in obedience to Him. Accept, O God, from You, and blessed are You, and there is no power of good or evil except from God the Supreme and the Great. O letter Kaf, accept, O God, from You, and bless me. The meanings of the remaining letters and worship are also similar to this.

And its incense is khardal and kafur. And its recitation is 28 times after every prayer, and breaking the fast also recite it 28 times; the desire will be obtained. Its breaking of the fast is this:

Answer, O letter Kaf, may God bless you and upon you, by the right of sura [unclear] safurat lahamit la hayth buyur haytajish sa'duwiy. Answer, may God bless you and upon you, and there is no power nor strength except in God the Most High the Magnificent. Ta.

[heading]
The Letter Lam

This letter is the definite article and is among the letters of the Greatest Name, by which the words of Bismillah are meant.

[heading]
By the Ism al-Latif the sick will find relief

In the Ism al-Latif this letter has appeared. Whoever writes this letter according to its numerical value and gives it to the sick, they will find relief.

[heading]
The operation of burning jinn

When you wish to burn jinn, then recite this oath and say: O letter Lam, by your authority burn this jinn immediately. It will burn forthwith. Its custodian's name is Ali-Tayil, and its light is white. When you enter its service, recite the دعوت 48 times after every prayer. The custodian will appear and make a covenant with you. And know that the letter Lam is called Sayf al-Talib (The Sword of the Seeker). Its amulet figure is this:

In the name of God the Compassionate the Merciful. Ta. O God, here I am. O God, answer me secretly. There is no sin upon me, for to You belongs all praise and to You belongs thanks. Make easy for me every difficulty, O God, O Latif, and to You belong all favors and blessings. O God, take possession of my sleep and magnify me.
