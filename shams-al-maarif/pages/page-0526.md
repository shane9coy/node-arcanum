Chapter: Regarding the Jafar of His Eminence Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq, which was chest-contained

Its method is as follows: The letters of the abjad are twenty-eight letters, and each letter has twenty-eight pages, and each page has twenty-eight lines, and each line has twenty-eight cells, and each cell contains 4 letters. The first letter is placed for the first part, the second letter for the line, and the third and fourth constitute the arrangement of the cell. Thus in the first cell four alifs are written, and in the last cell there are four ghayns. In this manner, so that in every district the single letters appear four times of their own accord. The total collection of Jafar pages amounts to 784 pages, and the total lines are 21,952, and the total cells are 614,656, and the total letters of all lines are 2,458,624. The purpose of this indication is that if the chest of the Divine Truth is open. Thus it is determined in the rules of indications. For this, according to the comprehensive method by virtue of the four degrees, understand this: I have opened this door for you and revealed the hidden secret.

Complete illumination falls upon the events of the world through this table:

And this is the table by which the names of kings become known. Call it "the Sword." Through this table you will be able to know the conditions from the time of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ until the Day of Resurrection, and the events that will befall every emperor and every person during the span of their life—what will happen to them—all of this can be known through this method. It is also known which sovereignty will come after one's sovereignty. In short, all conditions become known. May Allah grant us the success to work and act in the excellent manner.

I extracted these days from the principles of Jafar, and I found them completely correct. It never errs. The method of its extraction is as follows: When you wish to determine the lifespan of a king, take the numerical values of the letters of his name according to the greater abjad, and see whether the letters of his name are single—that is, several letters less than ten. Then expand this name until repeated letters occur in it and the complete tree is formed. Among these there should be repeated letters, such as in Mustafa, or in Ali, or some letters repeated two or three times. Thus for such names there is no need for expansion, but in them the same process of purification is carried out. If only one of the letters is repeated, then it is taken from al-Ghani. First look at the name: if it is al-Ghani, then add to it a number equal to forty-eight thousand, and the addition is complete.
