Chapter 36: The Properties of the Name Shakur 'Divine Victory and Mystical Revelations in All Affairs'
Whoever frequently recites this name, all his deeds will be accepted by God Almighty, and in every good endeavor God Almighty will assist him. In this name there are many secrets for those who practice mystical contemplation, which they witness in the places of verification. Its number is 526. Thus, what points to elevation—its number points to what has appeared from the High Place—is the number five hundred (500), which points to the fruit of all things, which is the most praiseworthy degree. This spirit is rectangular in form. Its components are 666, which point toward the name of God Almighty, Al-Dhiyān. In this there is training upon the number, just as one of us trains his child. Its letter-names are 85, which point toward two great names of God Almighty: Al-Muqaddir and Al-Jawwād. The expedience of its temperament is this:

| Sh | K | W | R |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 19 | 21 | 99 | 7 |
| 302 | 62 | 4 | 198 |
| 5 | 197 | 303 | 21 |

Chapter 37: The Properties of the Name 'Alī' 'Fortune and Acquisition of Knowledge and Honor'
Whoever frequently recites this name, God Almighty shall grant him greatness, and he shall not be humiliated before anyone. Whoever sees him shall love him. God's praise shall be with him. He shall attain the rank of kingship. The subtle mysteries of the Holy Ka'bah shall be revealed to him. Whoever frequently recites this name, God Almighty shall greatly elevate his worth and rank. Whoever sees him shall love him in his heart. Whoever he asks for something shall obey him. He shall see in his time great elevation and in himself good fortune. In this there are wonderful and strange secrets for the masters and great ones and for seekers of knowledge and lights. If the Supreme Name is added to this, it becomes a very great dhikr. Whoever imprints its design upon a golden ring and, having offered incense of ūd 'anbar, wears it, whoever sees it shall become humble and obedient before him. After the predecessors, most of the Abbasid caliphs used to perform this practice, and it continued to be practiced until the author's time. God Almighty, by its blessing, maintained their rule. Someone said to Ma'mūn: "The kings of Persia have conquered you. What arrangement have you made?" So Ma'mūn showed him his ring, upon which the second design was [engraved], and said: "As long as this remains with us, no one can prevail over us." The time of the rising of the Moon is appropriate for it, and this is its design:

The number of this name is 110, and of Al-Muqaddir is 700; the total is 810. Its alms-giving is what appears from this design.
