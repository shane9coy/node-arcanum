# Page 523 (English translation)
And Mosul and the lands of Bakr and Aden will be destroyed by the killing wind. The scholar of the Two Tribes said secretly: "This shall be by the command of my Lord, the Owner of Glory."

And charity and almsgiving will be nothing, and offspring will not do good to parents. Many people and great powers will exist, and the entire earth will spread forth like the flames of fire strike, and Makkah Sharif and the land of Yemen will all be corrupted by plague and severe illnesses, and Madinah Sharif and the lands when all are slain, and the plains around them will be desolate, and Mosul and the lands of Bakr and the people of Sind will all be slain and corrupted. All of this the Scholar of the Two Tribes spoke truly, and by God's command it shall be so.

[Heading underlined:] The Messenger of God ﷺ said: God reveals hidden secrets through the poetry of poets.

One day Gabriel, peace be upon him, brought two apples from Paradise and presented them to the Messenger of God ﷺ. At that time Imam Hasan and Imam Husayn were present before him. The Prophet ﷺ gave one apple to each of them. Those young princes took the apples to their teacher and gave them to him. God endowed their teacher with wisdom and made him speak. Thus the secrets of the unseen began to be revealed through him, until the Messenger of God ﷺ also heard of it. He said to him: "Revealing the secrets of narration is forbidden." This account is very famous among scholars, and it has been said that God Almighty has hidden treasures whose keys are the tongues of poets, and the Messenger, peace be upon him, said: "There are hidden secrets of God Almighty that He reveals through the tongues of poets," and he said: "If there were no world and hereafter, the secrets would not be revealed." The Noble Prophet ﷺ described in detail the state of the emergence of wars and trials.

[Heading underlined:] From the narration of Hudhayfah, the Messenger described every event of the Day of Judgment in detail.

Hudhayfah may God be pleased with him says: "I swear by God, the Messenger of God ﷺ left no troublemaker or corruptor whose description he did not give, up until the establishment of the Hour, and the number of those troublemakers reaches three hundred." The Messenger of God mentioned by name their tribes and the names of their fathers. Whoever memorized it will remember it, and whoever forgot it will forget. Know that the causes of the corruption of the world will arise as follows: the corruption of hair will be by a fierce and forceful wind, and the corruption of the city of Madinah al-Tayyibah will be by famine, and the corruption of Basra will be by water, and the city of Tand will be corrupted by plague, and the city of Hala will be corrupted by sand, and Yemen will be corrupted by locusts, and Samarkand will be corrupted by the swords of the Banu Taymur, and Persia by drought and Egypt by the Nile, and Andalusia by the sword, and in 900 AH the Romans will take [something]. Then after that Muhammad Mahdi, the Master of the Age, will come into their possession, and the Rose Garden of the Meridian and the Gift will also be conquered by the aforementioned Master, and at the time when the aforementioned Imam emerges, the earth will be filled with oppression and injustice, but the Imam of justice and equity will fill it with them.

[Heading underlined:] The Government of Muhammad Mahdi:

If even one day of the world remains, it is inevitable that a person from the progeny of Fatimah, may God be pleased with her, the Master of the Age, Muhammad Mahdi, will rule. His name will be Muhammad, and he will distribute wealth equally and establish justice among the subjects, and he will conquer the cities of Rome, and the page of the shrine of the lineage of Ishmael and Isaac, peace be upon them, will enter into his obedience. All religions will cease to exist, only the people of ablution and coffee will remain. The trial of the Dajjal will also be attained by Mahdi, peace be upon him, which the scholars of verification have placed in the city of Madinah.

[Heading underlined:] The Descent of Isa, peace be upon him:

After that, Isa, peace be upon him, will descend from Bayt al-Maqdis and will kill the Dajjal, and the paths of Islam will be opened throughout all the earth.
