This is the supplication:
O Allah, I ask You, O Pure One, by what You have deposited within the letter Ra of the hidden secrets, that You subjugate for me the servants of this letter. Indeed, You are over all things powerful.

The Letter Sin:
The letter Sin is a speaking letter, warm, airy, terrestrial. It is moist and temperate.

Properties and benefits: Its square, if a pregnant woman in the pain of childbirth looks upon it, the child will be born immediately. If its triangle is written on a copper vessel and ground with water and olive oil, and given to whoever has been bitten by a venomous creature, there will be relief. The effect of poison will end.

For the expansion of sustenance:
If this letter is engraved in its composite numerical form on a silver plate during the first hour of Friday and set in a ring for use, sustenance will be expanded, every difficulty will become easy, in all respects there will be sufficiency, the hand will never be empty of wealth. If the Sin is engraved on a clay tablet and placed in a wall, flies will not enter the house.

For relief from lameness:
If this is engraved on glass at the opening and shown to the one who is lame, there will be healing. The seal of the supplication is this:

O Allah, I ask You, O Holy, O Swift, O Peace, by what You have deposited within the letter Sin of the hidden secrets, that You subjugate for me Your noble angels. Indeed, You are over all things powerful.

The Letter Shin:
Shin is a warm, speaking letter. Its rank is the last of the first order. Its dryness has combined with heat and become temperate.

Properties and benefits: Moderation takes effect quickly. If this letter is written fifteen times on paper on the day of Sunday when the sun is in the sign of Aries, and it is scented and bound on the head, Allah the Most High will send fear upon him. He will be granted awe and majesty. Everyone will love him and obey him.

For every person to be loved:
If they write its celestial composite in its own hour, and engrave its seal separately and keep it with them, the jinn and humans will breathe their love for them. If it is written on any cloth or sheet that is under the feet, then only the aforementioned mentioned in the section of Sin [illegible]
