# Page 335 (English translation)
The Lord of the angels and the Spirit upon the Throne established Himself, and upon the Ark He encompassed, and to Him belong the most beautiful names. There is no repeller of what He has decreed, nor preventer of what He has given. He does what He wills in His dominion, and He commands what He pleases among His creation, and He has power over all things.

There is no god except Allah; the entire universe belongs to Him. No one can overcome Him. Allah is light, and His essence is pure. His light overcomes every light. He is the Most-Returning, the Omnipotent, and the Truth. There is no god except Allah—the Holy One, Lord of the angels and the Spirit. His are the most beautiful names. There is no one to repel what He decrees, and there is no one to prevent what He bestows. What He wills He does in His kingdom, and what He wills He commands concerning His creation, and He has power over all things.

The operation for love between man and woman, the freeing of prisoners, and profit in trade

Write it on the membrane of a deer with musk and saffron, and fumigate it with fragrant incense. This name has many special properties: it is very useful for approaching kings, obtaining the release of prisoners, marriages of women, collecting expenses, creating love between men and women, and gaining profit in great endeavors and buying and selling. Keep it from impurity and avoid sins, for it contains the Greatest Name. It is narrated from the books of akhbar that upon the bed of Solomon, peace be upon him, there were such names; by their influence the jinn trembled and obeyed him. Whoever did not obey was burned. Upon the bed there were four names written in the Hebrew language, by whose blessing no jinn could disobey Solomon, peace be upon him, even up to the heavens. Upon this bed dwelt four great directors who were the greatest ministers among Solomon's jinn. Among humans, three hundred (300) were his ministers, among whom was Asif ibn Barkhiya. Among the jinn also there were three hundred (300), the greatest of whom were those four chiefs, whose names are: Mahmar Batil, Munfattil, Ad Labai, and Shukal. These names have a strange property for subjugating the jinn. Acquire knowledge of them, but do not reveal them to anyone. When you need something from them, speak thus:

"O company of helpers and ministers, command only what you are commanded: whoever fulfills my need and acts in my favor by the right of the Prophet of Allah, Solomon, peace be upon him." A mighty jinn from among the jinn said: "I will bring it to you before you rise from your place, and I am indeed strong and trustworthy regarding it. Indeed it is from Solomon, and in the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful: 'Do not act defiantly toward me, but come to me in submission.'"

"O company of helpers and ministers, command them to fulfill my need and to act in my favor for the sake of Solomon, peace be upon him." A jinn said: "I will bring it to you before you rise from your place, and I am strong and trustworthy. Indeed it is from Solomon: 'Do not be defiant toward me, but come to me in submission.'"

Write each name on its day when the body, clothes, and place are pure, during the auspicious hour. Fumigate with scented incense. After writing, read Surah Yasin and Surah Mulk in the shade and breathe upon it. This is useful for every purpose. The four names have four days:

The first: write Sirimat al-Ifrit during the first hour on Sunday at sunrise. Its assistant is Sirt al-Ifrit, and it is the lord of the hour. Its name is Munfattil Makrus. It is best to use it.

The second: write during the first hour on Tuesday. Its assistant is Bishukal al-Ifrit, and the lord of the hour is al-Murbat. Its name is Kashiktamush. Its divine name is Ad Labai.

The third: write during the first hour on Wednesday. Its servant is Ad Labai al-Ifrit, and the lord of the hour is Barqai al-Ifrit. Its name is Munfattil Maqu.

The fourth: write during the first hour on Thursday.
