And there is no power except in Allah, the Most High, the Most Great ط

Translation: "O You who give life and cause death, O Living, O Established, there is no god but You. Grant life to my heart with Your remembrance; I complain to You of my weakness and wretchedness. O Allah, bestow upon me from Your presence a bounty that assists me in my intentions. I have done [good deeds] for the sake of obeying You. O Allah, O Giver of blessings, without deserving, O Doer of good, O Magnificent, O Bestower of favors, O Giver of grace, O Most Merciful of the merciful. The letter Ya-H是我的下属 so that it fulfills my worldly needs. O my Master, You alone are the helper and upon You I rely. I call upon You by Your name to do for me that which I intend, and I am thirsty morning and evening, evening and morning, for the One by whose remembrance hearts find tranquility. And the Word is indeed with Us, and We are the Truth, and Your oaths are binding. How can one who disobeys Allah attain success? Accept my speech and present Yourself to me, and do all my affairs. O Mahraqael, accept my speech and present Yourself to me and fulfill my commands. May God grant blessing within you."

And its incense is sharp, corpulent, and its derivatives are as follows:
Respond, O Mahraqael, by the right of Ya-H 2 Barah 2 Masa' 2 Half 2. Respond and rely upon so-and-so, by the permission of Allah the Most High.

The Letter Kaf:
This letter is of the inner mysteries, and its origin is based on the letter Alif. In whatever affairs you can manipulate with Alif, you can also manipulate with Kaf. And when this letter is written on blue cloth together with the names of the Great Ones and their derivatives, and placed beneath the bezel of a ring, the wearer will obtain great acceptance. And if this letter is set in a copper plate and placed beneath the thym on the right side, the thym will burn, and it will appear as though a flame of fire has entered within it.

For protecting fields and gardens:
When this letter is written with Hanzalah and 'Aqil on four bricks and buried in the four corners of the field, it will remain protected from every affliction.

For healing a melancholic patient:
If this is written on a goat's skin and the person whose brain has been weakened by melancholia etc. carries it with him, he will be healed.
Its magical colors are green, and its invocation is this:
