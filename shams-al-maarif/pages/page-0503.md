# Page 503 (English translation)
**A Strong Memory That Remains Always Remembered:**
"I am the one who sends inspiration and revelation to the hearts of prophets, the righteous, and the good. Every person who reads it, God will grant him such a memory that whatever he hears will all be remembered. And if he writes it and keeps it with him, he will be beloved among all people." Ya Ahlūhab, or Wāh Wāh, and in another narration it is Wāh Wāh or Wāh. Samūdiyā Ṣāliḥīlū Hammam Ajab, or Ḥifā'īl Is'afā'īl—"That is, I am God, Lord of the worlds. Be, and it is." Shāhh Jabbār Bāzurg—"That is, I am the great king. It is by this name that God Almighty created the heavens and the earth, the Throne and the Chair."

**He Will Be Protected from the Evil of Jinn, Humans, and Demons:**
Whoever possesses these names will be protected from the evil of jinn, humans, and demons. Ya Shamkhaithā, or Rab Yabīj Hanṭā'īl—"That is, I am the essence who, whatever I decree, 'Be,' and it becomes. If I deny it, it will not come into existence. No one among creation has the power to oppose Me."

**Protection from Harm:**
Whoever possesses these names will remain under God's protection, and God will deliver him from every evil. And if these names are breathed upon water and given to a fearful person to drink, their fear will depart." Ya Hayṭalū, or Bārūd, or Tammā Shūmā—"That is, I am the mighty one over the assaults of evils and tribulations. I am the one who breaks and destroys them."

**The Wicked Will Be Scattered:**
Inscribe these names on a stone with an iron stylus, heat the stone in a furnace, then either kill a dog or cast it between two wicked people who have gathered for vice and debauchery, upon whom no effect for gathering will remain. While throwing the stone, recite this verse:
وَاَلْقَيْنَا بَيْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةَ وَالْبَغْضَاءَ اِلٰى يَوْمِ الْقِيٰمَةِ ۚ كُلَّمَا اَوْقَدُوْا نَارًا لِّلْحَرْبِ اَطْفَاَهَا اللّٰهُ اَلشَّيْطَانُ يَوْمَئِذٍ يَنْفَرِقُوْنَ يَا قَرِشِيَا شَرَاِبِيَا بِهِيَا
"And We cast between them enmity and hatred until the Day of Resurrection. Every time they kindled a fire for war, God extinguished it. The Shayṭān on that day they will be scattered. Yā Qarīshīyā Sharābīyā Bihiyā."

**The Enemy Will Not Be Able to See:**
"That is, I am the essence who hides the oppressed from the eyes of the disbelievers. Write this name on sand, sit upon it, and recite this verse: وَجَعَلْنَا مِنْ بَيْنِ اَيْدِيْهِمْ سَدًّا وَّمِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ سَدًّا فَاَغْشَيْنٰهُمْ فَهُمْ لَا يُبْصِرُوْنَ." Then say "Takk Shāhat al-Wujūh,"—"O Lord of faces, blind the sight of rulers and their eyes, and plunge their necks into darkness, until they cannot see me." "Ṣummun bukmun 'umyun fahum lā yubṣirūn." Then remain silent and speak to no one. He will remain hidden from the eyes of enemies until he himself speaks to someone, then he will trample his enemy underfoot.

اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّی اَسْئَلُكَ بِاسْمِكَ الْخَفِیِّ الطَّاهِرِ الْمُطَهَّرِ الْمُبٰرَكِ الْقُدُّوْسِ فَاِنْ مَنْ اَغْطَيْتَهُ بِحَقِّ لُطْفِكَ فَقَدْ غَلَى
"O Allah, I ask You by Your hidden, pure, purified, blessed, holy name—indeed whoever You have granted by the right of Your gentleness has exceeded [all bounds]." [damaged source] "By the fact that within this hidden gentleness of Yours is that You conceal him whom You have concealed in Your hidden gentleness; those people will be concealed."
