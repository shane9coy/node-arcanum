My need, indeed You are over all things All-Powerful. Answer, O servant, with this letter. Blessed are you in and on you, O Tawwāb (the Repentant).
Hayā Sahalgūn 2 Mrīmūth 2 Glory to You, there is no god but You, how great is Your majesty. Glorify Yourself, descended from the Throne to everyone, and whoever seeks help from You is saved. O Allah, grant my need with a thousand years, and there is no strength except by Allah the Exalted the Great. Answer, O angel Marfi'īl, by the right of Sarhīl, Seqīl, Tūsam, Tāh, Mūbah, Lawāb, al-Ajal, al-Wahā. Hayā. Blessed are you in and on you. Amen.

The Letter Tha (ث)

This letter is very beneficial against opponents. When it is written on a silver tablet with the adjuration and the practitioner keeps it with himself, he will be healed, and this letter performs all the functions of the letter Alif. When you write this letter on your palm and recite the adjuration and invocation.

For making someone your lover:

If you strike the chest of a certain person with it, that person will become your lover, and when you write it with the name of a certain person and recite the adjuration, that person will become submissive.

Kings and the wealthy will be obedient:

This letter has full efficacy for making kings and the wealthy obedient. When you take service through this letter, it will complete all your tasks for you. Its invocation is given during the daily prayer, and the adjuration is similar, until the servant appears. Its incense is ladu rūm, and if fought in battle, light it during recitation for forty days and the purpose will be achieved. Its talisman is as follows:

[A rectangular frame containing a central sigil composed of a crossed square shape with stylized loops at the corners, possibly representing the letter ث (Tha'). Around this central figure, within the rectangle, are groups of four small symbols resembling the letter ت (Ta') or ث (Tha') in each quadrant. The letter ث is repeated several times around the central figure and near the borders of the rectangle.]

And its invocation is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I have dwelt in Your power, O Allah, and Your existence in the eternity of eternities, without how or similitude. You created the clot, the leech-like thing, and the chewed morsel, and You clothed the bones with flesh. Ṭ. And You brought forth creation in the breath, and You made the sperm cast into the living womb of the fetus. O Allah, by the torment of the command and the spirit's borrowing: three thigh-words: Thaurat, Thār, Mahjath, Hibraj, Thali' al-shibr fi al-qalb. Answer, O Thirwā, servants of the letter Tha, by creating the second of love and the dwelling, and the ignorant of the night as a dwelling, and the sun and the moon as a reckoning. That is the decree of the Mighty, the Knowing.

Its adjuration is this: Answer, O servant, Ḥamālīl, by the right of Thiyā Nīd, Lamūs, Ḥanṣath, O Hrḥ 2.
