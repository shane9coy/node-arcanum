# Page 171 (English translation)
![Manuscript page 189](../source-text/.shams-al-maarif-pages/shams-al-maarif-Pages-189.jpg)

[Page 189]

And He directed unto the heaven when it was smoke, and said unto it and unto the earth: "Come willingly or unwillingly." They said: "We come willingly." Place for me between such and such.

Translation: Upon so-and-so, son of so-and-so, son of so-and-so, bring down such-and-such punishments and illnesses. Take the name of this punishment and affliction. By the rights of the Names, O Severe in seizure, O Honorable, O Lord, O Compeller, O Inheritor, O Dominant, O Creator, O God, O Firm Seizer, O Master, O the One who remains after the annihilation of creation—according to what He intended and according to what will come to pass by His power—and He is the One whose existence has connection, no beginning nor end. On the Day when God does not humiliate His Prophet ﷺ and the believers, this day is disgrace and humiliation for the disbelievers. O One of severe punishment and seizure, indeed your Lord's seizure is very severe. There will be wailing and lamentation for the wretched, and the tree of Zaqqūm shall be the sustenance of the sinners. O One of overwhelming power, O Dominance, O Incomparable, O Ancient, O One of lofty status—there is no heir to your dominion besides You. O One of irresistible power, He is the One who spoke it, and He is the most truthful of speakers, that the fire shall never be extinguished. So proceed toward the three-branched shade—not that shade shall be of any benefit, nor shall it serve as defense against the heat of the fire. O Inheritor, all affairs return to You. O He who will annihilate all worlds and will call out: "Whose is the sovereignty today?" The sovereignty belongs to God alone, the One and Compeller. Every soul, whether in an outward or inward matter, be it small or great, is compelled to return to You.

O God, bring destruction and punishment upon this person, and bring down all destructions today. I call upon many destructions. O Compeller, Your majesty of greatness is dominant over all things. No one with willpower can prevent Your might. You have adorned the subtle and rare energies within dense bodies with Your supreme majesty, and Truth has made it a true mirror of Your divinity, an appearance of Your power, and an attribute of Your eternity. The One of power belongs to might and divinity, and to Your dominion which You have established according to Your wisdom—and to Your divine decrees and the pattern of all things You have created, no one can comprehend without You, while Your status is lofty and Your sovereignty is exceedingly great. Every movement in the realms of the physical, the malakūt, and the jabarūt is encompassed by Your attribute as the Compeller. Through the favor of Your overwhelming power, O Strong and Capable One, O One who conquers through power, O Magnificent and Exalted One, O He who has distinguished the human world above every soul for the worship of God Most High, and who has created the world such that some dominate others—all this so that His power may become manifest and His wisdom become apparent. From Your majesty and power, manifest in this person that which will cause them to oppose me, so their faculties fail, and the spiritual fire before them is extinguished. Indeed Hell is the dwelling place for all disbelievers; We have created for Hell many jinn and humans. O Creator of the heavens and earth, I ask You by that power with which You created dust and the lower cosmos, and by that eternal wisdom upon which You created the heaven when it was smoke, and said to the earth: "Come willingly or by compulsion"—they said: "We have come willingly." I ask that You fulfill my need—so-and-so—and take the name of the need. God willing, it shall be fulfilled.
