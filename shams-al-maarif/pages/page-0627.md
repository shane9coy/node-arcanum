# Page 627 (English translation)
The form of this circle is as follows:

Jibreel Hay Qayyum | The Circle of the Greatest Name | Israfel Aziz Munkir

[A round figure with several concentric rings. In the outermost ring there is small script written with repetition. In the inner rings there is repetition of the word "Allah" and "La ilaha illa Allah" is written. In the center of the figure is Surah al-Fatiha, beginning with "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim" and the verses are written in circular form.]

The Hidden Circle [left side of the figure]
The Veiled Circle [right side of the figure]

Michael Jabiril Jameel | Azrael Shadeed Muntaqim

P. 260

### This circle is for every illness, subjugation, and all needs:

Khwarazmi writes that when I had completed its practice, I inquired from the saint about its properties. He said: The properties of this circle are without limit and endless. Among them is this property: when you need to meet a king or ruler, write this circle with musk, saffron, and camphor on white silk cloth, give it incense of frankincense, recite these names and keep them with you, then go to that king. Almighty God will make him kind to you and will put love for you in the hearts of all creation. Whoever sees you will be pleased with you. And if it is written with rose and saffron on a deer's membrane and a pregnant woman ties it on her belly, she will give birth easily.

### Protection from epilepsy, madness, and several other illnesses:

If someone suffering from epilepsy or madness or a weak person keeps this with him, Almighty God will give him well-being. Those afflicted by flatulence and bilious disorders are also healed by it. If written on a glass with rose and saffron and given to drink to the patient, he will be cured. For love, write it on Friday and keep it with you. For attracting benefit, warding off harm, obtaining blessings, and curing diseases, write it on deer's membrane in all circumstances.

### By reciting this same Greatest Name, Isa, upon him be peace, would resurrect the dead:

Through the blessings of this name, Isa, upon him be peace, would resurrect the dead—such is its great majesty. The method is to write it and place it before your prayer mat in your private chamber, then continue reciting its dhikr until the tongue flows freely, the effects of which are complete according to the ascetics [the text is unclear due to watermark in the lower portion of the image].
