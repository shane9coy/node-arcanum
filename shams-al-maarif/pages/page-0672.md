And its invocation (dhikr) is as follows:

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ o اَللّٰهُمَّ أَنْتَ الْعَالِمُ الْعَلِیْمُ عَلَّامُ الْغُیُوْبِ وَ عَالِمُ دَقَائِقِ الْأَسْرَارِ وَ الْخَفِیَّاتِ الْمُحْصِیْ لِكُلِّ ذَرَّةٍ تَفْصِیْلَ الْمُؤْتَلِفَاتِ بِمَا قَدَّرْتَ وَ رَتَّبْتَ فِي الظَّاهِرِ وَ الْبَاطِنِ مِنَ الْمَوْجُوْدَاتِ أَسْئَلُكَ بِإِحَاطَةِ عِلْمِكَ وَ تَفْصِیْلِ شَكْلِ قِدَمِكَ وَ نُفُوْذِ قَدَرِكَ وَ بِخِطَابِكَ بِأَنْوَارِ اِرْتِقَابِ حِكْمَتِكَ أَنْ تَخْرِقَ فِیْمَا بَیْنِیْ وَ بَیْنَكَ الْحِجَابَ لِأَطَّلِعَ عَلٰی مَا تَحْتَ ذَرَّةٍ مِنْ ذَرَّاتِ الْوُجُوْدِ فَأَبْتَهِجَ بِسِرِّ الْقِدَمِ وَ تَنْزِلَ عَلَیَّ الْقَدَمُ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا حَكِيْمُ اَسْئَلُكَ بِسِرِّ قُوَّتِكَ اَنْ تُسَخِّرَ لِیْ عِنْدَكَ عَنَانِیْلَ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَامُ لِیَقْضِیْ حَاجَتِیْ وَ یَكُوْنَ عَوْنًا لِیْ فِیْمَا اُرِيْدُ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا عَلِیْمُ يَا حَكِيْمُ

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O God, You are the Knower, the All-Knowing, the Knower of hidden things and the One who knows the details of secrets and concealed matters. You enumerate for every atom the specifics of compounds according to what You have decreed and arranged in the manifest and the hidden of all existing things. I ask You by the encompassing of Your knowledge, the detailing of the form of Your foot,1 the piercing of Your decree, and by Your addressing with the lights of awaiting Your wisdom, that You tear apart between me and You the veil so that I may look upon what is beneath an atom among the atoms of existence. Then I shall rejoice in the mystery of the foot, and may the foot descend upon me, O God, O Wise. I ask You by the mystery of Your power that You subjugate for me before You Ishmaelite, peace be upon him,2 to fulfill my need and be a helper for me in what I desire, O God, O Knower, O Wise.

Elevated ranks are attained through its invocation on the day of Friday:

The servant should continuously perform this invocation from sunrise until the time of prayer on the day of Friday, and having written the client's name around the square, he should keep it with himself. God the Most High shall correct his memory and grant him majestic ranks.

[Heading in a decorative box]
Chapter: The Merits of the Name "Qabid" (The Seizer)

Know that Qabid is He Who seizes the souls from bodies at the time of their transfer, and on the Day of Resurrection He will return them to the bodies. And He is the witness to that thing which did not previously exist, and this is the description of the Originators, and this is the description of Tawhid (divine unity) which brings things into existence. Without any precedent like it, all things appeared from Him, and to Him they shall return, and good and evil are from Him alone.

The Agent, the patient, the speaker, the one turning toward, the accepted one, the First, the Last, the Manifest, and the Hidden—He is all of these: The First, the Last, the Manifest, and the Hidden are names belonging to Him, and all the attributes are directed toward Him. That is, He is the Agent and the patient, and He is the speaker and the one turning toward and the accepted one. And at that time, all these names held one meaning. God the Most High says:

هُوَ الَّذِي يَبْدَؤُا الْخَلْقَ ثُمَّ يُعِيْدُهُ وَهُوَ اَهْوَنُ عَلَيْهِ
كَمَا بَدَاْنَا اَوَّلَ خَلْقٍ نُّعِيْدُهُ

Translation: It is He Who began creation and will repeat it, and it is easier for Him. Just as We began the first creation, We shall repeat it.

---

1. "Foot" (qadam) here refers to divine Footstool or a mystical attribute of God.
2. "Ishmaelite" (ʿInānīl)—likely a magical or spiritual entity name rather than a genealogical reference.
