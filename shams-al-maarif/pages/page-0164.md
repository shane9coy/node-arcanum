[Boxed heading: Chapter: Benefits of the Greatest Name]

Regarding the Greatest Name of God, it has been said that whoever performs the recitation of this Name should read from Surah Hadid to As-Saff, and from "Lu inzalna hadha l-Quran" to the end of Surah Hashr, and then say:
allaghumma ya man huwa kadha wa-la yakuunu ahadun kadha ghayahu an taf'ala biya ma huwa kadha wa-kadha
"O God, You are such, and none besides You is such, so do such and such for me." Some say that if one sincerely makes this supplication for a dead person, by God's command they will come back to life. In this manner it has been narrated that it has been transmitted from the Prophet, may peace be upon him, that after reciting the aforementioned verses, one should say in the supplication:
allaghumma inni as'aluka bi-ismika al-makhzuni l-maknuni t-tahiri l-mutahhari l-muqaddasi l-hayi l-qayyumi r-rahmani r-rahimi dhi l-jalali wa-l-ikram an tusalliya wa-tusallima 'ala sayyidina muhammadin wa-an taf'ala biya ma huwa kadha wa-kadha bi-rahmatika ya arhama r-rahimin
"O God, I ask You by Your hidden, concealed, pure, purifying, sacred name, and by the Living, the Sustaining, the Merciful, the Compassionate, and the Possessor of Majesty and Honor, that You send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, and that You do such and such for me, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful."
