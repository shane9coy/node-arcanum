Know that there are four movements. The first movement is unveiling. The second is the movement of seventy. The movement of unveiling is indeed the essential movement. This is the movement of the intellect, the movement of the first journey, and the movement of the soul. This is the voluntary and journeying movement. The second is the movement of essence, which is the movement of the East.

Thus, the first unveiling is for that first day on which Allah the Exalted created the spirits in the worlds of praise. That is the pavilion of the first journey; on that day He spoke to the intellect in the world of the hidden. These are the primary principles. The third day is for spiritual unveiling, on the day when Allah the Exalted took the covenant. The fourth day is for the second journey, which is the eternal day, but its last breath is unveiling.

Thus, the first unveiling is the first throne and the first journey and the first chair; then the second unveiling is the throne after the second and the chair of eternity. All these states and cycles are of the Rahmanic reality and the Rahimiyyah right. The Rahmanic reality is the crown of beneficence, to which the external traditional coinage relates. In relation to it are the subtle delicacies that are used for books.

The exposition of the crown of majesty: The Noble Prophet of Allah, peace be upon him, spoke thus and gave news from Allah the Exalted, that the veil of seventy is of light and darkness. If you remove them, their rays will burn wherever His gaze reaches. This veil is with respect to you and not with respect to Him, because their existence from both sides is false, and the body is kept in the veil while Allah the Exalted is not with the body. The third matter is that the veil, that is, curtains, for a perishable thing is an imperishable direction, and Allah the Exalted is pure of direction. Thus, what is meant by the veil of darkness is the denial of light, and what is meant by the veil of light is the veil of nakedness, which are from the essences of being. Their reality is that if this matter did not exist, these veils would disappear. Know that the delicate subtleties are those that elevate the universe when the parts of the subtle have not been separated from the parts of the dense.

The Crown of Numbers and the Constitution of Letters:
[This heading is underlined]

I inform you about it. I tell you the mysteries of numbers and the realities of letters. Know that the divine mysteries are divided into two categories: His knowledge of subtleties, densities, supernal things, infernal things, and the spiritual sovereignty. The numbers are in the mysteries of numbers and His manifestations are in the letters.

Thus, numbers are for the knowledge of spiritual things. Letters are for the corporeal ones and spiritual sovereignties. Numbers are the mystery of statements and letters are the mystery of actions. The world of the Throne is numbers and the world of the Chair is letters. Thus, the relationship of letters to numbers is like the relationship of the Chair to the Throne. Numbers have been understood as the power of divine power from the mystery of truth. Thus, Allah the Exalted Himself glorifies with the crown of numbers: "And sufficient are We as accountants" and in this verse is glorification with letters "when the Trumpet is blown" until the final verse. Since the Chair is connected with the encompassing Chair, therefore the rank of the letter has been deferred after numbers. Thus, from the mystery of numbers is understood the mystery of the divine intellect, and from the mystery of letters is understood the mystery of the spirit of spirits. Thus, the last rank of the intellect is the first rank of the supernal soul. That is the overflow of the intellect as the letters of grammar are effaced when effaced. The meaning of the letter is the word. Similarly, what is meant by number is the intellect, and there is between and for every intellect there is a middle and two sides. Thus, from the mystery of letters is understood the mystery of the supreme chair, the vast chair. This is because the essences of the supernal and infernal worlds are different. This is because they are different in the supreme chair, and their intellect and states in the divine chair are revealed from the first principles of the Throne.

Its first rank is from the subtlety of the realities of sovereignty, and the last degree of the infernal is the first degree of the supernal. Know that the world of inspiration is the crown of the intellect, and the second name of the vast chair is the complete intellect, the overflow of the gnostic. Thus, the first overflow is the overflow of majesty, and majesty is the crown of the intellect of letters and numbers entirely. This is the mystery with which individual reality has been acknowledged, and in this verse there is an indication toward it. The divine statement is: "Indeed, I am making upon the earth a vicegerent."
