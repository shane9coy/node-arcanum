This same is indicated in this verse as well:
" Indeed, the righteous will be in gardens and a river, in a seat of truth, with an all-powerful sovereign."
I say that whatever the early saints achieved in the progression of realities, wisdom, and stations of servitude was solely through connection with the names, until each name became the Greatest Name in relation to their station, and the relational attributes and qualities of Allah the Exalted manifested through them. When you hear of the Greatest Name in this discourse, its reality is this: when a person has come to know one name and has beencharacterized by it, then sought nearness with the saints through it, all things obtain assistance through the quality's connection with the name of the Essence, for the Original Presence is the essence, and this is the reality of connection. As Allah the Exalted has said: " Say, 'Allah,' then leave them engaged in their vain discourse." When the seeker ascends with truth-seeking and sincerity upon the path of the names, the reality of the nominal [state] and the mysteries of the divine kingdom will very quickly manifest upon him.

Chapter: The Investigation of the Name and the Sacred Law

People have differed regarding whether a name is derived from the noun or from the object named. There is a subtle indication herein, and know that there are two categories of those who walk toward Allah the Exalted: the seeker of the attribute and the seeker of the subsisting [name]. Here, the seeker of the subsisting — his connection is such that each name is his substitute. Thus, a noun is derived from the verbal noun. If this seeker by virtue [of his rank] progresses in ascension, the names of that degree will call to him, even though he is absorbed in the observation of the lights of manifestation among the meanings of the names. Thus, for him the name is derived from the lights of existence. A noun is derived from elevation. This should also be understood thus: the final [world]'s station is this, and the end of the world is annihilation. Your worldly attributes are few relative to the world, because Allah the Exalted has favored you with His names of deliberate [grace] so that you may fulfill their purpose in the world, as the Righteous Caliph [Abu Bakr] — may Allah be pleased with him — said: "If the seeking of Allah occurs, my certainty would not increase." The second matter is this: if you call upon Him with your own names, then you have called upon the transient with the transient, for when you are subsisting with yourself, you are with the transient, and when you are subsisting with Him, you are with the Eternal. See, then, how slight is the difference between the two. Allah the Exalted has said: "So flee to Allah" — but flee to Allah from your selves and attributes, and He has said that for Allah are the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them. This is the second indication. He mentioned His names with all the people of hearts, as Allah the Exalted says: "Indeed, the Muslim men and Muslim women..." Then He commanded that they remember Him by His names. So you were confounded, and this sea accepted the alteration [by which] the truth said: "What are these names?" Then He taught you the supplication thus: "Allahu Alladhi la ilaha illa hu al-Malk al-Quddus al-Salam al-Mumin al-Muhaymin al-'Aziz al-Jabbar al-Mutakabbir." He gave you knowledge: "Remember these most beautiful names," and the same is indicated in the verse "So glorify with praise of your Lord and ask His forgiveness" — that glorification is with [His] praise, and the seeking of truth is [its] requirement, and indeed these are the meanings of glorification in truth.
The seeker of the attribute is by virtue of the name and the attribute. Thus, a name is a connection with the right of some essential name, and this is the difference between the name and the object named. The glorification of Allah's Majesty is his right — Allah [is] first from the act and after the act is seeking forgiveness, and this is the praise of glorification that occurred that night, when the proof of reality is established, the unveiling of the names in the heart, but in praise there is truth-seeking, which belongs to the people of hearts [damaged source]
