Then connected to it is manifest faith—that is, believing in those deeds upon which forgiveness depends, because Allah the Most High has established a sign for faith by which it becomes known. The saying of Allah the Most High is:

"But Allah has made faith beloved to you and has beautified it in your hearts and has made disbelief, wickedness, and disobedience hateful to you"

Meaning: But Allah alone has made faith beloved to you and has adorned it in your hearts and has made disbelief, sin, and transgression disliked by you. So by this sign, the existence of faith is recognized, and this is in the station of the original innate nature which is the knowledge of the knower regarding the reality of divine particularization.

The prophetic tradition of the Prophet ﷺ also contains this same meaning, that His Eminence ﷺ said: The Holy Spirit has breathed into my heart that no soul will die until it has completed its allotted sustenance. So it is binding upon the reciter of this name to be adorned with outward and inward piety and to turn completely towards God.

**The mention of the Trustee of the Saints, through which they exercise authority in the world by this Name:**

This name is among the invocations of the saints, and whatever authority is exercised by the Name of Allah, this name also exercises, because the numerical values of both are equal. Recite it in seclusion according to the corresponding number. Its client will appear, and the reciter will obtain great knowledge from it. Its dhikr is:

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Trustee, the Guardian of what You have deposited in the details of events and in the world of dominion and the treasuries of the divine kingdom, the Disposer of affairs in the world of the Throne and the Chair and the secrets of the upper worlds. I ask You to bear witness in the station of reliance, and make me bear witness to that in my affairs, from the world of the Throne and the Chair and the secrets of the upper worlds to the world of grants, and that You realize my trust in You and my dependence upon You, so that I may be covered by Your complete covering through my trust in You, and be regarded by Your beautiful names and Your most noble attributes. O Allah, O Trustee, O Lord of the Worlds." o

**Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "The Strong"**
]

Al-Qawiyy is He who possesses complete strength. Know that strength and power are attributes of their possessor, as He has said:

"And Allah was ever Strong, Mighty" and "And Allah has power over all things."

When Allah the Most High created things with the intention He desired and according to the wisdom He ordained, it was from the attribute of their existence.

**Allah created and gave strength to the entire world by the strength of this His Name, and taught the lesson of monotheism:**

Thus He conferred favors upon them with the strength of the All-Glorious, and the breaths of life He placed within them were from the same lights upon which the vibrations and influences were established at the point. Then He created the Throne with its grandeur and loftiness by His divine wisdom and majesty [illegible/watermark]
