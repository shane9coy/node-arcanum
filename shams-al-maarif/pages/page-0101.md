[Heading in a decorative frame: Chapter: On the description of the auxiliaries of the four directions]

The auxiliaries of the Lord of the East are Damāʾīl, Ḥamāʾīl, and Samāʾīl. The auxiliaries of the Lord of the West are Jarmbīl, Muṣāʾīl, and Ramāʾīl. The auxiliaries of the Lord of the North are Firdāʾīl and Rifāʾīl. The auxiliaries of the Lord of the South are Samāʾīl, Madjīʾāʾīl, and Ḥarbāʾīlāʾīl.

[Heading in a decorative frame: Chapter: On names and invocations whose need is in every thing of worldly life]

When you are in the chapter of spring and you wish to recite the Fātiḥa invocation, recite this:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ o اَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْكَ يَا لَيَالِيْلُ وَ اَعْوَانُكَ فَرْحُوْيِيْلُ وَ طَاحُوْلُ وَالرَّبَّاحُ وَمَاسُوْلُ وَمَيْسُوْرُ وَ سَمَاوَطَشُ وَ عَلَى النَّفْسِ وَالْقَبْرِ مَا حَفَتْ بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ وَ بِاسْمِهِ الْمُؤَيَّدِ رَبِّ الْاٰخِرَةِ وَالْاُوْلٰی لَا غَايَةَ لَهٗ وَلَا مُنْتَهٰى لَهٗ مَا فِي السَّمٰوٰتِ وَمَا فِي الْاَرْضِ وَ مَا بَيْنَهُمَا وَمَا تَحْتَ الثَّرٰی اَللّٰهُمَّ غَالِبُ الْاَعْدَاءِ دَآئِمُ النَّعْمَآءِ رَحِيْمُ الرُّحَمَاءِ قَادِرٌ غَيْرُ مَقْدُوْرٍ وَّ قَاهِرٌ غَيْرُ مَقْهُوْرٍ عَادِلٌ يَوْمَ الْحَشْرِ وَالنُّشُوْرِ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَ الْعَلِيْمُ الْحَکِيْمُ الرَّحْمٰنُ الرَّحِيْمُ هُوَ اللّٰهُ الَّذِي لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَ الْمَلِكُ الْقُدُّوْسُ اِلٰی اٰخِرِھَا اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّی اَسْاَلُکَ یَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِیْنَ بِاسْمِكَ التَّامِّ يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّوْمُ اَشْهَدُ اَنَّ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ دُوْنَ اللّٰهِ بَاطِلٌ اٰمَنْتُ بِكَ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ لَا رَبَّ سِوَاكَ بِاسْمِكَ الْعَظِيْمِ الَّذِيْ فَضَّلْتَهٗ عَلٰی جَمِيْعِ اَسْمَآئِكَ كُلِّهَا اَنْ تَسْتَخْرَ لِيْ صَاحِبَ الدَّعْوَةِ وَ صَاحِبَ النَّافِزَةِ وَالنَّوَاحِي الْاَرْبَعَةِ يَكُوْنُوْنَ عَوْنًا لِيْ فِيْ قَضَآءِ حَاجَتِيْ بِادْنِكَ يَا اَللّٰهُ اِمَنِ انَّكَ اَنْتَ الْمُعِيْنُ وَ لَا يَقْضِيْ عَلَيْكَ اَجِيْبُوْا يَا مَعَاشِرَ الْاَرْوَاحِ وَافْعَلُوْا حَاجَتِيْ بِحَقِّ مَنْ لَهُ الْعِزَّةُ وَالْجَبَرُوْتُ وَ بِحَقِّ الْحَيِّ الَّذِي لَا يَمُوْتُ لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهٖ شَيْئٌ الْقَائِمُ الَّذِيْ اِسْمُهٗ لَا يُنْسٰی وَ نُوْرُهٗ لَا يُطْفٰی وَ عَرْشُهٗ لَا تَزُوْلُ وَ كُرْسِيُّهٗ لَا يَتَحَرَّكُ اَنْزَلَ عَلٰی عَبْدِهِ الْكِتَابَ اَسْاَلُکَ يَا اَللّٰهُ الَّذِيْ لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا هُوَ مَالِكُ الدُّنْيَا وَالْاٰخِرَةِ اَسْاَلُكَ اَنْ تَقْضِيَ حَاجَتِيْ وَاَنْ تُسَخِّرَ لِيْ رُوْحَانِيَّةَ خُدَّامِ هٰذِهِ الْاَسْمَاءِ اِنَّكَ عَلٰی كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ

"In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. I adjure you, O Layālīl, and your auxiliaries Farḫuwīl, Ṭāḥūl, al-Rabbāḥ, Māsūl, Maysūr, Samāwaṭash, and by the soul and the grave what is encompassed by the name of Allah and by His name the confirmed, Lord of the Hereafter and the First, to whom there is no end and no limit, whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth and whatever is between them and whatever is beneath the-Tharā (dust), O Allah, dominant over enemies, perpetual in blessings, merciful among the merciful, powerful without being overpowered, victorious without being defeated, just on the Day of Gathering and Resurrection, there is no god but He, the Knowing, the Wise, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. He is Allah beside whom there is no god, the King, the Holy, unto its end. O Allah, verily I ask You, O Lord of the Worlds, by Your complete name, O Living, O Eternal, I bear witness that everything other than Allah is vanity. I have believed in You, there is no god but You, there is no Lord beside You, by Your great name which You have preferred above all Your names entirely, that You make subservient to me the owner of the invocation and the owner of the rising and the four corners, that they may be helpers for me in the fulfillment of my need, by Your grace, O Allah. I believe that You are the Helper and no one prevails over You. Answer, O company of spirits, and do my need, by the right of Him to whom belongs majesty and omnipotence, and by the right of the Living who does not die, there is nothing like unto Him, the Standing One whose name is not forgotten and whose light is not extinguished and whose Throne does not shake and whose Chair does not move. He sent down upon His servant the Book. I ask You, O Allah, beside whom there is no god, Master of this world and the Hereafter, I ask You that You fulfill my need and that You make subservient to me the spirituality of the servants of these names. Indeed You are over all things capable."

"With the name of Allah who is the Great, the Merciful, and the Most Merciful. I adjure You, O Layālīl, and with Your auxiliaries Farḫuʾīl, Ṭāḥūlī, Samāḥ, Māsaḥal, Maysūr, etc., by the name of Allah who is the Mighty, Lord of the Hereafter and the First. There is no end to Him and no limit. His is whatever is in the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them and whatever is beneath the-Tharā. Allah is Knowing, dominant over enemies, perpetually bestowing blessings, merciful to the merciful. No one has power over Him. He is the Dominant over whom no one is dominant. On the Day of Gathering and Resurrection He will act with justice. There is no deity besides Him; He is Knowing, Wise, Raḥmān and Raḥīm. He is Allah besides whom there is no deity. He is the Sovereign, the Holy. O Lord of the Worlds, I ask You with Your complete name, O Living, O Eternal. I bear witness that everything other than Allah is vanity. I have brought faith in You; there is no god but You; there is no Lord besides You. By Your great [illegible]
