He presented it. You took it and you began to explain its interpretation, which I could not understand. Only this much did I understand: that you placed your witness finger on the letter jīm, which is in the angle of the triangle in the upper right half of the circle, and said that from here glory is produced. Then I knew that this is the Greatest Name of God and that it points to the Names of the Sacred Essence. I said, "Commander of the Faithful, I did not understand what you said." You said, "Muḥammad ibn Ṭalḥa will explain it to you." I had barely woken up when I came to Tikīlpūr and reached Muhammad ibn Ṭalḥa. He was my brother in faith. I narrated the entire story to him. He praised God Almighty, then began to explain the interpretation of the circle to me. He named it "The Well-Arranged Pearl in Explaining the Greatest Name" (Durru al-Munaẓẓamu fī Sharḥi al-Ismi al-A'ẓam). In another narration, it was also called "The Secret of the Greatest Name" (Fī Sirri al-A'ẓam). They say: after this, I saw the Prophet, peace be upon him, in a dream, appearing in the prayer niche. Ḥaḍrat ʿAlī, may God be pleased with him, was reciting the Greatest Name. He said to me: "The Sacred Name has not ceased to indicate others in its meaning." His Eminence the Prophet Muḥammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: "By the truth of God Most High, this is how Jibrāʾīl taught me." Then when I woke from the dream, I went to the sheikh and narrated the entire story to him. He remained silent for a while, then placing his hand behind him, he produced a paper on which these very words were written verbatim, namely: al-ismu al-muqaddasu lam yatawaqqafi ʿalā ghayrihi fī al-dalāla. I saw him and said: "Why do you not write it with explanation?" He said: "I thought that no one else would know it besides me." Then he recited istighfār and wrote it down. This is something that no one knows except the people of sincerity and purity, because it is the Greatest Name and the ancient secret. If you know it, the jinn and humans will obey you. Whoever recognizes it with a good heart and fears God Most High in secret and in public, by God's command success will be achieved in all your affairs. The form of the circle is this:

The letters of the triangle's angles

You should know that the letters that are in the angles of the triangle are the Names of the Seven Numbers, and they are the first letters of the Name of the Oneness (Aḥad) in this way: alif, bā', jīm, dāl, hā', wāw, zāy. And [unclear] is its first letter, which [unclear] for [unclear]. You say: yā allāh, yā bāʿith, yā jalīl, yā wājid, yā dayyān, yā hādī, yā ṭāhir. These seven names are called the Greatest Name.

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful.
