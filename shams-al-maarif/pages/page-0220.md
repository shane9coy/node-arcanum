# Page 220 (English translation)
O Allah, I ask You by Your elevated Name which You gave to whoever You willed among Your allies and inspired in Your chosen ones among Your beloved ones. I ask You, O Allah, to bring me sustenance from Your presence that may satisfy my poverty and repair my broken state and sever the bonds of Satan from my heart, for You are Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Sovereign, the Reckoner, the All-Giving, the Provider, the Victor, the Most High, the All-Knowing, the One Who Seizes, the One Who Stretches, the Abaser, the Exalter, the Bestower of Honor, the Humiliator, the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing, the Judge, the Just, the Most Subtle, the All-Aware, the Sufficer, the Self-Sufficient, the Greatest, the Most Generous, the Giver, the Provider, the Vast, the Most Thankful, Possessor of Grace and Blessings and Generosity and Honor. O Allah, I ask You by Your right and by the right of Your right and by Your goodness, O You of ancient goodness, O You whose goodness exceeds every goodness, O Master of this world and the next, O You who fulfill Your promise—there is no god except You. Glory be to You; truly I was among the wrongdoers.

O Allah, ease for me my sustenance from what is lawful and make it my portion. O Allah, answer my supplication by the right of the Surah of the Occurrence [al-Waqi'ah] and by the right of Your great Name and by the sanctity of our master and prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and upon his family, the pure and clean ones, and upon all his companions, and by the right of a powerful, victorious, providing, capable, returning [to mercy], Best of Providers, suffice me, the miserable, the poor, the seeing reward, not to be stoned with deprivation. O Allah, ease for me a lawful, pure sustenance and gather between me and it from Your lawful [provisions] and make it my portion in what is lawful, O Possessor of Glory and Generosity, in this hour, O Allah, O Sufficer, O Guarantor, O Trustee, help me with Your hidden gentleness, O Most Generous, O Most Merciful. O Allah, suffice me with Your lawful [provisions] instead of Your unlawful [ones], and with Your obedience instead of Your disobedience, and with Your grace instead of everyone besides You, O Allah, O Lord of mercy for this world and the next and their Lord of mercy, O Lord of the worlds. Entrust yourselves, O servants of this noble Surah, with all that I have commanded you and that which I have appointed you over, by the right of Ahya Sharahiya Adonai Asbat Aal Shaddai. I ask You, O Allah, to bless our master Muhammad and his companions and grant them abundant peace.

O Allah, I ask You—O Allah, I ask You, there is one Allah, O One, O Unique, O Self-Sufficient, O Allah, O He who establishes all, O Creator of the heavens and the earth, O Possessor of Glory and Generosity, O Sovereign, O Self-Sufficient, O One who makes self-sufficient, O Beloved, O Possessor of hidden grace, O Possessor of radiant light. Allah is the essence for whom greatness and magnificence belong; He is the wise and luminous One, the One of exalted honor. Allah is the essence who created every light with His own light. O servants of this Surah, O servants of Allah's greatest and most supreme Name, accept for the conquest of hearts of creation and for obtaining sustenance for me, with personal love move the spirituality of love. By this Name of Allah whose light illuminated the worlds, before whose greatness necks became humbled, whose awe shattered mountains into pieces. The face of the earth performed glorification with His praise, and the angels [performed glorification] from fear of Him.

Such is Allah, that essence; there is no deity except Him, and He is the Lord of the Great Throne. O Allah, I ask You by this elevated Name with which You granted knowledge to Your prophets and inspired it among Your chosen ones from among Your beloved ones. O Allah, I ask You to grant me such sustenance that may make me self-sufficient from Your presence and repair my broken state and sever the bonds of Satan from my heart. You are Allah, the Compassionate, the Sovereign, the Reckoner, the All-Giving, the Provider, the Victor, the All-Knowing, the One Who Seizes, the One Who Stretches, the Abaser, the Exalter, the Bestower of Honor...
