# Page 44 (English translation)
The Invocation of the Letter Fa

The invocation of the letter Alif is that it should be read one hundred and eleven times from the aforementioned number:

"Glory be to You; there is no deity except You, O Lord of all things and its inheritor, O God of all gods, exalted is Your glory, O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Originator of the heavens and earth."

And measure upon it every appropriate action and deed. You will succeed in your endeavor. Allah the Most High is the granter of success, and if the actions are for noble purposes rather than mischief, then work according to the mentioned composition and you will succeed.

"And Allah speaks the truth, and He guides to the way."

2. Batin

The second mansion is Batin, which is the letter Ba (ب). When the Moon descends into this mansion, a spiritual force emerges from it by divine command and rectifies wrath and anger. Movement is produced in the hearts of great people, leaders, and rulers because this mansion is in the sign of Aries and is the degree of the Sun. The Sun attains its exaltation when it reaches the twenty-first degree of this same sign on April fourth. The Sun is auspicious, and its temperament is hot and dry. For this reason, actions are performed for its exaltation in order to subjugate rulers, go to them, and obtain purposes and needs from them, because the actions of this hour are extremely suitable for love, subjugation of hearts, pacification of disputes, and alchemical operations.

3. Thurayya

The third mansion is Thurayya. Its letter is Jim (ج). When the Moon descends into it, a spirituality mixed with heat, moisture, and cold is produced from it. This spirituality is of intermediate degree. It is good for meeting nobles and obtaining benefit from rulers of the world and men of the pen, because in Thurayya the conjunction of all stars occurs.
