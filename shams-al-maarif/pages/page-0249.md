[He] who does not find anyone else for his need, seeks from you and inclines toward you, so-and-so, and taking the name of his need, he says:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-kulli ismin huwa laka sammayta bihi nafsa-ka aw anzaltahu fī kitābi-ka aw 'allamtahu aḥadan min khalqi-ka aw ista'thara bihi fī 'ilmi l-ghaybi 'indaka an taj'ala lī min amrī farijan wa makhrajan wa bayānan thābitan wa an taqḍiya ḥājatī.

"O Allah, I ask You by every name that belongs to You, which You have attributed to Yourself, or revealed in Your Book, or taught to one of Your creation, or preferred in the knowledge of the unseen with You—that You make for me in my affair an opening and a way out, and a clear statement, and that You fulfill my need."

"O Allah, I ask You by all Your names—by which You have named Yourself, or which You have revealed in Your Book, or which You have taught to one of Your creation, or which You have preferred in the knowledge of the unseen with You—that You create for me from my affair ease and expansion and a clear statement, and that You fulfill my need." And let him mention his need, whether it is acceptance of love or some difficult matter, and let him intend to know its outcome. One should fumigate this paper with incense and spittle, then wrap it and seal it with wax, place it in a clay vessel, seal it well from above, bind it tightly with thread, and throw it into flowing water. Then let him say: "Answer, O [He who answers] the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so"—that "I have released the heart of so-and-so son of so-and-so"—or fill a vessel with water, place it in it, and keep it at his head. If he remembers [the ritual] slightly, the need will be fulfilled.

[Boxed heading: Chapter: Works effective for protection from thieves, bandits, oppressors, and from fear and affliction]

This supplication is transmitted from Abd Allāh ibn ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, may Allah be pleased with them both. He said: "I have not seen any supplication accepted more quickly than this one. It protects from thieves, bandits, and the evil of oppressive rulers. One should recite it in fear and affliction." The supplication is:

Bi-smi llāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm Allāhumma yā mawḍi'a kulli shakwā wa yā shāhida kulli najwā wa yā 'ālima kulli khafiyya wa yā kāshifa kulli baliyya wa yā munjiya mūsā wa muḥammadin ṣallā llāhu 'alayhi wa sallama aw ibrāhīma l-khalīli ṣalawātu llāhi wa salāmu-hu 'alayhim ajma'īn ad'ū-ka yā ilāhī du'ā'a mani ishtaddat fāqatuhū wa ḍa'ufat quwwatuhū wa qallat ḥīlatuhū du'ā'a l-gharīqi l-malhūfi alladhī lā yajidu li-kashfi mā bihi illā anta lā ilāha illā anta yā arḥama l-rāḥimīna ikshif 'annā mā nazala binā min 'aduwwinā wa 'aduwwi-ka l-shayṭāni l-rajīmi yā rabba l-ālamīna innaka 'alā kulli shay'in qadīr wa āghūthā yā llāhu Allāhumma yā bādi'u lā badāyata laka yā dā'imu lā nifāda laka yā ḥayyu yā muḥyī l-mawtā yā qā'iman 'alā kulli nafsin bi-mā kasabat ilahanā anta llāhu l-'azīzu l-jabbāru lā ilāha illā anta ilāhan wāḥidan as'aluka bi-l-kalimāti l-tāmmāti l-amni wa l-'afwi wa l-'āfiyyati wa l-mu'āfāhati l-dā'imati fī l-dīni wa l-dunyā wa l-ākhirati wa l-ahli wa l-jasadi wa l-māli wa l-waladi wa l-muslimīna ajma'īna yā rabba l-ālamīna innaka 'alā kulli shay'in qadīr bi-raḥmatika yā arḥama l-rāḥimīna wa-kshi 'annī mā nazala bī min ḍīqin wa kullamā aradtu [damaged source] al-khalāṣa l-'ājila yā rabba l-ālamīna.

"In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, O Place of every complaint, O Witness of every secret conversation, O Knower of every hidden thing, O Remover of every affliction, O Savior of Moses, and Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, or Abraham, the Friend [of Allah], upon them all be Allah's prayers and peace—I call upon You, O my God, with the supplication of one whose distress has intensified, whose strength has weakened, and whose stratagem has diminished, the supplication of the drowning one crying for help who finds no one to remove what afflicts him except You. There is no god but You, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. Remove from us what has befallen us from our enemy and Your enemy, the accursed Satan. O Lord of the Worlds, surely You are over all things Powerful. And I cry for help, O Allah! O Allah, O Originator who has no origin, O Eternal who has no end, O Living, O Resurrector of the dead, O Sustainer over every soul according to its deeds. Our God, You are Allah, the Mighty, the Compeller—there is no god but You, One God. I ask You by the perfect words—for security, and forgiveness, and well-being, and constant protection in religion and world and the hereafter, and for family and body and wealth and children and all Muslims, O Lord of the Worlds. Surely You are over all things Powerful—by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And remove from me what has befallen me of distress, and whenever I wish [damaged source] swift deliverance, O Lord of the Worlds."

You are Allah, the Mighty, the Compeller—there is no god but You, One God.
