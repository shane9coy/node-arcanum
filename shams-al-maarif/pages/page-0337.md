# Page 337 (English translation)
Chapter: Safety in Sea Voyage

Some say that the ring of Solomon, upon him be peace, upon which his kingdom was established, contained the Greatest Name (Ism al-A'ẓam) which was written upon the heart of Adam, upon him be peace. From among some great benefits found in books is this verse: "And they said: Board upon it with the name of Allah, its course and its mooring. Surely my Lord is forgiving, merciful."

Whoever intends sea travel and desires safety should carve this verse upon a piece of wood from a date palm tree and attach it to the front of the ship. And according to one opinion, attach it to the back of the ship, then that ship will remain protected from drowning by the command of Allah the Most High. Ḥasan reports that the Noble Prophet, may peace be upon him, said: "My ummah has protection from drowning when they board a ship and recite: 'In the name of Allah, its course and its mooring,' and recite the verse 'They did not valorize Allah as is His due' to the end. Then stand facing the front of the ship and gesture to the right and left as after the declaration: 'By the command of the Exalted, with "In the name of Allah, its course and its mooring," Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Qāf, our sufficient one, Ḥā Mīm ʿAyn Sīn Sād Qāf, our protection, and Allah surrounds them' to the end of the sūrah."

Ibn ʿAbbās, may Allah be pleased with him, says that whoever boards a ship and recites this supplication: "In the name of Allah, the ship, and 'They did not valorize Allah as is His due,' by your blessings, or 'And they said: Board upon it' — recite this verse. Then Ibn ʿAbbās turned to his companions and said: "Whoever recites this verse and drowns, his blood is upon me." I thank (Allah) and say: "I went to the port of Damascus where twenty-two ships were departing. I boarded one of them and recited these verses. Those ships departed from there; at that time there was a very good wind, but later a storm arose, and no ship was saved on the shore except mine." ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar, may Allah be pleased with him, reports that these verses protect from drowning and destruction. "In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, and 'They did not valorize Allah as is His due,'" and say "And they said: Board upon it. So when you and those with you are firmly aboard the ship, say: Praise be to Allah, who has delivered us from the wrongdoing people. Surely Allah holds the heavens and the earth" to the end, and "And Allah surrounds them" to the end. Ibn ʿAbbās, may Allah be pleased with him, reports that whoever recites this supplication when boarding a ship or vessel will remain protected from drowning. The supplication is: "In the name of Allah, the Sovereign, to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth entirely, and the lofty mountains are lofty, and the overflowing seas are gathered. Preserve me, for You are the best Preserver, and You are the most merciful of the merciful. And 'They did not valorize Allah as is His due'" to the end, "and may Allah bless our master Muḥammad, and his family, and his companions, and grant them abundant peace, and all the prophets."
