Chapter: The Letters of Punishment

According to the people of knowledge, these are seven letters of punishment, and they should be written only for punishment. Begin with the letters of shin according to the arrangement of the days. Doing the opposite yields the opposite meaning. In this way, the supplication "One who takes vengeance, such-and-such" – whatever of punishment you wish, its name should come after the letter. After the day and the sign, recite this supplication:

"By the truth of these names, O Severe, O Mighty, O Unique, O Pure, O Inheritor, O Compeller, O Inheritor, O Originator. O Allah, O Severe, O One, after the annihilation of His creation, concerning the matter He wills and the power He has ordained. O One whose existence has no beginning and no end, O One whom none can approach except by his rank, and for whom there is no cessation. On the day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and those who believe with him. Indeed, disgrace today and evil are upon the disbelievers. O Severe in punishment. Indeed, the punishment of your Lord is severe. As for those who disbelieved, they shall have in the Fire groaning and gasping. Indeed, the tree of Zaqqum is the food of the sinful one, like molten brass, boiling in the bellies, like the boiling of scalding water. O Mighty, O Conqueror, O One who has no equal, and all the inner agonies are with Him. You are the Absolute Mighty, the Eternal, none inherits in your might except You. O Pure in power, O One who said – and He is the most truthful of speakers – 'No! Indeed, it is the Blaze that pulls away the extremities, neither shade nor shelter from the flame.' O Inheritor, You are the One to whom all matter returns. O One who annihilates the worlds and those in them, and calls out: 'To whom belongs the kingdom today?' To Allah, the One, the Conqueror. Everyone who has a call, whether of outward or inward command, whether few or many, returns to You. O Allah, send down upon So-and-so perdition and ruin and punishment and vengeance [Underlined in manuscript: perdition and ruin and punishment and vengeance, do not invoke today]. One perdition, and invoke much perdition. O Compeller, You are the One whose decree is enforced through the path of compulsion and upon everyone; no caution of the cautious one can avert it. And You are the One who has expelled the psychic forces and the cardiac forces from the depths of bodies. None is obligated to this except the One concerning whom it has been revealed, and You have made them a part of Your identity and a manifestation of Your victorious nature, and a description of Your eternity. Indeed, You possess power, majesty, glory, and awe. By the right of Your sovereignty, which You have chosen through precise ordaining and the decrees of Your divinity and the lights of Your knowledge – none knows except You. Exalted are Your affairs and great is Your dominion. Every movement in the world of dominion and sovereignty, and with it You have assisted the meaning of Your name 'Compeller,' by the right of what You have chosen through the best of the noble, eternal arrangement, the Glorious, the Exalted. O You who have chosen the coming world with the movement within it from the directions mixed with the spirit, through the reins of destinies and the manifestation of wisdom. Manifest in So-and-so son of his mother Your majesty and Your conquest, that through which his senses may be stilled at impacts and his spirit may be sheathed when I am present. Indeed, Hell is their promised appointment. And We have certainly created for Hell many of the jinn and mankind. O Originator of the heavens and earth, I ask You by the power with which You have ordained the upper and lower worlds, and by the right of the first word with which You originated the earth and heavens with Your true word: 'Then He directed Himself toward the heaven while it was smoke' – and He said to it and to the earth: 'Come, willingly or unwillingly.' They both said: 'We come willingly.' Do for me such-and-such.
