# Page 360 (English translation)
I take a divination with Your eternal Word, that it may show me what is hidden. O Allah, show me this night all that I have asked You and all that I have not asked You, and make clear to me those matters which I fear and dread. O Allah, if there is good for me, show me whiteness and greenness; but if there is evil toward me or against me, show it as blackness and redness, and send me a servant from the servants of this noble verse—Ayat al-Kursi—who may inform me in my dream what is concealed from me. O Allah, You are the Truth; show me the Truth. Surely You are over all things Able.

Then speak the name of your need, perform the prayer and glorification, sleep on your right side, and recite durood and salaam upon the blessed presence of the Holy Prophet ﷺ according to your ability, then sleep. The news will certainly become known—whether something is good or evil. If it is not known on the first night, perform the same practice on the second night. If it is still not known, perform it on the third night. Keep the intention pure; you will certainly succeed. Allah the Most High guides whom He wills to the straight path. When you obtain this, treasure it; this is a great blessing given to you—a blessing that will make you heedless of many dangers.

**Success in Abandoning Love and Affection:**

Among the special properties of Ayat al-Kursi is that if someone harbors love and affection for another and fears disgrace from the manifestation of this love, they should write Ayat al-Kursi in a china dish, writing it with saffron and rosewater, and along with the name of the person whose love they wish to forget, place it under the shade of the stars at night and drink it in the morning with head bowed. After drinking it for three days, their condition will improve. O such a person, they will forget—their love will be removed from their heart. Whoever has a good intention will achieve their purpose. May Allah the Most High grant me and you understanding of His obedience and the mysteries of His names. The special properties and benefits of Ayat al-Kursi are very numerous; for the fearful person, it has great benefit.

**A Proven Practice for Tranquility of Heart, Palpitation, Anxiety, and Liver Pain:**

If one performs the recitation of Ayat al-Kursi, their heart will find tranquility. Also, for heart pain, palpitation, anxiety, and liver pain, it is beneficial to write this verse and drink it, as mentioned previously. Write it in a clean vessel and drink it for three days along with prayer, making the intention at the time of drinking: "I intend to seek healing from the following ailment"—one should also mention the name of the disease. Allah the Most High will grant healing through the blessing of this noble verse. Allah the Most High is the Healer and the Pardoning One. For the disease of the spleen, write it and bind it upon the stomach; the pain will depart and the spleen disease will disappear.

**Treatment for Half-Headache and Complete Headache:**

For half-headache and complete headache, if possible, place it upon the open [right] palm and place the hand upon the head, and recite along with it: "Law anzalnaa hadha al-Qur'aana 'alaa jabalin" [unclear: until] "aakhiru sooratin" [unclear: through] "Dhaalika maa sakana fi al-layli wa al-nahaari wa huwa as-samii' al-'aleem" "Be still, O headache and throbbing, from the bearer of this my book, by the secret of 'We have sent down the Remembrance, and surely We are its Guardian.'"
