And by the meanings of the sounds and what You have deposited of the surplus of times, that You deliver me from the influence of the catastrophes of Satan, and repel his companion, and protect me from the severity of his veil, and from the spreading of his word and his reception; and that You overtake me with an eternal mercy from Your Oneness, leading to Your Side, complete in itself, obtained through its action, swift in its return, and by the existence from which tawhid descends with the special characteristics of magnification and glorification, O Possessor of gentle kindness, O Possessor of vast mercy upon the strong and the weak. I ask You by every name that belongs to You, by which You have named Yourself, and by which You have sent it down in Your Book, or taught it to one of Your creation, or kept it exclusively for Yourself in the knowledge of the unseen with You, that You repel from me calamities, and that You bring forth for me the extended existences from my circle, He is in hardship and distress—and You are the one who grants success with the most noble triumph, O Lord of the worlds.

[Boxed Heading: The Domination of the Dependent of the Name of the King and its Benefits]

The Name of the King is a great name; the name of its dependent is Qasīl, who is the greatest of the dependents among the angels of mercy. His garment is green. Under him are four rulers, each of whom is a ruler over one lakh (100,000) ranks, and in each rank there are a thousand dependents. When the one who makes remembrance performs the dhikr of this name, this dependent comes to him and fulfills his need, especially at the time when there is an intention for a matter in the heart. Instead of benefit, the operator will suffer severe loss of wealth and life in evil matters. Let this matter be well committed to memory. The supplication of the Name of the King is this:

O King, You are the one who has taken ownership of the necks of the tyrants by overwhelming strength and dominating power, and You are the vanquisher of kings and dominions, Possessor of the elevations and the orbits. You give Your goodness to whoever deserves it. I have come to You asking You by what You have spread in Your kingdom and Your grandeur, and by what You have disseminated in the grandeur of Your kingdom, and by what You have kept exclusively for Yourself in the worlds of holiness—let not Your creatures be humiliated—and by what You have concealed from the comprehension of the intelligent in the secret of the glories of Your mercy, and by what You have encompassed in the secret of Your secret in the rolling of the weighted sphere, and by what You have distinguished of symbols and gestures in the various kinds of hidden words stored in the interior of the interiors of eternity, that You guard me with Your noble protection from the sounds of Satan and his whisperings and his promptings, and from him who is bolder—my father al-Ḥārith—who made good into evil, and the sea into land, and benefit into harm, and the slight extraction from his layers, and the cunning of his stratagem and his deceit. And O You whose Throne is upon the water, according to what You know, and whose Chair is the action according to His will, grant me by Your universal gentleness and Your immense generosity a share of the Lord of the lights of the elevations, and honor me with Your perfect words in the refined attributes, so that the crime of ours may not be mentioned of the gnostics and the distinguished, and grant me from You gnosis in the essence of oneness, and a dominion that does not pass away, and a purity that does not perish—the ancient attributes of Your description, and a description that does not change, and speech from Your eternal knowledge, by which it does not shorten and does not diminish in summary or in detail. O Allāh, O Allāh, O Allāh. Allāh is sufficient for us and the best guardian. I ask You with the asking of a servant, humble, poor, submissive, who does not seek ambitions—the extraction of the much from the little, and the deep from the shallow, and the noble from the glorious, and the brief from the lengthy—and honor and the like. Amen, O Lord of the worlds. And the affair belongs to Allāh, and it is by wisdom [damaged source].
