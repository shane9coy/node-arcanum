# Page 170 (English translation)
Chapter: The Writing of Destructive Letters

According to one opinion, these seven letters are for causing torment and affliction. If you wish to write them for this purpose, these seven letters should be written as follows: first write the letter shin, then arrange the rest according to their order and the arrangement of their letters, and recite this prayer:

"O one who has caused to befall so-and-so son of so-and-so, or so-and-so daughter of so-and-so, a matter such-and-such—torment and sickness—by the right of Halum of the names, O Severe, O Mighty, O Reward, O Pure, O Inheritor, O Compeller, O Conqueror. O Allah, O Severe, O Reward, after the destruction of His creation, upon the matter He wills and the decree He has ordained. O one to whom existence has no connection and no end, O one who has no beginning and no cessation—on the day when Allah will not disgrace the Prophet and those who believe with him, surely the disgrace today and the evil are upon the disbelievers. O severe in punishment and retribution, surely the grasp of your Lord is severe. As for those who disbelieved, they are in the Fire, for them therein is wailing and groaning. Surely the tree of Zaqqum is the food of the sinful. O Mighty, O Victorious, O one to whom there is no limit, O one to whom generosity belongs—to the lowest—none assists you in your might but You. O Pure in power, O one who speaks—and He is the most truthful of speakers—'No, surely it is a blazing Flame, plucking away the skin, no shade and no protection from the flame.' O Inheritor of the throne, to whom all affairs return. O one who destroys the worlds and what is in them, and calls out: 'To whom belongs the kingdom today?' To Allah, the One, the Conqueror. And all are humbled to Him—the invocation, whether from the inward or the outward, little or much, returns to You purely. O Allah, descend upon so-and-so the piercing, and the ruin and the torment."

"Do not call upon ruin today as one, but call upon ruin in abundance. O Compeller, You are the one whose judgment is implemented through compulsion upon everyone—nothing repels it from the one who fears. And You are the one who bound the psychic forces and the cardiac forces within the depths of bodies by Your supreme might, which You regard as Your right, and made it the elite of Your divinity and the manifestation by Your power of compulsion and the attribute of Your eternity. And to You belongs power, might, glory, and divinity. And by the force of Your sovereign realm, which You have taken by the very essence of Your ordinations and the judgments of Your divinity and the lights of Your prohibitions, of which none knows except You—exalted is Your state and great is Your sovereignty. Every movement in the realm of dominion, sovereignty, and might is encompassed by the meaning of Your name 'the Compeller.' By the right of Your might, O Director of the eternal, glorious, exalted ordaining. O one who has compelled the human world by a movement—with the confusion within it mixed with the spirit—by the reins of destinies and permission, except my Lord. The compulsion of the world loosens some of it from another by the force of compulsion and the manifestation of wisdom. Manifest in so-and-so from the intensity of Your might and Your compulsion what will settle his senses upon the truths and extinguish his spirit upon occurrence. Surely Hell is placed as an ambush for the rebellious—a resort wherein they will linger for ages, from jinn and humans. O Conqueror of the heavens and earth, I ask You by the power with which You created the upper and lower worlds, and by the right of the first greatness with which You have conquered—"
