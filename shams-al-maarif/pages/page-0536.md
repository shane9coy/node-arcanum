# Page 536 (English translation)
Section on the letter Rā'. Its number is 200, and its expansion is Rā-Alif-Fā-Tā, their total 28. When multiplied, it yields 238. When this is subtracted, Diftāyil is produced. When one returns to the inner calculation, there were 200. Multiplying them twice produces Aṭṭāyil, and the apparent one is Mūjākal-Rāyil, and it is called istinkā' of Ḥārith.
Section on the explanation of the letter Shīn. Its number is 300, and its expansion is Sh-Y-N, and its kassra is Sh-Y-N-Ā; in total 41 letters. When multiplied, 404 is yielded. When this is calculated, Siāyil Mūjākil appears, and by the inner calculation, when the number is multiplied by the letters of Ẓafar Jad, 900 is yielded; Ḥiyāyil appeared. When one returns to the first, Dāliyāyil appeared.
Section on the explanation of the letter Tā'. Its number is 400, and its expansion is Tā, and the expansion of letters is 28. When these are multiplied among themselves, 824 is yielded. When this is calculated, Waqtiyā Tāyil is produced. The inner aspect is as follows: when the original number is multiplied by the letter Ẓafar Jad, 80 is obtained. Burṭiyā Tāyil appeared. Then when one returns to the first, Danrātāyil occurred.
Section on the explanation of the letter Thā'. Its number is 500, and its expansion is Thā, and its kassra is Thā-Alif; the total number is 316. When this is subtracted, 215 remain. Dastāyil appeared, and in the inner, when the number is multiplied by the expansion, it becomes 100. Then istinkā' is performed: Ruḥbtāyil appeared, and when Pardāsi is made rational, Datarnā Tāyil appeared.
Section on the explanation of the letter Khā'. Its number is 600. When multiplied and istinkā' is performed, 216 is yielded. In the inner, when the original number is multiplied by the first expansion, it becomes 100; Datarnā Tāyil occurred.
Section on the explanation of the letter Dhāl. Its number is 700, and its takhsīr is Dhāl-Alif-Mīm, and its expansion is 88. When multiplied, 425 is yielded. When these are subtracted, Luḍiyāyil appeared. When the number is multiplied by the original words, 448 is yielded, and Ḥaniyāyil is produced.
Section on the explanation of the letter Đād. These are Ẓalmānī letters. Its number is 800, and its takhsīr is Đād, and its expansion is Đād-Alif-Waw; the total number is 45. When multiplied, 425 is yielded. When this is subtracted, Mustaniyāyil occurred. Then the original number is multiplied by the original letters, yielding 24. Ḥaqbayīl is produced from this. Then istinkā' is performed on the original number; Aṭrāyil is produced.
Section on the explanation of the letter Ẓā'. This is a Ẓalmānī letter. Its number is 900, and its expansion is 26. When multiplied, 225 is yielded. When this is calculated, Livāyil is produced. In the inner, after the aforementioned number was 900, when multiplied by letters, 800 is yielded. Sufiyāyil appeared, and from the first number, Ḥukpayil appeared.
Section on the explanation of the letter Ghayn. Its number is 1000, and its expansion is Gh-Y-N-Ā; in total 22. When multiplied, 304 is yielded. Dirāyil appeared. In the inner, when letters are multiplied with Milāy, twenty-three thousand is yielded. In Plato's doctrine, according to its conformity, it is made rational: Ḥittiyāyil occurred, and according to our doctrine's conformity, Jayyāyil is [the name]. Understand this well with thorough verification, if God wills; you will be successful. As we have previously explained, that the name from the letter Alif has an appropriate numerical correspondence. Similarly, for every letter, one appropriate divine name from among the divine names is extracted, and together with this letter, it will serve as this Supreme Name. We have explained these topics with the utmost clarity. Whoever God wills will obtain benefits from them.
