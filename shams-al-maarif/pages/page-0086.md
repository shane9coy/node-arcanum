# Page 86 (English translation)
The Truth of Faith and the Mysteries of Divine Lights

Whoever writes its sirhad while keeping a forty-day fast, with complete purity, facing the qiblah, with the mention of the name "Mad al-Nazar" while the Moon is in Sa'd and the Sun is in hearing, no harm shall befall their operator. The truths of faith and divine lights shall manifest. They shall remain safe from all harm. Whoever keeps the fast on Friday and mentions it, all their needs shall be fulfilled by God's command. Any craftsman who writes it and keeps it with them, God Most High shall make sustenance so easy for them that they shall not even imagine it. It also has the special property of uniting hearts and generating affection and love. Its form and shape shall come after the transfer (death).

Know that the person upon whom the mysteries of the Mim have been unveiled observes the wonders of the world. Whoever needs to sharpen their memory should sit on Thursday facing the qiblah in purity and write the sirhad of this letter with the blessed name of the Noble Prophet Muhammad forty times for forty days. Then wash it, mix it with honey, drink it, and say: "O God, by the blessing of what I have obtained, make memory and understanding easy for me." Whoever fasts for forty days, by its blessing God Most High shall open their outer and inner. This is for the one who has understood its mysteries, for they see the power that is within them. In the mystery of every sage, the Mim is established.

The letter form of the Mim is also among the hidden mysteries. Whoever wishes to know their end should fast that day purely for God, break the fast with whatever is available, recite Surah al-Mulk with wudu, and sleep on the right side. Let them place this form beneath their head and speak to no one—God Most High shall tell them about their end according to the kind of estimate they intended. But this is becoming for those whose hearts are pure, sanctified, and who are people of spiritual discipline. Whoever writes it in a glass and drinks it, God Most High shall advance their understanding and wisdom. Whoever writes it and wears it around their neck shall speak wisdom. Whoever writes it along with "La ilaha illa Allah Muhammadun rasulu Allah" in the same manner, keeps it with them, binds it to their right arm, or writes it on cloth and wears it, shall be granted majesty and greatness.

The Practice of Befriending Jinn to Fulfill Desires

When you desire to befriend the jinn so that they fulfill your needs and always strive for your desires, keep a fast from Tuesday through Saturday. Perform ghusl and wear clean clothes. Each day recite Surah al-Ikhlas one thousand times, Surah Ya-Sin once, Surah Ad-Dukhan once, Surah Al-Muzzammil once, perform sujud, and recite Surah al-Mulk. When Saturday's 'Asr time comes—that is, the tenth hour—separate yourself from people and go to an empty house, a pure place. Taking seven pebbles in your hand, place on the first this verse: "And it is He who gives life and causes death, and to Him belongs the alternation of night and day." On the second: "But when He decrees a matter, He only says to it 'Be,' and it is." On the third: "So Allah will suffice them against them, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing." On the fourth: "Then when He calls you with a call from the earth, behold, you emerge." On the fifth: "And the fish swallowed him while he was blameworthy." On the sixth: "And the Trumpet will be blown, and behold, they will hasten forth from the graves to their Lord." On the seventh, recite Surah al-Mulk and say: "Peace be upon you, O jinn." And they will come before you and say: "And upon you peace."
