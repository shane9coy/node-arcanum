Chapter 68: Properties of the Name Samad, "The Operation for Removing Hunger and Poverty"
Whoever mentions this great name and noble head frequently, poverty departs from him. Its mention is appropriate for the people of spiritual practice, so that they may have no need for food and drink. If one in a state of want mentions this name, all his necessities will be fulfilled. If a hungry person mentions this name, he will not feel the torment of hunger. Its number is 242; this is the figure of the solitary (dhū al-fard) of the future. Its 121 numbers point toward the name al-Hasib; this refers to the knee of life. This comes from the proof of truth; its letter-numbers guide toward the name Mubīn. Its form is as follows:
