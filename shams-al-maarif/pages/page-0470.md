# Page 470 (English translation)
O You Heir, You are the Everlasting after the annihilation of all creation, and the Creator for manifesting the perfection of the form on the Day of Judgment. As You informed Your servants in Your Clear Book, You said: "To whom belongs the kingdom today? To Allah, the One, the Conqueror." I ask You by the benefits of the everlasting and by Your Mighty Power, to make me an heir of Your knowledge and Your forbearance, and an heir of the sciences of Your prophets and saints, and grant me its benefits and lead me to its end, O Lord of the worlds.

The name al-Rashid is one letter of the Greatest Name. Qadi is its ruler. Four rulers are its subjects. Each ruler has 514 rows. Each row has 514 angels. The supplication is this:

O Rashid, You are the one who guided Your saints to the path of salvation and connected Your beloved to the ocean of life and the spring of felicity, and You gathered the saints and prophets in the most complete states. I ask You, O Guardian of beauty, to guide me to You and grant me a good life turning towards You, O Lord of the worlds.

The name al-Sabur is one letter of the Greatest Name. Miya'il is its ruler. It has four rulers as subordinates. Each ruler has 298 rows. Each row has 298 angels. The supplication is this:

O Patient One, You are the one who magnified the forbearance of everything without hastening it, and You are the one who revived the hearts of those who love You with the light of oneness and Tawhid, then You taught him the first of every pure thing and the last of everything that returns to You. The matters and the dominions return to You after the annihilation of kings and the direction of matters to their end in guidance and correctness without doubt and with correct readiness, and for the hastening of rectification to the Abode of Resurrection, which neither stinginess compels You to delay reaching its merits, nor does anything disquiet You before Your satisfaction. I ask You by Your sovereignty and by Your friend of intimate speech, and by what is in the treasuries of Your concealed stores above, and by the glory of Your Face and the majesty of Your Throne and the inner chambers of Your sanctity, to make my supplication accepted and my call answered and my request granted, and make me a guide and one who is guided, and firm on Your straight path, O Lord of the worlds.

Chapter 31: The Properties of the Letters

Allah the Most High had mercy upon the nation of Muhammad, peace be upon him, and endowed them with the wealth of knowledge and revealed the secrets of revelation. Whatever revelation comes to the Prophet, the secrets of the nation are also hidden within it. Similarly, in the Holy Quran, which is the final book, there exists a hidden treasury of symbols and mysteries, for whose attainment the letters were utilized and incorporated into various rituals. The Holy Quran and the Sharia of Muhammad have abrogated all previous Sharias. Now the letters of the Quran are called "Huruf al-Arabi" (Arabic letters).

Syriac Alphabet of the Letters

Someone asked the Messenger, peace be upon him, "What are the Syriac letters?" He said, "They are these: alif, ba, ta, tha, jim, ha, kha, dal, dhal, ra, za, sin, shin, sad, dad, ta, za, ain, ghain, fa, qaf, kaf, lam, mim, nun, waw, ha, lam-alif, ya." Seven of these letters are from the Greatest Name, and all the scriptures were revealed in these letters. In them lies a very great treasury of secrets and mysteries. The importance of the Syriac language is also that it was the language of Adam, peace be upon him, and all the scriptures and books were revealed to Moses, David, and Jesus, peace be upon them.
