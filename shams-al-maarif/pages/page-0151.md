# Page 151 (English translation)
These are the letters, and they are the letters of the Wise Quran. Understand it and keep it hidden. Here I will mention its properties. These are the properties that the perfect ones have not even stated, and the experts have not. And these are their properties, regarding which Allah the Exalted says:

"We believe in it; all is from our Lord." And no one remembers except those endowed with understanding.

And the angels also, despite being informed of the heavenly and earthly Dominion, say this:

"Glory be to You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us. Indeed, You are the Knowing, the Wise."

[Decorative horizontal frame] Chapter: Treatment of Incurable Disease [Decorative horizontal frame]

Its meaning is this:

"And mix after three immediate [applications] with every affliction that afflicts the body and causes sickness."

That is, when a person is afflicted with an internal illness such as colic, weakness of the liver, or weakness of the heart, and the physicians have become helpless in treating him, then write these three names and the seal in this manner—seven times repeated—in a Chinese vessel, and place it at night in the shadow of the stars, and in the morning feed it to the patient.

[Decorative horizontal frame] Chapter: The Operation of Weakening the Oppressor [Decorative horizontal frame]

As for the verse: "The immediate [agent] of all types of punishment," the situation regarding this is that when someone oppresses you and you are unable to take revenge, then make an effigy. Write upon it his name and his mother's name. On each limb of this effigy, inscribe three rods and the Seal of Solomon. Make a wooden coffin like those used in processions, and place this effigy inside it and bury it. Bury it in a place where fire is near. As the wax effigy melts, great severity will descend upon that person and his body will disintegrate.

[Decorative horizontal frame] Chapter: The Operation of Destroying an Enemy [Decorative horizontal frame]

As for the verse "And the mission for the blood of every man," the situation regarding this is that write upon a clay pot the name of the person you desire and his mother's name. This should be written with the blood of some animal, on the day when the two luminaries are conjunct in one degree. Therefore, before their exit, inscribe the mission three rods praising [God] and write the seal of the desired [person] below it, and cast it into some standing water such as a well or similar. From that very moment the condition will be complete; the blood will flow regularly, and in a short time he will be destroyed.
