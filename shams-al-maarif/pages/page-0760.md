# Page 760 (English translation)
Of the Beautiful Names, these eighteen names are suitable for him:
Huwa Allāhu alladhī lā ilāha illā huwa al-Raḥmānu al-Raḥīmu al-Laṭīfu al-Ḥalīmu al-Ra'ūfu al-Ghafūru al-Mu'minu al-Baṣīru
al-Mujību al-Mughīthu al-Qarību al-Samī'u al-Sarī'u al-Karīmu dhū al-jalāli wa-al-ikrāmi dhū al-ṭawli al-Ẓāhiru al-Bāṭinu
al-Laṭīfu

By reciting this name, the supplication will be accepted and needs will be fulfilled:

Whoever recites the Name of the Swift (Sarīʿ) abundantly with supplication will have it accepted. And if there is a particular need desired from God, may He be exalted, let him write the name on his palm and raise his hand toward the sky, and let him recite it while striking it on the days of the week according to the prescribed method, then the desire will be obtained. After the mention of the Name, let him recite this supplication with presence of heart:
Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismika al-sarīʿu al-qarību al-mujību alladhī ajabta bihi fawātiha bi-raḥmatika wa
khawātima irādatika wa-surʿati ijābatika yā sarīʿan li-man qasadahu yā mujīban li-man sa'alahu yā mujīban li-man da'āhu
usri bi-qaḍā'i ḥājatī wa-bulūghi irādatī yā samīʿu yā qarību yā mujību yā sarīʿu

By striking the Name of the Swift on the days of the week, these numbers are produced: 42770. Let him recite according to their correspondence.

Let him perform taswīr of this name and engrave it on red agate, set it in a ring, and use it. For him, such a door will open that he will not even have knowledge of it; abundant sustenance will be obtained:

The Name Qarībun Muhayminun—let whoever performs taswīr of both of these and engraves them in a red agate ring, and writes this verse around it in the form of a circle:
Badīʿu al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi...

And after prayer and the recitation of the Name, let him wear this ring. God, may He be exalted, will fulfill for him whatever his desire may be, and He will open for him such a door that he will have no knowledge of it, and whatever task he needs to accomplish will be attained, to the extent that during the time of dhikr, the spiritual spirits will present themselves before him.

By its recitation, prosperity will be attained:

The Names al-Raḥmānu al-Raḥīmu are noble forms of dhikr. They are a source of safety for the creation. Whoever writes them abundantly on the day of Friday and keeps them with him will see no evil, and whoever continues to mention them frequently will find it very beneficial.

By the mention of this Name, love will be attained, sorrow and grief will be removed, and every difficulty will become easy:

The Name al-Laṭīfu al-Wāsiʿu al-Mushahadu—in solitude, for the purpose of love, this recitation is most wondrous in its effect. Whoever seeks love and has become characterized by a certain aim, circumstances will become connected to him accordingly. Especially, it is the rule regarding the Name of the Subtle (Laṭīf) that in dhikr one should gaze intently, and should not recite it combined with any other name, and should mention only it specifically; then wondrous effects will occur. If someone has fear or sorrow, it will be resolved immediately; tears will inevitably begin to flow from the eyes, in the manner of how it ends. Until when he ceases to recite the Name, no cause for fear will remain.
