# Page 408 (English translation)
Regarding the vastness of the Divine Mercy sciences which attain completion through effusion and subsisting from the particular effusion of the Noble Merciful One, for the witnessing of establishing nearness through the unknown proximity that effaces, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Opener, O Knowing! I ask You for the light, assistance, preservation, protection, the bringing of sustenance and blessing therein, hope, and good trust in You, and rising from other than You.

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. The one who constitutes Your command and the one who perfects through Your generosity, and a blessing from You—blessed is Your name and exalted is Your glory, and there is no god besides You. In You we have faith and upon You we rely. Accomplish for us, O Allah, through Your light and through the light of Your name, and render us oblivious to other than You, turning away toward You, O Allah, O Merciful, O Compassionate. Peace be upon a word from a Lord, the Merciful.

[This section appears to be a Urdu commentary/translation of Quran 16:66-68 about cattle, milk, and the blessings of God, discussing how God has subjected livestock for humanity, providing milk from between what is pure and impure (digested fodder and blood), dates and palms, and how God has created horses, mules, and donkeys for riding and adornment. God has other deities besides Him.]

O Allah, I ask You by this name with which You subjected the cosmos of creation and command for the sake of truth, which manifests for the causes of descent, an exemplar of existence of bellies and intestines, noble one whose attention was upon the unknown when You present it, with whom You wish—whatever multitude from it is a representation, which in Unity was intentional—from that ignorant one upon whom You commanded. O Knowing, O Forbearing, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord of the praising ones, by the name of the most noble Initiator, from the Presence of the Bestower I ask for the manifestation of the supreme form, with the body of the secret, with what is necessary from the perspective of generality and specificity and explanation, and from the generality of Mercifulness which was not determined through isolation and establishment. O special Merciful One, who lifts for the witnessing of establishing nearness with the unknown proximity, O Merciful, O Compassionate, O Opener, O Knowing—I ask You for protection, the limit of preservation, care, the provision of sustenance, blessing, hope therein, and good trust in You, and rising from other than You.

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. One who constitutes for Your command and one who perfects through Your generosity, a blessing from You—blessed is Your name, exalted is Your station, there is no god besides You. We have brought faith in You and relied upon You. O Allah, accomplish for us through Your light and the light of Your name. Render us hidden from other than You, turning toward You. O Allah, O Merciful, and peace be upon a word from the Lord, the Merciful.

Whoever recites this supplication constantly and asks Allah the Most High for a need, it will be fulfilled:

"Has not man seen that We created him from a sperm-drop? Yet behold, he is a clear disputant." [Quran 76:1-2]

Glory be to the One who relieves every grieving one. Glory be to the One who liberates every debtor. Glory be to the One who exonerates every imprisoned one. Glory be to the Knower of every secret thing. Glory be to the One whose treasures of dominion are between the kāf and the nūn. Glory be to the One who, when He intends a thing, merely says to it "Be," and it is. [Recite three times] Glory be to your Lord, Lord of might, [to the end]. In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, [to the end]. O Allah, place me among those upon whom You have bestowed blessings, among the prophets and the truthful ones. Amen, [to the end]. O Lord, O Merciful.

Has not man seen that We created him from a sperm-drop, then behold, he is a clear disputant? Glory be to the One who removes grief and trouble from every grieving one, grants release to every debtor, knows every hidden thing—regarding whom is the command of the Lord of the worlds, etc.
