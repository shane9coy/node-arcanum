703

WALĪ AL-MUKHTĀRĀT
The person who persists in this supplication attains the rank of walāyah (sanctity), and Allāh the Exalted opens for him the doors of good and keeps him safe from all harms.

[Centered box containing the following text:]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Ḥamīd"

Ḥamīd is the One who is worthy of praise, and He has declared His own praise Himself, and He alone possesses all majesty, beauty, and perfection. Know that the reality of praise and eulogy is that it is the secret of the Essence of the Most Pure, and this is the reason why He declared His own praise Himself. **He commanded all creation to offer praise—they all declared praise—then He declared His own praise:**
He commanded the Throne to offer His praise, and so the Throne offered praise; likewise He commanded the Chair, and it also offered praise—in short, He commanded all creatures and they all declared His praise. Thus He gathered all their praises in the Noble Qurʾān:
*Their cry therein shall be: "Glory be to Thee, O Allāh!" and their greeting therein shall be: "Peace!" and the last of their cry shall be: "Praise be to Allāh, Lord of the Worlds."*
And in the beginning of the Book there is also al-ḥamd (all praise belongs to Allāh); therefore whoever knows the secret of praise in general will bring every book with true praise. **There are four kinds of praise:**
Know that praise is of four types. Praise of the Eternal concerning the Eternal is one kind; praise of the Eternal concerning the contingent is another; praise of the contingent concerning the Eternal is a third; and praise of the contingent concerning the contingent is a fourth. Whoever desires to draw near through this Name must make praise and eulogy obligatory upon himself and abstain from base motives—indeed, every atom testifies that within it there is a secret. It is established upon divine wisdom, and if there is any occasion of grief or the like, nothing should escape the tongue. **Praise of Allāh should be offered on every occasion of joy and sorrow**
So let him say:
*Al-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿalā kulli ḥālin*
(All praise belongs to Allāh in every state)
And let him praise everything and find fault with no one; let him completely abandon backbiting and lying. If he commits backbiting, his praise will not be accepted. If he is in the bodily world, let him give thanks for health; if he is among the people of hearts, let him give thanks for this hunger and poverty toward God; and the greatest blessing is faith, so give thanks for it, and constantly remember it with abundant dhikr; and prepare the means for spiritual experiences in this station of the heart—that is, always remain occupied with the prescribed devotions and functions and the praise of Allāh. Recite the Name in seclusion according to its numerical value, and the desire will be fulfilled. And its dhikr is this:
*Bismillāhir-Raḥmānir-Raḥīm. Allāhumma anta al-Māliku al-Ḥamīd. Ḥamadtu nafsa ka binasfika fī azalika ʿizzatika lammā aʿlamta al-khāṣṣata min ʿibādika biḥamdika bimā awlaitahum min luṭfika insika wa aẓharta minal-inʿāmi mā awjadal-ḥamda wal-thanāa min al-khāṣṣi wal-ʿāmmi ʿalā niʿamika al-ʿiẓāmi wal-ikrāmi bihayyiʾati al-jalāl*
