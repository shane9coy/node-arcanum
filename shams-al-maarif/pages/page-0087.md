# Page 87 (English translation)
And He is the Hearing, the Knowing.

Before writing these verses, pray four rak'ahs (units of prayer). In the first rak'ah, after Surah al-Fatiha, read Surah Yasin; in the second, Surah Dukhan; in the third, the verse of prostration (Sajdah); and in the fourth, Surah al-Mulk. And at the end of each prostration, read this supplication:

*Subhana man laisa al-'izza wa qala bihi subhana man ta'attafa bi al-hamdi wa takarrama bihi subhana man ahsa kulla shay'in bi 'ilmihi subhana man la yanbaghi al-tasbih illa lahu subhana man idha arada shay'an an yaqula lahu kun fayakun subhana man idha arada shay'an kana wa malam yasha lam yakun subhana dhi al-manni wa al-fadli wa al-ni'am subhana dhi al-'ilmi wa al-hilmi subhana dhi al-tawali wa al-fadli subhana dhi al-'arshi wa al-lawhi wa al-qamari wa al-nur*

After that, raise your head from prostration and read this supplication:

*Allahumma inni as'aluka bi maqa'id al-'izzi min 'arshika wa muntaha al-rahma min kitabi wa as'aluka bi ismika al-'azim al-a'zam wa li-wajhika al-karim al-akram wa bi kalimatika al-tamma an tughayyir li 'awnan min sulaha' al-jinn yu'ayyununi 'ala ma uridu min hawajij al-dunya*

At this time, seven great and honored ones from among the Jinn of Muslims will appear before you and greet you. Before this, it is incumbent upon you to bind seven papers in their hand and place them on your head. Then begin the prayer. A small amount of kohl should also be with you. Then, when those Jinn are present, take out one of those papers and read it. Say to the Jinn: "Whose paper is this among you?" One of them will say: "It is mine." Ask them for their name and write it on top of the paper. Say to them: "Let them place their seal upon it." When they give their seal, take the cloth and kohl and place the seal beneath the paper, as one places seals on letters. Then say the same to each one and have each seal placed on the papers. Then say:

*Aqumtu 'alaykum bima fi hadhihi al-raq'ati min al-asmai illa mahadtartum wa ajabtum da'wati idha da'wtukum*

Then say:

*Insariqu baarak Allahu fikum wa 'alaykum*

Then dismiss them while reciting this. Keep those papers with yourself in some pure place. When there is need, call upon the Jinn. They will fulfill every need of yours, even food and drink and matters related to treasures. When you call them, they will, by the command of Allah the Exalted, quickly appear and fulfill your needs. However, this requires a person of strong heart, firm footing, and lofty ambition.

When there is need, you can call upon the Jinn, but you must keep the heart very strong and speak very little—indeed, minimize speech with them, because from seeing these Jinn the heart loses its control [reading uncertain: possibly "qabu"] and breaks. And if you become disgusted, burn the pieces of those papers right there, and they will depart.
