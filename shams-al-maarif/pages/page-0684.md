# Page 684 (English translation)
By the manifestation of secrets in the worlds with the encompassments and the vicissitudes of divine ordering, and by what You brought into existence from the great world, both noble and humble, and by what You will of excellent disposition and subjugation, I ask You by what lies hidden within the depths of secret mysteries and what appears of the subtleties of creation in the darkness of darknesses from the radiance of the rays of lights, that You attract my heart with the delicate unveiling to the witnessing of Your presence from the delicacies of secrets, so that my heart may delight in the mystery of subtleties and delicate things, and the eye of doubt regarding perplexities may be removed by the manifestation of those truths. O Allah, conceal me by the mystery of Your Subtle Name from the harm of one who hurts and of an envier, and by the right of Your Subtle Name, O Subtle, O All-Aware.

This prayer was recited by the practitioners of spiritual discipline.

[Section box: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "All-Aware" (Khabīr)]

He who recites this will have knowledge of everything.

All-Aware is the essence before whom no hidden thing can be so absent, and even the smallest particle that moves, He has knowledge of it. This is how one should recite it:

Yā Khabīru khabbirnī ʿan kadhā

In dreams, all states will become known. Their trust in God is complete. They give knowledge of treasures and hidden matters. If this name is written with musk and saffron and rosewater along with the name of the Angelic Regent, and placed beneath the head while sleeping, all knowledge will be known in the dream. And if written on a vessel and given to a slow-witted person to drink, their intellect will become clear. Theirdhikr is this:

Bismillāhir-Raḥmānir-Raḥīm. Allāhumma anta l-Khabīru l-Muṭṭaliʿu ʿalā khavāyā l-falaki wal-malakūt, ʿālimu bid-daqāʾiqi ʿilmikal-ghāmiḍi ilā bāṭini khavāyā kulli shayʾin wa bi-Rabbi ʿālimi sh-shahādati wal-jabarūt, asʾaluka bi-Khabīriyyatika aḥāṭat bi-wāṭini l-mawjūdāti fa-lā tutaḥarrika dharratun wa lā tantaliḥ ḥabbatun illā wa qad aḥāṭa bihā nūru l-mughayyabāt, asʾaluka an taksifa ʿan qalbī ḥijāba z-ẓulumāti an tunazzila anwāra l-murāqabati li-takūna khabaru l-asrāri sarāʾira ṣifātika mubtahijan bi-shuhūdi dhātik. Allāhumma ijʿalnī fī ḥiṣnikal-ḥaṣīni lā yumanu bihī fī jamīʿi l-awqāti wal-mawāṭini li-tuqīma bi-luṭfik. Allāhumma iḥruẓnī bi-ʿaynikal-latī lā tanāmu wa iknifnī bi-ruknikal-latī lā yuḍāmu. Yā Allāhu yā Khabīru yā Waddūd.

One who persists in this prayer will witness the marvelous and extraordinary things of divine craftsmanship.

[Section box: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Forbearing" (Ḥalīm)]

Forbearing is He who does not hasten to punish His disobedient ones; rather, He awaits the repentance and return of the disobedient with patience and restraint. This is a specific attribute of God Almighty, and it is not a general quality—one who is Forbearing is one who, despite having power, does not reveal anger and punishment.
