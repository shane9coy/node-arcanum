Chapter: The Merits of the Name "al-Ḥukm" (The Judgment)

Wisdom consists of knowledge, and no one's knowledge is better than God's knowledge, and along this path which leads to Him. The types of wisdom: Allah the Most High said: Wisdom is one of the attributes of the Essence, whose resolution makes manifest, and it has six parts. One is Ḥikmat fī al-Sirr (Wisdom in the Secret) and Ḥikmat fī al-Khalāṣ (Wisdom in Liberation) and Ḥikmat fī al-Rūḥ (Wisdom in the Spirit) and Ḥikmat fī al-Nafs (Wisdom in the Soul) and Ḥikmat fī al-Qalb (Wisdom in the Heart) and Ḥikmat fī al-Samʿ (Wisdom in Hearing). So from the Secret, what is meant is the first creation with which the Most High distinguished the worlds at the beginning with His knowledge, as much as He wished to guide them to recognize Him. Thus no gnostic knows it except to the extent of that Secret which Allah the Most High has deposited within him, until he accepted creation and became the manifestor of divine wisdom. In the vigil of this ritual it is essential to remain in a state of purity and to eat sparingly.

Muḥammad Saʿīd Qādirī

Its reciter—the one entrusted to him—will teach him three sciences

And whoever desires the revelation of a hidden science must, in the vigil with spiritual discipline, recite al-Ḥukm al-ʿAdl bi al-ʿIlm al-Ḥakīm. The guardian will come and teach him three sciences. One is the science of alchemy, the second is the science of the natures of minerals and the varieties of the earth, and the third is the science of divine unity.

And whoever recites this name after each prayer according to its numerical value, Allah the Most High will grant him understanding, discernment, and wisdom. And when in time of need he recites the name according to its numerical value in the vigil and then recites the supplication, the need will be fulfilled:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā Rabb al-ʿālamīna an taqḍī ḥājatī

(O Allah, I ask You, O Lord of the worlds, to fulfill my need)

And its dhikr (remembrance) is this:

Bi-smiLlāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm. Allāhumma al-Ḥakamu al-Ḥākimu al-Qāḍī bi-mā ḥakamta bī. Hab al-qidama bi-mā aẓhart(a) min makhlūqāti al-amāki wa al-afkāki wa jamīʿi al-ḥarakāti thumma ḥakamta ʿalā kulli wāhidin bi-milāʾi al-maʿlūmāti min al-qulūbi wa al-bafīniyyāti bi-mā sabaqa min tafṣīli al-azdādāti wa al-mushabbihāti. As'aluka bi-mā jaʿalta min tatwīri taqdīri ḥikmatika wa bi-mā takhṭu junnatan min al-qaḍāʾi fī al-lawḥi wa al-qalami an tusakhkhira lī khādimata hādhā al-ismi ʿabduka li-yakūna ḥājjīyayni bi-mā taʿlamīna min al-ṣalawāti bi-mā labbayka ʿalayhi al-salāmu wa an taktashifa lī ʿan ḥaqāʾiqi al-ashyāʾi yā Allāhu yā Ḥalīmu yā Ḥakīmu

(In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, O the Judge, O the Just, O the Decider by what You have judged concerning me. Grant the primordial through what You have manifested of the creations of the heavens and the foundations and all the movements, then You have judged upon every single one with the fullness of the known things from the hearts and the [reading uncertain] by what has preceded from the detailed exposition of the opposites and the similitudes. I ask You by what You have made from the unfolding of the ordaining of Your wisdom and by what You inscribe as a protection from the decree in the Guarded Tablet and the Pen, that You subjugate for me a servant of this name, Your servant, that they may be my arguments by what You know from the prayers, by what has answered You upon it—peace be upon him—and that You reveal to me the realities of things, O Allah, O Clement, O Wise)
