# Page 583 (English translation)
**Every letter has an effect:**
Know that letters are among creation. When they are read without solitude and spiritual discipline, they still produce waves of love and acceptance. The effect of the letter Alif is:

Answer, O great king Maṭmuṭlifīyā Baṭlī'ī'ēl, the greatest president

And the supplication is this:
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You, O one to whom greatness and glory and majesty and pride belong—O Allah, O deity, O Huwa, O our lord—I ask You by the greatest righteousness that You subjugate for me Your spirit and adorn me with it with light and beauty and acceptance, and that You grant me a secret from the secrets of the Alif, which I may employ for what I wish, by Your command, the moving letter, from waking and reception, by the honor of Your name, and by the fire and the light and the shadow and the heat, and by what was spoken during the day, and by what the Ancient One brought forth from His ancient essence, and by the secret that You placed in the Preserved Tablet from the category of similar matters, and by the secret of Your alif-shaped ink, and by Your prevailing command and its similitude and its shadow and its call and its gift and its completion, and by this and by the alif of the command, and by the right of Āhīyā Sharāhiyā Adūnāyā Aṣbā'ūt Āl Shaddāyī and the great command, the chief member, the greatest Tahaṭṭahilyālīl Maṭlayā'īl, that you trust in so-and-so—Waḥā al-Waḥā.

**Translation:** O Allah, I ask You, O essence for whose sake there is greatness and radiance and majesty and pride—O Allah, O essence from whom indication is made—I ask You, informing You, about the secrets of the Supreme Name, that You make Your servant subject to me, and grant me through the intercession of this Supreme Name light and beauty and acceptance, and grant me from among the secrets of the Alif a secret so that I may take from it what I wish. O letter that moves from waking and hardness, by the intercession of the greatness of Your name, and by the fire and light and shadow and warm wind, which were spoken, and which the Ancient One brought forth from His ancientness, and by the intercession of that secret regarding the knowledge of similar matters which You placed in the Preserved Tablet, and by the intercession of that secret with which You aided the letter Alif, and by the intercession of Your prevailing command, may Your servants come quickly to serve me and do this task of mine immediately, now forthwith.

**By reading this, love is produced in people's hearts:**
Whoever reads this supplication, Allah the Most High will place love for him in people's hearts. When you have some need from a servant or want to take revenge from someone, write the form of the letter Alif on a red number and keep throwing it into fire while continuously reading the supplication. The servant will appear and fulfill your need. Its abridged form is this:

Answer, O great king, lord, Ṭahāṭṭā'īl, the greatest president, and hasten, by the right of HH 2 YaHu 2 Shakīl Sal'ama'ū', answer and destroy Ṭafallalā, by the honor until—Waḥā al-'ajal.

O servant, so-and-so son of so-and-so, destroy him, and now appear before me quickly in the form.
