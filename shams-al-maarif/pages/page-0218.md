[The Invocation of Surah Kahf and Obtaining One Thousand Ashrafis]

When you intend to obtain the Red Brass (kibrīt aḥmar) and Tiya Ghayb, open the gate of this talisman and scatter its opportunities. Go to an empty house far away where no sounds reach. Sit on a sandy floor. Before this, perform ritual ablution and wear fine white garments. Then fumigate with fragrant incense such as laban, ambergris, 'ūd, etc. Avoid earnings from forbidden sources. Dwell in one place, then enter into spiritual discipline. Complete a forty-day regimen. Do not eat meat at all. Begin the regimen on the first day of the month that falls on a Friday.

After the prayer, sit in seclusion and recite Surah Kahf from beginning to end seven times after each prayer and at night. Fumigate with fragrance, then recite durood sharīf and read Surah Kahf forty (40) times, and after each reading, recite these words of glorification three times in sincerity and three times with durood sharīf. When the reading is complete, recite subḥān Allāh wal-ḥamd li-llāh walā ilāha illa Allāh wa-Allāhu akbar astaghfir Allāh one hundred times. Then after the morning prayer, with utmost humility and excellence, offer praise and glorification of Allāh ta'ālā, and with great humility, supplicate to Allāh. After completing this practice, rise and go outside the house. On the way, you will encounter the one assigned to this surah—he is a young, handsome person. He will greet you. Respond to him with excellent character. A most exquisite fragrance will emanate from him. He will give you a bag of one thousand (1000) ashrafis. He will impose conditions upon you: visit the cemetery every Friday and do not forget the poor and the needy. He will say to you: "O servant of Allāh, if you recite this every month in this manner, you will receive one thousand (1000) ashrafis each month. Offer gratitude to Him and dismiss him. Conceal your secret, for Allāh knows all things best."

[The Invocation of Surah Waqi'ah and Its Abundant Benefits]

Know that this surah is the key to the door of Tungri. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Ten surahs repel ten things." Surah Fātiḥa prevents the punishment of Allāh. Surah Yā-Sīn prevents famine. Surah Dukhkān prevents the tribulations of the Day of Judgment. Surah Ikhlāṣ and Surah Falaq prevent the envy of the envious. Surah Mulk prevents grave punishment. Surah Kāfirūn prevents disbelief at the moment of death. Surah Naṣr prevents satanic whispers.

Know that this surah's invocation possesses many special properties. If any person recites Surah Waqi'ah after the five prayers, they will be protected from poverty and hardship. When you wish to go to the presence of kings, ministers, and rulers, recite Surah Waqi'ah and speak these words:

tawakkalū yā khuddāma hādhihi al-sūrati al-sharīfati bi-'aqdi lisāni kadha bi-ḥaqqi sūrat al-wāqi'ati 'alaykum wa-innahū la-qasamun law ta'lamūna 'aẓīm(un). Fulān...

"O servants of this noble surah, I bind you by the tongue of so-and-so for the granting of your speech; by the right of Surah Waqi'ah upon you, and indeed it is a mighty oath if you but knew."

The following is outside this purpose:

ṣamamtukum bayna a'yunikum wa-sharrukum taḥta arjulikum wa-khasha'ati al-aṣwātu li-l-raḥmāni fa-lā tasma'ūna illā hamsā. Tawakkalū yā khuddāma hādhihi al-asmā'i wal-sūrati al-sharīfati bi-mahmūbin dī luṭfin khafiyyin bi-maṣa'in dhī nūrin bahiyyin lā yatakallamūna illā man adhana lahu al-raḥmānu wa-qāla ṣawābā(n).

"Present yourselves, O servants of these names and the noble surah, by the praised one, with a subtle grace, with a radiant light of splendor. They do not speak except what the Most Merciful permits, and they spoke aright."

"Present yourselves, O servants of this surah, O people of the word, before so-and-so son of so-and-so, who hears my speech and obeys my command, by the right of there is no deity but He, the Almighty, the Wise."
