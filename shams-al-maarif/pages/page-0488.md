# Page 488 (English translation)
The second throne of mercy

Allah the Almighty says: "The Most Merciful rose over the Throne" – this applies to the reality of existence with which earth and heaven are established. This is why the sky remains established without pillars. This sublime and lofty realm, encompassing from one end to the other, is connected through its upper adornments to the throne, which it maintains.

The keys of earth and heaven are with Him:

What are the keys of earth and heaven, the reality of the throne, the authority over contraction and expansion, and the extremity of height and lowness? The throne's course is spiritual. Its ascent is mystical. Its witnessing is intellectual. Its perception is intuitive and visionary. Its adornment is throne-like. No one can perceive it. Its body is not like other bodies; rather, it was created according to [uncertain: similarity of endowment] with concealment. The truth is that it was created alongside the One who is Absolute and Supreme.

The limit of Jibreel's flight is to this throne:

The Spirit of Trustworthiness, Jibreel (peace be upon him), flies to this throne as his limit. The angels of the highest heaven dwell in this place. In this same place, the sound of the Pen's movement and writing is heard.

The true irrevocable decree:

This Pen is that line which writes and does not change. What the visionary reality demands is written upon the Tablet of Attributes. Those who are worthy understand the gnostic signs, becoming keepers of these sacred subtleties. The secrets have been unveiled to them.

The third noble throne

Allah the Almighty says: "Possessor of the noble throne." This throne is the self of elevation. All spirits honor it. The first throne has neither direction nor veil. Prophets (peace be upon them) and saintly friends are honored by degrees alongside it. The reality of the intellect has perceived the knowledge of the elevated realm – this is the extent of its reach. The dwelling of the holy spirits is also in this very place.

Near the noble throne, spirits were bound to bodies:

Near the noble throne, spirits accepted the binding of bodies and took on these forms. Allah granted spirits bodies and forms for His knowledge, arranging them in the visible realm with various shapes. The limit of the realm of spirits is the noble throne itself. Through the manifestation of lights and their aid, forms were given to letters, and the realm of the intermediate world was revealed, created, and encompassed by the domain of vision above it.

For human understanding and knowledge, these are the realities whose outward aspect is divine power and whose inward aspect is the divine command. Whoever has acquired both perfections has obtained both the spiritual sciences and the exoteric knowledge of gnosis.

Liberation from bodily bondage is through presence:

Whoever has not reached this station of presence, remaining bound in his physical bondage and remaining merely an assistant to the talismans of earth and heaven – that which is legally [damaged]
