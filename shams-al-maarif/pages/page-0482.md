*Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā ghaniyyu yā ghiyāthu yā ghaffāru yā ghaffūru bimā aw da'tahu harfa l-ghayni mina l-asrāri*

**The Letter Fa:**
Fa is a mute, cold, dry letter. Its world of creation in summary form [has been exemplified as being twenty-two years old] [unclear]. 

Properties and benefits: This causes difficulty and hardship in easy matters. It causes delays in completing works. It creates discord between enemies and associates. Its heat is diminished and its dryness is increased. If it is written on an iron plate on Tuesday during the last days of the Moon's conjunction and buried in the center of enemies or opponents, they will destroy each other through fighting and come to an end. If it is written during an inauspicious hour and buried in a place, that place will soon become desolate.

**Protection from snakes and scorpions:**
If it is written twenty times on a lead plate, and on the other side an image of a snake or scorpion is made, and it is buried in the middle of a house, then a snake or scorpion will never come to that place. If a brass or any metal plate needs to be buried in the earth, apply balasan oil on it before burying, so that rust will not damage it. The sages of ancient times used to preserve their talismans in this manner and bury them.

**Business will be destroyed:**
If one wishes to ruin someone's trade or close their business. Extract their name in "Fa" and place it in the place of commerce. Their work will come to an end. If "Fa" is written repeatedly on the door of a place, no one will be willing to dwell there.

**The man will always remain traveling:**
Make a triangle of its number, write it on a strip from the wing of the king of pigeons, and write the letter "Fa" around it. The man whose name is written with it will always remain traveling—his purpose will be fulfilled. Its form is this:
