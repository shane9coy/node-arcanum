# Page 417 (English translation)
The duty for Saturday:

"That he may warn whoever is alive" [Having read to the end, read this:] "Not those who have earned wrath upon themselves, nor those who are astray" Amen. O Manifest, O Mighty, O Master, O Guardian, O Protector, O Powerful, O Great. Answer, O Kafsāyīl, and you, O Maymūn, by the right of "Not those who have earned wrath upon themselves, nor those who are astray," and by the right of the Prevailing One over His servants, the Great, the Most High, and by the right of the King, the Conqueror, to whom belongs His command—Ṭuṭlakh—and by the right of Jakhṭīlīl, Laqfanjal, "the truth has occurred and what they were doing has become void." Answer, O servant of this Surah. Glory be to the One who releases every indebted one. Glory be to the One who purifies every imprisoned one. Glory be to Him who caused water to flow in seas and springs. Glory be to the One who removes sorrow from every grieving one. Glory be to Him who placed His treasures between the kāf and the nūn. Glory be to Him who, when He desires a thing, merely says to it "Be," and it is. End [of recitation] 41 times.

O Allah, make the servant of this Surah subservient to me, as You made the sea subservient to Moses, and the fire to Abraham, and iron and the mountains to David, and the jinn, mankind, and the satans and the wind to Solomon, and the sun, the moon, the stars, and all things to our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. O Allah, make Kafsāyīl and Maymūn subservient to me, by the right of Your great, most great Name, and by the right of Your most beautiful Names. O Allah, O Swift, O Near, O Responsive, O Outspreader, O Loving—3 times. O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Returner, O Doer of what He wills. I ask You by the light of Your Face, which has filled the foundations of Your Throne, and by Your power, with which You have power over all of Your creation, and by Your mercy, which encompasses everything—there is no god except You, O Helper of those who seek help. Grant me aid—3 times.

O Allah, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. O Allah, make the servant of this Surah subservient to me. "Indeed, they love them as they love Allah. But those who believe are strongest in love for Allah" (Quran 2:165). "Indeed, Allah loves them and they love Him. No, do not obey him, and prostrate and draw near" (Quran 96:19). O Allah, answer my supplication by the right of Surah Ya-Sin. "Indeed, You are over all things competent" (Quran 65:12). And there is no power and no strength except by Allah, the Exalted, the Great.

The seven hundred invocations have been completed—recognize their value. (Translation of the second part is complete.)
