# Page 133 (English translation)
131

O Allah, subdue for me Your green servant, for You are over all things powerful.

And in the days of seclusion, after recitation, perform fumigation with 'ūd, lubān, and jāwī, and during the course of spiritual discipline do not speak to anyone; then you will obtain whatever of the good things of this world and the hereafter you desire. And likewise, for love, write the produced talisman of Surah al-Fātiḥa in the hour of Venus and keep it with you, and recite this supplication; the desire will be fulfilled.

This supplication is:

In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds. Trust, O Jibrīl, you and your helpers, by the right of the Mighty, the Compeller, the Generous, the Bestower, the Conqueror. O Allah, cast love of [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of the Beneficent, the Merciful, Master of the Day of Judgment, and by the right of Allah, the Living, the Sustainer, the One, the Unique. Trust, O Isrāfīl, you and your helpers, and cast love of [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of "It is You we worship and You we ask for help" and by the right of the King, the Omnipotent, the Exalted, the Originator, the Glorious. Trust, O Rūqiyāl, you and your helpers, and cast love of [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of "Guide us to the straight path" and by the right of the Unique, the Living, the Sustainer. Trust, O Nūriyāl, you and your helpers, and cast love of [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of the One, the Knowing, the Magnanimous, the Generous. Trust, O 'Izrā'īl, you and your helpers, hearing and obedient, and cast love of [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of "the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have evoked Your anger or of those who are astray," and by the right of the Conqueror, the Mighty, the Majestic, the Great. Trust, you and your helpers, hearing and obedient, and cast love of [so-and-so] and [so-and-so] into the heart of [so-and-so] by the right of "they love them as they love Allah, and those who believe are most intense in love for Allah."

The established practice for protection from every ailment:

His presence, the Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said that for every ailment, recite Surah al-Fātiḥa seven times, Ayat al-Kursī seven times, and the Mu'awwidhatayn seven times over water and blow upon it, and drink it for three days. Allah the Exalted says: "Have it written." By God, the knowers of Allah say that in Surah al-Fātiḥa there are one thousand apparent properties and one thousand hidden properties; if it is written in any vessel and after washing it the sick person is made to drink it, he will benefit from it.

His presence, the Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said that when I go out of the house, I recite Surah al-Fātiḥa and the Mu'awwidhatayn, and I am then in safety and security from every evil. His presence said that for one whose house is being built, recite Surah al-Fātiḥa seven times for his house and place it in the four corners, and say "O Allah, I ask You for healing from every ailment," and healing from every ailment will occur.

O Allah, I ask You for healing from every ailment.
