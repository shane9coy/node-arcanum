The Blessing of the Great Name:

Shīʿī reports that the secrets of Allah are in every book. In the Holy Quran, His secrets are the openings of the surahs. Some say that the Great Name consists of: two verses in al-Baqarah, one in Āl ʿImrān, three in Surah al-Anʿām, two in al-Aʿrāf, two in Surah al-Anfāl, one in Surah al-Raʿd, one in Maryam, four in Ṭā-Hā, one in al-Muʾminūn, one in Surah al-Naml, one in Surah al-Rūm, one in al-Sajdah, two in Surah Yā-Sīn, three in Fāṭir, one in al-Jāthiyah, two in Surah al-Raḥmān, three in Surah al-Ḥashr, one in Surah al-Mulk, and two in Surah al-Ikhlāṣ.

Shaʿbī reports: I saw in a dream a person saying to me, "Go to so-and-so, for we have given him knowledge that he may teach you the Great Name." I went to him in the morning, and he said to me, "You are the explanation." I had seen in a dream at night that I would teach you the Great Name. Behold, all the verses in the Holy Quran in which "Lā ilāha illā huwa" appears are the Great Name.
