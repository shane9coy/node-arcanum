And We send down from the Quran that which is a healing and mercy for the believers, and when I am ill, it is He who cures me. Say: It is guidance and a healing for those who believe.

Then wash it with honey, recite the noble verse seven times upon it while blowing, and give it to the patient to drink, and by the command of Allah the Almighty, he will be cured. Among its properties is that if phlegm has harmed him, take seven pebbles of salt, recite Ayat al-Kursi seven times upon each one of them, and let him eat one pebble in the morning on an empty stomach—his phlegm will be eliminated. Some have transmitted that he used to see disturbing things in his dreams, then he mentioned it to an elder, and the elder said: When you lie down to sleep, recite A'udhu billah three times and begin with Ayat al-Kursi. When you reach "Neither does He care for their keeping safe, and He is the Most High, the Most Great," repeat it three times and sleep. After that, they will not show you any of those things. He did so and the matter went away.

Protection from the Evil of Rulers:
Among its properties is that when you must go to a ruler and fear his evil, upon entering his place, recite "Kashahat al-Wujuh" three times, then Ayat al-Kursi three times, and recite this supplication:

Allahumma alqi 'alayya min zinataka wa mahabbatika wa karamatika wa thamarati rububiyyatika ma tanharu bihi al-qulubu wa tuthillu lahu al-nufusu wa tabriqqu lahu al-absaru wa tataballadu lahu al-afkaru wa yakhlusu lahu kullu mutakabbirin jabbarin ya 'Azizu ya Ghaffaru ya Allahu ya Wahidu ya Ahadu Allahumma ihfazni fi ma mallaktani bima anta amlaku bihi minni wa amdidni bi-raqiqatin min rava'iqi ismika al-Hafizi fa-akhtifu bihi absara al-mawjudati wa albisni dir'an min kifayatika wa mala'ikatika wa qallidni bi-sayfi nusratika wa karamatika wa jamalika wa tawwajni bi-taj karamatika wa 'izzika wa raqqini bi-rida'in minka wa 'afiyatika wa arkabni markaba al-najati ila al-mamat wa amdidni bi-raqiqatin min rava'iqi asma'ika al-qahriyyati idfa' biha 'anni man aradani bi-su'in min jami'i khalqika kama sakhkharta al-bahra li-Musa 'alayhi al-salamu wa layyin li qulubihim kama ananta al-hadida li-Dawud 'alayhi al-salamu fa-inna-hum la yantiquna illa bi-idhnika natawi yahum ilayka fi qabdhatika qallibha kayfa tasha'u ya muqalliba al-qulubi ya 'Allama al-ghuyubi alifati qalbi fulanin ibni fulanata

And if you wish, say this:

Alqaytu ghadaba al-nasi bi-la ilaha illa Allahu wa istajlabtu mawaddatahum wa mahabbatahum bi-Muhammadin rasuli Allahi 'alayhi al-salatatu wa al-salamu fa-lama ra'aynahu akbarnahu wa qatta'na aydiyahunna wa qulna hash lillah ma hadha basharan in hadha illa malakun karimun wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahi al-'Aliyyi al-'Azimi

(Translation) O Allah, pour upon me from Your beauty, love, generosity, and the light of Your Lordship that which makes hearts fear and souls submit, before which eyes lower and thoughts are stilled, and before which every proud tyrant bows. O Mighty, O Forgiving, O Allah, O One, O Unique, O Allah—protect me among the things You have given me dominion over, for You are more deserving of them than I. I seek refuge with Allah, asking for protection within the shield of Your sufficiency, and with the celestial beings of Your names. Let me don the armor of Your protection, gird myself with the sword of Your victory, generosity, and beauty, place upon my head the crown of Your honor and might, wrap me in the cloak of Your grace and well-being, mount me upon the vehicle of salvation through life, and assist me with one of the guardians of Your names. Drive away every evil from me [damaged source]...
