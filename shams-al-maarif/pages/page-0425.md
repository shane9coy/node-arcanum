and revelation and knowledge, hidden and manifest, O Lord of the Worlds

O King, You are the Essence who has mastery over the necks of the mighty with Your strength, dominance, and sovereign power—You who overcome kings and sultans, who possesses the degrees and the spheres, and for whom exists whoever supplicates to You. I ask You by that which You have spread throughout Your malakut and jabrut, and by that which You have established in the government of Your jabrut, and glorious is it, by which You have impressed Your stamp in Your world of divine sanctity (lahut), and by which You have made it hidden from the comprehension of the common people beyond the reach of intellect, and it is the hidden secret of mercy, and by which You have recorded it in Your hidden secret which is in the Greatest (Kibriya), and by which You have connected it with penetrating infusion in the arrangement of the treasury of totality, the inner depths of descent—that You protect me with Your own firm protection from the voices of Satan, his imaginings and whisperings, and desires, who made good appear as evil and the sea as land, and made the bitter into loss—may his malice be preserved from treachery and deception in the layers of earth and heaven, O Essence whose Throne was upon water, You alone are the Knower, and Your action accords with His will.

O Mim, guarantor of grace and generous nobility, grant me such favor that I may obtain the honor of the paths of knowledges and wisdoms, and with the words of the Kingdom, favor me in death and life; from Yourself grant me gnosis in the essence of the دعوت (daʿwah), and may the Kingdom not be lost—in Your ancient attributes grant attribute, and from Your eternal knowledge grant speech, neither less nor more. Summary and detail, O Generous Veil, suffice for us, O Allah, who is the Accomplisher of affairs, and the best of accomplishers. I ask You as one who is incapable, wretched, humiliated, and covetous asks: the numerous from the few, the lawful from the cosmos, the sublime from the cosmos, the brief from the lengthy, and looking upon the court of sight, O Essence from whom issues intellect and command, from whom comes manifestation and terminus, who is with Your knowledge. Revelation and knowledge and testimony are Yours, O Lord of the Worlds.

[Boxed heading: The key for subjugating the affairs of religion and world belonging to the client of the Name Quddus]

The Name Quddus is more suitable than the Name Malik. It contains spiritual proximity. Its client is named Aynayil. Whoever mentions this name, his client comes to him and fulfills his needs. Know that the correctness of all dhikrs, names, and invocations depends upon lawful sustenance—both apparent and hidden—purity, sincerity of intention, and good character. Knowing favorable times is also necessary. When you have kept all these matters in mind and begin the operation, you will succeed very quickly. When the keys of religion and world come into your hands, it is then obligatory upon you not to think of anything other than the hereafter. Allah the Most High has said: "And the final outcome is better and more enduring." The supplication of the Name Quddus is this:

"O Quddus, You are the One sanctified absolutely, and You are the Pure by Your grace in the regions, and You are the Originator of the subtleties of knowledge upon the pages of scrolls. By You the inward and outward are sanctified, and from You the visions and inner realities are illuminated, and by You the mysteries of the spirits of the horizons and consciences are unveiled from the movements of thoughts. I ask You for the hidden things of shadows and sights, and for turning toward the path of reverence and neediness, and by the secret which You have encompassed in the pavilions of Your holiness, and by the light of Your greatness in the seats of Your servants' honor, and by what You have written beneath the apron of Your greatness and the cloak of Your majesty, and by what You have hidden in the garment of Your glory, and by what You have made known to Your saints and the intellects of Your prophets—through the like of the word of His intimate friend."
