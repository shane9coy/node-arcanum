# Page 38 (English translation)
They keep themselves so far removed from the desires and appetites of their selves that the love of God becomes theirs. Every one of their words becomes a word of wisdom. Every one of their movements attains an elevated rank. Their soul loves the realities of things, the subtleties of the Islamic sciences, and the lights of the Sacred Law to such a degree that the traces of love become evident upon it, and it sees the world of heights in the state of unveiling. All the varieties of blessings that He has prepared for His friends and the punishment for the disbelievers become present before this soul. Thus it proceeds further toward divine love and longing. Its intellect contemplates the divine reality and the secrets of the divine signs more deeply. Then, as this love grows, its worldly attachments are abandoned. Because of this love, it fulfills every command of God, understanding that there is good and benefit in His knowledge. As long as it remains oriented toward love, it sees the secrets of the celestial world. It becomes honored with divine revelation and the realities of the highest realms.

We return to our purpose. The benefits of the letter Dal: whoever writes the letter Dal thirty-five times (which is its numerical value) in a square shape on white silk cloth at the time when the Moon is in its own house enjoying [the aspect of] Jupiter. Write the letter Dal thirty-five times around Mars and at that same time place it inside a ring and wear it with complete purification. It is also fitting that he should be fasting and maintain purity of the inner self.

**The Doors of Sustenance and Good Shall Be Opened:**

By the blessing of this practice, God the Most High will open for him the doors of sustenance and good forever. Whoever writes the seventh name of God, "Al-Qa'im," and also writes this shall obtain this benefit as well. We have described many properties of the name Al-Qa'im and the letter Dal in our book "Mazhar al-Huda wa Asrar al-Iltiba'"—see them there.

**Achieving Purposes with Kings:**

Whoever writes this talisman and keeps it with him shall find all his purposes achieved with kings and rulers. Whoever sees him shall love him and acquire much. Every person shall write this talisman in this manner on yellow silk cloth and keep it with him; much shall be given to him. However, it should be written at the time when the Moon is at the ascending node of Cancer or the descending node of Jupiter, enjoying [the aspect of] Jupiter, and it should be accompanied by good incense. The talisman is as follows:-
