# Page 669 (English translation)
Section: The Virtues of the Name "Alim"

Alim is the One who is knowing along with His attributes and perfection, and He has encompassed everything along with the apparent and hidden, the first and the last. The attribute of God, may He be exalted, is complete.

### The Knowledge of God Is Unlimited; the Knowledge of the Created Is Limited:

The knowledge of God, may He be exalted, cannot be dependent [on anything], except that the knowledge of the created is within limits—that is, it is limited. As much as He has decreed for it, so that through it the created may attain proximity to God. The details are as follows: God, may He be exalted, created the malakut of lights, and He also created those things that He bound with His names, and they are established in the malakut.

### Each Name Is a Mirror of Another Name; the Angels and the Lights of the World of Secrets, All Their Sciences Are Connected to Alim:

Each name is opposite to another. Then God, may He be exalted, created the jabrut and the malakut, and He created the angels from the lights of the Throne, because He created the Throne from the "Supreme Sovereign" along with the names of attributes. He created the angels of every letter along with the lights of the Chair, and they are established with the names of attributes. The worlds of the Chair are established within it, and the angels of the world of testimony [shahadah] He created from within the Tablet, because they are established with the names of actions. Thus the angels of the kingdom [malak tasharruf] are established, and the angels of the jabrut with administration, and the angels of the malakut with decree and dispensation. When God, may He be exalted, willed to manifest the differences of these worlds along with various sciences, so that His knowledge might appear in His wisdom, and His wisdom in His power, and His power in His will.

### The Creation of Adam Is the Masterpiece of Knowledge and Wisdom, Which God Has Proudly Declared in "the Best Form"

He created the Lord, Father of Humanity, Adam, and He made what is opposite to him—within His world of sovereignty, that is, in his body—and He appointed for each of his limbs a name. He taught him all things that have happened or will happen, and He told him the names of all things. God, may He be exalted, says:

"And He creates what you do not know"

Then He created his wife Hawwa from his rib, and He poured divine lights upon them, and He brought aid through the lights of their spirits' gifts. Then He made them vicegerents on earth, and the names of attributes and the names of actions manifested upon them, through which their form became complete. He says:

"We have certainly created mankind in the best form"

Then He placed reason within them and aided them with [the help of] their limbs. This is the creation of this lower world, and when the upper world becomes apparent—that is, the Throne and what is seen—[it is] with the appointed sustenance that comes from the decree. Thus routes lead toward the nations, and pure spirits perceive along with the light of the archetype. All the upper and lower worlds have deposited within them divine secrets and the truth of divine signs and the effects of divine productions, and certainly God, may He be exalted, has deposited within this Adam all the spirits whose differences [are linked] to the lights and the wonderful motions of the heavens to the lights, and despite this, there is endurance that is in the atom of the sphere's claim of motion with the human.
