# Page 547 (English translation)
Know that by " باب " is meant calling upon the helpers—that is, making an incantation. Extract precisely from the first part of the name of the seeker and the desired one with a delicate letter in the place of truth. Whether the name is complete or the desired is first or last, the intent is to proceed with precedence or delay or the complete name. If, therefore, the name is complete, in which there is neither precedence nor delay, neither intent nor desired—the name is upon the complete form. The letters for the " باب of calculation" are: five, ninety, the betrayer (18), the thirty (30), and twenty-nine (29)—this is the complete name. The " باب of speech" does not involve a return. And if there is precedence and delay in the name, such as Dāwūd; and if there is delay and precedence, such as Yaʿqūb; and if there is delay with intent, such as Dāwūd. If, therefore, the name is arranged and precedence, it is complete, like Aḥmad and Jaʿfar; and if it is desired, like Malika. Take each of the letters with its right along with the delicate letters at its beginning from the beginning—though its name be complete or desired. If the name of the seeker relates to the " باب " from the name Aḥmad, then extract from it, for this is permissible provided it is not excluded from the beginning.

Second composition: Jaʿfarī Muḥammad Marmūsī Mūsā ʿAynī ibn Mūsā Hāshimī, who is the teacher, says—Ḥasan ibn ʿAlī Sirāj Hamadānī: When you wish to work by this method, first determine the name of the seeker and the desired and the name of their mother. If one of their names is unknown, then convert the name of the seeker and the desired into numbers, and extract two lines from this " باب." Each line will be a name: in one, the name of the seeker with the desired; and in the other, the name of their mother. Write this on a complete piece of paper. Then write the name after both lines, and extract two lines and convert to numbers, in which the first letter of one is the first of its letters and the first of the letters of the name of the seeker. Then write after it the names and helpers and the oath and the incantation. This is the explanation of the extraction for the " باب of seeker and desired," and the like of the incantation is:

I swear upon you, O angels of the Lord of Glory, respond to So-and-so by the right of these names; and what I have recited—hasten to these helpers to fulfill my need. The revelation, the haste, the hour, by That which has obligated obedience upon you, and by the glory of God, your Lord, and by what I swear upon these helpers. And whoever among them deviates from our command, We will cause him to taste the torment of the Blaze. Answer, O company of helpers, for these names—unless God sends upon you the angels of torment for these names. The revelation, the haste, the hour, by That which has obligated obedience upon you, by the glory of God and by the light of the face of God.

By its blessing, whatever you wish will be obtained.

Explanation of the Great Chapter. The name Qāʾim is one degree. From the twenty-nine degrees of the Great Intermediary, it is complete, and sixty-four desired fruits along with the letter that is excluded—these are the interpretation. Its first degree is at the end. After the degree, letter by letter, and name after name, and from the place of exit of the source to the table, the ruling is: avoid roughness. This is the beginning of the first source. From the Great Chapter, it is the first degree. From the chapter of speech, and its final letter is y. Then change the last to the first. Then you will find the first letter y and m at the end, then change the last to the first. Degree by degree to the place of exit of the name—thus you will find l. Its line will be ṣ in the table of the magic square, and k at the end. Then change: the last upon the first, and until the tenth word of the source, the source will come out for you. Thus this is the emanation of the first source. From the Great Chapter, you will find y in the first and b at the end. Then again, the j that is taken will be the name, but she will bid farewell.
