For Fulfilling Desires:

Whoever recites the Name of the Creator 5115 times in the specified place, whatever need he desires will be fulfilled. The operative spirit will manifest according to the capacity of the practitioner, and will perform spiritual signs. The operative is Latmayil for him, and is among those under Michael's command, and he recites this glorification:

*Glory be to the Creator, the Maker, the Fashioner*

And his spiritual discipline lasts forty days. During this period, the proofs of matters are revealed.

*In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Creator of original existents and their Composer, and You are He who brought them forth from non-existence by the power of disposition through will, which You bestowed from what preceded from Your knowledge in eternity. You are the Inventor of all kinds of things according to whatever You will of their creation and emergence from the darknesses of the unseen with the best arrangement and details. I ask You, O Originator of things and Cause of life, to descend into my heart a spiritual light that draws through union to witnessing You, and to subjugate for me Your servant Mfājīl, servant of this noble Name, so that he may stand me upon the secrets of invention and grant me thereby the greatest bliss, and the fulfillment of words through manifestation from Your supreme attributes. Grant me this, O Allah, O Creator.*

Every person who recites this dhikr will have the secrets of the words revealed to him.

Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Bārī"

'Creator' and 'Bārī' are synonymous with slight difference:

*Khāliq* (Creator) and *Bārī* (Maker) have the same meaning because He is the One who created from dust. This verse testifies to it: "He is the One who created you from dust." The Arabs call "turāb" (dust) "thurá al-burbá." The meaning of "thurá" is dust and "burbá" means created being.

But there are slight differences among these Names. If there were no difference, the Names would be synonymous, and then the Most High Lord would not have said:

"And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them."

It is known that the word "ijād" (creation) and "ibdā'" (origination) are applied when rational essences are brought from non-existence into existence, and the Name "Creator" applies to every created thing.

Notification: Allah the Most High created reason and placed it in the realm of intellectual knowledge. Then He created the world in a garment of subtlety. Then He transferred it to the realm of form. Thus these are all divine signs. The world of composition belongs to it, and the light of the conception of God is composition. Then He created bodies for their sake, and in the composition of bodies He created their parts and placed names upon them, because the meaning of the Names lies in comparison—Bārī, Creator, and Provider—although the form is one and the motion is one and the will is one.
