[Boxed Title: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Kareem"]

Kareem is one who when he has the power to do so, forgives, and when he makes a promise, fulfills it, and when he gives, he gives to the extent of making one wealthy, and all other matters belong to Allah the Most High and He is sufficient.

Details of the benefits obtained from the generosity of Allah:

The first grace (karam) is intended by "honor" (takrim), which is the garment of creation, meaning the manifestation of the spirit, the establishment of the covenant, and bringing the world from non-existence to existence. The second grace is binding with intellect, and then another grace is the invitation of the prophets and the deficiency of sacred wisdom, and bringing it into our hearts until we believe in it. Had His grace not been upon us, what opportunity would we have had to believe? This is His extreme grace that the disbelievers worship others besides Him and commit acts of disobedience against Him, yet He does not hasten to punish them. This is due to His special grace that whoever does one good deed receives the reward of ten good deeds, and whoever commits one sin receives only one sin's worth of sin, and when he turns to Allah and repents, Allah changes all his evils into good deeds. This is from His great grace.

Allah feels ashamed when punishing the sinners, but the servant feels no shame when committing sins:

It is mentioned in some heavenly scriptures that Allah the Most High says: My servant has not been just, for I feel ashamed when punishing him for it, and he feels no shame when disobeying Me.

The dialogue of a saint with Allah:

A saint supplicated to Allah the Most High: O Lord Most High, I have a need for something very insignificant, and I feel ashamed to ask You for something so insignificant, and I also feel ashamed asking anyone other than You. It was commanded: Ask Me, even if it is for salt for flour or fodder for a donkey.

Whoever performs the spiritual retreat of this name, good fortune and generosity shall be his portion, and when the aspirant reads its virtues, Martaqayil, the assigned angel, descends and fulfills his need. Its dhikr is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the Generous, the Bestower of gifts, the Munificent with grace eternal, upon righteousness Your signs are many, with goodness abundant, despite gratitude scanty, and You overlook the great sin for the servant who is submissive and humble. I ask You, O Generous One, by the extent of Your noble grace, the Manifestor of generosity since eternity, that You honor me with Your grace from Your generosity and from the divine subtleties of the highest realms, and the sacred mysteries of the divine, manifested for the holy presence, and that You provide me with the finest blessings of earthly bounties, with sustenance purified from vile doubts, and that You grant me in that strength according to my turning toward You with obedience that leads to You. O Allah, honor me with the removal of malice from grudges and with the conquest of adversaries, O Lord of the worlds.

Protection from jinn and humans is obtained, and generosity and awe are acquired.

Whoever recites this dhikr, Allah the Most High will protect him from the evil of jinn and humans, and he will be one whose prayers are answered.
