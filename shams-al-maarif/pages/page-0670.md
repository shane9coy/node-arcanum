# Page 670 (English translation)
Every one of them is related to degrees in the heavens by temperament and time. The Most High established this human with divine perfections and luminous lineages.

<u>The human became a knower of the principle of 'the Straight Path,' and thus was counted among the People of the Right:</u>
He placed his right side beneath the Straight Path because these perfections are compounded within this creation, and these sciences are deposited within his heart, the sanctuary of gnosis. Then He poured forth the gifts within him, for every particle of existence contains a particle of realities, and among the gifts is contained a knower. The total divine names are 99, and each name pertains to a named entity. For this reason, according to his capacity and readiness, He established these names upon this form of the human being in the body, and now he has become a knower of the principles of things. Through this very Straight Path he recognized those things that are within him. Thus he became one of the People of the Right, and whoever adopted the doctrine of Hellfire became one of the People of the Left and among the astray.

<u>Notice: Allah loves the number seven. He created the heavens, earth, proximate angels, planets, Paradise, and seven days:</u>
Allah the Most High created seven heavens and seven earths, seven stations, seven devils, seven planets, seven proximate angels, seven names of attributes, seven names of actions, seven names of essence, and seven Paradises. Know that the burning ones are also seven, around which all seven realities revolve, and upon them is the readiness for the lights of the gifts. Thus each one brings aid upon the other's throne.

<u>The qutb is one:</u>
Aside from the qutb, who takes aid from the Exalted Throne and pours forth his grace upon others—for this reason all seven successors receive aid from the qutb through the intermediary of four, and they bring aid to the remaining ones, and four to forty—and the grace of all of them relates to the Throne. These are the form of the human being and his names and attributes, and whatever is on his right and left is also within this.

<u>Paradise is beneath the feet of the parents:</u>
The Noble Messenger ﷺ said: Paradise lies beneath the feet of mothers.
Allah the Most High placed His sovereignty as a trust among His creation and made it His manifestation through His perfection. Thus He taught Adam all His names, then after the universal mode, the lights of the Truth the Most High descended upon them through the letters [damaged source/watermark] which were compounded.
