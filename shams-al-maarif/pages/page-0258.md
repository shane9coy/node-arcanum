# Page 258 (English translation)
Grant me from You a Furqan (discernment) with which You expand my chest and elevate my rank; You are my face and my glory, and to You is the return and the destination. You who repair the broken and have mercy on the poor: there is no god but Allah, the Wise, the Great; there is no god but Allah, Lord of the Great Throne; there is no god but Allah, Lord of the heavens and earth and Lord of the Noble Throne. O Allah, Lord of Jibril and Mikail and Israfiil and Izra'il and Ibrahim and Isma'il and Ishaq and Ya'qub: protect me, pardon me, and do not subject me to any of Your creation, O Allah, with something for which I have no strength, O Hearer of supplications, O Responder to calls. Indeed, God will suffice you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. I have put my trust in the Living Who does not die, and praise be to God Who did not take a son and had no partner in sovereignty and had no protector against humiliation, and magnify Him greatly: Allah is greater, most great.

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from what I fear and警戒, and I seek refuge in Allah, there is no god but He, the Sustainer of the heavens lest they fall upon the earth except by His permission, from every stubborn tyrant and rebellious Satan. O Allah, I ask You for safety from poverty and safety from rejection and safety from disgrace and safety from worry and safety from grief and safety from debt and safety from ignorance and safety from trials and safety from destruction and safety from trembling. O Allah, improve our outcome in all matters and deliver us from the shame of this world and the punishment of the Hereafter. O Allah, I ask You by Muhammad, the witnessing, the perfect, the opener, the seal—light of the lights of knowledges and the good of the secrets of knowledges and the chosen one of Your creation and the secret of Your knowledge and the mirror of Your essence and the station of Your attributes—and I ask You by the light of Your face and the spread of Your mercy and by the seven and the eight and their secrets connected to the sovereignty of Allah.

O One, O Self-Sufficient, O Living, O Eternal: that You grant me from Your knowledge an intellect, and from Your life a spirit, and from Your will a judgment, and from Your power an action, and from Your words a tongue, and from Your hearing an understanding, and from Your sight a revelation, and from Your encompassment a standing. And bestow upon me a kingdom through You—a goodness by which the necks of the arrogant bow and to which the souls of the tyrants submit. For Yours is the praise, O Lord, over every beginning, and Yours is gratitude over every ending. Indeed, You are the Self-Sufficient, the Praiseworthy. O Allah, clothe me upon my garments with Your mercy by Your glory, and protect me with a guardian of Your preservation and protection, and adorn me with the robe of awe, and seat me upon the throne of greatness crowned with the crown of splendor, and spread over me the pavilions of preservation and victory upon the banner of glory and the spread of sustenance. Fill my inner self with fear and mercy, and my outer self with greatness and awe, and make me the master of the forelock of every stubborn tyrant and rebellious Satan. Guard me from error and misstep, and support me in speech and action. O Allah, I ask You by what Your essence has contained of that which no one knows besides You—that You bless our master Muhammad, the Muhammadian Essence and the Ahmadite Grace, sun of the heaven of secrets and the manifestor of lights and the pole of the orbit of beauty and the center of the circuit of glory. O Allah, I ask You by the secret of that and by the secret of Your essence: that You grant safety to my fear, and accept my excuse, and remove my cowardice and my sorrow, and the laziness of my heart, and make me worthy of Your love, and grant me contentment, and do not make me one transformed
