The Letter Kaf's Prayer

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā khallāqu yā khāliqu yā khāfiḍu yā khabīru kh kh kh kh kh yā khafiyyu al-luṭfi an tusakhkhira lī khuddāma hādhihi al-ḥurūfi li-yatamma amruhum bihi innaka ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun

The Letter Dal:

Dal is a speaking letter. Knowledge and wisdom are its insertions. Its star is Mercury. It is cold. The movements and stations are attributed to the letter Dal. Its properties are very numerous.

Properties and Benefits: For kidney swelling, writing Dal four times removes the swelling. Writing it 67 times on fire embers eliminates burning pain; wounds will not occur either. Filling its number 35 in a talismanic figure and writing Dal on each corner, engraved on a lapis lazuli board (a green-colored stone), and keeping it with oneself.

For Intestinal Consumption:

One will not suffer intestinal illness. Its square should be engraved on a board made of silver and quicksilver during Mercury's hour while it is in its exaltation. Looking at it four times daily and supplicating Allah the Most High. The secrets of the letter Dal will manifest upon it. He will obtain knowledge and wisdom. Whatever he wishes to obtain, God will grant him. Its form is this: d 44 He should recite this prayer:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā dā'imu al-ʿizzi yā dhā al-jūdi bimā awdaʿtahu ḥarfa al-dāli mina al-asrāri al-maknūnati wa al-anwāri al-makhzūnati an tusakhkhira lī khuddāma hādhihi al-ḥurūfi innamā amruhum bihi innaka ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun

d 44

[Inscribed below this prayer: niṭāqu]

The Letter Dhal:

Dhal possesses both speaking and silent qualities. Its temperament is hot and dry. It is masculine. Its descent is like that of Dal.

Its operations occur in both seasons. May Allah the Most High grant correct standing, for sustenance and method will become known to you.

Properties and Benefits: Writing Dhal 7 times in a Chinese vessel, mixing with honey and licking it eliminates phlegm. If for toothache, writing it 81 times in a gold or silver vessel and washing it with honey and rose water, administering it for seven consecutive days to one suffering from cold illness effects a cure. If one fills the descent of Dhal, for example filling alif-lam-lam-lam-alif and engraving it as 9 dh dh for an amulet on Monday during Mars's hour on an iron board. Writing these names on the four corners: Qādirun Muqtadirun Qawiyyun Qā'imun, and binding this tablet to the right arm. The prayer is:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā dhā al-faḍli al-ʿaẓīmi yā dhā al-manni wa al-jūdi wa al-karami yā dhā al-iḥsāni wa al-imtināni yā dhā al-jalāli wa al-ikrāmi yā dhā al-baṭshi al-shadīdi yā dhā al-ghufūri yā dhā al-ṣfaḥi as'aluka bimā awdaʿtahu ḥarfa al-dhāli mina al-asrāri al-maknūnati wa al-anwāri al-makhzūnati an tusakhkhira lī malā'ikataka al-kirāma innaka ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīrun
