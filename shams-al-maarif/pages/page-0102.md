# Page 102 (English translation)
The sage who gave precedence to all his names, saying: "O Lord of the call, O Lord of the Fatiha, and the Nurani Arb'iyah, subjugate them to me, that they may assist me in fulfilling my needs, by your command. O Allah, truly You command and nothing is commanded upon You. O assembly of spirits, accept and fulfill my need on his behalf, to whom belong honor and dominion, and on his behalf who does not die—there is no likeness or similitude to Him. He is eternal; His name is not forgotten. His light is not extinguished. His throne does not perish. His chair does not move. He sent down the Book upon His servant. O Allah, I ask You beside whom there is no deity, He is the Lord of this world and the hereafter. I ask You to fulfill my need and subjugate the spiritual servants of these names. Truly You are capable of all things."

When you are engaged in the noble act and wish to recite the Fatiha prayer, recite it in this manner:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I adjure you, O Dani'il, and upon your helpers, O Hamna'il and Hazma'il and Samkha'il, and upon the great winds: Laqamhuwan, Mardud, and Azuz, and upon the sun and moon: Ma 'ud fasaduwayn. In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. There is no power nor strength except by Allah, the Most High, the Most Great. O Allah, I ask You by Your being the Living who does not die, the Victorious who is never defeated, the Creator who is not created, the All-Seeing who does not sleep, the All-Hearing who is not deaf, the Dominant who is not dominated, the Sufficient who is not fed upon, the Self-Subsisting who does not sleep, the Trustworthy who never breaks promises, the Just and the Wise who does not err, the Rich who does not become poor, the Treasure who does not diminish, the Forbearing who does not hasten, the Manifest who cannot be denied, the Unique who has no second, the Bestower who does not refuse, the Swift who does not neglect nor delay, the Eternal who does not wear out, the Responder who does not wish in vain, the Everlasting who does not perish, the One who has no likeness, the Omnipotent who does not delay. O Allah, I ask You, O Living who does not die, Creator who is not created, Self-Subsisting who does not sleep, the Truthful who does not break promises, the Just who does not oppress, the Hidden who is not diminished, the All-Hearing who is not deaf—there is no deity except You, O Lord of the worlds. I ask You by Your glory to fulfill my need and subjugate all the spiritual beings to me. O Allah, O Magnificent, by Your hidden name and by the right of Your majesty and the light of Your face, truly this is easy for You. I adjure you all by Allah the Magnificent and by the right of the great names of Allah, Most High. Hayya al-'ajala al-waha al-sa'ata, Allah bless you.

I adjure you, O Dani'il, and your helpers. O Allah, I ask You, truly You are living and do not die. He is the Victorious, no one is victorious over Him. He is the Creator, uncreated. He is the All-Seeing, there can be no doubt about Him, He is not deaf. The One who conquers is not conquered. He is not dependent upon anything. You are independent, not sustained by food. He is Self-Subsisting, He does not sleep. He is faithful, He does not break promises. You are the Judge, You do not oppress. You are the Rich, You do not become poor. Such a treasury that never diminishes. He is Forbearing, He does not hasten. Such is the renowned one whose denial is impossible. He is One, He has no second. The forgiver, He is not refused. The one who acts quickly, He is not slow. He does not forget, He remains forever. He does not concern Himself with [worldly matters]. He is the acceptor of prayer. He is everlasting, He does not perish. He is unique, no likeness has He. He is the Omnipotent, He does not delay. O Allah, I ask You, O Living who does not die. He is the Creator, uncreated. He is Self-Subsisting, He is not slow. He is truthful, He does not go against [His word]. He is just, He does not oppress. He is hidden, He is not present [in a visible form], yet He hears, He is not deaf. O Lord of the worlds! I ask You by Your glory to fulfill my need and subjugate all the spiritual beings. O Allah, O Magnificent, by Your hidden name, by Your majesty and the light of Your essence, this task is most easy for You. I adjure by Allah the Magnificent and by the right of Allah, Most High, who is most great.
