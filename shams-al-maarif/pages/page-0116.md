**The Subtle Secrets of the Letter Shin:**

Since the shape of Shin is like the shape of Alif, there is a very excellent correspondence between them. Shin also contains three letters: Shin-Ya-Nun, and Alif also contains three letters: Alif-Lam-Fa. Although other letters besides Shin are also composed of three letters, their Throne is not like the Throne of Shin, because no letter possesses such complete correspondence. The saying of Allah the Exalted "Allah has witnessed" (shahida Allah) contains the solidity of the unity of the two realms, the reference to non-existence, and the reference to the worlds. The letter Shin is the Chair of the Throne of Alif. Because every subtle thing is a Throne, and every dense thing is a Chair. The Chair bears the Throne, for you see that the letter Mim is the Chair of the Throne of Shin.

And in truth, every subtle thing stands with a dense thing. For this reason, the letter Alif is the lightest and most subtle of all, which is due to the change, and the manifest stands before it. There is no equal to it in all the letters, nor can its extremity be known by anything other than itself. Thus it points to priority and ultimacy, because the world of the Chair has more correspondence with the world of the Throne. Do you not see that the Chair is the place of forms, and the Throne is the place of lights that have reached all the world? Alif is the collection of units, tens, and hundreds. Every person who ponders upon the letter Shin will become acquainted with its realities and wonders, and will witness the secrets of the knowledge of letters. Since Shin is the last rank of the Throne of existence and non-existence, therefore Ali-wa-tasil (existence and connection) is also the last rank. Similarly, the end of the letter Shin is Nun, which bears the essence of the collection of all. Nun is that fish upon whose back the world stands. Nun takes help from Shin, and all the world takes help from Nun. Likewise, the praised one (al-mahmud) also takes help from Nun. Allah the Exalted says: "Nun. By the pen and what they inscribe." Therefore the pen takes help from the interior of Nun, which is the manifest of that matter whose interior is the Kaf (meaning: it has passed), which indicates the hidden secret. That is the secret of Shin. Whoever writes the letter Shin once on a day suitable for their need, that is, if it is a need for good, then on a day of good, and if it is a need for evil, then in the first hour of a day of evil, their purpose will be fulfilled. Some days are for evil, such as Saturday and Tuesday, and some are for good, such as Thursday and Friday.

The secrets of the letter Shin in the corporeal world are beyond counting. If someone has pain in any of their limbs, or a woman has labor pain, then by writing this and holding it in the hand, there will be relief. It also has a very great خاصت for harming anyone. See, this letter is found in the name "Shafi" (intercessor). So look at its properties. Whoever has knowledge of the rank of Shin and becomes acquainted with its natural relationship at the station between Shin and detail, that is, Shin-Ya-Nun, regarding their natures and numerical relationships, will witness its secrets. He will see its excellences with his own eyes. He will become acquainted with the states of separations and determinations. The Ain of the Throne takes help from what is above everything. The path of mercy takes help from which there is no mercy above it, except the form of the Merciful. Shin takes help from testimony, above which there is no testimony, nor any purpose besides it. So see what testimonies are upon your throne of perfection, and the departed material essence finds the path of mercy. Glory belongs to Allah, for His Messenger, peace be upon him, and for the believers. The glory of Allah is its perpetuation, and the glory of the prophets is the existence of prophethood. The glory of the believers is the existence of faith. All these ranks are contained in the letter Shin.
