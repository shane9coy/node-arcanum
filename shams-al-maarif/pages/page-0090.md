The people have said much regarding the Essence of the Most High—whether man can know Him or not. They say that it is not possible to know. Their saying is that everything is known through direct perception that is present before one's eyes. If revelation is present and existent; if it is hidden, then it is known through reason.

The Essence of Allah the Most High is such that it has no form, and no one can see it with their eyes. Allah the Most High has said: "Vision comprehends Him not, but He comprehends all vision, and He is the Subtle, the Aware." Some researchers say that when the footstep of Allah the Most High is without beginning, His permanence is without end, His guidance is beyond number, and His attributes are established as being beyond the attribute of reason, then it is necessarily incumbent that those who describe Him cannot attain the full reality of His attributes. Because if they were to attain the full reality of His attributes, limits and examples would necessarily apply to Him, which would indicate death, passage, and annihilation—and this is impossible for Allah the Most High, since the Divine majesty is elevated above these things.

Hazrat Akkasi, may Allah have mercy upon him, says that Hazrat Jibreel, peace be upon him, came to the presence of our Prophet with the Greatest Name, written on a leaf of Paradise. It was sealed with musk. Within it was written this phrase:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-smiika al-makhzūni aẓ-ẓāhir al-muṭahhari al-quddūsi al-ḥayyi al-qayyūmi ar-raḥmāni ar-raḥīmi dhī al-jalāli wa-al-ikrāmi

"O Allah, I ask You by Your hidden, concealed, pure, holy, Living, Self-Subsisting, Most Gracious, Most Merciful Name, Possessor of Majesty and Honor."

Hazrat Anas, may Allah be pleased with him, relates that a woman said, "O Messenger of Allah, teach this to me." He replied, "We do not teach this to women and children."

The Supplication to be Used During Critical Times

A scholar requested from an imam, "Write down for me some supplication that will be useful in dire circumstances." So he wrote this supplication:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka bi-ismika innaka anta Allāhu al-muqaddasu 'an ḥaqā'iqi maḥḍi at-takhṣīṣi wa bi-annaka anta Allāhu 'alā kulli ḥālin min aḥwāli al-jaddi wa-at-ta'dīli wa bi-annaka anta Allāhu al-muqaddasu bi-khaṣā'iṣi laysa ka-mithlihi shay'un wa huwa as-samī'u al-baṣīru an tuṣalliya 'alā sayyidinā Muḥammadin ṣallallāhu 'alayhi wa sallama wa an taqḍiya ḥawā'iji kullahā qaḍā'an yakūnu fīhi khayru ad-dunyā wa-al-ākhirati maḥfūẓan bi-ar-ri'āyati mina al-āfāti malhūẓan bi-khaṣā'iṣi al-jināyāti yā 'iwādan bi-al-khayrāti yā man huwa lī al-ḥaqīqatu ahlu at-taqwā wa ahlu al-maghfirati wa ahlu al-ḥasanāti allāhumma innahā mas'alu khadimin li-'izzi rubūbiyyatika bi-iẓhāri mas'alatin innaka 'allāmu al-ghuyūbi wa shāhidu ḥaqā'iqi al-maṭālibi bi-qudratihā li-al-qulūbi fa-tamim'mahā bi-jamīli al-khātimi yā khayra al-maṭlūbi wa ṣallallāhu 'alā sayyidinā Muḥammadin wa ālihi aṭ-ṭayyibīna aṭ-ṭāhirīna

"O Allah, I ask You by Your name—and You are Allah, the Holy, beyond the realities of pure specification—and by the fact that You are Allah under all conditions of effort and amendment—and by the fact that You are Allah, the Holy, possessing attributes such that nothing is like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing—that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—and that You fulfill all my needs with a fulfillment in which lies the good of this world and the next, preserved by protection from calamities, noticed by the special qualities of sins, O Responder with blessings, O He who is to me the Truth, worthy of piety, worthy of forgiveness, and worthy of good deeds. O Allah, this is the request of a servant for the glory of Your Lordship, by revealing a request—truly, You are the Knower of the unseen and the Witness of the realities of requests by His power over hearts—so complete it with a beautiful ending, O Best of those requested, and may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad and his pure, clean family."

"O Allah, I ask by Your name—and You are Allah, the Most Holy, beyond all that is in the world, upon which every truth stands. Allah, the Most Holy, is Your name, whose like no thing exists, and You are the Hearing, the Seeing. And may blessings and peace be upon our master, the noble Muhammad.
