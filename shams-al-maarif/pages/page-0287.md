Chapter 42: The Properties of the Glorious Name: "Grandeur among People and Deliverance from the Oppressor"

The mention of this name produces grandeur and awe among people; whoever writes it and keeps it with him, every tyrant will fear him. His Eminence Sheikh Zain al-Abidin al-Kafi may God be pleased with him states that this name has a very great effect for those seeking awe and majesty. Whoever frequently mentions this name, no one will be able to look upon him with anything but awe. If a tyrant sees him, he will tremble with fear, and as long as he remains in his presence, his awe will dominate him. Its number is 73, and this is the number of "Glorious" (Jalil), because "Jalil" means His multitude and grace, for there is no narrowness in Him. In it, the half-point has appeared for the purpose of indicating plurality. Therefore, its letters are more than the numbers of the names, and they are 341, which point to the names of God the Exalted: al-Samad, al-Muid, and Ali. Jalil is the One upon whom reliance is placed in every matter, so that every good may benefit and reach, and every evil may be granted respite. This is its form:

[Box heading in red]
Chapter 43: The Properties of the Generous Name: "Without Doubt, Sustenance and Countless Benefits"

Whoever persists in the mention of this name, God the Exalted will grant him sustenance without hardship; if he was in poverty, great abundance will occur. If the names al-Wahhab and Dhu al-Tawl are added to it, he will witness wondrous things. Know that the name Karim, al-Dhu al-Hab, and Dhu al-Tawl are from the Glorious Names. If one persists in their mention, sustenance will come with such ease that it would not even be imagined. Whoever keeps this design with him will know how his needs were fulfilled without any hardship. This is the form of the design of this Generous Name:
