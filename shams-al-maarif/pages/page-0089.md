**A Proven Method of Revelation**

The people of dhikr have indicated that every sincere person, having become free from carnal desires, ugly morals, and evil deeds, should seclude themselves with struggle and exertion, close the doors of the senses, open the eyes of sight and hearing, and with heart devoted to the realm of the divine kingdom, continuously say "Allah, Allah" with the tongue of the heart. Until such time that no material remains in his soul and he sees nothing except Allah, at that moment a window will open before him, and he will see those things that appear in dreams, and the souls of angels and prophets (peace be upon them) and beautiful forms will appear before him. Upon this, the kingdom of heaven and earth will be revealed to him. He will see such things whose explanation and comprehension are impossible. Our master the Noble Prophet (may peace and blessings be upon him) said: The earth was presented before me, and I saw all its eastern and western regions. And Allah the Exalted said to His Holy Prophet (may peace and blessings be upon him): "And remember the name of your Lord and devote yourself to Him with complete devotion." The meaning of tabattul (devotion) is to sever connection from everything, to undergo transformation and change, and to bow with helplessness and humility entirely towards Him. This method belongs to the Sufi masters of this art.

**Wonders of the Earth Become Manifest**

Know that from the special properties of lordship, there is knowledge of the Beautiful Names of Allah and the upper and lower attributes, especially the knowledge of the Supreme Name, which Allah the Exalted has made exclusive for His servants—there is no deity except Him, He is the Sole Provider and the Giver. He is One and Unique. Some of the saints said to a friend of Allah: I wish to teach you something through which your need will be fulfilled. He said: Yes, so he said: Be constant in the remembrance of Allah. By day you should say "Allah, Allah," and by night also be constant. Establish this according to your ability and make the remembrance of Allah obligatory, so that you keep yourself connected to Allah through this remembrance day and night. [damaged source]
