# Page 109 (English translation)
Whoever mentions the Name of Dhul-Jalal wal-Ikram (Lord of Glory and Generosity), Allah the Most High will grant him everything he asks and desires. Whoever mentions the Name al-Muqassit (the Just) will act with justice in all his affairs. Through the mention of the Name al-Jami' (the Gatherer), every lost thing will be found. Through the mention of Allah the Most High's Name Ya Ghani (O Rich), the means of wealth and sustenance will become easy for him. The one who mentions the Name Ya Mughni (O Sufficient) will become indifferent to all creation. Whoever frequently mentions the Name al-Mani' (the Preventer), Allah the Most High will protect him from every harm. If he writes it 161 times on the city wall during the hour of Venus, Allah the Most High will protect that city. The wise ones had written it on the wall of the fortress called Qal'a Mar Din, and no one could conquer it, and by Allah's command no harm befell it. Whoever mentions the Name al-Darr (the Harmer) can harm whichever tyrant he wishes. Whoever mentions the Name al-Nafi' (the Benefiter), if he is in distress or illness, Allah the Most High gives him wellness. Whoever is constant in its dhikr will reach such a state that the knowledgeable will be in harmony with him, and whoever he places his hand upon will be healed. If he engraves it on a silver ring in Mars' position elevated to the Moon's dignity, and the patient wears it, he will be healed. This Name contains an allusion to the Lower Name; its letters allude to both Names, which are for healing. Whoever mentions the Name Ya Nur (O Light), Allah the Most High will illuminate his heart. When combined with Ya Nafi', it is a cure for every ailment considered incurable. It should be written and given to drink. There is a great square (talisman) for this Name, and this is its form:

Whoever mentions the Name of Allah al-Hadi (the Guide), his heart will have more light, and He will grant him unveiling (kashf) about the mysteries of his knowledge. For one for whom the outward and inward affairs of religion and the world become difficult, it is advisable that he pray two rak'ahs with Surah al-Ikhlas and Ayat al-Kursi, and perform the litany of this Name such that while reciting, his breath escapes him—he will then achieve his goal. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Ya Bad'i' (O Wonderful) upon him are revealed the wonders of divine sciences and heavenly mysteries. Through the mention of the Name Ya Baqi (O Everlasting), there is blessing in every endeavor. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Ya Warith (O Inheritor) and desires to become the inheritor of a dear one and relatives, Allah the Most High will make him the inheritor of them all. Whoever mentions the Name Ya Rashid (the Rightly Guided), all his affairs will have good endings. Whoever frequently mentions the Name Ya Sabur (O Patient), Allah the Most High will keep him steadfast in all hardships and expediencies.

And Allah speaks the truth, and He guides to the path, and may Allah bless our master Muhammad and his family and companions, and grant them peace!
