And when affliction becomes known to you, and you have read it, immediately ابو ایمان النور والراس البرواغ (Abu Ayman al-Nur wal-Ras al-Bargh) is "he will appear" certainly. From seeing him you will not fear. So request from him by contemplation because when he remains in your contemplation, then you will not be separate from him, and his heart is his path. Much fear should be had from him, and he shows many tricks. If you light a fine incense at that time, it is much better. And these are names for protection and preservation, for going to kings. And when you desire to throw stones at an oppressor or to destroy him, then join this name with some name from these demons, and make both into one name and recite the interpretation, and make one amulet and write the طومار around it, and recite the interpretation seven times. Then the demon will be set upon your enemy, and he will afflict him however you wish. Neither will the demon be able to separate from that person, nor will that person be able to distance the demon from himself, and he cannot open the knot you have tied.
