# Page 208 (English translation)
208

That you subjugate for me the spirituality and servants of this blessed surah, for truly you have power over all things, by the truth of the Greatest Name, and by the right of the Merciful's reminder, for the sake of the Lord of the Worlds. Angels of my Lord, accept my words. By Him I swear to you, you who obey the names of Allah, accept and receive, for truly I adjure you by oath, O Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful, and all praise belongs to Allah the Most High who sustains all worlds. O Ruqayyīl, for the sake of this name written upon the heart of the moon and sun, and by the truth of Allah the Greatest, O Lord of the Worlds!

And by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O Ruqayyāʾīl, attend—you and your helpers, your tribes, your clans, and whoever is under your rule. Answer and be an aid to me for what I desire, by the truth of what I have recited to you from the Greatest Name of Allah. O Allah, be for me a helper and supporter. I adjure you, O Samsamāʾīl, by the truth of the Lord of this Lofty Structure, answer. O Jibrāʾīl, by the truth of the Name written upon the heart of the moon, and by the truth of Allah, the One, the Subduer, answer. O White Bull, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, Jibrāʾīl, and by the truth of Allah, the Exalted, the Most High, answer and be an aid to me for what I desire. Answer, O Red One, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O Samsamāʾīl, attend—you and your helpers, your clans, you and your tribes, and all who obey you. Answer all of you and do what I desire of you, by the truth of "Holy, Holy," Lord of the Angels and the Spirit. Answer and be obedient to His names, listening. Answer, O Mīkāʾīl, by the truth of the Signs and the Wise Reminder, and by He who created the heavens and the earth and is Knowing of all things. Answer, O Barqān, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O Mīkāʾīl, attend—you and your helpers, your tribes, your clans, by the truth of Him who said to the heavens and the earth, "Come, willingly or unwillingly." They said, "We come willingly." Answer, O Ṣarfayyāʾīl, by the truth of the Living, the Subsisting King, and by the truth of the five prayers. Answer, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O Ṣarfayyāʾīl. Attend—you and your helpers, your clans, your tribes, and all who obey you; let none of you be absent, by the truth of these Great Names and by the Greatest Name, Allah fifteen times. O Allah, be for me a helper and supporter. Answer, O ʿInyāʾīl, by the truth of Friday and by the truth of Him who gathers the people for a Day in which there is no doubt. Answer, O Zawbaʿa, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O ʿInyāʾīl. Attend—you and your helpers, your clans, your tribes, and whoever is under your rule. Answer, O Kasfayyāʾīl, by the truth of what is subjected between heaven and earth, by the truth of the Holy King, the Judge, and by the truth of the Exalted, the Most High, Lord of Allah the Most High. Answer, O Maymūn, by the truth of the King who prevails over you whose command is supreme, O Kasfayyāʾīl, attend—you and your helpers, your tribes, your clans, and whoever is under your rule. Answer, O companies of the spiritual spirits, the higher and the earthly, and be for me an aid for what I [unclear] from the earthly earth. Answer by the truth of what you control from the rank of the names of Allah the Most High [unclear] [unclear], and hear my address and understand what I desire. So, O companies of spirits, by the truth of the spiritual sovereignty, attend to my place, this oasis, immediately [damaged source]
