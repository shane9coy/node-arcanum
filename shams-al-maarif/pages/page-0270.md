Chapter 13: On the Properties of the Name of Bārī Regarding Facilitating Works

The property of this name is that it makes the most difficult tasks easy. For laborers and workers, the recitation of this name is proven effective. Whoever recites it with devotion, upon him is revealed the realm of similitude. If he is a physician, his treatment is successful in all diseases, and all his patients receive healing. Its number is 213. This number is an odd deficient rectangular number. Its numbers 214 point to the name of Allāh the Most High, Al-Ḥanān (The Most Merciful). This is set in the world. The jīm is for all, the alif is for beginning and ending and joining, and the mīm is for completion. This is placed in the triangular number. This is its square form.

Chapter 14: On the Innumerable Benefits of the Name of Muṣawwir (The Fashioner)

Whoever recites this name frequently, upon him is made easy the seeing of similitudes and their reality. Whoever traces its square upon things of Jupiter and keeps it with himself, no work of his goes wrong. If a steadfast person recites it frequently, the lower and upper specific forms descend upon him, and this matter can only be understood by one who is of the people of unveiling and insight. Whoever recites this name frequently and intends to learn lawful painting and drawing, Allāh the Most High will make this learning easy for him. Its virtue number is 343. This is an imperfect tablet (lūḥ al-badīʿ). Its 21+566 point to two great names of Allāh the Most High: Al-Karīm (The Most Generous) and Al-Muṣliḥ (The Reformer). This directional number 330 points to the name of Allāh the Most High, Al-Qāhir (The Conqueror). This is the method of the people of secrets. Its letters' names are 390, pointing to two great names of Allāh the Most High: Al-Māniʿ (The Preventer) and Al-Akram (The Most Munificent), and this is its square.
