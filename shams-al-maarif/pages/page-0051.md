Tuesday's day:
The first hour is of Mars. In it are works of hatred, enmity, shedding blood, and making someone ill. The second hour is of the Sun. Nothing should be done in it either. The third hour is of Venus. It is good for engagement and marriage. The fourth hour is of Mercury. It is good for attracting customers, buying and selling, and commerce. The fifth hour is of the Moon. Nothing should be done in it; it is very unlucky. The sixth hour is of Saturn. It is good for unraveling affairs, eye inflammation, and diseases, etc. The seventh hour is of Jupiter. Perform in it whatever works of love and affection you desire. The eighth hour is of Mars. In it, works of shedding blood, destroying the enemy, and making someone ill should be done. The ninth hour is of the Sun, which is good for contracting women, love, and marriage. The tenth hour is of Venus. Nothing should be done in it because it is not good. The eleventh hour is of Mercury, which is for divorce and separation from kin. The twelfth hour is of the Moon. It is for enmity, hostility, corruption, removing someone from a place, and conditions of divorce, etc.

Wednesday's day:
The first hour is of Mercury. It is good for acceptance and love works. The second hour is of the Moon. Nothing should be done in it. The third hour is of Saturn. It is for making someone ill and shedding blood. The fourth hour is of Jupiter. All good works should be done in it because this hour is very good. The fifth hour is of Mars. People should fight, quarrel, and do corrupt work in it because it is a great hour. The sixth hour is of the Sun. It is best for engagement, ritual travel, and every work. The seventh hour is of Venus. Do whatever you wish in it; it is a good hour. The eighth hour is of Mercury. It is good for children's crying and writing amulets for the evil eye. The ninth hour is of the Moon. Works of separation, enmity, shedding blood, etc., should not be done in it. The tenth hour is of Saturn. It is good for going to kings, rulers, and dignitaries. The eleventh hour is of Jupiter. Meeting officials and writing copies of gifts is best in it. The twelfth hour is of Mars. It is for evil and enmity works.

Thursday's day:
The first hour is of Jupiter. It is for seeking sustenance and customers, and for acceptance. The second hour is of Mars. Do not travel in it. Take revenge from the enemy and perform the work of shedding blood. The third hour is of the Sun. Do not travel in it. Perform in it the works of acceptance, love, and affection. The fourth hour is of Venus. Works of love and marriage are performed in it. The fifth hour is of Mercury. This hour of Mercury is best and good for every good work. The sixth hour is of the Moon. It is best for travel for engagement and marriage, and for all good works.
