# Page 350 (English translation)
350

The Noble Servants of these Names and the Obedient Ones to these Oaths, they trust and they comply with whatever he orders them:
"Assist so-and-so son of so-and-so, and let God do what is required, and make him subservient to his hand, and he will not oppose him in any matter.
Hayya al-Waha al-Ajal as-Sa'ah, may God bless you and upon you."

O servants of these Names and Letters, place the spiritual love for the Great Name of God between so-and-so son of so-and-so so that it spreads over them. Seize his heart and mind, that he may speak nothing except my Name, see nothing except my Name, hear nothing except my Word, and fear nothing. Indeed, you are among those who bring peace, and I have placed my love upon you. Love is obtained and absorbed—absorb it in the heart like sugar. Draw forth the love. Place upon him the garment of love and the crown of awe. The light of the knowledge of the Glorious Names, and the great Oaths—the majesty of God, before it every tyrant is humiliated; before the command of God the Most High every arrogant one stands still. Do not turn away on either side, for I am with you, hearing and seeing. Do not fear being seized, and do not be afraid. When these women saw the Prophet Joseph, peace be upon him, they found him extremely beautiful. These women cut their hands and said, "By God, this is not a human being—this is a noble angel." O servants of these Names, you do not fail to fulfill the need of so-and-so son of so-and-so, nor do you disobey God. Whatever he commands you, do the work of so-and-so son of so-and-so, as I have commanded you to fulfill my need.

"He who said to the heavens and the earth, 'Come willingly or unwillingly,' they said, 'We came willingly.'" In the same way for him, may so-and-so son of so-and-so become obedient to him in whatever he requests from him.

The owner of this Day and the owner of this Hour, you and your helpers, present yourselves and become helpers for fulfilling the need of so-and-so son of so-and-so. By the right of these great Verses and the Exalted Names, and by the right of the King of Glory, hear and obey, and fulfill the obedience of so-and-so son of so-and-so, that he may speak good of so-and-so son of so-and-so or fall silent.

By this trust which I have presented before them, they offer excuses. O so-and-so, accept the excuse and fulfill what he requests and desires. By the right of God, the Praiseworthy and the Glorious, that every thing's life is in Your hand—whosoever disobeys You has burned, and every tyrant is humiliated. He alone is victorious over His servants; His is all dominion, and You have power over all things. I ask You to subdue for these Names Your noble angels and servants, and fulfill the obedience of so-and-so son of so-and-so.

Write this talisman and come: write it on a paper together with Indian frankincense, jawi, mastaki, saffron, seeds of mallows, and crow's claw, burn them and fumigate with the dry smoke. Under it, write: "I fold the tongue of so-and-so as this paper is folded." Then upon it, recite this love incantation and blow. This operation is the most severe and most effective. God is the best.
