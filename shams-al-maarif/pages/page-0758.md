**The one who recites Al-Hafiz will have no fear of theft, robbery, or any kind of danger**

The name Al-Hafiz (the Preserver): the person who performs the taksir of its letters and keeps it with himself, and continues the remembrance of this name, will not fear anything, nor will any thief or bandit be able to transgress against him. And if he goes to a place of fear, he will remain safe and his heart will be at peace.

**The litany of this [name] is very beneficial for kings and the wealthy:**

These are certain divine names for the singular heart, for dispelling obsessive whispers (waswasa), inspiring awe (hayba), and repelling frightening things. Kings and wealthy people should keep this litany, because by their blessing, the establishment of the authority of the kingdom and people is achieved, and self-restraint over desire and anger is attained. For the people of spiritual wayfaring (ahl al-suluk), looking upon them is very appropriate, because within these names are secrets of majesty, awe, inner nobility, purity from all vices, and lofty aspiration, and the remembrance of the angels is also included within them. The friends of Allah obtain unveiling (kashf) through their blessing, and the good fortune of true knowledge (ma'rifa) is achieved. The spiritual influence of the people of wayfaring and the special properties of the letters are within these names, and the Greatest Name (Ism A'zam) is also among them. These are all 24 names, apart from the Name of the Essence (Ism al-Dhat), and no name is repeated among them.

**Huwa Allah al-ladhi la ilaha illa huwa al-Malk al-Quddus al-Salam al-Mu'min al-Muhaymin al-Aziz al-Jabbar al-Mutakabbir al-Muta'ali al-'Aliyy al-'Azim al-Jalil Dhu al-Jalal wa al-Ikram al-Majid al-Rafi' al-Ghani al-Mughni al-Wahid al-Wali al-Hafiz al-Muqaddim al-Mu'akhkhir al-Barr**

**The one who recites this supplication and name before a king, he will become humiliated and obedient**

From among these [names], two: *Al-Malk* (the King) and *Al-Quddus* (the Holy)—when these are mentioned before any king, that king will become humiliated and obedient. It is appropriate for kings to remember them; by their blessing, their kingdom will remain established. For the wayfarer (salik), their remembrance in seclusion is beneficial. *Al-Quddus* (the Holy) and *Qayyam* (the Sustainer)—the person who engraves both of these names and keeps them with himself and continues his litany will take the lead on horseback in passages. During their recitation and practice, ambergris and costmary (qust) incense should be lit. And if this engraving is placed upon the head, pain will depart. The name *Quddus* derives from holiness (quds), which means purity.

The name *Al-'Aliyy al-'Azim* (the Exalted, the Magnificent)—among these, taksir has also been performed with the addition of alif-lam. Engrave it, give it the incense of agarwood and ambergris, and wear it on your hand—the person who looks at it will be filled with awe. It is said that from the time of al-'Abbas al-Saffah and afterward, Abu 'Abdullah used to keep this ring with himself, and by its blessing, their caliphate remained established.
