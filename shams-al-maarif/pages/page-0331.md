# Page 331 (English translation)
We uncovered from you your covering, and today your sight is sharp.

Take black pepper and write this with saffron; then strike it upon his head, and he shall become obedient. If there is such a person whose presence is not possible, then from a distance, having imagined him, remove this talisman from him; say "Allahu Akbar" seventy times, seven times. Then keep this paper with yourself—indeed, by the mercy of Allah the Most High, the desired object will come running after you. This operation should be written at the time of sunrise on Thursday.

[Heading: Chapter: The Seal Talisman for Subduing]

This name should be written on Wednesday with the water of "lawn" [clay], saffron, and rose water upon the stars of Saturn. Write it with the name of the desired one, then give it the incense of ban zikr, and hang it in the air. Perform a seven-day vigil—eat only barley bread, olive oil, and raisins, but do not eat to fullness. No person should be present in seclusion. Keep fast among these [days]. Recite these names after every prayer, and whoever you wish, whether ordinary [person], call the absent one, make the present one repent, turn the paper into gold or silver—but do not reveal your secret to anyone. He will obtain his purpose. This is the seal of obedience; in it are the names of the swift [Sari'] and the elevated Talisman [Tali' Rafi']. Its number is 72,600. These numbers come together in three letters; there is in it glorification and subjugation. The names are these:

Halahu halu al-dhat wa al-lawh wa al-qalam ya rabbahu za'ul awsil kaza ila kaza awsil al-mawaddah baynahuma
Bi-taytayf sulti' asmatun tawan hakhash bufash hayurash tahlur al-irtibat hayurash ya zawsh al-shaqqum mihrafish baslamhat ba'd al-qursh balshafuri ghalashafish mihraqish ajibbu ayyataha al-arwah al-'izam bi-l-ism al-makhzun al-maknu ajib ya salim ya mahlakun

Its talisman is this:
