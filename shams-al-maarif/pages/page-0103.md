# Page 103 (English translation)
103

When you intend to call the one in office, recite this prayer:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I adjure you, O Aynāʾīl and Harfāʾīl and Sarjiyāʾīl, and by the fierce wind and by the sun and the moon—I ask you to descend to my place and manifest everything I command you and everything I request from you. I ask You, O Allah, O Light of Light, O Director of affairs, O Knower of secrets: You are Allah, the Sovereign, the Conqueror. There is no deity except You, and no object of worship besides You, O Allah (three times). By the right of these great names, Allah (three times), the Most High, the Great, the Wise, the Generous, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Unique, the Self-Sufficient, Allah, who did not beget nor was begotten, and none is equal to Him. I ask You by Your glory and Your establishment upon Your throne, that You fulfill my need and subjugate for me the lord of sleep and the hour and the circuit and the four directions. Truly, You are over all things capable. Indeed, You decree and nothing is decreed against You, O Allah (three times). You are Allah, there is no deity except You. You are hidden, therefore You are not seen, and Your light is not perceived. I have believed in You and trusted in You. You are Allah, to whom all Your creation is gentle and whom all submits to You. You are Allah, the Conqueror, the Exalted—Your majesty is exalted above Your throne, nothing and no one among Your creation can describe Your greatness. O Light of Light, the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth are illuminated by Your light, O Allah (three times). You are exalted above having any associate, O Light of Light—by the praise of Your light, every light, O Possessor, and every believer. And You are the Everlasting, You do not change nor fade, O Allah. You are the Compassionate, the Merciful. By Your mercy, Your anger and Your wrath are extinguished, and by it You grant me felicity from Yourself, and that You house me in Your Paradise, which You have made the dwelling of the best of Your creation, O Allah.

[Marginalia: خوب = Good]

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful—I adjure you, O Aynāʾīl, O Harfāʾīl, O Sarjiyāʾīl. By the fierce wind, the sun, and the moon, I ask you to come to this place of mine and fulfill completely what I have commanded you and what I request from you. O Allah, I ask You, O Light of Light, O Director of affairs, O Knower of secrets—You are Allah, the Sovereign, the Conqueror. There is no deity except You. O Allah, by the right of these great names—Allah, the Exalted, the Great, the Wise, the Generous, the Living, the Self-Subsisting, the Unique, and the Self-Sufficient. He is the essence who did not beget anyone nor was begotten by anyone, and none is equal to Him. I ask You by Your glory and Your establishment upon Your throne that You fulfill my need and subjugate for me the lord of the Day of Hour, the Circuit, and the four directions. Surely You are over all things capable. Surely You decree and no decree is made against You. You alone are He besides whom there is no deity. I have brought faith in You and placed my trust in You—You are the essence before whom all creation is humble and base. You are Allah, the Conqueror. Your majesty is Exalted. Your essence is elevated above Your throne. The people of the heavens and the earth obtained illumination from Your light. Your essence is above having any partner. Everything shall perish and You alone remain. O Allah, You are the Compassionate, the Merciful. By Your mercy, Your anger and Your wrath are extinguished, and from Yourself You grant such felicity as You have given to Your chosen creation.

Its procedure is this: call the one of proximity in this manner:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. I adjure you, O Dardiyāʾīl, O Knower of Your throne, Kharfiyāʾīl, and Madhkhaʾīl and Marfiyāʾīl, and by the winds, the hostile and the departing, and by the descent of the sun and the moon [unclear/watermarked]
