# Page 344 (English translation)
**Protection from Grave Punishment**
Among the special properties of this verse is that when it is written on the shroud of the deceased, at the head in the middle and at the feet, the deceased will not be punished in the grave. However, [unclear: the two angels Munkar and Nakir] treat them gently. This verse is the greatest verse in the Noble Quran because it began with the especial name of the Divine Essence.

**Shipwreck Protection and Fulfillment of Every Need:**
Honor its value. In every difficulty and time of need and necessity, supplicate with it. A narration from a saint states that he was on a ship when a black wind blew, from which very few ships escape. This saint wrote Ayat al-Kursi on paper and threw it into the wind [unclear: in a cloth] and spread both hands and supplicated to Allah the Most High:

**"اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ بِاسْمِكَ الْعَظِيمِ اللَّهُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ إِلَى آخِرِهِ وَأَسْأَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ بِبَرَكَتِهَا أَنْ تُنْجِيَنَا مِمَّا نَزَلَ بِنَا وَأَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ وَكَاشِفُ الْكُرُوبِ وَأَسْأَلُكَ اللَّهُمَّ بِجَاهِ حَبِيبِكَ ﷺ الْأَكْرَمِ مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ"**

"O Allah, I ask You by Your great name. Allah, there is no god but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all existence, until the end. And I ask You, O Allah, by its blessing that You save us from what has befallen us, for You are the Knower of the unseen and the Revealer of distress. And I ask You, O Allah, by the rank of Your beloved, the most honored Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace." Read the complete Ayat al-Kursi and I ask You by its blessing, O Allah, that You deliver us from what has descended upon us. Indeed You are the Knower of all secrets and the Remover of troubles. O Allah, I ask You by the honor of Your beloved, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. Before the saint finished his supplication completely, Allah the Most High had saved him from this whirlpool [unclear: and calamity] and they all arrived safely and in well-being.

**Healing from Every Illness:**
Among the properties of this verse is that for whoever has an illness in their body, write Ayat al-Kursi three times on a china vessel with musk, saffron, and rose water, along with **"لو أنزلنا هذا القرآن"** until the end, and **"لو أن قرآناً سيرت به الجبال أو قطعت به الأرض أو كلم به الموتى بل لله الأمر جميعاً"** until there. Then after writing, read Ayat al-Kursi seven times and blow upon it, and also fumigate with perfumes. For three days, morning and evening, then [unclear: give it to the patient] to drink. Allah the Most High will grant them healing. If an amulet is written from it for eye pain, it is extremely effective. For eye pain, write this verse and the Verse of Light **"اللَّهُ نُورُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ"** up to the mim, and bind it on the hand; it is beneficial. If clear white water flows from the eye, write the complete "Say: He is Allah, the One" and write this supplication and bind it on the hand:

**"حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ الْعَضُدُ يَا غِيَاثِي فِي الشَّدَائِدِ حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ أَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا الرَّمَدُ الْمَرْمُودُ الْمُسْتَعْسِكُ بِعُرُوقِ الرَّأْسِ وَالْخُلُودِ فَإِنْ أَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْكَ لِيُوسُفَ بْنِ يَعْقُوبَ وَ بِقَمِيصِهِ الْمَقْدُودِ وَ بِحَقِّ تَوْرَاةِ مُوسَى وَإِنْجِيلِ عِيسَى وَ زَبُورِ دَاوُدَ وَ بِحَقِّ الْقُرْآنِ الْعَظِيمِ وَ بِمُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ سِرَاجِ الْوُجُودِ وَ سِرَاجِ الرَّبِّ الْمَعْبُودِ اذْهَبْ أَيُّهَا الرَّمَدُ عَنْ حَامِلِ كِتَابِي هَذَا بِحَقِّ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ وَ بِأَلْفِ أَلْفِ لَا حَوْلَ وَ لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ وَصَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَ عَلَى آلِهِ وَصَحْبِهِ وَسَلَّمَ"**

"Allah is sufficient for me—the Powerful One. O my helper in hardships, Allah is sufficient—the Self-Sufficient, He did not beget nor was He begotten, and there is none equal to Him. I swear upon you, O rejected ophthalmia, clinging to the veins of the head and the eye sockets. For by the oath of Yusuf ibn Ya'qub and his bloodstained garment, and by the truth of the Torah of Musa and the Gospel of Isa and the Psalms of Dawud, and by the truth of the mighty Quran, and by Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, the lamp of existence and the lamp of the Lord worshipped—depart, O ophthalmia, from the bearer of this my book, by the right of there is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and by a thousand thousand 'there is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Most Great.' And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, and his family, and his companions, and grant them peace."

Allah is sufficient for me, the Self-Sufficient. O my helper in hardships. Allah alone is the helper, who has no offspring nor [damaged: no equal...]
