Take out the carbuncles' ash from inside the ulcer. In technical terminology this is called "saniyā." Place it in an earthen bowl, make a solution of wisdom (gul hikmat), and keep it over fire for seven nights like a glass-blower burns in a furnace (bāwārī battī). Keep it for seven days continuously. When it becomes red like saffron, the calcined substance (kistā) will be prepared. Keep it in a glass vessel carefully, and that white water that was first extracted from the hair (balū) distil it seven times again, and whatever of its density (thiqal) remains is the murghīstā. Burn this in a vessel after making a good solution of wisdom. Then dissolve it in this water and distil it seven times again, and each time dissolve the density in the water. Until this density is completely dissolved. Then add the saffron-colored body and sal ammoniac to this density, mix well, and add that water and distil it. When all the water is drawn up and the density remains completely dry, apply the clay of zubaiq (impure clay) — that is, spread it on top — and work it, and continue the refinement of zubaiq until whiteness begins to gleam in it. Then whatever body you apply this to will become silver.

**The Elixir for Making Gold:**

If you intend to make yellow (gold), then follow this procedure: Take the ash of hair, which is also called ardh (earth), and double it with red (ahmar) and distil it until it becomes yellow. This is called nahhās (copper). Keep its water that has been extracted separately. Then take the saffron-colored body and add to it twelve times its amount of purified spirit, that is, the mentioned water, and distil it seven times, and each time dissolve the density and mix it into the water. Then distil the precipitate that was first extracted, adding to it three parts of yellow water, and keep the fire under the ulcer for two watches (two periods of time). Then after it cools, open the ulcer; red water will come out. Dissolve this again in a vessel and distil it three times. Now this color is complete. Place it in a glass vessel with an open mouth and put it in the ulcer, and place the ulcer in a copper vessel filled with water, and light a fire beneath. Until the color remains inside the glass and the water becomes pure. At that time, take a small amount: one part earth, one part color, one part water of life, and one part sal ammoniac. Put all in one glass vessel and place another glass vessel on top of it, and seal the joint of both firmly. If it is hot, dry it in the sun. Place it on a strong naked fire until the sixteenth (or forty-fifth) degree, so that all the water is absorbed into the earth. Then take it out and remake it. By doing this several times, the operation will be completed. Take it out, fill it in a glass vessel, and keep it with you. At the time of need, apply one part upon another part, and render thanks to God. Gold will be prepared.
