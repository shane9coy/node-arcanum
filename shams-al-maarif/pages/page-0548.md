Then change the last to the first. Degree by degree, as above, the twenty-eight names from the composition will be produced one after another, and these are the conquered ṣudūr of the chapter of action. You will find from the Great Chapter that its first will be alif and its last will be yā, and all the speech will revert to them in the twenty-eight degrees in which the first will be alif and the last will be yā, and the letter yā will be produced which is external. Therefore, as much as possible for you, change according to the chapters of sovereignty, fasting, and all year nights, and write the azīma in another place, and produce the ṣudūr from sixty compositions.

The transformation of the Small Chapter, which matches the speech of Fīlūsh, is as follows: according to him, there are twenty-two degrees of the Name Qā'im. When the ṣudūr and compositions are combined, they become thirty-two degrees. The Name has twenty-two degrees in each name, and these are the first ṣudūr. From the Small Chapter, you will find alif in the first degree and bā' in the last, which change the last to the first. Degree by degree, when you obtain it, the first will be bā' and the last will be bā'. Then when you transform in this way, eleven names will be produced first and eleven after, and on the thirteenth occasion, the name that emerges is the first conquered ṣudūr. From the Small Chapter you will find alif in the first and bā' in the last. Then the first of the speech chapter names will become the last, and know that its first in the beginning was alif and the last was thā'; thus now all speech has been conquered. Then change the last to the first. Degree by degree, until twenty-two, this will be sixty small rewards. You will find alif in its first and thā' in its last. Thus establish this in one name equally. But from this, four degrees of the external become the internal, repeat them in its sixty, and the tongue, where the four letters exit, from these. Therefore, when you have combined the degrees in one name, present them and the name of the speech chapter of the first. Thus at that time you will perceive the external and internal with the eye, because the difference of each art from it is separate, and it will be that which has been combined from each art: ten chapters without name in one line, as I explain. But make the change a flame, from which they will be produced. But write their vocabularies. This book that speaks to you, hear it, because from this arrangement infinite transformations are possible.

Chapter: Three Chapters—Small, Great, and Connected

Know that Hayākil and Tāl and Qalb and Ilāhī and Hawānā and Maghlūba and Murattaba Fī and Anwār and Kalāmiyya and Kalāmiyya—these belong to the Great Chapter of the ṣudūr, when they are combined upon all degrees. And these are the Malakūt and the Amīra and Harās (those who terrify) and Fizhā'a and Qārūra and Shihla—these all belong to the conquered chapter.

And know that Ghalba Ṣaghīra and Jāhiyya and Ṭard wa Jadhb and Maghlūb Ṣaghīr—these are all for the chapters. By the method of Balqata, son of Marāhān, Shāh al-Qawāri', and the same Saturn and Scorpio, and the extraction which has been completed for the chapters.
