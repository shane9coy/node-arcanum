# Page 414 (English translation)
412

He made rivers and springs flow, He placed His treasures between the Qaf and Nun. O Allah, place my love in the heart of this servant of this noble Surah. O, make him subservient to me, just as You made the sea subservient for Moses (peace be upon him), fire for Abraham (peace be upon him), the mountain and iron for David (peace be upon him), and jinn and humans for Solomon (peace be upon him). The sun, moon, stars, and all things were made subservient for our Noble Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him). O Allah, I ask You to make this client subservient to me to fulfill my need, by virtue of Your Greatest Name of Beautiful Names, may He fulfill my need. O Swift, O Near, O Responder, O Expander, O Loving - 3 times. O Great One of the Throne, I ask You by virtue of Your power with which You filled the pillars of Your Throne. By Your power with which You have made all Your creation subject to Your authority, and by Your mercy which encompasses all things, there is no god besides You, O Helper of those seeking help. Help me - 3 times, and in this very hour, help me in my practice, fulfill my need. O Allah, by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful ones, place my love in the heart of the servant of this Surah, regarding these verses.

Tuesday's Wird (ritual):

"They said: 'We have been given ill omens concerning you, O you who would disbelieve.' [But it is) because they were a people who did not believe] O King of the Day of Judgment! O Turner of Hearts, answer! O Father of Muhriz, hearing and obeying, by the right of the Turner of Hearts and Eyes, and by the right of the Victorious King whose command prevails over you, and by the right of Fuhatahayahal - 'And when the promise of my Lord comes, He will make it level; and the promise of my Lord is true.' I swear to you by the right of the Victorious King whose command prevails over you, O Ibn Muhriz al-Ahmar, and by the right of Kazahayil. Glorified be He who relieves every grieving one. Glorified be He who made the seas and springs flow. Glorified be He whose treasures are between the Qaf and Nun. Glorified be He who, when He intends a thing, says to it 'Be' and it is. - 21 times

O Allah, make the servant of this Surah subservient to me, just as You made the sea subservient for Musa, and fire for Ibrahim, and the mountains and iron for Dawud, and the jinn and humans and devils for Sulayman (peace and blessings be upon them), and the sun and moon and stars and all things for our master Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him). Make the servant of this day subservient to me, who comes to me and fulfills my need. And by the right of Your Great and Greatest Name, and by the right of Your Beautiful Names, O Swift, O Near, O Expander, O Loving - 3 times. O Possessor of the Glorious Throne, O Originator, O Restorer, O Doer of what He wills. I ask You by the light of Your countenance which filled the pillars of Your Throne, and by Your power with which You have power over all of Your creation, and by Your mercy which encompassed all things. O Helper of those seeking help, help me - 3 times - and have mercy upon me by Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, place my love in the heart of the servant of this Surah.
