# Page 658 (English translation)
A most subtle admonition for completing the composition:

When God Almighty intended to complete the composition with His name "Creator," He brought assistance to it through the name "the Firmament" and the name "the Fashioner," and He manifested His theophany upon it through the name "the Ever-Able," and the most excellent was obtained through it. Therefore at that time spiritual birth was obtained by it, and these are the signs of prophethood, and this is the first of all creations. And for this reason the Messenger of God ﷺ has spoken with this saying [unclear]:

Al-tāʾibu mina l-dhanbi ka-man lā dhanba lahu

That is: "The one who repents from sin is like one who has committed no sin." And in another hadith:

Kharaja min dhunūbihi ka-yawmi waladathu ummuhu

He emerged from his sins as though the day his mother gave birth to him.

Thus this is the first mode of the noble births. Then when they [the adepts] completed the intellect—which they have transcribed in the Register of Divine Providence, and which consists of three hundred and forty-five lines—meaning the name Al-Qadīr, and the verification of the stations of the Name is through knowledge of the spiritual states and modes. When you desire to obtain nearness in the court of the Lord through this name, it is necessary that you achieve depth and perfection in humility, continuous contemplation, spiritual retreat in the mysteries and realities of divine oneness. And when you wish to enter into seclusion, observe a retreat of forty days.

The method of the litany of "Creator, Fashioner, Former":

Recite the litany of these three names: Khāliq, Bāriʾ, Muṣawwir, until their effect becomes dominant over you and those states come upon you in waves, and you converse with them. This recitation of the names should be performed at all times. And if this name is engraved upon silver and the one suffering from a head ailment places it upon his head, he will be cured. And recite its recitation 242 times by multiplication from the square, and write it upon a silver leaf and keep it with you—the familiar spirit will be present. This is the chief of the four princes, and each prince has under him ninety-nine rows of one hundred familiar spirits each. Whenever you recite the name corresponding to this number, every intellect inclines toward it. And recite this:

Yā Allāhu yā Bāriʾ yā Fattāḥu ftah ʿalaynā sirra ghaybika lā ilāha illā anta l-muʿṭī l-hādī

The effect of this name has such wondrous and extraordinary qualities. Its dhikr is as follows:

Bismillāhi l-Raḥmāni l-Raḥīmi. Allāhumma anta l-Bāriʾu, abrazta l-ʿālama l-aʿlā mina l-jawhari l-faṭīmi, wa abrazta arwāḥan mina l-amri l-bahīyi l-khafīyi, wa abdaʿta l-ʿālama l-suflīya bimā huwa khayrun minhu li-amrika l-ʿalīyi, wa jamaʿta bayna l-muḍāddāti bi-ẓuhūri l-birri l-aẓhari l-jalīyi, wa tashābakat bi-mashābikihā l-arwāḥu wa kathāʾifu l-ashbāḥi ḥattā jazā qalamu l-tadbīri bimā shiʾta mina l-fasādi wa l-ṣalāḥi. As'aluka yā mūjida l-mawjūdāti mina l-maʿdūmāti wa mudabbira l-aflāki bi-daqāʾiqi l-ḥarakāti an tudabbiranī min kulli shay'in wa qāṭiʿin yaqṭaʿunī ʿanka. Allāhumma yā man lajā min ḥawādithi l-zamāni najjinī mina l-khaṭāʾi wa l-nisyāni wa l-kasali wa l-khidhlāni wa min khidhīyi l-dīni wa min kulli shāghilin yashghulunī ʿanka yā Allāhu yā Bāriʾu as'aluka an tusakhkhira lī ʿibādaka li-istīʿānati [unclear] ʿalā amrī bi-ḥaqqi ismika l-Bāriʾ ṭāʾ.
