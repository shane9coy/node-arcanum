# Page 238 (English translation)
Help me through this until by its means I immerse myself in the sea of the intimate ones, in lights, and through the breeze of greatness keep my existence there alive with Your favor. Help me through my spirituality until I become an aspirant of felicity and, through Your guidance, a far-removed cause of honor. O God, I ask with sincerity worthy of truthfulness and patience, and then with the blessedness of enjoyment and certainty—I ask [unclear] that You grant me, through Your generosity, that I become a possessor of truthfulness along with Your angels, awake with glory, a holder of conviction, and a helper through certainty, through which I may seek assistance for the reform of my religious and otherworldly affairs. And You Yourself are my helper without distress, who has begotten no one, nor was begotten by anyone, and there is none equal to Him. O God, be sufficient for me through the sufficiency of Your sufficiency until I seek no refuge among any of Your creatures. And through the light of this luminosity, illuminate me so that I may succeed among Your intimate servants with the breeze of felicity. Indeed, You are over all things capable, and You are worthy of acceptance. O Most Merciful of the merciful.

The form of this noble surah is very comprehensive; we have abbreviated its explanation so that the discourse does not become lengthy.
This is the form of the Ikhlās talisman:

Chapter: The Recommended Supplication for the Abundance of Sustenance

It is related in the noble hadith that a person came before the presence of the Noble Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said: "O Messenger of Allah, the world has turned its face away from me and my hand has become constrained." The Chosen One, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, replied: "Are you not aware of the glorification of the angels, through which sustenance is given to creation?" He said: "O Messenger of Allah, what is it?" He said: "This glorification is it:

Glory be to Allah the Magnificent. Glory be to Him who bestows favors and upon whom no favor is bestowed. Glory be to Him who gives sanctuary and upon whom no sanctuary is sought against. Glory be to Him who disclaims power and strength entirely to Himself. Glory be to Him whose glorification is a favor from Him upon whoever relies upon Him. Glory be to Him through whose praise everything glorifies. Glory be to You—there is no god but You—and with Your praise, O He through whom all glorifies. Deal with me, for indeed I am anxious."

Allah, the Exalted and Most High—glory be to His pure essence. Glorification belongs to His pure essence who does favors and upon whom no favor is done. Glorification belongs to His pure essence who gives sanctuary and upon whom no one is given sanctuary. Glorification belongs to Him who saves from power and strength. Glorification belongs to Allah whose glorification is a favor from Him and upon whom rests reliance. Glorification belongs to Him through whose praise everything glorifies. Glorification belongs to You—there is no god except You, and to You belongs all praise, O He through whom all glorifies. Have mercy upon me, for I am despairing.
