# Page 138 (English translation)
And the secret of birth is contained in the three hours of spirits. Thus each hour stands with the secret of one of these letters.
They emphasize the letters of divine oneness. The division of its twelve hours is from divine knowledge, and the human world is composed of motion and rest. The word is necessary for it. For the disclosure of its stages, the night was created, which is the substance of its concealment and return. For the world, there is a reality with the secret of shadow and halo. The evolution of spirits is under the wisdom of blessing through the progression of intellects and the repose of humanity. Thus twelve hours were made for the night. For this reason, the circle of Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, peace be upon him, consists of twelve months, and his letters are also twelve.
There is one letter for each hour, because the declaration of divine oneness lā ilāha illallāhu muḥammadun rasūlullāhi is completed. Similarly, the circle of day is completed from the circle of night. The wisdom of Allah the Exalted was completed with the mingling of mercy in the night and day. Thus the Most High has commanded:

"And out of His mercy He made for you the night and the day, that you may rest therein, and that you may seek His grace, and that you may be grateful."

Its meaning is that whoever has pronounced lā ilāha illallāhu with the conditions we have mentioned, it is as though he has worshipped Allah the Exalted for a complete year. The Noble Prophet, may peace be upon him, has said that the best speech that I and the prophets before me have spoken is lā ilāha illallāhu. Let it be known that opposite to four letters, there are twenty-four worlds of the seven celestial and terrestrial intermediary realms, eleven heavens, and twelve. Each world has one intermediary realm and four required things.
These worlds are the beginnings of the truths of emanation. There are twenty-four (24) worlds in total. Each world is a luminous letter in reality. Thus the single letter bears the manifestation of each of these worlds, while the reality of the celestial and terrestrial world is attributed to the Essence of the Throne. The secret of their form is written with two lines along with luminous light. My meaning is white light and green light. Both are in lā ilāha illallāhu muḥammadun rasūlullāhi. Write these two luminous lines beneath the Throne. Understand the reality of the ten subtle luminaries. The eight angels who carry the Throne—from them proceed the spirits, intellects, and luminous bodies. The entire celestial world is light and the Light of Lights. That is the Throne. Allah the Exalted is the one who illuminates this light. Each sphere has three targets, from which each luminous letter was created, then each sovereignty and power extends to the limit. The light of sovereignty assists the intellects, the light of the aggregated assists the spirits, and the light of the kingdom assists the heart. These become twenty-four. The heavens are for the eight, which result from multiplying three by eight. For this reason, whoever has reached the perfection of the Throne through lā ilāha illallāhu muḥammadun rasūlullāhi, the Pure Word ascends to the Throne because it has attribution to this station. Its ascension is in the realm of power and the ascent in sovereignty.
Two other benefits of the Pure Word:
Allah the Exalted has said:

"To Him ascends the pure word, and the righteous deed He elevates."

In a noble hadith, it is stated that whoever sleeps after reciting the Pure Word with purification and the mentioned light, his spirit passes the night beneath the Throne and obtains sustenance according to his acceptance. Similarly, whoever recites the Pure Word at the time of seeing the moon, he remains protected from all illnesses. Whoever recites this Pure Word upon entering a city, he remains protected from the trials of evil. Whoever recites the Pure Word with the intention that the conditions of the unseen be revealed to him, his unveiling will be granted, provided he observes the conditions we have mentioned.
