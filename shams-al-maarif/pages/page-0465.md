**Chapter 29: The Hidden Dispositions and Luminous Methods of the Divine Names**

The properties of the divine names of Allah the Almighty—Al-Mun'im (The Bestower), Al-Mutafadhdhal (The Most Gracious), Al-Muhsin (The Beneficent), Al-Jawwad (The Generous), Al-Rafi' (The Exalter), Al-Basit (The Expander), Al-Qabid (The Constrictor), Al-Mujib (The Responder), Al-Sami' (The Hearing)—are that the one who recites them shall always be filled with the blessings of Allah the Almighty and remain in His grace and beneficence. His courage advances in knowledge and understanding. His flaws remain hidden, and his supplication is accepted. All his needs are swiftly fulfilled. There is increase in his grace, the strength of his faith, his acuity, his comprehension, the preservation of blessings, and divine inspiration in his thoughts.

**Ritual for the Fulfillment of Every Need:**
The divine names Al-Mun'im and Al-Mutafadhdhal are both great names. Whoever recites these two names and then asks Allah the Almighty for whatever he desires, it shall be granted to him from all that is hidden. The one who remembers the names Al-Muhsin and Al-Jawwad always remains intoxicated with the grace and bounty of Allah the Almighty; he obtains good and blessing. Whoever writes the imprint of these two names on fine paper and keeps it with him, his morals and conduct shall be rectified. His soul shall be improved. His remembrance of it is recommended and beneficial, and whoever acquires the quality of piety through its remembrance, Allah the Almighty shall grant him excellent character.

**Ritual for Ever Remaining Prosperous:**
The divine names Al-Rafi' and Al-Basit of Allah the Almighty are very great names. These two names are the function of the angels of the Throne. Allah the Almighty grants the one who recites these names strength of knowledge, body, and wealth, as well as advancement and elevated status. Whoever has these two names engraved on a gold ring and wears it shall remain ever prosperous. The divine names Al-Mujib and Al-Sami' of Allah the Almighty are very great names. The one who recites these two names, whatever supplication he makes to Allah the Almighty, is immediately accepted.

**Ritual for the Acceptance of Supplication:**
Whoever writes Al-Mujib on his left palm and Al-Sami' on his right, then raises his hands toward the heavens and makes whatever supplication to Allah the Almighty, it shall be immediately accepted. The one who remembers the names Al-Mujib and Al-Sami', whatever he asks from Allah the Almighty, He grants to him. Their properties manifest very quickly. The name of the custodian of the name Al-Tawwab (The Oft-Returning) is Batāʾīl. He rules over four, and beneath each are 408 angelic hosts, and in each row are 408 angels assigned. The supplication of the name Al-Tawwab is this:

*Yā tawwābu anta al-tawwābu ʿalā man tāba wa-al-muqarribu li-man anāba wa-anta alladhī bi-yadika nūru karamika ʿalā qulūbi al-ṭṭālibīna wa-anta alladhī aḥyayta arwāḥa ahl al-rūḥi wa-al-maʾābi ḥattā rajaʿū ilayka wa-hādū ilayka bi-sarāʾirihim wa-tābū ilayka bi-qulūbihim wa-mālū ilayka bi-ẓawāhirihim minka al-khawfu wa-al-tʾyīdu wa-ilayka maʾālu al-qarībi wa-al-baʿīdi asʾaluka allāhumma bi-nūri al-tawbati wa-jināʾi al-awbati wa-kamāli al-raʾfati wa-al-raḥmati an tu-razziganī al-inābata ilayka sirran wa-jahran wa-al-wuqūfa ladayka ḥukman wa-amran wa-ikhṭifnī bi-karamika ḥattā lā anhaza ilā [unclear/watermarked] al-tālifati ʿannā wa qahrān wa-ijburnī bi-karamika innaka [unclear/watermarked] bi-qawlihi sayajʿalu allāhu baʿda ʿusrin yusran*
