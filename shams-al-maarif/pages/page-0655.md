# Page 655 (English translation)
On the authorities and the scholar, I say:
The details of the six remaining worlds
Six worlds are recorded within it, of which the first world is the degree of rank, and this is the first world, and this world is the secret of specialization with the establishment in divine unity (tawhid). It is in accord with the eternal destiny, every intellect with it from the head. Thus it is intellect and spirit and intellect, but spirit is with intellect, thus there are two spirits, but intellect is the spirit of spirit. It is with every soul and with spirit, thus they are spirit, and spirit is the spirit of the soul, and the heart is with the soul, and the name "heart" is the body of the most specific, and the soul is the spirit of the heart, but it is body, and the heart is its field. Thus these are all six worlds, and this is the straight path.
The bodily things on the Day of Resurrection—the more dense they are, the longer their day will be:
Thus bodily things on the Day of Recompense, according to their measure as they wish, and due to the predominance of qualities, will be fifty thousand years. They will be on their own path, and the day of the people of hearts is one thousand years, and the day of the people of souls is like one day, and the day of the people of realities is like one instant of a degree, and the day of the people of subtleties is like one minute, and the second, third, and fourth. The layer of the bodies of the presence is equal to whoever went astray and reached the lowest degree of dispute.
Hell has degrees alongside it:
Hell also has degrees, of which all seven degrees are for the subtleties, and this is their path. He approaches it who has been completed and has fulfilled his path, but he sees with faith everywhere, otherwise the remainder is written, and its end on the Day of Judgment is known, and he is among those whose day measure is fifty thousand years.
Clarification: God taught Adam the names of things
God the Most High says:
"He created you from weakness" up to His saying: "He creates what He wills."
Thus these subtleties and these realities are the names of degrees, and the reason for this is that God the Most High had taught Adam the names of these things, despite their disclosure and extraction. Thus He placed some of them after humanity and kept their secret in human nature, and He made their meanings the locus of knowledge which is the study of the divine name, and God the Most High commanded you to traverse the human names so that you may obtain the reality of the divine realities.
The first created thing is your very essence, and the name of it is the name "Creator."
God the Most High says: "And We made from water every living thing." Thus water has a role in the life of the body. It enters into matters pertaining to water, and this is from His proof which was obtained from the divine Throne above the river, and that which was established upon water, about whose glory He said: "And His throne was upon the water."
They write destiny during forty days in the womb upon sperm while reciting "Creator," "Maker," "Fashioner":
It has been established that the lights of the name "Creator" become material, and they recite upon this sperm for forty days, and when they have completed forty [days] and the spirits have become united with them, they impose upon it the name with the power of the divine name "Creator." They pass through the eternal decree and writing upon their destiny with the name "Creator," "Maker," "Fashioner," taking their things from the name of destiny. This is because the sacred lights entered through this name and became destiny, so whoever He wills, He gives.
