# Page 363 (English translation)
[Boxed Heading: The Numerous Benefits of Certain Verses]

It is very useful for protection from enemies and for remaining safe from thieves and bandits. A saint has said: We once halted for rest on the bank of a river during a journey. Some people came nearby and said, "Staying in this place is useless." Upon hearing this, all my traveling companions fled from there out of fear, but I remained there lying in bed, because I had heard from Hadhrat Ibn Umar a hadith that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever recites thirty-three verses of the Glorious Quran at night, no wild beast or thief or bandit will be able to harm him until morning." Every harmful one is a thief; whoever recites them, his life and his family will remain safe. That night I slept. During that very night I saw a group of people approaching me with bare swords, but they could not come near me. In the morning I departed from there. On the way I met an old man riding a horse who said to me, "Are you a human or a jinn?" I said, "I am a human, from the children of Adam." He said, "Then what is the reason that we attacked you more than seventy times during the night? Every time we approached you, an iron wall appeared before us." I said, "I have heard from Hadhrat Ibn Umar a hadith that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever recites thirty-three verses of the Glorious Quran, no wild beast or thief can harm him that night.'" When this old man heard this, he dismounted from his horse and, after kissing my head, said, "I promise that from today I will not commit theft or banditry."

Those blessed verses are these: "The last four verses of Surah Al-Baqarah, from li'Llahi ma fi al-samawat直至 end. Three verses of Surah Al-A'raf, from inna rabbakkumu al-lahu alladhi سے直至 muhsinin, [unclear] up to eleven verses of Surah Sad. Two verses from the end of Surah Al-Isra', from quli ud'wu al-laha直至 end. Two verses of Surah Al-Rahman, from ya ma'shara al-jinn直至 shuwath. And Surah Al-Hashr, from law anzalna hadha al-qur'an直至 end of Surah, and two verses of Surah Al-Jinn, from wa innahu ta'ala jaddu rabbin直到 shatatan. It should be known that these are called the "Thirty-Three Verses of Protection." People say that in them is a cure for a hundred ailments, such as leprosy, vitiligo, and other diseases.

It is narrated from Hadhrat Muhammad bin Ali, who said: "I recited these verses upon an old man who had been struck by paralysis, and blew upon him, and he immediately regained comfort—so great is the blessing of the Speech of Allah Almighty. Whoever inscribes this design on a silver ring or on a tablet during the first hour of Friday, that is, within one hour after sunrise, will see in himself a wonderful effect of love, acceptance, awe, and expansion of sustenance.
