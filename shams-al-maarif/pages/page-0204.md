# Page 204 (English translation)
[Top Center: 204]

Piety and the people of forgiveness are [the subjects of this practice].
If one performs prostration and then requests one's need, it will be accepted. Then recite:

*Wa arzuqnā wa jamīʿa al-muslimīna wa al-muslimāti wa al-muʾminīna wa al-muʾmināti al-aḥyāʾi minhum wa al-amwāti*
*Bi-ḥurmatī hādhihi al-sūrati al-mubārakti khaira al-dunyā wa al-ākhirati wa iṣrifi ʿannā wa ʿanhum bi-ḥurmati al-qurʾāni al-ʿaẓīmi*
*Wa bi-ḥurmati sūratic al-anʿāmi sharra al-dunyā wa ʿadhāba al-ākhirati wa sharra khalqika ajmaʿīna bi-raḥmatika yā arḥama al-rāḥimīna*
*Allāhumma ṣalli ʿalā muḥammadin wa ʿalā āli muḥammadin bi-qadri kulli yawmin maʿlūmin laka*

Grant sustenance to us and to all Muslim men and Muslim women, believing men and believing women, among them the living and the dead, with the sanctity of this blessed surah, the good of this world and the hereafter. By the sanctity of the noble Quran and by the sanctity of Surah Al-An'am, remove from us and from them the evil of this world and the punishment of the hereafter and the evil of all Your creation, through Your mercy, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad according to the measure of every day known to You.

This should be repeated three times. Upon reaching [the verse] *Rabbuka al-ghaniyyu dhū al-raḥmati in yashā yudhhabkum wa yastakhlif min baʿdikum* (133), recite it [that many times]. Then:

*Rabbanā innaka jāmiʿu al-nāsi li-yawmin lā rayba fīhi inna Allāha lā yukhlifu al-mīʿāda Rabbanā wa ijʿal lanā min ladunka walīyan wa ijʿal lanā min ladunka naṣīrā* (55)

Read [this verse] 55 times.

When you have completed the surah, recite this supplication:

*Bi-smi Allāhi al-raḥmāni al-raḥīmi yā sarīʿa al-ḥisābi yā shadīda al-ʿiqābi yā ghafūru yā raḥīmu yā khāliqu kulli shayʾin yā fāṭira al-samāwāti wa al-arḍi yā fāliqa al-aṣbāḥi yā musabbibata al-asbābi yā muftiḥa al-abwābi yā qāḍiya al-ḥājāti yā mujība al-daʿawāti yā wāfira al-ḥasanāti yā walīya al-ḥasanāti yā muqīla al-ʿatharāti yā muḥyiya al-amwāti yā nūra al-arḍi wa al-samāwāti yā ghāfira al-khaṭīʾāti yā sātira al-ʿawrāti yā rāfiʿa al-darajāti yā dāfiʿa al-balāyāti yā qāḍiya al-ḥājāti iqḍi ḥājatī fī hādhihi al-sāʿati yā ilāha al-awwalīna wa al-ākhirīna yā dhā al-jalāli wa al-ikrāmi* (3 times)
*Innamā amruhu idhā arāda shayʾan an yaqūla lahu kun fayakūnu fa-subḥāna alladhī bi-yadihi malakūtu kulli shayʾin wa ilayhi turjaʿūna*

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Swift in Reckoning, O Severe in Punishment, O Forgiving, O Merciful, O Creator of all things, O Fashioner of the heavens and the earth, O Splitter of the dawn, O Cause of causes, O Opener of doors, O Fulfiller of needs, O Answerer of supplications, O Abundant in good deeds, O Protector of good deeds, O Pardoner of faults, O Reviver of the dead, O Light of the earth and heavens, O Forgiver of sins, O Concealer of faults, O Elevator of ranks, O Averter of misfortunes, O Fulfiller of needs—fulfill my need in this very hour, O God of the first ones and the last ones, O Possessor of majesty and honor—three times. Indeed, His command when He intends a thing is only to say to it "Be," and it is. Glory be to He in whose hand is the sovereignty of all things, and to Him you shall return.

Whichever need one requests shall be fulfilled, provided one recites this supplication one thousand (1000) times:

*Bi-smi Allāhi al-raḥmāni al-raḥīmi Allāhumma innī asʾaluka mūjibāti raḥmatika wa ʿazāʾima maghfiratika*

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, I ask You [for] the things that necessitate Your mercy and the certain means of Your forgiveness.
