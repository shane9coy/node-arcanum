Or Shamkhayith or La-hiya-l-Lawhi-a-Bih Li-na, meaning "I am that essence whose obedience everything performs, and all the bonds of heaven and earth are with me, and in the houses they only obey Him." Alwahija wa ya Sakhra Khadimin wa ya Taisha Salita Al-Mawla, meaning "I am that essence that helps the servants and has mercy upon them when they are in bitterness."

The guardian will inform him in a dream:
The person who writes these names on a mirror and sleeps with it beside his head, and wishes to learn about something from the guardian, will know it in a dream. Yasamha'ata Lura La, meaning "I am that essence that is unique in its oneness, and is eternal, and the most merciful."

Every need will be fulfilled by reciting these names:
The person who recites these names will have his need fulfilled, and his words will be from the heavenly scripture. And if he mixes the first name with it and engraves it on a ring and wears it, he will obtain great acceptance, and wherever he goes, his need will be fulfilled.

The method of the operation of the names:
And the rule for the operation of the names is: fast for three days and obtain external and internal purity. Then if he wishes to destroy an enemy, he should write the name and that person's name and his mother's name on a dhatura leaf and bury it in their house. And whatever disease or illness he intends to cause, thinking of it will cause it to happen. And this operation should be done on Monday during the Chasht time, and this incense should be lit: sandalwood and sandal. And if he writes the name of some illness that he wishes to cause to spread, such as black cough or cold fever, etc., he should face the direction of Adda and not do it for more than seven days, or else that person will perish, and questions will be asked of him. And if this same name is engraved on a silver plate during the hour of Venus and kept with him, and he goes for some need, that need will be fulfilled. And if this name is written on a deer's membrane and bound inside the wing of a nasr bird, and he resolves to reach the houses, they will be caused to reach that very place.

For gaining acceptance and for hiding oneself:
And when you desire acceptance and rank among people, write this on a pure vessel, wash it with olive oil, fill it in a glass bottle, and when you go somewhere, apply it to your face. Whatever need you go for will be fulfilled, and whoever sees you will feel love. And if a person writes this name on a fox's skin and keeps it with him and goes among enemies, as long as he remains silent, no one will see him.

The method for meeting jinn and obtaining answers from them:
If you wish to see the jinn and meet them, and you also desire that the jinn obey you, write these names on a goat's skin and burn it in an earthen vessel. When it becomes ash, apply it to your eyes. The jinn will become visible and the conversations of the world will be heard. Then if you wish to inquire something from the jinn, recite the names from beginning to end and say: "O Sultan of the tribes of jinn, obey me and assign a jinn to my presence so that it may give me the answer." At that very moment, you will see the host of the jinn before you, and whatever you ask them, they will give you the answer now. And if your purpose is to see the jinn, perform spiritual retreat in a clean house every Friday for three days, recite the names one hundred times after each prayer. The guardian of each name will bring the jinn with countless attendants to present themselves before you, honoring you.
