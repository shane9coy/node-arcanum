Chapter 86: On the Properties of the Name Muqsit—Profit in Trade and Commerce

Whoever frequently mentions this name, to him shall be granted understanding of the mysteries of the balances, and its effect shall be in his inner self, and he shall remain free from excess and deficiency. Its image is written in the dignity of Mercury. Thus it is beneficial for takers and merchants. Its image is as follows:

Chapter 87: On the Properties of the Name Jāmiʿ—Return of the Lost and Escaped

This name is useful and suitable for various conditions. It pertains to the joints. Among its properties is that if this name is mentioned frequently, the lost shall be found and those who fled shall return. Do you not see that in it the jīm of Jāmiʿ, the alif of alif, the mīm of ṣūrah, and the ʿayn of ʿaṭf have been gathered?

These refer to the saying "Huwa al-hābiṭ" (He is the Descender). The names of its letters refer to "Mu'allif" and "Qāʾiduhum" (their leader). Its number is 114. This is an odd surplus number. Its 121 and 114 refer to the strong names, because gathering the various cannot occur without the power of gathering, and the specialization of Jāmiʿ is with the Day of Religion.
