# Page 453 (English translation)
Obtaining Sustenance Without Measure, Blessing in All Things

The one who maintains constant remembrance of the Name Al-Shakur (The Thankful) is granted many virtues by Allah the Most High. The properties of this Name possess a remarkable efficacy for the perpetuation of blessings. Blessings descend, and sustenance is obtained from places least expected. Whatever food or drink upon which this Name is mentioned will become blessed. Whoever recites these Names after every prayer shall never be poor. If the numerical values of the Name Al-Shakur are inscribed upon yellow silk in a four-by-four square and placed in a chest of wealth or a treasury, the wealth shall remain protected from all calamities.

Protection from Enemies

The one who recites the Names Al-Hasib (The Sufficient) and Al-Tawakkul (The Trustworthy) remains protected from the evil of enemies. If an oppressor extends his hand of oppression against an oppressed one, the reciter should recite these Names according to their numerical values during the afternoon time. He should speak these blessed words: "Allahumma inni ahtasibu bika wa atawakkalu 'alaika fi amri fulanin al-zalim" — then the neck of that oppressor shall immediately break.

Deliverance from Poverty and Destitution

For the reciter of the Names Al-Mu'ti (The Bestower) and Al-Ghani (The Self-Sufficient), springs of sustenance and rivers of worldly comfort flow forth. Through the blessing of these Names, a life of felicity and a death of martyrdom are attained. If a debtor recites these Names, his debt shall be repaid swiftly. The خاصیت (special property) of these Names is most powerful in removing poverty and destitution. Sustenance expands, and abundance increases in food and drink. Indeed, the remembrance of Allah the Most High is a mighty thing. The best of all worships is the Remembrance of God. It is obligatory upon every sincere person that while engaged in worldly affairs, he remains continuously occupied in the Remembrance of God. Remembrance should always be performed with the intention of obedience to Allah the Most High, never with any worldly motive.

That Which Is Least Expected Shall Be Attained

A great saint said: The person who remembers Allah the Most High with thoughts of the world — only the world shall be his portion. But the person who remembers Allah the Most High with the intention of worship, Allah the Most High shall grant him blessings that no one has heard of, no one has seen, and no one's heart has ever conceived. There is a Hadith Qudsi: Allah the Most High has said, "Whoever is prevented by My Remembrance from other supplications, I shall give him better than what I give to those who supplicate." Allah grants whom He wills His mercy, and Allah is possessor of great bounty and grace.

Practice for Attaining Martyrdom

The Name of Allah the Most High Al-Shahid (The Great Witness) is a mighty Name. Whoever maintains constant remembrance of this Name attains martyrdom. His موکل (deputy/attendant) is named Lurya'il. He rules over four officers, each of whom oversees 210 صف (ranks/rows), and in each row 210 مامور (deputies/agents) are assigned. The عکاشہ (spiritual visitor) comes to the reciter of this noble Name. The prayer of this Name is: "Ya Shahidu anta alladhi shahadta li-nafsika bil-wahdaniyyati wa anta al-'alimu alladhi a'allamta 'ibadaka bil-'izzati wa anta alladhi makkanna awliyaka fi 'awalam al-sahabi wa anta al-'alimu bil-ghaybi wal-shahadati wa... [unclear]... as'aluka allahumma... [unclear]... fi al-sutur tubayyinu li hiyaqiqa hujjatika wa tudahhihu liya daqa'iqa... [unclear]... bika... [unclear] anta allahu"
