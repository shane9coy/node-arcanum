# Page 29 (English translation)
In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful

The Greater Sun of the Sciences and the Subtle Secrets of the Knowledges

Urdu... Part One

Praise

Allah the Most High acquired all knowledge through the testimony of His oneness—such testimony of light through which the prophets, peace be upon them, gained all knowledge. Understand this. There is an arrangement in both testimonies that relates to the angels and the people of knowledge. That is, such a testimony through which one who knows the secret of these two testimonies has observed the inner things of both worlds with the station of unveiling connected to it. Every knower at this station has a connection that brings him to all wisdoms. This is my prayer to Allah, the Living, the Self-Subsisting: that He makes this book of mine an accepted truth in the Hereafter for me. May winds that bring joy and comfort be with me in my grave. May Allah the Most High make guidance clear for me and for you. May He grant us the lights of sanctity and the beautiful lights. Indeed, this is the luminous mercy and the path of the pure-hearted knowers, the method of the truthful and the righteous, through which one reaches the presence of the Lord of the Worlds. He is the Lord of lords. He is indeed the creator of forms and the one who shows manifestations. The Pure and Holy One, transcendent above all forms and shapes, is illuminated through the transformations of beauty—eternal, the First, established in both pre-eternity and eternity, the Everlasting. He is the one who elevates and lowers in the world of the higher and lower realms through His power, wisdom, and will. There is no deity except this great and exalted Essence. He is hidden within the veils of light, concealed behind all secrets. He is hidden from the sight of eyes. He sees all. He exists in His pre-eternity with His Essence and appears in eternity with His attributes. He is the First in pre-eternity and the Last in eternity. He appears at the boundary. He is free from accident and body. Directions and dimensions cannot encompass Him. Neither the rotation of a speck encompasses Him, nor does the passage of night and day befall Him.

I praise this Pure and Exalted Essence who placed everything with a measure. He is the knower of the hidden and the present.

I bear witness that Allah, the One without partner, has no need of anyone, and I bear witness that He is the one who gives souls the ancient body in the realm of the barrier, and I also bear witness that His knowledge encompasses the living and the dead creatures. He is the one who determines sustenance, the knower of the past and the future. He is the one who causes death and then revives.
