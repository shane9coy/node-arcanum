# Page 723 (English translation)
721

And the dhikr for it is this:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Allah, You are the Apparent through the attributes, the Hidden through the Essence, whom I cannot comprehend through the perception of the senses, the power of imagination, and fancy. And You are the Apparent, distinguished by mercy and generosity. And we look with the eye of the heart through the power of intellect by the method of deduction. And You are the Apparent through dominance, compulsion, majesty, and the attributes of greatness and perfection. I ask You by all Your most beautiful names and Your supreme words, that You manifest upon me from Your power what You have manifested over my desires, and by which I may overcome my enemies, and illuminate within my inner self with the light of Your hidden and apparent Essence. O He by whom my sins and heedlessness have passed away, and my self has been sanctified by the sanctification of Your Essence. O Allah, O the Pure, O the Hidden. There is no deity except You. Glory be to You, indeed I was among the wrongdoers.

The person who reads this supplication, Allah the Most High illuminates his heart and fulfills his desires.
