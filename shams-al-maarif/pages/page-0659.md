Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Musawwir"

Know that the Musawwir (Fashioner) of everything is He who drew its form, and this is the arrangement of its decree. Thus, "creation" means bringing into existence, and "form" means formation and the joining together of light. Allah the Most High says: "And We have certainly created you" – from this is the manifestation of power; the first command refers to "there was" and that world is the state of closure. Then He says: "Then We fashioned you" – this is an attribute accompanying an attribute, because between the day of creation and the day of manifestation, the span that passed is known to none except God. He says:
"O mankind! What has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Noble One, Who created you"
From this is intended the creation of power. "And proportioned you" refers to the internal, and the location of proportioning is the equilibrium of temperaments and the balance of limbs and their transformation. On the second day, and on the third day, the completion of creation from the third state is intended, which is mentioned in this verse:
"In whatever form He willed, He assembled you"
From this the secret of forming becomes manifest, and those are the forms of the True, and among the forms is the form of the spirit, and the spirit is the breath of God, and the breath itself is the secret of life.

Forms are of two kinds:
Forms are of two kinds: one apparent and one internal. The apparent is that from which the external shape becomes evident, and the internal is that which is seen with the eye of insight.
From this it is known that the world of bodies exists from the True through the spheres, and the internal form is what is expressed by nature. Thus, natures are intermediaries between bodies and actions, and the realities of the names and actions have manifested as a preserving memory alongside existence, and it is [unclear] the sage. The internal is that which manifests the soul. This is applied to inspiration. Indeed, this is the secret of the spirit, and it is the Divine Breath.

Allah created all beings with His names, attributes, and actions:
Allah the Most High created all beings with His names and actions. He created the existents through the spiritual path with invention, and He deposited in nature, with increase in actions and details, the days up to the first day. For this reason, they turned toward Him and yearned for His knowledge, and they aspired to His attributes.
