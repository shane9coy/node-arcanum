# Page 95 (English translation)
95

Translation: O Allah, I ask You by this Name of Yours with which You encompassed the world of creation and command in glory and darkness for the truth, which reveals all realities. The secrets and existence are exalted, and it is constant from the standpoint of the ignorant. By means of that which You guided and that which You wrote, You have made every [thing] witness it. In it there is no fault.

O Knowing One, O Forbearing One, O Opener, O Allah, O Lord, I ask You, O Allah, O Prostrating One, O Hearer, O Swift, O Majestic, O Exalted, O Accepted, O Good One, O Generous Giver, O Uplifter, O Witness, O Exalted in Rank, O Divinity, O Preventer, O Returner, O Giver of Benefit, O Allah, I ask You by the highest rank which connects between the stations of obligation. I ask You by that which You spread in the world of power (Jabarut) and that which You manifested in the world of dominion (Malakut). That which You chose in the knowledge of its importance. That which You concealed in the secret of Your mercy within the mysteries of the inner depths. That which You concealed in the secret of Your veil. And that which You placed within the secret of Your veil due to the reason of concealment.

Which have appeared upon blessings and salvation. Which is hidden from the types of qualities within the inner depths. Protect me with complete protection from the accidents, whispers, and evils of my soul's devils, O Satan, who is the enemy of good, who gives the bitterness of harm, and protect me from the evil of his deception. O Allah, I ask You that You grant me by Your breeze of grace and abundant generosity relative to the luminous angelic realm of knowledge-containing favor, and in Your words of praise and excellence in death and life, grant me greatness, so that I may ascend upon the saddle of knowledge and grant me Your gnosis, O Compassionate, O Bestower, O Lord of the Worlds!

The raising of the dead by Jesus

By this, Jesus, peace be upon him, used to raise the dead. This is the Greatest Name. This is the Great, the Most Great, the Pure, the Holy, the Divine, the "Knāna" by which all the heavens are not equal, as Asad ibn Musa narrated to me from Kulī, and he from Ibn Salih, that whoever keeps this Name of Forgiveness and Creation (ism al-ghufran wa al-takwin) while fasting, with pure clothes and body, on Sunday at the time of sunrise, written upon deerskin or upon pure clean paper, and gives it fumigation with Indian oud and red sandalwood, and binds it upon his wrist, then charity and blessings will be obtained for him such that he will marvel at it. There is no doubt that this is the Name by which the mission of Moses, peace be upon him, occurred. When the command came: "Indeed, I am Allah; there is no god but Me, so worship Me." And by this same Name, Zubaidah held Harun al-Rashid in her grip, to the extent that he would not do anything without Zubaidah's permission. Whoever writes this and gives it fumigation and hangs it in a place where the sun shines upon it continuously from morning until evening, great acceptance will be his among the people. And this is the Name:

Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā Allāhu yā Qāhiru yā Qayyumu yā Qā'imu yā Qarību yā Qadīru yā Qudduusu yā Qādiru yā Qayyumu yā Qahhāru Anta alladhī 'azzazta awliyā'aka bi-asmā'ika wa ja'altu anbiyā'aka bi-ihtifāli balā'ika wa qama'ta al-a'dā'a bi-sultāni qurbatika wa asmā'ika wa as'aluka bi-'izzika al-munī'i al-khatīri wa bi-jūdika al-'azīmi al-qadīri wa bi-haqqika 'alā khalqika mina al-jalīli wa al-qadīri an taj'alani 'azīzan fī qulūbi al-khāliqi malā misfā'a 'alayhim wa al-iftiqāri ilayka wa akrimnī bi-habā'ilika al-mu'taliyati fī asrāri sarā'irihim hatta anjū bihā wa lā raji'an ilayka
