The name Al-Muqaddim is a great name. It contains two letters of the Greatest Name. Its client's name is Tamāʾīl. Under it are four chiefs. Each chief rules over 184 ranks, and in each rank there are 184 clients. The client descends upon Zākir. The prayer of the name Al-Muqaddim is:

*O Advancer, You are the one who brought forward the people of sainthood to the Abode of Eternity and made them understand the secrets of the degrees of unveiling and witnessing, and illuminated their inner visions with the vision of the traces of the theophany of the King, the Worshipped. I ask You by Your power with which You have power over all of creation, and by Your mercy that spreads over the people of Your obedience and Your seas, that You make me an advancer in good deeds, racing toward You upon the horses of knowledge and obedience, turning toward You in the swiftest of times, O He in whose hand is the keyring of the hidden and the witnessed, and by Your power is the keyring of the earth and the heavens and the people of felicity and intercessions.*

The subjugation of the clients of various divine names

The name Al-Muʾakhkhir of God Almighty is a great name. Its client's name is Jibrāʾīl. It is chief over four commanders. Under each commander are 846 ranks, and in each rank 846 assigned clients. The prayer of the name Al-Muʾakhkhir is:

*O Delayer, You are the one who delayed Your mercy for the people of confusion, and You spread one [mercy] in order to place compassion between the people of the earth and the witnessed. And You are the one of strength and decrees, and You are the one who delays a thing as You love and choose, and advance whom You advance and delay whom You delay by means of the decrees. I ask You, O Allah, by the advancement of every advancer and the delay of every delayer, the advancement in all [good], and I take refuge in You from the harm of that which, when it became confusing, bewildered. And I ask You, O Allah, by the subtle mysteries of Your mercy, that You make me sound from ailments—for Allah—upon the succession of blessings, and grant me the encompassing of the greatest [knowledge] and the most radiant light and the nobler secret, O Possessor of generosity and blessings, O Lord of the worlds.*

The name Al-Awwal is a great name. Its client's name is Dardiyāʾīl, under whom are four rulers. Under each ruler are 37 ranks, and in each rank 37 assigned clients. The prayer of the name Al-Awwal is:

*O First, You are the one through whom the beginnings appeared, and You are the one whose existence preceded all the tribes. And You are the one who sent down the gifts in the mornings and evenings. And You are the Comprehensive, with whom there was none besides You, nor fate besides Your generosity and Your permanence. And You are the Conqueror over Your creation, and the Powerful over them by Your right, and the Knowing Disposer of their affairs, and the Sovereign who manages their actions and words. To You belongs righteousness and sovereignty and permanence, and by Your grace the essences of sovereignty and the malakut. I ask You by the secret of Your light in the world that You grant me precedence in good deeds and the existence of beneficial and righteous [things].*
