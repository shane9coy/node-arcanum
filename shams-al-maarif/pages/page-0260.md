# Page 260 (English translation)
He veils (darkens) the pupils, and he is among the savage souls. O Allah, I ask You with the secret of the letter Alif. O letter Alif, O wanderer, O mature one, O acceptor of repentance. O Allah, I ask You for this longing that comes from You, and the light that shows me Your path, and join me with spirit and basil, and grant me from You comfort with peace and divine pleasure. O expander, O one, O noble. O Allah, three times. O my Lord, who has no partner, I associate no one with Him. O Allah, whoever does evil and mischief with me, break his head, silence his tongue, put a bridle in his mouth, stop his deception, and place a barrier between me and him. O eternal, O praiseworthy, O glorious. O Allah, for the sake of the honored prophet Muhammad ﷺ. O Allah, I ask You with the comprehensive secret and the firm light that You grant me halal sustenance from Yourself, that opens my chest, elevates my stature. You are my qiblah and my support, and to You is the return and the end. You call to the goal and have mercy on the poor. There is no deity except Allah, who is knowing and patient. There is no deity except Allah, He is the Lord of the mighty Throne. There is no deity except Allah, who is the Lord of the heavens and the earth and the noble Throne. O Allah, Lord of Gabriel, Michael, Seraphiel, Azrael, Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, and Jacob. Grant me well-being and forgive me, and do not appoint over me any of Your creation with that which I have no strength. O Allah, O hearer of supplications, O acceptor of sins. Therefore, Allah is sufficient for all of you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing. I have placed my trust in the Living who does not die, and praise belongs to Allah who has no son, nor does any partner share His sovereignty, nor is there any guardian against humiliation. And say the takbir: Allahu Akbar, three times.

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from what You fear and from what I fear, and I seek refuge with Allah, there is no deity except Him, the one who restrains the heavens from falling upon the earth except by His command, every rebellious one and Satan from the desired.

O Allah, I seek refuge with You from poverty and debt, from blame, distress and grief, humiliation and oppression, tightness, envy, and apostasy. O Allah, make our end good in all matters, and protect us from the humiliation of this world and the punishment of the Hereafter. O Allah, I ask You on behalf of Muhammad ﷺ, the complete and beneficial master, the lights of the lights of the seals of mysteries, the secret of the mysteries of Your creation's chosen one, the mirror of Your essence and the witnessing place of Your attributes, I ask at the beginning of Your head. I ask with the light of Your essence, the expanse of Your mercy, the gift of gratitude and their mysteries that are difficult for You. O Allah, three times. O one, O self-sufficient, O living, O subsisting. That You grant me from Your knowledge: reason; from Your life: spirit; from Your will: knowledge; from Your power: action; from Your words: tongue; from Your hearing: understanding; from Your sight: unveiling; from Your encompassing: establishment; and from Your presence: grant me the mysteries of bestowal, before which the necks of tyrants bow and the souls of despots become obedient. For me, upon every beginning and every ending, gratitude belongs to You; truly You are rich and praiseworthy. O Allah, place me gently upon the carpet of Your mercy, and protect me with Your protection. Wrap me with the cloak of Your prophethood, seat me upon the throne of power with the trick of honor, cast over me the veil of Your majesty, spread over me the banner of honor, make sustenance easy for me, fill my hearts with Your fear and Your mercy, manifest for me greatness and awe, make me the master of hostility toward every rebellious tyrant and rejected Satan, protect me from error and mistake, grant me strength in speech and action. O Allah, I ask You, with You and with him upon whom Your essence depends—none knows except You—that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad ﷺ, whose very essence contains the light of the word Allah, the suns of the heaven of mysteries, the source of lights, the pole of the sphere of majesty, and the center of the orbit of beauty. O Allah, I ask You with this secret that is with You and with what is with me, that You grant me security from Yourself and remove my vain desires. Spread for me the carpet of power for my benefit, fulfill my obligation to me, take me to Yourself. [damaged source] O sincere one, in whom I have placed [damaged source]. Do not make me a trial with my soul, nor with my perception [damaged source]. Open my veil of secrets, my work upon my [damaged source].
