# Page 697 (English translation)
The heart of your Prophet Muhammad, may Allah bless him and grant him peace—verily, for the subjugation of the spiritual essence of Your name "al-Wadud," You are "The Loving One, The Praiseworthy, The Object of Worship." Answer, O King Hibāʾīl, [come] swiftly, hastily.

Upon the one who mentions this name, people will be gentle, the prayer will be accepted, and every desire will be fulfilled:

Whoever perpetually recites this dhikr, Almighty Allah softens the hearts of creation for his sake and accepts his supplication, and whatever he desires, He bestows upon him.

[Boxed heading: Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Majīd"]

"Majīd" is He whose noble essence is most generous. That is to say, when noble qualities of essence are accompanied by good actions, it is called "Majīd." Every owner [of this name] possesses glory, and the possessor of glory is noble. The synonyms of "Majīd" for emphasis are "Jalīl" (Glorious) and "Karīm" (Generous). Their meanings have already been explained.

Through this dhikr, one's rank shall be elevated and sustenance shall be expanded.

The practitioner should combine this name with [the formula] and maintain its recitation day and night; he shall obtain an elevated rank among creation. For obtaining sustenance, let him add the name "al-Razzāq" (The Provider).

The dismissed one shall be restored through this dhikr, and every desire shall be fulfilled:

Whoever has been removed from his position, let him engrave this name in a square shape upon a silver tablet, keep it with him, and recite it with the "Great Name" (ism jalīl); he shall obtain the position. And if a spiritual traveler maintains its recitation, every desire shall be fulfilled. Its form is as follows:

The dhikr of this name is:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful ۝ O Allah, You are al-Majīd, possessor of vast nobility, the Glorious, who bestows glory and gifts upon servants with increasing generosity. You have manifested the splendor of Your essence through Your beautiful actions and Your noble grace. By the light of Your essence, all the pillars [of creation] in the highest assembly have glorified You. I ask You by the honor of Your glory, O Majīd, over the people of praises, through the angels of Your majesty, O Majīd, over the people of glory, O Unique One in Your ancient and necessary speech. Glory be to You, You are the Glorious through the honor of Your great glory. Continue Your favors through Your actions and Your messengers as witness. Make our Lord glorious through Your light. O Allah, O Majīd, I ask You to subjugate for me the servant, the spiritual essence, the one who says [unclear] "As Allah wills, it comes to be, and what He does not will, does not come to be" [damaged source/watermark: "Indeed You are"] over all things, the Possessor of Power.
