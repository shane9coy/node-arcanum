# Page 590 (English translation)
**The Operation of Love:**
When you write this with the names of the wawīyah on the name of the person whom you wish to love, and recite it on Sunday, you will obtain love.
Enter into seclusion, light incense three times, and write the letter waw and bind it upon your head; and recite the invocation after every prayer twenty times. The client will present himself, and his light will shine like the sun. He will come and offer greetings, and he will say: "What is the task?" Tell him: "I desire that you serve me." He will say: "Very well." Then, whenever you call him, he will immediately present himself. His name is Ṭarīʾīl.
When you call him, have the letter waw engraved upon a gold ring when the moon is in its mansion for this letter, and light aloeswood and sandalwood; have him recite the incense [prayer] twenty times after every prayer. He will fulfill your need. His invocation is this:
 Bismillāhi al-Raḥmāni al-Raḥīm. Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā Wadūd yā Wahhāb yā Wālī yā Wājid yā Wārith yā Allāhumma as'aluka bi-birri asmā'ika al-ʿiẓāmi wa bi-nūri wajhi al-karīmi alladhī nārat bihi al-ẓulumātu an tuwaffiqanī wa tatawallanī bi-walāyatika wa takshifa lī al-ʿaṭā'a ʿan birri al-wāwi wa aʿṭinī taṣrīfahu yā Wahhāb hayyājan wa-ahbiṭ yā Ṭarīʾāyīl wa anta yā Dardīʾāyil bi-amri Allāhi wa bi-ḥaqqi mā taʿlamūna min ʿaẓīmi qudrati Allāhi wa bi-ḥaqqi Jibrā'īla wa Mīkā'īla wa Isrāfīla wa ʿIzrā'īla ajībū ayyuhā al-mulūku wal-tawī bi-ḥaqqi ḥarfi al-wāwi wa bi-ḥaqqi man khalaqakum wa khalaqahu hayyā yā mawlāya minka arjū wal-uṭlub al-madad wa ilayka rujūʿī as'aluka bi-mā qaddartahu fī al-lawḥi an taḥfiẓanī yā Ḥafīẓu wa rudd ʿannī man yasū'anī yā arḥama al-rāḥimīna al-wāḥa al-wāḥa al-tawī ṭāʿiʿīna ʿajjil bi-alf lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhi al-ʿAliyyi al-ʿAẓīm.
Translation: "O Allah, I ask of You, O Loving, O Bestower, O Protector, O Allah, O Inheritor, O Allah, I ask of You by the virtue of Your great names and by the light of Your noble face, by which all darknesses have been illuminated, that You grant me success and take me under Your guardianship through Your authority, and unveil for me the gifts through the virtue of the waw letter, and grant me mastery over its manipulation, O Great Creator. O Ṭarīʾāyīl and Dardīʾāyīl, descend both of you by the divine command, by the virtue of the great power of Allah which you know, and by the right of Jibrāʾīl, Mīkāʾīl, Isrāfīl, and ʿIzrāʾīl, O honored angels! That is, the waw letter, and by the right of the Pure Essence Who created you and created it: come quickly to serve me. O my Lord, from You I hope, and from You I seek assistance, and to You is my return. I ask of You by that which You have decreed in the Preserved Tablet, that You protect me, O Protector, and turn away from me whoever harms me, O Most Merciful of the merciful, swiftly. O angels, come quickly to me by a thousand: there is no power nor strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Great."

**For Making Someone Sick:**
When you intend to exercise domination over rain [note: possibly corruption] for a certain person, write these letters and names calmly along with the name of that person, and you intend to make him sick; dissolve it in water and have him drink it, and immediately that person will become ill. His lights of the names are these:
Jalb li-mā khalūṭāthāʾīl bi-ḍamḍam wa khūnavū Hāb ajib wa tawakkal bi-hādhā
When you read these names replacing "so-and-so" with his name, Allah Most High will inflict upon him an illness such that he will say: "Now I hope for good through Faḍl Muḥammad."
