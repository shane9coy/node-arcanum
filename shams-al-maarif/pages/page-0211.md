# Page 211 (English translation)
209

And He heals a healing that cannot be equaled. O Everlasting One, O Allah, I indeed ask You by the right of the mosques of Allah and by the right of Your righteous servants, and by the right of the bowing and prostrating ones, and by the right of the supplicants, for indeed You are Allah the Generous, and by the right of whoever called upon You, that You may grant me my desire and be my helper. O Allah, I indeed ask You by the right of whoever did not associate anyone with his Lord, that You may bear witness to me, and guide me, and help me, and grant me in my affair right guidance. O Allah, O You whose word this word is, I ask You by Your mighty word. And by the surah "Qul awḥī ilayya" (Say, it has been revealed to me) and by the wise promise. O Allah, O You who have counted everything in number and made the sea flow with water, and You cause creation to perish while You are eternal forever, O You whom describers cannot describe and cannot be described by standing or sitting, that You may subjugate for me the servants of this surah and the names, by the right of believers and with believers, for indeed You have power over all things.

O Allah, O servants of this spiritual invocation, O upon you, O company of the noble spiritual beings entrusted with the spheres, He who created you from His light and placed you beneath His throne, unless you answer, being listeners, you act according to what I desire. I adjure you by this invocation and the names and the surah, by the right of arfaz̲ 2 kalhawāẓ 2 baẓṭahawāẓ 2 kamaṭahawāẓ 2 anhawāẓ 2 fanwish 2. I adjure you, O Ruqāyāʾīl, the angel assigned to the sphere of the Sun, by the right of Allah there is no god but He, everything perishes except His face, His is the command and to Him you shall return. I adjure you, O Ruqāyāʾīl, by the presence of the Mudhahib, answer O Mudhahib, by the right of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O Ruqāyāʾīl, and by the right of yāh 2, unless you answer and hurry and do what I have commanded you. I adjure you, O Jibrāʾīl, by the presence of the White one, answer O White one, by the right of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O Jibrāʾīl, and by the right of sām, unless you answer and hurry and do what I have commanded you. I adjure you, O Samsamāʾīl, the angel assigned to the sphere of Mars, by the right of Him whose command is between kāf and nūn; indeed His command when He intends a thing is only to say to it "Be" and it is. Answer O Samsamāʾīl, by the presence of the Red king, answer O Red one, by the right of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O Samsamāʾīl, and by the right of damlīkh, unless you answer and hurry and do what I have commanded you. I adjure you, O Mīkāʾīl, the angel assigned to the sphere of Mercury, and by the right of Him whom the sights cannot attain while He attains the sights, and He is the Subtle, the Aware—the Shaqqār, answer O Mīkāʾīl, by the presence of Barqān, answer O Barqān, by the presence of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O Mīkāʾīl, and by the right of ahyā sharāhiyā, unless you answer and hurry and expedite and do what I have commanded you. I adjure you, O Ṣarfayāʾīl, the angel assigned to the sphere of Jupiter, by the right of Allah, the light of the heavens and the earth. Answer O Ṣarfayāʾīl, by the right of shamhūrush, answer O Shamhūrush, by the right of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O Ṣarfayāʾīl, and by the right of dahmish, unless you answer and expedite and hurry and do what I have commanded you. I adjure you, O ʿAnāyāʾīl, the angel assigned to the sphere of Venus, by the right of Him who knows what every female carries and what the wombs diminish and what they increase. Answer O ʿAnāyāʾīl, by the presence of Zawbaʿah, by the right of the king who overcomes you whose command it is, O ʿAnāyāʾīl, by the right of shaʿshaʿ and shaʿshūb.
