# Page 387 (English translation)
The Operation of Summoning an Absent Person or Beloved

When you wish to summon an absent person or beloved, write the coming Name upon a deer hide with a pen of basil, along with saffron and rose water. This operation should be performed in the first hour of Venus. If the desired one is at a very great distance, cast this parchment into a fierce fire. If they are near, bury it in a medium fire. Having read this amulet, appoint his attendants to present him—this operation is most excellent and swift in effect. The names are: Mahla Shalīṭaʾ, Tamahū

*Subḥāna-hiya al-Wahā al-Sāʿa al-ʿAjala al-Wahā al-Ṭāʿa lillāh wa-as-māʾihi fa-inna*

*makhlūqūn wa-innamā al-ṭāʿa lillāh wa-la-as-māʾihi wa-bi-ḥaqqi alladhī qāla li-l-samāwāti wa-l-arḍi iʿ'tīyā ṭawʿan aw-karhan*

*qālatā ataynā ṭāʿiʿīn al-wahā al-ʿajala al-sāʿa bi-iḥḍāri fulanāna ibni fulanah aw fulanah bint fulanah*

Obedience belongs to God, His Messenger ﷺ, and His Names. Truly we are created. Truly obedience belongs to God and His Names, and by the truth of Him who said to the heavens and earth: "Come, willingly or unwillingly." They said: "We have come obediently." O urgency, hasten! The hour [is呼唤] for the presentation of So-and-so son of So-and-so, or So-and-so daughter of So-and-so.

All Needs Are Fulfilled, Healing from Illnesses, Victory Over the Enemy

My brother, know that I was seated in the service of Shaykh ʿAbd al-Wāḥid al-Andalusī when suddenly a man came and greeted the Shaykh. The Shaykh responded to his greeting most excellently. Then that man drew near to the Shaykh and began to speak softly. The Shaykh gave him no answer. That man began to beg most humbly. When his insistence exceeded all bounds, the Shaykh said: "O man, keep a fast for three weeks and completely abstain from animal food. Then come to me afterward and I will fulfill your need." The man said "Very well" and departed. When three weeks were completed, he presented himself before the Shaykh and said: "Your Eminence, I have done what you commanded." The Shaykh said: "Complete forty days, then come to me."
