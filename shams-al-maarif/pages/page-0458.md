<u>Supplication of the Name al-Muhyi (the Life-Giver)</u>
The name al-Muhyi is one of the letters of the Greatest Name. Its agent is named Karmayil. Under him are four rulers. Each ruler has sixty-eight ranks under him, and each rank has sixty-eight subordinate agents. They reside in air and water. The supplication of the name al-Muhyi is this:

O Life-Giver, You are the One who gave life to the hearts of Your servants and Your allies with the light of unveiling and beautification, and You ensured the tastes of Your prophets with connection and manifestation. And You adorned Your beloved ones with the adornment of recognition, the most beautiful manifestation. I ask You by the life of Your face, the spreading of Your mercy and compassion, and by the extremity of Your favor, to grant me a pure, everlasting life after which I shall not die. Make me alive in both abodes, and grant me knowledge of the two worlds, O Lord of the lords.

<u>Subjugation of the Agent of the Name al-Mumit (the Death-Giver)</u>
In the name al-Mumit are two repeated letters mim from the Greatest Name. Its agent is named Furtamayil. Under it are four rulers. Each ruler oversees four hundred ninety ranks, and each rank has four hundred ninety agents. The supplication of the name al-Mumit is this:

O Death-Giver, You are the One who caused Your enemies to die with the force of patience. And You are the One who destroyed the Pharaohs with the might of Your wrath, secretly and openly. And You are the One who brought judgment and command upon those who associated partners with You in this abode. And You brought them to what You promised them in Hell and punishment, and no intercessor shall profit them in the varieties of the Reckoning. I ask You, O Allah, by Your subtle gentleness and by the Lord of truthfulness, to revive my heart with Your light and to cause my enemies to die with the light of Your manifestation, O Death-Giver.

<u>Supplication of the Name al-Hayy (the Living)</u>
In the name al-Hayy of Allah the Exalted there are abundant qualities related to the immediacy of life. Its agent is named Sadamiyayil. This one is chief over four rulers. Each ruler oversees eighteen ranks, and each rank has eighteen subordinate agents. The reciter's agent comes to him. The supplication of the name al-Hayy is this:

O Living One, You are the One who spread life throughout the horizons, and You completed the bounty of Your prophets without limit. And You forgave the people of love on the day of meeting. And You gave life to the seekers' lives through the life of Your knowledge. And You caused the souls of sinners to die through the heart of the sultan of Your might. And You brought forth Your prophet and raised him in the degree of the Most High, and You strengthened him with one of the allies of the victorious ones. And You refined him through the name of the Living One in the places of establishment.
