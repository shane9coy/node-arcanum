[Section 61]
<heading>
Chapter 61: The Properties of the Name Muḥyī (the Reviver), the Manifest and Hidden Life of the Heart, and Divine Grace and Generosity
</heading>
This is a luminous and Syriac name. Whoever invokes this name frequently, Almighty God will grant his heart manifest and hidden life, and with it, He will revive all things. This name belongs to Isrāfīl, upon him be peace. This name is related to the letter hāʾ. Whoever inscribes this name on a ring during the hour of żuhr on Friday and wears it, Almighty God will make his remembrance alive and raise his status, and he will witness divine grace beyond expression. Its numerical value is 68. It is an even-perfect number. Its parts (divisors) point to 159 divine names. The names of its letters (24) point to God's hidden names, because in the degrees of honor and trust, disgrace lies in the degrees of lowly ones. Its square (magic square) is this:

| 23 | 10 | 5 | 20 |

| 18 | 13 | 12 | 25 |

[Section 62]
<heading>
Chapter 62: The Properties of the Name Mumīt (the Destroyer), the Destruction of the Unjust and the Impious
</heading>
This is a magnificent and great-proven name. Whoever desires it, to destroy all the unjust and the impious, let him invoke this name frequently and curse the oppressor, and he will be destroyed instantly. But one should fear Almighty God and not use it unjustly. It has great power to inflame and strengthen desire. Whoever invokes this name so frequently that his action predominates over it, then whoever's name he mentions whose destruction he intends, that person will be destroyed immediately. Its numerical value is 295, and this is an odd-excess number. It has 525 parts (divisors), which with the number constitute the divine name's number. The names of its letters point to two glorious names: Amān and Mshī. Its square (magic square) is this:

| 95 | 107 | 99 | 76 |
| 93 | 65 | 93 | 110 |
| 110 | 100 | 87 | 104 |
| 102 | 54 | 6 | 98 |

<heading>
Chapter 63: The Properties of the Name Ḥayy (the Living), the Working of Long Life and the Revival of the Heart
</heading>
The name Ḥayy, ʿAlī, and Syriac. Whoever invokes it so frequently that his worlds conform to it and his action predominates over it, his life will be prolonged, and Almighty God will revive his heart with the light of tawḥīd. This remembrance (dhikr) is Lā ilāha illā Allāh, from Jibrāʾīl, upon him be peace. Its practice is for whoever it is appropriate for to discover the name of the work. Its numerical value is 28. This is an even-perfect number. This is the name from which all numbers branch out from one another. From among them, only those numbers can be taken which are names that have with them the life of this degree.
