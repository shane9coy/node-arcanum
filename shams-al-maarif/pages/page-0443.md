# Page 443 (English translation)
[Inside the frame] Chapter 24: The Fourth Method of Divine Mysteries and Strange Benefits and Properties [End of frame]

Among the ten Beautiful Names al-qadīm al-azalī al-wāḥid al-aḥad al-Ṣamad al-fard al-majīd al-mubdi' al-mu'īd, their properties are intertwined with the silk of special monotheism. Through them is explained the purification of the divine attributes of Allāh the Most High and being free from defects—which are generally attributed by disbelievers to His sacred essence. The one who remembers these names always remains protected and distant from sins.

Obtaining the Pleasure of Allāh the Most High:
The remembrance of the name al-qadīm al-azalī of Allāh the Most High is always engaged in obtaining the pleasure of Allāh the Most High; poverty and affluence do not concern him; the treasure of contentment is granted to him; he attains the station of asceticism and spiritual discipline. If a ruler performs the recitation of the aforementioned name, his rule will endure and no one will disobey him, neither among the army nor among the subjects. If one engraves the jinnī and numerical design of al-qadīm on a silver ring and keeps it with him, then the same property will manifest that manifests from the ten names mentioned above. And whoever maintains consistency in reading these names after the five prayers, Allāh the Most High will keep his family and offspring protected until the Day of Resurrection.

Protection from Hunger and Preventing Pregnancy:
In the name Ṣamad of Allāh the Most High there is a great magnification. If the people of spiritual discipline maintain consistency on the remembrance of this name, Allāh the Most High makes them unconcerned about food and drink. If one remembers this name in solitude, he will not suffer the affliction of hunger. Let the ignorant one not mention any other name along with it. If a woman remembers this name, pregnancy will not settle for her—that is, for the duration that she maintains consistency on the remembrance of this name, she will not become pregnant.

Increasing Status and Dignity:
Allāh the Most High elevates the status and dignity of the one who remembers the names al-fard al-majīd.

Safe Travel and Protection of Property and Residence:
If a traveler remembers the names al-mubdi' al-mu'īd before departing on a journey and during the journey, he will return from his journey safe and sound. And whatever thing of his has been stolen or lost, by remembering these names that thing will be found. If the numbers of these two names are written on fine paper and placed in the house before departing on the journey, then no evil or harm will befall the house during his absence. The mysteries and properties of these names are countless.

Deliverance from Hardships and Sorrow:
In the name al-muqītt of Allāh the Most High there is profound influence; it is excellent for obtaining deliverance from hardships. Its attendant's name is 'Afī'ā'īl, under whom are forty ranks, each rank containing 120 officers of ranks, and in each rank are 7,200 attendants assigned, who constantly show kindness and general benevolence. Rare angels will grant mercy to those who remain near the one who remembers this name.
