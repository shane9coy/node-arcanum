Expansion of Sustenance and Benefit in Trade

The saying of Allah the Exalted is:
*Faqultu istaghfiru rabbakum innahu kana ghaffaran yursilu assamaa 'alaykum midraran wayumdidkum bi'amwalin wa banina wayaj'al lakum jannatin wayaj'al lakum anharan*

This verse is proven effective for the increase of sustenance, the progress of trade, and the abundance of benefit and profit. Whoever has this seal engraved on a silver ring and wears it on his finger, Allah the Exalted will make sustenance easy for him, and amazing blessings will become visible. Its design is as follows:

For Obedience and Worship and Victory over the Enemy

The saying of Allah the Exalted is:
*Inna rabbaka ya'lamu annaka taqumu adna min thuluthayi allayli wa nisfihi wa thuluthihi wa ta'ifatum min alladhina ma'ak wallahu yuqaddiru allayla wanahara 'alima ann lan tuhsuwhu fataba 'alaykum* (Al-Muzzammil: 20)

This verse is very effective for one who intends to increase in worship and to turn towards Allah the Exalted. Its procedure is as follows: at the time of applying kohl, perform this noble verse at the time when the Friday prayer is being held, and recite these words: *Ghalaba Allah 'ala fulān* [Allah has dominion over so-and-so]. Then wash this seal with pure water, recite the above verse three times, blow upon it, and drink from it three times. Allah the Exalted will grant him the success for obedience and worship. The arm [amulet] of this seal is written here. Write Surah *Inna ja'alnahu li-fi 'uluwwin* on yellow cloth with yellow color during the hour of Mercury when the Moon is auspicious, and place it on your head; then whoever fights with him, this will dominate over him. Write it during the hour of the Sun while Mars is in opposition, and keep it with you; then you will never be wounded and will remain victorious over the enemy.
