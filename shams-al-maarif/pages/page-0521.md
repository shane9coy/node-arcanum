# Page 521 (English translation)
They will make the breasts of the Afghans, and Arabs and Turks will be above them, and they will have iron whips. The army of the Romans will be defeated and then repopulated. Shiraz will be rebuilt from its foundations, and Islam will not be with great heroes after this event. After this place, in this era of the Khawarij and their attack, there will also be a day when the Dajjal will be upon it, while the wicked will be masters of the cities—swift, treacherous, and false. And when they abandon the hundred and six, they will become like the tail of the news, and they will eat the secrets of the midwives. They will wear garments of various skins, and they will treat the unlawful alongside the lawful. That a person of Arab lineage will suddenly appear before them and very quickly seize the kingdoms, and they will conquer the cities without doubt. And many will pray with humility, supplicating in prayer, and after this Mahmud will appear and will possess the land of Syria without fighting.

[Arabic poem in two-column layout]

And various narrations, different accounts,
About a dog—assistance of the declination,
A Turkish and Roman and Egyptian—
Kings of the earth, great in deed.
Their encounter will be on the day of Tuesday,
The dawn prayer, the battle joining.
The Roman ruffians will appear from it,
And the cross will rise over the valleys.
They call Saleh with a voice saying,
As Satan does in that discourse.
And they gather in a group, an assembly,
For provisions by night with supplication.
And none of them returns to the land of Rome,
Except one man alone in haste.
A Turkish and Egyptian altogether,
They differ in saying and alleged speech.
The sword will flow in Basri killing,
To Hafsi of the graves with battle.
And they meet from among Hamdan searching,
As if the pillars of the moon had sunk.
These are indeed the signs of the Mahdi,
He will possess the lands without doubt.
And the rod will tremble in his hands,
And the wild beasts of the mountains will befriend him.
The lands will obey him and those upon it,
Deliver it to creation with perfection.
And he will bring the proofs that—
And establish its measure as a measured bushel.

They will go out of all of Syria and protect it, and in every state he will spend all his wealth. And a Roman army will come from the direction of Rum. It was said that Kamil is the son of Kamal. Along with him will be Roman Christians, and from Rum each one will descend swiftly riding horses towards his side, and the forests of Syria will become desolate and empty. They will make the breasts of the Afghans, and Arabs and Turks will erase them. The iron whip and the fleeing will be—the army of the Romans will be defeated and then repopulated. Shiraz will be rebuilt from its foundations, and the fortress with great heroes and Islam will not be in this. After this event, after this place, in this era of the Khawarij and their attack, there will also be a day when the Dajjal will be upon it. And they will meet a person from among the people of Imran—as it were, the one whose name is [unclear]. So [unclear] of their awe. And when they possess all the cities, the gates will become despised in their hands. And [unclear] soldiers of the lands and wild beasts will entrust in the mountains that his name remains and from the emergence of the stars and the beloved.
