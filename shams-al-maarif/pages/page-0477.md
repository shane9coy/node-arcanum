**He will not be affected by sleep:**
Write Ra on the skin of a chamois. Write the number 200 ten times around it. Whoever has this skin will not be affected by sleep.

**For release from imprisonment:**
If someone writes the number ten and 200 with iron ink on the door of the prison, also writing the name of the prisoner whom you wish to release, that prisoner will obtain release very quickly. The diagram is as follows:

The supplication is this:
Allāhumma innī as'aluka yā raḥmānu yā raḥīmu yā razzāqu yā rafī'u yā raqību yā rashīdu yā ra'ūfu yā rabbi bimā awda'tahu ḥarfa l-rā'i mina l-asrāri l-makhzūnati wa-l-anwāri l-maknūnati an tusakhkhira lī khuddāma hādhā l-ḥarfi l-sharīfi fīmā āmuruhum bihi innaka 'alā kulli shay'in qadīrun

**The letter Zay:**
The letter Zay is a vocal letter. Some write it as a silent letter. Since it is the turn of Saturn, it produces a strange and peculiar effect in good works.

**Benefits:**
Write Zay eleven times on pure silver during the day of Saturn when the Moon and Jupiter are in conjunction, and have it engraved; then tie it to yourself. He will be protected from the evil speech of creation and from harm. Every person will treat him with kindness.

**The spleen will be healed:**
If you write its letter in abjad and number on a lapis lazuli wooden board on the day of the full Moon when the Moon is in ascendance or among the inauspicious stars, and tie this board to the place of the spleen, healing will occur within a few days. The diagram is as follows:

**Everyone will love him:**
If you write this letter's number on sandalwood during the first hour of Thursday and place it toward your head or forehead, everyone will love him.

**For destroying a place:**
If you write this letter 99 times during Thursday in the hour of Jupiter and throw it into an abandoned place, that place will fall and be destroyed.
