Each layer of litharge, until you reach its [base], do the same with another layer of litharge: then calcine it with Armenian clay and apply philosophical clay, then place it on fire. Repeat this operation several times, and after removing from the fire, wash the litharge thoroughly with sweet water so that all the salt separates from it. Then dry it and, as mentioned, apply philosophical clay and dry it, then place on fire. Continue until the litharge becomes like سفیدے (white lead). At this time, mix it with oil and natron, bind it in a fine cloth, apply philosophical clay, and after it dries, place it in an iron pot, seal its mouth, and leave it in the furnace oven for one day and one night. Then remove it and, as mentioned, repeat the operation three times with oil and natron. Now this litharge will become like sal ammoniac, and it will be superior to silver in whiteness, and all odor and impurity will depart from it. Take one dirham of it, place it on ten dirhams of copper—it will become silver.

<u>Method of Making Silver from Mercury and Lead:</u>
Take equal parts of lead and mercury as much as you wish, heat them in a crucible, and add a pinch of سفیدہ ستیرہ (starch white lead) while sprinkling with pomegranate peel. In a short time, all the lead will become white ash. This mixture is the best of all. Do not wash it; wash it with sweet water, and after it dries, place an equal weight of ash from roasted cow bones in a vessel and place it [the mixture] on top of it. Apply philosophical clay and dry it, then place it in the fire of کنہار (acacia?) for one day and one night, then remove it. It will become white like white lead. Place one dirham of it on thirty dirhams of copper—it will become silver. Or place half a dirham of this кishтah (concoction) on ten dirhams of mercury—excellent silver will be prepared.

<u>Silver Recipe:</u>
Filtrate ten dirhams, five dirhams of sal ammoniac, dissolve with drops of mercury, place on fire in a crucible—both will be consumed. At that time, add one dirham of newborn emic sugar and کلاں (galls?) dissolved, throw the filtrate piece by piece. Do the same three times. Then place this same medicine on ten dirhams of mercury, apply philosophical clay, and let it remain on fire all night. From this mercury, place one dirham on ten dirhams of litharge—it will become silver.

<u>A Proven Turkish Method:</u>
Take equal parts of island of the moving quarter, Yemenite moon, rock salt, natron, sal ammoniac—everything of equal weight. Break each separately, then moisten in egg white and dry. Then when you wish to perform the operation, make small pieces of red copper, mix them with an equal weight of the aforementioned medicine, apply philosophical clay in a crucible, and turn it in olive oil twice—however much is appropriate. Add silver, melt, and prepare it.

<u>Silver Recipe (Remedy of Margīshā):</u>
It is said that apply philosophical clay in a crucible with soap and natron, and whatever pure [substance] emerges, collect it and throw away the residue. If you do this two or three times, it is better—it will become shiny like pure margīshā powder, but it will quickly become particles. Take it, moisten it in the essence of natron and vinegar, and place it on fire in a cauldron until the vinegar is well absorbed. This is such an elixir that there is no need for anything else before it. Apply it to litharge—the odor and impurity will all depart from it. Then place this litharge on red copper—it will become white. Add silver and sell it, and profit from this very day.

<u>Another Method of This Recipe:</u>
Heat the litharge in a box and extinguish it in strong vinegar. Then dissolve pomegranate juice, ajwain, in rose water and lemon essence, and extinguish [the litharge] in it three times. Afterwards, take twenty-one dirhams of بیضا دور (distant white), one dirham of pāhanī Allāh, one newborn [egg], and one dirham of filtrate—grind all of these and make them fine, mix them in a mortar, and heat in a crucible. Red [color] will appear. Place ten dirhams of it on ten dirhams of red copper—it will become silver. Add a little copper to all of them.
