**Victory over the Enemy**

By reading this supplication before an enemy, the enemy becomes defeated. It is:

تَعَزَّزْتُ بِرَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ وَالْجَبَرُوتِ وَ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى الْحَيِّ الَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ شَاهَتِ الْوُجُوهُ وَ عَمِيَتِ الْأَبْصَارُ وَ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ الْوَاحِدِ الْقَهَّارِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ

"I acquire honor with the Lord of Honor and Omnipotence. I have placed my trust in the Living One who does not die. Faces have become contemptible and eyes have become blind. I have placed my trust in Allah the Exalted, who is One and Conqueror. Power and strength belong to Allah, who is High and Great."

After reading this, blow three times toward the limit of the enemy's envy, or pass before them, and they will be defeated.

**A Muslim Jinn's Revealed Veil of Protection from Every Calamity**

I have seen a manuscript written by the hand of Faqih Alawi. It is narrated that Said ibn Musayyib met a jinn who had accepted faith at the hands of the Noble Prophet ﷺ. This jinn said to him: "I will teach you a veil (protective amulet). Whoever keeps it with him shall be protected from every calamity. If he ties it upon an animal or any thing, it shall be protected from theft and every kind of harm. If a traveler keeps it with him during a journey, no evil shall befall him. If it is recited over a sick person, they shall remain protected from smallpox. Said ibn Musayyib said: 'By all means, teach me.' The jinn said: 'It is this, and by an oath, these are the words.'"

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ كُلُّ ذِي مُلْكٍ فَمَمْلُوكٌ لِلَّهِ وَ كُلُّ ذِي قُوَّةٍ فَضَعِيفٌ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ وَ كُلُّ جَبَّارٍ فَصَغِيرٌ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ وَ كُلُّ ظَالِمٍ لَا تَعْرِضُ لَهُ مِنَ اللَّهِ حَصَلْتُ حَامِلَ كِتَابِي هَذَا بِأَحَدِيَّتِهِ مِنَ الْإِنْسِ وَ الْجِنِّ وَ الشَّيَاطِينِ وَ الْعَفَارِيتِ الْمُتَمَرِّدِينَ خَاتَمُ سُلَيْمَانَ بْنِ دَاوُدَ عَلَى أَفْوَاهِكُمْ وَ عَصَا مُوسَى عَلَى أَكْتَافِكُمْ وَ خَيْرُكُمْ بَيْنَ أَعْيُنِكُمْ وَ شَرُّكُمْ بَيْنَ أَرْجُلِكُمْ وَ لَا غَالِبَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ لَكُمْ وَ لِحَامِلِ كِتَابِي هَذَا فِي حِرْزِ اللَّهِ الْمَانِعِ الَّذِي لَا يُنَالُ مَنِ اعْتَزَّ بِهِ وَ لَا يُكْشَفُ مَنِ اسْتَتَرَ بِهِ سُبْحَانَ مَنْ أَلْجَمَ الْبَحْرَ بِكَلِمَاتِهِ سُبْحَانَ مَنْ أَطْفَأَ نَارَ إِبْرَاهِيمَ بِقُدْرَتِهِ وَ حِكْمَتِهِ سُبْحَانَ مَنْ تَوَاضَعَ لَهُ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ وَ أَقْبِلْ وَلَا تَخَفْ إِنَّكَ مِنَ الْآمِنِينَ لَا تَخَافُ دَرَكًا وَلَا تَخْشَى إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْأَعْلَى لَا تَخَافَا إِنَّنِي مَعَكُمَا أَسْمَعُ وَ أَرَى اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْ حَامِلَ كِتَابِي هَذَا وَ اسْتُرْهُ بِسِتْرِكَ الْوَاقِي فِي الْحُصَيْنِ فِي لَيْلِهِ وَ نَهَارِهِ وَ ظَعْنِهِ وَ قَرَارِهِ الَّذِي تَسْتُرُ بِهِ أَوْلِيَاءَكَ الْمُتَّقِينَ مِنْ أَعْدَائِكَ الظَّالِمِينَ الْكَافِرِينَ اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ عَادَاهُ فَعَادِهِ وَ مَنْ كَادَهُ فَكِدْهُ وَ مَنْ نَصَبَ لَهُ فَخًّا فَخُذْهُ وَ اطْفِ عَنْهُ نَارَ مَنْ أَرَادَ بِهِ عَدَاوَةً وَ حُزْنًا وَ فَرِّجْ عَنْهُ كُلَّ كُرْبَةٍ وَ هَمٍّ وَ ضَيْقٍ وَلَا تُحَمِّلْهُ مَا لَا يَقْوَى وَ لَا يُطِيقُ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْحَقُّ الْمُبِينُ وَ صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَى سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَ آلِهِ وَ صَحْبِهِ وَ سَلَّمَ

Translation: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Every possessor of dominion is a slave owned by Allah. Every powerful one is weak before Allah. Every tyrant is insignificant before Allah. [unclear] belongs to Allah [unclear]. I protect the bearer of this my book, with His Oneness, from the evil of mankind, jinn, satans, and rebellious afrites. The seal of Solomon son of David is upon your mouths, and the staff of Moses is upon your shoulders. Your good is before your eyes and your evil is beneath your feet, and there is no conqueror except Allah. For you and for the bearer of this my book, in the protective fortress of Allah the Preventer, whom no one who takes refuge in Him shall be reached, and no one who hides behind Him shall be uncovered. Glory be to Him who bridled the sea with His words. Glory be to Him who extinguished the fire of Abraham by His power and wisdom. Glory be to Him before whom everything is humble. And accept, and fear not, for you are among the secure. Do not fear pursuit, nor be afraid, for you are the Most High. Do not fear, surely I am with you both, Hearing and Seeing. O Allah, protect the bearer of this my book and cover him with Your covering that protects, in the fortified stronghold, in his night and day, in his travel and his settled state, with which You protect Your righteous allies from Your unjust, disbelieving enemies. O Allah, whoever opposes him, oppose them; and whoever schemes against him, scheme against them; and whoever sets a trap for him, seize him. Extinguish from him the fire of whoever intends for him enmity and sorrow. Relieve from him every distress, worry, and anguish. Do not burden him with what he cannot bear or endure. Surely You are the Manifest Truth. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
