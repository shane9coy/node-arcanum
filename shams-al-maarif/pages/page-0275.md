Chapter 21: Properties of the Name of the Seizer, the Destructive Enemy, Popularity Among the People

Whoever mentions this name, such majesty shall descend upon him that no one shall be able to approach him with harm. Whoever writes it upon a lead tablet in the hour of Saturn and recites it according to its numerical value, saying "O Allah, seize the heart of [so-and-so]," the supplication shall be accepted. This is the name of Azrael, peace be upon him, containing the secret of seizing souls, and its nature is venerable and magnificent. Sometimes its letters and numerical value are combined in reconciliation.

Whoever wishes to seize the soul of someone shall make it his constant recitation, and mentioning the name of the one whose destruction is desired shall cause that person to perish—but let him fear Allah Most High and not act unjustly. Whoever recites it frequently, the people shall turn to him. According to istikhara and the clarity of the inner self, he shall perceive signs of prosperity. The form of its talismanic image is as follows:

Row 1: ḍ, b, a, q

Row 3: b, ḍ, q, a
Row 4: 1, 39, 201, 202
Row 5: q, a, b, ḍ
Row 6: [symbol/divider], 31, 300, 304

The numerical value of this name is 903, indicating the Comprehensive Name, whose operation is that of the Avenger. This individual is an imperfect rectangle, its [something] is 505, and these point toward the name of Allah Most High, al-Rashid. Those who understand from this very passage have interpreted it as a sign before the attainment of guidance, as Allah Most High has said: "But if you perceive in them sound judgment, then deliver unto them their wealth" (Qur'an 4:5). Its letters' [something] is 110, pointing toward the name of Allah Most High, al-'Ali. The people of secrets say that after the complete recitation of its 122 letters, Allah shall depart from the Holy Quran, and beginning one thousand four hundred years later, it shall be gradually reversed until no faithful person remains upon the earth—every name shall know Allah. The people of light have said that when the age reaches the numerical value of this name, the signs of the Resurrection shall begin to appear. Thanks be to Allah that only so many years remain until the Hour, and this is the duration of the preservation of the Islamic community.
