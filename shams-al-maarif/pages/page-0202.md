This is the oath of the Verse of the Throne (Ayat al-Kursi):

O Allah, I ask You by the freshness of the breeze of the spirit of the fragrant gardens, by the spirits of the jewels of the palaces of the seas of the lights of the revealing of the mysteries of Your most great name, by which You gave existence to the thirst of livers, and watered the ones approaching the pool of Your hands seeking union with You, O You to whom belongs the most great name, and He is the most great, O You whose excellences have preceded all antiquity, and He is the most ancient, O You for whom there is no limit by which He is known, and He is the most knowing, I ask You by Your great and most great name, and by the light of Your generous and most generous name, and by that which the Pen has inscribed, that You send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad and upon his family and companions, and that You subjugate for me all that You have created, what I know of it and what I do not know. I have supplicated to You by Your name through which whoever is saved has been saved, and through which whoever has perished has perished. There is no deity except You; You are blessed and exalted, O Possessor of Majesty and Generosity.

O Allah, I ask You by the fragrance of the breeze of the spirit of the fragrant garden and its spirits, the jewels of the palaces, the seas, the lights, and the greatness and expansion of the mysteries of Your most great name, through whose manifestation the parched livers were filled, while those approaching the pool from the chambers of secrets were given to drink. O You whose existence is for the most great name, and You are most elevated. O You whose antiquity is prior to precedence, whose limit does not exist, by which He is fully knowing. I ask by Your great most great name and the light of Your generous name, and what the Pen has written—that You send blessings upon our master Muhammad (may the blessings and peace of Allah be upon him and his family) and his companions. And that You subjugate for me all Your creation, whether I know it or do not know it. I have supplicated to You by Your name through which whoever was saved was saved, and he who perished perished. There is no god but You, the Blessed and Exalted, O Lord of Majesty and Generosity.

The Second Prayer of This Verse

O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O You whose subsistence and generosity exist by His own self, and the subsistence of others' existence is by Him. There is no might nor power except through You. I have raised my hands to You and spread my palms before You, so do not disappoint my hope, for indeed You are the most generous of the generous. How could it not be so, when there is no existence except Yours alone? Indeed You are the Real, there is no deity except Allah. Cause us to exist through the expansion of Your name from the existence of Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. Ṭāʾ. Bāʾ. Rāʾ. Kāf. And may Allah send blessings upon our master Muhammad and upon his family and his pure, righteous companions. And all praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds. Ṭāʾ. Bāʾ.

O Living, O Self-Subsisting, O You whose subsistence exists by His own self, and the subsistence of others' existence is through Him. Power and strength are only from You. I have raised my hands toward You and spread my hands before You, that You do not frustrate my hope, for indeed You are the most generous. And how could it not be so, when there is no existence except from You, except for You? Indeed You are the Real. There is no deity except Allah. Bestow upon us through the expansion of Your name from the existence of Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful. And send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad (may the blessings and peace of Allah be upon him and his family) and his companions, the pure and righteous, and all praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
