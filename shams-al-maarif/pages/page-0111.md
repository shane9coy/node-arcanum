# Page 111 (English translation)
XVIII

And they are light and stars, and the letter occupies the position of one who traverses and is swift between the two relations. Every existing thing is established. All things have been gathered from which stability is obtained like that which is skimmed off. The meaning of shin has come to us with the secret of the letters. It has three reasons: first, it is in šarr (evil), šamīn (fat), šān (glory), ši'ār (symbol), and šatm (insult), and the meaning of nun appears like the adornment of beauty, the fifth, and the light of knowledge, and it is the adornment of those books from which their secrets become manifest. And the Truth is He from whom the hidden secret becomes manifest, so that it may guard the place of nun in the throat, upon which the apparent and the hidden of the Divine Essence depend. Therefore in its form there is only the hidden, as it is in the name which the named one is named. And the pivot of the journey depends upon the morals of people.

His Eminence Hasan al-Basri may God be pleased with him says that in the Noble Quran there is knowledge of all things. The knowledge of the Noble Quran is in these letters which are at the beginnings of the surahs. Know that all letters are in lam-alif, and the knowledge of lam-alif is in alif. The knowledge of alif is in the point, and the knowledge of the point is in the essential knowledge of pre-eternity and post-eternity. The knowledge of condition is in the unseen of air, and the unseen of air is in lā miṯla-hu šay'-un wa-huwa as-samī'u l-baṣīr [There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing].

Some say that shin is also one of the divine names among the divine names, just as all those letters are which are at the beginnings of the surahs. These are fourteen luminous letters without repetition. They are: alif, ha, ra, ta, kaf, lam, mim, nun, sin, 'ayn, sad, qaf, ha, ya.

It is narrated from the Commander of the Faithful Ibn Abbas, may God be pleased with him, that he says: The letters at the beginnings of the surahs are derived from the divine names. Abu al-'Aliyah says: From among these letters, each letter is a negation of one of the divine names. Alif is from Allah, lam is from Laṭīf [the Subtle], mim is from Malak [the King], sad is from Ṣādiq [the Truthful], ra is from Rabb [the Lord], kaf is from Karīm [the Generous], ta is from Tayyib [the Pure], sin is from Samī' [the Hearing], ha is from Hamīd [the Praiseworthy], qaf is from Qadīr [the Powerful], and nun is derived from Nūr [the Light]. Abu al-'Aliyah arranged them thus: alif-lam-mim, alif-lam-sad, alif-lam-mim-ra, kaf-ha-ya-'ayn-sad, ta-ha, ta-sin, ta-sin-mim, ya-sin, ha-mim, ha-mim-'ayn-sin-qaf, qaf, sad, nun.

The letter of supplication is a letter of indication along with the meaning of supplication. That is, the letter ha and the letter ya are placed in the middle. The remaining letters are derived from alif-lam-mim, alif-lam-sad, alif-lam-ra, kaf-ha-ya-'ayn-sad, ta-ha, ta-sin, ta-sin-mim, ya-sin, ha-mim, ha-mim-'ayn-sin-qaf, qaf, sad, nun, and the Noble Quran.

And from alif-lam-nun and alif-lam-qalam [Nun and the Pen]. The Commander of the Faithful Ibn Abbas says: Among their meanings are "I am Allah," "I see." Al-mujam [the Cluster] means "I am Allah, I know and I see." By alif is meant the Divine Essence. By lam is meant the name of Allah. By mim is meant the manifestation of knowledge from the Divine. Their arrangement is this: alif-lam-mim, alif-lam-sad, alif-lam-ra, kaf-ha-ya-'ayn-sad, ta-ha, ta-sin, ta-sin-mim, ya-sin, ha-mim, ha-mim-'ayn-sin-qaf, qaf, nun. Among these there are precisely fourteen letters without repetition. Abu al-'Aliyah indicated the saying of Ibn Abbas that the letters of the beginnings of surahs are the Greatest Name.

[Boxed Heading: The Benefits of the Beautiful Names]

The Beautiful Names correspond to the numbers of the degrees of Paradise. From these same names knowledge emerged, and to them it will return. From them the existents became manifest, and the existents are a luminous sign upon the Beautiful Names. But these names have permeated spirits and bodies, to such an extent that there is no individual among the existents with whom the names have not encompassed the eye, nose, and so on. The name of the Essence, that is Allah, is the comprehender of the meanings of all names. Alif is the stable letter from which all letters emerged. Thus this is like the intellect, knowledge, the Throne, and the spirit. The letter lam is one that joins the luminous and the most high, and its example is the Preserved Tablet, the Chair, and the soul. After lam is the letter mim, and this letter points to all, whose example is the body. Thus the intellect is the first creation, and it is the body of all creations. All the meanings of the letters are included in alif. In alif there is establishment and transfer, just as in alif there are all things in knowledge. Understand these so that the meanings of secrets may be unveiled to you. Know that the friends of God have discussed the knowledge of letters and names through many methods. Three things about these names are exclusive to them. One is that the meanings of their names are known through their inner qualities and through their inspiration. No one else can know them along with them. Second, that besides these special names, other names can also be known. Third, that is the name through which peace be upon him—Peace be upon him—with all the light of revelation became known.
