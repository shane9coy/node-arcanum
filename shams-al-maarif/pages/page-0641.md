# Page 641 (English translation)
And it is also mentioned therein. Thus He says regarding the common people:
"To Him submits whoever is in the heavens and the earth" (Qur'an 3:83)

And regarding the special ones, He says:
"So whom Allah wills to guide, He opens his breast to Islam" (Qur'an 6:125)

Translation: "He whose heart Allah wishes to guide, He opens his breast for the sake of Islam."

This is because Allah the Most High has attributed Islam to Himself, for He is in all of His creation, both the highest and the lowest, including among them the mineral kingdom and the plant kingdom. Thus this Islam is with creation.

Know that the reality of Islam is this: that the body is surrendered for the sake of compliance, the mind for contemplation, the lower soul for opposition to desires, and the spirit for remembrance—with submission, standing, constant vigilance, and true awakening.

The Degrees of Witnessing Islam

Know that the witnessing of Islam has three ranks: the lowest, the highest, and the middle. The lowest is the fulfillment of obligatory duties. The second, the higher station, is to accept [the divine will] gladly without objection. If this person dies, his resurrection will be in the Abode of Peace. The health of the intellect means being preserved from fallacies—meaning not associating any partner with the Divine. The health of the spirit means having mastery over the body. The health of the lower soul means accepting the angels. The Islam of bodies means that each [body] undertakes the service of the Lord according to its capacity.

The Types of Prayer:

The prayer of the intellect is to remain absorbed in greatness at all times, and the prayer of the spirit is that the theophany of the Names occurs upon it, and the prayer of the souls is that the bodily attachments that cause neglect of God are completely severed, and the prayer of the hearts is with the light of causes in the manner of the elevated ones, and the prayer of bodies is that they remain present before God in accordance with the command of Truth.

Notice: The qibla of each of the intellect, spirit, soul, heart, and body is separate:

Know that the qibla of the head is the Holy Essence, and the qibla of the intellect is the Compassionate Attributes, and the qibla of spirits is the Honored Names, and the qibla of souls is the Manifested Actions, and the qibla of the heart is the promise of the meeting [with God] according to the light, and the qibla of bodies is the Sacred House, and the light of secrets reaches the Sacred Essence, and the pilgrimage of the intellect is toward the House of Glory, and the pilgrimage of spirits is toward the Dominion, and the pilgrimage of souls is toward the House of Supplication, and the pilgrimage of hearts is toward the House of Divine Bounties, and the pilgrimage of bodies is toward the Ancient House.

The Types of Adhan:

And the adhan of secrets is the announcement of concealment, and the adhan of the intellect is the proof of divestment, and the adhan of spirits is the prophecy of response, and the adhan of souls is the standing in the essence of attraction, and the adhan of hearts is constant remembrance, and the adhan of bodies is calling the heedless.

No Harm Should Come to Any Muslim from Another Muslim:

A Muslim is one from whose hand and tongue other Muslims come to no harm. The spiritual discipline for this name is forty days. According to its number, reciting it daily is permissible in full. This action will manifest the perfection of unveiling and spiritual works. When this practice is combined with fasting, it will [result in] the disease of smallpox or some good deed, and when one engraves it on a silver ring, he will find [success] on the path of every endeavor. If someone enters deep seclusion and begins reciting the name after prayer according to its number—that is, as much as is contained within it—then Allah the Most High will grant him abundant blessings.
