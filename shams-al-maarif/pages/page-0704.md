# Page 704 (English translation)
Translation: "God is the very essence who sends down rain after His servants have despaired and spreads His mercy."
And indeed God alone placed His mercy within hearts, even though before that the fire of disbelief was blazing within those hearts.
The growth of faith is also like this, just as a crop grows—spiritual rain, sometimes less sometimes more, completes the faith and makes one a saint:
"So upon this, God poured rain of mercy," meaning He granted the success for faith and action. Then after that, light rain continues to fall, because
after the growth has sprouted, rain follows a pattern—sometimes it is light and sometimes heavy, since He alone is fully acquainted with the secrets of His creatures.
He brings them sustenance in accordance with their needs at the appropriate times, sending down rain when they require it. This continues in the same manner
until the growth reaches its perfection.

**The one who recites the five prayers submerges in the river of light:**
This is the example of the five prayers. They should be understood as spiritual sustenance and a luminous river. From reciting them, the servant remains always submerged in the river of light
and acquires the sustenance of the sovereign realm, and when he desires the tongue of union, he performs supererogatory prayers after the obligatory ones,
until in this manner his life is completed and he becomes a perfect saint.

**The record of deeds is preserved like a treasure:**
His deeds are folded and placed in the Column of the Ether, meaning just as people store grain in deep granaries, such is the ruling for prayer
and fasting. The month and the twelve hours of the day, and each hour's portion, are like each month's portion.
From the seclusion of this Name, the knowledge of the Splendid Reality is conveyed, and superiority at a certain rank is attained. When you enter seclusion with the conditions,
recite the Name and the noble verse until their lights become manifest. When the totality is complete, wondrous forms will come to you, and
in sleep or wakefulness they will appear to you according to your preparedness.

**You will attain the rank of perfect sainthood:**
After the coming of honor, you will become one of the saints. The special properties of this Name are very many. If for the sake of reassurance we write it and place it around a child's neck,
he will remain protected, and if any ruler has it engraved on a gold or silver ring and wears it, the awe of it will dominate people's hearts.
Every person will bow their head. He will become acquainted, able to command as he wishes, and his dhikr is this:
