# Page 175 (English translation)
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. O Lord, make it easy. O Allah, I ask You by You are Allah, there is no god but You. And I ask You by Your glory, which belongs to You through the majesty of Your oneness and uniqueness, and to You belongs the permanence of glory through the permanence of Your eternity. The doubts of those who seek to comprehend Your attributes have become exhausted before Your power, and the minds of those who know Your majesty's glory have become bewildered. O my Lord, immerse us in Your generosity and forbearance, inspire us with gratitude, draw us near to Your door, increase us in what You have given to Your beloved ones. Is all of that not from You? We have humbled ourselves before You and brought ourselves to You. How many times we have asked You, and You gave us more than we asked. And how many times we hoped in You, and You fulfilled our hope. You are the most forbearing in Your generosity, overlook us in the perfection of Your generosity, overlook the brokenness of the one You have not repaired. How few are those who are humble in their poverty, for whoever You do not protect from their distress, if meanness appears in them, You love them. How few are those who are humbled in their need, for whoever You do not shelter from their distress, if meanness appears in them, You love them. And O the regret of those whom You have distanced from Your court! O my Lord, if Your mercy is for the righteous, then where do the sinners go? O Allah, cover us with Your covering and pardon us with Your generosity, and grant us well-being with Your gentleness. O my Lord, if we are unable to repent, You are capable of forgiveness. O my Lord, we have devoted to You in most moments of faith in You and dependence on You. We have abandoned most desires attributed to You and passions towards You, so forgive us between them and do not disgrace us before You. O my Lord, our sins are small in comparison to Your pardon, even if they are great in comparison to Your prohibition. O my Lord, if You had wanted to destroy us, You would have destroyed us, and if You had wanted to impoverish us, You would have impoverished us. Pardon, O Allah, what causes us suffering, and do not deprive us of what You have honored us with. O my Lord, may what was destined be repaid to Your face, for it is Yours. O my Lord, we seek refuge in Your mercy when resources are exhausted, and we find rest in Your generosity when hope fades. We are poor, and we are poor before You, so our poverty is to You, and to You is our recourse. O my Lord, as we have humbled ourselves before You, have mercy on our humility before You. Make our desire for us before You, and what is with You, and do not deprive us through our sins, nor drive us away through our faults. O my Lord, if we have disobeyed You in ignorance, we have called upon You with reason, for You have taught us that we have a Lord who forgives and does not care. O my Lord, You are the most forbearing with the burdened, so we complain to You, and You are capable of fulfilling hopes and removing affliction. O Allah, O One who has secured from falling, has forgiven intentions, and has replaced them with good deeds. Reward us for gratitude, increase us in remembrance, make us devoted to Your command, guide us to gratitude, inspire us and our parents, our teachers, and all Muslim men and women, believing men and women, the living among them and the dead. Amen, and praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
