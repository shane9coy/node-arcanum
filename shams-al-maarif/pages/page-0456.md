# Page 456 (English translation)
Chapter 27: The Seventh Method — The Hidden Blessings of the Divine Names

Love in Hearts, Protection from the Evil of Jinn and Humans:

Among the special properties of the ten names of Allah the Most High — Al-Hakim, Al-Ra'uf, Al-Wadud, Al-Ghafur, Al-Hannan, Al-Latif, Al-Hafiz, Al-Raqib, Al-Barr, Al-Sami' — are: causing inclination, removing hatred, creating love and affection in hearts, eliminating sorrow and grief, and keeping safe from the evil of jinn and humans. The firmness of modesty, continuity in religion and worldly matters, and the attainment of health and blessings are also included.

The Practice for Acceptance of Repentance:

The names of Allah the Most High — Al-Hakim, Al-Ra'uf — are very beneficial for the acceptance of repentance and forgiveness of sins. If a sinner invokes these names, Allah the Most High grants him the success of repentance and protects him from further sins. For one who writes the inscription of the name Afuw' along with this name and keeps it with him, people perceive goodness instead of evil.

Acceptance of Prayer and Popularity Among Creation:

The names of Allah the Most High — Al-Wadud, Al-Ghafur — are very great names for the acceptance of repentance. The hearts of people will naturally incline toward one who invokes them. Whoever sees him will become his lover. If one writes these names on the palm or on the palm of the hand on Friday during the waxing moon and invokes these names according to the numerical correspondence, and keeps the inscription with him, the heart of any human toward whom he desires love will be affected.

Once a thief accosted a merchant, so he prayed by reciting "Yā Wadud, Yā Wadud, Yā Dha al-'Arshi al-Majid, Yā Mubdi', Yā Mu'id, Yā Fa''al lima Yurid," upon which Allah the Most High sent an angel to kill that thief. The angel came and killed the thief.

Love Produced in the Hearts of People:

The love of one who invokes the name Hannan is produced in the hearts of people. When the name Hannan is written 140 times on a pure vessel and washed off with egg white, and it is given to one burned by fire, he will immediately become well. There is also benefit from invoking this name in hot diseases.

The Practice for Removing Hardship and Grief:

The invocation of the name Yā Latif is very swift in removing sorrow and grief. How many sorrows and griefs are removed by its invocation! One who always invokes it — Allah the Most High swiftly removes his hardships. The Alif of this name is Latif, and it is a hidden bounty of the light of reason. One should invoke it at least 100 times. One who writes its inscription on red paper and keeps it with him, or engraves it on an agate ring and wears it — all people will be kind to him.

Protection from Jinn, Humans, and Harmful Animals:

One who invokes the numbers of the name Hafiz, or wears its written inscription, no one from among jinn, humans, and harmful beasts and animals will reach him — by the grace of Allah. Allah the Most High keeps His invoker protected from every calamity.

There is a strange and wonderful influence in the name Raqib for softening hearts and producing humility and submission in the heart of the fearful one. Modesty toward Allah the Most High is produced in the heart of the sincere one. His invoker becomes adorned with good manners and courtesy.
