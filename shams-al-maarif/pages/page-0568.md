# Page 568 (English translation)
566

**One tola of alixir turns one crore tolas of silver into gold:**

Such is the nature of your alixir that when its preparation has been completed with full caution and precision, one tola of this alixir can turn one crore tolas of silver into gold; and if any error occurs in the process, its transmuting power will be diminished in proportion to that error.

**Alchemy is the most difficult work:**

This error usually occurs in the quantities of the ingredients and the method of their combination, and there is no other work in the arts more difficult than this. Therefore, it is obligatory upon you to acquire both of these things: first, ascertain the precise weights of the ingredients, for the sages have kept them extremely concealed—apart from their own fellow sages, no others can come to know them. So let them extract them themselves and let them verify them with their own eyes many times.

**The second method of incorporating and mixing the ingredients together:**

The second method is regarding extraction—namely, how the ingredients should be mixed, which component should be introduced first and which later, for there is great caution required in introducing each ingredient at its proper time: neither earlier nor later. For example, when it is time to introduce water—meaning quicksilver [mercury]—at that time the fire should not be fierce and intense, and likewise when sulfur is introduced, quicksilver should not be introduced at that time; rather, each should be introduced at its proper time. The sages have not explained this clearly but have made it very confused and muddled. At the time when they need fire, they also need water, and the water in which the color dissolves, they set aside for the color alone; then they prepare fresh water anew and make the indigo, and with careful procedure they turn it white.

**Alchemy is a secret; it is not disclosed to everyone:**

And know that this noble secret is fitting only for kings and rulers. Upon seeing its magnificence and beauty, do not reveal it to others, even if it be your own son. And if you reveal it, remember that you will greatly regret it and will have to endure much sorrow.

**Alixir of the Egg (Kushtah Baydhah):**

Now I describe to you the method of this person who is a master of this art and regards his own statement as most truthful. I have not personally tested it myself, but I certainly know him to be truthful in speech. He says: Clean the eggshells as thoroughly as possible, boil them, and completely remove the inner membranes. Then dry them, grind them finely, fill them in an earthenware pot, and perform gill al-hikmah [a specific alchemical procedure]. Keep it in intense fire continuously for seven days—the alixir is ready. This is called Kalis al-Baydh [calcined egg white].

**Egg Oil:**

Then take a vessel of such a type that holes can be made in it, and there should be holes at the bottom of this vessel so that the essence of the eggs can exit through these holes. Then place this vessel in a pit, and inside the pit there should be a glass bottle opposite this hole so that whatever essence exudes from the eggs collects in that glass bottle. Then cover the eggs with a clay dish and spread a little ash on top of it so that the fire does not reach them. Place them and kindle a complete fire for one day. The sounds of the eggs will come to your ears, and the essence will begin to collect in the glass bottle. When the essence has exited, its weight should be equal to one bowl [of the eggs]. Afterward, remove the glass bottle and close it immediately. Do not let any vapor escape from it—this is the "blinded raja," and protect it by not allowing any agitation of its sediment. Then after this, put as much Kalis al-Baydh as you wish into a glass vessel with a narrow mouth, place the glass bottle's mouth in it, and pour in oil to a level three times that amount. Leave it for seven days. Then strain this essence, and strain it through a cotton cloth in such a way that only the liquid part comes out—that is the pure essence.
