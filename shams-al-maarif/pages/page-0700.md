# Page 700 (English translation)
God has manifested one of His names for every existing being so that it may correct it:
For every existing being, He has manifested one name among His names and placed that name upon it, so that attention may be directed toward the natural monotheism. Then the meanings of His name spread upon existing beings, and its connection to the aspect of divine craftsmanship is observed, and one observes whatever is true in every movement, soul, and action. It is incumbent upon one to practice spiritual discipline and worship with sincerity, so that every assigned mutable being fulfills his need, and truth becomes manifest within him, and upon hearing any matter, he becomes aware of its result.

Through dhikr, the needs are fulfilled by this name:
It is also designated for God, the Mighty and Glorious. Upon writing its talismanic form, whatever need one goes for will be fulfilled, and thereafter, whatever person whose name matches it should recite it and observe the confirmation.

It is beneficial for phlegmatic ailments:
If one writes it on a silver tablet and carries it [unclear: ہائم] with oneself, it will be beneficial. Its talismanic form is this:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ o اَللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ الْحَقُّ الْمُطْلَقُ الْمَوْجُوْدُ فِي حَقِيْقَةِ ذَاتِكَ الْمَوْصُوْفُ بِحَقَائِقِ الصِّفَاتِ الْحُسْنٰى فِي قُدُسِيَّتِكَ أَسْئَلُكَ بِسِرِّ أَنْوَارِ أَسْمَائِكَ الْحُسْنٰى أَنْ تُحَقِّقَ لِي كُلَّ حَقٍّ فِي الْوُجُوْدِ وَ تُبْطِلَ لِي كُلَّ بَاطِلٍ مَعْدُوْمٍ وَ مَفْقُوْدٍ أَسْئَلُكَ بِسِرِّ وُجُوْدِكَ الَّذِيْ حَقَّقْتَ بِهِ حَقَائِقِ صِفَاتِكَ أَنْ تَرْفَعَ فُؤَادِي بِحَقِّ الْحَقِّ إِلٰى شُهُوْدِ حَقَائِقِ ذَاتِكَ فَأَكُوْنَ بِكَ مَعَ وُجُوْدِ كُلِّ مَوْجُوْدٍ أَبَدًا دَائِماً يَا حَقُّ يَا مُبِيْنُ o

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O Allah, You are the absolute truth, existing in the reality of Your essence, characterized by the realities of the most beautiful attributes in Your sanctity. I ask You by the secret of the lights of Your most beautiful names to realize for me every truth in existence and to nullify for me every false thing, both non-existent and lost. I ask You by the secret of Your existence, through which You have realized the realities of Your attributes, to elevate my heart by the right of truth to the witnessing of the realities of Your essence, so that I may be with You in the existence of every existing thing forever and eternally. O Truth, O Manifest!

[Boxed Heading] Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Wakeel" (Agent) [/Boxed Heading]

Wakeel (Agent) is the one to whom all affairs are entrusted; He manages them however He wills.

The Categories of Wakeel:
There are two types of agents. One type is those to whom some matters are entrusted—they are imperfect. And the other type is the one to whom all affairs are entrusted—he is perfect, and he is God, the Mighty and Glorious. The meaning of agency (wakāla) is sponsorship (kafāla). Whoever establishes himself with complete resolve upon the correction of his inner self, God will bring to him the faith of steadfastness.

Trust in God (tawakkul) is of five kinds:
This trust has five categories. The first category is trust accompanied by the prioritization of the heart: God, the Most High, is one, is truth, is reality; there is no deity besides Him.
