# Page 30 (English translation)
Allah's servants and His Messenger. They are the sun of guidance and those who extract servants from polytheism and humiliation. Through whose call the heaven of monotheism has revolved. Through whose grandeur the sun has become bright and illuminated. Through whose sight the stars of misguidance have become dark and low. Through whose blessedness the morning of monotheism has become illuminated. May Allah the Most High send down the best blessings upon him ﷺ and upon his family. May He be pleased with his truthful and sincere companions. Such blessings through which his ranks become elevated and his degrees become the highest.
