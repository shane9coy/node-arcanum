# Page 134 (English translation)
Chapter: The method of reading the dhikr of the Seal Veil

I take refuge with the glory of God Almighty, the Mighty One, by the glory of His mighty strength, by the might of Hāyayl, Lajā, Ṣafā, Baṭash, Aṭmaqa, Habūsh, Garūsh, Mahāsh, Barakahā, Halīl, Amardīn, Hāyith, by Lamābiḥ, Yumdidkum, your Lord will aid you with the noble angels, by the divine names: Al-Mīm Al-Ṣād Kāf Hā Yā Ain Ṣād Ḥā Mīm Ain Sīn Qāf, and the Quran of remembrance, the verse Qāf, and the magnificent Quran Nun, and by the Pen and what they inscribe, and by the heaven and At-Ṭāriq, up to here, and by the Defliers in ranks, a reminder, up to here, and Surah Wa-n-Najm idhā hawā, Surah Al-Qamar, the whole, having read, and "and indeed it is a mighty oath if you but knew," "had We sent down this Quran," etc., "who whispered to me," beating, I have beaten all my body and hair and hands from the evil of the jinn and humans and the spiritual and the lower beings, by Tulūs, Dahrūs, Damūs, and by the Great Supreme Name and by the impenetrable veil for all the rebellious satans and the armies of Iblīs all together, by Lahafṭaf, Salfaṭaʿ, Asmāṭūn, Mahalsh, Kawhabūsh, Alaā, Qashū, descend, O spiritual spirits, all of you, and you, O Ṣarfīʾīl, and take refuge from such and such from the spirits and fear and fright and from the evil of those who emerge at night and day and from the evil of every stubborn rebellious satan, and by the right of the palm of the breezes, Aẓʿīmātā Kāf Hā Yā Ain Ṣād, I have sealed by the right of "Fatah Muʿtab" His saying is the Truth and to Him belongs the sovereignty on the Day when the Trumpet is blown, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed, and He is the Wise, the Aware, and by the right of this, Sharāhiyā, Adūnāyī, Aṣbāʾūt, Āl Shaddāy, Ahlūjīm, and "and indeed it is a mighty oath if you but knew," "so God will suffice you against them and He is the Hearing, the Knowing," respond, O servants of these names, and rely upon such and such.

I take refuge with the glory of God Almighty, who is the Mighty One, your Lord will help you with the noble angels. I have protected my body, hair, and limbs from all jinn and humans and the lower spiritual beings, along with the great supreme Name and from all rebellious satanic armies. O spiritual spirits, descend all of you, and you, O Ṣarfīʾīl, and take refuge from all those things that are with it, from spirits, fear, panic, evil, from emerging at night and day, and from every stubborn rebellious satan.
