# Page 708 (English translation)
708

It stands with him.
**The secret of life and eternal worship is known through the remembrance of the Divine Name:**
In the Divine Name there is the secret of devotion, and through its solitude the secret of life is obtained.
Its reciter receives two robes: one is the life of the heart, and the other is power in the sight. Through his seeing, illness ends and the sick becomes healthy.
Through remembrance in solitude, its patron gives the reciter two robes. Through one, the protection of his heart is achieved, and through the other, this power is created in his sight, so that any sick person he sees becomes healthy.
And the patron of the Ism Hay is Tayyāʾīl, and in the customs of rulers his sovereignty exists. According to some, these two names are the Ism Aʿẓam [the Greatest Name].

**If written on gold, silver, or deerskin and kept with oneself, every need is fulfilled:**
Whoever writes these and keeps them with himself, written on gold or silver or deerskin, and recites these according to their numbers, whatever need he asks from God will be fulfilled. And if he recites it constantly, he will remain healthy. Its form is this:
