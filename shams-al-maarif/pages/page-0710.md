# Page 710 (English translation)
708

I ask You, with the knowledge and encompassing power of dominion over the kingdom and sovereignty, to establish me in Your obedience in all that removes from me the darkness of brutishness and reveals to me the secret of subsistence, and raise me to the spiritual stations, O God, O Living One, O Subsisting One.

[Heading:]
Chapter: The Virtues of the Name "Qayyum"

Qayyum is the intensive form of "qiyam" (subsistence), and the meaning is that by which all existence stands, and without His existence the conception of anything is impossible. Therefore He alone is the Qayyum, because His subsistence is self-existent, and the subsistence of everything is through Him. The manifestation of this name appears only in the afterlife because its appearance is like a grain. It has appeared in every being, and He alone has established the realms of the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. The arrangement of the various states is also through His qiyyumiyyah (subsistence), and He is the One who perceives through the senses.

[Subheading:]
God established the intellects, the realm of dominion, the bodies, the spirits, paradise and hell through the attribute of Qayyum:

He alone established the intellects, and He alone established the malakutiyyah (the realm of dominion) and malakut, and He numbered them, and He established the bodies and the spirits and paradise, hell, and the like, and to Him alone every lord belongs, Most Exalted. He established them from the divine essence, the station of presence, and the station of the chosen one.

[Subheading:]
The establishment of the day is through hours, hours through degrees, degrees through minutes, and minutes through seconds:

They stand with the night and day, and the aggregate of days with the aggregate, and the day with the hours, and the hours with the degrees, and the degrees with the minutes, and the minutes with the seconds.

[Subheading:]
In the creation of man this same method was followed: the drop, the leech-like clot, bones, flesh, etc., stand with one another:

And the qayyum of the subtle realms is in the essence of the most specific soul. This is the method of establishment: the point of desire stands with the drop, the drop with the clot, the clot with bones, bones with flesh, flesh with veins, veins with arteries, arteries with flesh, flesh with blood, and blood stands with the attribute of His qawmiyyah, and this is His creative attribute. And nourishment stands with the body, and water with mercy, and mercy is His essential attribute.

[Subheading:]
The name and aggregate of the establishment of all things is the human being:

The pivot of all these established things is the human being. Thus the human being stands with his realms, and for this reason actions stand with knowledge, and knowledge with seeking, and seeking with abandonment. The day of recompense stands with his actions and his states, within the cycle of his station, which is through His qiyyumiyyah in exchange. Thus the action of the name Qayyum manifests in the afterlife, according to the secret that Almighty God deposited within it, on account of which most people have been heedless of it.

[Subheading:]
The knowledge of the divine names is superior and higher than all sciences:

Know that the knowledge of the divine names is an extremely noble and great science.
