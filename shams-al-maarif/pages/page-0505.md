[Boxed heading:]

Chapter 33: The Explanation of the Secrets of the Greatest Name

[/Boxed heading]

And the exposition of their stability and essential properties, which become manifest through this circle.

Know that this sacred circle is a treasure of secrets, and all the mysteries of existence are present within it, and it encompasses all types of purifications. For whoever possesses sound natural disposition, its secret is not hidden. And whoever possesses knowledge and reflection knows well that indeed the word Alif is its trinity. All its excellences are gathered in the Mother of Books, the truth of all its types is in the triangle, and these are the secrets of the lights and their governances. For the sake of governance, the categories of scriptural types are fundamentally three things: that which is necessary, that which is possible, or that which is impossible. And existents are of three kinds, and the categories of being are three: the command of God, creation. And the types of divine matters are also three: essence, attribute, action. And the types of affirmation are also three: pure affirmation, elevating affirmation, essence, establishment. And the types of lordship are also three: eternity, perpetuity, the moment. And the types of the world are also three: the world of power, the world of sovereignty, the kingdom. And the types of time are also three: paradise, hell, and the barrier. And the types of triangles are also three: this world, the intermediate realm, the afterlife. And the types of place are also three: past, present, and future. And the types of the world of realities are also three: spirit, heart, body. And the types of derivational principles are also three: single word, derived word, verbal noun. And all categories of words that come with the terminology of letter principles are: Alif of divine truth, established Alif of reflection. And the types of words are also three: connecting word, superior word, separating word. And the types of motion are these: movement, insertion, placement, quiescence. And these are the types of original vowels. The exposition of the trinity encompasses all these secrets regarding the Greatest Name and the seven worlds.
