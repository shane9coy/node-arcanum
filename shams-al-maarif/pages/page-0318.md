Properties, Secrets and Mysteries of the Holy Quran

Know that the Quran is the protected door of hidden knowledge, the sealed secret, the greatest secret, a healing for the tongue, and a sufficient remedy. Each of its mysteries is a treasure-talisman. In its seas of secrets there are precious depths of its lights. Extraction of great pearls is contingent upon familiarity with its hidden inner truths and its dawning. Its apparent benefits and supererogatory gains are examples of its revelation, its ancient invocations, its Most Supreme Names, and its spiritual secrets. Beyond this, only the firmly grounded and the gnostics are informed of its secrets.

This is the favor of Allah, which He bestows upon whom He wills, and Allah is the Possessor of all favor. Some have performed its apparent and traditional exegesis at the extreme limit of the literal meaning. Some have obtained illumination from its wisdoms—they achieved success with red vitriol. Some have dived into its depths and extracted ruby, pearl of Ahzar, green pearl, and chrysolite. Some have reached its farthest shores. They extracted from it the Greatest Antidote and musk of the foul-smelling kind. The Wise Quran is that thing before which all the former and latter ones have been rendered helpless. This is the firm rope of Allah, His luminous light, and His straight path. This is the ancient discourse of Allah and that sea whose wonders do not end nor do its rare marvels cease to exist. No one can describe its attribute and none can reach its heights. This is the pure and composed one, distinguishing between what is lawful and what is unlawful. This is a revelation from Allah, the Wise and Praiseworthy.

Know that scholars are of four kinds. One whose portion with Allah is the Hereafter. The second is the scholar whose portion with Allah is knowledge and gnosis. The third is one whose portion with Allah is the secret of the Hereafter. The fourth is one whose portion with Allah is this—that he desires the knowledge of the Hereafter.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: Sit with scholars and ask questions of the wise, for disagreement between knowledge, interpretation, and exegesis is well-known. Allah the Exalted said:

"I will turn away from My signs those who act arrogantly upon the earth."

Hazrat Ibn Abbas ؓ states: There are three categories of scholars in understanding the Holy Quran. The people of exegesis are the lowest of all—these are intermediate between the second people of interpretation. The third are the people of comprehension—these are the most excellent of all. The exegesis of the Holy Quran takes place through those things: reading knowledge and study, debating the statements of the predecessors, interpreting with piety and trustworthiness, and comprehension that is a gift from Allah the Exalted. Thus the people of comprehension speak by the command of Allah the Exalted, as there is the hadith qudsi that I become his tongue with which he speaks. One wise man says that the hand of Allah the Exalted is upon the mouths of the wise. When He decrees something, they speak.

Some scholars have said in the interpretation of this verse:

"And We did not send before you any messenger or prophet."

That what is meant by this is the people of comprehension who discuss the Magnificent Quran with wisdom. One companion ؓ said: You recite the Quran in the apparent while I recite the Quran in the inner. The purpose of this is that you may know the honor and greatness of the inner—that is, those people who obtained comprehension from Allah the Exalted along with the hidden secrets, the sacred lights, and the noble subtleties, which Allah the Exalted brings about for him through the lights of His own intention within the inner meaning of His verses. The Wise Quran is the book of sealed hidden secrets and the protected door. This is that foaming sea from which the former and the latter ones drank. Allah the Exalted said:

"We have neglected nothing in the Book."

The people of comprehension alone can understand this world.
