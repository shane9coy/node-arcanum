# Page 207 (English translation)
I ask You by the verses and the wise remembrances.
That You subjugate for me the spiritual essence and the servants of this blessed surah, for You are over all things competent. Answer, O angels of the Lord of the Worlds, by the right of the Greatest Name of God and by the right of this invocation and the wise remembrance. I adjure you, O angels of the Lord of the Worlds, by the right of the Name of God Talījīn, for I seek aid against you by God, the Compassionate, the Merciful, and by the praise of God, Lord of the Worlds. O Rūfāʾīl, by the right of the Name written upon the heart of the moon and the sun, and by the right of the Greatest Name, O Mudhahab, by the right of the Lord of the Worlds.

In the name of God, the beginning, who is the most generous, the most merciful—give it: "Surely it was sent down to me, that I am God's messenger—I ask You, O He who sent down revelation from above the seven heavens—that You make easy for me what I intend and desire. Subjugate for me the servants of this blessed surah, that they obey me in all those matters that I wish, for surely You are over all things competent. O God, O He to whom those fleeing flee, and O He whose forgiveness those coveting covet—surely a group of the jinn heard: O God, I ask You, O He who hears and sees, O there is none who sees and intercedes in the horizon—they said: 'Surely we have heard an amazing Quran that guides to the straight path, so we have believed in it, and we will not associate anyone with our Lord.'"
"O God, I also ask You for that which, from the page, is asked by You and by various prophets, upon them be peace, and by my Prophet—may God bless him and grant him peace—and by what the supplicants ask, that You subjugate for me the servants of this surah, that they be my helpers in it, in whatever I wish, for surely its rank is high and it has neither wife nor offspring. O God, I ask You, O He who has neither wife nor offspring, that You make my heart speak with wisdom and my tongue with knowledge, that You be my helper and supporter, and that You subjugate for me the hearts of all Your creation—and surely among people there were some who sought refuge with some of the jinn. So they increased in transgression, and they thought, as you thought, that God would not raise anyone. O God, I ask You, O He who lifts the heavens, O Creator of creatures, O He who fixed the mountains, O He who seized the time, and O He who sent down the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms, and the wise Quran, O He who gave the children of Adam distinction over all creatures—O Source of life, O Standing One, O He who does not sleep, O He who subjugated for Solomon, upon him be peace, the jinn and mankind—O God, I ask You, O God, that You subjugate for me all Your creation and all things, mention me in good. O He who does not sleep, O God, I ask You by Your greatest mighty Name and by the generous Light, that You subjugate for me the spiritual essence of this surah,
that they accept my speech and be my helpers in that which I wish. I have sought intercession with You, O He who does and whose command comes to be. O spiritual elder and luminous spirits, I adjure you by this Name which was written upon the heart of Adam, upon him be peace, and by the Name with which God gave you great distinction over many, for He alone is not the deity, He is the Lord of creation. O spiritual pure divine spirits, accept my speech; you are my helpers in those matters I wish, to the extent of God's—exalted be He—pleasure. This is my strength of belief, that you accept the speech that we have commanded. And the angels of God, Lord of the Worlds. O God, I seek aid by Your remembrance and Your pure Names; surely I keep my covenant and am truthful concerning You. I have sought aid from You; I take refuge in You; be my helper and grant me victory, O the sole Truth, for surely there is no helper for me with the Truth except You. I ask by Your dweller, by the verses of the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms, and the wise Quran of light, and by Your great [unclear], that I supplicate.
