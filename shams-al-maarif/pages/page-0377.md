[Victory and triumph, and success before the enemy and the ruler]

And We placed in the hearts of those who followed him tenderness and mercy and monasticism which they invented—We did not ordain it for them except seeking the pleasure of Allah.

When you intend this, after finishing the Friday prayer, write on the membrane of a deer [using] a chicken's egg, then give it the fumigation of ūd and ambergris. Then enclose it in a sheet of silver and keep it with you. Whoever goes to a ruler or enemy will succeed. The talisman is this:

[Protection from the harm of the unjust/oppressor]

Allah the Most High grants you success and teaches you the mysteries of His obedience and His names. Whoever wishes to go to a ruler or an oppressor or a leader, and due to fear of punishment from him wants to silence his tongue, and intends to do away with his wrath and fury even to the point of killing, should write the coming names on the membrane of a deer with musk and saffron and rose water, give it the fumigation of ūd and ambergris and musk, and placing it at the front of his necklace, go to the ruler—even if there is fear of being killed, by the blessing of these names no harm will reach him. Write these names in the hour of the Sun on the day of Sunday; if it is written in the ascendant, it is better. If you have doubt about it, write these names in the same manner, put them around the neck of a dog, and take it to be slaughtered—the dog will not be slaughtered but the knife will not touch it. Remove this talisman from its neck and take this talisman and go to the ruler, and there will be success.
