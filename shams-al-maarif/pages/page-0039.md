He should keep looking at both the blessed names Muhammad ﷺ and Ahmad ﷺ, and keep the pure thought of the Prophet ﷺ in his heart, and recite durood sharif at sunrise daily; then by the grace of the Almighty, He will grant him the means of great obedience and felicity, but this depends on sincerity of intention and purity of the inner self. This is a subtle secret. The blessed letter Dal is a letter from which the blessed and great names Muhammad and Ahmad were completed.

**Protection from enemies, fearlessness from jinn, and treatment for venomous creatures:**

Whoever writes the numerical form of the letter Dal and keeps it with him, Almighty Allah will protect him from harmful enemies, whether they are humans or jinn, etc. Writing it and giving it to drink benefits those with fever, and it is also beneficial for those bitten by scorpions and other venomous creatures.

The special property of this talisman is that it removes distress and sharpens understanding and intellect. On the condition that after washing it with rainwater, one drinks it mixed with pure honey. It also removes every complaint of whiteness [leukoderma/vitiligo], on the condition that this talisman is engraved on a copper plate and kept constantly in the hand, and Mars looks at it with a sweet gaze. Then if this plate is heated in fire and extinguished in water and given to drink to someone bitten by a scorpion, by the will of Allah, relief will come very quickly.

[Decorative Boxed Heading: **The Secrets of the Square**]

The square talisman is a collection of four digits. The four digits are the four mysteries of intellect, spirit, soul, and heart. The numerical value of alif is one. Therefore, when four is multiplied by four, it becomes sixteen. This is the explanation of the extreme of numerical value, because the Divine Throne, seven heavens, and seven earths together number sixteen. This number is the extreme of this square talisman, which has sixteen cells (houses). Then comes the division of this number, that is sixteen, into four parts (odd or even). Seven heavens and seven earths together make fourteen. Below this is the division of twelve, which is twelve signs of the zodiac. Below this is the division of eight, and the carriers of the Throne are eight. Below this is the division of six, and the directions are six, namely above, below, right, left, front, and back. Below this is the division of four. The prophets, the truthful, the martyrs, and the righteous are these four. Below this is the division of two, and the testimonies are two. La ilaha illa Allah and Muhammadun rasul Allah — these are the seven divisions, and the spirit (that is, the absolute) contains fourteen. The Chair consists of seven heavens and seven earths together making fourteen. The diagonal of the Ka'bah house is six, and its opposite is eighteen, and these are the mysteries of the science of the soul. The Tablet, the Pen, the Chair, the Throne, and the seven heavens are all twenty-two, and in it is the diagonal of eleven. The twenty-five human senses are: five exterior senses, five interior senses, five [damaged source], five motors, five perceivers, below which are right, left, front, back — eleven, and the diagonal is nine. The eight natural qualities of the human essence are: heat, coldness, moisture, dryness, agitation, stillness, light, fire (sun, fire), etc. The fifteen surfaces of heat [damaged source].
