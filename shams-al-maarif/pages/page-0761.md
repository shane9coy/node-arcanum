# Page 761 (English translation)
The names will have no effect of fire upon the reciter, nor will any anger be directed against him.

The names: Al-Ra'uf (The Piteous), Al-Halim (The Forbearing), Al-Hannan (The Most Merciful), Wal-Mannan (The Bestower) — these are great names. Whoever recites them is like Joseph; the leaping, flickering fire shall recede from him. It has been stated: whoever recites these names on an empty stomach to the extent that their state overwhelms him — if he takes fire in his hand, no harm shall befall him. If he strikes things with the awareness removed, its effect shall be nullified. The anger that comes upon a person — when he goes before the one he fears, it shall cause him no harm, and its mischief shall be driven away. As for the one overcome by desires, let him recite these names, and Allah the Exalted shall ward off his desires.

Chapter

The names Al-'Afuw (The Pardoner), Al-Ghafur (The Forgiving), Al-Ghaffar (The Much-Forgiving) are extremely swift in effect for dispelling the evil of harsh temperament. Pure is that Essence Who has deposited His mysteries within His names.

These names are the treasury of secrets; their practitioner shall become acquainted with every secret. Whoever places them in frequent recitation and study, their benefits shall become manifest upon him, and these names are among the highest of secrets. Their explanation is as follows: Al-Alf Al-M (garbled text) — he should inscribe them on a gold leaf in the first hour of Friday and engrave this verse as an amulet:

"And He it is Who created the heavens and the earth in truth, and He is aware" — etc.

And this is the beloved for seeking religious and worldly benefits, the safety from the oppressors, and the companionship for the lonely — and these are the names:

Al-Rahman (The Most Gracious), Al-Rahim (The Most Merciful), Al-Ra'uf (The Piteous), Al-'Afuw (The Pardoner), Al-Mannan (The Bestower), Al-Karim (The Generous), Dhu al-Tawl (Possessor of Bounty), Dhu al-Jalal wa al-Ikram (Possessor of Majesty and Generosity)

Through their recitation, all desires are attained. Whoever writes the name of "the swift one" and keeps it with himself, all his affairs shall be completed for him well and quickly. Whoever wishes to uncover a secret, let him recite it, and that secret shall be unveiled to him, for in this name there is great efficacy for the unveiling of hidden matters. For the pious, this recitation is very beneficial for dispelling the darkness of dangers. Whoever writes it and keeps it with himself, his religious and worldly affairs shall be rectified, and this supplication is also appropriate for him:

"My Lord, immerse me in the lights of the seas of Your generous names, a direct unveiling — may the essences of my existence witness what You have deposited in the essences of sovereignty and the divine realm, until I witness the movements of their mysteries through the mystery of Your power, the landmarks of darkness. Let no known thing remain, nor any secret subtleties be afflicted with the tricks of the perfection of the light of manifestation, until the darkness of compulsion vanishes. May the excitants of love toward You transform the beloved and the lover. O Turner of hearts, turn my heart toward obedience to You and following Your pleasure, or turn the heart of so-and-so. O Lord of the worlds."

Through the recitation of this verse, understanding of all sciences shall also be attained, and the manifestation of inner power shall occur.

And among the Quranic verses, this verse is appropriate for it:

"My Lord, indeed You have given me of the sovereignty" — etc.
