**Fourth Latifa:** For expansion and generosity, and it contains half of the Greatest Name. It also has the effect of uniting disparate people and separating enemies. Whoever persists in the remembrance of these names, no harmful person will trouble him. He is dominant over every rebellious and disobedient person. Reciting them before kings and tyrannical people protects from their evil. By its effect, haywanat [unclear], and hard-hearted people become subservient. Its ten names are: **al-'Azhiizu al-Qawiyyu al-Qaadiru Dhu al-Quwwati al-Matiinu al-Muqaddiru al-Jabbaaru al-Mutakabbiru al-Qaahiru al-Qahhaaru**

**Fifth Latifa:** It contains the Greatest Name; those of spiritual unveiling are blessed by it. Whoever persists in its remembrance, Allah the Exalted makes his affairs easy for him. Whoever recites it at midnight will witness marvels. Its constancy opens secrets. It provides protection from distress for the soul and body. It brings destruction upon enemies. Through the blessing of its remembrance, many secrets of the elevated sciences become revealed. The secrets of the Dominion become understood; all the world becomes subservient. These are sovereign words. Its ten names are: **al-Muhiiṭu al-'Aalimu ar-Rabbu ash-Shahiidu al-Hasiibu al-Fa''aalu al-Khallaqu al-Khaaliqu al-Baari'u al-Musawwiru**

**Sixth Latifa:** Its special properties are for preserving the sciences of the people of power and those of knowledge. Its remembrance purifies the hearts of the ascetics. They are ten names: **al-Baatinu al-Hafizhu al-Kaamilu al-Mubdi'u al-Mu'iidu al-Mughnii al-Muqittu al-Mujiidu adh-Dhaabitu al-Waasi'u**

**The practice for always remaining prosperous and wealthy:**

**Seventh Latifa:** This is from among the Greatest Names of the remembrances. For whoever recites it, there is healing from every disease. Whoever persists in the remembrance of these names at midnight will witness marvels; the complete state will become known. He will always be granted prosperity. These names are a means of drawing near to Allah for remembrance. They are ten names: **al-Fatahu al-Baasitu al-Hayyu al-Qayyuumu an-Nuuru adh-Dhaahiru al-Basiiru al-'Azhiizu al-Waduudu al-Waasi'u**

**Eighth Latifa:** For those seeking means, there is a great secret in it. Whoever complains of scarcity of sustenance—by it his need will be fulfilled, he will become their companion. For the people of guidance, there are very great secrets in it. They are ten names: **al-Qawiyyu al-Qaa'imu al-Qaahiru al-Hasiibu al-Wakiilu al-Kaafiyyu ar-Razzaaqu as-Salaamu al-Mu'minu ash-Shariifu**

**Ninth Latifa:** It contains fifteen names that exist in the realm of dominion, the realm of the Dominion, the hidden realm, and the elevated and lower realms. Whoever persists in the remembrance of these names on an empty stomach will discover within his self elevated ambition and advancement in inner affairs. The hearts of people will incline toward him. If he feels fear, it will depart. Those names are: **al-Muhsii al-Mumiitu al-Qaabizu al-Ba'ithu al-Waarithu ash-Shaafii al-Barru al-Jawwaadu al-Muhsinu al-Qayyuumu al-Awwalu al-Aakhiru adh-Dhaahiru al-Baatinu al-Qudduusu lam yalid wa lam yuulad wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahadun**

**The treatment for whisperings:**

Know that these latifas are very effective. Each one should be engraved on a sleeping ring whose bezel is of silver, if both the ring and the bezel are desired; or written on it. Then when intending to recite that latifa, wear this ring—the effect will come quickly, but success comes after fasting and spiritual discipline. The verse **wa immaa yanzaghannaka mina ash-shaytaani nazzaghun fa ista'idh bi-llaahi** is beneficial for whisperings, fear, panic, and distress. On the day of Friday at sunrise, write them on seven papers with rose water and saffron, and each day dissolve one paper in water and drink it. If he drinks one gulp of water, there will immediately be relief.

The Noble Prophet ﷺ said: "If one of you is struck by doubt—saying 'Who created this?' and he answers 'Allah the Exalted created it,' and then he says 'And who created Allah the Exalted?'—it is obligatory upon the people of faith to be vigilant and seek refuge with Allah the Exalted." In one tradition it is said: "People will always continue saying 'This thing is from the creation of Allah the Exalted,' and then [the questioning about] Allah the Exalted..."
