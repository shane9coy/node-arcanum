# Page 189 (English translation)
He gives a good reward for good deeds and gives them more out of His grace, and Allah gives sustenance without measure to whom He wills.
Say, "Do you help me with my wealth? Rather, what Allah has given me is better than what He has given you, He who first creates creation and will then recreate it, and He who gives you sustenance from the heaven and the earth." And We intend to favor those who were considered weak, that We may make them Imams and make them heirs. O our Lord, whatever good You have sent down to me—surely I am in need of good. We have settled them in the Sacred Sanctuary, drawing sustenance from every direction to give to them. Therefore, seek sustenance from His presence. Worship Him, give thanks to Him, and to Him you shall all return. Many creatures do not carry their own sustenance; Allah gives sustenance to them and to you, and He is the Hearing, the Knowing.
Have you not seen that Allah has subjected to you all that is in the heavens and the earth, and He has perfected His blessings upon you? Eat the sustenance of your Lord and give thanks to Him, for He opens His mercy to whom He wills among the people, and none can withhold it, and whoever withholds it—after him there is no sender—and He is the Powerful, the Wise.
Whatever you spend, He will repay it, and He is the best provider, and there is none in the heavens and the earth who can cause Him to become helpless regarding anything. Indeed, He is Knowing and Powerful.
Indeed, this is the sustenance of Allah; it shall not be exhausted. This is Our gift—He favors or withholds without measure. Whatever is with you will be exhausted, and whatever is with Allah remains. Allah, who created you, then gave you sustenance, then will cause you to die, then will resurrect you—and whoever fears Allah, Allah will make for him a way out and give him sustenance from where he does not expect. And Allah gives sustenance without measure to whom He wills.

Highly Effective Verses for Obtaining Sustenance

These verses hold great efficacy for obtaining sustenance:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ

"In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful"

فَعَسٰى اللّٰهُ اَنْ يَّاْتِیَ بِالْفَتْحِ الا یہ وَعِنْدَهٗ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَيْبِ الا یہ

"Perhaps Allah will bring the conquest [victory], and with Him are the keys of the unseen."

رَبَّنَا افْتَحْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَ قَوْمِنَا الا یہ

"Our Lord, judge between us and our people."

وَلَوْ اَنَّ اَهْلَ الْقُرٰى اٰمَنُوْا وَاتَّقَوْا لَفَتَحْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ بَرَکٰتٍ مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ وَالْاَرْضِ الا يه

"Had the people of the cities believed and been conscious of Allah, We would have opened upon them blessings from the heaven and the earth."

اِنْ تَسْتَفْتِحُوْا فَقَدْ جَآءَكُمُ الْفَتْحُ وَلَمَّا فَتَحُوْا مَتَاعَهُمْ الا یہ

"If you seek victory, then victory has come to you. And when they opened their goods [as a trial]."

وَاسْتَفْتَحُوْا وَخَابَ الا يه

"And they asked for victory, and disappointment seized them."

وَلَوْ فَتَحْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ بَابًا مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ الا یہ

"And had We opened for them a gate from the heaven."

رَبِّ اِنَّ قَوْمِیْ كَذَّبُوْنِ فَافْتَحِ الا يه

"O my Lord, indeed my people have denied. So open [a way for me]."

مَا يَفْتَحِ اللّٰهُ لِلنَّاسِ مِنْ رَّحْمَةٍ الا يه

"What Allah opens for people [of His mercy], no one can withhold."

حَتّٰى اِذَا جَآءُوْهَا وَفُتِحَتْ اَبْوَابُهَا اِنَّا فَتَحْنَا لَكَ فَتْحًا مُّبِيْنًا الا يه

"Until, when they come to it and its gates are opened, indeed We have opened for you a clear victory."

وَاَثَابَهُمْ فَتْحًا قَرِيْبًا الآيه

"And He will give them a victory that is near."

فَفَتَحْنَا اَبْوَابَ السَّمَآءِ بِمَآءٍ الا يه

"So We opened the gates of the sky with water."

نَصْرٌ مِّنَ اللّٰهِ وَفَتْحٌ قَرِيْبٌ

"Victory from Allah and an imminent victory."

وَفُتِحَتِ السَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتْ اَبْوَابًا

"And the heaven was opened and became gates."

اِذَا جَآءَ نَصْرُ اللّٰهِ وَالْفَتْحُ

"When comes the victory of Allah and the conquest."

اٰه- يَا فَتَّاحُ يَا رَزَّاقُ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا رَبَّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ

"Ah—O You who open, O Provider, O Allah, O Lord of the worlds."

وَ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلٰی سَيِّدِنَا مُحَمَّدٍ وَّ عَلٰی اٰلِهٖ وَصَحْبِهٖ وَسَلَّمَ

"And may Allah bless our master Muhammad and his family and companions, and grant him peace."

In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful. Perhaps Allah will bring victory, and with Him are the keys of the unseen.
(Verse) O our Lord, decide between us and our people in truth. If the people of the cities had believed and been conscious of Allah, We would have opened upon them blessings from the heaven and the earth.
(Verse) If you seek judgment, then judgment has come to you. And when they opened their provisions—
(Verse) And they sought judgment, and it failed them.
(Verse) And if We had opened for them a gate from the heaven—
(Verse) O Lord, indeed my people have denied—
(Verse) Allah does not open for the people any mercy—
(Verse) Until, when they come to it and its gates are opened: "Indeed, We have opened for you a clear victory."
(Verse) And Allah gave them a victory that is near.
(Verse) So We opened the gates of the heaven with water—
(Verse) Victory is from Allah and a victory that is near.
(Verse) And the heaven was opened and became gates.
(Verse) When the victory of Allah and the conquest comes—
(Verse) Ah—O Opener, O Provider, O Allah, O Lord of the worlds.
(Verse) And may Allah bless our master Muhammad and his family and companions, and grant him peace.
