# Page 313 (English translation)
Chapter 92: Properties of the Beneficial Name, Cure from Every Illness and Benefit in Affairs

In this noble name there is cure for every illness and success for every need. Whoever mentions this name frequently during illness, God Almighty will grant him recovery. If one mentions this name frequently according to his condition and blows upon it, so that his condition transforms, then whatever sick person he passes his hand over will be cured. If one engraves its square upon a silver ring during the prominence of the Moon, whoever wears it will be cured. You do not see that this name resembles the Name of Safety. Its letters' numbers point toward two noble names of God and Shafi. Around its square is written the verse: "And We send down from the Quran that which is a cure and mercy for the believers." This name is appropriate for one whose name is Qasim. Its numbers are 201; in Nur of the future its district is three.

This is from the noble numbers; its numbers are 170, pointing toward the suitable name. Its letters' numbers 428 point toward the name of God Almighty, the Strong in State. Its namesake has an addition of the Name of the King of Kings of the Dominion. Its triangle is numerical, with which a square of letters surrounds it. Writing it during the prominence of the Moon and keeping it with oneself, one will witness the wonders of God's Almighty's power. This is the form of its inscription.

Chapter 93: Properties of the Name of Light, the heart illuminated with the light of faith

This is the light of God Almighty, whose light illuminates the heart with faith. If the name of Light and the Beneficial name are placed in one inscription, one will witness matters of strange and wondrous secrets. Its number is 256. Its letters' numbers are from among its degrees in numbers. This is one less than the covenant of Noah, peace be upon him, from his origin, and it points toward Hum Kilil, peace be upon him; in addition, it points toward two noble names of God Almighty, Da'im and Hilm (Everlasting and Forbearance). Its letters point toward the name of God Almighty, the Manifest. Now the reference to the name of the Manifest has concluded [damaged source/end of page].
