# Page 538 (English translation)
536

The letter is 20 and its extraction is Gha'il and it is the interpretation of the Hour of the Sun. And: Vav, Lam, Alif, Mim, Shin, Ya, Nun, Mim, Sin, Ya, Nun—total 15 letters. And Ratī is 22 and its Ka'b is 459, and their extraction is Diya'il.

**Yawm al-Ithnayn (Monday)**
That is, the day of Monday. Its planet is the Moon, and it is both basiṭ and murakkab. Basiṭ is as follows: Alif, Lam, Alif, Lam, Alif, Tha, Nun, Ya, Nun, Alif, Lam, Alif, Ya, Nun—total 16 letters. And upon it is the operation (of the number). Its Jawzahar is 459 and the Moon is also basiṭ and murakkab. So basiṭ is: Alif, Lam, Alif, Lam, Alif, Qaf, Mim, Ra—total 14 letters. And upon it is the operation. But the collection of Ratī letters is 24 letters, and their Ka'b is 316, and their extraction is Dasi'il. This is one of various opinions.

**Yawm al-Thulathaa' (Tuesday)**
That is, the day of Tuesday. Its basiṭ is 22, its Ka'b is 304, and its extraction is Lam-ba'il. Its nair and star is Mars.

**Yawm al-Arbi'aa' (Wednesday)**
That is, the day of Wednesday. Its letters are 29 and their Ka'b is 815, and their extraction is Ani'il, and their star is Mercury.

**Yawm al-Khamiis (Thursday)**
That is, the day of Thursday. Its basiṭ is 74 and its Ka'b is 510 and its extraction is Intā'il, and its star is Jupiter.

**Yawm al-Jumu'ah (Friday)**
That is, the day of Friday. Its numerical basiṭ is 34 and its Ka'b [unclear] and its extraction is [unclear], and its planet is Venus.

**Yawm al-Sabt (Saturday)**
That is, the day of Saturday. Its numerical basiṭ is 29 and its Ka'b is 330 and its extraction is Kahi'il, and the star is Saturn.

Know that there are various methods of extraction, but I have mentioned this type which is most commonly used. So if you wish, subtract it from the original number. If you wish, take the word-number and subtract it. And if you wish, proceed by this method which I have explained. All the rules are correct. According to your capability, work. We present one example. Upon it, measure all others. For example, the total number is 211. From this, subtract the original number, meaning 51; 160 will remain. Extract them. It will be Asni'il.

**Second Procedure:** And the second procedure for the non-compound is this. Alif, Lam, Mim, Qaf, Lam—its letters are 15. When these are multiplied, they become 115, and this is from the original number. So when the first number was extracted, Hayā'il was obtained.

**Third Procedure:** And the third procedure is this: Alif, Ra, Jim, Dal, Lam, Ya, Shin, Ya, Nun, Sin, Ba, Ha, Ya, Nun, Mim, Alif, Shin, Lam, Alif, Shin, Ya, Nun—24 letters. When they were multiplied within themselves, 215 was obtained. Its extraction was performed and Hina'il was obtained. Then we returned to the original number and performed extraction. So the same result emerged that was stated. Upon this same basis, measure all operations.
