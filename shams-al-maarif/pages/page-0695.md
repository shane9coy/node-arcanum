# Page 695 (English translation)
**Description of the Attributes of Allah is Impossible:**

The True, the Unlimited—He alone is the All-Embracing, the Absolutely Vast. And if one looks at His knowledge, it is infinite—meaning, if seven seas of ink and all the growth of the earth were fashioned into pens and wrote [His words], this ink and these pens would be exhausted, but His words would not be exhausted. And if one looks at reality, there is neither sea nor growth. All are attributes of this Pure and Exalted Essence.

Thus it is known that the All-Embracing is He whose limit is nonexistent, and He—the Exalted—says:
"Rabbanā wasi'ta kulla shay'in rahmaṭan wa 'ilman" (Our Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge).

The servant should take his share of morality, knowledge, expansion, and grace from this name. So when the servant perceives in his inner self the expansion of perfect faith, let him know that the station of vastness has been granted to him—and this inner expansion is luminous. The spiritual retreat for this name is that one should dwell in an open and elevated place and recite this name after each prayer according to the number of Basṭ. The guardian [angel] will appear in a dream or while awake. Whoever constantly recites this name, difficult tasks will become easy for him, and he will pass from narrowness to spaciousness. And for the person whose name corresponds numerically to its value, this is the Greatest Name [of God]. When it is written and placed in a location, shop, or amulet, there will be blessing in it.

**Every Need Will Be Fulfilled and There Will Be Healing in Melancholic Diseases:**

If one engraves it on a ring and wears it, all his needs will be fulfilled, and it is also beneficial for one suffering from melancholic disease to recite it. Its talismanic square is as follows:

And its supplication is:
Bi-smi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm. Allāhumma anta al-wāsi'u al-muḥīṭu bi-daqā'iqi al-ma'lūmāti alladhī lā ya'zubu 'anhu atharu al-ḍamā'iri wal-khawāṭiri al-khafiyyāti. As'aluka bi-quwwati qudratika 'alā badhli l-iḥsāni bi-dawāmi l-faḍli 'alā l-'ibādi wal-imtināni an tuwassi'a makārima akhlāqī wa ma'ārifī wa an tūfīya ma'lūmātī mā yatatawassa'u asrārī wa-mawāridī li-tajalliyātika wa tatạāda'a anwārī bi-nūri 'ināyatik. Allāhumma wassi' 'alayya al-khayrāti wadfa' 'annī al-'atharāti yā Allāhu yā wāsi'u yā ḥalīmu as'aluka an tuwassi'a 'alā kulli amrin ḍayyiqin yufarriju minka yā wāsi'u al-maghfirati.
