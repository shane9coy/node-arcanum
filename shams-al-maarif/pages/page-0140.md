And it is from His mercy that He made for you night and day, so that you may find rest therein, and that you may seek His bounty, and so that you may give thanks. So this is the eternal delicate matter.

Know that the highest spiritual states and the lights of the surface are drawn toward the lights of the intellect, and the merciful spiritual states are confined within simple spirits, and the intellect is the pure pavilion upon which the delicate poverty has cast a veil. The light of the components of the divine kingdom is manifest alongside its inner aspect. So when you intend to understand them, understanding these signs is obligatory upon you.

Understand this by example: four birds belonging to one name "the hidden," another "the treasured," the third "the nearest," and the fourth "the greatest." Then draw these birds toward yourself and make them familiar. When you have firm grasp over them and have witnessed their secrets, go to the Holy Sanctum and slaughter them. Then place the part of the greatest upon the mountain of the birds' rank. Place the part of the bird of the desired beloved upon the mountain of life. Place the part of the bird of the treasured upon the mountain of the divine kingdom, and place the part of the bird of the hidden upon the mountain of sustenance. Call upon them with the call with which knowledge was given to you. They will come running to you. This is for one who has verified with the name of lofty honor and wisdom.

If you have understood the divine inspirational and luminous revelatory secret of the self, then you are the four birds. The first bird is life. The second is the bird of knowledge which brings one to God Almighty. The third is the bird of power, and the fourth is the bird of will which has become attached to the life of faithful living. One whose annihilation has occurred, and the verification of knowledge at the head of the liver in creation, and the head of power upon the mountain of composition, and the head of will upon the mountain of order.

Then call upon them with the tongue of wisdom. They will come running to you, and this becomes manifest when one achieves complete proximity to God Almighty, for it becomes the instance of the Holy Speech. The Holy Pronouncement states: "When a servant draws near to Me, I become his ear with which he hears, I become his eye with which he sees, and I become his tongue with which he speaks."

Know that the inheritor reported from God Almighty that when God Almighty consumed some from some, He then permitted it two breaths: one breath for cold and the second breath for heat. These two separate breaths are from one breath alone. Their distinction is due to the hidden delicacy. O you, understand from His saying "if He wills, He takes you" [unclear: except when therein] — it is the same claim from the Day. In the salt mountain of the afterlife there is the perception of pain, so its station is the truth.

Then call upon them, and they will come running to you. If you enter the components of annihilation and the varieties of witnessing, take the four birds and draw them toward yourself. Verify their existence in their witnessing. Then place a part from each upon every mountain. Place the bird of proximity upon the mountain of intellect, the bird of sincerity upon the mountain of spirit, the bird of martyrdom upon the mountain of heart, and the bird of capability upon the mountain of body. Then call upon them, and they will come running to you. Then if your station remains positive, you will begin to understand these matters.

Then take the four birds and draw them toward yourself. The bird of intellect — its head is life. The bird of spirit — it is the secret of knowledge. Then the bird of heart which is the head of perception. Then the bird of the secret which is the secret of power. Then place each part from them upon the mountain. Place the bird of intellect upon the mountain of primordial life, place the bird of the journey upon the mountain of afterlife life, place the bird of heart upon the mountain of eternal life. Then call upon them, and they will come running to you.

Know that for one who has obtained God's portion, there is not even a trace of change. Then its portion is the portion of the garment of the divine mountain, and the portion of the spiritual spirit, and the honor of the pure heart. If you wish to reach this station, then act in the manner we have described.

One of the masters of realities says: I boarded a boat in which there was turmoil. These were the waves of desire, and we passed between the waves of the winds. The boat continued with the favorable wind until I reached the shore of the sea. I saw a river whose waves were red from the flood of "ayn ya quwwat" of great majesty. The spring of the water of life flows continuously within it. Emerging from its water, a spring flows whose water — anyone who drinks from thirst — dies.
