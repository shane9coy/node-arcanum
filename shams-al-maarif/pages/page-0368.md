The Verse of the Chair was revealed in fifty words, which is in the Noble Quran, whose totality Moses did not hear. It protects the souls, the sublime spirit, the composed and the traveler, the resident and the wanderer. It cures the born-blind, the leper, and the sick. Its great justice is His kingdom of old, His path is straight, and His universal grace. He is Exalted and Great in the heavens and earth.

Chapter 19: Some Very Proven and Beneficial Figures and Talismans

May God Almighty grant me and you obedience and the secrets of His Names. The letters of every verse of the Noble Quran are in reality related to every number. If some purely numerical and septenary [letters] are collected and written, the secrets of the Prophet will be revealed through it. Know that according to the people of secrets, every verse has a form. Whoever sees this form with understanding, the lights of divine favor turn towards him, and whoever becomes acquainted with it heedlessly—all things come under his control. You do not see that those who have become acquainted with the secrets of the noble masters cure various illnesses, and as for those who fail in it, this is simply due to their ignorance, because they have not fully known the heedless and the natural dispositions. The foundation was placed on water and it did not become firm, or the pillow was placed on the successor and it did not become firm, because it is necessary that the tongue should become strong from the unknown. The properties of these letters are extremely rare and the benefits are wondrous—apart from a few specific complete ones, no one is acquainted with them.

In the practical operations of attracting hearts and spirits and so on, they are used from them. There are four categories: fiery, earthy, airy, and watery. This is the order according to the people of natural dispositions, and according to the people of divine scriptures the order is as follows: fiery, airy, earthy, and watery. Our purpose from this chapter is the composition upon which it stands. This is the picture of its circle, from which you will recognize the order of the watery and earthy letters. They are these: alif, ba, jim, ta, tha, kha, dhal are fiery. The airy letters are lam, mim, nun, sad, dad, 'ayn, and ghayn. The earthy letters are dal, ha, fa, qaf, sin, and ya. The heart of secrets prefers fiery letters over earthy letters and immerses them in water, because air does not restrain water. We have made everything clear for you; you should not be distressed. Strive, and you will succeed, as the Shaykh, may God Almighty have mercy on him, said:

"O God, do not cause weakness in any quest, for You are able to fulfill it."

"And as for the raindrop, when it falls repeatedly upon the hard rock, it makes its impression."

That is: "O God, do not make me incapable of obtaining my desire, for You are able to fulfill it, just as when the drop falls repeatedly upon the stone, it arranges its effects upon it. The one who strives will succeed. O heedless one, may your portion be elevated and raised."
