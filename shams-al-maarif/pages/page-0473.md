It is like the moon, silvery. Rather, men of knowledge call it the most cold and watery. According to them, its day is Thursday (Jum'ah al-Khamis). It is the planet Jupiter. Its metal is brass. But most physicians and scholars of astronomy call it cold, dry, and earthy.

Hippocrates' statement is: our letters are of four types: hot-dry, cold-dry, hot-moist, cold-moist. In Hippocrates' time, there was no arrangement other than the alphabetical order. When the 28 letters were divided by four, it came to seven each. From this division, their purpose is the rank: (1) first, (2) second, (3) third, (4) fourth, (5) fifth, (6) sixth, (7) seventh.

Benefits: Take the composite numerical value of Ba, expand it, then make a triangle of it, and write its letter of joy on unbaked bricks (that have not touched fire). Throw its client into seven types of wells, and its water will dry up. If you write the numerical value of this letter in the letters of the moon and give it to a bride, it will increase her beauty, attractiveness, and magnetism. If you write it on a lead tablet on the day of the week and hang it in a prison, the prisoners will be freed. Recite this supplication with these letters:

O Allah, I supplicate You, O Lord, O Creator, O Eternal, O Resurrector, O Blessed, by what You have placed within the letter Ba of the concealed secrets and hidden lights, that You may subordinate for me Your angels, servants of these letters, in what I have commanded them, in which there is Your pleasure. Indeed, You are over all things Omnipotent.

**The Letter Ta**

This letter has the quality cold-dry, like the letter Alif, but it has moisture along with dryness. It is in the rank of first degree and in the fourth degree. One of its qualities is attractiveness. The other is the numerical value of 400.

Benefits: If you write Ta on four raw bricks and bury them in the four corners of a field, the harvest will be very abundant. It will remain safe from damage. If these bricks are placed in a pile of grain, the grain will remain safe—no damage will occur. If Ta is engraved four hundred times on a copper tablet and attached to a boat, it will not sink. If it is made into the bezel of your ring and Ta is engraved on it and worn, it will not sink.

**House Safe from Theft:**

If this talisman is placed in the house, theft will not occur. The form of this talisman is:

[Diagram: A triangle with the Arabic letter "ت" at each vertex. Inside the triangle, near each vertex, the number "400" is written. Along each of the three sides on the exterior, the characters "ت ت" are written.]

According to calculation, every letter should be written equal to its number. Alif once, Ba twice, Ta four hundred times. You may calculate all letters by this proportion. But proportion has no role in the mysteries.

Benefits: Writing Ta four hundred times on cloth or paper of the color of jujube and keeping it—every person will love it; the hearts of people will incline toward it. This letter has two divine names. The supplication is:

O Allah, I supplicate You, O Constant, O Most Acceptant of repentance, by what You have placed within the letter Ta of the concealed secrets and hidden lights, that You may subordinate for me Your noble angels, servants of this letter. Indeed, You are over all things Omnipotent.

**The Letter Tha**

This also has a quality. It is hot-dry. Its rank is fourth. Because of this, it also has heat. Its [unclear] is luminous, and it is commonly used.
