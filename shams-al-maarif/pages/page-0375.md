# Page 375 (English translation)
375

(The practice of silencing enemies and ending conflicts)

That is because they believed and then disbelieved, so a seal was set upon their hearts, and they do not understand. They are turned away. (Quran 63:3)

This is for silencing enemies, making them silent, and removing conflict and dispute—to engrave this [figure] on an iron tablet when Mars is in Scorpio, and keeping it with you, face the enemy; the enemy's tongue shall be silenced and victory shall be achieved over him. This is the form of this talisman:

[A talismanic figure consisting of a square frame with two intersecting diagonal lines forming an "X" inside. Arabic letters such as "ع", "ل", "ح", "م", "ص" and numerals are written within the triangular sections and around the outer edges.]

(A talisman for abundance in sustenance and honor)

The Quranic verse is:
"Our Lord, upon You we have relied, and to You we have returned, and to You is the destination. Our Lord, do not make us a trial for those who have disbelieved, and forgive us, our Lord. Indeed, You are the Exalted in Might, the Wise." (Quran 60:4-5)

Engrave this verse on an iron ring and wear it, so that God Almighty may provide assistance and grant honor—even if he is someone despised. What he does not know shall be accomplished for him, and sustenance shall continue to be given to him; He shall be his supporter and helper, because in this [verse] there is the name of trust in God and honor. It is the command of God Almighty, and God Almighty guides whom He wills to the straight path. This is its talisman:

[A 7x7 square grid talisman (naqsh). Above the grid is a row of numbers (49 7 4 6 7 3 5 4) and possibly names of angels. The cells of the grid contain various Arabic letters like "ع", "م", "ر", "ز", "د", "ف", "ل" arranged in a recurring pattern. The left and right margins of the grid contain vertical text consisting of Arabic letters and names.]
