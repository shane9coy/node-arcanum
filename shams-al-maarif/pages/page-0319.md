# Page 319 (English translation)
317

Its apparent is the best and its interior is profound. Its wonders do not end and its marvels do not diminish. Each verse of the Glorious Quran has seven apparent and seven inner meanings: "literal expressions" (ʿibārāt), "indications" (ishārāt), "subtle insights" (laṭāʾif), and "realities" (ḥaqāʾiq). Thus, literal expressions are for the common people and indications are for the elite. Subtle insights are for the saints, subtle insights for the truthful and the lovers, and realities are for the prophets. Then beneath each word, nay, beneath each letter, rivers flow with waves. When one of the people of mysticism and sincere lovers reads the Holy Quran, for each letter a thousand understandings of divine grace are given to him. From each understanding there are a thousand linguistic meanings, and from each linguistic meaning a thousand lessons, and from one lesson the heavens and earth are established. Allah the Most High has said: "Whoever is given wisdom has indeed been given much good." Its meaning is the understanding of the Quran and its meanings. Some scholars say that each verse of the Glorious Quran contains sixty thousand understandings, and some say that the Glorious Quran comprises seventy thousand (77000) sciences. Some of the great masters have said that the reality of the Quran is the sustaining power of the heavens and earth from the day they were created until the Day of Resurrection. This is the reason that near the end of time, the Holy Quran will be lifted from the breasts. Allah the Most High will lift it.

Muḥammad Ḥanīf Ghifārī
