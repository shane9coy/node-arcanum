When body, spirit, and soul are gathered together, and that is the same "zayyiq" which they mean by "mixture" (khilṭ), and some say this is lead (raṣāṣ). And these are the sulfur-burning substances whose removal is the wisdom of the philosophers. So when those are removed, then remain the body of flight (jism al-ṭayrān). Then the philosophers further concealed this.

<u>Minerals, when governed by fire, become deadly poison:</u>

The intention of the philosophers in this exposition was the same as what I expounded to you: that when minerals are governed with fire, they become deadly poison for the human body because of their inflammation. And when the blessed part is governed, it becomes a cure for many diseases. And when the blessed parts are gathered and the complete elixir is prepared, it is a complete antidote for every severe disease and is used in many aspects of medicine.

<u>The experiment of the sage Jābir ibn Ḥayyān:</u>

Thus Jābir ibn Ḥayyān writes in his books that a woman had the disease of consumption (marad al-daqq) and her blood had become thin. I governed it with a small honored part in the proper manner of governing, through whose use that woman became completely healthy. The predominance of the fire of heat that was incorporated in her limbs was completely removed, and she was granted freshness and renewed vigor. In a short period, she became plump. Previously, she used to have herself bled every year, and she also stopped that.

And the philosophers' saying "innamā huwa al-murakkab al-muḥamarr ḥattā yastajūd" means that their intention is to introduce color into the earth. And in the appointed period, they introduce upon it sulfur, and zayyiq, and aqua vitae, and rose water, and water of mirdīk, and grape syrup, and five chlorides. And all these names mean introducing color into the earth—that is, upon the body. So when this water is gathered with the earth and color, then the zayyiq and sulfur are gathered in it. And in some ranks, they also call these parts "red sulfur" (kibrīt aḥmar), and by it they mean the elixir.

In the art of governing, there are two things that cause fear to the seeker: one is the duration of the governance, and the second is how much elixir should be administered.

<u>Three months are spent in preparing the elixir:</u>

Regarding the duration, there is much disagreement, and it is not among the things that can be known with certainty. The average is three months. Jābir states that a man of experience can shorten the work, and no harm occurs as a result. I swear by God, we also made it in a very short period. And I said this so that you may know that there is no strict limit on the duration. For example, if you put large pieces of meat in a pot and cook them, they will take a long time to cook. If you cut them into small pieces or make them into minced meat and put them on the fire and add hot water, they will cook very quickly. Besides this, this matter is not in contradiction with it, because those are the strict conditions and the appropriate path. But regarding the application of the embryo of the elixir upon the body, the philosophers have differed greatly. And they concealed the method, but I will clearly state it: when your cooked substance—that is, the elixir—becomes capable of remaining on the fire, and the temperament becomes correct like a child whose father's semen the woman swallowed—meaning the finest clay—and the womb also accepted it in the excellent manner, and the period of the work has also just passed, then that child grows with the praised part. In the same way, you should understand the preparation of the elixir.
