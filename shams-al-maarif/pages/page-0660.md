Three meanings were included within it. One: the keeping of the soul within the body. Second: the story of resurrection in the afterlife after the form has disintegrated. Third: the living of the dead in the sensory and celestial world.

**The Question of Abraham, upon whom be peace, was for three things:**

Allah the Exalted said:

**فَخُذْ اَرْبَعَةً مِّنَ الطَّیْرِ فَصُرْھُنَّ اِلَیْکَ**

"Take four birds and make them familiar to you."

The Names of Essence, the Names of Attributes, and the Lower Names—each one on a mountain, place one part from among them on each.

The mountains signify spiritual principles, that is, the roots. Accordingly, on the first day they placed the first part on Mount Qadr, and on the beautiful part of nature they placed the banner of the image-form, and one part on Mount of the Day of Interval, and one part on Mount of the Day of Resurrection.

**ثُمَّ ادْعُهُنَّ يَاْتِيْنَکَ سَعْيًا وَ اعْلَمْ اَنَّ اللّٰهَ عَزِيْزٌ حَکِيْمٌ**

"Then call them and they will come running to you, and know that Allah is Mighty, Wise."

When Abraham, upon whom be peace, observed the secret of nature, at that time he found the entire world composed of the same patterns, and he saw it established with these very Names. And certainty of truth was manifested to him. Then after that, Allah the Exalted showed him the wonders of the Kingdom, regarding which He says:

**وَ كَذٰلِكَ نُرِيْ اِبْرٰهِيْمَ مَلَكُوْتَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضِ**

"Thus We showed Abraham the kingdom of the heavens and the earth."

And these are the human and natural forms in the realities of witnessing and the mysteries of existence. That is, the perfection of His Essence was in the station of Abraham, upon whom be peace, just as the sun is among the stars.

**The increase and decrease of light among humans is like the stars:**

Know that some stars are bright and some are not bright. Thus it is the same with those upon whom the Divine Names have manifested—some are like bright stars, but among them also there are different degrees. Some star is brighter and people find guidance from it, and some is very small and dim. This is the same difference that exists among the ranks of people.

**Some faces will be bright like the sun and some like the moon and stars:**

Thus the Messenger, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said: The first group of my Ummah that enters Paradise—their faces will be bright like the sun. Then after them will be those whose faces will be bright like the moon and the stars, and this light of their faces will be according to the degree of their deeds and faith.

**The luminosity of faces will be in both worlds**

The manifestation of the righteous predecessors will be in both worlds, and it will remain established in both worlds. For this same reason, the luminous attributes and names are united with them within nature, by which the faces of the spirit become radiant. This is what manifests His Name the Creator, because whoever has infinite blessings is the one upon whom His mercy has been bestowed. We also know that this world is a marketplace in which only a small amount of perfect things can be contained, and whoever among people prefers any form obtains it.
