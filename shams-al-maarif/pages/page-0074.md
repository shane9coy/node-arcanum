# Page 74 (English translation)
Thus, there are under the command of His Excellency Gabriel, peace be upon him, angels whose number cannot be counted. Furthermore, each of these four angels has distinct thoughts and actions, and each has a separate day. Thus, His Excellency Gabriel, peace be upon him, has Monday, and his temperament is relatively cold. Israphel's day is Thursday, and his temperament is relatively hot. His Excellency Azrael, peace be upon him, has Saturday, his temperament is relatively cold, his nature is earthy, and it brings death. His Excellency Michael's day is Wednesday, and his temperament is mixed from the four temperaments. These four assigned angels have four separate magical squares. His Excellency Gabriel's is the heptagon [7x7], Israphel's is the quadrilateral [4x4], Azrael's is the triangle [3x3], and Michael's is the octagon [8x8]. These are the four magical squares; this is their form.

Know that there is great influence in these magical squares; whoever composes them correctly will find them accurate. One can accomplish whatever one wishes through them, but the condition is that one must fear God, the Most High. It is the duty of all righteous people not to cause harm to anyone. When one intends to perform any operation from among the four magical squares, one must extract the numerical sum of its numbers, increase it, and write it with the utmost precision so that one obtains one's desire. The heptagon should be written in this manner: write it at the time of sunrise on Monday; similarly the triangle, or write it on Saturday upon a silver sheet. If one wishes to write for beneficial purposes, let it be during an ascendant or under the honor of the Moon; indeed, those skilled should be numerous. As much as possible, remember that one should not [illegible] cause harm to anyone.
