# Page 227 (English translation)
Chapter: The Invitation of Surah Mulk and Its Many Benefits

Its composition is as follows: with purity, recite Surah Mulk three times, then recite the prayer that comes after it, and fumigate with something fragrant. There is a great secret in this. The prayer is this:

In the name of Allah, the Compassionate, the Merciful. O mountains, hymn His praises with him, and the birds! And We made iron malleable for him, saying: "Make coats of mail, and measure well the links of the coats of mail, and do righteous deeds; surely I am seeing what you do." Thus, O Lord of lords, and soften for me the hearts of all creation, among humans and jinn, by the right of these names: Maliki, Kuyidi, Sajafat, Batikam, al-Walsan, Badiid, Al sh mazes, man Kabir, Muzkanin, Zarf. You are You; every creature on it is perishable, and there remains the Face of your Lord, Possessor of Glory and Honor. I ask You, O Allah, to subjugate for me dominion and the celestial realm, until they become submissive to me with humility, awe, and love, and by the right of "Those who love them [i.e., believers] love Him as the love of Allah; and those who believe are stauncher in love for Allah" – "Had you spent all that is in the earth, you could not have harmonized their hearts, but Allah harmonized them; surely He is Almighty, Wise." And I ask You, O Allah, to carry out through my will the decree, fate, and the revolving celestial sphere, and to carry out my awe and love in the hearts of the two kinds – humans and jinn – all of them. Sovereigns, Bahram of armies, in battles. Allah has written: "I and My messengers shall surely prevail; surely Allah is Powerful, Almighty." And the king said – [small text note: al-Nawawi] – "Bring him to me; I shall single him out for myself." When he spoke to him, he said: "You are today surely with us glorified, trustworthy. And Allah prevails over His affair, though most people do not know. And We gave him from everything a provision. Tasum – You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. The Hour – there is no doubt in it – but most people do not believe. Say: 'The victory of Allah is near.' And give glad tidings to the believers. And there is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Great.

[Urdu Translation]

Translation: In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Ever Merciful. O mountains, hymn His praises with Him. We made iron malleable for Him, that they might make coats of mail, and We determined the measurement of the links, and do righteous deeds; surely I am seeing what you do. O Lord of lords, for me soften the hearts of all creation, both jinn and human, by the right of these names: Maliki, Kuyidi, Sajafat, Batikam, al-Walsan, Badiid, Alst maran, man Kabir, Murkur, Zanin, Razqa – You are; the world is perishable and only Your Great and Honorable Self will remain. O Allah, I ask You to subjugate dominion and the celestial realm for me, until those who come with humility, awe, and love become submissive. By the right of the verse that "they love them [i.e., believers] as the love of Allah," and those who have believed are stauncher in love for Allah – had you spent everything on earth, you could not have created love between their hearts, but Allah the Most High put love between their hearts. Surely He is Glorious, Honorable, Wise. O Allah, I ask You to carry out fate and destiny according to my will, and the revolving celestial sphere, and establish my awe and love in the hearts of humans and jinn of both worlds. Sovereigns and horsemen triumph. Allah has written: "I and My messenger shall surely prevail; surely Allah is Powerful and Honorable." And the king said: "Bring him to me; I will make him special for myself." When he spoke with him, he said: "Surely you are today glorified and trustworthy with us." O Allah, Allah prevails over His affair – read up to here. And We gave him the provisions of everything. We worship You, O Allah, and seek help from You alone. There is victory and near triumph from Allah, and give glad tidings to the believers that power and strength belong to Allah alone, who is Exalted and Great.
