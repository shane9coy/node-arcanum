# Page 122 (English translation)
No worship is worthy except for Him. He has no associate. There is none like unto Him, neither likeness nor similitude. His essence is of glory and majesty. And we bear witness that indeed our master Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, is the servant of God and His messenger. For every matter among the affairs of this world and the hereafter, we ask for help through His permission. O You who guide the astray to the right path, show us the straight path. The path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, who are the Truthful, the Martyrs, and the Righteous. Do not show the path of those upon whom wrath was incurred, nor that of the astray. Amen!

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful, the Lord of the First and the Last. He who creates whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth. He who sends the prophets, upon them be peace, and the messengers and the believers with truth. The Powerful, the Irresistible, the Magnificent, the Sufficient, the Merciful, the Lord who is One. The Lord of all worlds, the deity in every place, the one who is glorified with excellence in every tongue. The Ancient and the Illuminator—He has made manifest to all creation whatsoever He has created. Most pure is He before whom necks bowed down, and the lofty and eminent became humble before Him. For the light of the Living, the Eternal, faces were humbled, and whoever committed wrongfulness suffered loss. O Living One, O Eternal, O the Prior, O the Later, O the First, O the Last, O the Manifest, O the Hidden, O Guardian, O Source of Goodness, O You who grant success in righteousness, O Acceptor of repentance, O Avenger, O Forgiver, O the Merciful, O Master of the Kingdom, Possessor of Glory and Generosity, O the Sustainer, O the Eternal, O the Everlasting, O the Perpetual. Know that with the remembrance of God, hearts find tranquility. O Living One, O Eternal, You see me, You hear my speech, both my humility and低眉, and my complaint as well. You are my aim, my request, and my hope. I am in need of You. You are the Knower of the hidden and the manifest. Nothing in the earth or the heavens is hidden from You. You are the Lord of the Mighty Throne. I ask from You beneficial knowledge, the upright religion, certain truth, and profound wisdom. O Eternal, O You whose essence— I ask from You the opening of the mysteries of the Hidden of the Hidden, with what is within it, until I may witness the remainder of the spirit. O Eternal, O Light of the heavens and the earth, and Lord of the Mighty Throne, I ask from You that You send blessings and peace upon our master Muhammad, may God bless him and grant him peace, and that You open for me the secrets of Your names, and subjugate all Your creation for obedience. Attach my heart to worship. Grant me from Your lights of guidance and the knowledge of Your secrets, so that I may become illuminated through Your grace. O the Subtle, act with subtlety. O Light, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy!

And whatever is asked from among the desires of this world and the hereafter shall be fulfilled.

And this is the second supplication: in one place with solitude, after each prayer, the five times, with purification, eighteen times for fourteen days. The supplication is this:

In the name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful. Praise be to God, Lord of all worlds. The Illuminator of the insights of the gnostics with the lights of knowledge and certainty, and the Attractor of the traces of the verifiers with the attractions of proximity and empowerment, and the Opener of the locks of the hearts of the monotheists with the keys of monotheism, and the Attractor thereof with the attractions of proximity and manifest opening. He who made all things beautiful in their creation, and began the creation of man from clay, then made his offspring from an extract of insignificant water. The Compassionate, the Merciful, the Wise, the Exalted, the Great, the Eternal, the Ancient, the Hearing, the Knowing, who inscribed the verses of monotheism with the pens of power in the breasts of the people of instruction, and elevated the breasts of the people of guidance in the ways of the mystery of the people of knowledge for the people of sanctity. And suffice it that the lovers are crying out, and He raised Moses, the one spoken to, with the speech of honoring, and honored His noble prophet with His saying: "And We have certainly given you the seven oft-repeated verses and the Great Quran." Master of the Day of Judgment, the Vanquisher of the tyrants and the rebellious, and the Destroyer of the transgressors and the unjust, and the Humiliator of the heads of the oppressors and the people of wrongdoing and the heretics. That is God, your Lord; there is no deity except Him, Lord of all worlds.
