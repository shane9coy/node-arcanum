# Page 651 (English translation)
**He who acknowledges Allah's greatness is His friend:**

The person who attains sincere submission (tamkīn) is rare. And he who has perceived the greatness of the Lord—Allah, Exalted, grants him authority over his own existence. Whoever remembers this name becomes humble in his movements and rest, and draws near to Allah, Exalted, and such humility overcomes him that at all times he remains fearful of Allah, Exalted.

**The Messenger ﷺ said: Prayer without humility is worthless:**

The Master, the Noble Prophet ﷺ, saw a man playing with his beard during prayer and said: "If there were humility in his heart, humility would also be upon his limbs"—meaning he would not play with his beard but would perform prayer with humility.

**Heartfelt humility is absolutely essential:**

This is the name of the worshippers. Along with it, one should recite the noble verses and the litany of remembrances (adhkār), and heartfelt humility (khushūʿ al-qalb) is most essential. Whoever writes this and ties it upon his head, Allah the Exalted increases his status. For this name, there is a spiritual practice (riyāḍat) of twenty-one days. Each day he should recite according to its number. The attendant spirits (mukallat) will present themselves, and all the difficulties of the reciter are removed. He fulfills the reciter's desire, and when he commands, he destroys his enemies. His dhikr is as follows:

بِسْمِ اللہِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِیْمِ o اَللّٰھُمَّ اَنْتَ الْمُتَکَبِّرُ لَا کَبِیرَ غَیْرَکَ لَکَ الْکَمَالُ الْمُطْلَقُ ذَالِکَ الْجَبَرُوتُ الْقَھْرِیُّ لَا اِلٰہَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ یَا اَوَّلُ یَا اٰخِرُ یَا ظَاھِرُ یَا بَاطِنُ اَسْتَلِکَ یَا قَھَّارُ یَا اَللّٰہُ یَا رَبُّ اَللّٰھُمَّ اَقْھَرْ اَعْدَائِیْ وَاَحْیِ قَلْبِیْ وَ اَیِّدْنِیْ بِالْخُضُوْعِ وَالْخُشُوْعِ حَتّٰی یَخْشَعَ لَکَ قَلْبِیْ وَ جَوَارِحِیْ بِالْخُضُوْعِ اِلَیْکَ یَا مُتَکَبِّرُ یَا اَمَانَ الْخَائِفِیْنَ یَا رَبَّ الْعٰلَمِیْنَ

Every person who persists in this supplication, Allah the Exalted opens the doors of mercy upon him, and he attains the honor of spiritual unveiling (kashf).

[Boxed Header: **Chapter: The Merits of the Name "Khalq" (Creator)**]

The Creator is the same Absolute Maker who is creating every thing. There is no other creator.

**They call him "Creator" who creates a thing without a precedent, pattern, or anyone's counsel:**

The meaning of khalq (creation) is ibdāʿ (bringing into existence without prior example), and the meaning of ibdāʿ is to bring into being without a like or pattern. The world of the kingdom (mulk) and the world of the divine realm (malakūt) are the realm of creation (ikhtirāʿ). Its detailed explanation is that the world of secrets and the higher world is the world of the Divine Command (ʿālam al-amr), and the world of the unseen and the lower world—which is the world of creation (ʿālam al-khalq)—all of these are in need of the divine determining numbers. Allah the Exalted says: أَلَا لَهُ الْخَلْقُ وَالْأَمْرُ—"Behold, to Him belongs creation and the Command."

**The reciters of this name are among the greatest saints (awliyā'):**

Whoever drinks deeply of the remembrances (adhkār) of Allah the Exalted, his mention is established in the primordial origins of the cosmos. So much so that spiritual unveiling (kashf) is granted to him, and the world of lights is witnessed by him. The original name of the world of creation (ʿālam al-khalq)—and all [damaged source/watermarked] remain filled within his heart. Then after this, the manifestation of the order of spiritual beings (tartīb rūḥāniyāt) occurs, whose news comes [damaged source/watermarked] and that is of the True, Glorious and Exalted.
