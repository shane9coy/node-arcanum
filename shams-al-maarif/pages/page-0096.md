# Page 96 (English translation)
94

And grant me a portion of Your glorification for Your saints in [matters of] the present and wealth when You draw them toward Yourself, and make me mighty at the gate of truth with steadfastness and presence, so that I may become a sign unto You. And extend my glory in the hearts of the people of faith, so that I may attain the secret of Your Essence at the manifestation of proof and evidence. O Hanān! O Mannān! You are the One who hears the secret and the whispered confidences, and You are the One who knows wisdom and piety, and You are the One who manifests in the hearts of Your beloved the secret of opening and manifestation. Rather, You hear what is more minute and more hidden, and You see with Your eye that does not sleep and whereon nothing is hidden—the crawling of the black ant upon the hard rock beneath layers of dust in the dark night. O Allah, I ask You by the subtle matters which You have placed within the even and the odd, and by the intricacies which You have put within sight, and by the realities which You have joined between hearing and sight, and by the intricacies which You have concealed within sight so that hearing might benefit from them, and by the excellences which You have hidden within hearing so that it might take the place of sight—that You grant me secrets descending within the encompassment of sight, and the witnessing of lights established at the containment of hearing and sight. And grant me light toward You and the permanence of contemplation for what arrives from Your Supreme Sanctity, and support me in understanding the demands of the soul with the intricacies of disputation toward You, O Collector of every good and Repeller of every evil, O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, I ask You, O Conqueror, O Near, O Holy, O Subsisting, O Self-subsisting, O Near, I ask You by Your Oneness and Your Sammad characteristics, O Self-subsisting One who does not sleep, and King who is not distressed—I ask You to bless our master Muhammad and the family of our master Muhammad, and to fulfill all my needs, both what I desire and what I do not desire, wherein lies Your pleasure, O Most Merciful of those who show mercy. And may Allah bless our master Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
