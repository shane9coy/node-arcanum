Chapter 2

On the Arrangement of Works in Occupations and Hours

May God Almighty grant us and you the success to understand the mysteries of His obedience and His names. We know that God Almighty has also mentioned the sun and moon in His honored book, the Noble Quran, as it is stated in one verse: "Each floats in an orbit" meaning the moon and sun both travel in the sky.

The detail of this is that when the moon descends into the station of Naṭaḥ, then its letter is Alif, and the mysteries of Alif become manifest. Accordingly, when it passes through this station, at that very time the spirituality of Alif manifests from this station. There appears among the celestial bodies wrath and anger. Especially among great people, more so—each according to his status and rank feels within his heart the effect of anger and rage. If you consider this, you will come to know. In this hour, it is necessary for a person to adopt tranquility and contentment and spend it in remembrance of God with purification or in abundant supplication.

Because sometimes in this hour a person's nature becomes extremely disturbed, and the person does not know the cause of his disturbance; rather, he is amazed by this disturbed nature. The reason for this is that Alif holds the first rank among the degrees of numbers and letters. No letter equals or parallels it. For this reason, restlessness occurs in the lower world—therefore understand it.

In this very hour, when you wish to disturb a worldly tyrant and arrogant person, you can do so. This is suitable for causing disturbance because the letter Alif is hot and dry. Its color is red, and red color possesses the nature of hot, dry fire. Fire is burning and imprisoning. When you perform an imprecation with the hot and dry names, at the moment when the moon rises in the eastern horizon in the station of Naṭaḥ, the aforementioned operation will be correct. However, write the letter Alif one hundred and eleven (111) times on a piece of red copper or iron or on a brick along with the name of that person (whom you intend to disturb), and after giving the appropriate incense, bury it in his house; and recite these names one hundred and eleven times according to the number of Alif, then the matter will be accomplished. The detail of this is as follows:

The operation for destroying or disturbing an enemy:

For the person upon whom you intend to perform the operation, expand the letters of his name and see which of the four natures—namely heat, dryness, coldness, and moisture—is dominant among them. Then take from among the fiery letters, separate them, and place them in your hands on the aforementioned tablet; add to them the letters of Mars, Naṭaḥ, and the moon. From these letters, construct a divine name, and with it recite a supplication for your need and for subjugating him. As he prays, so shall he succeed. An example of this is as follows: Zayd knows that Umar is ill. Write Umar as: ʿ m r, and write as Mars like: m r y kh
