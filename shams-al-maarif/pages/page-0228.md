This all and Surah Mulk are of great benefit for defeating enemies, subduing opponents, and subjugating the envious and wrathful ones. Surah Mulk has been given the name "Munjiyah" (the one who saves). It will intercede. It has twenty-three verses. Value it because its benefits are very great.

The Invitation (Da'wat) of Alam Nashrah and its Properties

This surah has amazing and strange properties. When you intend to practice with it, then fast for three days for the sake of Allah the Most High, recite this prayer seventy (70) times and recite "Ya Muhammad" seventy (70) times. Its effect will be present immediately: it will make you independent and heedless of creation. If you give it knowledge, it will bring you to Mecca Sharif in a moment, and whatever you request from it, it will bring to you. The name of this practice is "Riya Kul." The prayer is this:

I ask You, O Light of Lights, who existed before the ages and times, the Transcendent Essence, the Active Agent without parallel, the Holy, the Pure, the Exalted, the Conqueror, whom no place encompasses and whom no time resembles, the Creator of places, times, and moments. Blessed are You from the jewel of lights, which is the light of eternal sovereignty. O Lord of the most noble, by Your essence grant life to the spiritual souls characterized by luminous knowledge and purity, which have no end. O my Creator, O You who sees yet is not seen. By the greatness of Your power, even the Cherubim cannot bear to lift their faces from the veils of Your light. O Allah, O Most Great, by the right of "If We had sent down this Quran upon a mountain, you would have seen it tremble," exalt my remembrance. And I ask You by the essence of perpetuity, the ancient power of divinity, and the authority of lordship, to deliver me from the sea of this perishable creation through Your creative power and mighty status, and illuminate their faces within the sanctuary of Your lights. Set me apart among the individuals, shield me from the misfortune of isolation and association with opponents and the wicked, and reveal to me the hidden subtleties. O my Sustainer, O You who cloaks with permanence and grandeur, O Sublime, O Most High, O First of the first. Indeed, You have power over all things. He is Allah, the Creator, the Maker, the Designer.

O Allah, I ask You—O Lahuuti, Light of Lights—who existed before times and before fleeting ages, the peerless Active Agent, the Holy, the Pure, the Exalted, the Conqueror, whom no place encompasses and whom no time resembles, the Creator of places, times, and moments. You blessed from the Lahuuti eternal sovereign essence of lights' jewel. O Lord, grant me the garment of life for the spiritual souls characterized by luminous knowledge. O Creator, who sees yet is not seen—by Your mighty power, even the Cherubim cannot bear to lift their faces from the veils of Your light. O Allah, O Most Great, by the right of "If We had sent down this Quran upon a mountain, you would have seen it tremble," exalt my remembrance. I ask by the essence of eternal perpetuity, the ancient power of divinity, and the authority of lordship: deliver me from the sea of this perishable creation through Your creative power and mighty status, and illuminate my face within the sanctuary of Your lights. Set me apart among the individuals, shield me from the misfortune of isolation and association with opponents and the wicked, and reveal to me the hidden subtleties. O my Sustainer, O You who cloaks with permanence and grandeur, O Sublime, O Most High, O First of the first. Indeed, You have power over all things. Allah is the Creator, the Maker, the Designer.

The one who, with awakened fortune, walks in astonishment—that practice which comes from Allah the Most High is a [damaged source] goodness. Recognize its value.
