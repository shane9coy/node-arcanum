# Page 160 (English translation)

[Page 158]

He will observe good things and rare mysteries, that his heart will become filled with the love of God Most High and become completely weary of creation. Then Most High will place His love in the hearts of people. Have someone work with him, for it is the Sirr al-Kibr [Secret of Pride].

The practice for the destruction and ruin of the oppressor:

The aforementioned Sheikh says: "Whoever performs this practice regularly and mentions the Names—Allah, or Sami', or Sari', or Ba'is, or Badi', or Adl, or Mujib—one thousand times with complete sincerity and presence of heart, then whichever oppressor he supplicates against will be thwarted and ruined. If any person has been wronged by an oppressor, it is incumbent upon him to perform the dhikr of these Names in the first hour of Saturday, the second hour of Sunday, the first hour of Monday, the third hour of Monday night, the fourth hour of Tuesday night, the first hour of Wednesday night, the fifth hour of Thursday night, and the fourth hour of Friday night—then certainly, by the power of God Most High, before a week passes the oppressor will be destroyed. You will witness a strange thing, and God alone grants success. This is the form of the practice:

[A circular talisman or naqsh. It consists of three concentric circles. The outermost ring contains an Arabic inscription, appearing to be a Quranic verse (starting with "And those who disbelieve are about to alarm..." [Quran 64:8]). The middle ring contains further Arabic phrases. The innermost circle is divided into four sections by a cross shape; each section contains divine names such as "Ya Allah", "Ya Sami'", "Ya Fa'al", and "Ya 'Ali". There is additional Arabic text written vertically on both the left and right sides of the main diagram.]
