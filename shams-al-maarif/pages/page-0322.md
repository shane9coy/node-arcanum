These numbers belong to the name of Muhammad ﷺ. The Alif is of the prophets and that Hamza. For Muhammad, it is Abdullah, and for Ahmad, it is Abdur-Rahman.

Subtlety (Latif): The Latif has nineteen days, and also thirty days. This is in comparison to Amin. This is Sunnah, it is not obligatory—understand this.

The Waw of "Al-hamdu lillah" is the pole of the circle in the conjunction, and the circle of names, because it is the numerical friend and of little knowledge, because it points towards the station of the Divine Essence, which is the most noble of stations. This is the destination of twenty-one letters, in which all letters are included in every way. On the seventh, the "swama Allah" is placed.

In the first book, it was revealed that whoever reads a Surah with these letters, Allah the Exalted makes Hell forbidden for him.

These letters are gathered in two verses of Surah Al-An'am. Know that these letters are safety from the unjust one besides Allah. Some Gnostics say that whoever writes Surah Al-Fatiha on a Chinese glass in the first hour of Friday with musk and camphor, washes it with rose water and fills a bottle with it, then applies it to his face when going before kings and rulers, will obtain great acceptance. When it is written on clean vessels and washed, and the face of the patient is washed with it, healing will be obtained by the command of Allah the Exalted. For patients with excessive forgetfulness, it should be written on Chinese vessels, washed with rose water, and given to drink—the disease of forgetfulness will be cured and whatever they hear will be memorized. Whoever has pain or weakness in the eye should stand facing the moon of the first, second, or third night and keep looking at it, and pass his hands over the eyes and read Surah Al-Fatiha ten times, saying "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim" and "Ameen" each time. Then after reading "Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad" three times, let him pass his hands over the eyes and say seven times: "Shifa'an min kulli da'in bi-rahmatika ya arham al-rahimin"—then Allah the Exalted will remove every illness and every disease from his eyes, and Allah the Exalted has power over all things.

It is narrated from Anas that the Noble Prophet ﷺ said: When you lie on your bed and have read Al-Fatiha and "Qul Huwa Allah," you will be safe from everything except death. Whatever jewels and powers we had, we have presented before you; now it belongs to you, Allah willing, that you obtain them from them. The command to read Surah Al-Fatiha in every prayer has been given. The Noble Prophet ﷺ said that there is no Surah like Al-Fatiha in the Torah, the Gospel, and the Psalms. In this is the clarification that it should be read abundantly, because it has many benefits. If its explanation were to be given, it would become the burden of many—understand this matter.

**Remedy for Eye Pain**

Abdullah ibn Mas'ud narrates: I complained of pain in my eye to the Noble Prophet ﷺ, and he ﷺ said: Have the Holy Quran shown to you. I looked at the Holy Quran, and my eye became well.

Abu Bakr al-Siddiq said: There is a secret in every heart, and in the Glorious Quran, Allah's secret is in the opening of the Surahs.

Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, said: Every book has an essence, and the essence of the Quran is the letters of the alphabet.

Ibn Abbas was asked about Alif-Lam-Mim and Ra-Ha-Mim, and he said: They are the children of "Ar-Rahman." It has also been said that these are two names of the Quran. Suri al-Ka'bi and Qatadah said likewise, that Allah the Exalted has taken an oath by these letters. This is the saying of Ibn Abbas and Ikrimah. Some say that one of these letters indicates the names and attributes of Allah the Exalted.

Ibn Abbas narrates that Alif indicates Allah, Lam indicates Latif, and Mim indicates Malak (the King).

**Innumerable Benefits and Properties of the Disconnected Letters, Strength of Intellect and Understanding, Marriage of a Virgin Girl**

The scholars say that the disconnected letters indicate the names and attributes of Allah the Exalted. It has also been said that Alif indicates blessings, Lam indicates grace, and Mim indicates glory, i.e., greatness. Some say that Alif indicates Allah, Lam indicates Jibreel, and Mim indicates Muhammad ﷺ. Some Gnostics say that Alif indicates the First of the Exalted, i.e., the extreme from whom everything originates, and Lam indicates the grace of the Creator Exalted, i.e., grace upon perfection, from which the manifest appears. Some indicate non-divine names. Some Gnostics say that through these letters, Allah the Exalted has placed a secret in the Quran.
