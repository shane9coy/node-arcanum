# Page 137 (English translation)
135

That which is without pillars has its apparent aspect above the Throne (ʿArsh), its inner aspect is "the gift of gifts," its beginning is "the first of the first" and its end is "the last of the last." Its right is Eternity (Azal) and its left is Everlastingness (Abad), and the circle which is the right of the highest point (Awj) has as its apparent aspect the letter Wāw and as its inner aspect the letter Alif. This circle expresses the letter Alif because the Alif is apparent in relation to that which is above all creation (fawq al-khalāʾiq). This is because above it is "above measure" (fawq qays), and that height is the station of Divine Unity which is without comparison, attachment, likeness, restriction, expansion, above or below, right or left, behind or before. So understand, as far as the matter of the Supreme Attributes is concerned, that it is the secret of that barzakh (intermediate realm) which lies between the two shared Alifs that are between the inner and apparent aspects of the circle. Then you will behold the Lordly realities, understand the curtain (sarādist), and confirm the true contemplation. If you have解开 this symbol, you have entered safely into the Paradise of Knowledges and have become established with the Merciful. If you have become established with it, then as if you have become established above it, and by being established with it you have become filled with the grace of Divine Mercy. You have become acquainted with the limit of the front, the limit of the back, the limit of the apparent, and the limit of the hidden, and things that give glad tidings of the realities will be for you, and those that warn against your actions will be for you, so you will meet those about whom it has been said:

"Al-akhsarīna aʿmālan alladhīna ḍalla saʿyuhum fī l-ḥayāh d-dunyā"

Meaning: those who are among the losers in terms of actions whose striving has been lost in the worldly life, and they think that we do good works—the masters of the Curtain are those who disbelieved in the realm of the Veil regarding the verses of Allah and His union. In the realm of the Curtain their works were wasted, and Allah the Exalted will place no weight for them on the Day of Resurrection in the realm of the Barzakh. Their recompense in the realm of the Veil is Hell. This is because they disbelieved in the realm of the Chair, and in the realm of the Curtain they mocked My verses. Similarly, in the realm of the Curtain they mocked My messengers. So if these people enter into the Merciful circle, the mysteries of the kingdom show mercy upon them.

Notice: From saying "Lā ilāha illā Allāh" two circles are produced—one of negation and one of affirmation. The circle of negation belongs to the realm of the non-existent regarding the circles of negation or affirmation. Similarly, the circle of affirmation: in this circle there are two lines. One is negation in the realm of intelligibles, and the second line is affirmation in the realm of actualities. The line of negation consists of five letters, therefore the ends of options and intentions are five, which the power confirms through the third existence of the kingdom. These five relations are for the lowest soul that severed them—it has progressed in the circle of affirmation along with degrees that correspond to the number of its letters, so that at that time its life may be with the locality of witnessing: power with grace, pleasure with wisdom, and its sight with insight. Its witnessing is with reality, its hearing with unveiling, its imagination comprehends the reality of Divine Unity, and its knowledge with witnessing comprehends the lights of subsistence. So the soul becomes pleased at being informed of past states. It is preserved from errors in the discourse of wisdom, and by obtaining the sight of insight it becomes aware of the working of inspiration, and by means of the secret its hearing in the realm of reality establishes for it the vision (rūʾiyā). Therefore there is excellence in speech with wisdom, sight with insight, and hearing with the secret. Since "Lā ilāha illā Allāh" contains twelve letters, the circle of the perfection of existents is in Paradise, animals, and existents; it has four seasons and comprises the four seasons of the year. Or it is for the restriction of the entire circle of the world.

So both forms are complete, in the sense that its description was divided in the first definition, and in this Lordly way there are twelve signs and each day has twelve hours. Each month is established with one letter, rather each letter makes its circuit in the month. So the answer of the seeker is: until Allah is First, all becomes apparent, wisdom is established, guidance and benefits become clear, fruits, trees, and vegetables become abundant. This was the complete statement. Its detail is as follows: Allah the Exalted bound the foundation in the world of description in one day with His gentle manifestation and Lordly wisdom, and arranged the day into twelve portions. Each watch is one hour in which is the secret of the month. So the secret of the action of the Lord is in three hours. This action is the effect of renewal, in the three hours of inheritance
