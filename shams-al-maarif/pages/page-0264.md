Second Chapter: Explanation of the Name al-Raḥmān of Allāh the Exalted, Its Properties and Benefits

Acceptance of Supplication:
Its magic square is 5×4. Whoever writes it with negligence and keeps it with him in the station of Saturn, he shall remain in the pleasure of Allāh the Exalted; whoever sees him shall become gentle, and many blessings shall come to him. If it is dissolved in water and given to one suffering from fever to drink, the fever shall depart immediately. Whoever mentions it abundantly, Allāh the Exalted shall cast upon him a gaze of favor. For one whose name is Abd al-Raḥmān, its remembrance is recommended. Whoever persists in its remembrance, Allāh the Exalted's mercy shall be with him in all his affairs. And this is its figure:

It is narrated from Ḥaḍrat Ja'far, peace be upon him, that whoever on the day of Friday, after the afternoon prayer until sunset, writes and recites "Allāh" or "Raḥmān," his supplication shall be accepted. Its number is 298. This is [unclear] an incomplete number. Its number 47 points toward the name of Allāh. Its letter-names are 49, and 14 are ambiguous names, pointing toward the manifest aspect.

Third Chapter: Explanation of the Name al-Raḥīm of Allāh the Exalted, Acceptance of Supplication and Healing of Ailments

This is a majestic name. It is placed in a 4×4 square with negligence. Upon its bearer, Allāh the Exalted's mercy is present in all circumstances. Whoever mentions it abundantly, his supplications are accepted. This name grants protection from calamities. Its time of extraction is the station of the Moon. It benefits all hot fevers. And these verses should also be written with it: "Wa nunazzilu mina l-Qur'āni mā huwa shifā'un wa raḥmatun lil'mu'minīna" That is: "And We send down from the Qur'ān what is a healing and a mercy for the believers." Its remembrance is most appropriate for him, and the name al-Ẓāhir (the Manifest) should also be combined with it. Its number is 258. Also, the name al-Raḥīm is a composite rectangle.
